<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Jeremiah 48:1">1</a> God says this about Moab: &quot;Trouble is coming to Nebo because it&#39;s destroyed. Kiriathaim is captured and shamed. Misgab is terrified and upset.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Jeremiah 48:2">2</a> Moab will not be praised anymore; in Heshbon, they plan to destroy it. Come, let&#39;s wipe it out as a nation. Madmen, you too will be destroyed; the sword will chase you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Jeremiah 48:3">3</a> Cries and loud noises will come from Horonaim, along with destruction and major damage.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Jeremiah 48:4">4</a> Moab is ruined; her young children have made a cry heard.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Jeremiah 48:5">5</a> As people climb up Luhith, they keep crying. As they go down to Horonaim, enemies hear a loud sound of ruin.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Jeremiah 48:6">6</a> Run away, save yourselves, and be like a bush in the desert.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Jeremiah 48:7">7</a> Because you trusted in what you did and in your riches, you will be captured: and Chemosh will be taken away as a prisoner with his priests and leaders.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Jeremiah 48:8">8</a> The destroyer will come to every city, and every city will not be spared; the valley will also wither, and the flat land will be ruined, as God has said.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Jeremiah 48:9">9</a> Give Moab wings to fly away and escape, because its cities will be empty, with no one living in them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Jeremiah 48:10">10</a> Cursed is the one who does God&#39;s work with trickery, and cursed is the one who holds back his sword from drawing blood.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Jeremiah 48:11">11</a> Moab has lived comfortably since he was young, settled without being disturbed, and has not been moved from one container to another, nor has he been taken captive; so his ways have stayed the same, and his character has not changed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Jeremiah 48:12">12</a> So, look, the time will come, God says, when I will send travelers to him who will make him wander, empty his containers, and smash his jars.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Jeremiah 48:13">13</a> Moab will feel shame for Chemosh just as the people of Israel felt shame for their trust in Bethel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Jeremiah 48:14">14</a> How can you say, &quot;We are powerful and strong warriors&quot;?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Jeremiah 48:15">15</a> Moab is destroyed, and people have left its towns, and its best young men have been killed, says the King, whose name is God of armies.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Jeremiah 48:16">16</a> Moab&#39;s disaster is coming soon, and his trouble is speeding up.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Jeremiah 48:17">17</a> Everyone around him, mourn for him; and everyone who knows his name, ask, &quot;How is the powerful stick broken, and the lovely stick!&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Jeremiah 48:18">18</a> You who live in Dibon, come down from your high place and sit in thirst; for the destroyer of Moab will attack you and break down your safe places.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Jeremiah 48:19">19</a> People of Aroer, stand by the road, watch, and ask both the person who is running away and the person who has escaped, &quot;What happened?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Jeremiah 48:20">20</a> Moab is embarrassed; it is destroyed: wail and weep; say it in Arnon, that Moab is ruined.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Jeremiah 48:21">21</a> God&#39;s judgment has come to the flat land, to Holon, to Jahazah, and to Mephaath.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Jeremiah 48:22">22</a> On Dibon, Nebo, and Bethdiblathaim,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Jeremiah 48:23">23</a> On Kiriathaim, on Bethgamul, and on Bethmeon,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Jeremiah 48:24">24</a> And on Kerioth, and on Bozrah, and on all the cities of Moab, far and near.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Jeremiah 48:25">25</a> Moab&#39;s strength is gone, and his power is broken, says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Jeremiah 48:26">26</a> Make him drunk because he acted arrogantly against God. Moab will roll in his own throw-up, and he will be mocked as well.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Jeremiah 48:27">27</a> Didn&#39;t you mock Israel? Did you catch him with thieves? You&#39;ve been happy talking about him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Jeremiah 48:28">28</a> You who live in Moab, leave the cities, live in the cliffs, and be like the dove that builds her nest on the edge of a cave&#39;s entrance.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Jeremiah 48:29">29</a> We have heard how proud Moab is, very proud indeed, of his greatness, his arrogance, his pride, and his conceited heart.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Jeremiah 48:30">30</a> I know his anger, says God, but it won&#39;t happen like that; his lies won&#39;t succeed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Jeremiah 48:31">31</a> So, I will wail for Moab and cry for all of Moab; my heart will grieve for the people of Kirheres.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Jeremiah 48:32">32</a> O Sibmah vine, I will cry for you like the cries of Jazer: your branches have spread across the sea, even to Jazer&#39;s sea: the destroyer has attacked your summer fruit and harvest.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Jeremiah 48:33">33</a> Happiness and joy have been removed from the fertile fields and the land of Moab; I have made the wine stop flowing from the winepresses: no one will step on grapes with cheers; their cheers won&#39;t be heard.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Jeremiah 48:34">34</a> People are screaming from Heshbon to Elealeh and Jahaz, and from Zoar to Horonaim, like young cows. Even Nimrim&#39;s waters will be empty.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Jeremiah 48:35">35</a> I will stop people in Moab from making sacrifices on the high hills and burning incense to their gods, says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Jeremiah 48:36">36</a> So my heart will cry for Moab like flutes, and my heart will be like flutes for the people of Kir Heres, for the wealth they gained is gone.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Jeremiah 48:37">37</a> Every head will be shaved, every beard trimmed; all hands will be scratched, and around the waist, there will be rough cloth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Jeremiah 48:38">38</a> People will mourn on all the rooftops in Moab and in its streets, because I have shattered Moab like a useless pot, says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="Jeremiah 48:39">39</a> They will cry out, &quot;How it is destroyed! How Moab has run away in shame!&quot; So Moab will become a joke and a terror to all nearby.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="Jeremiah 48:40">40</a> For this is what God says: Look, he will soar like an eagle and spread his wings over Moab.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="Jeremiah 48:41">41</a> Kerioth is captured, and the strongholds are taken by surprise, and on that day, the brave soldiers in Moab will be as scared as a woman having a baby.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="Jeremiah 48:42">42</a> Moab will be ruined and no longer be a nation because it boasted against God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v43"><a class="verse-num" href="#v43" aria-label="Jeremiah 48:43">43</a> Danger, a hole, and a trap will come to you, people of Moab, says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v44"><a class="verse-num" href="#v44" aria-label="Jeremiah 48:44">44</a> The one who runs from danger will fall into a hole, and whoever climbs out of the hole will get caught in a trap, because I will bring disaster on Moab when I punish them, says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v45"><a class="verse-num" href="#v45" aria-label="Jeremiah 48:45">45</a> Those who ran away stayed in the shade of Heshbon because of the enemy. But a fire will start from Heshbon, and a flame from Sihon, and it will burn the edges of Moab and the tops of the noisy people&#39;s heads.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v46"><a class="verse-num" href="#v46" aria-label="Jeremiah 48:46">46</a> Sadness comes to you, Moab! The followers of Chemosh are destroyed: your sons are captured, and your daughters are captured.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v47"><a class="verse-num" href="#v47" aria-label="Jeremiah 48:47">47</a> But I will restore Moab from captivity in the future, says God. This is God&#39;s judgment on Moab.</p>
