<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Jeremiah 3:1">1</a> People ask, &quot;If a man divorces his wife, and she leaves him to be with another man, can he take her back? Won&#39;t that make the land very unclean?&quot; But you have been unfaithful with many partners; still, come back to me, says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Jeremiah 3:2">2</a> Look up to the high places and see where you haven&#39;t been with anyone. You have waited along the paths for others, like an Arabian in the desert; and you have made the land impure with your unfaithfulness and your evil ways.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Jeremiah 3:3">3</a> So the rains have been stopped, and there was no late rain; your face was like a prostitute&#39;s, you refused to feel shame.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Jeremiah 3:4">4</a> Won&#39;t you from now on call out to me, &#39;My Father, you have led me since I was young&#39;?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Jeremiah 3:5">5</a> Will God stay angry forever? Will he keep his anger to the end? You have said and done bad things as much as you could.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Jeremiah 3:6">6</a> God also said to me in King Josiah&#39;s time, &quot;Have you seen what unfaithful Israel has done? She has gone up on every high mountain and under every green tree, and there acted like a prostitute.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Jeremiah 3:7">7</a> After she did all this, I said, &quot;Come back to me.&quot; But she did not return. And her unfaithful sister Judah saw this.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Jeremiah 3:8">8</a> I saw that, even though Israel had been unfaithful and I divorced her, her deceitful sister Judah was not afraid and also acted unfaithfully.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Jeremiah 3:9">9</a> She became impure because of her many lovers, and she made the land impure too, by worshiping idols made of wood and stone.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Jeremiah 3:10">10</a> Despite this, Judah, her unfaithful sister, did not fully turn back to me but only pretended to, says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Jeremiah 3:11">11</a> God told me, &quot;Israel, even though unfaithful, has proven to be better than the deceitful Judah.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Jeremiah 3:12">12</a> Go and speak these words to the north, and say, &quot;Come back, unfaithful Israel,&quot; says God, &quot;and I won&#39;t let my anger come down on you because I am merciful,&quot; says God, &quot;and I won&#39;t stay angry forever.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Jeremiah 3:13">13</a> Admit your wrongs, admit you&#39;ve sinned against God, by following foreign customs under every green tree, and not listening to me, says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Jeremiah 3:14">14</a> Come back to me, you who have turned away, says God; for I am committed to you: I will choose one person from each city and two from each family, and I will bring you to Zion.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Jeremiah 3:15">15</a> I will give you leaders who please me, and they will teach you with knowledge and understanding.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Jeremiah 3:16">16</a> When you grow and become many in the land, in those days, God says, they will no longer talk about the ark of God&#39;s covenant. It will not be thought of, remembered, or visited, and that will not happen again.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Jeremiah 3:17">17</a> At that time, people will call Jerusalem God&#39;s throne; all nations will come together there to honor God. And they will no longer follow their own evil thoughts.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Jeremiah 3:18">18</a> During that time, the people of Judah will join with the people of Israel, and together they will come from the northern lands to the land I gave your ancestors as their inheritance.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Jeremiah 3:19">19</a> But I said, &quot;How can I place you among the children and give you a beautiful land, a wonderful inheritance from many nations?&quot; And I said, &quot;You will call me &#39;My Father,&#39; and will not turn away from me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Jeremiah 3:20">20</a> Just as a wife dishonestly leaves her husband, so you have been dishonest with me, O people of Israel, says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Jeremiah 3:21">21</a> A cry was heard on the hills, the weeping and prayers of Israel&#39;s children: because they have twisted their path, and they have forgotten God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Jeremiah 3:22">22</a> Come back, you who have strayed, and I will heal your wandering. We are returning to you because you are our God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Jeremiah 3:23">23</a> Hoping for rescue from the hills or many mountains is truly useless: only from God is Israel&#39;s salvation.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Jeremiah 3:24">24</a> Shame has ruined our ancestors&#39; hard work since we were young; their animals, their sons, and their daughters.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Jeremiah 3:25">25</a> We lay in our shame, and our disgrace covers us: because we have sinned against God, both we and our ancestors, from our youth to this day, and have not listened to God&#39;s voice.</p>
