<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Jeremiah 23:1">1</a> God says, &quot;Trouble will come to the leaders who ruin and disperse the sheep of my land!&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Jeremiah 23:2">2</a> So this is what God of Israel says to the leaders who take care of my people: You have scattered my sheep and made them run away, and you did not care for them. Look, I will punish you for the bad things you&#39;ve done, says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Jeremiah 23:3">3</a> I will bring the remaining ones of my flock from all the countries where I sent them, and they will return to their homes; they will have many children and grow in number.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Jeremiah 23:4">4</a> I will put shepherds in charge of them who will take care of them; they will not be afraid or discouraged or be in need anymore, says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Jeremiah 23:5">5</a> Look, the time will come, God says, when I will bring forth a righteous descendant from David, and a King will rule successfully, carrying out fair and just decisions on the earth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Jeremiah 23:6">6</a> During his time, Judah will be saved and Israel will live in safety. And he will be called God Our Righteousness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Jeremiah 23:7">7</a> So, God says, &quot;Look, the time is coming when people won&#39;t say anymore, &#39;God is alive, who brought the Israelites out of Egypt.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Jeremiah 23:8">8</a> But God lives, who brought and led the descendants of Israel out of the north and from all countries where I had sent them; and they will live in their own land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Jeremiah 23:9">9</a> My heart is broken because of the prophets; my whole body trembles; I am like a drunk man, beaten by wine, because of God and his holy words.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Jeremiah 23:10">10</a> The land is full of cheaters; because of cursing, the land is sad; the nice parts of the desert have dried out, people&#39;s actions are bad, and their power is not used rightly.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Jeremiah 23:11">11</a> Both the prophet and the priest are unholy; yes, I have seen their evil in my house, says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Jeremiah 23:12">12</a> So their path will be like slippery roads in the dark: they will be pushed and will fall on it, because I will bring disaster on them, the time when I punish them, says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Jeremiah 23:13">13</a> I have seen foolishness in Samaria&#39;s prophets; they spoke messages from Baal and led my people Israel astray.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Jeremiah 23:14">14</a> I have also seen terrible things among Jerusalem&#39;s prophets: they are unfaithful and live in lies; they encourage the wicked so no one turns from their evil ways; to me, they are like Sodom and its people like Gomorrah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Jeremiah 23:15">15</a> So this is what God says about the prophets: I will give them bitter food to eat and poison water to drink, because the prophets of Jerusalem have spread corruption throughout the land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Jeremiah 23:16">16</a> God says, &quot;Don&#39;t listen to the prophets who speak to you; they fill you with false hopes. They talk about visions from their own minds, not from God.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Jeremiah 23:17">17</a> They keep telling those who hate me, &quot;God has said, you will have peace&quot;; and they tell everyone who follows their own desires, &quot;No harm will come to you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Jeremiah 23:18">18</a> Who has been in God&#39;s council and understood and listened to his message? Who has paid attention to his message and heard it?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Jeremiah 23:19">19</a> Look, a violent storm from God has come out in anger, a terrible storm: it will hit the wicked very hard.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Jeremiah 23:20">20</a> God&#39;s anger will not stop until He has done what He plans. In the future, you will fully understand this.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Jeremiah 23:21">21</a> I did not send these prophets, but they went; I did not speak to them, but they spoke my message.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Jeremiah 23:22">22</a> If they had followed my advice and had made my people listen to my words, then they would have turned away from their bad behavior and their wicked actions.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Jeremiah 23:23">23</a> Am I not a close God, says God, and not a distant one?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Jeremiah 23:24">24</a> Can anyone hide in secret places where I won&#39;t see him? says God. Don&#39;t I fill heaven and earth? says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Jeremiah 23:25">25</a> I have heard the prophets who tell lies in my name, saying, &quot;I had a dream, I had a dream.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Jeremiah 23:26">26</a> How long will the prophets who tell lies plan this in their hearts? Yes, they are prophets led by their own deceit.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Jeremiah 23:27">27</a> They try to make my people forget my name with the dreams they share with each other, just like their ancestors forgot my name for Baal.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Jeremiah 23:28">28</a> The prophet who has a dream should share that dream; and whoever has my message should speak it truthfully. What do the worthless bits matter compared to the grain? says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Jeremiah 23:29">29</a> Isn&#39;t my word like a fire? says God, and like a hammer that breaks the rock into pieces?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Jeremiah 23:30">30</a> So, I am against the prophets who take my words from each other, says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Jeremiah 23:31">31</a> Look, I am against the prophets, says God, who speak on their own and claim, &#39;God says.&#39;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Jeremiah 23:32">32</a> Look, I am against those who tell false dreams, says God, and share them, leading my people astray with their lies and carelessness; I did not send them or tell them to do this: so they will not help my people at all, says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Jeremiah 23:33">33</a> When the people, a prophet, or a priest ask you, &quot;What is God&#39;s message?&quot; You should say, &quot;What message? God says He will abandon you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Jeremiah 23:34">34</a> If a prophet, a priest, or anyone else claims, &quot;This is God&#39;s message,&quot; I will punish that person and his family.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Jeremiah 23:35">35</a> Everyone should ask their neighbor and their brother, &quot;What has God answered?&quot; and &quot;What has God said?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Jeremiah 23:36">36</a> You should not talk about God&#39;s burden anymore: everyone&#39;s own words will be their burden because you have twisted the words of the living God, our God of all.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Jeremiah 23:37">37</a> You should ask the prophet, &quot;What has God replied to you?&quot; and &quot;What has God said?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Jeremiah 23:38">38</a> Since you say, &quot;God&#39;s message,&quot; this is what God says: &quot;Because you call it &#39;God&#39;s message,&#39; even though I told you not to call it &#39;God&#39;s message,&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="Jeremiah 23:39">39</a> So, look, I myself will completely forget you, and I will leave you, and the city I gave to you and your ancestors, and I will throw you out of my sight.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="Jeremiah 23:40">40</a> I will make you suffer lasting shame and disgrace that will never be forgotten.</p>
