<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Jeremiah 19:1">1</a> God says, &quot;Go and get a clay jar from a potter, and bring some of the elders of the people and some of the senior priests.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Jeremiah 19:2">2</a> Go to the Valley of Hinnom, near the entrance of the east gate, and announce the words I will give you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Jeremiah 19:3">3</a> Say, &quot;Listen to God&#39;s message, kings of Judah and people of Jerusalem: God of the armies of heaven, the God of Israel, says, &#39;Look, I will bring disaster on this place, and whoever hears about it will have their ears ring.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Jeremiah 19:4">4</a> Because they left me and made this place strange, and burned incense here to other gods that neither they, their ancestors, nor Judah&#39;s kings knew, and filled this place with innocent blood;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Jeremiah 19:5">5</a> They also made high places for Baal, to burn their sons as sacrifices to Baal, something I never commanded or mentioned, nor did it ever cross my mind.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Jeremiah 19:6">6</a> So, look, the days are coming, says God, when this place will not be called Tophet or the Valley of Hinnom&#39;s son, but the Valley of Slaughter.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Jeremiah 19:7">7</a> I will ruin the plans of Judah and Jerusalem here; I will make them die by the sword in front of their enemies, by those who want to kill them: and I will leave their dead bodies as food for the birds in the sky, and for the animals on the land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Jeremiah 19:8">8</a> I will make this city empty and a place for hissing; everyone who passes by will be shocked and hiss at all its disasters.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Jeremiah 19:9">9</a> I will make them eat their sons&#39; and daughters&#39; flesh, and they will eat their friends&#39; flesh during the siege and hardship that their enemies and those who want to kill them will cause.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Jeremiah 19:10">10</a> Then you will break the bottle while the men with you watch.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Jeremiah 19:11">11</a> Tell them, &#39;This is what God says: Just as a clay pot is smashed and can&#39;t be fixed, I will break this nation and this city. They will bury the dead in Tophet until there&#39;s no more room to bury them.&#39;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Jeremiah 19:12">12</a> I will do this to this place, says God, and to its people, and even make this city like Tophet.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Jeremiah 19:13">13</a> The homes in Jerusalem and the homes of Judah&#39;s kings will be unclean like Tophet because on their roofs they burned incense to the sky&#39;s stars and gave drink offerings to other gods.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Jeremiah 19:14">14</a> Jeremiah returned from Tophet, where God had sent him to give his message. He stood in the courtyard of God&#39;s house and spoke to all the people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Jeremiah 19:15">15</a> God of Israel says, I will make all the bad things happen to this city and its towns that I said would happen, because they were stubborn and did not listen to my words.</p>
