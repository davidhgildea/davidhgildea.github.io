<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Jeremiah 16:1">1</a> God&#39;s message came to me, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Jeremiah 16:2">2</a> Do not marry or have sons or daughters here.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Jeremiah 16:3">3</a> God says this about the boys and girls born here, about their mothers who gave birth to them, and about their fathers who fathered them in this land;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Jeremiah 16:4">4</a> They will die painful deaths; no one will mourn for them; they won&#39;t be buried; they&#39;ll be like waste on the ground. They will be killed by war and hunger; and their bodies will be food for the birds and wild animals.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Jeremiah 16:5">5</a> God says, &quot;Do not go into the house of mourning, nor grieve or weep for them, for I have removed my peace from these people,&quot; says God, &quot;along with kindness and compassion.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Jeremiah 16:6">6</a> Everyone, both important and unimportant, will die in this land. They won&#39;t be buried, and no one will mourn, cut themselves, or shave their heads for them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Jeremiah 16:7">7</a> People will not cut themselves in grief for others or offer comfort for the dead, nor will anyone give a comforting drink for someone&#39;s father or mother.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Jeremiah 16:8">8</a> Do not go into the place where people feast, to sit with them and eat and drink.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Jeremiah 16:9">9</a> God, the God of Israel, says: I will make the sounds of joy and celebration, the voices of the bride and groom, stop in this place before your very eyes during your lifetime.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Jeremiah 16:10">10</a> When you tell these people everything I’ve said, they will ask you, &quot;Why has God declared such great disaster for us? What wrong have we done? What sin have we committed against our God?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Jeremiah 16:11">11</a> Then you will tell them, &#39;Your ancestors left me,&#39; says God, &#39;followed other gods, served and worshiped them, abandoned me, and didn&#39;t follow my laws.&#39;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Jeremiah 16:12">12</a> You have acted worse than your ancestors; look, each of you follows their own wicked desires, refusing to listen to me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Jeremiah 16:13">13</a> So I will throw you out of this land into a land you and your ancestors don&#39;t know. There you will worship other gods all the time. I will not be kind to you there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Jeremiah 16:14">14</a> So, listen, the days will come, God says, that people will no longer say, &quot;God lives, who brought the Israelites out of Egypt.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Jeremiah 16:15">15</a> But God lives, who brought the people of Israel from the northern land and all the places where he scattered them: and I will return them to their own land that I gave to their ancestors.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Jeremiah 16:16">16</a> Look, I will send many fishermen, says God, and they will fish for people; then I will send many hunters, and they will hunt for them on every mountain, on every hill, and from the rock holes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Jeremiah 16:17">17</a> I see all their actions; nothing they do is hidden from me, nor is their wrongdoing hidden from my sight.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Jeremiah 16:18">18</a> First, I will repay their wrongdoing and sin twice over, because they have polluted my land and filled my inheritance with the dead bodies of their disgusting and hateful things.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Jeremiah 16:19">19</a> God, my strength and safe place, when trouble comes, people from all over the world will turn to you and admit that what their ancestors believed was false and useless.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Jeremiah 16:20">20</a> Can a person make their own gods when they are not really gods?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Jeremiah 16:21">21</a> So, I will let them see who I am this time, let them know my power and strength; and they will understand that my name is God.</p>
