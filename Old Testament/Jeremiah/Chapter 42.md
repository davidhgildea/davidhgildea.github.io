<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Jeremiah 42:1">1</a> Then all the army leaders, Johanan son of Kareah, Jezaniah son of Hoshaiah, and everyone, both small and great, came forward.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Jeremiah 42:2">2</a> They said to the prophet Jeremiah, &quot;Please listen to our request and pray to God for us, for all of us who remain, because only a few of us are left from many, as you can see.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Jeremiah 42:3">3</a> So that God may show us the path to walk in and what we should do.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Jeremiah 42:4">4</a> Then the prophet Jeremiah said to them, &quot;I have listened to you; I will pray to God for you as you have asked; and whatever answer God gives, I will tell you everything; I will hide nothing from you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Jeremiah 42:5">5</a> Then they said to Jeremiah, &quot;May God be a true and trustworthy witness between us, if we do not follow all the instructions that your God gives you for us.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Jeremiah 42:6">6</a> Whether it is good or bad, we will listen to God, to whom we sent you; so that things will go well for us when we listen to God&#39;s voice.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Jeremiah 42:7">7</a> After ten days, God spoke to Jeremiah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Jeremiah 42:8">8</a> He called Johanan, Kareah&#39;s son, and all the army leaders with him, and all the people, from the least to the greatest.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Jeremiah 42:9">9</a> He said to them, &quot;This is what God, the God of Israel, says, to whom you sent me to offer your request before Him;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Jeremiah 42:10">10</a> If you choose to stay in this land, then I will strengthen you and not destroy you, and I will settle you and not uproot you, because I regret the harm I have caused you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Jeremiah 42:11">11</a> Do not fear the king of Babylon, whom you are scared of; do not fear him, says God: for I am with you to save you and rescue you from his power.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Jeremiah 42:12">12</a> I will show you kindness so that you will be shown kindness, and bring you back to your own land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Jeremiah 42:13">13</a> But if you say, &quot;We won&#39;t live in this land or listen to the voice of God,&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Jeremiah 42:14">14</a> No, we will go to Egypt, where we won&#39;t see war, hear the trumpet, or be hungry for bread; and we will live there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Jeremiah 42:15">15</a> So now, listen to what God tells you, you who remain from Judah. This is what the Lord Almighty, the God of Israel, says: If you are fully determined to go to Egypt and live there;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Jeremiah 42:16">16</a> It will happen that the sword you were afraid of will catch up with you in Egypt, and the hunger you feared will also reach you there in Egypt; and there you will die.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Jeremiah 42:17">17</a> Everyone who goes to live in Egypt will die by war, hunger, and disease. No one will survive or escape the disaster I will bring on them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Jeremiah 42:18">18</a> God of Israel says: Just as my anger has been poured out on the people of Jerusalem, it will be poured out on you when you go to Egypt. You will become a curse and a shocking example, and you will never see this place again.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Jeremiah 42:19">19</a> God has told you, people left from Judah, do not go to Egypt: be sure that I have warned you today.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Jeremiah 42:20">20</a> You pretended in your hearts when you sent me to God, saying, &quot;Ask God to help us, and tell us everything God says, and we will do it.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Jeremiah 42:21">21</a> Today I told you, but you did not listen to God or follow what he sent me to tell you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Jeremiah 42:22">22</a> So now, be sure that you will die from war, hunger, and disease in the place you want to go to live.</p>
