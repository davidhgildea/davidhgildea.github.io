<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Jeremiah 10:1">1</a> Listen to the message that God is giving you, people of Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Jeremiah 10:2">2</a> God says, &quot;Don&#39;t follow the heathen&#39;s ways, and don&#39;t be scared of the sky signs; the heathen are scared of them.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Jeremiah 10:3">3</a> The ways of the people are worthless: someone cuts down a tree from the woods, made by a worker using an axe.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Jeremiah 10:4">4</a> They decorate it with silver and gold; they use nails and hammers to secure it so it doesn&#39;t move.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Jeremiah 10:5">5</a> They stand straight like palm trees but cannot speak. They need to be carried since they cannot walk. Do not fear them; they cannot do harm, nor can they do good.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Jeremiah 10:6">6</a> No one is like you, God; you are great, and your name is powerful.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Jeremiah 10:7">7</a> Who wouldn&#39;t respect you, King of nations? It belongs to you; because among all the wise people of the nations, and in all their kingdoms, no one is like you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Jeremiah 10:8">8</a> But they are all senseless and foolish; their idol is just a teaching about worthless things.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Jeremiah 10:9">9</a> Silver made into sheets is brought from Tarshish and gold from Uphaz, made by craftsmen and metalworkers. Their clothes are blue and purple, all created by skilled artisans.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Jeremiah 10:10">10</a> God is the real God, the living God, and a forever king: when he is angry, the earth will shake, and the countries won&#39;t stand his anger.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Jeremiah 10:11">11</a> Tell them, &quot;The gods who did not create the sky and the earth will vanish from the earth and from under the sky.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Jeremiah 10:12">12</a> God made the earth with his power, set up the world with his wisdom, and spread out the skies with his understanding.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Jeremiah 10:13">13</a> When he speaks, many waters form in the sky, and he makes vapors rise from the earth&#39;s edges; he creates lightning with rain, and brings wind from his storerooms.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Jeremiah 10:14">14</a> Everyone is foolish in what they know; every metal worker is ashamed of their idol, because what they pour as an image is a lie, and there is no life in them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Jeremiah 10:15">15</a> They are worthless and make mistakes; when it&#39;s time for them to be judged, they will be destroyed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Jeremiah 10:16">16</a> Jacob&#39;s share is not like theirs: he is the maker of everything; and Israel is the tribe he inherits: God of all armies is his name.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Jeremiah 10:17">17</a> Collect your belongings from the land, person living in the strong place.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Jeremiah 10:18">18</a> God says, &quot;I will throw out the people living in the land this time and trouble them so they realize it.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Jeremiah 10:19">19</a> I am in trouble because I am hurt! My injury is serious, but I thought, This is truly sad, and I must endure it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Jeremiah 10:20">20</a> My tent is ruined, all its ropes are snapped; my children have left me, and they are gone. No one is left to put up my tent or hang my curtains.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Jeremiah 10:21">21</a> The leaders have become foolish and have not looked for God: so they will not succeed, and all their flocks will be spread out.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Jeremiah 10:22">22</a> Look, the sound of the report has arrived, and a big disturbance from the north to leave the towns of Judah empty, and a home for dragons.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Jeremiah 10:23">23</a> God, I understand that a person&#39;s path is not decided by themselves: it is not for the one who walks to choose their own way.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Jeremiah 10:24">24</a> God, correct me fairly, not in anger, or I will be destroyed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Jeremiah 10:25">25</a> Release your anger on the nations that don&#39;t know you, and on the peoples who don&#39;t pray to you: for they have attacked Jacob, destroyed him, and left his home empty.</p>
