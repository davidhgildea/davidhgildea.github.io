<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Jeremiah 9:1">1</a> I wish my head were full of water, and my eyes were a stream of tears, so I could cry day and night for the people who have been killed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Jeremiah 9:2">2</a> I wish I had a traveler&#39;s shelter in the desert so I could leave my people and get away from them; they are all unfaithful, a group of liars.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Jeremiah 9:3">3</a> They twist their words like a bow for lies; they are not brave for the truth on earth; they go from one bad act to another and do not know me, says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Jeremiah 9:4">4</a> Be careful of everyone around you, and don&#39;t trust any brother fully; for every brother may betray you, and every neighbor might spread lies.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Jeremiah 9:5">5</a> Everyone will trick their neighbor and not tell the truth; they have trained their tongue to lie and exhaust themselves doing wrong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Jeremiah 9:6">6</a> Your home is in the middle of lies; because of these lies, they choose not to know me, says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Jeremiah 9:7">7</a> So this is what God says: &quot;Look, I will test them and refine them; because what else can I do for my people&#39;s children?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Jeremiah 9:8">8</a> Their tongue is like a shot arrow; it tells lies. They talk kindly to their neighbor with their mouth, but in their heart, they set a trap.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Jeremiah 9:9">9</a> Won&#39;t I punish them for these acts? says God. Won&#39;t I take revenge on a nation like this with all my being?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Jeremiah 9:10">10</a> I will cry and wail for the mountains, and mourn for the desert settlements, because they are scorched and no one can travel through them; no one can hear the cattle, and the birds of the sky and the animals have fled; they have disappeared.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Jeremiah 9:11">11</a> I will turn Jerusalem into piles of rubble, a home for dragons; I will make the towns of Judah empty, with no one living there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Jeremiah 9:12">12</a> Who is the wise person who can understand this? And who has God spoken to so they can explain it, why the land is dying and scorched like a desert, empty and unused?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Jeremiah 9:13">13</a> God says, &quot;They have left my law which I gave them, have not listened to my voice, or followed it.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Jeremiah 9:14">14</a> But they followed the desires of their own heart and worshiped Baal, as their ancestors taught them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Jeremiah 9:15">15</a> So this is what God, the God of Israel, says: Look, I will make them, this nation, eat bitter plants, and drink poisoned water.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Jeremiah 9:16">16</a> I will spread them among nations that neither they nor their ancestors have known, and I will chase them with a sword until they are destroyed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Jeremiah 9:17">17</a> God says, &quot;Think about this, and ask the weeping women to come; invite the skilled women to come too.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Jeremiah 9:18">18</a> Let them hurry and cry for us, so our eyes fill with tears, and our eyelids overflow with water.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Jeremiah 9:19">19</a> A sound of crying comes from Zion, &quot;We are ruined! We are totally ashamed because we left our land, our homes have thrown us out.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Jeremiah 9:20">20</a> Listen to God&#39;s word, women, take in his message, show your daughters how to cry, and teach each other how to mourn.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Jeremiah 9:21">21</a> Death has climbed through our windows and entered our palaces, to take away children from outside, and young men from the streets.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Jeremiah 9:22">22</a> Say this, God says, &quot;Dead bodies will lie like waste on the ground, like cut grain left by the reaper, with no one to pick them up.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Jeremiah 9:23">23</a> God says, &quot;Don&#39;t let the wise man boast about his wisdom, don&#39;t let the strong man boast about his strength, don&#39;t let the rich man boast about his wealth.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Jeremiah 9:24">24</a> But if anyone wants to boast, let them boast about this: that they understand and know God, who shows kindness, fairness, and what is right on the earth, because God takes pleasure in these things.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Jeremiah 9:25">25</a> Look, a time is coming, says God, when I will punish both the circumcised and the uncircumcised.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Jeremiah 9:26">26</a> Egypt, Judah, Edom, Ammon&#39;s children, Moab, and everyone from faraway places living in the desert: all these nations are not circumcised, and all Israel&#39;s people are not circumcised in heart.</p>
