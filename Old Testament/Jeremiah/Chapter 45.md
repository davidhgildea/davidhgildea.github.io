<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Jeremiah 45:1">1</a> Jeremiah the prophet told Baruch, son of Neriah, these words after Baruch wrote them down as Jeremiah said them. This happened in the fourth year of Jehoiakim&#39;s rule, Josiah&#39;s son, who was the king of Judah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Jeremiah 45:2">2</a> God, the God of Israel, says this to you, O Baruch:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Jeremiah 45:3">3</a> You said, &quot;I am in trouble now! For God has made my sadness greater; I grew weak as I sighed, and I can&#39;t find peace.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Jeremiah 45:4">4</a> Tell him this: God says, &quot;Look, I will destroy what I have built and uproot what I have planted, all across the land.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Jeremiah 45:5">5</a> Are you looking for great things for yourself? Don&#39;t look for them, because I will bring trouble to everyone, says God, but I will let you escape with your life wherever you go.</p>
