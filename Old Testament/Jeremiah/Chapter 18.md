<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Jeremiah 18:1">1</a> The message that came to Jeremiah from God said,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Jeremiah 18:2">2</a> Get up and go to the potter&#39;s house, where I will make you hear my message.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Jeremiah 18:3">3</a> I went to the potter&#39;s house and saw him making something on the wheel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Jeremiah 18:4">4</a> The clay pot the potter was making got ruined in his hands, so he made a different pot in a way he thought was best.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Jeremiah 18:5">5</a> Then God spoke to me, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Jeremiah 18:6">6</a> People of Israel, can&#39;t I deal with you as a potter does? says God. Look, just as clay is in the potter&#39;s control, so are you in my control, people of Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Jeremiah 18:7">7</a> When I decide to talk about a nation or kingdom, to uproot, tear down, or destroy it;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Jeremiah 18:8">8</a> If that country, which I warned, stops doing bad things, I will not punish them as I planned.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Jeremiah 18:9">9</a> When I decide to talk about a nation or kingdom, to make it grow and succeed;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Jeremiah 18:10">10</a> If it does wrong in my eyes by not listening to me, then I will change my mind about the good I planned to do for them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Jeremiah 18:11">11</a> Now go and tell the people of Judah and those living in Jerusalem that God says: &quot;Look, I am planning trouble for you and making a plan against you. So turn away from your bad ways and do what is right.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Jeremiah 18:12">12</a> They said, &quot;There is no hope. We will follow our own plans and each of us will act on the evil desires of our heart.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Jeremiah 18:13">13</a> So God says, ask the nations, has anyone heard of such a thing: the virgin of Israel has done something very awful.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Jeremiah 18:14">14</a> Will a man abandon the snow of Lebanon from the mountain fields? Or will he leave behind the cold, flowing waters from afar?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Jeremiah 18:15">15</a> Because my people have forgotten me, they have burned incense to worthless things, causing them to stray from the old paths and walk on unmade ways.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Jeremiah 18:16">16</a> To turn their land into ruins, always empty; everyone who goes by will be shocked and shake their head.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Jeremiah 18:17">17</a> I will scatter them like an east wind before the enemy; I will show them my back, not my face, on their day of trouble.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Jeremiah 18:18">18</a> They said, &quot;Come, let&#39;s make plans against Jeremiah, because the teachings won&#39;t disappear from the priest, advice won&#39;t vanish from the wise, and messages won&#39;t stop from the prophet. Come, let&#39;s attack him with our words and not listen to anything he says.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Jeremiah 18:19">19</a> Listen to me, God, and pay attention to the voices of those who argue against me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Jeremiah 18:20">20</a> Should bad be paid back for good? They have made a trap for me. Remember I spoke to you on their behalf to do them good and to keep your anger from them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Jeremiah 18:21">21</a> So give their children to hunger, and let their blood be spilled by the sword; make their wives lose their children and become widows; and kill their men, let their young men die by the sword in war.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Jeremiah 18:22">22</a> Let a shout come from their houses when you bring soldiers suddenly against them, because they have dug a hole to capture me and hidden traps for my feet.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Jeremiah 18:23">23</a> God, you know their plans to kill me. Do not forgive their wrongs or erase their sins from your view. Let them fail in your presence when you are angry.</p>
