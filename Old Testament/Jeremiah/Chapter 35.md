<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Jeremiah 35:1">1</a> God&#39;s message came to Jeremiah when Jehoiakim son of Josiah was king of Judah, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Jeremiah 35:2">2</a> Go to the Rechabites&#39; house, talk to them, bring them to God&#39;s house, into a room, and offer them wine to drink.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Jeremiah 35:3">3</a> I brought Jaazaniah, son of Jeremiah, son of Habaziniah, his brothers, all his sons, and the whole Rechabite family.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Jeremiah 35:4">4</a> I took them into God&#39;s house, to the room of Hanan&#39;s descendants, Igdaliah&#39;s son, who was a man of God. This room was next to the rulers&#39; room and above Maaseiah son of Shallum&#39;s room, who guarded the door.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Jeremiah 35:5">5</a> I put pots full of wine and cups in front of the Rechabites&#39; sons and told them, &quot;Drink the wine.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Jeremiah 35:6">6</a> But they said, &quot;We will not drink wine, because Jonadab the son of Rechab, our father, told us, &#39;You and your sons must never drink wine.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Jeremiah 35:7">7</a> Do not build houses, plant seeds, grow vineyards, or own them. But always live in tents, so you can live long in the land where you are visitors.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Jeremiah 35:8">8</a> We have followed Jonadab, son of Rechab, our father&#39;s command by not drinking wine at any time, not us, our wives, our sons, or our daughters.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Jeremiah 35:9">9</a> We didn&#39;t build houses to live in, and we don&#39;t have vineyards, fields, or seeds.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Jeremiah 35:10">10</a> We have lived in tents and followed and done everything that Jonadab our father told us to do.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Jeremiah 35:11">11</a> But when Nebuchadnezzar, the king of Babylon, invaded our land, we decided to go to Jerusalem to escape the Chaldeans&#39; and Syrians&#39; armies, and now we live in Jerusalem.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Jeremiah 35:12">12</a> God spoke to Jeremiah, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Jeremiah 35:13">13</a> God, the ruler of all, says; Go and tell the people of Judah and Jerusalem, won&#39;t you listen and follow my words? says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Jeremiah 35:14">14</a> Jonadab, Rechab&#39;s son, told his sons not to drink wine, and they don&#39;t to this day. They follow their father&#39;s rule, but even though I talked to you early and often, you didn&#39;t listen to me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Jeremiah 35:15">15</a> I have also sent you all my prophets, telling them to get up early and go to you, saying, &#39;Turn away from your bad ways, change what you do, and don&#39;t follow other gods to worship them, so you can live in the land I gave to you and your ancestors.&#39; But you haven&#39;t listened or paid attention to me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Jeremiah 35:16">16</a> Because Jonadab&#39;s descendants followed their father&#39;s command, but these people did not listen to me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Jeremiah 35:17">17</a> So this is what God, the God of Israel, says: I will bring all the bad things I said would happen to Judah and everyone in Jerusalem, because I spoke to them and they did not listen; I called to them and they did not answer.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Jeremiah 35:18">18</a> Jeremiah told the Rechabites, &quot;God says, &#39;Because you have followed your ancestor Jonadab&#39;s orders, keeping his rules and doing everything he commanded you:&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Jeremiah 35:19">19</a> So this is what God, the God of Israel, says: Jonadab son of Rechab will always have a descendant to serve me.</p>
