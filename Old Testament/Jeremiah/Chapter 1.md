<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Jeremiah 1:1">1</a> These are the words of Jeremiah, Hilkiah&#39;s son, a priest from Anathoth in Benjamin&#39;s territory.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Jeremiah 1:2">2</a> God spoke to them when Josiah son of Amon was king of Judah, in the thirteenth year of his rule.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Jeremiah 1:3">3</a> It happened in the time of Jehoiakim, son of Josiah, king of Judah, up to the end of the eleventh year of Zedekiah, Josiah&#39;s son, king of Judah, until Jerusalem was taken away as prisoners in the fifth month.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Jeremiah 1:4">4</a> God spoke to me, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Jeremiah 1:5">5</a> Before I made you in the womb, I knew you; before you were born, I set you apart and appointed you as a prophet to the nations.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Jeremiah 1:6">6</a> Then I said, &quot;Oh, God! Look, I can&#39;t speak: I am just a child.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Jeremiah 1:7">7</a> But God told me, &quot;Don&#39;t say, &#39;I am a child.&#39; You must go wherever I send you and speak whatever I tell you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Jeremiah 1:8">8</a> Don&#39;t be scared of their looks: I am with you to save you, says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Jeremiah 1:9">9</a> Then God reached out His hand and touched my mouth. And God said to me, &quot;Look, I have placed my words in your mouth.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Jeremiah 1:10">10</a> Today, I have given you power over nations and kingdoms to uproot, tear down, destroy, and overthrow, to build and to plant.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Jeremiah 1:11">11</a> God&#39;s message came to me, saying, &quot;Jeremiah, what do you see?&quot; And I said, &quot;I see the branch of an almond tree.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Jeremiah 1:12">12</a> Then God said to me, &quot;You have seen correctly, for I will quickly carry out what I have said.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Jeremiah 1:13">13</a> God spoke to me again, asking, &quot;What do you see?&quot; I answered, &quot;I see a boiling pot, tilted from the north.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Jeremiah 1:14">14</a> Then God said to me, &quot;From the north, a bad thing will spread over all the people living in the land.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Jeremiah 1:15">15</a> Look, I will summon all the families from the northern kingdoms, says God. They will come and each will place his throne at the entrance of Jerusalem&#39;s gates, around all its walls, and near all the towns of Judah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Jeremiah 1:16">16</a> I will announce my decisions against them for all their evil, because they left me, burned incense to other gods, and worshiped what their hands made.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Jeremiah 1:17">17</a> Get ready, stand up, and tell them everything I tell you to say. Don&#39;t be afraid of them, or I will confuse you in front of them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Jeremiah 1:18">18</a> Today, I have made you like a protected city, an iron pillar, and bronze walls against the entire country, its kings, leaders, priests, and all the people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Jeremiah 1:19">19</a> They will fight against you, but they won&#39;t win, because I am with you, says God, to save you.</p>
