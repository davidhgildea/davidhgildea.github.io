<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Jeremiah 4:1">1</a> If you will come back, Israel, says God, come back to me. And if you will get rid of your bad things so I can&#39;t see them, then you will not be moved.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Jeremiah 4:2">2</a> You will swear, &quot;God is alive,&quot; honestly, fairly, and rightly; and nations will praise themselves in him, and they will take pride in him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Jeremiah 4:3">3</a> God says to the men of Judah and Jerusalem, &quot;Prepare your unused land for planting, and do not sow seeds among thorns.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Jeremiah 4:4">4</a> Make your hearts pure for God, you men of Judah and people of Jerusalem, or my anger will burn like a fire that no one can put out because of your bad actions.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Jeremiah 4:5">5</a> Announce in Judah and tell everyone in Jerusalem; say, &quot;Sound the trumpet throughout the land: shout loudly, come together, and let&#39;s go to the fortified cities.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Jeremiah 4:6">6</a> Raise a signal toward Zion. Get away and don&#39;t stop, for I will bring disaster from the north, and massive destruction.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Jeremiah 4:7">7</a> A lion has come out from his hiding place, and the one who destroys nations is coming; he has left his home to ruin your land; and your cities will be destroyed, with no one living in them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Jeremiah 4:8">8</a> Put on rough cloth to show you are sad, cry loudly and mourn, because God&#39;s strong anger has not been turned away from us.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Jeremiah 4:9">9</a> On that day, God says, the king will be filled with fear, and so will the princes; the priests will be shocked, and the prophets will be amazed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Jeremiah 4:10">10</a> I said, &quot;Ah, God! You have truly misled these people and Jerusalem by telling them they will have peace, while the threat of death is so near.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Jeremiah 4:11">11</a> Then people will say to Jerusalem, &quot;A harsh wind from the desert hills blows towards my people, not to cool or to clean them.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Jeremiah 4:12">12</a> A strong wind from there will come to me; now I will also pass judgment on them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Jeremiah 4:13">13</a> Look, he will rise like clouds, his chariots will be like a storm: his horses are faster than eagles. Trouble for us! We are ruined.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Jeremiah 4:14">14</a> Jerusalem, clean your heart of evil so you can be saved. How long will your useless thoughts stay in you?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Jeremiah 4:15">15</a> A voice announces from Dan, and spreads news of trouble from Mount Ephraim.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Jeremiah 4:16">16</a> Tell the nations; look, announce to Jerusalem that guards are coming from a far land, and they shout against the towns of Judah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Jeremiah 4:17">17</a> They surround her like guards in a field because she has rebelled against me, says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Jeremiah 4:18">18</a> Your actions have caused these things to happen to you; this is your wrongdoing, because it is harsh and it hurts you deeply.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Jeremiah 4:19">19</a> I am in deep pain and my heart hurts; it beats loudly inside me. I cannot stay quiet because, my soul, you have heard the war trumpet’s call.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Jeremiah 4:20">20</a> Destruction follows destruction; the whole land is ruined: suddenly my tents are destroyed, and my curtains in an instant.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Jeremiah 4:21">21</a> How long will I see the flag and hear the trumpet sound?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Jeremiah 4:22">22</a> My people are foolish, they don&#39;t know me; they are stupid children, without understanding. They are clever at doing wrong, but clueless about doing right.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Jeremiah 4:23">23</a> I saw the earth, and it was shapeless and empty; the skies were dark with no light.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Jeremiah 4:24">24</a> I saw the mountains, and look, they shook, and all the hills swayed gently.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Jeremiah 4:25">25</a> I saw, and look, there was no one, and all the birds of the sky had flown away.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Jeremiah 4:26">26</a> I saw that the fertile land had become a desert, and all its cities were destroyed because of God and his intense anger.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Jeremiah 4:27">27</a> God has said, the whole land will be empty; but I will not completely destroy it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Jeremiah 4:28">28</a> Because of this, the earth will be sad and the sky will turn dark; for I have said it, planned it, and will not change my mind or go back on it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Jeremiah 4:29">29</a> Everyone in the city will run away because of the noise of the horsemen and archers; they will hide in the bushes and climb the rocks. Every city will be empty, and no one will live there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Jeremiah 4:30">30</a> When you are ruined, what will you do? Even if you wear red clothes, even if you put on gold jewelry, even if you put makeup on your face, it&#39;s useless to try to look beautiful; your lovers will hate you, they will want to kill you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Jeremiah 4:31">31</a> I have heard a sound like a woman in labor, the agony of a first-time mother, the sound of Zion&#39;s daughter crying out, stretching out her hands and saying, &quot;I am in distress now, for I am exhausted by killers.&quot;</p>
