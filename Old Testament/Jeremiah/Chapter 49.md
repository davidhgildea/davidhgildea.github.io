<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Jeremiah 49:1">1</a> About the Ammonites, this is what God says: Does Israel have no children? Do they have no one to inherit their land? Why then does the king of the Ammonites take over the land of Gad, and why do his people live in its towns?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Jeremiah 49:2">2</a> So, listen, the time will come, God says, when I will make the sound of war heard in Rabbah of the Ammon people; it will become a ruined mound, and its towns will be set on fire. Then Israel will take over the land from those who took their land, God says.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Jeremiah 49:3">3</a> Cry out, O Heshbon, because Ai is destroyed: weep, you women of Rabbah, put on rough cloth; mourn, and walk back and forth by the fences; for their king will be taken as prisoner, along with his religious leaders and officials.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Jeremiah 49:4">4</a> Why do you boast about your valleys, your fertile valley, you unfaithful city? You trust in your riches and think, &#39;Who will attack me?&#39;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Jeremiah 49:5">5</a> Look, I will cause you to be very afraid, says God of all, from everyone around you; and you will all be forced to leave straight away; and no one will help those who stray.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Jeremiah 49:6">6</a> Afterward, God will restore the fortunes of the Ammonites&#39; descendants.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Jeremiah 49:7">7</a> About Edom, God says: Has wisdom disappeared from Teman? Has good advice ended for the wise? Is their wisdom gone?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Jeremiah 49:8">8</a> Run away, turn around, hide well, people of Dedan; because I will bring Esau&#39;s disaster on him, when it&#39;s time for me to punish him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Jeremiah 49:9">9</a> If grape pickers come to you, wouldn&#39;t they leave some grapes behind? If thieves come at night, they will destroy only until they have enough.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Jeremiah 49:10">10</a> I have left Esau exposed, revealed his hiding spots, and he can&#39;t hide: his descendants, his brothers, and his neighbors are ruined, and he no longer exists.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Jeremiah 49:11">11</a> Leave your children without a father to me; I will keep them safe, and your widows can rely on me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Jeremiah 49:12">12</a> God says, &quot;Look, those who were not supposed to drink from the cup have definitely drunk it; and do you think you will go unpunished? You will not go unpunished, but you will certainly drink from it.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Jeremiah 49:13">13</a> I promise, says God, that Bozrah will become ruined, shamed, empty, and cursed; all its cities will be forever deserted.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Jeremiah 49:14">14</a> I have heard news from God: a messenger has been sent to the nations saying, &quot;Come together and attack her; get ready for battle.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Jeremiah 49:15">15</a> I will make you unimportant among the nations and scorned by people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Jeremiah 49:16">16</a> Your fearsomeness has tricked you, with the arrogance of your heart, you who live in the rock cracks and hold the hilltops high. Even if you set your home as high as an eagle&#39;s, I will bring you down from there, says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Jeremiah 49:17">17</a> Edom will become a wasteland; everyone who passes by will be shocked and will scorn its many disasters.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Jeremiah 49:18">18</a> Just like when Sodom and Gomorrah and the nearby towns were destroyed, says God, no one will live there, nor will any person stay there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Jeremiah 49:19">19</a> Look, he will rise like a lion from the Jordan&#39;s banks against the powerful place: but I will quickly make him flee from there: and who is the capable man I can put in charge of it? Who is equal to me? Who can set a time for me? And who is the shepherd that can face me?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Jeremiah 49:20">20</a> Listen to God&#39;s plan against Edom and his intentions for the people of Teman: Even the weakest will lead them away; yes, their homes will be made empty and ruined.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Jeremiah 49:21">21</a> The earth shakes when they fall, and their loud cry is heard all the way to the Red Sea.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Jeremiah 49:22">22</a> Look, he will rise and soar like an eagle, and stretch his wings over Bozrah. On that day, the brave people of Edom will be as scared as a woman in labor.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Jeremiah 49:23">23</a> About Damascus: Hamath and Arpad are worried because they have heard bad news. They are afraid; there is sadness on the sea; it cannot calm down.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Jeremiah 49:24">24</a> Damascus has become weak and tries to run away; fear has grabbed her, and pain and sorrow have caught her like a woman giving birth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Jeremiah 49:25">25</a> Why is the praised city abandoned, the city of my happiness?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Jeremiah 49:26">26</a> So her young men will die in the streets, and all her soldiers will be wiped out on that day, says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Jeremiah 49:27">27</a> I will start a fire in the walls of Damascus, and it will burn down Benhadad&#39;s palaces.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Jeremiah 49:28">28</a> About Kedar and the kingdoms of Hazor, which King Nebuchadnezzar of Babylon will strike, this is what God says: Get up, attack Kedar, and take the goods of the eastern people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Jeremiah 49:29">29</a> They will take their tents and animals; they will take their covers, all their things, and camels; and they will shout to them, &quot;Fear is all around.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Jeremiah 49:30">30</a> Run away, go far away, hide well, people of Hazor, says God; because Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, has made plans against you and intends to attack you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Jeremiah 49:31">31</a> Get up and go to the rich country that lives safely, says God, a country without gates or bars, living on its own.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Jeremiah 49:32">32</a> They will lose their camels and lots of their livestock; I will scatter those in the far corners to every direction, and disaster will come at them from all sides, says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Jeremiah 49:33">33</a> Hazor will become a home for wild creatures and will be empty forever; no person will live there, nor will anyone stay there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Jeremiah 49:34">34</a> God&#39;s message that came to Jeremiah the prophet about Elam, at the start of King Zedekiah&#39;s rule in Judah, was:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Jeremiah 49:35">35</a> God says: I will break Elam&#39;s strongest weapon, their bow.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Jeremiah 49:36">36</a> I will send winds from all four directions against Elam and scatter its people in every direction; there will be no country where its refugees do not go.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Jeremiah 49:37">37</a> I will make Elam afraid before their enemies and those who want to kill them. I will bring disaster on them, with my intense anger, says God. I will send the sword after them until they are destroyed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Jeremiah 49:38">38</a> I will put my throne in Elam and destroy their king and leaders, says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="Jeremiah 49:39">39</a> In the future, God will restore Elam&#39;s fortunes.</p>
