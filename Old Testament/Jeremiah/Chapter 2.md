<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Jeremiah 2:1">1</a> God spoke to me, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Jeremiah 2:2">2</a> Go and tell Jerusalem, &#39;God says: I remember how kind you were when you were young, and how you loved me when we were newly together, following me in the desert, in a land where nothing was planted.&#39;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Jeremiah 2:3">3</a> Israel was sacred to God, and the first of his crops. Anyone who harms Israel will be punished; they will face trouble, says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Jeremiah 2:4">4</a> Listen to God&#39;s message, O family of Jacob, and all the clans of Israel:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Jeremiah 2:5">5</a> God asks, &quot;What wrong did your ancestors find in me that made them leave me, follow worthless things, and become worthless themselves?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Jeremiah 2:6">6</a> No one asked, &quot;Where is God who brought us out of Egypt, who led us through the wild land of deserts and pits, through a dry land and dark death&#39;s shadow, through a place where no one travels or lives?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Jeremiah 2:7">7</a> I brought you to a fertile land to eat its fruit and enjoy its richness, but when you came in, you made my land dirty and turned my property into something disgusting.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Jeremiah 2:8">8</a> The priests didn&#39;t ask, &quot;Where is God?&quot; and those who knew the law didn&#39;t know me: the leaders also sinned against me, and the prophets spoke for Baal, following things that bring no good.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Jeremiah 2:9">9</a> So I will still argue with you, says God, and with your grandchildren too.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Jeremiah 2:10">10</a> Travel to the islands of Chittim and look; then go to Kedar, think hard, and see if there&#39;s anything like this.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Jeremiah 2:11">11</a> Has a nation swapped its gods, which are not really gods? But my people have traded their honor for something that brings no benefit.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Jeremiah 2:12">12</a> Be shocked, you heavens, at this, and be terribly scared, be very empty, says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Jeremiah 2:13">13</a> My people did two bad things; they left me, the source of life-giving water, and dug their own wells, cracked wells that cannot hold water.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Jeremiah 2:14">14</a> Is Israel a servant? Is he a slave born in the house? Why is he destroyed?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Jeremiah 2:15">15</a> Young lions roared at him, screamed, and left his land empty: his cities are burned with no one living in them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Jeremiah 2:16">16</a> The children from Noph and Tahapanes have damaged the top of your head.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Jeremiah 2:17">17</a> Didn&#39;t you bring this on yourself by abandoning God, who guided you on your path?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Jeremiah 2:18">18</a> Why are you going to Egypt to drink the water of the Nile? Or why are you going to Assyria to drink the water of the Euphrates?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Jeremiah 2:19">19</a> Your bad behavior will teach you a lesson, and your wrongdoings will show you your mistake. Understand and realize that it&#39;s a bad and painful thing to have left God, and that you don&#39;t respect me, says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Jeremiah 2:20">20</a> Long ago, I removed your heavy yoke and broke your chains; but you said, &#39;I won&#39;t sin.&#39; Yet on every high hill and under every green tree, you wandered and acted unfaithfully.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Jeremiah 2:21">21</a> I planted you as a fine vine, all with good seeds: how then have you become a poor vine to me?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Jeremiah 2:22">22</a> Even if you use baking soda and lots of soap, your sins are still clearly seen by me, says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Jeremiah 2:23">23</a> How can you say, &#39;I am not dirty, I have not followed other gods&#39;? Look at your path in the valley, see what you have done: you are like a fast camel running on her paths.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Jeremiah 2:24">24</a> A wild donkey used to the desert, that breathes in the wind as it wants; who can make it change its ways when it&#39;s ready? Those who look for it won&#39;t get tired; when the time comes, they will find it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Jeremiah 2:25">25</a> Take care of your feet so they&#39;re not bare, and don&#39;t let yourself be thirsty; but you&#39;ve said, &quot;There&#39;s no hope; I love outsiders, and I&#39;ll follow them.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Jeremiah 2:26">26</a> Like a thief feels shame when caught, so the people of Israel feel ashamed; including their kings, leaders, priests, and prophets.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Jeremiah 2:27">27</a> They say to a piece of wood, &quot;You are my dad,&quot; and to a rock, &quot;You gave birth to me.&quot; They turn their back on me, not their face. But when they are in trouble, they&#39;ll call out, &quot;Get up and save us.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Jeremiah 2:28">28</a> Where are the gods you made? Let them help you when you&#39;re in trouble. You have as many gods as you have cities, Judah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Jeremiah 2:29">29</a> Why do you argue with me? You have all sinned against me, says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Jeremiah 2:30">30</a> I have punished your children for nothing; they did not learn their lesson. Your own violence has killed your prophets, like a deadly lion.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Jeremiah 2:31">31</a> People of this time, look at God&#39;s message. Have I been like a desert to Israel? A place of darkness? Why do my people say, &quot;We are our own masters; we won&#39;t come to you anymore&quot;?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Jeremiah 2:32">32</a> Can a young woman forget her jewelry, or a bride her wedding clothes? Yet my people have forgotten me for countless days.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Jeremiah 2:33">33</a> Why do you change your ways to get love? So you have also taught the bad ones your ways.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Jeremiah 2:34">34</a> In your clothes, they found the blood of innocent poor people: it was not hidden, but found on all of these.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Jeremiah 2:35">35</a> But you say, &quot;I am innocent, so surely God&#39;s anger will stop.&quot; I will argue with you because you say, &quot;I have not sinned.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Jeremiah 2:36">36</a> Why do you wander around so much to change your path? You will also feel ashamed of Egypt, just as you felt ashamed of Assyria.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Jeremiah 2:37">37</a> Yes, you will leave there with your hands on your head, because God has rejected the things you trust in, and you will not succeed with them.</p>
