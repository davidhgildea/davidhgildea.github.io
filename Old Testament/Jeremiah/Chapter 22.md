<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Jeremiah 22:1">1</a> God says, &quot;Go to the king of Judah&#39;s palace and say this message there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Jeremiah 22:2">2</a> Say, &quot;Listen to God&#39;s word, King of Judah, you who sit on David&#39;s throne, along with your officials and people who come through these gates.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Jeremiah 22:3">3</a> God says; Carry out fair justice and do what is right, and rescue those who have been robbed from their oppressors; and do not harm or be violent to foreigners, orphans, or widows, and do not kill innocent people here.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Jeremiah 22:4">4</a> If you really do this, then kings who sit on David&#39;s throne will come through the gates of this house, riding in chariots and on horses, along with their servants and people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Jeremiah 22:5">5</a> If you don&#39;t listen to these words, I promise by myself, says God, that this house will be left empty.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Jeremiah 22:6">6</a> God says to the royal house of Judah, &quot;You are like Gilead to me, and like the top of Lebanon. But I will surely turn you into a desert and cities without people.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Jeremiah 22:7">7</a> I will send destroyers against you, each with their weapons, and they will chop down your best cedar trees and throw them into the fire.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Jeremiah 22:8">8</a> Many countries will pass by this city, and each person will ask their neighbor, why has God done this to this great city?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Jeremiah 22:9">9</a> They will answer, &#39;Because they left the agreement with their God and worshiped and served other gods.&#39;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Jeremiah 22:10">10</a> Do not cry for the dead or feel sad for them; but cry hard for the one who is leaving, for they will not come back or see their homeland again.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Jeremiah 22:11">11</a> God says about Shallum, son of Josiah, king of Judah, who became king after his father Josiah: He has left this place and will not come back.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Jeremiah 22:12">12</a> He will die where he has been taken as a captive and will never see this land again.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Jeremiah 22:13">13</a> Pity the one who builds his house by unfair means and his rooms by injustice; who makes his neighbor work for free and does not pay him for his labor.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Jeremiah 22:14">14</a> Someone says, &quot;I will build myself a big house with spacious rooms, put in windows, line it with cedar wood, and paint it bright red.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Jeremiah 22:15">15</a> Will you be king because you surround yourself with cedar? Didn&#39;t your father eat, drink, and carry out fair judgment and justice, and then things went well for him?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Jeremiah 22:16">16</a> He decided justly for the poor and needy; this brought goodness: isn&#39;t this knowing me? says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Jeremiah 22:17">17</a> Your eyes and heart only want to take things, kill the innocent, oppress, and be violent.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Jeremiah 22:18">18</a> So this is what God says about Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah: No one will mourn for him, saying, &quot;Oh, my brother!&quot; or &quot;Oh, my sister!&quot; No one will mourn for him, saying, &quot;Oh, master!&quot; or &quot;Oh, his greatness!&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Jeremiah 22:19">19</a> He will be buried like a donkey, dragged away and thrown out beyond Jerusalem&#39;s gates.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Jeremiah 22:20">20</a> Go to Lebanon and shout; raise your voice in Bashan, and call out from the crossings, because all your friends are ruined.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Jeremiah 22:21">21</a> I spoke to you when you were doing well, but you said you wouldn&#39;t listen. This has been your way since you were young, not obeying my voice.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Jeremiah 22:22">22</a> The wind will destroy all your shepherds, and your friends will be taken away; then you will feel ashamed and embarrassed because of all your evil.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Jeremiah 22:23">23</a> You who live in Lebanon and build your nest in the cedar trees, how will you plead for mercy when pain comes, like the pain of a woman giving birth?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Jeremiah 22:24">24</a> I swear, says God, even if Coniah son of King Jehoiakim of Judah were like a ring on my right hand, I would still pull you off.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Jeremiah 22:25">25</a> I will hand you over to those who want to kill you, to those you are afraid of, to King Nebuchadnezzar of Babylon and the Chaldeans.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Jeremiah 22:26">26</a> I will throw you and your mother who gave birth to you out to another country where you were not born, and there you will die.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Jeremiah 22:27">27</a> But they will not go back to the land they want to return to.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Jeremiah 22:28">28</a> Is Coniah a hated, shattered statue? Is he a container that brings no joy? Why are he and his descendants thrown out and sent to a land they do not know?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Jeremiah 22:29">29</a> Earth, earth, earth, listen to God&#39;s message.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Jeremiah 22:30">30</a> God says, &quot;Mark this man as childless, a man who will not succeed in his lifetime: because none of his descendants will succeed, sitting on David&#39;s throne or ruling in Judah anymore.&quot;</p>
