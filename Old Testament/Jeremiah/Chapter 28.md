<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Jeremiah 28:1">1</a> That year, when Zedekiah started ruling Judah, in the fourth year, fifth month, the prophet Hananiah, Azur&#39;s son from Gibeon, spoke to me in God&#39;s house with priests and everyone there, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Jeremiah 28:2">2</a> God of Israel says, I have broken the power of the king of Babylon.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Jeremiah 28:3">3</a> In two years, I will bring back to this place all the items from God&#39;s house that King Nebuchadnezzar of Babylon took away and moved to Babylon.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Jeremiah 28:4">4</a> I will bring Jeconiah, son of King Jehoiakim of Judah, and all the Judah captives back from Babylon, says God; for I will break the power of the king of Babylon.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Jeremiah 28:5">5</a> Then the prophet Jeremiah spoke to the prophet Hananiah where the priests and all the people in God&#39;s house could hear.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Jeremiah 28:6">6</a> The prophet Jeremiah agreed, saying, &quot;Yes, let it happen: may God do this: may God make true what you have predicted, to return the items of God&#39;s house, and everyone taken as prisoners, from Babylon to this place.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Jeremiah 28:7">7</a> Please listen to this message that I am saying to you and to everyone.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Jeremiah 28:8">8</a> Prophets before us long ago spoke against many nations and large kingdoms about war, evil, and disease.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Jeremiah 28:9">9</a> The prophet who predicts peace will be recognized as truly sent by God when his prophecy comes true.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Jeremiah 28:10">10</a> Then Hananiah the prophet took the wooden bar from the neck of the prophet Jeremiah and broke it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Jeremiah 28:11">11</a> Hananiah said to everyone, &quot;This is what God says: &#39;I will break Nebuchadnezzar, the Babylonian king&#39;s, control from all countries in two years.&#39;&quot; And the prophet Jeremiah left.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Jeremiah 28:12">12</a> God spoke to the prophet Jeremiah after Hananiah the prophet had broken the yoke from Jeremiah&#39;s neck, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Jeremiah 28:13">13</a> Go and tell Hananiah, &#39;God says, &quot;You have broken the wooden yokes; now you will make iron yokes to replace them.&quot;&#39;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Jeremiah 28:14">14</a> God says, &quot;I have put an iron yoke on the necks of all these nations so they will serve Nebuchadnezzar, the king of Babylon, and they will serve him. I have also given him the animals of the fields.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Jeremiah 28:15">15</a> The prophet Jeremiah said to Hananiah, &quot;Listen, Hananiah, God did not send you; you are making these people believe a lie.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Jeremiah 28:16">16</a> So God says, &quot;Look, I will throw you off the earth. This year you will die because you have taught rebellion against God.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Jeremiah 28:17">17</a> The prophet Hananiah died that year in the seventh month.</p>
