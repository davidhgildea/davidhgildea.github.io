<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Song of Solomon 2:1">1</a> I am the rose of Sharon and the lily of the valleys.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Song of Solomon 2:2">2</a> Like a lily among thorns, so is my loved one among the women.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Song of Solomon 2:3">3</a> Just like an apple tree stands out from the forest trees, so my loved one stands out among other men. I enjoyed sitting in his shade, and his fruit tasted sweet to me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Song of Solomon 2:4">4</a> He took me to a feast, and he showed his love for me like a flag.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Song of Solomon 2:5">5</a> Support me with raisins, cheer me up with apples; I am overwhelmed with love.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Song of Solomon 2:6">6</a> His left hand is under my head, and his right hand hugs me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Song of Solomon 2:7">7</a> I ask you, young women of Jerusalem, by the gazelles and deer of the fields, not to disturb or awaken my love until he wishes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Song of Solomon 2:8">8</a> I hear my loved one&#39;s voice! Look, he&#39;s coming, jumping over the mountains, hopping on the hills.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Song of Solomon 2:9">9</a> My loved one is like a deer or a young stag: look, he stands behind our wall, he looks in at the windows, showing himself through the trellis.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Song of Solomon 2:10">10</a> My loved one spoke and said to me, Get up, my love, my beautiful one, and come with me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Song of Solomon 2:11">11</a> The winter is over, the rain has stopped.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Song of Solomon 2:12">12</a> Flowers show up on the earth; it&#39;s time for birds to sing, and we hear the turtle dove in our land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Song of Solomon 2:13">13</a> The fig tree grows its young figs, and the grapevines with new grapes smell sweet. Get up, my love, my beautiful one, and come with me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Song of Solomon 2:14">14</a> My dove hiding in the rocks and secret stairways, show me your face, let me hear your voice; your voice is sweet, and your face is lovely.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Song of Solomon 2:15">15</a> Catch the foxes for us, the small foxes that ruin the vineyards; our vineyards are blooming.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Song of Solomon 2:16">16</a> My loved one belongs to me, and I belong to him; he grazes among the flowers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Song of Solomon 2:17">17</a> Wait until the day starts and the shadows vanish, then come back, my loved one, and be like a deer or young stag on the Bether hills.</p>
