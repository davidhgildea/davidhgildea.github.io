<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Song of Solomon 8:1">1</a> I wish you were like my brother, who nursed at my mother&#39;s breasts! If I saw you outside, I would kiss you and no one would look down on me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Song of Solomon 8:2">2</a> I would guide you and take you to my mother&#39;s home, where she would teach me. I would give you spiced wine to drink, made from my pomegranates.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Song of Solomon 8:3">3</a> His left hand should be under my head, and his right hand should hug me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Song of Solomon 8:4">4</a> I tell you, O women of Jerusalem, do not disturb or wake up my love until he wants to.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Song of Solomon 8:5">5</a> Who is this coming from the desert, leaning on her loved one? I woke you up under the apple tree. There your mother gave birth to you; there she who had you, gave birth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Song of Solomon 8:6">6</a> Place me like a seal on your heart, like a seal on your arm; because love is as strong as death; jealousy is as harsh as the tomb: its flames are fiery and intense.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Song of Solomon 8:7">7</a> No amount of water can put out love, and floods cannot drown it. If someone tried to trade everything in their house for love, it would be totally rejected.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Song of Solomon 8:8">8</a> We have a young sister, and she doesn&#39;t have breasts: what will we do for our sister when someone wants to marry her?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Song of Solomon 8:9">9</a> If she is a wall, we will build a silver palace on her; if she is a door, we will cover her with cedar planks.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Song of Solomon 8:10">10</a> I am like a wall, and my breasts are like strong towers: then in his eyes, I was someone who had found favor.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Song of Solomon 8:11">11</a> Solomon owned a vineyard in Baalhamon; he rented it out to farmers; each was to pay him a thousand silver coins for its produce.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Song of Solomon 8:12">12</a> My vineyard is mine and it&#39;s before me. You, Solomon, get a thousand, and the workers who take care of the fruits get two hundred.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Song of Solomon 8:13">13</a> You who live in the gardens, your friends listen to your voice: let me hear it too.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Song of Solomon 8:14">14</a> Hurry, my loved one, and be like a deer or a young stag on the spice-covered hills.</p>
