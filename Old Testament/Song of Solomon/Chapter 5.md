<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Song of Solomon 5:1">1</a> I have come into my garden, my sister, my partner: I have collected my myrrh and spices; I have eaten my honeycomb and honey; I have drunk my wine and milk. Eat, friends; drink, yes, drink a lot, my loved one.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Song of Solomon 5:2">2</a> I am asleep, but my heart is awake: it&#39;s my loved one&#39;s voice knocking, saying, &quot;Let me in, my sister, my love, my pure one, my dove; because my head is wet with dew, and my hair with the dampness of the night.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Song of Solomon 5:3">3</a> I took off my coat; why should I put it back on? I cleaned my feet; why should I get them dirty again?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Song of Solomon 5:4">4</a> My loved one reached in through the door opening, and I was deeply stirred for him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Song of Solomon 5:5">5</a> I got up to open the door for my loved one; my hands dripped with myrrh, and my fingers with fragrant myrrh, on the lock&#39;s handles.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Song of Solomon 5:6">6</a> I opened the door for my loved one, but he had left and was gone. My heart sank when he spoke. I looked for him but couldn&#39;t find him. I called him, but he didn&#39;t answer me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Song of Solomon 5:7">7</a> The guards patrolling the city found me and beat me; they hurt me. The wall keepers took my veil away.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Song of Solomon 5:8">8</a> I ask you, women of Jerusalem, if you see my loved one, please tell him I am deeply in love.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Song of Solomon 5:9">9</a> What makes your beloved better than any other, O most beautiful among women? What makes your beloved stand out that you give us such a command?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Song of Solomon 5:10">10</a> My loved one is fair and has a healthy red glow, the most outstanding among ten thousand.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Song of Solomon 5:11">11</a> His head is like the finest gold, his hair is thick and black as a raven.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Song of Solomon 5:12">12</a> His eyes are like dove&#39;s eyes by streams of water, bathed in milk, and perfectly placed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Song of Solomon 5:13">13</a> His cheeks are like a garden of sweet spices, and sweet flowers; his lips are like lilies, dripping with the sweet scent of myrrh.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Song of Solomon 5:14">14</a> His hands are like gold rings with beryl stones; his stomach is like polished ivory decorated with sapphires.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Song of Solomon 5:15">15</a> His legs are like marble columns on gold bases: his face is like Lebanon, as grand as the cedars.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Song of Solomon 5:16">16</a> His words are very sweet; yes, he is completely beautiful. This is my loved one, and this is my friend, O women of Jerusalem.</p>
