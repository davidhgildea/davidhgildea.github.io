<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Song of Solomon 4:1">1</a> Look, you are beautiful, my love; look, you are beautiful; your eyes are like doves&#39; eyes among your hair: your hair is like a group of goats seen on Mount Gilead.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Song of Solomon 4:2">2</a> Your teeth are like a group of sheep with even cuts, freshly washed, where each has a twin, and none is without offspring.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Song of Solomon 4:3">3</a> Your lips are like a red ribbon, and your words are lovely. Your cheeks are like pieces of pomegranate behind your hair.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Song of Solomon 4:4">4</a> Your neck is like a strong tower built to hold weapons, with a thousand shields hanging on it, all belonging to strong warriors.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Song of Solomon 4:5">5</a> Your two breasts are like two young deer that are twins, which browse among the lilies.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Song of Solomon 4:6">6</a> Until daylight comes and shadows go away, I will go to the myrrh mountain and the frankincense hill.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Song of Solomon 4:7">7</a> You are completely beautiful, my love; you have no flaws.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Song of Solomon 4:8">8</a> Come with me from Lebanon, my partner, with me from Lebanon: look from the top of Amana, from the top of Shenir and Hermon, from where the lions live, from the mountains where the leopards are.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Song of Solomon 4:9">9</a> You have captured my heart, my sister, my wife; you have captured my heart with just one look from your eye, with just one necklace around your neck.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Song of Solomon 4:10">10</a> Your love is so beautiful, my sister, my partner! Your love is better than wine, and your perfume smells better than all spices!</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Song of Solomon 4:11">11</a> Your lips, my partner, drip like honeycomb: honey and milk are beneath your tongue; and your clothes smell like the scent of Lebanon.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Song of Solomon 4:12">12</a> My sister, my partner, is like a private garden, a locked spring, a sealed fountain.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Song of Solomon 4:13">13</a> Your garden is like a pomegranate orchard with nice fruits, henna, and nard plants.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Song of Solomon 4:14">14</a> Nard and saffron; sweet cane and cinnamon, along with every frankincense tree; myrrh and aloes, with all the best spices.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Song of Solomon 4:15">15</a> A garden&#39;s fountain, a spring of fresh water, and rivers from Lebanon.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Song of Solomon 4:16">16</a> Wake up, north wind; and come, south wind; blow on my garden, so its fragrances spread. Let my loved one enter his garden and enjoy its delicious fruits.</p>
