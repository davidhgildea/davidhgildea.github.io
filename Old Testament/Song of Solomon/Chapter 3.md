<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Song of Solomon 3:1">1</a> At night in my bed, I searched for the one my soul loves: I searched for him, but did not find him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Song of Solomon 3:2">2</a> I will get up now and walk through the city, in the streets and squares, looking for the one my heart loves. I looked for him but did not find him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Song of Solomon 3:3">3</a> The guards walking through the city found me. I asked them, &quot;Have you seen the one I love deeply?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Song of Solomon 3:4">4</a> I had just left them when I found the one my heart loves. I held him tightly and wouldn&#39;t let go until I brought him to my mother&#39;s home, to the room of the one who gave birth to me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Song of Solomon 3:5">5</a> I ask you, women of Jerusalem, by the deer and the wild does, do not disturb or wake my love until he wants to.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Song of Solomon 3:6">6</a> Who is this coming from the desert like smoke columns, scented with myrrh and incense, with all the spice seller&#39;s powders?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Song of Solomon 3:7">7</a> Look at his bed, which belongs to Solomon; sixty brave men surround it, the brave ones of Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Song of Solomon 3:8">8</a> They all carry swords, skilled in fighting: each man has his sword on his leg because of fear at night.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Song of Solomon 3:9">9</a> King Solomon built himself a carriage from the wood of Lebanon.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Song of Solomon 3:10">10</a> He made its pillars of silver, its base of gold, its cover of purple, and its middle was decorated with love, for the women of Jerusalem.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Song of Solomon 3:11">11</a> Go out, daughters of Zion, and see King Solomon wearing the crown his mother gave him on his wedding day, and on the day he was very happy.</p>
