<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Song of Solomon 6:1">1</a> Where has your loved one gone, most beautiful of women? Where did your loved one turn? Let us look for him with you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Song of Solomon 6:2">2</a> My loved one has gone to his garden, to the spice beds, to eat in the gardens, and to pick lilies.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Song of Solomon 6:3">3</a> I belong to my loved one, and my loved one belongs to me; he feeds among the lilies.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Song of Solomon 6:4">4</a> You are beautiful, my love, like Tirzah, lovely like Jerusalem, awesome like an army with flags.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Song of Solomon 6:5">5</a> Look away from me, because your eyes overpower me. Your hair is like a group of goats seen on the hills of Gilead.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Song of Solomon 6:6">6</a> Your teeth are like a flock of clean sheep where each one has twins, and none are without young.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Song of Solomon 6:7">7</a> Your cheeks behind your hair are like pieces of a pomegranate.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Song of Solomon 6:8">8</a> There are sixty queens, eighty concubines, and countless virgins.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Song of Solomon 6:9">9</a> My pure dove is one of a kind; she is her mother&#39;s only child and the best one she ever had. Other women saw her and praised her; even queens and wives shared their admiration for her.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Song of Solomon 6:10">10</a> Who is she that appears like the morning, beautiful like the moon, bright like the sun, and awe-inspiring like an army with flags?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Song of Solomon 6:11">11</a> I went to the nut garden to look at the valley&#39;s fruits, and to check if the vine was healthy and the pomegranates were budding.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Song of Solomon 6:12">12</a> Before I knew it, my desire swept me away like the chariots of Amminadib.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Song of Solomon 6:13">13</a> Come back, come back, O Shulamite; come back, come back, so we can see you. What will you see in the Shulamite? She is like two armies facing each other.</p>
