<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Song of Solomon 7:1">1</a> Your feet are lovely in sandals, O princess! Your graceful legs are like precious gems, shaped by a skilled artist.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Song of Solomon 7:2">2</a> Your belly button is like a round cup, always full of drink; your stomach is like a pile of wheat surrounded by lilies.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Song of Solomon 7:3">3</a> Your two breasts are like two young deer that are twins.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Song of Solomon 7:4">4</a> Your neck is like a tower made of ivory; your eyes are like fish ponds in Heshbon, near the entrance of Bathrabbim; your nose is like the tower of Lebanon facing Damascus.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Song of Solomon 7:5">5</a> Your head is like Mount Carmel, and your hair is as purple; the king is captivated by your tresses.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Song of Solomon 7:6">6</a> You are so beautiful and lovely, O love, with so much joy!</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Song of Solomon 7:7">7</a> Your body is like a palm tree, and your breasts are like bunches of grapes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Song of Solomon 7:8">8</a> I said, I will climb the palm tree and grasp its branches. Your breasts will be like bunches of grapes, and your breath will smell like apples.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Song of Solomon 7:9">9</a> Your mouth is like fine wine for my loved one, smoothly gliding and waking the words of sleepers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Song of Solomon 7:10">10</a> I belong to the one I love, and he longs for me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Song of Solomon 7:11">11</a> Come, my loved one, let&#39;s go out to the field; let&#39;s stay overnight in the villages.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Song of Solomon 7:12">12</a> Let&#39;s go early to the vineyards; let&#39;s see if the vines have budded, if the grapes are growing, and if the pomegranates are flowering: there I will show you my love.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Song of Solomon 7:13">13</a> The mandrakes smell sweet, and at our gates there are all kinds of nice fruits, both new and old, that I have saved for you, my dear.</p>
