<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Song of Solomon 1:1">1</a> The best song, which is Solomon&#39;s.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Song of Solomon 1:2">2</a> Let him kiss me with his mouth&#39;s kisses: because your love is better than wine.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Song of Solomon 1:3">3</a> Because your perfumes smell so good, your name is like perfume spreading out, so young women love you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Song of Solomon 1:4">4</a> Pull me, and we&#39;ll run after you. The king brought me into his rooms. We&#39;ll be happy and celebrate in you. We will think about your love more than wine. Good people love you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Song of Solomon 1:5">5</a> I am dark and beautiful, O women of Jerusalem, like the tents of Kedar, like Solomon&#39;s drapes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Song of Solomon 1:6">6</a> Do not stare at me because I am dark, for the sun has darkened me. My siblings were mad at me; they made me watch over the vineyards, but I did not take care of my own vineyard.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Song of Solomon 1:7">7</a> Tell me, you whom my soul loves, where you feed your flock and where you rest them at noon, for why should I be like one who wanders beside the flocks of your friends?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Song of Solomon 1:8">8</a> If you don&#39;t know, most beautiful woman, follow the path where the sheep walked, and feed your young goats near the shepherds&#39; tents.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Song of Solomon 1:9">9</a> I have compared you, my love, to a group of horses in Pharaoh&#39;s chariots.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Song of Solomon 1:10">10</a> Your cheeks look lovely with rows of jewels, your neck with gold chains.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Song of Solomon 1:11">11</a> We will create gold borders for you with silver dots.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Song of Solomon 1:12">12</a> While the king is at his table, my perfume spreads its fragrance.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Song of Solomon 1:13">13</a> My loved one is like a bundle of myrrh to me; he will rest between my breasts all night.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Song of Solomon 1:14">14</a> My loved one is to me like a bunch of henna flowers in the vineyards of En Gedi.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Song of Solomon 1:15">15</a> Look, you are beautiful, my love; look, you are beautiful; your eyes are like doves.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Song of Solomon 1:16">16</a> Look, you are beautiful, my loved one, yes, delightful; our bed is also fresh and green.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Song of Solomon 1:17">17</a> The support beams of our house are made of cedar, and the beams of our roof are fir.</p>
