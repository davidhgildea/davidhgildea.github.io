<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Nahum 2:1">1</a> The destroyer has come before you: guard the fortress, watch the road, strengthen your waist, and power up greatly.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Nahum 2:2">2</a> God has taken away Jacob&#39;s greatness, just like Israel&#39;s; because the enemies have stripped them bare and ruined their vine branches.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Nahum 2:3">3</a> The warriors&#39; shields are red, the brave wear red clothes: on the day of battle, the chariots will blaze with fire, and the evergreen trees will shake violently.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Nahum 2:4">4</a> Chariots will speed through the streets, bumping into each other on wide roads; they will look like bright torches and move as fast as lightning.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Nahum 2:5">5</a> He will list his brave men: they will trip as they walk; they will rush to its wall, and the protection will be set up.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Nahum 2:6">6</a> The river gates will be opened, and the palace will fall apart.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Nahum 2:7">7</a> Huzzab will be captured and taken away; she will be led by her maids who sound like cooing doves, beating on their chests.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Nahum 2:8">8</a> Nineveh is an ancient city like a water pond, but its people will run away. &quot;Stop, stop,&quot; they will shout, but no one will turn around.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Nahum 2:9">9</a> Take the silver loot, take the gold loot; there is no end to the riches and beauty from all the nice things.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Nahum 2:10">10</a> It is empty and ruined and destroyed; hearts are faint, knees knock, all hips hurt, and every face grows pale.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Nahum 2:11">11</a> Where is the home of the lions and the place where the young lions eat, where the lion, the old lion, walked, and the lion&#39;s cub, without fear?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Nahum 2:12">12</a> The lion tore enough for his cubs, choked food for his lionesses, and filled his lairs with prey, and his dens with torn animals.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Nahum 2:13">13</a> Look, I am against you, says God, and I will burn your chariots in smoke, and the sword will kill your young lions: I will take away your hunted animals from the land, and no one will hear your messengers anymore.</p>
