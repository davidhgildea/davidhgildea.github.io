<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Genesis 36:1">1</a> These are the descendants of Esau, also known as Edom.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Genesis 36:2">2</a> Esau married Canaanite women: Adah, daughter of Elon the Hittite, and Aholibamah, granddaughter of Zibeon the Hivite through her father Anah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Genesis 36:3">3</a> Bashemath, Ishmael&#39;s daughter and Nebajoth&#39;s sister.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Genesis 36:4">4</a> Adah had Eliphaz for Esau; and Bashemath had Reuel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Genesis 36:5">5</a> Aholibamah had three sons named Jeush, Jaalam, and Korah. These were Esau&#39;s sons, born to him in Canaan.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Genesis 36:6">6</a> Esau took his wives, sons, daughters, everyone in his house, his livestock, all his animals, and all his belongings, which he had in Canaan, and moved to another place, away from his brother Jacob.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Genesis 36:7">7</a> Their wealth was too much for them to live together; the land where they were outsiders could not support them because of their livestock.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Genesis 36:8">8</a> Esau lived in the Mount Seir area; Esau is also known as Edom.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Genesis 36:9">9</a> These are the descendants of Esau, who was the ancestor of the Edomites in Mount Seir.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Genesis 36:10">10</a> These are the names of Esau&#39;s sons: Eliphaz, son of Adah, Esau&#39;s wife, and Reuel, son of Bashemath, Esau&#39;s wife.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Genesis 36:11">11</a> Eliphaz&#39;s sons were Teman, Omar, Zepho, Gatam, and Kenaz.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Genesis 36:12">12</a> Timna was a secondary wife to Eliphaz, Esau&#39;s son, and she gave birth to Amalek for Eliphaz. These were the sons of Adah, Esau&#39;s wife.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Genesis 36:13">13</a> Reuel&#39;s sons were Nahath, Zerah, Shammah, and Mizzah. These were the sons of Bashemath, Esau&#39;s wife.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Genesis 36:14">14</a> Aholibamah, daughter of Anah and granddaughter of Zibeon, was Esau&#39;s wife. She had three sons for Esau: Jeush, Jaalam, and Korah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Genesis 36:15">15</a> These were chiefs of Esau&#39;s sons: Eliphaz&#39;s sons, Esau&#39;s first son; Chief Teman, Chief Omar, Chief Zepho, Chief Kenaz.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Genesis 36:16">16</a> Chief Korah, Chief Gatam, and Chief Amalek: these are the chiefs from Eliphaz in Edom; these were Adah&#39;s sons.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Genesis 36:17">17</a> These are the sons of Reuel, Esau&#39;s son: Chief Nahath, Chief Zerah, Chief Shammah, Chief Mizzah. These are the chiefs from Reuel in Edom; these are the sons of Bashemath, Esau&#39;s wife.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Genesis 36:18">18</a> These are the sons of Esau&#39;s wife Aholibamah: leaders Jeush, Jaalam, and Korah. These leaders were Aholibamah&#39;s sons, who was Anah&#39;s daughter and Esau&#39;s wife.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Genesis 36:19">19</a> These are Esau&#39;s sons, who is also known as Edom, and these are their chiefs.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Genesis 36:20">20</a> These are the sons of Seir the Horite, who lived in the land: Lotan, Shobal, Zibeon, and Anah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Genesis 36:21">21</a> Dishon, Ezer, and Dishan were chiefs of the Horites, Seir&#39;s descendants in Edom.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Genesis 36:22">22</a> Lotan&#39;s children were Hori and Hemam; Lotan&#39;s sister was Timna.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Genesis 36:23">23</a> Shobal&#39;s children were Alvan, Manahath, Ebal, Shepho, and Onam.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Genesis 36:24">24</a> These are Zibeon&#39;s children: Ajah and Anah. Anah is the one who found the mules in the desert while taking care of his father Zibeon&#39;s donkeys.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Genesis 36:25">25</a> Anah&#39;s children were Dishon and his daughter Aholibamah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Genesis 36:26">26</a> These are Dishon&#39;s children: Hemdan, Eshban, Ithran, and Cheran.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Genesis 36:27">27</a> Ezer&#39;s children are Bilhan, Zaavan, and Akan.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Genesis 36:28">28</a> Dishan&#39;s children are Uz and Aran.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Genesis 36:29">29</a> These are the chiefs from the Horites: Chief Lotan, Chief Shobal, Chief Zibeon, Chief Anah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Genesis 36:30">30</a> Chief Dishon, chief Ezer, chief Dishan: these are the chiefs from the family of Hori, among the chiefs in the land of Seir.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Genesis 36:31">31</a> These are the kings who ruled in Edom before any king ruled the Israelites.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Genesis 36:32">32</a> Bela, Beor&#39;s son, became king in Edom, and his city was called Dinhabah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Genesis 36:33">33</a> Bela died, and Jobab, Zerah&#39;s son from Bozrah, became king after him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Genesis 36:34">34</a> Jobab died, and Husham from the land of Temani became king after him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Genesis 36:35">35</a> Husham died, and then Hadad, son of Bedad who defeated Midian in Moab&#39;s field, became king. His city was called Avith.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Genesis 36:36">36</a> Hadad died, and Samlah from Masrekah became king after him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Genesis 36:37">37</a> Samlah died, and Saul from Rehoboth near the river became king after him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Genesis 36:38">38</a> Saul died, and Baalhanan, Achbor&#39;s son, became king after him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="Genesis 36:39">39</a> Baalhanan, Achbor&#39;s son, died, and Hadar became king after him. His city was called Pau, and his wife was Mehetabel, Matred&#39;s daughter and Mezahab&#39;s granddaughter.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="Genesis 36:40">40</a> Here are the names of the leaders from Esau&#39;s family, organized by their groups and places: Leader Timnah, Leader Alvah, Leader Jetheth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="Genesis 36:41">41</a> Duke Aholibamah, Duke Elah, Duke Pinon,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="Genesis 36:42">42</a> Duke Kenaz, Duke Teman, Duke Mibzar,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v43"><a class="verse-num" href="#v43" aria-label="Genesis 36:43">43</a> Chief Magdiel, chief Iram: these are the chiefs of Edom, living in the land they own: he is Esau, the ancestor of the Edomites.</p>
