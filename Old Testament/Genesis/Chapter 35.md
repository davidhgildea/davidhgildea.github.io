<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Genesis 35:1">1</a> God told Jacob, &quot;Get up and go to Bethel to live. There, build an altar to God who showed Himself to you when you were running away from your brother Esau.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Genesis 35:2">2</a> Jacob told his family and everyone with him, &quot;Get rid of the foreign gods you have, make yourselves clean, and change your clothes.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Genesis 35:3">3</a> Let&#39;s get up and go to Bethel. There I will build an altar to God, who helped me when I was in trouble and who has been with me wherever I have gone.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Genesis 35:4">4</a> They gave Jacob all the foreign gods they had and their earrings; Jacob buried them under the oak near Shechem.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Genesis 35:5">5</a> They traveled, and God&#39;s terror was on the surrounding cities, so they did not chase Jacob&#39;s sons.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Genesis 35:6">6</a> Jacob arrived at Luz, in Canaan, also known as Bethel, with everyone who was with him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Genesis 35:7">7</a> He made an altar there and named the place Elbethel, because God had shown himself to him as he was running from his brother.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Genesis 35:8">8</a> Rebekah&#39;s caregiver Deborah died and they buried her under an oak tree near Bethel. They named the place &quot;Oak of Weeping.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Genesis 35:9">9</a> God showed Himself to Jacob again after he left Padanaram and blessed him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Genesis 35:10">10</a> God said to him, &quot;Your name is Jacob. Your name will no longer be Jacob, but Israel will be your name.&quot; And he named him Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Genesis 35:11">11</a> God told him, &quot;I am God Almighty: have many children and grow into a nation and many nations will come from you, and you will have kings among your descendants.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Genesis 35:12">12</a> I will give you and your descendants the land I gave to Abraham and Isaac.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Genesis 35:13">13</a> God left him at the place where he had spoken with him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Genesis 35:14">14</a> Jacob put up a stone pillar where he had talked with God. He poured out a drink offering on it, and also poured oil on it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Genesis 35:15">15</a> Jacob named the place where God spoke with him Bethel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Genesis 35:16">16</a> They left Bethel, and were not far from Ephrath when Rachel started to give birth, and it was difficult.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Genesis 35:17">17</a> When she was giving birth with difficulty, the midwife told her, &quot;Don&#39;t be afraid; you will have another son too.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Genesis 35:18">18</a> As she was dying, she named her son Benoni, but his father named him Benjamin.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Genesis 35:19">19</a> Rachel died and was buried on the road to Ephrath, now called Bethlehem.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Genesis 35:20">20</a> Jacob put up a stone marker on her burial site; this is Rachel&#39;s grave marker to this day.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Genesis 35:21">21</a> Israel traveled and set up his tent past the Tower of Edar.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Genesis 35:22">22</a> When Israel lived in that land, Reuben slept with Bilhah, his father&#39;s partner, and Israel found out. Jacob had twelve sons.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Genesis 35:23">23</a> Leah&#39;s sons: Reuben, Jacob&#39;s first son, Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, and Zebulun.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Genesis 35:24">24</a> Rachel&#39;s sons are Joseph and Benjamin.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Genesis 35:25">25</a> Bilhah, Rachel&#39;s servant, had two sons: Dan and Naphtali.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Genesis 35:26">26</a> Zilpah, Leah&#39;s servant, had two sons with Jacob: Gad and Asher. They were born to Jacob in Padanaram.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Genesis 35:27">27</a> Jacob went to his father Isaac in Mamre, to the town of Arbah, now called Hebron, where Abraham and Isaac had lived.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Genesis 35:28">28</a> Isaac lived for 180 years.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Genesis 35:29">29</a> Isaac passed away, died, and joined his ancestors, being very old: and his sons Esau and Jacob buried him.</p>
