<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Genesis 37:1">1</a> Jacob lived in the land where his father had been a foreigner, in Canaan.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Genesis 37:2">2</a> This lists Jacob&#39;s family. Seventeen-year-old Joseph was tending sheep with his brothers, the sons of Bilhah and Zilpah, his father&#39;s wives. He told his father about the bad things they were doing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Genesis 37:3">3</a> Israel loved Joseph more than his other children because Joseph was born when Israel was already old, and he gave Joseph a colorful coat.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Genesis 37:4">4</a> When his brothers saw that their father loved him more than them, they hated him and could not speak kindly to him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Genesis 37:5">5</a> Joseph had a dream and shared it with his brothers, which made them hate him even more.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Genesis 37:6">6</a> He said to them, &quot;Please listen to this dream I had.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Genesis 37:7">7</a> Look, we were tying up bundles of grain in the field when suddenly my bundle stood up straight. Then your bundles gathered around and bowed to mine.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Genesis 37:8">8</a> His brothers asked him, &quot;Will you really rule over us? Will you actually be our boss?&quot; They disliked him even more because of his dreams and what he said.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Genesis 37:9">9</a> He had another dream and shared it with his brothers, saying, &quot;Look, I dreamed again; the sun, the moon, and eleven stars bowed down to me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Genesis 37:10">10</a> He told his father and brothers, and his father scolded him, saying, &quot;What kind of dream did you have? Will your mother, brothers and I actually come to bow down to you on the ground?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Genesis 37:11">11</a> His brothers were jealous of him, but his father paid attention to what was said.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Genesis 37:12">12</a> His brothers went to graze their father&#39;s sheep in Shechem.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Genesis 37:13">13</a> Israel said to Joseph, &quot;Aren&#39;t your brothers pasturing the sheep in Shechem? Come, I&#39;ll send you to them.&quot; And he replied, &quot;I&#39;m here.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Genesis 37:14">14</a> He told him, &quot;Please go and check if your brothers and the flocks are okay; then come back and tell me.&quot; So he sent him from the Hebron valley, and he went to Shechem.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Genesis 37:15">15</a> A man found him lost in the field and asked, &quot;What are you looking for?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Genesis 37:16">16</a> He said, &quot;I&#39;m looking for my brothers. Please tell me where they are taking care of their sheep.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Genesis 37:17">17</a> The man said, &quot;They left this place; I heard them plan to go to Dothan.&quot; So Joseph followed his brothers and found them in Dothan.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Genesis 37:18">18</a> When they saw him from far away, before he got close to them, they planned together to kill him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Genesis 37:19">19</a> They said to each other, &quot;Look, here comes the dreamer.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Genesis 37:20">20</a> Let&#39;s go now, kill him, throw him into a pit, and say a wild animal ate him. Then we&#39;ll see what happens to his dreams.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Genesis 37:21">21</a> Reuben heard them and saved him from their hands, saying, Let&#39;s not kill him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Genesis 37:22">22</a> Reuben told them, &quot;Don&#39;t kill him. Just throw him into this pit in the desert, but don&#39;t hurt him.&quot; He planned to save him from them and return him to his father.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Genesis 37:23">23</a> When Joseph reached his brothers, they took off his colorful coat that he was wearing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Genesis 37:24">24</a> They grabbed him and threw him into a hole. The hole was empty without any water in it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Genesis 37:25">25</a> They sat down to eat bread, and when they looked up, they saw a group of Ishmaelites coming from Gilead with their camels carrying spices, balm, and myrrh to take to Egypt.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Genesis 37:26">26</a> Judah asked his brothers, &quot;What do we gain if we kill our brother and hide his blood?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Genesis 37:27">27</a> Come, let&#39;s sell him to the Ishmaelites and not hurt him; he is our brother, our own flesh. And his brothers agreed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Genesis 37:28">28</a> Some Midianite traders passed by; they pulled Joseph out of the hole and sold him to the Ishmaelites for twenty silver coins, and they took Joseph to Egypt.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Genesis 37:29">29</a> Reuben went back to the pit and saw that Joseph was not there, so he tore his clothes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Genesis 37:30">30</a> He went back to his brothers and said, &quot;The boy is gone; where can I go now?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Genesis 37:31">31</a> They took Joseph&#39;s coat, killed a young goat, and dipped the coat in the blood.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Genesis 37:32">32</a> They sent the colorful coat and took it to their father, saying, &quot;We found this. See if it&#39;s your son&#39;s coat or not.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Genesis 37:33">33</a> He recognized it and said, &quot;This is my son&#39;s coat; a wild animal must have eaten him. Joseph has surely been torn apart.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Genesis 37:34">34</a> Jacob tore his clothes, wore rough fabric around his waist, and grieved for his son for many days.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Genesis 37:35">35</a> All his sons and daughters tried to comfort him, but he wouldn&#39;t be comforted and said, &quot;I will mourn for my son until I die.&quot; So he cried for his son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Genesis 37:36">36</a> The Midianites sold him to Potiphar in Egypt, who was an official of the king and the captain of the guard.</p>
