<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Genesis 33:1">1</a> Jacob looked and saw Esau coming with four hundred men. He split the children between Leah, Rachel, and the two servant women.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Genesis 33:2">2</a> He placed the servant women and their kids first, then Leah and her kids in the middle, and Rachel and Joseph last.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Genesis 33:3">3</a> He went ahead of them, bowed down to the ground seven times as he approached his brother.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Genesis 33:4">4</a> Esau ran to meet him, hugged him, cried on his shoulder, and kissed him; and they both cried.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Genesis 33:5">5</a> He looked up, saw the women and children, and asked, &quot;Who are these with you?&quot; He answered, &quot;These are the children God has kindly given to me, your servant.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Genesis 33:6">6</a> The female servants and their children approached and bowed down.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Genesis 33:7">7</a> Leah and her children approached and bowed down. Then Joseph and Rachel came close and they also bowed down.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Genesis 33:8">8</a> He asked, &quot;What&#39;s the purpose of all the animals I met?&quot; And he replied, &quot;They are to gain favor in your eyes.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Genesis 33:9">9</a> Esau said, &quot;I have enough, my brother; keep what you have for yourself.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Genesis 33:10">10</a> Jacob said, &quot;Please, if I have found favor with you, accept my gift from me. Seeing you is like seeing the face of God since you have accepted me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Genesis 33:11">11</a> Please accept my gift to you; God has been good to me, and I have all I need. He insisted, and the other person accepted it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Genesis 33:12">12</a> He said, &quot;Let&#39;s start our trip, let&#39;s go, and I will lead you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Genesis 33:13">13</a> He said to him, &quot;You know the children are delicate, and the animals with young are with me. If anyone drives them too hard, even for one day, they will all die.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Genesis 33:14">14</a> Please, let my lord go ahead of his servant, and I will follow slowly at the pace of the cattle and children until I meet my lord in Seir.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Genesis 33:15">15</a> Esau said, &quot;Can I leave some of my people with you?&quot; But he answered, &quot;Why do that? I just want to be on good terms with you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Genesis 33:16">16</a> So Esau went back to Seir that day.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Genesis 33:17">17</a> Jacob traveled to Succoth, built a house for himself, and made shelters for his animals; that&#39;s why the place is named Succoth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Genesis 33:18">18</a> Jacob arrived at Shalem, a city near Shechem in Canaan, after traveling from Padanaram and set up his tent outside the city.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Genesis 33:19">19</a> He bought a piece of land where he had pitched his tent from Hamor&#39;s descendants, the father of Shechem, for a hundred coins.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Genesis 33:20">20</a> He built an altar there and named it God of Israel.</p>
