<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Genesis 25:1">1</a> Abraham married another woman, and her name was Keturah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Genesis 25:2">2</a> She gave birth to Zimran, Jokshan, Medan, Midian, Ishbak, and Shuah for him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Genesis 25:3">3</a> Jokshan had Sheba and Dedan as sons. Dedan&#39;s sons were Asshurim, Letushim, and Leummim.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Genesis 25:4">4</a> Midian&#39;s sons were Ephah, Epher, Hanoch, Abidah, and Eldaah. All these were Keturah&#39;s children.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Genesis 25:5">5</a> Abraham gave everything he owned to Isaac.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Genesis 25:6">6</a> Abraham gave presents to his other sons, whose mother was a concubine, and sent them away from his son Isaac to the east land while he was still alive.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Genesis 25:7">7</a> Abraham lived 175 years.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Genesis 25:8">8</a> Then Abraham passed away and died at an old age, as an old man who lived a long life, and he joined his ancestors.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Genesis 25:9">9</a> Isaac and Ishmael, his sons, buried him in the Machpelah cave, in Ephron the Hittite&#39;s field near Mamre.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Genesis 25:10">10</a> Abraham bought the field from the Heth sons; there, Abraham and his wife Sarah were buried.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Genesis 25:11">11</a> After Abraham died, God blessed his son Isaac, who lived near Lahairoi well.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Genesis 25:12">12</a> Here are the descendants of Ishmael, Abraham&#39;s son, born to Hagar the Egyptian, Sarah&#39;s servant, by Abraham:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Genesis 25:13">13</a> These are the names of Ishmael&#39;s sons, listed by their names, in order of their birth: Ishmael&#39;s first son is Nebajoth, then Kedar, Adbeel, and Mibsam.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Genesis 25:14">14</a> Mishma, Dumah, and Massa,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Genesis 25:15">15</a> Hadar, Tema, Jetur, Naphish, and Kedemah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Genesis 25:16">16</a> These are Ishmael&#39;s sons, with their names, by their towns and fortresses; twelve leaders for their tribes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Genesis 25:17">17</a> Ishmael lived 137 years. Then he died and joined his ancestors.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Genesis 25:18">18</a> They lived from Havilah to Shur, near Egypt on the way to Assyria; and he died with all his brothers around.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Genesis 25:19">19</a> These are the descendants of Isaac, Abraham&#39;s son: Abraham was the father of Isaac.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Genesis 25:20">20</a> Isaac was 40 when he married Rebekah, who was the daughter of Bethuel from Syria and the sister of Laban, also from Syria.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Genesis 25:21">21</a> Isaac prayed to God for his wife because she could not have children, and God answered his prayer, so his wife Rebekah became pregnant.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Genesis 25:22">22</a> The babies inside her were fighting, and she wondered, &quot;Why is this happening to me?&quot; So she went to ask God for answers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Genesis 25:23">23</a> God said to her, &quot;Two nations are in your womb, and two different groups of people will come from you; one group will be stronger than the other, and the older will serve the younger.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Genesis 25:24">24</a> When the time came for her to give birth, she had twins in her belly.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Genesis 25:25">25</a> The first baby was born red and covered with hair like a coat, and they named him Esau.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Genesis 25:26">26</a> After that, his brother came out, grabbing Esau&#39;s heel, and they named him Jacob. Isaac was sixty years old when they were born.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Genesis 25:27">27</a> The boys grew up: Esau became a skilled hunter, loving the outdoors; Jacob was a quiet man, staying among the tents.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Genesis 25:28">28</a> Isaac loved Esau because he ate Esau&#39;s wild game, but Rebekah loved Jacob.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Genesis 25:29">29</a> Jacob cooked stew, and Esau returned from the field, feeling weak.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Genesis 25:30">30</a> Esau said to Jacob, &quot;Please give me some of that red stew because I am very tired.&quot; That is why he was also called Edom.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Genesis 25:31">31</a> Jacob said, Sell me your birthright today.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Genesis 25:32">32</a> Esau said, &quot;Look, I&#39;m about to die; what good is my birthright to me?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Genesis 25:33">33</a> Jacob said, &quot;Promise me today&quot;; so he promised to him, and he sold his right as the firstborn to Jacob.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Genesis 25:34">34</a> Jacob gave Esau bread and lentil stew; Esau ate, drank, got up, and left, showing he cared little for his birthright.</p>
