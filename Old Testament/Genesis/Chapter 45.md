<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Genesis 45:1">1</a> Joseph could not hold back in front of everyone present; he shouted, &quot;Everyone leave me!&quot; No one stayed with him when Joseph revealed his identity to his brothers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Genesis 45:2">2</a> He cried loudly, and the Egyptians and Pharaoh&#39;s household heard it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Genesis 45:3">3</a> Joseph said to his brothers, &quot;I am Joseph. Is our father still alive?&quot; But his brothers couldn&#39;t answer him because they were upset to see him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Genesis 45:4">4</a> Joseph told his brothers, &quot;Please come closer to me.&quot; They came closer. Then he said, &quot;I am your brother Joseph, the one you sold into Egypt.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Genesis 45:5">5</a> So now, don&#39;t be sad or mad at yourselves for selling me here; because God sent me ahead of you to save lives.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Genesis 45:6">6</a> For two years now, there has been famine in the land, and for the next five years, there will be no plowing or harvesting.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Genesis 45:7">7</a> God sent me ahead of you to keep your family alive on the earth, and to save your lives with a big rescue.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Genesis 45:8">8</a> It was not you who sent me here, but God. He has made me like a father to Pharaoh, the boss of his household, and the leader of all Egypt.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Genesis 45:9">9</a> Hurry and go to my father, and tell him, &#39;Your son Joseph says, God has made me ruler over all of Egypt: come to me, don&#39;t delay.&#39;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Genesis 45:10">10</a> You will live in the land of Goshen, close to me, with your children, grandchildren, sheep, cattle, and all your possessions.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Genesis 45:11">11</a> I will provide for you there, because five more years of famine are coming, so that you, your family, and all you have won&#39;t become poor.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Genesis 45:12">12</a> Look, your eyes and my brother Benjamin&#39;s eyes can see that it is really me talking to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Genesis 45:13">13</a> Tell my father about all my success in Egypt and everything you&#39;ve seen; hurry and bring my father here.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Genesis 45:14">14</a> He hugged his brother Benjamin and cried; and Benjamin cried on his shoulder.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Genesis 45:15">15</a> He kissed all his brothers and cried on them; then his brothers spoke with him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Genesis 45:16">16</a> People in Pharaoh&#39;s palace heard that Joseph&#39;s brothers had come, and this news made Pharaoh and his servants very happy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Genesis 45:17">17</a> Pharaoh said to Joseph, &quot;Tell your brothers to do this: Load your animals and go to the land of Canaan.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Genesis 45:18">18</a> Bring your father and your families to me, and I will give you the best parts of Egypt, and you will have plenty to eat.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Genesis 45:19">19</a> God has ordered you to do this: Get carts from Egypt for your children and wives, and bring your father here.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Genesis 45:20">20</a> Don&#39;t worry about your belongings; all the best of Egypt is yours.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Genesis 45:21">21</a> The Israelites did as told, and Joseph gave them carts as Pharaoh ordered, and also gave them food for the journey.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Genesis 45:22">22</a> He gave clothes to all of them, but he gave Benjamin three hundred silver coins and five sets of clothes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Genesis 45:23">23</a> He sent his father the following: ten donkeys carrying Egypt&#39;s best goods, and ten female donkeys loaded with grain, bread, and food for his journey.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Genesis 45:24">24</a> He sent his brothers off, and they left. He told them, &quot;Make sure you don&#39;t argue on the way.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Genesis 45:25">25</a> They left Egypt and arrived in Canaan to meet their father Jacob.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Genesis 45:26">26</a> They told him, &quot;Joseph is still alive and he rules over all of Egypt.&quot; But Jacob felt weak because he did not believe them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Genesis 45:27">27</a> They told him everything Joseph said to them, and when he saw the carts Joseph sent to bring him, Jacob their father felt better.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Genesis 45:28">28</a> Israel said, &quot;It is enough; my son Joseph is still alive. I will go and see him before I die.&quot;</p>
