<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Genesis 49:1">1</a> Jacob called his sons and said, &quot;Come here so I can tell you what will happen to you in the future.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Genesis 49:2">2</a> Come together and listen, children of Jacob; pay attention to Israel, your father.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Genesis 49:3">3</a> Reuben, you are my firstborn, my strength, the first sign of my power, the best in honor, and the best in force.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Genesis 49:4">4</a> Like water, you will not succeed; for you slept in your father&#39;s bed and soiled it—he climbed onto my bed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Genesis 49:5">5</a> Simeon and Levi are brothers; tools of violence are in their homes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Genesis 49:6">6</a> My soul, do not join their secrets; do not be part of their group, my respect. In their anger, they killed a person, and in their stubbornness, they destroyed a wall.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Genesis 49:7">7</a> Their anger is cursed because it was strong; their rage is cursed because it was harsh. I will separate them in Jacob and spread them out in Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Genesis 49:8">8</a> Judah, your brothers will praise you; your hand will be on your enemies&#39; neck; your father&#39;s children will bow down to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Genesis 49:9">9</a> Judah is like a young lion: after hunting, my son, you have rested. He lies down like a lion, and like a mighty lion; who dares to wake him?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Genesis 49:10">10</a> Judah will always have a ruler, and a leader will come from his descendants, until Shiloh comes; and the people will follow him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Genesis 49:11">11</a> He tied his young donkey to the grapevine, and his colt to the best vine; he washed his clothes in wine, and his robes in grape juice.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Genesis 49:12">12</a> His eyes will look red from wine, and his teeth will be white from milk.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Genesis 49:13">13</a> Zebulun will live by the sea port; he will be a safe place for ships, and his land will reach to Sidon.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Genesis 49:14">14</a> Issachar is a strong donkey lying down between two loads.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Genesis 49:15">15</a> He saw that rest was good and the land beautiful; he bent his shoulder to work and became a laborer who pays taxes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Genesis 49:16">16</a> Dan will judge his people like one of the Israelite tribes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Genesis 49:17">17</a> Dan will be like a snake on the road, a viper on the path, that bites the horse&#39;s heels, making its rider fall off.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Genesis 49:18">18</a> I have waited for your salvation, God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Genesis 49:19">19</a> Gad will be attacked by a group, but in the end, he will win.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Genesis 49:20">20</a> From Asher, his food will be rich, and he will provide fine foods fit for a king.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Genesis 49:21">21</a> Naphtali is a free deer; he speaks beautiful words.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Genesis 49:22">22</a> Joseph is like a fruitful vine, a fruitful vine near a spring, whose branches climb over the wall.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Genesis 49:23">23</a> The archers hurt him badly, shot at him, and hated him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Genesis 49:24">24</a> His bow stayed strong, and his arms got strong by the mighty God of Jacob; from there comes the shepherd, the rock of Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Genesis 49:25">25</a> God, your father&#39;s God, will help you; the Almighty will bless you with heaven&#39;s blessings, the deep&#39;s riches, and the fruitfulness of breast and womb.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Genesis 49:26">26</a> Your father&#39;s blessings are greater than those of my ancestors, reaching to the farthest everlasting hills. They will rest on Joseph&#39;s head and on the forehead of the one set apart from his brothers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Genesis 49:27">27</a> Benjamin will hunt like a wolf: in the morning he will eat the prey, and at night he will share the loot.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Genesis 49:28">28</a> These are the twelve tribes of Israel: their father spoke to each of them and gave them his blessing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Genesis 49:29">29</a> He gave them instructions, saying, &quot;I will soon join my ancestors: bury me beside my fathers in the cave in Ephron the Hittite&#39;s field.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Genesis 49:30">30</a> In the cave in Machpelah field, in front of Mamre in Canaan, Abraham bought it with the field from Ephron the Hittite to own as a burial place.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Genesis 49:31">31</a> They buried Abraham and his wife Sarah there; they also buried Isaac and his wife Rebekah there; and I buried Leah there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Genesis 49:32">32</a> The buying of the field and the cave in it was from the Heth people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Genesis 49:33">33</a> After Jacob finished giving instructions to his sons, he pulled his legs up onto the bed, passed away, and joined his ancestors.</p>
