<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Genesis 39:1">1</a> Joseph was taken to Egypt; Potiphar, an Egyptian officer of Pharaoh and captain of the guard, bought him from the Ishmaelites who had brought him there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Genesis 39:2">2</a> God was with Joseph, and he succeeded in everything; he lived in his Egyptian master&#39;s house.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Genesis 39:3">3</a> His master noticed that God was with him and that God made everything he did successful.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Genesis 39:4">4</a> Joseph won his favor and worked for him. He put him in charge of his house and everything he owned was given to him to look after.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Genesis 39:5">5</a> From the time he became the manager of the house and everything in it, God blessed the Egyptian&#39;s home because of Joseph; everything he owned, both in the home and in the fields, was blessed by God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Genesis 39:6">6</a> He trusted Joseph with everything he had and didn&#39;t worry about anything except the food he ate. Joseph was handsome and well-liked.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Genesis 39:7">7</a> After some time, his master&#39;s wife noticed Joseph and said, &quot;Sleep with me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Genesis 39:8">8</a> But he refused and told his master&#39;s wife, &quot;Look, my master doesn&#39;t know what I have in the house, and he has entrusted everything he owns to me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Genesis 39:9">9</a> No one in this house is greater than me; he has kept nothing from me except you, because you are his wife. How can I then do such a terrible wrong and sin against God?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Genesis 39:10">10</a> As she talked to Joseph every day, he did not listen to her or agree to sleep with her or spend time with her.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Genesis 39:11">11</a> Around that time, Joseph entered the house to do his work, and none of the men of the house were inside.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Genesis 39:12">12</a> She grabbed his clothes and said, &quot;Sleep with me,&quot; but he left his clothes in her hand and ran away.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Genesis 39:13">13</a> When she saw he had left his clothes with her and ran away,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Genesis 39:14">14</a> She called to the men of her house and said to them, &quot;Look, he has brought a Hebrew here to make fun of us. He came to me to sleep with me, but I screamed loudly.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Genesis 39:15">15</a> When he heard me raise my voice and cry out, he left his clothes with me and ran away.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Genesis 39:16">16</a> She kept his clothing with her until her husband came home.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Genesis 39:17">17</a> She said to him, &quot;The Hebrew servant you brought here came to make fun of me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Genesis 39:18">18</a> As I raised my voice and screamed, he left his clothes with me and ran away.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Genesis 39:19">19</a> When his master heard what his wife said, &quot;This is how your servant treated me,&quot; he became very angry.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Genesis 39:20">20</a> Joseph&#39;s boss arrested him and put him in the jail where the king&#39;s prisoners were kept, and he stayed in the jail.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Genesis 39:21">21</a> God was with Joseph, showed him kindness, and made the prison guard like him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Genesis 39:22">22</a> The prison guard put Joseph in charge of all the prisoners; whatever was done there, Joseph did it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Genesis 39:23">23</a> The prison guard did not worry about anything in his care; because God was with Joseph, and God made everything Joseph did successful.</p>
