<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Genesis 14:1">1</a> During the time when Amraphel ruled Shinar, Arioch ruled Ellasar, Chedorlaomer ruled Elam, and Tidal ruled many nations;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Genesis 14:2">2</a> These kings fought against Bera of Sodom, Birsha of Gomorrah, Shinab of Admah, Shemeber of Zeboiim, and the king of Bela, which is Zoar.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Genesis 14:3">3</a> All these were united in the Siddim Valley, now the Salt Sea.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Genesis 14:4">4</a> For twelve years they obeyed Chedorlaomer, but in the thirteenth year, they rebelled.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Genesis 14:5">5</a> In the fourteenth year, Chedorlaomer and the kings with him defeated the Rephaims in Ashteroth Karnaim, the Zuzims in Ham, and the Emins in Shaveh Kiriathaim.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Genesis 14:6">6</a> The Horites lived in Seir mountain, reaching to Elparan near the desert.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Genesis 14:7">7</a> They went back and came to Enmishpat, known as Kadesh, and defeated all the land of the Amalekites, and also the Amorites who lived in Hazezontamar.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Genesis 14:8">8</a> The kings of Sodom, Gomorrah, Admah, Zeboiim, and Bela (also called Zoar) went out and fought a battle with them in the Siddim Valley.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Genesis 14:9">9</a> With Chedorlaomer, the king of Elam, Tidal, the king of nations, Amraphel, the king of Shinar, and Arioch, the king of Ellasar; four kings joined together against five.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Genesis 14:10">10</a> The Valley of Siddim was full of tar pits. The kings of Sodom and Gomorrah ran away, but they fell into the pits. Those who were left ran to the hills.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Genesis 14:11">11</a> They took all the possessions of Sodom and Gomorrah, all their food, and left.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Genesis 14:12">12</a> They captured Lot, Abram&#39;s nephew, who lived in Sodom, along with his possessions, and left.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Genesis 14:13">13</a> Someone who had escaped came and told Abram, the Hebrew, who was living near Mamre the Amorite, brother of Eshcol and Aner. These men were allies of Abram.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Genesis 14:14">14</a> When Abram learned his brother was captured, he equipped his skilled servants, born in his home, 318 in number, and chased the captors to Dan.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Genesis 14:15">15</a> He split his group and, with his servants, attacked at night, defeated them, and chased them to Hobah, north of Damascus.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Genesis 14:16">16</a> He took back all the items, and also returned his brother Lot, his items, the women, and the people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Genesis 14:17">17</a> The king of Sodom went to meet him after he came back from defeating Chedorlaomer and the kings with him, at the Valley of Shaveh, also called the king&#39;s valley.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Genesis 14:18">18</a> Melchizedek, the king of Salem, brought out bread and wine; he was a priest of the most high God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Genesis 14:19">19</a> He blessed him and said, &quot;May Abram be blessed by God Most High, owner of heaven and earth.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Genesis 14:20">20</a> May God, the Most High, be blessed, for he has given your enemies over to you. And Abram gave him a tenth of everything.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Genesis 14:21">21</a> The king of Sodom said to Abram, &quot;Give me the people, and keep the goods for yourself.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Genesis 14:22">22</a> Abram said to the king of Sodom, &quot;I have raised my hand to God, the highest God, the owner of heaven and earth.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Genesis 14:23">23</a> I won&#39;t take anything from you, not even a thread or a shoestring, so you can&#39;t say you made Abram rich.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Genesis 14:24">24</a> Keep only what the young men have eaten and the share for the men who joined me—Aner, Eshcol, and Mamre; they should get their share.</p>
