<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Genesis 40:1">1</a> After these events, the Egyptian king&#39;s cupbearer and baker upset their king.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Genesis 40:2">2</a> The king was very angry with two of his officials, the head of the cupbearers and the head of the bakers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Genesis 40:3">3</a> He put them in jail at the guard captain&#39;s house, where Joseph was held.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Genesis 40:4">4</a> The guard captain put Joseph in charge of them, and he took care of them. They stayed there for some time.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Genesis 40:5">5</a> Both the king&#39;s cupbearer and the baker, who were in prison, had a dream on the same night, and each dream had its own meaning.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Genesis 40:6">6</a> Joseph came to them in the morning, saw them, and noticed they were sad.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Genesis 40:7">7</a> He asked Pharaoh&#39;s officials who were with him in his master&#39;s prison, &quot;Why do you look so unhappy today?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Genesis 40:8">8</a> They told him, &quot;We had a dream, but no one can explain it.&quot; Joseph said to them, &quot;Doesn&#39;t God give meanings to dreams? Please share them with me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Genesis 40:9">9</a> The head servant told his dream to Joseph and said, &quot;In my dream, I saw a grapevine in front of me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Genesis 40:10">10</a> The vine had three branches. It looked like it was budding, its flowers bloomed, and its bunches of grapes ripened.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Genesis 40:11">11</a> I held Pharaoh&#39;s cup and squashed the grapes into it, then I handed the cup to Pharaoh.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Genesis 40:12">12</a> Joseph told him, &quot;This is what it means: the three branches represent three days.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Genesis 40:13">13</a> In three days, Pharaoh will free you and give you back your job. Then you will serve Pharaoh his drink like you did before when you were his cupbearer.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Genesis 40:14">14</a> Please remember me when things go well for you, and be kind to me. Ask Pharaoh to free me from this prison.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Genesis 40:15">15</a> I was indeed kidnapped from the land of the Hebrews, and I have done nothing here to deserve being thrown into a dungeon.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Genesis 40:16">16</a> When the head baker saw that the dream reading was good, he told Joseph, &quot;I was also in my dream, and look, I had three white baskets on my head.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Genesis 40:17">17</a> In the top basket, there were all kinds of baked goods for the king, and birds ate them from the basket on my head.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Genesis 40:18">18</a> Joseph replied, &quot;This is what it means: The three baskets represent three days.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Genesis 40:19">19</a> In three days, the king will remove your head and hang you on a tree; the birds will eat your flesh.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Genesis 40:20">20</a> On the third day, Pharaoh&#39;s birthday, he threw a party for all his workers and honored his main cupbearer and baker in front of them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Genesis 40:21">21</a> He gave the chief butler his job back, and he handed the cup to the Pharaoh.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Genesis 40:22">22</a> He executed the chief baker, just as Joseph had explained to them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Genesis 40:23">23</a> The head servant did not remember Joseph; he forgot him.</p>
