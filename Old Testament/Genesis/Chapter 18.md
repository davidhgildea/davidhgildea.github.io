<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Genesis 18:1">1</a> God appeared to him in the fields of Mamre while he was sitting at the entrance of his tent during the hottest part of the day.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Genesis 18:2">2</a> He looked up and saw three men standing nearby. When he noticed them, he ran from his tent to greet them and bowed down to the ground.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Genesis 18:3">3</a> He said, &quot;My God, if I have now found favor with you, please do not leave your servant.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Genesis 18:4">4</a> Please bring some water, wash your feet, and rest under the tree.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Genesis 18:5">5</a> I will get some bread to refresh you. Then you can move on since you&#39;ve stopped by your servant&#39;s place. They replied, &quot;Go ahead, just as you&#39;ve said.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Genesis 18:6">6</a> Abraham hurried to the tent to Sarah and said, &quot;Quickly prepare three measures of good flour, knead it, and bake some bread on the fire.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Genesis 18:7">7</a> Abraham ran to his cattle, took a young and good calf, and handed it to a servant; and the servant hurried to prepare it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Genesis 18:8">8</a> He brought them butter, milk, and the cooked calf, placed it in front of them, and stood by them under the tree while they ate.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Genesis 18:9">9</a> They asked him, &quot;Where is Sarah your wife?&quot; He replied, &quot;Look, she&#39;s in the tent.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Genesis 18:10">10</a> He said, &quot;I will definitely come back to you when the time comes; and then, Sarah your wife will have a son.&quot; And Sarah heard this from the entrance of the tent, which was behind him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Genesis 18:11">11</a> Abraham and Sarah were very old, and Sarah could no longer have children.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Genesis 18:12">12</a> So Sarah laughed quietly, thinking, &quot;Now that I am old, can I enjoy life again, with my husband old too?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Genesis 18:13">13</a> God said to Abraham, &quot;Why did Sarah laugh and say, &#39;Can I really have a child when I am old?&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Genesis 18:14">14</a> Is anything too hard for God? I will come back to you at the set time next year, and Sarah will have a son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Genesis 18:15">15</a> Then Sarah denied it, saying, &quot;I didn&#39;t laugh,&quot; because she was scared. But he said, &quot;No, you did laugh.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Genesis 18:16">16</a> The men got up from there, looked toward Sodom, and Abraham walked with them to help them on their way.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Genesis 18:17">17</a> God thought, &quot;Should I keep what I am going to do from Abraham?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Genesis 18:18">18</a> Abraham will surely grow into a great and strong nation, and all the nations on earth will be blessed through him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Genesis 18:19">19</a> I know he will tell his children and family to follow God&#39;s way and to do what is right and fair, so God can do for Abraham what He promised.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Genesis 18:20">20</a> God said, &quot;Because Sodom and Gomorrah&#39;s cry is loud and their sin is very bad.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Genesis 18:21">21</a> I will go down now to see if what they have done matches the outcry that has reached me; if it doesn&#39;t, I will find out.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Genesis 18:22">22</a> The men looked away from there and headed toward Sodom, while Abraham still stood in front of God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Genesis 18:23">23</a> Abraham approached and asked, &quot;Will you also destroy the good people along with the bad?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Genesis 18:24">24</a> Maybe if there are 50 good people in the city, will you still destroy it and not save it for the sake of the 50 good people inside?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Genesis 18:25">25</a> You would never do such a thing, to kill the good people along with the bad ones, making the good and bad the same. Surely, the Judge of all the earth will do what is right.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Genesis 18:26">26</a> And God said, If I find fifty good people in Sodom, I will save the whole city for them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Genesis 18:27">27</a> Abraham replied, &quot;Look, I&#39;ve decided to talk to God, even though I am only dust and ashes.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Genesis 18:28">28</a> What if five righteous people are missing from the fifty? Will you destroy the whole city for just five? And He said, If I find forty-five, I won&#39;t destroy it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Genesis 18:29">29</a> He spoke to him once more, saying, &quot;What if only forty are found there?&quot; And He answered, &quot;I will not do it for the sake of the forty.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Genesis 18:30">30</a> He said to him, &quot;Please don&#39;t be angry, God, and I will speak: Maybe there will be thirty good people found there.&quot; And God replied, &quot;If I find thirty there, I will not destroy it.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Genesis 18:31">31</a> He said, &quot;Look, I&#39;ve decided to talk to God. Maybe there will be twenty good people there.&quot; God replied, &quot;If I find twenty good people, I won&#39;t destroy it for their sake.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Genesis 18:32">32</a> He said, &quot;Please don&#39;t be mad, God, and I will say this just one more time: What if ten good people are there?&quot; God replied, &quot;I won&#39;t destroy it if I find ten.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Genesis 18:33">33</a> After talking with Abraham, God left, and Abraham went back to his place.</p>
