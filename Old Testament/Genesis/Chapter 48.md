<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Genesis 48:1">1</a> After these events, someone informed Joseph, &quot;Look, your father is ill,&quot; so he brought his two sons, Manasseh and Ephraim, with him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Genesis 48:2">2</a> Someone told Jacob, &quot;Look, your son Joseph is coming to you,&quot; and Jacob gathered his strength and sat up in bed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Genesis 48:3">3</a> Jacob told Joseph, &quot;God Almighty appeared to me at Luz in Canaan and blessed me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Genesis 48:4">4</a> He told me, &#39;Look, I will make you have many children and increase your numbers. I will turn you into a large group of people, and I will give this land to your descendants after you to own forever.&#39;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Genesis 48:5">5</a> Your two sons, Ephraim and Manasseh, born in Egypt before I arrived, are now mine just like Reuben and Simeon.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Genesis 48:6">6</a> Your children born after them will belong to you and take their brothers&#39; family name in the inheritance.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Genesis 48:7">7</a> When I was returning from Padan, Rachel died near me in Canaan before we reached Ephrath, and I buried her on the way to Ephrath, which is Bethlehem.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Genesis 48:8">8</a> Israel saw Joseph&#39;s sons and asked, &quot;Who are they?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Genesis 48:9">9</a> Joseph told his father, &quot;These are my sons, whom God has given to me here.&quot; Then he said, &quot;Please bring them to me, so I can bless them.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Genesis 48:10">10</a> Israel&#39;s eyes were weak from old age, and he could not see well. He brought them close, kissed them, and hugged them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Genesis 48:11">11</a> Israel said to Joseph, I never expected to see you again, but now, God has let me see your children too.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Genesis 48:12">12</a> Joseph took them from between his knees and bowed down with his face to the ground.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Genesis 48:13">13</a> Joseph took both Ephraim in his right hand to his father&#39;s left, and Manasseh in his left hand to his father&#39;s right, and brought them close to him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Genesis 48:14">14</a> Israel reached out his right hand and put it on Ephraim&#39;s head, the younger one, and his left hand on Manasseh&#39;s head, moving his hands purposely because Manasseh was the older.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Genesis 48:15">15</a> He blessed Joseph and said, &quot;God, who my ancestors Abraham and Isaac followed, the God who has taken care of me all my life until today,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Genesis 48:16">16</a> The angel who saved me from all harm, bless the boys. Let them carry on my name and the names of my ancestors Abraham and Isaac, and let them become a large group on the earth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Genesis 48:17">17</a> When Joseph saw his father put his right hand on Ephraim&#39;s head, he was unhappy. He lifted his father&#39;s hand to move it from Ephraim&#39;s head to Manasseh&#39;s head.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Genesis 48:18">18</a> Joseph said to his father, &quot;No, my father, this one is the oldest; place your right hand on his head.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Genesis 48:19">19</a> His father said no, and replied, I know, my son, I know. He too will become a group of people and be great. Yet his younger brother will be even greater, and his descendants will form many nations.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Genesis 48:20">20</a> On that day, he gave them a blessing, saying, &quot;Israel will use your name to give blessings, praying, &#39;May God make you like Ephraim and Manasseh,&#39;&quot; and he put Ephraim ahead of Manasseh.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Genesis 48:21">21</a> Israel said to Joseph, &quot;Look, I am dying, but God will be with you and will take you back to your ancestors&#39; land.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Genesis 48:22">22</a> I have also given you an extra share beyond your brothers, which I took from the Amorite using my sword and bow.</p>
