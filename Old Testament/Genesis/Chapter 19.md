<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Genesis 19:1">1</a> Two angels came to Sodom in the evening; Lot was sitting at the entrance of Sodom. When Lot saw them, he got up to greet them and bowed down with his face to the ground.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Genesis 19:2">2</a> He said, &quot;Please, come to my house and stay the night. Wash your feet, and you can leave early in the morning.&quot; But they answered, &quot;No, we&#39;ll stay outside all night.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Genesis 19:3">3</a> He insisted they stay with him, so they did and went into his house. He prepared a meal for them, baked bread without yeast, and they ate.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Genesis 19:4">4</a> Before they went to bed, the men of the city, the men of Sodom, surrounded the house, all men, both old and young, from every part of the city.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Genesis 19:5">5</a> They called to Lot and asked, &quot;Where are the men who came to you tonight? Bring them out to us so we can meet them.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Genesis 19:6">6</a> Lot went outside to meet them and closed the door behind him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Genesis 19:7">7</a> Please, my friends, don&#39;t do this evil.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Genesis 19:8">8</a> Look, I have two daughters who have never been with a man. Let me bring them to you, and you can do what you think is right to them. But don&#39;t do anything to these men, because they are under the protection of my roof.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Genesis 19:9">9</a> They said, &quot;Move back.&quot; Then they said, &quot;This man came to stay here and now he wants to judge us. We&#39;ll treat you worse than them.&quot; They pushed hard against the man, Lot, and came close to breaking the door.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Genesis 19:10">10</a> The men reached out their hand, pulled Lot inside the house with them, and closed the door.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Genesis 19:11">11</a> They struck the people at the house entrance with blindness, everyone from least to greatest, so they got tired trying to find the door.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Genesis 19:12">12</a> The men asked Lot, &quot;Do you have anyone else here? Your sons-in-law, your sons, your daughters, and anyone you have in the city, take them out of this place.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Genesis 19:13">13</a> We will destroy this place because their cries have become very loud and God has sent us to destroy it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Genesis 19:14">14</a> Lot went out and talked to his sons-in-law, who were married to his daughters, and said, &quot;Get up, leave this place; because God will destroy this city.&quot; But to his sons-in-law, he seemed like he was joking.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Genesis 19:15">15</a> When morning came, the angels hurried Lot, saying, &quot;Get up, take your wife and your two daughters who are with you, or you will be swept away in the city&#39;s sin.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Genesis 19:16">16</a> As he hesitated, the men took his hand, his wife&#39;s hand, and the hands of his two daughters because God was kind to him; they led him out and placed him outside the city.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Genesis 19:17">17</a> When they took them outside, he said, &quot;Run for your life; don&#39;t look back or stop anywhere in the valley; go to the mountains or you will be swept away.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Genesis 19:18">18</a> And Lot said to them, &quot;Oh, please no, sir.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Genesis 19:19">19</a> Look, I have found favor with you, and you have shown me great kindness by saving my life. But I can&#39;t run to the mountain because some harm might catch me, and I would die.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Genesis 19:20">20</a> Look, this city is close enough to run to, and it&#39;s small. Please let me run there—it&#39;s small, right?—and I will live.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Genesis 19:21">21</a> He told him, &quot;Look, I have agreed to your request about this matter too, so I will not destroy the city you&#39;ve spoken for.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Genesis 19:22">22</a> Hurry, go there; because I can&#39;t do anything until you arrive there. That&#39;s why the city was named Zoar.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Genesis 19:23">23</a> The sun had risen over the earth when Lot went into Zoar.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Genesis 19:24">24</a> God made sulfur and fire rain down on Sodom and Gomorrah from the sky.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Genesis 19:25">25</a> He destroyed those cities, the whole area, everyone living in the cities, and everything growing on the land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Genesis 19:26">26</a> His wife looked back and turned into a salt pillar.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Genesis 19:27">27</a> Abraham got up early in the morning to the place where he had stood before God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Genesis 19:28">28</a> He looked toward Sodom and Gomorrah and all the land of the plain, saw smoke rising from the land like smoke from a furnace.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Genesis 19:29">29</a> When God destroyed the plain cities, God thought of Abraham and took Lot out of the destruction as he ruined the cities where Lot lived.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Genesis 19:30">30</a> Lot left Zoar and lived in the mountains with his two daughters because he was afraid to live in Zoar, so they stayed in a cave.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Genesis 19:31">31</a> The older daughter said to the younger, &quot;Our father is old, and there are no men around to be with us in the way that is usual everywhere.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Genesis 19:32">32</a> Let&#39;s get our father to drink wine and sleep with him to keep our family going.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Genesis 19:33">33</a> That night they gave their father wine to drink, and the oldest daughter slept with her father without him noticing when she lay down or when she got up.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Genesis 19:34">34</a> The next day, the older sister told the younger, &quot;Look, I slept with our father last night. Let&#39;s get him drunk with wine again tonight; then you go and sleep with him, so we can have children from our father.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Genesis 19:35">35</a> That night they also gave their father wine to drink, and the younger daughter went and slept with him. He didn&#39;t notice when she lay down or when she got up.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Genesis 19:36">36</a> Both of Lot&#39;s daughters became pregnant by their father.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Genesis 19:37">37</a> The firstborn had a son and named him Moab; he is the ancestor of the Moabites to this day.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Genesis 19:38">38</a> The younger daughter also gave birth to a son and named him Benammi. He is the ancestor of the Ammonites.</p>
