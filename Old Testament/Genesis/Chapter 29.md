<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Genesis 29:1">1</a> Jacob continued his travel and reached the land of the eastern people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Genesis 29:2">2</a> He saw a well in the field, with three groups of sheep nearby; they used that well to give water to the sheep, and a large stone covered the well&#39;s opening.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Genesis 29:3">3</a> All the flocks were brought there. They moved the stone from the well&#39;s opening, gave water to the sheep, and put the stone back over the well&#39;s opening where it was before.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Genesis 29:4">4</a> Jacob asked them, &quot;My brothers, where are you from?&quot; They replied, &quot;We are from Haran.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Genesis 29:5">5</a> He asked them, &quot;Do you know Laban, Nahor&#39;s son?&quot; They replied, &quot;Yes, we know him.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Genesis 29:6">6</a> He asked them, &quot;Is he doing well?&quot; They replied, &quot;He is well,&quot; and look, Rachel, his daughter, is coming with the sheep.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Genesis 29:7">7</a> He said, &quot;Look, the day is still long; it&#39;s not time to bring the cattle together. Give the sheep water and go feed them.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Genesis 29:8">8</a> They said, &quot;We can&#39;t until all the sheep are together and the stone is moved from the well&#39;s opening; then we give the sheep water.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Genesis 29:9">9</a> While he was still talking to them, Rachel arrived with her father&#39;s sheep because she was taking care of them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Genesis 29:10">10</a> When Jacob saw Rachel, Laban&#39;s daughter, and Laban&#39;s sheep, he went up, moved the stone from the well, and watered Laban&#39;s sheep.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Genesis 29:11">11</a> Jacob kissed Rachel, then he cried out loud.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Genesis 29:12">12</a> Jacob explained to Rachel that he was her dad&#39;s relative and the son of Rebekah, then she quickly went to tell her dad.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Genesis 29:13">13</a> When Laban heard about his nephew Jacob, he ran to greet him, hugged and kissed him, and took him to his home. Then Jacob told Laban everything.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Genesis 29:14">14</a> Laban said to him, &quot;You are truly my family, my own flesh and bone.&quot; And he stayed with Laban for a month.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Genesis 29:15">15</a> Laban said to Jacob, &quot;Just because you are my brother, should you work for me for free? Tell me, what do you want to be paid?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Genesis 29:16">16</a> Laban had two daughters: the older was named Leah, and the younger was named Rachel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Genesis 29:17">17</a> Leah had soft eyes; but Rachel was very beautiful.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Genesis 29:18">18</a> Jacob loved Rachel and said, &quot;I will work for you seven years to marry your younger daughter Rachel.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Genesis 29:19">19</a> Laban said, &quot;It&#39;s better for me to give her to you than to another man. Stay with me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Genesis 29:20">20</a> Jacob worked seven years for Rachel; they felt like only a few days to him because he loved her so much.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Genesis 29:21">21</a> Jacob said to Laban, &quot;Give me my wife because the time is complete, so I can be with her.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Genesis 29:22">22</a> Laban brought all the local men together and prepared a big meal.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Genesis 29:23">23</a> In the evening, he brought his daughter Leah to him, and they were together.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Genesis 29:24">24</a> Laban gave his daughter Leah his servant Zilpah to be her maid.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Genesis 29:25">25</a> In the morning, he saw it was Leah. He asked Laban, &quot;What have you done to me? Didn&#39;t I work for you to marry Rachel? Why have you tricked me?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Genesis 29:26">26</a> Laban said, &quot;In our country, we don&#39;t allow the younger daughter to marry before the older one.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Genesis 29:27">27</a> Finish her seven days, and we will let you marry the other one if you work for me for another seven years.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Genesis 29:28">28</a> Jacob did this and finished the week; then he also married Rachel, his daughter.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Genesis 29:29">29</a> Laban gave his daughter Rachel his servant Bilhah to be her maid.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Genesis 29:30">30</a> He also went to Rachel, loved Rachel more than Leah, and worked for seven more years.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Genesis 29:31">31</a> When God saw that Leah was not loved, he let her have children, but Rachel could not have children.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Genesis 29:32">32</a> Leah became pregnant, had a son, and named him Reuben, because she said, &quot;God has seen my troubles; now my husband will love me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Genesis 29:33">33</a> She became pregnant again and gave birth to a son. She said, &quot;God has heard that I was unloved, so He has given me another son.&quot; She named him Simeon.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Genesis 29:34">34</a> She became pregnant again and had a son. She said, &quot;Now my husband will feel close to me, because I have given birth to three sons for him.&quot; That is why she named him Levi.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Genesis 29:35">35</a> She became pregnant again and had a son. She said, &quot;Now I will praise God.&quot; So she named him Judah and stopped having children.</p>
