<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Genesis 16:1">1</a> Sarai, Abram&#39;s wife, had no children. She had a servant from Egypt named Hagar.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Genesis 16:2">2</a> Sarai said to Abram, &quot;Look, God has kept me from having children. Please sleep with my servant; maybe I can have children through her.&quot; And Abram listened to what Sarai said.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Genesis 16:3">3</a> Sarai, Abram&#39;s wife, took Hagar, her Egyptian maid, after Abram lived ten years in Canaan, and gave her to her husband Abram to be his wife.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Genesis 16:4">4</a> He slept with Hagar, and she became pregnant. When she realized she was pregnant, she looked down on her mistress.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Genesis 16:5">5</a> Sarai said to Abram, &quot;I&#39;m blaming you for this: I let you be with my servant, but now she&#39;s pregnant, she looks down on me. Let God decide who&#39;s right, me or you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Genesis 16:6">6</a> But Abram told Sarai, &quot;Look, your servant is under your control; do what you think is best.&quot; And when Sarai was harsh with her, she ran away.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Genesis 16:7">7</a> God&#39;s messenger met her by a water spring in the desert, near the spring on the road to Shur.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Genesis 16:8">8</a> He asked, &quot;Hagar, Sarai&#39;s servant, where did you come from and where are you going?&quot; She answered, &quot;I&#39;m running away from my mistress Sarai.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Genesis 16:9">9</a> The angel of God told her, &quot;Go back to your mistress and do as she says.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Genesis 16:10">10</a> God&#39;s messenger said to her, I will greatly increase your descendants so much that they will be too many to count.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Genesis 16:11">11</a> The angel of God told her, &quot;Look, you are going to have a baby boy and you will name him Ishmael; because God has heard about your troubles.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Genesis 16:12">12</a> He will be a wild person; he will fight against everyone, and everyone will fight against him; he will live near all his family members.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Genesis 16:13">13</a> She named God who spoke to her, &quot;You are the God who sees me,&quot; because she thought, &quot;I have seen the One who sees me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Genesis 16:14">14</a> So they named the well &quot;The Well of the Living One Who Sees Me.&quot; It is located between Kadesh and Bered.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Genesis 16:15">15</a> Hagar gave birth to Abram&#39;s son, and Abram named the son she had, Ishmael.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Genesis 16:16">16</a> Abram was 86 years old when Hagar gave birth to Ishmael for Abram.</p>
