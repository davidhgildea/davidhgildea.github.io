<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Genesis 5:1">1</a> This book lists Adam&#39;s descendants. On the day God made humans, he made them to look like himself.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Genesis 5:2">2</a> God created them male and female; he blessed them and named them Human when they were created.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Genesis 5:3">3</a> Adam lived 130 years, had a son who looked like him, and named him Seth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Genesis 5:4">4</a> Adam lived 800 years after the birth of Seth, and he had other sons and daughters.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Genesis 5:5">5</a> Adam lived for 930 years, and then he died.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Genesis 5:6">6</a> Seth lived 105 years and had a son named Enos.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Genesis 5:7">7</a> After Seth had Enos, he lived 807 years and had other sons and daughters.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Genesis 5:8">8</a> Seth lived 912 years, and then he died.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Genesis 5:9">9</a> Enos was ninety years old when he became the father of Cainan.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Genesis 5:10">10</a> After Enos had his son Cainan, he lived 815 more years and had other sons and daughters.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Genesis 5:11">11</a> Enos lived for 905 years; then he died.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Genesis 5:12">12</a> Cainan was seventy years old when he became the father of Mahalaleel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Genesis 5:13">13</a> Cainan lived 840 years after he had Mahalaleel, and had more sons and daughters.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Genesis 5:14">14</a> Cainan lived for 910 years, and then he died.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Genesis 5:15">15</a> Mahalaleel was sixty-five years old when he became the father of Jared.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Genesis 5:16">16</a> After Mahalaleel had Jared, he lived 830 years and had more sons and daughters.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Genesis 5:17">17</a> Mahalaleel lived for 895 years; then he died.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Genesis 5:18">18</a> Jared lived 162 years and then had a son named Enoch.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Genesis 5:19">19</a> After Jared had Enoch, he lived for 800 more years and had other sons and daughters.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Genesis 5:20">20</a> Jared lived 962 years, and then he died.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Genesis 5:21">21</a> Enoch was 65 years old when he became the father of Methuselah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Genesis 5:22">22</a> Enoch lived closely with God for 300 years after Methuselah was born, and he had other sons and daughters.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Genesis 5:23">23</a> Enoch lived for 365 years.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Genesis 5:24">24</a> Enoch lived close to God, and then he disappeared because God took him away.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Genesis 5:25">25</a> Methuselah lived 187 years and had a son named Lamech.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Genesis 5:26">26</a> After Methuselah became the father of Lamech, he lived 782 more years and had other sons and daughters.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Genesis 5:27">27</a> Methuselah lived 969 years, then he died.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Genesis 5:28">28</a> Lamech lived 182 years and had a son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Genesis 5:29">29</a> He named him Noah, saying, &quot;He will bring us comfort from our hard work and the pain of our hands caused by the ground that God has cursed.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Genesis 5:30">30</a> After Lamech had Noah, he lived for 595 more years and had other sons and daughters.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Genesis 5:31">31</a> Lamech lived a total of 777 years, and then he died.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Genesis 5:32">32</a> Noah was 500 years old when he had his sons Shem, Ham, and Japheth.</p>
