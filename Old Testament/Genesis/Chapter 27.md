<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Genesis 27:1">1</a> When Isaac got old and could no longer see well, he called for his oldest son Esau and said, &quot;My son.&quot; Esau said to him, &quot;Here I am.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Genesis 27:2">2</a> He said, &quot;Look, I am old and don&#39;t know when I will die.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Genesis 27:3">3</a> Please take your weapons, your arrows, and your bow, go to the fields, and get me some deer meat.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Genesis 27:4">4</a> Prepare tasty food for me, the kind I like, and bring it to me so I can eat and then bless you before I die.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Genesis 27:5">5</a> Rebekah heard when Isaac spoke to his son Esau. And Esau went to the field to hunt for deer meat and to bring it back.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Genesis 27:6">6</a> Rebekah said to her son Jacob, &quot;Look, I heard your father talking to your brother Esau, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Genesis 27:7">7</a> Bring me some deer meat and cook it well so I can eat and give you my blessing before I die.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Genesis 27:8">8</a> Now, my son, listen to what I say and do it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Genesis 27:9">9</a> Go to the herd now and bring me two good young goats. I will cook a tasty meal for your father, one he likes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Genesis 27:10">10</a> Bring it to your father to eat, so he can bless you before he dies.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Genesis 27:11">11</a> Jacob told his mother Rebekah, &quot;Look, my brother Esau is very hairy, and I have smooth skin.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Genesis 27:12">12</a> My father might touch me, and I will appear to him as a liar; and I will bring a curse on myself, not a blessing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Genesis 27:13">13</a> His mother said to him, &quot;Let any curse fall on me, my son. Just listen to me and go get them for me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Genesis 27:14">14</a> He went, got them, and brought them to his mother; and his mother cooked tasty food that his father liked.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Genesis 27:15">15</a> Rebekah took fine clothes belonging to her oldest son Esau, which she had at home, and dressed her younger son Jacob in them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Genesis 27:16">16</a> She put the goat kids&#39; skins on his hands and on his smooth neck.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Genesis 27:17">17</a> She gave the tasty meat and bread she made to her son Jacob.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Genesis 27:18">18</a> He went to his father and said, &quot;My father.&quot; His father replied, &quot;I am here. Who are you, my son?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Genesis 27:19">19</a> Jacob told his father, &quot;I am Esau, your firstborn; I did as you told me. Get up, please, sit and eat the meat I brought, so you can bless me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Genesis 27:20">20</a> And Isaac asked his son, &quot;How did you find it so fast, my son?&quot; And he said, &quot;Because God brought it to me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Genesis 27:21">21</a> Isaac said to Jacob, &quot;Please come closer so I can touch you, my son, to know if you are really my son Esau or not.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Genesis 27:22">22</a> Jacob went close to his father Isaac; Isaac touched him and said, &quot;The voice sounds like Jacob, but the hands feel like Esau&#39;s hands.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Genesis 27:23">23</a> He didn&#39;t recognize him because his hands were hairy like his brother Esau&#39;s, so he gave him a blessing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Genesis 27:24">24</a> He asked, &quot;Are you really my son Esau?&quot; And he answered, &quot;I am.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Genesis 27:25">25</a> He said, &quot;Bring it to me so I can eat the meat from my son and then I will give you a blessing.&quot; He gave it to him and he ate; he also gave him wine and he drank.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Genesis 27:26">26</a> Isaac said to him, &quot;Come here now and kiss me, my son.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Genesis 27:27">27</a> He came close and kissed him, and when he smelled his clothes, he blessed him and said, &quot;The scent of my son is like the fragrance of a field that God has blessed.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Genesis 27:28">28</a> May God give you dew from the sky, rich soil on the earth, and an abundance of grain and wine.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Genesis 27:29">29</a> Let people serve you and nations respect you. Be a leader to your brothers, and may your mother&#39;s sons respect you. May those who curse you be cursed, and those who bless you be blessed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Genesis 27:30">30</a> After Isaac finished blessing Jacob and while Jacob had just left, Esau returned from hunting.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Genesis 27:31">31</a> He also prepared tasty food and took it to his father. He said to his father, &quot;Please get up and eat your son&#39;s hunted meat, so you can give me your blessing.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Genesis 27:32">32</a> Isaac said to him, &quot;Who are you?&quot; And he answered, &quot;I am your son, your firstborn, Esau.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Genesis 27:33">33</a> Isaac shook with great fear and asked, &quot;Who is the one who caught the deer and brought it to me? I ate it all before you arrived, and I gave him a blessing. Yes, he will stay blessed.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Genesis 27:34">34</a> When Esau heard what his father said, he wept loudly and bitterly, and said to his father, &quot;Please bless me too, my father.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Genesis 27:35">35</a> He said, &quot;Your brother came deceitfully and took your blessing.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Genesis 27:36">36</a> He asked, &quot;Isn&#39;t he correctly called Jacob? He has cheated me twice: he took my birthright and now he has taken my blessing. Do you have any blessing left for me?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Genesis 27:37">37</a> Isaac replied to Esau, &quot;Look, I have made him your leader, and I have made all his brothers his helpers; I have also given him grain and wine. So what can I do for you now, my son?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Genesis 27:38">38</a> Esau said to his father, &quot;Do you have only one blessing, my father? Bless me too, father.&quot; Then Esau cried loudly.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="Genesis 27:39">39</a> Isaac, his father, replied, &quot;Look, your home will be in the richest parts of the land, and from the dew of the sky above.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="Genesis 27:40">40</a> You will live by your sword and serve your brother; but when you gain power, you will break free from his control.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="Genesis 27:41">41</a> Esau hated Jacob for the blessing his father gave him. Esau thought, &quot;When the time of mourning for my father is near, I will kill my brother Jacob.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="Genesis 27:42">42</a> Rebekah heard what Esau, her older son, said. She sent for Jacob, her younger son, and told him, &quot;Look, your brother Esau wants to kill you to feel better.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v43"><a class="verse-num" href="#v43" aria-label="Genesis 27:43">43</a> Now, my son, listen to me; get up, escape to my brother Laban in Haran.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v44"><a class="verse-num" href="#v44" aria-label="Genesis 27:44">44</a> Stay with him for a few days until your brother&#39;s anger fades away.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v45"><a class="verse-num" href="#v45" aria-label="Genesis 27:45">45</a> Wait until your brother is no longer angry with you and forgets what you did to him; then I will send for you and bring you back. Why should I lose both of you in one day?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v46"><a class="verse-num" href="#v46" aria-label="Genesis 27:46">46</a> Rebekah told Isaac, &quot;I am tired of living because of Heth&#39;s daughters. If Jacob marries one of Heth&#39;s daughters, like the local women, what&#39;s the point of my life?&quot;</p>
