<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Genesis 34:1">1</a> Dinah, Leah&#39;s daughter whom she had with Jacob, went out to meet the local girls.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Genesis 34:2">2</a> When Shechem, son of Hamor the Hivite and ruler of the land, saw her, he grabbed her, slept with her, and dishonored her.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Genesis 34:3">3</a> He felt a deep connection with Dinah, Jacob&#39;s daughter, loved her, and spoke gently to her.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Genesis 34:4">4</a> Shechem said to his father Hamor, &quot;Get me this girl to be my wife.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Genesis 34:5">5</a> Jacob found out someone had mistreated his daughter Dinah. His sons were out in the fields with the livestock, so Jacob stayed quiet until they returned.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Genesis 34:6">6</a> Hamor, Shechem&#39;s father, went to talk to Jacob.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Genesis 34:7">7</a> Jacob&#39;s sons returned from the fields when they heard the news. They felt deeply hurt and very angry because someone did something very foolish in Israel by sleeping with Jacob&#39;s daughter, which should never have happened.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Genesis 34:8">8</a> Hamor spoke to them, saying, &quot;My son Shechem deeply desires to marry your daughter. Please let her become his wife.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Genesis 34:9">9</a> Marry with us, give us your daughters, and take our daughters for yourselves.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Genesis 34:10">10</a> You will live with us, and the land will be open to you; live in it, trade in it, and own property in it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Genesis 34:11">11</a> Shechem said to her father and brothers, &quot;I hope you will favor me, and I will give whatever you ask of me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Genesis 34:12">12</a> Ask for any amount of money or gifts for a dowry, and I will pay what you ask; but let me marry the young woman.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Genesis 34:13">13</a> Jacob&#39;s sons lied to Shechem and his father Hamor when they spoke because Shechem had disgraced their sister Dinah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Genesis 34:14">14</a> They told them, &quot;We can&#39;t do this and let our sister marry a man who is not circumcised; that would be shameful for us.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Genesis 34:15">15</a> We agree with this condition: if every man among you gets circumcised, like us.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Genesis 34:16">16</a> We will let you marry our daughters, and we will marry your daughters, and we will live with you, and we will become one group of people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Genesis 34:17">17</a> But if you won&#39;t listen to us and agree to be circumcised, then we will take our daughter and leave.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Genesis 34:18">18</a> Hamor and his son Shechem liked what they heard.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Genesis 34:19">19</a> The young man did not hesitate to do it because he liked Jacob&#39;s daughter, and he was more respectable than everyone in his family.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Genesis 34:20">20</a> Hamor and his son Shechem went to the entrance of their city and talked with the men of their city, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Genesis 34:21">21</a> These men are friendly towards us; so let them live in the land and do business there; the land is big enough for them; we can marry their daughters, and they can marry ours.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Genesis 34:22">22</a> The men will agree to live with us and become one people only if every male among us gets circumcised just like they are.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Genesis 34:23">23</a> Won&#39;t their animals, belongings, and all their beasts become ours? Just agree with them, and they will live with us.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Genesis 34:24">24</a> Everyone in the city listened to Hamor and his son Shechem, and every man was circumcised just as they were.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Genesis 34:25">25</a> On the third day, when they were in pain, Jacob&#39;s two sons, Simeon and Levi, Dinah&#39;s brothers, each took a sword, attacked the city with courage, and killed all the men.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Genesis 34:26">26</a> They killed Hamor and his son Shechem with their swords, took Dinah from Shechem&#39;s house, and left.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Genesis 34:27">27</a> Jacob&#39;s sons attacked the dead men and looted the city because those men had dishonored their sister.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Genesis 34:28">28</a> They took their sheep, oxen, donkeys, and everything in the city and fields.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Genesis 34:29">29</a> They captured all their riches, children, and wives, and took everything in the house.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Genesis 34:30">30</a> Jacob said to Simeon and Levi, &quot;You have caused me problems, making me look bad to the people here, the Canaanites and Perizzites. We are few in number; they will unite against me, attack, and wipe out me and my family.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Genesis 34:31">31</a> They asked, &quot;Should he treat our sister like a prostitute?&quot;</p>
