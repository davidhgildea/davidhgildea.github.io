<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Genesis 12:1">1</a> God told Abram, &quot;Leave your country, your relatives, and your father&#39;s home, and go to a land I will show you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Genesis 12:2">2</a> I will make you into a great nation, and I will bless you, and make your name great; and you will be a blessing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Genesis 12:3">3</a> I will bless those who bless you and curse those who curse you; through you, all the earth&#39;s families will be blessed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Genesis 12:4">4</a> Abram left as God had told him, and Lot went too; Abram was seventy-five years old when he left Haran.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Genesis 12:5">5</a> Abram took his wife Sarai, his nephew Lot, everything they owned, and all the people they had in Haran. They set out for Canaan and arrived there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Genesis 12:6">6</a> Abram traveled through the land to Sichem, to the Moreh plain. The Canaanite people were living in the land at that time.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Genesis 12:7">7</a> God showed himself to Abram and said, &quot;I will give this land to your children.&quot; There Abram built an altar to God who had appeared to him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Genesis 12:8">8</a> He moved from there to a mountain east of Bethel, set up his tent with Bethel to the west and Hai to the east, built an altar to God, and called on God&#39;s name.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Genesis 12:9">9</a> Abram traveled on, heading south.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Genesis 12:10">10</a> There was not enough food in the land, so Abram went to Egypt to stay because the lack of food was very severe in the land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Genesis 12:11">11</a> When he was about to enter Egypt, he said to his wife Sarai, &quot;Look, I know you are a beautiful woman.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Genesis 12:12">12</a> So when the Egyptians see you, they will say, &#39;This is his wife,&#39; and they will kill me but let you live.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Genesis 12:13">13</a> Please say you are my sister, so that things will go well for me because of you, and my life will be spared thanks to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Genesis 12:14">14</a> When Abram arrived in Egypt, the Egyptians saw that his wife was very beautiful.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Genesis 12:15">15</a> Pharaoh&#39;s leaders saw her, praised her to Pharaoh, and she was brought into Pharaoh&#39;s house.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Genesis 12:16">16</a> He treated Abram well because of her, giving him sheep, cows, donkeys, male servants, female servants, female donkeys, and camels.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Genesis 12:17">17</a> God punished Pharaoh and his household with severe plagues because of Sarai, Abram&#39;s wife.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Genesis 12:18">18</a> Pharaoh called Abram and asked, &quot;What have you done to me? Why didn&#39;t you tell me she was your wife?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Genesis 12:19">19</a> Why did you say, &#39;She is my sister&#39;? I could have married her because of that. Now, here is your wife, take her and leave.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Genesis 12:20">20</a> The king told his men about him, and they made him leave with his wife and all his belongings.</p>
