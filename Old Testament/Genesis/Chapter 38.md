<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Genesis 38:1">1</a> At that time, Judah left his brothers and stayed with a man from Adullam named Hirah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Genesis 38:2">2</a> Judah saw a Canaanite man&#39;s daughter named Shuah; he married her and slept with her.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Genesis 38:3">3</a> She became pregnant, gave birth to a son, and he named him Er.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Genesis 38:4">4</a> She became pregnant again and had a son; she named him Onan.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Genesis 38:5">5</a> She became pregnant again and had a son. She named him Shelah. She was in Chezib when she gave birth to him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Genesis 38:6">6</a> Judah found a wife for his oldest son Er, whose name was Tamar.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Genesis 38:7">7</a> Er, Judah&#39;s eldest son, was evil in God&#39;s eyes, and God killed him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Genesis 38:8">8</a> Judah said to Onan, &quot;Sleep with your brother&#39;s wife, marry her, and have children for your brother.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Genesis 38:9">9</a> Onan knew the child would not be his; so when he slept with his brother&#39;s wife, he wasted his seed on the ground, to avoid giving a child to his brother.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Genesis 38:10">10</a> What he did upset God, so God also took his life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Genesis 38:11">11</a> Judah told Tamar, his daughter-in-law, &quot;Stay a widow at your father&#39;s house until my son Shelah grows up.&quot; He thought Shelah might die like his brothers. So Tamar went to live in her father&#39;s house.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Genesis 38:12">12</a> After some time, Judah&#39;s wife, the daughter of Shuah, died. Judah felt better and went to see his sheep shearers in Timnath with his friend Hirah, from Adullam.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Genesis 38:13">13</a> Someone told Tamar, &quot;Look, your father-in-law is going to Timnath to cut his sheep&#39;s wool.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Genesis 38:14">14</a> She took off her widow&#39;s clothes, covered herself with a veil, wrapped up, and sat down in an open area on the road to Timnath; because she realized that Shelah had grown up and she hadn&#39;t been given to him as his wife.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Genesis 38:15">15</a> When Judah saw her, he thought she was a prostitute because she had covered her face.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Genesis 38:16">16</a> On the road, he turned to her and said, &quot;Please let me sleep with you.&quot; (He didn&#39;t know she was his daughter-in-law.) And she asked, &quot;What will you give me to sleep with me?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Genesis 38:17">17</a> He said, &quot;I will send you a young goat from the herd.&quot; She asked, &quot;Will you give me something to keep until you send it?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Genesis 38:18">18</a> He asked, &quot;What should I give you as a promise?&quot; She replied, &quot;Your seal ring, your arm bands, and your walking stick that you have with you.&quot; So he gave them to her, slept with her, and she became pregnant by him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Genesis 38:19">19</a> She got up, left, took off her veil, and dressed in her widow&#39;s clothes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Genesis 38:20">20</a> Judah sent the young goat with his friend from Adullam to get his collateral back from the woman, but he could not find her.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Genesis 38:21">21</a> He asked the people there, &quot;Where is the prostitute who was out in the open by the road?&quot; But they said, &quot;There is no prostitute here.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Genesis 38:22">22</a> He went back to Judah and said, &quot;I can&#39;t find her; and the local men said there was no prostitute here.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Genesis 38:23">23</a> Judah said, &quot;Let her keep it so we won&#39;t be embarrassed. Look, I sent the young goat, but you didn&#39;t find her.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Genesis 38:24">24</a> Three months later, someone told Judah, &quot;Your daughter-in-law Tamar has acted like a prostitute, and now she&#39;s pregnant because of it.&quot; Judah said, &quot;Bring her out and let her be burned.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Genesis 38:25">25</a> When she was brought out, she sent a message to her father-in-law, saying, &quot;I am pregnant by the man who owns these things.&quot; And she asked, &quot;Please, look and see who these belong to—the signet, bracelets, and staff.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Genesis 38:26">26</a> Judah recognized them and said, &quot;She has been more right than I, because I did not give her to my son Shelah.&quot; He did not sleep with her again.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Genesis 38:27">27</a> During her labor, she discovered she was having twins.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Genesis 38:28">28</a> When she was giving birth, one baby reached out his hand, and the midwife tied a red string around it, saying, &quot;This one came out first.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Genesis 38:29">29</a> As he pulled back his hand, his brother came out. She asked, &quot;How did you come out first?&quot; So he was named Pharez.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Genesis 38:30">30</a> Later, his brother came out, the one with the red string on his hand; they named him Zarah.</p>
