<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Genesis 46:1">1</a> Israel traveled with everything he owned, went to Beersheba, and gave sacrifices to the God of his father Isaac.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Genesis 46:2">2</a> God spoke to Israel in a night vision, saying, &quot;Jacob, Jacob.&quot; And he replied, &quot;I&#39;m here.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Genesis 46:3">3</a> He said, &quot;I am God, your father&#39;s God. Do not be afraid to go to Egypt, because I will make you into a great nation there.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Genesis 46:4">4</a> I will go down to Egypt with you, and I will surely bring you back again, and Joseph will close your eyes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Genesis 46:5">5</a> Jacob left Beersheba, and Israel&#39;s sons took Jacob their father, their children, and their wives in the carts that Pharaoh had provided for him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Genesis 46:6">6</a> They took their animals and belongings, which they had acquired in Canaan, and went to Egypt, with Jacob and all his descendants.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Genesis 46:7">7</a> He brought his sons, grandsons, daughters, granddaughters, and all his family with him to Egypt.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Genesis 46:8">8</a> These are the names of Israel&#39;s children who went to Egypt: Jacob and his sons, Reuben, Jacob&#39;s oldest.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Genesis 46:9">9</a> Reuben&#39;s sons were Hanoch, Phallu, Hezron, and Carmi.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Genesis 46:10">10</a> Simeon&#39;s sons were Jemuel, Jamin, Ohad, Jachin, Zohar, and Shaul, whose mother was a Canaanite woman.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Genesis 46:11">11</a> The sons of Levi were Gershon, Kohath, and Merari.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Genesis 46:12">12</a> Judah had sons; Er, Onan, Shelah, Pharez, and Zarah. But, Er and Onan died in Canaan. Pharez had sons, Hezron and Hamul.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Genesis 46:13">13</a> Issachar&#39;s sons were Tola, Phuvah, Job, and Shimron.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Genesis 46:14">14</a> Zebulun&#39;s sons were Sered, Elon, and Jahleel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Genesis 46:15">15</a> Leah had six sons and a daughter named Dinah with Jacob in Padanaram. In total, Jacob had thirty-three children.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Genesis 46:16">16</a> Gad&#39;s sons were Ziphion, Haggi, Shuni, Ezbon, Eri, Arodi, and Areli.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Genesis 46:17">17</a> Asher&#39;s sons were Jimnah, Ishuah, Isui, and Beriah, and their sister was Serah. Beriah&#39;s sons were Heber and Malchiel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Genesis 46:18">18</a> These are Zilpah&#39;s sons, given by Laban to his daughter Leah, and she had them with Jacob—a total of sixteen people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Genesis 46:19">19</a> Rachel, Jacob&#39;s wife, had two sons: Joseph and Benjamin.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Genesis 46:20">20</a> In Egypt, Joseph had two sons named Manasseh and Ephraim, born to him by Asenath, who was the daughter of Potipherah, the priest of On.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Genesis 46:21">21</a> Benjamin&#39;s sons were Belah, Becher, Ashbel, Gera, Naaman, Ehi, Rosh, Muppim, Huppim, and Ard.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Genesis 46:22">22</a> Rachel had fourteen children with Jacob.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Genesis 46:23">23</a> Dan&#39;s sons: Hushim.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Genesis 46:24">24</a> Naphtali&#39;s sons were Jahzeel, Guni, Jezer, and Shillem.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Genesis 46:25">25</a> These are Bilhah&#39;s sons, whom Laban gave to his daughter Rachel, and she had them for Jacob: in total, there were seven people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Genesis 46:26">26</a> Everyone who went to Egypt with Jacob, except for the wives of his sons, totaled 66 people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Genesis 46:27">27</a> Joseph had two sons born to him in Egypt. In total, seventy people from Jacob&#39;s family came to Egypt.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Genesis 46:28">28</a> He sent Judah ahead to Joseph to show the way to Goshen, and they arrived in Goshen.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Genesis 46:29">29</a> Joseph prepared his chariot, went to meet his father Israel in Goshen, showed himself to him, hugged him tightly, and cried on his shoulder for a long time.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Genesis 46:30">30</a> Israel said to Joseph, &quot;Now I can die happily because I have seen you and you are still alive.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Genesis 46:31">31</a> Joseph told his brothers and his father&#39;s family, &quot;I will go and tell Pharaoh, &#39;My brothers and my father&#39;s family from Canaan have come to me.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Genesis 46:32">32</a> The men are shepherds because their job has been to take care of animals; and they have brought their sheep, their cattle, and everything they own.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Genesis 46:33">33</a> When the king of Egypt asks you what you do,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Genesis 46:34">34</a> Tell them, &quot;We have always worked with animals, from when we were young to now, just like our ancestors did. This is so you can live in Goshen, because Egyptians dislike all shepherds.&quot;</p>
