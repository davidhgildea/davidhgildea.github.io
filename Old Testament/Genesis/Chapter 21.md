<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Genesis 21:1">1</a> God visited Sarah as he promised, and did to Sarah what he had said.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Genesis 21:2">2</a> Sarah became pregnant and gave birth to a son for Abraham in his old age, at the exact time God had told him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Genesis 21:3">3</a> Abraham named his son, who Sarah gave birth to, Isaac.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Genesis 21:4">4</a> Abraham circumcised his son Isaac when he was eight days old, as God told him to do.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Genesis 21:5">5</a> Abraham was a hundred years old when his son Isaac was born to him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Genesis 21:6">6</a> Sarah said, &quot;God has made me laugh, and everyone who hears this will laugh with me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Genesis 21:7">7</a> She asked, &quot;Who would have thought to tell Abraham that Sarah could nurse children? Yet I have given him a son in his old age.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Genesis 21:8">8</a> The child grew and was weaned. Abraham had a big feast on the day Isaac was weaned.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Genesis 21:9">9</a> Sarah saw Hagar the Egyptian&#39;s son, whom she had given to Abraham, making fun.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Genesis 21:10">10</a> So she told Abraham, &quot;Send away this servant woman and her son, because the son of this servant woman will not share the inheritance with my son, Isaac.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Genesis 21:11">11</a> Abraham was very upset about his son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Genesis 21:12">12</a> God told Abraham, &quot;Don&#39;t be upset because of the boy and your servant. Listen to everything Sarah tells you, because your descendants will be named through Isaac.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Genesis 21:13">13</a> I will also make a nation from the son of the servant woman, because he is your descendant.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Genesis 21:14">14</a> Abraham got up early in the morning, took some bread and a water bottle, gave them to Hagar, put them on her shoulder along with the child, and sent her off. Then she left and walked around in the Beersheba desert.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Genesis 21:15">15</a> The water in the bottle ran out, and she put the child under a bush.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Genesis 21:16">16</a> She went and sat down some distance away, about as far as an arrow can fly, because she said, &quot;I can&#39;t watch the boy die.&quot; So she sat away from him, cried out loud, and wept.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Genesis 21:17">17</a> God heard the boy crying, and God&#39;s angel spoke to Hagar from heaven, saying, &quot;What&#39;s wrong, Hagar? Don&#39;t be afraid, because God has heard the boy crying where he is.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Genesis 21:18">18</a> Get up, pick up the boy, and hold him; because I will make him into a great nation.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Genesis 21:19">19</a> God made her see, and she noticed a water well; she went, filled her jar with water, and gave the boy a drink.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Genesis 21:20">20</a> God was with the boy; he grew up, lived in the desert, and became a bowman.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Genesis 21:21">21</a> He lived in the Paran desert, and his mother got him a wife from Egypt.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Genesis 21:22">22</a> At that time, Abimelech and Phichol, his army commander, told Abraham, &quot;God is with you in everything you do.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Genesis 21:23">23</a> Now promise me here before God that you will not lie to me, or to my son, or to my grandson. Instead, treat me and the land where you have lived as a foreigner with the same kindness I have shown you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Genesis 21:24">24</a> Abraham said, &quot;I will make a promise.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Genesis 21:25">25</a> Abraham scolded Abimelech about a water well that Abimelech&#39;s workers had forcefully taken.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Genesis 21:26">26</a> Abimelech said, &quot;I don&#39;t know who did this; you didn&#39;t tell me, and I haven&#39;t heard about it until today.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Genesis 21:27">27</a> Abraham took sheep and cattle and gave them to Abimelech; and they both made an agreement.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Genesis 21:28">28</a> Abraham placed seven female sheep from the flock apart.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Genesis 21:29">29</a> Abimelech asked Abraham, &quot;What do these seven young female sheep you have placed apart mean?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Genesis 21:30">30</a> He said, &quot;Take these seven young female sheep from me so they will be proof that I dug this well.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Genesis 21:31">31</a> So he named that place Beersheba, because they both made a promise there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Genesis 21:32">32</a> So they agreed on a promise at Beersheba. Then Abimelech and Phichol, the leader of his army, went back to the land of the Philistines.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Genesis 21:33">33</a> Abraham planted trees in Beersheba and prayed to God, who lives forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Genesis 21:34">34</a> Abraham lived in the Philistines&#39; land for a long time.</p>
