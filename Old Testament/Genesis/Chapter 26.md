<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Genesis 26:1">1</a> There was not enough food in the country, in addition to the earlier food shortage during Abraham&#39;s time. Isaac went to see Abimelech, the king of the Philistines, in Gerar.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Genesis 26:2">2</a> God appeared to him and said, &quot;Do not go down to Egypt; live in the land I will tell you about.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Genesis 26:3">3</a> Stay in this land, and I will be with you and bless you. I will give all these countries to you and your descendants, and I will keep the promise I made to your father Abraham.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Genesis 26:4">4</a> I will make your children as many as the stars in the sky, and I will give them all these lands; through your descendants, all the world&#39;s nations will be blessed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Genesis 26:5">5</a> Abraham listened to me and followed my orders, commands, rules, and laws.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Genesis 26:6">6</a> Isaac lived in Gerar.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Genesis 26:7">7</a> The local people asked him about his wife; he said, &quot;She is my sister,&quot; because he was afraid to say, &quot;She is my wife.&quot; He thought they might kill him because of Rebekah, as she was very beautiful.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Genesis 26:8">8</a> When he had been there a long time, King Abimelech of the Philistines looked out a window and saw Isaac being affectionate with his wife Rebekah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Genesis 26:9">9</a> Abimelech called Isaac and said, &quot;She is really your wife; why did you say, &#39;She is my sister&#39;?&quot; Isaac answered, &quot;I thought I might be killed because of her.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Genesis 26:10">10</a> Abimelech asked, &quot;What have you done to us? Someone could easily have slept with your wife, and you would have made us guilty.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Genesis 26:11">11</a> Abimelech told everyone, &quot;Anyone who harms this man or his wife will definitely be killed.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Genesis 26:12">12</a> Then Isaac planted crops in that land, and in that same year, he got a hundred times more than he planted, and God blessed him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Genesis 26:13">13</a> The man became very successful and continued to prosper until he was extremely wealthy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Genesis 26:14">14</a> He owned many sheep, cattle, and had many workers: the Philistines were jealous of him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Genesis 26:15">15</a> All the wells that his father&#39;s servants had dug during Abraham&#39;s time, the Philistines had blocked them and filled them with dirt.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Genesis 26:16">16</a> Abimelech said to Isaac, &quot;Leave us; you are much stronger than we are.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Genesis 26:17">17</a> Isaac left that place and set up his tent in the Gerar valley and lived there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Genesis 26:18">18</a> Isaac reopened the water wells that were dug in his father Abraham&#39;s time, because the Philistines had filled them after Abraham died. He named them what his father had named them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Genesis 26:19">19</a> Isaac&#39;s workers dug in the valley and found a well with flowing water.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Genesis 26:20">20</a> The herders from Gerar fought with Isaac&#39;s herders, saying, &quot;The water is ours!&quot; So, he named the well Esek because they argued with him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Genesis 26:21">21</a> They dug another well, and people also fought over it; so he named it Sitnah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Genesis 26:22">22</a> He moved from there, dug another well, and no one fought over it. He named it Rehoboth, saying, &quot;Now God has given us space, and we will grow in this land.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Genesis 26:23">23</a> He left that place and went to Beersheba.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Genesis 26:24">24</a> That night, God appeared to him and said, &quot;I am the God of your father Abraham. Do not be afraid, for I am with you. I will bless you and give you many descendants to honor my servant Abraham.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Genesis 26:25">25</a> He built an altar there, called on God&#39;s name, set up his tent, and Isaac&#39;s servants dug a well.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Genesis 26:26">26</a> Abimelech from Gerar, his friend Ahuzzath, and Phichol, his army&#39;s leader, went to him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Genesis 26:27">27</a> Isaac asked them, &quot;Why do you come to me if you hate me and have made me leave you?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Genesis 26:28">28</a> They said, &quot;We clearly saw that God was with you, so we thought, let&#39;s make a promise between us and you, and let&#39;s agree together.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Genesis 26:29">29</a> Please do us no harm, as we have not harmed you and have only treated you well, sending you off peacefully. You are now blessed by God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Genesis 26:30">30</a> He prepared a meal for them, and they ate and drank.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Genesis 26:31">31</a> They got up early in the morning, made promises to each other, and then Isaac sent them away, and they left him peacefully.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Genesis 26:32">32</a> That same day, Isaac&#39;s workers came and told him about the well they had dug, and said to him, &quot;We have found water.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Genesis 26:33">33</a> He named it Shebah, so the city is called Beersheba to this day.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Genesis 26:34">34</a> Esau was forty when he married Judith, Beeri the Hittite&#39;s daughter, and Bashemath, Elon the Hittite&#39;s daughter.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Genesis 26:35">35</a> They made Isaac and Rebekah very unhappy.</p>
