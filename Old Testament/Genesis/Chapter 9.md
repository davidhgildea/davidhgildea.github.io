<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Genesis 9:1">1</a> God blessed Noah and his sons and said to them, &quot;Have many children, increase in number, and fill the earth.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Genesis 9:2">2</a> Every land animal, bird in the sky, creature that moves on the ground, and fish in the sea will be afraid of you; they are all given into your control.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Genesis 9:3">3</a> All living creatures that move shall be food for you; just as I have given you all plants.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Genesis 9:4">4</a> Do not eat meat that still has its life in it, which is its blood.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Genesis 9:5">5</a> I will certainly demand an accounting for human lifeblood; from every animal and from each person I will demand it, and I will require it from anyone who takes another person&#39;s life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Genesis 9:6">6</a> Anyone who takes a person&#39;s life must have their own life taken by others because God made humans in his own image.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Genesis 9:7">7</a> You, be fruitful and increase in number; produce a lot on the earth and grow more.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Genesis 9:8">8</a> God spoke to Noah and his sons, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Genesis 9:9">9</a> I will make my promise with you and your descendants after you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Genesis 9:10">10</a> And with every animal with you, the birds, the livestock, and all the wild animals on earth with you; from all that came out of the ark, to every wild animal on earth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Genesis 9:11">11</a> I will make my promise with you, no living things will be wiped out by a flood again, and there will not be another flood to ruin the earth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Genesis 9:12">12</a> God said, &quot;This is the sign of the promise I make between me and you and every living creature with you, for all time to come.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Genesis 9:13">13</a> I put my rainbow in the cloud, and it will be a sign of an agreement between me and the earth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Genesis 9:14">14</a> When I bring a cloud over the earth, you will see the bow in the cloud.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Genesis 9:15">15</a> I will remember my agreement with you and every living being; never again will the waters turn into a flood to destroy all life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Genesis 9:16">16</a> The rainbow will be in the sky, and when I see it, I will remember the forever promise between God and every living being of all kinds on the earth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Genesis 9:17">17</a> God told Noah, &quot;This is the sign of the promise I made with all living things on earth.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Genesis 9:18">18</a> Noah&#39;s sons who left the ark were Shem, Ham, and Japheth; and Ham was Canaan&#39;s father.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Genesis 9:19">19</a> Noah had three sons, and from them the whole world was populated.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Genesis 9:20">20</a> Noah started to farm and planted a vineyard.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Genesis 9:21">21</a> He drank the wine, got drunk, and lay uncovered inside his tent.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Genesis 9:22">22</a> Ham, Canaan&#39;s father, saw his father naked and told his two brothers outside.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Genesis 9:23">23</a> Shem and Japheth took a cloth, put it on their shoulders, walked backward, and covered their father&#39;s bare body. Their faces were turned away, and they did not see their father unclothed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Genesis 9:24">24</a> Noah woke up from drinking wine and realized what his youngest son had done to him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Genesis 9:25">25</a> He said, &quot;Cursed is Canaan; he will be the lowest servant to his brothers.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Genesis 9:26">26</a> He said, &quot;God bless Shem; Canaan will be his servant.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Genesis 9:27">27</a> God will make Japheth&#39;s land and people grow, and he will live with Shem&#39;s family; Canaan will serve him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Genesis 9:28">28</a> Noah lived for 350 years after the flood.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Genesis 9:29">29</a> Noah lived for 950 years, then he died.</p>
