<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Genesis 4:1">1</a> Adam was intimate with his wife Eve; she became pregnant and gave birth to Cain, saying, &quot;I have had a son with God&#39;s help.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Genesis 4:2">2</a> She also gave birth to his brother Abel. Abel took care of sheep, while Cain worked the soil.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Genesis 4:3">3</a> After some time, Cain gave some of his crops as an offering to God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Genesis 4:4">4</a> Abel also gave the best of his sheep and their fat. And God respected Abel and his gift.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Genesis 4:5">5</a> God did not accept Cain and his offering. Cain became very angry, and his face showed it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Genesis 4:6">6</a> God asked Cain, &quot;Why are you angry? And why do you look upset?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Genesis 4:7">7</a> If you do good, won&#39;t you be accepted? But if you don&#39;t do good, sin is waiting to enter. It desires to control you, but you must rule over it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Genesis 4:8">8</a> Cain spoke to his brother Abel, and while they were in the field, Cain attacked Abel and killed him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Genesis 4:9">9</a> God asked Cain, &quot;Where is your brother Abel?&quot; Cain replied, &quot;I don&#39;t know. Am I supposed to look after my brother?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Genesis 4:10">10</a> He asked, &quot;What have you done? Your brother&#39;s blood calls out to me from the ground.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Genesis 4:11">11</a> You are now cursed from the ground, which opened up to take your brother&#39;s blood from your hands.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Genesis 4:12">12</a> When you farm the land, it will no longer give you its best crops; you will be a wanderer and a runaway on the earth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Genesis 4:13">13</a> Cain said to God, &quot;My punishment is too much for me to handle.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Genesis 4:14">14</a> Look, you have forced me to leave the land today; I will be hidden from you and I will wander the earth; anyone who finds me will kill me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Genesis 4:15">15</a> God said to him, &quot;So, if anyone kills Cain, they will be punished seven times more.&quot; And God put a mark on Cain to prevent anyone who found him from killing him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Genesis 4:16">16</a> Cain left God&#39;s presence and lived in the land of Nod, east of Eden.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Genesis 4:17">17</a> Cain was intimate with his wife; she became pregnant and gave birth to Enoch. Then he built a city and named it Enoch, after his son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Genesis 4:18">18</a> Enoch had a son named Irad. Irad had a son named Mehujael. Mehujael had a son named Methusael. Methusael had a son named Lamech.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Genesis 4:19">19</a> Lamech married two women: one named Adah and the other named Zillah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Genesis 4:20">20</a> Adah gave birth to Jabal: he became the ancestor of those who live in tents and keep livestock.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Genesis 4:21">21</a> His brother&#39;s name was Jubal; he was the first musician who played the harp and flute.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Genesis 4:22">22</a> Zillah also had Tubalcain, a teacher of all metalworkers in brass and iron, and Tubalcain&#39;s sister was Naamah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Genesis 4:23">23</a> Lamech told his wives, Adah and Zillah, &quot;Listen to me; you, Lamech&#39;s wives, hear what I say: I have killed a man because he wounded me, and a young man because he hurt me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Genesis 4:24">24</a> If God punishes someone seven times for hurting Cain, then for hurting Lamech, God will punish seventy-seven times.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Genesis 4:25">25</a> Adam was close with his wife again, and she had a son named Seth. She said, &quot;God has given me another child to replace Abel, who Cain killed.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Genesis 4:26">26</a> Seth also had a son, and he named him Enos. This is when people began to pray to God.</p>
