<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Genesis 10:1">1</a> Here are the family lines of Noah&#39;s sons: Shem, Ham, and Japheth. These sons had their own children after the flood.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Genesis 10:2">2</a> Japheth&#39;s sons were Gomer, Magog, Madai, Javan, Tubal, Meshech, and Tiras.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Genesis 10:3">3</a> Gomer&#39;s sons were Ashkenaz, Riphath, and Togarmah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Genesis 10:4">4</a> Javan&#39;s sons were Elishah, Tarshish, Kittim, and Dodanim.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Genesis 10:5">5</a> These people spread out over the islands and lands of non-Jewish peoples; each group by its language and family, in their own countries.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Genesis 10:6">6</a> Ham&#39;s sons were Cush, Mizraim, Phut, and Canaan.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Genesis 10:7">7</a> Cush had several sons: Seba, Havilah, Sabtah, Raamah, and Sabtechah. Raamah had two sons: Sheba and Dedan.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Genesis 10:8">8</a> Cush had a son named Nimrod, who became a powerful leader on Earth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Genesis 10:9">9</a> He was a strong hunter before God; that&#39;s why people say, &quot;Like Nimrod, a strong hunter before God.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Genesis 10:10">10</a> His kingdom started with Babel, Erech, Accad, and Calneh in Shinar&#39;s land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Genesis 10:11">11</a> From that country, Asshur left and built Nineveh, the city Rehoboth, and Calah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Genesis 10:12">12</a> Resen is between Nineveh and Calah; it is a great city.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Genesis 10:13">13</a> Mizraim had sons named Ludim, Anamim, Lehabim, and Naphtuhim.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Genesis 10:14">14</a> And the people of Pathrus, Casluh, (from whom the Philistines came), and Caphtor.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Genesis 10:15">15</a> Canaan had Sidon, his oldest son, and Heth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Genesis 10:16">16</a> And the Jebusite, the Amorite, and the Girgasite,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Genesis 10:17">17</a> The Hivite, the Arkite, and the Sinite,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Genesis 10:18">18</a> The Arvadites, Zemarites, and Hamathites were there too. Later, the Canaanite families spread out.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Genesis 10:19">19</a> The Canaanites&#39; land started at Sidon, went to Gerar and then to Gaza; from there it reached all the way to Sodom, Gomorrah, Admah, Zeboim, and Lasha.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Genesis 10:20">20</a> These are Ham&#39;s sons, grouped by their families, languages, lands, and nations.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Genesis 10:21">21</a> Shem, the ancestor of all Eber&#39;s descendants and Japheth&#39;s older brother, also had children.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Genesis 10:22">22</a> Shem&#39;s children were Elam, Asshur, Arphaxad, Lud, and Aram.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Genesis 10:23">23</a> Aram&#39;s children were Uz, Hul, Gether, and Mash.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Genesis 10:24">24</a> Arphaxad had a son named Salah, and Salah had a son named Eber.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Genesis 10:25">25</a> Eber had two sons. One was named Peleg, because the earth was divided during his life. His brother was named Joktan.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Genesis 10:26">26</a> Joktan was the father of Almodad, Sheleph, Hazarmaveth, and Jerah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Genesis 10:27">27</a> Hadoram, Uzal, and Diklah</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Genesis 10:28">28</a> And Obal, Abimael, and Sheba,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Genesis 10:29">29</a> Ophir, Havilah, and Jobab were all Joktan&#39;s sons.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Genesis 10:30">30</a> Their home was from Mesha to Sephar, a mountain in the east.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Genesis 10:31">31</a> These are Shem&#39;s sons, by their families, languages, in their countries, and by their nations.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Genesis 10:32">32</a> These are the families of Noah&#39;s sons, grouped by their descendants, within their countries. And from these families the nations were spread out over the earth after the flood.</p>
