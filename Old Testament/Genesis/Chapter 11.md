<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Genesis 11:1">1</a> Everyone on earth spoke the same language.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Genesis 11:2">2</a> As they traveled from the east, they found a flat area in the land of Shinar and they settled there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Genesis 11:3">3</a> They said to each other, &quot;Come, let&#39;s make bricks and bake them well.&quot; They used bricks instead of stones and tar for mortar.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Genesis 11:4">4</a> They said, &quot;Come on, let&#39;s build a city and a tower that reaches to the sky, so we can make a name for ourselves and not be spread out over the whole world.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Genesis 11:5">5</a> God came down to see the city and the tower that people were building.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Genesis 11:6">6</a> God said, &quot;Look, the people are united, and they all speak the same language; this is what they have started to do: now they can do anything they think of.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Genesis 11:7">7</a> Let&#39;s go down and mix up their language, so they can&#39;t understand each other.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Genesis 11:8">8</a> So God spread them out from there all over the earth, and they stopped building the city.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Genesis 11:9">9</a> So they named it Babel, because there God mixed up everyone&#39;s language and scattered them all over the world.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Genesis 11:10">10</a> These are Shem&#39;s family records: Shem was 100 years old and fathered Arphaxad two years after the flood.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Genesis 11:11">11</a> After Shem became the father of Arphaxad, he lived 500 years and had other sons and daughters.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Genesis 11:12">12</a> Arphaxad was 35 years old when he became the father of Salah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Genesis 11:13">13</a> After Arphaxad became the father of Salah, he lived 403 years and had other sons and daughters.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Genesis 11:14">14</a> Salah was thirty years old when he became the father of Eber.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Genesis 11:15">15</a> Salah lived for 403 years after Eber&#39;s birth and had more sons and daughters.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Genesis 11:16">16</a> Eber lived 34 years and then became the father of Peleg.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Genesis 11:17">17</a> Eber lived for 430 years after the birth of Peleg and had more sons and daughters.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Genesis 11:18">18</a> Peleg was thirty years old when he became the father of Reu.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Genesis 11:19">19</a> Peleg lived for 209 years after Reu was born, and he had more sons and daughters.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Genesis 11:20">20</a> Reu was thirty-two when he became the father of Serug.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Genesis 11:21">21</a> Reu lived for 207 years after Serug was born, and he had more sons and daughters.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Genesis 11:22">22</a> Serug was thirty years old when he had Nahor.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Genesis 11:23">23</a> Serug lived for 200 years after Nahor was born and had more sons and daughters.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Genesis 11:24">24</a> Nahor lived 29 years and had a son named Terah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Genesis 11:25">25</a> After Nahor had his son Terah, he lived 119 more years and had more sons and daughters.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Genesis 11:26">26</a> Terah lived for 70 years and had three sons: Abram, Nahor, and Haran.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Genesis 11:27">27</a> Here are the descendants of Terah: Terah was the father of Abram, Nahor, and Haran; and Haran was the father of Lot.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Genesis 11:28">28</a> Haran died before his father Terah in his birthplace, in Ur of the Chaldees.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Genesis 11:29">29</a> Abram and Nahor married. Abram&#39;s wife was Sarai, and Nahor&#39;s wife was Milcah, Haran&#39;s daughter, who was also Iscah&#39;s father.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Genesis 11:30">30</a> Sarai could not have children; she had none.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Genesis 11:31">31</a> Terah took his son Abram, his grandson Lot, and his daughter-in-law Sarai, Abram&#39;s wife; and they left Ur of the Chaldees to go to Canaan. But they stopped at Haran and lived there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Genesis 11:32">32</a> Terah lived for 205 years and then he died in Haran.</p>
