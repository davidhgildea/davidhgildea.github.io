<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Genesis 42:1">1</a> When Jacob learned that there was grain in Egypt, he asked his sons, &quot;Why are you just standing around looking at each other?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Genesis 42:2">2</a> He said, &quot;Look, I&#39;ve heard there&#39;s grain in Egypt. Go there and buy some for us, so we can survive and not die.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Genesis 42:3">3</a> Joseph&#39;s ten brothers went to Egypt to buy grain.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Genesis 42:4">4</a> Jacob did not send Joseph&#39;s brother Benjamin with his other sons, because he feared something bad might happen to him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Genesis 42:5">5</a> The Israelites also came to buy grain because there was a famine in Canaan.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Genesis 42:6">6</a> Joseph was the ruler of the land, and he was the one who sold grain to everyone there. When Joseph&#39;s brothers arrived, they bowed down to him with their faces to the ground.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Genesis 42:7">7</a> Joseph saw his brothers and recognized them, but he acted like a stranger towards them and spoke harshly to them. He asked them, &quot;Where do you come from?&quot; And they replied, &quot;We come from the land of Canaan to buy food.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Genesis 42:8">8</a> Joseph recognized his brothers, but they did not recognize him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Genesis 42:9">9</a> Joseph remembered his dreams about them and said to them, &quot;You are spies; you have come to find out about our weak spots.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Genesis 42:10">10</a> They said to him, &quot;No, sir, we have come to buy food.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Genesis 42:11">11</a> We are all sons of one man; we are honest men, your servants are not spies.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Genesis 42:12">12</a> He told them, &quot;No, you have come to see the bareness of the land.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Genesis 42:13">13</a> They said, &quot;We are twelve brothers, sons of one man in Canaan. The youngest is with our father today, and one is gone.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Genesis 42:14">14</a> Joseph said to them, &quot;This is what I meant when I told you, &#39;You are spies.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Genesis 42:15">15</a> This will test you: You will not leave here unless your youngest brother comes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Genesis 42:16">16</a> Choose one of you to go get your brother, and the rest will stay in jail. This will test if you&#39;re telling the truth. If not, as sure as Pharaoh lives, you must be spies.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Genesis 42:17">17</a> He kept them all together in prison for three days.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Genesis 42:18">18</a> On the third day, Joseph said to them, &quot;Do this and you will live, because I respect God.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Genesis 42:19">19</a> If you are honest men, have one of your brothers stay tied up in your prison, and the rest of you go bring grain for the hunger in your homes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Genesis 42:20">20</a> Bring your younger brother to me; then I&#39;ll know you&#39;re telling the truth and you won&#39;t die. And they did it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Genesis 42:21">21</a> They said to each other, &quot;We are truly guilty about our brother because we saw his deep distress when he pleaded with us, and we didn&#39;t listen. That&#39;s why this trouble has come to us.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Genesis 42:22">22</a> Reuben replied, &quot;Didn&#39;t I tell you not to harm the boy? But you wouldn&#39;t listen. Now we must answer for his blood.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Genesis 42:23">23</a> They did not know that Joseph could understand them, because he was speaking to them through an interpreter.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Genesis 42:24">24</a> He turned away from them and cried. Then he went back, talked with them, took Simeon, and tied him up while they watched.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Genesis 42:25">25</a> Joseph told them to fill their bags with grain, put each man&#39;s money back in his bag, and give them food for their journey. And so he did for them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Genesis 42:26">26</a> They loaded their donkeys with the grain and left that place.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Genesis 42:27">27</a> When one of them opened his bag to feed his donkey at the inn, he saw his money; it was right on top inside his bag.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Genesis 42:28">28</a> He told his brothers, &quot;My money is back, and look, it&#39;s in my bag.&quot; Their hearts sank, and they were scared, saying to each other, &quot;What has God done to us?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Genesis 42:29">29</a> They went to their father Jacob in Canaan and told him everything that happened to them, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Genesis 42:30">30</a> The man, who rules the land, spoke to us harshly and thought we were spies.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Genesis 42:31">31</a> We told him, &quot;We are honest people; we are not spies.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Genesis 42:32">32</a> We are twelve brothers, sons of our father. One is gone, and the youngest is with our father in Canaan today.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Genesis 42:33">33</a> The man, the leader of the land, told us, &quot;I will know you are honest men if you leave one of your brothers with me. Take food for your families to survive the hunger and then leave.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Genesis 42:34">34</a> Bring your youngest brother to me, so I can know you&#39;re not spies but honest men. Then, I will release your brother, and you can trade in the land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Genesis 42:35">35</a> When they emptied their bags, they found that each man&#39;s money was in his bag. When they and their father saw the money, they were scared.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Genesis 42:36">36</a> Jacob their father said to them, &quot;You have left me without my children: Joseph is gone, Simeon is gone, and now you want to take Benjamin: everything is against me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Genesis 42:37">37</a> Reuben said to his father, &quot;Kill my two sons if I don&#39;t bring him back to you. Trust me with him, and I&#39;ll return him to you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Genesis 42:38">38</a> He said, &quot;My son won&#39;t go with you because his brother is dead, and he&#39;s the only one left. If he gets hurt on your journey, you will make me so sad that I will die.&quot;</p>
