<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Genesis 50:1">1</a> Joseph laid his face on his father&#39;s face, cried over him, and kissed him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Genesis 50:2">2</a> Joseph told his doctors to prepare his father&#39;s body, and the doctors did so for Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Genesis 50:3">3</a> He was prepared for forty days, as is the custom for the embalmed, and the Egyptians grieved for him seventy days.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Genesis 50:4">4</a> After his mourning time ended, Joseph spoke to Pharaoh&#39;s officials and said, &quot;If you think well of me, please tell Pharaoh.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Genesis 50:5">5</a> My father asked me to promise, saying, &quot;Look, I am dying; bury me in the grave I made for myself in Canaan.&quot; So please let me go and bury my father, and then I will come back.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Genesis 50:6">6</a> Pharaoh said, &quot;Go and bury your father, just as he made you promise.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Genesis 50:7">7</a> Joseph went to bury his father, and all of Pharaoh&#39;s servants, the leaders of his household, and the elders of Egypt went with him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Genesis 50:8">8</a> Everyone from Joseph&#39;s family, his brothers, and their father&#39;s family went, but they left their young children, sheep, and cattle in Goshen.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Genesis 50:9">9</a> Chariots and horsemen went with him, forming a large group.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Genesis 50:10">10</a> They reached Atad&#39;s threshing floor, across the Jordan River, where they grieved deeply with loud crying; and he mourned his father for seven days.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Genesis 50:11">11</a> When the local people, the Canaanites, saw the sadness at Atad&#39;s threshing floor, they said, &quot;This is deep sorrow for the Egyptians.&quot; That&#39;s why they named the place Abelmizraim, which is across the Jordan.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Genesis 50:12">12</a> His sons did as he told them to.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Genesis 50:13">13</a> His sons took him to Canaan and buried him in Machpelah cave, in the field that Abraham purchased from Ephron the Hittite, near Mamre.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Genesis 50:14">14</a> Joseph went back to Egypt with his brothers and everyone who joined him to bury his father, after they buried his father.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Genesis 50:15">15</a> When Joseph&#39;s brothers saw that their father had died, they said, &quot;Joseph might hate us and will surely pay us back for all the bad things we did to him.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Genesis 50:16">16</a> They sent someone to tell Joseph, &quot;Before your father died, he told us to say,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Genesis 50:17">17</a> Tell Joseph, please forgive your brothers&#39; wrongdoing and sin, because they hurt you. And now, please forgive the wrong that God your father&#39;s servants have done. Joseph cried when they talked to him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Genesis 50:18">18</a> His brothers also went and bowed down in front of him, saying, &quot;Look, we are your servants.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Genesis 50:19">19</a> Joseph said to them, &quot;Don&#39;t be afraid. Am I in God&#39;s place?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Genesis 50:20">20</a> You planned to do me harm, but God turned it into good, to achieve what we see today: to save many lives.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Genesis 50:21">21</a> So do not be afraid. I will provide for you and your children. He reassured them and spoke gently to them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Genesis 50:22">22</a> Joseph lived in Egypt with his family and he lived to be 110 years old.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Genesis 50:23">23</a> Joseph saw his great-grandchildren through Ephraim, and Machir&#39;s children, Manasseh&#39;s descendants, were raised on Joseph&#39;s lap.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Genesis 50:24">24</a> Joseph told his brothers, &quot;I am about to die, but God will come to you and take you from this land to the land promised to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Genesis 50:25">25</a> Joseph made the children of Israel promise, saying, &quot;God will definitely come to you, and you must take my bones from here.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Genesis 50:26">26</a> Joseph died at the age of 110, and they preserved his body. He was placed in a coffin in Egypt.</p>
