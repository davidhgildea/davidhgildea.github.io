<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Genesis 6:1">1</a> When people started to increase in number on the earth and they had daughters,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Genesis 6:2">2</a> God&#39;s sons noticed that human women were beautiful, so they married any they wanted.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Genesis 6:3">3</a> God said, &quot;My spirit will not always argue with humans because they are mortal; their lifespan will be 120 years.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Genesis 6:4">4</a> There were giants on the earth long ago; and even later, when God&#39;s sons married human women, and they had children, those children grew up to be strong and famous old-time heroes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Genesis 6:5">5</a> God saw that people were very wicked on earth, and all the thoughts in their hearts were always only evil.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Genesis 6:6">6</a> God regretted making humans on the earth, and he felt deeply sad.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Genesis 6:7">7</a> God said, I will wipe out humans whom I made from the earth; along with animals, creatures that crawl, and birds of the sky; because I regret that I made them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Genesis 6:8">8</a> Noah found favor with God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Genesis 6:9">9</a> These are Noah&#39;s family records: Noah was a fair and blameless man among his people, and he lived close to God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Genesis 6:10">10</a> Noah had three sons: Shem, Ham, and Japheth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Genesis 6:11">11</a> The world was also bad in God&#39;s eyes, and it was full of violence.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Genesis 6:12">12</a> God saw the earth, and it was spoiled; all living things had ruined their ways on the earth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Genesis 6:13">13</a> God told Noah, &quot;All living things are about to end because of me; the earth is full of violence because of them; and I will destroy them along with the earth.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Genesis 6:14">14</a> Build an ark with gopher wood; make rooms inside, and cover it inside and outside with tar.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Genesis 6:15">15</a> Make the boat like this: It should be 300 cubits long, 50 cubits wide, and 30 cubits high.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Genesis 6:16">16</a> Make a window for the ark, and finish it to a cubit from the top; put the door of the ark in its side, and make it with lower, second, and third decks.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Genesis 6:17">17</a> Look, I will send a flood to cover the earth and destroy every living thing that breathes; everything on earth will die.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Genesis 6:18">18</a> I will make my promise to you; you, your sons, your wife, and your sons&#39; wives will go into the boat with you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Genesis 6:19">19</a> Take two of every kind of living creature, one male and one female, onto the ark to keep them alive with you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Genesis 6:20">20</a> Different kinds of birds, farm animals, and every kind of creature that crawls on the ground will come to you two by two to keep them alive.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Genesis 6:21">21</a> Take for yourself every kind of food people eat; collect it, and it will be food for you and for them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Genesis 6:22">22</a> Noah did everything God told him to do.</p>
