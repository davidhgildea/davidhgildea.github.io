<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Genesis 24:1">1</a> Abraham was old and advanced in years, and God had blessed Abraham in every way.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Genesis 24:2">2</a> Abraham said to his oldest servant, who managed everything he owned, &quot;Please put your hand under my thigh.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Genesis 24:3">3</a> I will make you promise by God, the God of heaven and earth, not to get a wife for my son from the Canaanite girls where I live.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Genesis 24:4">4</a> Go to my homeland and my family, and find a wife for my son Isaac.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Genesis 24:5">5</a> The servant asked, &quot;What if the woman doesn&#39;t want to come back with me to this place? Should I take your son back to the country you came from?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Genesis 24:6">6</a> Abraham told him, &quot;Make sure you don&#39;t take my son back there.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Genesis 24:7">7</a> God of heaven, who took me from my father&#39;s house and my relatives&#39; land, who spoke to me and promised me, saying, &#39;I will give this land to your descendants,&#39; will send an angel ahead of you, and you will get a wife for my son from there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Genesis 24:8">8</a> If the woman doesn&#39;t want to come with you, you are free from my promise. Just don&#39;t take my son back there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Genesis 24:9">9</a> The servant placed his hand under his master Abraham&#39;s thigh and made a promise to him about this subject.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Genesis 24:10">10</a> The servant took ten of his master&#39;s camels and left, carrying all his master&#39;s wealth. He then set out for Mesopotamia, to Nahor&#39;s city.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Genesis 24:11">11</a> He made his camels kneel outside the city near a water well when it was evening, the time when women come out to get water.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Genesis 24:12">12</a> He said, &quot;God of my master Abraham, please let me be successful today and show kindness to my master Abraham.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Genesis 24:13">13</a> Look, I am standing by the water well; and the city men&#39;s daughters come out to get water.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Genesis 24:14">14</a> Let it happen that the young woman I ask to lower her jar so I can have a drink, and who offers to water my camels too, be the one you have chosen for your servant Isaac. This is how I will know you have been kind to my master.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Genesis 24:15">15</a> Before he finished talking, Rebekah came out. She was born to Bethuel, Milcah&#39;s son and Nahor&#39;s wife (Abraham&#39;s brother), carrying a pitcher on her shoulder.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Genesis 24:16">16</a> The young woman was very pretty, had never been with a man, and she went to the well, filled her jar, and came back up.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Genesis 24:17">17</a> The servant hurried to her and asked, &quot;Please let me have a little water from your pitcher to drink.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Genesis 24:18">18</a> She said, &quot;Drink, sir,&quot; and quickly lowered her jar onto her hand to give him a drink.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Genesis 24:19">19</a> After she gave him water, she said, &quot;I will also get water for your camels until they finish drinking.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Genesis 24:20">20</a> She hurried, poured her jar into the trough, ran back to the well to get more water, and filled it up for all the camels.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Genesis 24:21">21</a> The man watched her in silence, wondering if God had made his trip successful.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Genesis 24:22">22</a> After the camels finished drinking, the man gave her a gold earring weighing half a shekel and two gold bracelets for her wrists weighing ten shekels.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Genesis 24:23">23</a> He asked, &quot;Whose daughter are you? Please tell me, is there space at your father&#39;s house for us to stay?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Genesis 24:24">24</a> She said to him, &quot;I am Bethuel&#39;s daughter, Milcah&#39;s grandson who gave birth to Nahor&#39;s child.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Genesis 24:25">25</a> She also told him, &quot;We have plenty of straw and feed, and a place to stay.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Genesis 24:26">26</a> The man lowered his head and honored God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Genesis 24:27">27</a> He said, &quot;Praise God of my master Abraham, who has not denied my master his kindness and honesty. While I was on the journey, God guided me to my master&#39;s family&#39;s home.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Genesis 24:28">28</a> The young woman ran and told her mother&#39;s family about these things.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Genesis 24:29">29</a> Rebekah had a brother named Laban, and Laban hurried out to the man at the well.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Genesis 24:30">30</a> When he saw the earring and bracelets on his sister&#39;s hands, and heard her saying what the man told her, he went to the man who was standing by the camels near the well.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Genesis 24:31">31</a> He said, &quot;Come in, you who are blessed by God; why are you standing outside? I have made the house ready and have space for the camels.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Genesis 24:32">32</a> The man entered the house, took off his camels&#39; gear, gave them straw and food, and brought water to wash his own and his companions&#39; feet.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Genesis 24:33">33</a> Food was placed in front of him to eat, but he said, &quot;I won&#39;t eat until I explain my mission.&quot; Then they told him, &quot;Go ahead and speak.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Genesis 24:34">34</a> He said, &quot;I am Abraham&#39;s servant.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Genesis 24:35">35</a> God has blessed my master a lot; he has become very important: and God has given him sheep, cattle, silver, gold, male servants, female servants, camels, and donkeys.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Genesis 24:36">36</a> Sarah, my master&#39;s wife, had a son for my master when she was old, and he has given him everything he owns.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Genesis 24:37">37</a> My master made me promise not to get a wife for his son from the Canaanite women where we live.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Genesis 24:38">38</a> Go to my father&#39;s house and to my relatives, and find a wife for my son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="Genesis 24:39">39</a> And I said to my boss, Maybe the woman won&#39;t come with me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="Genesis 24:40">40</a> He told me, &#39;God, whom I follow, will send an angel with you to make your journey successful; and you will find a wife for my son from my relatives and my father&#39;s family.&#39;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="Genesis 24:41">41</a> Then you will be free from my promise when you go to my family; and if they do not give you someone, you will be free from my promise.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="Genesis 24:42">42</a> I came to the well today and said, &quot;God of my master Abraham, if you will make my journey successful:&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v43"><a class="verse-num" href="#v43" aria-label="Genesis 24:43">43</a> Look, I&#39;m standing by the water well. When a young woman comes to get water, and I ask her for a little drink from her jar,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v44"><a class="verse-num" href="#v44" aria-label="Genesis 24:44">44</a> She said to me, &#39;Drink, and I will also get water for your camels.&#39; Let her be the one God has chosen for my master&#39;s son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v45"><a class="verse-num" href="#v45" aria-label="Genesis 24:45">45</a> Before I finished thinking to myself, Rebekah came out with a jar on her shoulder. She went down to the well and drew water. Then I asked her, &quot;Please let me have a drink.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v46"><a class="verse-num" href="#v46" aria-label="Genesis 24:46">46</a> She quickly took her jar off her shoulder, said &quot;Drink, and I&#39;ll water your camels too.&quot; So I drank, and she watered the camels as well.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v47"><a class="verse-num" href="#v47" aria-label="Genesis 24:47">47</a> I asked her, &quot;Who is your father?&quot; She replied, &quot;I am Bethuel&#39;s daughter, the son of Nahor, whom Milcah gave birth to for him.&quot; Then I put the earring on her face and the bracelets on her hands.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v48"><a class="verse-num" href="#v48" aria-label="Genesis 24:48">48</a> I bowed my head, worshipped God, and blessed God of my master Abraham who guided me correctly to choose my master&#39;s brother&#39;s daughter for his son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v49"><a class="verse-num" href="#v49" aria-label="Genesis 24:49">49</a> If you will treat my master with kindness and honesty, tell me. If not, tell me, so I can go either to the right or to the left.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v50"><a class="verse-num" href="#v50" aria-label="Genesis 24:50">50</a> Then Laban and Bethuel replied, &quot;This matter is from God: we cannot say anything bad or good to you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v51"><a class="verse-num" href="#v51" aria-label="Genesis 24:51">51</a> Look, Rebekah is here; take her and go so she can become the wife of your master&#39;s son, as God has said.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v52"><a class="verse-num" href="#v52" aria-label="Genesis 24:52">52</a> When Abraham&#39;s servant heard what they said, he bowed down to the ground to worship God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v53"><a class="verse-num" href="#v53" aria-label="Genesis 24:53">53</a> The servant took out silver and gold jewelry and clothes, and gave them to Rebekah. He also gave valuable gifts to her brother and her mother.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v54"><a class="verse-num" href="#v54" aria-label="Genesis 24:54">54</a> They ate and drank, he and the men with him, and stayed all night; they got up in the morning, and he said, Let me go to my master.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v55"><a class="verse-num" href="#v55" aria-label="Genesis 24:55">55</a> Her brother and mother said, &quot;Let the girl stay with us for a few days, at least ten; then she can go.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v56"><a class="verse-num" href="#v56" aria-label="Genesis 24:56">56</a> He said to them, &quot;Don&#39;t stop me, since God has made my journey successful; let me go so I can return to my master.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v57"><a class="verse-num" href="#v57" aria-label="Genesis 24:57">57</a> We will ask the young woman what she thinks.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v58"><a class="verse-num" href="#v58" aria-label="Genesis 24:58">58</a> They called Rebekah and asked her, &quot;Will you go with this man?&quot; She said, &quot;I will go.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v59"><a class="verse-num" href="#v59" aria-label="Genesis 24:59">59</a> They sent off their sister Rebekah, her nurse, Abraham&#39;s servant, and his men.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v60"><a class="verse-num" href="#v60" aria-label="Genesis 24:60">60</a> They blessed Rebekah and said to her, &quot;You are our sister. May you be the mother of many millions and may your descendants conquer the lands of their enemies.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v61"><a class="verse-num" href="#v61" aria-label="Genesis 24:61">61</a> Rebekah and her maids got up, they rode on the camels, and followed the man. The servant took Rebekah and left.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v62"><a class="verse-num" href="#v62" aria-label="Genesis 24:62">62</a> Isaac returned from near the well Lahairoi; he was living in the southern land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v63"><a class="verse-num" href="#v63" aria-label="Genesis 24:63">63</a> Isaac went out to think in the field in the evening; when he looked up, he saw camels approaching.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v64"><a class="verse-num" href="#v64" aria-label="Genesis 24:64">64</a> Rebekah looked up, and when she saw Isaac, she got down from the camel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v65"><a class="verse-num" href="#v65" aria-label="Genesis 24:65">65</a> She had asked the servant, &quot;Who is that man walking in the field to meet us?&quot; The servant replied, &quot;It&#39;s my boss.&quot; So she took a veil and covered herself.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v66"><a class="verse-num" href="#v66" aria-label="Genesis 24:66">66</a> The servant told Isaac everything he had done.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v67"><a class="verse-num" href="#v67" aria-label="Genesis 24:67">67</a> Isaac took Rebekah into his mother Sarah&#39;s tent, married her, and loved her. Isaac felt better after his mother died.</p>
