<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Genesis 15:1">1</a> After these events, God spoke to Abram in a vision, saying, &quot;Do not be afraid, Abram: I am your protector and your very great reward.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Genesis 15:2">2</a> Abram said, &quot;God, what will you give me since I have no children, and my house manager is Eliezer from Damascus?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Genesis 15:3">3</a> Abram said, &quot;Look, you have given me no children. So, someone born in my house will inherit everything.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Genesis 15:4">4</a> Look, God&#39;s message came to him, saying, &quot;This person will not be your heir; but your own future child will be your heir.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Genesis 15:5">5</a> He took him outside and said, &quot;Look up at the sky and count the stars if you can.&quot; Then he said to him, &quot;That&#39;s how many descendants you will have.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Genesis 15:6">6</a> He believed in God, and it was credited to him as being right.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Genesis 15:7">7</a> He said to him, &quot;I am God who took you from Ur of the Chaldees to give you this land to own it.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Genesis 15:8">8</a> He asked, &quot;God, how can I know that I will receive it?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Genesis 15:9">9</a> He said to him, &quot;Bring me a three-year-old cow, a three-year-old female goat, a three-year-old ram, a dove, and a young pigeon.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Genesis 15:10">10</a> He took all these animals, split them in half, and put the halves opposite each other, but he did not split the birds.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Genesis 15:11">11</a> When birds landed on the dead bodies, Abram scared them off.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Genesis 15:12">12</a> As the sun set, Abram fell into a deep sleep and suddenly, a terrifying darkness came over him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Genesis 15:13">13</a> He told Abram, &quot;Be certain that your descendants will live in a foreign land where they are not the owners, and they will serve the people of that land. They will be treated badly for four hundred years.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Genesis 15:14">14</a> I will judge the nation they serve, and afterward, they will leave with many possessions.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Genesis 15:15">15</a> You will go to your ancestors in peace and be buried when you have lived a long life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Genesis 15:16">16</a> In the fourth generation, they will come back here because the Amorites&#39; wrongdoing is not complete yet.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Genesis 15:17">17</a> When the sun set and it was dark, a smoking fire pot and a flaming torch moved between the pieces of the offering.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Genesis 15:18">18</a> On that day, God made a promise to Abram, saying, &quot;I have given your descendants this land, from the Egypt river to the big river, the Euphrates river.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Genesis 15:19">19</a> The Kenites, the Kenizzites, and the Kadmonites,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Genesis 15:20">20</a> The Hittites, the Perizzites, and the Rephaims,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Genesis 15:21">21</a> The Amorites, the Canaanites, the Girgashites, and the Jebusites.</p>
