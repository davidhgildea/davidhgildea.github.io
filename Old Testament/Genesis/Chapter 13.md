<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Genesis 13:1">1</a> Abram left Egypt with his wife, all his possessions, and Lot, heading south.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Genesis 13:2">2</a> Abram was very wealthy with many animals, silver, and gold.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Genesis 13:3">3</a> He traveled from the south to Bethel, to the spot where he first pitched his tent, between Bethel and Ai.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Genesis 13:4">4</a> To the altar spot he first made, Abram called on God&#39;s name there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Genesis 13:5">5</a> Lot, who traveled with Abram, had sheep, cattle, and tents.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Genesis 13:6">6</a> The land could not support them living together because they had too much stuff, so they could not stay together.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Genesis 13:7">7</a> Abram&#39;s cattle herders and Lot&#39;s cattle herders argued, and the Canaanite and Perizzite people were living in the land at that time.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Genesis 13:8">8</a> Abram said to Lot, &quot;Please, let&#39;s not have any arguments between you and me, or between our herders, because we are family.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Genesis 13:9">9</a> Isn&#39;t all the land in front of you? Please separate from me. If you choose the left side, I will go to the right; if you go to the right side, I will go to the left.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Genesis 13:10">10</a> Lot looked around and saw the whole Jordan Valley, which had plenty of water everywhere, like God&#39;s garden or the land of Egypt, before God destroyed Sodom and Gomorrah, all the way to Zoar.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Genesis 13:11">11</a> Lot picked the whole Jordan valley for himself; then Lot traveled east. So they went their separate ways.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Genesis 13:12">12</a> Abram lived in Canaan, and Lot lived in the cities of the plain, and set up his tent near Sodom.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Genesis 13:13">13</a> The Sodom men were very bad and sinned a lot against God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Genesis 13:14">14</a> God said to Abram, after Lot had left him, &quot;Now, lift up your eyes and look from where you are, to the north, south, east, and west.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Genesis 13:15">15</a> I will give you and your descendants all the land you can see, forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Genesis 13:16">16</a> I will make your descendants as many as the dust of the earth; if someone can count the dust, then they can count your descendants too.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Genesis 13:17">17</a> Get up, walk around the land through its length and width; for I will give it to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Genesis 13:18">18</a> Abram moved his tent, went to live in Mamre&#39;s plain in Hebron, and built an altar for God there.</p>
