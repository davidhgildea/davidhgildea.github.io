<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Genesis 20:1">1</a> Abraham traveled from there to the southern lands and lived between Kadesh and Shur, and stayed in Gerar.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Genesis 20:2">2</a> Abraham said about his wife Sarah, &quot;She is my sister.&quot; So Abimelech, the king of Gerar, sent for and took Sarah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Genesis 20:3">3</a> God came to Abimelech in a dream at night and said, &quot;You are as good as dead because of the woman you have taken; she is already married.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Genesis 20:4">4</a> Abimelech had not been with her, and he said, &quot;God, will you also kill a righteous nation?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Genesis 20:5">5</a> Didn&#39;t he tell me, &#39;She is my sister&#39;? And she also said, &#39;He is my brother.&#39; I did this with a clear heart and clean hands.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Genesis 20:6">6</a> God spoke to him in a dream, saying, &quot;Yes, I know you did this with a pure heart. That&#39;s why I kept you from sinning against me and did not let you touch her.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Genesis 20:7">7</a> Now, give the man back his wife, because he is a prophet. He will pray for you, and you will live. But if you don&#39;t return her, know that you will surely die, you and everyone who belongs to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Genesis 20:8">8</a> So Abimelech got up early in the morning, called all his workers, and spoke about all these things to them; and the men were very scared.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Genesis 20:9">9</a> Then Abimelech called Abraham and asked him, &quot;What have you done to us? How have I wronged you, that you have brought a great sin on me and my kingdom? You have done things to me that should not have been done.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Genesis 20:10">10</a> Abimelech asked Abraham, &quot;What did you see that made you do this?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Genesis 20:11">11</a> And Abraham said, &quot;I thought, &#39;This place does not respect God, and they will kill me to take my wife.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Genesis 20:12">12</a> She really is my sister, my father&#39;s daughter but not my mother&#39;s, and she became my wife.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Genesis 20:13">13</a> When God made me leave my father&#39;s home, I told her, &quot;Please be kind to me, and wherever we go, say &#39;He is my brother.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Genesis 20:14">14</a> Abimelech gave Abraham sheep, oxen, male servants, and female servants, and returned his wife Sarah to him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Genesis 20:15">15</a> Abimelech said, &quot;Look, my land is in front of you: live wherever you like.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Genesis 20:16">16</a> God said to Sarah, &quot;Look, I have given your brother a thousand silver coins. He will protect you in the eyes of everyone with you and everyone else.&quot; This was her correction.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Genesis 20:17">17</a> Abraham prayed to God, and God healed Abimelech, his wife, and his female servants, so they could have children.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Genesis 20:18">18</a> God had made it so no woman in Abimelech&#39;s house could have children because of Sarah, Abraham&#39;s wife.</p>
