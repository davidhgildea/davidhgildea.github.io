<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Genesis 41:1">1</a> After two whole years, Pharaoh had a dream: he was standing by the river.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Genesis 41:2">2</a> Look, seven healthy and fat cows came out of the river and they ate in a grassy field.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Genesis 41:3">3</a> Then seven other skinny and ugly cows came up from the river and stood next to the first cows by the riverbank.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Genesis 41:4">4</a> The skinny and ugly cows ate the seven healthy and fat cows. Then Pharaoh woke up.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Genesis 41:5">5</a> He slept again and had another dream: seven healthy and full ears of corn grew on one stalk.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Genesis 41:6">6</a> Look, seven skinny ears of grain, damaged by the east wind, grew up after them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Genesis 41:7">7</a> The seven skinny heads of grain swallowed up the seven healthy and full heads. Then Pharaoh woke up and realized it was a dream.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Genesis 41:8">8</a> In the morning, he was worried, so he called all of Egypt&#39;s magicians and wise men. Pharaoh shared his dream with them, but no one could explain it to him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Genesis 41:9">9</a> The head servant spoke to the king and said, &quot;Today, I remember what I did wrong.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Genesis 41:10">10</a> The king was angry with his servants and locked me up in the guard captain&#39;s house, me and the head baker.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Genesis 41:11">11</a> We both had a dream on the same night, me and him; each of our dreams meant something different.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Genesis 41:12">12</a> A young Hebrew man, who worked for the guard&#39;s commander, was with us. We shared our dreams with him, and he explained what each dream meant for each person.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Genesis 41:13">13</a> Just as he explained to us, it happened: he returned me to my job, and he hanged the other man.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Genesis 41:14">14</a> Then Pharaoh sent for Joseph, who was quickly brought out of the prison. He shaved, put on new clothes, and went to Pharaoh.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Genesis 41:15">15</a> Pharaoh said to Joseph, &quot;I had a dream and no one can explain it. But I&#39;ve heard that you can understand and explain dreams.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Genesis 41:16">16</a> Joseph said to Pharaoh, &quot;I don&#39;t have the power. God will give Pharaoh a peaceful answer.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Genesis 41:17">17</a> Pharaoh said to Joseph, &quot;In my dream, I was standing by the river.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Genesis 41:18">18</a> Look, seven healthy and good-looking cows came up from the river and grazed in the grassland.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Genesis 41:19">19</a> Then seven other cows came up after them, thin, very ugly, and skinny, such as I have never seen in all of Egypt because they were so bad.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Genesis 41:20">20</a> The skinny and sickly cows ate up the first seven healthy cows.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Genesis 41:21">21</a> After they ate them, you couldn&#39;t tell they had eaten; they still looked bad as before. Then I woke up.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Genesis 41:22">22</a> I had a dream where seven healthy and full ears of grain grew on a single stalk.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Genesis 41:23">23</a> Look, seven thin and dried up ears of grain, damaged by the east wind, grew after the others.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Genesis 41:24">24</a> The skinny ears of grain swallowed up the seven healthy ears, and I explained this to the magicians, but no one could tell me what it meant.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Genesis 41:25">25</a> Joseph said to Pharaoh, &quot;Pharaoh&#39;s dream means the same thing: God has shown Pharaoh what He will do.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Genesis 41:26">26</a> The seven good cows are seven years; and the seven good heads of grain are seven years: the dream is the same.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Genesis 41:27">27</a> The seven skinny and bad-looking cows that followed are seven years, and the seven thin ears of grain ruined by the east wind will be seven years of hunger.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Genesis 41:28">28</a> This is what I told Pharaoh: God is showing Pharaoh what he is going to do.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Genesis 41:29">29</a> Look, seven years of great abundance will come to the whole land of Egypt.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Genesis 41:30">30</a> After those seven good years, seven years of hunger will follow. All the abundance in Egypt will be forgotten, and the hunger will destroy the land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Genesis 41:31">31</a> The abundance will not be noticed in the land because of the coming famine; it will be very severe.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Genesis 41:32">32</a> The dream came to Pharaoh two times because God has decided it will happen soon.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Genesis 41:33">33</a> So now, Pharaoh should choose a smart and wise man to be in charge of Egypt.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Genesis 41:34">34</a> Pharaoh should do this: he should choose leaders for the land and collect one-fifth of Egypt&#39;s crops during the seven good years.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Genesis 41:35">35</a> Let them collect all the food from the good years that are coming, and store grain under Pharaoh&#39;s control, and keep food in the cities.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Genesis 41:36">36</a> That food will be kept to supply the land during the seven years of famine that will happen in Egypt, so the land won&#39;t be destroyed by the famine.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Genesis 41:37">37</a> Pharaoh and all his servants thought the idea was good.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Genesis 41:38">38</a> Pharaoh asked his servants, &quot;Can we find anyone like this man who has the Spirit of God in him?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="Genesis 41:39">39</a> Pharaoh said to Joseph, &quot;Since God has shown you all this, no one is as smart and wise as you are.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="Genesis 41:40">40</a> You will be in charge of my house, and all my people will follow your commands; only I will have more power than you because of my throne.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="Genesis 41:41">41</a> Pharaoh said to Joseph, &quot;Look, I have made you the ruler over all of Egypt.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="Genesis 41:42">42</a> The king took his ring off his hand, gave it to Joseph, dressed him in fine linen clothes, and put a gold necklace around his neck.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v43"><a class="verse-num" href="#v43" aria-label="Genesis 41:43">43</a> He let him ride in his second chariot, and as he went, people shouted, &quot;Bow down!&quot; He appointed him to rule over all of Egypt.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v44"><a class="verse-num" href="#v44" aria-label="Genesis 41:44">44</a> Pharaoh told Joseph, &quot;I am Pharaoh, and no one in Egypt will do anything without your permission.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v45"><a class="verse-num" href="#v45" aria-label="Genesis 41:45">45</a> Pharaoh named Joseph Zaphnathpaaneah and gave him Asenath, the daughter of Potipherah, the priest of On, as his wife. Joseph traveled all through Egypt.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v46"><a class="verse-num" href="#v46" aria-label="Genesis 41:46">46</a> Joseph was thirty when he appeared before Pharaoh, the king of Egypt. He left Pharaoh&#39;s presence and traveled all over Egypt.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v47"><a class="verse-num" href="#v47" aria-label="Genesis 41:47">47</a> During the seven good years, the earth produced lots of food.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v48"><a class="verse-num" href="#v48" aria-label="Genesis 41:48">48</a> He collected all the food from the seven years in Egypt and stored it in the cities. He put the food from the fields around each city in that city.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v49"><a class="verse-num" href="#v49" aria-label="Genesis 41:49">49</a> Joseph collected so much grain like the sand of the sea that he stopped keeping track because it was too much to count.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v50"><a class="verse-num" href="#v50" aria-label="Genesis 41:50">50</a> Joseph had two sons before the famine started. Asenath, Potipherah the priest of On&#39;s daughter, was their mother.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v51"><a class="verse-num" href="#v51" aria-label="Genesis 41:51">51</a> Joseph named his first son Manasseh because, he said, God has made me forget all my hard work and my family back home.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v52"><a class="verse-num" href="#v52" aria-label="Genesis 41:52">52</a> He named the second one Ephraim, saying, &quot;God has made me fruitful in the land where I suffered.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v53"><a class="verse-num" href="#v53" aria-label="Genesis 41:53">53</a> The seven plentiful years in Egypt came to an end.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v54"><a class="verse-num" href="#v54" aria-label="Genesis 41:54">54</a> The seven years of famine started, just as Joseph had said, and the famine was in every country, but in Egypt, there was still food.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v55"><a class="verse-num" href="#v55" aria-label="Genesis 41:55">55</a> When Egypt was very hungry, the people begged the king for food. The king told the Egyptians, &quot;Go to Joseph; do whatever he tells you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v56"><a class="verse-num" href="#v56" aria-label="Genesis 41:56">56</a> There was a famine everywhere on earth; Joseph opened all the storehouses and sold food to the Egyptians; and the famine became very severe in Egypt.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v57"><a class="verse-num" href="#v57" aria-label="Genesis 41:57">57</a> People from every country went to Egypt to buy grain from Joseph because the famine was very severe everywhere.</p>
