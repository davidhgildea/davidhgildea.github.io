<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Genesis 23:1">1</a> Sarah lived to be 127 years old.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Genesis 23:2">2</a> Sarah died in Kirjatharba, also called Hebron, in the land of Canaan, and Abraham came to mourn and cry for her.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Genesis 23:3">3</a> Abraham got up from near his deceased and said to Heth&#39;s sons,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Genesis 23:4">4</a> I am a foreigner and traveler among you. Please let me have a place to bury my dead, so I can bury them and not see them anymore.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Genesis 23:5">5</a> The Heth people replied to Abraham, saying to him,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Genesis 23:6">6</a> Listen to us, sir: you are a respected leader among us. Choose any of our burial places to bury your dead. No one here will refuse you their grave for you to bury your dead.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Genesis 23:7">7</a> Abraham stood up and bowed to the local people, including the Heth family members.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Genesis 23:8">8</a> He talked with them, saying, &quot;If you agree that I should bury my dead away from me, listen to me and ask Ephron son of Zohar for help.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Genesis 23:9">9</a> He should sell me the cave of Machpelah that he owns, located at the edge of his field. I will pay its full price so it can be my burial site among you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Genesis 23:10">10</a> Ephron lived with the Heth people and in front of them, and all who entered his city&#39;s gate, Ephron the Hittite replied to Abraham, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Genesis 23:11">11</a> No, my lord, listen to me: I give you the field and the cave in it; I give it to you in front of my people&#39;s sons; bury your dead.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Genesis 23:12">12</a> Abraham bowed down to the local people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Genesis 23:13">13</a> He spoke to Ephron with the people of the land listening, saying, &quot;But if you will give it, please listen to me: I will pay you money for the field. Accept it from me, so I can bury my dead there.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Genesis 23:14">14</a> Ephron replied to Abraham, saying to him,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Genesis 23:15">15</a> Sir, please listen to me: the land costs four hundred silver coins; what difference does that make between us? Go ahead and bury your dead.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Genesis 23:16">16</a> Abraham listened to Ephron and paid him the 400 silver coins he had mentioned in front of the Heth&#39;s sons, which was the usual money used in trade.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Genesis 23:17">17</a> Ephron&#39;s field in Machpelah, near Mamre, including the field, the cave in it, and all the trees around its edges, was secured.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Genesis 23:18">18</a> To Abraham as his own land, seen by the Heth people, in front of everyone at his city gate.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Genesis 23:19">19</a> After this, Abraham buried his wife Sarah in the cave at Machpelah field near Mamre, which is Hebron in Canaan.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Genesis 23:20">20</a> The field and the cave in it were guaranteed to Abraham as a burial place by the Heth sons.</p>
