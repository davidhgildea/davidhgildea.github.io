<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 142:1">1</a> I called out to God with my voice; with my voice, I asked God for help.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 142:2">2</a> I told God all my problems; I showed Him my troubles.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 142:3">3</a> When I felt very upset, you knew where I was going. People have secretly set a trap for me on the path I took.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 142:4">4</a> I looked to my right and saw no one recognized me; I had nowhere to run; nobody cared about me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 142:5">5</a> I called to you, God; I said, You are my safe place and my share among those who are alive.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 142:6">6</a> Listen to my cry, for I am very weak; save me from those chasing me, for they are stronger than me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 142:7">7</a> Free me from my troubles so I can thank you. Good people will surround me because you are good to me.</p>
