<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 19:1">1</a> The skies show God&#39;s greatness; and the sky displays what his hands have made.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 19:2">2</a> Every day speaks, and every night teaches knowledge.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 19:3">3</a> Every language and speech can hear their voice.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 19:4">4</a> Their message has spread all over the world, and their words reach the ends of the earth. God has made a home in the sky for the sun.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 19:5">5</a> It is like a groom coming out of his room, happy like a runner ready to race.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 19:6">6</a> He comes from the farthest part of the sky, moves in a circle to the ends, and nothing can hide from its heat.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 19:7">7</a> God&#39;s law is perfect and refreshes the soul. God&#39;s promises are trustworthy, giving wisdom to the simple.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 19:8">8</a> God&#39;s laws are fair, making the heart glad; God&#39;s rule is clear, giving light to the eyes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 19:9">9</a> Respecting God is pure, lasting forever; God&#39;s decisions are completely true and fair.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 19:10">10</a> They are more valuable than gold, even a lot of pure gold; they are also sweeter than honey, even honey from the comb.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 19:11">11</a> Your teachings warn your servant, and great reward comes from following them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 19:12">12</a> Who can know their own mistakes? Cleanse me from my hidden faults.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Psalms 19:13">13</a> Help me avoid sins done in pride; don&#39;t let them control me. Then I will be blameless and free from serious guilt.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Psalms 19:14">14</a> May the words I speak and the thoughts in my heart please you, God, my strength and my savior.</p>
