<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 33:1">1</a> Be happy with God, you good people, because it&#39;s good for those who are right to give praise.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 33:2">2</a> Praise God with the harp: sing to him with the psaltery and a ten-stringed instrument.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 33:3">3</a> Sing a new song to God; play well with loud sounds.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 33:4">4</a> God&#39;s message is true; everything he does is honest.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 33:5">5</a> He loves what is right and fair; the world is filled with God&#39;s kindness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 33:6">6</a> God made the heavens by his word; and all the stars with his breath.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 33:7">7</a> He brings the sea waters together like a pile; he stores the depths in storerooms.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 33:8">8</a> Let everyone on earth fear God; let all the people of the world be amazed by him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 33:9">9</a> He spoke, and it happened; he gave an order, and it lasted.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 33:10">10</a> God makes the plans of nations fail; He causes the schemes of people to have no effect.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 33:11">11</a> God&#39;s plan lasts forever, his thoughts reach through all generations.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 33:12">12</a> Happy is the country that has God as its God; and the people God has picked to be His own.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Psalms 33:13">13</a> God looks from heaven; he sees all humans.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Psalms 33:14">14</a> From where he lives, he watches everyone on earth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Psalms 33:15">15</a> He shapes everyone&#39;s heart the same; he looks at all they do.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Psalms 33:16">16</a> No king is saved by having a big army; a strong man is not rescued by his great power.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Psalms 33:17">17</a> A horse is not reliable for protection; it cannot save anyone by its great power.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Psalms 33:18">18</a> Look, God watches over those who respect him, those who wait for his kindness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Psalms 33:19">19</a> To save them from death and to sustain them in times of hunger.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Psalms 33:20">20</a> Our soul waits for God: He is our help and our protector.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Psalms 33:21">21</a> Our heart will be happy in God because we have trusted in his holy name.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Psalms 33:22">22</a> God, let your kindness be with us, just as we trust in you.</p>
