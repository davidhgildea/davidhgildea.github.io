<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 32:1">1</a> Happy is the person whose wrongdoings are forgiven, and whose sins are hidden.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 32:2">2</a> Happy is the person whom God does not blame for wrongdoing, and who is honest in their heart.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 32:3">3</a> When I stayed quiet, my bones grew weak because I groaned all day long.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 32:4">4</a> Day and night your hand was hard on me: my energy became like the dryness of summer.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 32:5">5</a> I admit my sin to you, and I haven&#39;t hidden my wrongs. I decided to tell you about my sins, and you forgave me. Selah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 32:6">6</a> Every good person will pray to you when you can be found. In times of great floods, they will not reach him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 32:7">7</a> You are my hiding place; you will protect me from trouble; you will surround me with songs of rescue. Pause.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 32:8">8</a> I will teach you and show you the way to go; I will guide you with my eye.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 32:9">9</a> Do not be like the horse or mule that have no understanding and need a bit and bridle to be controlled, or they will not come to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 32:10">10</a> The wicked will have many troubles, but if you trust in God, you will be surrounded by mercy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 32:11">11</a> Be happy in God, and be joyful, you good people: and shout happily, all you who are honest in heart.</p>
