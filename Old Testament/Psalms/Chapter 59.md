<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 59:1">1</a> Save me from my enemies, my God; protect me from those who attack me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 59:2">2</a> Rescue me from people who do wrong, and protect me from violent men.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 59:3">3</a> Look, they are hiding to attack me; powerful people are against me, not because of my wrongs or my sins, O God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 59:4">4</a> They run and get ready to attack me for no reason: wake up to help me and see.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 59:5">5</a> You then, God of all, the God of Israel, wake up to check on all the nations: do not show kindness to any evil wrongdoers. Pause and think.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 59:6">6</a> They come back at night, making sounds like dogs, and roam around the city.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 59:7">7</a> Look, they spew from their mouths—sharp words are on their lips: for who, they think, hears us?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 59:8">8</a> But you, God, will laugh at them; you will mock all the nations.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 59:9">9</a> I will rely on you because of your strength: for God is my protector.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 59:10">10</a> God&#39;s kindness will go before me; God will let me see victory over my foes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 59:11">11</a> Do not kill them, or my people might forget. Scatter them by your power and defeat them, God our protector.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 59:12">12</a> Because of the wrong things they say and the lies from their lips, let them be caught in their pride. They say curses and lies.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Psalms 59:13">13</a> Destroy them in anger, destroy them so they are no more; and let them know that God rules in Jacob, all over the earth. Pause.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Psalms 59:14">14</a> Let them come back at night; they sound like dogs as they walk around the city.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Psalms 59:15">15</a> Let them search for food and complain if they are not full.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Psalms 59:16">16</a> I will sing about your power; yes, I will sing loudly about your kindness in the morning because you have been my protection and shelter when I was in trouble.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Psalms 59:17">17</a> To you, my strength, I will sing: for God is my protector, and the God who shows me mercy.</p>
