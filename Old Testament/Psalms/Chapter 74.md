<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 74:1">1</a> God, why have you rejected us forever? Why is your anger burning against the sheep of your field?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 74:2">2</a> Remember your people whom you long ago bought; the tribe you saved, which is your heritage; this Mount Zion where you live.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 74:3">3</a> Walk toward the endless ruins; see all the harm the enemy has done in the holy place.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 74:4">4</a> Your enemies shout in your meeting places; they put up their flags as symbols.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 74:5">5</a> A man was known for how much he used axes on large trees.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 74:6">6</a> But now they smash its carved art all at once with axes and hammers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 74:7">7</a> They have thrown fire into your holy place; they have polluted it by tearing down where your name lives to the ground.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 74:8">8</a> In their minds, they planned, &quot;Let&#39;s wipe them out as one group; they have burned down all of God&#39;s meeting places in the country.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 74:9">9</a> We no longer see our signs; there is no prophet left, and no one among us knows how long this will last.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 74:10">10</a> God, how long will the enemy insult us? Will the foe disrespect your name forever?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 74:11">11</a> Why do you pull back your hand, even your strong hand? Take it out from inside your coat.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 74:12">12</a> God has always been my King, saving people all over the world.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Psalms 74:13">13</a> You split the sea with your power; you crushed the sea monsters&#39; heads in the waters.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Psalms 74:14">14</a> You crushed the heads of the sea monster and gave it as food to the people living in the desert.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Psalms 74:15">15</a> You split open fountains and streams; you dried up powerful rivers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Psalms 74:16">16</a> The day is yours, the night is also yours; you have made the light and the sun.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Psalms 74:17">17</a> You have set all the earth&#39;s boundaries: you have made summer and winter.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Psalms 74:18">18</a> Remember that the enemy has insulted you, God, and that the foolish people have disrespected your name.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Psalms 74:19">19</a> Do not give your gentle one&#39;s life to many evil people; never forget your poor people&#39;s group.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Psalms 74:20">20</a> Pay attention to the agreement; the world&#39;s dark places are full of cruel homes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Psalms 74:21">21</a> God, don&#39;t let the oppressed go back feeling ashamed; let the poor and needy praise your name.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Psalms 74:22">22</a> Get up, God, defend your own case: Remember how the foolish person insults you every day.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Psalms 74:23">23</a> Do not ignore the voice of your enemies: the noise of those who oppose you grows stronger all the time.</p>
