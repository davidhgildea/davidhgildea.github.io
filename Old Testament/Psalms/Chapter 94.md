<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 94:1">1</a> God, who owns revenge, show yourself.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 94:2">2</a> Rise up, you judge of the earth: give the proud what they deserve.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 94:3">3</a> God, how long will the wicked win?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 94:4">4</a> How long will they keep on speaking harsh words and all the wrongdoers brag about themselves?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 94:5">5</a> They crush your people, God, and harm your cherished ones.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 94:6">6</a> They kill the widow and the foreigner, and they murder the orphan.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 94:7">7</a> They say, God won&#39;t see, and the God of Jacob won&#39;t pay attention.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 94:8">8</a> Listen, you senseless people: and you fools, when will you become wise?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 94:9">9</a> The one who made the ear, can&#39;t he hear? The one who created the eye, can&#39;t he see?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 94:10">10</a> The one who punishes nations, won&#39;t he correct? The one who teaches people knowledge, won&#39;t he know?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 94:11">11</a> God knows what people think, and that it is meaningless.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 94:12">12</a> Happy is the person you correct, God, and teach from your law.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Psalms 94:13">13</a> So you can give him peace from hard times until a trap is made for the evil.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Psalms 94:14">14</a> God will not reject His people or abandon His inheritance.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Psalms 94:15">15</a> Justice will come back to what is right, and all the honest-hearted will support it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Psalms 94:16">16</a> Who will stand up for me against those who do evil? Or who will stand up for me against those who commit wrongs?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Psalms 94:17">17</a> If God hadn&#39;t helped me, I would have been very close to complete silence.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Psalms 94:18">18</a> When I said, &quot;I&#39;m slipping,&quot; your kindness, God, supported me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Psalms 94:19">19</a> When I have many worries, your comforts make my soul happy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Psalms 94:20">20</a> Can a throne of evil be your partner, making trouble through its laws?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Psalms 94:21">21</a> They join together against the good person and blame the innocent.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Psalms 94:22">22</a> God is my protector, and He is the strong rock where I am safe.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Psalms 94:23">23</a> He will make them suffer for their sins and will completely destroy them because of their evil; yes, our God will wipe them out.</p>
