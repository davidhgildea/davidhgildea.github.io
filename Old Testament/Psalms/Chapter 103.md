<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 103:1">1</a> Praise God, O my soul, and everything in me, praise his holy name.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 103:2">2</a> Praise God, my soul, and don&#39;t forget all the good things he does.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 103:3">3</a> God forgives all your sins; God heals all your sicknesses.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 103:4">4</a> God saves your life from destruction; God blesses you with kindness and compassion.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 103:5">5</a> God fills your hunger with good things, making you feel young again like an eagle.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 103:6">6</a> God carries out what is right and fair for all who are mistreated.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 103:7">7</a> He showed his ways to Moses, his deeds to the Israelites.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 103:8">8</a> God is kind and good, slow to get angry, and full of mercy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 103:9">9</a> God won&#39;t always scold us, nor stay angry forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 103:10">10</a> God has not treated us as our sins deserve; nor has He repaid us based on our wrongdoings.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 103:11">11</a> Just as the sky is high above the ground, so is God&#39;s kindness to those who respect Him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 103:12">12</a> Just as the east is far from the west, so God has taken our wrongs far away from us.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Psalms 103:13">13</a> Just as a father shows kindness to his children, so God shows kindness to those who respect him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Psalms 103:14">14</a> God knows how we are made; He remembers that we are like dust.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Psalms 103:15">15</a> A person&#39;s life is like grass: they grow like a field flower.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Psalms 103:16">16</a> The wind blows over it, and it disappears; and its place will not recognize it anymore.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Psalms 103:17">17</a> God&#39;s kindness lasts forever for those who respect Him, and His goodness extends to their grandchildren.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Psalms 103:18">18</a> Those who follow God&#39;s promise and remember to obey his rules.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Psalms 103:19">19</a> God has set up his throne in the sky; and his kingdom controls everything.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Psalms 103:20">20</a> Praise God, you strong angels who obey his commands and listen to what he says.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Psalms 103:21">21</a> Praise God, all his armies; you who serve him and do what he wants.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Psalms 103:22">22</a> Praise God, all his creations everywhere he rules: praise God, my inner self.</p>
