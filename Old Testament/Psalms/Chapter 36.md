<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 36:1">1</a> The bad person&#39;s wrongdoing tells me deep down that they don&#39;t respect God at all.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 36:2">2</a> He praises himself until his wrongdoings are seen as hateful.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 36:3">3</a> What he says is wrong and untruthful: he&#39;s stopped being wise and doing good.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 36:4">4</a> He plans trouble on his bed; he chooses a path that is not good; he does not hate evil.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 36:5">5</a> Your kindness, God, is as high as the heavens; your faithfulness extends to the clouds.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 36:6">6</a> Your goodness is as big as the mountains; your decisions are very deep: God, you protect both people and animals.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 36:7">7</a> Your love is wonderful, God! That&#39;s why people find safety in the shelter of your wings.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 36:8">8</a> They will be fully content with the good things from your house; and you will let them drink from your river of joys.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 36:9">9</a> With you is the source of life; by your light, we see light.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 36:10">10</a> Keep showing your love to those who know you, and your fairness to those who are true in their hearts.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 36:11">11</a> Don&#39;t let proud people attack me, and don&#39;t let the wicked push me away.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 36:12">12</a> The evil doers have fallen; they are pushed down and can&#39;t get back up.</p>
