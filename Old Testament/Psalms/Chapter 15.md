<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 15:1">1</a> God, who will live in your tent? Who will stay on your sacred mountain?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 15:2">2</a> The person who lives rightly, does what is just, and tells the truth in their heart.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 15:3">3</a> He who does not talk badly about others with his tongue, does not harm his neighbor, and does not insult his neighbor.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 15:4">4</a> He looks down on those who are bad, but respects those who respect God. He keeps his promises, even when it costs him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 15:5">5</a> Someone who does not lend money for interest or take bribes to hurt the innocent will always stand firm.</p>
