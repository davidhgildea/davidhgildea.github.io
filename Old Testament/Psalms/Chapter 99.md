<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 99:1">1</a> God rules; let the people shake with fear: he sits among the angels; let the earth quake.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 99:2">2</a> God is great in Zion; He is above everyone.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 99:3">3</a> Let them praise your great and awesome name; it is holy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 99:4">4</a> The king&#39;s power shows love for fairness; you set up equality, you carry out fair judgment and what is right in Jacob.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 99:5">5</a> Praise God our God, and bow down at the place he rests his feet; because he is holy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 99:6">6</a> Moses and Aaron were among his priests, and Samuel was among those who called on his name; they called on God, and he answered them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 99:7">7</a> He spoke to them from the cloud: they followed his instructions and the laws he gave them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 99:8">8</a> You answered them, O God our God: you were a God who forgave them, even though you punished their misdeeds.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 99:9">9</a> Praise God, our God, and bow down on his sacred mountain; for God, our God, is pure.</p>
