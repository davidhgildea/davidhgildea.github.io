<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 20:1">1</a> God hears you in times of trouble; may the God of Jacob protect you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 20:2">2</a> May God send you help from his holy place and give you support from Zion.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 20:3">3</a> Remember all your gifts, and accept your burnt offering.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 20:4">4</a> Give you what your heart wants and make all your plans succeed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 20:5">5</a> We will be happy because of your rescue, and we will raise our flags in the name of our God: may God answer all your requests.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 20:6">6</a> Now I know that God saves the one he has chosen; he will listen to him from his holy place in heaven with the powerful help of his right hand.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 20:7">7</a> Some rely on chariots, and some on horses, but we will trust in the name of our God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 20:8">8</a> They have fallen, but we have risen and stand tall.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 20:9">9</a> Save us, God: may the king listen to us when we call.</p>
