<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 135:1">1</a> Praise God. Praise the name of God; praise him, all you servants of God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 135:2">2</a> You who stand in God&#39;s house, in the courts of our God&#39;s house.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 135:3">3</a> Praise God because God is good. Sing praises to His name because it is nice.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 135:4">4</a> God has chosen Jacob for himself, and Israel as his special treasure.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 135:5">5</a> I know that God is great and that our Lord is greater than all other gods.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 135:6">6</a> Whatever God wanted to do, he did in heaven and on earth, in the seas and in all deep places.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 135:7">7</a> God makes the mist rise from the earth&#39;s edges; he creates lightning for the rain; he brings the wind from his storerooms.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 135:8">8</a> Who struck down the firstborn of Egypt, both of humans and animals.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 135:9">9</a> God sent signs and miracles into the middle of Egypt, upon Pharaoh, and upon all his servants.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 135:10">10</a> God defeated large nations and killed powerful kings.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 135:11">11</a> Sihon, the king of the Amorites, Og, the king of Bashan, and all the kingdoms of Canaan.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 135:12">12</a> God gave their land as a heritage, a heritage to his people Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Psalms 135:13">13</a> Your name, O God, lasts forever; and people will remember you, O God, through all generations.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Psalms 135:14">14</a> God will judge his people, and he will change his mind about his servants.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Psalms 135:15">15</a> The gods of other nations are made of silver and gold and are crafted by human hands.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Psalms 135:16">16</a> They have mouths but do not talk; they have eyes but do not see;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Psalms 135:17">17</a> They have ears, but they don&#39;t hear; they also don&#39;t breathe through their mouths.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Psalms 135:18">18</a> Those who make idols become like them; so does everyone who trusts in them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Psalms 135:19">19</a> Praise God, O people of Israel; praise God, O family of Aaron.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Psalms 135:20">20</a> Praise God, O family of Levi: all who respect God, praise God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Psalms 135:21">21</a> May God, who lives in Jerusalem, be blessed. Praise God.</p>
