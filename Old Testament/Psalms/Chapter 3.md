<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 3:1">1</a> God, so many are making trouble for me! Many are turning against me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 3:2">2</a> Many say about me, &quot;God won&#39;t save him.&quot; (Pause)</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 3:3">3</a> But you, God, are a protector for me; my honor, and the one who lifts my head high.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 3:4">4</a> I called to God with my voice, and He listened to me from His holy mountain. Selah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 3:5">5</a> I lay down and slept; I woke up because God supported me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 3:6">6</a> I will not fear thousands of people surrounding me, who are against me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 3:7">7</a> Get up, God; save me, my God: because you have hit all my enemies on the cheek; you have broken the teeth of the wicked.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 3:8">8</a> God gives salvation; your blessing rests on your people. Pause.</p>
