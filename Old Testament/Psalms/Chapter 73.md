<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 73:1">1</a> God is truly good to Israel and to those who have a pure heart.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 73:2">2</a> But as for me, I almost lost my footing; my steps nearly slipped.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 73:3">3</a> I felt jealous of foolish people when I saw the success of the wicked.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 73:4">4</a> In their death, they face no struggles, and they remain strong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 73:5">5</a> They don&#39;t have troubles like other people; they aren&#39;t afflicted like everyone else.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 73:6">6</a> So pride surrounds them like a chain; violence covers them like clothes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 73:7">7</a> They have puffy eyes from being rich: they own more than anyone could desire.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 73:8">8</a> They are bad, talking badly about making others suffer. They talk with great pride.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 73:9">9</a> They speak against God above, and their words spread across the world.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 73:10">10</a> So his people come back here, and they are given plenty to drink.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 73:11">11</a> And they ask, &quot;Does God know? Does the Most High have knowledge?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 73:12">12</a> Look, these are the wicked, who succeed in life; they grow rich.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Psalms 73:13">13</a> I truly cleaned my heart for nothing, and washed my hands being innocent.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Psalms 73:14">14</a> All day I suffer and am punished each morning.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Psalms 73:15">15</a> If I decide to speak like this, I realize I would upset your children&#39;s generation.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Psalms 73:16">16</a> When I tried to understand this, it was too hard for me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Psalms 73:17">17</a> Until I went into God&#39;s sanctuary; then I understood their fate.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Psalms 73:18">18</a> You truly put them in risky places; you threw them down to ruin.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Psalms 73:19">19</a> How suddenly they are ruined! They are completely destroyed by fears.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Psalms 73:20">20</a> Like a dream when someone wakes up; so God, when you wake up, you will look down on their appearance.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Psalms 73:21">21</a> My heart was sad, and I felt a sharp pain inside.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Psalms 73:22">22</a> I was foolish and did not understand; I was like an animal before you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Psalms 73:23">23</a> I am always with you; you hold my right hand.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Psalms 73:24">24</a> You will guide me with your advice, and later take me to heaven.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Psalms 73:25">25</a> Who else do I have in heaven but you? And on earth, I want no one but you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Psalms 73:26">26</a> My body and heart may grow weak, but God is the strong source of my heart and my share forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Psalms 73:27">27</a> Those far from you will die; you destroy all who are unfaithful to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Psalms 73:28">28</a> It is good for me to get close to God. I trust in God so I can tell about all your deeds.</p>
