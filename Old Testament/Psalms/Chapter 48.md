<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 48:1">1</a> God is great, and deserves high praise in the city of our God, on his holy mountain.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 48:2">2</a> Mount Zion is beautifully placed, the joy of the entire world, on the north side, the city of the great King.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 48:3">3</a> God is known in her strong buildings as a place of safety.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 48:4">4</a> Look, the kings gathered and went by together.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 48:5">5</a> They saw it and were amazed; they were distressed and hurried away.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 48:6">6</a> They were very afraid and in pain like a woman giving birth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 48:7">7</a> You break the ships of Tarshish with an east wind.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 48:8">8</a> We have heard and now we see in the city of God, the city of our God: God will make it last forever. Pause and think about that.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 48:9">9</a> We have thought of your kindness, God, in the middle of your temple.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 48:10">10</a> God, your name reaches to the ends of the earth, just like your praise; your right hand is filled with fairness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 48:11">11</a> Let Mount Zion be happy, let Judah&#39;s daughters be joyful, because of your decisions.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 48:12">12</a> Walk around Zion, circle it, and count its towers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Psalms 48:13">13</a> Pay close attention to her strong walls, look carefully at her palaces; so you can tell the next generation about them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Psalms 48:14">14</a> This God is our God forever: He will guide us until we die.</p>
