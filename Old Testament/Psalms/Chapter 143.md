<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 143:1">1</a> Listen to my prayer, God, pay attention to my requests: answer me because you are faithful and just.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 143:2">2</a> Do not judge your servant, because no one alive can be seen as good before you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 143:3">3</a> The enemy has chased my soul; he has struck my life down to the ground; he has forced me to live in darkness, like those long dead.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 143:4">4</a> So my spirit feels crushed inside me; my heart inside me feels empty.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 143:5">5</a> I remember the old days; I think about all you have done; I reflect on what your hands have made.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 143:6">6</a> I reach out to you with my hands: I deeply desire you, like dry ground needs water. Pause and think about that.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 143:7">7</a> Listen to me quickly, God; my spirit is weak. Do not hide from me, or I will be like those who fall into the pit.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 143:8">8</a> Let me hear your kindness in the morning, for I trust in you. Show me the way I should go, because I give my heart to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 143:9">9</a> God, save me from my enemies; I run to you for protection.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 143:10">10</a> Show me how to follow your will, because you are my God. Your spirit is kind; guide me to a life of integrity.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 143:11">11</a> God, give me life for the sake of your name; for the sake of your righteousness, rescue my soul from trouble.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 143:12">12</a> In your kindness, get rid of my enemies and wipe out all who trouble me, because I am your servant.</p>
