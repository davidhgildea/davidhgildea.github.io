<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 121:1">1</a> I will look up to the hills, where my help comes from.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 121:2">2</a> My help comes from God, who made heaven and earth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 121:3">3</a> God won&#39;t let your foot slip; he who watches over you won&#39;t fall asleep.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 121:7">7</a> God will protect you from all harm; He will keep your life safe.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 121:8">8</a> God will protect you when you leave and when you return, now and always.</p>
