<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 108:1">1</a> God, my heart is steady; I will sing and praise you with all my heart.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 108:2">2</a> Wake up, stringed instruments and harp: I will wake up early.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 108:3">3</a> I will praise you, God, among the people; and I will sing praises to you among the nations.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 108:4">4</a> Your kindness is vast, beyond the skies, and your truth goes up to the clouds.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 108:5">5</a> God, be lifted up higher than the heavens; let your glory be over all the earth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 108:6">6</a> Help your loved ones be rescued: save them with your power, and respond to me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 108:7">7</a> God has spoken in his sacred place; I will be happy, I will split Shechem, and measure out the valley of Succoth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 108:8">8</a> Gilead belongs to me; Manasseh is mine; Ephraim is my strong leader; Judah makes my laws.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 108:9">9</a> Moab is my washbasin; I will toss my shoe over Edom; I will be victorious over Philistia.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 108:10">10</a> Who will take me into the fortified city? Who will guide me into Edom?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 108:11">11</a> Won&#39;t you, God, who have rejected us, come with our armies?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 108:12">12</a> Help us in trouble, because human help is useless.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Psalms 108:13">13</a> With God&#39;s help, we will be brave; He will defeat our enemies.</p>
