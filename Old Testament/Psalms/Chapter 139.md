<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 139:1">1</a> God, you have looked into my heart and know everything about me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 139:2">2</a> You know when I sit and when I stand, you understand my thoughts from far away.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 139:3">3</a> You surround my path and my rest, and you know all my ways.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 139:4">4</a> God, before I speak, you know all of my words.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 139:5">5</a> You surround me from behind and in front, and place your hand on me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 139:6">6</a> This knowledge is too amazing for me; it is too much, I can&#39;t grasp it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 139:7">7</a> Where can I go to escape your spirit? Where can I run to hide from you?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 139:8">8</a> If I go up to heaven, you are there; if I lie down in the depths, you are there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 139:9">9</a> If I fly with the dawn&#39;s wings and live at the farthest part of the sea;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 139:10">10</a> Even there, your hand will guide me, and your right hand will hold me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 139:11">11</a> If I say, &quot;Surely the darkness will hide me,&quot; even the night will be bright around me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 139:12">12</a> Even darkness can&#39;t hide from you; night shines like day: darkness and light are the same to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Psalms 139:13">13</a> You have created my innermost parts; you knit me together in my mother&#39;s womb.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Psalms 139:14">14</a> I will praise you because I am made in an amazing and wonderful way; your works are marvelous, and I know this very well.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Psalms 139:15">15</a> You saw who I really was before I was born; when I was being formed in secret, intricately crafted as if deep within the earth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Psalms 139:16">16</a> Your eyes saw my unfinished body; all my parts were written in your book before any of them existed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Psalms 139:17">17</a> God, your thoughts are so precious to me! There are so many of them!</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Psalms 139:18">18</a> If I try to count them, they are more than the sand: when I wake up, I am still with you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Psalms 139:19">19</a> You will surely kill the wicked, God; so you violent people, get away from me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Psalms 139:20">20</a> They speak badly about you, God, and your enemies misuse your name.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Psalms 139:21">21</a> Don&#39;t I hate those who hate you, God? And don&#39;t I feel upset with those who oppose you?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Psalms 139:22">22</a> I strongly dislike them: I consider them my enemies.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Psalms 139:23">23</a> Look inside me, God, and understand my heart: test me, and know what I think.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Psalms 139:24">24</a> Check if there&#39;s any badness in me, and guide me on the forever path.</p>
