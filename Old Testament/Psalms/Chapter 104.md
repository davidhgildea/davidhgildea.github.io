<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 104:1">1</a> Praise God, my soul. O my God, you are very great; you are dressed in glory and splendor.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 104:2">2</a> You cover yourself with light like clothes: you spread out the skies like a curtain.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 104:3">3</a> He sets the beams for his rooms in the waters. He makes the clouds his carriage. He walks on the wings of the wind.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 104:4">4</a> God makes his angels spirits and his servants like a blazing fire.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 104:5">5</a> Who set the earth&#39;s foundations so it would never move.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 104:6">6</a> You covered it with the deep like a garment; the waters stood over the mountains.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 104:7">7</a> At your scolding they ran away; at the sound of your thunder they hurried off.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 104:8">8</a> They climb the mountains; they go down into the valleys to the place you have created for them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 104:9">9</a> You have set a limit they cannot cross; they cannot come back to cover the earth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 104:10">10</a> He sends streams into the valleys; they flow between the hills.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 104:11">11</a> They provide water for every animal in the field; the wild donkeys satisfy their thirst.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 104:12">12</a> The birds of the sky will live there, singing in the branches.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Psalms 104:13">13</a> He gives water to the hills from his home; the earth has all it needs from what you have done.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Psalms 104:14">14</a> God makes grass grow for the cows, and plants for people to use, so they can get food from the ground.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Psalms 104:15">15</a> Wine cheers people&#39;s hearts, oil makes their faces glow, and bread gives them strength.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Psalms 104:16">16</a> God&#39;s trees are full of juice; the cedars of Lebanon, which he has planted.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Psalms 104:17">17</a> Birds build their nests there; the stork makes its home in the fir trees.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Psalms 104:18">18</a> High hills give wild goats safety; rocks give shelter to hyraxes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Psalms 104:19">19</a> God made the moon to mark the seasons; the sun knows when to set.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Psalms 104:20">20</a> You make darkness, and it becomes night: when all the forest animals come out.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Psalms 104:21">21</a> Young lions roar for their food and look to God for it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Psalms 104:22">22</a> The sun rises, they come together, and lie down in their dens.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Psalms 104:23">23</a> A person goes out to work and works until evening.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Psalms 104:24">24</a> God, how many are your works! In wisdom, you made them all: the earth is filled with your wealth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Psalms 104:25">25</a> This big, vast sea is home to countless creeping things, both tiny and huge creatures.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Psalms 104:26">26</a> Ships sail there; the great sea creature you made plays in the waters.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Psalms 104:27">27</a> They all wait for you to give them their food at the right time.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Psalms 104:28">28</a> You give to them, and they collect; you open your hand, and they are satisfied with good things.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Psalms 104:29">29</a> When you hide your face, they are scared; when you take their breath away, they die and turn back to dust.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Psalms 104:30">30</a> You send out your spirit, and they are created; you renew the earth&#39;s surface.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Psalms 104:31">31</a> God&#39;s glory will last forever; God will be happy with his creations.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Psalms 104:32">32</a> God looks at the earth, and it shakes; he touches the mountains, and they smoke.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Psalms 104:33">33</a> I will sing to God as long as I live: I will praise my God as long as I exist.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Psalms 104:34">34</a> I will enjoy thinking about God: I will be happy with God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Psalms 104:35">35</a> Let the sinners vanish from the earth, and the wicked exist no more. Praise God, my soul. Praise God.</p>
