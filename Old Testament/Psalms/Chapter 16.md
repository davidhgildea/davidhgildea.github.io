<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 16:1">1</a> Keep me safe, God, because I trust in you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 16:2">2</a> I told God, &quot;You are my Lord; my goodness means nothing to you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 16:3">3</a> But to the holy people on earth, and to the outstanding ones, who bring me great joy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 16:4">4</a> People who follow other gods will have many troubles. I will not pour out blood offerings to them or speak their names.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 16:5">5</a> God is my chosen share and my cup; you support my destiny.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 16:6">6</a> My boundaries fall in nice places; yes, I have a wonderful inheritance.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 16:7">7</a> I will bless God, who has given me advice; even at night, my thoughts teach me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 16:8">8</a> I always keep God in my thoughts: because he is by my side, I will stay strong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 16:9">9</a> So my heart is happy, and I proudly celebrate; my body too will rest with hope.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 16:10">10</a> You will not leave my soul in hell; nor will you let your Holy One decay.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 16:11">11</a> You will show me the way to life: being with you is complete happiness; at your side, there are joys that never end.</p>
