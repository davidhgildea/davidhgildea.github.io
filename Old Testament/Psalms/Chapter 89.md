<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 89:1">1</a> I will sing about God&#39;s kindness forever; with my words, I will tell everyone about your faithfulness through all times.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 89:2">2</a> I have said that kindness will last forever; you will set your faithfulness firmly in the skies.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 89:3">3</a> I have made a promise with my chosen, I have sworn to David my servant.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 89:4">4</a> I will make your descendants last forever, and build your kingdom for all generations. Pause and think.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 89:5">5</a> The sky will praise your miracles, God: your faithfulness too, among the holy people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 89:6">6</a> Who in heaven can compare to God? Who among the powerful is like God?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 89:7">7</a> God should be greatly respected by his holy people and revered by all who are around him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 89:8">8</a> God of all, who is as mighty as you? Who matches your faithfulness?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 89:9">9</a> You control the furious sea: when its waves rise, you calm them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 89:10">10</a> You have shattered Rahab like someone killed; you have spread out your enemies with your powerful arm.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 89:11">11</a> The skies and the earth belong to you; you created the world and everything in it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 89:12">12</a> You created the north and the south: Tabor and Hermon will be happy in your name.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Psalms 89:13">13</a> You have a powerful arm; your hand is strong and your right hand is lifted high.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Psalms 89:14">14</a> Fairness and right decisions support your rule; kindness and honesty will be ahead of you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Psalms 89:15">15</a> Happy are the people who know the joyful sound: they will walk, God, in the light of your presence.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Psalms 89:16">16</a> They will be happy all day because of you, God, and your goodness will lift them up.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Psalms 89:17">17</a> For you are the source of their power; and by your kindness, we are made strong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Psalms 89:18">18</a> God is our protector; and the Holy One of Israel is our king.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Psalms 89:19">19</a> You spoke in a vision to your holy one and said, I have given help to someone strong; I have raised up a chosen one from the people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Psalms 89:20">20</a> I have chosen David as my servant; I have anointed him with my sacred oil.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Psalms 89:21">21</a> I will support him with my hand; my arm will also make him strong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Psalms 89:22">22</a> The enemy will not force him to pay; nor will the wicked person hurt him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Psalms 89:23">23</a> I will defeat his enemies in front of him and trouble those who hate him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Psalms 89:24">24</a> My loyalty and kindness will stay with him, and through my power, his strength will be lifted up.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Psalms 89:25">25</a> I will give him power over the sea and control over the rivers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Psalms 89:26">26</a> He will call out to me, &#39;You are my father, my God, and the rock who saves me.&#39;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Psalms 89:27">27</a> I will also make him my most important child, greater than all the rulers of the world.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Psalms 89:28">28</a> I will always show him kindness, and my agreement with him will always be strong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Psalms 89:29">29</a> I will make his descendants last forever, and his throne will endure as long as the sky.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Psalms 89:30">30</a> If his children ignore my rules and do not follow my decisions;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Psalms 89:31">31</a> If they do not follow my laws and do not obey my commands;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Psalms 89:32">32</a> I will punish their sins with a rod and their wrongdoings with whipping.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Psalms 89:33">33</a> I will not completely remove my kindness from him, nor let my faithfulness end.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Psalms 89:34">34</a> I will not break my promise or change what I have said.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Psalms 89:35">35</a> I have sworn by my holiness that I will not lie to David.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Psalms 89:36">36</a> His descendants will last forever, and his rule will be like the sun in my presence.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Psalms 89:37">37</a> It will last forever like the moon, and be a reliable witness in the sky. Pause.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Psalms 89:38">38</a> But you have rejected and despised, you have been angry with your chosen one.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="Psalms 89:39">39</a> You have broken the agreement with your servant; you have disrespected his crown by throwing it to the ground.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="Psalms 89:40">40</a> You have torn down all his fences; you have destroyed his strong defenses.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="Psalms 89:41">41</a> Everyone who goes by robs him; he&#39;s a disgrace to his neighbors.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="Psalms 89:42">42</a> You gave strength to his foes; you made all his enemies happy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v43"><a class="verse-num" href="#v43" aria-label="Psalms 89:43">43</a> You have also dulled the edge of his sword, and have not allowed him to stand strong in battle.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v44"><a class="verse-num" href="#v44" aria-label="Psalms 89:44">44</a> You have ended his glory and thrown his throne down to the ground.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v45"><a class="verse-num" href="#v45" aria-label="Psalms 89:45">45</a> You have cut short the days of his youth; you have wrapped him in disgrace. Pause.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v46"><a class="verse-num" href="#v46" aria-label="Psalms 89:46">46</a> How long, God? Will you hide yourself forever? Will your anger keep burning like fire?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v47"><a class="verse-num" href="#v47" aria-label="Psalms 89:47">47</a> Remember how brief my life is; why have you created everyone for no purpose?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v48"><a class="verse-num" href="#v48" aria-label="Psalms 89:48">48</a> Who is the person that will live and not die? Can he save himself from the power of the grave? Pause and think.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v49"><a class="verse-num" href="#v49" aria-label="Psalms 89:49">49</a> God, where are your past acts of love, which you promised to David in your faithfulness?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v50"><a class="verse-num" href="#v50" aria-label="Psalms 89:50">50</a> Remember, God, how your servants are insulted; I carry the insults of many peoples deep in my heart.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v51"><a class="verse-num" href="#v51" aria-label="Psalms 89:51">51</a> Your enemies have insulted you, God; they have insulted the path your chosen one has walked.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v52"><a class="verse-num" href="#v52" aria-label="Psalms 89:52">52</a> May God be praised always. So be it, and so be it.</p>
