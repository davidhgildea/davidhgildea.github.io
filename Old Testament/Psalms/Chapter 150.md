<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 150:1">1</a> Praise God. Praise him in his holy place; praise him in the sky that shows his strength.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 150:2">2</a> Praise God for his powerful deeds; praise him for his great majesty.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 150:3">3</a> Praise God with the trumpet&#39;s sound; praise God with the psaltery and harp.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 150:4">4</a> Praise God with drums and dancing; praise God with strings and pipes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 150:5">5</a> Praise God with loud cymbals; praise God with clashing cymbals.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 150:6">6</a> Let everything that breathes praise God. Praise God.</p>
