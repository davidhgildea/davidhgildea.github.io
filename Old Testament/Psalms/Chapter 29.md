<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 29:1">1</a> Give to God, you powerful ones, give God glory and power.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 29:2">2</a> Give God the honor that belongs to His name; worship God in the beauty of being holy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 29:3">3</a> God&#39;s voice is over the waters; the glorious God thunders; God is over the vast waters.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 29:4">4</a> God&#39;s voice is strong; God&#39;s voice is majestic.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 29:5">5</a> God&#39;s voice breaks the cedars; yes, God breaks the cedars of Lebanon.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 29:6">6</a> He makes them leap like a calf; Lebanon and Sirion like a young wild ox.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 29:7">7</a> God&#39;s voice splits the flames of fire.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 29:8">8</a> God&#39;s voice shakes the desert; God shakes the Kadesh desert.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 29:9">9</a> God&#39;s voice makes the deer give birth and uncovers the forests; in his temple, everyone talks about his greatness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 29:10">10</a> God sits above the flood; yes, God reigns as King forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 29:11">11</a> God will give his people strength; God will bless his people with peace.</p>
