<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 27:1">1</a> God is my light and my rescue; who should I fear? God is the power of my life; who should I be scared of?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 27:2">2</a> When the bad people, my enemies, attacked me to destroy me, they tripped and fell.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 27:3">3</a> Even if an army surrounds me, I won&#39;t be afraid; even if I am threatened by war, I will remain confident.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 27:4">4</a> I want one thing from God and I will look for it: to live in God&#39;s house all my life, to see how beautiful God is, and to learn in his holy place.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 27:5">5</a> When trouble comes, God will protect me in His shelter; He will keep me safe in His tent and lift me onto a rock.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 27:6">6</a> Now I will hold my head high above my enemies around me. So I will give joyful sacrifices in his tent; I will sing and praise God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 27:7">7</a> Listen, God, when I call out loud: be kind to me and respond to me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 27:8">8</a> When you said, &quot;Look for me,&quot; my heart responded, &quot;I will look for you, God.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 27:9">9</a> Don&#39;t hide from me; don&#39;t turn me away in anger. You have been my help; don&#39;t leave or abandon me, God who saves me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 27:10">10</a> When my father and my mother leave me, then God will take care of me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 27:11">11</a> Teach me your way, God, and guide me on a clear path because of my enemies.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 27:12">12</a> Do not give me up to my enemies&#39; wishes; false witnesses have stood against me, speaking cruelty.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Psalms 27:13">13</a> I would have lost hope if I didn&#39;t believe I would see God&#39;s goodness while I&#39;m alive.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Psalms 27:14">14</a> Wait for God; be brave, and He will make your heart strong; I say, wait for God.</p>
