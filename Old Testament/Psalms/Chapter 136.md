<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 136:1">1</a> Give thanks to God, for He is good; His love lasts forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 136:2">2</a> Give thanks to the God of all: for his kindness lasts forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 136:3">3</a> Give thanks to the God of gods: for his kindness lasts forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 136:4">4</a> To the one who alone does great miracles: because his kindness lasts forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 136:5">5</a> He made the skies using wisdom: His kindness lasts forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 136:6">6</a> To the one who spread the earth over the waters: because his kindness lasts forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 136:7">7</a> He made the big lights: His kindness lasts forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 136:8">8</a> The sun rules by day: for his kindness lasts forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 136:9">9</a> The moon and stars govern the night; God&#39;s kindness lasts forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 136:10">10</a> To the one who struck down Egypt&#39;s firstborn: because his kindness lasts forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 136:11">11</a> God led Israel out from among them because his kindness lasts forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 136:12">12</a> With a powerful hand and an extended arm: because his kindness lasts forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Psalms 136:13">13</a> He split the Red Sea in two: His love lasts forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Psalms 136:14">14</a> God led Israel through it because his kindness lasts forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Psalms 136:15">15</a> God overthrew Pharaoh and his army in the Red Sea because His kindness lasts forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Psalms 136:16">16</a> To the one who guided his people through the desert: because his kindness lasts forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Psalms 136:17">17</a> He struck down mighty kings: for his love lasts forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Psalms 136:18">18</a> He killed great kings: because His kindness lasts forever:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Psalms 136:19">19</a> Give thanks to God for defeating Sihon, king of the Amorites; His kindness lasts forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Psalms 136:20">20</a> And Og, the king of Bashan, because God&#39;s kindness lasts forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Psalms 136:21">21</a> God gave their land as a heritage because his mercy lasts forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Psalms 136:22">22</a> It&#39;s a gift to Israel, his servant, because God&#39;s kindness lasts forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Psalms 136:23">23</a> God remembered us when we were helpless, because his kindness lasts forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Psalms 136:24">24</a> God has saved us from our enemies because his kindness lasts forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Psalms 136:25">25</a> God gives food to all creatures; His kindness lasts forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Psalms 136:26">26</a> Give thanks to the God of heaven because his kindness lasts forever.</p>
