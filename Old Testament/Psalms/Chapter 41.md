<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 41:1">1</a> Happy is the one who thinks about the poor; God will save him when trouble comes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 41:2">2</a> God will protect him and keep him alive; he will be happy on earth, and you will not let his enemies defeat him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 41:3">3</a> God will give him strength when he is sick in bed; you will take care of him during his illness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 41:4">4</a> I said, &quot;God, be kind to me: make my soul better; for I have done wrong against you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 41:5">5</a> My enemies say bad things about me, asking, &quot;When will he die and his name be forgotten?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 41:6">6</a> If he comes to visit, he speaks nonsense; his heart collects evil thoughts, and when he leaves, he spreads them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 41:7">7</a> Everyone who hates me talks secretly against me; they plan to harm me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 41:8">8</a> They say a terrible sickness sticks to him; and now that he&#39;s down, he will not get up again.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 41:9">9</a> Yes, my close friend whom I trusted, who shared my food, has turned against me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 41:10">10</a> But you, God, be kind to me, and lift me up, so I can pay them back.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 41:11">11</a> I know you favor me because my enemy hasn&#39;t defeated me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 41:12">12</a> As for me, you support me in my honesty, and place me in your presence forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Psalms 41:13">13</a> Praise God of Israel forever and ever. Amen.</p>
