<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 51:1">1</a> Be kind to me, God, because of your love; because of your great compassion, wipe away my wrongdoings.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 51:2">2</a> Clean me completely from my wrongs, and wash away all my sins.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 51:3">3</a> I admit my wrongs, and my sin is always in my sight.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 51:4">4</a> I have sinned only against you and done this wrong in your view: so you are right when you speak and fair when you judge.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 51:5">5</a> I was born sinful, and my mother became pregnant with me in sin.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 51:6">6</a> You want honesty deep inside us; in our secret hearts, you teach us wisdom.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 51:7">7</a> Clean me with hyssop, and I will be clean: wash me, and I will be whiter than snow.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 51:8">8</a> Let me hear joy and happiness, so the bones you have crushed can be happy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 51:9">9</a> Turn away from my sins and erase all my wrongs.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 51:10">10</a> Make my heart clean, God; and give me a new, good spirit inside.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 51:11">11</a> Do not push me away from you; and do not take your holy spirit from me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 51:12">12</a> Give me back the happiness of your salvation; and support me with a generous spirit.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Psalms 51:13">13</a> Then I will teach wrongdoers your paths, and sinners will turn back to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Psalms 51:14">14</a> Save me from being guilty of causing death, O God, the God who saves me, and my tongue will sing loudly of your goodness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Psalms 51:15">15</a> God, open my lips; and my mouth will speak your praise.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Psalms 51:16">16</a> You do not want sacrifices, or I would give them; you are not pleased with burnt offerings.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Psalms 51:17">17</a> God values a broken spirit: a heart that is truly sorry, God, you will not reject.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Psalms 51:18">18</a> Do what makes you happy for Zion: rebuild Jerusalem&#39;s walls.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Psalms 51:19">19</a> Then you will be happy with the right sacrifices, with burnt offerings and complete burnt offerings; then they will present bulls on your altar.</p>
