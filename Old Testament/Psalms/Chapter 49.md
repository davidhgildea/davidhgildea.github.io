<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 49:1">1</a> Listen to this, everyone; pay attention, all who live in the world:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 49:2">2</a> Everyone, both unimportant and important, both rich and poor, together.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 49:3">3</a> I will speak wise words; my heart will think deeply with understanding.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 49:4">4</a> I will listen carefully to a story; I will explain my mysterious idea with music on the harp.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 49:5">5</a> Why should I be afraid on bad days, when the wrongs I&#39;ve done surround me?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 49:6">6</a> Those who trust in their money and brag about how much they have;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 49:7">7</a> No one can save their brother by paying a ransom to God for him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 49:8">8</a> To save a life is costly, and it never stops.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 49:9">9</a> He should live forever and not decay.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 49:10">10</a> He sees that wise people die, just like fools and senseless people, and they leave their riches to others.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 49:11">11</a> They think their homes will last forever and their places will be there for all time; they name their lands after themselves.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 49:12">12</a> Even though a person is respected, they do not last; they are like the animals that die.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Psalms 49:13">13</a> Their path is foolish, but their descendants praise their words. Pause.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Psalms 49:14">14</a> They are put in the grave like sheep; death will eat them up; in the morning, the good will rule over them; their beauty will fade away in the grave, far from their home.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Psalms 49:15">15</a> God will rescue my life from the grip of death because he will take me in.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Psalms 49:16">16</a> Don&#39;t be afraid when someone becomes rich, when their house gains more wealth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Psalms 49:17">17</a> When he dies, he takes nothing with him; his fame does not follow him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Psalms 49:18">18</a> While he was alive, he praised himself; and people will compliment you if you are good to yourself.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Psalms 49:19">19</a> He will join his ancestors in death; they will never again see the light of life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Psalms 49:20">20</a> A person with honor, who lacks understanding, is like animals that die.</p>
