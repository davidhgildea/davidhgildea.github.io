<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 95:1">1</a> Come, let&#39;s sing to God: let&#39;s shout joyfully to the rock who saves us.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 95:2">2</a> Let&#39;s come before God with thanksgiving and make a joyful noise to him with songs.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 95:3">3</a> God is a great God and a great King above all gods.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 95:4">4</a> In his hand are the world&#39;s depths; the hills&#39; power is also his.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 95:5">5</a> The sea belongs to God, and he created it; his hands shaped the land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 95:6">6</a> Come, let&#39;s worship and bow down; let&#39;s kneel before God our creator.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 95:7">7</a> He is our God; we are his people, the sheep he cares for. If you listen to his voice today,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 95:8">8</a> Do not make your heart hard, as when you challenged me, and as on the day of testing in the desert.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 95:9">9</a> When your ancestors tested me, tried me, and saw what I did.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 95:10">10</a> For forty years I was upset with that generation; I said, &quot;They are people whose hearts go astray, and they do not understand my paths.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 95:11">11</a> I promised in my anger that they would not enter my place of rest.</p>
