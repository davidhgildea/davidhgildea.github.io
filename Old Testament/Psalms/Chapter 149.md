<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 149:1">1</a> Praise God. Sing to God a new song, and praise Him when the faithful gather together.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 149:2">2</a> Let Israel be happy with their creator; let Zion&#39;s children be joyful in their King.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 149:3">3</a> Let them praise his name with dancing and sing to him with tambourine and harp.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 149:4">4</a> God enjoys his people; he will honor the humble with salvation.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 149:5">5</a> Let God&#39;s holy people be happy and praise Him; let them sing loudly on their beds.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 149:6">6</a> Let them speak God&#39;s high praises and hold a double-edged sword in their hand.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 149:7">7</a> To carry out revenge on the nations and punishment on the peoples.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 149:8">8</a> To tie up their kings with chains, and their leaders with iron shackles.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 149:9">9</a> To carry out the written judgment on them: all God&#39;s saints have this honor. Praise God.</p>
