<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 81:1">1</a> Sing loudly to God, our strength; make happy sounds to the God of Jacob.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 81:2">2</a> Sing a song, and bring the drum, the lovely harp with the zither.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 81:3">3</a> Sound the trumpet at the new moon, at the set time, on our special festival day.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 81:4">4</a> This was a rule for Israel, and a law from the God of Jacob.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 81:5">5</a> He made this a law in Joseph to be a witness, when he traveled through Egypt, where I heard a language I did not understand.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 81:6">6</a> I took the burden off his shoulder; his hands were freed from carrying heavy pots.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 81:7">7</a> You called for help in trouble, and I saved you; I answered you from the thunder&#39;s hiding place; I tested you at the waters of Meribah. Pause.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 81:8">8</a> Listen, my people, and I will speak to you: Israel, if you listen to me;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 81:9">9</a> You must not have any foreign gods; you should not bow down to any foreign gods.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 81:10">10</a> I am your God who brought you out of Egypt. Open your mouth wide, and I will fill it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 81:11">11</a> But my people did not listen to me; Israel did not want me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 81:12">12</a> So I let them follow their own desires, and they did what they thought was best.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Psalms 81:13">13</a> I wish my people had listened to me and Israel had followed my paths!</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Psalms 81:14">14</a> I would quickly defeat their enemies and act against their opponents.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Psalms 81:15">15</a> God&#39;s enemies should have surrendered to him, but their punishment will last forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Psalms 81:16">16</a> He should have given them the best wheat to eat, and I should have satisfied you with honey from the rock.</p>
