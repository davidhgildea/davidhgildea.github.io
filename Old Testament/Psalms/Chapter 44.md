<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 44:1">1</a> We have heard, O God, our ancestors told us what you did in their time, long ago.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 44:2">2</a> How you drove out nations with your hand, and settled them; how you troubled the people, and forced them out.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 44:3">3</a> They did not take the land with their own swords, nor did their own strength save them. It was your hand, your strength, and the brightness of your face, because you favored them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 44:4">4</a> You are my King, God: order victory for Jacob.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 44:5">5</a> With your help, we will defeat our enemies; with your name, we will walk over those who attack us.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 44:6">6</a> I will not rely on my bow, nor will my sword rescue me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 44:7">7</a> You have saved us from our enemies and made those who hated us feel ashamed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 44:8">8</a> We proudly trust in God all day, and forever praise your name. Pause.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 44:9">9</a> But you have rejected us, made us feel ashamed, and do not go with our armies.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 44:10">10</a> You make us retreat from the enemy, and those who hate us take our belongings for themselves.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 44:11">11</a> You have made us like sheep set for slaughter and have spread us among the nations.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 44:12">12</a> You sell your people for nothing and do not gain any profit from their sale.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Psalms 44:13">13</a> You make us a disgrace to our neighbors, an object of scorn and ridicule to those around us.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Psalms 44:14">14</a> You make us a joke among the nations, a thing people shake their heads at.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Psalms 44:15">15</a> I am always upset, and shame covers my face.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Psalms 44:16">16</a> Because of those who insult and speak against God, and because of the enemy who seeks revenge.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Psalms 44:17">17</a> All this has happened to us, but we have not forgotten you, and we have not broken your agreement.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Psalms 44:18">18</a> Our heart has not turned away, nor have our steps left your path.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Psalms 44:19">19</a> Even though you have crushed us in the dragon&#39;s lair and shrouded us in deep darkness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Psalms 44:20">20</a> If we&#39;ve forgotten our God&#39;s name, or reached out to a foreign god;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Psalms 44:21">21</a> Won&#39;t God find this out? He knows the heart&#39;s secrets.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Psalms 44:22">22</a> Yes, because of you we are being killed all day long; we are considered as sheep to be killed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Psalms 44:23">23</a> Wake up, why are you sleeping, God? Get up, do not reject us forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Psalms 44:24">24</a> Why do you hide your face and forget about our suffering and hardship?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Psalms 44:25">25</a> We feel very low, like we&#39;re pressed down to the ground; we are as close to the earth as if our stomach sticks to it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Psalms 44:26">26</a> Get up to help us, and save us because of your kindness.</p>
