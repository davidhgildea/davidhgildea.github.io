<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 42:1">1</a> Like a deer that longs for streams of water, my soul longs for you, God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 42:2">2</a> I long for God, for the living God. When can I go and meet with God?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 42:3">3</a> I cry all day and night, and people keep asking me, &quot;Where is your God?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 42:4">4</a> When I think about these things, I share all my feelings: because I used to go with the crowd, I joined them to worship at God&#39;s house, with happy and thankful shouting, with many who were celebrating.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 42:5">5</a> Why are you sad, my soul? And why are you troubled inside me? Put your hope in God: because I will still praise him for his support.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 42:6">6</a> My God, I am deeply sad inside me; so I will think of you from the area of the Jordan River, near the Hermon mountains and Mount Mizar.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 42:7">7</a> The deep waters call to the deep at the sound of your waterfalls; all your waves and breakers have swept over me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 42:8">8</a> God will show his kindness during the day, and at night I will sing a song about him and pray to the God who gives me life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 42:9">9</a> I will ask God, my rock, &quot;Why have you forgotten me? Why must I walk in sadness because the enemy is crushing me?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 42:10">10</a> It&#39;s like there&#39;s a sword in my bones; my enemies mock me, constantly asking, &quot;Where is your God?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 42:11">11</a> Why are you sad, my soul? And why are you upset inside me? Put your hope in God: because I will still praise him, who is the one who makes me well, and my God.</p>
