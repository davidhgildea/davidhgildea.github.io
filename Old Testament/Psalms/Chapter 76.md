<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 76:1">1</a> God is known in Judah; His name is great in Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 76:2">2</a> God&#39;s tent is in Salem, and his home is in Zion.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 76:3">3</a> There he broke the arrows, the shield, the sword, and the battle. Pause.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 76:4">4</a> You are more splendid and wonderful than the mountains of prey.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 76:5">5</a> The brave are ruined, they have slept their last sleep; and none of the strong found their strength.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 76:6">6</a> When you scold, God of Jacob, both chariots and horses fall into a deep sleep.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 76:7">7</a> You, yes you, are to be feared: and who can stand before you when you are angry?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 76:8">8</a> You made your judgment heard from heaven; the earth was afraid and became quiet.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 76:9">9</a> When God stood up to judge and to rescue all the gentle people on earth. Pause.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 76:10">10</a> Human anger will end up praising you; you will hold back the rest of the anger.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 76:11">11</a> Promise and give to God: everyone around Him should bring gifts to the one who should be respected.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 76:12">12</a> He will end the power of rulers; he is fearsome to the kings of the world.</p>
