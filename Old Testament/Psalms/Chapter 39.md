<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 39:1">1</a> I decided to watch how I act, so I won&#39;t sin with what I say; I will control my words when bad people are around me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 39:2">2</a> I stayed quiet, didn&#39;t even speak what&#39;s good; my sadness grew.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 39:3">3</a> I felt a strong burning in my heart as I thought deeply; then I began to speak.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 39:4">4</a> God, let me know my life&#39;s end and how many days I have; let me realize how short-lived I am.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 39:5">5</a> Look, you have made my life very short, and my lifetime seems like nothing to you. Truly, even at his healthiest, every person is completely fleeting.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 39:6">6</a> Everyone goes through life putting on a show for no reason; they worry for nothing. They pile up wealth, without knowing who will end up with it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 39:7">7</a> And now, God, what do I wait for? My hope is in you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 39:8">8</a> Save me from all my wrongdoings; don&#39;t let the foolish shame me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 39:9">9</a> I was silent, I did not open my mouth; because you did it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 39:10">10</a> Take away your punishment; I am worn out from your strong hand.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 39:11">11</a> When you correct people for their wrongs with scolding, you cause their beauty to fade away like a moth: truly, all people are fleeting. Pause and think.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 39:12">12</a> Listen to my prayer, God, and hear my cry; do not ignore my tears: for I am a stranger to you, and a traveler, just like my ancestors were.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Psalms 39:13">13</a> Please give me rest so I can get strong again before I leave and disappear.</p>
