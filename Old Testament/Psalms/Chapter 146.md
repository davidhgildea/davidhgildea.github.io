<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 146:1">1</a> Praise God. Praise God, my soul.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 146:2">2</a> As long as I live, I will praise God: I will sing to my God as long as I exist.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 146:3">3</a> Do not trust in leaders or in any human who cannot save you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 146:4">4</a> When he stops breathing, he goes back to the ground; on that day his plans end.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 146:5">5</a> The person who has the God of Jacob to help him and who hopes in God is happy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 146:6">6</a> God made the sky, the land, the sea, and everything in them; God always keeps promises.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 146:7">7</a> God judges for the oppressed and gives food to the hungry. God sets the prisoners free.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 146:8">8</a> God opens the eyes of the blind; God lifts up those who are bent over; God loves the good people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 146:9">9</a> God protects foreigners; He supports orphans and widows; but He makes the path of the wicked difficult.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 146:10">10</a> God will rule forever, your God, O Zion, for all generations. Praise God.</p>
