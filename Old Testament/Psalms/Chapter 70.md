<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 70:1">1</a> Hurry, God, to save me; hurry to help me, God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 70:2">2</a> May those who want to harm me feel ashamed and confused; may they be turned away and filled with embarrassment.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 70:3">3</a> Let those who mock and say, &quot;Aha, aha,&quot; be pushed back and shamed for their actions.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 70:4">4</a> May all who look for you be happy and joyful in you; and may those who love your rescue always say, &quot;May God be praised.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 70:5">5</a> I am poor and need help; hurry to me, God. You are my support and rescuer; God, do not delay.</p>
