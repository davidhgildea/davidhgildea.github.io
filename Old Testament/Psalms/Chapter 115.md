<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 115:1">1</a> Not for us, God, not for us, but for your name give glory, because of your kindness and your truth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 115:2">2</a> Why should other nations ask, &quot;Where is their God now?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 115:3">3</a> Our God is in heaven: he does whatever he wants.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 115:4">4</a> Their idols are made of silver and gold; they are made by human hands.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 115:5">5</a> They have mouths but don&#39;t speak; they have eyes but don&#39;t see.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 115:6">6</a> They have ears but cannot hear, and they have noses but cannot smell.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 115:7">7</a> They have hands but do not touch; they have feet but do not walk; they do not make sounds with their throats.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 115:8">8</a> Those who make idols are like them; so is everyone who trusts in them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 115:9">9</a> Israel, trust in God: He is their help and shield.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 115:10">10</a> Family of Aaron, trust in God; He is their help and protector.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 115:11">11</a> If you respect God, trust in God: He is their help and protector.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 115:12">12</a> God has remembered us; He will bless us; He will bless Israel&#39;s people; He will bless Aaron&#39;s family.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Psalms 115:13">13</a> God will bless those who respect Him, no matter if they are unimportant or important.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Psalms 115:14">14</a> God will make you and your children more and more prosperous.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Psalms 115:15">15</a> You are blessed by God who made the sky and the earth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Psalms 115:16">16</a> The skies, and even the highest skies, belong to God; but He has given the earth to people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Psalms 115:17">17</a> Dead people cannot praise God, nor can anyone who goes into the quietness of death.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Psalms 115:18">18</a> We will praise God now and forever. Praise God.</p>
