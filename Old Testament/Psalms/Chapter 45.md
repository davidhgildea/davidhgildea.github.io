<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 45:1">1</a> My heart is overflowing with a noble theme; I am sharing what I have composed about the king: my tongue is like the pen of a skilled writer.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 45:2">2</a> You are more beautiful than any person; your words are full of grace; that&#39;s why God has blessed you forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 45:3">3</a> Put your sword on your side, most powerful one, with your honor and greatness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 45:4">4</a> In your greatness, ride successfully for truth, humility, and justice; and your right hand will show you powerful things.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 45:5">5</a> Your arrows are sharp and strike deep into the king&#39;s enemies, making the people fall before you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 45:6">6</a> Your throne, O God, lasts forever and ever; your kingdom&#39;s ruling staff is fair.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 45:7">7</a> You love what is right and hate evil; therefore, God, your God, has chosen you and made you happier than your friends.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 45:8">8</a> Your clothes have the scent of myrrh, aloe, and cassia from the grand palaces where they brought you joy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 45:9">9</a> The daughters of kings were among your respected women; the queen stood at your right side, dressed in Ophir&#39;s gold.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 45:10">10</a> Listen, daughter, think about this and pay attention; also forget your own people and your father&#39;s home.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 45:11">11</a> The king will really like how you look: because he is your Lord; and you should worship him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 45:12">12</a> The woman from Tyre will come with a present; the wealthy will seek your kindness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Psalms 45:13">13</a> The king&#39;s daughter is completely splendid inside; she wears clothes made of pure gold.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Psalms 45:14">14</a> She will be presented to the king in embroidered clothes; her virgin friends following her will also be brought to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Psalms 45:15">15</a> They will be brought with joy and happiness; they will go into the king&#39;s palace.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Psalms 45:16">16</a> Your children will take the place of your ancestors, and you can make them leaders everywhere in the world.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Psalms 45:17">17</a> I will make your name remembered in all generations: so the people will praise you forever and ever.</p>
