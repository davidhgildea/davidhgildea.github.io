<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 79:1">1</a> God, foreigners have entered your land; they have made your holy temple dirty; they have turned Jerusalem into ruins.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 79:2">2</a> They gave your servants&#39; dead bodies as food to the birds of the sky, and your holy people&#39;s flesh to the earth&#39;s animals.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 79:3">3</a> They poured out their blood like water all around Jerusalem, and no one was there to bury them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 79:4">4</a> We have become a disgrace to our neighbors, mocked and laughed at by those around us.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 79:5">5</a> How long, God? Will you be angry always? Will your jealousy burn like fire?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 79:6">6</a> Show your anger to the nations that don&#39;t know you, and to the kingdoms that don&#39;t call on your name.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 79:7">7</a> They have destroyed Jacob and ruined his home.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 79:8">8</a> God, don&#39;t remember our past sins. Quickly show us your compassion, because we are very weak.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 79:9">9</a> Help us, God who saves us, for the sake of your name&#39;s glory; rescue us and take away our sins for your name&#39;s sake.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 79:10">10</a> Why should other nations ask, &quot;Where is their God?&quot; Let it be known to these nations that you avenge the spilled blood of your servants.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 79:11">11</a> Let the groans of the captive reach you; save those sentenced to die by your great power.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 79:12">12</a> Give back to our neighbors seven times the insults they have given you, God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Psalms 79:13">13</a> We, your people and sheep of your pasture, will always thank you; we will praise you for all generations.</p>
