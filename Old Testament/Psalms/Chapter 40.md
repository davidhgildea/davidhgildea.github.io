<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 40:1">1</a> I waited patiently for God; He turned to me and heard my cry.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 40:2">2</a> God lifted me from a terrible pit and from sticky mud, placed my feet on solid ground, and made my steps firm.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 40:3">3</a> He has given me a new song to sing, a hymn of praise to our God. Many will see this and be in awe, and will trust in God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 40:4">4</a> Happy is the person who trusts in God and does not honor the proud or those who believe in lies.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 40:5">5</a> God, you have done so many amazing things and your thoughts for us are countless. If I tried to list them, there would be too many to count.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 40:6">6</a> You do not want sacrifices or offerings; you have made me listen. You do not need burnt offerings or sin offerings.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 40:7">7</a> I said, &quot;Look, I&#39;m coming: the book says about me,&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 40:8">8</a> I enjoy doing what you want, my God; yes, your rules are in my heart.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 40:9">9</a> I have told of being good in the large group of people; look, I have not held back my words, God, you know.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 40:10">10</a> I have not kept your goodness secret in my heart; I have spoken of your faithfulness and your rescue: I have not hidden your kindness and your truth from the many people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 40:11">11</a> Don&#39;t keep your kindness and mercy from me, God; may your love and truth always protect me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 40:12">12</a> Countless troubles surround me; my sins have caught up to me, and I cannot see a way out. They outnumber the hairs on my head, and my heart is overwhelmed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Psalms 40:13">13</a> Please, God, rescue me; God, hurry to assist me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Psalms 40:14">14</a> May those who want to harm me feel ashamed and frustrated; let them retreat and be embarrassed who desire my bad luck.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Psalms 40:15">15</a> Let them be left empty because they are ashamed and mock me, saying, &quot;Aha, aha.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Psalms 40:16">16</a> Let everyone who searches for you be happy and joyful in you; let those who love your rescue always say, &quot;God is great.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Psalms 40:17">17</a> I am poor and in need, but God thinks of me; you are my help and my rescuer; please hurry, my God.</p>
