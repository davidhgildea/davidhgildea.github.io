<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 132:1">1</a> God, remember David and all his hardships.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 132:2">2</a> He promised to God and made a vow to the powerful God of Jacob.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 132:3">3</a> I will not enter my home or go to my bed;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 132:4">4</a> I will not let my eyes sleep or my eyelids close.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 132:5">5</a> Until I find a place for God, a home for the powerful God of Jacob.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 132:6">6</a> We heard about it in Ephratah and found it in the forest fields.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 132:7">7</a> We will enter his tents: we will worship at his footrest.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 132:8">8</a> Get up, God, and go to your resting place; you and the chest that shows your power.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 132:9">9</a> May your priests wear goodness, and may your holy people yell happily.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 132:10">10</a> For your servant David&#39;s sake, do not reject your chosen one.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 132:11">11</a> God has truly promised David; he will not break his promise; I will put one of your descendants on your throne.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 132:12">12</a> If your children follow my agreement and the lessons I give them, their children will rule on your throne forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Psalms 132:13">13</a> God has chosen Zion; he wants it as his home.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Psalms 132:14">14</a> This is where I will rest forever; I will live here because I want it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Psalms 132:15">15</a> I will greatly bless her food supply; I will fill her needy with bread.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Psalms 132:16">16</a> I will dress her priests in salvation, and her holy people will loudly celebrate with joy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Psalms 132:17">17</a> I will make David&#39;s power grow there; I have set up a light for my chosen one.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Psalms 132:18">18</a> I will cover his enemies with shame, but his own crown will thrive.</p>
