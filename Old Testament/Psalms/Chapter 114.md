<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 114:1">1</a> When Israel left Egypt, Jacob&#39;s family left a nation with a different language.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 114:2">2</a> Judah was his holy place, and Israel was under his rule.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 114:3">3</a> The sea saw it and ran away; the Jordan River turned back.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 114:4">4</a> The mountains jumped like rams, and the small hills like young sheep.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 114:5">5</a> What was wrong with you, O sea, that you ran away? O Jordan, why did you turn back?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 114:6">6</a> You mountains, that jumped like rams; and you small hills, like lambs?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 114:7">7</a> Shake, earth, at the presence of God, at the presence of the God of Jacob.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 114:8">8</a> God turned the rock into a pool of water, the flint into a flowing spring.</p>
