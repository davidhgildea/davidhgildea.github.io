<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 6:1">1</a> God, do not scold me in your anger or discipline me in your rage.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 6:2">2</a> Be kind to me, God, because I am weak; God, heal me, for my bones are in pain.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 6:3">3</a> I am deeply troubled; but God, how long will it be?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 6:4">4</a> Come back, God, save my life; oh, rescue me because you are merciful.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 6:5">5</a> When we die, we can&#39;t remember you; who will thank you from the grave?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 6:6">6</a> I am tired from my sighing; all night I make my bed wet; I soak my couch with my tears.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 6:7">7</a> My eye is worn out from sorrow; it grows weak because of all my enemies.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 6:8">8</a> Leave me, all you who do wrong; for God has heard my crying.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 6:9">9</a> God has heard my request; God will accept my prayer.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 6:10">10</a> May all my enemies feel embarrassed and very upset; may they turn back and suddenly feel shame.</p>
