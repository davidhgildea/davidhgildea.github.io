<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 28:1">1</a> I will call out to you, God my rock; do not ignore me, or if you stay silent, I will be like those who fall into the grave.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 28:2">2</a> Listen to my pleas for help, when I call to you, when I raise my hands toward your sacred place.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 28:3">3</a> Do not take me away with the wicked, or with those who do wrong, who say kind words to their neighbors while planning evil in their hearts.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 28:4">4</a> Give them what they deserve for what they have done, pay them back for their evil actions, and give them exactly what their hands have worked for.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 28:5">5</a> Because they ignore what God has done and don&#39;t pay attention to his actions, he will ruin them and not help them grow.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 28:6">6</a> Praise God, because he has heard my requests for help.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 28:7">7</a> God is my strength and protector; my heart trusted in him, and he has helped me. So, my heart is very happy; and I will praise him with my song.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 28:8">8</a> God is their strength, and he gives power to his chosen one for salvation.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 28:9">9</a> Help your people and bless what you have given them; take care of them and raise them up always.</p>
