<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 56:1">1</a> God, be kind to me because people want to defeat me; they fight and press me down every day.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 56:2">2</a> My enemies want to defeat me every day; there are many who attack me, O God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 56:3">3</a> When I am scared, I will trust you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 56:4">4</a> I will praise God for his word; I trust in God and will not be afraid of what people can do to me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 56:5">5</a> Every day they twist what I say; all they think about is how to harm me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 56:6">6</a> They come together, they hide, they watch every move I make, waiting to take my life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 56:7">7</a> Can they avoid punishment through wrongdoing? In your anger, bring down the people, God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 56:8">8</a> You know about my wandering. Collect my tears in your bottle. Aren&#39;t they listed in your book?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 56:9">9</a> When I call out to you, my enemies will turn away; I know this because God supports me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 56:10">10</a> I will praise God for his word; I will praise him for his promises.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 56:11">11</a> I trust in God: I won&#39;t be afraid of what people can do to me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 56:12">12</a> I am bound by my promises to you, God; I will give you praise.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Psalms 56:13">13</a> You have saved my life from death; won&#39;t you keep my feet from tripping, so I can walk before God in the light where people live?</p>
