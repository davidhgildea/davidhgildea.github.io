<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 64:1">1</a> Listen to me, God, when I pray; keep me safe from my enemies&#39; fear.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 64:2">2</a> Protect me from the hidden plans of the evil; from the rebellion of those who do wrong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 64:3">3</a> They sharpen their tongues like swords and aim their words like arrows.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 64:4">4</a> They shoot from hiding at the innocent; they shoot suddenly and are not afraid.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 64:5">5</a> They boost each other in a bad plan: they talk about setting traps secretly; they say, Who will see us?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 64:6">6</a> They look for sins; they carry out a thorough search: everyone&#39;s secret thoughts and heart are hidden deep.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 64:7">7</a> God will shoot them with an arrow; they will be quickly wounded.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 64:8">8</a> They will be trapped by their own words, and everyone who sees them will run away.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 64:9">9</a> Everyone will be afraid and will tell about what God has done; they will think carefully about his actions.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 64:10">10</a> Good people will be happy with God, trust in him, and all who are true in heart will celebrate.</p>
