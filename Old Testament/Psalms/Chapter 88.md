<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 88:1">1</a> God, who saves me, I have called out to you day and night.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 88:2">2</a> Let my prayer reach you: listen to my call for help.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 88:3">3</a> My soul is filled with troubles, and my life is close to ending.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 88:4">4</a> I am grouped with those who descend into the pit; I am like a man without strength.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 88:5">5</a> Free among the dead, like those killed that lie in the grave, whom you no longer remember: and they are separated from your care.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 88:6">6</a> You have put me in the deepest pit, in dark places, in the depths.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 88:7">7</a> Your anger weighs heavily on me, and you have hit me with all your waves. Pause.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 88:8">8</a> You have pushed my friends far away from me; you have made me disgusting to them: I am trapped and can&#39;t get out.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 88:9">9</a> My eye cries because I am suffering: God, I call to you every day, I reach out my hands to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 88:10">10</a> Will you show wonders to the dead? Will the dead rise and praise you? Pause.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 88:11">11</a> Can anyone speak of your kindness in death, or of your faithfulness in ruin?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 88:12">12</a> Can your miracles be known in the dark? Can your goodness be known in the land of forgetting?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Psalms 88:13">13</a> I have cried out to you, God, and in the morning my prayer will come before you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Psalms 88:14">14</a> God, why do you reject me? Why do you hide your face from me?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Psalms 88:15">15</a> I have been suffering and close to death since I was young; I am overwhelmed by your fears.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Psalms 88:16">16</a> Your fierce anger has swept over me; your fears have isolated me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Psalms 88:17">17</a> They surrounded me every day like water; they all closed in on me together.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Psalms 88:18">18</a> You have pushed my loved ones and friends far away from me, and made my companions disappear into darkness.</p>
