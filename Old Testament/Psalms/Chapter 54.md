<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 54:1">1</a> God, save me because of your name and decide about me with your power.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 54:2">2</a> Listen to my prayer, God; pay attention to what I say.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 54:3">3</a> Foreigners have attacked me, and bullies chase my life; they do not consider God. Pause.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 54:4">4</a> Look, God helps me; God supports those who keep me safe.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 54:5">5</a> God will pay back my enemies with evil and destroy them because you are truthful.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 54:6">6</a> I will gladly offer sacrifices to you: I will praise your name, God, because it is good.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 54:7">7</a> God has saved me from all my troubles, and I have seen my enemies get what they deserved.</p>
