<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 145:1">1</a> I will praise you, my God, the king; and I will bless your name always and forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 145:2">2</a> I will bless you every day; I will praise your name forever and ever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 145:3">3</a> God is great and deserves much praise; no one can understand how great he is.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 145:4">4</a> One generation will tell the next about your works and talk about your powerful deeds.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 145:5">5</a> I will talk about the glorious honor of your majesty and your amazing works.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 145:6">6</a> People will talk about how powerful your amazing acts are, and I will tell others about your greatness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 145:7">7</a> They will speak often of your great kindness and sing about your fairness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 145:8">8</a> God is kind and caring; patient and very merciful.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 145:9">9</a> God is good to everyone, and his compassion is on all he has made.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 145:10">10</a> Everything you have made will praise you, God; and your holy people will bless you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 145:11">11</a> They will talk about the greatness of your kingdom and discuss your power.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 145:12">12</a> To show people his powerful works and the splendid beauty of his kingdom.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Psalms 145:13">13</a> Your kingdom lasts forever, and your rule continues through all time.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Psalms 145:14">14</a> God supports all who fall and lifts up all who are bent over.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Psalms 145:15">15</a> Everyone looks to you, and you give them their food at the right time.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Psalms 145:16">16</a> You open your hand and satisfy the needs of every living creature.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Psalms 145:17">17</a> God is fair in all he does, and good in all his actions.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Psalms 145:18">18</a> God is close to everyone who calls on him, to all who call on him honestly.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Psalms 145:19">19</a> He will fulfill the wishes of those who respect him; he will listen to their cries and save them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Psalms 145:20">20</a> God protects everyone who loves Him, but He will destroy all the wicked.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Psalms 145:21">21</a> I will praise God with my words; may everyone honor his holy name always and forever.</p>
