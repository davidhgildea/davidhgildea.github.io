<p class="verse" id="v126"><a class="verse-num" href="#v126" aria-label="Psalms 119:126">126</a> It is time for you, God, to act, because they have ignored your law.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v127"><a class="verse-num" href="#v127" aria-label="Psalms 119:127">127</a> So I love your rules more than gold, even the purest gold.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v128"><a class="verse-num" href="#v128" aria-label="Psalms 119:128">128</a> So I consider all your rules about everything to be good; and I dislike every untrue path.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v129"><a class="verse-num" href="#v129" aria-label="Psalms 119:129">129</a> Your teachings are amazing; that&#39;s why I follow them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v130"><a class="verse-num" href="#v130" aria-label="Psalms 119:130">130</a> Your words bring light; they give understanding to the simple.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v131"><a class="verse-num" href="#v131" aria-label="Psalms 119:131">131</a> I opened my mouth and gasped because I desired your commands.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v132"><a class="verse-num" href="#v132" aria-label="Psalms 119:132">132</a> Please look at me and show me kindness, as you usually do for those who love your name.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v133"><a class="verse-num" href="#v133" aria-label="Psalms 119:133">133</a> Guide me by your teachings, and don&#39;t let any wrong control me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v134"><a class="verse-num" href="#v134" aria-label="Psalms 119:134">134</a> Save me from human oppression, and I will follow your rules.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v135"><a class="verse-num" href="#v135" aria-label="Psalms 119:135">135</a> Let your face shine on your servant; and teach me your rules.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v136"><a class="verse-num" href="#v136" aria-label="Psalms 119:136">136</a> Tears flow from my eyes like rivers because people do not follow your law.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v137"><a class="verse-num" href="#v137" aria-label="Psalms 119:137">137</a> You are right, God, and your decisions are fair.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v138"><a class="verse-num" href="#v138" aria-label="Psalms 119:138">138</a> The commands you have given are just and very trustworthy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v139"><a class="verse-num" href="#v139" aria-label="Psalms 119:139">139</a> My passion has overwhelmed me because my enemies have ignored your words.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v140"><a class="verse-num" href="#v140" aria-label="Psalms 119:140">140</a> Your word is very pure, so your servant loves it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v141"><a class="verse-num" href="#v141" aria-label="Psalms 119:141">141</a> I am unimportant and not respected, but I still remember your teachings.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v142"><a class="verse-num" href="#v142" aria-label="Psalms 119:142">142</a> Your goodness lasts forever, and your rules are true.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v143"><a class="verse-num" href="#v143" aria-label="Psalms 119:143">143</a> Problems and pain have grabbed me, but I still delight in following your commands.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v144"><a class="verse-num" href="#v144" aria-label="Psalms 119:144">144</a> Your rules are always right: help me understand them so I can live.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v145"><a class="verse-num" href="#v145" aria-label="Psalms 119:145">145</a> I called out with all my heart; hear me, God: I will follow your rules.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v146"><a class="verse-num" href="#v146" aria-label="Psalms 119:146">146</a> I called to you; rescue me, and I will follow your instructions.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v147"><a class="verse-num" href="#v147" aria-label="Psalms 119:147">147</a> I rose before dawn and cried for help; I put my hope in your word.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v148"><a class="verse-num" href="#v148" aria-label="Psalms 119:148">148</a> I stay awake before the night watches begin so I can think about your words.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v149"><a class="verse-num" href="#v149" aria-label="Psalms 119:149">149</a> Listen to my voice because of your kindness: God, give me life according to your decision.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v150"><a class="verse-num" href="#v150" aria-label="Psalms 119:150">150</a> Those who seek trouble are close by; they are far from your laws.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v151"><a class="verse-num" href="#v151" aria-label="Psalms 119:151">151</a> You are close, God, and all your commands are true.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v152"><a class="verse-num" href="#v152" aria-label="Psalms 119:152">152</a> About your instructions, I have long known that you established them forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v153"><a class="verse-num" href="#v153" aria-label="Psalms 119:153">153</a> See my suffering and rescue me, because I have not forgotten your law.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v154"><a class="verse-num" href="#v154" aria-label="Psalms 119:154">154</a> Defend my case and save me; give me life as you have promised.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v155"><a class="verse-num" href="#v155" aria-label="Psalms 119:155">155</a> God&#39;s rescue is not near the bad people because they do not follow your rules.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v156"><a class="verse-num" href="#v156" aria-label="Psalms 119:156">156</a> Your kindness is great, God; give me life as you have decided.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v157"><a class="verse-num" href="#v157" aria-label="Psalms 119:157">157</a> I have many persecutors and enemies, but I do not turn away from your teachings.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v158"><a class="verse-num" href="#v158" aria-label="Psalms 119:158">158</a> I saw the rule-breakers and was upset because they did not follow your words.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v159"><a class="verse-num" href="#v159" aria-label="Psalms 119:159">159</a> Think about how much I love your rules: give me life, God, because of your kindness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v160"><a class="verse-num" href="#v160" aria-label="Psalms 119:160">160</a> Your word has been true since the start, and all your fair decisions last forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v161"><a class="verse-num" href="#v161" aria-label="Psalms 119:161">161</a> Rulers have attacked me for no reason, but my heart deeply respects your word.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v162"><a class="verse-num" href="#v162" aria-label="Psalms 119:162">162</a> I am happy with your word, like someone who finds a great treasure.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v163"><a class="verse-num" href="#v163" aria-label="Psalms 119:163">163</a> I hate and can&#39;t stand lying; but I love your law.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v164"><a class="verse-num" href="#v164" aria-label="Psalms 119:164">164</a> I praise you seven times a day for your fair decisions.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v165"><a class="verse-num" href="#v165" aria-label="Psalms 119:165">165</a> Those who love your teachings have great peace and are not easily upset.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v166"><a class="verse-num" href="#v166" aria-label="Psalms 119:166">166</a> God, I have hoped for your salvation and followed your commands.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v167"><a class="verse-num" href="#v167" aria-label="Psalms 119:167">167</a> My heart follows your teachings; I love them very much.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v168"><a class="verse-num" href="#v168" aria-label="Psalms 119:168">168</a> I have followed your commands and your teachings: because all my paths are known to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v170"><a class="verse-num" href="#v170" aria-label="Psalms 119:170">170</a> Hear my plea, God: save me as you have promised.</p>
