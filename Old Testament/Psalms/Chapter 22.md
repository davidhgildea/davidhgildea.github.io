<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 22:1">1</a> My God, my God, why have you left me alone? Why are you so far from saving me and from hearing my cries?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 22:2">2</a> God, I call out for help during the day, but you do not hear me; and at night, I don&#39;t stay quiet.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 22:3">3</a> But you are holy, O you who are praised by Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 22:4">4</a> Our ancestors trusted in you; they trusted, and you rescued them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 22:5">5</a> They called to you and were saved; they trusted in you and were not disappointed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 22:6">6</a> I am like a worm, not a man; scorned by everyone and looked down on by people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 22:7">7</a> Everyone who sees me mocks me; they sneer and shake their heads, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 22:8">8</a> He trusted in God to rescue him; let God save him since he takes pleasure in him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 22:9">9</a> You are the one who brought me out of the womb; you made me feel safe as I nursed at my mother&#39;s breasts.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 22:10">10</a> From birth I was placed in your care; you have been my God since my mother carried me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 22:11">11</a> Stay close to me, because trouble is near and no one is here to help.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 22:12">12</a> Many strong bulls have surrounded me; powerful bulls of Bashan have encircled me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Psalms 22:13">13</a> They opened their mouths wide at me, like a hungry, roaring lion.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Psalms 22:14">14</a> I am drained like water, and all my bones are disjointed; my heart is like melted wax within me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Psalms 22:15">15</a> My strength has dried up like a clay pot; my tongue sticks to my gums; and you have brought me to the edge of death.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Psalms 22:16">16</a> Dogs surround me; a group of evil people has trapped me; they have pierced my hands and feet.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Psalms 22:17">17</a> I can count all my bones; they just look and stare at me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Psalms 22:18">18</a> They divide my clothes among themselves and gamble for my outfit.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Psalms 22:19">19</a> But don&#39;t be far from me, God; hurry to help me, my strength.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Psalms 22:20">20</a> Save my life from the sword; rescue me from the dog&#39;s grip.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Psalms 22:21">21</a> Rescue me from the lion&#39;s mouth; You have answered me from the wild oxen&#39;s horns.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Psalms 22:22">22</a> I will tell my brothers your name; I will praise you among the people gathered together.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Psalms 22:23">23</a> You who respect God, praise him; all of you from Jacob&#39;s family, honor him; and respect him, everyone from Israel&#39;s family.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Psalms 22:24">24</a> God has not ignored or hated the suffering of the suffering person; He did not turn away from him, but when he called for help, God listened.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Psalms 22:25">25</a> I will praise you in a large gathering and fulfill my promises in front of those who respect God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Psalms 22:26">26</a> Humble people will eat and be happy; they who look for God will praise him; your heart will live forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Psalms 22:27">27</a> People everywhere will remember and turn to God; all families of nations will worship before you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Psalms 22:28">28</a> The kingdom belongs to God, and he rules over the nations.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Psalms 22:29">29</a> Everyone who is wealthy on earth will eat and worship; those who die will kneel before God, and no one can save their own life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Psalms 22:30">30</a> A child will serve God; they will be known as God&#39;s people for generations.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Psalms 22:31">31</a> They will come and tell a future generation about God&#39;s goodness, because he has done this.</p>
