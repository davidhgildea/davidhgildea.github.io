<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 71:1">1</a> In you, God, I place my trust: may I never be confused.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 71:2">2</a> Save me by your goodness, and help me escape. Listen to me and rescue me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 71:3">3</a> Be my strong home where I can always go; you ordered my rescue because you are my rock and my safe place.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 71:4">4</a> Save me, my God, from the wicked, from the unjust and cruel person.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 71:5">5</a> You are my hope, God; you have been my trust since I was young.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 71:6">6</a> You have supported me since birth; you brought me out of my mother&#39;s body: I will always praise you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 71:7">7</a> I am a surprise to many; but you are my strong shelter.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 71:8">8</a> May my mouth always be full of your praise and honor.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 71:9">9</a> Don&#39;t reject me when I get old; don&#39;t leave me when my strength is gone.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 71:10">10</a> My enemies talk about me; and those who want to hurt me plan together.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 71:11">11</a> They say, &quot;God has left him: chase him and catch him, for no one will save him.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 71:12">12</a> God, don&#39;t be far from me; God, hurry to help me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Psalms 71:13">13</a> Let those who are against me be confused and destroyed; let those who want to harm me be covered with shame and disgrace.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Psalms 71:14">14</a> I will always have hope and will praise you more and more.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Psalms 71:15">15</a> I will always talk about your goodness and your help, because I can&#39;t count them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Psalms 71:16">16</a> I will go with God&#39;s strength; I will talk about your righteousness, yours alone.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Psalms 71:17">17</a> God, you have taught me since I was young, and until now I have told of your wonderful deeds.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Psalms 71:18">18</a> Even now when I am old and have gray hair, God, do not leave me alone until I have told this generation about your strength and shown all those yet to come your power.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Psalms 71:19">19</a> Your goodness, O God, is so great, you have done amazing things: O God, there is no one like you!</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Psalms 71:20">20</a> You, who have shown me great and hard troubles, will make me live again, and will lift me up from the deep places of the earth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Psalms 71:21">21</a> You will make me greater and give me comfort on all sides.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Psalms 71:22">22</a> I will praise you with the lyre for your faithfulness, my God; I will sing to you with the harp, Holy One of Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Psalms 71:23">23</a> I will be very happy as I sing to you; my soul, which you have saved, will rejoice.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Psalms 71:24">24</a> My tongue will speak of your goodness all day long because those who want to harm me are confused and ashamed.</p>
