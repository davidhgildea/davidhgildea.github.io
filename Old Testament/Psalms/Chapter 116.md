<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 116:1">1</a> I love God, because he has heard my voice and my pleas.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 116:2">2</a> Because he listened to me, I will call on him as long as I live.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 116:3">3</a> Death&#39;s sorrows surrounded me, and hell&#39;s pains grabbed me: I encountered trouble and sadness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 116:4">4</a> I called out to God, &quot;Please, God, save me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 116:5">5</a> God is kind and fair; yes, our God is compassionate.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 116:6">6</a> God protects the uncomplicated; I was down, and he aided me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 116:7">7</a> Come back to your peace, my soul; because God has been very good to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 116:8">8</a> You have saved my life from death, my eyes from crying, and my feet from tripping.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 116:9">9</a> I will live a good life before God among the living.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 116:10">10</a> I believed, so I spoke: I was very troubled.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 116:11">11</a> I quickly said, &quot;Everyone lies.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 116:12">12</a> How can I repay God for all the good things he has done for me?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Psalms 116:13">13</a> I will take the cup of rescue and call on the name of God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Psalms 116:14">14</a> I will keep my promises to God now in front of all his people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Psalms 116:15">15</a> God values the death of his faithful ones greatly.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Psalms 116:16">16</a> God, truly I am your servant; I am your servant and the son of your female servant: you have set me free from my chains.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Psalms 116:17">17</a> I will give you a sacrifice of thanks and call on the name of God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Psalms 116:18">18</a> I will keep my promises to God now in front of all his people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Psalms 116:19">19</a> In the courtyards of God&#39;s house, in the center of you, Jerusalem, praise God.</p>
