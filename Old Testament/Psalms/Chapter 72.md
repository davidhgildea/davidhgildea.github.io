<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 72:1">1</a> God, give the king your justice, and your righteousness to the king&#39;s son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 72:2">2</a> He will judge your people fairly and give fair decisions for your poor.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 72:3">3</a> The mountains will give peace to the people, and the small hills will do it by being fair.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 72:4">4</a> He will judge the poor fairly, save the children of the needy, and crush the oppressor.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 72:5">5</a> People will respect you as long as the sun and moon last, for all time to come.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 72:6">6</a> He will come down like rain on cut grass, like showers that soak the earth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 72:7">7</a> During his time, good people will thrive; and there will be plenty of peace as long as the moon lasts.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 72:8">8</a> He will rule from one sea to another, and from the river to the ends of the earth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 72:9">9</a> People living in the wild will kneel before him, and his enemies will lick the ground.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 72:10">10</a> The kings of Tarshish and the islands will bring gifts; the kings of Sheba and Seba will present offerings.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 72:11">11</a> Yes, all kings will bow down to him; all countries will serve him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 72:12">12</a> God will save the needy when they call out; the poor too, and those with no one to help them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Psalms 72:13">13</a> He will help the poor and needy and save the lives of those in need.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Psalms 72:14">14</a> He will save them from lies and harm, and their lives will be valuable to him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Psalms 72:15">15</a> He will live, and he will receive gold from Sheba; people will always pray for him, and he will be praised every day.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Psalms 72:16">16</a> A small amount of grain will grow in the land, even on the mountains; its produce will sway like the trees of Lebanon, and the city people will thrive like the grass on the ground.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Psalms 72:17">17</a> His name will last forever; it will continue as long as the sun shines. People will be blessed through him; all countries will praise him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Psalms 72:18">18</a> Praise be to God, the God of Israel, who alone does wonderful things.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Psalms 72:19">19</a> May his wonderful name be blessed forever; may the whole earth be full of his glory. Amen, Amen.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Psalms 72:20">20</a> David, Jesse&#39;s son, has finished his prayers.</p>
