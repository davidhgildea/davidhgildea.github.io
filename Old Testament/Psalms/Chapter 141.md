<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 141:1">1</a> God, I call to you: hurry to me; listen to my voice when I call to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 141:2">2</a> Let my prayer come to you like incense, and the raising of my hands like the evening offering.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 141:3">3</a> God, guard what I say; protect what I speak.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 141:4">4</a> Don&#39;t let my heart turn to evil things, to do bad deeds with people who do wrong; and don&#39;t let me enjoy their tasty foods.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 141:5">5</a> Let a good person correct me; it will be a kindness. Let them tell me when I’m wrong; it will be like fine oil on my head. And I will still pray for them when they are in trouble.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 141:6">6</a> When their leaders fall on rocky ground, they will listen to my words; for they are pleasant.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 141:7">7</a> Our bones are spread out at the edge of the grave, like wood chopped and split on the ground.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 141:8">8</a> I look to you, God: I trust in you; do not let my soul be left without help.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 141:9">9</a> Protect me from the traps set by others and the tricks of those who do wrong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 141:10">10</a> Let the wicked get caught in their own traps, while I get away safely.</p>
