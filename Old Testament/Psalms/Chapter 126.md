<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 126:1">1</a> When God freed the captives of Zion, we were like those who dream.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 126:2">2</a> Our mouths were full of laughter and our tongues sang with joy. Then the nations said, &quot;God has done great things for them.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 126:3">3</a> God has done great things for us; we are happy about it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 126:4">4</a> Bring us back from being captives, God, like streams in the desert.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 126:5">5</a> Those who plant with tears will harvest with joy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 126:6">6</a> The one who goes out and cries while carrying valuable seeds will surely come back with joy, bringing his harvest with him.</p>
