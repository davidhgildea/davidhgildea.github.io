<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 138:1">1</a> I will praise you with all my heart; I will sing praises to you before other gods.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 138:2">2</a> I will worship facing your holy temple and thank your name for your kindness and truth; because you have made your word greater than your name.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 138:3">3</a> On the day I called out, you answered me and made my soul strong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 138:4">4</a> Every king on earth will praise you, God, when they hear what you say.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 138:5">5</a> Yes, they will sing about God&#39;s paths: for God&#39;s glory is great.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 138:6">6</a> Even though God is great, he cares for the humble, but he keeps his distance from the proud.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 138:7">7</a> Even when I walk through trouble, you will bring me back to life. You will act against my enemies&#39; anger, and your power will save me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 138:8">8</a> God will complete what is important to me; Your kindness, God, lasts forever; do not abandon what Your hands have made.</p>
