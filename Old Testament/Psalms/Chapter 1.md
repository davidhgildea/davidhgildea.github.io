<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 1:1">1</a> Happy is the person who does not follow bad advice, does not hang out with sinners, and does not join those who make fun of others.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 1:2">2</a> He enjoys following God&#39;s law and thinks about it all day and night.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 1:3">3</a> He will be like a tree by the water that grows fruit at the right time; its leaves will not dry out, and everything he does will succeed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 1:4">4</a> The wicked are not like this; they are like the dry husks that the wind blows away.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 1:5">5</a> So, the wicked won&#39;t stand when judged, nor will sinners be with the group of good people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 1:6">6</a> God knows the path of good people, but the way of the bad will disappear.</p>
