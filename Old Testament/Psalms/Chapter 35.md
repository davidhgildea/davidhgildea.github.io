<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 35:1">1</a> God, defend me against those who argue with me; battle those who battle me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 35:2">2</a> Grab your shield and armor and stand up to help me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 35:3">3</a> Get your spear and block the path of those chasing me; tell me, &quot;I am your rescue.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 35:4">4</a> Let those who want to harm me be confused and embarrassed; let those who plan my downfall be turned away and thrown into disarray.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 35:5">5</a> Let them be like dust in the wind and let God&#39;s angel chase them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 35:6">6</a> Let their path be dark and slippery, and let God&#39;s angel chase them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 35:7">7</a> They have set a trap for me without reason; they have dug a pit to capture me even though I did nothing wrong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 35:8">8</a> Let disaster surprise him; and let the trap he set catch him: let him fall into that very disaster.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 35:9">9</a> My soul will be happy in God; it will celebrate His rescue.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 35:10">10</a> Every bone in my body will say, &quot;God, who is like you? You rescue the weak from those too strong for them, the poor and needy from those who rob them.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 35:11">11</a> Lying witnesses stood against me; they accused me of things I didn&#39;t know.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 35:12">12</a> They repaid my good with evil and hurt my spirit.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Psalms 35:13">13</a> But when they were sick, I wore sackcloth, I made my soul humble with fasting, and my prayer stayed with me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Psalms 35:14">14</a> I acted as if he were my friend or brother; I was very sad and bent over, like someone grieving for their mother.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Psalms 35:15">15</a> During my hard times, they were happy and came together. Yes, the worthless people united against me without my knowing; they attacked me nonstop.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Psalms 35:16">16</a> At parties, two-faced mockers angrily bit at me with their teeth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Psalms 35:17">17</a> God, how long will you watch? Save my life from their ruin, my precious one from the lions.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Psalms 35:18">18</a> I will thank you in the large gathering; I will praise you among many people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Psalms 35:19">19</a> Do not let my enemies unfairly be happy over me, nor let those who hate me for no reason mock me with their eyes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Psalms 35:20">20</a> They don&#39;t talk about peace; instead, they plan lies against the peaceful people in the land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Psalms 35:21">21</a> Yes, they opened their mouths wide against me and said, &quot;Aha, aha, we have seen it with our own eyes.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Psalms 35:22">22</a> You have seen this, God: do not stay quiet: God, do not be far from me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Psalms 35:23">23</a> Get up and pay attention to my case, my God and my Lord.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Psalms 35:24">24</a> Judge me, God, by your fairness; don&#39;t let them be happy at my expense.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Psalms 35:25">25</a> Don&#39;t let them think, &quot;This is just what we wanted,&quot; or say, &quot;We have defeated him.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Psalms 35:26">26</a> May those who take joy in my trouble be embarrassed and confused; may those who boast against me be covered in shame and disgrace.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Psalms 35:27">27</a> Let those who support my just cause shout for joy and be happy; may they always say, &quot;May God be praised, who delights in the well-being of his servant.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Psalms 35:28">28</a> My tongue will talk about your goodness and praise you all day long.</p>
