<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 24:1">1</a> The earth belongs to God, and everything in it; the world, and everyone who lives in it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 24:2">2</a> He founded the earth on the seas and set it firm on the waters.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 24:3">3</a> Who will go up to God&#39;s hill? Or who will stand in his sacred place?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 24:4">4</a> The person with clean hands and a pure heart, who has not chased after worthless things or lied under oath.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 24:5">5</a> He will be blessed by God and given righteousness by the God who saves him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 24:6">6</a> These people seek God; they look for your presence, O Jacob. Pause.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 24:7">7</a> Raise your heads, you gates; open up, you ageless doors, so the glorious King can enter.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 24:8">8</a> Who is this glorious king? God, strong and powerful, God mighty in war.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 24:9">9</a> Raise your heads, you gates; lift up, you ancient doors, so the glorious King can enter.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 24:10">10</a> Who is this glorious king? God, the ruler of all, is the glorious king. Pause and think about this.</p>
