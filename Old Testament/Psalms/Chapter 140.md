<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 140:1">1</a> Save me, God, from the bad person; protect me from the violent one.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 140:2">2</a> They plan trouble in their hearts; they always meet to fight.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 140:3">3</a> They have made their tongues sharp like a snake&#39;s; snake venom is under their lips. Pause and think about this.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 140:4">4</a> God, protect me from the wicked; keep me safe from the violent man who plans to make me fall.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 140:5">5</a> The proud have set a trap for me, and ropes; they have laid a net beside the path; they have placed snares for me. Pause and think about that.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 140:6">6</a> I said to God, &quot;You are my God; listen to my pleas for help, O God.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 140:7">7</a> God, the source of my rescue, you have protected me during battle.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 140:8">8</a> Do not give the wicked what they want, God; do not let their evil plans succeed, so they won&#39;t become proud. Pause.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 140:9">9</a> Let the trouble their words cause come back on them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 140:10">10</a> Let hot coals fall on them; let them be thrown into the fire, into deep holes, so they can&#39;t get up again.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 140:11">11</a> Don&#39;t let a person who speaks evil be secure on earth: evil will chase the violent man to bring him down.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 140:12">12</a> I know that God will support the cause of the suffering and the rights of the poor.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Psalms 140:13">13</a> Truly, good people will thank your name; honest people will live with you.</p>
