<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 61:1">1</a> Listen to my cry, God; pay attention to my prayer.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 61:2">2</a> From the farthest part of the earth, I will call to you when I am feeling too weak: take me to the place that is stronger than I am.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 61:3">3</a> You have been a safe place for me and a strong tower against the enemy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 61:4">4</a> I will live in your tent forever: I will find shelter under your wings. Pause.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 61:5">5</a> God, you have heard my promises; you have given me the inheritance of those who respect your name.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 61:6">6</a> You will extend the king&#39;s life, and his years will be like many generations.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 61:7">7</a> He will live with God forever: please make ready kindness and honesty to protect him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 61:8">8</a> I will praise your name forever and keep my promises every day.</p>
