<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 68:1">1</a> May God stand up, may his enemies scatter; may those who hate him run away from him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 68:2">2</a> Like smoke is blown away, blow them away; as wax melts before fire, let the wicked vanish in God&#39;s presence.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 68:3">3</a> Let good people be happy; let them celebrate in front of God; yes, let them be very joyful.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 68:4">4</a> Sing to God, sing praises to His name; praise Him who rides through the skies, who is called JAH, and be happy in His presence.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 68:5">5</a> God is a protector of orphans and a defender of widows in his sacred home.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 68:6">6</a> God places lonely people into families; he frees the chained; but the rebellious live in a barren place.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 68:7">7</a> God, when you went out before your people, when you walked through the desert; pause and think about that.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 68:8">8</a> The earth trembled, the skies poured down rain before God: even Mount Sinai shook before God, the God of Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 68:9">9</a> God, you sent a lot of rain, which strengthened your people when they were tired.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 68:10">10</a> Your people have lived there; God, you have provided for the needy with your goodness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 68:11">11</a> God gave the message: many people spread it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 68:12">12</a> Army rulers ran away quickly; the woman who stayed home shared the loot.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Psalms 68:13">13</a> Even if you lay among the ashes, you will be like a dove with silver wings and golden feathers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Psalms 68:14">14</a> When God spread out kings there, it was as white as snow on Salmon.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Psalms 68:15">15</a> God&#39;s hill is like the hill of Bashan; a tall hill like the hill of Bashan.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Psalms 68:16">16</a> Why do you jump, you tall mountains? This is the mountain where God chooses to live; yes, God will live there forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Psalms 68:17">17</a> God has twenty thousand chariots and many angels; God is with them, as at Sinai, in the sacred place.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Psalms 68:18">18</a> You went up high, you took prisoners: you got gifts for people, even for those against you, so that God could live with them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Psalms 68:19">19</a> God blesses us every day with good things, the God who saves us. Pause and think about this.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Psalms 68:20">20</a> Our God is the God who saves us; and God controls life and death.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Psalms 68:21">21</a> God will hurt the heads of his enemies and the hairy heads of those who keep sinning.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Psalms 68:22">22</a> God said, &quot;I will bring them back from Bashan, I will bring my people back from the depths of the sea.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Psalms 68:23">23</a> So your foot will step in your enemies&#39; blood, and your dogs will lick it too.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Psalms 68:24">24</a> People have seen how you move, O God; they&#39;ve seen how my God, my King, moves in the holy place.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Psalms 68:25">25</a> Singers led the way, musicians followed; young women with drums were among them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Psalms 68:26">26</a> Praise God together, everyone; praise the Lord, O descendants of Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Psalms 68:27">27</a> Benjamin&#39;s group with their leader, Judah&#39;s chiefs and their advisors, Zebulun&#39;s leaders, and Naphtali&#39;s leaders are there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Psalms 68:28">28</a> Your God has ordered your power: make us stronger, God, for what you have done for us.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Psalms 68:29">29</a> Because of your temple in Jerusalem, kings will bring you gifts.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Psalms 68:30">30</a> Tell off the group of soldiers, the many strong ones, and the young ones of the nations, until they all give in with silver gifts: send away the people who enjoy fighting.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Psalms 68:31">31</a> Leaders will come from Egypt; Ethiopia will quickly reach out to God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Psalms 68:32">32</a> Sing to God, you kingdoms of the earth; sing praises to God; pause and think.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Psalms 68:33">33</a> To the one who rides on the highest heavens, which existed long ago, listen, he sends out his voice, a powerful voice.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Psalms 68:34">34</a> Give God credit for being strong: He is most excellent above Israel, and his power is in the skies.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Psalms 68:35">35</a> God, you are awesome from your holy places. The God of Israel gives strength and power to his people. God is blessed.</p>
