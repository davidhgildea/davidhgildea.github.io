<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 18:1">1</a> I will love you, God, my strength.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 18:2">2</a> God is my rock, my fortress, and my rescuer; my God gives me strength and I trust in Him; He protects me, saves me, and is like a high tower to me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 18:3">3</a> I will call on God, who deserves praise: so I will be saved from my enemies.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 18:4">4</a> Death&#39;s pains surrounded me, and the rush of wicked people scared me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 18:5">5</a> Hell&#39;s pains surrounded me; death&#39;s traps stopped me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 18:6">6</a> In my trouble, I called to God and shouted for help. He heard my voice from his home, and my call reached his ears.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 18:7">7</a> The earth shook and quaked; the hill bases moved and shook because God was angry.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 18:8">8</a> Smoke went up from his nose, and fire from his mouth burned everything; it lit up coals.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 18:9">9</a> He bent the sky down and came down; darkness was under his feet.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 18:10">10</a> He rode on a cherub and flew; he soared on the wind&#39;s wings.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 18:11">11</a> He made darkness his hiding place; around him were dark waters and heavy clouds in the sky.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 18:12">12</a> As the light shone in front of him, his dark clouds moved away, with hailstones and burning coals.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Psalms 18:13">13</a> God thundered in the sky, and the Most High shouted; there were hailstones and coals of fire.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Psalms 18:14">14</a> Yes, God shot his arrows and spread them out; he sent lightning and confused them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Psalms 18:15">15</a> The water paths were shown, and the world&#39;s bases were uncovered when you, God, scolded, at the puff of your breath.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Psalms 18:16">16</a> From above, God sent for me; he rescued me from deep waters.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Psalms 18:17">17</a> God saved me from my powerful enemy and from those who hated me, because they were too strong for me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Psalms 18:18">18</a> They stopped me on the day of my trouble, but God was my support.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Psalms 18:19">19</a> God led me out into a wide open space; he saved me because he was pleased with me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Psalms 18:20">20</a> God rewarded me for doing right; he paid me back because my hands were clean.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Psalms 18:21">21</a> I have followed God&#39;s paths and not turned away from my God to do wrong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Psalms 18:22">22</a> I kept all his rules in mind, and I did not ignore his teachings.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Psalms 18:23">23</a> I was also honest before God, and I kept myself from doing wrong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Psalms 18:24">24</a> So God rewarded me because of my good behavior and because I kept my hands clean in his view.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Psalms 18:25">25</a> You are kind to those who are kind, and you are honest to those who are honest.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Psalms 18:26">26</a> You are pure with the pure, and you act tricky with the tricky.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Psalms 18:27">27</a> You will save the suffering people but will humble the proud.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Psalms 18:28">28</a> You will light my lamp; God will brighten my darkness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Psalms 18:29">29</a> With your help, I have charged through an army; and with my God, I have jumped over a wall.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Psalms 18:30">30</a> God&#39;s way is perfect. His words are tested and true. He protects all who trust in Him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Psalms 18:31">31</a> Who is God besides the Lord? Or who is a stronghold besides our God?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Psalms 18:32">32</a> God gives me strength and makes my path perfect.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Psalms 18:33">33</a> God makes my feet as steady as a deer&#39;s, and places me on high ground.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Psalms 18:34">34</a> God teaches my hands to fight, so that I can break a steel bow with my arms.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Psalms 18:35">35</a> You have given me the shield of your salvation; your right hand has supported me, and your kindness has made me strong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Psalms 18:36">36</a> You have made my path wide so I didn&#39;t fall.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Psalms 18:37">37</a> I chased my enemies and caught them. I did not stop until they were defeated.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Psalms 18:38">38</a> I have hurt them so they can&#39;t get up; they have fallen beneath my feet.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="Psalms 18:39">39</a> You have given me strength for the fight; you have made those who attacked me surrender to me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="Psalms 18:40">40</a> You have also let me defeat my enemies; so I can destroy those who hate me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="Psalms 18:41">41</a> They called for help, but no one saved them; even when they called to God, he did not answer.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="Psalms 18:42">42</a> I crushed them into dust blown away by the wind; I threw them out like mud in the streets.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v43"><a class="verse-num" href="#v43" aria-label="Psalms 18:43">43</a> You have rescued me from fighting among the people; you have made me the leader of nations; people I did not know will now serve me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v44"><a class="verse-num" href="#v44" aria-label="Psalms 18:44">44</a> When they hear about me, they will obey me: foreigners will yield to me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v45"><a class="verse-num" href="#v45" aria-label="Psalms 18:45">45</a> Foreigners will grow weak and be scared from their hidden spots.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v46"><a class="verse-num" href="#v46" aria-label="Psalms 18:46">46</a> God lives; my protector is blessed; let the God who saves me be honored.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v47"><a class="verse-num" href="#v47" aria-label="Psalms 18:47">47</a> God avenges me and makes people submit to me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v48"><a class="verse-num" href="#v48" aria-label="Psalms 18:48">48</a> God saves me from my enemies; you raise me above my attackers; you save me from violent people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v49"><a class="verse-num" href="#v49" aria-label="Psalms 18:49">49</a> So I will thank you, God, among the nations, and sing praises to your name.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v50"><a class="verse-num" href="#v50" aria-label="Psalms 18:50">50</a> He gives great rescue to his king and shows kindness to his chosen one, to David and his descendants forever.</p>
