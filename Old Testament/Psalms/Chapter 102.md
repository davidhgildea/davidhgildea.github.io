<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 102:1">1</a> Listen to my prayer, God, and hear my call for help.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 102:2">2</a> Do not hide from me when I am in trouble; listen to me: answer quickly when I call you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 102:3">3</a> My days vanish like smoke, and my bones are as burned out as a fireplace.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 102:4">4</a> My heart is struck down and dried up like grass; I even forget to eat my food.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 102:5">5</a> Because of my crying, my bones stick to my skin.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 102:6">6</a> I am like a pelican in the wild: I am like an owl in the desert.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 102:7">7</a> I watch and am like a lone sparrow on the rooftop.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 102:8">8</a> My enemies insult me all day; those who hate me are always plotting against me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 102:9">9</a> I have eaten ashes as if they were bread and mixed my drink with tears.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 102:10">10</a> Because you were angry and upset, you picked me up and then threw me down.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 102:11">11</a> My days are like a fading shadow; I am wilting like grass.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 102:12">12</a> But you, God, will last forever; and you will be remembered by all generations.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Psalms 102:13">13</a> You will stand up and be kind to Zion because it is time to help her; yes, the exact time has arrived.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Psalms 102:14">14</a> Your servants enjoy her stones and care for her dust.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Psalms 102:15">15</a> So the nations will respect God&#39;s name, and all the earth&#39;s rulers your greatness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Psalms 102:16">16</a> When God rebuilds Zion, he will show his greatness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Psalms 102:17">17</a> God will pay attention to the poor&#39;s prayer and won&#39;t reject it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Psalms 102:18">18</a> This will be recorded for future generations: and the people yet to be made will praise God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Psalms 102:19">19</a> God has looked down from his holy place above; from heaven he watched over the earth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Psalms 102:20">20</a> To listen to the cries of the prisoners; to free those who are set to die;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Psalms 102:21">21</a> To tell of God&#39;s name in Zion, and his praise in Jerusalem;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Psalms 102:22">22</a> When the people and the kingdoms come together to serve God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Psalms 102:23">23</a> God made me weaker on my journey; He cut my life shorter.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Psalms 102:24">24</a> I said, O my God, do not take me away when my life is half done; your years last through all generations.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Psalms 102:25">25</a> Long ago, you created the earth&#39;s foundation, and the skies are made by your hands.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Psalms 102:26">26</a> They will be destroyed, but you will last. Yes, they will all wear out like clothes; you will change them like an outfit, and they will be changed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Psalms 102:27">27</a> You remain the same, and your years will never end.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Psalms 102:28">28</a> Your servants&#39; children will live on, and their descendants will be secure in your presence.</p>
