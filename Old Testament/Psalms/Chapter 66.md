<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 66:1">1</a> Celebrate happily before God, all you lands.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 66:2">2</a> Sing to honor God&#39;s name; make your praises of him wonderful.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 66:3">3</a> Tell God, &quot;How awesome are your deeds! Because of your great power, your enemies will come to you in submission.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 66:4">4</a> Everyone on earth will worship you and sing to you; they will sing about your name. Pause.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 66:5">5</a> Come and see what God has done; he is awesome in his actions among people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 66:6">6</a> He turned the sea into dry land; they walked through the river on foot; there we were happy in him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 66:7">7</a> He rules forever by his power; his eyes watch the nations: rebellious people should not lift themselves up. Pause.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 66:8">8</a> Praise our God, everyone, and let his praise be heard.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 66:9">9</a> God keeps us alive and does not let us stumble.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 66:10">10</a> God, you have tested us; you have refined us like silver.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 66:11">11</a> You caught us in a trap; you put a heavy burden on our backs.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 66:12">12</a> You let people walk over us; we went through fire and water, but you brought us to a place of abundance.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Psalms 66:13">13</a> I will enter your house with offerings; I will fulfill my promises to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Psalms 66:14">14</a> What I said with my lips and spoke with my mouth when I was in trouble.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Psalms 66:15">15</a> I will give you burnt offerings of young animals and the scent of rams; I will present bulls and goats. Pause and think about that.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Psalms 66:16">16</a> Come and listen, all who respect God, and I will tell what he did for my life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Psalms 66:17">17</a> I called out to God with my mouth, and I praised him with my tongue.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Psalms 66:18">18</a> If I keep sin in my heart, God will not listen to me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Psalms 66:19">19</a> God truly heard me; he listened to my prayer.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Psalms 66:20">20</a> Praise God, who did not ignore my prayer or withdraw his kindness from me.</p>
