<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 31:1">1</a> In you, God, I trust; let me never be embarrassed: save me because you are good.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 31:2">2</a> Lean down your ear to me; rescue me quickly: be my solid rock, a safe place to protect me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 31:3">3</a> You are my rock and safe place; so for your name&#39;s sake, lead and guide me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 31:4">4</a> Rescue me from the trap secretly set for me; for you are my power.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 31:5">5</a> I give my spirit into your care; you have saved me, God of truth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 31:6">6</a> I dislike those who follow false beliefs, but I trust in God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 31:7">7</a> I will be happy and celebrate your kindness because you have seen my problems; you have understood my struggles.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 31:8">8</a> You haven&#39;t trapped me with my enemy; you&#39;ve given me space to move freely.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 31:9">9</a> Please be kind to me, God, because I am in trouble; my eyes are weak from sadness, and so are my heart and my stomach.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 31:10">10</a> I am worn out with sadness, and I sigh year after year. My strength is weak because of my wrongs, and my bones are wasting away.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 31:11">11</a> All my enemies insulted me, especially my neighbors, and my friends were afraid of me: people who saw me outside ran away from me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 31:12">12</a> People have forgotten me like someone dead and gone; I am like a shattered pot.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Psalms 31:13">13</a> I have heard many people talk badly about me; I was surrounded by fear. While they planned together against me, they aimed to end my life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Psalms 31:14">14</a> I trusted in you, God: I said, You are my God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Psalms 31:15">15</a> My life is in your hands; save me from my enemies and those who chase me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Psalms 31:16">16</a> Let your face shine on your servant; save me because of your mercy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Psalms 31:17">17</a> God, don&#39;t let me be ashamed because I have called on you. Make the wicked be ashamed and silent in death.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Psalms 31:18">18</a> Silence the lying lips that say hurtful things with pride and scorn against good people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Psalms 31:19">19</a> Oh how wonderful is your kindness, which you have set aside for those who respect you; which you have made for those who rely on you in front of all people!</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Psalms 31:20">20</a> You will hide them in the secret of your presence from human pride; you will keep them safely in a shelter from the conflict of speech.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Psalms 31:21">21</a> God is blessed because he has shown me his wonderful kindness in a strong city.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Psalms 31:22">22</a> In my rush, I thought I was lost to you, but you heard my call for help when I cried out to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Psalms 31:23">23</a> Love God, all his holy people, because God protects those who are loyal and gives many blessings to those who act with pride.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Psalms 31:24">24</a> Have courage, and God will make your heart strong, all who hope in God.</p>
