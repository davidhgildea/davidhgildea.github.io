<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 30:1">1</a> I will praise you, God, because you have lifted me up and have not let my enemies be happy about my troubles.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 30:2">2</a> God, I called out to you and you healed me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 30:3">3</a> God, you have lifted my soul from the grave; you have kept me alive so I wouldn&#39;t fall into the pit.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 30:4">4</a> Sing to God, all his holy people, and thank him when you remember his goodness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 30:5">5</a> God&#39;s anger lasts only a moment, but his favor brings life; though sadness lasts through the night, happiness comes with the morning.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 30:6">6</a> When I was doing well, I thought I would always be secure.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 30:7">7</a> God, because of your kindness, you made my position strong: when you turned away, I was upset.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 30:8">8</a> I cried to you, God; and to God I pleaded for help.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 30:9">9</a> What good comes from my death if I go down to the grave? Can dust praise you or tell of your truth?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 30:10">10</a> Listen, God, and show me kindness; God, be my helper.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 30:11">11</a> You have changed my sadness into dancing; you took away my mourning clothes and dressed me with joy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 30:12">12</a> So I can sing about your greatness and always praise you. God, I will thank you forever.</p>
