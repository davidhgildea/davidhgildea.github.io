<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 90:1">1</a> God, you have been our home through all the ages.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 90:2">2</a> Before the mountains existed, or before you made the earth and the world, from forever in the past to forever in the future, you are God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 90:3">3</a> You turn people back to dust and tell them, &quot;Come back, everyone.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 90:4">4</a> To you, a thousand years are like yesterday when it is over, or like a few hours in the night.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 90:5">5</a> You sweep them away like a flood; they are like sleep: in the morning they are like growing grass.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 90:6">6</a> In the morning it blooms and grows; in the evening it is chopped down and dries up.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 90:7">7</a> We are worn out by your anger and overwhelmed by your fury.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 90:8">8</a> You have put our wrongdoings in front of you, our hidden sins in the light of your presence.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 90:9">9</a> All our days vanish in your anger; we live our years like a story that&#39;s told.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 90:10">10</a> Our lives last about 70 years or, if we are strong, 80 years. But even the best of these years are full of hard work and pain. Soon they pass, and we are gone.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 90:11">11</a> Who understands how powerful your anger is? Your wrath matches the fear you deserve.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 90:12">12</a> Help us to count our days so we learn to grow wiser.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Psalms 90:13">13</a> Come back, God, how much longer? Change your mind about your servants.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Psalms 90:14">14</a> Please let us experience your kindness early, so we can be happy and joyful all our lives.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Psalms 90:15">15</a> Make us happy for as long as the times you let us have troubles, and for as many years as we have known sadness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Psalms 90:16">16</a> Show your work to your servants and your greatness to their children.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Psalms 90:17">17</a> May God&#39;s beauty be with us: make the work of our hands strong; yes, make our work strong.</p>
