<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 118:1">1</a> Give thanks to God; for he is good: his kindness lasts forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 118:2">2</a> Let Israel say now, God&#39;s kindness lasts forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 118:3">3</a> Let Aaron&#39;s family now say, God&#39;s kindness lasts forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 118:4">4</a> Let those who respect God say, his kindness lasts forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 118:5">5</a> I called to God when I was in trouble; God answered me and put me in a spacious place.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 118:6">6</a> God is with me; I will not be afraid: what can anyone do to me?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 118:7">7</a> God supports me along with those who help me; therefore, I will see my wishes fulfilled against those who hate me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 118:8">8</a> It is better to trust in God than to rely on people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 118:9">9</a> It is better to trust in God than to rely on leaders.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 118:10">10</a> All countries surrounded me, but with God&#39;s help, I will defeat them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 118:11">11</a> They surrounded me; yes, they surrounded me: but in God&#39;s name, I will defeat them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 118:12">12</a> They surrounded me like bees; they were put out like a thorn fire: because in the name of God I will defeat them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Psalms 118:13">13</a> You pushed me hard to make me fall, but God helped me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Psalms 118:14">14</a> God is my strength and song, and has become my salvation.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Psalms 118:15">15</a> People are celebrating and being saved in the homes of the good; God&#39;s powerful hand does great things.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Psalms 118:16">16</a> God&#39;s right hand is lifted high; God&#39;s right hand does mighty things.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Psalms 118:17">17</a> I will not die, but live, and tell of what God has done.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Psalms 118:18">18</a> God has punished me a lot, but he hasn&#39;t let me die.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Psalms 118:19">19</a> Open the right way for me: I will enter and thank God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Psalms 118:20">20</a> This gate belongs to God, and the good people will go in through it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Psalms 118:21">21</a> I will praise you because you have heard me and have become my salvation.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Psalms 118:22">22</a> The stone that the builders rejected has become the most important cornerstone.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Psalms 118:23">23</a> This is what God has done; it is wonderful to us.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Psalms 118:24">24</a> This is the day that God has made; we will celebrate and be happy in it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Psalms 118:25">25</a> Please save us now, I ask you, God; God, I ask you, bring us success now.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Psalms 118:26">26</a> Happy is the one who comes in God&#39;s name: we have given you blessings from God&#39;s house.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Psalms 118:27">27</a> God is Lord, who has given us light; tie the offering with ropes, all the way to the altar&#39;s corners.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Psalms 118:28">28</a> You are my God, and I will praise you; you are my God, I will lift you up.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Psalms 118:29">29</a> Give thanks to God, for he is good; his kindness lasts forever.</p>
