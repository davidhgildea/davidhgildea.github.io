<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 43:1">1</a> God, judge me and fight for me against a nation without godliness; save me from the lying and unfair person.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 43:2">2</a> You are the God of my strength; why have you rejected me? Why must I go around sad because my enemies are oppressing me?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 43:3">3</a> God, send out your light and your truth to guide me; may they lead me to your holy mountain and to the places where you live.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 43:4">4</a> Then I will go to God&#39;s altar, to God who is my great joy; and with the harp, I will praise you, O my God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 43:5">5</a> Why are you sad, my soul? And why are you troubled inside me? Put your hope in God: for I will still praise him, who makes me well, and is my God.</p>
