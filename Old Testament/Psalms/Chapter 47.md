<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 47:1">1</a> Clap your hands, everyone; shout to God with happy voices.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 47:2">2</a> God is supreme and awesome; he is a mighty ruler over the whole earth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 47:3">3</a> He will bring the people under our control, and the nations under our feet.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 47:4">4</a> God will pick our land for us, the best land of Jacob whom He loved. Pause.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 47:5">5</a> God has ascended with cheers; God with the blast of a trumpet.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 47:6">6</a> Praise God, praise him; sing to our King, praise him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 47:7">7</a> God is the king of the whole earth: sing praises with insight.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 47:8">8</a> God rules over the nations; God sits on His holy throne.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 47:9">9</a> The leaders of the nations come together, including the people who worship the God of Abraham: all the defenders of the world are under God&#39;s power: He is highly honored.</p>
