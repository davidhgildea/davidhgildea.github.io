<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 124:1">1</a> If God hadn&#39;t been with us, let Israel say so now.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 124:2">2</a> If God had not been with us when people attacked us:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 124:3">3</a> Then they would have swallowed us alive, when their anger flared up against us.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 124:4">4</a> Then the waters would have drowned us, the stream would have swept over us.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 124:5">5</a> Then the high waters would have swept over us.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 124:6">6</a> Praise God, who has not let us become victims to their teeth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 124:7">7</a> Our soul has escaped like a bird from the hunter&#39;s trap; the trap is broken, and we have escaped.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 124:8">8</a> Our help comes from God, who created the sky and the earth.</p>
