<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 69:1">1</a> Help me, God; the waters have come up to my soul.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 69:2">2</a> I am sinking in deep mud where I can&#39;t stand; I&#39;ve come into deep waters where the floods sweep over me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 69:3">3</a> I am tired from crying; my throat is dry; my eyes are weak as I wait for my God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 69:4">4</a> Those who hate me for no reason outnumber the hairs on my head; those who attack me without cause are strong. Yet I gave back what I didn&#39;t steal.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 69:5">5</a> God, you know my foolishness; my sins are not hidden from you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 69:6">6</a> God, don&#39;t let those who wait for you be embarrassed because of me. Don&#39;t let those who look for you be confused because of me, God of Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 69:7">7</a> I have faced insults and shame for you; my face is hidden by disgrace.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 69:8">8</a> I have become a stranger to my brothers, and a foreigner to my mother&#39;s children.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 69:9">9</a> My passion for your house has consumed me; the insults aimed at you have fallen on me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 69:10">10</a> When I cried and disciplined myself with fasting, it became my shame.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 69:11">11</a> I also made sackcloth my clothing, and I turned into a saying among them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 69:12">12</a> Those who sit at the gate talk about me; and I am the song of drunk people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Psalms 69:13">13</a> As for me, I pray to you, God, at the right time: O God, because of your great mercy, listen to me and save me with your faithful help.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Psalms 69:14">14</a> Save me from the mud and don&#39;t let me drown; rescue me from those who hate me and from the deep waters.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Psalms 69:15">15</a> Don&#39;t let the floodwaters overwhelm me, nor let the depths swallow me, and don&#39;t let the pit close over me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Psalms 69:16">16</a> Listen to me, God, because your kindness is good; turn to me because you are very merciful.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Psalms 69:17">17</a> Do not hide from me, your servant; I am in trouble: listen to me quickly.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Psalms 69:18">18</a> Come close to my soul and save it; rescue me because of my enemies.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Psalms 69:19">19</a> You know of my disgrace, my shame, and my dishonor: all my enemies are in front of you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Psalms 69:20">20</a> Insults have broken my heart; I am very sad. I hoped for someone to show sympathy, but nobody did; I looked for comforters, but found none.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Psalms 69:21">21</a> They also gave me bitter food to eat; and when I was thirsty, they gave me vinegar to drink.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Psalms 69:22">22</a> May their table turn into a trap before them, and let what should have been for their good become a snare.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Psalms 69:23">23</a> Make their eyes so dark that they cannot see; and keep their backs always shaking.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Psalms 69:24">24</a> Pour out your anger on them, and let your fierce anger catch them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Psalms 69:25">25</a> Let their home be empty; and let no one live in their tents.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Psalms 69:26">26</a> They chase after the one you have struck, and they talk to hurt those you have wounded.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Psalms 69:27">27</a> Add wrong upon their wrongs, and don&#39;t let them share in your goodness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Psalms 69:28">28</a> Remove them from the book of life, and do not list them with the good people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Psalms 69:29">29</a> I am poor and sad: God, let your salvation lift me up.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Psalms 69:30">30</a> I will praise God with a song and honor him with thanks.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Psalms 69:31">31</a> This will make God happier than a cow or a bull with horns and hooves.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Psalms 69:32">32</a> The humble will see this and be happy, and the hearts of those who seek God will be filled with life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Psalms 69:33">33</a> God hears the poor and does not scorn his captives.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Psalms 69:34">34</a> Let the sky and earth praise God, the seas, and all that moves in them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Psalms 69:35">35</a> God will rescue Zion and rebuild the towns of Judah so people can live there and own it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Psalms 69:36">36</a> His servants&#39; descendants will inherit the land, and those who love his name will live there.</p>
