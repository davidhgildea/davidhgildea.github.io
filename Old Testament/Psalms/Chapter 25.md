<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 25:1">1</a> To you, God, I lift up my soul.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 25:2">2</a> My God, I trust in you; don&#39;t let me be embarrassed, don&#39;t let my enemies win over me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 25:3">3</a> Yes, let no one who looks to you be embarrassed; let those who sin without reason feel shame.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 25:4">4</a> Show me your ways, God; teach me your paths.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 25:5">5</a> Guide me in your truth and teach me, for you are the God who saves me; I wait for you all day long.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 25:6">6</a> God, remember your tender mercy and love, for they have always been.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 25:7">7</a> Don&#39;t remember the wrongs I did when I was young, or my mistakes. Please, for your goodness, remember me with your mercy, God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 25:8">8</a> God is good and fair, so he shows sinners the right path.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 25:9">9</a> God will guide the gentle in making fair decisions; and God will teach them his path.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 25:10">10</a> All of God&#39;s ways are kind and true for those who follow his agreement and his teachings.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 25:11">11</a> For your name&#39;s sake, God, forgive my sin; for it is very big.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 25:12">12</a> Who is the man that respects God? God will show him the path to choose.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Psalms 25:13">13</a> He will live in peace, and his children will inherit the land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Psalms 25:14">14</a> God shares His secrets with those who respect Him; He will show them His agreement.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Psalms 25:15">15</a> I always look to God; He will free my feet from the trap.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Psalms 25:16">16</a> Turn to me and be kind to me; I am lonely and in trouble.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Psalms 25:17">17</a> My heart&#39;s troubles have grown; please rescue me from my distress.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Psalms 25:18">18</a> See my suffering and my pain; and forgive all my wrongs.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Psalms 25:19">19</a> Think about my enemies; they are numerous; and they hate me fiercely.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Psalms 25:20">20</a> Protect my life, and save me; do not let me be disappointed, because I trust in you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Psalms 25:21">21</a> Let honesty and goodness protect me; I wait for you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Psalms 25:22">22</a> Save Israel, God, from all his problems.</p>
