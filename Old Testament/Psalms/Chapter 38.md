<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 38:1">1</a> God, don&#39;t scold me in your anger or discipline me in your rage.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 38:2">2</a> Your arrows are stuck in me, and your hand presses on me hard.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 38:3">3</a> My body is not healthy because of your anger; my bones are not at peace because of my sin.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 38:4">4</a> My wrongdoings overwhelm me; they&#39;re like a heavy burden, too much for me to carry.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 38:5">5</a> My wounds smell bad and are infected because I acted foolishly.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 38:6">6</a> I am upset; I am bent over a lot; I spend the whole day sad.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 38:7">7</a> My body is full of a terrible sickness, and my flesh is not healthy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 38:8">8</a> I am weak and very hurt; I have cried out because my heart is troubled.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 38:9">9</a> God, you know all I want; you hear my sighs.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 38:10">10</a> My heart beats fast, my strength is leaving me; even the light in my eyes is gone.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 38:11">11</a> My loved ones and friends keep their distance from my pain; and my relatives stand far away.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 38:12">12</a> Those who want to kill me set traps, and those who want to harm me talk about hurting me and plan lies all day.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Psalms 38:13">13</a> But I was like someone who cannot hear, and like someone who cannot speak, I did not open my mouth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Psalms 38:14">14</a> I was like someone who doesn&#39;t hear and doesn&#39;t speak any criticisms.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Psalms 38:15">15</a> I put my hope in you, God, and I know you will listen, my God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Psalms 38:16">16</a> I said, &quot;Listen to me so that they won&#39;t celebrate if I stumble and use it against me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Psalms 38:17">17</a> I am about to fall, and my sadness is always with me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Psalms 38:18">18</a> I will admit my wrongs; I will be sorry for my sin.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Psalms 38:19">19</a> My enemies are active and powerful, and those who hate me for no reason have become many.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Psalms 38:20">20</a> Those who repay good with evil are against me, because I do what is right.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Psalms 38:21">21</a> Do not leave me, God; my God, please stay close to me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Psalms 38:22">22</a> Hurry to help me, God my savior.</p>
