<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 10:1">1</a> Why do you stand far away, God? Why do you hide yourself in times of trouble?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 10:2">2</a> In his pride, the wicked chases the poor: may they get caught in the traps they&#39;ve set.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 10:3">3</a> The wicked brags about what they want and praises greedy people, who God hates.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 10:4">4</a> Bad people, because of their proud faces, will not look for God. God is not in any of their thoughts.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 10:5">5</a> His ways are always harsh; your decisions are too high for him to see: he sneers at all his enemies.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 10:6">6</a> He thinks to himself, I won&#39;t be shaken; I&#39;ll never have trouble.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 10:7">7</a> His mouth is full of curses, lies, and trickery; under his tongue is trouble and nonsense.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 10:8">8</a> He sits in the hidden spots of the towns, secretly killing the innocent; his eyes are sneakingly watching the poor.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 10:9">9</a> He hides quietly like a lion in its den, waiting to trap the poor; he catches the poor by pulling them into his net.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 10:10">10</a> He bends down and acts humble so that the poor may be defeated by his powerful followers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 10:11">11</a> He thinks to himself, &quot;God has forgotten; He hides His face and will never see it.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 10:12">12</a> Get up, God; lift your hand. Don&#39;t forget the humble.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Psalms 10:13">13</a> Why does the wicked disrespect God? He thinks to himself, You won&#39;t hold him accountable.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Psalms 10:14">14</a> You have seen it; for you watch trouble and spite, to repay it with your hand: the poor trust in you; you are the helper of the orphans.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Psalms 10:15">15</a> Break the power of the wicked and the wrongdoer; search for their evil until you find none.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Psalms 10:16">16</a> God is King forever and ever; the nations have vanished from his land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Psalms 10:17">17</a> God, you have heard the desires of the humble: you will make their hearts ready, you will listen to them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Psalms 10:18">18</a> To make fair decisions for orphans and those suffering, so that no person can bully them again.</p>
