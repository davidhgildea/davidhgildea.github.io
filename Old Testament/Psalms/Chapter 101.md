<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 101:1">1</a> I will sing about love and fairness to you, O God, I will sing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 101:2">2</a> I will act wisely in a good way. Oh, when will you come to me? I will live in my house with a pure heart.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 101:3">3</a> I will not look at evil things; I hate the actions of those who stray; they will not stick to me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 101:4">4</a> I will stay away from deceitful people; I will not associate with the wicked.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 101:5">5</a> Anyone who secretly talks badly about their neighbor, I will remove; I will not tolerate anyone with arrogant eyes and a proud heart.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 101:6">6</a> I will watch over the loyal people of the land, so they can live with me; anyone who lives a blameless life will serve me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 101:7">7</a> Anyone who cheats will not live in my house; a liar will not stay near me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 101:8">8</a> I will quickly get rid of all the bad people in the land to remove all those who do evil from God&#39;s city.</p>
