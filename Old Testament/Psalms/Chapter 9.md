<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 9:1">1</a> I will praise you, God, with all my heart; I will tell everyone about your amazing deeds.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 9:2">2</a> I will be happy and celebrate in you; I will sing praises to your name, O Most High.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 9:3">3</a> When my enemies turn back, they will stumble and die in your presence.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 9:4">4</a> You have supported my right and my cause; you sat on the throne making fair judgments.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 9:5">5</a> You have scolded the nations, you have destroyed the wicked, you have erased their name forever and ever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 9:6">6</a> Enemy, your destructions are finished forever: you have ruined cities; their remembrance is gone with them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 9:7">7</a> God will last forever; he has set up his throne for judging.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 9:8">8</a> He will judge the world fairly and make just decisions for the people with honesty.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 9:9">9</a> God will also be a safe place for the oppressed, a safe place in times of trouble.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 9:10">10</a> Those who know your name will trust in you, God, because you have never abandoned those who look for you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 9:11">11</a> Sing praises to God, who lives in Zion; tell everyone about his actions.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 9:12">12</a> When God looks into murders, he remembers the victims: he does not ignore the cries of the humble.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Psalms 9:13">13</a> God, be kind to me. Look at the troubles I have from those who hate me, you who save me from near death.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Psalms 9:14">14</a> I want to tell all your praise at the gates of Zion&#39;s city; I will be happy because you save me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Psalms 9:15">15</a> The nations have fallen into the pit they dug; their own feet are caught in the net they hid.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Psalms 9:16">16</a> God is known by the justice He gives; the evil are caught by what they do. Reflect on this. Pause.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Psalms 9:17">17</a> Evil people will go to a place of punishment, and so will every nation that forgets God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Psalms 9:18">18</a> The needy will not always be forgotten, and the hopes of the poor will never be lost.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Psalms 9:19">19</a> Get up, God; don&#39;t let people win; judge the nations in your presence.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Psalms 9:20">20</a> Make them afraid, God, so the nations will realize they are only human. Pause.</p>
