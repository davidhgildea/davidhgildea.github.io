<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 26:1">1</a> Judge me, God; for I have lived with honesty. I have also trusted in God; so I will not fall.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 26:2">2</a> Check me, God, and test me; see what I really want and what&#39;s in my heart.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 26:3">3</a> Your kindness is always in my sight, and I live by your truth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 26:4">4</a> I haven&#39;t spent time with worthless people, nor will I join those who pretend to be what they are not.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 26:5">5</a> I hate the group of wrongdoers and will not sit with the wicked.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 26:6">6</a> I will wash my hands to show I am innocent and come close to your altar, God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 26:7">7</a> So I can speak out with thanks and tell everyone about your amazing deeds.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 26:8">8</a> God, I love your house&#39;s living place, and where your honor stays.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 26:9">9</a> Don&#39;t group me with sinners, or my life with violent people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 26:10">10</a> They do wrong with their hands, and their right hand is full of bribes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 26:11">11</a> But as for me, I will live with honesty: save me and show me mercy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 26:12">12</a> My foot stands on level ground; in the gatherings, I will bless God.</p>
