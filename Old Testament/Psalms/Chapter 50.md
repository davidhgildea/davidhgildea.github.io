<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 50:1">1</a> The powerful God has spoken and called to the earth from where the sun rises to where it sets.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 50:2">2</a> From Zion, the perfect place of beauty, God has shone forth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 50:3">3</a> God will come and will not be silent. A fire will burn before Him, and it will be very stormy around Him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 50:4">4</a> He will call to the skies above and the earth below to judge his people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 50:5">5</a> Bring together those who are devoted to me, those who have made a promise to me through sacrifice.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 50:6">6</a> The skies will show how right and good He is, because God is the judge. Pause and think about that.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 50:7">7</a> Listen, my people, and I will speak; Israel, listen, and I will witness against you: I am God, your God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 50:8">8</a> I will not scold you for your sacrifices or burnt offerings, which have always been before me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 50:9">9</a> I will not take any bulls from your house or goats from your pens.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 50:10">10</a> Every animal in the forest is mine, and the cattle on a thousand hills belong to me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 50:11">11</a> I know all the birds of the mountains, and the wild animals of the fields are mine.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 50:12">12</a> If I were hungry, I wouldn&#39;t tell you because the world is mine and everything in it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Psalms 50:13">13</a> Will I eat bull meat, or drink goat blood?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Psalms 50:14">14</a> Give thanks to God; and keep your promises to the Most High.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Psalms 50:15">15</a> Ask for my help when you have trouble: I will save you, and you will praise me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Psalms 50:16">16</a> God says to the wicked, &quot;Why do you talk about my laws or say you follow my agreement?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Psalms 50:17">17</a> You hate advice and ignore what I say.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Psalms 50:18">18</a> When you saw a thief, you agreed with him, and you joined in with adulterers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Psalms 50:19">19</a> You use your mouth for evil and your tongue tells lies.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Psalms 50:20">20</a> You sit and talk against your brother; you spread lies about your own mother&#39;s son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Psalms 50:21">21</a> You have done these things, and I stayed quiet; you thought I was just like you: but I will correct you and show you clearly.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Psalms 50:22">22</a> Think about this, you who forget God, or I will tear you apart, and no one will save you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Psalms 50:23">23</a> Whoever offers praise honors me, and those who live rightly will see God&#39;s salvation.</p>
