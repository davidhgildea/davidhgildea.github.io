<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 148:1">1</a> Praise God. Praise God from the sky: praise him up high.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 148:2">2</a> Praise him, all his angels; praise him, all his armies.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 148:3">3</a> Praise God, sun and moon; praise Him, all you shining stars.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 148:4">4</a> Praise God, you highest heavens and the waters above the sky.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 148:5">5</a> Let them praise God&#39;s name because when he gave the order, they were made.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 148:6">6</a> God has set them up forever; He has given an order that will never end.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 148:7">7</a> Praise God from the earth, you sea creatures, and all ocean depths.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 148:8">8</a> Fire and hail, snow and clouds; stormy wind carrying out God&#39;s command:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 148:9">9</a> Mountains and all hills; trees with fruit and all cedars.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 148:10">10</a> Animals, all livestock; creatures that crawl, and birds that fly:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 148:11">11</a> Earth&#39;s kings and everyone, leaders and all earth&#39;s judges:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 148:12">12</a> Young people and girls; elderly people and children:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Psalms 148:13">13</a> Let them praise God&#39;s name, for only His name is wonderful; His majesty is higher than earth and heaven.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Psalms 148:14">14</a> He also lifts up the strength of his people, the honor of all his holy ones; even the children of Israel, a people close to him. Praise God.</p>
