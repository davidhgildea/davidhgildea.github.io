<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 105:1">1</a> Give thanks to God; call on his name; tell people about what he has done.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 105:2">2</a> Sing to him, sing songs to him; talk about all his amazing works.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 105:3">3</a> Be proud of his holy name; let the hearts of those who seek God be happy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 105:4">4</a> Look for God and his power; always try to see him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 105:5">5</a> Remember the amazing things God has done, his miracles, and the decisions he has spoken.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 105:6">6</a> Descendants of Abraham, his servant, and children of Jacob, his chosen one.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 105:7">7</a> He is our God; His decisions are everywhere on earth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 105:8">8</a> God has always remembered his promise, the command he gave for thousands of generations.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 105:9">9</a> God made this promise to Abraham and swore an oath to Isaac.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 105:10">10</a> God made it a law for Jacob and an eternal covenant for Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 105:11">11</a> I will give you the land of Canaan, your inheritance.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 105:12">12</a> They were only a few people, very few, and foreigners there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Psalms 105:13">13</a> As they moved from one nation to another and from one kingdom to a different group of people;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Psalms 105:14">14</a> God did not let anyone harm them; indeed, he corrected kings because of them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Psalms 105:15">15</a> God says, &quot;Do not hurt the people I have chosen, and do not harm my messengers.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Psalms 105:16">16</a> He caused a famine in the land; he broke the support of bread.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Psalms 105:17">17</a> God sent a man ahead of them, named Joseph, who was sold as a servant.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Psalms 105:18">18</a> They hurt his feet with chains; he was put in irons.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Psalms 105:19">19</a> Until the time that his word came true, God&#39;s promise tested him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Psalms 105:20">20</a> The king sent for him and set him free; the leader of the people released him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Psalms 105:21">21</a> He made him the master of his home and the leader of all he owned.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Psalms 105:22">22</a> To tie up his leaders as he wishes; and to give his wise men knowledge.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Psalms 105:23">23</a> Israel went to Egypt; Jacob stayed in Ham&#39;s land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Psalms 105:24">24</a> He made his people grow in number greatly; and made them stronger than their enemies.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Psalms 105:25">25</a> He made them hate his people and treat his servants with deceit.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Psalms 105:26">26</a> God sent Moses, his servant, and Aaron, whom he chose.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Psalms 105:27">27</a> They showed his miracles to them, and amazing things in Ham&#39;s land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Psalms 105:28">28</a> God sent darkness and it became dark, but they did not rebel against his word.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Psalms 105:29">29</a> He turned their water into blood and killed their fish.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Psalms 105:30">30</a> Their land had many frogs, even in their kings&#39; rooms.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Psalms 105:31">31</a> He spoke, and different types of flies and lice appeared throughout their lands.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Psalms 105:32">32</a> He sent them ice instead of rain, and fire that burned in their land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Psalms 105:33">33</a> He struck down their grapevines and fig trees, and broke the trees in their territory.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Psalms 105:34">34</a> He spoke, and locusts and caterpillars came in endless numbers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Psalms 105:35">35</a> They ate all the plants in the land and consumed the crops from their soil.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Psalms 105:36">36</a> He also struck down every firstborn in their land, the most powerful of all.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Psalms 105:37">37</a> He also led them out with silver and gold, and all of them were strong and healthy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Psalms 105:38">38</a> Egypt was happy when they left because they were afraid of them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="Psalms 105:39">39</a> He put a cloud as a cover and a fire to shine at night.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="Psalms 105:40">40</a> The people requested, and he provided birds, and filled them with food from heaven.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="Psalms 105:41">41</a> God opened the rock, and water rushed out; it flowed in the dry areas like a river.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="Psalms 105:42">42</a> God remembered his sacred promise to his servant Abraham.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v43"><a class="verse-num" href="#v43" aria-label="Psalms 105:43">43</a> He led his people out with joy, and his chosen ones with happiness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v44"><a class="verse-num" href="#v44" aria-label="Psalms 105:44">44</a> God gave them the lands of the nations, and they received the work of the peoples.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v45"><a class="verse-num" href="#v45" aria-label="Psalms 105:45">45</a> So they could follow his rules and obey his laws. Praise God.</p>
