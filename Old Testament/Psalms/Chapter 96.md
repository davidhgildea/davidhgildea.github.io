<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 96:1">1</a> Sing a new song to God; let everyone on earth sing to God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 96:2">2</a> Sing to God, praise His name; show His salvation every day.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 96:3">3</a> Tell everyone about God&#39;s greatness and the amazing things he does for all people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 96:4">4</a> God is great and deserves much praise; we should respect Him more than all other gods.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 96:5">5</a> All the gods of other nations are just idols, but God created the skies.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 96:6">6</a> Honor and greatness are in front of him; power and beauty are in his holy place.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 96:7">7</a> Give to God, all families of the people, give to God glory and strength.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 96:8">8</a> Give God the honor that fits his name; bring a gift and enter his courts.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 96:9">9</a> Worship God in the beauty of being holy; let all the earth respect him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 96:10">10</a> Tell the nations that God rules: the earth is firm and cannot be shaken; he will judge everyone fairly.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 96:11">11</a> Let the sky be happy, and the earth be joyful; let the sea and everything in it make loud sounds.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 96:12">12</a> Let the field be happy, and everything in it; then all the trees of the forest will celebrate.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Psalms 96:13">13</a> Before God, for he comes to judge the earth: he will judge the world fairly and the people with truth.</p>
