<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 91:1">1</a> Whoever lives in the hidden place of the Most High will stay safe under the protection of the Almighty.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 91:2">2</a> I will say about God, He is my safe place and strong shelter: my God; in Him I will trust.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 91:3">3</a> God will surely save you from the hunter&#39;s trap and from deadly disease.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 91:4">4</a> God will protect you like a bird covers its young with feathers; you will find safety under his wings: his faithfulness will be your defense and protector.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 91:5">5</a> Do not be afraid of dangers at night or arrows that fly during the day.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 91:6">6</a> Not for the sickness that moves in the dark; not for the ruin that destroys at midday.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 91:7">7</a> A thousand will fall near you, and ten thousand by your side, but it will not come close to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 91:8">8</a> You will only watch and see what happens to the wicked.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 91:9">9</a> Because you have made God, who is my protection, even the highest place, your home.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 91:10">10</a> No harm will happen to you, nor will any sickness come close to your home.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 91:11">11</a> God will send his angels to look after you and guard you wherever you go.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 91:12">12</a> They will carry you in their hands, so you won&#39;t hurt your foot on a stone.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Psalms 91:13">13</a> You will walk on the lion and snake; you will step on the young lion and the dragon.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Psalms 91:14">14</a> Because he loves me, I will rescue him. I will protect him because he knows my name.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Psalms 91:15">15</a> He will call to me, and I will reply; I will be with him in trouble, save him, and give him honor.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Psalms 91:16">16</a> I will give him a long life and show him how I can save.</p>
