<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 122:1">1</a> I was happy when they told me, &quot;Let&#39;s go to God&#39;s house.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 122:2">2</a> We will stand inside your gates, Jerusalem.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 122:3">3</a> Jerusalem is built as a city closely united.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 122:4">4</a> The tribes go up, the tribes of God, to show Israel&#39;s proof, to thank God&#39;s name.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 122:7">7</a> May there be peace inside your walls and wealth within your homes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 122:8">8</a> For the sake of my friends and companions, I will say, &quot;May peace be in you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 122:9">9</a> For the sake of God&#39;s house, I will work for your benefit.</p>
