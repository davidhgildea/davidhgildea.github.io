<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 60:1">1</a> God, you have rejected us, scattered us, and been unhappy with us; please come back to us.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 60:2">2</a> You have made the earth shake; you have split it open: repair its cracks, for it is quaking.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 60:3">3</a> You have shown your people tough times; you have made us drink the wine of confusion.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 60:4">4</a> You have given a flag to those who respect you, so it can be shown because of the truth. Pause.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 60:5">5</a> Let your loved one be rescued; save them with your power and listen to me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 60:6">6</a> God spoke in his holy place; I will be glad, I will split Shechem, and share out the Succoth valley.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 60:7">7</a> Gilead belongs to me, and Manasseh is mine; Ephraim is my strong head; Judah makes my laws.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 60:8">8</a> Moab is like my washbasin; I will throw my shoe over Edom; Philistia, celebrate because of me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 60:9">9</a> Who will take me into the fortified city? Who will guide me into Edom?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 60:10">10</a> Won&#39;t you, God, who rejected us before, and you, God, who did not go with our armies?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 60:11">11</a> Help us in trouble, for human help is useless.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 60:12">12</a> With God&#39;s help, we will act bravely because he will defeat our enemies.</p>
