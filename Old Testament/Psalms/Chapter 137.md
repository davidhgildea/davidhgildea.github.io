<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 137:1">1</a> By the rivers of Babylon, there we sat and cried when we thought of Zion.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 137:2">2</a> We hung our harps on the willow trees there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 137:3">3</a> There, our captors asked us for a song; and our tormentors asked for joy, saying, &quot;Sing us one of the Zion songs.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 137:4">4</a> How can we sing God&#39;s song in a foreign place?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 137:5">5</a> If I forget you, Jerusalem, may my right hand lose its skill.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 137:6">6</a> If I don&#39;t remember you, may my tongue stick to the top of my mouth; if I don&#39;t value Jerusalem more than my greatest happiness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 137:7">7</a> God, remember the people of Edom and what they did when Jerusalem fell; they said, &quot;Tear it down, tear it down, to its very foundation.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 137:8">8</a> O daughter of Babylon, doomed to destruction; happy will he be who repays you for what you have done to us.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 137:9">9</a> He will be happy who takes your babies and smashes them against the rocks.</p>
