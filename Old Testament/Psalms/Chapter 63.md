<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 63:1">1</a> God, you are my God; I will look for you early. My soul is thirsty for you, my body longs for you in a dry and thirsty land with no water.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 63:2">2</a> To see your power and your glory, just as I have seen you in the holy place.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 63:3">3</a> Because your kindness is better than life, my lips will praise you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 63:4">4</a> I will bless you as long as I live; I will raise my hands in your name.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 63:5">5</a> My soul will be happy as with the best food; and my mouth will joyfully praise you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 63:6">6</a> When I think of you on my bed and reflect on you during the night time.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 63:7">7</a> Because you have helped me, I will be happy in your protection.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 63:8">8</a> I follow you closely with all my heart; your strong hand supports me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 63:9">9</a> Those who try to destroy me will be brought down to the deepest parts of the earth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 63:10">10</a> They will die by the sword and become food for foxes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 63:11">11</a> The king will be happy in God; everyone who makes promises by him will be proud: but the mouths of liars will be shut.</p>
