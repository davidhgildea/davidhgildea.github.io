<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 58:1">1</a> Are you really talking fair, group of people? Are you judging honestly, you people?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 58:2">2</a> Yes, in your hearts you plan evil; you think about the harm your hands can do in the world.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 58:3">3</a> From birth, the wicked are lost; they go wrong from the start, telling lies.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 58:4">4</a> Their poison is like snake venom; they are like the deaf cobra that covers its ears.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 58:5">5</a> Who do not listen to the voice of snake charmers, no matter how clever their charms.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 58:6">6</a> God, break their teeth in their mouths; smash the young lions&#39; strong teeth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 58:7">7</a> Let them disappear like flowing water: when he aims his bow to fire his arrows, let them break apart.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 58:8">8</a> Like a melting snail, let each of them vanish: like a stillborn baby, that they never see the sun.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 58:9">9</a> Before your pots get hot from the thorns, God will sweep them away with a storm, alive and in his anger.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 58:10">10</a> Good people will be happy when they see punishment; they will wash their feet in the blood of the bad people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 58:11">11</a> A person will say, &quot;Surely there is a reward for good people; truly, God is the one who judges on earth.&quot;</p>
