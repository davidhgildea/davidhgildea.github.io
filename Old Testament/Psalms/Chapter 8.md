<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 8:1">1</a> God, our Lord, how wonderful is your name all over the earth! You have put your greatness higher than the sky.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 8:2">2</a> God has given strength through the words of children and babies to silence the enemy and the avenger.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 8:3">3</a> When I look at your skies, the creation of your hands, the moon and the stars you set in place;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 8:4">4</a> What is a person that you think of him? And a human that you visit him?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 8:5">5</a> You made him a little less than the angels and crowned him with glory and honor.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 8:6">6</a> You made him ruler over everything you created; you put everything under his control.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 8:7">7</a> Every sheep and cow, and the wild animals of the field.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 8:8">8</a> Birds in the sky, fish in the sea, and everything that moves through the sea paths.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 8:9">9</a> God, our Master, how wonderful is your name throughout the earth!</p>
