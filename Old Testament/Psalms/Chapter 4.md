<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 4:1">1</a> Listen to me when I call, God who makes me right. You gave me more room when I was in trouble. Be kind to me and listen to my prayer.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 4:2">2</a> People, how long will you turn my honor into shame? How long will you love what is empty and chase lies? Pause and think.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 4:3">3</a> Know that God has chosen the godly person for himself; God will listen when I call to him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 4:4">4</a> Be amazed, do not sin; talk to your own heart on your bed, and be quiet. Pause.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 4:5">5</a> Give the right sacrifices, and trust in God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 4:6">6</a> Many people ask, &quot;Who will show us any good?&quot; God, make your face shine on us.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 4:7">7</a> You have filled my heart with joy, more than they have when their grain and wine abound.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 4:8">8</a> I will lie down and sleep in peace, because you, God, alone keep me safe.</p>
