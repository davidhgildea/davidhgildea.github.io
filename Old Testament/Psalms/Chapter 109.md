<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 109:1">1</a> God of my praise, do not stay silent.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 109:2">2</a> Wicked and deceitful people have opened their mouths against me; they have lied about me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 109:3">3</a> They surrounded me with hateful words and attacked me for no reason.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 109:4">4</a> Even though I show love, they are my enemies; but I still pray.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 109:5">5</a> They have paid me back bad for good, and hate for my love.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 109:6">6</a> Put an evil person in charge of him, and let Satan be by his side.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 109:7">7</a> When he is judged, let him be found guilty, and may his prayer be counted as sin.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 109:8">8</a> May his time be short; may someone else replace him in his job.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 109:9">9</a> May his children have no father, and his wife be a widow.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 109:10">10</a> May his children wander and beg, always seeking food from ruined places.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 109:11">11</a> Let the thief take everything he owns; and let outsiders take what he has worked for.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 109:12">12</a> Let no one show him kindness, and no one care for his orphaned children.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Psalms 109:13">13</a> Let his descendants disappear; and let their name be erased in the next generation.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Psalms 109:14">14</a> Let God remember his fathers&#39; wrongs and not erase his mother&#39;s sins.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Psalms 109:15">15</a> Keep them always before God, so that he may erase the memory of them from the earth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Psalms 109:16">16</a> Because he did not remember to show mercy but chased after the poor and needy, intending to kill those whose hearts were crushed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Psalms 109:17">17</a> Since he liked to curse, let curses come to him; since he did not enjoy blessing, let it be far from him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Psalms 109:18">18</a> He wore curses like clothes, so let them soak into him like water and like oil into his bones.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Psalms 109:19">19</a> May it be to him like the clothes he wears and the belt he always has on.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Psalms 109:20">20</a> Let this be the payback for my enemies from God, and for those who say bad things about me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Psalms 109:21">21</a> Help me, God, for the sake of your name; since your mercy is good, rescue me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Psalms 109:22">22</a> I am poor and in need, and my heart is hurt inside me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Psalms 109:23">23</a> I have vanished like a shadow when it fades; I am shaken back and forth like a locust.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Psalms 109:24">24</a> My knees are weak from not eating, and I&#39;ve lost weight.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Psalms 109:25">25</a> I also became someone they insulted; when they saw me, they shook their heads.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Psalms 109:26">26</a> Help me, God my God; save me because of your kindness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Psalms 109:27">27</a> So they will understand this is your work; that you, God, have done it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Psalms 109:28">28</a> Let them curse, but you bless: when they stand up, let them be embarrassed; but let your servant be happy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Psalms 109:29">29</a> Let my enemies be covered with shame and wrap themselves in their confusion like a cloak.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Psalms 109:30">30</a> I will praise God a lot with my words; yes, I will praise him in front of many people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Psalms 109:31">31</a> He will stand by the poor to protect them from those who judge them wrongly.</p>
