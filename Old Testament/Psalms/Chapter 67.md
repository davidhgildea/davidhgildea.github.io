<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 67:1">1</a> God, be kind to us and bless us; let his face shine on us. Pause.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 67:2">2</a> So that your path can be known on Earth, your salvation among all the nations.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 67:3">3</a> Let the people thank you, God; let everyone thank you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 67:4">4</a> Let the countries be happy and sing with joy: because you will judge the people fairly and lead the countries on earth. Pause and think about that.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 67:5">5</a> God, let the people praise you; let everyone praise you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 67:6">6</a> Then the earth will give its crops, and God, our God, will bless us.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 67:7">7</a> God will bless us, and everyone on earth will respect him.</p>
