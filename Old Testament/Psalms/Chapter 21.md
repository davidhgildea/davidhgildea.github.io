<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 21:1">1</a> The king will be happy because of your power, God; he will be very glad because you save him!</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 21:2">2</a> You have granted him what his heart wanted, and you have not denied what he asked with his lips. Pause.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 21:3">3</a> You go before him with good blessings; you place a crown of pure gold on his head.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 21:4">4</a> He asked you for life, and you gave it to him, a long life that lasts forever and ever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 21:5">5</a> God&#39;s glory is great because of your saving help: you have given him honor and greatness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 21:6">6</a> You have made him forever blessed; you have filled him with joy by your presence.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 21:7">7</a> The king trusts in God, and with the kindness of the Most High, he will not be shaken.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 21:8">8</a> Your hand will discover all your enemies: your strong hand will find those who hate you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 21:9">9</a> You will make them like a blazing furnace when you are angry: God will consume them in his fury, and the fire will eat them up.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 21:10">10</a> You will destroy their fruit from the earth and their offspring among people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 21:11">11</a> They planned to harm you: they thought of a wicked plan, which they cannot carry out.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 21:12">12</a> So you will make them run away when you prepare to shoot your arrows at them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Psalms 21:13">13</a> Let yourself be lifted up, God, by your own power; then we will sing and praise your strength.</p>
