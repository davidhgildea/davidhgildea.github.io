<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 11:1">1</a> I trust in God; why do you tell me to run away like a bird to the mountains?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 11:2">2</a> Look, the evil ones pull back their bows, they put their arrows on the strings to secretly shoot at the good-hearted.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 11:3">3</a> If the base is ruined, what can good people do?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 11:5">5</a> God tests the good people, but hates the evil ones who love violence.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 11:6">6</a> God will rain traps, fire, and sulfur on the wicked, along with a terrible storm: this will be what fills their cup.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 11:7">7</a> God loves good people; He sees those who are honest.</p>
