<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 110:1">1</a> God said to my Lord, &quot;Sit at my right side until I make your enemies a place for your feet.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 110:2">2</a> God will send out your mighty scepter from Zion: rule in the middle of your enemies.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 110:3">3</a> Your people will willingly follow you when you show your power. Dressed in holy beauty like the fresh morning dew, you have the vitality of youth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 110:4">4</a> God has made a promise that cannot be changed: You are a priest forever, in the way of Melchizedek.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 110:5">5</a> God at your side will defeat kings when he&#39;s angry.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 110:6">6</a> He will judge the nations, fill the lands with dead bodies, and strike down rulers across many countries.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 110:7">7</a> He will drink from the stream on his path; because of this, he will hold his head high.</p>
