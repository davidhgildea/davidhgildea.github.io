<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 52:1">1</a> Why do you brag about your evil deeds, powerful person? God&#39;s goodness lasts forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 52:2">2</a> The tongue plans trouble; it&#39;s like a sharp blade, working dishonestly.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 52:3">3</a> You love evil more than good, and lying more than speaking the truth. Pause.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 52:4">4</a> You love to say harmful things, deceitful tongue.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 52:5">5</a> God will destroy you forever, take you away, and remove you from your home, ending your life on earth. Pause and think.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 52:6">6</a> The good people will see, be in awe, and will laugh at him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 52:7">7</a> Look, this is the man who did not rely on God for strength; instead, he trusted in his great wealth and became strong through his wrongdoing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 52:8">8</a> I am like a healthy olive tree in God&#39;s house; I rely on God&#39;s kindness forever and always.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 52:9">9</a> I will praise you forever because you have done it; I will trust in your name for it is good in the eyes of your faithful people.</p>
