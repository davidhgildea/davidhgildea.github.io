<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 86:1">1</a> Lean down your ear, God, listen to me: I am poor and in need.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 86:2">2</a> Keep me safe, because I am devoted to you; my God, rescue your servant who relies on you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 86:3">3</a> Show me kindness, God, for I call out to you every day.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 86:4">4</a> Make your servant&#39;s heart happy, because I turn to you, God, with all my heart.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 86:5">5</a> You, God, are good and always ready to forgive; you are rich in kindness to all who call on you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 86:6">6</a> Listen, God, to my prayer; pay attention to my requests.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 86:7">7</a> When I have problems, I will ask for your help because you will respond to me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 86:8">8</a> There are no gods like you, God; no deeds can match what you do.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 86:9">9</a> Every nation you created will come and worship before you, God; they will honor your name.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 86:10">10</a> You are great and do amazing things; you are the only God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 86:11">11</a> Teach me your way, God; I will follow your truth: make my heart whole to respect your name.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 86:12">12</a> I will praise you, God, with all my heart, and I will honor your name forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Psalms 86:13">13</a> Your kindness to me is great; you have saved me from the deepest darkness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Psalms 86:14">14</a> God, proud people rise against me, and groups of violent men chase my soul; they do not consider you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Psalms 86:15">15</a> But you, God, are filled with compassion and kindness, very patient, and rich in mercy and truth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Psalms 86:16">16</a> God, please pay attention to me and show me kindness; give your power to your servant and rescue your servant&#39;s mother&#39;s child.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Psalms 86:17">17</a> Show me a sign of your goodness, so those who hate me will see it and feel shame, because you, God, have helped and comforted me.</p>
