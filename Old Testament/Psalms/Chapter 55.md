<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 55:1">1</a> Listen to my prayer, God, and do not ignore my request.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 55:2">2</a> Listen to me and hear me: I am sad and complaining, and I am loud.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 55:3">3</a> Because of the enemy&#39;s voice and the wicked&#39;s cruelty, they blame me for wrongs and hate me in anger.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 55:4">4</a> My heart hurts deeply inside me, and fears of death have come over me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 55:5">5</a> I am scared and shaking, and horror has taken over me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 55:6">6</a> I wished for wings like a dove so I could fly away and find peace.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 55:7">7</a> Look, then I would go far away and stay in the desert. Pause.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 55:8">8</a> I would hurry to escape from the windy storm and tempest.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 55:9">9</a> God, destroy and confuse their speech because I have seen violence and conflict in the city.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 55:10">10</a> Day and night they move around on its walls; trouble and sadness are in its center.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 55:11">11</a> Evil is everywhere in it: lies and trickery never leave its streets.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 55:12">12</a> It wasn&#39;t an enemy who insulted me; if it were, I could have endured it. It wasn&#39;t someone who hated me who boasted against me; if it were, I could have hidden from him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Psalms 55:13">13</a> But you were my equal, my guide, and my friend.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Psalms 55:14">14</a> We shared good advice together and went to God&#39;s house with others.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Psalms 55:15">15</a> May death take them, and may they quickly go down to hell; for evil is in their homes and with them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Psalms 55:16">16</a> I will call on God, and God will save me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Psalms 55:17">17</a> I will pray in the evening, in the morning, and at noon. I will cry out loudly, and God will hear me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Psalms 55:18">18</a> God has saved my life peacefully from the fight against me, because many were with me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Psalms 55:19">19</a> God will listen and punish them, even the one from long ago. Pause. They don&#39;t change, so they don&#39;t fear God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Psalms 55:20">20</a> He has attacked those who were at peace with him; he has broken his agreement.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Psalms 55:21">21</a> His mouth spoke nice words like butter, but his heart planned for war; his words were gentle like oil, but they were really sharp swords.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Psalms 55:22">22</a> Give your worries to God, and he will support you; he will never let good people be shaken.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Psalms 55:23">23</a> But you, God, will send the violent and lying people to a place of ruin: they won&#39;t live even half their expected years; but I will rely on you.</p>
