<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 77:1">1</a> I called out to God loudly; I called to God, and he listened to me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 77:2">2</a> On the day I was in trouble, I looked for God. My pain continued all night and did not stop, and my heart would not be comforted.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 77:3">3</a> I thought of God and felt upset; I complained, and my spirit felt crushed. Pause.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 77:4">4</a> You keep me awake: I am so upset that I can&#39;t talk.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 77:5">5</a> I have thought about the old days, the years from long ago.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 77:6">6</a> I remember my night song; I talk with my heart; my spirit searches carefully.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 77:7">7</a> Will God reject us forever? Will he never show favor again?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 77:8">8</a> Has his kindness completely disappeared forever? Will his promise never come true?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 77:9">9</a> Has God forgotten to be kind? Has he in anger closed off his compassion? Pause and think about that.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 77:10">10</a> And I thought, &quot;This is my weakness, but I will remember the years of God&#39;s powerful works.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 77:11">11</a> I will remember what God has done; I will surely remember the amazing things you did long ago.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 77:12">12</a> I will think about all your works and talk about what you have done.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Psalms 77:13">13</a> Your path, God, is in the holy place: who is as mighty as our God?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Psalms 77:14">14</a> You are the God who performs miracles; you have shown your power to the people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Psalms 77:15">15</a> With your strong arm, you rescued your people, the descendants of Jacob and Joseph. Pause.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Psalms 77:16">16</a> The waters saw you, God, the waters saw you; they were scared: even the deep waters were disturbed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Psalms 77:17">17</a> The clouds released water; the sky made noise; your arrows flew everywhere.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Psalms 77:18">18</a> Your thunder&#39;s sound was in the sky; the lightning lit up the world; the ground quaked and shook.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Psalms 77:19">19</a> Your way is in the sea, your path is in the big waters, and no one knows your footsteps.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Psalms 77:20">20</a> You led your people like sheep with Moses and Aaron guiding them.</p>
