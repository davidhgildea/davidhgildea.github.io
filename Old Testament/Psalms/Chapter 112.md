<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 112:1">1</a> Praise God. Happy is the person who respects God and loves to follow His rules.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 112:2">2</a> His children will be powerful on earth; the descendants of the good will be blessed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 112:3">3</a> Wealth and riches will be in his house, and his goodness will last forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 112:4">4</a> For good people, light shines in the dark: they are kind, caring, and fair.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 112:5">5</a> A good person is kind and lends; they manage their matters wisely.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 112:6">6</a> He will surely not be shaken forever; the good person will always be remembered.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 112:7">7</a> He will not fear bad news: his heart is steady, trusting in God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 112:8">8</a> His heart is steady, he will not be scared, until he sees what he wants happen to his enemies.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 112:9">9</a> He has shared, he has given to the needy; his goodness lasts forever; his strength will be honored.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 112:10">10</a> Bad people will see it and be upset; they will grind their teeth and fade away; what bad people want will vanish.</p>
