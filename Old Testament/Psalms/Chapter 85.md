<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 85:1">1</a> God, you have been good to your land; you have freed Jacob&#39;s captives.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 85:2">2</a> You have forgiven your people&#39;s sins; you have covered all their wrongs. Pause and think about that.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 85:3">3</a> You have removed all your anger; you have turned away from the intensity of your rage.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 85:4">4</a> God of our salvation, turn us back to you and stop being angry with us.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 85:5">5</a> Will you stay angry with us forever? Will you keep your anger through all generations?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 85:6">6</a> Will you not bring us back to life so your people can be happy in you?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 85:7">7</a> Show us your mercy, God, and give us your salvation.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 85:8">8</a> I will listen to what God will say: He promises peace to His people and His holy ones, but they must not go back to foolish ways.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 85:9">9</a> God&#39;s rescue is close to those who respect him; so that honor may live in our land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 85:10">10</a> Kindness and truth have come together; fairness and peace have embraced.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 85:11">11</a> Truth will grow from the ground; and fairness will look down from the sky.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 85:12">12</a> Yes, God will give what is good, and our land will produce its crops.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Psalms 85:13">13</a> Goodness will lead the way for him; and will guide us on his path.</p>
