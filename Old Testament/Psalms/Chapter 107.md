<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 107:1">1</a> Give thanks to God, because He is good; His love lasts forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 107:2">2</a> Let those saved by God say so, whom he has saved from the enemy&#39;s power.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 107:3">3</a> God brought them together from the east, west, north, and south.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 107:4">4</a> They roamed the desert on a lonely path and found no city to live in.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 107:5">5</a> They were hungry and thirsty, and they felt weak deep inside.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 107:6">6</a> They called out to God in their trouble, and he rescued them from their distress.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 107:7">7</a> He guided them on the right path so they could reach a city to live in.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 107:8">8</a> I wish people would thank God for his kindness and for the amazing things he does for humans!</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 107:9">9</a> God satisfies the longing person and fills the hungry person with good things.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 107:10">10</a> Those who sit in darkness and near death, trapped in suffering and chains;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 107:11">11</a> They disobeyed God&#39;s words and scorned the advice of the Most High.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 107:12">12</a> So God made them weak with hard work; they stumbled, and no one was there to help them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Psalms 107:13">13</a> They called out to God in their trouble, and he rescued them from their problems.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Psalms 107:14">14</a> He led them from darkness and the shadow of death, and broke their chains apart.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Psalms 107:15">15</a> I wish people would thank God for his kindness and his amazing deeds for humanity!</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Psalms 107:16">16</a> He has smashed the bronze gates and cut through the iron bars.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Psalms 107:17">17</a> Fools suffer because they rebel and do wrong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Psalms 107:18">18</a> Their soul hates all kinds of food; and they are close to dying.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Psalms 107:19">19</a> Then they call to God in their trouble, and he saves them from their distresses.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Psalms 107:20">20</a> He sent his message, healed them, and saved them from their troubles.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Psalms 107:21">21</a> I wish people would thank God for His kindness and for His amazing deeds to humans!</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Psalms 107:22">22</a> Let them offer thanksgiving sacrifices and joyfully talk about what God has done.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Psalms 107:23">23</a> Those who sail on the sea in ships, doing work on the vast waters;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Psalms 107:24">24</a> They see God&#39;s deeds and his amazing works in the ocean.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Psalms 107:25">25</a> He commands, and a stormy wind rises, lifting the waves.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Psalms 107:26">26</a> They rise up to the sky, they sink down to the deep: their spirit is overwhelmed by distress.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Psalms 107:27">27</a> They sway back and forth, and stumble like a drunk person, and don&#39;t know what to do.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Psalms 107:28">28</a> Then they call out to God in their trouble, and he saves them from their distresses.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Psalms 107:29">29</a> He turns the storm into a calm so that the waves become still.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Psalms 107:30">30</a> Then they are happy because they are calm; so he takes them to the place they wanted to reach.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Psalms 107:31">31</a> I wish people would thank God for his kindness and his amazing deeds for all people!</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Psalms 107:32">32</a> Let them praise God among the people, and honor him in the gathering of the elders.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Psalms 107:33">33</a> He turns rivers into deserts and springs of water into dry land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Psalms 107:34">34</a> A productive land becomes empty because of the evil of those who live there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Psalms 107:35">35</a> He turns the desert into pools of water and parched land into springs.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Psalms 107:36">36</a> There he lets the hungry live, so they can build a city to live in.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Psalms 107:37">37</a> Plant the fields and grow vineyards to produce a harvest of fruit.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Psalms 107:38">38</a> He blesses them, and they increase a lot; he does not let their animals become fewer.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="Psalms 107:39">39</a> They are reduced and weakened by hardship, trouble, and sadness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="Psalms 107:40">40</a> He shows disrespect to rulers and makes them walk in the desert, where there are no paths.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="Psalms 107:41">41</a> God lifts the poor from trouble and gives them many children like a herd of sheep.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="Psalms 107:42">42</a> Good people will see it and be happy, while all wrongdoers will be silent.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v43"><a class="verse-num" href="#v43" aria-label="Psalms 107:43">43</a> Anyone who is wise and pays attention to these things will understand God&#39;s kindness.</p>
