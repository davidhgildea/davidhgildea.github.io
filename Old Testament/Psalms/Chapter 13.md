<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 13:1">1</a> How long will you forget me, God? Forever? How long will you hide your face from me?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 13:3">3</a> Think about me and listen, God: keep my eyes open, so I don&#39;t fall into the sleep of death.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 13:6">6</a> I will sing to God, because he has been very good to me.</p>
