<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 75:1">1</a> We thank you, God, we give you thanks, because your name is close and your amazing deeds show it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 75:2">2</a> When I gather the people, I will judge fairly.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 75:3">3</a> The earth and everyone living on it are falling apart; I support its foundations. Pause and think about that.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 75:4">4</a> I told the foolish, &quot;Don&#39;t act foolishly,&quot; and to the wicked, &quot;Don&#39;t raise your power.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 75:5">5</a> Don&#39;t raise your horn proudly; don&#39;t talk with arrogance.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 75:6">6</a> Success doesn&#39;t come from the east, west, or south.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 75:7">7</a> God is the judge: He brings down one and lifts up another.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 75:8">8</a> In God&#39;s hand is a cup filled with strong red wine, mixed well; He pours it, and all the wicked on earth must drink its bitter remains.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 75:9">9</a> I will always tell of God&#39;s deeds; I will sing praise to Jacob&#39;s God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 75:10">10</a> I will cut off all the power of the wicked, but the power of the good people will be lifted up.</p>
