<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 83:1">1</a> God, do not be silent; do not stay quiet, God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 83:2">2</a> Look, your enemies are causing chaos: and those who hate you have become arrogant.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 83:3">3</a> They have made sneaky plans against your people and plotted against those you protect.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 83:4">4</a> They said, &quot;Come, let&#39;s destroy them as a nation, so no one remembers the name Israel anymore.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 83:5">5</a> They have agreed together to join forces against you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 83:6">6</a> The tents of Edom, and the Ishmaelites; of Moab, and the Hagarenes;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 83:7">7</a> Gebal, Ammon, and Amalek; the Philistines and the people of Tyre;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 83:8">8</a> Assur has also teamed up with them; they have helped Lot&#39;s descendants. Pause.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 83:9">9</a> Treat them like you did the Midianites and like you did Sisera and Jabin at the Kishon River.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 83:10">10</a> They died at Endor and became like manure on the ground.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 83:11">11</a> Make their leaders like Oreb and Zeeb; make all their princes like Zebah and Zalmunna.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 83:12">12</a> They said, &quot;Let&#39;s take God&#39;s houses for ourselves.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Psalms 83:13">13</a> My God, make them like a spinning wheel; like dry plants before the wind.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Psalms 83:14">14</a> Just as fire burns wood, and flames set the mountains ablaze;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Psalms 83:15">15</a> Chase them with your wind and scare them with your storm.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Psalms 83:16">16</a> Make them feel ashamed so that they will look for you, God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Psalms 83:17">17</a> May they be confused and troubled forever; yes, may they be embarrassed and die.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Psalms 83:18">18</a> People will know that you, named God, are the highest above all the earth.</p>
