<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 62:1">1</a> My soul truly waits for God; my rescue comes from Him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 62:2">2</a> He alone is my rock and my savior; he protects me; I won&#39;t be shaken much.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 62:3">3</a> How long will you plan harm against someone? You will all be killed: you will be like a leaning wall or a shaky fence.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 62:4">4</a> They only plot to knock him from his high place; they enjoy lying; they praise with their words, but inside they curse. Pause and think about that.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 62:5">5</a> My soul, wait only for God; my hope comes from him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 62:6">6</a> God is my only rock and savior; he protects me; I won&#39;t be shaken.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 62:7">7</a> God is my savior and my honor; my strong protector and my shelter is in God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 62:8">8</a> Always trust in God; people, share your deepest feelings with Him: God is our safe place. Selah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 62:9">9</a> Truly, people of low rank are worthless, and those of high rank are not truthful; if weighed, they are all less than worthless together.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 62:10">10</a> Do not trust in harsh treatment, and do not be proud of stealing. If you get richer, do not fix your heart on the money.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 62:11">11</a> God has spoken once; I have heard this twice: power belongs to God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 62:12">12</a> To you, God, belongs kindness: because you give to each person based on what they do.</p>
