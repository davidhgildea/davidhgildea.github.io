<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 111:1">1</a> Praise God. I will praise God with all my heart, among the good people and the gathered crowd.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 111:2">2</a> God&#39;s works are great, and all who enjoy them seek them out.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 111:3">3</a> His work is noble and wonderful; and his goodness lasts forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 111:4">4</a> He made his amazing deeds unforgettable: God is kind and very compassionate.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 111:5">5</a> He has provided food to those who respect him; he will always remember his agreement.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 111:6">6</a> He has shown his people the power of his actions, so that he can give them the lands of other nations.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 111:7">7</a> What God does is true and fair; all his rules are certain.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 111:8">8</a> They remain forever and ever, and are made with truth and honesty.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 111:9">9</a> He brought freedom to his people; he ordered his agreement to last forever: his name is holy and worthy of respect.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 111:10">10</a> Being afraid of God is the start of being wise; those who follow His rules understand things well: His praise lasts always.</p>
