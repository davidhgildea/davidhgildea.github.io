<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 87:1">1</a> His base is in the sacred mountains.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 87:2">2</a> God loves the gates of Zion more than any homes of Jacob.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 87:3">3</a> People say wonderful things about you, O city of God. Pause and think about that.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 87:4">4</a> I will talk about Egypt and Babylon to my people; look at Philistia, Tyre, and Ethiopia; this person was born there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 87:5">5</a> People will say, &quot;This person and that person were born in Zion,&quot; and God himself will make her strong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 87:7">7</a> Singers and musicians will be there: all my sources of life are with you.</p>
