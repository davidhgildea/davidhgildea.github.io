<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 57:1">1</a> Have mercy on me, God, have mercy on me, for my soul trusts in you; yes, under the protection of your wings I will find shelter until these disasters have passed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 57:2">2</a> I will call out to God above all; to God who does everything for me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 57:3">3</a> God will send help from heaven to save me from my enemies&#39; insults. God will show his kindness and truth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 57:4">4</a> My soul is with lions; I&#39;m surrounded by people burning with anger, men whose teeth are like spears and arrows, and whose tongues are as sharp as swords.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 57:5">5</a> God, be honored high above the skies; let your greatness be over the whole earth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 57:6">6</a> They set a trap for me; I am deeply troubled. They dug a hole in my path, but they have fallen into it themselves. Pause.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 57:7">7</a> My heart is steady, God, my heart is steady; I will sing and praise you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 57:8">8</a> Wake up, my honor; wake up, stringed instruments and harp: I will wake up early.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 57:9">9</a> I will praise you, God, among the people; I will sing to you among the nations.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 57:10">10</a> Your kindness reaches to the sky, and your truth goes up to the clouds.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 57:11">11</a> God, be lifted high above the heavens; let your glory be over all the earth.</p>
