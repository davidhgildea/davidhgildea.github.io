<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 23:1">1</a> God is my shepherd; I will not lack anything.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 23:2">2</a> God lets me rest in green fields; he guides me by calm waters.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 23:3">3</a> He renews my strength. He guides me in the right paths, honoring his name.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 23:4">4</a> Even when I go through the darkest path, I&#39;m not scared of anything bad because you are with me, and your stick and cane make me feel safe.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 23:5">5</a> You prepare a meal for me in front of my enemies; you put oil on my head; my cup is very full.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 23:6">6</a> Goodness and kindness will be with me every day of my life, and I will live in God&#39;s house forever.</p>
