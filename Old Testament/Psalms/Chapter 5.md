<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 5:1">1</a> Listen to my words, God, think about what I am silently praying.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 5:2">2</a> Listen to my cry for help, my King and God, for I will pray to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 5:3">3</a> You will hear my voice in the morning, God; in the morning, I will send my prayer to you and wait for your answer.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 5:4">4</a> You are not a God who likes evil; wrongdoers cannot stay with you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 5:5">5</a> Fools cannot be near you; you dislike all who do wrong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 5:6">6</a> You will destroy those who tell lies: God will hate the violent and deceitful person.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 5:7">7</a> But as for me, I will come into your house because of your great mercy; and I will worship you with respect, facing your holy temple.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 5:8">8</a> Guide me, God, in your goodness because of my enemies; make your path clear to me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 5:9">9</a> They are not loyal; they are very bad inside; their throat is like an open grave; they use their tongues to deceive.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 5:10">10</a> God, destroy them; let them be ruined by their own plans; throw them out because of their many sins, for they have turned against you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 5:11">11</a> Let everyone who trusts in you be happy; let them always sing for joy, because you protect them. Let those who love your name be joyful in you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 5:12">12</a> God, you will bless good people; you will surround them with your favor as with a shield.</p>
