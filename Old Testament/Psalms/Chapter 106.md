<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 106:1">1</a> Praise God. Give thanks to God, for he is good and his kindness lasts forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 106:2">2</a> Who can speak of God&#39;s powerful deeds? Who can show all the praise he deserves?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 106:3">3</a> Happy are those who follow what is just, and the one who does what is right all the time.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 106:4">4</a> Remember me, God, with the kindness you show to your people: come to save me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 106:5">5</a> So I can see the good things for your chosen ones, be happy with your people&#39;s joy, and celebrate with your heritage.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 106:6">6</a> We have done wrong like our ancestors, we have acted badly, we have behaved wickedly.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 106:7">7</a> Our ancestors did not understand your miracles in Egypt; they did not remember your many acts of kindness but angered you at the sea, at the Red Sea.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 106:8">8</a> Yet he saved them for his name&#39;s sake, to show his great power.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 106:9">9</a> He scolded the Red Sea, and it dried up; so he guided them through the deep parts as if through a desert.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 106:10">10</a> He saved them from the one who hated them and rescued them from their enemy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 106:11">11</a> The water covered their enemies: not even one survived.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 106:12">12</a> Then they believed what he said; they praised him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Psalms 106:13">13</a> They quickly forgot what he had done; they did not wait for his advice.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Psalms 106:14">14</a> They desired too much in the wilderness and tested God in the desert.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Psalms 106:15">15</a> He granted their wish, but made their life empty.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Psalms 106:16">16</a> They were jealous of Moses in the camp, and of Aaron, God&#39;s holy one.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Psalms 106:17">17</a> The ground opened up and swallowed Dathan and then closed over Abiram&#39;s group.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Psalms 106:18">18</a> A fire started among them; the flame consumed the wicked.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Psalms 106:19">19</a> They created a calf in Horeb and worshipped the metal statue.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Psalms 106:20">20</a> So they turned their honor into the likeness of a cow that eats grass.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Psalms 106:21">21</a> They forgot God their savior who did great things in Egypt.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Psalms 106:22">22</a> Amazing things in the land of Egypt, and scary events by the Red Sea.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Psalms 106:23">23</a> So he said he would end them, but Moses, his chosen one, stood before him to stop his anger, or he would have ended them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Psalms 106:24">24</a> Yes, they looked down on the beautiful land and did not trust what he said.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Psalms 106:25">25</a> But they complained in their tents and did not listen to God&#39;s voice.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Psalms 106:26">26</a> So he raised his hand against them, to knock them down in the desert.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Psalms 106:27">27</a> To destroy their children among different countries and spread them out across various lands.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Psalms 106:28">28</a> They also followed Baalpeor and ate offerings for the dead.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Psalms 106:29">29</a> Their actions angered God, and a plague spread among them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Psalms 106:30">30</a> Phinehas stood up and carried out justice; and so the disease was stopped.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Psalms 106:31">31</a> God considered that as true goodness forever for all people to come.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Psalms 106:32">32</a> They also made him angry at the Waters of Strife, which caused trouble for Moses because of them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Psalms 106:33">33</a> Because they angered him, he spoke without thinking.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Psalms 106:34">34</a> They did not wipe out the nations, which God had told them to do.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Psalms 106:35">35</a> But they mixed with other nations and adopted their ways.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Psalms 106:36">36</a> They worshiped their idols, which became a trap for them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Psalms 106:37">37</a> Yes, they sacrificed their sons and daughters to demons.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Psalms 106:38">38</a> They killed innocent people, including their own sons and daughters, whom they offered to Canaan&#39;s idols, and the land was tainted with blood.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="Psalms 106:39">39</a> They became unclean by what they did and were unfaithful with their own ideas.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="Psalms 106:40">40</a> God became very angry with his people and came to detest his own chosen ones.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="Psalms 106:41">41</a> He let other nations defeat them, and their enemies ruled over them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="Psalms 106:42">42</a> Their enemies pressured them, and they came under their control.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v43"><a class="verse-num" href="#v43" aria-label="Psalms 106:43">43</a> God saved them many times, but they angered him with their advice and were punished for their sins.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v44"><a class="verse-num" href="#v44" aria-label="Psalms 106:44">44</a> God noticed their suffering when he heard their call for help.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v45"><a class="verse-num" href="#v45" aria-label="Psalms 106:45">45</a> God remembered his promise to them and felt sorry for them because of his great kindness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v46"><a class="verse-num" href="#v46" aria-label="Psalms 106:46">46</a> He also made all who took them captive feel sorry for them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v47"><a class="verse-num" href="#v47" aria-label="Psalms 106:47">47</a> Save us, God, and bring us back from among the nations, to thank your holy name and to celebrate your praise.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v48"><a class="verse-num" href="#v48" aria-label="Psalms 106:48">48</a> Let&#39;s praise the God of Israel forever and ever; and let everyone say, &quot;Yes.&quot; Praise God.</p>
