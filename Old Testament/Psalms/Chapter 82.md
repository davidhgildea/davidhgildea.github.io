<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 82:1">1</a> God stands in the gathering of the powerful; he judges among the leaders.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 82:2">2</a> How long will you make unfair decisions and favor the wrong people? Pause and think.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 82:3">3</a> Protect the poor and children without parents; treat the troubled and poor fairly.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 82:4">4</a> Help the poor and those in need; rescue them from the power of the wicked.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 82:5">5</a> They do not know or understand; they continue in darkness: all the world&#39;s foundations are unsettled.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 82:6">6</a> I have said, you are gods; and you are all children of the most High.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 82:7">7</a> You will die like humans and fall like any leader.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 82:8">8</a> Get up, God, and judge the world, because you will receive all the nations as your own.</p>
