<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 98:1">1</a> Sing to God a new song; for he has done wonderful things: his right hand, and his holy arm, have won the victory.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 98:2">2</a> God has shown his salvation; he has revealed his goodness for all nations to see.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 98:3">3</a> God remembered his kindness and faithfulness to Israel&#39;s people: everyone on earth has seen our God&#39;s salvation.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 98:4">4</a> Celebrate with joy to God, everyone on earth: be loud and happy, and sing praises.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 98:5">5</a> Sing to God with the harp; use the harp and sing psalms.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 98:6">6</a> Play trumpets and cornets to make happy sounds for God, the King.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 98:7">7</a> Let the sea make noise, and everything in it; the earth, and everyone who lives on it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 98:8">8</a> Let the rivers applaud: let the hills celebrate together.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 98:9">9</a> Before God; for he comes to judge the earth: he will judge the world fairly, and treat all people equally.</p>
