<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 12:1">1</a> Help, God; for good people are gone; the loyal ones are missing among humans.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 12:2">2</a> They say meaningless things to each other; they speak with flattering lips and a double heart.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 12:3">3</a> God will stop all flattering lips and the tongue that speaks proud words.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 12:4">4</a> Those who say, &quot;We will succeed with our words; our lips belong to us. Who rules over us?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 12:5">5</a> Because poor people are hurt and needy people sigh, I will get up now, says God; I will save them from those who blow against them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 12:6">6</a> God&#39;s words are pure: like silver refined in a clay furnace, made pure seven times.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 12:7">7</a> You will protect them, God; you will keep them safe from this generation forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 12:8">8</a> Evil people are everywhere when the worst ones get power.</p>
