<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 80:1">1</a> Listen, Shepherd of Israel, you who guide Joseph like sheep; you who sit above the angels, show your light.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 80:2">2</a> Before the tribes of Ephraim, Benjamin, and Manasseh, wake up your power and come save us.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 80:3">3</a> Bring us back, God, make your face bright; then we will be saved.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 80:4">4</a> God of all, how long will you stay angry when your people pray to you?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 80:5">5</a> You feed them with tears for bread; and make them drink tears in abundance.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 80:6">6</a> You make us fight with our neighbors, and our enemies laugh at us.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 80:7">7</a> Bring us back, God of all, make your face shine on us, and we will be saved.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 80:8">8</a> You brought a vine from Egypt; you drove out the nations and planted it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 80:9">9</a> You prepared space for it, made its roots grow deep, and it spread throughout the land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 80:10">10</a> The hills were hidden by its shadow, and its branches were like the great cedars.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 80:11">11</a> She stretched her branches out to the sea and her limbs to the river.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 80:12">12</a> Why have you broken down her walls, so that everyone who goes by can pick from her?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Psalms 80:13">13</a> The wild pig from the forest destroys it, and the wild animal of the field eats it up.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Psalms 80:14">14</a> Please come back, God of power. Look from heaven, see, and take care of this vine.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Psalms 80:15">15</a> The vineyard your right hand planted and the shoot you strengthened for yourself.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Psalms 80:16">16</a> It is burned with fire, it is chopped down: they die by the scolding of your face.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Psalms 80:17">17</a> Let your hand be on the man you support, on the human being you have strengthened for your purpose.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Psalms 80:18">18</a> We won&#39;t turn away from you; give us life, and we will call on your name.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Psalms 80:19">19</a> Bring us back, God of all, make your face shine on us; then we will be saved.</p>
