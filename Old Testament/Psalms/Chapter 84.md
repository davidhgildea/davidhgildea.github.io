<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 84:1">1</a> How lovely are your homes, O God of armies!</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 84:2">2</a> My soul longs and even grows weak for God&#39;s courtyards; my heart and body cry out for the living God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 84:3">3</a> Yes, the sparrow has found a home, and the swallow a nest for herself, where she can lay her chicks, by your altars, O God of all, my King and my God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 84:4">4</a> Happy are those who live in your house; they will always praise you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 84:5">5</a> Happy is the person who finds strength in you; whose heart follows your paths.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 84:6">6</a> Traveling through the Valley of Baca, they turn it into a place with springs; the rain also fills the ponds.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 84:7">7</a> They grow stronger and stronger, and each appears before God in Zion.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 84:8">8</a> God of all, listen to my prayer: pay attention, God of Jacob. Pause.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 84:9">9</a> See, O God our protector, and gaze at your chosen one&#39;s face.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 84:10">10</a> One day in your courtyards is better than a thousand days elsewhere. I prefer to be a gatekeeper in the home of my God than to live in the homes of the wicked.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 84:11">11</a> God is like the sun and a protector: God will give kindness and honor: He won&#39;t keep back any good thing from those who live rightly.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 84:12">12</a> God of all, happy is the person who trusts in you.</p>
