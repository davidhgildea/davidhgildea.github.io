<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 7:1">1</a> God, I trust in you; save me from all who chase me and rescue me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 7:2">2</a> So he won&#39;t tear my soul apart like a lion and rip it to pieces when there is no one to save me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 7:3">3</a> God, if I did this; if I have done wrong;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 7:4">4</a> If I have given harm to someone who was at peace with me; (yes, I have freed someone who is my enemy for no reason:)</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 7:5">5</a> Let my enemy chase my soul and capture it; let him trample my life into the ground and drag my honor in the dust. Pause.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 7:6">6</a> Get up, God, with your anger, rise up because of my enemies&#39; fury, and wake up to give me the justice you have ordered.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 7:7">7</a> The community will surround you; because of them, come back to your high place.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 7:8">8</a> God will judge the people: judge me, God, based on my goodness and my honesty within me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 7:9">9</a> Let the bad actions of the wicked stop; but support the good people: because God, who is fair, tests our thoughts and feelings.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 7:10">10</a> God protects me and saves those with honest hearts.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 7:11">11</a> God judges good people and is angry with bad people every day.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 7:12">12</a> If he does not change, God will sharpen his sword; he has drawn his bow and is prepared.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Psalms 7:13">13</a> He has also prepared deadly tools for him; he sets his arrows against those who chase.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Psalms 7:14">14</a> Look, he struggles with wrongdoing, plans trouble, and gives birth to lies.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Psalms 7:15">15</a> He dug a pit and a trench and fell into the hole he made.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Psalms 7:16">16</a> His trouble will come back on him, and his violence will fall on his own head.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Psalms 7:17">17</a> I will thank God for His goodness, and sing to honor the name of God Most High.</p>
