<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 37:1">1</a> Don&#39;t worry about wrongdoers, and don&#39;t be jealous of those who do evil.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 37:2">2</a> They will quickly be chopped down like grass and dry up like green plants.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 37:3">3</a> Trust in God, and do good; then you will live safely in the land, and you will have plenty to eat.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 37:4">4</a> Enjoy being close to God, and he will give you what your heart wants.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 37:5">5</a> Trust your path to God; believe in him, and he will make it happen.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 37:6">6</a> He will make your goodness shine like the light, and your fair decisions like the midday sun.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 37:7">7</a> Trust in God and wait calmly for Him; don&#39;t worry about those who succeed in their bad plans.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 37:8">8</a> Stop being angry, leave behind rage; do not worry yourself at all to do wrong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 37:9">9</a> Those who do wrong will be removed, but those who wait for God will receive the earth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 37:10">10</a> Soon the wicked will be gone; you will look for them, but they won&#39;t be there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 37:11">11</a> The gentle will inherit the land and enjoy great peace.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 37:12">12</a> The bad person makes plans against the good person and shows anger by grinding his teeth at him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Psalms 37:13">13</a> God will laugh at him because He sees that his time is coming.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Psalms 37:14">14</a> Evil people have pulled out their swords and aimed their bows to knock down the poor and needy, and to kill those who live honestly.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Psalms 37:15">15</a> Their swords will stab their own hearts, and their bows will snap.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Psalms 37:16">16</a> A small amount that a good person has is better than the wealth of many bad people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Psalms 37:17">17</a> The power of the bad will be broken, but God supports the good.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Psalms 37:18">18</a> God knows the days of the good people, and their inheritance will last forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Psalms 37:19">19</a> They will not feel shame when times are bad; and during famine, they will have enough.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Psalms 37:20">20</a> But the bad people will die, and God&#39;s enemies will vanish like lamb fat in fire; they will disappear like smoke.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Psalms 37:21">21</a> The wicked borrow and do not repay, but the good are kind and give.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Psalms 37:22">22</a> Those blessed by God will inherit the land; those cursed by Him will be destroyed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Psalms 37:23">23</a> God guides a good person&#39;s path and finds joy in their journey.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Psalms 37:24">24</a> If he falls, he will not be completely defeated because God supports him with his hand.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Psalms 37:25">25</a> I was once young, and now I&#39;m old; but I&#39;ve never seen a good person abandoned, or their children having to beg for food.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Psalms 37:26">26</a> He is always kind and gives to others; and his children are blessed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Psalms 37:27">27</a> Turn away from bad things and do good things; live forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Psalms 37:28">28</a> God loves fairness and does not abandon his faithful people; they are protected forever, but the descendants of the wicked will be destroyed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Psalms 37:29">29</a> Good people will receive the land and live there always.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Psalms 37:30">30</a> The righteous person&#39;s mouth speaks wisdom, and their tongue discusses fairness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Psalms 37:31">31</a> God&#39;s law is in his heart; he will not slip.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Psalms 37:32">32</a> The bad person watches the good person and tries to kill him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Psalms 37:33">33</a> God will not let him be defeated or declare him guilty when he is on trial.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Psalms 37:34">34</a> Wait for God, follow His path, and He will raise you up to receive the land; when the wicked are removed, you will see it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Psalms 37:35">35</a> I have seen evil people with much power, growing strong like a healthy tree.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Psalms 37:36">36</a> He died, and suddenly he was gone. I looked for him, but he could not be found.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Psalms 37:37">37</a> Notice the good person, and see the honest one: for a peaceful end awaits that person.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Psalms 37:38">38</a> Wrongdoers will all be destroyed: the future of the wicked will end.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="Psalms 37:39">39</a> God saves good people; he is their protector when trouble comes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="Psalms 37:40">40</a> God will help them and rescue them; He will save them from the wicked because they rely on Him.</p>
