<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 17:1">1</a> Listen to me, God, hear my cry, pay attention to my prayer that comes from honest lips.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 17:2">2</a> Let your decision come from your presence; let your eyes see what is fair.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 17:3">3</a> You have tested my heart; you have visited me at night; you have examined me and will find no wrong; I have decided that my mouth will not sin.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 17:4">4</a> About what people do, following what you have said, I have stayed away from the harmful ways.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 17:5">5</a> Guide my steps on your paths, so I don&#39;t slip.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 17:6">6</a> I have called to you, because you will listen to me, God: turn your ear to me, and listen to what I say.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 17:7">7</a> Show your wonderful love, O God, who saves with your right hand those who trust in you from their enemies.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 17:8">8</a> Protect me like you would your most cherished possession; hide me in the safety of your wings.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 17:9">9</a> Save me from the wicked who attack me, from my deadly enemies who surround me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 17:10">10</a> They are surrounded by their own fat; they speak with proud mouths.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 17:11">11</a> They have surrounded us wherever we go. They watch us closely, looking to the ground.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 17:12">12</a> Like a lion hungry for its catch, and like a young lion hiding and waiting.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Psalms 17:13">13</a> Stand up, God, let him be defeated and pushed down: save me from the evil one who is your weapon.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Psalms 17:14">14</a> You save me from people, God, from worldly people who get their share in this life, whose stomach you fill with your treasures: they have many children and leave what&#39;s left to their little ones.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Psalms 17:15">15</a> I will see your face in fairness; I will be content when I wake up to be like you.</p>
