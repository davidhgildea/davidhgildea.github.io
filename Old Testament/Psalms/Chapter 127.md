<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 127:1">1</a> Unless God builds the house, those who work on it waste their effort; unless God protects the city, the guard stays awake for nothing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 127:2">2</a> It&#39;s useless to wake up early and go to bed late, working hard for a living; God gives rest to those he loves.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 127:3">3</a> Children are a gift from God, and the babies born to us are his reward.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 127:4">4</a> Like arrows in a strong person&#39;s hand are the children born in one&#39;s youth.</p>
