<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 34:1">1</a> I will always bless God; I will constantly praise him with my words.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 34:2">2</a> I will proudly speak of God; the humble will hear and be happy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 34:3">3</a> Let&#39;s praise God together and lift up his name.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 34:4">4</a> I looked for God, and he listened to me, and saved me from all my worries.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 34:5">5</a> They looked at him and found brightness; their faces showed no shame.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 34:6">6</a> This man in need cried out, and God heard him, and rescued him from all his troubles.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 34:7">7</a> God&#39;s angel camps around those who respect him and saves them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 34:8">8</a> Try and discover that God is good: happy is the person who trusts in Him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 34:9">9</a> Respect God, all you who are his holy people, because those who respect him will have everything they need.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 34:10">10</a> Young lions may go hungry, but those who look for God will not lack anything good.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 34:11">11</a> Come, children, listen to me: I will teach you to reverence God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 34:12">12</a> Who is the person who wants a long life to enjoy good things?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Psalms 34:13">13</a> Control your tongue from evil, and keep your lips from speaking deceit.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Psalms 34:14">14</a> Turn away from bad things, and do good things; look for peace, and keep trying to get it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Psalms 34:15">15</a> God watches over good people, and he listens to their calls for help.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Psalms 34:16">16</a> God is against those who do evil, to remove any memory of them from the earth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Psalms 34:17">17</a> Good people cry out, and God hears them, and saves them from all their troubles.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Psalms 34:18">18</a> God is close to those with a broken heart and saves those with a humble spirit.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Psalms 34:19">19</a> Righteous people have many troubles, but God saves them from every one.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Psalms 34:20">20</a> He protects all his bones; not even one is broken.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Psalms 34:21">21</a> Wrongdoing will kill the bad people; those who dislike good people will end up alone.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Psalms 34:22">22</a> God saves the souls of his servants, and none who trust in him will be abandoned.</p>
