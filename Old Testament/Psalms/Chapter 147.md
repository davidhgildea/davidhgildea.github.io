<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 147:1">1</a> Praise God: because it is good to sing to our God; it is enjoyable; and praise looks good.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 147:2">2</a> God builds up Jerusalem; he brings together Israel&#39;s scattered people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 147:3">3</a> He heals those with a broken heart, and wraps their wounds.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 147:4">4</a> He counts the number of the stars; he calls each one by its name.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 147:5">5</a> Our God is great and very powerful; his wisdom has no end.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 147:6">6</a> God lifts up the humble; he throws the wicked down to the ground.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 147:7">7</a> Sing to God with thanks; play the harp to praise our God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 147:8">8</a> God covers the sky with clouds, gets rain ready for the earth, and makes grass grow on the mountains.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 147:9">9</a> He gives food to the animals and feeds the crying young ravens.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 147:10">10</a> God does not enjoy the power of the horse; he does not like the strength of a person&#39;s legs.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 147:11">11</a> God finds joy in those who respect him and in those who trust in his kindness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 147:12">12</a> Praise God, O Jerusalem; praise your God, O Zion.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Psalms 147:13">13</a> God has made your city gates strong; He has blessed your children inside your walls.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Psalms 147:14">14</a> He brings peace to your land and gives you the best wheat.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Psalms 147:15">15</a> He sends out his command to the earth: his message spreads very fast.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Psalms 147:16">16</a> He gives snow like wool; he spreads the frost like ashes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Psalms 147:17">17</a> He throws his ice like small pieces: who can withstand his cold?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Psalms 147:18">18</a> He sends out his word and melts them; he makes his wind blow, and the waters flow.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Psalms 147:19">19</a> He shows his words to Jacob, his laws and decisions to Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Psalms 147:20">20</a> God has not treated any other nation like this; they have not understood his laws. Praise God.</p>
