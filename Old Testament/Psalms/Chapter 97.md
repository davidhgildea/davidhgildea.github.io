<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 97:1">1</a> God rules; let the earth be happy; let the many islands be joyful.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 97:2">2</a> Clouds and darkness surround God; fairness and justice are where his throne is.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 97:3">3</a> A fire goes before him and burns up his enemies all around.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 97:4">4</a> God&#39;s lightning lit up the world; the earth saw it and shook.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 97:5">5</a> The hills melted like wax when God appeared, when the God of the whole earth came near.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 97:6">6</a> The sky shows God&#39;s goodness, and everyone sees his greatness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 97:7">7</a> Let those who worship idols and are proud of their false gods be confused: let all gods bow down to Him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Psalms 97:8">8</a> Zion heard and was happy; the women of Judah were joyful because of your decisions, God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Psalms 97:9">9</a> For you, God, are higher than all the earth; you are lifted up far above all gods.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Psalms 97:10">10</a> You who love God, hate evil; God protects his holy people and saves them from the wicked.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Psalms 97:11">11</a> Light is given to the good, and joy to the honest in heart.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Psalms 97:12">12</a> Be happy with God, you good people; and say thanks when you think of his goodness.</p>
