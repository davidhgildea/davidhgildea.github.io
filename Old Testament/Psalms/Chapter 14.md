<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Psalms 14:1">1</a> The fool thinks to himself, there is no God. They are bad, they do terrible things, no one does good.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Psalms 14:2">2</a> God looked from heaven at people to see if anyone understood and looked for God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Psalms 14:3">3</a> Everyone has turned away, they have all become corrupt; no one does good, not even one.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Psalms 14:4">4</a> Do all the wrongdoers lack understanding? They devour my people as if eating bread, without calling on God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Psalms 14:5">5</a> They were greatly afraid because God is with those who are righteous.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Psalms 14:6">6</a> You have made the advice of the poor seem worthless because God is their shelter.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Psalms 14:7">7</a> I wish God would save Israel from Zion! When God frees his people, Jacob will be happy, and Israel will be joyful.</p>
