<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="2 Kings 9:1">1</a> The prophet Elisha called one of the student prophets and said to him, &quot;Tie up your clothes and take this jar of oil in your hand, and go to Ramoth-gilead.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="2 Kings 9:2">2</a> When you get there, find Jehu, the son of Jehoshaphat, the son of Nimshi. Go inside, ask him to stand up from his brothers, and take him to a private room.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="2 Kings 9:3">3</a> Take the bottle of oil, pour it on his head, and say, &quot;God says, &#39;I have made you king over Israel.&#39;&quot; Then open the door, run away, and don&#39;t stop.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="2 Kings 9:4">4</a> The young man, the prophet, went to Ramothgilead.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="2 Kings 9:5">5</a> When he arrived, he saw the army leaders sitting and said, &quot;I have a message for you, captain.&quot; Jehu asked, &quot;Which one of us?&quot; He replied, &quot;For you, captain.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="2 Kings 9:6">6</a> He got up, went into the house, put oil on his head, and said, &quot;God of Israel says, I have made you king over God&#39;s people, over Israel.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="2 Kings 9:7">7</a> You will strike down the family of Ahab your master, so I can get revenge for the murder of my prophets and all of God&#39;s servants killed by Jezebel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="2 Kings 9:8">8</a> Everyone from Ahab&#39;s family will die; I will destroy every male in Ahab&#39;s family, whether free or imprisoned in Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="2 Kings 9:9">9</a> I will make Ahab&#39;s family like Jeroboam&#39;s family, the son of Nebat, and like Baasha&#39;s family, the son of Ahijah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="2 Kings 9:10">10</a> Dogs will eat Jezebel in Jezreel&#39;s land, and no one will bury her. He opened the door and ran away.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="2 Kings 9:11">11</a> Then Jehu went out to his master&#39;s servants. One asked him, &quot;Is everything okay? Why did this crazy man come to you?&quot; Jehu replied, &quot;You know the man and what he talks about.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="2 Kings 9:12">12</a> They said, &quot;That&#39;s not true; tell us.&quot; He replied, &quot;He told me this: &#39;God says, I have chosen you to be king of Israel.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="2 Kings 9:13">13</a> They hurried, each man took his clothes, laid them on the steps under him, and blowing trumpets, said, &quot;Jehu is king.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="2 Kings 9:14">14</a> Jehu, son of Jehoshaphat and grandson of Nimshi, plotted against Joram. Joram was defending Ramoth-gilead with all of Israel&#39;s help against Hazael, the king of Syria.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="2 Kings 9:15">15</a> King Joram went back to recover in Jezreel from injuries the Syrians caused him during his battle with Hazael, the king of Syria. Jehu said, &quot;If you agree, make sure no one leaves the city to report this in Jezreel.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="2 Kings 9:16">16</a> So Jehu rode in a chariot to Jezreel because Joram was there. Ahaziah, the king of Judah, had come to visit Joram.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="2 Kings 9:17">17</a> A guard on the tower in Jezreel saw Jehu&#39;s group coming and said, &quot;I see a group.&quot; Joram said, &quot;Send a horseman to ask if they come in peace.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="2 Kings 9:18">18</a> Someone rode out to meet him and said, &quot;The king asks, &#39;Is everything peaceful?&#39;&quot; Jehu replied, &quot;What concern is peace to you? Follow me.&quot; The lookout reported, &quot;The messenger reached them but isn&#39;t coming back.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="2 Kings 9:19">19</a> He sent another rider who went to them and said, &quot;The king asks, &#39;Is everything peaceful?&#39;&quot; Jehu replied, &quot;What does peace matter to you? Follow me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="2 Kings 9:20">20</a> The guard reported, &quot;He reached them but hasn&#39;t returned; his driving style is like Jehu, Nimshi&#39;s son, because he drives very fast.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="2 Kings 9:21">21</a> Joram said, &quot;Get ready.&quot; His chariot was prepared. Joram, king of Israel, and Ahaziah, king of Judah, each rode in their chariots to confront Jehu. They met him on Naboth the Jezreelite&#39;s land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="2 Kings 9:22">22</a> When Joram saw Jehu, he asked, &quot;Is it peace, Jehu?&quot; Jehu replied, &quot;How can there be peace while your mother Jezebel does so many acts of unfaithfulness and witchcraft?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="2 Kings 9:23">23</a> Joram turned around, ran away, and said to Ahaziah, &quot;It&#39;s a trap, Ahaziah.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="2 Kings 9:24">24</a> Jehu pulled his bow with all his strength and hit Jehoram between his arms; the arrow came out through his heart, and he slumped down in his chariot.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="2 Kings 9:25">25</a> Jehu told his officer Bidkar, &quot;Pick him up and throw him into Naboth the Jezreelite&#39;s field. Remember when you and I rode together after Ahab&#39;s father, God placed this judgment on him.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="2 Kings 9:26">26</a> Indeed, I saw yesterday the murder of Naboth and his sons, says God; and I will repay you on this piece of land, says God. Now, take him and throw him onto this piece of land, following God&#39;s command.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="2 Kings 9:27">27</a> But when Ahaziah the king of Judah saw it, he ran through the garden house. Jehu chased him, saying, &quot;Hit him in his chariot too.&quot; They did that near Gur, close to Ibleam. He escaped to Megiddo and died there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="2 Kings 9:28">28</a> His servants took him by chariot to Jerusalem and buried him in his tomb with his ancestors in the city of David.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="2 Kings 9:29">29</a> In the eleventh year of Joram, Ahaziah, Ahab&#39;s son, started to rule over Judah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="2 Kings 9:30">30</a> When Jehu arrived in Jezreel, Jezebel heard about it. She put makeup on her face, did her hair, and looked out the window.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="2 Kings 9:31">31</a> As Jehu came through the gate, she asked, &quot;Did Zimri have peace after he killed his master?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="2 Kings 9:32">32</a> He looked up at the window and asked, &quot;Who&#39;s with me? Who?&quot; Two or three eunuchs peered down at him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="2 Kings 9:33">33</a> He said, &quot;Throw her down.&quot; So they threw her down, and her blood splattered on the wall and horses, and he trampled her.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="2 Kings 9:34">34</a> When he came in, he ate and drank, and said, Go and see that cursed woman, and bury her, because she is the daughter of a king.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="2 Kings 9:35">35</a> They went to bury her, but only found her skull, feet, and the palms of her hands.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="2 Kings 9:36">36</a> So they returned and reported to him. He said, &quot;This is God&#39;s message, which He gave through His servant Elijah the Tishbite: &#39;In the area of Jezreel, dogs will eat Jezebel&#39;s body.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="2 Kings 9:37">37</a> Jezebel&#39;s dead body will be like manure on the ground in Jezreel, so no one can say, &quot;This is Jezebel.&quot;</p>
