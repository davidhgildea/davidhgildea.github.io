<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="2 Kings 2:1">1</a> When God was about to take Elijah to heaven in a whirlwind, Elijah and Elisha left Gilgal together.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="2 Kings 2:2">2</a> Elijah said to Elisha, &quot;Please stay here because God has sent me to Bethel.&quot; Elisha replied, &quot;As long as God lives and you are alive, I will not leave you.&quot; So they went to Bethel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="2 Kings 2:3">3</a> The prophet students in Bethel went to Elisha and told him, &quot;Do you know God will take your leader away from you today?&quot; He replied, &quot;Yes, I know; be quiet.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="2 Kings 2:4">4</a> Elijah told him, &quot;Elisha, please stay here because God has sent me to Jericho.&quot; Elisha replied, &quot;As surely as God lives and you are alive, I won&#39;t leave you.&quot; So they went to Jericho together.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="2 Kings 2:5">5</a> The prophets&#39; sons in Jericho approached Elisha and asked, &quot;Do you know that God will take your leader away from you today?&quot; He replied, &quot;Yes, I know; be quiet.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="2 Kings 2:6">6</a> Elijah told him, &quot;Please stay here, because God has sent me to Jordan.&quot; He replied, &quot;As surely as God lives, and as you live, I won&#39;t leave you.&quot; So they both went together.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="2 Kings 2:7">7</a> Fifty sons of the prophets went and watched from a distance while the two of them stood by the Jordan River.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="2 Kings 2:8">8</a> Elijah took his cloak, rolled it up, struck the water, and it split apart so they both could cross on dry land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="2 Kings 2:9">9</a> When they had crossed over, Elijah said to Elisha, &quot;Tell me what I can do for you before I am taken from you.&quot; Elisha replied, &quot;Please, let me inherit a double share of your spirit.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="2 Kings 2:10">10</a> He said, &quot;You&#39;ve asked for something difficult. But if you see me as I am being taken from you, you will get what you asked for. If you don&#39;t see me, you won&#39;t get it.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="2 Kings 2:11">11</a> As they were walking and talking, suddenly a fiery chariot and fiery horses appeared and separated them; then Elijah was taken up into heaven by a whirlwind.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="2 Kings 2:12">12</a> Elisha saw it and yelled, &quot;My father, my father, the chariots and horsemen of Israel!&quot; He saw him no more, grabbed his clothes, and tore them in half.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="2 Kings 2:13">13</a> He picked up Elijah’s cloak that had fallen from him, went back, and stood by the Jordan River’s edge.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="2 Kings 2:14">14</a> He took Elijah&#39;s cloak that dropped, hit the water, and said, &quot;Where is the God of Elijah?&quot; After he struck the water, it split apart, and Elisha crossed over.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="2 Kings 2:15">15</a> When the prophet students in Jericho saw him, they said, &quot;Elijah&#39;s spirit now rests on Elisha.&quot; They went to meet him and bowed down to the ground before him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="2 Kings 2:16">16</a> They said to him, &quot;Look, we have fifty strong men with us. Please let them go and look for your master. Maybe God&#39;s Spirit lifted him and put him on a mountain or in a valley.&quot; But he answered, &quot;Do not send them.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="2 Kings 2:17">17</a> When they pressed him until he felt embarrassed, he said, &quot;Go.&quot; So they sent fifty men who searched for three days but did not find him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="2 Kings 2:18">18</a> When they returned to him (since he stayed in Jericho), he said, &quot;Didn&#39;t I tell you not to go?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="2 Kings 2:19">19</a> The city men told Elisha, &quot;Please look, the place of this city is nice, as you see: but the water is bad, and the land does not produce.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="2 Kings 2:20">20</a> He said, &quot;Bring me a new jar and put salt in it.&quot; And they brought it to him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="2 Kings 2:21">21</a> He went to the water spring, threw in the salt, and said, &quot;God says, &#39;I have made these waters healthy; from now on, there will be no more death or unfruitful land here.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="2 Kings 2:22">22</a> The waters are still healed today, just as Elisha had said.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="2 Kings 2:23">23</a> He went from there to Bethel, and as he was walking along the road, some children came out of the city and made fun of him, saying, &quot;Go on up, you baldy! Go on up, you baldy!&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="2 Kings 2:24">24</a> He turned around, saw them, and cursed them in God&#39;s name. Then two bears came out of the woods and attacked forty-two of the youths.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="2 Kings 2:25">25</a> He left there for Mount Carmel, and then he went back to Samaria.</p>
