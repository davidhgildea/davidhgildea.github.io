<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="2 Kings 1:1">1</a> Moab fought against Israel after Ahab died.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="2 Kings 1:2">2</a> Ahaziah fell through a grid in his upstairs room in Samaria and was injured. He sent messengers and told them, &quot;Go, ask Baalzebub, the god of Ekron, if I will get better from my illness.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="2 Kings 1:3">3</a> The angel said to Elijah the Tishbite, &quot;Get up, go to meet the king of Samaria&#39;s messengers, and ask them, &#39;Do you look for advice from Baalzebub, the god of Ekron, because you think there is no God in Israel?&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="2 Kings 1:4">4</a> So now, God says, &quot;You will not get up from the bed you are lying on; you will certainly die.&quot; Then Elijah left.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="2 Kings 1:5">5</a> When the messengers returned to him, he asked them, &quot;Why have you come back?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="2 Kings 1:6">6</a> They told him, &quot;A man came to meet us and he said, &#39;Go back to the king who sent you and tell him, &quot;God says, &#39;Are you asking Baalzebub, the god of Ekron, for help because there&#39;s no God in Israel? You won&#39;t get up from the bed you&#39;re lying on; you will definitely die.&#39;&quot;&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="2 Kings 1:7">7</a> He asked them, &quot;What kind of man met you and spoke these words to you?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="2 Kings 1:8">8</a> They replied, &quot;He was a hairy man, wearing a leather belt around his waist.&quot; And he said, &quot;That&#39;s Elijah the Tishbite.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="2 Kings 1:9">9</a> The king sent a leader of fifty men with his fifty to him. He approached and saw him sitting on the hilltop. He said to him, &quot;Man of God, the king orders you to come down.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="2 Kings 1:10">10</a> Elijah replied to the officer of fifty, &quot;If I am a man of God, may fire fall from the sky and burn you and your fifty men.&quot; And fire fell from the sky and burned him and his fifty men.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="2 Kings 1:11">11</a> Once more, he sent another captain with his fifty men. He spoke to him, saying, &quot;Man of God, the king orders you to come down at once.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="2 Kings 1:12">12</a> Elijah replied, &quot;If I am a man of God, may fire fall from the sky and burn you and your fifty men.&quot; And God&#39;s fire fell from the sky and burned him and his fifty men.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="2 Kings 1:13">13</a> He sent another leader of fifty with his soldiers. This third leader went to Elijah, knelt down before him, begged him, and said, &quot;Please, man of God, value my life and the lives of these fifty men serving you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="2 Kings 1:14">14</a> Look, fire came down from the sky and burned up the two leaders and their groups of fifty; so now, please value my life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="2 Kings 1:15">15</a> God&#39;s angel said to Elijah, &quot;Go with him. Don&#39;t be afraid of him.&quot; So Elijah got up and went with him to the king.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="2 Kings 1:16">16</a> He told him, &quot;God says, &#39;Because you sent messengers to ask Baalzebub, the god of Ekron, instead of asking me, isn&#39;t it because you think there&#39;s no God in Israel to seek advice from? So, you won&#39;t leave the bed you&#39;re lying on; you will definitely die.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="2 Kings 1:17">17</a> He died as God told through Elijah. Jehoram became king after him in the second year of Jehoram, Jehoshaphat&#39;s son, king of Judah, because he had no son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="2 Kings 1:18">18</a> The other things Ahaziah did are written in the history book of Israel&#39;s kings, aren&#39;t they?</p>
