<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="2 Kings 4:1">1</a> A woman whose husband had died, who was one of the prophets&#39; sons, called out to Elisha, &quot;My husband, your servant, is dead. You know he respected God. Now a creditor is here to take my two sons as slaves.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="2 Kings 4:2">2</a> Elisha asked her, &quot;What can I do for you? Tell me, what do you have at home?&quot; She replied, &quot;Your servant has nothing at home except a jar of oil.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="2 Kings 4:3">3</a> He said, &quot;Go, ask your neighbors for empty jars. Get a lot of them.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="2 Kings 4:4">4</a> When you enter, close the door behind you and your sons, pour oil into all the jars, and put aside the ones that are full.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="2 Kings 4:5">5</a> She left him, closed the door behind her and her sons, they brought the containers to her, and she filled them up.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="2 Kings 4:6">6</a> When the jars were all full, she told her son, &quot;Bring me another jar.&quot; But he replied, &quot;There are no more jars.&quot; Then the oil stopped flowing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="2 Kings 4:7">7</a> She went to the man of God and told him. He said, &quot;Go, sell the oil, pay what you owe, and use the rest to live on with your children.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="2 Kings 4:8">8</a> One day, Elisha went to Shunem, where a notable woman lived; she urged him to have some bread. From then on, whenever he came by, he would stop there to eat bread.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="2 Kings 4:9">9</a> She told her husband, &quot;Look, I think this is a holy man of God who keeps passing by us.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="2 Kings 4:10">10</a> Let&#39;s build a small room on the wall. We&#39;ll put a bed, a table, a chair, and a lamp in it for him. So when he visits, he can stay there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="2 Kings 4:11">11</a> One day he went there, entered the room, and rested.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="2 Kings 4:12">12</a> He told his servant Gehazi, &quot;Call this woman from Shunem.&quot; When Gehazi called her, she came and stood before him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="2 Kings 4:13">13</a> He told him, &quot;Tell her, &#39;Look, you have done so much for us; what can we do for you? Do you want us to speak to the king or the army commander on your behalf?&#39;&quot; And she replied, &quot;I live among my own people.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="2 Kings 4:14">14</a> He asked, &quot;So what can be done for her?&quot; Gehazi replied, &quot;Truly, she has no child, and her husband is old.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="2 Kings 4:15">15</a> He said, &quot;Call her.&quot; When he called her, she stood at the door.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="2 Kings 4:16">16</a> He said, &quot;By this time next year, you will hold a son in your arms.&quot; But she replied, &quot;No, my lord, you man of God, do not deceive your servant.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="2 Kings 4:17">17</a> The woman became pregnant and gave birth to a son at the time Elisha had told her, when it was due.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="2 Kings 4:18">18</a> When the child grew up, one day he went to his father with the harvesters.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="2 Kings 4:19">19</a> He told his father, &quot;My head hurts.&quot; His father said to a boy, &quot;Take him to his mother.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="2 Kings 4:20">20</a> After he took him and brought him to his mother, he sat on her lap until noon, and then he died.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="2 Kings 4:21">21</a> She went up, put him on the man of God&#39;s bed, closed the door, and left.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="2 Kings 4:22">22</a> She asked her husband, &quot;Please send one of the young men and a donkey with me so I can go to the man of God and return.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="2 Kings 4:23">23</a> He asked, &quot;Why do you want to go to him today? It&#39;s not the new moon or the Sabbath.&quot; She answered, &quot;It will be okay.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="2 Kings 4:24">24</a> She put a saddle on a donkey, and told her servant, &quot;Go ahead; don&#39;t slow down the ride for me unless I tell you to.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="2 Kings 4:25">25</a> She went to the man of God at Mount Carmel. When the man of God saw her from a distance, he said to his servant Gehazi, &quot;Look, there is the woman from Shunem.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="2 Kings 4:26">26</a> Please go now and meet her. Ask her if she is okay, if her husband is okay, and if her child is okay. She replied, &quot;All is well.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="2 Kings 4:27">27</a> When she arrived at the hill where the man of God was, she grabbed his feet. Gehazi tried to push her away, but the man of God said, &quot;Leave her be; she is deeply troubled, and God has kept the reason hidden from me and hasn&#39;t told me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="2 Kings 4:28">28</a> Then she said, &quot;Did I ask for a son from you, God? Didn&#39;t I say, &#39;Don&#39;t trick me&#39;?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="2 Kings 4:29">29</a> He told Gehazi, &quot;Tie your clothes tight, take my staff in your hand, and go. If you meet anyone, don&#39;t say hello; if they greet you, don&#39;t reply. Put my staff on the child&#39;s face.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="2 Kings 4:30">30</a> The child&#39;s mother said, &quot;As long as God lives and you are alive, I won&#39;t leave you.&quot; So he got up and went with her.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="2 Kings 4:31">31</a> Gehazi went ahead of them and put the staff on the child&#39;s face; but there was no sound or response. So he returned to meet him and told him, &quot;The child has not woken up.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="2 Kings 4:32">32</a> When Elisha entered the house, he saw the child was dead and lying on his bed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="2 Kings 4:33">33</a> He went in, closed the door behind them both, and prayed to God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="2 Kings 4:34">34</a> He went up, lay on the child, put his mouth on the child&#39;s mouth, his eyes on the child&#39;s eyes, his hands on the child&#39;s hands, extended himself on the child, and the child&#39;s body became warm.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="2 Kings 4:35">35</a> He went back and forth inside the house, then went up and lay on top of the boy. The boy sneezed seven times and opened his eyes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="2 Kings 4:36">36</a> He called Gehazi and said, &quot;Call this woman from Shunem.&quot; So he called her. When she came to him, he said, &quot;Pick up your son.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="2 Kings 4:37">37</a> She went in, knelt at his feet, bowed down, picked up her son, and left.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="2 Kings 4:38">38</a> Elisha went back to Gilgal where there was a famine. The prophets&#39; sons were with him. He told his helper, &quot;Put on the big pot and cook some stew for the prophets&#39; sons.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="2 Kings 4:39">39</a> Someone went to the field to pick herbs, found a wild vine, collected a bunch of wild gourds, and came back to cut them up into the pot of soup, because they didn&#39;t know what they were.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="2 Kings 4:40">40</a> They served the food to the men to eat. But while they were eating the stew, they shouted, &quot;Man of God, there is poison in this stew!&quot; So they could not eat it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="2 Kings 4:41">41</a> But he said, &quot;Then bring flour.&quot; And he threw it into the pot; and he said, &quot;Serve it to the people, so they can eat.&quot; And there was nothing bad in the pot.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="2 Kings 4:42">42</a> A man from Baalshalisha came and brought the man of God twenty barley loaves and fresh ears of corn. He said, &quot;Give it to the people to eat.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v43"><a class="verse-num" href="#v43" aria-label="2 Kings 4:43">43</a> His servant asked, &quot;How can I feed a hundred men with this?&quot; He answered, &quot;Give it to the people to eat, because God says they will eat and have some left over.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v44"><a class="verse-num" href="#v44" aria-label="2 Kings 4:44">44</a> He placed the food before them, and they ate and left some, as God had said.</p>
