<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="2 Kings 5:1">1</a> Naaman, the leader of the Syrian king&#39;s army, was important to his king and respected because through him, God had let Syria win battles. He was also very brave, but he had leprosy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="2 Kings 5:2">2</a> The Syrians had attacked in groups and had taken a young girl prisoner from Israel; she served Naaman&#39;s wife.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="2 Kings 5:3">3</a> She told her mistress, &quot;I wish my master were with the prophet in Samaria, because he could cure his leprosy.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="2 Kings 5:4">4</a> Someone went and told his master, &quot;The girl from Israel said this and this.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="2 Kings 5:5">5</a> The king of Syria said, &quot;Go ahead, go, and I will send a letter to the king of Israel.&quot; He left, taking with him ten talents of silver, six thousand gold coins, and ten sets of clothes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="2 Kings 5:6">6</a> He took the letter to the king of Israel, which said, &quot;When you get this letter, know that I have sent my servant Naaman to you so you can heal him of his leprosy.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="2 Kings 5:7">7</a> When the king of Israel read the letter, he tore his clothes and said, &quot;Am I God, able to kill or bring to life, that this man sends someone to me to heal his leprosy? See how he is trying to start a fight with me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="2 Kings 5:8">8</a> When Elisha, the man of God, found out that the king of Israel had torn his clothes, he sent a message to the king asking, &quot;Why have you torn your clothes? Let him come to me, and he will learn that there is a prophet in Israel.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="2 Kings 5:9">9</a> Naaman came with his horses and chariot, and stopped at the door of Elisha&#39;s house.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="2 Kings 5:10">10</a> Elisha sent a messenger to him, saying, &quot;Go and bathe in the Jordan River seven times, and your skin will be healed, and you will be clean.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="2 Kings 5:11">11</a> But Naaman was angry and left, saying, &quot;Look, I thought he would surely come out to me, stand, and pray to God, then wave his hand over the spot and heal the skin disease.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="2 Kings 5:12">12</a> Aren&#39;t Abana and Pharpar, rivers of Damascus, better than all Israel&#39;s waters? Can&#39;t I wash in them and be clean? So he left, angry.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="2 Kings 5:13">13</a> His helpers came close and spoke to him, saying, &quot;If the prophet had told you to do something big, you would have done it, right? So, shouldn&#39;t you do it when he simply tells you, &#39;Wash and be clean&#39;?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="2 Kings 5:14">14</a> He went down and dipped himself seven times in the Jordan River, as God&#39;s man had said. His skin became like a young child&#39;s, and he was clean.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="2 Kings 5:15">15</a> He went back to the man of God with his group. He stood in front of him and said, &quot;Now I understand there is no God anywhere on earth except in Israel. Please accept a gift from me, your servant.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="2 Kings 5:16">16</a> He said, &quot;As God lives, before whom I serve, I won&#39;t take anything.&quot; Though he was pressed to accept, he still refused.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="2 Kings 5:17">17</a> Naaman asked, &quot;Please, can your servant take two mules&#39; load of soil? Because from now on, your servant will not offer burnt offerings or sacrifices to other gods, only to God.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="2 Kings 5:18">18</a> Please God, forgive me, your servant, for when my boss goes into Rimmon&#39;s temple to pray and leans on me, and I kneel down in Rimmon&#39;s temple, please forgive me for this.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="2 Kings 5:19">19</a> He said to him, &quot;Leave in peace.&quot; So he left and went a short distance away.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="2 Kings 5:20">20</a> Gehazi, Elisha&#39;s helper, thought, &quot;Look, my boss didn&#39;t take what Naaman the Syrian offered. But as God lives, I&#39;ll chase after him and get something from him.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="2 Kings 5:21">21</a> So Gehazi ran after Naaman. When Naaman saw him coming, he got off his chariot to meet him and asked, &quot;Is everything okay?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="2 Kings 5:22">22</a> He said, &quot;Everything is fine. My master sent me, saying, &#39;Look, two young prophets from Mount Ephraim have just come to me. Please give them some silver and two sets of clothes.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="2 Kings 5:23">23</a> Naaman said, &quot;Please, take two talents.&quot; He insisted, so he put two talents of silver in two bags, along with two sets of clothes, and placed them on two of his servants; and they carried them ahead of him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="2 Kings 5:24">24</a> When he got to the tower, he took the items from their hands, placed them in the house, and allowed the men to leave, and they went away.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="2 Kings 5:25">25</a> He went in and stood before his boss. Elisha asked him, &quot;Where did you come from, Gehazi?&quot; And he replied, &quot;Your servant didn&#39;t go anywhere.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="2 Kings 5:26">26</a> He asked him, &quot;Didn&#39;t I feel it when the man came back from his chariot to meet you? Is this the time to take money, clothes, olive groves, vineyards, sheep, cattle, male and female servants?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="2 Kings 5:27">27</a> Naaman&#39;s leprosy will stick to you and your descendants forever. He left with skin as white as a leper&#39;s.</p>
