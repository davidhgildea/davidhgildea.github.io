<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="2 Kings 21:1">1</a> Manasseh was twelve when he started to rule, and he ruled for fifty-five years in Jerusalem. His mother was named Hephzibah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="2 Kings 21:2">2</a> He did evil things that God hated, imitating the wicked practices of the nations that God had thrown out before the Israelites.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="2 Kings 21:3">3</a> He rebuilt the high places that his father Hezekiah had torn down; he set up altars for Baal and made a sacred garden, just like Ahab king of Israel did; and he worshipped the whole army of heaven and served them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="2 Kings 21:4">4</a> He made altars in God&#39;s house, where God said, &quot;I will place my name in Jerusalem.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="2 Kings 21:5">5</a> He made altars for all the sky&#39;s stars in the two yards of God&#39;s house.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="2 Kings 21:6">6</a> He made his son walk through fire, tracked seasons for omens, cast spells, and talked with spirits and sorcerers. He did many evil things, making God very angry.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="2 Kings 21:7">7</a> He placed an idol of the grove he made in the temple, where God told David and his son Solomon, &quot;In this temple and Jerusalem, which I chose from all the Israelite tribes, I will put my name forever.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="2 Kings 21:8">8</a> I will not make Israel leave the land I gave their ancestors, as long as they follow all my commands and the laws Moses gave them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="2 Kings 21:9">9</a> But they did not listen, and Manasseh led them to do more evil than the nations God had destroyed before the Israelites.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="2 Kings 21:10">10</a> God spoke through his prophets, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="2 Kings 21:11">11</a> Because Manasseh, the king of Judah, did these terrible things and acted more wickedly than the Amorites before him, making Judah sin with his idols.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="2 Kings 21:12">12</a> So this is what God of Israel says: Look, I will bring such disaster on Jerusalem and Judah that anyone who hears about it will feel their ears buzzing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="2 Kings 21:13">13</a> I will judge Jerusalem as I did Samaria and the family of Ahab. I will clean Jerusalem like someone cleans a dish, scrubbing it and flipping it over.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="2 Kings 21:14">14</a> I will abandon the rest of my people, give them to their enemies, and they will be taken and stolen by all their enemies.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="2 Kings 21:15">15</a> They did evil things that upset me from the time their ancestors left Egypt until today.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="2 Kings 21:16">16</a> Manasseh killed many innocent people, filling Jerusalem with blood from one end to the other; this was in addition to the sin of leading Judah to do evil, which was wrong in God&#39;s eyes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="2 Kings 21:17">17</a> The other things Manasseh did, his actions, and his sins are written in the chronicles of the kings of Judah, right?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="2 Kings 21:18">18</a> Manasseh died and was buried in his own garden, the garden of Uzza, and his son Amon became king after him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="2 Kings 21:19">19</a> Amon was 22 years old when he became king, and he ruled for two years in Jerusalem. His mother was Meshullemeth, the daughter of Haruz from Jotbah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="2 Kings 21:20">20</a> He did evil things that God saw, just like his father Manasseh did.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="2 Kings 21:21">21</a> He followed in his father&#39;s footsteps, worshiping the idols his father worshiped.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="2 Kings 21:22">22</a> He left God of his ancestors and did not follow God&#39;s way.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="2 Kings 21:23">23</a> Amon&#39;s servants plotted against him and killed the king in his own home.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="2 Kings 21:24">24</a> The people of the land killed all who plotted against King Amon, and they made Josiah, his son, king in his place.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="2 Kings 21:25">25</a> The other things Amon did are written in the book of the history of Judah&#39;s kings, aren&#39;t they?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="2 Kings 21:26">26</a> He was buried in his tomb in Uzza&#39;s garden, and his son Josiah became king after him.</p>
