<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Zephaniah 1:1">1</a> God&#39;s message came to Zephaniah, Cushi&#39;s son, Gedaliah&#39;s grandson, Amariah&#39;s great-grandson, and Hizkiah&#39;s great-great-grandson, during Josiah&#39;s time, Amon&#39;s son, who was king of Judah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Zephaniah 1:2">2</a> I will completely destroy everything from the land, says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Zephaniah 1:3">3</a> I will destroy people and animals; I will destroy the birds in the sky and the fish in the sea, along with the obstacles that the wicked face: and I will wipe out people from the earth, says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Zephaniah 1:4">4</a> I will reach out my hand against Judah, and against everyone living in Jerusalem; I will eliminate the followers of Baal from this place, along with the idolatrous priests.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Zephaniah 1:5">5</a> Those who worship the stars from their rooftops, and those who worship and make promises by God, and those who make promises by Malcham.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Zephaniah 1:6">6</a> And those who turned away from God; and those who did not look for God, or ask about Him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Zephaniah 1:7">7</a> Be quiet before God, because God&#39;s day is near: God has prepared a sacrifice and invited His guests.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Zephaniah 1:8">8</a> On the day of God&#39;s sacrifice, I will punish the leaders, the king&#39;s children, and all who wear foreign clothes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Zephaniah 1:9">9</a> On that day, I will also punish everyone who jumps over the doorway and fills their master&#39;s house with violence and lies.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Zephaniah 1:10">10</a> On that day, God says there will be a sound of crying from the Fish Gate, a wailing from the second area, and a loud crash from the hills.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Zephaniah 1:11">11</a> Cry out, people of Maktesh, because all the traders are destroyed; everyone who handles silver is wiped out.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Zephaniah 1:12">12</a> At that time, I will search Jerusalem with lamps and punish those who are comfortable and think God will neither help nor harm.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Zephaniah 1:13">13</a> So their possessions will be stolen, and their homes will be empty. They will build houses but not live in them, and they will grow grapes but not drink the wine.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Zephaniah 1:14">14</a> God&#39;s great day is close, very close, and is coming quickly. Even the shouts on that day will make strong men cry loudly.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Zephaniah 1:15">15</a> That day is a day of anger, a day of problems and suffering, a day of destruction and ruin, a day of darkness and sadness, a day of clouds and deep shadow.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Zephaniah 1:16">16</a> A day of trumpet blasts and warnings against the walled cities and tall towers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Zephaniah 1:17">17</a> I will cause trouble for people, making them stumble like blind people, because they sinned against God. Their blood will spill like dust, and their bodies will be like waste.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Zephaniah 1:18">18</a> Their silver and gold won&#39;t save them when God is angry. The whole land will be consumed by the fire of his jealousy. He will quickly remove everyone living in the land.</p>
