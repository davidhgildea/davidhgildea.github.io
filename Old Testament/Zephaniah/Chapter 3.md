<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Zephaniah 3:1">1</a> Sadness to the dirty and tainted city, to the one that oppresses!</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Zephaniah 3:2">2</a> She did not listen; she did not accept correction; she did not trust in God; she did not draw close to her God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Zephaniah 3:3">3</a> Her leaders inside her city are like loud lions; her judges are like wolves at night; they don&#39;t even leave bones until the next day.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Zephaniah 3:4">4</a> Her prophets are unreliable and deceitful; her priests have made the holy place dirty and have broken the law.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Zephaniah 3:5">5</a> The fair God is in the middle of it all; he will not act wrongly: every morning he shows his fair decisions, he does not fail; but the unfair have no shame.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Zephaniah 3:6">6</a> I have destroyed the nations: their towers are empty; I have made their streets empty, so no one passes by: their cities are ruined, with no people, with no one living there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Zephaniah 3:7">7</a> I thought, &#39;You will surely respect God and accept guidance, so your homes won&#39;t be destroyed, no matter how I punish you.&#39; But they got up early and ruined everything they did.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Zephaniah 3:8">8</a> Wait for me, says God, until the day I come to punish. I plan to bring together nations and kingdoms, so I can unleash my fury and intense anger on them. The whole earth will be consumed by the fire of my jealousy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Zephaniah 3:9">9</a> Then I will give the people a clear language, so they can all call on God&#39;s name and serve him together.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Zephaniah 3:10">10</a> From beyond Ethiopia&#39;s rivers, my worshipers, the scattered people&#39;s children, will bring me gifts.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Zephaniah 3:11">11</a> On that day, you will not feel shame for all your wrong actions against me; because I will remove from you those who take pride in your arrogance, and you will no longer act superior because of my sacred hill.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Zephaniah 3:12">12</a> I will also leave among you a suffering and poor people, and they will trust in God&#39;s name.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Zephaniah 3:13">13</a> The remaining people of Israel will not do wrong, nor will they tell lies; they will not have deceitful speech: because they will eat and rest, and no one will scare them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Zephaniah 3:14">14</a> Sing, daughter of Zion; yell, Israel; be happy and celebrate with all your heart, daughter of Jerusalem.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Zephaniah 3:15">15</a> God has removed your punishments and thrown out your enemies. The King of Israel, God Himself, is with you; you will not see evil again.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Zephaniah 3:16">16</a> On that day, they will tell Jerusalem, &quot;Don&#39;t be afraid,&quot; and to Zion, &quot;Don&#39;t let your hands grow weak.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Zephaniah 3:17">17</a> God among you is powerful; he will save you, he will be happy with great joy; he will find peace in his love, he will delight in you with singing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Zephaniah 3:18">18</a> I will bring together those who are sad because of the holy meeting, who belong to you, and felt its disgrace as a heavy load.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Zephaniah 3:19">19</a> Look, I will fix everything that hurts you at that time. I will rescue the limping one and bring back those who were scattered. I will give them honor and recognition in all the places where they were disgraced.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Zephaniah 3:20">20</a> At that time, I will bring you back when I gather you. I will make your name honored and praised by all people on earth, when I free you and you see it, says God.</p>
