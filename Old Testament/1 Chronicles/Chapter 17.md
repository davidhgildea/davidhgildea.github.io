<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Chronicles 17:1">1</a> One day, while David was at home, he said to Nathan the prophet, &quot;Look, I live in a house made of cedar wood, but God&#39;s covenant chest is still under a tent.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Chronicles 17:2">2</a> Then Nathan told David, &quot;Do all that you want to do; because God is with you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Chronicles 17:3">3</a> That night, God spoke to Nathan and said,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Chronicles 17:4">4</a> Go and tell David my servant, God says, You must not build a house for me to live in.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Chronicles 17:5">5</a> Since the day I led Israel up to this day, I have not lived in a house; instead, I moved from one tent to another and from one shelter to another.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Chronicles 17:6">6</a> Everywhere I have gone with all Israel, did I ask any of the leaders of Israel, whom I told to take care of my people, &#39;Why haven&#39;t you built me a cedar house?&#39;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Chronicles 17:7">7</a> So now, tell my servant David this: God says, &quot;I chose you from the pasture and from tending sheep to be the leader of my people Israel.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Chronicles 17:8">8</a> I have always been with you wherever you have gone, and I have defeated all your enemies for you. I have given you a reputation as great as any of the famous people on the earth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Chronicles 17:9">9</a> I will choose a place for my people Israel and make them settle there. They will live there and not be uprooted again; the children of wickedness will not harm them anymore like before.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Chronicles 17:10">10</a> Ever since I told judges to lead my people Israel, I will also defeat all your enemies. Also, I tell you that God will build you a house.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Chronicles 17:11">11</a> When your time ends and you join your ancestors, I will bring up one of your descendants, one of your sons, and make his kingdom strong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Chronicles 17:12">12</a> He will build a house for me, and I will make his throne last forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 Chronicles 17:13">13</a> I will be his father, and he will be my son. I will not remove my mercy from him as I did with the one before you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="1 Chronicles 17:14">14</a> I will establish him in my house and kingdom forever; his throne will be secure forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="1 Chronicles 17:15">15</a> Nathan told David everything just as he had seen and heard.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="1 Chronicles 17:16">16</a> King David came and sat before God and said, &quot;Who am I, God, and what is my house, that you have brought me this far?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="1 Chronicles 17:17">17</a> Even this was a small thing to you, God; you have also talked about your servant&#39;s family for a long time ahead, and you have treated me like someone with high status, O God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="1 Chronicles 17:18">18</a> What more can David say to you about the honor of your servant? You know your servant well.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="1 Chronicles 17:19">19</a> God, for your servant&#39;s benefit and following your own will, you have done all these great things and revealed them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="1 Chronicles 17:20">20</a> God, no one is like you, and there is no other God except you, just as we have heard.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="1 Chronicles 17:21">21</a> What other nation on earth is like your people, Israel, whom God saved to be his own, to make himself a great and feared name by removing other nations from before your people whom he rescued from Egypt?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="1 Chronicles 17:22">22</a> You made Israel your people forever, and you, God, became their God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="1 Chronicles 17:23">23</a> So now, God, let what you have said about your servant and his family be true forever, and do as you have promised.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="1 Chronicles 17:24">24</a> Let it be firm, so your name can be honored forever, as people say, &quot;God commanded the armies is the God of Israel and is a God to Israel.&quot; And let David&#39;s family, your servant&#39;s house, stand strong in your presence.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="1 Chronicles 17:25">25</a> You, my God, have told your servant that you will build a house for him: so your servant felt moved to pray to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="1 Chronicles 17:26">26</a> And now, God, you are God, and have promised this kindness to your servant.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="1 Chronicles 17:27">27</a> So now, please bless your servant&#39;s house so it will always be before you. Because when you bless, God, it is blessed forever.</p>
