<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Chronicles 7:1">1</a> Issachar had four sons: Tola, Puah, Jashub, and Shimrom.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Chronicles 7:2">2</a> Tola&#39;s sons were Uzzi, Rephaiah, Jeriel, Jahmai, Jibsam, and Shemuel, leaders of their family. They were brave and strong. During David&#39;s time, their family had 22,600 men.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Chronicles 7:3">3</a> Uzzi&#39;s sons were Izrahiah, and Izrahiah&#39;s sons were Michael, Obadiah, Joel, and Ishiah, five leaders in total.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Chronicles 7:4">4</a> With them, according to their families, were 36,000 soldiers grouped by ancestors, because they had many wives and children.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Chronicles 7:5">5</a> Their relatives in all the Issachar families were brave and strong warriors, counted in their family records as 87,000 men.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Chronicles 7:6">6</a> Benjamin had three sons: Bela, Becher, and Jediael.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Chronicles 7:7">7</a> Bela had five sons: Ezbon, Uzzi, Uzziel, Jerimoth, and Iri. They were leaders in their family and strong warriors. Their family records showed 22,034 descendants.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Chronicles 7:8">8</a> Becher&#39;s sons were Zemira, Joash, Eliezer, Elioenai, Omri, Jerimoth, Abiah, Anathoth, and Alameth. These are all Becher&#39;s sons.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Chronicles 7:9">9</a> The count of these men, according to their family lines and generations, leaders of their ancestors&#39; houses, brave warriors, was twenty thousand and two hundred.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Chronicles 7:10">10</a> Jediael&#39;s sons were Bilhan, and Bilhan&#39;s sons were Jeush, Benjamin, Ehud, Chenaanah, Zethan, Tharshish, and Ahishahar.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Chronicles 7:11">11</a> All these were Jediael&#39;s sons, leaders of their families, brave warriors, numbering 17,200 men ready for war and battle.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Chronicles 7:12">12</a> Shuppim and Huppim, Ir&#39;s children, and Hushim, Aher&#39;s sons.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 Chronicles 7:13">13</a> Naphtali&#39;s sons were Jahziel, Guni, Jezer, and Shallum; Bilhah was their mother.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="1 Chronicles 7:14">14</a> Manasseh&#39;s sons were Ashriel, born from his wife, and Machir, Gilead&#39;s father, born from his Aramean concubine.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="1 Chronicles 7:15">15</a> Machir married Huppim and Shuppim&#39;s sister, named Maachah; the second was named Zelophehad, who had daughters.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="1 Chronicles 7:16">16</a> Maachah, Machir&#39;s wife, had a son and named him Peresh; his brother was named Sheresh; the sons of Sheresh were Ulam and Rakem.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="1 Chronicles 7:17">17</a> Ulam&#39;s sons were Bedan. These were Gilead&#39;s sons, Machir&#39;s son, Manasseh&#39;s son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="1 Chronicles 7:18">18</a> His sister Hammoleketh had three children: Ishod, Abiezer, and Mahalah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="1 Chronicles 7:19">19</a> Shemidah&#39;s sons were Ahian, Shechem, Likhi, and Aniam.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="1 Chronicles 7:20">20</a> Ephraim&#39;s sons were Shuthelah, his son Bered, his son Tahath, his son Eladah, and another son named Tahath.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="1 Chronicles 7:21">21</a> Zabad&#39;s son, Shuthelah, Ezer, and Elead were killed by the people of Gath who were from that land because they tried to steal their cattle.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="1 Chronicles 7:22">22</a> Ephraim, their father, was sad for many days, and his brothers came to make him feel better.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="1 Chronicles 7:23">23</a> When he was with his wife, she got pregnant and had a baby boy. He named him Beriah because they had trouble in the family.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="1 Chronicles 7:24">24</a> His daughter Sherah built lower Bethhoron, upper Bethhoron, and Uzzensherah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="1 Chronicles 7:25">25</a> Rephah was his son, along with Resheph, then Telah his son, and then Tahan his son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="1 Chronicles 7:26">26</a> Laadan&#39;s son, Ammihud&#39;s son, Elishama&#39;s son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="1 Chronicles 7:27">27</a> Not his son, Joshua his son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="1 Chronicles 7:28">28</a> Their belongings and homes were in Bethel and its surrounding towns, to the east Naaran, to the west Gezer and its towns; also Shechem and its towns, all the way to Gaza and its towns.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="1 Chronicles 7:29">29</a> Near Manasseh&#39;s land, were the cities of Bethshean, Taanach, Megiddo, and Dor. The descendants of Joseph, Israel&#39;s son, lived in these places.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="1 Chronicles 7:30">30</a> Asher&#39;s children were Imnah, Isuah, Ishuai, Beriah, and their sister, Serah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="1 Chronicles 7:31">31</a> The sons of Beriah were Heber and Malchiel, and Malchiel was the father of Birzavith.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="1 Chronicles 7:32">32</a> Heber had children: Japhlet, Shomer, Hotham, and their sister Shua.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="1 Chronicles 7:33">33</a> Japhlet&#39;s sons were Pasach, Bimhal, and Ashvath. These are Japhlet&#39;s children.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="1 Chronicles 7:34">34</a> Shamer&#39;s sons were Ahi, Rohgah, Jehubbah, and Aram.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="1 Chronicles 7:35">35</a> Helem&#39;s sons were Zophah, Imna, Shelesh, and Amal.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="1 Chronicles 7:36">36</a> Zophah&#39;s sons were Suah, Harnepher, Shual, Beri, and Imrah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="1 Chronicles 7:37">37</a> Bezer, Hod, Shamma, Shilshah, Ithran, and Beera.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="1 Chronicles 7:38">38</a> Jether&#39;s sons were Jephunneh, Pispah, and Ara.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="1 Chronicles 7:39">39</a> Ulla&#39;s sons were Arah, Haniel, and Rezia.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="1 Chronicles 7:40">40</a> All these were Asher&#39;s children, leaders of their family, brave and strong. They were top among rulers. The total of their skilled warriors was 26,000 men.</p>
