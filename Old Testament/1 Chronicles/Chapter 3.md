<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Chronicles 3:1">1</a> These were David&#39;s sons born in Hebron: the first, Amnon, with Ahinoam from Jezreel; the second, Daniel, with Abigail from Carmel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Chronicles 3:2">2</a> The third, Absalom, Maachah&#39;s son and Talmai the king of Geshur&#39;s grandson; the fourth, Adonijah, Haggith&#39;s son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Chronicles 3:3">3</a> The fifth son was Shephatiah with Abital; the sixth was Ithream with Eglah, his wife.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Chronicles 3:4">4</a> He had six children in Hebron, where he ruled for seven and a half years; then in Jerusalem, he ruled for thirty-three years.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Chronicles 3:5">5</a> In Jerusalem, he had four sons by Bathshua, Ammiel&#39;s daughter: Shimea, Shobab, Nathan, and Solomon.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Chronicles 3:6">6</a> Ibhar, Elishama, and Eliphelet too.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Chronicles 3:7">7</a> Nogah, Nepheg, and Japhia,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Chronicles 3:8">8</a> Elishama, Eliada, and Eliphelet, nine.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Chronicles 3:9">9</a> All these were David&#39;s sons, not including the sons from his other women, and their sister Tamar.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Chronicles 3:10">10</a> Solomon&#39;s son was Rehoboam, his son was Abia, his son was Asa, and his son was Jehoshaphat.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Chronicles 3:11">11</a> Joram&#39;s son, Ahaziah&#39;s son, Joash&#39;s son,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Chronicles 3:12">12</a> Amaziah&#39;s son, Azariah&#39;s son, Jotham&#39;s son,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 Chronicles 3:13">13</a> Ahaz&#39;s son, Hezekiah&#39;s son, Manasseh&#39;s son,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="1 Chronicles 3:14">14</a> Amon was his son, and then Josiah was his son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="1 Chronicles 3:15">15</a> Josiah&#39;s sons were, the oldest Johanan, the next Jehoiakim, the third Zedekiah, and the youngest Shallum.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="1 Chronicles 3:16">16</a> Jehoiakim&#39;s sons were Jeconiah and then Zedekiah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="1 Chronicles 3:17">17</a> Jeconiah&#39;s children were Assir and his son Salathiel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="1 Chronicles 3:18">18</a> Malchiram, Pedaiah, Shenazar, Jecamiah, Hoshama, and Nedabiah were there too.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="1 Chronicles 3:19">19</a> Pedaiah had sons: Zerubbabel and Shimei. Zerubbabel&#39;s children were Meshullam, Hananiah, and their sister Shelomith.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="1 Chronicles 3:20">20</a> Hashubah, Ohel, Berechiah, Hasadiah, and Jushabhesed were five men.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="1 Chronicles 3:21">21</a> Hananiah&#39;s sons were Pelatiah and Jesaiah. Rephaiah&#39;s, Arnan&#39;s, Obadiah&#39;s, and Shechaniah&#39;s were their families.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="1 Chronicles 3:22">22</a> The children of Shechaniah were Shemaiah, and Shemaiah&#39;s children were Hattush, Igeal, Bariah, Neariah, and Shaphat—six in total.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="1 Chronicles 3:23">23</a> Neariah had three sons: Elioenai, Hezekiah, and Azrikam.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="1 Chronicles 3:24">24</a> Elioenai&#39;s sons were Hodaiah, Eliashib, Pelaiah, Akkub, Johanan, Dalaiah, and Anani, seven in total.</p>
