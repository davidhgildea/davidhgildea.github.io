<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Chronicles 4:1">1</a> Judah&#39;s sons were: Pharez, Hezron, Carmi, Hur, and Shobal.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Chronicles 4:2">2</a> Reaiah, Shobal&#39;s son, had a son named Jahath; Jahath had sons named Ahumai and Lahad. These are the clans of the Zorathites.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Chronicles 4:3">3</a> These were Etam&#39;s descendants: Jezreel, Ishma, and Idbash; their sister was named Hazelelponi.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Chronicles 4:4">4</a> Penuel was Gedor&#39;s father, and Ezer was Hushah&#39;s father. They were Hur&#39;s sons, who was Ephrath&#39;s first child and Bethlehem&#39;s father.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Chronicles 4:5">5</a> Ashur from Tekoa had two wives, Helah and Naarah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Chronicles 4:6">6</a> Naarah had sons named Ahuzam, Hepher, Temeni, and Haahashtari. These were Naarah&#39;s sons.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Chronicles 4:7">7</a> Helah&#39;s sons were Zereth, Jezoar, and Ethnan.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Chronicles 4:8">8</a> Coz had children Anub and Zobebah, and the relatives of Aharhel, Harum&#39;s son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Chronicles 4:9">9</a> Jabez was more respected than his brothers; his mother named him Jabez, saying, &quot;Because I gave birth to him in pain.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Chronicles 4:10">10</a> Jabez prayed to the God of Israel and said, &quot;Please bless me greatly, expand my territory, be with me, and keep me from harm so that I will not suffer.&quot; And God gave him what he asked for.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Chronicles 4:11">11</a> Chelub, Shuah&#39;s brother, had a son named Mehir, who was Eshton&#39;s father.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Chronicles 4:12">12</a> Eshton had sons called Bethrapha, Paseah, and Tehinnah, who was father of Irnahash. These men were from Rechah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 Chronicles 4:13">13</a> Kenaz had two sons named Othniel and Seraiah, and Othniel had a son named Hathath.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="1 Chronicles 4:14">14</a> Meonothai fathered Ophrah, and Seraiah fathered Joab, who was the leader in the Valley of Craftsmen because they were skilled workers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="1 Chronicles 4:15">15</a> Caleb, Jephunneh&#39;s son, had three sons: Iru, Elah, and Naam. Elah had a son named Kenaz.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="1 Chronicles 4:16">16</a> Jehaleleel&#39;s sons were Ziph, Ziphah, Tiria, and Asareel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="1 Chronicles 4:17">17</a> Ezra had sons named Jether, Mered, Epher, and Jalon. He also had a daughter named Miriam, another son named Shammai, and Ishbah, who became the father of Eshtemoa.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="1 Chronicles 4:18">18</a> Jehudijah, his wife, had three sons: Jered, who started the family of Gedor; Heber, who started the family of Socho; and Jekuthiel, who started the family of Zanoah. All of them were the sons of Bithiah, Pharaoh&#39;s daughter, whom Mered married.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="1 Chronicles 4:19">19</a> Hodiah&#39;s sons, Naham&#39;s sister and the father of Keilah from Garm and Eshtemoa from Maachah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="1 Chronicles 4:20">20</a> Shimon&#39;s sons were Amnon, Rinnah, Benhanan, and Tilon. Ishi&#39;s sons were Zoheth and Benzoheth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="1 Chronicles 4:21">21</a> Shelah, Judah&#39;s son, had sons: Er, who started Lecah&#39;s family; Laadah, who started Mareshah&#39;s family; and families who made fine linen in Ashbea&#39;s house.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="1 Chronicles 4:22">22</a> Jokim and the men of Chozeba, Joash, and Saraph ruled in Moab, and Jashubilehem. These are old stories.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="1 Chronicles 4:23">23</a> These were the potters and the people who lived near the plants and bushes; there they lived and worked for the king.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="1 Chronicles 4:24">24</a> Simeon&#39;s sons were Nemuel, Jamin, Jarib, Zerah, and Shaul.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="1 Chronicles 4:25">25</a> Shallum&#39;s son, Mibsam&#39;s son, Mishma&#39;s son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="1 Chronicles 4:26">26</a> Mishma&#39;s sons were Hamuel, then Zacchur, and then Shimei.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="1 Chronicles 4:27">27</a> Shimei had sixteen sons and six daughters, but his brothers didn&#39;t have many children, nor did their family grow as much as the family of Judah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="1 Chronicles 4:28">28</a> They lived in Beersheba, Moladah, and Hazarshual.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="1 Chronicles 4:29">29</a> In Bilhah, in Ezem, and in Tolad,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="1 Chronicles 4:30">30</a> In Bethuel, in Hormah, and in Ziklag,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="1 Chronicles 4:31">31</a> In Bethmarcaboth, Hazarsusim, Bethbirei, and Shaaraim were their cities until David became king.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="1 Chronicles 4:32">32</a> Their villages were Etam, Ain, Rimmon, Tochen, and Ashan—five cities.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="1 Chronicles 4:33">33</a> They had villages near those cities, up to Baal, where they lived and kept their family records.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="1 Chronicles 4:34">34</a> Meshobab, Jamlech, and Joshah, who was Amaziah&#39;s son,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="1 Chronicles 4:35">35</a> Joel and Jehu, Josibiah&#39;s son, Seraiah&#39;s grandson, Asiel&#39;s great-grandson.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="1 Chronicles 4:36">36</a> Elioenai, Jaakobah, Jeshohaiah, Asaiah, Adiel, Jesimiel, and Benaiah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="1 Chronicles 4:37">37</a> Ziza, son of Shiphi, grandson of Allon, great-grandson of Jedaiah, and descendant of Shimri and Shemaiah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="1 Chronicles 4:38">38</a> These named were leaders in their families, and their ancestors&#39; family grew a lot.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="1 Chronicles 4:39">39</a> They went to Gedor&#39;s entrance, to the valley&#39;s east side, to find grazing land for their sheep.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="1 Chronicles 4:40">40</a> They found rich and good grasslands, the land was spacious, calm, and peaceful; for the people from Ham had lived there long ago.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="1 Chronicles 4:41">41</a> These named people came during Hezekiah&#39;s rule in Judah, attacked their tents, destroyed the local dwellings, and lived there since it was good land for their sheep.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="1 Chronicles 4:42">42</a> Some from Simeon&#39;s tribe, five hundred men, went to Mount Seir, led by Pelatiah, Neariah, Rephaiah, and Uzziel, Ishi&#39;s sons.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v43"><a class="verse-num" href="#v43" aria-label="1 Chronicles 4:43">43</a> They defeated the remaining Amalekites who had escaped, and lived there until today.</p>
