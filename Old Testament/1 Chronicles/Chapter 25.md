<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Chronicles 25:1">1</a> David and the leaders assigned the sons of Asaph, Heman, and Jeduthun to prophesy using harps, lyres, and cymbals. They counted how many people each job needed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Chronicles 25:2">2</a> Asaph&#39;s sons were Zaccur, Joseph, Nethaniah, and Asarelah. They worked under Asaph and made prophecies following the king&#39;s instructions.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Chronicles 25:3">3</a> From Jeduthun: Jeduthun&#39;s sons were Gedaliah, Zeri, Jeshaiah, Hashabiah, and Mattithiah, six in total. They worked under their father Jeduthun, who played the harp to thank and praise God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Chronicles 25:4">4</a> Heman had these sons: Bukkiah, Mattaniah, Uzziel, Shebuel, Jerimoth, Hananiah, Hanani, Eliathah, Giddalti, Romamtiezer, Joshbekashah, Mallothi, Hothir, and Mahazioth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Chronicles 25:5">5</a> All these were Heman&#39;s sons; he was the king&#39;s prophet and spoke God&#39;s messages. God gave Heman fourteen sons and three daughters.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Chronicles 25:6">6</a> All these were guided by their father to make music in God&#39;s house, using cymbals, stringed instruments, and harps, to serve in God&#39;s house following the king&#39;s command to Asaph, Jeduthun, and Heman.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Chronicles 25:7">7</a> The total number of musicians skilled in God&#39;s songs, including their relatives, was 288.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Chronicles 25:8">8</a> They drew lots to take turns, from the least to the greatest, from the teacher to the student.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Chronicles 25:9">9</a> The first turn came for Asaph&#39;s group, led by Joseph. The second went to Gedaliah and his group, which included his brothers and sons, making twelve in total.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Chronicles 25:10">10</a> The third group was Zaccur&#39;s, he, his sons, and his brothers, totaling twelve.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Chronicles 25:11">11</a> The fourth one was Izri; he, his sons, and his brothers totaled twelve.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Chronicles 25:12">12</a> The fifth group was Nethaniah, his sons and his brothers, totaling twelve.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 Chronicles 25:13">13</a> Bukkiah was the sixth, with his sons and brothers totaling twelve.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="1 Chronicles 25:14">14</a> The seventh was Jesharelah; he and his sons and brothers totaled twelve.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="1 Chronicles 25:15">15</a> The eighth group was led by Jeshaiah, including him, his sons, and his brothers, totaling twelve people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="1 Chronicles 25:16">16</a> The ninth group was for Mattaniah, along with his sons and brothers, totaling twelve.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="1 Chronicles 25:17">17</a> The tenth group was for Shimei, his sons, and brothers—there were twelve of them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="1 Chronicles 25:18">18</a> Azareel was the eleventh, with his sons and brothers adding up to twelve.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="1 Chronicles 25:19">19</a> Hashabiah was the twelfth, along with his sons and brothers, making twelve in all.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="1 Chronicles 25:20">20</a> The thirteenth lot fell to Shubael, and together with his sons and brothers, they numbered twelve.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="1 Chronicles 25:21">21</a> Mattithiah&#39;s group, with his sons and brothers, numbered twelve.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="1 Chronicles 25:22">22</a> The fifteenth lot fell to Jeremoth, along with his sons and brothers, totaling twelve.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="1 Chronicles 25:23">23</a> The sixteenth group was Hananiah, his sons, and his brothers, totaling twelve people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="1 Chronicles 25:24">24</a> Joshbekashah&#39;s group, including himself, his sons, and his brothers, was twelve people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="1 Chronicles 25:25">25</a> Hanani&#39;s group, which included himself, his sons, and his brothers, had twelve members.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="1 Chronicles 25:26">26</a> Mallothi&#39;s group, including himself, his sons, and his brothers, was twelve.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="1 Chronicles 25:27">27</a> Eliathah&#39;s group, including himself, his sons, and his brothers, was twelve.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="1 Chronicles 25:28">28</a> The twenty-first was to Hothir, he and his sons and brothers totaled twelve.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="1 Chronicles 25:29">29</a> Giddalti, along with his sons and brothers, totaled twelve for the twenty-second group.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="1 Chronicles 25:30">30</a> The twenty-third group was Mahazioth, he, his sons, and his brothers, totaling twelve.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="1 Chronicles 25:31">31</a> The twenty-fourth group was Romamti-ezer, along with his sons and brothers, totaling twelve.</p>
