<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Chronicles 2:1">1</a> These are Israel&#39;s sons: Reuben, Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, and Zebulun.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Chronicles 2:2">2</a> Dan, Joseph, Benjamin, Naphtali, Gad, Asher.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Chronicles 2:3">3</a> Judah&#39;s sons were Er, Onan, and Shelah. These three were born to him by Shua&#39;s daughter, a Canaanite woman. Er, Judah&#39;s first son, was wicked in God&#39;s eyes, so God took his life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Chronicles 2:4">4</a> Tamar, his daughter-in-law, gave birth to Pharez and Zerah for him. Judah had a total of five sons.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Chronicles 2:5">5</a> Pharez&#39;s sons were Hezron and Hamul.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Chronicles 2:6">6</a> Zerah&#39;s sons were Zimri, Ethan, Heman, Calcol, and Dara: five in total.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Chronicles 2:7">7</a> Carmi had a son named Achar, who caused trouble in Israel by sinning with the cursed item.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Chronicles 2:8">8</a> Ethan&#39;s son was Azariah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Chronicles 2:9">9</a> Hezron&#39;s sons were Jerahmeel, Ram, and Chelubai.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Chronicles 2:10">10</a> Ram fathered Amminadab; and Amminadab fathered Nahshon, the leader of Judah&#39;s descendants.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Chronicles 2:11">11</a> Nahshon had a son named Salma, and Salma had a son named Boaz.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Chronicles 2:12">12</a> Boaz had Obed, and Obed had Jesse.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 Chronicles 2:13">13</a> Jesse had his first son Eliab, his second son Abinadab, and his third son Shimma.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="1 Chronicles 2:14">14</a> Nethaneel was fourth, Raddai was fifth,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="1 Chronicles 2:15">15</a> Ozem was the sixth, David was the seventh.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="1 Chronicles 2:16">16</a> His sisters were Zeruiah and Abigail. Zeruiah&#39;s sons were Abishai, Joab, and Asahel, three in total.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="1 Chronicles 2:17">17</a> Abigail gave birth to Amasa, and Amasa&#39;s father was Jether the Ishmaelite.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="1 Chronicles 2:18">18</a> Caleb, Hezron&#39;s son, had kids with his wife Azubah and Jerioth. Their sons were Jesher, Shobab, and Ardon.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="1 Chronicles 2:19">19</a> After Azubah died, Caleb married Ephrath, who gave birth to Hur.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="1 Chronicles 2:20">20</a> Hur had a son named Uri, and Uri had a son named Bezaleel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="1 Chronicles 2:21">21</a> Later, Hezron married the daughter of Machir, father of Gilead, when he was 60 years old, and she gave birth to Segub.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="1 Chronicles 2:22">22</a> Segub was the father of Jair, who owned twenty-three cities in Gilead.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="1 Chronicles 2:23">23</a> He captured Geshur and Aram with Jair&#39;s villages and Kenath with its surrounding towns, a total of sixty cities. All these were the lands of Machir, Gilead&#39;s father.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="1 Chronicles 2:24">24</a> After Hezron died in Calebephratah, his wife Abiah had a son named Ashur, who became the founder of Tekoa.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="1 Chronicles 2:25">25</a> Jerahmeel, Hezron&#39;s oldest son, had these sons: Ram, his firstborn, Bunah, Oren, Ozem, and Ahijah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="1 Chronicles 2:26">26</a> Jerahmeel also had another wife named Atarah; she was Onam&#39;s mother.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="1 Chronicles 2:27">27</a> Ram&#39;s firstborn Jerahmeel had sons: Maaz, Jamin, and Eker.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="1 Chronicles 2:28">28</a> Onam&#39;s sons were Shammai and Jada. Shammai&#39;s sons were Nadab and Abishur.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="1 Chronicles 2:29">29</a> Abishur&#39;s wife was named Abihail, and she had two sons for him, Ahban and Molid.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="1 Chronicles 2:30">30</a> Nadab&#39;s sons were Seled and Appaim, but Seled died without having any kids.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="1 Chronicles 2:31">31</a> Appaim&#39;s sons were Ishi, Ishi&#39;s sons were Sheshan, and Sheshan&#39;s children were Ahlai.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="1 Chronicles 2:32">32</a> Jada, the brother of Shammai, had two sons: Jether and Jonathan. But Jether died without having any children.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="1 Chronicles 2:33">33</a> Jonathan&#39;s sons were Peleth and Zaza. These were Jerahmeel&#39;s sons.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="1 Chronicles 2:34">34</a> Sheshan had only daughters, not sons. Sheshan&#39;s servant was an Egyptian named Jarha.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="1 Chronicles 2:35">35</a> Sheshan gave his daughter as a wife to Jarha, his servant, and she gave birth to a son named Attai.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="1 Chronicles 2:36">36</a> Attai fathered Nathan, and Nathan fathered Zabad.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="1 Chronicles 2:37">37</a> Zabad had a son named Ephlal, who had a son named Obed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="1 Chronicles 2:38">38</a> Obed had a son named Jehu, and Jehu had a son named Azariah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="1 Chronicles 2:39">39</a> Azariah had a son named Helez, and Helez had a son named Eleasah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="1 Chronicles 2:40">40</a> Eleasah had a son named Sisamai, and Sisamai had a son named Shallum.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="1 Chronicles 2:41">41</a> Shallum had a son named Jekamiah, and Jekamiah had a son named Elishama.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="1 Chronicles 2:42">42</a> Caleb&#39;s brother Jerahmeel had sons. Mesha was the oldest, and he became the father of Ziph. Mareshah&#39;s sons became the fathers of Hebron.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v43"><a class="verse-num" href="#v43" aria-label="1 Chronicles 2:43">43</a> Hebron&#39;s sons were Korah, Tappuah, Rekem, and Shema.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v44"><a class="verse-num" href="#v44" aria-label="1 Chronicles 2:44">44</a> Shema had Raham, who became the father of Jorkoam, and Rekem had Shammai.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v45"><a class="verse-num" href="#v45" aria-label="1 Chronicles 2:45">45</a> Shammai&#39;s son was Maon, and Maon was Bethzur&#39;s father.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v46"><a class="verse-num" href="#v46" aria-label="1 Chronicles 2:46">46</a> Ephah, Caleb&#39;s woman, had Haran, Moza, and Gazez; and Haran had Gazez.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v47"><a class="verse-num" href="#v47" aria-label="1 Chronicles 2:47">47</a> Jahdai&#39;s sons were Regem, Jotham, Gesham, Pelet, Ephah, and Shaaph.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v48"><a class="verse-num" href="#v48" aria-label="1 Chronicles 2:48">48</a> Maachah, who was with Caleb, had two sons named Sheber and Tirhanah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v49"><a class="verse-num" href="#v49" aria-label="1 Chronicles 2:49">49</a> She also gave birth to Shaaph, who was Madmannah&#39;s ancestor, Sheva, who was Machbenah&#39;s ancestor, and the ancestor of Gibea; Caleb&#39;s daughter was Achsa.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v50"><a class="verse-num" href="#v50" aria-label="1 Chronicles 2:50">50</a> Caleb, Hur&#39;s son and Ephratah&#39;s oldest, had these sons: Shobal started Kirjathjearim.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v51"><a class="verse-num" href="#v51" aria-label="1 Chronicles 2:51">51</a> Salma was the ancestor of Bethlehem; Hareph was the ancestor of Bethgader.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v52"><a class="verse-num" href="#v52" aria-label="1 Chronicles 2:52">52</a> Shobal, the father of Kirjathjearim, had sons: Haroeh and some of the Manahethites.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v53"><a class="verse-num" href="#v53" aria-label="1 Chronicles 2:53">53</a> The families of Kirjathjearim were the Ithrites, the Puhites, the Shumathites, and the Mishraites. From these families came the Zareathites and the Eshtaulites.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v54"><a class="verse-num" href="#v54" aria-label="1 Chronicles 2:54">54</a> Salma&#39;s children included Bethlehem, the people of Netophah, Ataroth, Joab&#39;s family, half of the people of Manahath, and the Zorites.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v55"><a class="verse-num" href="#v55" aria-label="1 Chronicles 2:55">55</a> The families of writers who lived in Jabez were the Tirathites, the Shimeathites, and the Suchathites. They are the Kenites who come from Hemath, the ancestor of the Rechab family.</p>
