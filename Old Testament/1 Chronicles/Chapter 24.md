<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Chronicles 24:1">1</a> These are the groups of Aaron&#39;s sons: Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Chronicles 24:2">2</a> Nadab and Abihu died before their father and had no kids, so Eleazar and Ithamar became priests.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Chronicles 24:3">3</a> David assigned roles to them: Zadok from Eleazar&#39;s descendants and Ahimelech from Ithamar&#39;s descendants, based on their duties in their service.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Chronicles 24:4">4</a> There were more leaders from Eleazar&#39;s sons than from Ithamar&#39;s sons, and they were split up this way: Sixteen leaders from Eleazar&#39;s family and eight from Ithamar&#39;s family.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Chronicles 24:5">5</a> They were separated by drawing lots, each group equally; because leaders of the sanctuary and leaders of God&#39;s house came from both Eleazar&#39;s descendants and Ithamar&#39;s descendants.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Chronicles 24:6">6</a> Shemaiah, Nethaneel&#39;s son and a writer, recorded names for the king, leaders, priest Zadok, Ahimelech (Abiathar&#39;s son), and the head priests and Levites. They chose one main family for Eleazar and one for Ithamar.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Chronicles 24:7">7</a> The first turn went to Jehoiarib, the second to Jedaiah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Chronicles 24:8">8</a> The third to Harim, the fourth to Seorim.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Chronicles 24:9">9</a> The fifth lot came out for Malchijah, the sixth for Mijamin.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Chronicles 24:10">10</a> The seventh to Hakkoz, the eighth to Abijah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Chronicles 24:11">11</a> The ninth for Jeshuah, the tenth for Shecaniah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Chronicles 24:12">12</a> Eliashib got the eleventh turn, and Jakim got the twelfth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 Chronicles 24:13">13</a> The thirteenth is to Huppah, the fourteenth is to Jeshebeab.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="1 Chronicles 24:14">14</a> The fifteenth lot fell to Bilgah, the sixteenth to Immer.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="1 Chronicles 24:15">15</a> The seventeenth lot fell to Hezir, the eighteenth to Aphses.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="1 Chronicles 24:16">16</a> The nineteenth lot fell to Pethahiah, the twentieth to Jehezekel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="1 Chronicles 24:17">17</a> The twenty-first lot was for Jachin, the twenty-second lot was for Gamul.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="1 Chronicles 24:18">18</a> The twenty-third lot fell to Delaiah, the twenty-fourth to Maaziah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="1 Chronicles 24:19">19</a> These were the rules for them in serving and entering God&#39;s house, following their customs under the leadership of Aaron their father, as God of Israel had commanded him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="1 Chronicles 24:20">20</a> The other descendants of Levi included: from Amram&#39;s line, Shubael; and from Shubael&#39;s line, Jehdeiah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="1 Chronicles 24:21">21</a> About Rehabiah: Isshiah was the first of Rehabiah&#39;s sons.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="1 Chronicles 24:22">22</a> From the Izharites, Shelomoth; from Shelomoth&#39;s sons, Jahath.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="1 Chronicles 24:23">23</a> Hebron&#39;s sons were Jeriah first, Amariah second, Jahaziel third, and Jekameam fourth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="1 Chronicles 24:24">24</a> Uzziel&#39;s sons included Michah, and Michah&#39;s sons included Shamir.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="1 Chronicles 24:25">25</a> Michah&#39;s brother was Isshiah. Isshiah&#39;s son was Zechariah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="1 Chronicles 24:26">26</a> Merari&#39;s sons were Mahli and Mushi; and Jaaziah&#39;s son was Beno.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="1 Chronicles 24:27">27</a> Merari&#39;s sons were Jaaziah, Beno, Shoham, Zaccur, and Ibri.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="1 Chronicles 24:28">28</a> Mahli&#39;s family included Eleazar, but he had no children.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="1 Chronicles 24:29">29</a> About Kish: Kish&#39;s son was Jerahmeel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="1 Chronicles 24:30">30</a> Mushi&#39;s sons were Mahli, Eder, and Jerimoth. These were the Levites&#39; sons, grouped by their fathers&#39; families.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="1 Chronicles 24:31">31</a> They also cast lots like their brothers, the sons of Aaron, in front of King David, Zadok, Ahimelech, and the leaders of the priests&#39; and Levites&#39; families, with the most important fathers facing their younger brothers.</p>
