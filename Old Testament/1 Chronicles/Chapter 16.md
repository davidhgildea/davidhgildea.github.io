<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Chronicles 16:1">1</a> They brought God&#39;s ark and placed it inside the tent David set up for it; they gave burnt sacrifices and peace offerings to God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Chronicles 16:2">2</a> After David finished giving the burnt offerings and the peace offerings, he blessed the people in God&#39;s name.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Chronicles 16:3">3</a> He gave every person in Israel, both men and women, a loaf of bread, a nice piece of meat, and a bottle of wine.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Chronicles 16:4">4</a> He chose some Levites to serve before God&#39;s ark, to keep records, and to thank and praise the God of Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Chronicles 16:5">5</a> Asaph was the leader, with Zechariah, Jeiel, Shemiramoth, Jehiel, Mattithiah, Eliab, Benaiah, and Obed-edom next to him. Jeiel played the psalteries and harps, while Asaph played the cymbals.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Chronicles 16:6">6</a> Benaiah and Jahaziel, who were priests, always played trumpets in front of God&#39;s covenant box.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Chronicles 16:7">7</a> On that day, David first gave this psalm to thank God to Asaph and his brothers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Chronicles 16:8">8</a> Give thanks to God, call on his name, tell everyone about what he has done.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Chronicles 16:9">9</a> Sing to him, sing songs to him, talk about all his amazing works.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Chronicles 16:10">10</a> Celebrate God&#39;s holy name: let those who look for God be happy in their hearts.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Chronicles 16:11">11</a> Look for God and his power, always search for his presence.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Chronicles 16:12">12</a> Remember the amazing things God has done, his miracles, and the decisions he has spoken.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 Chronicles 16:13">13</a> You descendants of Israel, God&#39;s servants, you offspring of Jacob, his chosen people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="1 Chronicles 16:14">14</a> He is our God; his decisions are everywhere on earth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="1 Chronicles 16:15">15</a> Always remember God&#39;s agreement, the promise he gave for a thousand generations.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="1 Chronicles 16:16">16</a> About the promise God made to Abraham, and the pledge to Isaac;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="1 Chronicles 16:17">17</a> God made it a law for Jacob and a forever promise to Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="1 Chronicles 16:18">18</a> God said, &quot;I will give you the land of Canaan as your inheritance.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="1 Chronicles 16:19">19</a> When you were only a few people, strangers in the land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="1 Chronicles 16:20">20</a> As they moved from one country to another and from one kingdom to a different nation;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="1 Chronicles 16:21">21</a> He did not let anyone harm them; indeed, he scolded kings for their protection.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="1 Chronicles 16:22">22</a> &quot;God said, &#39;Do not harm my chosen ones, and do not hurt my prophets.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="1 Chronicles 16:23">23</a> Sing to God, all the earth; show his salvation every day.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="1 Chronicles 16:24">24</a> Tell everyone about God&#39;s greatness; share the amazing things He does with all people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="1 Chronicles 16:25">25</a> God is great and deserves much praise; we should also fear him more than any other gods.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="1 Chronicles 16:26">26</a> All other people&#39;s gods are just idols, but God created the skies.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="1 Chronicles 16:27">27</a> God&#39;s presence brings glory and honor; His place is filled with strength and joy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="1 Chronicles 16:28">28</a> Give to God, you families of the people, give to God glory and strength.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="1 Chronicles 16:29">29</a> Give God the honor that belongs to his name; bring a gift and come before him; worship God in the beauty of being holy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="1 Chronicles 16:30">30</a> All the earth, be in awe of God; the world will be firm and not shake.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="1 Chronicles 16:31">31</a> Let the sky be happy, and the earth be joyful, and let people tell all countries, &quot;God rules.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="1 Chronicles 16:32">32</a> Let the sea make noise and everything in it; let the fields be happy and everything in them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="1 Chronicles 16:33">33</a> The trees of the forest will sing when God comes, because he is coming to judge the world.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="1 Chronicles 16:34">34</a> Give thanks to God, for he is good, for his kindness lasts forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="1 Chronicles 16:35">35</a> Say to God, &quot;Save us, Savior, bring us together, and free us from other nations, so we can thank your holy name and find joy in praising you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="1 Chronicles 16:36">36</a> Praise God of Israel forever and ever. And all the people said, &quot;Amen,&quot; and praised God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="1 Chronicles 16:37">37</a> He left Asaph and his brothers there in front of God&#39;s covenant chest to serve continually, as each day needed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="1 Chronicles 16:38">38</a> Obededom and his 68 brothers, along with the sons of Jeduthun and Hosah, served as gatekeepers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="1 Chronicles 16:39">39</a> Priest Zadok and his fellow priests served at God&#39;s tent on the high ground in Gibeon.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="1 Chronicles 16:40">40</a> To give burnt sacrifices to God on the altar for burnt sacrifices always in the morning and in the evening, and to do everything that is written in God&#39;s law that he gave to Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="1 Chronicles 16:41">41</a> Along with them were Heman and Jeduthun, and the others who were selected and named, to thank God, because his mercy lasts forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="1 Chronicles 16:42">42</a> Heman and Jeduthun joined them with trumpets and cymbals to make music and with God&#39;s instruments. Jeduthun&#39;s sons were gatekeepers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v43"><a class="verse-num" href="#v43" aria-label="1 Chronicles 16:43">43</a> All the people went to their own homes, and David went back to bless his family.</p>
