<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Chronicles 8:1">1</a> Benjamin had a first son named Bela, a second named Ashbel, and a third named Aharah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Chronicles 8:2">2</a> Nohah was the fourth, and Rapha was the fifth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Chronicles 8:3">3</a> Bela&#39;s sons were Addar, Gera, and Abihud.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Chronicles 8:4">4</a> Abishua, Naaman, and Ahoah,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Chronicles 8:5">5</a> And Gera, and Shephuphan, and Huram.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Chronicles 8:6">6</a> Ehud&#39;s sons were leaders of Geba&#39;s families, and they were moved to Manahath.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Chronicles 8:7">7</a> Naaman, Ahiah, and Gera were sent away, and they had Uzza and Ahihud.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Chronicles 8:8">8</a> Shaharaim had children in Moab after he sent his family away; his wives were Hushim and Baara.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Chronicles 8:9">9</a> He had children with his wife Hodesh: Jobab, Zibia, Mesha, and Malcham.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Chronicles 8:10">10</a> Jeuz, Shachia, and Mirma were his sons, leaders of their families.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Chronicles 8:11">11</a> Hushim had two sons: Abitub and Elpaal.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Chronicles 8:12">12</a> Elpaal&#39;s sons were Eber, Misham, and Shamed, who built the cities of Ono and Lod and their surrounding towns.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 Chronicles 8:13">13</a> Beriah and Shema were also leaders of Aijalon&#39;s founding families, and they forced out the people of Gath.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="1 Chronicles 8:14">14</a> Ahio, Shashak, and Jeremoth,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="1 Chronicles 8:15">15</a> Zebadiah, Arad, and Ader,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="1 Chronicles 8:16">16</a> Michael, Ispah, and Joha were the sons of Beriah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="1 Chronicles 8:17">17</a> Zebadiah, Meshullam, Hezeki, and Heber,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="1 Chronicles 8:18">18</a> Ishmerai, Jezliah, and Jobab were the sons of Elpaal.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="1 Chronicles 8:19">19</a> Jakim, Zichri, and Zabdi,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="1 Chronicles 8:20">20</a> Elienai, Zilthai, and Eliel,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="1 Chronicles 8:21">21</a> Adaiah, Beraiah, and Shimrath were Shimhi&#39;s sons.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="1 Chronicles 8:22">22</a> Ishpan, Heber, and Eliel,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="1 Chronicles 8:23">23</a> Abdon, Zichri, and Hanan,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="1 Chronicles 8:24">24</a> Hananiah, Elam, and Antothijah,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="1 Chronicles 8:25">25</a> Iphedeiah and Penuel were Shashak&#39;s sons.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="1 Chronicles 8:26">26</a> Shamsherai, Shehariah, and Athaliah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="1 Chronicles 8:27">27</a> Jaresiah, Eliah, and Zichri were the sons of Jeroham.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="1 Chronicles 8:28">28</a> These were leaders of the families, important men by their ancestry. They lived in Jerusalem.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="1 Chronicles 8:29">29</a> In Gibeon, the founder of the city lived; his wife&#39;s name was Maachah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="1 Chronicles 8:30">30</a> His oldest son was Abdon, and he also had Zur, Kish, Baal, and Nadab.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="1 Chronicles 8:31">31</a> Gedor, Ahio, and Zacher.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="1 Chronicles 8:32">32</a> Mikloth had a son named Shimeah. They also lived near their relatives in Jerusalem.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="1 Chronicles 8:33">33</a> Ner was the father of Kish, Kish was the father of Saul, Saul was the father of Jonathan, Malchishua, Abinadab, and Eshbaal.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="1 Chronicles 8:34">34</a> Jonathan&#39;s son was Meribbaal, and Meribbaal had a son named Micah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="1 Chronicles 8:35">35</a> Micah&#39;s sons were Pithon, Melech, Tarea, and Ahaz.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="1 Chronicles 8:36">36</a> And Ahaz was the father of Jehoadah; Jehoadah was the father of Alemeth, Azmaveth, and Zimri; and Zimri was the father of Moza.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="1 Chronicles 8:37">37</a> Moza had a son named Binea. Binea&#39;s son was Rapha, Rapha&#39;s son was Eleasah, and Eleasah&#39;s son was Azel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="1 Chronicles 8:38">38</a> Azel had six sons named Azrikam, Bocheru, Ishmael, Sheariah, Obadiah, and Hanan. These were all Azel&#39;s sons.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="1 Chronicles 8:39">39</a> Eshek&#39;s brother had three sons: Ulam the first, Jehush the second, and Eliphelet the third.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="1 Chronicles 8:40">40</a> Ulam&#39;s sons were brave warriors, skilled with bows, and had lots of sons and grandsons, totaling 150. All these were from Benjamin&#39;s family.</p>
