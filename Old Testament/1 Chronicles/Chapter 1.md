<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Chronicles 1:1">1</a> Adam, Seth, Enosh.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Chronicles 1:2">2</a> Kenan, Mahalaleel, Jared,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Chronicles 1:3">3</a> Enoch, Methuselah, Lemach.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Chronicles 1:4">4</a> Noah, Shem, Ham, and Japheth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Chronicles 1:5">5</a> Japheth&#39;s sons are Gomer, Magog, Madai, Javan, Tubal, Meshech, and Tiras.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Chronicles 1:6">6</a> Gomer&#39;s sons were Ashkenaz, Riphath, and Togarmah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Chronicles 1:7">7</a> Javan&#39;s sons were Elishah, Tarshish, Kittim, and Dodanim.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Chronicles 1:8">8</a> Ham&#39;s sons were Cush, Mizraim, Put, and Canaan.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Chronicles 1:9">9</a> Cush had sons named Seba, Havilah, Sabta, Raamah, and Sabtecha. Raamah had sons named Sheba and Dedan.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Chronicles 1:10">10</a> Cush had a son named Nimrod, who became very powerful on earth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Chronicles 1:11">11</a> Mizraim had children named Ludim, Anamim, Lehabim, and Naphtuhim.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Chronicles 1:12">12</a> And people from Pathrus, Casluh, who were the ancestors of the Philistines, and from Caphtor.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 Chronicles 1:13">13</a> Canaan had Zidon, his first son, and Heth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="1 Chronicles 1:14">14</a> The Jebusite, the Amorite, and the Girgashite too,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="1 Chronicles 1:15">15</a> The Hivite, the Arkite, and the Sinite,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="1 Chronicles 1:16">16</a> The people from Arvad, from Zemar, and from Hamath.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="1 Chronicles 1:17">17</a> Shem&#39;s sons were Elam, Asshur, Arphaxad, Lud, Aram, Uz, Hul, Gether, and Meshech.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="1 Chronicles 1:18">18</a> Arphaxad had a son named Shelah, and Shelah had a son named Eber.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="1 Chronicles 1:19">19</a> Eber had two sons: one was named Peleg, for during his time the earth was divided, and his brother was named Joktan.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="1 Chronicles 1:20">20</a> Joktan had children named Almodad, Sheleph, Hazarmaveth, and Jerah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="1 Chronicles 1:21">21</a> Hadoram, Uzal, and Diklah too,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="1 Chronicles 1:22">22</a> Ebal, Abimael, and Sheba,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="1 Chronicles 1:23">23</a> Ophir, Havilah, and Jobab were all Joktan&#39;s sons.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="1 Chronicles 1:24">24</a> Shem, Arphaxad, Shelah,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="1 Chronicles 1:25">25</a> Eber, Peleg, Reu,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="1 Chronicles 1:26">26</a> Serug, Nahor, Terah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="1 Chronicles 1:27">27</a> Abram is Abraham.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="1 Chronicles 1:28">28</a> Abraham&#39;s sons: Isaac and Ishmael.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="1 Chronicles 1:29">29</a> This lists their families: Ishmael&#39;s first son was Nebaioth, followed by Kedar, Adbeel, and Mibsam.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="1 Chronicles 1:30">30</a> Mishma, Dumah, Massa, Hadad, and Tema</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="1 Chronicles 1:31">31</a> Jetur, Naphish, and Kedemah are Ishmael&#39;s sons.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="1 Chronicles 1:32">32</a> Abraham&#39;s other wife Keturah had sons: Zimran, Jokshan, Medan, Midian, Ishbak, and Shuah. Jokshan had two sons: Sheba and Dedan.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="1 Chronicles 1:33">33</a> Midian&#39;s sons were Ephah, Epher, Henoch, Abida, and Eldaah. They were all Keturah&#39;s sons.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="1 Chronicles 1:34">34</a> Abraham had a son named Isaac. Isaac&#39;s sons were Esau and Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="1 Chronicles 1:35">35</a> Esau&#39;s sons: Eliphaz, Reuel, Jeush, Jaalam, and Korah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="1 Chronicles 1:36">36</a> Eliphaz&#39;s sons were Teman, Omar, Zephi, Gatam, Kenaz, Timna, and Amalek.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="1 Chronicles 1:37">37</a> Reuel&#39;s sons were Nahath, Zerah, Shammah, and Mizzah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="1 Chronicles 1:38">38</a> Seir&#39;s sons were Lotan, Shobal, Zibeon, Anah, Dishon, Ezar, and Dishan.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="1 Chronicles 1:39">39</a> Lotan&#39;s sons were Hori and Homam, and Timna was Lotan&#39;s sister.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="1 Chronicles 1:40">40</a> Shobal&#39;s sons were Alian, Manahath, Ebal, Shephi, and Onam. Zibeon&#39;s sons were Aiah and Anah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="1 Chronicles 1:41">41</a> Anah&#39;s sons were Dishon. Dishon&#39;s sons were Amram, Eshban, Ithran, and Cheran.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="1 Chronicles 1:42">42</a> Ezer&#39;s sons were Bilhan, Zavan, and Jakan. Dishan&#39;s sons were Uz and Aran.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v43"><a class="verse-num" href="#v43" aria-label="1 Chronicles 1:43">43</a> These were the kings who ruled in Edom before any king ruled over Israel: Bela son of Beor, and his city was called Dinhabah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v44"><a class="verse-num" href="#v44" aria-label="1 Chronicles 1:44">44</a> After Bela died, Jobab, Zerah&#39;s son from Bozrah, became king in his place.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v45"><a class="verse-num" href="#v45" aria-label="1 Chronicles 1:45">45</a> After Jobab died, Husham from Temanite lands became ruler in his place.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v46"><a class="verse-num" href="#v46" aria-label="1 Chronicles 1:46">46</a> When Husham died, Hadad son of Bedad, who defeated Midian in Moab&#39;s fields, became king after him. His city was named Avith.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v47"><a class="verse-num" href="#v47" aria-label="1 Chronicles 1:47">47</a> After Hadad died, Samlah from Masrekah became the ruler in his place.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v48"><a class="verse-num" href="#v48" aria-label="1 Chronicles 1:48">48</a> After Samlah died, Shaul from Rehoboth on the river became king in his place.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v49"><a class="verse-num" href="#v49" aria-label="1 Chronicles 1:49">49</a> After Saul died, Baalhanan, Achbor&#39;s son, became king in his place.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v50"><a class="verse-num" href="#v50" aria-label="1 Chronicles 1:50">50</a> After Baalhanan died, Hadad became king. His city was named Pai, and his wife was Mehetabel, Matred&#39;s daughter and Mezahab&#39;s granddaughter.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v51"><a class="verse-num" href="#v51" aria-label="1 Chronicles 1:51">51</a> Hadad also died. The leaders of Edom were: Leader Timnah, Leader Aliah, Leader Jetheth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v52"><a class="verse-num" href="#v52" aria-label="1 Chronicles 1:52">52</a> Leader Aholibamah, leader Elah, leader Pinon,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v53"><a class="verse-num" href="#v53" aria-label="1 Chronicles 1:53">53</a> Chief Kenaz, Chief Teman, Chief Mibzar,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v54"><a class="verse-num" href="#v54" aria-label="1 Chronicles 1:54">54</a> Leader Magdiel, leader Iram. These are the leaders of Edom.</p>
