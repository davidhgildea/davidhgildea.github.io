<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Chronicles 11:1">1</a> All Israel came to David in Hebron and said, &quot;Look, we are your own flesh and bone.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Chronicles 11:2">2</a> Even before, when Saul was king, you were the one who led Israel: and God told you, &quot;You will take care of my people Israel, and you will lead them.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Chronicles 11:3">3</a> So all the leaders of Israel went to the king at Hebron. There, in Hebron, David agreed with them before God; and they made David the king over Israel, as God had said through Samuel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Chronicles 11:4">4</a> David and all of Israel went to Jerusalem, also known as Jebus, where the Jebusites lived in the land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Chronicles 11:5">5</a> The people of Jebus told David, &quot;You will not come here.&quot; But David captured the fortress of Zion, which is the city of David.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Chronicles 11:6">6</a> David said, &quot;Whoever attacks the Jebusites first will become the leader and commander.&quot; So Joab, Zeruiah&#39;s son, led the attack and became the leader.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Chronicles 11:7">7</a> David lived in the stronghold, so they named it David&#39;s city.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Chronicles 11:8">8</a> He built the city all around, from Millo and around it, and Joab fixed the rest of the city.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Chronicles 11:9">9</a> David became more and more powerful because God was with him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Chronicles 11:10">10</a> These men were also leaders of the strong warriors who supported David and helped make him king over all Israel, as God had promised concerning Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Chronicles 11:11">11</a> David had strong soldiers; Jashobeam, from Hachmon, led the captains. He fought and defeated three hundred enemies at once with his spear.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Chronicles 11:12">12</a> After him came Eleazar, son of Dodo, the Ahohite, who was one of the three strongest warriors.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 Chronicles 11:13">13</a> He was with David at Pasdammim, where the Philistines had come to fight, and there was a field of barley; the people ran away from the Philistines.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="1 Chronicles 11:14">14</a> They took their position in the middle of the land, saved it, and killed the Philistines; and God rescued them with a great victory.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="1 Chronicles 11:15">15</a> Three of the thirty leaders went to the rock to meet David at the cave of Adullam, while the Philistine army camped in Rephaim Valley.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="1 Chronicles 11:16">16</a> David was in the stronghold, and the Philistines&#39; fort was at Bethlehem at that time.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="1 Chronicles 11:17">17</a> David felt a deep desire and said, &quot;I wish someone would bring me water from the Bethlehem well by the gate!&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="1 Chronicles 11:18">18</a> The three men fought through the Philistine army and got water from the Bethlehem well by the gate. They brought it to David, but he wouldn&#39;t drink it and poured it out to God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="1 Chronicles 11:19">19</a> He said, &quot;God stop me from doing this. Should I drink what these men risked their lives to get? They risked their lives to bring it.&quot; So he refused to drink it. This is what the three bravest did.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="1 Chronicles 11:20">20</a> Abishai, Joab&#39;s brother, was the leader of the three. By using his spear to defeat three hundred, he killed them and became famous among the three.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="1 Chronicles 11:21">21</a> Among the three, he was the most respected because he was their leader, but he did not reach the level of the top three.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="1 Chronicles 11:22">22</a> Benaiah, Jehoiada&#39;s son, from Kabzeel, who was brave and did many things, killed two strong Moab men and also killed a lion in a pit on a snowy day.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="1 Chronicles 11:23">23</a> He killed an Egyptian, a very tall man about seven and a half feet tall; the Egyptian had a spear as thick as a weaver&#39;s rod; but he attacked with just a stick, grabbed the spear from the Egyptian, and killed him with it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="1 Chronicles 11:24">24</a> Benaiah, son of Jehoiada, did these things and was known as one of the three mightiest.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="1 Chronicles 11:25">25</a> Look, he was respected among the thirty, but did not reach the top three; and David put him in charge of his bodyguards.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="1 Chronicles 11:26">26</a> The brave soldiers included Asahel, Joab&#39;s brother, and Elhanan, Dodo&#39;s son from Bethlehem.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="1 Chronicles 11:27">27</a> Shammah from Harar, Helez from Pela.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="1 Chronicles 11:28">28</a> Ira, son of Ikkesh from Tekoa, and Abiezer from Anathoth,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="1 Chronicles 11:29">29</a> Sibbecai from Hushath, Ilai from Ahoah,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="1 Chronicles 11:30">30</a> Maharai from Netophah, Heled, Baanah&#39;s son, from Netophah,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="1 Chronicles 11:31">31</a> Ithai, Ribai&#39;s son from Gibeah of Benjamin&#39;s descendants, Benaiah from Pirathon,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="1 Chronicles 11:32">32</a> Hurai from the Gaash creeks, Abiel the Arbathite.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="1 Chronicles 11:33">33</a> Azmaveth from Baharum, Eliahba from Shaalbon,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="1 Chronicles 11:34">34</a> Hashem&#39;s sons from Gizon, Jonathan, Shage&#39;s son from Harar,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="1 Chronicles 11:35">35</a> Ahiam, Sacar the Hararite&#39;s son, Eliphal, Ur&#39;s son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="1 Chronicles 11:36">36</a> Hepher from Mekerah, Ahijah from Pelon.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="1 Chronicles 11:37">37</a> Hezro from Carmel, Naarai, Ezbai&#39;s son,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="1 Chronicles 11:38">38</a> Joel Nathan&#39;s brother, Mibhar Haggeri&#39;s son,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="1 Chronicles 11:39">39</a> Zelek from Ammon, Naharai from Beroth, Joab&#39;s armor carrier, Zeruiah&#39;s son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="1 Chronicles 11:40">40</a> Ira from Ithra, Gareb from Ithra,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="1 Chronicles 11:41">41</a> Uriah from Hittite people, Zabad son of Ahlai,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="1 Chronicles 11:42">42</a> Adina, Shiza&#39;s son from the tribe of Reuben, was a leader, and he was with thirty others.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v43"><a class="verse-num" href="#v43" aria-label="1 Chronicles 11:43">43</a> Hanan, Maachah&#39;s son, and Joshaphat, from Mithni.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v44"><a class="verse-num" href="#v44" aria-label="1 Chronicles 11:44">44</a> Uzzia from Ashterath, Shama and Jehiel who are Hothan&#39;s sons from Aroer,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v45"><a class="verse-num" href="#v45" aria-label="1 Chronicles 11:45">45</a> Jediael, Shimri&#39;s son, and his brother Joha, the Tizite,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v46"><a class="verse-num" href="#v46" aria-label="1 Chronicles 11:46">46</a> Eliel from Mahavah, Jeribai, and Joshaviah, Elnaam&#39;s sons, and Ithmah from Moab,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v47"><a class="verse-num" href="#v47" aria-label="1 Chronicles 11:47">47</a> Eliel, Obed, and Jasiel from Mesoba.</p>
