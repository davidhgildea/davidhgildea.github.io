<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Chronicles 23:1">1</a> When David was old, he made his son Solomon the king of Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Chronicles 23:2">2</a> He brought together all the leaders of Israel, the priests, and the Levites.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Chronicles 23:3">3</a> The Levites were counted starting at age thirty and older: altogether, they were 38,000 men.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Chronicles 23:4">4</a> 24,000 people were to work on God&#39;s house, and 6,000 were officers and judges.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Chronicles 23:5">5</a> Also, four thousand were gatekeepers; and four thousand praised God with the instruments that I, David, made for praising.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Chronicles 23:6">6</a> David split them into groups among Levi&#39;s sons: Gershon, Kohath, and Merari.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Chronicles 23:7">7</a> From the Gershon family were Laadan and Shimei.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Chronicles 23:8">8</a> Laadan&#39;s sons were leader Jehiel, Zetham, and Joel, three in total.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Chronicles 23:9">9</a> Shimei&#39;s sons were Shelomith, Haziel, and Haran—three in total. They were the leaders of the Laadan family.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Chronicles 23:10">10</a> Shimei had four sons: Jahath, Zina, Jeush, and Beriah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Chronicles 23:11">11</a> Jahath was the leader, Zizah was next, but Jeush and Beriah did not have many children, so they were counted as one family.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Chronicles 23:12">12</a> Kohath&#39;s sons were Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel—four in total.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 Chronicles 23:13">13</a> Amram&#39;s sons were Aaron and Moses. Aaron was set apart to make the most sacred things holy, along with his sons forever. They burned incense before God, served him, and blessed his name always.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="1 Chronicles 23:14">14</a> About Moses, a man of God, his sons were called from the Levi tribe.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="1 Chronicles 23:15">15</a> Moses had two sons named Gershom and Eliezer.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="1 Chronicles 23:16">16</a> Shebuel was the main son of Gershom.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="1 Chronicles 23:17">17</a> Eliezer&#39;s sons were led by Rehabiah. Eliezer had no other sons, but Rehabiah had many.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="1 Chronicles 23:18">18</a> Izhar&#39;s son was chief, and his name was Shelomith.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="1 Chronicles 23:19">19</a> Hebron&#39;s sons were: Jeriah first, Amariah second, Jahaziel third, and Jekameam fourth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="1 Chronicles 23:20">20</a> Uzziel&#39;s sons were: Micah first, and Jesiah second.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="1 Chronicles 23:21">21</a> Merari&#39;s sons were Mahli and Mushi. Mahli&#39;s sons were Eleazar and Kish.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="1 Chronicles 23:22">22</a> Eleazar died without sons, only daughters; and Kish&#39;s sons, their cousins, married them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="1 Chronicles 23:23">23</a> Mushi&#39;s sons were Mahli, Eder, and Jeremoth, three in total.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="1 Chronicles 23:24">24</a> These were Levi&#39;s sons by their family lines; the heads of the families, who were counted by name, did the work for serving in God&#39;s house, starting at age twenty and older.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="1 Chronicles 23:25">25</a> David said, God of Israel has given his people peace so they can live in Jerusalem forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="1 Chronicles 23:26">26</a> And also to the Levites; they will not carry the tent or any of its equipment for its service anymore.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="1 Chronicles 23:27">27</a> David&#39;s final instructions were to count the Levites who were 20 years old or older.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="1 Chronicles 23:28">28</a> Their job was to help Aaron&#39;s sons in serving God&#39;s house, in the courtyards, rooms, and in cleaning all holy items, and in the work of serving in God&#39;s house.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="1 Chronicles 23:29">29</a> For the bread displayed, for the fine flour for grain offerings, for the unleavened bread, for what is baked in the pan, for what is fried, and for all kinds of measurements and amounts.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="1 Chronicles 23:30">30</a> And to stand every morning to thank and praise God, and also in the evening.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="1 Chronicles 23:31">31</a> To offer all burnt sacrifices to God on the sabbaths, at the new moons, and during the set festivals, in the amount and way God told them, always before God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="1 Chronicles 23:32">32</a> They must take care of the meeting tent, the holy area, and be responsible for the service of their brothers, the sons of Aaron, in God&#39;s house.</p>
