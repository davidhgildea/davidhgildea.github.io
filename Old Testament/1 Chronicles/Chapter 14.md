<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Chronicles 14:1">1</a> Hiram, the king of Tyre, sent messengers to David with cedar wood, stone workers, and carpenters to build a house for him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Chronicles 14:2">2</a> David realized that God had established him as king over Israel because his kingdom was highly honored due to the people of Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Chronicles 14:3">3</a> David married more women in Jerusalem and had more sons and daughters.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Chronicles 14:4">4</a> These are the names of his children born in Jerusalem: Shammua, Shobab, Nathan, and Solomon.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Chronicles 14:5">5</a> Ibhar, Elishua, and Elpalet,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Chronicles 14:6">6</a> Nogah, Nepheg, and Japhia,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Chronicles 14:7">7</a> Elishama, Beeliada, and Eliphalet.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Chronicles 14:8">8</a> When the Philistines learned David was made king over all of Israel, they all went to find David. David found out and went to face them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Chronicles 14:9">9</a> The Philistines came and spread out in the Rephaim Valley.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Chronicles 14:10">10</a> David asked God, &quot;Should I go fight the Philistines? Will you give them to me?&quot; And God said to him, &quot;Go; I will give them to you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Chronicles 14:11">11</a> They went to Baalperazim, and David defeated them there. Then David said, God has helped me defeat my enemies like water bursting through. So they named that place Baalperazim.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Chronicles 14:12">12</a> After they had abandoned their gods, David ordered them to be burned.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 Chronicles 14:13">13</a> The Philistines once more spread out in the valley.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="1 Chronicles 14:14">14</a> David asked God again, and God told him, &quot;Do not chase them; go around them and attack from near the mulberry trees.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="1 Chronicles 14:15">15</a> When you hear a rustling in the mulberry trees, then move out to fight because God has gone ahead of you to strike down the Philistine army.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="1 Chronicles 14:16">16</a> David did as God told him, and they defeated the Philistine army from Gibeon to Gazer.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="1 Chronicles 14:17">17</a> David became famous in all the lands and God made all nations fear him.</p>
