<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Chronicles 29:1">1</a> King David told everyone, &quot;My son Solomon, who God chose alone, is still young and unexperienced, and the task is huge. This palace is not for a human, but for God.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Chronicles 29:2">2</a> I have made ready with all my strength for God&#39;s house: gold for the gold items, silver for the silver, bronze for the bronze, iron for the iron, and wood for the wooden items. Also onyx, setting stones, sparkling stones, stones of many colors, all kinds of precious stones, and plenty of marble.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Chronicles 29:3">3</a> I love my God&#39;s house so much that I have given my own gold and silver to it, more than everything else I&#39;ve provided for the sacred place.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Chronicles 29:4">4</a> Even three thousand units of gold from Ophir and seven thousand units of pure silver to cover the house walls.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Chronicles 29:5">5</a> Gold for gold items, silver for silver items, and for all kinds of work done by skilled workers. And who is ready to dedicate their service today to God?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Chronicles 29:6">6</a> The leaders of the families, the heads of the Israelite tribes, and the commanders of thousands and of hundreds, along with the supervisors of the king&#39;s projects, gave freely.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Chronicles 29:7">7</a> God gave to serve God&#39;s house: 5,000 talents of gold, 10,000 drams, 10,000 talents of silver, 18,000 talents of brass, and 100,000 talents of iron.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Chronicles 29:8">8</a> People who had precious stones brought them to God&#39;s house treasury, through Jehiel the Gershonite.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Chronicles 29:9">9</a> The people were happy because they gave freely and with true hearts to God, and King David was also very happy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Chronicles 29:10">10</a> David praised God in front of everyone and said, &quot;Praise to you, God of Israel, our father, forever and ever.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Chronicles 29:11">11</a> Yours, O God, is the greatness, the power, the glory, the victory, and the majesty: for everything in heaven and on earth is yours; yours is the kingdom, O God, and you are honored as the leader above all.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Chronicles 29:12">12</a> Wealth and honor come from you, God; you rule over everything. Power and strength are in your hand; you have the ability to make anyone great and strong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 Chronicles 29:13">13</a> So now, our God, we thank you and honor your wonderful name.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="1 Chronicles 29:14">14</a> Who am I and who are my people that we can freely give like this? Everything comes from you, and we have given you only what comes from you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="1 Chronicles 29:15">15</a> We are outsiders before you, and travelers, just like our ancestors: our time on earth is like a shadow, with no one staying forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="1 Chronicles 29:16">16</a> God, all these things we&#39;ve gathered to build a house for your holy name come from your hand, and they are all yours.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="1 Chronicles 29:17">17</a> I also know, my God, that you test the heart and you like honesty. I have gladly given all this with an honest heart. And now I see with joy your people here giving to you willingly.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="1 Chronicles 29:18">18</a> O God of Abraham, Isaac, and Israel, our ancestors, always keep this in the hearts and minds of your people, and make their hearts loyal to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="1 Chronicles 29:19">19</a> Give my son Solomon a pure heart to follow your commands, your guidance, and your laws, to do everything, and to build the temple for which I have provided.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="1 Chronicles 29:20">20</a> David told everyone, &quot;Now praise God your God.&quot; Everyone praised the God of their ancestors, bowed their heads, and worshiped God and the king.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="1 Chronicles 29:21">21</a> They offered sacrifices and burnt offerings to God the day after, including a thousand bulls, a thousand rams, and a thousand lambs, along with their drink offerings, and many more sacrifices for all the people of Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="1 Chronicles 29:22">22</a> They ate and drank with great joy before God that day. They made Solomon, David&#39;s son, king again, anointed him to serve God as the supreme leader, and appointed Zadok as the priest.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="1 Chronicles 29:23">23</a> Then Solomon sat on God&#39;s throne as king in place of his father David, succeeded, and all Israel followed him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="1 Chronicles 29:24">24</a> All the leaders, strong men, and King David&#39;s sons too, accepted Solomon as king.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="1 Chronicles 29:25">25</a> God made Solomon very great in the eyes of all Israel, and gave him a kind of royal splendor no other king in Israel had ever had.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="1 Chronicles 29:26">26</a> David, Jesse&#39;s son, ruled over all Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="1 Chronicles 29:27">27</a> He ruled over Israel for forty years; seven years in Hebron and thirty-three years in Jerusalem.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="1 Chronicles 29:28">28</a> He died at a ripe old age, with a long life, wealth, and respect, and his son Solomon became king after him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="1 Chronicles 29:29">29</a> The things King David did, from beginning to end, are recorded in Samuel the seer&#39;s book, in Nathan the prophet&#39;s book, and in Gad the seer&#39;s book.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="1 Chronicles 29:30">30</a> Throughout his rule and power, and the events that happened to him, to Israel, and to all the kingdoms of the lands.</p>
