<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Chronicles 22:1">1</a> Then David said, &quot;This is God&#39;s house, and this is the altar for Israel&#39;s burnt offerings.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Chronicles 22:2">2</a> David ordered to bring together the foreigners in Israel; he assigned stonecutters to shape stones to construct God&#39;s house.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Chronicles 22:3">3</a> David collected lots of iron for the gate door nails and connectors, and an immeasurable amount of brass.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Chronicles 22:4">4</a> Many cedar trees were brought to David by the people of Sidon and Tyre because they had a lot of cedar wood.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Chronicles 22:5">5</a> David said, &quot;My son Solomon is young and gentle, and the temple that will be built for God must be very magnificent, famous, and glorious in all countries. So, I will now prepare for it.&quot; Thus, David made many preparations before he died.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Chronicles 22:6">6</a> He called his son Solomon and told him to build a house for the God of Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Chronicles 22:7">7</a> David said to Solomon, &quot;My son, I planned to build a house for God&#39;s name.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Chronicles 22:8">8</a> God&#39;s message came to me: &quot;You have spilled a lot of blood and fought many wars. You cannot build a house for my name because you have caused much bloodshed on the earth in front of me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Chronicles 22:9">9</a> Look, you will have a son who will be a peaceful man; I will make sure he has peace from all his enemies around. His name will be Solomon, and I will bring peace and calm to Israel during his life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Chronicles 22:10">10</a> He will build a house for my name; he will be my son, and I will be his father; and I will set up his kingdom&#39;s throne over Israel forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Chronicles 22:11">11</a> Now, my son, may God be with you; succeed and build God&#39;s house, as he has told you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Chronicles 22:12">12</a> May God give you wisdom and understanding, and instruct you about Israel, so you can follow God&#39;s law.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 Chronicles 22:13">13</a> You will succeed if you carefully follow the rules and decisions that God gave Moses for Israel: be strong and brave; do not be afraid or discouraged.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="1 Chronicles 22:14">14</a> Now, look, in my difficult time, I have prepared for God&#39;s house one hundred thousand talents of gold and one million talents of silver; and so much brass and iron they can&#39;t be weighed because there&#39;s so much; I have also prepared wood and stone; and you can add more.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="1 Chronicles 22:15">15</a> You also have many workers: stonecutters, carpenters, and all kinds of skilled people for every job.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="1 Chronicles 22:16">16</a> There&#39;s so much gold, silver, bronze, and iron that it can&#39;t be counted. Get up and start working, and God will be with you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="1 Chronicles 22:17">17</a> David also told all the leaders of Israel to support his son Solomon, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="1 Chronicles 22:18">18</a> Isn&#39;t God with you? Hasn&#39;t he given you peace all around? For he has let us defeat the people of the land, and now the land is under God&#39;s control and his people&#39;s.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="1 Chronicles 22:19">19</a> Focus your heart and soul on searching for God; get up and build the holy place for God, to put the ark of God&#39;s agreement and God&#39;s sacred items in the temple made for God&#39;s name.</p>
