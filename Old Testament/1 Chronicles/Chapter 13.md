<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Chronicles 13:1">1</a> David talked with the leaders of thousands and hundreds, and with every chief.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Chronicles 13:2">2</a> David said to all the people of Israel, &quot;If it seems good to you and it is God&#39;s will, let&#39;s send a message to our brothers everywhere who remain in the land of Israel, and to the priests and Levites in their towns and surrounding areas, so they can join us.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Chronicles 13:3">3</a> Let&#39;s bring back God&#39;s ark to us; we didn&#39;t ask for its guidance when Saul was king.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Chronicles 13:4">4</a> And the whole group agreed to do it because everyone thought it was the right thing to do.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Chronicles 13:5">5</a> David brought all the people of Israel from the Shihor River near Egypt to Hemath to move God&#39;s ark from Kirjathjearim.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Chronicles 13:6">6</a> David and all of Israel went to Baalah, also known as Kirjathjearim in Judah, to bring back the ark of God, called by His name and resting between the cherubim.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Chronicles 13:7">7</a> They moved God&#39;s ark on a new cart from Abinadab&#39;s house; Uzza and Ahio drove the cart.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Chronicles 13:8">8</a> David and everyone in Israel danced and played music for God with all their strength, using songs, harps, lyres, tambourines, cymbals, and trumpets.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Chronicles 13:9">9</a> When they got to Chidon&#39;s threshing floor, Uzza reached out to steady the ark because the oxen tripped.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Chronicles 13:10">10</a> God became angry with Uzza and struck him down because he touched the ark, and Uzza died there in front of God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Chronicles 13:11">11</a> David was upset because God had punished Uzza, so that place is named Perezuzza even now.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Chronicles 13:12">12</a> David was scared of God that day and asked, &quot;How can I bring God&#39;s ark to my house?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 Chronicles 13:13">13</a> David did not bring the ark to his own city but took it to the house of Obed-edom, a man from Gath.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="1 Chronicles 13:14">14</a> God&#39;s chest stayed with Obededom&#39;s family in his home for three months. And God blessed Obededom&#39;s home and all his belongings.</p>
