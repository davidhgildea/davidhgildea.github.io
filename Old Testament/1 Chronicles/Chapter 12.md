<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Chronicles 12:1">1</a> These people came to David at Ziklag when he was still hiding from Saul, Kish&#39;s son. They were strong warriors who helped in battle.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Chronicles 12:2">2</a> They carried bows and could throw stones and shoot arrows with both their right and left hands, like Saul&#39;s relatives from Benjamin.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Chronicles 12:3">3</a> The leader was Ahiezer, followed by Joash, both sons of Shemaah from Gibeon; Jeziel and Pelet were Azmaveth&#39;s sons; also, Berachah and Jehu from Anathoth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Chronicles 12:4">4</a> Ismaiah from Gibeon was a strong warrior among the thirty leaders, as were Jeremiah, Jahaziel, Johanan, and Josabad from Gederah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Chronicles 12:5">5</a> Eluzai, Jerimoth, Bealiah, Shemariah, and Shephatiah from Haruph.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Chronicles 12:6">6</a> Elkanah, Jesiah, Azareel, Joezer, and Jashobeam were Korahites.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Chronicles 12:7">7</a> Joelah and Zebadiah, Jeroham of Gedor&#39;s sons.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Chronicles 12:8">8</a> Some strong warriors from the tribe of Gad joined David in the wilderness fortress. They were skilled in battle, could handle shields and swords, had faces like lions, and were as fast as deer on the mountains.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Chronicles 12:9">9</a> Ezer was the first, Obadiah was the second, Eliab was the third.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Chronicles 12:10">10</a> Mishmannah was number four, Jeremiah was number five.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Chronicles 12:11">11</a> Attai was the sixth, Eliel was the seventh.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Chronicles 12:12">12</a> Johanan was the eighth, Elzabad was the ninth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 Chronicles 12:13">13</a> Jeremiah was the tenth, Machbanai was the eleventh.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="1 Chronicles 12:14">14</a> These were children of Gad, leaders of the army: even the smallest leader commanded a hundred, and the greatest commanded a thousand.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="1 Chronicles 12:15">15</a> These are the people who crossed the Jordan River in the first month, when it had flooded over all its edges; and they made all the valley dwellers run away, both to the east and to the west.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="1 Chronicles 12:16">16</a> Some of the people of Benjamin and Judah went to the stronghold to meet David.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="1 Chronicles 12:17">17</a> David met them and said, &quot;If you have come peacefully to help me, my heart will join with yours. But if you&#39;re here to betray me to my enemies, when I&#39;ve done no wrong, may God, the God of our ancestors, see it and judge.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="1 Chronicles 12:18">18</a> The spirit filled Amasai, the leader of the captains. He said, &quot;We are with you, David, and support you, son of Jesse. May you have peace, and may your helpers have peace because God supports you.&quot; Then David welcomed them and made them leaders of his troops.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="1 Chronicles 12:19">19</a> Some from the tribe of Manasseh joined David when he went with the Philistines to fight Saul; but they did not aid them. The Philistine leaders, after discussing it, sent him away, saying, &quot;He might turn against us and go back to Saul, putting us in danger.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="1 Chronicles 12:20">20</a> On his way to Ziklag, some men from Manasseh joined him: Adnah, Jozabad, Jediael, Michael, Jozabad, Elihu, and Zilthai, who were leaders of groups of a thousand from Manasseh.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="1 Chronicles 12:21">21</a> They helped David fight the raiders because they were all brave warriors and leaders in the army.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="1 Chronicles 12:22">22</a> Every day, more people came to support David, and gradually there was a huge group, as large as God&#39;s group.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="1 Chronicles 12:23">23</a> These are the counts of the groups prepared for battle who came to David in Hebron to shift Saul&#39;s kingdom to him, following God&#39;s command.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="1 Chronicles 12:24">24</a> The soldiers from Judah with shields and spears were 6,800, prepared to fight in the war.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="1 Chronicles 12:25">25</a> From the Simeon family, seven thousand one hundred strong warriors for battle.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="1 Chronicles 12:26">26</a> From the Levi tribe, there were four thousand six hundred people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="1 Chronicles 12:27">27</a> Jehoiada was the leader of Aaron&#39;s descendants, and he had three thousand seven hundred people with him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="1 Chronicles 12:28">28</a> Zadok, a young brave man, had twenty-two captains in his father&#39;s family.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="1 Chronicles 12:29">29</a> From the tribe of Benjamin, Saul&#39;s relatives, there were three thousand; until now, most of them had remained loyal to Saul&#39;s family.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="1 Chronicles 12:30">30</a> From the descendants of Ephraim, 20,800 brave and well-known warriors were counted.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="1 Chronicles 12:31">31</a> From half the tribe of Manasseh, eighteen thousand were named to come and make David king.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="1 Chronicles 12:32">32</a> From the tribe of Issachar, there were 200 leaders who understood the times and knew what Israel should do; their relatives supported them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="1 Chronicles 12:33">33</a> From Zebulun, 50,000 skilled warriors ready for battle with all their weapons went out. They were united in purpose and not divided in loyalty.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="1 Chronicles 12:34">34</a> From Naphtali, there were a thousand leaders, along with thirty-seven thousand men with shields and spears.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="1 Chronicles 12:35">35</a> From the Danites, there were 28,600 skilled soldiers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="1 Chronicles 12:36">36</a> From Asher, forty thousand skilled warriors went out to fight.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="1 Chronicles 12:37">37</a> Across the Jordan River, the Reubenites, Gadites, and half the tribe of Manasseh were ready for war with all kinds of weapons, totaling 120,000 soldiers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="1 Chronicles 12:38">38</a> All these warriors, able to maintain order, came to Hebron with a true heart to make David the king over all Israel, and every other person in Israel also agreed with one heart to make David king.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="1 Chronicles 12:39">39</a> They stayed with David for three days, eating and drinking because their relatives had made preparations for them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="1 Chronicles 12:40">40</a> People nearby, including those from Issachar, Zebulun, and Naphtali, brought bread on donkeys, camels, mules, and oxen, along with meat, flour, fig cakes, raisin clusters, wine, oil, more oxen, and lots of sheep, because there was great joy in Israel.</p>
