<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Chronicles 15:1">1</a> David built himself homes in the city of David, made a spot ready for God&#39;s ark, and set up a tent for it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Chronicles 15:2">2</a> David said, only the Levites should carry God&#39;s ark because God chose them to carry it and to serve Him forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Chronicles 15:3">3</a> David brought all of Israel to Jerusalem to move God&#39;s ark to the spot he had set up for it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Chronicles 15:4">4</a> David gathered Aaron&#39;s descendants and the Levites.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Chronicles 15:5">5</a> From Kohath&#39;s descendants, Uriel was the leader, and with him were 120 relatives.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Chronicles 15:6">6</a> From Merari&#39;s descendants: Asaiah was the leader, with 220 of his relatives.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Chronicles 15:7">7</a> From Gershom&#39;s descendants, Joel was the leader, with 130 in his family.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Chronicles 15:8">8</a> From Elizaphan&#39;s descendants: Shemaiah was the leader, with two hundred of his relatives.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Chronicles 15:9">9</a> From Hebron&#39;s sons, Eliel was the leader, with eighty of his brothers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Chronicles 15:10">10</a> From Uzziel&#39;s sons: Amminadab was the leader, and he had 112 brothers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Chronicles 15:11">11</a> David called for the priests Zadok and Abiathar, and for the Levites Uriel, Asaiah, Joel, Shemaiah, Eliel, and Amminadab.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Chronicles 15:12">12</a> He said to them, &quot;You are the leaders of the Levites&#39; ancestors. Make yourselves and your brothers holy so you can bring God&#39;s ark to the place I have made ready for it.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 Chronicles 15:13">13</a> Because we didn&#39;t do it right the first time, God punished us since we didn&#39;t follow the proper way to seek him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="1 Chronicles 15:14">14</a> The priests and Levites made themselves pure to carry the ark of God of Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="1 Chronicles 15:15">15</a> The Levite children carried God&#39;s ark on their shoulders with the poles in place, as Moses ordered by God&#39;s command.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="1 Chronicles 15:16">16</a> David told the Levite leaders to choose their relatives to sing and play music with lyres, harps, and cymbals, making joyful sounds with loud voices.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="1 Chronicles 15:17">17</a> The Levites chose Heman, Joel&#39;s son; and from his brothers, they chose Asaph, Berechiah&#39;s son; and from the Merari family, Ethan, Kushaiah&#39;s son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="1 Chronicles 15:18">18</a> Also with them were their fellow workers of the next rank: Zechariah, Ben, Jaaziel, Shemiramoth, Jehiel, Unni, Eliab, Benaiah, Maaseiah, Mattithiah, Elipheleh, Mikneiah, Obededom, and Jeiel, the gatekeepers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="1 Chronicles 15:19">19</a> The singers, Heman, Asaph, and Ethan, were chosen to play bronze cymbals.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="1 Chronicles 15:20">20</a> Zechariah, Aziel, Shemiramoth, Jehiel, Unni, Eliab, Maaseiah, and Benaiah played the harps following the Alamoth style.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="1 Chronicles 15:21">21</a> Mattithiah, Elipheleh, Mikneiah, Obededom, Jeiel, and Azaziah played their harps tuned to the octave to lead the music.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="1 Chronicles 15:22">22</a> Chenaniah, the head of the Levites, was in charge of the music. He taught others about music because he was talented.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="1 Chronicles 15:23">23</a> Berechiah and Elkanah were guards for the ark.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="1 Chronicles 15:24">24</a> Shebaniah, Jehoshaphat, Nethaneel, Amasai, Zechariah, Benaiah, and Eliezer, who were priests, blew trumpets in front of God&#39;s ark, while Obededom and Jehiah guarded the doors of the ark.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="1 Chronicles 15:25">25</a> David, the leaders of Israel, and the commanders of groups of a thousand went to happily move God&#39;s covenant chest from Obed-edom&#39;s home.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="1 Chronicles 15:26">26</a> When God helped the Levites carrying the ark of God&#39;s covenant, they sacrificed seven bulls and seven rams.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="1 Chronicles 15:27">27</a> David wore a fine linen robe, and so did all the Levites carrying the ark, the singers, and Chenaniah who led them in music. David also wore a linen ephod.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="1 Chronicles 15:28">28</a> So all of Israel carried the ark of God&#39;s covenant with shouting, with cornet sounds, trumpets, cymbals, and made music with lyres and harps.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="1 Chronicles 15:29">29</a> When the ark of God&#39;s covenant arrived at David&#39;s city, Michal, Saul&#39;s daughter, watched from a window as King David danced and played music, and she felt contempt for him in her heart.</p>
