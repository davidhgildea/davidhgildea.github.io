<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Chronicles 27:1">1</a> The Israelite people were organized by their families, with leaders and commanders for groups of one thousand and one hundred, and officials who worked for the king on different tasks. These groups took turns each month over the year, with each group having twenty-four thousand members.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Chronicles 27:2">2</a> For the first division in the first month, Jashobeam son of Zabdiel was in charge, and his division had 24,000 men.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Chronicles 27:3">3</a> A leader of the army for the first month came from Perez&#39;s descendants.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Chronicles 27:4">4</a> In the second month, Dodai the Ahohite was in charge, with Mikloth as the leader, and they had 24,000 men in their group.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Chronicles 27:5">5</a> The third army leader for the third month was Benaiah, Jehoiada&#39;s son, a head priest, and he had 24,000 men in his group.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Chronicles 27:6">6</a> This is Benaiah, who was a strong warrior among the thirty leaders and ranked above them. His son Ammizabad was in his division.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Chronicles 27:7">7</a> The fourth leader for the fourth month was Asahel, Joab&#39;s brother, with his son Zebadiah following him. He commanded 24,000 men.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Chronicles 27:8">8</a> The leader for the fifth month was Shamhuth the Izrahite; he had 24,000 men in his group.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Chronicles 27:9">9</a> The sixth leader for the sixth month was Ira, Ikkesh&#39;s son from Tekoa, with 24,000 men in his group.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Chronicles 27:10">10</a> The leader for the seventh month was Helez the Pelonite from the tribe of Ephraim, with 24,000 men in his group.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Chronicles 27:11">11</a> The eighth leader for the eighth month was Sibbecai the Hushathite, from the Zarhites, and he commanded 24,000 soldiers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Chronicles 27:12">12</a> The ninth leader for the ninth month was Abiezer from Anetoth, a Benjaminite, and he had 24,000 men in his group.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 Chronicles 27:13">13</a> The leader for the tenth month was Maharai from Netophah, of the Zarhites, and he had 24,000 men in his group.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="1 Chronicles 27:14">14</a> The leader for the eleventh month was Benaiah from Pirathon, who was from the tribe of Ephraim, and he was in charge of 24,000 men.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="1 Chronicles 27:15">15</a> The twelfth leader for the twelfth month was Heldai the Netophathite, from Othniel&#39;s family, and his group had twenty-four thousand men.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="1 Chronicles 27:16">16</a> In charge of the tribes of Israel: the leader of the Reubenites was Eliezer, Zichri&#39;s son, and for the Simeonites, Shephatiah, Maachah&#39;s son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="1 Chronicles 27:17">17</a> From the Levites, Hashabiah son of Kemuel; from the descendants of Aaron, Zadok.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="1 Chronicles 27:18">18</a> From Judah, Elihu, one of David&#39;s brothers; from Issachar, Omri, Michael&#39;s son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="1 Chronicles 27:19">19</a> From Zebulun, Ishmaiah son of Obadiah; from Naphtali, Jerimoth son of Azriel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="1 Chronicles 27:20">20</a> Ephraim&#39;s descendant was Hoshea, Azaziah&#39;s son. From Manasseh&#39;s half tribe, Joel was Pedaiah&#39;s son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="1 Chronicles 27:21">21</a> From the half-tribe of Manasseh in Gilead, Iddo son of Zechariah; from Benjamin, Jaasiel son of Abner.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="1 Chronicles 27:22">22</a> From the tribe of Dan, Azareel son of Jeroham was the leader. These men were the chiefs of Israel&#39;s tribes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="1 Chronicles 27:23">23</a> David did not count the people under twenty years old, because God had promised to make Israel as numerous as the stars in the sky.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="1 Chronicles 27:24">24</a> Joab, Zeruiah&#39;s son, started counting, but did not finish because God&#39;s anger fell on Israel for it; the count was not included in King David&#39;s records.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="1 Chronicles 27:25">25</a> Azmaveth, son of Adiel, was in charge of the king&#39;s treasures, and Jehonathan, son of Uzziah, was responsible for the storehouses in the fields, cities, villages, and fortresses.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="1 Chronicles 27:26">26</a> Ezri, Chelub&#39;s son, oversaw the workers who tilled the soil.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="1 Chronicles 27:27">27</a> Shimei from Ramah took care of the vineyards. Zabdi from Shiphmi managed the grapes for the wine storage.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="1 Chronicles 27:28">28</a> Baalhanan from Geder took care of the olive and sycamore trees in the lowlands, and Joash took care of the oil storerooms.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="1 Chronicles 27:29">29</a> Shitrai from Sharon took care of the herds in Sharon, and Shaphat, Adlai&#39;s son, took care of the herds in the valleys.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="1 Chronicles 27:30">30</a> Obil the Ishmaelite was in charge of the camels, and Jehdeiah the Meronothite was in charge of the donkeys.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="1 Chronicles 27:31">31</a> Jaziz the Hagerite was in charge of the flocks. All these men were leaders of King David&#39;s property.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="1 Chronicles 27:32">32</a> Jonathan, David&#39;s uncle, was an advisor, a wise man, and a writer; and Jehiel, Hachmoni&#39;s son, stayed with the king&#39;s sons.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="1 Chronicles 27:33">33</a> Ahithophel was the king&#39;s advisor, and Hushai the Archite was the king&#39;s friend.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="1 Chronicles 27:34">34</a> After Ahithophel came Jehoiada, son of Benaiah, and Abiathar. Joab was the commander of the king’s army.</p>
