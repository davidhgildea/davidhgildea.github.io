<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Haggai 2:1">1</a> In the seventh month, on the twenty-first day, God&#39;s message came through the prophet Haggai, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Haggai 2:2">2</a> Talk to Zerubbabel, son of Shealtiel, the leader of Judah, and to Joshua, son of Josedech, the high priest, and to the rest of the people, and say,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Haggai 2:3">3</a> Who among you saw this temple when it was first built? And what do you think of it now? Doesn&#39;t it seem like nothing compared to before?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Haggai 2:4">4</a> Be strong now, Zerubbabel, says God; and be strong, Joshua, son of Josedech, the high priest; and be strong, all you people of the land, says God, and work: because I am with you, says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Haggai 2:5">5</a> Based on the promise I made to you when you left Egypt, my spirit stays with you: do not be afraid.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Haggai 2:6">6</a> God says: After a little while, I will shake the sky, the earth, the sea, and the land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Haggai 2:7">7</a> I will shake all nations, and what every nation wants will come. I will fill this house with glory, says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Haggai 2:8">8</a> The silver and the gold belong to me, says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Haggai 2:9">9</a> The splendor of this new house will be greater than the old one, says God, and here I will bring peace, says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Haggai 2:10">10</a> On the 24th day of the ninth month, in the second year of King Darius, God&#39;s message came through the prophet Haggai, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Haggai 2:11">11</a> God says; Now ask the priests about the law and tell them,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Haggai 2:12">12</a> If someone carries sacred meat in their clothes and their clothes touch bread, soup, wine, oil, or any food, will it become sacred? The priests said no.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Haggai 2:13">13</a> Then Haggai asked, &quot;If someone unclean from a dead body touches any of these, does it become unclean?&quot; And the priests replied, &quot;It will become unclean.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Haggai 2:14">14</a> Haggai replied, &quot;This is how these people and this nation are before God, and so is everything they do; even their offerings are unclean.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Haggai 2:15">15</a> Please think about this day and the past, before any stone was set on another in God&#39;s temple.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Haggai 2:16">16</a> Since that time, when someone went to a pile of twenty measures, there were only ten; when someone went to the wine vat to get fifty containers, there were only twenty.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Haggai 2:17">17</a> I struck you with blight, mildew, and hail, which ruined your work; but you still didn&#39;t turn to me, says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Haggai 2:18">18</a> Think about this day and from now on, from the 24th day of the ninth month, since the day the foundation of God&#39;s temple was set, think about it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Haggai 2:19">19</a> Is the seed still in the barn? Yes, the grapevine, fig tree, pomegranate, and olive tree haven&#39;t produced fruit yet. But from today, I will bless you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Haggai 2:20">20</a> Once more, on the twenty-fourth day of the month, God&#39;s message came to Haggai.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Haggai 2:21">21</a> Tell Zerubbabel, the leader of Judah, I will shake the sky and the ground.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Haggai 2:22">22</a> I will topple the rulers of nations, destroy the power of foreign kingdoms, overturn their war chariots and riders; the horses and riders will fall, each killed by his comrade&#39;s sword.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Haggai 2:23">23</a> On that day, says God, I will choose you, Zerubbabel, my servant, the son of Shealtiel, says God, and I will make you like a seal ring, because I have chosen you, says God.</p>
