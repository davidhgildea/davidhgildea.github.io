<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Haggai 1:1">1</a> In Darius&#39;s second year as king, on the first day of the sixth month, God&#39;s message came through the prophet Haggai to Zerubbabel, Shealtiel&#39;s son and the governor of Judah, and to Joshua, Josedech&#39;s son and the high priest.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Haggai 1:2">2</a> God says, &quot;These people claim it&#39;s not the time to build God&#39;s house.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Haggai 1:3">3</a> God&#39;s message came through the prophet Haggai, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Haggai 1:4">4</a> Is it time for you to live in your paneled houses while this house remains in ruins?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Haggai 1:5">5</a> So now, God says this: Think about what you&#39;re doing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Haggai 1:6">6</a> You planted a lot but harvest little; you eat but aren&#39;t full; you drink but aren&#39;t satisfied; you dress but aren&#39;t warm; and you earn money only to put it into a pocket with holes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Haggai 1:7">7</a> God says; Think about what you do.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Haggai 1:8">8</a> Go to the mountain, get wood, and make the house; I will enjoy it and be honored, says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Haggai 1:9">9</a> You expected a lot, but see, it turned out to be little; and when you brought it home, I scattered it. Why? says God. Because my house is ruined, while each of you rushes to care for your own house.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Haggai 1:10">10</a> So the sky above you doesn&#39;t give dew, and the earth doesn&#39;t produce crops.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Haggai 1:11">11</a> I called for dryness on the land, the mountains, the grain, the new wine, the oil, the crops, the people, the animals, and all the work they do.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Haggai 1:12">12</a> Then Zerubbabel, Shealtiel&#39;s son, and Joshua, Josedech&#39;s son, the high priest, with all the remaining people, listened to God&#39;s voice and Haggai the prophet&#39;s words, because God had sent him, and the people respected God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Haggai 1:13">13</a> Then Haggai, God&#39;s messenger, delivered God&#39;s message to the people, saying, &quot;I am with you,&quot; says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Haggai 1:14">14</a> God motivated the spirit of Zerubbabel, son of Shealtiel, leader of Judah, and Joshua, son of Josedech, the high priest, and the spirits of all the remaining people; and they came and worked on God&#39;s house.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Haggai 1:15">15</a> On the twenty-fourth day of the sixth month, in King Darius&#39;s second year.</p>
