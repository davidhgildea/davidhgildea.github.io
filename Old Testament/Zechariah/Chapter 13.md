<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Zechariah 13:1">1</a> On that day, there will be a spring made available to David&#39;s family and to the people of Jerusalem for cleansing from sin and impurity.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Zechariah 13:2">2</a> On that day, God says, I will remove the idols from the land, and they will be forgotten. I will also get rid of the false prophets and evil spirits from the land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Zechariah 13:3">3</a> When someone still claims to speak for God, his own parents will say, &quot;You must not live because you tell lies in God&#39;s name.&quot; They will be the ones to end his life for doing so.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Zechariah 13:4">4</a> On that day, the prophets will feel shame for every vision when they have prophesied; they won&#39;t wear rough clothes to trick people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Zechariah 13:5">5</a> But he will say, &quot;I am not a prophet, I am a farmer; a person taught me to care for animals since I was young.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Zechariah 13:6">6</a> Someone will ask him, &quot;What are these wounds on your hands?&quot; And he will reply, &quot;These are the wounds I got at my friends&#39; house.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Zechariah 13:7">7</a> Wake up, sword, against my shepherd, and against the man close to me, says God. Strike the shepherd, and the sheep will run away; I will act against the small ones.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Zechariah 13:8">8</a> God says that in the whole land, two-thirds will be cut off and die, but one-third will be left.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Zechariah 13:9">9</a> I will take a third of the people through fire and make them pure like silver, and test them like gold. They will call on me, and I will listen. I will say, &#39;These are my people,&#39; and they will say, &#39;God is our God.&#39;</p>
