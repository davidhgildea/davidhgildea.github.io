<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Zechariah 4:1">1</a> The angel who was speaking with me came back and woke me up like someone being woken from sleep.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Zechariah 4:2">2</a> He asked me, &quot;What do you see?&quot; And I answered, &quot;I see a golden lampstand with a bowl at the top and seven lights on it, with seven tubes going to the lights.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Zechariah 4:3">3</a> Two olive trees were next to it, one on the right side of the bowl and one on the left side.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Zechariah 4:4">4</a> So I replied to the angel speaking with me, asking, &quot;What are these, sir?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Zechariah 4:5">5</a> The angel speaking with me replied and asked, &quot;Do you not know what these are?&quot; And I answered, &quot;No, sir.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Zechariah 4:6">6</a> He replied to me, &quot;This is what God tells Zerubbabel: &#39;Not by strength or by force, but by my spirit,&#39; says God.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Zechariah 4:7">7</a> Who are you, great mountain? Before Zerubbabel, you will become flat land: and he will present the top stone amid shouts of &#39;Grace, grace to it!&#39;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Zechariah 4:8">8</a> Also, God spoke to me, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Zechariah 4:9">9</a> Zerubbabel&#39;s hands have started building this house; his hands will also complete it; and you will know that God has sent me to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Zechariah 4:10">10</a> Who has looked down on small beginnings? They will celebrate and see the measuring line in Zerubbabel&#39;s hand along with the seven; they represent God&#39;s eyes, which move throughout the entire world.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Zechariah 4:11">11</a> I answered and asked him, &quot;What are these two olive trees on the right and left sides of the lampstand?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Zechariah 4:12">12</a> I replied again and asked him, &quot;What are these two olive branches that pour out golden oil through the two gold pipes?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Zechariah 4:13">13</a> He answered and asked, &quot;Do you not know what these are?&quot; And I said, &quot;No, sir.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Zechariah 4:14">14</a> He said, &quot;These are the two chosen ones who stand by God of the whole earth.&quot;</p>
