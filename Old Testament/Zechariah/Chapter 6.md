<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Zechariah 6:1">1</a> I turned, looked up, and saw four chariots coming from between two brass mountains.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Zechariah 6:2">2</a> In the first cart were red horses; and in the second cart black horses.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Zechariah 6:3">3</a> In the third chariot were white horses; in the fourth chariot were speckled and strong brown horses.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Zechariah 6:4">4</a> Then I replied to the angel who was speaking with me, &quot;What are these, sir?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Zechariah 6:5">5</a> The angel replied to me, &quot;These are the four spirits of the sky, sent out from the presence of God who rules over the whole earth.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Zechariah 6:6">6</a> The black horses inside go to the north; the white follow them; and the gray head south.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Zechariah 6:7">7</a> The horse went out to travel back and forth across the land, and God said, &quot;Go, move back and forth across the land.&quot; So they traveled back and forth across the land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Zechariah 6:8">8</a> He called out to me and said, &quot;Look, those going to the north country have calmed my spirit there.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Zechariah 6:9">9</a> God&#39;s message came to me, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Zechariah 6:10">10</a> Choose some men from the captives, from Heldai, Tobijah, and Jedaiah, who have arrived from Babylon. Go on the same day to the house of Josiah, Zephaniah&#39;s son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Zechariah 6:11">11</a> Then take silver and gold, make crowns, and put them on the head of Joshua, son of Josedech, the high priest.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Zechariah 6:12">12</a> Tell him this message from God: Look at the man called The BRANCH; he will rise from where he is and build God&#39;s temple.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Zechariah 6:13">13</a> He will build God&#39;s temple; he will have honor and sit and govern on his throne; he will be a priest on his throne, and peace will be the plan between the two.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Zechariah 6:14">14</a> The crowns will be for Helem, Tobijah, Jedaiah, and Hen, Zephaniah&#39;s son, as a reminder in God&#39;s temple.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Zechariah 6:15">15</a> People from far away will come and build God&#39;s temple, and you will realize that God has sent me to you. This will happen if you carefully listen to and obey God.</p>
