<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Zechariah 5:1">1</a> Then I turned, raised my eyes, and saw a flying scroll.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Zechariah 5:2">2</a> He asked me, &quot;What do you see?&quot; I replied, &quot;I see a flying scroll; it is twenty units long and ten units wide.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Zechariah 5:3">3</a> He told me, &quot;This is the curse that will spread across the whole earth: everyone who steals will be punished as shown here; and everyone who lies under oath will be punished as shown there.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Zechariah 5:4">4</a> I will make this happen, says God of all, and it will go into the thief&#39;s house, and into the house of anyone who lies using my name: it will stay in the middle of his house, and destroy it, with its wood and stones.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Zechariah 5:5">5</a> The angel who was speaking to me went out and then said to me, &quot;Look up now and see what is coming out.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Zechariah 5:6">6</a> I asked, &quot;What is that?&quot; He replied, &quot;This is a measuring basket that is being sent out.&quot; He also said, &quot;This represents them throughout the whole world.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Zechariah 5:7">7</a> Then I saw a piece of lead lifted up, and there was a woman sitting inside the basket.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Zechariah 5:8">8</a> He said, &quot;This is evil.&quot; He threw it into the ephah and put the lead cover over its opening.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Zechariah 5:9">9</a> I looked up and saw two women coming with the wind in their wings. They had wings like a stork&#39;s wings. They lifted the basket up into the sky, between the earth and heaven.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Zechariah 5:10">10</a> Then I asked the angel who was speaking with me, &quot;Where are they taking the basket?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Zechariah 5:11">11</a> He told me to build a house for it in the land of Shinar: it will be set up and stand on its own foundation.</p>
