<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Zechariah 3:1">1</a> He showed me Joshua the high priest standing before God&#39;s angel, and Satan standing at his right side to oppose him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Zechariah 3:2">2</a> God said to Satan, &quot;God corrects you, Satan; God who chose Jerusalem corrects you. Isn&#39;t this like a stick saved from the fire?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Zechariah 3:3">3</a> Joshua was wearing dirty clothes and stood in front of the angel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Zechariah 3:4">4</a> He replied to those standing before him, &quot;Remove the dirty clothes from him.&quot; Then he said to him, &quot;See, I have taken away your sin, and I will dress you in new clothes.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Zechariah 3:5">5</a> I said, &quot;Put a clean turban on his head.&quot; So they put a clean turban on his head and gave him clothes to wear. And God&#39;s messenger stood by.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Zechariah 3:6">6</a> The angel of God warned Joshua, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Zechariah 3:7">7</a> God says: If you follow my ways and keep my commands, then you will also govern my house and guard my courts, and I will give you places to walk among those who stand here.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Zechariah 3:8">8</a> Listen, Joshua the high priest, you and your friends who sit with you: they are men to be marveled at. Look, I will bring out my servant, the Branch.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Zechariah 3:9">9</a> Look at the stone I put in front of Joshua. This one stone has seven eyes on it. I will carve its design, says God, and in one day, I will take away the sin of that land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Zechariah 3:10">10</a> On that day, says God, everyone will invite their neighbor to sit together under the vine and the fig tree.</p>
