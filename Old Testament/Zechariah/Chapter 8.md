<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Zechariah 8:1">1</a> God spoke to me once more, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Zechariah 8:2">2</a> God says; I felt very jealous for Zion, and my jealousy for her was very strong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Zechariah 8:3">3</a> God says, &quot;I have come back to Zion, and I will live in the heart of Jerusalem. Jerusalem will be known as the City of Truth, and God&#39;s holy mountain will be called the Sacred Mountain.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Zechariah 8:4">4</a> God says: Old men and women will still live in Jerusalem&#39;s streets, each with a cane because of their great age.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Zechariah 8:5">5</a> The city&#39;s streets will be filled with boys and girls playing there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Zechariah 8:6">6</a> God says: Even if this seems amazing to the few people left today, wouldn&#39;t it seem amazing to me as well? says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Zechariah 8:7">7</a> God says, &quot;Look, I will rescue my people from the east and from the west.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Zechariah 8:8">8</a> I will bring them to live in the center of Jerusalem. They will be my people, and I will be their God, in truth and fairness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Zechariah 8:9">9</a> God says; Stay strong, all who hear the prophets&#39; words today, spoken since the time the temple foundation was laid, so that the temple can be built.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Zechariah 8:10">10</a> Before these days, people and animals did not get paid; there was no peace for anyone going or coming because of the trouble. I made everyone turn against their neighbor.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Zechariah 8:11">11</a> But now I will not act towards the remaining people as I did before, says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Zechariah 8:12">12</a> The seeds will grow well; the vine will produce fruit, the earth will yield its crops, and the sky will give its dew; and I will let the survivors among the people have all these things.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Zechariah 8:13">13</a> It will happen that, just as you were a curse among the nations, O people of Judah and Israel, so I will rescue you, and you will be a blessing. Do not be afraid, but be strong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Zechariah 8:14">14</a> God says, &quot;Just as I planned to punish you when your ancestors made me angry, says God, and I did not change my mind.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Zechariah 8:15">15</a> So, I have decided once more to be good to Jerusalem and the people of Judah: do not be afraid.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Zechariah 8:16">16</a> Do these things: Tell the truth to each other; make honest and peaceful decisions in your communities.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Zechariah 8:17">17</a> Do not think of hurting others in your hearts, and do not like lying promises, because I hate all these things, says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Zechariah 8:18">18</a> God&#39;s message came to me, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Zechariah 8:19">19</a> God says: The fasts in the fourth, fifth, seventh, and tenth months will bring joy, happiness, and cheerful festivals to Judah&#39;s people; so love honesty and peace.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Zechariah 8:20">20</a> God says; It will happen, that people will come from many cities.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Zechariah 8:21">21</a> People from one city will go to another, saying, &quot;Let&#39;s go quickly to worship God and to seek God. I will go too.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Zechariah 8:22">22</a> Yes, many people and powerful nations will come to look for God in Jerusalem and to pray to God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Zechariah 8:23">23</a> God says, in those days, ten men from different nations and languages will grab the clothes of a Jewish man, saying, &quot;We want to go with you because we&#39;ve heard that God is with you.&quot;</p>
