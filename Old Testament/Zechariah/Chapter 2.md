<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Zechariah 2:1">1</a> I looked up once more, and saw a man holding a measuring tape.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Zechariah 2:2">2</a> Then I asked, &quot;Where are you going?&quot; And he answered me, &quot;To measure Jerusalem, to find out how wide and how long it is.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Zechariah 2:3">3</a> The angel who was speaking with me left, and another angel came out to meet him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Zechariah 2:4">4</a> He said to him, &quot;Go quickly, tell the young man, &#39;Jerusalem will be filled with people and animals, and it won&#39;t need walls.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Zechariah 2:5">5</a> I, says God, will be like a protective wall of fire around her, and I will be the honor within her.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Zechariah 2:6">6</a> Come, come, leave the land of the north, says God, for I have scattered you in all directions like the four winds of the sky, says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Zechariah 2:7">7</a> Save yourself, Zion, who lives among the people of Babylon.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Zechariah 2:8">8</a> God, the Lord of all, says this: After the glory, he sent me to the nations that robbed you. Because whoever harms you, harms the apple of his eye.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Zechariah 2:9">9</a> Look, I will wave my hand at them, and they will become loot for their own slaves. Then you will know that God has sent me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Zechariah 2:10">10</a> Sing and be happy, daughter of Zion; for look, I am coming and I will live among you, says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Zechariah 2:11">11</a> Many countries will join with God on that day and will be my people. I will live among you, and you will realize that God has sent me to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Zechariah 2:12">12</a> God will take Judah as his own in the sacred land and will choose Jerusalem once more.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Zechariah 2:13">13</a> Everyone be quiet before God, for he has risen from his sacred home.</p>
