<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Zechariah 10:1">1</a> Ask God for rain when the late rains come; then God will send thunderclouds and give everyone rain for the plants in the fields.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Zechariah 10:2">2</a> The idols have said meaningless things, and the fortune-tellers have seen lies and told untrue dreams; they offer empty comfort. So the people wandered like sheep, distressed because they had no leader.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Zechariah 10:3">3</a> I became angry with the shepherds and punished the goats. God has taken care of his people, Judah, and made them strong like a fine horse in battle.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Zechariah 10:4">4</a> From him comes the cornerstone, from him the tent peg, from him the bow for battle, from him every ruler together.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Zechariah 10:5">5</a> They will be like strong warriors, stepping on their enemies in the street mud during the fight: and they will battle because God is with them, and the horse riders will be confused.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Zechariah 10:6">6</a> I will make the people of Judah strong, and I will rescue the people of Joseph. I will bring them back and settle them because I care for them. They will be as if I had never rejected them. I am God, their God, and I will listen to them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Zechariah 10:7">7</a> The people of Ephraim will be strong, and their hearts will be happy like when drinking wine; yes, their children will see it and be happy; their hearts will be joyful in God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Zechariah 10:8">8</a> I will call them and bring them together; for I have saved them: and they will multiply as they did before.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Zechariah 10:9">9</a> I will scatter them among the nations, and in distant lands they will remember me; they will live with their children and return.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Zechariah 10:10">10</a> I will bring them back from Egypt and gather them from Assyria; I will take them to Gilead and Lebanon, where there won&#39;t be enough room for them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Zechariah 10:11">11</a> He will go through the sea in trouble, hit the sea&#39;s waves, and the river&#39;s depths will dry up. Assyria&#39;s pride will be lowered, and Egypt&#39;s power will leave.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Zechariah 10:12">12</a> I will make them strong in God; they will live and act in his name, says God.</p>
