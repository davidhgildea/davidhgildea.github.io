<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Judges 3:1">1</a> These are the nations God left to test Israel, especially those in Israel who had not experienced all the Canaan wars.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Judges 3:2">2</a> The idea was that Israel&#39;s descendants should learn about war, especially those who had never known it before.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Judges 3:3">3</a> Specifically, five rulers of the Philistines, all the Canaanites, the Sidonians, and the Hivites living in Mount Lebanon, from Mount Baal Hermon to the entrance of Hamath.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Judges 3:4">4</a> They were used to test Israel, to see if they would follow God&#39;s commands given to their ancestors through Moses.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Judges 3:5">5</a> The Israelites lived among the Canaanites, Hittites, Amorites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Judges 3:6">6</a> They married their daughters and gave their daughters to their sons and worshiped their gods.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Judges 3:7">7</a> The Israelites did bad things that God saw, forgot God, and worshiped Baal and the Asherah poles.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Judges 3:8">8</a> So God was very angry with Israel, and he let the king of Mesopotamia, Chushanrishathaim, defeat them. The people of Israel had to serve Chushanrishathaim for eight years.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Judges 3:9">9</a> When the Israelites called out to God, God gave them a rescuer, Othniel the son of Kenaz and Caleb&#39;s younger brother, who saved them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Judges 3:10">10</a> God&#39;s Spirit came upon him, and he led Israel. He went to battle, and God helped him defeat Chushanrishathaim, the king of Mesopotamia; he overpowered Chushanrishathaim.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Judges 3:11">11</a> The land was peaceful for forty years. Then Othniel, Kenaz&#39;s son, passed away.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Judges 3:12">12</a> The Israelites did wrong before God again, and God gave Eglon, the king of Moab, power over Israel because they had done wrong in God&#39;s eyes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Judges 3:13">13</a> He brought together the people of Ammon and Amalek, attacked Israel, and took over the city of palm trees.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Judges 3:14">14</a> The Israelites served King Eglon of Moab for eighteen years.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Judges 3:15">15</a> When the Israelites called out to God, God gave them a savior, Ehud, Gera&#39;s son from the tribe of Benjamin, who was left-handed. Through him, the Israelites sent a gift to Eglon, the king of Moab.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Judges 3:16">16</a> Ehud made a two-edged dagger about eighteen inches long and tied it under his clothes on his right thigh.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Judges 3:17">17</a> He gave the gift to Eglon, the king of Moab, who was very fat.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Judges 3:18">18</a> After he finished giving the gift, he let the people who carried the gift go.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Judges 3:19">19</a> However, he turned back from the stone-cutting place near Gilgal and said, &quot;I have a private message for you, king.&quot; The king replied, &quot;Be quiet.&quot; Then everyone with him left.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Judges 3:20">20</a> Ehud went to him while he was sitting alone in a summer room. Ehud said, &quot;I have a message from God for you.&quot; Then he stood up from his chair.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Judges 3:21">21</a> Ehud reached out with his left hand, took the dagger from his right thigh, and stabbed it into his stomach.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Judges 3:22">22</a> The handle followed the blade into the body; the fat closed over the blade, so he couldn&#39;t pull the knife from his stomach; and the insides spilled out.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Judges 3:23">23</a> Then Ehud went out through the entryway, closed the doors of the room behind him, and locked them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Judges 3:24">24</a> After he left, his servants arrived, and seeing the locked room doors, they thought, &quot;He must be resting in his cool room.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Judges 3:25">25</a> They waited until they felt embarrassed. When the doors to the room didn&#39;t open, they used a key to unlock them and found their master lying dead on the ground.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Judges 3:26">26</a> Ehud got away while they waited, went past the stone-cutting places, and ran to Seirath.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Judges 3:27">27</a> When he arrived, he sounded a trumpet on Mount Ephraim, and the Israelites followed him down, with him leading the way.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Judges 3:28">28</a> He told them, &quot;Follow me, for God has given us victory over the Moabites, our enemies.&quot; So they followed him, captured the crossing places of the Jordan River that led to Moab, and did not allow anyone to cross.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Judges 3:29">29</a> They killed about ten thousand strong and brave men from Moab at that time, and not a single man escaped.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Judges 3:30">30</a> So Moab was defeated that day by Israel, and the land was at peace for eighty years.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Judges 3:31">31</a> After him, Shamgar son of Anath killed 600 Philistine men with an ox-goad and he also saved Israel.</p>
