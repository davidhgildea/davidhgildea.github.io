<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Judges 8:1">1</a> The men from Ephraim asked him, &quot;Why did you treat us like this, not calling us when you went to fight the Midianites?&quot; And they argued with him strongly.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Judges 8:2">2</a> He asked them, &quot;What have I done compared to you? Aren&#39;t even the leftovers of Ephraim&#39;s grapes better than Abiezer&#39;s full harvest?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Judges 8:3">3</a> God has given you control over Midian&#39;s leaders, Oreb and Zeeb. What could I have done compared to you? Then they were no longer angry with him after he said this.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Judges 8:4">4</a> Gideon reached the Jordan river, crossed it, and with his three hundred men who were tired but still chasing the enemy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Judges 8:5">5</a> He asked the people of Succoth, &quot;Please give some bread to my followers; they&#39;re tired, and I&#39;m chasing Zebah and Zalmunna, the Midianite leaders.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Judges 8:6">6</a> The leaders of Succoth said, &quot;Do you have Zebah and Zalmunna captured already, that we should give bread to your soldiers?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Judges 8:7">7</a> Gideon said, &quot;So when God gives me Zebah and Zalmunna, I&#39;ll hurt your skin with wild thorns and prickly plants.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Judges 8:8">8</a> He went from there to Penuel and spoke to them in the same way; and the people of Penuel replied just as the people of Succoth had.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Judges 8:9">9</a> He also told the men of Penuel, &quot;When I come back safely, I will tear down this tower.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Judges 8:10">10</a> Now Zebah and Zalmunna were in Karkor with their armies, about fifteen thousand men, the only survivors from the eastern people&#39;s armies, because one hundred and twenty thousand swordsmen had died.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Judges 8:11">11</a> Gideon traveled along the path near the tent-dwellers east of Nobah and Jogbehah and attacked the army, because the army felt safe.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Judges 8:12">12</a> When Zebah and Zalmunna ran away, he chased them and captured the two Midianite kings, Zebah and Zalmunna, and defeated their whole army.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Judges 8:13">13</a> Gideon, Joash&#39;s son, came back from the fight before sunrise.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Judges 8:14">14</a> They caught a young man from Succoth, asked him questions, and he told them about Succoth&#39;s leaders and elders, all seventy-seven men.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Judges 8:15">15</a> He went to the people of Succoth and said, &quot;Here are Zebah and Zalmunna. You mocked me before, asking, &#39;Do you have Zebah and Zalmunna captured so we should give your tired men bread?&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Judges 8:16">16</a> He gathered the city elders, wild thorns, and briers, and with these, he punished the people of Succoth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Judges 8:17">17</a> He destroyed the tower of Penuel and killed the city&#39;s men.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Judges 8:18">18</a> He asked Zebah and Zalmunna, &quot;What kind of men did you kill at Tabor?&quot; They replied, &quot;They looked like you, every one of them seemed like a prince&#39;s son.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Judges 8:19">19</a> He said, &quot;They were my brothers, my own mother&#39;s sons. If you had let them live, I wouldn&#39;t kill you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Judges 8:20">20</a> He told his oldest son Jether, &quot;Get up and kill them.&quot; But the boy did not pull out his sword because he was scared, since he was still young.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Judges 8:21">21</a> Then Zebah and Zalmunna said, &quot;Get up and attack us, for a man&#39;s power matches his own ability.&quot; And Gideon got up, killed Zebah and Zalmunna, and removed the decorations from their camels&#39; necks.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Judges 8:22">22</a> The men of Israel said to Gideon, &quot;Be our leader, you, your son, and your grandson, because you saved us from Midian.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Judges 8:23">23</a> Gideon said to them, &quot;I will not be your ruler, nor will my son be your ruler: God will be your ruler.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Judges 8:24">24</a> Gideon said to them, &quot;I have a favor to ask: please give me the gold earrings you took from your captives.&quot; (They had gold earrings because they were Ishmaelites.)</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Judges 8:25">25</a> They replied, &quot;We will gladly give them.&quot; They laid out a cloth and each man threw in the earrings he had taken.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Judges 8:26">26</a> The gold earrings he asked for weighed 1,700 shekels, not counting the fancy clothes, necklaces, purple garments worn by Midianite kings, or the chains on their camels&#39; necks.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Judges 8:27">27</a> Gideon made a special vest from them and placed it in his city, Ophrah, and all Israel chased after it, which trapped Gideon and his family.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Judges 8:28">28</a> Midian was defeated by the Israelites, so they were no longer a threat. And there was peace in the land for forty years during Gideon&#39;s time.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Judges 8:29">29</a> Jerubbaal, Joash&#39;s son, went and lived in his own house.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Judges 8:30">30</a> Gideon had seventy sons of his own, because he had many wives.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Judges 8:31">31</a> His companion in Shechem also had a son for him, and he named him Abimelech.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Judges 8:32">32</a> Gideon, son of Joash, died at an old age and was buried in his father Joash&#39;s tomb, in Ophrah of the Abiezrites.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Judges 8:33">33</a> After Gideon died, the Israelites turned back and followed other gods, choosing Baalberith as their god.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Judges 8:34">34</a> The Israelites forgot God, who had saved them from all their enemies around them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Judges 8:35">35</a> They did not show kindness to Jerubbaal&#39;s family, Gideon, despite all the good he had done for Israel.</p>
