<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Judges 10:1">1</a> After Abimelech, Tola son of Puah, Dodo&#39;s son from the tribe of Issachar, became a leader to protect Israel. He lived in Shamir in the Ephraim hills.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Judges 10:2">2</a> He ruled Israel for twenty-three years, then he died and was buried in Shamir.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Judges 10:3">3</a> After him, Jair from Gilead became a leader and judged Israel for twenty-two years.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Judges 10:4">4</a> He had thirty sons who rode on thirty young donkeys, and they owned thirty cities called Havothjair, which exist to this day in Gilead.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Judges 10:5">5</a> Jair died and was buried in Camon.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Judges 10:6">6</a> The Israelites did bad things again before God, worshipped Baal and Ashtaroth, and the gods of Syria, Zidon, Moab, Ammon&#39;s children, and the Philistines, and left God and did not worship Him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Judges 10:7">7</a> God became very angry with Israel and handed them over to the Philistines and to the Ammonites.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Judges 10:8">8</a> That year they troubled and mistreated the Israelites for eighteen years. This happened to all the Israelites living across the Jordan River in the land of the Amorites in Gilead.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Judges 10:9">9</a> The Ammonites crossed the Jordan to also fight against Judah, Benjamin, and the Ephraimites, causing great trouble for Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Judges 10:10">10</a> The people of Israel called out to God, saying, &quot;We have sinned against you, for we have left our God and also worshiped Baalim.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Judges 10:11">11</a> God asked the Israelites, &quot;Didn&#39;t I save you from the Egyptians, the Amorites, the Ammonites, and the Philistines?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Judges 10:12">12</a> The people of Sidon, the Amalekites, and the Maonites also troubled you. When you called out to me, I saved you from them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Judges 10:13">13</a> You have left me and served other gods, so I will not save you anymore.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Judges 10:14">14</a> Go and call out to the gods you picked; let them save you when you are in trouble.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Judges 10:15">15</a> The children of Israel told God, &quot;We have sinned. Do to us whatever you think is best, but please, save us today.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Judges 10:16">16</a> They removed the foreign gods from among them and worshiped God; and he felt sorry for Israel&#39;s suffering.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Judges 10:17">17</a> The people of Ammon came together and set up camp in Gilead. The people of Israel also gathered and set up camp in Mizpeh.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Judges 10:18">18</a> The people and leaders of Gilead asked each other, &quot;Who will start the fight against the Ammonites? He will become the leader of everyone in Gilead.&quot;</p>
