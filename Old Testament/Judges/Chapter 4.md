<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Judges 4:1">1</a> The Israelites once more did bad things that God saw, after Ehud died.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Judges 4:2">2</a> God gave them over to Jabin, the king of Canaan, who ruled in Hazor. Sisera, the commander of his army, lived in Harosheth of the nations.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Judges 4:3">3</a> The Israelites cried out to God because he had 900 iron chariots and for twenty years he greatly oppressed the Israelites.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Judges 4:4">4</a> Deborah, a prophetess and Lapidoth&#39;s wife, led Israel during that time.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Judges 4:5">5</a> She lived under Deborah&#39;s palm tree between Ramah and Bethel in the hills of Ephraim, and the Israelites came to her for decisions.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Judges 4:6">6</a> She sent for Barak, son of Abinoam, from Kedesh in Naphtali and told him, &quot;Hasn&#39;t God of Israel ordered you to go to Mount Tabor and take ten thousand men from Naphtali and Zebulun with you?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Judges 4:7">7</a> I will bring Sisera, the leader of Jabin&#39;s army, to you at the Kishon River, with his chariots and troops; and I will give him into your control.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Judges 4:8">8</a> Barak said to her, &quot;If you go with me, I will go; but if you don&#39;t go with me, I won&#39;t go.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Judges 4:9">9</a> She said, &quot;I will definitely go with you. However, this trip you are making won&#39;t bring you glory, because God will let a woman defeat Sisera.&quot; Then Deborah got up and went with Barak to Kedesh.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Judges 4:10">10</a> Barak summoned Zebulun and Naphtali to Kedesh, and he led ten thousand troops. Deborah also went with him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Judges 4:11">11</a> Heber the Kenite, from the family of Hobab, Moses&#39; father-in-law, had moved away from the Kenites and set up his tent near Kedesh in the Zaanaim area.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Judges 4:12">12</a> They told Sisera that Barak, son of Abinoam, had gone up to Mount Tabor.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Judges 4:13">13</a> Sisera brought together all his chariots, nine hundred iron chariots, and all his men, from Harosheth of the Nations to the Kishon River.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Judges 4:14">14</a> Deborah told Barak, &quot;Get up; today is the day God has given Sisera to you. Hasn&#39;t God gone ahead of you?&quot; So Barak went down from Mount Tabor with ten thousand men following him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Judges 4:15">15</a> God confused Sisera and all his chariots and soldiers with the edge of the sword in front of Barak; Sisera got down from his chariot and ran away on foot.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Judges 4:16">16</a> But Barak chased the chariots and army to Harosheth of the Nations, and all Sisera&#39;s army was killed by the sword; no one survived.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Judges 4:17">17</a> However, Sisera ran to Jael&#39;s tent, the wife of Heber the Kenite, because there was peace between King Jabin of Hazor and Heber&#39;s family.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Judges 4:18">18</a> Jael went to meet Sisera and told him, &quot;Come in, sir, come in to my place; don&#39;t be afraid.&quot; When he went in to her tent, she covered him with a blanket.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Judges 4:19">19</a> He said to her, &quot;Please give me some water to drink; I&#39;m thirsty.&quot; She opened a container of milk, gave him a drink, and covered him up.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Judges 4:20">20</a> He told her again, &quot;Stand at the tent door. If a man comes and asks you, &#39;Is there a man here?&#39; you must say &#39;No.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Judges 4:21">21</a> Jael, Heber&#39;s wife, got a tent peg and a hammer, quietly approached him, drove the peg into his head, and pinned it to the ground because he was sound asleep and tired. And so, he died.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Judges 4:22">22</a> Look, as Barak chased Sisera, Jael went out to meet him and said, &quot;Come, I will show you the man you are looking for.&quot; And when he went into her tent, there lay Sisera dead with a nail in his head.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Judges 4:23">23</a> God defeated King Jabin of Canaan that day for the Israelites.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Judges 4:24">24</a> The Israelites succeeded and overcame King Jabin of Canaan until they had defeated him completely.</p>
