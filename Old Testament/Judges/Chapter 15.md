<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Judges 15:1">1</a> After some time, during wheat harvest, Samson brought a young goat to his wife and said, &quot;I will go to my wife&#39;s room.&quot; But her father wouldn&#39;t let him enter.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Judges 15:2">2</a> Her father said, &quot;I really thought you hated her, so I gave her to your friend. Isn&#39;t her younger sister more beautiful? Please take her instead.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Judges 15:3">3</a> Samson said about them, &quot;Now I will be less at fault than the Philistines, even though I upset them.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Judges 15:4">4</a> Samson caught three hundred foxes, tied their tails together in pairs, and put a torch between each pair of tails.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Judges 15:5">5</a> After he lit the torches, he released them into the Philistines&#39; growing grain, and they burned up the stacks and the growing grain, along with the vineyards and olive trees.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Judges 15:6">6</a> Then the Philistines asked, &quot;Who did this?&quot; They were told, &quot;Samson, the Timnite&#39;s son-in-law, because he took his wife and gave her to his friend.&quot; So the Philistines went and burned her and her father to death.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Judges 15:7">7</a> Samson told them, &quot;Even though you did this, I will get revenge on you, and then I&#39;ll stop.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Judges 15:8">8</a> He struck them hard, causing many to die, then went and lived on top of Etam rock.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Judges 15:9">9</a> Then the Philistines moved in, camped in Judah, and spread out in Lehi.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Judges 15:10">10</a> The men of Judah asked, &quot;Why have you come to fight us?&quot; They replied, &quot;We&#39;ve come to capture Samson and to do to him what he did to us.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Judges 15:11">11</a> Three thousand men from Judah went up to the top of the rock Etam and said to Samson, &quot;Don&#39;t you know that the Philistines rule over us? What have you done to us?&quot; He replied, &quot;I did to them what they did to me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Judges 15:12">12</a> They told him, &quot;We have come to tie you up and hand you over to the Philistines.&quot; Samson said to them, &quot;Promise me that you won&#39;t attack me yourselves.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Judges 15:13">13</a> They said to him, &quot;No, we won&#39;t kill you. But we will tie you up tightly and give you to them.&quot; Then they tied him with two new ropes and led him up from the rock.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Judges 15:14">14</a> When he arrived at Lehi, the Philistines yelled at him; but God&#39;s Spirit rushed into him strongly, and the ropes on his arms turned as weak as burnt flax, and the bindings fell off his hands.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Judges 15:15">15</a> He found a fresh donkey jawbone, reached out, took it, and killed a thousand men with it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Judges 15:16">16</a> Samson said, &quot;I used a donkey&#39;s jawbone to kill a thousand men.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Judges 15:17">17</a> After he finished speaking, he threw the jawbone from his hand and named that place Ramathlehi.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Judges 15:18">18</a> He was very thirsty, and prayed to God, saying, &quot;You have given your servant this great victory; must I now die of thirst and fall into the hands of those who are not circumcised?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Judges 15:19">19</a> God split open a space in the jawbone, and water flowed out. After he drank, his strength returned, and he felt better. So he named that place Enhakkore, which is still in Lehi today.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Judges 15:20">20</a> He was Israel&#39;s leader for twenty years during the time of the Philistines.</p>
