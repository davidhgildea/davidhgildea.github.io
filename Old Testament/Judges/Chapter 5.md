<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Judges 5:1">1</a> Deborah and Barak, Abinoam&#39;s son, sang on that day, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Judges 5:2">2</a> Praise God for rescuing Israel when the people freely gave themselves to help.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Judges 5:3">3</a> Listen, kings; pay attention, princes; I will sing to God; I will praise the God of Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Judges 5:4">4</a> God, when you came from Seir, when you marched from Edom&#39;s land, the earth shook, and the sky poured rain, the clouds also poured water.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Judges 5:5">5</a> The mountains melted before God, including Sinai before the God of Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Judges 5:6">6</a> During the time of Shamgar, Anath&#39;s son, and when Jael lived, the main roads were empty, and travelers took side paths.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Judges 5:7">7</a> People in the villages stopped, they stopped in Israel, until I, Deborah, stood up, until I stood up as a mother in Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Judges 5:8">8</a> They picked new gods; then war broke out in the cities: did anyone see a shield or spear among forty thousand in Israel?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Judges 5:9">9</a> I care deeply for the leaders of Israel who volunteered among the people. Praise God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Judges 5:10">10</a> Talk, you who ride white donkeys, you who make decisions, and you who travel on the roads.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Judges 5:11">11</a> Those saved from the archers&#39; noise by the water wells will talk about God&#39;s good deeds, including his kindness to his people in Israel&#39;s villages. Then God&#39;s people will go to the city gates.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Judges 5:12">12</a> Wake up, wake up, Deborah: wake up, wake up, sing a song: get up, Barak, and take your prisoners, you son of Abinoam.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Judges 5:13">13</a> Then he let those who were left rule over the leaders of the people: God made me rule over the strong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Judges 5:14">14</a> From Ephraim came warriors against Amalek; following you, Benjamin, with your people; from Machir came leaders, and from Zebulun, those skilled with the writer&#39;s pen.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Judges 5:15">15</a> The leaders of Issachar supported Deborah; Issachar and Barak too: Barak went down to the valley on foot. The people of Reuben seriously considered their choices.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Judges 5:16">16</a> Why did you stay among the sheep pens, listening to the flocks&#39; cries? The people of Reuben deeply questioned themselves.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Judges 5:17">17</a> Gilead lived across the Jordan River, and why did Dan stay with ships? Asher stayed by the sea coast and lived in his harbors.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Judges 5:18">18</a> Zebulun and Naphtali were people who risked their lives to the point of death on the high parts of the field.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Judges 5:19">19</a> Kings came and fought, then Canaan&#39;s kings fought near Megiddo&#39;s waters; they got no money.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Judges 5:20">20</a> They battled from the sky; the stars in their paths battled against Sisera.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Judges 5:21">21</a> The Kishon River carried them off, that old river, the Kishon River. Oh, my soul, you have walked on power.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Judges 5:22">22</a> Then the horses&#39; hooves were broken from the galloping, the galloping of the strong ones.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Judges 5:23">23</a> God&#39;s messenger said, &quot;Curse Meroz; curse its people harshly because they did not help God fight against the strong enemies.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Judges 5:24">24</a> Jael, the wife of Heber the Kenite, will be more blessed than other women, and in the tent, she will be the most blessed among women.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Judges 5:25">25</a> He asked for water, and she gave him milk; she served cream in a fine bowl.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Judges 5:26">26</a> She held the nail and took the worker&#39;s hammer in her right hand. With the hammer she hit Sisera, knocked off his head after she had stabbed and gone through his temples.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Judges 5:27">27</a> He knelt at her feet, fell, and lay there. Where he knelt, he fell dead.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Judges 5:28">28</a> Sisera&#39;s mother looked out the window and cried through the lattice, &quot;Why is his chariot taking so long to come? Why are the wheels of his chariot delayed?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Judges 5:29">29</a> Her smart women replied to her, yes, she even answered herself.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Judges 5:30">30</a> Did they not hurry? Did they not share the loot, with each man getting a girl or two, and Sisera getting colorful embroidered garments, colorful and richly worked cloths, suitable to wrap around the victors&#39; necks?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Judges 5:31">31</a> May all your enemies die, God, but may those who love him shine like the sun at its strongest. And the land was at peace for forty years.</p>
