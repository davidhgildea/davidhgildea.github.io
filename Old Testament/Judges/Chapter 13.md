<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Judges 13:1">1</a> The Israelites did bad things again before God, and God let the Philistines rule over them for forty years.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Judges 13:2">2</a> A man named Manoah from Zorah, part of the Danite family, had a wife who could not have children.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Judges 13:3">3</a> God&#39;s messenger appeared to the woman and said to her, &quot;Look, you have no children, but you will become pregnant and have a son.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Judges 13:4">4</a> Be careful, please, do not drink wine or strong alcohol, and do not eat anything unclean.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Judges 13:5">5</a> Look, you will become pregnant and have a son; his hair must never be cut because he will be dedicated to God as a Nazarite from birth, and he will start to rescue Israel from the Philistines.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Judges 13:6">6</a> The woman went and said to her husband, &quot;A man of God came to me with a face like an angel of God, very awe-inspiring. I didn&#39;t ask where he was from, and he didn&#39;t tell me his name.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Judges 13:7">7</a> He told me, &quot;Look, you will get pregnant and have a son. So do not drink wine or strong alcohol, and do not eat anything impure, because the boy will be dedicated to God as a Nazarite from birth until he dies.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Judges 13:8">8</a> Then Manoah prayed to God, saying, &quot;Please God, let the man of God you sent us come again and teach us what we should do for the child who will be born.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Judges 13:9">9</a> God listened to Manoah&#39;s prayer, and God&#39;s angel returned to the woman while she was sitting in the field, but her husband Manoah was not with her.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Judges 13:10">10</a> The woman quickly ran to her husband, showed him and said, &quot;Look, the man who visited me the other day has appeared to me again.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Judges 13:11">11</a> Manoah got up, followed his wife, and met the man. He asked, &quot;Are you the man who spoke to my wife?&quot; And the man replied, &quot;I am.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Judges 13:12">12</a> Manoah said, &quot;Let your words happen. How should we raise the child and what should we do for him?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Judges 13:13">13</a> The angel told Manoah, &quot;Make sure the woman follows all that I told her.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Judges 13:14">14</a> She must not eat anything from the grapevine, drink wine or strong drinks, or eat anything impure: she should follow all that I instructed her to do.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Judges 13:15">15</a> Manoah said to God&#39;s angel, &quot;Please stay with us until we prepare a young goat for you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Judges 13:16">16</a> The angel said to Manoah, &quot;Even if you keep me here, I won&#39;t eat your bread. If you want to give a burnt offering, you must give it to God.&quot; Manoah didn&#39;t know he was an angel of God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Judges 13:17">17</a> Manoah asked the angel of God, &quot;What is your name, so we can honor you when your words come true?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Judges 13:18">18</a> The angel of God asked him, &quot;Why do you want to know my name, when it is a secret?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Judges 13:19">19</a> Manoah took a young goat with a food offering and sacrificed it on a rock to God; the angel did amazing things, and Manoah and his wife watched.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Judges 13:20">20</a> When the fire rose up to the sky from the altar, God&#39;s angel went up in the fire of the altar. Manoah and his wife saw this and fell with their faces to the ground.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Judges 13:21">21</a> The angel of God did not show up again to Manoah and his wife. Then Manoah realized that he was an angel of God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Judges 13:22">22</a> Manoah said to his wife, &quot;We will surely die because we have seen God.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Judges 13:23">23</a> But his wife said to him, &quot;If God wanted to kill us, he wouldn&#39;t have accepted our burnt and grain offerings, nor would he have shown us all this or told us such things now.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Judges 13:24">24</a> The woman had a son and named him Samson. The boy grew up and God blessed him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Judges 13:25">25</a> God&#39;s Spirit started to stir him now and then in Dan&#39;s camp, between Zorah and Eshtaol.</p>
