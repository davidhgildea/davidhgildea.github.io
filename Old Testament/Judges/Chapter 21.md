<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Judges 21:1">1</a> The men of Israel promised in Mizpeh, &quot;None of us will let our daughters marry men from Benjamin.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Judges 21:2">2</a> The people went to God&#39;s house, stayed there until evening, raised their voices, and cried a lot.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Judges 21:3">3</a> And said, O God of Israel, why has this happened in Israel, that today one tribe is missing in Israel?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Judges 21:4">4</a> The next day, the people got up early, built an altar there, and made burnt offerings and peace offerings.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Judges 21:5">5</a> The Israelites asked, &quot;Who from all Israel&#39;s tribes didn&#39;t join the group to meet with God? They had strongly promised to kill anyone who didn&#39;t come to Mizpeh to meet with God.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Judges 21:6">6</a> The people of Israel felt sorry for their brother Benjamin and said, &quot;Today one tribe is missing from Israel.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Judges 21:7">7</a> What can we do to find wives for those who are left, since we have promised with a strong oath that we will not let them marry our daughters?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Judges 21:8">8</a> They asked, &quot;Who from the tribes of Israel didn&#39;t come to Mizpeh to God?&quot; And no one from Jabeshgilead came to the camp for the meeting.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Judges 21:9">9</a> The people were counted, and look, no one from Jabesh-gilead was there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Judges 21:10">10</a> The group sent twelve thousand brave men and told them, &quot;Go and defeat the people of Jabeshgilead with the sword, including the women and children.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Judges 21:11">11</a> This is what you must do: You must kill every man and every woman who has slept with a man.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Judges 21:12">12</a> They found 400 young women in Jabesh Gilead who had never slept with a man, and they brought them to the camp at Shiloh in Canaan.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Judges 21:13">13</a> The whole group sent messengers to talk to the Benjamite people at the rock Rimmon, inviting them peacefully.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Judges 21:14">14</a> Benjamin returned then, and they were given wives from the saved women of Jabeshgilead, but there were not enough women for them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Judges 21:15">15</a> The people felt sorry for Benjamin because God had caused a split among the tribes of Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Judges 21:16">16</a> Then the community leaders asked, &quot;What will we do to find wives for the remaining men when the women of Benjamin have been killed?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Judges 21:17">17</a> They said, &quot;There must be a share for the survivors of Benjamin, so that a tribe is not wiped out from Israel.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Judges 21:18">18</a> But we can&#39;t give them our daughters as wives because the Israelites have made a promise, saying, &quot;May the man who gives a wife to Benjamin be cursed.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Judges 21:19">19</a> They said, &quot;Look, every year there&#39;s a festival for God in Shiloh, north of Bethel, east of the road leading from Bethel to Shechem, and south of Lebonah.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Judges 21:20">20</a> They told the people of Benjamin, &quot;Go and hide in the vineyards.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Judges 21:21">21</a> Look, and if the girls from Shiloh come out to dance, then come out of the vineyards, and each of you catch a wife from the girls of Shiloh, and go to the land of Benjamin.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Judges 21:22">22</a> When their fathers or brothers come to complain to us, we will ask them to forgive them for our sake: we didn&#39;t save a wife for each man in the war because you didn&#39;t give them their wives now, so you aren&#39;t to blame.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Judges 21:23">23</a> The men of Benjamin did this, and each took a wife from the dancing women they captured. They went back to their land, fixed their cities, and lived there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Judges 21:24">24</a> The Israelites left from there at that time, each person to their own tribe and family, and each went to their own land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Judges 21:25">25</a> At that time, there was no king in Israel; everyone just did what they thought was right.</p>
