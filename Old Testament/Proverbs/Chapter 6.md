<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Proverbs 6:1">1</a> My son, if you promise to pay for your friend, if you have shaken hands with a stranger,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Proverbs 6:2">2</a> You are trapped by what you said, caught by your own words.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Proverbs 6:3">3</a> Do this now, my child, and save yourself, when you are in your friend&#39;s power; go, lower yourself, and make peace with your friend.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Proverbs 6:4">4</a> Do not let your eyes fall asleep, or your eyelids get drowsy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Proverbs 6:5">5</a> Escape like a deer from the hunter, and like a bird from the person who catches birds.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Proverbs 6:6">6</a> Look at the ant, you lazy person; watch how it does things and learn.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Proverbs 6:7">7</a> Without a leader, watcher, or ruler,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Proverbs 6:8">8</a> She gets her food in the summer and collects her food at harvest time.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Proverbs 6:9">9</a> How long will you sleep, you lazy person? When will you get up from your sleep?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Proverbs 6:10">10</a> Just a bit more sleep, a bit more rest, a bit more folding of the hands to rest.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Proverbs 6:11">11</a> Your poverty will come like a traveler, and your need like an armed person.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Proverbs 6:12">12</a> A bad person, an evil man, speaks with a twisted mouth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Proverbs 6:13">13</a> He signals with his eyes, he communicates with his feet, he instructs with his fingers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Proverbs 6:14">14</a> He has evil plans in his heart, he always thinks of harm; he spreads conflict.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Proverbs 6:15">15</a> So his trouble will come quickly; he will be quickly destroyed with no cure.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Proverbs 6:16">16</a> God hates these six things; indeed, seven are detestable to Him:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Proverbs 6:17">17</a> An arrogant stare, a deceitful speech, and hands that harm the innocent,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Proverbs 6:18">18</a> A heart that plans evil ideas, feet that quickly run to trouble,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Proverbs 6:19">19</a> A person who lies as a witness and someone who causes arguments among brothers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Proverbs 6:20">20</a> My child, follow your father&#39;s instruction and do not ignore your mother&#39;s teaching.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Proverbs 6:21">21</a> Keep them always in your heart, and wrap them around your neck.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Proverbs 6:22">22</a> When you go, it will guide you; when you sleep, it will watch over you; and when you wake up, it will speak to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Proverbs 6:23">23</a> The commandment is a lamp, the law is light, and corrections that teach are the path to life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Proverbs 6:24">24</a> To protect you from a bad woman, from the smooth talk of a stranger.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Proverbs 6:25">25</a> Do not desire her beauty in your heart; do not let her capture you with her eyes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Proverbs 6:26">26</a> A loose woman can reduce a man to poverty, and a cheating wife can endanger a valuable life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Proverbs 6:27">27</a> Can someone hold fire close and not burn their clothes?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Proverbs 6:28">28</a> Can someone walk on hot coals without burning their feet?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Proverbs 6:29">29</a> Anyone who sleeps with his neighbor&#39;s wife or touches her will not be innocent.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Proverbs 6:30">30</a> People do not hate a thief if he steals to feed himself when he is hungry.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Proverbs 6:31">31</a> If he is caught, he must repay seven times what he took; he must give everything he owns.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Proverbs 6:32">32</a> Anyone who cheats with a woman is unwise; doing this harms his own life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Proverbs 6:33">33</a> He will get hurt and disgrace, and his shame will never go away.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Proverbs 6:34">34</a> Jealousy makes a man very angry, so he won&#39;t show mercy when he takes revenge.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Proverbs 6:35">35</a> He will not accept a payment; nor will he be satisfied, even if you give many gifts.</p>
