<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Proverbs 17:1">1</a> It is better to have a little with peace than a house full of food with fighting.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Proverbs 17:2">2</a> A smart servant will be in charge instead of a son who brings shame, and will share in the family inheritance.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Proverbs 17:3">3</a> A melting pot is for silver, and a furnace is for gold, but God tests the heart.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Proverbs 17:4">4</a> A bad person listens to lies, and a liar pays attention to hurtful words.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Proverbs 17:5">5</a> Anyone who mocks the poor insults God, and those who are happy when others suffer will be punished.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Proverbs 17:6">6</a> Grandchildren are a joy to the elderly; and children take pride in their parents.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Proverbs 17:7">7</a> Good words are not fitting for a fool; even less are lies suitable for a leader.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Proverbs 17:8">8</a> A gift is like a valuable gem to the person who has it: wherever it goes, it succeeds.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Proverbs 17:9">9</a> Whoever forgives an offense shows love; but one who keeps talking about the issue pushes friends apart.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Proverbs 17:10">10</a> A correction is understood better by a wise person than a hundred beatings are by a fool.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Proverbs 17:11">11</a> A bad person looks for trouble, so a harsh messenger will be sent to him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Proverbs 17:12">12</a> It&#39;s better to meet a mother bear who has lost her cubs than a fool doing something stupid.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Proverbs 17:13">13</a> Anyone who pays back good with evil will always have evil in their home.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Proverbs 17:14">14</a> Strife starts like a water leak, so stop arguing before it gets worse.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Proverbs 17:15">15</a> Anyone who says the bad person is right, and anyone who says the good person is wrong, both are hated by God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Proverbs 17:16">16</a> Why does a fool have money to buy wisdom when he has no desire for it?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Proverbs 17:17">17</a> A friend always loves, and a brother is there for tough times.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Proverbs 17:18">18</a> A person without good sense shakes hands in agreement, promising to pay for his friend.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Proverbs 17:19">19</a> Someone who loves fighting loves to sin; and one who makes their entrance grand is looking for ruin.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Proverbs 17:20">20</a> Someone with a crooked heart finds no good, and a person with a twisted tongue gets into trouble.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Proverbs 17:21">21</a> Whoever has a foolish child brings sorrow to themselves; a fool&#39;s parent has no happiness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Proverbs 17:22">22</a> A happy heart is good for you like medicine, but a sad spirit dries up the bones.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Proverbs 17:23">23</a> A bad person takes a bribe secretly to twist fair decisions.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Proverbs 17:24">24</a> The wise person keeps wisdom in view, but a fool&#39;s gaze wanders to the ends of the earth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Proverbs 17:25">25</a> A silly son makes his father sad and his mother bitter.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Proverbs 17:26">26</a> It is wrong to punish the innocent, and it is not good to hit leaders for doing right.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Proverbs 17:27">27</a> Someone with knowledge uses few words: a person with understanding has a calm spirit.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Proverbs 17:28">28</a> Even a fool seems wise if they stay silent, and a person who keeps their mouth shut is thought to be smart.</p>
