<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Proverbs 1:1">1</a> These are wise sayings from Solomon, David&#39;s son, who was the king of Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Proverbs 1:2">2</a> To understand wisdom and teaching; to grasp the meaning of insightful words;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Proverbs 1:3">3</a> To learn wisdom, fairness, right decisions, and equality;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Proverbs 1:4">4</a> To give cleverness to the simple, to the young person knowledge and good judgment.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Proverbs 1:5">5</a> A wise person listens and learns more; someone who understands will find good advice.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Proverbs 1:6">6</a> To grasp a wise saying and its meaning, the words of the wise, and their riddles.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Proverbs 1:7">7</a> Respecting God is the start of understanding, but fools hate learning and guidance.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Proverbs 1:8">8</a> My child, listen to your father&#39;s teaching and do not ignore your mother&#39;s rules.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Proverbs 1:9">9</a> They will be like a beautiful crown on your head and a necklace around your neck.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Proverbs 1:10">10</a> My son, if sinners tempt you, do not agree.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Proverbs 1:11">11</a> If they say, &quot;Come with us, let&#39;s hide and wait to hurt someone innocent for no reason,&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Proverbs 1:12">12</a> Let&#39;s consume them fully alive as the grave does; completely, like those who descend into the pit.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Proverbs 1:13">13</a> We will find valuable treasures; we will fill our homes with loot.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Proverbs 1:14">14</a> Join us and share our money.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Proverbs 1:15">15</a> My son, do not go along with them; keep your foot from following their path.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Proverbs 1:16">16</a> Their feet rush to do bad things, and they hurry to spill blood.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Proverbs 1:17">17</a> Surely, setting a net where a bird can see it is useless.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Proverbs 1:18">18</a> They set a trap for themselves; they hide and wait to take their own lives.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Proverbs 1:19">19</a> Greedy people&#39;s ways take life from those they harm.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Proverbs 1:20">20</a> Wisdom shouts outside; she raises her voice in the streets:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Proverbs 1:21">21</a> She calls out in the busiest places, at the city gates she speaks her words, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Proverbs 1:22">22</a> How long will you who are naive love being simple-minded? And how long will mockers enjoy mocking and fools hate understanding?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Proverbs 1:23">23</a> Listen to my correction: I will share my spirit with you and teach you my words.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Proverbs 1:24">24</a> I called, but you refused; I reached out my hand, but no one paid attention.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Proverbs 1:25">25</a> You have ignored all my advice and did not want my correction.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Proverbs 1:26">26</a> I will laugh when trouble comes for you; I will make fun when you are afraid.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Proverbs 1:27">27</a> When your fear arrives like ruins, and your destruction sweeps in like a storm; when distress and suffering fall upon you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Proverbs 1:28">28</a> They will call for me, but I won&#39;t respond; they will look for me, but won&#39;t find me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Proverbs 1:29">29</a> They hated knowledge and did not choose to respect God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Proverbs 1:30">30</a> They rejected my advice and ignored all my warnings.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Proverbs 1:31">31</a> So they will eat the results of their actions and be full of their own schemes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Proverbs 1:32">32</a> Rejecting knowledge will kill the naive, and the success of fools will ruin them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Proverbs 1:33">33</a> Whoever listens to me will live safely and be at peace, without fear of harm.</p>
