<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Proverbs 30:1">1</a> Agur, Jakeh&#39;s son, said this prophecy: he told Ithiel, and also Ithiel and Ucal.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Proverbs 30:2">2</a> I am surely less wise than anyone, and I lack a man&#39;s understanding.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Proverbs 30:3">3</a> I have not learned wisdom, nor do I know about the holy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Proverbs 30:4">4</a> Who has gone up to heaven and come down? Who has held the wind in his hands? Who has wrapped up the waters in a cloak? Who has set up all the earth&#39;s edges? What&#39;s his name, and what&#39;s his son&#39;s name, if you know it?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Proverbs 30:5">5</a> Every word from God is pure; he protects those who trust in him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Proverbs 30:6">6</a> Do not add to his words, or God will correct you, and you will be shown to be a liar.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Proverbs 30:7">7</a> I ask you for two things; don&#39;t refuse me before I die.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Proverbs 30:8">8</a> Keep me away from useless things and dishonesty; don&#39;t make me poor or rich; give me just the food I need.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Proverbs 30:9">9</a> So I don&#39;t become too full, forget you, and ask, &quot;Who is God?&quot; or become poor, steal, and disrespect my God&#39;s name.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Proverbs 30:10">10</a> Do not blame someone&#39;s servant to their boss, or they might curse you and you&#39;ll be found at fault.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Proverbs 30:11">11</a> A generation curses their father and doesn&#39;t bless their mother.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Proverbs 30:12">12</a> Some people think they are clean, but they are not free from their dirt.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Proverbs 30:13">13</a> A group of people exist who are very proud; they look at others with arrogance.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Proverbs 30:14">14</a> Some people are like sword teeth and knife jaws, they eat up the poor and needy from the world and among people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Proverbs 30:15">15</a> The leech has two daughters that cry, &quot;Give, give.&quot; There are three things never full, even four that never say, &quot;Enough&quot;:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Proverbs 30:16">16</a> The grave, the empty womb, the dry earth, and the fire that never says &#39;Enough.&#39;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Proverbs 30:17">17</a> The eye that makes fun of its father, and hates to listen to its mother, the ravens of the valley will pluck it out, and young eagles will eat it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Proverbs 30:18">18</a> There are three things that are amazing to me, yes, four things I don&#39;t understand:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Proverbs 30:19">19</a> How an eagle flies in the sky; how a snake moves on a rock; how a ship sails in the sea; and how a man acts with a woman.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Proverbs 30:20">20</a> This is how an unfaithful woman acts: she eats, cleans her mouth, and says, I&#39;ve done nothing wrong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Proverbs 30:21">21</a> The earth is troubled by three things, and there are four it can&#39;t stand:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Proverbs 30:22">22</a> When a servant becomes a leader and a fool gets plenty to eat.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Proverbs 30:23">23</a> A disliked woman when she gets married; and a servant girl who inherits from her lady.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Proverbs 30:24">24</a> There are four small things on earth, but they are very wise:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Proverbs 30:25">25</a> Ants are not strong, yet they gather their food in the summer.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Proverbs 30:26">26</a> The rock badgers are weak creatures, yet they make their homes in the rocks.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Proverbs 30:27">27</a> The locusts don&#39;t have a king, but they all go out in groups.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Proverbs 30:28">28</a> The spider uses its hands to make a web and lives in king&#39;s homes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Proverbs 30:29">29</a> Three things move gracefully, indeed, four look good as they go:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Proverbs 30:30">30</a> A lion, the strongest among animals, doesn&#39;t turn away from anything.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Proverbs 30:31">31</a> A greyhound; a male goat also; and a king, who cannot be opposed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Proverbs 30:32">32</a> If you acted foolishly by being proud, or if you planned something bad, put your hand over your mouth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Proverbs 30:33">33</a> When you stir milk, you make butter, and when you squeeze a nose, you get blood; so when you force anger, you cause fighting.</p>
