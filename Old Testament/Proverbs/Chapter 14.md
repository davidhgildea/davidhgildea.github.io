<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Proverbs 14:1">1</a> A smart woman builds up her home, but a foolish one tears it down by herself.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Proverbs 14:2">2</a> Someone who lives rightly respects God, but a person with corrupt ways despises Him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Proverbs 14:3">3</a> Foolish people speak with pride which brings punishment, but wise people&#39;s words keep them safe.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Proverbs 14:4">4</a> Where there are no oxen, the feeding trough is empty; but a strong ox brings a big harvest.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Proverbs 14:5">5</a> A trustworthy person won&#39;t lie, but a liar will tell lies.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Proverbs 14:6">6</a> Someone who mocks others looks for wisdom but does not find it; yet knowledge comes easily to someone who has understanding.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Proverbs 14:7">7</a> Leave the company of a foolish person when you realize they do not speak wise words.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Proverbs 14:8">8</a> Smart people know their own path; but foolish people trick others.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Proverbs 14:9">9</a> Fools laugh at doing wrong, but God&#39;s favor is with the good people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Proverbs 14:10">10</a> The heart understands its own sadness; and an outsider does not share its happiness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Proverbs 14:11">11</a> The wicked person&#39;s house will be destroyed, but the good person&#39;s home will thrive.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Proverbs 14:12">12</a> A path may seem right to a person, but it ends in death.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Proverbs 14:13">13</a> Even when laughing, the heart can be sad; and joy can finish with sadness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Proverbs 14:14">14</a> Someone who turns away in heart will have to live with their own choices, and a good person will be content with themselves.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Proverbs 14:15">15</a> A naive person believes everything, but a wise one thinks carefully about their steps.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Proverbs 14:16">16</a> A wise person respects God and avoids evil, but a fool gets angry and feels sure of himself.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Proverbs 14:17">17</a> Someone who gets angry quickly acts foolishly, and a person who plans evil is disliked.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Proverbs 14:18">18</a> Simple people gain foolishness, but careful ones are rewarded with knowledge.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Proverbs 14:19">19</a> Bad people bow down to good people; and the wicked stand at the doors of those who are right.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Proverbs 14:20">20</a> Poor people are disliked even by their neighbors, but rich people have many friends.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Proverbs 14:21">21</a> Whoever looks down on their neighbor does wrong, but the one who is kind to the poor is happy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Proverbs 14:22">22</a> Aren&#39;t those who plan evil wrong? But kindness and honesty will be for those who plan good.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Proverbs 14:23">23</a> In every kind of hard work there is benefit, but just talking leads to poverty.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Proverbs 14:24">24</a> Wise people&#39;s wealth is their crown, but foolish people&#39;s foolishness is just that.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Proverbs 14:25">25</a> A truthful person saves lives, but a liar tells lies.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Proverbs 14:26">26</a> Respecting God brings true security; his children will have a safe place.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Proverbs 14:27">27</a> Being afraid to disobey God is a source of life, helping to avoid deadly traps.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Proverbs 14:28">28</a> A king is honored with a lot of people, but a prince will be ruined without them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Proverbs 14:29">29</a> Someone patient in anger has much understanding, but a quick-tempered person shows foolishness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Proverbs 14:30">30</a> A healthy heart is the life of the body, but jealousy is the decay of the bones.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Proverbs 14:31">31</a> Whoever mistreats the poor insults God, but the one who respects God is kind to the poor.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Proverbs 14:32">32</a> The bad person is pushed away because of his badness: but the good person has hope even when he dies.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Proverbs 14:33">33</a> Wisdom stays in the heart of a person with understanding, but a fool&#39;s thoughts become known.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Proverbs 14:34">34</a> Being good lifts up a country, but sin shames any nation.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Proverbs 14:35">35</a> The king likes a smart servant, but he&#39;s angry at one who brings disgrace.</p>
