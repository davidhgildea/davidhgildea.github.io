<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Proverbs 31:1">1</a> The sayings of King Lemuel, the teaching his mother gave him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Proverbs 31:2">2</a> What should I say, my son? What, child of my body? What, child of my promises?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Proverbs 31:3">3</a> Do not give your strength to women, or your paths to what ruins kings.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Proverbs 31:4">4</a> Kings should not drink wine, Lemuel, nor should leaders drink strong alcohol.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Proverbs 31:5">5</a> They might drink, forget the rules, and twist the justice due to those who are suffering.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Proverbs 31:6">6</a> Give hard liquor to someone who is dying, and wine to those who are very sad.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Proverbs 31:7">7</a> Let him drink and forget his lack of money, and not recall his troubles anymore.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Proverbs 31:8">8</a> Speak up for those who cannot speak, in the defense of all who are headed for ruin.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Proverbs 31:9">9</a> Speak up, make fair decisions, and defend the rights of the poor and needy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Proverbs 31:10">10</a> Who can find a good woman? Her value is much higher than jewels.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Proverbs 31:11">11</a> Her husband&#39;s heart trusts her deeply, so he lacks nothing of value.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Proverbs 31:12">12</a> She will treat him well and never badly all her life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Proverbs 31:13">13</a> She looks for wool and flax and works eagerly with her hands.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Proverbs 31:14">14</a> She is like the traders&#39; boats; she gets her food from far away.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Proverbs 31:15">15</a> She gets up while it&#39;s still night, and provides food for her family, and shares with her servant girls.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Proverbs 31:16">16</a> She thinks about a field, buys it, and with her own work, she plants a vineyard.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Proverbs 31:17">17</a> She wraps her waist with power and makes her arms strong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Proverbs 31:18">18</a> She sees that her goods are valuable; her lamp does not go out at night.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Proverbs 31:19">19</a> She puts her hands to the spindle and holds the distaff.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Proverbs 31:20">20</a> She extends her hand to the poor; yes, she offers her hands to those in need.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Proverbs 31:21">21</a> She is not worried about the cold for her family, because they all wear warm red clothes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Proverbs 31:22">22</a> She makes herself patterned blankets; her clothes are silk and purple.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Proverbs 31:23">23</a> Her husband is respected in the community where he meets with the local leaders.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Proverbs 31:24">24</a> She makes good linen cloth and sells it; she also delivers belts to the shopkeeper.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Proverbs 31:25">25</a> She wears strength and respect; she will be glad in the future.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Proverbs 31:26">26</a> She speaks wisely and kindly advice is on her tongue.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Proverbs 31:27">27</a> She takes good care of her family and does not waste time doing nothing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Proverbs 31:28">28</a> Her children stand up and call her blessed; her husband too, and he praises her.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Proverbs 31:29">29</a> Many women have been good, but you are the best of them all.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Proverbs 31:30">30</a> Being liked for charm is misleading, and beauty is shallow: but a woman who respects God will be honored.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Proverbs 31:31">31</a> Give her the rewards of her work, and let her actions praise her publicly.</p>
