<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Proverbs 5:1">1</a> My son, listen to my wisdom, and pay attention to my understanding.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Proverbs 5:2">2</a> So you will value good judgment and your words will hold wisdom.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Proverbs 5:3">3</a> A strange woman&#39;s lips are sweet like honey, and her words are smoother than oil.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Proverbs 5:4">4</a> But her end is as bitter as a bitter plant and sharp like a sword with two edges.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Proverbs 5:5">5</a> Her feet lead to death; her steps reach hell.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Proverbs 5:6">6</a> Be careful thinking about the way of life; its paths are shifting, and you can&#39;t fully understand them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Proverbs 5:7">7</a> Listen to me now, children, and do not turn away from what I say.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Proverbs 5:8">8</a> Stay far away from her, and don&#39;t go near her house door.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Proverbs 5:9">9</a> Don&#39;t let others take your honor or your life be controlled by the cruel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Proverbs 5:10">10</a> Be careful that outsiders do not take your riches, and that your hard work does not end up in a foreigner&#39;s home.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Proverbs 5:11">11</a> You will be sad in the end, when your body is wasted away.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Proverbs 5:12">12</a> I say, &quot;Why did I hate to be corrected, and why did my heart reject discipline?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Proverbs 5:13">13</a> I did not listen to my teachers or pay attention to those who taught me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Proverbs 5:14">14</a> I was close to being completely evil when I was among the people and the group.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Proverbs 5:15">15</a> Drink water from your own tank, and flowing water from your own well.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Proverbs 5:16">16</a> Let your springs flow freely, and streams of water fill the streets.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Proverbs 5:17">17</a> Keep them for yourself alone, and not for others with you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Proverbs 5:18">18</a> May your source of life be blessed; be happy with the wife you married when you were young.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Proverbs 5:19">19</a> Let her be like a caring deer and a delightful doe; let her breasts always satisfy you, and always be captivated by her love.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Proverbs 5:20">20</a> Why would you, my son, be enchanted by a woman you don&#39;t know and hug someone else&#39;s wife?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Proverbs 5:21">21</a> God sees all that people do, and he thinks about everywhere they go.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Proverbs 5:22">22</a> The wicked person will be caught by their own wrongdoings and held fast by the ropes of their sins.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Proverbs 5:23">23</a> He will die for lack of teaching, and because of his great foolishness, he will lose his way.</p>
