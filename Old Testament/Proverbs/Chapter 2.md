<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Proverbs 2:1">1</a> My child, if you accept my words and keep my commands with you;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Proverbs 2:2">2</a> Listen closely to wisdom, and focus your mind on understanding.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Proverbs 2:3">3</a> Yes, if you call out for knowledge and raise your voice for understanding;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Proverbs 2:4">4</a> If you seek wisdom like silver and search for it as for hidden treasures;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Proverbs 2:5">5</a> Then you will understand what it means to respect God and you will discover God&#39;s knowledge.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Proverbs 2:6">6</a> God gives wisdom; from His mouth come knowledge and understanding.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Proverbs 2:7">7</a> He saves true wisdom for the good people: he protects those who act honestly.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Proverbs 2:8">8</a> He protects the paths of fairness and guards the way of his faithful people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Proverbs 2:9">9</a> Then you will understand what is right, fair, and just—every good way.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Proverbs 2:10">10</a> When wisdom comes into your heart, and knowledge is pleasing to your soul;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Proverbs 2:11">11</a> Wisdom will protect you, and understanding will guard you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Proverbs 2:12">12</a> To save you from the path of the bad man, from the one who says twisted things.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Proverbs 2:13">13</a> They leave the right paths to walk in dark ways.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Proverbs 2:14">14</a> They enjoy doing wrong and take pleasure in the twisted ways of the wicked.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Proverbs 2:15">15</a> Their ways are dishonest, and they are stubborn in their paths.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Proverbs 2:16">16</a> To save you from the foreign woman, from the outsider who seduces with her words;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Proverbs 2:17">17</a> She leaves the companion of her youth and forgets the promise of her God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Proverbs 2:18">18</a> Her house leads to death, and her paths go to the dead.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Proverbs 2:19">19</a> Everyone who goes to her does not come back, nor do they find the way to life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Proverbs 2:20">20</a> So you can follow the path of good people and stay on the way of those who do right.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Proverbs 2:21">21</a> Good people will live in the land, and the blameless will stay in it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Proverbs 2:22">22</a> The evil people will be removed from the earth, and those who break the rules will be completely taken out of it.</p>
