<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Proverbs 9:1">1</a> Wisdom built her house; she carved out its seven pillars.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Proverbs 9:2">2</a> She has killed her animals; she has mixed her wine; she has also set her table.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Proverbs 9:3">3</a> She has sent out her servant girls; she calls out from the city&#39;s highest spots.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Proverbs 9:4">4</a> If you are simple, come this way; anyone lacking understanding, she says to him,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Proverbs 9:5">5</a> Come, eat my bread, and drink the wine I have mixed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Proverbs 9:6">6</a> Leave the foolish behind and live; walk on the path of wisdom.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Proverbs 9:7">7</a> The person who corrects a mocker invites insult, and the one who tells a wicked person they are wrong risks being shamed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Proverbs 9:8">8</a> Don&#39;t correct a mocker, or he will dislike you; advise a wise person, and he will appreciate you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Proverbs 9:9">9</a> Guide a smart person, and they will become smarter; show a fair person, and their knowledge will grow.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Proverbs 9:10">10</a> Being in awe of God is the start of wisdom, and knowing what is sacred leads to understanding.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Proverbs 9:11">11</a> With my help, your life will last longer, and you&#39;ll have more years to live.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Proverbs 9:12">12</a> If you are wise, you will benefit yourself: but if you mock, you alone will suffer.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Proverbs 9:13">13</a> A silly woman is loud: she is naive, and knows nothing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Proverbs 9:14">14</a> She sits at her house door, on a chair in the city&#39;s high spots.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Proverbs 9:15">15</a> To invite people who are traveling straight on their paths:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Proverbs 9:16">16</a> If you are simple, come here; if you lack understanding, she says to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Proverbs 9:17">17</a> Taken water tastes good, and hidden bread is nice to eat.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Proverbs 9:18">18</a> He doesn&#39;t know that the dead are there; her guests are in the depths of the grave.</p>
