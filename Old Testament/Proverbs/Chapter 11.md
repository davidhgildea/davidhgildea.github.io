<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Proverbs 11:1">1</a> Using dishonest scales is disgusting to God, but accurate weights make him happy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Proverbs 11:2">2</a> When you are proud, you will be embarrassed, but if you are humble, you will be wise.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Proverbs 11:3">3</a> The honesty of good people will lead them, but the dishonesty of the unfaithful will ruin them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Proverbs 11:4">4</a> Money is no help on the day of anger, but doing right saves from death.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Proverbs 11:5">5</a> The goodness of a flawless person guides their path, but the evil ones will stumble because of their own evil.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Proverbs 11:6">6</a> The goodness of honest people will save them, but wrongdoers will be caught by their own bad actions.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Proverbs 11:7">7</a> When a bad person dies, their expectations end; and the hopes of unfair people disappear.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Proverbs 11:8">8</a> The good person is saved from trouble, and the bad one takes their place.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Proverbs 11:9">9</a> A fake person with their words can hurt others, but the right people will be saved by knowing the truth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Proverbs 11:10">10</a> When things go well for good people, the city is happy; and when bad people die, there are cheers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Proverbs 11:11">11</a> The city flourishes when good people bless it, but it falls apart when evil people talk against it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Proverbs 11:12">12</a> Someone without wisdom looks down on their neighbor, but a person who understands stays calm.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Proverbs 11:13">13</a> Someone who gossips tells secrets, but a trustworthy person keeps them hidden.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Proverbs 11:14">14</a> Without advice, people fail; with many advisers, they are safe.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Proverbs 11:15">15</a> Someone who promises to pay for another will suffer, but whoever refuses to promise will be safe.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Proverbs 11:16">16</a> A kind woman keeps respect, and powerful men keep wealth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Proverbs 11:17">17</a> A kind person benefits their own soul, but a cruel one harms themselves.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Proverbs 11:18">18</a> Bad people do tricky things, but those who do right will get a real reward.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Proverbs 11:19">19</a> Doing right leads to life, but chasing evil leads to death.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Proverbs 11:20">20</a> People with twisted hearts are hated by God, but those who follow a straight path bring him joy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Proverbs 11:21">21</a> Even if the wicked join forces, they will not escape punishment, but the descendants of the righteous will be saved.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Proverbs 11:22">22</a> Like a gold ring in a pig&#39;s nose, so is a beautiful woman who lacks good judgment.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Proverbs 11:23">23</a> Righteous people desire only good things, but the wicked expect anger.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Proverbs 11:24">24</a> Some give freely and gain more; others keep too much and become poor.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Proverbs 11:25">25</a> A generous person will prosper, and whoever refreshes others will be refreshed themselves.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Proverbs 11:26">26</a> Someone who keeps corn for himself will be cursed by people, but the one who sells it will be blessed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Proverbs 11:27">27</a> Whoever looks for good finds kindness, but whoever searches for trouble, it will find them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Proverbs 11:28">28</a> Whoever trusts in their wealth will fail, but the good person will thrive like a tree branch.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Proverbs 11:29">29</a> Whoever causes trouble in their home will gain nothing, and the foolish person will serve the wise-hearted.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Proverbs 11:30">30</a> Righteous people produce life-giving fruit; whoever brings others to the right path is wise.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Proverbs 11:31">31</a> Look, good people will be rewarded on earth; even more, so will the bad and the sinful.</p>
