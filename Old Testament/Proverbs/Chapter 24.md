<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Proverbs 24:1">1</a> Do not be jealous of bad people, nor wish to spend time with them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Proverbs 24:2">2</a> Their heart plans to destroy, and their lips speak of trouble.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Proverbs 24:3">3</a> A house is built with wisdom and set up with understanding.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Proverbs 24:4">4</a> With knowledge, the rooms will be filled with all valuable and beautiful treasures.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Proverbs 24:5">5</a> A smart person is powerful; yes, a person who knows a lot gets even stronger.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Proverbs 24:6">6</a> With smart advice, you will plan your battles well; having many advisors brings safety.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Proverbs 24:7">7</a> Wisdom is too much for a fool; he doesn&#39;t speak in public.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Proverbs 24:8">8</a> Anyone who plans to do bad will be known as a troublemaker.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Proverbs 24:9">9</a> Thinking foolish thoughts is wrong, and people hate a mocker.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Proverbs 24:10">10</a> If you give up when trouble comes, you don&#39;t have much strength.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Proverbs 24:11">11</a> If you don&#39;t save those who are being led to death, and those about to be killed;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Proverbs 24:12">12</a> If you say, &quot;We didn&#39;t know about it,&quot; doesn&#39;t God, who knows what&#39;s in your heart, see it? And the one who watches over you, doesn&#39;t He know it? Won&#39;t He repay everyone for what they&#39;ve done?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Proverbs 24:13">13</a> My son, eat honey because it is good; and the honeycomb, which tastes sweet to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Proverbs 24:14">14</a> Understanding wisdom will be good for you. When you find it, you will be rewarded, and your hopes will not be dashed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Proverbs 24:15">15</a> Don&#39;t set a trap, wicked person, at the home of the good person; don&#39;t destroy where they rest.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Proverbs 24:16">16</a> A good person may fall down seven times but gets up again, while the bad will stumble into trouble.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Proverbs 24:17">17</a> Do not be happy when your enemy falls, and do not feel glad when they trip.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Proverbs 24:18">18</a> So that God does not see it and get unhappy, causing him to stop being angry with that person.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Proverbs 24:19">19</a> Don&#39;t worry about bad people, and don&#39;t be jealous of the wicked.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Proverbs 24:20">20</a> Evil people won&#39;t be rewarded; the light of the wicked will be extinguished.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Proverbs 24:21">21</a> My son, respect God and the king, and do not associate with those who like to rebel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Proverbs 24:22">22</a> Their disaster will come quickly; and who knows the destruction that will come upon them both?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Proverbs 24:23">23</a> Wise people know this too: It is not good to favor some people over others when making a decision.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Proverbs 24:24">24</a> Anyone who tells the wicked they are right will be cursed by people and despised by nations.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Proverbs 24:25">25</a> Those who correct others will find joy, and blessings will come to them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Proverbs 24:26">26</a> Everyone likes someone who gives the right answer.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Proverbs 24:27">27</a> Get your work ready outside, and make it ready for yourself in the field; then build your house.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Proverbs 24:28">28</a> Do not accuse your neighbor without reason, and do not lie.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Proverbs 24:29">29</a> Do not say, &quot;I will treat him as he treated me; I will give the man what he deserves for what he did.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Proverbs 24:30">30</a> I passed by the lazy person&#39;s field and the vineyard of someone who lacks sense.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Proverbs 24:31">31</a> Look, it was all covered with thorns, and nettles had spread over its surface, and its stone wall was torn down.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Proverbs 24:32">32</a> I saw it, thought about it carefully, observed it, and learned a lesson.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Proverbs 24:33">33</a> Just a bit more sleep, a bit more rest, a bit more of hugging your arms to sleep.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Proverbs 24:34">34</a> Your poverty will come like a traveler; your need like an armed man.</p>
