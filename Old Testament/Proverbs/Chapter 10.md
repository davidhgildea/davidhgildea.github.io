<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Proverbs 10:1">1</a> Solomon&#39;s proverbs. A smart son makes his father happy, but a silly son saddens his mother.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Proverbs 10:2">2</a> Money gained by wrongdoings is useless: but being right with God saves from death.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Proverbs 10:3">3</a> God will not let good people go hungry; but he will keep the wicked from getting what they want.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Proverbs 10:4">4</a> The person who is lazy becomes poor, but the hard worker becomes rich.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Proverbs 10:5">5</a> Someone who gathers crops in summer is smart, but someone who sleeps during the harvest brings shame.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Proverbs 10:6">6</a> Blessings are on the head of the fair, but the wicked&#39;s mouth is covered with violence.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Proverbs 10:7">7</a> People remember good people with happiness, but the names of bad people will be forgotten.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Proverbs 10:8">8</a> A wise person listens to instructions, but a foolish talker will fail.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Proverbs 10:9">9</a> Anyone who lives rightly walks safely; but those who follow crooked paths will be exposed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Proverbs 10:10">10</a> Someone who winks causes trouble, but a talking fool will fail.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Proverbs 10:11">11</a> A good person&#39;s words give life, but the evil hide behind violent talk.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Proverbs 10:12">12</a> Hatred causes fights, but love forgives all wrongs.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Proverbs 10:13">13</a> Someone with understanding speaks wise words, but a person without understanding gets punished.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Proverbs 10:14">14</a> Wise people save knowledge, but a fool&#39;s words lead to trouble.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Proverbs 10:15">15</a> A rich person&#39;s money is like a protective city; a poor person&#39;s ruin is their lack of wealth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Proverbs 10:16">16</a> The work of good people leads to life; the actions of bad people lead to sin.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Proverbs 10:17">17</a> Someone who follows advice is on the path to life, but someone who ignores correction goes wrong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Proverbs 10:18">18</a> Someone who hides hate with lies, and someone who spreads false rumors, is foolish.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Proverbs 10:19">19</a> When people talk a lot, they often sin. But if someone keeps quiet, they are smart.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Proverbs 10:20">20</a> The words of a good person are like valuable silver; a bad person&#39;s thoughts have little value.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Proverbs 10:21">21</a> The words of good people help many, but fools die because they lack wisdom.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Proverbs 10:22">22</a> God&#39;s blessing brings wealth, without any sadness added to it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Proverbs 10:23">23</a> A fool enjoys causing trouble, but a wise person has understanding.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Proverbs 10:24">24</a> What the wicked fear will happen to them, but what the good people want will be given to them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Proverbs 10:25">25</a> Like a tornado that goes by, the bad person disappears; but the good person stands strong forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Proverbs 10:26">26</a> Like vinegar hurts teeth and smoke stings the eyes, so a lazy person troubles those who ask for their help.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Proverbs 10:27">27</a> Respecting God extends life, but the wicked will have shorter lives.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Proverbs 10:28">28</a> Righteous people&#39;s hope will bring joy, but the wicked&#39;s desires will vanish.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Proverbs 10:29">29</a> God&#39;s way gives strength to the good, but will destroy those who do wrong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Proverbs 10:30">30</a> Good people will always be safe, but the bad will not stay on earth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Proverbs 10:31">31</a> The just person&#39;s mouth speaks wisdom, but the deceitful tongue will be silenced.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Proverbs 10:32">32</a> The good person&#39;s words are right, but the bad person&#39;s words are twisted.</p>
