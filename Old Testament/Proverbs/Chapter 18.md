<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Proverbs 18:1">1</a> A person with strong desires sets themselves apart, searches, and gets involved with all kinds of knowledge.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Proverbs 18:2">2</a> A fool does not enjoy learning, only showing off their own opinions.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Proverbs 18:3">3</a> When evil people come, disrespect also comes, and with it comes shame and blame.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Proverbs 18:4">4</a> A person&#39;s words are like deep water, and wisdom is like a flowing stream.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Proverbs 18:5">5</a> It is not right to favor the wicked or to deny justice to the righteous.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Proverbs 18:6">6</a> A foolish person&#39;s words lead to arguments, and their mouth invites punishment.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Proverbs 18:7">7</a> A foolish person&#39;s words cause their ruin, and their lips trap their own life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Proverbs 18:8">8</a> A gossip&#39;s words hurt like wounds; they reach deep inside a person.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Proverbs 18:9">9</a> The person who is lazy in their work is just like someone who wastes a lot.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Proverbs 18:10">10</a> God&#39;s name is like a strong tower: the good people run to it and are safe.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Proverbs 18:11">11</a> A rich person&#39;s money is like a protective city, and in their mind, it&#39;s like a tall wall.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Proverbs 18:12">12</a> Before ruin, a person&#39;s heart is proud, but before getting honor, there must be humility.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Proverbs 18:13">13</a> If someone replies to a topic without listening first, it is foolish and embarrassing for them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Proverbs 18:14">14</a> A person&#39;s spirit can endure sickness; but who can live with a broken spirit?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Proverbs 18:15">15</a> A careful person&#39;s heart gains knowledge; a wise person&#39;s ear looks for knowledge.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Proverbs 18:16">16</a> A person&#39;s gift opens the way and brings them to meet important people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Proverbs 18:17">17</a> Someone who speaks first in his argument appears right, until another comes and examines him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Proverbs 18:18">18</a> Drawing lots stops fights and decides between powerful people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Proverbs 18:19">19</a> An upset brother is tougher to win back than a fortified city, and their arguments are as secure as the locks on a fortress.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Proverbs 18:20">20</a> A person will be content with what they say; they will get enough from what they speak.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Proverbs 18:21">21</a> Life and death lie in what we say: those who enjoy talking will face the results.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Proverbs 18:22">22</a> Whoever finds a wife finds something good and gets favor from God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Proverbs 18:23">23</a> The poor beg kindly, but the rich reply harshly.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Proverbs 18:24">24</a> A person with friends should act friendly, and some friends are more loyal than a brother.</p>
