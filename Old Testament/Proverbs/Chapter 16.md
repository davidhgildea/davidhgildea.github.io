<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Proverbs 16:1">1</a> A person plans in their heart and speaks with their tongue, but God decides both.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Proverbs 16:2">2</a> A person thinks all their ways are right, but God judges their motives.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Proverbs 16:3">3</a> Give your work to God, and your plans will succeed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Proverbs 16:4">4</a> God made everything for his own purpose, even the bad people for the day of trouble.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Proverbs 16:5">5</a> Everyone with a proud heart is hated by God; even if they unite, they will not avoid punishment.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Proverbs 16:6">6</a> Through kindness and honesty, wrong is made right; and by respecting God, people avoid evil.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Proverbs 16:7">7</a> When a person&#39;s actions please God, even his enemies live peacefully with him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Proverbs 16:8">8</a> It is better to have a little with fairness than to have a lot of money without justice.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Proverbs 16:9">9</a> A person plans their path, but God guides their steps.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Proverbs 16:10">10</a> A king&#39;s words are God&#39;s words: he is fair in his decisions.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Proverbs 16:11">11</a> A fair scale and balance belong to God: every weight in the bag is made by him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Proverbs 16:12">12</a> Kings hate doing evil: a kingdom is built on fairness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Proverbs 16:13">13</a> Kings enjoy honest words; they value those who speak the truth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Proverbs 16:14">14</a> A king&#39;s anger is like a messenger of death, but a wise person can calm it down.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Proverbs 16:15">15</a> The king&#39;s smiling face brings life; his kindness is like a refreshing rain cloud.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Proverbs 16:16">16</a> It is much better to gain wisdom than gold, and gaining understanding is more valuable than getting silver.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Proverbs 16:17">17</a> The right path for good people is to avoid evil; those who follow their path protect their life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Proverbs 16:18">18</a> Pride leads to ruin, and arrogance comes before a fall.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Proverbs 16:19">19</a> It is better to be humble with the poor than to share the riches with the proud.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Proverbs 16:20">20</a> Anyone who deals with a problem wisely will succeed; and a person who trusts in God will be happy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Proverbs 16:21">21</a> Smart people are known for their good judgment, and kind words help others learn.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Proverbs 16:22">22</a> Understanding is a source of life to those who have it, but teaching fools is useless.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Proverbs 16:23">23</a> A wise person&#39;s heart guides their words and gives knowledge to what they say.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Proverbs 16:24">24</a> Nice words are like honey, sweet to the heart, and good for the body.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Proverbs 16:25">25</a> A path may seem right to someone, but it can end in death.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Proverbs 16:26">26</a> The person who works, works for themselves; their own hunger forces them to do it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Proverbs 16:27">27</a> A wicked person stirs up trouble, and their words burn like a fire.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Proverbs 16:28">28</a> A stubborn person causes conflict, and a gossip breaks apart close friends.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Proverbs 16:29">29</a> A rough person tricks their friend and guides them down a bad path.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Proverbs 16:30">30</a> He closes his eyes to plan bad things; when he moves his lips, he causes trouble.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Proverbs 16:31">31</a> Grey hair is a mark of honor when it comes from living a good life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Proverbs 16:32">32</a> Someone who stays calm is stronger than a powerful person, and controlling your emotions is better than conquering a city.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Proverbs 16:33">33</a> People throw lots to make a decision, but God decides the outcome.</p>
