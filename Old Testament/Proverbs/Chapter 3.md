<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Proverbs 3:1">1</a> My son, don&#39;t forget my teachings; keep my commands in your heart.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Proverbs 3:2">2</a> They will add to you many days, a long life, and peace.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Proverbs 3:3">3</a> Do not let kindness and honesty leave you; tie them around your neck and write them on your heart.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Proverbs 3:4">4</a> You will find favor and good understanding with both God and people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Proverbs 3:5">5</a> Trust God with all your heart; do not rely on your own understanding.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Proverbs 3:6">6</a> In everything you do, recognize God, and he will guide your paths.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Proverbs 3:7">7</a> Don&#39;t think you know everything; respect God, and stay away from bad things.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Proverbs 3:8">8</a> It will be good for your body and strength for your bones.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Proverbs 3:9">9</a> Honor God with your possessions and with the first part of everything you earn.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Proverbs 3:10">10</a> Your storerooms will be filled with lots of food, and your vats will overflow with new wine.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Proverbs 3:11">11</a> My son, do not hate God&#39;s discipline; do not get tired of his correction.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Proverbs 3:12">12</a> God corrects those he loves, just as a father corrects the son he enjoys.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Proverbs 3:13">13</a> The person who finds wisdom and gains understanding is happy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Proverbs 3:14">14</a> Its goods are better than silver, and its profits better than gold.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Proverbs 3:15">15</a> She is more valuable than rubies; nothing you want can compare to her.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Proverbs 3:16">16</a> She offers a long life with her right hand; with her left hand, she brings wealth and respect.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Proverbs 3:17">17</a> Her ways are nice, and all her paths bring peace.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Proverbs 3:18">18</a> She is like a life-giving tree to those who hold on to her; and everyone who keeps her is happy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Proverbs 3:19">19</a> God used wisdom to create the earth and with understanding set up the heavens.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Proverbs 3:20">20</a> With his knowledge, the deep places are split open, and the clouds release the dew.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Proverbs 3:21">21</a> My son, always keep wisdom and good judgement in sight.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Proverbs 3:22">22</a> They will bring life to you and charm to how you carry yourself.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Proverbs 3:23">23</a> You will walk safely on your path, and you will not trip.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Proverbs 3:24">24</a> When you lie down, you will not be scared; you will rest, and your sleep will be peaceful.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Proverbs 3:25">25</a> Do not be scared of sudden fear, or the destruction of the wicked, when it happens.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Proverbs 3:26">26</a> God will be your trust, and will protect you from getting caught in a trap.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Proverbs 3:27">27</a> Don&#39;t hold back good from those who deserve it when you can do it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Proverbs 3:28">28</a> Don&#39;t tell your neighbor, &quot;Go away and come back later, I&#39;ll give it to you tomorrow,&quot; when you have it with you now.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Proverbs 3:29">29</a> Do not plan to harm your neighbor, who lives trustingly near you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Proverbs 3:30">30</a> Do not argue with someone for no reason if they have not harmed you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Proverbs 3:31">31</a> Do not envy the bully, and do not follow any of his ways.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Proverbs 3:32">32</a> God hates deceitful people but shares secrets with the good.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Proverbs 3:33">33</a> God&#39;s curse is on the wicked&#39;s house, but he blesses the home of the righteous.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Proverbs 3:34">34</a> God mocks the mockers but gives kindness to the humble.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Proverbs 3:35">35</a> Smart people will receive honor, but fools will get disgrace.</p>
