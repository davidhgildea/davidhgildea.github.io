<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Proverbs 27:1">1</a> Don&#39;t brag about tomorrow; you don&#39;t know what the day will bring.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Proverbs 27:2">2</a> Let someone else praise you, not your own mouth; an outsider, not your own lips.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Proverbs 27:3">3</a> A rock is heavy, and sand is weighty, but a fool&#39;s anger is heavier than both.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Proverbs 27:4">4</a> Anger is harsh, and temper is wild, but who can face jealousy?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Proverbs 27:5">5</a> Public correction is better than hidden love.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Proverbs 27:6">6</a> A friend&#39;s wounds can be trusted, but an enemy&#39;s kisses are lies.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Proverbs 27:7">7</a> A satisfied person despises honey; but to a hungry person, even bitter food is sweet.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Proverbs 27:8">8</a> Like a bird that strays from its nest, so is a person who strays from their home.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Proverbs 27:9">9</a> Lotion and fragrance make the heart happy, just as a friend&#39;s sincere advice is sweet.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Proverbs 27:10">10</a> Don&#39;t abandon your friend or your father&#39;s friend, and don&#39;t go to your brother&#39;s house when you&#39;re in trouble; a close neighbor is better than a distant brother.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Proverbs 27:11">11</a> My son, be smart and make me happy, so I can reply to the one who criticizes me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Proverbs 27:12">12</a> A wise person sees trouble ahead and hides; but the foolish keep going and suffer for it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Proverbs 27:13">13</a> Take his coat if he promises to pay for someone he doesn&#39;t know, and get something from him to guarantee payment for a woman he&#39;s not familiar with.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Proverbs 27:14">14</a> If someone blesses their friend with a loud voice early in the morning, it will seem like a curse.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Proverbs 27:15">15</a> Constant dripping on a rainy day and a nagging woman are the same.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Proverbs 27:16">16</a> Anyone who hides her is like trying to hide the wind, and their actions will reveal themselves, just like the scent of perfume on their hand.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Proverbs 27:17">17</a> Iron makes iron sharp; so a person makes their friend&#39;s face bright.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Proverbs 27:18">18</a> Whoever takes care of the fig tree will eat its fruit; just as someone who serves their master well will be respected.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Proverbs 27:19">19</a> Just as water reflects a face back to a face, so one person&#39;s heart reflects another person&#39;s heart.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Proverbs 27:20">20</a> Hell and destruction are never full, just like a person&#39;s eyes are never satisfied.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Proverbs 27:21">21</a> Just as a pot is for melting silver and a furnace is for purifying gold, so a person is tested by being praised.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Proverbs 27:22">22</a> Even if you grind a fool in a bowl with grain using a crushing tool, his foolishness will not leave him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Proverbs 27:23">23</a> Work hard to understand the condition of your sheep, and pay close attention to your cattle.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Proverbs 27:24">24</a> Wealth does not last forever: and will a crown be passed down through every generation?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Proverbs 27:25">25</a> The hay shows up, the young grass appears, and mountain herbs are collected.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Proverbs 27:26">26</a> Use sheep for clothes, and goats to buy a field.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Proverbs 27:27">27</a> You will have enough goat&#39;s milk for your food, your family&#39;s food, and to support your servant girls.</p>
