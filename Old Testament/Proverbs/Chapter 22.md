<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Proverbs 22:1">1</a> Having a good reputation is better than having a lot of money, and being liked is better than having silver and gold.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Proverbs 22:2">2</a> Rich and poor people come together; God made all of them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Proverbs 22:3">3</a> A wise person sees danger and hides, but the naive keep going and suffer for it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Proverbs 22:4">4</a> By being humble and respecting God come wealth, respect, and life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Proverbs 22:5">5</a> Thorns and traps are in the path of the stubborn; the one who protects their own soul stays away from them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Proverbs 22:6">6</a> Teach a child how to live right, and even when old, they won&#39;t stray from it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Proverbs 22:7">7</a> The rich rule the poor, and the borrower becomes the lender&#39;s servant.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Proverbs 22:8">8</a> The person who does wrong will get useless results; their anger will not succeed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Proverbs 22:9">9</a> The person with a generous eye will be blessed, because he shares his food with the needy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Proverbs 22:10">10</a> Remove the mocker, and fighting will end; yes, conflict and insults will stop.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Proverbs 22:11">11</a> Someone who loves a pure heart and speaks kindly will be a friend to the king.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Proverbs 22:12">12</a> God&#39;s eyes protect knowledge and He overthrows the words of the wrongdoer.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Proverbs 22:13">13</a> The lazy person says, &quot;There is a lion outside, I will be killed in the streets.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Proverbs 22:14">14</a> The words of unfamiliar women are like a deep hole: a man disliked by God will fall into it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Proverbs 22:15">15</a> A child&#39;s heart holds foolishness; but strict discipline removes it far from them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Proverbs 22:16">16</a> Whoever mistreats the poor to get more wealth, and gives to the rich, will definitely become poor.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Proverbs 22:17">17</a> Listen closely and hear the wise words, and take my knowledge to heart.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Proverbs 22:18">18</a> It is good if you remember these things in your heart; they will be ready on your lips.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Proverbs 22:19">19</a> I have told you today so you can trust in God, just you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Proverbs 22:20">20</a> I have written to you excellent things about advice and knowledge.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Proverbs 22:21">21</a> I want you to clearly understand the true words so you can give true answers to those who ask you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Proverbs 22:22">22</a> Do not steal from the poor because they are poor, and do not make life hard for those who are suffering at the city gate.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Proverbs 22:23">23</a> God will defend their cause and ruin those who have ruined them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Proverbs 22:24">24</a> Do not be friends with someone who is always angry, and do not go with someone who has a bad temper.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Proverbs 22:25">25</a> So you won&#39;t learn his ways and trap your soul.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Proverbs 22:26">26</a> Don&#39;t be someone who shakes hands in agreement, or who guarantees to pay someone else&#39;s debts.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Proverbs 22:27">27</a> If you have nothing to pay, why should he take your bed from under you?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Proverbs 22:28">28</a> Do not move the old boundary markers set by your ancestors.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Proverbs 22:29">29</a> Do you see a person who works hard in their work? They will be in the presence of rulers; they will not be in the presence of ordinary people.</p>
