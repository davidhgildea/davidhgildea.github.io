<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Proverbs 26:1">1</a> Like snow in summer and rain during harvest, honor does not suit a fool.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Proverbs 26:2">2</a> Just like a bird that wanders or a swallow that flies, a curse without cause won&#39;t come.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Proverbs 26:3">3</a> A whip for the horse, a harness for the donkey, and a stick for the fool&#39;s back.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Proverbs 26:4">4</a> Do not answer a fool in the same foolish way, or you will be just like him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Proverbs 26:5">5</a> Reply to a fool as his nonsense deserves, so he doesn&#39;t think he&#39;s wise.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Proverbs 26:6">6</a> Sending a message through a fool is like cutting off your own feet and bringing trouble on yourself.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Proverbs 26:7">7</a> A disabled person&#39;s legs are not the same; similarly, a wise saying sounds wrong when a foolish person says it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Proverbs 26:8">8</a> Like tying a stone in a sling, so is giving honor to a fool.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Proverbs 26:9">9</a> Like a thorn sticks in a drunk person&#39;s hand, a wise saying is misunderstood by fools.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Proverbs 26:10">10</a> The great God who made everything rewards both the fool and those who do wrong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Proverbs 26:11">11</a> Like a dog that goes back to its vomit, a fool repeats his foolish actions.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Proverbs 26:12">12</a> Do you see a person who thinks they are very wise? A fool has more chance of finding wisdom than that person.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Proverbs 26:13">13</a> The lazy person says, &quot;There is a lion on the road; a lion is in the streets.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Proverbs 26:14">14</a> Just as a door swings on its hinges, a lazy person turns over in bed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Proverbs 26:15">15</a> The lazy person hides his hand in his pocket; it bothers him to lift it to his mouth again.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Proverbs 26:16">16</a> The lazy person thinks he is smarter than seven people who give good reasons.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Proverbs 26:17">17</a> Someone who interferes in a fight that&#39;s not their own is like someone who grabs a dog by its ears.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Proverbs 26:18">18</a> Like a crazy person who throws torches, arrows, and death,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Proverbs 26:19">19</a> This is like the person who tricks their friend and then says, &quot;I was only joking!&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Proverbs 26:20">20</a> When there&#39;s no wood, the fire goes out; just as when there&#39;s no gossip, arguments stop.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Proverbs 26:21">21</a> Just as coals heat coals and wood fuels fire, a quarrelsome person starts arguments.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Proverbs 26:22">22</a> A gossip&#39;s words hurt like wounds, reaching deep inside a person.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Proverbs 26:23">23</a> Hot words and a bad heart are like a broken pot covered with cheap silver.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Proverbs 26:24">24</a> The one who hates hides it with his words and keeps lies inside himself.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Proverbs 26:25">25</a> When he talks nicely, don&#39;t trust him: because his heart hides seven horrible things.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Proverbs 26:26">26</a> Who hides hate with lies, their evil will be exposed to everyone.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Proverbs 26:27">27</a> Anyone who digs a hole will fall into it; and if someone rolls a stone, it will roll back on them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Proverbs 26:28">28</a> A dishonest speaker dislikes those they hurt; and smooth talk can lead to destruction.</p>
