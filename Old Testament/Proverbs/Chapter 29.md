<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Proverbs 29:1">1</a> Anyone who keeps making the same mistakes and refuses to accept correction will suddenly be ruined beyond repair.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Proverbs 29:2">2</a> When good people lead, everyone is happy; but when bad people rule, everyone is sad.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Proverbs 29:3">3</a> Anyone who loves wisdom makes their father happy, but someone who hangs out with prostitutes wastes their money.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Proverbs 29:4">4</a> A king makes a country strong by fair judgement, but someone who takes bribes ruins it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Proverbs 29:5">5</a> Someone who praises their neighbor too much is setting a trap for them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Proverbs 29:6">6</a> When a bad person does wrong, they get caught in a trap, but a good person is happy and sings.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Proverbs 29:7">7</a> Good people think about the needs of the poor; but bad people don&#39;t care to know them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Proverbs 29:8">8</a> Mocking people trap a city, but wise people calm anger.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Proverbs 29:9">9</a> If a wise person argues with a foolish one, whether they get angry or laugh, there is no peace.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Proverbs 29:10">10</a> Violent people dislike those who are honest, but righteous people care about their well-being.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Proverbs 29:11">11</a> A foolish person says everything they think; but a wise person keeps it to themselves for later.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Proverbs 29:12">12</a> If a leader listens to lies, all their followers become bad.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Proverbs 29:13">13</a> The poor person and the liar come across each other; God gives light to both of their eyes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Proverbs 29:14">14</a> The king who judges the poor fairly, his rule will last forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Proverbs 29:15">15</a> Discipline and correction lead to wisdom, but a child left alone brings shame to his mother.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Proverbs 29:16">16</a> When more wicked people exist, wrongdoing grows, but good people will see them fail.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Proverbs 29:17">17</a> Discipline your child, and they will bring you peace; they will bring joy to your heart.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Proverbs 29:18">18</a> Where there is no guidance, people fail; but those who follow the rules will be happy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Proverbs 29:19">19</a> A worker won&#39;t change just by talking to them; even if they get it, they won&#39;t respond.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Proverbs 29:20">20</a> Do you see someone who talks without thinking? A fool has more hope than him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Proverbs 29:21">21</a> The one who raises his servant with care from a young age will eventually have him as his own son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Proverbs 29:22">22</a> A mad person causes trouble, and someone very angry commits many sins.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Proverbs 29:23">23</a> A person&#39;s pride will cause their downfall, but respect will support those who are humble.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Proverbs 29:24">24</a> Anyone who partners with a thief hates themselves: they hear cursing but do not report it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Proverbs 29:25">25</a> Being afraid of people can trap you, but trusting in God keeps you safe.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Proverbs 29:26">26</a> Many people try to win the leader&#39;s approval; but God gives each person&#39;s decision.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Proverbs 29:27">27</a> A dishonest person disgusts the honest, and someone who lives rightly is hated by the wicked.</p>
