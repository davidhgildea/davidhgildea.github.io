<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Proverbs 8:1">1</a> Doesn&#39;t wisdom call out? And understanding raise her voice?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Proverbs 8:2">2</a> She stands at the top of high places, beside the roads, at the crossroads.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Proverbs 8:3">3</a> She calls out at the city gates, where people enter, at the doors.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Proverbs 8:4">4</a> I call to you, men; my voice is for all people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Proverbs 8:5">5</a> You simple people, learn to be wise; and you fools, learn to understand.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Proverbs 8:6">6</a> Listen, for I will talk about great things; and when I open my lips, I will say what is right.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Proverbs 8:7">7</a> I will speak only truth; I hate to say anything wicked.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Proverbs 8:8">8</a> Everything I say is right; there&#39;s nothing wrong or twisted in my words.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Proverbs 8:9">9</a> Everything is clear to someone who understands, and correct for those who gain knowledge.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Proverbs 8:10">10</a> Accept my teaching instead of silver, and wisdom rather than the finest gold.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Proverbs 8:11">11</a> Wisdom is better than jewels; nothing you want can equal it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Proverbs 8:12">12</a> I, Wisdom, live with good sense, and I discover knowledge of clever designs.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Proverbs 8:13">13</a> Being afraid of God means hating evil: I hate pride, arrogance, evil behavior, and lying speech.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Proverbs 8:14">14</a> I give advice and true wisdom; I am insight; I have power.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Proverbs 8:15">15</a> Through me, kings rule and leaders make fair laws.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Proverbs 8:16">16</a> Through me, leaders govern, and nobles, including all the earth&#39;s judges.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Proverbs 8:17">17</a> I love those who love me; and those who look for me early will find me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Proverbs 8:18">18</a> Wealth and honor are with me; yes, lasting wealth and fairness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Proverbs 8:19">19</a> My fruit is more valuable than gold, even pure gold; and my earnings are better than the best silver.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Proverbs 8:20">20</a> I guide on the path of what is right, through the ways of fair decisions.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Proverbs 8:21">21</a> I want to help those who love me get wealth, and I will make their treasures full.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Proverbs 8:22">22</a> God chose me at the start of His path, before His ancient works.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Proverbs 8:23">23</a> I was chosen from forever, from the start, before the earth existed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Proverbs 8:24">24</a> Before the oceans existed, I was born; before there were springs filled with water.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Proverbs 8:25">25</a> Before the mountains were in place, before the hills I was born.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Proverbs 8:26">26</a> Before God made the earth, the fields, or the highest dust particles of the world.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Proverbs 8:27">27</a> When he made the sky, I was there: when he drew a circle on the deep:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Proverbs 8:28">28</a> When he made the clouds up high: when he made the deep springs strong:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Proverbs 8:29">29</a> When he told the sea its limits, so the waters would not disobey his command, when he set the earth&#39;s foundations:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Proverbs 8:30">30</a> I was with God, like someone raised with him; I was his joy every day, always happy in his presence.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Proverbs 8:31">31</a> I celebrated in the world he made; I enjoyed being with people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Proverbs 8:32">32</a> Listen to me now, children: happy are those who follow my teachings.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Proverbs 8:33">33</a> Listen to advice, be smart, and don&#39;t ignore it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Proverbs 8:34">34</a> Happy is the person who listens to me, keeping watch at my doors every day, waiting by the doorframes of my house.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Proverbs 8:35">35</a> Whoever finds me finds life and will receive favor from God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Proverbs 8:36">36</a> Whoever sins against me hurts their own soul; all who hate me love death.</p>
