<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Proverbs 21:1">1</a> The king&#39;s mind is under God&#39;s control, like water channels; He directs it wherever He chooses.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Proverbs 21:2">2</a> Everyone thinks their own way is right, but God knows what&#39;s in their hearts.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Proverbs 21:3">3</a> God prefers fairness and justice over sacrifices.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Proverbs 21:4">4</a> A haughty look, a proud heart, and the evil doings of the wicked are all sinful.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Proverbs 21:5">5</a> Careful thinkers plan to be rich; but those who rush can expect to be poor.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Proverbs 21:6">6</a> Getting riches by lying is useless and leads to death.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Proverbs 21:7">7</a> The stolen goods of bad people will ruin them because they won&#39;t act fairly.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Proverbs 21:8">8</a> People&#39;s ways can be twisted and odd, but those who are pure do what is right.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Proverbs 21:9">9</a> It&#39;s better to live in a corner of the roof than with a quarrelsome woman in a big house.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Proverbs 21:10">10</a> A bad person wants to do wrong, and he doesn&#39;t care about his neighbor.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Proverbs 21:11">11</a> When the mocker is punished, the naive become smart; and when the smart are taught, they gain knowledge.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Proverbs 21:12">12</a> The good person thinks carefully about the home of the bad, but God brings down the bad because of their evil ways.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Proverbs 21:13">13</a> Anyone who ignores the cry of the poor will also cry out and not be heard.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Proverbs 21:14">14</a> Giving a gift quietly can calm down anger; a hidden bribe can ease great fury.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Proverbs 21:15">15</a> Good people find joy in being fair, but those who do wrong will meet with ruin.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Proverbs 21:16">16</a> The person who leaves the path of understanding will stay among the dead.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Proverbs 21:17">17</a> Someone who loves fun will be poor; a person who loves wine and oil won&#39;t get rich.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Proverbs 21:18">18</a> Bad people will take the place of good people, and those who do wrong will take the place of the honest.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Proverbs 21:19">19</a> It is better to live in the desert than with a quarrelsome and angry woman.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Proverbs 21:20">20</a> A wise person has valuable treasure and oil at home; but a foolish person wastes it all.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Proverbs 21:21">21</a> Those who pursue what is right and kind find life, goodness, and respect.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Proverbs 21:22">22</a> A wise person climbs into a strong city and breaks down the city&#39;s strong defenses.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Proverbs 21:23">23</a> Whoever controls their speech protects themselves from problems.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Proverbs 21:24">24</a> His name is Proud and Arrogant, someone who acts with angry pride.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Proverbs 21:25">25</a> The lazy person&#39;s wants destroy him, because he won&#39;t work with his hands.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Proverbs 21:26">26</a> He always wants more all day long, but a good person gives freely without holding back.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Proverbs 21:27">27</a> God hates the wicked&#39;s offering; it&#39;s even worse when they offer it with evil intentions.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Proverbs 21:28">28</a> A liar will fail, but a person who listens will always speak the truth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Proverbs 21:29">29</a> A bad person makes their face stubborn, but a good person stays on the right path.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Proverbs 21:30">30</a> No wisdom, understanding, or advice can succeed against God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Proverbs 21:31">31</a> People get horses ready for battle day, but God gives safety.</p>
