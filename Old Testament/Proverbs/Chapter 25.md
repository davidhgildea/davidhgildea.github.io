<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Proverbs 25:1">1</a> These are also sayings of Solomon, which the men of Hezekiah, king of Judah, wrote down.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Proverbs 25:2">2</a> God finds glory in hiding things; a king&#39;s honor is to discover them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Proverbs 25:3">3</a> The sky is high, the earth is deep, and a king’s thoughts are hard to understand.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Proverbs 25:4">4</a> Remove the impurities from the silver, and a vessel for the silversmith will be made.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Proverbs 25:5">5</a> Remove the bad people from the king&#39;s presence, and his rule will be secure and good.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Proverbs 25:6">6</a> Do not push yourself forward in front of the king, and do not stand where important people are.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Proverbs 25:7">7</a> It is better to be invited to come up higher than to be told to move down in front of a leader you have seen.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Proverbs 25:8">8</a> Don&#39;t rush to argue, or you won&#39;t know what to do when your neighbor embarrasses you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Proverbs 25:9">9</a> Talk about your problem with your neighbor directly, and don&#39;t tell someone else their secret.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Proverbs 25:10">10</a> So the listener won&#39;t embarrass you, and your bad reputation won&#39;t disappear.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Proverbs 25:11">11</a> The right word at the right time is like golden apples in a silver setting.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Proverbs 25:12">12</a> Like a gold earring or a fine gold decoration, so is wise advice to someone who listens.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Proverbs 25:13">13</a> Like the coolness of snow at harvest time, a trustworthy messenger is to those who send him; he revives the spirits of his employers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Proverbs 25:14">14</a> Anyone who brags about a fake gift is like clouds and wind that bring no rain.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Proverbs 25:15">15</a> With patient persistence, a ruler can be convinced, and gentle speech can overcome strong resistance.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Proverbs 25:16">16</a> Have you found honey? Eat only as much as you need, so you don&#39;t overeat and throw it up.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Proverbs 25:17">17</a> Stop visiting your neighbor&#39;s house too much, or he will get tired of you and start to dislike you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Proverbs 25:18">18</a> A person who lies about others is as harmful as a club, a sword, or a sharp arrow.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Proverbs 25:19">19</a> Trusting someone who is not loyal when there is trouble is like having a broken tooth or a sprained foot.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Proverbs 25:20">20</a> Like someone who takes off a coat on a cold day or pours vinegar on soda, is a person who sings songs to a sad heart.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Proverbs 25:21">21</a> If your enemy is hungry, give him food; if he is thirsty, give him water.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Proverbs 25:22">22</a> You will make him feel guilty, like putting hot coals on his head, and God will reward you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Proverbs 25:23">23</a> The north wind blows rain away; just like an angry look stops mean gossip.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Proverbs 25:24">24</a> It&#39;s better to live in a small corner on the roof than in a big house with a quarrelsome woman.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Proverbs 25:25">25</a> Like cold water to a thirsty person, good news from a far place is so refreshing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Proverbs 25:26">26</a> A good person bending before the bad is like a dirty water source or a spoiled stream.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Proverbs 25:27">27</a> Eating too much honey is not good, and seeking your own praise is not really praise.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Proverbs 25:28">28</a> A person who can&#39;t control their emotions is like a city with broken walls and no defense.</p>
