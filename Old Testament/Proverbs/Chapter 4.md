<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Proverbs 4:1">1</a> Listen, children, to a father&#39;s teaching, and pay attention to gain wisdom.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Proverbs 4:2">2</a> I give you good teaching, do not abandon my rules.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Proverbs 4:3">3</a> I was my father&#39;s child, gentle and the only one loved by my mother.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Proverbs 4:4">4</a> He also taught me and told me, &quot;Keep my words in your heart: follow my rules, and you will live.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Proverbs 4:5">5</a> Gain wisdom, gain insight: don&#39;t forget; don&#39;t turn away from what I say.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Proverbs 4:6">6</a> Do not abandon her, and she will protect you: love her, and she will take care of you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Proverbs 4:7">7</a> Wisdom is the most important; so get wisdom, and with all you have, get understanding.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Proverbs 4:8">8</a> Honor her, and she will lift you up; she will give you respect when you hold her tightly.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Proverbs 4:9">9</a> She will give you a beautiful crown to make you look good; you will receive a glorious crown from her.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Proverbs 4:10">10</a> Listen, my child, and accept what I say; then you will live a long life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Proverbs 4:11">11</a> I have taught you in the way of wisdom; I have guided you on right paths.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Proverbs 4:12">12</a> When you go, your steps will not be tight; and when you run, you will not trip.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Proverbs 4:13">13</a> Hold on to teaching tightly; don&#39;t let it go: protect it, because it is your life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Proverbs 4:14">14</a> Don&#39;t follow the path of the wicked or walk in the way of bad people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Proverbs 4:15">15</a> Stay away, do not go near it, turn from it and keep going.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Proverbs 4:16">16</a> They can&#39;t sleep until they do wrong; they miss sleep if they haven&#39;t made someone stumble.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Proverbs 4:17">17</a> They eat the food of evil and drink the wine of violence.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Proverbs 4:18">18</a> The way of good people is like the sunrise, getting brighter and brighter until full daylight.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Proverbs 4:19">19</a> Wicked people live in darkness and don&#39;t understand what makes them fall.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Proverbs 4:20">20</a> My son, listen to my words; pay attention to what I say.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Proverbs 4:21">21</a> Always keep them in your sight; store them deep in your heart.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Proverbs 4:22">22</a> They give life to those who find them and health to everyone&#39;s body.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Proverbs 4:23">23</a> Protect your heart carefully, because from it come the important things in life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Proverbs 4:24">24</a> Stop talking in a twisted way, and keep dishonest words far from you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Proverbs 4:25">25</a> Keep your eyes looking directly forward, and your gaze straight ahead of you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Proverbs 4:26">26</a> Think carefully about where you walk, and make sure all your actions are steady.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Proverbs 4:27">27</a> Do not turn right or left: keep away from evil.</p>
