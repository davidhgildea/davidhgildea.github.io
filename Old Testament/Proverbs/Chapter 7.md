<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Proverbs 7:1">1</a> My son, remember my words and keep my instructions close to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Proverbs 7:2">2</a> Follow my commands and have life; treat my teachings as precious as your own eyes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Proverbs 7:3">3</a> Tie them to your fingers, write them on your heart.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Proverbs 7:4">4</a> Tell wisdom, &quot;You are my sister,&quot; and consider understanding like a close relative.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Proverbs 7:5">5</a> To help you stay away from the foreign woman, the one who uses sweet words to seduce.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Proverbs 7:6">6</a> From my house window, I looked out through the frame.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Proverbs 7:7">7</a> I saw among the naive, I noticed among the young men, a young man lacking sense.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Proverbs 7:8">8</a> Walking down the street near her corner; he took the path to her house.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Proverbs 7:9">9</a> In the dim light, in the evening, in the very dark night:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Proverbs 7:10">10</a> Look, a woman came to meet him, dressed like a prostitute and with a sneaky heart.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Proverbs 7:11">11</a> She is noisy and defiant; she doesn&#39;t stay at home.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Proverbs 7:12">12</a> She is outside, now in the streets, and hides, waiting at every corner.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Proverbs 7:13">13</a> She grabbed him, kissed him, and with a bold face said to him,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Proverbs 7:14">14</a> I bring peace offerings; today I have kept my promises.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Proverbs 7:15">15</a> So, I came out to meet you, to look for you eagerly, and I found you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Proverbs 7:16">16</a> I have decorated my bed with patterned covers, with artistic designs, with high-quality Egyptian linen.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Proverbs 7:17">17</a> I have made my bed smell nice with myrrh, aloes, and cinnamon.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Proverbs 7:18">18</a> Let&#39;s enjoy love until morning; let&#39;s comfort ourselves with loving.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Proverbs 7:19">19</a> The good man is not at home; he has gone on a long trip.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Proverbs 7:20">20</a> He has taken a bag of money and will return on the set day.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Proverbs 7:21">21</a> With her smooth talk she persuaded him, with her sweet words she led him on.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Proverbs 7:22">22</a> He follows her at once, like an ox going to be killed, or like a fool walking into punishment in the stocks.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Proverbs 7:23">23</a> Until an arrow pierces his liver; like a bird rushing into a trap, not knowing it will cost its life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Proverbs 7:24">24</a> Listen to me now, children, and pay attention to what I say.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Proverbs 7:25">25</a> Do not let your heart turn to her ways; do not wander on her paths.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Proverbs 7:26">26</a> She has made many people hurt; even strong men have been killed by her.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Proverbs 7:27">27</a> Her house is the path to destruction, leading down to the rooms of death.</p>
