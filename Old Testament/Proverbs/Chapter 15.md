<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Proverbs 15:1">1</a> A gentle reply calms anger, but harsh words cause tempers to rise.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Proverbs 15:2">2</a> The wise person uses knowledge correctly, but a fool&#39;s mouth spills out nonsense.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Proverbs 15:3">3</a> God sees everything everywhere, watching both the bad and the good.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Proverbs 15:4">4</a> A healthy tongue is like a tree of life, but a twisted tongue can break the spirit.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Proverbs 15:5">5</a> A foolish person hates his father&#39;s teaching, but someone who listens to correction is wise.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Proverbs 15:6">6</a> In the home of the good person, there is a lot of value, but the earnings of the bad person bring problems.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Proverbs 15:7">7</a> The wise spread knowledge with their words, but the foolish do not.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Proverbs 15:8">8</a> God hates the wicked&#39;s offerings, but loves the good person&#39;s prayers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Proverbs 15:9">9</a> Evil ways disgust God, but he loves those who seek to do what is right.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Proverbs 15:10">10</a> Correction is hard for someone who leaves the right path, and if you hate being corrected, you will die.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Proverbs 15:11">11</a> God sees everything in death and destruction, so he surely knows human hearts.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Proverbs 15:12">12</a> Someone who mocks others doesn&#39;t like being corrected; they won&#39;t seek advice from the wise.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Proverbs 15:13">13</a> A happy heart makes a smiling face, but a sad heart crushes the spirit.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Proverbs 15:14">14</a> A person with understanding seeks knowledge, but fools enjoy saying silly things.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Proverbs 15:15">15</a> Every day is bad for the suffering person, but a happy heart always feels like a feast.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Proverbs 15:16">16</a> It is better to have a small amount with respect for God than to have a lot of wealth with problems.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Proverbs 15:17">17</a> A meal of vegetables with love is better than a big cooked ox with hate.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Proverbs 15:18">18</a> An angry person causes fights, but one who is patient calms down arguments.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Proverbs 15:19">19</a> The lazy person&#39;s path is full of thorns, but the good person&#39;s path is smooth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Proverbs 15:20">20</a> A smart son makes his father happy, but a silly man looks down on his mother.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Proverbs 15:21">21</a> A foolish person enjoys being unwise, but a smart person acts with integrity.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Proverbs 15:22">22</a> Without advice, plans fail; but with many advisers, they succeed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Proverbs 15:23">23</a> A person feels happy with the reply they give; and a timely word, how wonderful it is!</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Proverbs 15:24">24</a> The path to life leads upward for the wise to keep them from going down to the grave.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Proverbs 15:25">25</a> God will destroy the proud person&#39;s house, but he will protect the widow&#39;s land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Proverbs 15:26">26</a> Wicked people&#39;s thoughts disgust God, but pure people&#39;s words are nice.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Proverbs 15:27">27</a> A person who wants to get rich quick will cause problems for their family, but someone who refuses bribes will have a good life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Proverbs 15:28">28</a> The good person thinks carefully before answering, but the wicked person spills out evil words.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Proverbs 15:29">29</a> God is far from the bad people, but he listens to the prayers of the good people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Proverbs 15:30">30</a> Bright eyes make the heart happy, and good news gives the body strength.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Proverbs 15:31">31</a> The ear that listens to life&#39;s corrections stays with the wise.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Proverbs 15:32">32</a> Whoever ignores advice hates themselves, but someone who listens to correction gains understanding.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Proverbs 15:33">33</a> Respecting God teaches wisdom; before getting honor, you must be humble.</p>
