<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Proverbs 19:1">1</a> It is better to be poor and honest than to be a liar and foolish.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Proverbs 19:2">2</a> It&#39;s not good for a person to lack knowledge, and those who hurry with their feet do wrong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Proverbs 19:3">3</a> A person&#39;s foolishness twists their path, and their heart blames God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Proverbs 19:4">4</a> Having money brings many friends; but being poor pushes people away.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Proverbs 19:5">5</a> A liar will be punished, and someone who tells lies will not go free.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Proverbs 19:6">6</a> Many will ask a ruler for favors, and everyone is a friend to a person who gives gifts.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Proverbs 19:7">7</a> All the poor person&#39;s brothers dislike him, and even more, his friends stay away from him. He tries to talk to them, but they are not there for him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Proverbs 19:8">8</a> The person who gains wisdom loves themselves; the one who holds on to understanding will discover what is good.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Proverbs 19:9">9</a> Someone who lies as a witness will not go free, and those who tell lies will be destroyed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Proverbs 19:10">10</a> Enjoyment doesn&#39;t suit a fool, and it&#39;s even worse when a servant becomes the boss of nobles.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Proverbs 19:11">11</a> A wise person controls their anger; and their honor is to forgive a wrong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Proverbs 19:12">12</a> A king&#39;s anger is like a lion&#39;s roar; but his kindness is like morning dew on the grass.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Proverbs 19:13">13</a> A foolish son brings trouble to his father; a quarreling wife is like constant dripping.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Proverbs 19:14">14</a> Homes and wealth are gifts from parents, but a wise wife comes from God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Proverbs 19:15">15</a> Laziness leads to deep sleep; a person who does not work will go hungry.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Proverbs 19:16">16</a> Whoever obeys the command protects their life; but whoever ignores their ways will die.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Proverbs 19:17">17</a> Whoever shows kindness to the poor lends to God; and God will repay what they have given.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Proverbs 19:18">18</a> Discipline your child while there is still hope, and do not give in because of their tears.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Proverbs 19:19">19</a> A person with much anger will face consequences: if you save him, you will have to do it again.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Proverbs 19:20">20</a> Listen to advice and accept teaching so you can be wise in the future.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Proverbs 19:21">21</a> People have many plans in their heart, but God&#39;s advice will last.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Proverbs 19:22">22</a> A person&#39;s kindness makes them desirable, and someone who is poor is better than one who lies.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Proverbs 19:23">23</a> Respecting God leads to life; those who do so will live content and untouched by harm.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Proverbs 19:24">24</a> A lazy person hides their hand in their pocket and won&#39;t even bring it to their mouth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Proverbs 19:25">25</a> Punish a mocker, and the naive will learn caution; correct a wise person, and they will gain knowledge.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Proverbs 19:26">26</a> Someone who uses up his father&#39;s resources and drives his mother away brings shame and disgrace as a child.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Proverbs 19:27">27</a> Stop listening, my son, to teaching that leads you away from true knowledge.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Proverbs 19:28">28</a> A dishonest witness mocks fairness, and the words of the wicked are greedy for evil.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Proverbs 19:29">29</a> Punishments are ready for mockers, and beatings for the backs of fools.</p>
