<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Proverbs 23:1">1</a> When you sit down to eat with a ruler, think carefully about what is in front of you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Proverbs 23:2">2</a> If you love to eat too much, it’s better to put a knife to your own throat.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Proverbs 23:3">3</a> Don&#39;t want his tasty food; it&#39;s tricky to eat.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Proverbs 23:4">4</a> Do not work to become rich; stop relying on your own knowledge.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Proverbs 23:5">5</a> Will you focus on what is not? For sure, wealth grows wings and flies off like an eagle toward the sky.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Proverbs 23:6">6</a> Do not eat the food of someone with a greedy heart, and don&#39;t crave their fancy dishes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Proverbs 23:7">7</a> As a person thinks in their heart, so are they: They say to you, &quot;Eat and drink,&quot; but their heart is not with you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Proverbs 23:8">8</a> You will throw up the food you ate and waste your nice words.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Proverbs 23:9">9</a> Don&#39;t talk to a fool; they will scorn the wisdom in what you say.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Proverbs 23:10">10</a> Do not move the old boundary stones; and do not go into the fields of orphans.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Proverbs 23:11">11</a> Their protector is strong; he will defend their case against you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Proverbs 23:12">12</a> Open your heart to learning, and listen to wise words.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Proverbs 23:13">13</a> Do not avoid disciplining the child: if you punish him with a rod, he will not die.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Proverbs 23:14">14</a> You should discipline him with a rod, and save his soul from death.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Proverbs 23:15">15</a> My son, if your heart is wise, then my heart will be happy, even mine.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Proverbs 23:16">16</a> Yes, my heart will be glad when your lips say what is right.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Proverbs 23:17">17</a> Do not envy wrongdoers, but always respect God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Proverbs 23:18">18</a> For sure, there is an end; and your hope will not be destroyed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Proverbs 23:19">19</a> Listen, my child, be smart, and lead your heart on the right path.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Proverbs 23:20">20</a> Don&#39;t hang out with heavy drinkers or those who eat meat to excess.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Proverbs 23:21">21</a> Drunk and overeaters will become poor, and being too sleepy will dress a person in rags.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Proverbs 23:22">22</a> Listen to your father who gave you life, and do not disrespect your mother when she gets old.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Proverbs 23:23">23</a> Buy the truth and do not sell it; also buy wisdom, teaching, and insight.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Proverbs 23:24">24</a> The good person&#39;s parent will be very happy; and the one who has a wise child will be joyful because of them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Proverbs 23:25">25</a> Your father and your mother will be happy, and the woman who gave birth to you will be joyful.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Proverbs 23:26">26</a> My child, give me your heart, and let your eyes watch my ways.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Proverbs 23:27">27</a> A prostitute is like a deep hole; and a foreign woman is like a tight trap.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Proverbs 23:28">28</a> She waits like a hunter for her target and leads to more people doing wrong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Proverbs 23:29">29</a> Who has sadness? Who has pain? Who has fights? Who has pointless talk? Who has injuries for no reason? Who has red eyes?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Proverbs 23:30">30</a> Those who stay long at the wine; those who go to look for mixed wine.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Proverbs 23:31">31</a> Don&#39;t look at the wine when it&#39;s red, when it sparkles in the cup, when it goes down smoothly.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Proverbs 23:32">32</a> In the end, it bites like a snake and poisons like a viper.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Proverbs 23:33">33</a> You will see strange women, and your heart will say twisted things.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Proverbs 23:34">34</a> Yes, you will be like someone lying down in the middle of the sea, or like someone lying on the top of a sail mast.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Proverbs 23:35">35</a> You will say, &quot;They hit me, but I wasn&#39;t hurt; they beat me, but I didn&#39;t feel it. When will I wake up? I will go back to it again.&quot;</p>
