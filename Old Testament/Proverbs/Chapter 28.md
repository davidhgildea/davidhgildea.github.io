<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Proverbs 28:1">1</a> Bad people run away even when no one chases them, but good people are as brave as a lion.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Proverbs 28:2">2</a> When a country sins, it has many rulers, but a wise and knowledgeable man will help it last longer.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Proverbs 28:3">3</a> A poor person who mistreats other poor people is like a rainstorm that destroys all the crops.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Proverbs 28:4">4</a> Those who ignore God&#39;s laws praise the bad people, but those who follow the laws stand against them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Proverbs 28:5">5</a> Bad people do not understand what is right, but those who look for God understand everything.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Proverbs 28:6">6</a> It&#39;s better to be poor and honest than to be dishonest and rich.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Proverbs 28:7">7</a> Whoever follows the law is a wise child, but he who hangs out with wild people brings shame to his parent.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Proverbs 28:8">8</a> Whoever makes money through charging too much interest and unfair profit will end up collecting wealth for someone who cares for the poor.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Proverbs 28:9">9</a> Whoever ignores the law, even their prayers are detestable.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Proverbs 28:10">10</a> Anyone who leads good people to do wrong will fall into their own trap, but the honest will get good things.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Proverbs 28:11">11</a> The rich man thinks he is smart, but the poor with insight can see through him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Proverbs 28:12">12</a> When good people are happy, there is great joy; but when bad people gain power, others go into hiding.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Proverbs 28:13">13</a> Whoever hides their sins won&#39;t succeed, but the one who admits and stops them will receive mercy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Proverbs 28:14">14</a> The person who always respects God is happy, but someone who becomes stubborn will get into trouble.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Proverbs 28:15">15</a> Like a loud lion and a wild bear, so is a bad leader to the poor.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Proverbs 28:16">16</a> A ruler who lacks understanding is also very harsh, but someone who hates greed will live a long time.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Proverbs 28:17">17</a> Anyone who hurts another person must run away to a deep hole; nobody should help him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Proverbs 28:18">18</a> Anyone who lives in a good way will be safe, but someone who chooses to do wrong will suddenly fail.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Proverbs 28:19">19</a> The one who works their land will have lots of bread, but the one who chases after worthless people will have plenty of poverty.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Proverbs 28:20">20</a> A loyal person will have many blessings, but someone rushing to get rich won&#39;t stay guiltless.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Proverbs 28:21">21</a> Showing favoritism is wrong: a person might sin just for a small piece of food.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Proverbs 28:22">22</a> Someone who rushes to get rich has bad intentions and does not realize that they will become poor.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Proverbs 28:23">23</a> Someone who corrects a person will later be appreciated more than someone who uses words to flatter.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Proverbs 28:24">24</a> Anyone who steals from their father or mother and says it&#39;s not wrong is a friend of someone who destroys.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Proverbs 28:25">25</a> A person with a proud heart causes conflict, but one who trusts in God will prosper.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Proverbs 28:26">26</a> Anyone who trusts only in themselves is foolish, but someone who acts wisely will be safe.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Proverbs 28:27">27</a> Whoever gives to the poor will not be in need, but one who ignores them will face many troubles.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Proverbs 28:28">28</a> When bad people gain power, others hide; but when they die, the good grow in number.</p>
