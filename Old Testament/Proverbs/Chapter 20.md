<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Proverbs 20:1">1</a> Wine can mock you, strong drink can cause trouble, and anyone fooled by it is not smart.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Proverbs 20:2">2</a> Being afraid of a king is like hearing a lion roar: whoever makes him angry harms themselves.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Proverbs 20:3">3</a> It is honorable for a person to avoid arguments, but a foolish person keeps interfering.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Proverbs 20:4">4</a> The lazy person won&#39;t plow because it&#39;s cold; so they will have to beg during harvest time and will get nothing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Proverbs 20:5">5</a> Advice in a person&#39;s heart is deep; but a wise person can bring it out.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Proverbs 20:6">6</a> Many people brag about how good they are, but finding someone faithful is hard.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Proverbs 20:7">7</a> A good person lives honestly, and their children are fortunate because of them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Proverbs 20:8">8</a> A king sitting on the judgment throne chases away all evil with his look.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Proverbs 20:9">9</a> Who can claim, I have cleaned my heart, I am free from my sin?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Proverbs 20:10">10</a> Different weights and different measures, both are hated by God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Proverbs 20:11">11</a> A child&#39;s actions show if their behavior is good and if what they do is right.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Proverbs 20:12">12</a> God made both the ear that hears and the eye that sees.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Proverbs 20:13">13</a> Don&#39;t love sleeping too much, or you will become poor; wake up, and you will have enough food to eat.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Proverbs 20:14">14</a> It&#39;s no good, it&#39;s no good, says the buyer; but when he leaves, then he brags.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Proverbs 20:15">15</a> There is gold and many rubies, but wise words are a valuable treasure.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Proverbs 20:16">16</a> Take his clothes if he promises for a stranger; get a security from him for an unknown woman.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Proverbs 20:17">17</a> Food gained by cheating may taste good, but later it will feel like eating rocks.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Proverbs 20:18">18</a> Every plan is set by advice: and with wise guidance, you should fight a battle.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Proverbs 20:19">19</a> Someone who spreads gossip tells secrets; so don&#39;t associate with a person who talks too much.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Proverbs 20:20">20</a> Anyone who curses their father or mother will have their light snuffed out in complete darkness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Proverbs 20:21">21</a> Getting an inheritance quickly at first may not lead to a blessing in the end.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Proverbs 20:22">22</a> Do not say, &quot;I will pay back evil;&quot; but wait for God, and He will rescue you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Proverbs 20:23">23</a> Different weights that cheat are disgusting to God; a dishonest scale is bad.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Proverbs 20:24">24</a> A person&#39;s paths are directed by God; so how can a person understand their own way?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Proverbs 20:25">25</a> A trap is set for the person who eats sacred things and then asks questions after making promises.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Proverbs 20:26">26</a> A wise king spreads out the bad people and rolls justice over them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Proverbs 20:27">27</a> A person&#39;s spirit is like God&#39;s light, shining deep inside them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Proverbs 20:28">28</a> Kindness and truth protect the king; his rule is supported by kindness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Proverbs 20:29">29</a> Young men are honored for their strength, and older men are respected for their gray hair.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Proverbs 20:30">30</a> A bruise cleans out badness: in the same way, punishment cleans the inside of a person.</p>
