<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Proverbs 13:1">1</a> A smart son listens to his father&#39;s teaching, but someone who mocks does not listen to correction.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Proverbs 13:2">2</a> A person will enjoy benefits from the good things they say, but those who break rules will experience harm.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Proverbs 13:3">3</a> Someone who controls their words protects their life, but someone who talks too much will find trouble.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Proverbs 13:4">4</a> The lazy person wants things but has nothing, while the hardworking person will prosper.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Proverbs 13:5">5</a> A good person hates lying, but an evil person is disgusting and will be shamed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Proverbs 13:6">6</a> Goodness protects the honest person, but evil brings down the wrongdoer.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Proverbs 13:7">7</a> Some people act rich but have nothing; others act poor but have great wealth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Proverbs 13:8">8</a> A man&#39;s wealth is his life&#39;s ransom; but the poor do not hear harsh words.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Proverbs 13:9">9</a> Good people shine brightly, but the light of the wicked will go out.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Proverbs 13:10">10</a> Pride leads to arguments, but wisdom is with those who take good advice.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Proverbs 13:11">11</a> Money earned unfairly will decrease, but those who work hard will gain more.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Proverbs 13:12">12</a> When you must wait for something you hope for, it makes you feel sad; but when you get what you want, it makes you very happy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Proverbs 13:13">13</a> Anyone who ignores God&#39;s word will be destroyed, but the one who respects the command will be rewarded.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Proverbs 13:14">14</a> The teachings of the wise are like a fountain of life, helping to avoid death&#39;s traps.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Proverbs 13:15">15</a> Clear understanding brings approval; but the path of wrongdoers is difficult.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Proverbs 13:16">16</a> A wise person acts with understanding, but a fool shows his foolishness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Proverbs 13:17">17</a> A bad messenger gets into trouble, but a reliable envoy brings wellness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Proverbs 13:18">18</a> Being poor and ashamed will happen to someone who ignores teaching, but the one who listens to correction will be respected.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Proverbs 13:19">19</a> When you get what you want, it feels good inside, but fools hate to stop doing bad things.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Proverbs 13:20">20</a> Someone who spends time with wise people will become wise, but someone who is friends with fools will get into trouble.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Proverbs 13:21">21</a> Evil follows those who sin, but good will be given to those who do right.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Proverbs 13:22">22</a> A good person leaves wealth for their grandchildren, and a sinner&#39;s riches will go to those who are right.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Proverbs 13:23">23</a> The poor person&#39;s farm has plenty of food, but some is wasted because of bad decisions.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Proverbs 13:24">24</a> Someone who doesn&#39;t discipline their child dislikes them, but one who loves their child corrects them early on.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Proverbs 13:25">25</a> Good people eat and are satisfied, but the wicked will be left wanting.</p>
