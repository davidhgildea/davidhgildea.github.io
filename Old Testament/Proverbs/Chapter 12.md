<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Proverbs 12:1">1</a> Anyone who loves learning loves knowledge, but someone who hates correction is foolish.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Proverbs 12:2">2</a> A good person gains God&#39;s favor, but God will condemn a person with evil plans.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Proverbs 12:3">3</a> Evil actions won&#39;t make a person successful, but good people will remain strong and secure.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Proverbs 12:4">4</a> A good woman is her husband&#39;s pride, but a woman who brings shame is like decay in his bones.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Proverbs 12:5">5</a> Good people think right thoughts, but the advice of the wicked is to lie.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Proverbs 12:6">6</a> Wicked people&#39;s words are like traps set to hurt others, but honest people will be saved by what they say.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Proverbs 12:7">7</a> Evil people are defeated and gone, but the home of the good will last.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Proverbs 12:8">8</a> People will praise a man for his wisdom, but someone with a bad heart will be disliked.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Proverbs 12:9">9</a> Someone who is looked down on but has a servant is better off than someone who acts important but has no food.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Proverbs 12:10">10</a> A good person cares for their animal&#39;s life, but even the kindness of a bad person is harsh.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Proverbs 12:11">11</a> Whoever works their land will have plenty of food, but whoever chases empty dreams lacks wisdom.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Proverbs 12:12">12</a> Bad people want what other bad people have, but good people produce good results.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Proverbs 12:13">13</a> Evil people get caught by their own words, but the good get out of trouble.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Proverbs 12:14">14</a> A person will be happy with the good things they say, and they will get back what their own work deserves.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Proverbs 12:15">15</a> A fool thinks they are always right, but a wise person listens to advice.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Proverbs 12:16">16</a> A foolish person quickly shows anger, but a wise person hides embarrassment.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Proverbs 12:17">17</a> The person who speaks the truth shows what is right, but a liar tells lies.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Proverbs 12:18">18</a> Some people speak sharp words like a sword&#39;s stab, but wise people&#39;s words bring healing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Proverbs 12:19">19</a> Truthful words will last forever, but lies last only a moment.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Proverbs 12:20">20</a> People who plan evil have lies in their hearts, but those who advise peace find joy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Proverbs 12:21">21</a> No harm will come to the good person, but the bad will be filled with trouble.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Proverbs 12:22">22</a> God hates lying lips, but loves those who speak the truth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Proverbs 12:23">23</a> A wise person keeps knowledge to themselves, but a fool&#39;s heart shouts out silliness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Proverbs 12:24">24</a> Hard workers will be leaders, but lazy people will be servants.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Proverbs 12:25">25</a> Sadness in a person&#39;s heart weighs them down: but a kind word cheers them up.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Proverbs 12:26">26</a> A good person is better than their neighbor, but the wicked are led astray by their own ways.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Proverbs 12:27">27</a> The lazy person doesn&#39;t cook what they catch hunting, but a hardworking person&#39;s wealth is valuable.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Proverbs 12:28">28</a> Living right brings life: there is no death on that path.</p>
