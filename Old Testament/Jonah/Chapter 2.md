<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Jonah 2:1">1</a> Then Jonah prayed to God from inside the fish.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Jonah 2:2">2</a> I called out to God because of my suffering, and he listened to me; from deep inside the grave I cried, and you heard my voice.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Jonah 2:3">3</a> You threw me into the deep sea; I was surrounded by water; all your waves and billows rolled over me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Jonah 2:4">4</a> Then I said, I am thrown away from your view; but I will look again towards your holy temple.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Jonah 2:5">5</a> Water surrounded me, even to my life; deep water closed around me and plants tangled around my head.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Jonah 2:6">6</a> I went down to the mountain bases; the earth trapped me forever: but you, God, brought my life up from ruin.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Jonah 2:7">7</a> When I felt weak inside, I remembered God, and my prayer reached you in your holy temple.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Jonah 2:8">8</a> Those who believe in false things ignore the kindness meant for them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Jonah 2:9">9</a> I will offer sacrifice to you with thankful voice; I will fulfill my promises. Salvation comes from God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Jonah 2:10">10</a> God spoke to the fish, and it threw up Jonah onto dry land.</p>
