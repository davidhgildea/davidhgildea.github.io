<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Jonah 1:1">1</a> God spoke to Jonah, Amittai&#39;s son, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Jonah 1:2">2</a> Get up, go to Nineveh, the big city, and speak out against it, because their badness has reached me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Jonah 1:3">3</a> Jonah got up to run away to Tarshish to escape from God, and he went to Joppa. There he found a boat going to Tarshish. He paid for the trip and got on board to sail with them to Tarshish, away from God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Jonah 1:4">4</a> But God made a strong wind blow over the sea, and a big storm came up, making the ship nearly break apart.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Jonah 1:5">5</a> The sailors were scared and each prayed to his own god. They threw the ship&#39;s cargo into the sea to make it lighter. But Jonah had gone below deck, where he lay down and fell into a deep sleep.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Jonah 1:6">6</a> The captain went to him and said, &quot;What are you doing sleeping? Get up and pray to your God; maybe God will notice us, and we won&#39;t die.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Jonah 1:7">7</a> They all said to each other, &quot;Come, let&#39;s draw lots to find out who is responsible for this trouble.&quot; They drew lots and the lot pointed to Jonah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Jonah 1:8">8</a> They asked him, &quot;Please tell us why this trouble is happening to us. What is your job? Where do you come from? What&#39;s your country? And what people do you belong to?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Jonah 1:9">9</a> He told them, &quot;I am a Hebrew; and I respect God, the God of heaven, who created the sea and the land.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Jonah 1:10">10</a> The men became very scared and asked him, &quot;Why did you do this?&quot; They knew he was running away from God because he had told them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Jonah 1:11">11</a> Then they asked him, &quot;What should we do to you to make the sea calm for us?&quot; because the sea was stormy and turbulent.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Jonah 1:12">12</a> He told them, &quot;Pick me up and throw me into the sea, and then the sea will become calm for you. I know that this big storm has come because of me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Jonah 1:13">13</a> The men rowed hard to reach the land, but they couldn&#39;t because the sea got rough and stormy against them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Jonah 1:14">14</a> So they called out to God, saying, &quot;Please, God, we beg you, don&#39;t let us die because of this man&#39;s life, and don&#39;t make us guilty of killing an innocent person: for you, God, have done as you wanted.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Jonah 1:15">15</a> So they picked up Jonah and threw him into the sea, and the sea stopped raging.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Jonah 1:16">16</a> The men became very afraid of God, and they gave a sacrifice to God and promised to do things for Him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Jonah 1:17">17</a> God had sent a big fish to swallow Jonah. Jonah was inside the fish for three days and nights.</p>
