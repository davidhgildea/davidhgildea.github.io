<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Jonah 3:1">1</a> God spoke to Jonah again, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Jonah 3:2">2</a> Get up, go to Nineveh, that big city, and tell them the message I have given you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Jonah 3:3">3</a> Jonah got up and went to Nineveh, as God had told him. Nineveh was a very big city; it took three days to go through it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Jonah 3:4">4</a> Jonah started to go into the city, traveling for one day, and he shouted, &quot;In forty days, Nineveh will be destroyed.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Jonah 3:5">5</a> The people of Nineveh trusted God, announced a fast, and wore rough clothes, from the most important to the least.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Jonah 3:6">6</a> News reached the king of Nineveh, and he got up from his throne, took off his robe, put on sackcloth, and sat in ashes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Jonah 3:7">7</a> He had it announced and spread through Nineveh by the king&#39;s and his nobles&#39; order, saying, &quot;No person or animal, no herd or flock, should eat or drink anything.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Jonah 3:8">8</a> Let people and animals wear rough cloth, and call out to God strongly; yes, let everyone stop their bad ways and stop hurting others with their hands.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Jonah 3:9">9</a> Who knows if God will change his mind and stop being so angry, so we won&#39;t die?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Jonah 3:10">10</a> God saw what they did, that they stopped doing evil, so he changed his mind about the punishment he had planned to give them, and he didn&#39;t do it.</p>
