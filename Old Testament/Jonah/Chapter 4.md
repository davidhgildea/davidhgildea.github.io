<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Jonah 4:1">1</a> Jonah was very upset by this, and he became angry.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Jonah 4:2">2</a> He prayed to God and said, &quot;I knew this would happen, God. That&#39;s why I ran away to Tarshish. I knew you are kind and merciful, not easily angered, full of love, and you change your mind about punishing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Jonah 4:3">3</a> So now, God, please take my life from me; it&#39;s better for me to die than to live.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Jonah 4:4">4</a> Then God asked, &quot;Is it right for you to be angry?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Jonah 4:5">5</a> Jonah left the city, sat on its east side, built himself a shelter, and sat in its shade to see what would happen to the city.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Jonah 4:6">6</a> God made a plant grow over Jonah to give him shade and help him feel better. Jonah was very happy about the plant.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Jonah 4:7">7</a> God made a worm come up the next morning, and it attacked the plant so that it dried up.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Jonah 4:8">8</a> When the sun rose, God sent a strong east wind; the sun blazed on Jonah&#39;s head so much that he became weak, wished to die, and thought, &quot;It&#39;s better for me to die than to live.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Jonah 4:9">9</a> God asked Jonah, &quot;Is it right for you to be angry about the plant?&quot; And he answered, &quot;Yes, I am right to be angry enough to die.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Jonah 4:10">10</a> God said, &quot;You feel sorry for the plant you didn&#39;t work for or make grow. It appeared overnight and died overnight.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Jonah 4:11">11</a> Shouldn&#39;t I have mercy on Nineveh, that large city, with more than 120,000 people who do not know right from left, and many animals too?</p>
