<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="2 Chronicles 24:1">1</a> Joash became king at seven and ruled for forty years in Jerusalem. His mother, Zibiah, was from Beersheba.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="2 Chronicles 24:2">2</a> Joash did what was right in God&#39;s eyes all the time Jehoiada the priest was alive.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="2 Chronicles 24:3">3</a> Jehoiada married two women and they had sons and daughters together.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="2 Chronicles 24:4">4</a> After that, Joash decided to fix God&#39;s house.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="2 Chronicles 24:5">5</a> He brought the priests and Levites together and told them, &quot;Go to the towns of Judah and collect money from all Israel every year to fix God&#39;s house. Make sure you do it quickly.&quot; But the Levites did not hurry.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="2 Chronicles 24:6">6</a> The king asked Jehoiada the leader, &quot;Why haven&#39;t you made the Levites collect taxes from Judah and Jerusalem as Moses, God&#39;s servant, and Israel&#39;s assembly commanded for the testimony tent?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="2 Chronicles 24:7">7</a> Athaliah&#39;s sons, who were evil, destroyed God&#39;s house and gave all the holy items from God&#39;s house to Baalim.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="2 Chronicles 24:8">8</a> By the king&#39;s order, they made a box and placed it outside the gate of God&#39;s house.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="2 Chronicles 24:9">9</a> They announced in Judah and Jerusalem that everyone should bring to God the offerings that Moses, God&#39;s servant, commanded Israel to give in the wilderness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="2 Chronicles 24:10">10</a> All the leaders and people were happy, gave, and put into the chest until they finished.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="2 Chronicles 24:11">11</a> When the box was delivered to the king&#39;s office by the Levites and they noticed a lot of money inside, the king&#39;s writer and the high priest&#39;s official would empty the box, take it, and return it to its spot. They did this every day and collected a great deal of money.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="2 Chronicles 24:12">12</a> The king and Jehoiada paid workers to serve in God&#39;s house, and they hired builders and woodworkers to fix it. They also paid those who worked with iron and bronze to repair God&#39;s house.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="2 Chronicles 24:13">13</a> The workers did their job, completed the work, arranged God&#39;s house as it should be, and made it strong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="2 Chronicles 24:14">14</a> When they were done, they gave the leftover money to the king and Jehoiada, who used it to make items for God&#39;s house – items for service and sacrifices, along with bowls, and gold and silver things. They presented burnt sacrifices in God&#39;s house all the time Jehoiada lived.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="2 Chronicles 24:15">15</a> Jehoiada grew very old and died when he lived a long life; he was one hundred and thirty years old at his death.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="2 Chronicles 24:16">16</a> They buried him in the city of David with the kings because he did good things for Israel, for God, and for his own family.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="2 Chronicles 24:17">17</a> After Jehoiada died, the leaders of Judah bowed before the king, and the king listened to them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="2 Chronicles 24:18">18</a> They left their ancestors&#39; God&#39;s house and worshiped idols and sacred trees; because of this sin, anger came upon Judah and Jerusalem.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="2 Chronicles 24:19">19</a> God sent messengers to the people to bring them back to Him; they spoke out against the people&#39;s ways, but the people would not listen.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="2 Chronicles 24:20">20</a> God&#39;s Spirit came over Zechariah, son of Jehoiada the priest. While standing before the people, he said, &quot;God asks why do you disobey God&#39;s commands, so that you can&#39;t succeed? You have abandoned God, so he has abandoned you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="2 Chronicles 24:21">21</a> They plotted against him and threw stones at him, following the king&#39;s orders, in the courtyard of God&#39;s house.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="2 Chronicles 24:22">22</a> King Joash forgot the good things Jehoiada, his father, did for him and killed Jehoiada&#39;s son. As he was dying, he said, &quot;God will see this and make you pay.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="2 Chronicles 24:23">23</a> At the end of the year, Syria&#39;s army attacked. They came to Judah and Jerusalem, killed all the leaders, and sent their valuables to the king of Damascus.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="2 Chronicles 24:24">24</a> The Syrian army attacked with just a few soldiers, but God gave them victory over a much larger group because they had abandoned the God of their ancestors. So they punished Joash.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="2 Chronicles 24:25">25</a> After they left him because he was very sick, his own servants plotted against him because he had killed the sons of priest Jehoiada. They killed him in his bed, and he died. They buried him in the city of David, but not in the kings&#39; tombs.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="2 Chronicles 24:26">26</a> These are the ones who plotted against him: Zabad, son of Shimeath from Ammon, and Jehozabad, son of Shimrith from Moab.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="2 Chronicles 24:27">27</a> About his sons, the heavy tasks given to him, and the fixing of God&#39;s house, you can read in the book of kings&#39; history. And his son Amaziah became king after him.</p>
