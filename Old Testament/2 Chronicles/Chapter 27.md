<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="2 Chronicles 27:1">1</a> Jotham was 25 years old when he became king, and he ruled for 16 years in Jerusalem. His mother&#39;s name was Jerushah, and she was the daughter of Zadok.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="2 Chronicles 27:2">2</a> He did what was right in God&#39;s eyes, just like his father Uzziah did, but he didn&#39;t go into God&#39;s temple. Yet the people still acted wrongly.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="2 Chronicles 27:3">3</a> He built the tall gate for God&#39;s house, and on the Ophel wall he built a lot.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="2 Chronicles 27:4">4</a> He also built towns in the hills of Judah, and in the woods he constructed strongholds and towers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="2 Chronicles 27:5">5</a> He also battled the king of the Ammonites and defeated them. That year, the Ammonites gave him a hundred talents of silver, ten thousand measures of wheat, and ten thousand of barley. The Ammonites paid him the same amounts in the second and third years too.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="2 Chronicles 27:6">6</a> Jotham grew strong because he lived rightly before God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="2 Chronicles 27:7">7</a> The other things Jotham did, his wars, and his life are all written in the book of the kings of Israel and Judah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="2 Chronicles 27:8">8</a> He was twenty-five years old when he started to rule, and he ruled for sixteen years in Jerusalem.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="2 Chronicles 27:9">9</a> Jotham died and was buried with his ancestors in the city of David. Then his son Ahaz became king.</p>
