<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="2 Chronicles 32:1">1</a> After this, Sennacherib, the king of Assyria, arrived. He entered Judah, surrounded the fortified cities, and planned to conquer them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="2 Chronicles 32:2">2</a> When Hezekiah saw that Sennacherib had come and intended to attack Jerusalem,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="2 Chronicles 32:3">3</a> He made a plan with his leaders and strong men to block the water from the springs outside the city, and they helped him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="2 Chronicles 32:4">4</a> Many people came together and they blocked all the springs and the stream that flowed through the land, asking, &quot;Why should the kings of Assyria arrive and find plenty of water?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="2 Chronicles 32:5">5</a> He made himself strong, fixed the broken wall, built it higher to the towers, added another wall outside, fixed Millo in David&#39;s city, and made lots of darts and shields.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="2 Chronicles 32:6">6</a> He appointed military leaders over the people, brought them to him at the city gate&#39;s entrance, and spoke encouraging words to them, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="2 Chronicles 32:7">7</a> Be brave and strong, don&#39;t be scared or upset because of the king of Assyria or the big crowd with him; we have more on our side than he does.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="2 Chronicles 32:8">8</a> He has only human power, but we have God to help us and to fight our battles. And the people relied on what Hezekiah, the king of Judah, said.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="2 Chronicles 32:9">9</a> After this, Sennacherib, the king of Assyria, sent his servants to Jerusalem while he was attacking Lachish with all his army. They went to Hezekiah, the king of Judah, and to all the people in Jerusalem, and said,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="2 Chronicles 32:10">10</a> Sennacherib, king of Assyria, says, &quot;What are you trusting in that makes you stay in Jerusalem under siege?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="2 Chronicles 32:11">11</a> Isn&#39;t Hezekiah convincing you to let yourselves die from hunger and thirst by saying, &quot;God will save us from the king of Assyria&quot;?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="2 Chronicles 32:12">12</a> Didn&#39;t Hezekiah remove his high places and altars, and order Judah and Jerusalem to worship at only one altar and burn incense on it?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="2 Chronicles 32:13">13</a> Don&#39;t you know what I and my ancestors did to all the people of other countries? Could the gods of those countries save their lands from me?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="2 Chronicles 32:14">14</a> Which god of the nations that my ancestors destroyed could save their people from me, so that your God could save you from me?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="2 Chronicles 32:15">15</a> So now, don&#39;t let Hezekiah trick you or convince you like this. Don&#39;t believe him. No god of any nation or kingdom could save their people from my power, or the power of my ancestors. How can your God rescue you from me?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="2 Chronicles 32:16">16</a> His servants spoke even more against God and against His servant Hezekiah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="2 Chronicles 32:17">17</a> He also wrote letters to insult God and to speak against Him, saying, “Just like the gods of other countries did not save their people from me, so Hezekiah&#39;s God will not save His people from me.”</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="2 Chronicles 32:18">18</a> They shouted in the Jewish language at the people on Jerusalem&#39;s wall to scare them and make them anxious, so they could capture the city.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="2 Chronicles 32:19">19</a> They spoke against the God of Jerusalem like they did against the earthly people&#39;s gods, which were made by human hands.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="2 Chronicles 32:20">20</a> King Hezekiah and the prophet Isaiah, Amoz&#39;s son, prayed and called out to God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="2 Chronicles 32:21">21</a> God sent an angel who killed all the brave warriors, the leaders, and the commanders in the Assyrian king&#39;s camp. So the king went back home feeling ashamed. And when he entered his god&#39;s temple, his own sons killed him with a sword.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="2 Chronicles 32:22">22</a> So God saved Hezekiah and the people of Jerusalem from the power of Sennacherib, the king of Assyria, and from all others, and took care of them on every side.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="2 Chronicles 32:23">23</a> Many people brought gifts to God in Jerusalem, and presents to King Hezekiah of Judah, making him honored in the eyes of all nations from then on.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="2 Chronicles 32:24">24</a> During that time, Hezekiah was very sick. He prayed to God, and God spoke to him and showed him a sign.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="2 Chronicles 32:25">25</a> But Hezekiah did not repay the kindness shown to him, for he became proud. So anger came upon him, and upon Judah and Jerusalem.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="2 Chronicles 32:26">26</a> Despite his pride, Hezekiah humbled himself and so did the people of Jerusalem, so God&#39;s anger did not come on them during Hezekiah&#39;s time.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="2 Chronicles 32:27">27</a> Hezekiah had a lot of wealth and honor. He built storage places for silver, gold, precious stones, spices, shields, and all kinds of valuable items.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="2 Chronicles 32:28">28</a> Storage buildings for extra grain, wine, and oil; pens for all kinds of animals, and shelters for flocks.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="2 Chronicles 32:29">29</a> He also gave him towns and many sheep and cattle, because God had blessed him with great wealth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="2 Chronicles 32:30">30</a> Hezekiah also blocked the upper flow of Gihon&#39;s water and directed it straight to the west side of David&#39;s city, and he succeeded in all he did.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="2 Chronicles 32:31">31</a> However, during the time of the messengers from the rulers of Babylon, who came to him to ask about the miracle that happened in the country, God allowed him to be tested to see what was truly in his heart.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="2 Chronicles 32:32">32</a> The other things Hezekiah did and his kind acts are recorded in Isaiah the prophet&#39;s vision, Isaiah being Amoz&#39;s son, and in the history book of the kings of Judah and Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="2 Chronicles 32:33">33</a> Hezekiah died like his ancestors, and they buried him in the best tomb of David&#39;s descendants. All of Judah and the people of Jerusalem honored him when he died. Then his son Manasseh became king.</p>
