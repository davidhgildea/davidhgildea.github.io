<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="2 Chronicles 1:1">1</a> Solomon, David&#39;s son, became strong as a king, and God was with him and made him very great.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="2 Chronicles 1:2">2</a> Then Solomon spoke to all Israel, including the leaders of thousands and hundreds, the judges, every ruler, and all the family heads.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="2 Chronicles 1:3">3</a> Solomon and all the people with him went to the high place in Gibeon because that&#39;s where God&#39;s meeting tent, which Moses, God&#39;s servant, had made in the desert, was located.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="2 Chronicles 1:4">4</a> David had moved the ark of God from Kirjathjearim to the spot he had set up for it; he had made a tent for it in Jerusalem.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="2 Chronicles 1:5">5</a> The bronze altar made by Bezaleel, Uri&#39;s son, Hur&#39;s grandson, was placed in front of God&#39;s Tabernacle. Solomon and the people went to pray there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="2 Chronicles 1:6">6</a> Solomon went to the bronze altar in front of God, which was at the meeting tent, and gave a thousand burnt offerings on it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="2 Chronicles 1:7">7</a> That night, God appeared to Solomon and said to him, &quot;Ask what you want me to give you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="2 Chronicles 1:8">8</a> Solomon said to God, &quot;You have shown great kindness to my father David and have made me king in his place.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="2 Chronicles 1:9">9</a> Now, God, let your promise to my father David be true, for you have made me king over a people as numerous as the dust of the earth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="2 Chronicles 1:10">10</a> Please give me wisdom and knowledge so I can lead your people. Who else can judge such a great group?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="2 Chronicles 1:11">11</a> God told Solomon, &quot;Since you wanted this and did not ask for money, wealth, honor, or the death of your enemies, and you did not even ask for a long life, but you asked for wisdom and knowledge to make good decisions for my people I have put you in charge of as their king:&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="2 Chronicles 1:12">12</a> God has given you wisdom and knowledge; and you will receive riches, wealth, and honor more than any kings before you, and no future king will match what you have.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="2 Chronicles 1:13">13</a> Solomon returned from his trip to the sacred site at Gibeon to Jerusalem, leaving the meeting tent, and ruled over Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="2 Chronicles 1:14">14</a> Solomon collected chariots and soldiers on horses. He had 1,400 chariots and 12,000 soldiers on horses. He put them in the chariot cities and near the king in Jerusalem.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="2 Chronicles 1:15">15</a> The king made silver and gold as common in Jerusalem as stones, and he made cedar trees as plentiful as the sycamore trees in the lowlands.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="2 Chronicles 1:16">16</a> Solomon got horses from Egypt and also linen yarn. The king&#39;s traders bought the yarn for a certain amount of money.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="2 Chronicles 1:17">17</a> They brought a chariot from Egypt for six hundred silver coins, and a horse for one hundred and fifty coins: and in this way, they provided horses for all the Hittite and Syrian kings through their connections.</p>
