<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="2 Chronicles 28:1">1</a> Ahaz was twenty when he became king, and he ruled for sixteen years in Jerusalem. However, he did not do what was right in God&#39;s eyes, unlike his ancestor David.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="2 Chronicles 28:2">2</a> He followed the paths of Israel&#39;s kings, and also made metal idols for Baals.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="2 Chronicles 28:3">3</a> He also burned incense in the Valley of Ben Hinnom and burned his children in the fire, following the shameful practices of the nations that God had driven out before the Israelites.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="2 Chronicles 28:4">4</a> He also made sacrifices and burned incense on the high places, on the hills, and under every green tree.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="2 Chronicles 28:5">5</a> So, God handed him over to the king of Syria; they beat him, took many of his people as prisoners, and brought them to Damascus. He was also given into the hands of the king of Israel, who defeated him with heavy losses.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="2 Chronicles 28:6">6</a> Pekah, son of Remaliah, killed 120,000 brave men in Judah in one day because they abandoned the God of their ancestors.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="2 Chronicles 28:7">7</a> Zichri, a strong man from Ephraim, killed Maaseiah the king&#39;s son, Azrikam the house leader, and Elkanah who was close to the king.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="2 Chronicles 28:8">8</a> The Israelites captured 200,000 of their own people, including women and children, took a lot of their possessions, and brought them to Samaria.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="2 Chronicles 28:9">9</a> A prophet named Oded was there. He met the army going to Samaria and said, &quot;Look, God was angry with Judah, so he let you defeat them. But you killed them in such anger that it has reached heaven.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="2 Chronicles 28:10">10</a> You plan to make the people of Judah and Jerusalem your slaves. But aren&#39;t you also guilty of sins against God?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="2 Chronicles 28:11">11</a> Listen to me now and return the prisoners you have taken from your relatives, because God is very angry with you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="2 Chronicles 28:12">12</a> Some leaders from the Ephraim family, Azariah son of Johanan, Berechiah son of Meshillemoth, Jehizkiah son of Shallum, and Amasa son of Hadlai, stood up against the soldiers returning from battle.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="2 Chronicles 28:13">13</a> He told them, &quot;Do not bring the prisoners here because we have already sinned against God, and you plan to make us sin more and do wrong. Our wrongdoing is serious, and there is strong anger towards Israel.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="2 Chronicles 28:14">14</a> The soldiers let go of the prisoners and the loot in front of the leaders and all the people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="2 Chronicles 28:15">15</a> The men who were named stood up, took the prisoners, and used the loot to dress all the naked ones among them. They gave them clothes, shoes, food, and drink, and put oil on their wounds. They put all the weak ones on donkeys and took them to Jericho, the city of palm trees, to their own people, and then they went back to Samaria.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="2 Chronicles 28:16">16</a> At that time, King Ahaz asked the kings of Assyria for help.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="2 Chronicles 28:17">17</a> The Edomites attacked Judah again, defeated them, and took prisoners.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="2 Chronicles 28:18">18</a> The Philistines attacked and took over the towns in the lowlands and in the southern part of Judah. They captured Bethshemesh, Ajalon, Gederoth, Shocho and their surrounding villages, as well as Timnah, Gimzo and their villages, and then lived there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="2 Chronicles 28:19">19</a> God made Judah weak because of Ahaz king of Israel; Ahaz caused great shame to Judah and greatly disobeyed God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="2 Chronicles 28:20">20</a> The king of Assyria, Tilgath-pilneser, came to him and caused him trouble, but did not help him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="2 Chronicles 28:21">21</a> Ahaz took some things from God&#39;s house, the king&#39;s house, and the princes&#39; houses and gave them to the king of Assyria, but it did not help him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="2 Chronicles 28:22">22</a> During his hard times, he sinned even more against God: this was King Ahaz.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="2 Chronicles 28:23">23</a> He offered sacrifices to the gods of Damascus, who had defeated him. He thought, &quot;Since the gods of the Syrian kings aid them, I will sacrifice to them so they might help me.&quot; But those gods caused his downfall and that of all Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="2 Chronicles 28:24">24</a> Ahaz collected God&#39;s house items, broke them, closed God&#39;s house doors, and made altars in all parts of Jerusalem.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="2 Chronicles 28:25">25</a> In each city of Judah, he built high places to burn incense to other gods, making God, the God of his ancestors, angry.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="2 Chronicles 28:26">26</a> The other things he did and all his actions, from beginning to end, are recorded in the book of the kings of Judah and Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="2 Chronicles 28:27">27</a> Ahaz died and was buried in Jerusalem, but not in the royal tombs, and his son Hezekiah became king after him.</p>
