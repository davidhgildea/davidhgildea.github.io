<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="2 Chronicles 9:1">1</a> When the queen of Sheba heard about Solomon&#39;s fame, she went to test him with difficult questions in Jerusalem. She brought a large group, camels carrying spices, lots of gold, and valuable gems. When she arrived, she talked with Solomon about everything she was thinking.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="2 Chronicles 9:2">2</a> Solomon answered all her questions, and he hid nothing from her.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="2 Chronicles 9:3">3</a> When the queen of Sheba saw Solomon&#39;s wisdom and the house he built,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="2 Chronicles 9:4">4</a> The food on his table, the way his servants sat, how his officials served, and their clothes; also his cupbearers, and their clothes; and the steps he took to go up to God&#39;s house; all this left her breathless.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="2 Chronicles 9:5">5</a> She told the king, &quot;The stories I heard in my country about your achievements and your wisdom are true.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="2 Chronicles 9:6">6</a> I didn&#39;t believe what they said until I came and saw for myself. And truly, they didn&#39;t tell me even half of how wise you are; you are even greater than what I heard.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="2 Chronicles 9:7">7</a> Your men are happy, and your servants who always stand before you and listen to your wisdom are happy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="2 Chronicles 9:8">8</a> God blesses you because he was pleased to make you king on his throne, for God&#39;s sake: God loved Israel and wanted them to last forever, so he made you their king to bring them fairness and justice.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="2 Chronicles 9:9">9</a> She gave the king 120 talents of gold, lots of spices, and precious stones. No one had ever given such spices as the Queen of Sheba gave to King Solomon.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="2 Chronicles 9:10">10</a> Huram&#39;s workers and Solomon&#39;s workers, who brought gold from Ophir, also brought algum trees and precious stones.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="2 Chronicles 9:11">11</a> The king used the algum trees to make balconies for God&#39;s house and the royal palace, and he made harps and stringed instruments for musicians. No one had ever seen anything like them in Judah before.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="2 Chronicles 9:12">12</a> King Solomon gave the Queen of Sheba everything she wanted, beyond what she had brought to the king. Then she went back to her land with her servants.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="2 Chronicles 9:13">13</a> Every year, Solomon received 666 talents of gold.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="2 Chronicles 9:14">14</a> Merchants and traders brought things. Also, Arabian kings and regional leaders brought gold and silver to Solomon.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="2 Chronicles 9:15">15</a> King Solomon made two hundred large shields of hammered gold; each shield used six hundred pieces of gold.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="2 Chronicles 9:16">16</a> He made three hundred shields out of hammered gold, using three hundred gold coins for each shield. The king placed them in the Lebanon Forest House.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="2 Chronicles 9:17">17</a> The king made a big throne out of ivory and covered it with pure gold.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="2 Chronicles 9:18">18</a> The throne had six steps and a gold footrest attached to it, with armrests on each side of the seat and two lions next to the armrests.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="2 Chronicles 9:19">19</a> Twelve lions were on each side of the six steps; nothing like this was made in any other kingdom.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="2 Chronicles 9:20">20</a> All of King Solomon&#39;s cups were gold, and all items from the Lebanon forest house were pure gold: none were silver, because silver was not valued in Solomon&#39;s time.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="2 Chronicles 9:21">21</a> The king&#39;s ships sailed to Tarshish with Huram&#39;s servants. Every three years, the Tarshish ships returned with gold, silver, ivory, monkeys, and peacocks.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="2 Chronicles 9:22">22</a> King Solomon was richer and wiser than all other kings on earth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="2 Chronicles 9:23">23</a> All the kings of the world wanted to meet Solomon to hear the wisdom that God had given him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="2 Chronicles 9:24">24</a> They each brought gifts: silver and gold items, clothes, gear, spices, horses, and mules, regularly every year.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="2 Chronicles 9:25">25</a> Solomon had four thousand spaces for horses and chariots, and twelve thousand riders; he placed them in the chariot cities and with the king in Jerusalem.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="2 Chronicles 9:26">26</a> He ruled over all the kings from the river to the land of the Philistines, and up to Egypt&#39;s border.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="2 Chronicles 9:27">27</a> The king made silver as common in Jerusalem as stones, and he made cedar trees as plentiful as the sycamore trees in the low plains.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="2 Chronicles 9:28">28</a> They brought horses to Solomon from Egypt and from all countries.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="2 Chronicles 9:29">29</a> The other things Solomon did, from beginning to end, are written in Nathan the prophet&#39;s book, in Ahijah the Shilonite&#39;s prophecies, and in the visions that Iddo the seer had about Jeroboam son of Nebat, right?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="2 Chronicles 9:30">30</a> Solomon ruled in Jerusalem over all Israel for forty years.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="2 Chronicles 9:31">31</a> Solomon died and was buried in his father David&#39;s city. Then his son Rehoboam became king.</p>
