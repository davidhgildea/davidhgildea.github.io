<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="2 Chronicles 16:1">1</a> In the thirty-sixth year of King Asa&#39;s rule, King Baasha of Israel attacked Judah and built Ramah to stop anyone from leaving or entering King Asa&#39;s territory.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="2 Chronicles 16:2">2</a> Asa took silver and gold from God&#39;s house and the king&#39;s palace and sent it to King Benhadad of Syria, who lived in Damascus, with a message.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="2 Chronicles 16:3">3</a> We have an agreement, just like our fathers did. Look, I&#39;ve sent you silver and gold. Now, end your agreement with Baasha, the king of Israel, so he will leave me alone.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="2 Chronicles 16:4">4</a> Benhadad listened to King Asa and ordered his army leaders to attack Israel&#39;s cities; they captured Ijon, Dan, Abelmaim, and all the warehouse cities of Naphtali.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="2 Chronicles 16:5">5</a> When Baasha heard about it, he stopped building Ramah and his work stopped too.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="2 Chronicles 16:6">6</a> King Asa and all the people of Judah removed the stones and wood from Ramah, which Baasha had been using to build, and with them, they built Geba and Mizpah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="2 Chronicles 16:7">7</a> At that time, Hanani the seer went to Asa, the king of Judah, and told him, &quot;Because you depended on the king of Syria and not on God, the army of the king of Syria has escaped from you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="2 Chronicles 16:8">8</a> Didn&#39;t the Ethiopians and Lubims have a large army with many chariots and horsemen? But because you trusted in God, He gave them over to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="2 Chronicles 16:9">9</a> God&#39;s eyes move across the whole earth, to show his power to those who are fully committed to him. But you have acted foolishly, and from now on you will face wars.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="2 Chronicles 16:10">10</a> Asa became very angry with the prophet and locked him up because of his anger over this matter. At that time, Asa also treated some of the people harshly.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="2 Chronicles 16:11">11</a> Look, the deeds of Asa, from beginning to end, are recorded in the book of the kings of Judah and Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="2 Chronicles 16:12">12</a> In the thirty-ninth year of his rule, Asa became very sick in his feet, and his sickness got very severe. But even in his sickness, he did not ask God for help, only the doctors.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="2 Chronicles 16:13">13</a> Asa died after ruling for forty-one years and was buried with his ancestors.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="2 Chronicles 16:14">14</a> They buried him in his own tomb that he had built for himself in the city of David. They placed him on a bed filled with sweet smells and many spices made by the apothecaries. They had a very big fire for him.</p>
