<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="2 Chronicles 15:1">1</a> God&#39;s Spirit came to Azariah, Oded&#39;s son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="2 Chronicles 15:2">2</a> He went to meet Asa and said, &quot;Listen to me, Asa, and all of Judah and Benjamin: God is with you when you are with Him. If you look for Him, you will find Him; but if you leave Him, He will leave you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="2 Chronicles 15:3">3</a> Israel has been a long time without the real God, without a priest to teach, and without law.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="2 Chronicles 15:4">4</a> But when they were in trouble and turned to God, and looked for him, they found him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="2 Chronicles 15:5">5</a> During those times, people had no peace whether they left or arrived, as everyone living in the countries faced great troubles.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="2 Chronicles 15:6">6</a> One nation destroyed another, and one city destroyed another; for God troubled them with all kinds of problems.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="2 Chronicles 15:7">7</a> Stay strong, and don&#39;t let your hands get weak: your work will be rewarded.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="2 Chronicles 15:8">8</a> When Asa heard these words and Oded the prophet&#39;s message, he felt brave. He removed the terrible idols from all over Judah and Benjamin, and from the cities he had captured in the hills of Ephraim. He also fixed God&#39;s altar in front of God&#39;s temple entrance.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="2 Chronicles 15:9">9</a> He brought together all of Judah and Benjamin, and the foreigners from Ephraim, Manasseh, and Simeon, because many from Israel joined him when they saw that God was with him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="2 Chronicles 15:10">10</a> They came together in Jerusalem in the third month, during the fifteenth year of Asa&#39;s rule.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="2 Chronicles 15:11">11</a> At that time, they gave to God from the things they had taken seven hundred oxen and seven thousand sheep.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="2 Chronicles 15:12">12</a> They promised to search for God of their ancestors with all their heart and soul.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="2 Chronicles 15:13">13</a> Anyone who does not seek the God of Israel must be put to death, no matter who they are, young or old, man or woman.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="2 Chronicles 15:14">14</a> They promised to God loudly, with shouts, trumpets, and horns.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="2 Chronicles 15:15">15</a> All of Judah was happy about their promise; they made it sincerely and looked for God with all their desire. They found God, and God gave them peace on every side.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="2 Chronicles 15:16">16</a> Asa took away his grandmother Maachah&#39;s title as queen because she made an idol in a sacred garden. Asa chopped down the idol, crushed it, and burned it in the Kidron Valley.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="2 Chronicles 15:17">17</a> But the worship places on the hills were not removed from Israel; still, Asa was completely devoted to God all his life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="2 Chronicles 15:18">18</a> He brought into God&#39;s house the items his father and he had dedicated: silver, gold, and containers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="2 Chronicles 15:19">19</a> There was no more war until the thirty-fifth year of Asa&#39;s rule.</p>
