<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="2 Chronicles 17:1">1</a> Jehoshaphat, his son, became king after him and made himself strong against Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="2 Chronicles 17:2">2</a> He put troops in all the protected cities of Judah, and put guards in Judah&#39;s land and in the cities of Ephraim that his father Asa had captured.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="2 Chronicles 17:3">3</a> God was with Jehoshaphat because he followed the good ways of his ancestor David and did not worship Baalim.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="2 Chronicles 17:4">4</a> He looked for guidance from his father&#39;s God and lived by His rules, not following Israel&#39;s actions.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="2 Chronicles 17:5">5</a> God made his kingdom strong; everyone from Judah gave gifts to Jehoshaphat; and he became very rich and honored.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="2 Chronicles 17:6">6</a> His heart became proud following God&#39;s ways; he also removed the worship sites and sacred trees from Judah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="2 Chronicles 17:7">7</a> In the third year of his rule, he sent his leaders, Benhail, Obadiah, Zechariah, Nethaneel, and Michaiah, to teach in the towns of Judah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="2 Chronicles 17:8">8</a> He sent Levites with them, including Shemaiah, Nethaniah, Zebadiah, Asahel, Shemiramoth, Jonathan, Adonijah, Tobijah, Tobadonijah, and also Elishama and Jehoram, who were priests.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="2 Chronicles 17:9">9</a> They taught in Judah with God&#39;s law book and traveled to all Judah&#39;s cities to teach the people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="2 Chronicles 17:10">10</a> The fear of God spread to all the neighboring kingdoms around Judah, so they did not go to war against Jehoshaphat.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="2 Chronicles 17:11">11</a> Some Philistines gave Jehoshaphat gifts and silver, and the Arabians gave him 7,700 rams and 7,700 male goats.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="2 Chronicles 17:12">12</a> Jehoshaphat became very powerful; he built strongholds and storage cities in Judah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="2 Chronicles 17:13">13</a> He was very busy in the towns of Judah, and the soldiers, strong and brave, were in Jerusalem.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="2 Chronicles 17:14">14</a> Here are the numbers for each family group: From Judah, the leaders of thousands; Adnah was the top leader, and with him were 300,000 brave soldiers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="2 Chronicles 17:15">15</a> Next to him stood Jehohanan the leader, with 280,000 men.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="2 Chronicles 17:16">16</a> Next to him was Amasiah, son of Zichri, who volunteered to serve God; he was accompanied by two hundred thousand brave warriors.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="2 Chronicles 17:17">17</a> From the tribe of Benjamin, Eliada, a brave warrior, was there with 200,000 armed men carrying bows and shields.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="2 Chronicles 17:18">18</a> Next to him was Jehozabad, with him one hundred and eighty thousand men ready for battle.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="2 Chronicles 17:19">19</a> These served the king, along with those the king placed in the protected cities all over Judah.</p>
