<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="2 Chronicles 11:1">1</a> When Rehoboam arrived in Jerusalem, he assembled 180,000 skilled soldiers from the tribes of Judah and Benjamin to battle against Israel and take back his kingdom.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="2 Chronicles 11:2">2</a> God&#39;s message came to Shemaiah, God&#39;s man, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="2 Chronicles 11:3">3</a> Tell Rehoboam, Solomon&#39;s son and king of Judah, and all the people in Judah and Benjamin, this message:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="2 Chronicles 11:4">4</a> God says, &quot;Do not go up to fight against your relatives. Everyone go back home, because I made this happen.&quot; And they listened to what God said and stopped their advance against Jeroboam.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="2 Chronicles 11:5">5</a> Rehoboam lived in Jerusalem and built cities for protection in Judah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="2 Chronicles 11:6">6</a> He built Bethlehem, Etam, and Tekoa.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="2 Chronicles 11:7">7</a> Bethzur, Shoco, and Adullam.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="2 Chronicles 11:8">8</a> Gath, Mareshah, and Ziph,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="2 Chronicles 11:9">9</a> Adoraim, Lachish, and Azekah,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="2 Chronicles 11:10">10</a> Zorah, Aijalon, and Hebron, which are in Judah and Benjamin, are fortified cities.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="2 Chronicles 11:11">11</a> He strengthened the fortresses, placed leaders there, and stocked them with food, oil, and wine.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="2 Chronicles 11:12">12</a> In each city he placed shields and spears, making them very strong with Judah and Benjamin supporting him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="2 Chronicles 11:13">13</a> The priests and Levites from all over Israel came to join him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="2 Chronicles 11:14">14</a> The Levites left their lands and property, and went to Judah and Jerusalem because Jeroboam and his sons stopped them from serving as priests for God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="2 Chronicles 11:15">15</a> He appointed priests for the high places, for the demons, and for the statues of calves he made.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="2 Chronicles 11:16">16</a> After them, people from all Israel&#39;s tribes who decided to seek God went to Jerusalem to offer sacrifices to the God of their ancestors.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="2 Chronicles 11:17">17</a> They made the kingdom of Judah stronger and made Rehoboam, Solomon&#39;s son, strong for three years: for three years they followed the ways of David and Solomon.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="2 Chronicles 11:18">18</a> Rehoboam married Mahalath, Jerimoth&#39;s daughter and David&#39;s granddaughter, and Abihail, Eliab&#39;s daughter and Jesse&#39;s granddaughter.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="2 Chronicles 11:19">19</a> She had sons with him; Jeush, Shamariah, and Zaham.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="2 Chronicles 11:20">20</a> After her, he married Maachah, Absalom&#39;s daughter, who gave birth to Abijah, Attai, Ziza, and Shelomith for him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="2 Chronicles 11:21">21</a> Rehoboam loved Maachah, Absalom&#39;s daughter, more than all his other wives and concubines. He had eighteen wives and sixty concubines, fathered twenty-eight sons and sixty daughters.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="2 Chronicles 11:22">22</a> Rehoboam appointed Abijah, Maachah&#39;s son, as the leader over his brothers because he planned to make him king.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="2 Chronicles 11:23">23</a> He acted wisely, spreading his children across the lands of Judah and Benjamin to every protected city, providing them with plenty of food. And he wanted many wives.</p>
