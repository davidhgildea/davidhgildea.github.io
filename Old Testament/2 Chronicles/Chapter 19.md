<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="2 Chronicles 19:1">1</a> King Jehoshaphat of Judah went back to his home in Jerusalem safely.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="2 Chronicles 19:2">2</a> Jehu, son of Hanani, the seer, met him and said to King Jehoshaphat, &quot;Should you help the wicked and love those who hate God? For this, God&#39;s anger is on you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="2 Chronicles 19:3">3</a> Still, good things are in you because you removed the idol worship places from the land and made your heart ready to look for God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="2 Chronicles 19:4">4</a> Jehoshaphat lived in Jerusalem and he traveled among the people from Beersheba to Mount Ephraim, leading them back to God, the God of their ancestors.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="2 Chronicles 19:5">5</a> He placed judges in the country in all the protected cities of Judah, in each city.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="2 Chronicles 19:6">6</a> He told the judges, &quot;Be careful in what you do, because you are not judging for humans, but for God, who is with you when you make judgments.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="2 Chronicles 19:7">7</a> So now let the fear of God be with you; be careful and do it: because there is no wrong with our God, no favoritism, or acceptance of bribes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="2 Chronicles 19:8">8</a> In Jerusalem, Jehoshaphat appointed Levites, priests, and Israelite leaders to judge for God and settle disputes when they came back to Jerusalem.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="2 Chronicles 19:9">9</a> He instructed them, &quot;Do this respecting God, faithfully and with a true heart.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="2 Chronicles 19:10">10</a> Whenever your fellow people from the cities come to you with a problem, whether it&#39;s about a crime, a law, or rules and decisions, you must warn them not to disobey God, so that they and you will not face God&#39;s anger. Do this, and you will not do wrong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="2 Chronicles 19:11">11</a> Look, Amariah the main priest is in charge of all God&#39;s issues; and Zebadiah, Ishmael&#39;s son, is the leader for all the king&#39;s business: and the Levites will work for you. Act bravely, and God will be with the good.</p>
