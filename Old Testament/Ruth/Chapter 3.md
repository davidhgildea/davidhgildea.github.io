<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Ruth 3:1">1</a> Then Naomi, her mother-in-law, said to her, &quot;My daughter, shouldn&#39;t I find a peaceful place for you, so you can be happy?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Ruth 3:2">2</a> Isn&#39;t Boaz, who is related to us, the one whose young women you were with? Look, he is separating the barley on the threshing floor tonight.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Ruth 3:3">3</a> Clean yourself, then put on perfume and your clothes, and go to the threshing floor. But don&#39;t let the man see you until he has finished eating and drinking.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Ruth 3:4">4</a> When he lies down, notice where he is, then go, uncover his feet, lie down, and he will explain what you should do.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Ruth 3:5">5</a> She told her, &quot;I will do everything you tell me to do.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Ruth 3:6">6</a> She went to the threshing floor and did everything her mother-in-law told her to.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Ruth 3:7">7</a> After Boaz finished eating and drinking, feeling happy, he went to sleep by the pile of grain. Then she quietly approached, uncovered his feet, and lay down.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Ruth 3:8">8</a> At midnight, the man became scared and turned around, and he saw a woman lying at his feet.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Ruth 3:9">9</a> He asked, &quot;Who are you?&quot; She replied, &quot;I am Ruth, your servant. Please cover me with your cloak, because you are a close relative.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Ruth 3:10">10</a> He said, &quot;You are blessed by God, my daughter, because you have shown more kindness now than before. You did not go after young men, whether they were poor or rich.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Ruth 3:11">11</a> Now, my daughter, do not be afraid; I will do all that you ask, because everyone in my town knows you are a good woman.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Ruth 3:12">12</a> It is true that I am one of your relatives, but there is another relative closer than me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Ruth 3:13">13</a> Stay tonight, and in the morning, if he agrees to help you as a relative should, good; let him help. But if he does not want to help as a relative, then I will help you, as surely as God lives. Rest until morning.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Ruth 3:14">14</a> She stayed at his feet until morning, and she got up before anyone could recognize each other. And he said, &quot;Don&#39;t let anyone know that a woman was here on the threshing floor.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Ruth 3:15">15</a> He also said, &quot;Bring the veil you&#39;re wearing and hold it out.&quot; So when she did, he poured six scoops of barley into it and placed it on her back, then she went back to the city.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Ruth 3:16">16</a> When she went to her mother-in-law, she asked, &quot;Who are you, my daughter?&quot; And she explained everything the man had done to her.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Ruth 3:17">17</a> She said, &quot;He gave me these six measures of barley and told me, &#39;Don&#39;t go to your mother-in-law empty-handed.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Ruth 3:18">18</a> She said, &quot;Stay here, my daughter, until you find out what happens. The man won&#39;t rest until he sorts it out today.&quot;</p>
