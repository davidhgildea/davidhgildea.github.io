<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Ruth 2:1">1</a> Naomi had a relative from her husband&#39;s family who was very wealthy, and his name was Boaz.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Ruth 2:2">2</a> Ruth from Moab said to Naomi, &quot;Let me go to the fields to pick up leftover grain from someone who will show me kindness.&quot; Naomi replied, &quot;Go ahead, my daughter.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Ruth 2:3">3</a> She went out, came, and picked up leftover grain in the field behind the harvesters. By chance, she ended up in a part of the field owned by Boaz, who was related to Elimelech.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Ruth 2:4">4</a> Boaz arrived from Bethlehem and said to the harvesters, &quot;God be with you.&quot; They replied, &quot;God bless you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Ruth 2:5">5</a> Then Boaz asked his servant in charge of the harvesters, &quot;Who is this young woman?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Ruth 2:6">6</a> The worker in charge of the harvesters replied, &quot;She is the young woman from Moab who returned with Naomi from the land of Moab.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Ruth 2:7">7</a> She said, &quot;Please let me pick up leftover grain behind the harvesters among the bundles.&quot; So she went, and has been working from morning until now, except for a short rest in the shelter.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Ruth 2:8">8</a> Boaz said to Ruth, &quot;Didn&#39;t you hear, my daughter? Don&#39;t go to pick grain in another field, or leave this place, but stay close to my women workers.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Ruth 2:9">9</a> Watch the field where they are harvesting, and follow them. Haven&#39;t I told the men not to bother you? When you&#39;re thirsty, go to the jars and drink what the men have filled.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Ruth 2:10">10</a> Then she knelt down, bowed to the ground, and said to him, &quot;Why have I found favor in your eyes, that you notice me, even though I am a foreigner?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Ruth 2:11">11</a> Boaz replied to her, &quot;I&#39;ve been told about everything you did for your mother-in-law after your husband died, and how you left your parents and your birthplace to come to a people you didn&#39;t know before.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Ruth 2:12">12</a> God repay your work, and may you get a full reward from the God of Israel, under whose protection you have come to trust.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Ruth 2:13">13</a> Then she said, &quot;Please look on me with kindness, sir, because you have comforted me and spoken kindly to me, your servant, even though I am not like one of your maidservants.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Ruth 2:14">14</a> Boaz said to her, &quot;Come here at mealtime, eat some bread, and dip your piece in the vinegar.&quot; She sat next to the harvesters, and he handed her some roasted grain. She ate, was satisfied, and had some left over.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Ruth 2:15">15</a> When she got up to collect grain, Boaz told his workers, &quot;Let her gather grain even among the bundles, and do not bother her.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Ruth 2:16">16</a> Drop some extra grain for her on purpose and leave it so she can pick it up, and do not scold her.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Ruth 2:17">17</a> She picked grain in the field until evening, then she threshed what she had collected, and it was about an ephah of barley.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Ruth 2:18">18</a> She picked it up, went to the city, and her mother-in-law saw what she had gathered. She then brought out and gave to her the leftovers she had kept after eating enough.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Ruth 2:19">19</a> Her mother-in-law asked her, &quot;Where did you gather grain today? Where did you work? May God bless the one who noticed you.&quot; Then she told her mother-in-law who she had worked with and said, &quot;The man I worked with today is named Boaz.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Ruth 2:20">20</a> Naomi said to her daughter-in-law, &quot;May God bless him for his kindness to both the living and the dead.&quot; Naomi also told her, &quot;That man is one of our close relatives.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Ruth 2:21">21</a> Ruth, from Moab, said, &quot;He also told me to stay close to his young men until they finish all his harvest.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Ruth 2:22">22</a> Naomi said to Ruth, her daughter-in-law, &quot;It&#39;s good, my daughter, for you to go out with his young women, so that you are not met in any other field.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Ruth 2:23">23</a> She stayed close to Boaz&#39;s servant girls to pick grain until the barley and wheat harvests were finished; and she lived with her mother-in-law.</p>
