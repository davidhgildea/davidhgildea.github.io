<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Ezra 7:1">1</a> After these events, during King Artaxerxes&#39; rule of Persia, Ezra, who was Seraiah&#39;s son, Azariah&#39;s grandson, and Hilkiah&#39;s great-grandson,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Ezra 7:2">2</a> The son of Shallum was Zadok&#39;s son, and Zadok was Ahitub&#39;s son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Ezra 7:3">3</a> He was Amariah&#39;s son, Azariah&#39;s grandson, and Meraioth&#39;s great-grandson.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Ezra 7:4">4</a> Zerahiah&#39;s son, Uzzi&#39;s son, Bukki&#39;s son,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Ezra 7:5">5</a> Abishua&#39;s son, Phinehas&#39;s son, Eleazar&#39;s son, Aaron the high priest&#39;s son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Ezra 7:6">6</a> Ezra left Babylon; he was a skilled writer in Moses&#39; law, which God of Israel had given. The king gave him everything he asked for because God&#39;s favor was with him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Ezra 7:7">7</a> Some Israelites, priests, Levites, singers, gatekeepers, and Nethinim went up to Jerusalem in King Artaxerxes&#39; seventh year.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Ezra 7:8">8</a> He arrived in Jerusalem in the fifth month of the seventh year of the king&#39;s rule.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Ezra 7:9">9</a> He started to leave Babylon on the first day of the first month, and he arrived in Jerusalem on the first day of the fifth month because God was helping him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Ezra 7:10">10</a> Ezra had made up his mind to study God&#39;s law, follow it, and teach God&#39;s rules and decisions in Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Ezra 7:11">11</a> This is the letter that King Artaxerxes gave to Ezra the priest and scribe, who writes down God&#39;s commands and laws for Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Ezra 7:12">12</a> King Artaxerxes to Ezra the priest, a writer skilled in the law of God in heaven, wishes perfect peace.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Ezra 7:13">13</a> I issue an order that all Israelites, priests, and Levites in my kingdom who want to go to Jerusalem may go with you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Ezra 7:14">14</a> Since the king and his seven advisors sent you to find out about Judah and Jerusalem according to your God&#39;s law that you have with you;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Ezra 7:15">15</a> To bring the silver and gold, which the king and his advisors have willingly given to the God of Israel, who lives in Jerusalem.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Ezra 7:16">16</a> Take all the silver and gold you can find in the whole Babylon area, along with the voluntary gifts from the people and priests given freely for their God&#39;s temple in Jerusalem.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Ezra 7:17">17</a> Use this money to quickly buy bulls, rams, lambs, their grain offerings, and their drink offerings, and sacrifice them on the altar of God&#39;s house in Jerusalem.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Ezra 7:18">18</a> Do whatever you and your brothers think is best with the remaining silver and gold, according to God&#39;s will.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Ezra 7:19">19</a> Give the tools provided for the work of your God&#39;s house to God in Jerusalem.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Ezra 7:20">20</a> Whatever else you need for God&#39;s house, give it from the king&#39;s treasury.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Ezra 7:21">21</a> I, King Artaxerxes, order all the treasurers across the river to quickly do whatever Ezra the priest, who knows God&#39;s law, asks of you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Ezra 7:22">22</a> To a hundred talents of silver, a hundred measures of wheat, a hundred baths of wine, a hundred baths of oil, and as much salt as needed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Ezra 7:23">23</a> Do whatever the God of heaven has ordered, carefully and for the good of the God of heaven&#39;s temple. Why should anger come upon the king&#39;s kingdom and his children?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Ezra 7:24">24</a> We also tell you that no taxes, payments, or fees should be put on the priests, Levites, singers, gatekeepers, temple servants, or workers of God&#39;s house.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Ezra 7:25">25</a> Ezra, use the wisdom of God you have to appoint officials and judges who can rule over the people across the river, all who know God&#39;s laws; and teach the laws to those who don&#39;t know them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Ezra 7:26">26</a> Anyone who does not follow God&#39;s law or the king&#39;s law should be punished quickly, whether by death, exile, taking their possessions, or putting them in jail.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Ezra 7:27">27</a> May God, the God of our ancestors, be praised for inspiring the king to make the temple in Jerusalem beautiful.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Ezra 7:28">28</a> God has shown me mercy in front of the king, his advisors, and all the powerful nobles. I felt strong because God was with me, and I brought together leaders from Israel to go with me.</p>
