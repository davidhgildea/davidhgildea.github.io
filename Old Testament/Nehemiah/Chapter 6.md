<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Nehemiah 6:1">1</a> When Sanballat, Tobiah, Geshem the Arabian, and our other enemies heard that I had built the wall with no gaps left (though I had not yet installed the doors in the gates at that time),</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Nehemiah 6:2">2</a> Sanballat and Geshem sent me a message, saying, &quot;Come, let&#39;s meet in one of the villages on the plain of Ono.&quot; But they planned to hurt me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Nehemiah 6:3">3</a> I sent messengers to them, saying, &quot;I&#39;m doing important work and can&#39;t stop to come down. Why should the work stop while I leave it to come to you?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Nehemiah 6:4">4</a> They sent me the same message four times; and I replied in the same way each time.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Nehemiah 6:5">5</a> Sanballat sent his servant to me for the fifth time with an open letter in his hand.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Nehemiah 6:6">6</a> The message said, &quot;The nations are hearing, and Gashmu also says, you and the Jews plan to revolt. That&#39;s why you&#39;re rebuilding the wall, to become their king, they say.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Nehemiah 6:7">7</a> You have also chosen prophets to speak about you in Jerusalem, saying, &quot;There is a king in Judah.&quot; And now these words will be reported to the king. So come, let us talk together.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Nehemiah 6:8">8</a> Then I sent a message to him, saying, &quot;Nothing like what you are saying is happening; you are making it up in your mind.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Nehemiah 6:9">9</a> They all scared us by saying, &quot;Their hands will be too weak to do the work, so it won&#39;t be finished.&quot; Now, God, give me strong hands.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Nehemiah 6:10">10</a> Later, I went to the home of Shemaiah, Delaiah’s son, Mehetabeel’s grandson, who was confined inside. He suggested, &quot;Let&#39;s meet at God&#39;s house, inside the temple, and close the temple doors because they are coming to kill you; indeed, at night they will come to kill you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Nehemiah 6:11">11</a> I thought, should someone like me run away? And who like me would go into the temple to save his life? I won&#39;t go in.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Nehemiah 6:12">12</a> I realized that God had not sent him; he made this prophecy against me because Tobiah and Sanballat paid him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Nehemiah 6:13">13</a> He was paid to make me scared, so I would sin and they could discredit me with bad things to say.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Nehemiah 6:14">14</a> God, remember Tobiah and Sanballat for what they have done, and also the prophetess Noadiah and the other prophets who tried to scare me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Nehemiah 6:15">15</a> The wall was completed on the twenty-fifth day of the month Elul, in fifty-two days.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Nehemiah 6:16">16</a> When all our enemies and the nations around us heard about this, they felt very discouraged because they realized that our God had done this work.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Nehemiah 6:17">17</a> During that time, the leaders of Judah sent many letters to Tobiah, and Tobiah&#39;s letters came to them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Nehemiah 6:18">18</a> Many people in Judah were loyal to him because he was married to Shecaniah&#39;s daughter, Arah&#39;s granddaughter, and his son Johanan had married Meshullam&#39;s daughter, Berechiah&#39;s granddaughter.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Nehemiah 6:19">19</a> They also told me about his good works and shared my words with him. Tobiah sent letters to scare me.</p>
