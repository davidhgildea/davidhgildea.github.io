<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Nehemiah 12:1">1</a> Now, these are the priests and the Levites who went up with Zerubbabel, son of Shealtiel, and Jeshua: Seraiah, Jeremiah, Ezra.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Nehemiah 12:2">2</a> Amariah, Malluch, Hattush.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Nehemiah 12:3">3</a> Shechaniah, Rehum, Meremoth,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Nehemiah 12:4">4</a> Iddo, Ginnetho, Abijah,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Nehemiah 12:5">5</a> Miamin, Maadiah, Bilgah,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Nehemiah 12:6">6</a> Shemaiah, Joiarib, and Jedaiah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Nehemiah 12:7">7</a> Sallu, Amok, Hilkiah, Jedaiah. These were the main priests and their relatives&#39; leaders when Jeshua was alive.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Nehemiah 12:8">8</a> Additionally, the Levites: Jeshua, Binnui, Kadmiel, Sherebiah, Judah, and Mattaniah, who was in charge of giving thanks, he and his brothers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Nehemiah 12:9">9</a> Bakbukiah and Unni, their brothers, stood across from them on guard.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Nehemiah 12:10">10</a> Jeshua had a son named Joiakim, Joiakim had a son named Eliashib, and Eliashib had a son named Joiada.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Nehemiah 12:11">11</a> Joiada became the father of Jonathan, and Jonathan became the father of Jaddua.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Nehemiah 12:12">12</a> During Joiakim&#39;s time, there were leading priests from different families: Meraiah from Seraiah&#39;s family and Hananiah from Jeremiah&#39;s family.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Nehemiah 12:13">13</a> From Ezra came Meshullam; from Amariah came Jehohanan.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Nehemiah 12:14">14</a> From Melicu came Jonathan; from Shebaniah came Joseph.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Nehemiah 12:15">15</a> From Harim&#39;s family, Adna; from Meraioth&#39;s family, Helkai;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Nehemiah 12:16">16</a> From Iddo came Zechariah; from Ginnethon came Meshullam.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Nehemiah 12:17">17</a> Abijah had Zichri; Miniamin and Moadiah had Piltai.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Nehemiah 12:18">18</a> From Bilgah, Shammua; from Shemaiah, Jonathan;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Nehemiah 12:19">19</a> From Joiarib&#39;s family, Mattenai; from Jedaiah&#39;s family, Uzzi.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Nehemiah 12:20">20</a> From Sallai came Kallai; from Amok came Eber.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Nehemiah 12:21">21</a> From Hilkiah came Hashabiah; from Jedaiah came Nethaneel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Nehemiah 12:22">22</a> During the time of Eliashib, Joiada, Johanan, and Jaddua, the Levites and the heads of the families were listed, as well as the priests, up to the rule of Darius the Persian.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Nehemiah 12:23">23</a> The leaders among Levi&#39;s descendants were recorded in the chronicles up to the time of Johanan, son of Eliashib.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Nehemiah 12:24">24</a> The leaders of the Levites were Hashabiah, Sherebiah, and Jeshua son of Kadmiel. With their brothers opposite them, they were to praise and give thanks as David, a man of God, had ordered, one group facing another.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Nehemiah 12:25">25</a> Mattaniah, Bakbukiah, Obadiah, Meshullam, Talmon, and Akkub were gatekeepers guarding the entrances to the gates.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Nehemiah 12:26">26</a> These events happened when Joiakim, son of Jeshua and grandson of Jozadak, was alive, during the time Nehemiah was governor and Ezra was priest and writer.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Nehemiah 12:27">27</a> At the opening of Jerusalem&#39;s wall, they invited the Levites from everywhere to celebrate with joy, using thank yous, songs, cymbals, stringed instruments, and harps.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Nehemiah 12:28">28</a> The singers&#39; children came together from the area around Jerusalem and from the Netophathi villages.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Nehemiah 12:29">29</a> From Gilgal, as well as from Geba and Azmaveth fields, for the singers had built villages around Jerusalem.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Nehemiah 12:30">30</a> The priests and the Levites made themselves, the people, the gates, and the wall clean.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Nehemiah 12:31">31</a> Then I led the leaders of Judah up onto the wall and organized two large groups of thankful people. One group walked on the right side of the wall towards the Dung Gate.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Nehemiah 12:32">32</a> Hoshaiah and half of Judah&#39;s leaders followed them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Nehemiah 12:33">33</a> Azariah, Ezra, and Meshullam,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Nehemiah 12:34">34</a> Judah, Benjamin, Shemaiah, and Jeremiah,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Nehemiah 12:35">35</a> Some priests&#39; sons had trumpets, including Zechariah, who was Jonathan&#39;s son, Shemaiah&#39;s grandson, Mattaniah&#39;s great-grandson, Michaiah&#39;s great-great-grandson, Zaccur&#39;s great-great-great-grandson, and Asaph&#39;s great-great-great-great-grandson.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Nehemiah 12:36">36</a> His brothers Shemaiah, Azarael, Milalai, Gilalai, Maai, Nethaneel, and Judah, Hanani, played David&#39;s musical instruments, with God’s man Ezra leading them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Nehemiah 12:37">37</a> At the Fountain Gate in front of them, they climbed the stairs to the City of David, went along the wall above David&#39;s house, and reached the Water Gate on the east side.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Nehemiah 12:38">38</a> The other group that was thanking God went opposite them, with me following, and half of the people were along the wall from past the furnace tower to the wide wall.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="Nehemiah 12:39">39</a> Above the Ephraim gate, the old gate, the fish gate, the Hananeel tower, and the Meah tower, as far as the sheep gate, they stopped at the prison gate.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="Nehemiah 12:40">40</a> Two groups of thankful people, along with me and half of the leaders, stood in God&#39;s house.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="Nehemiah 12:41">41</a> The priests Eliakim, Maaseiah, Miniamin, Michaiah, Elioenai, Zechariah, and Hananiah had trumpets.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="Nehemiah 12:42">42</a> Maaseiah, Shemaiah, Eleazar, Uzzi, Jehohanan, Malchijah, Elam, and Ezer were there. The singers sang loudly with their leader Jezrahiah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v43"><a class="verse-num" href="#v43" aria-label="Nehemiah 12:43">43</a> On that day, they gave many sacrifices and were happy because God had made them very joyful. The women and children were happy too. The happiness of Jerusalem was heard from far away.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v44"><a class="verse-num" href="#v44" aria-label="Nehemiah 12:44">44</a> At that time, people were chosen to take care of the storage rooms for the treasures, the offerings, the first crops, and the tithes. They collected from the city fields what was legally given to the priests and Levites: because the people of Judah were happy with the priests and Levites who served.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v45"><a class="verse-num" href="#v45" aria-label="Nehemiah 12:45">45</a> The singers and gatekeepers followed God&#39;s orders and the rules for purification, as directed by David and his son Solomon.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v46"><a class="verse-num" href="#v46" aria-label="Nehemiah 12:46">46</a> In the old times of David and Asaph, there were lead singers and songs of praise and thanks to God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v47"><a class="verse-num" href="#v47" aria-label="Nehemiah 12:47">47</a> During the time of Zerubbabel and Nehemiah, all of Israel provided daily shares to the singers and gatekeepers. They set apart sacred gifts for the Levites, and the Levites dedicated them to Aaron&#39;s descendants.</p>
