<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Nehemiah 2:1">1</a> In the month of Nisan, during King Artaxerxes&#39;s twentieth year, there was wine in front of him. I picked up the wine and gave it to the king. I had never been sad in front of him before.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Nehemiah 2:2">2</a> So the king asked me, &quot;Why do you look sad when you&#39;re not ill? This must be deep sadness.&quot; I felt very afraid.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Nehemiah 2:3">3</a> I told the king, &quot;May the king live always. Why shouldn&#39;t I look sad when the city where my ancestors are buried is ruined, and its gates have been destroyed by fire?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Nehemiah 2:4">4</a> Then the king asked me, &quot;What do you request?&quot; So I prayed to God in heaven.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Nehemiah 2:5">5</a> I told the king, &quot;If it makes the king happy, and if I have your approval, please let me go to Judah, to the city where my ancestors are buried, so I can rebuild it.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Nehemiah 2:6">6</a> The king asked me, with the queen sitting beside him, &quot;How long will your trip take? And when will you come back?&quot; The king was happy to let me go, and I told him when I would return.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Nehemiah 2:7">7</a> Also, I said to the king, &quot;If it&#39;s okay with the king, please give me letters for the governors across the river so they can help me travel to Judah.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Nehemiah 2:8">8</a> I also wrote a letter to Asaph, who takes care of the king&#39;s forest, asking for wood to build beams for the palace gates, the city wall, and the house I will live in. The king gave me what I asked for because God was kind to me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Nehemiah 2:9">9</a> I went to the leaders across the river and gave them the king&#39;s letters. The king had sent army officers and cavalry with me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Nehemiah 2:10">10</a> When Sanballat from Horon and Tobiah the Ammonite official heard about it, they were very upset that someone had come to help the people of Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Nehemiah 2:11">11</a> I came to Jerusalem and stayed there for three days.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Nehemiah 2:12">12</a> I got up at night with a few men; I didn&#39;t tell anyone what God had inspired me to do in Jerusalem, and I didn&#39;t bring any animals except the one I was riding.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Nehemiah 2:13">13</a> I went out at night through the valley gate, past the dragon spring and the trash gate, and I looked at Jerusalem&#39;s walls, which were torn down, and its gates, which were burned by fire.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Nehemiah 2:14">14</a> Then I went to the Fountain Gate and to the king&#39;s pool, but there was no room for the animal I was riding to get through.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Nehemiah 2:15">15</a> I went up at night by the creek, looked at the wall, turned around, entered through the valley gate, and went back.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Nehemiah 2:16">16</a> The leaders did not know where I went or what I did; I had not yet told anything to the Jews, the priests, the nobles, the leaders, or the others working.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Nehemiah 2:17">17</a> I told them, &quot;You see the trouble we&#39;re in. Jerusalem is ruined, its gates burned by fire. Come, let&#39;s rebuild Jerusalem&#39;s wall, so we won&#39;t be disgraced anymore.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Nehemiah 2:18">18</a> I told them how God had helped me and what the king had said to me. They replied, &quot;Let&#39;s start building.&quot; So they prepared to do this good work.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Nehemiah 2:19">19</a> When Sanballat the Horonite, Tobiah the servant, the Ammonite, and Geshem the Arabian heard this, they laughed at us, looked down on us, and said, &quot;What are you doing? Are you rebelling against the king?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Nehemiah 2:20">20</a> I replied to them, &quot;The God of heaven will help us succeed; so we, His servants, will start building. But you have no share, no claim, or any history in Jerusalem.&quot;</p>
