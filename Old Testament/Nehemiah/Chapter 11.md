<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Nehemiah 11:1">1</a> The leaders lived in Jerusalem. The other people drew lots so that one out of every ten would live in Jerusalem, the holy city, and the other nine in different cities.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Nehemiah 11:2">2</a> The people praised everyone who willingly chose to live in Jerusalem.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Nehemiah 11:3">3</a> These are the leaders of the area who lived in Jerusalem: but in the Judah towns, everyone lived on their own land, including Israelites, priests, Levites, temple workers, and descendants of Solomon&#39;s servants.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Nehemiah 11:4">4</a> In Jerusalem lived some of Judah&#39;s and Benjamin&#39;s people. Of Judah&#39;s descendants, there was Athaiah, Uzziah&#39;s son, Zechariah&#39;s grandson, Amariah&#39;s great-grandson, Shephatiah&#39;s great-great-grandson, from Perez&#39;s family.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Nehemiah 11:5">5</a> Maaseiah, son of Baruch, grandson of Colhozeh, great-grandson of Hazaiah, great-great-grandson of Adaiah, great-great-great-grandson of Joiarib, and great-great-great-great-grandson of Zechariah, from the family of Shiloni.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Nehemiah 11:6">6</a> All of Perez&#39;s sons living in Jerusalem were 468 brave men.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Nehemiah 11:7">7</a> These are Benjamin&#39;s sons: Sallu, Meshullam&#39;s son, Joed&#39;s grandson, Pedaiah&#39;s great-grandson, Kolaiah&#39;s great-great-grandson, Maaseiah&#39;s great-great-great-grandson, Ithiel&#39;s great-great-great-great-grandson, and Jesaiah&#39;s great-great-great-great-great-grandson.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Nehemiah 11:8">8</a> After him Gabbai and Sallai, nine hundred twenty-eight.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Nehemiah 11:9">9</a> Joel, Zichri&#39;s son, was in charge, and Judah, Senuah&#39;s son, was second in command of the city.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Nehemiah 11:10">10</a> From the priests: Jedaiah, Joiarib&#39;s son, and Jachin.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Nehemiah 11:11">11</a> Seraiah, son of Hilkiah, grandson of Meshullam, great-grandson of Zadok, descendant of Meraioth and Ahitub, was in charge of God&#39;s house.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Nehemiah 11:12">12</a> Eight hundred and twenty-two of their brothers worked on the house. One was Adaiah, whose father was Jeroham, whose father was Pelaliah, whose father was Amzi, whose father was Zechariah, whose father was Pashur, whose father was Malchiah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Nehemiah 11:13">13</a> His brothers, leaders of the families, were 242; and Amashai, son of Azareel, grandson of Ahasai, great-grandson of Meshillemoth, and great-great-grandson of Immer.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Nehemiah 11:14">14</a> Their brothers, strong and brave, 128 in number; and Zabdiel, the son of a great man, was in charge.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Nehemiah 11:15">15</a> From the Levites: Shemaiah, son of Hashub, grandson of Azrikam, great-grandson of Hashabiah, from the family of Bunni.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Nehemiah 11:16">16</a> Shabbethai and Jozabad, leaders of the Levites, were in charge of the external work of God&#39;s house.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Nehemiah 11:17">17</a> Mattaniah, Micha&#39;s son, Zabdi&#39;s grandson, and Asaph&#39;s great-grandson, led the prayers of thanks. Bakbukiah was second to him, and Abda, Shammua&#39;s son, Galal&#39;s grandson, and Jeduthun&#39;s great-grandson, was with his brothers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Nehemiah 11:18">18</a> There were 284 Levites in the holy city.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Nehemiah 11:19">19</a> The gatekeepers, Akkub, Talmon, and their brothers who guarded the gates, were 172 in total.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Nehemiah 11:20">20</a> The rest of Israel, including the priests and the Levites, lived in the towns of Judah, each in their own property.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Nehemiah 11:21">21</a> The Nethinims lived in Ophel, and Ziha and Gispa were in charge of them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Nehemiah 11:22">22</a> The person in charge of the Levites in Jerusalem was Uzzi, Bani&#39;s son, Hashabiah&#39;s grandson, Mattaniah&#39;s great-grandson, and Micha&#39;s great-great-grandson. The descendants of Asaph, who were singers, managed the work of God&#39;s house.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Nehemiah 11:23">23</a> The king ordered that the singers receive a set amount every day.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Nehemiah 11:24">24</a> Pethahiah, son of Meshezabeel from the descendants of Zerah of Judah, assisted the king in all issues related to the people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Nehemiah 11:25">25</a> In the villages with their lands, some people from Judah lived in Kirjatharba, its villages, Dibon, its villages, and Jekabzeel, its villages.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Nehemiah 11:26">26</a> In Jeshua, in Moladah, and in Bethphilet,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Nehemiah 11:27">27</a> At Hazarshual, at Beersheba, and in their villages.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Nehemiah 11:28">28</a> In Ziklag, in Mekonah, and in their villages,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Nehemiah 11:29">29</a> The places were Enrimmon, Zareah, and Jarmuth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Nehemiah 11:30">30</a> Zanoah, Adullam, and their villages, Lachish and its fields, Azekah and its villages. People lived from Beersheba to the Hinnom Valley.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Nehemiah 11:31">31</a> The families of Benjamin from Geba lived in Michmash, Aija, Bethel, and their nearby small towns.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Nehemiah 11:32">32</a> In Anathoth, Nob, Ananiah,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Nehemiah 11:33">33</a> Hazor, Ramah, Gittaim,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Nehemiah 11:34">34</a> Hadid, Zeboim, Neballat,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Nehemiah 11:35">35</a> Lord, Ono, valley of workers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Nehemiah 11:36">36</a> The Levites had groups in both Judah and Benjamin.</p>
