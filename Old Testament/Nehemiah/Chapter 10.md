<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Nehemiah 10:1">1</a> The ones who made the agreement were Nehemiah, the governor, son of Hachaliah, and Zidkijah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Nehemiah 10:2">2</a> Seraiah, Azariah, Jeremiah,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Nehemiah 10:3">3</a> Pashur, Amariah, Malchijah,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Nehemiah 10:4">4</a> Hattush, Shebaniah, Malluch.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Nehemiah 10:5">5</a> Harim, Meremoth, Obadiah,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Nehemiah 10:6">6</a> Daniel, Ginnethon, Baruch,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Nehemiah 10:7">7</a> Meshullam, Abijah, Mijamin.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Nehemiah 10:8">8</a> Maaziah, Bilgai, Shemaiah: these were the religious leaders.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Nehemiah 10:9">9</a> The Levites included Jeshua, son of Azaniah, Binnui from the Henadad family, and Kadmiel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Nehemiah 10:10">10</a> Their brothers, Shebaniah, Hodijah, Kelita, Pelaiah, Hanan,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Nehemiah 10:11">11</a> Michael, Rehob, Hashabiah,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Nehemiah 10:12">12</a> Zaccur, Sherebiah, Shebaniah,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Nehemiah 10:13">13</a> Hodijah, Bani, Beninu.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Nehemiah 10:14">14</a> The leaders of the people: Parosh, Pahathmoab, Elam, Zatthu, Bani.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Nehemiah 10:15">15</a> Bunni, Azgad, Bebai.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Nehemiah 10:16">16</a> Adonijah, Bigvai, Adin.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Nehemiah 10:17">17</a> After Hezekiah, Azur,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Nehemiah 10:18">18</a> Hodijah, Hashum, Bezai,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Nehemiah 10:19">19</a> Hariph, Anathoth, Nebai,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Nehemiah 10:20">20</a> Magpiash, Meshullam, Hezir.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Nehemiah 10:21">21</a> Meshezabeel, Zadok, Jaddua.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Nehemiah 10:22">22</a> Pelatiah, Hanan, Anaiah,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Nehemiah 10:23">23</a> Hoshea, Hananiah, Hashub,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Nehemiah 10:24">24</a> This part of the text appears to be a list of names and does not form a complete sentence. Without additional context, a translation is not possible. If this is meant to be a simplification of a biblical passage, please provide the full verse for an accurate translation.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Nehemiah 10:25">25</a> Rehum, Hashabnah, Maaseiah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Nehemiah 10:26">26</a> Ahijah, Hanan, Anan.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Nehemiah 10:27">27</a> Malluch, Harim, Baanah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Nehemiah 10:28">28</a> The other people, the priests, the Levites, the gatekeepers, the singers, the temple servants, and all who had chosen to follow God&#39;s laws instead of the local customs, including their wives, their sons, and their daughters who were able to understand;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Nehemiah 10:29">29</a> They stayed close to their kin and leaders, and took a solemn promise, committing to follow God&#39;s law that Moses, God&#39;s servant, gave them, and to follow and obey all of God&#39;s commands, rules, and laws.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Nehemiah 10:30">30</a> We will not give our daughters to the people of the land, nor take their daughters for our sons.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Nehemiah 10:31">31</a> If people in the land bring goods or food to sell on the Sabbath, we will not buy from them on the Sabbath or a holy day; and we will not farm in the seventh year or demand payment of debts.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Nehemiah 10:32">32</a> We agreed to give a third of a shekel each year for our God&#39;s house.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Nehemiah 10:33">33</a> For the bread put on display, for the regular grain offering, for the regular burnt offering, on the Sabbaths, on the new moon festivals, for the appointed feasts, for the sacred items, and for the sin offerings to ask for forgiveness for Israel, and for all the work in God&#39;s house.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Nehemiah 10:34">34</a> We threw dice to decide which priests, Levites, and people would bring wood to God&#39;s house like our ancestors did, at set times each year, to burn on God&#39;s altar, as the law says.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Nehemiah 10:35">35</a> To bring the first crops from our land and the first of all tree fruits, every year, to God&#39;s house.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Nehemiah 10:36">36</a> We also give our firstborn sons and the firstborn of our livestock, as the law says, and the firstborn of our herds and our flocks, to bring to God&#39;s house, for the priests working in God&#39;s house.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Nehemiah 10:37">37</a> We should give the first of our dough, gifts, and all kinds of tree fruits, wine, and oil to the priests in the storerooms of God&#39;s house; and we should give a tenth of our crops to the Levites so they can have their share in all our farming towns.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Nehemiah 10:38">38</a> The priest, who is Aaron&#39;s son, will be with the Levites when they collect the tithes. The Levites will take a tenth of these tithes to our God&#39;s house, into the storage rooms.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="Nehemiah 10:39">39</a> The people of Israel and the Levi tribe will take their grain, new wine, and oil to the storage rooms, where the holy things, the priests, the gatekeepers, and the singers are. We will not neglect our God&#39;s house.</p>
