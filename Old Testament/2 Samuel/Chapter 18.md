<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="2 Samuel 18:1">1</a> David counted the people with him and put leaders in charge of groups of a thousand and groups of a hundred.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="2 Samuel 18:2">2</a> David sent a third of the troops with Joab, another third with Abishai, Joab&#39;s brother, and the last third with Ittai the Gittite. The king told the people, &quot;I will definitely come with you too.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="2 Samuel 18:3">3</a> The people said, &quot;You must not go out: if we run away, they won&#39;t bother with us; even if half of us die, they won&#39;t bother. But you are as valuable as ten thousand of us. So it&#39;s better for you to help us from inside the city.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="2 Samuel 18:4">4</a> The king said to them, &quot;I will do what you think is best.&quot; The king stood by the gate, and all the people came out in groups of hundreds and thousands.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="2 Samuel 18:5">5</a> The king told Joab, Abishai, and Ittai, &quot;Please be kind to the young man Absalom for me.&quot; Everyone heard when the king ordered the captains to take care of Absalom.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="2 Samuel 18:6">6</a> The people went out to fight against Israel, and the battle happened in the forest of Ephraim.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="2 Samuel 18:7">7</a> The Israelites were killed by David&#39;s men, and that day 20,000 men died in a big battle.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="2 Samuel 18:8">8</a> The fight spread out across the land, and the forest took more lives that day than the sword.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="2 Samuel 18:9">9</a> Absalom met David&#39;s servants. Absalom rode on a mule, which passed under a big oak tree&#39;s dense branches, and his head got stuck in the oak. He was left hanging between the sky and the ground, and the mule under him ran off.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="2 Samuel 18:10">10</a> A man saw this, told Joab, and said, &quot;Look, I saw Absalom hanging in an oak tree.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="2 Samuel 18:11">11</a> Joab said to the man who told him, &quot;You saw him, so why didn&#39;t you kill him right there? I would have given you ten silver coins and a belt.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="2 Samuel 18:12">12</a> The man said to Joab, &quot;Even if I got a thousand silver coins, I wouldn&#39;t hurt the king&#39;s son because we heard the king tell you, Abishai, and Ittai, &#39;Don&#39;t harm the young man Absalom.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="2 Samuel 18:13">13</a> Otherwise, I would have lied about my own life, because nothing is hidden from the king, and you would have opposed me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="2 Samuel 18:14">14</a> Joab said, &quot;I can&#39;t wait like this with you.&quot; He grabbed three sharp rods and stuck them into Absalom&#39;s heart, while Absalom was still alive in the oak tree.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="2 Samuel 18:15">15</a> Ten young men carrying Joab&#39;s weapons surrounded Absalom, attacked him, and killed him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="2 Samuel 18:16">16</a> Joab sounded the trumpet, and the people stopped chasing Israel because Joab held them back.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="2 Samuel 18:17">17</a> They took Absalom, threw him into a big hole in the forest, and put a huge pile of stones on him; then all Israel ran away to their own tents.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="2 Samuel 18:18">18</a> While he was alive, Absalom set up a monument for himself in the King&#39;s Valley because he said, &quot;I have no son to carry on my name.&quot; He named the monument after himself, and it is still called Absalom&#39;s Monument to this day.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="2 Samuel 18:19">19</a> Then Ahimaaz, Zadok&#39;s son, said, &quot;Let me run now and tell the king that God has taken revenge against his enemies for him.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="2 Samuel 18:20">20</a> Joab told him, &quot;You won&#39;t bring news today, but you will bring news another day. Today you won&#39;t bring any news, because the king&#39;s son has died.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="2 Samuel 18:21">21</a> Joab said to Cushi, &quot;Go tell the king what you have seen.&quot; Cushi bowed to Joab and ran.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="2 Samuel 18:22">22</a> Ahimaaz, son of Zadok, said to Joab again, &quot;Please, let me run after Cushi.&quot; Joab asked, &quot;Why do you want to run, my son, when you have no news?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="2 Samuel 18:23">23</a> He said, &quot;Please let me run.&quot; And the person told him, &quot;Run.&quot; So Ahimaaz ran through the flat land and passed Cushi.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="2 Samuel 18:24">24</a> David sat between the two gates, and the lookout went up to the roof over the gate, onto the wall, looked up, and saw a man running by himself.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="2 Samuel 18:25">25</a> The guard called out and informed the king. The king said, &quot;If he is alone, he brings news.&quot; He hurried and got closer.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="2 Samuel 18:26">26</a> The guard saw another man running and called to the gatekeeper, &quot;Look, another man is running by himself.&quot; The king said, &quot;He too is bringing news.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="2 Samuel 18:27">27</a> The lookout said, I think the way the first one runs looks like Ahimaaz, Zadok&#39;s son. And the king said, He&#39;s a good man, coming with good news.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="2 Samuel 18:28">28</a> Ahimaaz called out and said to the king, &quot;Everything is fine.&quot; He bowed to the ground before the king and said, &quot;Praise be to God, your God, who has handed over the men who raised their hand against my king.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="2 Samuel 18:29">29</a> The king asked, &quot;Is Absalom, the young man, okay?&quot; Ahimaaz replied, &quot;When Joab sent the king&#39;s servant and me, your servant, I saw a big commotion, but I didn&#39;t know what it was about.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="2 Samuel 18:30">30</a> The king told him, &quot;Move over there and wait.&quot; So he moved aside and waited.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="2 Samuel 18:31">31</a> Look, Cushi came and Cushi said, &quot;News, my king: for God has punished those who were against you today.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="2 Samuel 18:32">32</a> The king asked Cushi, &quot;Is the young man Absalom okay?&quot; Cushi replied, &quot;May all the king&#39;s enemies and those who wish to harm you be like that young man.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="2 Samuel 18:33">33</a> The king was deeply upset and walked up to the room above the gate, crying. As he walked, he said, &quot;Oh my son Absalom, my son, my son Absalom, I wish I had died instead of you, oh Absalom, my son, my son!&quot;</p>
