<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="2 Samuel 22:1">1</a> David sang to God this song when God saved him from all his enemies and from Saul.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="2 Samuel 22:2">2</a> He said, &quot;God is my rock, my fortress, and my savior.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="2 Samuel 22:3">3</a> God is my protector; I will trust in him: he is my shield, my strength to save me, my safe place, and my shelter, my savior; you protect me from harm.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="2 Samuel 22:4">4</a> I will call on God, who deserves praise: so I will be saved from my enemies.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="2 Samuel 22:5">5</a> When the waves of death surrounded me, the attacks of evil men scared me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="2 Samuel 22:6">6</a> The pains of death surrounded me; death&#39;s traps were stopping me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="2 Samuel 22:7">7</a> In my trouble, I called to God and shouted for help. He heard my voice from his place, and my call reached his ears.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="2 Samuel 22:8">8</a> The earth shook and quaked; the bases of the sky trembled and moved because God was angry.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="2 Samuel 22:9">9</a> Smoke rose from his nose, and fire from his mouth consumed; coals blazed from it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="2 Samuel 22:10">10</a> He bent the skies and came down; darkness was under his feet.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="2 Samuel 22:11">11</a> He rode on a cherub and flew; he appeared on the wings of the wind.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="2 Samuel 22:12">12</a> He made dark shelters around him, with dark waters and heavy clouds in the sky.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="2 Samuel 22:13">13</a> Bright light from God made coals of fire start burning.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="2 Samuel 22:14">14</a> God thundered from heaven, and the highest one spoke aloud.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="2 Samuel 22:15">15</a> He shot arrows and spread them out; he sent lightning and confused them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="2 Samuel 22:16">16</a> The paths of the sea were shown, the base of the earth was uncovered, when God scolded, with the strong wind from his nose.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="2 Samuel 22:17">17</a> He reached down from on high and took hold of me; he pulled me out of deep waters.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="2 Samuel 22:18">18</a> God saved me from my powerful enemy and from those who hated me because they were too strong for me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="2 Samuel 22:19">19</a> They stopped me when I was in trouble, but God supported me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="2 Samuel 22:20">20</a> God brought me out into a spacious place; he freed me because he was pleased with me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="2 Samuel 22:21">21</a> God rewarded me for doing right; he repaid me because my hands were clean.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="2 Samuel 22:22">22</a> I have followed God&#39;s paths and have not turned away from my God to do wrong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="2 Samuel 22:23">23</a> I kept all God&#39;s rules in mind, and I did not turn away from his laws.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="2 Samuel 22:24">24</a> I was also honest before God, and I have kept myself from doing wrong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="2 Samuel 22:25">25</a> So God has rewarded me for doing right; He has seen my clean actions.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="2 Samuel 22:26">26</a> You are kind to those who are kind, and you are good to those who are good.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="2 Samuel 22:27">27</a> You are pure to those who are pure, but you seem not good to those who are not good.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="2 Samuel 22:28">28</a> You will save those who are suffering, but you are watching the proud, to bring them down.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="2 Samuel 22:29">29</a> You are my light, God, and God will brighten my darkness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="2 Samuel 22:30">30</a> With your help, I have charged through an army; with my God, I have jumped over a wall.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="2 Samuel 22:31">31</a> God&#39;s way is perfect; his word is proven; he protects all who trust in him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="2 Samuel 22:32">32</a> Who is God besides the LORD? Who is strong like our God?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="2 Samuel 22:33">33</a> God is my strength and power, and he makes my path perfect.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="2 Samuel 22:34">34</a> He makes my feet as sure as a deer&#39;s: and places me on high ground.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="2 Samuel 22:35">35</a> He teaches my hands to fight; so that my arms can break a steel bow.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="2 Samuel 22:36">36</a> You have given me the shield of your salvation, and your kindness has made me strong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="2 Samuel 22:37">37</a> You have made my steps wide beneath me; so my feet did not slide.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="2 Samuel 22:38">38</a> I chased my enemies and defeated them; I did not stop until I had completely beaten them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="2 Samuel 22:39">39</a> I have destroyed them and injured them so they could not get up; yes, they have fallen beneath my feet.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="2 Samuel 22:40">40</a> You have given me strength to fight; you have made those who attacked me fall under my control.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="2 Samuel 22:41">41</a> You have also let me defeat my enemies and destroy those who hate me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="2 Samuel 22:42">42</a> They looked for help, but no one could save them; they even cried to God, but he did not respond.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v43"><a class="verse-num" href="#v43" aria-label="2 Samuel 22:43">43</a> I crushed them into dust on the ground, stomped them into mud on the street, and scattered them everywhere.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v44"><a class="verse-num" href="#v44" aria-label="2 Samuel 22:44">44</a> You have also saved me from my people&#39;s conflicts and kept me as the leader of nations; people I didn&#39;t know will serve me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v45"><a class="verse-num" href="#v45" aria-label="2 Samuel 22:45">45</a> Foreigners will come to me and obey as soon as they hear me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v46"><a class="verse-num" href="#v46" aria-label="2 Samuel 22:46">46</a> Foreigners will grow weak and be scared from their hiding spots.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v47"><a class="verse-num" href="#v47" aria-label="2 Samuel 22:47">47</a> God lives; praise to my rock; and may the God who saves me be lifted high.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v48"><a class="verse-num" href="#v48" aria-label="2 Samuel 22:48">48</a> God avenges me and makes other people submit to me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v49"><a class="verse-num" href="#v49" aria-label="2 Samuel 22:49">49</a> You lead me away from my enemies; you lift me up above my opponents; you save me from violent people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v50"><a class="verse-num" href="#v50" aria-label="2 Samuel 22:50">50</a> So I will thank you, God, among the nations, and sing praises to your name.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v51"><a class="verse-num" href="#v51" aria-label="2 Samuel 22:51">51</a> He is the saving tower for his king and shows kindness to his chosen one, David, and his descendants forever.</p>
