<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="2 Samuel 3:1">1</a> There was a long war between Saul&#39;s family and David&#39;s family: but David&#39;s family became stronger and stronger, while Saul&#39;s family became weaker and weaker.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="2 Samuel 3:2">2</a> David had sons in Hebron. His first son was Amnon, whose mother was Ahinoam from Jezreel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="2 Samuel 3:3">3</a> David&#39;s second son was Chileab, with his wife Abigail who was Nabal the Carmelite&#39;s widow, and the third son was Absalom, whose mother was Maacah, the daughter of Talmai, the king of Geshur.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="2 Samuel 3:4">4</a> The fourth was Adonijah, Haggith&#39;s son; the fifth was Shephatiah, Abital&#39;s son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="2 Samuel 3:5">5</a> David&#39;s sixth son, Ithream, was born from his wife Eglah while they were in Hebron.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="2 Samuel 3:6">6</a> During the war between Saul&#39;s family and David&#39;s family, Abner became a strong leader for Saul&#39;s family.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="2 Samuel 3:7">7</a> Saul had a partner named Rizpah, Aiah&#39;s daughter. Ishbosheth asked Abner, &quot;Why did you sleep with my father&#39;s partner?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="2 Samuel 3:8">8</a> Abner was very angry because of Ishbosheth&#39;s words and said, &quot;Am I worth nothing, who has been loyal to Saul your father&#39;s family, his brothers, and his friends today, and haven&#39;t handed you over to David, yet you blame me today for a problem with this woman?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="2 Samuel 3:9">9</a> May God do to Abner, and even more, unless I do to him as God has promised to David.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="2 Samuel 3:10">10</a> To move the kingdom from Saul&#39;s family and to establish David&#39;s rule over Israel and Judah, from Dan to Beersheba.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="2 Samuel 3:11">11</a> He could not reply to Abner at all, because he was afraid of him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="2 Samuel 3:12">12</a> Abner sent people to David to speak for him, asking, &quot;Who owns the land?&quot; He also said, &quot;Make a deal with me, and I will support you to unite all of Israel under you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="2 Samuel 3:13">13</a> He said, &quot;Okay, I will make an agreement with you. But I demand one thing: you cannot see me unless you bring Michal, Saul&#39;s daughter, when you come to see me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="2 Samuel 3:14">14</a> David sent messengers to Ishbosheth, Saul&#39;s son, saying, &quot;Give me my wife Michal, whom I married for a hundred Philistine foreskins.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="2 Samuel 3:15">15</a> Ishbosheth sent for her and took her from her husband, Phaltiel the son of Laish.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="2 Samuel 3:16">16</a> Her husband followed her, crying as he went to Bahurim. Then Abner told him, &quot;Go back.&quot; And he went back.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="2 Samuel 3:17">17</a> Abner spoke to the leaders of Israel, saying, &quot;You once wanted David to be your king.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="2 Samuel 3:18">18</a> Go ahead and do it because God has said about David, &quot;Through my servant David, I will rescue my people Israel from the Philistines and all their enemies.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="2 Samuel 3:19">19</a> Abner also spoke to the people of Benjamin and then went to tell David in Hebron everything that seemed right to Israel and all the Benjaminites.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="2 Samuel 3:20">20</a> Abner went to see David in Hebron with twenty men. David gave a feast for Abner and his men.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="2 Samuel 3:21">21</a> Abner told David, &quot;I will get up and go to bring all of Israel to you, the king, so they can join with you. Then you will rule over everything you want.&quot; And David let Abner go, and he left peacefully.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="2 Samuel 3:22">22</a> David&#39;s men and Joab returned from chasing a group and brought back a lot of loot, but Abner was not with David in Hebron; David had let him go, and he left peacefully.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="2 Samuel 3:23">23</a> When Joab and his army arrived, they told Joab, &quot;Abner, Ner&#39;s son, came to the king, and the king let him go, and he left peacefully.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="2 Samuel 3:24">24</a> Then Joab went to the king and asked, &quot;What have you done? Look, Abner came to you; why did you send him away so he&#39;s completely gone?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="2 Samuel 3:25">25</a> You know Abner, Ner&#39;s son, came to trick you, to find out where you go and what you do.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="2 Samuel 3:26">26</a> When Joab left David, he sent people to get Abner and they brought him back from Sirah&#39;s well, but David didn&#39;t know about it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="2 Samuel 3:27">27</a> After Abner came back to Hebron, Joab pulled him aside in the gate to talk privately and then struck him below the fifth rib, and Abner died for killing Joab&#39;s brother Asahel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="2 Samuel 3:28">28</a> After David heard this, he said, &quot;I and my kingdom are innocent before God forever concerning the death of Abner, Ner&#39;s son.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="2 Samuel 3:29">29</a> Let the blame fall on Joab and his family; may his family never be without someone who is sick, has a skin disease, needs a cane to walk, dies by the sword, or goes hungry.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="2 Samuel 3:30">30</a> Joab and his brother Abishai killed Abner because he had killed their brother Asahel at Gibeon in the battle.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="2 Samuel 3:31">31</a> David said to Joab and all the people with him, &quot;Tear your clothes, put on sackcloth, and weep for Abner.&quot; And King David walked behind the coffin.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="2 Samuel 3:32">32</a> They buried Abner in Hebron, and the king cried aloud at Abner&#39;s grave; all the people also cried.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="2 Samuel 3:33">33</a> The king mourned for Abner, saying, &quot;Did Abner die like a fool?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="2 Samuel 3:34">34</a> Your hands were not tied, nor were your feet chained; like someone falls before evil men, so you fell. And all the people cried for you again.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="2 Samuel 3:35">35</a> When everyone came to get David to eat before evening, David vowed, &quot;May God deal with me, and even more, if I eat bread or anything else before the sun sets.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="2 Samuel 3:36">36</a> Everyone saw what happened, and they liked it; just as everything the king did made the people happy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="2 Samuel 3:37">37</a> Everyone and all of Israel knew that day that the king did not want to kill Abner, Ner&#39;s son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="2 Samuel 3:38">38</a> The king told his servants, &quot;Don&#39;t you know that today a prince and a great man has fallen in Israel?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="2 Samuel 3:39">39</a> Today, even though I am the anointed king, I am weak, and these sons of Zeruiah are too strong for me. God will pay back those who do evil for their wickedness.</p>
