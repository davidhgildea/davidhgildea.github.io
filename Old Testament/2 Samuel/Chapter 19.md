<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="2 Samuel 19:1">1</a> Someone told Joab, &quot;Look, the king is crying and grieving for Absalom.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="2 Samuel 19:2">2</a> That day, the people&#39;s victory changed to sadness because they heard that the king was greatly saddened about his son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="2 Samuel 19:3">3</a> That day, the people sneaked into the city like those who, feeling ashamed, quietly escape during a fight.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="2 Samuel 19:4">4</a> The king covered his face and cried out loudly, &quot;My son Absalom, my son, my son Absalom!&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="2 Samuel 19:5">5</a> Joab went to the king&#39;s house and said, &quot;Today you have embarrassed all your servants who have saved your life, your sons&#39; and daughters&#39; lives, your wives&#39; lives, and your concubines&#39; lives.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="2 Samuel 19:6">6</a> Because you love your enemies and hate your friends, you have shown today that you care for neither leaders nor followers. Today I realize that if Absalom had survived and all of us had died, you would have been happy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="2 Samuel 19:7">7</a> Get up now, go out, and talk kindly to your servants, because I promise by God, if you don&#39;t go out, no one will stay with you tonight, and this will be worse for you than all the bad things that have happened to you since you were young until now.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="2 Samuel 19:8">8</a> The king got up and sat at the gate. People told everyone, &quot;Look, the king is sitting at the gate.&quot; All the people went to see the king, because the Israelites had all run away to their tents.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="2 Samuel 19:9">9</a> All the people across Israel&#39;s tribes were arguing, saying, &quot;The king rescued us from our enemies and saved us from the Philistines; but now he has run away from the land because of Absalom.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="2 Samuel 19:10">10</a> Absalom, who we made our leader, has died in battle. So why don&#39;t you talk about bringing the king back?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="2 Samuel 19:11">11</a> King David sent a message to Zadok and Abiathar, the priests, telling them, &quot;Talk to the leaders of Judah and ask them, &#39;Why are you the last ones to bring the king back to his home when all of Israel is talking about it, even at his home?&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="2 Samuel 19:12">12</a> You are my brothers, you are my own flesh and bones: so why are you the last to bring back the king?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="2 Samuel 19:13">13</a> Tell Amasa, aren&#39;t you my family, my own flesh and bone? May God deal with me, even worse, if you are not always the army commander instead of Joab.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="2 Samuel 19:14">14</a> He made all the men of Judah agree as one; they told the king, &quot;Come back, you and all your helpers.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="2 Samuel 19:15">15</a> The king went back and arrived at the Jordan River. People from Judah went to Gilgal to meet the king and help him cross the Jordan.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="2 Samuel 19:16">16</a> Shimei, Gera&#39;s son from the tribe of Benjamin, who was from Bahurim, hurried and went down with the men of Judah to meet King David.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="2 Samuel 19:17">17</a> A thousand men from Benjamin were with him, along with Ziba, a servant from Saul&#39;s house, and his fifteen sons and twenty servants; they crossed the Jordan River ahead of the king.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="2 Samuel 19:18">18</a> A ferry boat took the king&#39;s family across and did what he wanted. Shimei, Gera&#39;s son, bowed before the king when he crossed the Jordan.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="2 Samuel 19:19">19</a> He said to the king, &quot;Please don&#39;t blame me for doing wrong, and don&#39;t remember the bad thing I did when you, the king, left Jerusalem. Don&#39;t keep it in your mind.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="2 Samuel 19:20">20</a> I, your servant, know I have sinned. That&#39;s why I am the first one from Joseph&#39;s family to meet the king today.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="2 Samuel 19:21">21</a> But Abishai, Zeruiah&#39;s son, replied, &quot;Shouldn&#39;t Shimei die for cursing God&#39;s chosen one?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="2 Samuel 19:22">22</a> David said, &quot;What&#39;s my business with you, sons of Zeruiah? Why are you causing trouble for me today? Should anyone be killed in Israel today? Don&#39;t I know that today I am king over Israel?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="2 Samuel 19:23">23</a> So the king told Shimei, &quot;You will not die.&quot; And the king promised him that.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="2 Samuel 19:24">24</a> Mephibosheth, Saul&#39;s son, met the king without caring for his feet, cutting his beard, or washing his clothes since the king left until he returned safely.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="2 Samuel 19:25">25</a> When he arrived in Jerusalem to see the king, the king asked him, &quot;Why didn&#39;t you come with me, Mephibosheth?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="2 Samuel 19:26">26</a> He replied, &quot;My king, my servant tricked me. I planned to saddle a donkey to ride and go to the king, because I am lame.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="2 Samuel 19:27">27</a> He has lied about me to you, the king, but you, the king, are like an angel of God. So do what you think is right.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="2 Samuel 19:28">28</a> Everyone in my father&#39;s family was as good as dead before you, the king, but you allowed me, your servant, to eat at your table. So, what right do I have to ask anything more from you, the king?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="2 Samuel 19:29">29</a> The king said to him, &quot;Why do you talk more about your issues? I&#39;ve decided, you and Ziba will share the land.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="2 Samuel 19:30">30</a> Mephibosheth told the king, &quot;Yes, let him take everything, since you, the king, have returned home safely.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="2 Samuel 19:31">31</a> Barzillai from Gilead left Rogelim and crossed the Jordan River with the king to help him get across.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="2 Samuel 19:32">32</a> Barzillai was a very old man, eighty years old, and he had given the king food while he stayed at Mahanaim, because he was a very important man.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="2 Samuel 19:33">33</a> The king told Barzillai, &quot;Come with me and I will take care of you in Jerusalem.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="2 Samuel 19:34">34</a> Barzillai asked the king, &quot;How long do I have left to live that I should go with the king to Jerusalem?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="2 Samuel 19:35">35</a> I am eighty years old today: and can I tell right from wrong? Can your servant taste what I eat or drink? Can I still hear the voices of singers, both men and women? Why then should I be a burden to you, the king?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="2 Samuel 19:36">36</a> I will cross the Jordan River with the king for a short distance. Why should the king give me such a reward?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="2 Samuel 19:37">37</a> Please let me, your servant, go back to die in my city near my parents&#39; graves. But let Chimham go with you, and you can do for him what you think is best.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="2 Samuel 19:38">38</a> The king said, &quot;Chimham will come with me, and I will be good to him as you wish. Whatever you ask of me, I will do for you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="2 Samuel 19:39">39</a> All the people crossed the Jordan River. When the king crossed, he kissed Barzillai and blessed him, then Barzillai went back to his home.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="2 Samuel 19:40">40</a> Then the king continued to Gilgal, with Chimham going with him; all the people of Judah led the king, and also half the people of Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="2 Samuel 19:41">41</a> Look, all the men of Israel came to the king and asked him, &quot;Why did our brothers, the men of Judah, secretly take you, the king, his family, and all of David&#39;s men across the Jordan river?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="2 Samuel 19:42">42</a> All the men of Judah replied to the men of Israel, &quot;The king is related to us, so why are you angry about this? Did we eat at the king&#39;s expense, or did he give us any present?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v43"><a class="verse-num" href="#v43" aria-label="2 Samuel 19:43">43</a> The men of Israel responded to the men of Judah, saying, &quot;We have ten shares in the king and a stronger claim on David than you. So why did you look down on us? Why wasn&#39;t our advice considered first in bringing back our king?&quot; But the men of Judah&#39;s responses were harsher than those of the men of Israel.</p>
