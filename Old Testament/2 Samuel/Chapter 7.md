<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="2 Samuel 7:1">1</a> When the king was in his house, and God had given him peace from all his enemies;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="2 Samuel 7:2">2</a> The king said to the prophet Nathan, &quot;Look, I live in a cedar house, but God&#39;s ark stays in a tent.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="2 Samuel 7:3">3</a> Nathan told the king, &quot;Go ahead and do everything you want to do because God is with you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="2 Samuel 7:4">4</a> That night, God&#39;s message came to Nathan, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="2 Samuel 7:5">5</a> Go and tell my servant David, God says, Will you build me a house to live in?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="2 Samuel 7:6">6</a> Since I led the people of Israel out of Egypt, I have never lived in a house. Instead, I have moved in a tent and in a shelter.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="2 Samuel 7:7">7</a> In every place where I have been with all of Israel&#39;s children, did I ever ask any tribe I told to take care of my people Israel, &quot;Why don&#39;t you build me a cedar house?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="2 Samuel 7:8">8</a> So now, tell my servant David this: God says, &#39;I chose you from the pasture and from tending the sheep to be the leader of my people, Israel.&#39;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="2 Samuel 7:9">9</a> I was with you wherever you went, and I defeated all your enemies, making your name as great as the most famous people on earth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="2 Samuel 7:10">10</a> I will provide a home for my people Israel and settle them so they can live in their own place without moving again; evil people will not trouble them anymore, like in the past.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="2 Samuel 7:11">11</a> Ever since I appointed leaders for my people Israel and gave you peace from all your enemies, God has promised to build you a house.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="2 Samuel 7:12">12</a> When your time comes to an end and you die, I will raise up your descendants to follow you, one who will come from your own body, and I will make his kingdom strong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="2 Samuel 7:13">13</a> He will build a house for my name, and I will establish his kingdom&#39;s throne forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="2 Samuel 7:14">14</a> I will be his father, and he will be my son. If he does wrong, I will correct him with punishment from people and their whippings.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="2 Samuel 7:15">15</a> My kindness will not leave him, as I took it from Saul, whom I removed before you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="2 Samuel 7:16">16</a> Your family and kingdom will last forever; your rule will go on forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="2 Samuel 7:17">17</a> Nathan told David everything he had heard and seen.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="2 Samuel 7:18">18</a> Then King David went in, sat before God, and said, &quot;Who am I, God, and what is my family, that you have brought me this far?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="2 Samuel 7:19">19</a> This was still a small thing to you, God, but you also promised good things for your servant&#39;s family far into the future. Is this how people normally act, God?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="2 Samuel 7:20">20</a> What more can David say to you? You, God, know your servant.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="2 Samuel 7:21">21</a> For the sake of your promise and your own desire, you have done all these amazing things so that your servant could understand them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="2 Samuel 7:22">22</a> You are great, God, because there is no one like you, and there is no other God, just as we have heard.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="2 Samuel 7:23">23</a> What other nation on earth is like your people Israel? God chose them to be his own, to make himself known, and to perform great and awe-inspiring acts for them—clearing out nations and their gods to free Israel from Egypt.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="2 Samuel 7:24">24</a> You have made the people of Israel your own people forever, and you, God, have become their God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="2 Samuel 7:25">25</a> Now, God, make true your promise about your servant and his family forever, and do as you have promised.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="2 Samuel 7:26">26</a> May your name be honored always, as people say, &quot;God rules over Israel,&quot; and may David&#39;s family remain strong in your presence.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="2 Samuel 7:27">27</a> You, God of all and God of Israel, have told your servant that you will build a house for him. So your servant has decided to pray this prayer to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="2 Samuel 7:28">28</a> And now, God, you are that God, your words are true, and you have promised this goodness to your servant.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="2 Samuel 7:29">29</a> Please bless your servant&#39;s house so it can last forever in your presence. You, God, have promised this; may your blessing make your servant&#39;s house forever blessed.</p>
