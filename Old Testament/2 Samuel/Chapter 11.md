<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="2 Samuel 11:1">1</a> After a year ended, when kings usually went to war, David sent Joab, his officers, and all Israel. They defeated the Ammonites and surrounded Rabbah. But David stayed in Jerusalem.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="2 Samuel 11:2">2</a> One evening, David got up from his bed, walked on the palace roof, and saw a very beautiful woman bathing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="2 Samuel 11:3">3</a> David sent someone to find out about the woman. Someone asked, &quot;Isn&#39;t this Bathsheba, Eliam&#39;s daughter, Uriah the Hittite&#39;s wife?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="2 Samuel 11:4">4</a> David sent people to get her. She came to him, and they slept together after she was clean from her impurity. Then she went back home.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="2 Samuel 11:5">5</a> The woman became pregnant, and she sent word to David, saying, &quot;I am expecting a baby.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="2 Samuel 11:6">6</a> David sent a message to Joab: &quot;Send me Uriah the Hittite.&quot; So Joab sent Uriah to David.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="2 Samuel 11:7">7</a> When Uriah came to him, David asked how Joab was, how the people were, and how the war was going.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="2 Samuel 11:8">8</a> David told Uriah, &quot;Go to your house and wash your feet.&quot; Uriah left the king&#39;s house, and a serving of food from the king was sent after him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="2 Samuel 11:9">9</a> Uriah slept at the entrance of the king&#39;s house with all his master&#39;s servants and did not go down to his own home.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="2 Samuel 11:10">10</a> When they told David, &quot;Uriah did not go home,&quot; David asked Uriah, &quot;Didn&#39;t you come from a trip? Why didn&#39;t you go home?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="2 Samuel 11:11">11</a> Uriah said to David, &quot;The ark, and Israel, and Judah stay in tents; and my leader Joab, and my lord&#39;s servants, camp in the fields. Should I then go to my house to eat, drink, and be with my wife? As you live, and as your spirit lives, I will not do this.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="2 Samuel 11:12">12</a> David told Uriah, &quot;Stay here today as well, and tomorrow I will let you go.&quot; So Uriah stayed in Jerusalem that day and the next day.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="2 Samuel 11:13">13</a> When David invited him, he ate and drank in front of David, and David got him drunk. In the evening, he went to sleep on his bed with his master&#39;s servants instead of going home.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="2 Samuel 11:14">14</a> In the morning, David wrote a letter to Joab and sent it with Uriah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="2 Samuel 11:15">15</a> He wrote in the letter: Place Uriah at the front where the fighting is fiercest and then pull back so he will be struck down and die.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="2 Samuel 11:16">16</a> When Joab saw the city, he sent Uriah to a place where he knew strong soldiers were.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="2 Samuel 11:17">17</a> The city men came out, fought with Joab, and some of David&#39;s servants died, including Uriah the Hittite.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="2 Samuel 11:18">18</a> Then Joab sent a message to tell David everything about the war.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="2 Samuel 11:19">19</a> He gave the messenger instructions, saying, &quot;After you finish reporting the war news to the king,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="2 Samuel 11:20">20</a> If the king gets angry and asks you why you came so close to the city during the battle, didn&#39;t you realize they would shoot arrows from the wall?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="2 Samuel 11:21">21</a> Who killed Abimelech, son of Jerubbesheth? Wasn&#39;t it a woman who dropped a millstone on him from the wall so he died in Thebez? Why did you go close to the wall? Then you must say, &#39;Your servant Uriah the Hittite is also dead.&#39;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="2 Samuel 11:22">22</a> The messenger went, came, and told David everything Joab had sent him to say.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="2 Samuel 11:23">23</a> The messenger told David, &quot;The men were too strong for us. They attacked us in the open field, but we fought them off all the way to the city gate.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="2 Samuel 11:24">24</a> The archers shot arrows at your servants from the wall; some of the king&#39;s servants have died, and your servant Uriah the Hittite is also dead.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="2 Samuel 11:25">25</a> David told the messenger, &quot;Tell Joab, &#39;Don&#39;t be upset about this because the sword takes one as well as another. Fight harder against the city, destroy it, and cheer him up.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="2 Samuel 11:26">26</a> When Uriah&#39;s wife learned that her husband Uriah had died, she grieved for him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="2 Samuel 11:27">27</a> After the mourning ended, David brought her to his house, she became his wife, and had a son for him. But what David did made God unhappy.</p>
