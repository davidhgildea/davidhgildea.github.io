<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="2 Samuel 9:1">1</a> David asked, &quot;Is there anyone still alive from Saul&#39;s family, so I can be kind to them for Jonathan&#39;s sake?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="2 Samuel 9:2">2</a> Saul&#39;s household had a servant named Ziba. When they brought him to David, the king asked, &quot;Are you Ziba?&quot; He replied, &quot;I am your servant.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="2 Samuel 9:3">3</a> The king asked, &quot;Is there no one left from Saul&#39;s family to whom I can show God&#39;s kindness?&quot; Ziba told the king, &quot;Jonathan has a son who cannot walk.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="2 Samuel 9:4">4</a> The king asked him, &quot;Where is he?&quot; Ziba answered the king, &quot;Look, he&#39;s at the house of Machir, Ammiel&#39;s son, in Lodebar.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="2 Samuel 9:5">5</a> Then King David sent for him and brought him from Machir&#39;s house, the son of Ammiel, in Lodebar.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="2 Samuel 9:6">6</a> When Mephibosheth, Jonathan&#39;s son and Saul&#39;s grandson, came to David, he bowed down to show respect. David called out, &quot;Mephibosheth!&quot; And he replied, &quot;Here I am, your servant!&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="2 Samuel 9:7">7</a> David told him, &quot;Don&#39;t be afraid. I will definitely be kind to you because of your father Jonathan. I will give back to you all the land of your grandfather Saul, and you will always eat at my table.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="2 Samuel 9:8">8</a> He bowed and said, &quot;What is your servant, that you would notice a worthless dog like me?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="2 Samuel 9:9">9</a> The king called Ziba, Saul&#39;s servant, and said to him, &quot;I have given your master&#39;s son everything that belonged to Saul and his family.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="2 Samuel 9:10">10</a> You, your sons, and your workers must farm the land for him, and you must bring in the crops so that your master&#39;s son will have food to eat; but Mephibosheth, your master&#39;s son, will always eat bread at my table. Ziba had fifteen sons and twenty workers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="2 Samuel 9:11">11</a> Ziba told the king, &quot;I will do everything you, the king, have told me to do.&quot; The king said about Mephibosheth, &quot;He will eat at my table like one of my sons.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="2 Samuel 9:12">12</a> Mephibosheth had a young son named Micha. Everyone living in Ziba&#39;s house served Mephibosheth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="2 Samuel 9:13">13</a> Mephibosheth lived in Jerusalem because he always ate at the king&#39;s table, and he could not walk well with either of his feet.</p>
