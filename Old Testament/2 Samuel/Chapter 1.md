<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="2 Samuel 1:1">1</a> After Saul died and David came back from defeating the Amalekites, David stayed in Ziklag for two days.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="2 Samuel 1:2">2</a> On the third day, a man left Saul&#39;s camp with torn clothes and dirt on his head. When he reached David, he fell to the ground to show respect.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="2 Samuel 1:3">3</a> David asked him, &quot;Where did you come from?&quot; He replied, &quot;I escaped from the Israelite camp.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="2 Samuel 1:4">4</a> David asked him, &quot;What happened? Please tell me.&quot; He replied, &quot;The soldiers ran away from the fight, many are dead or dying; and Saul with his son Jonathan are both dead too.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="2 Samuel 1:5">5</a> David asked the young man who told him, &quot;How do you know that Saul and Jonathan his son are dead?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="2 Samuel 1:6">6</a> The young man who told me said, &quot;I was at Mount Gilboa by chance, and there was Saul leaning on his spear; the chariots and riders were close behind him.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="2 Samuel 1:7">7</a> When he looked back, he saw me and called to me. I answered, &quot;I&#39;m here.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="2 Samuel 1:8">8</a> He asked me, &quot;Who are you?&quot; And I replied, &quot;I am an Amalekite.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="2 Samuel 1:9">9</a> He said to me again, &quot;Please stand on me and kill me, because I&#39;m in great pain and still alive.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="2 Samuel 1:10">10</a> I stood over him and killed him because I knew he couldn&#39;t survive after he fell. I took the crown from his head and the bracelet from his arm, and I brought them here to my lord.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="2 Samuel 1:11">11</a> Then David grabbed his clothes and tore them; and all the men with him did the same.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="2 Samuel 1:12">12</a> They were sad, cried, and didn&#39;t eat until evening for Saul, for his son Jonathan, for God&#39;s people, and for Israel, because they had died in battle.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="2 Samuel 1:13">13</a> David asked the man who brought him news, &quot;Where are you from?&quot; He replied, &quot;I am the son of a foreigner, an Amalekite.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="2 Samuel 1:14">14</a> And David asked him, &quot;Weren&#39;t you scared to raise your hand to kill God&#39;s chosen one?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="2 Samuel 1:15">15</a> David called a young man and said, &quot;Go close and attack him.&quot; So he struck him, and he died.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="2 Samuel 1:16">16</a> David said to him, &quot;You are responsible for your own death; your own words have condemned you, saying, &#39;I killed God&#39;s chosen one.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="2 Samuel 1:17">17</a> David mourned for Saul and his son Jonathan with this sad song:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="2 Samuel 1:18">18</a> He also told them to teach the children of Judah how to use the bow; see, it is written in the book of Jasher.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="2 Samuel 1:19">19</a> Israel&#39;s beauty is killed on your hills: how the strong have fallen!</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="2 Samuel 1:20">20</a> Don&#39;t talk about it in Gath, don&#39;t announce it in Askelon&#39;s streets; so the Philistine girls won&#39;t be happy, so the girls of those not circumcised won&#39;t celebrate.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="2 Samuel 1:21">21</a> Mountains of Gilboa, may you have no dew or rain, or fields for harvests, because there the strong&#39;s shield was thrown away without care, Saul&#39;s shield, as if he was never anointed with oil.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="2 Samuel 1:22">22</a> Jonathan&#39;s bow did not turn away from the blood of the dead, and the strong ones&#39; fat, and Saul&#39;s sword did not come back without success.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="2 Samuel 1:23">23</a> Saul and Jonathan were kind and nice while they lived, and even in death, they stayed together. They were faster than eagles and stronger than lions.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="2 Samuel 1:24">24</a> Women of Israel, cry for Saul, who dressed you in red with beautiful things, who added gold jewelry to your clothes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="2 Samuel 1:25">25</a> The strong have fallen in battle! Oh Jonathan, you were killed on the heights.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="2 Samuel 1:26">26</a> I am sad for you, my brother Jonathan: you were very kind to me: your love for me was amazing, more than the love of women.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="2 Samuel 1:27">27</a> The strong have fallen, and the tools of battle are gone!</p>
