<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="2 Samuel 13:1">1</a> After this, David&#39;s son Absalom had a beautiful sister named Tamar, and David&#39;s son Amnon loved her.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="2 Samuel 13:2">2</a> Amnon was so upset that he became sick because of his sister Tamar; she was a virgin, and Amnon found it difficult to do anything to her.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="2 Samuel 13:3">3</a> Amnon had a friend named Jonadab, who was the son of Shimeah, David&#39;s brother, and Jonadab was very clever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="2 Samuel 13:4">4</a> He asked him, &quot;Why are you, the king&#39;s son, getting thinner every day? Won&#39;t you tell me?&quot; Amnon replied, &quot;I love Tamar, my brother Absalom&#39;s sister.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="2 Samuel 13:5">5</a> Jonadab told him, &quot;Lie down on your bed and pretend to be sick. When your father comes to see you, ask him, &#39;Please let my sister Tamar come and give me food. Let her prepare the food in front of me so I can watch and eat from her hand.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="2 Samuel 13:6">6</a> Amnon laid down and pretended to be sick. When the king came to visit him, Amnon asked the king, &quot;Please let my sister Tamar come and make some cakes where I can see her, so I can eat from her hand.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="2 Samuel 13:7">7</a> David sent a message to Tamar, saying, &quot;Go to your brother Amnon&#39;s house and prepare food for him.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="2 Samuel 13:8">8</a> Tamar went to her brother Amnon&#39;s house where he was lying down. She mixed flour, shaped it, and cooked cakes where he could see her.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="2 Samuel 13:9">9</a> She got a pan and put the food in front of him, but he wouldn&#39;t eat. Amnon said, &quot;Send everyone away from me.&quot; So everyone left him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="2 Samuel 13:10">10</a> Amnon said to Tamar, &quot;Bring the food to the room so I can eat from your hand.&quot; Tamar took the cakes she made and brought them to the room for her brother Amnon.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="2 Samuel 13:11">11</a> When she brought him the food, he grabbed her and said, &quot;Come sleep with me, my sister.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="2 Samuel 13:12">12</a> She replied, &quot;No, my brother, don&#39;t force me. Such things should not happen in Israel. Don&#39;t do this foolish act.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="2 Samuel 13:13">13</a> Where can I hide my shame? And you will be like a fool in Israel. So please, ask the king; he won&#39;t keep me from you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="2 Samuel 13:14">14</a> However, he refused to listen to her and, being stronger than her, overpowered her and slept with her.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="2 Samuel 13:15">15</a> Then Amnon hated her very much; the hate he felt for her was stronger than his previous love. Amnon said to her, &quot;Get up and leave.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="2 Samuel 13:16">16</a> She told him, &quot;No reason for this; sending me away is worse than what you did before.&quot; But he didn&#39;t listen to her.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="2 Samuel 13:17">17</a> He called his servant who worked for him and said, &quot;Now take this woman out of here and lock the door behind her.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="2 Samuel 13:18">18</a> She wore a robe of many colors because that&#39;s what unmarried princesses wore. Afterward, his servant took her out and locked the door behind her.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="2 Samuel 13:19">19</a> Tamar put ash on her head, tore her colorful robe, placed her hand on her head, and walked away crying.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="2 Samuel 13:20">20</a> Absalom, her brother, asked her, &quot;Did Amnon, your brother, hurt you? Stay quiet now, my sister; he&#39;s your brother. Don&#39;t worry about this.&quot; So Tamar stayed alone in her brother Absalom&#39;s home.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="2 Samuel 13:21">21</a> When King David heard about all this, he was very angry.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="2 Samuel 13:22">22</a> Absalom said nothing, good or bad, to his brother Amnon because Absalom hated Amnon for hurting their sister Tamar.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="2 Samuel 13:23">23</a> Two years later, Absalom held a sheep shearing event in Baalhazor, near Ephraim, and he invited all the king&#39;s sons.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="2 Samuel 13:24">24</a> Absalom went to the king and said, &quot;Look, I have people who are shearing sheep. Please let the king and his servants join me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="2 Samuel 13:25">25</a> The king told Absalom, &quot;No, my son, we should not all go now, so we don&#39;t become a burden to you.&quot; Even though he urged him, he did not go, but gave him his blessing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="2 Samuel 13:26">26</a> Then Absalom said, &quot;If not, please let my brother Amnon go with us.&quot; And the king asked, &quot;Why should he go with you?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="2 Samuel 13:27">27</a> But Absalom insisted until he convinced Amnon and all the king&#39;s sons to go with him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="2 Samuel 13:28">28</a> Absalom told his servants, &quot;Watch when Amnon gets happy from drinking wine, and when I tell you to hit Amnon, then kill him. Don&#39;t be afraid; haven&#39;t I given you orders? Be brave and strong.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="2 Samuel 13:29">29</a> Absalom&#39;s servants did to Amnon what Absalom ordered. Then all the king&#39;s sons got up, each one mounted his mule, and they all ran away.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="2 Samuel 13:30">30</a> While they were traveling, news reached David that Absalom had killed all the king&#39;s sons and not one of them had survived.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="2 Samuel 13:31">31</a> Then the king got up, tore his clothes, and lay on the ground; all his servants stood nearby with their clothes torn.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="2 Samuel 13:32">32</a> Jonadab, Shimeah&#39;s son and David&#39;s nephew, said, &quot;My lord should not think that all the young princes have been killed. Only Amnon is dead. This was planned by Absalom since the day he hurt his sister Tamar.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="2 Samuel 13:33">33</a> So now, let the king not worry, thinking all his sons are dead. Only Amnon is dead.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="2 Samuel 13:34">34</a> Absalom ran away. The young man on guard looked up and saw many people coming from the hillside behind him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="2 Samuel 13:35">35</a> Jonadab told the king, &quot;Look, the king&#39;s sons are coming; it&#39;s just as your servant said.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="2 Samuel 13:36">36</a> After he finished speaking, the king&#39;s sons arrived, cried loudly, and wept, and the king and all his servants also wept bitterly.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="2 Samuel 13:37">37</a> Absalom ran away and went to Talmai, son of Ammihud, the king of Geshur. David grieved for his son every day.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="2 Samuel 13:38">38</a> Absalom ran away to Geshur and stayed there for three years.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="2 Samuel 13:39">39</a> King David deeply wanted to see Absalom because he felt better about Amnon now that he was dead.</p>
