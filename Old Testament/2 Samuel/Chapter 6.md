<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="2 Samuel 6:1">1</a> David brought together thirty thousand select men of Israel again.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="2 Samuel 6:2">2</a> David got up and went with everyone from Baale of Judah to move the ark of God with the name called God of the armies who lives among the cherubim.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="2 Samuel 6:3">3</a> They placed God&#39;s ark on a new cart and took it from Abinadab&#39;s house in Gibeah. Abinadab&#39;s sons, Uzzah and Ahio, guided the new cart.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="2 Samuel 6:4">4</a> They took it from Abinadab&#39;s house in Gibeah, going with God&#39;s box, and Ahio walked in front of it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="2 Samuel 6:5">5</a> David and everyone from Israel made music for God with all kinds of wooden instruments, like harps, lyres, drums, flutes, and cymbals.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="2 Samuel 6:6">6</a> When they reached Nachon&#39;s threshing floor, Uzzah reached out to the Ark of God and grabbed it because the oxen stumbled.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="2 Samuel 6:7">7</a> God became very angry with Uzzah and struck him down because of his mistake, and Uzzah died there next to the ark of God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="2 Samuel 6:8">8</a> David was upset because God had punished Uzzah, and he named that place Perezuzzah, which it is still called today.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="2 Samuel 6:9">9</a> David was scared of God that day and asked, &quot;How can the ark of God come to me?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="2 Samuel 6:10">10</a> So David did not take God&#39;s chest to his city, but he took it to the house of Obededom from Gath.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="2 Samuel 6:11">11</a> The chest of God stayed in the home of Obededom the Gittite for three months, and God blessed Obededom and his whole family.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="2 Samuel 6:12">12</a> Someone told King David, &quot;God has blessed Obededom&#39;s house and everything he has because of the ark of God.&quot; So David went and happily moved the ark of God from Obededom&#39;s house to the city of David.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="2 Samuel 6:13">13</a> When those carrying the ark of God had walked six steps, he offered oxen and fattened animals as sacrifices.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="2 Samuel 6:14">14</a> David danced before God with all his strength, wearing a linen garment.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="2 Samuel 6:15">15</a> So David and all of Israel&#39;s family moved the ark of God with loud cheers and trumpet blasts.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="2 Samuel 6:16">16</a> As God&#39;s ark entered David&#39;s city, Saul&#39;s daughter Michal saw King David jumping and dancing before God through a window and she felt contempt for him inside.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="2 Samuel 6:17">17</a> They brought God&#39;s ark and put it where it should be, in the tent David set up for it. David made burnt offerings and peace offerings to God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="2 Samuel 6:18">18</a> After David finished offering burnt and peace offerings, he blessed the people in God&#39;s name.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="2 Samuel 6:19">19</a> He gave out to everyone there, both women and men, a loaf of bread, a good serving of meat, and a bottle of wine. Then all the people went home.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="2 Samuel 6:20">20</a> David went back to bless his family. Michal, Saul&#39;s daughter, met David and said, &quot;The king of Israel looked foolish today, exposing himself in front of the servant girls like a shameless man!&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="2 Samuel 6:21">21</a> David said to Michal, &quot;I did it for God, who picked me instead of your dad and his family to be the leader of God&#39;s people, Israel. That&#39;s why I will celebrate before God.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="2 Samuel 6:22">22</a> I will be even more undignified than this, and I will be humble in my own eyes. But by the female servants you mentioned, I will be respected.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="2 Samuel 6:23">23</a> So Michal, Saul&#39;s daughter, had no children until she died.</p>
