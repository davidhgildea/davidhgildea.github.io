<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="2 Samuel 2:1">1</a> After this, David asked God, &quot;Should I go to one of Judah&#39;s cities?&quot; And God told him, &quot;Go.&quot; Then David asked, &quot;Which city should I go to?&quot; And God answered, &quot;To Hebron.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="2 Samuel 2:2">2</a> David went up there with his two wives, Ahinoam from Jezreel and Abigail, who was Nabal&#39;s wife from Carmel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="2 Samuel 2:3">3</a> David took his soldiers and their families to live in the towns near Hebron.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="2 Samuel 2:4">4</a> The men of Judah came and made David king over Judah. They told David that the men of Jabesh Gilead buried Saul.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="2 Samuel 2:5">5</a> David sent messengers to the men of Jabeshgilead and said to them, &quot;God bless you for being so kind to your leader Saul by burying him.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="2 Samuel 2:6">6</a> Now God show you kindness and honesty, and I will also repay you for this kindness because you have done this.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="2 Samuel 2:7">7</a> So now, make your hands strong and be brave, because your leader Saul has died, and the people of Judah have made me their king.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="2 Samuel 2:8">8</a> Abner, Ner&#39;s son and leader of Saul&#39;s army, took Ishbosheth, Saul&#39;s son, to Mahanaim.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="2 Samuel 2:9">9</a> They made him king over Gilead, the Ashurites, Jezreel, Ephraim, Benjamin, and all of Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="2 Samuel 2:10">10</a> Ishbosheth, Saul&#39;s son, was forty when he started to rule over Israel and ruled for two years. The people of Judah supported David.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="2 Samuel 2:11">11</a> David ruled as king in Hebron over Judah for seven years and six months.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="2 Samuel 2:12">12</a> Abner, Ner&#39;s son, and Ishbosheth&#39;s servants, Saul&#39;s son, left Mahanaim for Gibeon.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="2 Samuel 2:13">13</a> Joab, Zeruiah&#39;s son, and David&#39;s servants left and met by the pool of Gibeon. They sat down, one group on one side of the pool and the other group on the opposite side.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="2 Samuel 2:14">14</a> Abner said to Joab, &quot;Let the young men get up and show their skills in front of us.&quot; Joab replied, &quot;Let them get up.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="2 Samuel 2:15">15</a> Then twelve men from Benjamin who were with Ishbosheth, Saul&#39;s son, stood up and crossed over, along with twelve of David&#39;s servants.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="2 Samuel 2:16">16</a> They each grabbed their companion by the head and stuck their sword into the other&#39;s side, and they fell down together. Therefore, that place was named Field of Swords, in Gibeon.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="2 Samuel 2:17">17</a> There was a very tough fight that day; and Abner and the Israelites were defeated by David&#39;s men.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="2 Samuel 2:18">18</a> Zeruiah had three sons there: Joab, Abishai, and Asahel. Asahel could run as fast as a wild deer.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="2 Samuel 2:19">19</a> Asahel chased Abner without turning either right or left from following him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="2 Samuel 2:20">20</a> Then Abner turned around and asked, &quot;Are you Asahel?&quot; And he replied, &quot;Yes, I am.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="2 Samuel 2:21">21</a> Abner told him, &quot;Move to your right or left, grab one of the soldiers, and take his armor.&quot; But Asahel wouldn&#39;t stop chasing him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="2 Samuel 2:22">22</a> Abner said to Asahel again, &quot;Stop following me. Why should I hit you and knock you down? How could I then face your brother Joab?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="2 Samuel 2:23">23</a> Even though he wouldn&#39;t move aside, Abner hit him with the back end of the spear below his chest, so the spear went through him; he collapsed there and died. Everyone who arrived where Asahel had fallen and died stopped in their tracks.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="2 Samuel 2:24">24</a> Joab and Abishai chased after Abner, and the sun set as they reached the hill of Ammah, near Giah on the road through the desert of Gibeon.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="2 Samuel 2:25">25</a> The descendants of Benjamin joined together behind Abner, forming one group, and stood on a hilltop.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="2 Samuel 2:26">26</a> Then Abner called out to Joab, &quot;Can&#39;t you see that endless fighting will only bring bitterness in the end? How long will it be before you order your men to stop chasing their own brothers?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="2 Samuel 2:27">27</a> Joab said, &quot;As sure as God lives, if you hadn&#39;t spoken, by morning the people would have all stopped following their leader.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="2 Samuel 2:28">28</a> Joab sounded a trumpet, and everyone stopped, no longer chasing Israel or fighting.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="2 Samuel 2:29">29</a> Abner and his men walked all night across the plain, crossed the Jordan River, traveled through Bithron, and arrived at Mahanaim.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="2 Samuel 2:30">30</a> Joab stopped chasing Abner. When he brought all the people together, David&#39;s servants were missing nineteen men, including Asahel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="2 Samuel 2:31">31</a> David&#39;s men defeated the Benjamin tribe and Abner&#39;s soldiers, so 360 men died.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="2 Samuel 2:32">32</a> They carried Asahel and buried him in his father&#39;s tomb in Bethlehem. Joab and his men traveled all night and arrived at Hebron at dawn.</p>
