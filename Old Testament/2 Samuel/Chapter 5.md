<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="2 Samuel 5:1">1</a> All the tribes of Israel came to David at Hebron and said, &quot;Look, we are your own flesh and blood.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="2 Samuel 5:2">2</a> Before, when Saul was our king, you were the one who led Israel out and in. And God told you, &#39;You will take care of my people Israel, and you will be their leader.&#39;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="2 Samuel 5:3">3</a> All the leaders of Israel went to see the king at Hebron. There, David made an agreement with them in Hebron before God, and they made David king over Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="2 Samuel 5:4">4</a> David was thirty when he became king, and he ruled for forty years.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="2 Samuel 5:5">5</a> He ruled over Judah in Hebron for seven years and six months, and in Jerusalem, he ruled over all Israel and Judah for thirty-three years.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="2 Samuel 5:6">6</a> The king and his men went to Jerusalem to meet the Jebusites who lived there. The Jebusites told David, &quot;You can&#39;t come in here unless you get rid of the blind and lame people.&quot; They thought, &quot;David can&#39;t come in here.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="2 Samuel 5:7">7</a> David captured the fortress of Zion, which is now called the city of David.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="2 Samuel 5:8">8</a> David said that day, &quot;Whoever climbs through the water shaft and defeats the Jebusites, including the blind and lame whom David dislikes, will become the leader.&quot; That&#39;s why people say, &quot;The blind and lame cannot enter the house.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="2 Samuel 5:9">9</a> David lived in the fortress and named it David&#39;s city. He then built it up from the Millo and inside.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="2 Samuel 5:10">10</a> David continued to become powerful, and God was with him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="2 Samuel 5:11">11</a> Hiram, king of Tyre, sent messengers to David with cedar trees, carpenters, and masons, and they built a house for David.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="2 Samuel 5:12">12</a> David realized that God had made him king over Israel, and that God had lifted up his kingdom for the benefit of the people of Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="2 Samuel 5:13">13</a> David married more women and added them to his other wives in Jerusalem after he left Hebron, and he had more sons and daughters.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="2 Samuel 5:14">14</a> Here are the names of his children born to him in Jerusalem: Shammuah, Shobab, Nathan, and Solomon.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="2 Samuel 5:15">15</a> Ibhar, Elishua, Nepheg, and Japhia were also there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="2 Samuel 5:16">16</a> Elishama, Eliada, and Eliphalet.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="2 Samuel 5:17">17</a> When the Philistines learned that David had been made king over Israel, they all went to look for David; and when David heard about it, he went to the fortress.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="2 Samuel 5:18">18</a> The Philistines came and scattered throughout the valley of Rephaim.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="2 Samuel 5:19">19</a> David asked God, &quot;Should I go to fight the Philistines? Will you give them to me?&quot; God said to David, &quot;Go, because I will surely give the Philistines to you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="2 Samuel 5:20">20</a> David came to Baalperazim, David defeated them there, and said, &quot;God has burst through my enemies before me, like a flood.&quot; So he named that place Baalperazim.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="2 Samuel 5:21">21</a> There they left their idols, and David and his men burned them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="2 Samuel 5:22">22</a> The Philistines came again and spread out in Rephaim Valley.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="2 Samuel 5:23">23</a> When David asked God, He said, &quot;Do not attack them straight on; instead, go around them and surprise them near the mulberry trees.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="2 Samuel 5:24">24</a> When you hear a sound like marching in the tops of the mulberry trees, act quickly, for that means God is going ahead of you to defeat the Philistine army.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="2 Samuel 5:25">25</a> David did as God had commanded him and struck down the Philistines from Geba to Gazer.</p>
