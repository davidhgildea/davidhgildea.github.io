<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="2 Samuel 23:1">1</a> These are the final words of David. David, Jesse&#39;s son, spoke, the man lifted up, chosen by the God of Jacob, and Israel&#39;s beloved song writer, said.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="2 Samuel 23:2">2</a> God&#39;s Spirit spoke through me, and His words were on my tongue.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="2 Samuel 23:3">3</a> The God of Israel said, the Rock of Israel spoke to me: Whoever leads people must be fair, leading with respect for God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="2 Samuel 23:4">4</a> He will be like the light of dawn when the sun rises, on a cloudless morning; like fresh grass growing from the ground when the sun shines brightly after rain.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="2 Samuel 23:5">5</a> Even though my family is not close to God, He has made a lasting agreement with me, well-planned and certain; this is all that saves me and all I want, even though He doesn&#39;t make it prosper.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="2 Samuel 23:6">6</a> The wicked will all be like thorns pushed aside, because they cannot be picked up with hands.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="2 Samuel 23:7">7</a> Anyone who touches them must be armed with iron and a spear&#39;s shaft; they will be completely burned with fire right there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="2 Samuel 23:8">8</a> These are the names of David&#39;s strong warriors: The leader is the Tachmonite, who sat in the main chair, was Adino the Eznite. He raised his spear and beat eight hundred enemies at once.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="2 Samuel 23:9">9</a> After him came Eleazar, son of Dodo the Ahohite. He was one of David&#39;s three brave warriors. He stood up to the Philistines gathered for battle when the Israelites had retreated.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="2 Samuel 23:10">10</a> He got up, fought the Philistines until his hand got tired, and his hand stuck to the sword. God gave a great victory that day, and the people followed him just to take things from the defeated.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="2 Samuel 23:11">11</a> After him came Shammah, Agee the Hararite&#39;s son. The Philistines grouped into a force at a field of lentils, but the people ran away from the Philistines.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="2 Samuel 23:12">12</a> He stood in the middle of the field, defended it, killed the Philistines, and God gave a great victory.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="2 Samuel 23:13">13</a> Three of the top thirty soldiers went to see David at Adullam cave during harvest time, while the Philistine army camped in Rephaim Valley.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="2 Samuel 23:14">14</a> David was in a stronghold, and the Philistines&#39; fort was in Bethlehem at that time.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="2 Samuel 23:15">15</a> David wished and said, &quot;I want someone to bring me water from the Bethlehem well near the gate!&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="2 Samuel 23:16">16</a> The three strong men fought through the Philistine army, and got water from the Bethlehem well near the gate, and brought it to David. But he wouldn&#39;t drink it and instead poured it out to God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="2 Samuel 23:17">17</a> He said, &quot;I could never do this, God! Isn&#39;t this the blood of the men who risked their lives?&quot; So he refused to drink it. This is what the three brave men did.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="2 Samuel 23:18">18</a> Abishai, Joab&#39;s brother and Zeruiah&#39;s son, was the leader of the three. He raised his spear against three hundred and killed them, earning fame among the three.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="2 Samuel 23:19">19</a> Wasn&#39;t he the most respected of the three? So he became their leader, but he did not reach the level of the top three.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="2 Samuel 23:20">20</a> Benaiah, son of Jehoiada and a brave man from Kabzeel, did many brave things. He killed two strong Moabite warriors and also killed a lion in a pit on a snowy day.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="2 Samuel 23:21">21</a> He killed an Egyptian, a handsome man. The Egyptian carried a spear, but he confronted him with only a stick, took the spear from the Egyptian, and killed him with it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="2 Samuel 23:22">22</a> Benaiah, son of Jehoiada, did these things and was famous among the three mighty men.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="2 Samuel 23:23">23</a> He was more respected than the thirty, but he did not reach the level of the top three. David made him the leader of his bodyguards.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="2 Samuel 23:24">24</a> Asahel, Joab&#39;s brother, was one of the thirty; Elhanan, the son of Dodo from Bethlehem.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="2 Samuel 23:25">25</a> Shammah from Harod, Elika from Harod,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="2 Samuel 23:26">26</a> Helez from Palti, Ira the son of Ikkesh from Tekoa,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="2 Samuel 23:27">27</a> Abiezer from Anathoth, Mebunnai the Hushathite,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="2 Samuel 23:28">28</a> Zalmon from Ahoah, Maharai from Netophah,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="2 Samuel 23:29">29</a> Heleb, Baanah&#39;s son, from Netophah; Ittai, Ribai&#39;s son, from Gibeah in Benjamin.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="2 Samuel 23:30">30</a> Benaiah from Pirathon, Hiddai from the Gaash valleys,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="2 Samuel 23:31">31</a> Abialbon from Arbath, Azmaveth from Barhum,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="2 Samuel 23:32">32</a> Eliahba from Shaalbim, a son of Jashen, Jonathan,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="2 Samuel 23:33">33</a> Shammah from Harar, Ahiam, son of Sharar from Harar,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="2 Samuel 23:34">34</a> Eliphelet, Ahasbai&#39;s son, from Maachah, and Eliam, Ahithophel&#39;s son, from Gilo.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="2 Samuel 23:35">35</a> Hezrai from Carmel, Paarai from Arab.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="2 Samuel 23:36">36</a> Igal, Nathan&#39;s son from Zobah, and Bani from Gad,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="2 Samuel 23:37">37</a> Zelek from Ammon, Naharai from Beeroth, Joab&#39;s son of Zeruiah&#39;s armor carrier,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="2 Samuel 23:38">38</a> Ira from Ithra, Gareb from Ithra,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="2 Samuel 23:39">39</a> Uriah the Hittite: thirty-seven total.</p>
