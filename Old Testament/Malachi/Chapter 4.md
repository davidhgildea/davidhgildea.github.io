<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Malachi 4:1">1</a> Look, the day is coming, it will burn like a furnace; all the proud and evil-doers will be like dry grass: the coming day will burn them up, God says, leaving them without root or branch.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Malachi 4:2">2</a> For those of you who respect my name, the Sun of goodness will rise with healing rays; and you will go out and play freely, like young calves let out of their pens.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Malachi 4:3">3</a> You will walk on the wicked; they will be like dust under your feet on the day I do this, says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Malachi 4:4">4</a> Remember the laws Moses, my servant, gave you, which God told him at Horeb for all Israel, including the rules and decisions.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Malachi 4:5">5</a> Look, I will send you the prophet Elijah before the great and terrible day of God comes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Malachi 4:6">6</a> He will make fathers&#39; hearts close to their children and children&#39;s hearts close to their fathers, so I won&#39;t come and punish the earth with a curse.</p>
