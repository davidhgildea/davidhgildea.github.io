<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Malachi 2:1">1</a> And now, priests, this order is for you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Malachi 2:2">2</a> If you won&#39;t listen and don&#39;t take seriously giving honor to my name, says God of all, I will send a curse on you, and I will turn your blessings into curses—indeed, I have already done it because you don&#39;t take it to heart.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Malachi 2:3">3</a> Look, I will ruin your crops and put waste on your faces, the waste from your special feasts; and someone will carry you off with it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Malachi 2:4">4</a> You will understand that I sent this order to you so that my agreement would be with Levi, says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Malachi 2:5">5</a> I made a promise to him of life and peace; and I gave them to him because he respected me and was in awe of my name.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Malachi 2:6">6</a> He spoke the truth, and no wrong was on his lips; he walked with me in peace and fairness, turning many from sin.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Malachi 2:7">7</a> The priest&#39;s mouth should share knowledge, and people should look for teaching from him, because he is God&#39;s messenger.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Malachi 2:8">8</a> You have left the right path; you made many people trip over the law; you have ruined the agreement of Levi, says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Malachi 2:9">9</a> Because of this, I have made you despised and lowly in front of everyone, because you have not followed my ways and have been unfair in applying the law.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Malachi 2:10">10</a> Don&#39;t we all have one father? Didn&#39;t one God make us? Why then do we betray each other, breaking the agreement of our ancestors?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Malachi 2:11">11</a> Judah has been unfaithful, and a terrible thing has happened in Israel and Jerusalem; Judah has disgraced the sacredness of God, which he cherished, and has married a woman who worships a different god.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Malachi 2:12">12</a> God will remove the man who does this, both the leader and the follower, from the homes of Jacob, and anyone who makes an offering to God of hosts.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Malachi 2:13">13</a> You have done this again, making God&#39;s altar wet with tears, weeping, and loud crying, so much that God doesn&#39;t care about the offerings, or accept them happily from you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Malachi 2:14">14</a> But you ask, &quot;Why?&quot; It is because God has been a witness between you and the wife of your youth, whom you have betrayed, although she is your partner, the wife you promised to stay with.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Malachi 2:15">15</a> Did not God make them one? Yet he had a spirit left. Why one? To find a godly offspring. So watch over your spirit, and do not betray the wife of your youth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Malachi 2:16">16</a> God, the God of Israel, says he hates divorce: for it covers one&#39;s clothes with violence, says God of the armies: so be careful in your spirit, and do not be unfaithful.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Malachi 2:17">17</a> You have tired God with your words. But you ask, &quot;How have we tired him?&quot; When you say, &quot;Anyone who does evil is good to God, and he enjoys them,&quot; or &quot;Where is the God who judges?&quot;</p>
