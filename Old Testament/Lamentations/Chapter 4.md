<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Lamentations 4:1">1</a> The gold has lost its shine! The purest gold is no longer bright! The sacred stones are scattered at the beginning of every street.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Lamentations 4:2">2</a> The valuable children of Zion, once worth as much as fine gold, are now treated like common clay pots made by a potter.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Lamentations 4:3">3</a> Sea creatures nurse their young, but my people have become heartless, like ostriches in the desert.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Lamentations 4:4">4</a> The baby&#39;s tongue sticks to the top of its mouth because it&#39;s so thirsty; the little children beg for bread, but nobody gives any to them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Lamentations 4:5">5</a> People who ate fine foods are now alone in the streets; those who wore red clothes hug trash heaps.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Lamentations 4:6">6</a> The punishment for my people&#39;s wrongs is worse than what Sodom faced, which was destroyed suddenly without anyone helping.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Lamentations 4:7">7</a> Her holy people were cleaner than snow, they were brighter than milk, their bodies were redder than jewels, and they shone like sapphires.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Lamentations 4:8">8</a> Their faces are darker than black; they are unrecognizable in the streets: their skin sticks to their bones; it is dried up and has become like a stick.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Lamentations 4:9">9</a> People killed by the sword are better off than those who die of hunger, for the hungry waste away, suffering for lack of food from the fields.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Lamentations 4:10">10</a> Compassionate women have cooked their own children; they became their food during my people&#39;s ruin.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Lamentations 4:11">11</a> God has carried out his anger; he has unleashed his intense wrath and started a fire in Zion that has consumed its foundations.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Lamentations 4:12">12</a> Earth&#39;s rulers and everyone living in the world would not have believed that the foe and enemy could come into Jerusalem&#39;s gates.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Lamentations 4:13">13</a> Because her prophets sinned and her priests did wrong, spilling the blood of innocent people among her,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Lamentations 4:14">14</a> They wandered around like blind people in the streets; they made themselves dirty with blood, so no one could touch their clothes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Lamentations 4:15">15</a> They called out to them, &quot;Leave; you are unclean; go away, do not touch.&quot; As they ran and roamed, among the nations it was said, &quot;They will not stay there anymore.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Lamentations 4:16">16</a> God&#39;s anger has scattered them; He will no longer look after them: they did not respect the priests, they did not honor the old people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Lamentations 4:17">17</a> We keep looking for help that never comes, and we have waited for a country that cannot rescue us.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Lamentations 4:18">18</a> They chase us so we can&#39;t walk in our streets; our end is close, our days are over, for our end has arrived.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Lamentations 4:19">19</a> Our enemies chase us faster than eagles in the sky; they chased us on the mountains and set traps for us in the desert.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Lamentations 4:20">20</a> The one we breathed through, God&#39;s chosen one, was captured by them, and we thought we would live safely under his protection even among foreign nations.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Lamentations 4:21">21</a> Be happy and glad, you daughter of Edom, living in the land of Uz; the cup will also come to you: you will get drunk and undress.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Lamentations 4:22">22</a> Your punishment is finished, daughter of Zion; you won&#39;t be taken away anymore: but he will punish your sins, daughter of Edom; he will expose your wrongs.</p>
