<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Lamentations 3:1">1</a> I am the man who has seen suffering because of his anger.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Lamentations 3:2">2</a> He has guided me and taken me to a place of darkness, not light.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Lamentations 3:3">3</a> Certainly, he has turned against me; he keeps acting against me all day long.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Lamentations 3:4">4</a> He made my skin and flesh old; he broke my bones.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Lamentations 3:5">5</a> He has built against me and surrounded me with bitterness and hardship.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Lamentations 3:6">6</a> He has put me in dark places, like those long dead.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Lamentations 3:7">7</a> God has surrounded me so I can&#39;t escape; He has made my chains heavy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Lamentations 3:8">8</a> When I cry and yell, God ignores my prayer.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Lamentations 3:9">9</a> He has blocked my paths with cut stone; he has made my ways twisted.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Lamentations 3:10">10</a> He was to me like a bear waiting to attack, and like a lion hiding.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Lamentations 3:11">11</a> He has changed my path and torn me apart; he has left me lonely.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Lamentations 3:12">12</a> He has aimed his bow, and made me the target for his arrow.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Lamentations 3:13">13</a> He made the arrows from his bag hit my body.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Lamentations 3:14">14</a> I was mocked by all my people; they sang about me all day long.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Lamentations 3:15">15</a> God has filled me with bitterness; he has made me very bitter.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Lamentations 3:16">16</a> He has also broken my teeth with gravel stones; he has covered me with ashes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Lamentations 3:17">17</a> You took my peace far away; I forgot what good fortune is.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Lamentations 3:18">18</a> I said, &quot;My strength and my hope are gone because of God.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Lamentations 3:19">19</a> I remember my suffering and my sadness, the bitterness and the poison.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Lamentations 3:20">20</a> I still remember them deep in my heart, and I am humbled.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Lamentations 3:21">21</a> I remember this and so I have hope.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Lamentations 3:22">22</a> It is because of God&#39;s kindness that we are not destroyed, for His compassion never ends.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Lamentations 3:23">23</a> Every morning they are new; your faithfulness is great.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Lamentations 3:24">24</a> God is all I need, says my soul; so I will put my hope in Him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Lamentations 3:25">25</a> God is good to those who wait for Him, to the person who looks for Him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Lamentations 3:26">26</a> It is good for a person to have hope and to wait quietly for God to save.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Lamentations 3:27">27</a> It is good for a person to deal with hard times when young.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Lamentations 3:28">28</a> He sits alone and stays quiet because he has taken it upon himself.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Lamentations 3:29">29</a> He puts his mouth to the ground; perhaps there is hope.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Lamentations 3:30">30</a> He offers his face to the one who hits him; he is overwhelmed with insults.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Lamentations 3:31">31</a> God will not reject forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Lamentations 3:32">32</a> Even though God may bring sadness, He will show mercy because of His great kindness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Lamentations 3:33">33</a> God does not cause pain or sadness to people without reason.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Lamentations 3:34">34</a> To step on all the captives of the earth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Lamentations 3:35">35</a> To deny a person justice in the presence of God,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Lamentations 3:36">36</a> God does not approve of ruining a person&#39;s case.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Lamentations 3:37">37</a> Who is the one who can make something happen if God has not commanded it?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Lamentations 3:38">38</a> Doesn&#39;t both good and bad come from the mouth of God?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="Lamentations 3:39">39</a> Why should a living person complain, a person for being punished for their sins?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="Lamentations 3:40">40</a> Let&#39;s examine our ways, and turn back to God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="Lamentations 3:41">41</a> Let&#39;s raise our hearts and hands to God in heaven.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="Lamentations 3:42">42</a> We have done wrong and rebelled; you have not forgiven us.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v43"><a class="verse-num" href="#v43" aria-label="Lamentations 3:43">43</a> You have been angry with us, chased us, killed us without mercy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v44"><a class="verse-num" href="#v44" aria-label="Lamentations 3:44">44</a> You have surrounded yourself with a cloud, so our prayers cannot get through.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v45"><a class="verse-num" href="#v45" aria-label="Lamentations 3:45">45</a> You have made us like trash and garbage among the people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v46"><a class="verse-num" href="#v46" aria-label="Lamentations 3:46">46</a> All our enemies have spoken against us.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v47"><a class="verse-num" href="#v47" aria-label="Lamentations 3:47">47</a> Fear and a trap have come upon us, along with ruin and destruction.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v48"><a class="verse-num" href="#v48" aria-label="Lamentations 3:48">48</a> My eyes flow with tears for the ruin of my people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v49"><a class="verse-num" href="#v49" aria-label="Lamentations 3:49">49</a> My eye cries non-stop without any break.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v50"><a class="verse-num" href="#v50" aria-label="Lamentations 3:50">50</a> Until God looks down from heaven and sees.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v51"><a class="verse-num" href="#v51" aria-label="Lamentations 3:51">51</a> My eyes hurt my heart because of what happens to the girls of my city.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v52"><a class="verse-num" href="#v52" aria-label="Lamentations 3:52">52</a> My enemies chased me hard, like a bird, for no reason.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v53"><a class="verse-num" href="#v53" aria-label="Lamentations 3:53">53</a> They have ended my life in the prison, and thrown a stone at me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v54"><a class="verse-num" href="#v54" aria-label="Lamentations 3:54">54</a> Water poured over my head; then I thought, I am finished.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v55"><a class="verse-num" href="#v55" aria-label="Lamentations 3:55">55</a> I called your name, God, from the deep pit.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v56"><a class="verse-num" href="#v56" aria-label="Lamentations 3:56">56</a> You have heard my voice; do not ignore me when I sigh and cry out.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v57"><a class="verse-num" href="#v57" aria-label="Lamentations 3:57">57</a> You came close when I called you; you said, Don&#39;t be afraid.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v58"><a class="verse-num" href="#v58" aria-label="Lamentations 3:58">58</a> God, you have fought for my soul; you have saved my life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v59"><a class="verse-num" href="#v59" aria-label="Lamentations 3:59">59</a> God, you have seen the wrong done to me: judge my case.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v60"><a class="verse-num" href="#v60" aria-label="Lamentations 3:60">60</a> You have seen all their revenge and all their plans against me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v61"><a class="verse-num" href="#v61" aria-label="Lamentations 3:61">61</a> You have heard their insults, God, and all their plans against me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v62"><a class="verse-num" href="#v62" aria-label="Lamentations 3:62">62</a> The words of those against me and their plans all day long.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v63"><a class="verse-num" href="#v63" aria-label="Lamentations 3:63">63</a> I see them when they sit and when they stand; I am their music.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v64"><a class="verse-num" href="#v64" aria-label="Lamentations 3:64">64</a> Give them what they deserve, God, based on what they have done.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v65"><a class="verse-num" href="#v65" aria-label="Lamentations 3:65">65</a> Make their hearts sad, and put your curse on them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v66"><a class="verse-num" href="#v66" aria-label="Lamentations 3:66">66</a> Punish and wipe them out in anger from beneath the skies of God.</p>
