<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Lamentations 2:1">1</a> Why has God covered Zion&#39;s people with darkness in his anger? He has thrown down Israel&#39;s beauty from the sky to the ground and forgotten his footstool in his anger.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Lamentations 2:2">2</a> God has destroyed all of Jacob&#39;s homes, without mercy. He has torn down Judah&#39;s defenses in anger. He has brought them to the ground and dishonored the kingdom and its rulers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Lamentations 2:3">3</a> God has cut off all of Israel&#39;s strength in his fierce anger; he has pulled back his support facing the enemy, and he burned against Jacob like a fire that consumes everything around it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Lamentations 2:4">4</a> He has aimed his bow like an enemy; he stood ready with his right hand like an opponent, and killed everything beautiful in Zion&#39;s place of worship; he unleashed his anger like fire.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Lamentations 2:5">5</a> God was like an enemy: He swallowed up Israel, destroyed all its palaces, ruined its strongholds, and made the people of Judah mourn and cry.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Lamentations 2:6">6</a> He has forcefully taken away his shelter, as if from a garden; he has ruined the meeting places: God made the special feasts and rest days be forgotten in Zion, and has shown anger towards the king and the priest.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Lamentations 2:7">7</a> God has rejected his altar, hated his holy place, let enemies take over the palace walls; they are loud in God&#39;s house, like during a big celebration.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Lamentations 2:8">8</a> God decided to tear down Zion&#39;s wall: He marked it for destruction and didn&#39;t stop. So, the defenses and wall mourn; they fall apart together.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Lamentations 2:9">9</a> Her gates have fallen into the ground; he has destroyed and broken her bars: her king and leaders are among the nations: there is no law anymore; her prophets also receive no visions from God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Lamentations 2:10">10</a> The leaders of Zion&#39;s people sit quietly on the ground. They have thrown dust on their heads and wear rough cloth. The young women of Jerusalem look down sadly.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Lamentations 2:11">11</a> I cry until I can cry no more, I am deeply upset, and I feel broken inside because my people are destroyed; even the children and babies are fainting in the city streets.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Lamentations 2:12">12</a> They ask their mothers, &quot;Where is food and drink?&quot; when they faint like those hurt in the city streets, when their life spills into their mothers&#39; laps.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Lamentations 2:13">13</a> What can I use to testify for you? What can I compare you to, O daughter of Jerusalem? What can I make equal to you to comfort you, O virgin daughter of Zion? Your wound is as big as the sea; who can heal you?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Lamentations 2:14">14</a> Your prophets have seen useless and foolish visions for you; they have not revealed your sins to free you from captivity; instead, they have presented you with false troubles and reasons for exile.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Lamentations 2:15">15</a> Everyone who goes by claps at you; they hiss and shake their heads at the city of Jerusalem, asking, &quot;Is this the city people say is so beautiful, the joy of the whole world?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Lamentations 2:16">16</a> All your enemies have spoken against you: they hiss and grind their teeth: they say, We have swallowed her up: surely this is the day we waited for; we have found it, we have seen it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Lamentations 2:17">17</a> God did what he planned; he completed his word from long ago: he destroyed without mercy; he let your enemies celebrate over you, he gave strength to your rivals.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Lamentations 2:18">18</a> Their heart called out to God, O wall of Zion&#39;s people, let tears flow like a river day and night: do not stop; do not let your eyes stop crying.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Lamentations 2:19">19</a> Get up, yell out at night: at the start of the guard shifts, pour out your feelings like water before God: raise your hands to him for the lives of your young children, who are weak from hunger on every street corner.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Lamentations 2:20">20</a> Look, God, and see who you have done this to. Will women eat their own children, those a hand&#39;s breadth long? Will the priest and prophet be killed in God&#39;s holy place?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Lamentations 2:21">21</a> Both young and old are lying on the streets; my young women and men have been killed by the sword. You have killed them in your anger; you showed no mercy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Lamentations 2:22">22</a> You called my fears around me like on a special day, so that when God was angry, no one escaped or was left: my enemy destroyed those I cared for and raised.</p>
