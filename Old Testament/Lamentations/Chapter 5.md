<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Lamentations 5:1">1</a> Remember, God, what has happened to us; look, and see our disgrace.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Lamentations 5:2">2</a> Our property has gone to outsiders, our homes to foreigners.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Lamentations 5:3">3</a> We are like children without parents; our mothers are like women whose husbands have died.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Lamentations 5:4">4</a> We have to buy our own water to drink; we must pay for the wood we use.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Lamentations 5:5">5</a> We are persecuted and work hard without rest.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Lamentations 5:6">6</a> We have submitted to the Egyptians and Assyrians to have enough bread.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Lamentations 5:7">7</a> Our ancestors did wrong, they are gone; and we suffer for their sins.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Lamentations 5:8">8</a> Slaves have become our masters: no one saves us from their power.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Lamentations 5:9">9</a> We get our bread at the risk of our lives because of the danger in the desert.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Lamentations 5:10">10</a> Our skin turned very dark as if burned because of the terrible hunger.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Lamentations 5:11">11</a> They assaulted the women in Zion, and the young women in the towns of Judah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Lamentations 5:12">12</a> Leaders are hung by their hands; the faces of the old are not respected.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Lamentations 5:13">13</a> They made the young men work at grinding grain, and the children stumbled under heavy loads of wood.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Lamentations 5:14">14</a> The older leaders no longer sit in the city gates, and the young men have stopped their music.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Lamentations 5:15">15</a> Our happiness has ended; our dancing has changed to sadness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Lamentations 5:16">16</a> Our crown has fallen: sad for us, because we have done wrong!</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Lamentations 5:17">17</a> Because of this, we are discouraged; for these things, our vision is blurred.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Lamentations 5:18">18</a> Because Mount Zion is empty, foxes walk on it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Lamentations 5:19">19</a> You, God, last forever; your rule goes on from one generation to the next.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Lamentations 5:20">20</a> Why do you forget us forever and abandon us for such a long time?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Lamentations 5:21">21</a> Bring us back to you, God, and we will return; make our days like they used to be.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Lamentations 5:22">22</a> You have completely rejected us; you are very angry with us.</p>
