<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Amos 2:1">1</a> God says, &quot;Because Moab sinned three times, even four, I will not cancel their punishment. They burned the bones of Edom&#39;s king to ashes.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Amos 2:2">2</a> I will send fire to Moab, and it will burn the palaces of Kirioth. Moab will die amid noise, shouts, and trumpet blasts.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Amos 2:3">3</a> I will remove the judge from the middle and kill all the leaders with him, says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Amos 2:4">4</a> God says this: Because Judah has sinned three times, even four, I won&#39;t stop their punishment. They rejected God&#39;s law and did not follow His rules. Their lies led them astray, like their ancestors before them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Amos 2:5">5</a> I will send fire to Judah, and it will burn down the palaces of Jerusalem.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Amos 2:6">6</a> God says: Because Israel has sinned three and even four times, I won&#39;t stop their punishment. They traded innocent people for money and the needy for sandals.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Amos 2:7">7</a> They chase after worthless things instead of helping the poor and twist the paths of the humble. Both a man and his father misuse the same girl, dishonoring my sacred name.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Amos 2:8">8</a> They lie down on clothes taken as pledges near every altar, and they drink wine taken as fines in the temple of their god.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Amos 2:9">9</a> But I destroyed the Amorite before them, who was as tall as cedar trees and as strong as oaks; I destroyed their fruit from above and their roots from below.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Amos 2:10">10</a> I also took you out of Egypt and guided you for 40 years in the desert to take over the land of the Amorites.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Amos 2:11">11</a> I chose some of your sons to be prophets and some of your young men to be Nazarites. Isn&#39;t it true, people of Israel? says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Amos 2:12">12</a> But you gave the Nazarites wine to drink and told the prophets not to speak God&#39;s words.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Amos 2:13">13</a> Look, I am weighed down by you, like a cart is weighed down when it is full of grain bundles.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Amos 2:14">14</a> So the fast will not escape, the strong will not use their strength, and the brave will not save themselves.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Amos 2:15">15</a> The person using the bow won&#39;t be able to stand; the fast runner won&#39;t save themselves; and the horse rider won&#39;t escape.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Amos 2:16">16</a> The brave one among the warriors will run away without clothes on that day, says God.</p>
