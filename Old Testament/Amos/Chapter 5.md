<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Amos 5:1">1</a> Listen to this message I speak against you, which is a sad song, O people of Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Amos 5:2">2</a> The young woman of Israel has fallen; she will not get up again: she is left alone on her land; there is no one to help her up.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Amos 5:3">3</a> God says: The city that sends out a thousand will only have a hundred left, and the one that sends out a hundred will only have ten left for the people of Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Amos 5:4">4</a> God says to the people of Israel, &quot;Look for me, and you will live.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Amos 5:5">5</a> Do not look for Bethel, do not go to Gilgal, and do not travel to Beersheba. Gilgal will be taken captive, and Bethel will be destroyed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Amos 5:6">6</a> Look for God, and you will live; or he might burst out like fire in Joseph&#39;s house, consume it, and no one in Bethel can put it out.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Amos 5:7">7</a> You who make fair decisions bitter, and abandon what is right in the world,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Amos 5:8">8</a> Look for the one who created the seven stars and Orion, who changes deep darkness into morning, and turns day into night: who commands the sea waters and pours them over the earth: God is his name.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Amos 5:9">9</a> God gives power to the weak to stand against the mighty, so the weak can attack the stronghold.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Amos 5:10">10</a> They dislike the person who corrects others publicly, and they detest the one who speaks honestly.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Amos 5:11">11</a> Since you step on the poor and take their wheat, you build stone houses but won&#39;t live in them; you plant vineyards but won&#39;t drink their wine.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Amos 5:12">12</a> I know your many wrongs and your big sins: they hurt good people, they accept bribes, and they push away the poor from getting their rights at the gate.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Amos 5:13">13</a> So the wise will stay quiet at that time, because it is a bad time.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Amos 5:14">14</a> Look for good, not bad, so you may live, and then God will be with you as you have said.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Amos 5:15">15</a> Dislike what&#39;s bad, like what&#39;s good, and set up fair decisions in your communities. Maybe then God will show kindness to the survivors of Joseph&#39;s family.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Amos 5:16">16</a> So God, the Lord of all, says: There will be crying in all the streets and people will say in every road, &quot;Oh no, oh no!&quot; Farmers will be called to grieve, and those good at grieving to cry.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Amos 5:17">17</a> In all the grape gardens there will be crying, because I will come through you, says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Amos 5:18">18</a> You who wish for God&#39;s day, why do you want it? That day is dark, not light.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Amos 5:19">19</a> It&#39;s like a man running from a lion, only to meet a bear; or he goes into his house, rests his hand on the wall, and a snake bites him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Amos 5:20">20</a> Won&#39;t God&#39;s day be full of darkness, without any light? Very dark, with no brightness at all?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Amos 5:21">21</a> I hate and reject your festivals, and I won&#39;t enjoy your formal gatherings.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Amos 5:22">22</a> Even if you give me burnt sacrifices and food offerings, I will not accept them: I will not consider the peace sacrifices of your fat animals.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Amos 5:23">23</a> Stop singing your songs to me; I won&#39;t listen to your music.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Amos 5:24">24</a> Let fair judgment flow like water, and let justice be strong like a flowing river.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Amos 5:25">25</a> Did you give me sacrifices and offerings in the desert for forty years, O people of Israel?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Amos 5:26">26</a> You have carried the tent of your god Moloch and the star of your god Chiun, which you made for yourselves.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Amos 5:27">27</a> So I will make you captive and send you away beyond Damascus, says God, who is called the God of armies.</p>
