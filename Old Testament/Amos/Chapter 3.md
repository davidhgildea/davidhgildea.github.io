<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Amos 3:1">1</a> Listen to what God has said against you, children of Israel, against the entire family I led out of Egypt:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Amos 3:2">2</a> I have known only you among all the families on earth; so I will punish you for all your wrongs.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Amos 3:3">3</a> Can two people walk together if they do not agree?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Amos 3:4">4</a> Will a lion roar in the woods without catching prey? Will a young lion shout from its den if it has caught nothing?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Amos 3:5">5</a> Can a bird be caught in a trap on the ground if no one has set one for it? Can someone lift a trap from the ground if it hasn&#39;t caught anything?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Amos 3:6">6</a> If a trumpet sounds in the city, won&#39;t the people be scared? If there&#39;s trouble in a city, isn&#39;t it God who has caused it?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Amos 3:7">7</a> God will do nothing without revealing his plan to his prophets.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Amos 3:8">8</a> The lion has roared, who wouldn&#39;t be afraid? God has spoken, who wouldn&#39;t tell the message?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Amos 3:9">9</a> Announce in the palaces of Ashdod and Egypt, say, &quot;Gather on the Samaria mountains and see the big upsets and the oppressed people there.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Amos 3:10">10</a> They do not know how to do right, says God, who keep violence and theft in their homes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Amos 3:11">11</a> So God says: An enemy will surround the country; they will reduce your power, and your grand houses will be robbed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Amos 3:12">12</a> God says, &quot;Like a shepherd saves from the lion&#39;s mouth two legs or a piece of an ear, so will the Israelites be saved who live in Samaria on the edge of a bed, and in Damascus on a sofa.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Amos 3:13">13</a> Listen and speak out in the house of Jacob, says God, the God of all.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Amos 3:14">14</a> On the day I punish Israel for their sins, I will also destroy the altars of Bethel; the altar horns will be cut off and fall to the ground.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Amos 3:15">15</a> I will destroy the winter homes and the summer homes; the ivory houses will be gone, and the big houses will end, says God.</p>
