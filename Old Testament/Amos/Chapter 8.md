<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Amos 8:1">1</a> God showed me this: Look, a basket of summer fruit.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Amos 8:2">2</a> He asked, &quot;Amos, what do you see?&quot; I answered, &quot;A basket of summer fruit.&quot; Then God told me, &quot;The end has come for my people Israel; I will not spare them anymore.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Amos 8:3">3</a> On that day, the temple songs will turn to wails, says God. Dead bodies will be everywhere; they will be thrown out quietly.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Amos 8:4">4</a> Listen, you who trample on the needy and try to destroy the poor of the land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Amos 8:5">5</a> Asking, &quot;When will the new moon pass so we can sell grain? And the Sabbath, so we can offer wheat? Reducing the measure, increasing the price, and cheating with dishonest scales?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Amos 8:6">6</a> So we can buy the poor with money, and the needy for a pair of shoes, and even sell the bad part of the wheat.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Amos 8:7">7</a> God has promised because of Jacob&#39;s greatness, I will surely remember their deeds always.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Amos 8:8">8</a> Won&#39;t the earth shake because of this, and all who live in it be sad? It will surge like a river; it will be thrown out and sink like the river of Egypt.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Amos 8:9">9</a> On that day, God says, I will make the sun set at noon and darken the earth on a bright day.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Amos 8:10">10</a> I will change your celebrations into sadness, and all your music into cries of sorrow; I will make everyone wear rough cloth around their waist, and shave every head; I will make the sadness as deep as for an only child, and the end as bitter as a terrible day.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Amos 8:11">11</a> Look, days will come, says God, when I will cause a famine in the land. It won&#39;t be for lack of bread or thirst for water, but a famine of hearing God&#39;s words.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Amos 8:12">12</a> They will wander from sea to sea, and from north to east, they will rush around trying to find God&#39;s message, but they will not find it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Amos 8:13">13</a> On that day, the beautiful young women and young men will become weak from thirst.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Amos 8:14">14</a> Those who swear by Samaria&#39;s sin and say, &quot;Your god, O Dan, is alive,&quot; and &quot;The way of Beersheba lives,&quot; will fall and never get up again.</p>
