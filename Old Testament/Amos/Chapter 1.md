<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Amos 1:1">1</a> These are the words of Amos, one of the shepherds from Tekoa. He had visions about Israel when Uzziah was king of Judah, and when Jeroboam, Joash&#39;s son, was king of Israel. This happened two years before the earthquake.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Amos 1:2">2</a> He said, God will roar from Zion and speak loudly from Jerusalem; the shepherds&#39; homes will be sad, and the top of Carmel will dry up.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Amos 1:3">3</a> God says: Because Damascus has sinned many times, I will not stop their punishment. They crushed Gilead with iron tools.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Amos 1:4">4</a> I will send a fire to Hazael&#39;s house, and it will destroy Benhadad&#39;s palaces.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Amos 1:5">5</a> I will break the defenses of Damascus, end the people from Aven Valley, and their leaders from Eden. The Syrians will be taken as prisoners to Kir, says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Amos 1:6">6</a> God says: Because Gaza has sinned many times, I won&#39;t stop their punishment. They took away a whole group of captives and handed them over to Edom.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Amos 1:7">7</a> I will send a fire against the wall of Gaza, and it will burn down its palaces.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Amos 1:8">8</a> I will remove the people from Ashdod and the ruler from Ashkelon, and I will attack Ekron. The remaining Philistines will die, says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Amos 1:9">9</a> God says, &quot;Because Tyre has sinned many times, I will not stop their punishment. They handed over captives to Edom and forgot their treaty of friendship.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Amos 1:10">10</a> I will send a fire on the wall of Tyre that will destroy its palaces.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Amos 1:11">11</a> God says: Because Edom sinned many times, I will not stop punishing them. They chased their own family with weapons, showed no mercy, stayed angry all the time, and never forgave.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Amos 1:12">12</a> I will send a fire to Teman, and it will burn down the strong houses of Bozrah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Amos 1:13">13</a> God says, &quot;Because the people of Ammon committed three sins, and even four, I will not stop their punishment. They attacked pregnant women in Gilead to expand their own land.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Amos 1:14">14</a> I will start a fire in the walls of Rabbah, and it will burn down its palaces amid battle cries on the day of combat, and with a storm on the day of the whirlwind.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Amos 1:15">15</a> Their king and his leaders will be captured and taken away, says God.</p>
