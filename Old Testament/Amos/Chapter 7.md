<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Amos 7:1">1</a> God showed me this: I saw him create locusts at the start of the crops&#39; second growth, after the king&#39;s harvest.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Amos 7:2">2</a> When they finished eating the land&#39;s grass, I said, &quot;Please God, forgive us. How can Jacob survive? He is too small.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Amos 7:3">3</a> God changed his mind about this: It will not happen, says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Amos 7:4">4</a> God showed me this: God called for judgment by fire, which consumed the deep waters and ate up the land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Amos 7:5">5</a> I said, &quot;Please stop, God. How can Jacob recover? He is too weak.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Amos 7:6">6</a> God changed his mind about this: This will not happen either, says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Amos 7:7">7</a> He showed me: and look, God stood on a wall built straight with a plumb line, holding a plumb line.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Amos 7:8">8</a> God asked me, &quot;Amos, what do you see?&quot; I answered, &quot;A plumbline.&quot; God said, &quot;Look, I will put a plumbline among my people Israel; I won&#39;t overlook their actions anymore.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Amos 7:9">9</a> The special places of Isaac will be empty, and the holy places of Israel will be ruined; and I will attack the family of Jeroboam with a sword.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Amos 7:10">10</a> Then Amaziah, the priest of Bethel, sent a message to Jeroboam, king of Israel, saying, &quot;Amos has plotted against you among the people of Israel: the country cannot handle everything he says.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Amos 7:11">11</a> Amos says this: Jeroboam will die by the sword, and Israel will definitely be taken captive from their land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Amos 7:12">12</a> Amaziah told Amos, &quot;You, the seer, go away to the land of Judah, eat your bread there, and give your messages there.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Amos 7:13">13</a> Do not prophesy again in Bethel; it is the king&#39;s temple and the royal palace.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Amos 7:14">14</a> Amos replied to Amaziah, &quot;I was not a prophet or the son of a prophet; I was a shepherd and I picked sycamore fruit.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Amos 7:15">15</a> God chose me as I tended the sheep and God told me, &quot;Go, speak my words to the people of Israel.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Amos 7:16">16</a> Listen now to God&#39;s message: You say, &quot;Do not speak against Israel, and do not say anything against the family of Isaac.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Amos 7:17">17</a> So God says: Your wife will be unfaithful in the city, your sons and daughters will be killed with swords, your land will be split up, and you will die in a dirty land. Israel will definitely be taken as prisoners out of their land.</p>
