<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Amos 6:1">1</a> Trouble awaits those who are comfortable in Zion and rely on Mount Samaria, the famous leaders of the nations where the people of Israel go!</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Amos 6:2">2</a> Go to Calneh and look; then visit the great city of Hamath, and afterward go to Gath of the Philistines. Are they stronger than these kingdoms? Is their land larger than yours?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Amos 6:3">3</a> You who push away the day of punishment and bring violence close.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Amos 6:4">4</a> They lie on ivory beds, lounge on their sofas, eat lambs from the flock, and calves from the pen.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Amos 6:5">5</a> They sing to the music of the harp and make up their own musical instruments, like David.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Amos 6:6">6</a> They drink wine from large bowls and use the finest oils to anoint themselves, but they do not care about the suffering of Joseph&#39;s people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Amos 6:7">7</a> So now they will be taken away as prisoners with those who were first taken away, and the feasting of those who lounged will end.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Amos 6:8">8</a> God has sworn by himself, says the God of all, I despise Jacob&#39;s pride and hate his strongholds: so I will hand over the city and everything in it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Amos 6:9">9</a> If ten people are left in one house, they will die.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Amos 6:10">10</a> A man&#39;s uncle will carry him, and the one who cremates him, to remove the bones from the house. He will ask anyone nearby, &quot;Is anyone else with you?&quot; When he says, &quot;No,&quot; he will be told, &quot;Be quiet; we must not speak of God&#39;s name.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Amos 6:11">11</a> Look, God commands, and he will strike the big house, making large cracks, and the small house with splits.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Amos 6:12">12</a> Can horses run on rocky ground? Can you plow with oxen on rocks? You have made fair decisions bitter, and turned what is right into poison.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Amos 6:13">13</a> You who celebrate meaningless things, saying, &quot;Haven&#39;t we gained power by our own strength?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Amos 6:14">14</a> But look, I will bring against you a nation, O people of Israel, says God, and they will trouble you from the entrance of Hemath to the wilderness river.</p>
