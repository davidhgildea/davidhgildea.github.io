<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Isaiah 46:1">1</a> Bel bends down, Nebo bows, their idols are on the animals and livestock: your carts carry a heavy load; they weigh down the tired animals.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Isaiah 46:2">2</a> They bend low, they kneel down together; they cannot save the heavy load, but they themselves are carried off as prisoners.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Isaiah 46:3">3</a> Listen to me, O family of Jacob, and all the survivors of the family of Israel, who have been supported by me since birth, whom I have carried since you were born.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Isaiah 46:4">4</a> Even when you are old, I am with you; I will support you when your hair turns gray. I created you, and I will take care of you; I will sustain you and save you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Isaiah 46:5">5</a> Who do you think is like me? Who can you say I am equal to or compare me with?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Isaiah 46:6">6</a> They pour out gold from their bags, weigh silver on scales, pay a goldsmith to make it into a god; then they bow down and worship it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Isaiah 46:7">7</a> They carry him on the shoulder, they lift him up, and put him in his spot, and he stands; he won&#39;t move from his spot. Even if someone calls to him, he can&#39;t reply or save that person from their problems.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Isaiah 46:8">8</a> Remember this and act like men: think about it again, you who break rules.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Isaiah 46:9">9</a> Think about the past, because I am God, and there is no other; I am God, and no one is like me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Isaiah 46:10">10</a> Telling the future from the start, and from long ago the things not yet happened, saying, My plan will last, and I will do all I want.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Isaiah 46:11">11</a> God calls a hungry bird from the east, the man who carries out my plans from a distant land: yes, I have said it, and I will make it happen; I have planned it, and I will do it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Isaiah 46:12">12</a> Listen to me, you strong-minded, who are far from doing right:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Isaiah 46:13">13</a> I am making my righteousness come soon; it won&#39;t be far away, and my help won&#39;t be delayed: I will set rescue in Zion, for Israel who I honor.</p>
