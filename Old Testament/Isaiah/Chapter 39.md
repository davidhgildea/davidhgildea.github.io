<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Isaiah 39:1">1</a> At that time, Merodachbaladan, son of Baladan and king of Babylon, sent letters and a gift to Hezekiah because he heard that Hezekiah had been ill and had gotten better.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Isaiah 39:2">2</a> Hezekiah was happy with them and showed them his treasury, the silver, gold, spices, fine oil, his armory, and everything in his treasures: he kept nothing hidden from them in his house or his rule.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Isaiah 39:3">3</a> Isaiah the prophet went to King Hezekiah and asked him, &quot;What did these men say, and where did they come from to visit you?&quot; Hezekiah replied, &quot;They came to me from a distant country, from Babylon.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Isaiah 39:4">4</a> He asked, &quot;What did they see in your house?&quot; And Hezekiah replied, &quot;They saw everything in my house; there&#39;s nothing in my treasures I didn&#39;t show them.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Isaiah 39:5">5</a> Then Isaiah said to Hezekiah, &quot;Listen to the message from God:&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Isaiah 39:6">6</a> The time will come when everything in your house and everything your ancestors saved up until now will be taken to Babylon; nothing will remain, says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Isaiah 39:7">7</a> From your children, whom you will father, they will take some to be eunuchs in the palace of the Babylonian king.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Isaiah 39:8">8</a> Hezekiah told Isaiah, &quot;God&#39;s message you gave is good. Also, there will be peace and truth while I live.&quot;</p>
