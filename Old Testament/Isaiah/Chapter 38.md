<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Isaiah 38:1">1</a> Hezekiah was very sick and near death. The prophet Isaiah, Amoz&#39;s son, came to him and said, &quot;God says, &#39;Get your home ready, because you are going to die; you will not survive.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Isaiah 38:2">2</a> Hezekiah turned to face the wall and prayed to God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Isaiah 38:3">3</a> He said, &quot;Please remember, God, how I have lived in truth before you with a faithful heart, doing what is good in your eyes.&quot; And Hezekiah cried a lot.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Isaiah 38:4">4</a> God spoke to Isaiah, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Isaiah 38:5">5</a> Go and tell Hezekiah, &#39;God, the God of your ancestor David, says: I have heard your prayer and seen your tears. Look, I will extend your life by fifteen years.&#39;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Isaiah 38:6">6</a> I will save you and this city from the king of Assyria&#39;s control, and I will protect this city.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Isaiah 38:7">7</a> This will be a sign for you from God: God will do what he has said.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Isaiah 38:8">8</a> Look, I will make the shadow that has moved down Ahaz&#39;s sundial go back ten steps. So the shadow moved back ten steps from where it had gone down.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Isaiah 38:9">9</a> This is the message from Hezekiah, the king of Judah, when he was sick and then got better from his illness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Isaiah 38:10">10</a> I said when my life was being cut short, I will go to the gates of death: I will miss out on the rest of my years.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Isaiah 38:11">11</a> I said, I will not see God, God, among those alive: I will no longer see people with those on earth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Isaiah 38:12">12</a> My life has passed and is gone like a shepherd&#39;s tent: I have ended my own life like a weaver: God will finish me off with wasting illness: from day to night you will end me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Isaiah 38:13">13</a> I thought until morning that, like a lion, he would break all my bones; from day to night you will finish me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Isaiah 38:14">14</a> Like a bird or a swallow, I chirped: I cried like a dove: my eyes are tired from looking up: Oh God, I am overwhelmed; help me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Isaiah 38:15">15</a> What should I say? God has spoken to me and made it happen: I will walk gently all my life feeling the bitterness in my heart.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Isaiah 38:16">16</a> God, by these things people live, and in all these is my spirit&#39;s life: so you will heal me and let me live.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Isaiah 38:17">17</a> Look, I was very bitter when I wanted peace, but because you love me, you saved me from total destruction; you have put all my sins out of sight.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Isaiah 38:18">18</a> The grave cannot praise you, death cannot honor you; those who go into the pit cannot hope for your truth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Isaiah 38:19">19</a> Living people will praise you, just like I do today; parents will tell their children about your truth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Isaiah 38:20">20</a> God was ready to save me, so we will sing my songs with stringed instruments every day of our life in God&#39;s house.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Isaiah 38:21">21</a> Isaiah said, &quot;Let them take a lump of figs and put it on the boil as a poultice, and he will get better.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Isaiah 38:22">22</a> Hezekiah also asked, &quot;What is the sign that I will go to God&#39;s house?&quot;</p>
