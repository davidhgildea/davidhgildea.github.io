<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Isaiah 7:1">1</a> During the time of Ahaz, Jotham&#39;s son, and grandson of Uzziah, king of Judah, Rezin the king of Syria and Pekah, Remaliah&#39;s son and king of Israel, attacked Jerusalem but could not defeat it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Isaiah 7:2">2</a> The house of David was informed, &quot;Syria has joined forces with Ephraim.&quot; Then his and his people&#39;s hearts shook like trees in the forest shake in the wind.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Isaiah 7:3">3</a> God told Isaiah, &quot;Go out now to meet Ahaz, along with your son Shearjashub, at the end of the water channel of the upper pool on the road to the launderer&#39;s field.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Isaiah 7:4">4</a> Tell him, &quot;Be careful and stay calm; don&#39;t be afraid or weak because of the dying fires of Rezin&#39;s anger with Syria, and the son of Remaliah.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Isaiah 7:5">5</a> Because Syria, Ephraim, and Remaliah&#39;s son have planned something bad against you, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Isaiah 7:6">6</a> Let&#39;s attack Judah, trouble it, make a gap for us, and place Tabeal&#39;s son as king there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Isaiah 7:7">7</a> God says, &quot;It will not happen, nor will it take place.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Isaiah 7:8">8</a> Syria&#39;s capital is Damascus, and Damascus&#39;s leader is Rezin. In 65 years, Ephraim will be destroyed and no longer be a nation.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Isaiah 7:9">9</a> Ephraim&#39;s capital is Samaria, and the leader of Samaria is Remaliah&#39;s son. If you don&#39;t believe, you will not last.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Isaiah 7:10">10</a> God spoke to Ahaz again, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Isaiah 7:11">11</a> Ask for a sign from God; you can ask for it to be deep down or high up.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Isaiah 7:12">12</a> Ahaz said, I will not ask, nor will I test God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Isaiah 7:13">13</a> He said, &quot;Listen, family of David; is it not enough for you to tire people? Will you also tire my God?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Isaiah 7:14">14</a> God will give you a sign: A young woman will become pregnant, have a son, and name him Immanuel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Isaiah 7:15">15</a> He will eat butter and honey to learn how to reject what is bad and choose what is good.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Isaiah 7:16">16</a> Before the child learns to reject evil and choose good, the land you hate will be abandoned by its two kings.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Isaiah 7:17">17</a> God will bring upon you, your people, and your ancestors&#39; house, times unlike any since Ephraim separated from Judah—the king of Assyria included.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Isaiah 7:18">18</a> In that day, God will call for the flies from Egypt&#39;s farthest rivers and the bees from Assyria.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Isaiah 7:19">19</a> They will come and rest in the empty valleys, in the rock crevices, and among all the thorny plants and bushes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Isaiah 7:20">20</a> On that day, God will use a hired razor, from the people across the river, the king of Assyria, to shave the head, the hair on the feet, and also remove the beard.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Isaiah 7:21">21</a> On that day, a person will take care of a young cow and two sheep.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Isaiah 7:22">22</a> Because there will be so much milk, people will eat butter; everyone left in the land will eat butter and honey.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Isaiah 7:23">23</a> On that day, every place with a thousand grapevines, each worth a thousand silver coins, will be overtaken by weeds and thorns.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Isaiah 7:24">24</a> People will come there with arrows and bows, because the whole land will be covered with thorns and briers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Isaiah 7:25">25</a> On every hill plowed with tools, there won&#39;t be fear of sharp plants; instead, it will be a place for oxen and for smaller animals to walk.</p>
