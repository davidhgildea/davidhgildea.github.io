<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Isaiah 24:1">1</a> Look, God will make the earth empty, destroy it, flip it over, and scatter its people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Isaiah 24:2">2</a> And it will happen to the people just like the priest; to the servant like his master; to the maid like her mistress; to the buyer like the seller; to the lender like the borrower; to the one who charges interest just like the one who pays it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Isaiah 24:3">3</a> The land will be completely emptied and totally ruined because God has said this.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Isaiah 24:4">4</a> The earth is sad and withers, the world weakens and withers, the proud people of the earth become weak.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Isaiah 24:5">5</a> The earth is polluted because of its people; they&#39;ve broken the laws, changed the rules, and violated the eternal agreement.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Isaiah 24:6">6</a> So the curse has consumed the earth, and those living in it are left empty; that&#39;s why the people of the earth are scorched, with only a few men remaining.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Isaiah 24:7">7</a> The new wine is sad, the vine is weak, and all the happy people sigh.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Isaiah 24:8">8</a> The sound of tambourines stops, the noise of celebrators ends, the happiness of the harp stops.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Isaiah 24:9">9</a> They won&#39;t enjoy wine with singing; hard liquor will taste bad to those who drink it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Isaiah 24:10">10</a> The city of chaos is destroyed: every house is closed so that no one can enter.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Isaiah 24:11">11</a> People in the streets cry for wine; all happiness has faded, the land&#39;s joy has disappeared.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Isaiah 24:12">12</a> The city is left in ruins, and the gate is struck with devastation.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Isaiah 24:13">13</a> When this happens across the land among the people, it will be like the shaking of an olive tree and like picking the last grapes after the harvest is over.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Isaiah 24:14">14</a> They will raise their voices, they will sing about God&#39;s greatness, they will shout loud from the sea.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Isaiah 24:15">15</a> So praise God when you are in trouble, and honor the name of the God of Israel in the islands of the sea.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Isaiah 24:16">16</a> We have heard songs of praise from the farthest parts of the earth, singing glory to the good people. But I cried out, &#39;I am so weak, so weak, how terrible for me! The cheaters have cheated; yes, the cheaters have cheated a lot.&#39;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Isaiah 24:17">17</a> Fear, a pit, and a trap are coming for you, people of the earth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Isaiah 24:18">18</a> It will happen that the person running away from the sound of terror will fall into a hole; and the one who gets out of the hole will be caught in a trap. This is because the skies above are open, and the earth&#39;s foundations are trembling.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Isaiah 24:19">19</a> The earth is completely shattered, the earth is totally melted, the earth is shaken a lot.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Isaiah 24:20">20</a> The earth will sway back and forth like a drunk person and will be moved like a hut; its wrongdoing will weigh it down, and it will fall, never to get up again.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Isaiah 24:21">21</a> On that day, God will punish the powerful beings above and the kings on the earth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Isaiah 24:22">22</a> They will be brought together like captives in a hole and locked up in jail, and after a long time, they will be checked on.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Isaiah 24:23">23</a> Then the moon will be embarrassed, and the sun ashamed, when God rules on Mount Zion and in Jerusalem, and before his elders with glory.</p>
