<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Isaiah 3:1">1</a> Look, God will remove from Jerusalem and Judah all support, the entire supply of bread, and all the water.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Isaiah 3:2">2</a> The strong man, the soldier, the judge, the prophet, the wise person, and the elder,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Isaiah 3:3">3</a> The leader of fifty, the respected man, the advisor, the skilled worker, and the good speaker.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Isaiah 3:4">4</a> I will make children their leaders, and young ones will rule over them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Isaiah 3:5">5</a> People will mistreat each other, neighbors will act badly towards one another, the young will disrespect the old, and the lowly will act rudely towards the respected.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Isaiah 3:6">6</a> If a man grabs his brother from his father&#39;s family and says, &quot;You have clothes, be our leader and take charge of this mess.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Isaiah 3:7">7</a> On that day, he will promise, saying, &quot;I do not want to heal; for there is neither food nor clothes in my house: do not make me a leader of the people.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Isaiah 3:8">8</a> Jerusalem is destroyed, and Judah has fallen, because their speech and actions are against God, angering his glorious presence.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Isaiah 3:9">9</a> Their faces show their guilt; they brag about their sins like Sodom and don&#39;t hide them. Trouble to them, for they have brought evil upon themselves.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Isaiah 3:10">10</a> Tell the good people that things will go well for them, because they will enjoy what their actions have earned.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Isaiah 3:11">11</a> Trouble will come to the bad person; he will be paid back for what he has done.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Isaiah 3:12">12</a> My people are pushed around by children and ruled by women. My people, your leaders mislead you and ruin your paths.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Isaiah 3:13">13</a> God stands up to argue his case and stands to judge the people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Isaiah 3:14">14</a> God will judge the elders and leaders of his people because you have destroyed the vineyard, and the robbed goods of the poor are in your homes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Isaiah 3:15">15</a> Why are you hurting my people and crushing the poor? says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Isaiah 3:16">16</a> God says, because the women of Zion are proud, walking with their heads high and flirting with their eyes, strutting as they go, and their feet make sounds.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Isaiah 3:17">17</a> So God will strike the tops of Zion&#39;s daughters&#39; heads with sores, and God will expose their private parts.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Isaiah 3:18">18</a> On that day, God will remove their ankle bracelets, headbands, and crescent necklaces.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Isaiah 3:19">19</a> The chains, the bracelets, and the scarves,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Isaiah 3:20">20</a> The caps, leg accessories, headbands, perfume boxes, and earrings,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Isaiah 3:21">21</a> The rings and nose rings</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Isaiah 3:22">22</a> The different sets of clothes, the cloaks, the headscarves, and the hairpins,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Isaiah 3:23">23</a> The eyeglasses, the nice cloth, the head coverings, and the scarves.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Isaiah 3:24">24</a> It will happen that where there was once perfume, there will be a bad smell; instead of a belt, torn clothes; instead of nicely done hair, baldness; instead of fine clothes, rough sackcloth; and scars will replace beauty.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Isaiah 3:25">25</a> Your men will die by the sword, and your strong will fall in battle.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Isaiah 3:26">26</a> Her gates will cry and grieve; and she, being empty, will sit on the ground.</p>
