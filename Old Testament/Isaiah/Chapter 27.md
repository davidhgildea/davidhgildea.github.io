<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Isaiah 27:1">1</a> On that day, God with His fierce, mighty sword will punish Leviathan, the sharp serpent, even Leviathan, the twisted serpent; and He will kill the sea dragon.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Isaiah 27:2">2</a> On that day, sing to her, &quot;A vineyard of red wine.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Isaiah 27:3">3</a> I am God and I take care of it; I will water it all the time so that no one damages it, I will protect it day and night.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Isaiah 27:4">4</a> I am not angry: who would challenge me with briers and thorns in battle? I would march through them, I would burn them all.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Isaiah 27:5">5</a> Let him grab my power so he can be at peace with me, and he will be at peace with me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Isaiah 27:6">6</a> He will help the descendants of Jacob to grow strong: Israel will bloom and flourish and spread their fruit all over the world.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Isaiah 27:7">7</a> Did God strike him as he struck those who attacked him? Or was he killed like those he killed?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Isaiah 27:8">8</a> When it grows, you will argue with it, but God calms the strong wind on the east wind&#39;s day.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Isaiah 27:9">9</a> Through this, Jacob&#39;s wrongdoing will be cleansed, and completely removing his sin will come from turning all the altar stones into crushed chalk; the sacred poles and idols will no longer remain standing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Isaiah 27:10">10</a> The protected city will become empty, the place left and forgotten like a desert: there the calf will graze, lie down, and eat its branches.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Isaiah 27:11">11</a> When its branches dry up, they get broken off. Women come to burn them. These people don&#39;t understand, so the one who created them will not pity them, and the one who shaped them will not be kind to them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Isaiah 27:12">12</a> On that day, God will clear the land from the Euphrates River to the brook of Egypt, and you, the people of Israel, will be gathered one by one.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Isaiah 27:13">13</a> On that day, a loud trumpet will sound, and those about to die in Assyria, along with the exiles in Egypt, will return and worship God on the holy mountain in Jerusalem.</p>
