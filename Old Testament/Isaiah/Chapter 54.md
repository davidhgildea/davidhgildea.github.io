<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Isaiah 54:1">1</a> Sing, you who couldn&#39;t have children; shout with joy, you who never gave birth. Be happy, because you have more children now than the woman who has a husband, says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Isaiah 54:2">2</a> Make your tent bigger and stretch out its covers. Don&#39;t hold back, make the ropes longer, and make the pegs stronger.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Isaiah 54:3">3</a> You will spread out to the right and to the left; your descendants will settle in other nations and live in empty towns.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Isaiah 54:4">4</a> Do not be afraid; you will not be embarrassed. Do not feel confused; you will not be disgraced. You will forget the shame of your youth and no longer remember the dishonor of being a widow.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Isaiah 54:5">5</a> Your Creator is your partner; God is his name; and your Savior, the Holy One of Israel, will be known as the God of the entire world.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Isaiah 54:6">6</a> God has chosen you like a woman abandoned and sad in spirit, like a young wife rejected, says your God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Isaiah 54:7">7</a> I left you alone for a short time, but I will bring you back with great kindness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Isaiah 54:8">8</a> I was upset with you for a short time and turned away; but I will show you endless kindness and compassion, says your God, the Redeemer.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Isaiah 54:9">9</a> This is like Noah&#39;s flood to me: just as I promised that Noah&#39;s flood would never again cover the earth, so I promise I will not be angry with you or scold you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Isaiah 54:10">10</a> Even if the mountains go away and the hills move, my kindness will not leave you and my peace agreement will not move, says God who has mercy on you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Isaiah 54:11">11</a> You who are troubled and storm-tossed, and without comfort, see, I will set your stones with pretty colors and lay your foundations with sapphires.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Isaiah 54:12">12</a> I will make your windows out of agate, your gates out of sparkling stones, and all your borders out of beautiful stones.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Isaiah 54:13">13</a> All your children will be taught by God, and they will have great peace.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Isaiah 54:14">14</a> You will be set up in what is right: you will be safe from being hurt and you won&#39;t be scared; and you won&#39;t be terrified because it won&#39;t get close to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Isaiah 54:15">15</a> Look, they will definitely come together, but not because of me: anyone who comes together against you will fail because of you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Isaiah 54:16">16</a> Look, I made the blacksmith who fans the coals into flame and forges a tool for his work; I also made the destroyer to ruin.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Isaiah 54:17">17</a> No weapon made against you will succeed, and you will prove wrong every tongue that accuses you. This is the right of God&#39;s servants, and their goodness comes from God.</p>
