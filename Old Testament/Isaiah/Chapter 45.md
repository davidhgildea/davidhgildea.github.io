<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Isaiah 45:1">1</a> God says to his chosen one, Cyrus, whose right hand I have held, to defeat nations for him; and I will weaken kings, to open gates with two parts for him; and the gates will not be closed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Isaiah 45:2">2</a> I will go ahead of you and make the twisted paths straight. I will break the bronze gates into pieces and cut through the iron bars.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Isaiah 45:3">3</a> I will give you treasures from dark places and hidden wealth from secret spots so you can know that I, God, who calls you by name, am the God of Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Isaiah 45:4">4</a> For the sake of my servant Jacob and my chosen Israel, I have called you by name and given you a special title, even though you do not know me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Isaiah 45:5">5</a> I am God, and there is no other; there is no other God besides me. I gave you strength, even though you did not know me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Isaiah 45:6">6</a> So that people can understand from where the sun rises to where it sets that there is no one except me. I am God, and there is no one else.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Isaiah 45:7">7</a> I create light and make darkness; I bring peace and create trouble; I, God, do all these things.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Isaiah 45:8">8</a> Heavens above, open up and let the skies rain down goodness; let the earth split open and salvation grow, with goodness rising up at the same time; I, God, have made this happen.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Isaiah 45:9">9</a> How terrible for someone who argues with their Creator! Let a broken piece of pottery argue with other broken pieces on the ground. Can the clay question the one shaping it, saying, &quot;What are you making?&quot; or criticize the work, saying, &quot;You have no hands&quot;?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Isaiah 45:10">10</a> How terrible for someone who asks their father, &quot;What are you fathering?&quot; or asks a woman, &quot;What are you giving birth to?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Isaiah 45:11">11</a> God, the Holy One of Israel, your Creator, says, &quot;Ask me about the future of my children, and tell me what to do with what I have made.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Isaiah 45:12">12</a> I made the earth and created people on it. I stretched out the sky with my own hands, and I command all the stars.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Isaiah 45:13">13</a> I have brought him up to be good, and I will guide all he does: he will build my city, and he will release my prisoners, not for money or a gift, says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Isaiah 45:14">14</a> God says, &quot;Egypt&#39;s work and Ethiopia&#39;s trade, and the tall Sabean people, will come to you and belong to you. They will follow you, coming in chains. They will bow down to you and beg you, saying, &#39;Truly, God is with you; there is no other, there is no other god.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Isaiah 45:15">15</a> Truly, you are a God who hides yourself, O God of Israel, the Savior.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Isaiah 45:16">16</a> They will all feel shame and be embarrassed together; those who make idols will be completely confused.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Isaiah 45:17">17</a> Israel will be saved by God with eternal salvation; you will never be ashamed or confused forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Isaiah 45:18">18</a> God, who made the heavens, says: I formed the earth and created it to last. I did not make it for nothing; I made it to be lived in. I am God, and there is no one else.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Isaiah 45:19">19</a> I have not spoken in secret or in hidden places on earth. I did not tell Jacob&#39;s descendants to look for me without finding me. I, God, speak what is right and announce what is true.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Isaiah 45:20">20</a> Gather and come close, you who have survived from other countries. Those who make idols from wood have no understanding, and they pray to a god who cannot rescue them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Isaiah 45:21">21</a> Tell others and bring them close; yes, let them plan together: who announced this long ago? who spoke of it back then? It was I, God, wasn&#39;t it? There is no other God besides me; a fair God and a Savior; there is no one else.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Isaiah 45:22">22</a> Turn to me and be saved, all you people of the earth, because I am God, and there is no other.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Isaiah 45:23">23</a> I have promised by myself, the word has left my mouth in truth and won&#39;t come back, that to me every knee will bend, every tongue will promise.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Isaiah 45:24">24</a> Someone will definitely say, &quot;I find righteousness and strength in God.&quot; People will come to him, and all who are angry with him will be embarrassed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Isaiah 45:25">25</a> All of Israel&#39;s descendants will be made right with God and will take pride in Him.</p>
