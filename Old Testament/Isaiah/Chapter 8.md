<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Isaiah 8:1">1</a> God told me, &quot;Get a large scroll and write on it with a normal pen about Mahershalalhashbaz.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Isaiah 8:2">2</a> I chose trustworthy people to witness, Uriah the priest and Zechariah, Jeberechiah&#39;s son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Isaiah 8:3">3</a> I went to the prophetess; she became pregnant and had a son. Then God told me, &quot;Name him Mahershalalhashbaz.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Isaiah 8:4">4</a> Before the child learns to say &quot;My father&quot; or &quot;My mother,&quot; the wealth of Damascus and the goods of Samaria will be taken by the king of Assyria.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Isaiah 8:5">5</a> God spoke to me again, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Isaiah 8:6">6</a> Since these people reject the gentle flowing waters of Shiloah and are happy with Rezin and the son of Remaliah;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Isaiah 8:7">7</a> Now then, see how God brings the river&#39;s strong and many waters against them, including the Assyrian king and his might. He will rise above all his streams and overflow all his banks.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Isaiah 8:8">8</a> He will pass through Judah, flooding and crossing over, reaching up to the neck; his stretched-out wings will cover the width of your land, Immanuel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Isaiah 8:9">9</a> Join together, people, and you will be destroyed; listen, all you from distant lands: prepare for battle, and you will be destroyed; prepare for battle, and you will be destroyed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Isaiah 8:10">10</a> Make plans together, but they will fail; say what you want, but it won&#39;t happen, because God is with us.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Isaiah 8:11">11</a> God spoke to me forcefully and taught me not to follow this people&#39;s ways, saying.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Isaiah 8:12">12</a> Don&#39;t call everything a conspiracy, like these people do, and don&#39;t fear what they fear; do not be terrified.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Isaiah 8:13">13</a> Make God your only holy one; fear him and be in awe of him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Isaiah 8:14">14</a> He will be a safe place, but also a stumbling stone and a rock that causes offense to both the families of Israel, and a trap and a snare to the people of Jerusalem.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Isaiah 8:15">15</a> Many of them will stumble, fall, get hurt, be caught, and be captured.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Isaiah 8:16">16</a> Keep the teaching safe, put the law in the hearts of my followers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Isaiah 8:17">17</a> I will wait for God, who hides from the people of Jacob, and I will seek Him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Isaiah 8:18">18</a> Look, I and the children God has given me are for signs and wonders in Israel from God who lives on Mount Zion.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Isaiah 8:19">19</a> When people tell you to ask for help from those who talk to spirits and wizards who whisper and murmur, shouldn&#39;t you ask for help from your God instead of looking for answers among the dead?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Isaiah 8:20">20</a> Refer to God&#39;s law and message: if they do not agree with this, it is because they lack understanding.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Isaiah 8:21">21</a> They will go through it in great trouble and hunger, and when they are hungry, they will become upset, curse their king and God, and look up.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Isaiah 8:22">22</a> They will look to the earth and see distress and darkness, the gloom of suffering, and they will be pushed into darkness.</p>
