<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Isaiah 30:1">1</a> Trouble to the stubborn children, says God, who make plans without including me, and who make agreements without my spirit, only adding more wrongdoings to their sins.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Isaiah 30:2">2</a> They go to Egypt without asking me, seeking help from Pharaoh&#39;s power and putting their trust in Egypt&#39;s protection!</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Isaiah 30:3">3</a> So, relying on Pharaoh will embarrass you, and trusting in Egypt will bring you confusion.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Isaiah 30:4">4</a> His leaders were in Zoan, and his messengers went to Hanes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Isaiah 30:5">5</a> They all felt ashamed because of a nation that could not bring them any benefit or help, but only brought them shame and disgrace.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Isaiah 30:6">6</a> The heavy loads of the southern animals: they go to a land of trouble and pain, where lions and poisonous snakes live. They will put their wealth on young donkeys and their valuables on camels, and take them to a people who won&#39;t help them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Isaiah 30:7">7</a> The Egyptians will try to help, but it will be useless and pointless; that&#39;s why I said, &quot;They can only sit still.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Isaiah 30:8">8</a> Go, write this down on a tablet in front of them, and record it in a book, so it will last forever and be remembered always.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Isaiah 30:9">9</a> These are defiant people, dishonest kids, kids who won&#39;t listen to God&#39;s teachings.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Isaiah 30:10">10</a> They tell the seers, &quot;Don&#39;t see,&quot; and the prophets, &quot;Don&#39;t tell us the truth. Tell us nice things, lie to us.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Isaiah 30:11">11</a> Move away from the road, step aside from the path, and stop making us deal with the Holy One of Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Isaiah 30:12">12</a> Therefore, the Holy One of Israel says, because you reject this message and rely on oppression and deceit, and depend on them:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Isaiah 30:13">13</a> This wrong will be like a crack in a wall that&#39;s about to collapse—a bulge in a tall wall that will break suddenly, without warning.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Isaiah 30:14">14</a> He will smash it like a potter&#39;s jar shattered into bits; he won&#39;t hold back. So, when it breaks, there won&#39;t be a piece left to carry coals from the fire or scoop water from a well.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Isaiah 30:15">15</a> God, the Holy One of Israel, says this: You will be saved by turning back and resting; your strength will be in calmness and trust, but you did not want this.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Isaiah 30:16">16</a> But you said, &quot;No, we will escape on horses.&quot; So you will run away. And you said, &quot;We will ride fast horses.&quot; So those chasing you will be fast.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Isaiah 30:17">17</a> A thousand will run away because one person scolds them; because five scold you, you will run away. You will be left alone like a flag on a mountain, like a banner on a hill.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Isaiah 30:18">18</a> So God will wait to be kind to you, and then he will rise up to show you mercy. For God decides what is right; happy are all who wait for him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Isaiah 30:19">19</a> The people will live in Zion in Jerusalem; you will not cry anymore. God will be very kind to you when you call out; when he hears, he will respond to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Isaiah 30:20">20</a> Even if God gives you hard times and suffering, your teachers won&#39;t be hidden away anymore, and you will be able to see your teachers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Isaiah 30:21">21</a> You will hear a voice behind you saying, &quot;This is the path, follow it,&quot; whether you go right or left.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Isaiah 30:22">22</a> You will also make your silver idols and your gold statues unclean. You will throw them away like a dirty cloth and say to them, &quot;Get away from here.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Isaiah 30:23">23</a> God will send rain for your seeds you plant in the soil; you&#39;ll have bread from the earth&#39;s bounty, and it will be rich and abundant. On that day, your livestock will graze in spacious meadows.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Isaiah 30:24">24</a> The oxen and young donkeys that plow the land will eat pure food, which has been sifted with a shovel and a fan.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Isaiah 30:25">25</a> On every tall mountain and every high hill, there will be rivers and streams of water on the day of the great killing, when the towers collapse.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Isaiah 30:26">26</a> The moon will shine as brightly as the sun, and the sun will shine seven times brighter, like seven days combined, when God repairs his people&#39;s harm and heals their injuries.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Isaiah 30:27">27</a> Look, God&#39;s name comes from afar, blazing with his anger, and the weight is great: his lips are full of wrath, and his tongue is like a consuming fire.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Isaiah 30:28">28</a> His breath, like a flood, will come up to the neck, to separate the nations with a useless sieve; and there will be a bit in the mouths of the people, making them wander off course.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Isaiah 30:29">29</a> You will sing like on a holy night celebration; your hearts will be joyful like when someone plays a flute going to God&#39;s mountain, to the great God of Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Isaiah 30:30">30</a> God will make his mighty voice heard and show the striking power of his arm, with fierce anger, and with flames of a consuming fire, with storms, and strong winds, and hailstones.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Isaiah 30:31">31</a> Because of God&#39;s command, the Assyrian will be defeated, the one who struck with a stick.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Isaiah 30:32">32</a> Everywhere the staff touches, as God decides, there will be music with drums and harps; and with it, He will fight in the trembling battles.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Isaiah 30:33">33</a> Tophet was set up long ago; it is made ready for the king. It is deep and wide, with a big pile of fire and wood. God&#39;s breath, like a sulfur stream, lights it.</p>
