<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Isaiah 53:1">1</a> Who has believed what we said? And to whom has God shown his power?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Isaiah 53:2">2</a> He will grow up before God like a young plant and as a root from dry soil. He has no attractive form or beauty; when we see him, nothing in his appearance makes us want him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Isaiah 53:3">3</a> People dislike and ignore him; he is a man who knows sadness and pain: we all turned away from him; he was disliked, and we did not value him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Isaiah 53:4">4</a> He surely took on our pains and carried our sadness, but we thought he was punished by God, hit by him, and suffering.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Isaiah 53:5">5</a> He was hurt for our wrongs, he was beaten for our sins. The punishment that brought us peace was on him, and by his wounds we are healed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Isaiah 53:6">6</a> We all, like sheep, have wandered off; each of us has gone our own way; and God has put on him the wrongs of us all.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Isaiah 53:7">7</a> He was mistreated and he suffered, but he did not speak: he is led like a lamb to be killed, and like a sheep is silent before its shearers, so he does not speak.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Isaiah 53:8">8</a> He was freed from jail and trial. Who will speak of his descendants? He was removed from the living because my people did wrong, and he suffered for them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Isaiah 53:9">9</a> He was buried with bad people, and although wealthy when he died, he had not been violent or lied.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Isaiah 53:10">10</a> God chose to hurt him; God made him suffer. When you offer his life for sin, he will see his children, live a long life, and God&#39;s will succeed through him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Isaiah 53:11">11</a> He will see the results of his suffering and be content. Through his understanding, my good servant will make many people right with God, because he will carry their wrongs.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Isaiah 53:12">12</a> So I will give him a share with the great, and he will share the winnings with the mighty, because he gave his life to death: he was counted with the wrongdoers; he took on many people&#39;s sins and spoke up for the wrongdoers.</p>
