<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Isaiah 36:1">1</a> In the fourteenth year of King Hezekiah&#39;s rule, Sennacherib, the king of Assyria, attacked all the fortified cities of Judah and captured them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Isaiah 36:2">2</a> The king of Assyria sent Rabshakeh from Lachish to Jerusalem to King Hezekiah with a big army. He stood near the water channel of the upper pool on the road by the laundry workers&#39; field.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Isaiah 36:3">3</a> Eliakim, son of Hilkiah, the palace manager, Shebna the secretary, and Joah, son of Asaph the record keeper, went to meet him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Isaiah 36:4">4</a> Rabshakeh said to them, Tell Hezekiah, The great king, the king of Assyria, asks, &#39;What is this trust you have?&#39;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Isaiah 36:5">5</a> I say, you claim (but those are just empty words) that I have advice and power for war. Now, who do you rely on when you rebel against me?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Isaiah 36:6">6</a> Look, you rely on the support of this weak reed, which is Egypt. If someone leans on it, it will break and stab their hand. That&#39;s how Pharaoh, the king of Egypt, is to all who trust in him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Isaiah 36:7">7</a> But if you say to me, &quot;We trust in God our God,&quot; isn&#39;t it the same one whose high places and altars Hezekiah removed, telling Judah and Jerusalem, &quot;You must worship at this altar&quot;?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Isaiah 36:8">8</a> So now, please promise my master, the king of Assyria, and I will give you two thousand horses, if you have enough riders for them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Isaiah 36:9">9</a> How can you then scare off even a single one of my master&#39;s lowest officers and rely on Egypt for chariots and cavalry?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Isaiah 36:10">10</a> Have I come to attack this land without God&#39;s guidance? God told me to go and destroy it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Isaiah 36:11">11</a> Eliakim, Shebna, and Joah said to Rabshakeh, &quot;Please talk to your servants in the Syrian language, because we understand it. Don&#39;t talk to us in the Jewish language where the people on the wall can hear.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Isaiah 36:12">12</a> But the official said, &quot;Did my master send me to your master and to you to say these things? Didn&#39;t he send me to the people sitting on the wall, so that they will have to eat their waste and drink their urine with you?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Isaiah 36:13">13</a> Then Rabshakeh stood up and shouted in the Jewish language, &quot;Listen to the words of the great king, the king of Assyria.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Isaiah 36:14">14</a> The king says, don&#39;t let Hezekiah trick you; he won&#39;t be able to save you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Isaiah 36:15">15</a> Do not let Hezekiah convince you to trust in God, saying, &quot;God will surely save us: this city will not be given to the king of Assyria.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Isaiah 36:16">16</a> Do not listen to Hezekiah. The king of Assyria says, &quot;Make a deal with me by giving me a present, and come out to me. Then each of you can eat from your own grapevines and fig trees, and drink water from your own wells.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Isaiah 36:17">17</a> Until I come and bring you to a place like your own, a land with grain and wine, a land with bread and vineyards.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Isaiah 36:18">18</a> Be careful that Hezekiah doesn&#39;t convince you by saying, &#39;God will save us.&#39; Has any nation&#39;s god rescued its land from the king of Assyria?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Isaiah 36:19">19</a> Where are the gods of Hamath and Arphad? Where are the gods of Sepharvaim? Could they save Samaria from my power?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Isaiah 36:20">20</a> Which gods of these lands have saved their lands from me, that God should save Jerusalem from me?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Isaiah 36:21">21</a> They stayed quiet and did not speak a word to him because the king had ordered, &quot;Do not answer him.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Isaiah 36:22">22</a> Eliakim, Hilkiah&#39;s son who managed the household, Shebna the secretary, and Joah, Asaph&#39;s son and the historian, went to Hezekiah with their clothes torn and told him what Rabshakeh had said.</p>
