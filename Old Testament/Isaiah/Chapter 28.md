<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Isaiah 28:1">1</a> Trouble is coming for the proud crown, for the drunk people of Ephraim, whose beautiful glory is like a dying flower, on the head of the rich valleys of those drunk with wine.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Isaiah 28:2">2</a> Look, God has a powerful and strong one who, like a hailstorm and a destructive storm, like a big flood of overflowing waters, will throw down to the ground with force.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Isaiah 28:3">3</a> The proud crown, the drunk people of Ephraim, will be stepped on.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Isaiah 28:4">4</a> The beautiful crown of the fertile valley will wither away like a flower, and be like early fruit before summer; when someone sees it, they will eat it as soon as it&#39;s in their hand.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Isaiah 28:5">5</a> On that day, God will be like a crown of glory and a beautiful tiara for the remaining people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Isaiah 28:6">6</a> To give discernment to those who judge and power to those who push back enemies to their gates.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Isaiah 28:7">7</a> But they have made mistakes because of wine, and strong drink has led them astray; the priest and the prophet have made errors because of strong drink, they are overwhelmed by wine, they wander off the path because of strong drink; they make mistakes in their visions, they trip up in their decisions.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Isaiah 28:8">8</a> Every table is covered with vomit and dirty, leaving no clean spot.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Isaiah 28:9">9</a> Who will he teach knowledge? Who will he help understand the teachings? Those who have outgrown milk and no longer nurse.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Isaiah 28:10">10</a> Rule should be followed by rule; line by line; here a bit, there a bit.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Isaiah 28:11">11</a> With unclear speech and a different language, he will talk to these people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Isaiah 28:12">12</a> He said to them, &quot;This is the place where you can give rest to the tired, and here they can be refreshed. But they wouldn&#39;t listen.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Isaiah 28:13">13</a> God&#39;s teaching was given to them bit by bit, rule by rule; here a little, there a little, so they might stumble, fall, and be caught.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Isaiah 28:14">14</a> Listen to God&#39;s message, you mocking leaders of the people in Jerusalem.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Isaiah 28:15">15</a> Because you said, &quot;We have an agreement with death and a deal with the grave; when disaster sweeps by, it won&#39;t reach us: for we&#39;ve made lies our shelter, and under untruths we&#39;ve hidden.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Isaiah 28:16">16</a> So God says, &quot;Look, I place in Zion a stone for a foundation, a tested stone, a valuable cornerstone, a solid foundation. Whoever believes will not rush.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Isaiah 28:17">17</a> I will use justice as a measuring line and righteousness as a plumb line; hail will destroy the shelter of lies, and water will flood the hiding place.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Isaiah 28:18">18</a> Your deal with death will be canceled, and your pact with the grave will not last; when the sweeping punishment comes through, you will be crushed by it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Isaiah 28:19">19</a> Once it starts, it will sweep over you; every morning and night it will come, and just hearing the news will be very distressing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Isaiah 28:20">20</a> The bed is too short for someone to stretch out on, and the blanket too small to wrap around oneself.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Isaiah 28:21">21</a> God will rise up like he did at Mount Perazim, he will be angry like he was in the Valley of Gibeon, to do what he must, his unusual work; and to complete his task, his unexpected task.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Isaiah 28:22">22</a> So now, don&#39;t mock or your punishment will grow. I have heard from God that he has decided to destroy the entire earth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Isaiah 28:23">23</a> Listen and hear my voice; pay attention and hear my words.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Isaiah 28:24">24</a> Does the farmer plow all day to plant seeds? Does he break up the lumps of soil on his land?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Isaiah 28:25">25</a> When he has smoothed out the ground, doesn&#39;t he spread the fitches and scatter the cumin, and put the best wheat, the chosen barley, and the rye where they belong?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Isaiah 28:26">26</a> God guides him with wisdom and teaches him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Isaiah 28:27">27</a> You don&#39;t use a heavy tool to thresh the fitches, nor do you drive a cart wheel over the cummin; instead, you beat the fitches with a stick and the cummin with a rod.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Isaiah 28:28">28</a> Grain for bread is crushed, but not forever; it won&#39;t be ground endlessly or broken with the cart&#39;s wheel, nor smashed by horse riders.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Isaiah 28:29">29</a> This also comes from God, who is amazing in giving advice and outstanding in what He does.</p>
