<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Isaiah 65:1">1</a> People who did not ask for me are now looking for me; those who did not seek me have found me. I said, &quot;Here I am, here I am,&quot; to a nation that was not called by my name.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Isaiah 65:2">2</a> I have reached out all day to stubborn people who follow their own bad paths and thoughts.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Isaiah 65:3">3</a> A nation that always makes me angry to my face; they offer sacrifices in gardens and burn incense on brick altars.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Isaiah 65:4">4</a> They stay near the graves and sleep in tombs, eat pig meat, and their pots hold disgusting things.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Isaiah 65:5">5</a> They say, &quot;Keep away, don&#39;t come near me, because I am more holy than you.&quot; These people are like irritating smoke in my nose, a fire that keeps burning all day.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Isaiah 65:6">6</a> Look, it is recorded before me: I will not stay quiet, but will pay back, even repay into their lap.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Isaiah 65:7">7</a> Your wrongs, and your ancestors&#39; wrongs, says God, who worshiped on the mountains and insulted me on the hills: so I will give back their past actions to them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Isaiah 65:8">8</a> God says, &quot;Like new wine in the grape cluster, and someone says, &#39;Don&#39;t destroy it; there&#39;s a blessing in it,&#39; I will do the same for my servants, so I won&#39;t destroy them all.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Isaiah 65:9">9</a> I will produce descendants from Jacob, and from Judah someone to inherit my mountains; those I choose will possess it, and my workers will live there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Isaiah 65:10">10</a> Sharon will be a place for flocks, and the valley of Achor will be a resting place for herds, for my people who have looked for me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Isaiah 65:11">11</a> But you abandon God, forget my holy hill, set a table for idols, and provide drinks for them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Isaiah 65:12">12</a> So I will count you for the sword, and all of you will kneel for killing: because when I called, you did not answer; when I spoke, you did not listen; instead you did bad things in front of me, and chose what I did not like.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Isaiah 65:13">13</a> So this is what God says: Look, my servants will eat, but you will be hungry. Look, my servants will drink, but you will be thirsty. Look, my servants will be happy, but you will be ashamed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Isaiah 65:14">14</a> Look, my servants will sing with happy hearts, but you will cry because your hearts are sad, and you will wail because your spirits are troubled.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Isaiah 65:15">15</a> You will leave your name as a curse for my chosen ones: because God will kill you, and will give his servants a different name.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Isaiah 65:16">16</a> Anyone who asks for a blessing on earth will ask for a blessing from the God of truth. Anyone who makes a promise on earth will promise by the God of truth. The past troubles are forgotten, and they are hidden from my sight.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Isaiah 65:17">17</a> Look, I am making new heavens and a new earth: and the old things will not be remembered or thought about anymore.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Isaiah 65:18">18</a> Be happy and celebrate always because of what I create. Look, I make Jerusalem a place of joy and its people a delight.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Isaiah 65:19">19</a> I will be happy in Jerusalem and delighted with my people. No more will there be the sound of crying or the sound of tears in the city.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Isaiah 65:20">20</a> No babies will die young, nor old people before their time. Everyone will live to be at least 100 years old. But if someone at 100 years old is a sinner, they will be cursed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Isaiah 65:21">21</a> They will build houses and live in them; they will plant vineyards and eat their fruit.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Isaiah 65:22">22</a> People won&#39;t build homes for others to live in, or plant for others to eat. Just like trees live long, my people will have long lives and enjoy what they make with their own hands.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Isaiah 65:23">23</a> They will not work for nothing, nor have children for misfortune, because they are the children of God&#39;s blessed ones, and their descendants with them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Isaiah 65:24">24</a> Before they even ask, I will respond; while they are still talking, I will listen.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Isaiah 65:25">25</a> The wolf and the lamb will eat together, and the lion will eat grass like the ox; the snake will eat dust. They will not harm or ruin anything on my holy mountain, says God.</p>
