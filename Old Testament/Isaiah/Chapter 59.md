<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Isaiah 59:1">1</a> Look, God&#39;s hand is not too short to save; nor is his ear too dull to hear.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Isaiah 59:2">2</a> Your wrongs have come between you and God, and your sins have hidden his face from you, so he will not listen.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Isaiah 59:3">3</a> Your hands are dirty with blood, and your fingers with wrong; your lips have told lies, your tongue whispers twisted things.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Isaiah 59:4">4</a> No one asks for justice or argues for truth; they rely on what&#39;s empty and tell lies; they make trouble and cause wrongdoing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Isaiah 59:5">5</a> They hatch poisonous snake eggs and spin spider webs: whoever eats their eggs dies, and when one is stepped on, a viper hatches.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Isaiah 59:6">6</a> Their webs can&#39;t be made into clothes, nor can they cover themselves with what they do: their actions are sinful, and they do violent things.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Isaiah 59:7">7</a> Their feet rush to do bad things, and they hurry to spill innocent blood. They plan sinful things; ruin and damage are on their way.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Isaiah 59:8">8</a> They do not know the way to peace; their paths are not fair: whoever walks in them will not find peace.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Isaiah 59:9">9</a> So justice is far from us, and fairness doesn&#39;t reach us. We hope for light but only find darkness; we look for brightness but walk in gloom.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Isaiah 59:10">10</a> We search for the wall like blind people, and we fumble as though we can&#39;t see. We trip at midday as if it were dark; we are in empty spaces like the dead.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Isaiah 59:11">11</a> We all growl like bears and sadly cry like doves; we hope for fairness, but find none; for rescue, but it&#39;s far away from us.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Isaiah 59:12">12</a> We have many sins against you, and our wrongs show our guilt; our sins are always with us, and we know our bad acts.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Isaiah 59:13">13</a> By breaking rules and lying about God, turning away from our God, talking about hurting others and rebelling, thinking and saying untrue things from our heart.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Isaiah 59:14">14</a> Judgment is avoided, and justice is distant because truth has failed in public, and fairness cannot come in.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Isaiah 59:15">15</a> Yes, truth is lacking; and those who avoid doing wrong become victims. God saw this and was unhappy because there was no justice.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Isaiah 59:16">16</a> He saw no one was there and was surprised no one intervened; so he himself brought salvation, and his goodness supported him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Isaiah 59:17">17</a> He wore fairness like armor on his chest and put on a helmet of rescue on his head; he dressed himself in revengeful clothes and wrapped intense passion around himself like a cape.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Isaiah 59:18">18</a> God will pay back everyone based on what they have done; he will show anger to his foes and settle scores with his enemies; he will also repay those who live on the islands.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Isaiah 59:19">19</a> People will respect God&#39;s name in the west and his greatness where the sun rises. When enemies attack like a rushing water, God&#39;s Spirit will raise a flag to stand against them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Isaiah 59:20">20</a> God will come to Zion and to those in Jacob who stop doing wrong, says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Isaiah 59:21">21</a> I promise you this, says God: My Spirit with you and the words I&#39;ve given you will always be on your lips and on the lips of your children and grandchildren, now and forever, says God.</p>
