<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Isaiah 1:1">1</a> This is the vision that Isaiah, son of Amoz, had about Judah and Jerusalem during the time when Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah were kings of Judah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Isaiah 1:2">2</a> Listen, skies, and pay attention, earth: God has spoken, I have raised and cared for children, but they have turned against me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Isaiah 1:3">3</a> The ox knows its owner, and the donkey knows where its master feeds it, but Israel doesn&#39;t know, my people don&#39;t think about it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Isaiah 1:4">4</a> Oh sinful country, people full of wrongdoing, offspring of those who do evil, kids who spoil things: they have left God, they have made the Holy One of Israel angry, they have turned away.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Isaiah 1:5">5</a> Why should you be punished again? You will only rebel more and more: your entire mind is ill, and your entire heart is weak.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Isaiah 1:6">6</a> From the bottom of the foot to the top of the head, there is no health in it; only wounds, bruises, and open sores. They have not been cleaned, bandaged, or treated with ointment.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Isaiah 1:7">7</a> Your country is empty, your cities are burned: strangers eat up your land in front of you, and it is empty, as destroyed by outsiders.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Isaiah 1:8">8</a> The Daughter of Zion is left like a small house in a vineyard, like a shelter in a cucumber field, like a city surrounded by enemies.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Isaiah 1:9">9</a> If God had not left us a small group of survivors, we would have become like Sodom, and we would have been like Gomorrah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Isaiah 1:10">10</a> Listen to God&#39;s message, you leaders of Sodom; pay attention to our God&#39;s rules, people of Gomorrah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Isaiah 1:11">11</a> Why do you offer me so many sacrifices? says God. I have had enough of burnt rams and fat from well-fed animals; I take no pleasure in the blood of bulls, lambs, or goats.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Isaiah 1:12">12</a> When you come to meet me, who asked you to walk in my courtyards?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Isaiah 1:13">13</a> Stop bringing worthless gifts; I hate the smell of incense. New moon celebrations, Sabbaths, and meetings—I cannot stand them; they are full of sin, even the special gatherings.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Isaiah 1:14">14</a> I hate your new moons and celebrations; they bother me, and I am tired of putting up with them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Isaiah 1:15">15</a> When you raise your hands, I will look away from you; even when you pray a lot, I won&#39;t listen: your hands have blood on them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Isaiah 1:16">16</a> Clean yourselves, make yourselves pure; stop doing wrong things that I see; stop doing bad things.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Isaiah 1:17">17</a> Learn to do good; seek fairness, help those who are suffering, defend orphans, and argue for widows.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Isaiah 1:18">18</a> Come now, and let&#39;s talk this over, says God: even if your sins are bright red, they will become as white as snow; even if they are deep red like crimson, they will be like wool.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Isaiah 1:19">19</a> If you are willing and obey, you will enjoy the best from the land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Isaiah 1:20">20</a> If you refuse and rebel, you will be destroyed by the sword because God has said it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Isaiah 1:21">21</a> How has the loyal city turned into a prostitute! It was once full of fairness; goodness made its home there; but now there are killers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Isaiah 1:22">22</a> Your silver has turned to waste, your wine diluted with water.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Isaiah 1:23">23</a> Your leaders are disobedient and friends with thieves: they all love getting gifts and chase after rewards: they do not defend orphans, nor do they care about the needs of widows.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Isaiah 1:24">24</a> So God, the powerful One of Israel, says, &quot;I will get relief from my foes and take revenge on my enemies.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Isaiah 1:25">25</a> I will intervene for you, completely remove your impurities, and get rid of all your lesser metals.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Isaiah 1:26">26</a> I will bring back your judges like before, and your advisors as in the start; then you will be known as the city of rightness, the loyal city.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Isaiah 1:27">27</a> Zion will be saved through justice, and those who turn to her will be brought back with fairness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Isaiah 1:28">28</a> The ruin of wrongdoers and sinners will happen at the same time, and those who abandon God will be destroyed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Isaiah 1:29">29</a> They will feel shame for the oak trees you wanted, and you will be embarrassed about the gardens you picked.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Isaiah 1:30">30</a> You will be like an oak tree with withering leaves, and like a garden without water.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Isaiah 1:31">31</a> The strong will be like tinder, and their creator like a spark; they will both burn, and no one will put them out.</p>
