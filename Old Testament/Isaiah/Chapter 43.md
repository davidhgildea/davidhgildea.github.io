<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Isaiah 43:1">1</a> But now, God who made you, Jacob, and shaped you, Israel, says: Don&#39;t be afraid, for I have saved you; I have called you by your name; you are mine.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Isaiah 43:2">2</a> When you go through the waters, I will be with you; and when you go through the rivers, they won&#39;t flood you: when you walk through the fire, you won&#39;t be burned; and the flame won&#39;t set you on fire.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Isaiah 43:3">3</a> I am your God, the Holy One of Israel, your Savior: I traded Egypt, Ethiopia, and Seba for your freedom.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Isaiah 43:4">4</a> Because you are valuable to me, you are honored, and I love you: so I will trade other people for you and give nations in exchange for your life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Isaiah 43:5">5</a> Do not be afraid, for I am with you. I will bring your offspring from the east and gather you from the west.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Isaiah 43:6">6</a> I will tell the north, &#39;Let go,&#39; and the south, &#39;Don&#39;t hold back.&#39; Bring my sons from distant places, and my daughters from the ends of the earth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Isaiah 43:7">7</a> Everyone who is known by my name, I created for my glory; I shaped them and made them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Isaiah 43:8">8</a> Bring out the blind who can see, and the deaf who can hear.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Isaiah 43:9">9</a> Let all countries come together, and the people gather: who can tell us about this and show us what happened before? Let them present their witnesses to prove they are right, or listen and say, &quot;It is true.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Isaiah 43:10">10</a> You are my witnesses, says God, and the servant I have chosen: so you will know and believe me, and realize that I am the one: there was no God before me, nor will there be any after me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Isaiah 43:11">11</a> I am God, and there is no one else who can save.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Isaiah 43:12">12</a> I have told you, saved you, and shown you myself when there was no other god with you: so you are my witnesses, says God, that I am the only God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Isaiah 43:13">13</a> Yes, before there was a day, I am he; no one can rescue from my hand: I will act, and who can stop it?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Isaiah 43:14">14</a> God says, &quot;I am your rescuer, the Holy One of Israel. For you, I went to Babylon, brought down all their leaders, and the Chaldeans who shout in the ships.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Isaiah 43:15">15</a> I am God, your Holy One, the maker of Israel, your King.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Isaiah 43:16">16</a> God says, who makes a way in the sea and a path in the strong waters.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Isaiah 43:17">17</a> God brings out chariots and horses, armies and strength; they fall and can&#39;t get up; they are snuffed out like a flame.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Isaiah 43:18">18</a> Don&#39;t remember past things or think about old things.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Isaiah 43:19">19</a> Look, I will do something new; it will happen soon. Won&#39;t you be aware of it? I will make a path in the wild and streams in the dry land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Isaiah 43:20">20</a> The wild animals will honor me, the dragons and the owls, because I provide water in the wild and streams in the desert to give my chosen people something to drink.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Isaiah 43:21">21</a> I created these people for myself; they will tell of my praise.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Isaiah 43:22">22</a> You have not called on me, O Jacob; you have grown tired of me, O Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Isaiah 43:23">23</a> You have not brought me the young animals for your burnt offerings; you have also not honored me with your sacrifices. I have not made you work hard with offerings or tired you with incense.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Isaiah 43:24">24</a> You did not buy me sweet cane with money, nor did you offer me the best parts of your sacrifices; but you made me work with your sins, you tired me out with your wrongdoings.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Isaiah 43:25">25</a> I am the one who erases your wrongs for my own sake, and I will not remember your sins.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Isaiah 43:26">26</a> Remind me; let&#39;s discuss it together. You state your case to prove you&#39;re right.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Isaiah 43:27">27</a> Your first ancestor sinned, and your guides have disobeyed me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Isaiah 43:28">28</a> So I have disgraced the sacred leaders, and let Jacob be cursed, and Israel be insulted.</p>
