<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Isaiah 19:1">1</a> Egypt&#39;s trouble. Look, God rides on a fast cloud and will come to Egypt. Egypt&#39;s idols will shake before him, and Egypt&#39;s courage will fail completely.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Isaiah 19:2">2</a> I will make Egyptians fight against each other: they will battle against their own brothers and neighbors, city against city, and kingdom against kingdom.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Isaiah 19:3">3</a> Egypt&#39;s spirit will weaken within; I will spoil its plans. They will turn to idols, spellcasters, mediums, and sorcerers for guidance.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Isaiah 19:4">4</a> I will let the Egyptians be ruled by a mean ruler; a harsh king will control them, says God, the God over all.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Isaiah 19:5">5</a> The sea will lose its water, and the river will empty and dry up.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Isaiah 19:6">6</a> They will redirect the rivers far away; the streams of protection will be drained and dry up: the reeds and plants will wilt.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Isaiah 19:7">7</a> The reeds by the streams, at the stream mouths, and all that is planted by the streams, will dry up, blow away, and disappear.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Isaiah 19:8">8</a> Fishers will be sad, everyone who fishes in the streams will grieve, and those who lay nets on the waters will grow weak.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Isaiah 19:9">9</a> People who work with fine linen and make nets will be ashamed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Isaiah 19:10">10</a> They will fail in their plans, all who build waterways and fishponds.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Isaiah 19:11">11</a> The leaders of Zoan are foolish, and Pharaoh&#39;s wise advisors have become senseless. How can you tell Pharaoh, &quot;I am the son of the wise, descended from ancient kings&quot;?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Isaiah 19:12">12</a> Where are they? Where are your wise men? Let them tell you now and explain what God has planned for Egypt.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Isaiah 19:13">13</a> The leaders of Zoan have turned into fools, the leaders of Noph are tricked; they have led Egypt astray, even those who should support its people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Isaiah 19:14">14</a> God has mixed a corrupt spirit among them; they have made Egypt go wrong in all its actions, like a drunk man stumbling in his own sick.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Isaiah 19:15">15</a> Egypt will not have any work to do, from the greatest to the least.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Isaiah 19:16">16</a> On that day, Egypt will be like scared women because of the powerful shaking hand of God over it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Isaiah 19:17">17</a> The land of Judah will scare Egypt; anyone talking about it will be frightened because of God&#39;s plan against it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Isaiah 19:18">18</a> On that day, five cities in Egypt will speak Canaan&#39;s language and promise loyalty to God. One city will be named &quot;City of Destruction.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Isaiah 19:19">19</a> On that day, there will be an altar for God in the middle of Egypt and a monument at its border for God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Isaiah 19:20">20</a> It will be a sign and evidence to God in Egypt. They will pray to God because of their oppressors, and He will send them a savior, a mighty one, who will rescue them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Isaiah 19:21">21</a> God will be known to Egypt, and the Egyptians will know God at that time; they will offer sacrifices and gifts, and they will make a promise to God and keep it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Isaiah 19:22">22</a> God will punish Egypt, but then heal it; they will turn back to God, and when they ask for help, He will heal them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Isaiah 19:23">23</a> On that day, there will be a road from Egypt to Assyria. Assyrians will go to Egypt, and Egyptians will go to Assyria, and they will work together.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Isaiah 19:24">24</a> On that day, Israel will be one of three with Egypt and Assyria, a blessing in the land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Isaiah 19:25">25</a> God will bless them, saying, &quot;Egypt, my people, will be blessed, Assyria, my creation, and Israel, my inheritance.&quot;</p>
