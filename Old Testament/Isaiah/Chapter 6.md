<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Isaiah 6:1">1</a> The year King Uzziah died, I saw God on a high and exalted throne, and his robe filled the temple.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Isaiah 6:2">2</a> Above it stood the seraphim: each one had six wings; with two they covered their faces, with two they covered their feet, and with two they flew.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Isaiah 6:3">3</a> One called to another, saying, “Holy, holy, holy, is God of all: the whole earth is full of his glory.”</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Isaiah 6:4">4</a> The door&#39;s posts shook with the sound of the shout, and the house was filled with smoke.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Isaiah 6:5">5</a> I said, &quot;I&#39;m in trouble! I am ruined because I have dirty lips and live among people with dirty lips, yet I have seen the King, God.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Isaiah 6:6">6</a> One of the seraphs flew to me with a live coal in his hand that he took from the altar with tongs.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Isaiah 6:7">7</a> He put it on my mouth and said, &quot;See, this has touched your lips; your guilt is gone, and your sin is forgiven.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Isaiah 6:8">8</a> I also heard God&#39;s voice asking, &quot;Who should I send, and who will go for us?&quot; Then I said, &quot;Here I am; send me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Isaiah 6:9">9</a> He said, &quot;Go and tell these people, &#39;You may hear, but you will not understand; you may see, but you will not know what you&#39;re seeing.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Isaiah 6:10">10</a> Make these people&#39;s hearts dull, their ears deaf, and blind their eyes; so they won&#39;t see with their eyes, hear with their ears, understand with their hearts, turn back, and be healed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Isaiah 6:11">11</a> I asked, &quot;God, how long will this last?&quot; And he replied, &quot;Until the cities are empty, the houses have no one living in them, and the land is completely deserted.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Isaiah 6:12">12</a> God has taken people far away, and there is a big emptiness in the land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Isaiah 6:13">13</a> In it, a tenth will remain, and it will revive and be used for food. Like a terebinth or an oak tree that keeps its strength even when it loses its leaves, so will the holy people be the strength of the land.</p>
