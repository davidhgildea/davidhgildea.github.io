<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Isaiah 4:1">1</a> On that day, seven women will grab one man and say, &quot;We will eat our own food and wear our own clothes; just let us have your name to remove our shame.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Isaiah 4:2">2</a> On that day, God&#39;s branch will be beautiful and glorious, and the earth&#39;s produce will be outstanding and attractive for the survivors in Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Isaiah 4:3">3</a> Everyone who stays in Zion and survives in Jerusalem will be called holy, each one listed among the living in Jerusalem.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Isaiah 4:4">4</a> When God cleans the dirt from Zion&#39;s daughters and removes Jerusalem&#39;s blood by judging and burning.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Isaiah 4:5">5</a> God will make a cloud and smoke over every home on Mount Zion and over its gatherings by day, and a bright, burning fire by night; all the splendid things will have protection.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Isaiah 4:6">6</a> There will be a shelter to provide shade from the heat during the day, and a safe place to protect from storms and rain.</p>
