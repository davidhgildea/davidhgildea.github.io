<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Isaiah 5:1">1</a> I will sing a song for my loved one about his vineyard. My loved one has a vineyard on a fertile hill.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Isaiah 5:2">2</a> He built a fence around it, removed the stones, planted the best vines, built a tower in the middle, and made a winepress in it. He expected it to grow grapes, but it grew wild grapes instead.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Isaiah 5:3">3</a> Now, people of Jerusalem and Judah, please decide who is right—me or my vineyard.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Isaiah 5:4">4</a> What more could I have done for my vineyard that I didn&#39;t do? Why did it grow wild grapes when I expected it to produce good grapes?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Isaiah 5:5">5</a> Now I will tell you what I will do to my vineyard: I will remove its hedge, and it will be devoured; I will tear down its wall, and it will be trampled.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Isaiah 5:6">6</a> I will make it a wasteland: it won&#39;t be trimmed or tilled; instead, thistles and thorns will grow. I will tell the clouds not to rain on it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Isaiah 5:7">7</a> God&#39;s vineyard is the people of Israel, and the Judah men are his favored plants. He expected fairness, but found abuse; he wanted rightness, but heard pleas for help.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Isaiah 5:8">8</a> Pity those who combine house to house and field to field until there&#39;s no more space, just to live alone on the land!</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Isaiah 5:9">9</a> God told me, &quot;Truly, many big and beautiful houses will be empty with no one living in them.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Isaiah 5:10">10</a> Indeed, ten acres of vineyard will produce only one bath of wine, and a homer of seed will produce only an ephah of grain.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Isaiah 5:11">11</a> Trouble awaits those who get up early to start drinking and keep on until night, until they are heated by wine.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Isaiah 5:12">12</a> At their parties, they have harps, violins, drums, flutes, and wine, but they don&#39;t care about what God has done or pay attention to what he has made.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Isaiah 5:13">13</a> My people have been taken away as captives because they lack knowledge; their leaders are starving, and many are parched with thirst.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Isaiah 5:14">14</a> So hell has grown bigger and opened wide its mouth with no limit: and their splendor, crowds, show of wealth, and those who celebrate, will all go down into it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Isaiah 5:15">15</a> The average person will be lowered, and the powerful person will be made humble, and the proud will be brought down.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Isaiah 5:16">16</a> God will be honored when he judges, and the holy God will be made pure through justice.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Isaiah 5:17">17</a> The lambs will eat as they do, and strangers will eat in the empty lands of the wealthy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Isaiah 5:18">18</a> Trouble awaits those who pull sin along with ropes of useless things, and drag wrongdoings as if with a heavy cart rope.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Isaiah 5:19">19</a> Those who say, &quot;Let God hurry; let Him speed up His work so we may see it. And let the plan of the Holy One of Israel come closer and happen, so we may know it!&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Isaiah 5:20">20</a> Trouble comes to those who say bad things are good, and good things are bad; who replace light with darkness, and darkness with light; who exchange bitter for sweet, and sweet for bitter.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Isaiah 5:21">21</a> Pity those who think they are smart and wise by themselves!</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Isaiah 5:22">22</a> Trouble comes to those who are good at drinking wine and those who mix strong drinks well.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Isaiah 5:23">23</a> They declare the guilty innocent for bribes and deny the good their rights!</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Isaiah 5:24">24</a> So just as fire eats up straw and flames burn up dry grass, their roots will rot, and their flowers will turn to dust because they have rejected God&#39;s law and ignored the words of Israel&#39;s Holy God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Isaiah 5:25">25</a> So God is angry with his people; he has reached out to punish them and struck them down. The mountains shook, and their bodies lay torn in the streets. Yet even with all this, God&#39;s anger has not stopped, and his hand is still raised to strike again.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Isaiah 5:26">26</a> He will raise a flag for distant nations and whistle for them from the earth&#39;s end. Look, they will quickly and speedily come.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Isaiah 5:27">27</a> No one will get tired or fall; no one will rest or sleep; their belts won&#39;t come undone, nor will their shoe straps break.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Isaiah 5:28">28</a> Their arrows are sharp, their bows ready, their horses&#39; hooves hard as rock, and their wheels spin like a storm.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Isaiah 5:29">29</a> Their growling will sound like a lion&#39;s; they will roar like young lions; they will growl, grab their catch, take it to a safe place, and no one will rescue it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Isaiah 5:30">30</a> On that day, they will shout at them like the sea&#39;s roar. If someone looks at the land, they will see darkness and sadness, and even the sky above will be dark.</p>
