<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Isaiah 40:1">1</a> Comfort my people, comfort them, says your God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Isaiah 40:2">2</a> Talk kindly to Jerusalem, and tell her that her struggle is over, that her wrongs are forgiven, because she has received from God twice as much as she deserved for all her sins.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Isaiah 40:3">3</a> The voice of someone shouting in the wilderness says, &quot;Get ready for God&#39;s coming; clear a straight path for our God in the desert.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Isaiah 40:4">4</a> Every valley will be lifted up, and every mountain and hill will be brought down; the crooked will be straightened, and the rough areas will be smoothed out.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Isaiah 40:5">5</a> God&#39;s glory will be shown, and everyone will see it because God has said it would happen.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Isaiah 40:6">6</a> The voice said, &quot;Shout.&quot; And he asked, &quot;What should I shout?&quot; All people are like grass, and their beauty is like the flowers in the field.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Isaiah 40:7">7</a> The grass dries up, the flower wilts when God&#39;s breath blows on it; indeed, people are like grass.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Isaiah 40:8">8</a> The grass dries up, the flower wilts, but God&#39;s word will last forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Isaiah 40:9">9</a> O Zion, who brings good news, go up the high mountain; O Jerusalem, who brings good news, raise your voice loudly; raise it, do not be afraid; tell the cities of Judah, See your God!</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Isaiah 40:10">10</a> Look, God will come with power, and his arm will have authority: see, his reward is with him, and his task is in front of him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Isaiah 40:11">11</a> He will care for his flock like a shepherd: he will gather the young lambs with his arm, hold them close, and gently guide the mothers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Isaiah 40:12">12</a> Who has measured the waters in the palm of his hand, and measured the sky with his hand&#39;s width, and calculated the earth&#39;s dust in a container, and weighed the mountains on scales, and the hills with a balance?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Isaiah 40:13">13</a> Who has guided the Spirit of God, or as his advisor, has taught him?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Isaiah 40:14">14</a> Who gave him advice, and who taught him, and showed him how to judge rightly, and gave him knowledge, and showed him how to understand?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Isaiah 40:15">15</a> Look, the countries are like a drop from a bucket, and they are like fine dust on scales: look, he picks up the islands as if they are very small.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Isaiah 40:16">16</a> Lebanon does not have enough wood for a fire, nor enough animals for a sacrifice.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Isaiah 40:17">17</a> To God, all nations are nothing; they are worth less than nothing, just emptiness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Isaiah 40:18">18</a> Who will you compare to God? Or what image will you compare to him?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Isaiah 40:19">19</a> The worker makes a carved idol, the goldsmith covers it with gold, and makes silver chains for it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Isaiah 40:20">20</a> The poor person without a gift picks a lasting tree and finds a skilled craftsman to make a carved idol that won&#39;t fall over.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Isaiah 40:21">21</a> Haven&#39;t you known? Haven&#39;t you heard? Hasn&#39;t someone told you from the start? Haven&#39;t you understood since the world was made?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Isaiah 40:22">22</a> God sits above the circle of the earth, and its people are like grasshoppers; He stretches out the sky like a curtain and spreads it out like a tent for living.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Isaiah 40:23">23</a> God reduces rulers to nothing; He makes the judges of the earth worthless.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Isaiah 40:24">24</a> Yes, they will not be planted; yes, they will not be sown; yes, their stem will not root in the ground: and when he breathes on them, they will dry up, and the storm will sweep them away like straw.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Isaiah 40:25">25</a> Who can you compare to me? Who is my equal? asks the Holy One.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Isaiah 40:26">26</a> Look up to the sky and see who made these stars. God brings them out one by one and knows each by name. Because of his great strength and power, not a single one is missing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Isaiah 40:27">27</a> Why do you say, Jacob, and complain, Israel, &quot;My path is hidden from God, and my rights are ignored by my God&quot;?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Isaiah 40:28">28</a> Don&#39;t you know? Haven&#39;t you heard that the eternal God, the creator of the whole earth, never gets tired or weary? No one can understand how deep his wisdom is.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Isaiah 40:29">29</a> He gives strength to the tired and gives more power to the weak.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Isaiah 40:30">30</a> Young people will get tired and worn out, and young men will stumble and fall.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Isaiah 40:31">31</a> Those who trust in God will gain new strength; they will soar high on wings like eagles; they will run without getting tired; they will walk without giving up.</p>
