<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Isaiah 44:1">1</a> Listen now, Jacob my servant, and Israel, whom I have chosen:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Isaiah 44:2">2</a> God who created you and shaped you from birth, who will support you, says: Do not be afraid, Jacob, my servant, and Jesurun, whom I have selected.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Isaiah 44:3">3</a> I will give water to the thirsty and send streams over the dry land. I will pour my spirit on your descendants and my blessing on your children.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Isaiah 44:4">4</a> They will grow like plants in the grass, like willow trees by the streams.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Isaiah 44:5">5</a> One will say, &quot;I belong to God&quot;; another will use the name of Jacob for himself; and another will write with his hand, &quot;I am God&#39;s,&quot; and will take the name of Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Isaiah 44:6">6</a> God, the King of Israel, and his rescuer, The Lord of all, says: I am the first, and I am the last; and there is no other God besides me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Isaiah 44:7">7</a> Who else but me can call, announce, and set everything since I chose the old people? Let them reveal the future and what will happen.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Isaiah 44:8">8</a> Do not be afraid or scared; haven&#39;t I told you before and made it clear? You are my witnesses. Is there any other God besides me? No, there is no other God; I do not know of any.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Isaiah 44:9">9</a> People who create idols are without purpose; their cherished objects won&#39;t help them; and they testify to their own ignorance, as they cannot see or understand, leading to their shame.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Isaiah 44:10">10</a> Who has made a god or cast an idol that is no use at all?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Isaiah 44:11">11</a> Look, all his friends will feel shame; and the craftsmen, they are just people: let them all come and stand up; but they will be afraid, and they will all feel shame together.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Isaiah 44:12">12</a> The blacksmith uses tongs to work in the coals, shapes it with hammers, and uses his arms&#39; strength; but he gets hungry and weak, doesn&#39;t drink water, and feels faint.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Isaiah 44:13">13</a> The woodworker measures with a ruler, draws lines, shapes the wood with tools, uses a compass to draw circles, and creates a figure like a person, as beautiful as a person, to stay in the house.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Isaiah 44:14">14</a> He cuts down cedars and picks the cypress and oak, which he makes strong among the forest trees: he plants an ash tree, and the rain helps it grow.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Isaiah 44:15">15</a> Then it will be used by someone to make a fire; he will take some of it to keep warm, he will start a fire and cook bread. Also, he makes a god and bows down to it; he makes it into an idol and worships it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Isaiah 44:16">16</a> He burns some of it in the fire; with some he eats meat; he cooks a meal and is full. Yes, he warms himself and says, &quot;Ah, I&#39;m warm, I&#39;ve seen the fire.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Isaiah 44:17">17</a> With the rest, he makes an idol, his carved image. He bows down to it, worships it, and prays to it, saying, &quot;Save me, for you are my god.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Isaiah 44:18">18</a> They do not know or understand because God has closed their eyes so they cannot see and their hearts so they cannot understand.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Isaiah 44:19">19</a> No one thinks to themselves, nor do they realize or understand enough to say, &quot;I have burned some of it in a fire; yes, I have also baked bread on its embers; I have cooked meat and eaten it. Should I then use the rest to make something disgusting? Should I bow down to a piece of wood?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Isaiah 44:20">20</a> He eats ashes: a tricked heart has led him wrong, so he can&#39;t save himself, or think, &quot;Isn&#39;t there a false thing in my hand?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Isaiah 44:21">21</a> Remember this, Jacob and Israel, because you are my servant. I made you; you are my servant. Israel, I will not forget you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Isaiah 44:22">22</a> I have wiped away your wrongdoings like a heavy cloud, and your sins like a mist. Come back to me, because I have saved you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Isaiah 44:23">23</a> Sing, you heavens, because God has done it; shout, you depths of the earth; burst into song, you mountains, O forest, and every tree in it; for God has saved Jacob and shown his glory in Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Isaiah 44:24">24</a> God says, &quot;I am your savior and creator from birth. I am God who makes everything, who stretched out the skies without help, who spread out the earth on my own.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Isaiah 44:25">25</a> God frustrates the signs of liars and drives diviners crazy; God makes wise men retreat and turns their knowledge into foolishness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Isaiah 44:26">26</a> God confirms the words of his servant and carries out the plans of his messengers; he tells Jerusalem, &quot;You will be populated,&quot; and to the towns of Judah, &quot;You will be rebuilt, and I will restore your ruined places.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Isaiah 44:27">27</a> He tells the waters, &quot;Dry up,&quot; and I will dry your rivers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Isaiah 44:28">28</a> God calls Cyrus his leader, who will do all that God wants; he will order that Jerusalem be rebuilt and the temple&#39;s foundation be laid.</p>
