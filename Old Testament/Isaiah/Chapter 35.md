<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Isaiah 35:1">1</a> The wild and lonely areas will be happy for them; and the desert will be joyful and bloom like a rose.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Isaiah 35:2">2</a> It will bloom a lot and be happy with joy and singing. Lebanon&#39;s beauty will belong to it, as well as the greatness of Carmel and Sharon. They will see God&#39;s glory and greatness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Isaiah 35:3">3</a> Make weak hands strong and steady shaking knees.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Isaiah 35:4">4</a> Tell those who are scared, &quot;Be brave, don&#39;t be afraid. Look, your God will come to punish, God will come to repay; he will come and rescue you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Isaiah 35:5">5</a> The eyes of those who cannot see will be opened, and the ears of those who cannot hear will be cleared.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Isaiah 35:6">6</a> Then the crippled person will jump like a deer, and the mute person&#39;s tongue will sing. For water will flow in the wasteland, and rivers in the dry land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Isaiah 35:7">7</a> The dry ground will turn into pools, and the thirsty earth will have water springs. Where dragons once lived, there will be grass with reeds and rushes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Isaiah 35:8">8</a> A road will be there, a path called the Holy Way. Impure people won&#39;t travel on it. It&#39;s for God&#39;s people; even if they are not wise, they will not go the wrong way.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Isaiah 35:9">9</a> No lion or fierce animal will be there, none will be found there; but those saved by God will walk there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Isaiah 35:10">10</a> God&#39;s rescued people will come back and arrive at Zion singing, with endless joy. They will be happy and joyful, while sadness and sighing will disappear.</p>
