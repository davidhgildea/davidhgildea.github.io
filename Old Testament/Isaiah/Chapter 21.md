<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Isaiah 21:1">1</a> The heavy message about the sea desert. Like storms in the south move through; it comes from the desert, from a scary land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Isaiah 21:2">2</a> A terrible vision is told to me; the cheater cheats, and the destroyer destroys. Attack, Elam: surround, Media; I have ended all its groaning.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Isaiah 21:3">3</a> My waist is full of pain; cramps have gripped me, like a woman in labor. I was bent over when I heard it; I was shocked when I saw it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Isaiah 21:4">4</a> My heart beat fast, and fear scared me: the night that was once joyful for me has become a time of fear.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Isaiah 21:5">5</a> Set the table, stay alert in the tower, eat and drink: get up, leaders, and get the shield ready.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Isaiah 21:6">6</a> God told me, &quot;Go, put a guard on duty, and let him report what he sees.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Isaiah 21:7">7</a> He saw a chariot with two horsemen, a donkey chariot, and a camel chariot; and he listened carefully with great attention.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Isaiah 21:8">8</a> He shouted, &quot;A lion! Sir, I&#39;m always on the lookout during the day, and I stay at my guard post all night.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Isaiah 21:9">9</a> Look, here comes a chariot with two riders. He replied, &quot;Babylon has fallen, has fallen; and all the idols of its gods are shattered on the ground.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Isaiah 21:10">10</a> My people, whom I will thresh and the grain of my threshing floor: what I&#39;ve heard from God, the God of Israel, I have told you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Isaiah 21:11">11</a> The heavy message of Dumah. Someone from Seir calls to me, “Guard, how much of the night is left? Guard, how much of the night is left?”</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Isaiah 21:12">12</a> The guard said, &quot;Morning is coming, and also the night. If you want to ask, then ask. Come back, come.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Isaiah 21:13">13</a> The heavy task is on Arabia. You will stay in the woods in Arabia, you traveling groups of Dedanites.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Isaiah 21:14">14</a> The people of Tema brought water to the thirsty and gave bread to the fugitives.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Isaiah 21:15">15</a> They ran away from the swords, the pulled-out sword, the bent bow, and the harshness of war.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Isaiah 21:16">16</a> God told me that within one year, like the time a hired worker counts, all Kedar&#39;s pride will disappear.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Isaiah 21:17">17</a> The remaining archers from the strong warriors of Kedar&#39;s descendants will decrease because God of Israel has said so.</p>
