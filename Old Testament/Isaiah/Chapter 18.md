<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Isaiah 18:1">1</a> Trouble comes to the land with many winged insects, which is past the rivers of Ethiopia.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Isaiah 18:2">2</a> God sends messengers over the sea, in boats made of reeds on the waters, telling them, &quot;Go quickly to a nation that is spread out and stripped, to a people feared from the start until now; a country measured and crushed, whose land rivers have ruined!&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Isaiah 18:3">3</a> Everyone in the world and living on the earth, look when God raises a flag on the mountains; and when God sounds a trumpet, listen.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Isaiah 18:4">4</a> God said to me, &quot;I will rest, and I will look down from my home like the sun&#39;s warmth on plants, and like dew in the heat of harvest time.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Isaiah 18:5">5</a> Before the harvest, when the bud is mature and the sour grape is getting ripe in the flower, he will cut off the shoots with pruning knives and remove and trim the branches.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Isaiah 18:6">6</a> Birds of the mountains and animals of the earth will gather on them; birds will spend summer on them, and all earth&#39;s animals will spend winter on them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Isaiah 18:7">7</a> At that time, a gift will be given to God from a scattered and stripped people, and from a nation always feared; a country measured and crushed, whose land rivers have ruined, to where God lives, Mount Zion.</p>
