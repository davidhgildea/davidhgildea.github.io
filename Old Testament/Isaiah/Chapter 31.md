<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Isaiah 31:1">1</a> Pity those who look to Egypt for help, relying on horses, trusting in many chariots and strong horsemen, but not turning to Israel&#39;s Holy One or seeking God!</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Isaiah 31:2">2</a> He is wise too and will cause trouble. He won&#39;t take back his words; instead, he will stand up against the bad people&#39;s house and those who do wrong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Isaiah 31:3">3</a> The Egyptians are human, not God; their horses are flesh, not spirit. When God reaches out, both the helper and the helped will fall, and they will all fail together.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Isaiah 31:4">4</a> God told me, like a lion or a young lion growling over its catch, isn&#39;t scared when shepherds come against it and doesn&#39;t care about their noise, so will God come to fight for Mount Zion and its hill.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Isaiah 31:5">5</a> Like birds flying, God will protect Jerusalem; He will also save it and watch over it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Isaiah 31:6">6</a> Turn back to God, who the people of Israel have greatly turned away from.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Isaiah 31:7">7</a> On that day, everyone will throw away their silver and gold idols, which you made with your hands and which cause you to sin.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Isaiah 31:8">8</a> Then the Assyrian will be defeated by the sword, not by a powerful man; and the sword, not by an ordinary man, will destroy him. But he will run away from the sword, and his soldiers will be scattered.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Isaiah 31:9">9</a> He will go to his strong place because he is scared, and his leaders will fear the flag, says God, who has fire in Zion and a furnace in Jerusalem.</p>
