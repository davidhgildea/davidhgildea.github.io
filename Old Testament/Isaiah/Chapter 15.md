<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Isaiah 15:1">1</a> Moab&#39;s heavy problem. Because at night, Moab&#39;s city Ar is destroyed and silenced; at night, Moab&#39;s city Kir is also destroyed and silenced.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Isaiah 15:2">2</a> He has gone up to Bajith and Dibon&#39;s high places to cry. Moab will wail for Nebo and Medeba. Everyone will be bald and every beard shaved off.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Isaiah 15:3">3</a> In their streets, they will wear rough cloth: on their house roofs and in the streets, everyone will cry loudly, shedding many tears.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Isaiah 15:4">4</a> Heshbon and Elealeh will cry out, their voices heard all the way to Jahaz. So, Moab&#39;s armed men will scream; his life will be hard for him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Isaiah 15:5">5</a> I will cry for Moab; its people will run to Zoar, like a young cow: They will cry while going up the hill of Luhith; on the road to Horonaim, they will shout because everything is destroyed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Isaiah 15:6">6</a> The waters of Nimrim will become empty; the hay has dried up, the grass is gone, and nothing green is left.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Isaiah 15:7">7</a> So they will take away the wealth they have gathered and stored to the Brook of Willows.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Isaiah 15:8">8</a> The cry has spread all around Moab&#39;s borders; the wailing reaches Eglaim, and the wailing extends to Beerelim.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Isaiah 15:9">9</a> The waters of Dimon will be filled with blood because I will send more troubles to Dimon, lions to attack those from Moab who escape, and those who are left in the land.</p>
