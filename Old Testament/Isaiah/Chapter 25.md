<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Isaiah 25:1">1</a> God, you are my God; I will lift you up, I will praise your name; because you have done amazing things; your long-ago plans are faithful and true.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Isaiah 25:2">2</a> You have turned a city into a pile of rubble; a fortified city into ruins. A place where foreigners lived, it will no longer be a city; it will never be rebuilt.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Isaiah 25:3">3</a> So, powerful people will honor you; cities of fierce nations will fear you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Isaiah 25:4">4</a> You have been a help to the poor, a support to those in need when in trouble, a shelter from the storm, a shade from the heat, when the fierce ones attack like a storm against a wall.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Isaiah 25:5">5</a> You will quiet the noise of outsiders, like heat in a dry place; as a cloud&#39;s shadow cools the heat, so the power of the cruel ones will be cut down.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Isaiah 25:6">6</a> In this mountain, God will prepare a feast for all people with rich foods and aged wines, with fine meats and the best wines.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Isaiah 25:7">7</a> He will remove the veil covering all nations and the shroud over all peoples on this mountain.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Isaiah 25:8">8</a> He will completely defeat death; God will wipe away all tears; and He will remove His people&#39;s shame from the whole earth: because God has declared it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Isaiah 25:9">9</a> On that day, people will say, &quot;Look, this is our God; we have waited for him to save us. This is God; we&#39;ve waited for him. We will be happy and celebrate his rescue.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Isaiah 25:10">10</a> In this mountain, God&#39;s hand will rest, and Moab will be stepped on under him, just like straw is stepped on for the manure pile.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Isaiah 25:11">11</a> He will stretch out his arms among them, like a swimmer stretches out to swim: and he will lower their pride along with their treasures.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Isaiah 25:12">12</a> God will tear down the strong fortress in your high walls, flatten and reduce it to dust.</p>
