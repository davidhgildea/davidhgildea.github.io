<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Isaiah 22:1">1</a> The trouble with the Valley of Vision. What&#39;s wrong with you that you&#39;ve all gone up to the rooftops?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Isaiah 22:2">2</a> You are busy and noisy, a city full of chaos, a city of joy: your dead were not killed by the sword, nor died in war.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Isaiah 22:3">3</a> All your leaders have run away together, they&#39;ve been captured by the archers; everyone found in you is tied up, after having fled from a distance.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Isaiah 22:4">4</a> So I said, &quot;Turn away from me; I will cry hard. Do not try to comfort me because my people have been destroyed.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Isaiah 22:5">5</a> It is a day of trouble, stomping, and confusion by God in the vision valley, tearing down walls, and shouting to the mountains.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Isaiah 22:6">6</a> Elam carried the arrows with chariots and horsemen, and Kir took off the shield cover.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Isaiah 22:7">7</a> Your best valleys will be filled with chariots, and horsemen will line up at the gate.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Isaiah 22:8">8</a> He removed Judah&#39;s protection, and you looked for weapons in the Forest House on that day.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Isaiah 22:9">9</a> You have also seen the breaks in the wall of the city of David; there are many. And you collected the water from the lower pool.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Isaiah 22:10">10</a> You have counted the houses of Jerusalem, and you have torn down some to strengthen the wall.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Isaiah 22:11">11</a> You also made a trench between the two walls for the water from the old pool, but you did not consider its creator or show respect to the one who made it long ago.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Isaiah 22:12">12</a> On that day, God called for crying, sorrow, shaved heads, and wearing rough cloth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Isaiah 22:13">13</a> Look, happiness and fun, killing cows and sheep, eating meat, and drinking wine: let&#39;s eat and drink because tomorrow we will die.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Isaiah 22:14">14</a> God told me, &quot;This sin will not be forgiven until you die,&quot; says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Isaiah 22:15">15</a> This is what God says: Go to Shebna, the treasurer who is in charge of the palace, and tell him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Isaiah 22:16">16</a> What do you have here? Who is with you that you&#39;ve carved a tomb here, like one who cuts a tomb up high and chisels a home for himself in the rock?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Isaiah 22:17">17</a> Look, God will take you away with strong force and will surely hide you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Isaiah 22:18">18</a> God will surely throw you around roughly like a ball into a big land; there you will die, and there your splendid chariots will become your master&#39;s disgrace.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Isaiah 22:19">19</a> I will force you out of your position, and he will bring you down from your high status.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Isaiah 22:20">20</a> On that day, I will call my servant Eliakim, Hilkiah&#39;s son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Isaiah 22:21">21</a> I will dress him in your robe, give him your belt to make him strong, and put him in charge of your duties. He will be like a father to the people of Jerusalem and to the family of Judah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Isaiah 22:22">22</a> I will put the key to David&#39;s house on his shoulder; he will open, and no one can close; he will close, and no one can open.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Isaiah 22:23">23</a> I will secure him like a nail in a firm place; and he will be like a magnificent seat of honor for his family.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Isaiah 22:24">24</a> They will place on him all the honor of his family, the children and descendants, every small container, from cups to all types of jugs.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Isaiah 22:25">25</a> On that day, God says, the secure nail will be taken out, cut down, and fall; and the load on it will be removed because God has said so.</p>
