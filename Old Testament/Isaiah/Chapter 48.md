<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Isaiah 48:1">1</a> Listen to this, you people of Jacob, named after Israel, who came from Judah&#39;s line, who swear by God&#39;s name and name the God of Israel, but not honestly or rightly.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Isaiah 48:2">2</a> They say they belong to the holy city and rely on the God of Israel; God of the heavenly armies is his name.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Isaiah 48:3">3</a> I told about the past from the start; they came from my mouth, I showed them; I acted quickly, and they happened.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Isaiah 48:4">4</a> Because I knew you are stubborn, your neck is strong like iron, and your forehead like brass.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Isaiah 48:5">5</a> From the start, I told you what would happen; I showed you before it happened so you wouldn&#39;t claim your idol or carved or cast image made it happen.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Isaiah 48:6">6</a> You have heard, now you see all of this; won&#39;t you tell it? I have shown you new things from now on, even secrets you did not know.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Isaiah 48:7">7</a> They were made just now, not long ago; before today, you did not hear them, so you can&#39;t say, &quot;Look, I knew about them.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Isaiah 48:8">8</a> Yes, you did not hear; yes, you did not know; yes, since your ear was not open: because I knew you would be very unfaithful, and you were named a rule-breaker from birth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Isaiah 48:9">9</a> For my reputation, I will hold back my anger, and for my honor, I will stop myself for your sake so I don&#39;t destroy you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Isaiah 48:10">10</a> Look, I have made you pure but not like silver; I have picked you in the hard times.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Isaiah 48:11">11</a> For my own sake, yes, for my own sake, I will act: why should my name be dishonored? I will not give my glory to anyone else.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Isaiah 48:12">12</a> Listen to me, Jacob and Israel, whom I have called; I am the one; I am the first, and I am also the last.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Isaiah 48:13">13</a> My hand has set the earth&#39;s foundation, and my right hand spread out the skies: when I call them, they rise up at once.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Isaiah 48:14">14</a> Everyone, come together and listen; who among them has announced these events? God loves him; he will fulfill his wishes on Babylon, and his power will be against the Chaldeans.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Isaiah 48:15">15</a> I, yes I, have spoken; I have called him: I have brought him, and he will succeed on his path.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Isaiah 48:16">16</a> Come close to me, listen to this: I have not spoken in secret from the start; I have been there since it began, and now God and his Spirit have sent me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Isaiah 48:17">17</a> God says, &quot;I am your Redeemer, the Holy One of Israel; I am the Lord your God who teaches you to succeed and guides you on the right path.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Isaiah 48:18">18</a> If only you had listened to my commands! Then your peace would be like a river, and your goodness like the sea&#39;s waves.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Isaiah 48:19">19</a> Your descendants would have been as many as the sand, and the children born to you like its pebbles; their name would not have been erased or wiped out in my presence.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Isaiah 48:20">20</a> Leave Babylon, run away from the Chaldeans, sing out loud and announce, share the news everywhere on earth; say, God has saved his servant Jacob.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Isaiah 48:21">21</a> They were not thirsty when God guided them through the deserts; he made water flow from the rock for them; he split the rock, and water poured out.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Isaiah 48:22">22</a> God says there is no peace for the wicked.</p>
