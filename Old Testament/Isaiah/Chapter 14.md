<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Isaiah 14:1">1</a> God will show mercy to Jacob and will still choose Israel, and will place them in their own land; and outsiders will join them and stick with Jacob&#39;s family.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Isaiah 14:2">2</a> The people will take them and bring them to their place; the Israelites will own them in God&#39;s land as servants and maids. They will capture those who had captured them, and they will govern over those who had oppressed them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Isaiah 14:3">3</a> In the future, when God gives you rest from your sadness, your fear, and the tough slavery you were forced to work in,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Isaiah 14:4">4</a> You will say this saying against the king of Babylon: How has the bully stopped! The rich city is no more!</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Isaiah 14:5">5</a> God has broken the stick of the bad people and the rod of the leaders.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Isaiah 14:6">6</a> The one who struck the people in anger with unending blows, who ruled the nations with fury, is being chased, with no one to stop it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Isaiah 14:7">7</a> The whole world is calm and peaceful: they burst into song.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Isaiah 14:8">8</a> Yes, the fir trees are happy about you, and the cedars of Lebanon say, &quot;Since you&#39;ve been gone, no woodcutter has come to cut us down.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Isaiah 14:9">9</a> Hell below is stirred up to welcome you when you arrive; it wakes up the dead for you, all the important leaders on earth; it has made all the kings of the nations rise from their thrones.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Isaiah 14:10">10</a> Everyone will speak and say to you, &quot;Have you also become weak like us? Are you now the same as we are?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Isaiah 14:11">11</a> Your grandeur has been brought down to the grave, along with the sound of your stringed instruments; worms are spread beneath you, and they cover you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Isaiah 14:12">12</a> You have fallen from heaven, O bright star, son of the morning! You have been cut down to the earth, you who weakened the nations!</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Isaiah 14:13">13</a> For you have thought to yourself, I will go up to heaven, I will raise my throne higher than God&#39;s stars; I will sit on the mountain where the gods meet, in the far north.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Isaiah 14:14">14</a> I will go up higher than the clouds; I will be like God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Isaiah 14:15">15</a> You will be brought down to hell, to the edge of the deep pit.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Isaiah 14:16">16</a> People who see you will stare and think, &quot;Is this the man who made the earth tremble and shook kingdoms?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Isaiah 14:17">17</a> God made the world empty and ruined its cities; He did not let the prisoners go free from their prison.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Isaiah 14:18">18</a> Every king of the nations lies in honor, each one in his own tomb.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Isaiah 14:19">19</a> But you are thrown out of your tomb like a hated branch, dressed like those killed with a sword, going down to the rocks of the pit, like a body stepped on.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Isaiah 14:20">20</a> You will not be buried with them because you ruined your land and killed your people. The children of those who do evil will never be honored.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Isaiah 14:21">21</a> Get ready to punish his children for their fathers&#39; wrongs; so they won&#39;t thrive, take over the land, or cover the earth with their towns.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Isaiah 14:22">22</a> I will fight against them, says God, and I will remove from Babylon its name and survivors, children, and relatives, says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Isaiah 14:23">23</a> I will make it a place for owls and fill it with water holes; I will clean it out with a broom of ruin, says God of all.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Isaiah 14:24">24</a> God has promised, saying, just as I have planned, it will happen; and just as I have decided, it will remain.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Isaiah 14:25">25</a> I will defeat the Assyrian in my land, and crush him on my mountains. Then their slavery will end, and their burden will be lifted from their shoulders.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Isaiah 14:26">26</a> This is the plan made for the whole earth, and this is the hand reaching out to all nations.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Isaiah 14:27">27</a> God has a plan, and who can change it? His power is at work, and who can stop it?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Isaiah 14:28">28</a> The year King Ahaz died, this heavy message came.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Isaiah 14:29">29</a> Do not be happy, all of Palestine, because the stick of the one who hit you is broken: a venomous snake will come from the serpent&#39;s family, and it will produce a burning flying serpent.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Isaiah 14:30">30</a> The poorest firstborn will have food, and those in need will rest safely. But I will destroy your roots with hunger, and what&#39;s left of you will be killed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Isaiah 14:31">31</a> Cry out, O gate; weep, O city; all of you in Palestine are breaking down: because smoke will come from the north, and no one will be by themselves at the set times.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Isaiah 14:32">32</a> What should we tell the nation&#39;s messengers? That God has established Zion, and his needy people will find safety in it.</p>
