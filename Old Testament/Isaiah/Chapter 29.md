<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Isaiah 29:1">1</a> Sadness to Ariel, the city where David lived! Keep adding year after year; continue the sacrifices.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Isaiah 29:2">2</a> I will trouble Ariel, and there will be sadness and pain; and Ariel will be to me as Ariel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Isaiah 29:3">3</a> I will surround you with my camp, attack you with a siege mound, and build towers to besiege you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Isaiah 29:4">4</a> You will be brought low, and you will speak from the ground, and your words will be soft from the dust, and your voice will be like a ghost from the ground, and your words will be a whisper from the dust.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Isaiah 29:5">5</a> Many strangers among you will be like tiny dust, and many cruel people will vanish like chaff blown away, and all this will happen quickly, in a moment.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Isaiah 29:6">6</a> You will be visited by God with thunder, earthquake, loud noise, storm, tempest, and flames of consuming fire.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Isaiah 29:7">7</a> The crowds from all countries battling Ariel, and all attacking her and her defenses, making her suffer, will be like a dream seen in the night.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Isaiah 29:8">8</a> It will be like a hungry person who dreams of eating, but wakes up still hungry, or like a thirsty person who dreams of drinking, but wakes up thirsty and weak. This is how it will be for all the nations that fight against God&#39;s holy mountain.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Isaiah 29:9">9</a> Wait and be amazed; shout out loud, they act drunk, but not from wine; they stumble, but not from strong drink.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Isaiah 29:10">10</a> God has made you fall into a deep sleep and has shut your eyes. The prophets and leaders, the seers, he has covered.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Isaiah 29:11">11</a> The whole vision is like a sealed book to you. When it is given to someone who can read and they are asked, &quot;Please read this,&quot; they say, &quot;I can&#39;t, because it is sealed.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Isaiah 29:12">12</a> The book is given to someone who can&#39;t read, and they are asked, &quot;Please read this,&quot; but he answers, &quot;I can&#39;t read.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Isaiah 29:13">13</a> So God said, &quot;Although these people come close to me with their words and honor me with their lips, their hearts are far from me, and their fear of me is just a human command they&#39;ve been taught.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Isaiah 29:14">14</a> So, I will do an amazing thing for these people, a truly amazing thing that will amaze them: their wise people will lose their wisdom, and their smart people will not be able to understand.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Isaiah 29:15">15</a> Trouble awaits those who try to hide their plans from God; they do their deeds in secret and think, &quot;Who sees us? Who knows what we&#39;re doing?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Isaiah 29:16">16</a> Thinking you can twist things will be seen as silly as clay questioning the potter: Can what is made say to the one who made it, &quot;You didn&#39;t make me&quot;? Or can a creation say to its creator, &quot;You don&#39;t know what you&#39;re doing&quot;?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Isaiah 29:17">17</a> Won&#39;t it soon happen that Lebanon becomes a fertile land, and the fertile land will be considered like a forest?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Isaiah 29:18">18</a> On that day, the deaf will hear the words of the book, and the blind will see, even from darkness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Isaiah 29:19">19</a> Humble people will find more joy in God, and the needy will celebrate the Holy One of Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Isaiah 29:20">20</a> The dreadful one is defeated, the mocker is destroyed, and all who look for evil are stopped.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Isaiah 29:21">21</a> They make a person guilty because of a single word, set traps for those who correct in public, and twist the truth to harm the innocent for no reason.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Isaiah 29:22">22</a> So God, who saved Abraham, says about Jacob&#39;s family: Jacob won&#39;t be ashamed now, nor will his face turn pale.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Isaiah 29:23">23</a> But when he sees his children, my creation, around him, they will honor my name, respect the Holy One of Jacob, and fear the God of Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Isaiah 29:24">24</a> Those who were confused will understand, and those who complained will learn what to believe.</p>
