<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Isaiah 12:1">1</a> On that day, you will say, &quot;God, I will praise you. Even though you were angry with me, your anger has gone, and you have comforted me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Isaiah 12:2">2</a> Look, God is my savior; I will trust and not fear: for God is my strength and my song; he has also become my savior.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Isaiah 12:3">3</a> So with happiness you will get water from the springs of rescue.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Isaiah 12:4">4</a> On that day you will say, &quot;Praise God, ask for his help, tell everyone what he has done, and remember that his name is great.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Isaiah 12:5">5</a> Sing to God; for He has done wonderful things: this is known all over the earth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Isaiah 12:6">6</a> Shout loudly, you who live in Zion, because the Holy One of Israel is great among you.</p>
