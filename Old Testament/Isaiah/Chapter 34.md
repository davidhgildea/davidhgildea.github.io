<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Isaiah 34:1">1</a> Come close, all you countries, to listen; and pay attention, all you peoples: let the earth and everything on it hear; the world and all that comes out of it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Isaiah 34:2">2</a> God is angry with all nations and furious with their armies; he has completely destroyed them and handed them over to be killed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Isaiah 34:3">3</a> Their dead bodies will be thrown away, and they will smell bad. The mountains will flow with their blood.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Isaiah 34:4">4</a> Every star in the sky will disappear, and the sky will close up like a scroll. All the stars will fall, like leaves drop from a grapevine, and like figs drop from a fig tree.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Isaiah 34:5">5</a> My sword will be ready in the sky; it will fall on Edom and the people I have decided to punish, for punishment.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Isaiah 34:6">6</a> God&#39;s sword is covered with blood, it is thick with fat, from the blood of lambs and goats, and from the fat of ram&#39;s kidneys: because God has a sacrifice in Bozrah and a big killing in the land of Idumea.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Isaiah 34:7">7</a> The unicorns will come down with them, and the young bulls with the grown bulls; their land will be wet with blood, and their soil rich with fat.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Isaiah 34:8">8</a> It is the day of God&#39;s revenge, and the time to pay back for the dispute over Zion.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Isaiah 34:9">9</a> Its rivers will turn into tar, its soil into sulfur, and its ground will burn like tar.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Isaiah 34:10">10</a> It will not stop burning day or night; its smoke will rise forever. It will remain empty from one generation to another; no one will ever pass through it again.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Isaiah 34:11">11</a> The cormorant and bittern will live there; the owl and raven will make it their home. God will mark it with lines of chaos and stones of emptiness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Isaiah 34:12">12</a> They will invite its nobles to rule, but no one will be there, and all its leaders will become nothing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Isaiah 34:13">13</a> Thorns will grow in her palaces, nettles and brambles in her strongholds; it will be a home for dragons and a place for owls.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Isaiah 34:14">14</a> Desert animals will meet with island creatures, and goat-demons will call to each other; the night bird will also settle there and find a resting place.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Isaiah 34:15">15</a> The great owl will build its nest there, lay eggs, hatch them, and protect them under its wings; vultures will also meet there, each with its partner.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Isaiah 34:16">16</a> Look in God&#39;s book and read: none of these will be missing, none will be without a partner, because God has ordered it, and his spirit has brought them together.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Isaiah 34:17">17</a> He has decided their share by casting lots, and his hand has distributed it to them with measuring lines: they will own it forever, and they will live there from one generation to the next.</p>
