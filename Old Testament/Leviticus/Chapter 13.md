<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Leviticus 13:1">1</a> God spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Leviticus 13:2">2</a> When a person has a swelling, a rash, or a shiny spot on their skin that may be a sign of leprosy, they must go to Aaron the priest or one of his sons who are priests.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Leviticus 13:3">3</a> The priest will check the skin disease: if the hair turns white and the disease seems deeper than the skin, it is leprosy. The priest will see this and say the person is not clean.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Leviticus 13:4">4</a> If there&#39;s a white bright spot on the skin and it&#39;s not deeper than the skin and the hair hasn&#39;t turned white, the priest must isolate the person with the sickness for seven days.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Leviticus 13:5">5</a> On the seventh day, the priest will examine the person, and if the disease has not spread and is not active, the priest will isolate the person for another seven days.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Leviticus 13:6">6</a> On the seventh day, the priest will look at the person again. If the infection looks darker and hasn&#39;t spread on the skin, the priest will say they are clean. It&#39;s only a scab. Then they must wash their clothes and they will be clean.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Leviticus 13:7">7</a> If the scab spreads widely on the skin after the priest has checked him for being clean, the priest must examine him again.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Leviticus 13:8">8</a> If the priest sees the scab spreading on the skin, the priest must say he is unclean because it is leprosy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Leviticus 13:9">9</a> If a man has leprosy, he must go to the priest.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Leviticus 13:10">10</a> The priest must look at him, and if there&#39;s a white swelling on the skin that has turned the hair white, and there is fresh, red skin in the swelling.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Leviticus 13:11">11</a> The skin disease is serious and old, and the priest will say he is unclean; he won&#39;t isolate him because he is already unclean.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Leviticus 13:12">12</a> If someone&#39;s skin breaks out with leprosy all over from head to foot, wherever the priest checks;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Leviticus 13:13">13</a> The priest will look, and if the skin disease has spread over all the person&#39;s skin, he will declare the person with the disease clean because it has all become white; the person is clean.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Leviticus 13:14">14</a> But if he sees raw flesh, he must be considered unclean.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Leviticus 13:15">15</a> The priest will look at the sore skin and say it is not clean: because sore skin is not clean: it is a disease.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Leviticus 13:16">16</a> If the sore heals and turns white, he should go to the priest.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Leviticus 13:17">17</a> The priest will look at the person and if the disease has become white, the priest will declare that the person with the disease is clean.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Leviticus 13:18">18</a> If someone had a boil on their skin and it healed,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Leviticus 13:19">19</a> If there&#39;s a white swelling or a bright white and reddish spot where the boil was, show it to the priest.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Leviticus 13:20">20</a> If the priest sees that the mark looks deeper than the skin and the hair in it has turned white, the priest must say the person is unclean. It is a case of leprosy that has come from the sore.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Leviticus 13:21">21</a> If the priest sees it and there are no white hairs, and it&#39;s not below the skin but a bit dark, then the priest must isolate him for seven days.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Leviticus 13:22">22</a> If it spreads a lot on the skin, then the priest must say he is not clean: it is a disease.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Leviticus 13:23">23</a> If the bright spot stays the same and does not spread, it is a healed boil; the priest will say he is clean.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Leviticus 13:24">24</a> If there is a burning sore on someone&#39;s skin and the healed part turns white or reddish-white.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Leviticus 13:25">25</a> The priest will look at it, and if the hair in the bright area has turned white and it appears to be deeper than the skin, it is leprosy that has come from the sore. So, the priest will say he is not clean; it is leprosy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Leviticus 13:26">26</a> If the priest sees the spot and there is no white hair in it, if it&#39;s not deeper than the skin but is dark, then the priest will isolate the person for seven days.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Leviticus 13:27">27</a> On the seventh day, the priest will examine him, and if the skin infection has spread widely, the priest will declare him unclean because it is leprosy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Leviticus 13:28">28</a> If the shiny spot stays the same and does not spread on the skin, but gets a little darker, it is a scar from the burn. The priest will say he is clean because it is just the swelling from the burn.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Leviticus 13:29">29</a> If a man or woman has a disease on their head or beard;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Leviticus 13:30">30</a> The priest will look at the sore, and if it is deeper than the skin and has a thin yellow hair, the priest will declare the person unclean. It is a dry scaly infection, a form of leprosy on the head or beard.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Leviticus 13:31">31</a> If the priest sees the scall infection and it&#39;s not deeper than the skin, and there&#39;s no black hair in it, then the priest will quarantine the person with the scall infection for seven days.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Leviticus 13:32">32</a> On the seventh day, the priest will examine the sore. If it hasn&#39;t spread, there&#39;s no yellow hair, and it&#39;s not deeper than the skin,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Leviticus 13:33">33</a> He must shave his hair, but not the scall. The priest will isolate the person with the scall for another seven days.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Leviticus 13:34">34</a> On the seventh day, the priest will check the scall. If the scall has not spread on the skin or looks deep, the priest will say he is clean. Then he must wash his clothes and will be clean.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Leviticus 13:35">35</a> If the rash spreads on the skin after he is cleaned;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Leviticus 13:36">36</a> The priest will look at him and if the scall has spread on the skin, the priest won&#39;t look for yellow hair; he is unclean.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Leviticus 13:37">37</a> If the sore hasn&#39;t spread and black hair has grown in it, the sore is healed and the person is clean; the priest will declare them clean.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Leviticus 13:38">38</a> If a man or woman has shiny white spots on their skin;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="Leviticus 13:39">39</a> Then the priest will look, and if there are light white spots on their skin, it&#39;s just a common freckle; they are clean.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="Leviticus 13:40">40</a> If a man loses his hair and becomes bald, he is still clean.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="Leviticus 13:41">41</a> Anyone whose hair has fallen out from the front of their head is bald on the forehead but is still clean.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="Leviticus 13:42">42</a> If there is a white and red sore on the bald head or forehead, it is leprosy that has developed on the bald spot.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v43"><a class="verse-num" href="#v43" aria-label="Leviticus 13:43">43</a> The priest will look at it, and if there is a white-red swelling on his bald head or forehead, it is like leprosy on the skin.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v44"><a class="verse-num" href="#v44" aria-label="Leviticus 13:44">44</a> He has leprosy and is not clean. The priest must say he is completely not clean; his disease is on his head.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v45"><a class="verse-num" href="#v45" aria-label="Leviticus 13:45">45</a> The person with leprosy who has the sickness must tear their clothes, leave their head uncovered, cover their upper lip, and shout, &quot;Unclean, unclean.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v46"><a class="verse-num" href="#v46" aria-label="Leviticus 13:46">46</a> Every day the sickness is in him, he is unclean and must live alone outside the camp.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v47"><a class="verse-num" href="#v47" aria-label="Leviticus 13:47">47</a> The piece of clothing that has leprosy, whether it is made of wool or linen;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v48"><a class="verse-num" href="#v48" aria-label="Leviticus 13:48">48</a> Whether it&#39;s in the threads, in linen, in wool, in leather, or in anything made of leather;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v49"><a class="verse-num" href="#v49" aria-label="Leviticus 13:49">49</a> If the infection on the clothes or skin looks green or red, whether on the threads or in the fabric or on something made of skin, it is a sign of leprosy and must be shown to the priest.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v50"><a class="verse-num" href="#v50" aria-label="Leviticus 13:50">50</a> The priest will examine the disease and isolate the affected person for seven days.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v51"><a class="verse-num" href="#v51" aria-label="Leviticus 13:51">51</a> On the seventh day, he will check the disease. If the disease has spread in the fabric, whether the threads or the weave, or in leather, or in anything made of leather, the disease is a harmful skin condition; it is not clean.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v52"><a class="verse-num" href="#v52" aria-label="Leviticus 13:52">52</a> He must burn the clothing, whether it is woven or knitted from wool or linen, or made of leather, if it has the disease on it. It is a persistent infection and must be burned with fire.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v53"><a class="verse-num" href="#v53" aria-label="Leviticus 13:53">53</a> If the priest looks and sees the disease has not spread in the cloth, whether woven or knitted, or in anything made of leather;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v54"><a class="verse-num" href="#v54" aria-label="Leviticus 13:54">54</a> The priest will order them to wash the item with the disease, and he will isolate it for another seven days.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v55"><a class="verse-num" href="#v55" aria-label="Leviticus 13:55">55</a> The priest will look at the sickness after washing it. If the sickness hasn&#39;t changed color or spread, it&#39;s not clean. You must burn it because the disease is deep, whether it shows on the inside or outside.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v56"><a class="verse-num" href="#v56" aria-label="Leviticus 13:56">56</a> If the priest sees that the spot is darker after it has been washed, he must cut it out of the fabric, leather, or woven or knitted material.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v57"><a class="verse-num" href="#v57" aria-label="Leviticus 13:57">57</a> If the problem remains in the cloth, whether in the threads or in anything made of skin, it is a spreading disease: you must burn the infected item with fire.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v58"><a class="verse-num" href="#v58" aria-label="Leviticus 13:58">58</a> If you wash a piece of clothing, whether woven or knitted, or any leather item, and the disease goes away, you must wash it again and then it will be clean.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v59"><a class="verse-num" href="#v59" aria-label="Leviticus 13:59">59</a> This is the rule for a disease like leprosy in clothes made of wool or linen, whether in the threads or in anything made of skin, to say if it is clean or not clean.</p>
