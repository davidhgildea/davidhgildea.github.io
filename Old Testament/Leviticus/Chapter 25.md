<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Leviticus 25:1">1</a> God spoke to Moses on Mount Sinai and said.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Leviticus 25:2">2</a> Talk to the people of Israel and tell them, when you enter the land I am giving you, the land must rest for God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Leviticus 25:3">3</a> For six years you should plant your fields, trim your vineyard, and collect its fruit.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Leviticus 25:4">4</a> In the seventh year, the land must rest and have a sabbath for God. You must not plant your fields or trim your vineyards.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Leviticus 25:5">5</a> Do not cut what grows by itself in your harvest or pick grapes from your unpruned vines; this is a year for the land to rest.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Leviticus 25:6">6</a> The land&#39;s rest will provide food for you, your servants, your maids, your workers, and any travelers staying with you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Leviticus 25:7">7</a> Your animals and the wild creatures in your land will have all the crops to eat.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Leviticus 25:8">8</a> You must count seven sets of seven years, adding up to forty-nine years in total.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Leviticus 25:9">9</a> You will blow the trumpet to announce the Jubilee on the tenth day of the seventh month, and on the Day of Atonement, the trumpet will be heard everywhere in your land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Leviticus 25:10">10</a> Make the fiftieth year holy, and announce freedom all over the country to everyone living there: it will be a special celebration for you; and everyone will go back to their own property, and everyone will go back to their own family.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Leviticus 25:11">11</a> That fiftieth year will be a special celebration for you: you must not plant seeds, harvest crops that grow on their own, or pick grapes from untrimmed vines.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Leviticus 25:12">12</a> It is a special year; it should be sacred to you. You can eat what grows in the fields.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Leviticus 25:13">13</a> In the year of this special celebration, each person should go back to their own property.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Leviticus 25:14">14</a> If you sell something to your neighbor or buy something from your neighbor&#39;s hand, do not cheat each other.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Leviticus 25:15">15</a> Based on the years left after the Jubilee, you will buy from your neighbor, and according to the years of crops, he will sell to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Leviticus 25:16">16</a> The more years there are, the higher the price will be, and the fewer years there are, the lower the price will be, because it&#39;s sold based on how many years of crops it can produce.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Leviticus 25:17">17</a> Do not treat each other unfairly, but respect your God; for I am your God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Leviticus 25:18">18</a> So you should follow my rules, obey my laws, and act on them; then you will live safely in the land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Leviticus 25:19">19</a> The land will produce its crops, you will eat as much as you need, and you will live there safely.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Leviticus 25:20">20</a> If you ask, &quot;What will we eat in the seventh year if we don&#39;t plant or harvest our crops?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Leviticus 25:21">21</a> Then I will order my blessing for you in the sixth year, and it will produce crops for three years.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Leviticus 25:22">22</a> You will plant crops in the eighth year and eat from the old produce until the ninth year; you will eat from the old supplies until the new crops are ready.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Leviticus 25:23">23</a> The land must not be sold permanently, because the land is mine and you are only travelers and temporary residents with me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Leviticus 25:24">24</a> In all the land you own, you must provide a way to buy it back.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Leviticus 25:25">25</a> If your brother becomes poor and sells some of his property, and a relative comes to buy it back, then they should buy back what their brother sold.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Leviticus 25:26">26</a> If the man has no one to buy it back for him, but he can afford to buy it back himself;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Leviticus 25:27">27</a> Let him calculate the years since he sold it and give back the extra amount to the person he sold it to, so that person can return to his property.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Leviticus 25:28">28</a> If he can&#39;t pay back, what he sold stays with the buyer until the Year of Jubilee. Then it will be returned, and he will get his property back.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Leviticus 25:29">29</a> If someone sells a house in a city with walls, they can buy it back within one year after the sale.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Leviticus 25:30">30</a> If the house in the city with walls is not bought back within a year, then it will belong forever to the person who bought it, and it will not be returned in the Jubilee.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Leviticus 25:31">31</a> The houses in unwalled villages will be treated like country land: they can be bought back, and they will be freed in the Jubilee.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Leviticus 25:32">32</a> Despite this, the Levites can buy back the cities and houses they own at any time.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Leviticus 25:33">33</a> If someone buys a house from the Levites, that house in the Levites&#39; town becomes theirs again in the Jubilee year, because the houses in Levite cities are their permanent property among the Israelites.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Leviticus 25:34">34</a> But the land around their cities cannot be sold; it is theirs forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Leviticus 25:35">35</a> If your brother becomes poor and cannot support himself with you, you must help him. Even if he is a stranger or a visitor, so he can live with you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Leviticus 25:36">36</a> Do not charge him interest or profit, but respect God, so your brother can survive with you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Leviticus 25:37">37</a> You must not lend him your money with interest, nor give him your food to get more back.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Leviticus 25:38">38</a> I am God, your God, who brought you out of Egypt to give you the land of Canaan and to be your God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="Leviticus 25:39">39</a> If your nearby brother becomes poor and sells himself to you, don&#39;t make him work like a slave.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="Leviticus 25:40">40</a> Like a paid worker and a temporary resident, he will stay with you and work for you until the Jubilee year.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="Leviticus 25:41">41</a> He will then leave you, along with his children, and go back to his family and his ancestors&#39; property.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="Leviticus 25:42">42</a> They are my servants, whom I freed from Egypt; they must not be sold as slaves.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v43"><a class="verse-num" href="#v43" aria-label="Leviticus 25:43">43</a> Do not treat him harshly, but respect your God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v44"><a class="verse-num" href="#v44" aria-label="Leviticus 25:44">44</a> You can have male and female slaves from the foreign nations around you; you can buy male and female slaves from them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v45"><a class="verse-num" href="#v45" aria-label="Leviticus 25:45">45</a> You can buy foreigners who live with you and their families born in your land; they will belong to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v46"><a class="verse-num" href="#v46" aria-label="Leviticus 25:46">46</a> You will receive them as a heritage for your children after you, to own as property; they will be your servants forever. But do not rule harshly over your fellow Israelites.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v47"><a class="verse-num" href="#v47" aria-label="Leviticus 25:47">47</a> If a foreigner or stranger becomes wealthy among you, and your fellow countryman becomes poor and sells himself to the foreigner living among you, or to a foreigner&#39;s family.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v48"><a class="verse-num" href="#v48" aria-label="Leviticus 25:48">48</a> After he is sold, he can be bought back; one of his relatives can buy him back.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v49"><a class="verse-num" href="#v49" aria-label="Leviticus 25:49">49</a> His uncle or his cousin can buy him back, or any close relative from his family can do it; or if he gets the money, he can buy himself back.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v50"><a class="verse-num" href="#v50" aria-label="Leviticus 25:50">50</a> He must calculate with the one who bought him, starting from the year he was sold until the jubilee year. The cost of his sale will depend on how many years are left, just like the wage of a hired worker.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v51"><a class="verse-num" href="#v51" aria-label="Leviticus 25:51">51</a> If many years remain, he must pay back the redemption price from the money he was bought with.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v52"><a class="verse-num" href="#v52" aria-label="Leviticus 25:52">52</a> If only a few years are left until the Year of Jubilee, he must calculate that with him and pay back the cost of his freedom based on the remaining years.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v53"><a class="verse-num" href="#v53" aria-label="Leviticus 25:53">53</a> He shall be treated like a worker hired year by year; he must not be treated harshly in your presence.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v54"><a class="verse-num" href="#v54" aria-label="Leviticus 25:54">54</a> If he is not set free within those years, then he and his children will be released in the Year of Jubilee.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v55"><a class="verse-num" href="#v55" aria-label="Leviticus 25:55">55</a> The people of Israel are my servants; they are the ones I freed from Egypt. I am your God.</p>
