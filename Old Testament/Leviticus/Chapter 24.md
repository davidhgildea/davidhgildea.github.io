<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Leviticus 24:1">1</a> God spoke to Moses, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Leviticus 24:2">2</a> Tell the Israelites to bring you pure, crushed olive oil for the lamps so they will keep burning all the time.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Leviticus 24:3">3</a> Aaron must keep the lamps burning from evening until morning before God inside the tent of meeting. This is to be a lasting rule for all your descendants.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Leviticus 24:4">4</a> He will arrange the lamps on the clean lampstand before God all the time.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Leviticus 24:5">5</a> Take good flour and make twelve cakes; each cake should have two-tenths of an ephah of flour.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Leviticus 24:6">6</a> Place them in two lines, six in each line, on the clean table before God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Leviticus 24:7">7</a> Put pure frankincense on each row so it will be on the bread as a reminder, an offering to God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Leviticus 24:8">8</a> Every Sabbath, he should arrange it before God always, as it is an eternal promise from the people of Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Leviticus 24:9">9</a> Aaron and his sons will own it, and they will eat it in a sacred place. It is very holy to them as part of God&#39;s offerings made with fire, a rule to follow always.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Leviticus 24:10">10</a> The son of an Israeli woman and an Egyptian man went out among the Israelis, and he and an Israeli man fought in the camp.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Leviticus 24:11">11</a> The son of the Israelite woman spoke against God&#39;s name and cursed. They took him to Moses; his mother was Shelomith, daughter of Dibri from the Dan tribe.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Leviticus 24:12">12</a> They kept him in custody to find out what God wanted.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Leviticus 24:13">13</a> God spoke to Moses, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Leviticus 24:14">14</a> Take the person who cursed outside the camp; everyone who heard them must touch their head, and then everyone in the assembly must throw stones at them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Leviticus 24:15">15</a> You will tell the people of Israel, if anyone curses their God, they will be guilty of sin.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Leviticus 24:16">16</a> Anyone who speaks against God’s name must be put to death. The whole community must stone them. It doesn&#39;t matter if they are a foreigner or born in the land; if they speak against God’s name, they must be put to death.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Leviticus 24:17">17</a> Whoever kills a person must surely be put to death.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Leviticus 24:18">18</a> If someone kills an animal, they must replace it; one animal for another.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Leviticus 24:19">19</a> If a person hurts their neighbor, they should be hurt in the same way.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Leviticus 24:20">20</a> Injury for injury, eye for eye, tooth for tooth: if someone hurts another, the same hurt will be given to them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Leviticus 24:21">21</a> If someone kills an animal, they must replace it; if someone kills a person, they must be put to death.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Leviticus 24:22">22</a> You will have one law, for both the foreigner and the native-born, because I am your God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Leviticus 24:23">23</a> Moses told the Israelites to take the person who cursed outside the camp and throw stones at him. The Israelites did what God told Moses.</p>
