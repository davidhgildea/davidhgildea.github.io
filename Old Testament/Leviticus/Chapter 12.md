<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Leviticus 12:1">1</a> God spoke to Moses, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Leviticus 12:2">2</a> Talk to the Israelites and say, if a woman has a baby and it is a boy, she will be unclean for seven days, just like when she is on her period.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Leviticus 12:3">3</a> On the eighth day, the skin of his private part should be cut.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Leviticus 12:4">4</a> She must then wait thirty-three days to be purified from her bleeding. She must not touch anything sacred or go to the holy place until her purification period is over.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Leviticus 12:5">5</a> If she gives birth to a girl, she will be unclean for two weeks like during her period. She will stay in the blood of her cleansing for 66 days.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Leviticus 12:6">6</a> When the time of her cleansing for a son or daughter is complete, she must bring a one-year-old lamb for a burnt offering and a young pigeon or dove for a sin offering to the entrance of the meeting tent, to the priest.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Leviticus 12:7">7</a> Who will present it to God and provide a sacrifice for her; she will then be purified from her bleeding. This is the rule for a woman who has given birth to a boy or a girl.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Leviticus 12:8">8</a> If she can&#39;t afford a lamb, she must bring two doves or two young pigeons; one for a sacrifice to honor God, and the other for a sin offering. The priest will perform a ceremony to forgive her sins, and then she will be pure.</p>
