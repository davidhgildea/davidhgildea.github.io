<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Leviticus 1:1">1</a> God called to Moses and spoke to him from the tent where people gather, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Leviticus 1:2">2</a> Talk to the people of Israel and tell them: If anyone wants to give an offering to God, it should be a farm animal from the herd or flock.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Leviticus 1:3">3</a> If he brings a burnt offering from the herd, it must be a flawless male. He must present it by his own choice at the entrance of the meeting tent before God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Leviticus 1:4">4</a> He must place his hand on the head of the burnt offering, and it will be accepted on his behalf to forgive him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Leviticus 1:5">5</a> He must kill the bull before God, and Aaron&#39;s sons, the priests, will take the blood and sprinkle it all around the altar near the tent of meeting&#39;s entrance.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Leviticus 1:6">6</a> He will remove the skin of the burned offering and cut it into pieces.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Leviticus 1:7">7</a> Aaron&#39;s sons, who are priests, will put fire on the altar and arrange the wood on the fire.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Leviticus 1:8">8</a> The priests, who are Aaron&#39;s sons, will place the pieces, the head, and the fat on the wood that is burning on the altar.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Leviticus 1:9">9</a> He must wash its inner parts and legs with water. Then the priest will burn everything on the altar as a burnt offering, a fire-made offering with a pleasing aroma to God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Leviticus 1:10">10</a> If his sacrifice comes from the flock, from the sheep or goats, for a burnt offering, he must present a male without defects.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Leviticus 1:11">11</a> He will kill the offering on the north side of the altar in front of God, and Aaron&#39;s sons, the priests, will sprinkle its blood all around on the altar.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Leviticus 1:12">12</a> He will cut it into pieces, including the head and the fat, and the priest will arrange them on the wood that is burning on the altar.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Leviticus 1:13">13</a> He must wash the insides and legs with water: then the priest must take and burn it all on the altar. It is a burnt offering, a fire-offering, with a pleasing aroma to God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Leviticus 1:14">14</a> If his burnt offering to God is birds, he must bring turtledoves or young pigeons.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Leviticus 1:15">15</a> The priest will take it to the altar, remove its head, and burn it on the altar. The blood will be drained at the side of the altar.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Leviticus 1:16">16</a> He will remove its stomach and its feathers and throw them near the altar on the east side, at the place for ashes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Leviticus 1:17">17</a> He should split it to its wings, but not cut it completely. The priest will burn it on the altar with wood on the fire. It is a burnt offering, a fire sacrifice, that smells good to God.</p>
