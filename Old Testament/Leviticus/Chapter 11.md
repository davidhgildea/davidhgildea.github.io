<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Leviticus 11:1">1</a> God spoke to Moses and Aaron, telling them,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Leviticus 11:2">2</a> Tell the people of Israel, &quot;You can eat these animals from all the animals on the earth.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Leviticus 11:3">3</a> Whatever animal has a split hoof and chews the cud among the animals, you can eat.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Leviticus 11:4">4</a> You must not eat these animals that chew the cud or have split hooves: the camel chews the cud but does not have a split hoof; it is unclean for you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Leviticus 11:5">5</a> The rock badger, because it chews its food again but does not have split hooves, is unclean for you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Leviticus 11:6">6</a> The rabbit, because it chews the cud but does not have split hooves, is unclean for you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Leviticus 11:7">7</a> Pigs have split hooves but don&#39;t chew their food again; they are not clean for you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Leviticus 11:8">8</a> Do not eat their meat or touch their dead bodies; they are unclean for you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Leviticus 11:9">9</a> You can eat any sea creature with fins and scales from the water, sea, or river.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Leviticus 11:10">10</a> All creatures in the sea or rivers that do not have fins and scales, and every living thing in the water, are not to be eaten by you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Leviticus 11:11">11</a> You should find them disgusting; do not eat their meat, and consider their dead bodies disgusting too.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Leviticus 11:12">12</a> Anything in the water that doesn&#39;t have fins or scales is disgusting to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Leviticus 11:13">13</a> You must hate these birds and never eat them because they are dirty: the eagle, the vulture, and the kite.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Leviticus 11:14">14</a> Also the vulture and every kind of kite.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Leviticus 11:15">15</a> All kinds of ravens.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Leviticus 11:16">16</a> The owl, the nighthawk, the cuckoo, and the hawk, each of its own kind,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Leviticus 11:17">17</a> Also the little owl, the cormorant, and the large owl,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Leviticus 11:18">18</a> The swan, the pelican, and the vulture,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Leviticus 11:19">19</a> Also the stork, all types of herons, the lapwing, and the bat.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Leviticus 11:20">20</a> All birds that crawl on four legs must disgust you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Leviticus 11:21">21</a> You can eat any flying insects that walk on four legs and have joints above their legs to jump on the earth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Leviticus 11:22">22</a> You can eat these: different types of locusts, bald locusts, beetles, and grasshoppers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Leviticus 11:23">23</a> But all other flying insects that have four legs will be disgusting to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Leviticus 11:24">24</a> You will be unclean if you touch the dead body of these animals until evening.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Leviticus 11:25">25</a> Anyone who touches any part of a dead animal must wash their clothes and will be unclean until the evening.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Leviticus 11:26">26</a> The bodies of any animals that have split hooves but are not completely split or do not chew their food well are not clean for you; anyone who touches them will be unclean.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Leviticus 11:27">27</a> Any animal that walks on its paws among all the four-legged animals is unclean to you; anyone who touches their dead body will be unclean until evening.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Leviticus 11:28">28</a> Whoever carries their dead body must wash their clothes and will be unclean until evening; they are unclean to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Leviticus 11:29">29</a> These animals are also unclean for you among the ones that crawl on the ground: the weasel, the mouse, and the tortoise of its kind.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Leviticus 11:30">30</a> The ferret, the chameleon, the lizard, the snail, and the mole.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Leviticus 11:31">31</a> These crawling creatures are impure to you: anyone who touches them after they die will be impure until evening.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Leviticus 11:32">32</a> If any of these dead creatures falls on something, that thing will become unclean. This includes any wooden object, clothes, leather, or bag—anything used for work. It must be put in water and will be unclean until evening; then it will be clean.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Leviticus 11:33">33</a> If something unclean falls into a clay pot, everything inside it becomes unclean, and you must break the pot.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Leviticus 11:34">34</a> All food that can be eaten will become unclean if water touches it; any drink that can be drunk from any container will become unclean too.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Leviticus 11:35">35</a> Anything that touches any part of their dead body will be unclean; this includes ovens and cooking stoves, which must be destroyed because they are unclean and will be unclean to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Leviticus 11:36">36</a> Even so, a spring or well with lots of water will be clean, but anything that touches a dead body will be unclean.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Leviticus 11:37">37</a> If any piece of their dead body touches seeds that are to be planted, the seeds are still clean.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Leviticus 11:38">38</a> If water touches the seed and any part of a dead body touches it, it becomes unclean to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="Leviticus 11:39">39</a> If an animal you can eat dies, anyone who touches its dead body will be unclean until evening.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="Leviticus 11:40">40</a> Anyone who eats from the dead body must wash their clothes and will be unclean until evening; and anyone who carries the dead body must wash their clothes and will be unclean until evening.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="Leviticus 11:41">41</a> Every crawling creature on earth is unclean and must not be eaten.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="Leviticus 11:42">42</a> Do not eat any creature that crawls on its belly, walks on all fours, or has many legs. They are not to be eaten because they are disgusting.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v43"><a class="verse-num" href="#v43" aria-label="Leviticus 11:43">43</a> Do not make yourself disgusting with any crawling creature, nor make yourself dirty with them, so you don&#39;t become unclean through them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v44"><a class="verse-num" href="#v44" aria-label="Leviticus 11:44">44</a> I am your God, so make yourselves holy and be holy because I am holy. Do not make yourselves unclean with any crawling creature on the earth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v45"><a class="verse-num" href="#v45" aria-label="Leviticus 11:45">45</a> I am God who brought you out of Egypt to be your God. So you must be holy, because I am holy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v46"><a class="verse-num" href="#v46" aria-label="Leviticus 11:46">46</a> This is the rule for animals, birds, all creatures that live in the water, and every creature that crawls on the ground.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v47"><a class="verse-num" href="#v47" aria-label="Leviticus 11:47">47</a> To show the difference between what is unclean and clean, and between animals you can eat and those you cannot.</p>
