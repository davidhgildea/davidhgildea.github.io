<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Leviticus 18:1">1</a> God spoke to Moses, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Leviticus 18:2">2</a> Tell the people of Israel, &#39;I am God, your God.&#39;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Leviticus 18:3">3</a> Do not follow the customs of Egypt where you lived, nor the customs of Canaan where I am taking you. Do not follow their rules.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Leviticus 18:4">4</a> Follow my rules and keep my laws, and live by them. I am God, your God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Leviticus 18:5">5</a> You must follow my rules and decisions: if a person does this, they will have a good life by them: I am God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Leviticus 18:6">6</a> None of you should come close to a relative to see them naked: I am God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Leviticus 18:7">7</a> Do not see your father&#39;s or your mother&#39;s naked body; she is your mother, so do not look at her nakedness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Leviticus 18:8">8</a> Do not see your father&#39;s wife undressed; she is your father&#39;s privacy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Leviticus 18:9">9</a> Do not see the private parts of your sister, either your father&#39;s daughter or your mother&#39;s daughter, whether she was born in the same house or elsewhere.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Leviticus 18:10">10</a> Do not see the private parts of your son&#39;s daughter or your daughter&#39;s daughter; their privacy is your privacy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Leviticus 18:11">11</a> Do not have sexual relations with your stepsister, the daughter of your father&#39;s wife; she is your sister.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Leviticus 18:12">12</a> Do not see your father&#39;s sister without clothes; she is your father&#39;s close relative.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Leviticus 18:13">13</a> Do not see your aunt undressed; she is your mother&#39;s close relative.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Leviticus 18:14">14</a> Do not see your uncle&#39;s nakedness or be close to his wife; she is your aunt.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Leviticus 18:15">15</a> Do not see your daughter-in-law without clothes; she is your son&#39;s wife; do not see her without clothes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Leviticus 18:16">16</a> Do not see your brother&#39;s wife without clothes; it is like seeing your brother without clothes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Leviticus 18:17">17</a> Do not see a woman and her daughter or her granddaughter naked; it is wrong because they are close relatives.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Leviticus 18:18">18</a> Do not marry your wife&#39;s sister while your wife is alive, to cause her trouble by having both of them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Leviticus 18:19">19</a> Also, do not go near a woman to see her without clothes while she is set apart during her monthly period.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Leviticus 18:20">20</a> Do not have sex with your neighbor&#39;s wife and make yourself impure.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Leviticus 18:21">21</a> Do not let any of your children be burned in sacrifice to Molech, and do not dishonor your God&#39;s name: I am God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Leviticus 18:22">22</a> Do not have sexual relations with a man as one does with a woman; it is disgusting.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Leviticus 18:23">23</a> Do not have sexual relations with any animal; it makes you unclean. A woman must not offer herself to an animal to have sexual relations with it; this is a perversion.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Leviticus 18:24">24</a> Do not make yourselves unclean with any of these actions; for by all these things, the nations I am driving out before you have become unclean.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Leviticus 18:25">25</a> The land is dirty: so I punish its wrongs, and the land throws out its people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Leviticus 18:26">26</a> You must follow my laws and decisions, and must not do any of these disgusting things—neither anyone from your people nor any foreigner living with you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Leviticus 18:27">27</a> The people who lived in the land before you did all these terrible things, and they made the land unclean.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Leviticus 18:28">28</a> So that the land does not vomit you out when you make it dirty, as it vomited out the nations before you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Leviticus 18:29">29</a> Anyone who does any of these disgusting things will be separated from their community.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Leviticus 18:30">30</a> So you must follow my rules and not do any of these disgusting things that were done before you, so you do not become unclean by them: I am God, your God.</p>
