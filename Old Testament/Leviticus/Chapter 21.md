<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Leviticus 21:1">1</a> God told Moses, &quot;Tell Aaron&#39;s priest sons, they must not make themselves unclean for a dead person among their community.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Leviticus 21:2">2</a> But for his close family, for his mother, father, son, daughter, and brother.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Leviticus 21:3">3</a> He can become unclean for his sister if she has never been married and is close to him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Leviticus 21:4">4</a> He must not make himself unclean, as a leader among his people, and dishonor himself.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Leviticus 21:5">5</a> They must not shave their heads, trim the edges of their beards, or cut their skin.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Leviticus 21:6">6</a> They must be holy for their God, and not disrespect their God&#39;s name: because they present offerings made with fire and food to their God, they must be holy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Leviticus 21:7">7</a> They must not marry a woman who sells her body or is not respected; they should not marry a woman who has been divorced, because he is holy to his God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Leviticus 21:8">8</a> You must make him holy, because he presents the food of your God; he must be sacred to you, for I, who make you holy, am holy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Leviticus 21:9">9</a> If a priest&#39;s daughter dishonors herself by becoming a prostitute, she dishonors her father and must be burned to death.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Leviticus 21:10">10</a> The chief priest among his brothers, who has had the anointing oil poured on his head and is dedicated to wear the special clothes, must not let his hair be loose or tear his clothes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Leviticus 21:11">11</a> He must not touch any dead person or make himself unclean, even for his father or mother.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Leviticus 21:12">12</a> He must not leave the holy place or make it unclean, because he wears the anointing oil from God. I am God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Leviticus 21:13">13</a> He must marry a woman who is a virgin.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Leviticus 21:14">14</a> He must not marry a widow, divorced woman, someone unholy, or a prostitute. Instead, he must marry a virgin from his own community.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Leviticus 21:15">15</a> He must not dishonor his children among his people, for I, God, make him holy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Leviticus 21:16">16</a> God spoke to Moses, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Leviticus 21:17">17</a> Tell Aaron that anyone from his descendants who has a flaw cannot come near to offer food to God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Leviticus 21:18">18</a> Anyone with a defect must not come near: a blind person, someone who can&#39;t walk, anyone with a disfigured face, or any deformity.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Leviticus 21:19">19</a> Or a man with a broken foot or broken hand.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Leviticus 21:20">20</a> Or curved spine, or a short person, or one with a flawed eye, or with skin disease, or with sores, or with injured testicles;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Leviticus 21:21">21</a> A man from Aaron the priest&#39;s family with any defect must not come near to offering God&#39;s fire sacrifices; he has a defect and cannot offer food to his God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Leviticus 21:22">22</a> He may eat his God&#39;s bread, from both the very holy and the holy parts.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Leviticus 21:23">23</a> He must not enter behind the curtain or approach the altar, because he has a flaw; he must not make my holy places impure, for I, God, make them holy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Leviticus 21:24">24</a> Moses told Aaron, his sons, and all the Israelites.</p>
