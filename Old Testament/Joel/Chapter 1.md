<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Joel 1:1">1</a> God&#39;s message that came to Joel, Pethuel&#39;s son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Joel 1:2">2</a> Listen, old men, and all who live in the land, pay attention. Has anything like this happened in your time or in your ancestors&#39; time?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Joel 1:3">3</a> Tell your children about it, let them tell their children, and those children tell the next generation.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Joel 1:4">4</a> What the palmerworm did not eat, the locust did; and what the locust did not eat, the cankerworm did; and what the cankerworm did not eat, the caterpillar did.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Joel 1:5">5</a> Wake up, you who drink too much, and cry; scream, all of you wine lovers, because the fresh wine is taken away from your lips.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Joel 1:6">6</a> A nation has invaded my land, powerful and countless, with teeth like a lion&#39;s, and with the fangs of a mighty lion.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Joel 1:7">7</a> He has destroyed my vine and stripped my fig tree. He has completely bared it and thrown it aside; its branches are now white.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Joel 1:8">8</a> Mourn like a young woman wrapped in rough cloth for the husband of her young days.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Joel 1:9">9</a> The food offering and the drink offering are stopped at God&#39;s house; the priests, God&#39;s servants, are sad.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Joel 1:10">10</a> The crops are gone, the earth is sad; the grain is ruined, the new wine is gone, the oil is failing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Joel 1:11">11</a> Feel ashamed, you farmers; cry out, you vine growers, because the wheat and barley are ruined; the field crops are destroyed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Joel 1:12">12</a> The grapevine has dried up, and the fig tree is weak; the pomegranate tree, the palm tree, and the apple tree, all the trees in the fields have dried up because happiness has faded away from people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Joel 1:13">13</a> Get ready and cry, priests; wail, servants of the altar; come, spend the night in mourning, servants of my God, because the grain and drink offerings are no longer brought to your God&#39;s temple.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Joel 1:14">14</a> Set a special time to fast, call a serious meeting, bring together the leaders and everyone who lives in the land to God&#39;s house, and pray to God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Joel 1:15">15</a> Oh no, the day is coming! It&#39;s the day of God, and it will bring destruction like a disaster from the powerful one.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Joel 1:16">16</a> Isn&#39;t the food taken away from us, along with joy and happiness from God&#39;s house?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Joel 1:17">17</a> The seeds have rotted under the dirt, the storehouses are empty, the barns are ruined; because the grain has dried up.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Joel 1:18">18</a> The animals cry out in pain! The herds of cows are confused because there is no grass to eat; even the sheep are left with nothing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Joel 1:19">19</a> God, I will call out to you: because the fire has eaten up the wild land&#39;s grass, and the flame has burned every tree in the fields.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Joel 1:20">20</a> The wild animals cry out to you because the water streams are dry, and fire has burned the wild grasslands.</p>
