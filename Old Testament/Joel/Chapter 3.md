<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Joel 3:1">1</a> For, look, in those days and at that time, when I bring back the people of Judah and Jerusalem,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Joel 3:2">2</a> I will gather all nations and take them to the Valley of Jehoshaphat. There, I will argue for my people and my land, Israel, because they have been scattered among the nations and my land has been divided.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Joel 3:3">3</a> They have drawn lots for my people, traded a boy for a prostitute, and sold a girl for wine to drink.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Joel 3:4">4</a> Yes, and what do you have to do with me, O Tyre, Zidon, and all the coasts of Palestine? Will you pay me back? If you pay me back, I will quickly return your payment on your own head.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Joel 3:5">5</a> Because you took my silver and gold, and brought my precious things into your temples:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Joel 3:6">6</a> You have sold the people of Judah and Jerusalem to the Greeks, so you could send them far from their land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Joel 3:7">7</a> I will lift them from the place where you sold them and bring the consequences back on you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Joel 3:8">8</a> I will sell your sons and daughters to the people of Judah, and they will sell them to the Sabeans, a distant nation, because God has said so.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Joel 3:9">9</a> Tell this to the nations: Get ready for battle, wake up the strong fighters, let all the soldiers come close; let them move forward.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Joel 3:10">10</a> Turn your farming tools into swords and your cutting tools into spears; let those who feel weak say, I am strong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Joel 3:11">11</a> Come together and join, all you nations, and gather around: call your strong ones to come down, God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Joel 3:12">12</a> Let the nations wake up and come to the Valley of Jehoshaphat; for there I will sit to judge all the surrounding nations.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Joel 3:13">13</a> Use the sickle, the crops are ready: come, gather them; the vat is full, it&#39;s overflowing because there is so much evil.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Joel 3:14">14</a> Many people, many people in the valley of choice: for God&#39;s day is close in the valley of choice.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Joel 3:15">15</a> The sun and the moon will become dark, and the stars will stop shining.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Joel 3:16">16</a> God will roar from Zion and shout from Jerusalem; the sky and land will tremble. But God will be a safe place for his people, and a stronghold for the people of Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Joel 3:17">17</a> You will know that I am God living in Zion, my holy mountain. Then Jerusalem will be a holy city, and no strangers will pass through it anymore.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Joel 3:18">18</a> On that day, the mountains will drip with sweet wine, the hills will overflow with milk, all of Judah&#39;s streams will run with water, and a spring will come out of God&#39;s house and water the valley of Shittim.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Joel 3:19">19</a> Egypt will become ruined, and Edom will turn into a barren wasteland because of the harm done to the people of Judah, since they have spilled the blood of the innocent in their country.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Joel 3:20">20</a> Judah will live forever, and Jerusalem will last from one generation to the next.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Joel 3:21">21</a> I will purify their blood that I haven&#39;t purified before, because God lives in Zion.</p>
