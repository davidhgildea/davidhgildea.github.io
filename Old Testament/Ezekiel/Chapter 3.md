<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Ezekiel 3:1">1</a> He also told me, &quot;Human, eat what you find; eat this scroll, and then go and talk to the people of Israel.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Ezekiel 3:2">2</a> I opened my mouth, and he made me eat the scroll.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Ezekiel 3:3">3</a> He said to me, &quot;Human, eat what you find; eat this scroll, and go speak to the house of Israel.&quot; So I opened my mouth, and he gave me the scroll to eat. And he said to me, &quot;Son of man, feed your belly and fill your stomach with this scroll that I give you.&quot; So I ate, and it was in my mouth like honey in sweetness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Ezekiel 3:4">4</a> He told me, &quot;Human, go to the people of Israel and talk to them with my words.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Ezekiel 3:5">5</a> You are not sent to people with a strange language, but to the people of Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Ezekiel 3:6">6</a> Not to people with unfamiliar speech and difficult language that you cannot understand. If I had sent you to them, they would have listened to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Ezekiel 3:7">7</a> But the people of Israel will not listen to you because they don&#39;t listen to me; all the people of Israel are stubborn and hard-hearted.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Ezekiel 3:8">8</a> Look, I have made your face tough against their faces, and your forehead tough against their foreheads.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Ezekiel 3:9">9</a> I have made your forehead as hard as the hardest rock: do not fear them or be frightened by their faces, even if they are a stubborn people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Ezekiel 3:10">10</a> He also told me, &quot;Human, take all my words that I will tell you into your heart, and listen with your ears.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Ezekiel 3:11">11</a> Go to the captives, to your people&#39;s children, talk to them and say, &#39;This is what God says,&#39; whether they listen or not.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Ezekiel 3:12">12</a> Then the spirit lifted me up, and I heard a loud sound behind me. It said, &quot;Praise the glory of God from where He is.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Ezekiel 3:13">13</a> I also heard the sound of the living creatures&#39; wings touching each other, the sound of the wheels beside them, and the sound of a loud rush.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Ezekiel 3:14">14</a> The Spirit lifted me up and took me away. I felt bitter and angry inside, but God&#39;s hand was powerfully on me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Ezekiel 3:15">15</a> I went to the captives at Telabib, who lived by the Chebar River, and I sat with them. I stayed there, shocked, for seven days.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Ezekiel 3:16">16</a> After seven days, God spoke to me, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Ezekiel 3:17">17</a> Human, I have appointed you as a lookout for the people of Israel: so listen to what I say and warn them for me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Ezekiel 3:18">18</a> When I tell a bad person they will die and you don&#39;t warn them or tell them to stop their bad ways to save their life, that person will die for their wrongs, but I will hold you responsible for their death.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Ezekiel 3:19">19</a> But if you warn the bad person, and they do not stop being bad or change their bad ways, they will die because of their sins; but you have saved yourself.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Ezekiel 3:20">20</a> When a good person stops doing right and does wrong, and if I make him fall, he will die. Because you did not warn him, he will die for his sin, and his good deeds will be forgotten; but I will hold you responsible for his death.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Ezekiel 3:21">21</a> If you warn a good person not to sin, and they don&#39;t sin, they will surely live because they listened to the warning, and you have saved yourself too.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Ezekiel 3:22">22</a> God&#39;s hand was on me, and he said, &quot;Get up, go out to the flat land, and I will speak with you there.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Ezekiel 3:23">23</a> I got up and went out to the flat land. I saw God&#39;s glory there, just like the glory I saw by the Chebar River, and I fell down with my face to the ground.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Ezekiel 3:24">24</a> Then the spirit came into me, stood me on my feet, and talked to me, and said, &quot;Go, lock yourself inside your house.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Ezekiel 3:25">25</a> But you, human, see, they will tie you up with ropes, and you will be bound by them, and you will not be able to move around among them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Ezekiel 3:26">26</a> I will make your tongue stick to the roof of your mouth so you can&#39;t talk or correct them, because they are a disobedient people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Ezekiel 3:27">27</a> But when I talk to you, I will open your mouth, and you will tell them, &#39;This is what God says: Whoever wants to listen, let them listen; and whoever doesn&#39;t, let them not listen.&#39; They are stubborn people.</p>
