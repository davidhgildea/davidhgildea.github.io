<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Ezekiel 30:1">1</a> God spoke to me again, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Ezekiel 30:2">2</a> Human, speak God&#39;s message: Cry out, &#39;What a terrible day!&#39;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Ezekiel 30:3">3</a> The day is coming soon, even God&#39;s day is close, a dark day; it will be the time for non-believers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Ezekiel 30:4">4</a> A sword will come to Egypt, and Ethiopia will be in great pain when people are killed in Egypt, their crowds taken, and their strong bases destroyed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Ezekiel 30:5">5</a> Ethiopia, Libya, Lydia, all mixed peoples, Chub, and allies will fall by the sword together.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Ezekiel 30:6">6</a> God says, &quot;Those who support Egypt will fall; their great power will be brought low. They will fall by the sword from the stronghold of Syene,&quot; says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Ezekiel 30:7">7</a> They will be empty among empty lands, and her cities will be among ruined cities.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Ezekiel 30:8">8</a> They will know that I am God when I start a fire in Egypt and all her allies are ruined.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Ezekiel 30:9">9</a> On that day, I will send messengers in ships to scare the unprepared Ethiopians, and they will feel great pain like in the time of Egypt, because look, it&#39;s coming.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Ezekiel 30:10">10</a> God says, I will also end the large number of people in Egypt by the hand of Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Ezekiel 30:11">11</a> He, his people, and the fiercest from different nations will come to destroy the land. They will attack Egypt with their swords and fill the land with the dead.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Ezekiel 30:12">12</a> I will dry up the rivers and hand the land over to the wicked; I will turn the land empty, along with everything in it, through strangers. I, God, have said this.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Ezekiel 30:13">13</a> God says, I will destroy the idols and stop their images in Noph; there will be no more a prince in Egypt, and I will bring fear to Egypt.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Ezekiel 30:14">14</a> I will make Pathros empty, and I will start a fire in Zoan, and I will carry out judgements in No.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Ezekiel 30:15">15</a> I will pour out my anger on Sin, the fortress of Egypt; and I will destroy the many people of No.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Ezekiel 30:16">16</a> I will start a fire in Egypt: Sin will be in great pain, No will be torn apart, and Noph will suffer every day.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Ezekiel 30:17">17</a> The young men of Aven and Pibeseth will die by the sword, and these cities will be taken captive.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Ezekiel 30:18">18</a> In Tehaphnehes too, the day will turn dark when I break Egypt&#39;s chains. Its proud power will end, a cloud will cover it, and its towns will be taken captive.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Ezekiel 30:19">19</a> I will carry out punishments in Egypt, and they will understand that I am God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Ezekiel 30:20">20</a> In the eleventh year, on the first day of the first month, God&#39;s message came to me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Ezekiel 30:21">21</a> Human, I have made Pharaoh, the king of Egypt, weak; his power will not be restored, he won&#39;t be healed or able to use his strength to fight.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Ezekiel 30:22">22</a> So God says: I am against the king of Egypt, Pharaoh. I will break his powerful arms, both the strong one and the already broken one, and make him drop his sword.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Ezekiel 30:23">23</a> I will spread the Egyptians among different nations and scatter them throughout the countries.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Ezekiel 30:24">24</a> I will make the king of Babylon strong and give him my sword, but I will break the power of Pharaoh, and he will moan in front of him like someone fatally injured.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Ezekiel 30:25">25</a> I will give strength to Babylon&#39;s king, while Egypt&#39;s pharaoh will lose power; they will realize I am God when I give my sword to Babylon&#39;s king to use against Egypt.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Ezekiel 30:26">26</a> I will spread the Egyptians among the nations and scatter them in different countries; then they will understand that I am God.</p>
