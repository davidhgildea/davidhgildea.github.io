<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Ezekiel 11:1">1</a> The spirit picked me up and took me to the east entrance of God&#39;s house, facing east. There, at the entrance gate, I saw twenty-five men. Among them were Jaazaniah, Azur&#39;s son, and Pelatiah, Benaiah&#39;s son, who were leaders of the people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Ezekiel 11:2">2</a> He said to me, &quot;Human, these are the people who plan trouble and give evil advice in this city.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Ezekiel 11:3">3</a> They say, &quot;It&#39;s not close; let&#39;s build houses: this city is the pot, and we are the meat.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Ezekiel 11:4">4</a> So tell them what will happen, speak out, human.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Ezekiel 11:5">5</a> God&#39;s Spirit came on me and He said, &quot;Speak: This is what God says; you, the people of Israel, have said these things, and I know every thought that comes into your minds.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Ezekiel 11:6">6</a> You have increased the number of your dead in this city, and you have filled its streets with the dead.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Ezekiel 11:7">7</a> So God says: &quot;The people you killed and left in the city are like meat, and the city is like a pot. But I will take you out of the city.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Ezekiel 11:8">8</a> You have been afraid of the sword; and I will bring a sword upon you, says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Ezekiel 11:9">9</a> I will take you out of there, give you to foreigners, and carry out punishments on you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Ezekiel 11:10">10</a> You will die by the sword; I will judge you at Israel&#39;s border; and you will know that I am God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Ezekiel 11:11">11</a> This city won&#39;t be your cooking pot, nor will you be the meat inside it; instead, I will judge you at Israel&#39;s border.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Ezekiel 11:12">12</a> You will understand that I am God because you have not followed my laws or done what I said but have acted like the foreigners around you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Ezekiel 11:13">13</a> When I was prophesying, Pelatiah, Benaiah&#39;s son, died. I fell face down, shouted loudly, and said, &quot;Ah God! Are you going to completely destroy what&#39;s left of Israel?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Ezekiel 11:14">14</a> God spoke to me again, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Ezekiel 11:15">15</a> Human, your relatives, your own family, and all the people of Israel are those to whom the people of Jerusalem have said, &quot;Stay away from God: this land is ours to own.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Ezekiel 11:16">16</a> So say, God says this: Even though I sent them away among other nations, and I have spread them across different lands, I will still be like a small place of safety for them in the lands where they go.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Ezekiel 11:17">17</a> So say this: God says, &quot;I will bring you together from the people, gather you from the countries where you were spread out, and I will give you the land of Israel.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Ezekiel 11:18">18</a> They will go there and remove all its disgusting things and all its horrors.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Ezekiel 11:19">19</a> I will give them one heart, and put a new spirit in you; I will remove the heart of stone from their body, and give them a heart of flesh.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Ezekiel 11:20">20</a> So they can follow my rules, obey my commands, and do them: and they will be my people, and I will be their God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Ezekiel 11:21">21</a> But as for those who follow their wicked hearts and disgusting actions, I will make sure their actions come back on them, says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Ezekiel 11:22">22</a> Then the cherubim lifted their wings, and the wheels were beside them; and God&#39;s glory was above them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Ezekiel 11:23">23</a> God&#39;s glory rose from the center of the city and rested on the mountain to the east of the city.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Ezekiel 11:24">24</a> Later, the spirit lifted me up. In a vision from God&#39;s spirit, I was taken to the land of the exiles in Chaldea. There, the vision I saw left me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Ezekiel 11:25">25</a> Then I told the captives everything that God had shown me.</p>
