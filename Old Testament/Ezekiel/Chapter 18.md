<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Ezekiel 18:1">1</a> God spoke to me once more, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Ezekiel 18:2">2</a> Why do you use this saying about Israel&#39;s land: The parents ate sour grapes, and now their children&#39;s teeth feel rough?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Ezekiel 18:3">3</a> God says, as long as I live, you will not use this saying in Israel anymore.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Ezekiel 18:4">4</a> Look, every person belongs to me; just as a father&#39;s person is mine, so is a child&#39;s person mine: the person who sins will die.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Ezekiel 18:5">5</a> If a person is fair and does what is legal and right,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Ezekiel 18:6">6</a> He has not eaten on the mountains, nor looked up to the idols of Israel, nor been with his neighbor&#39;s wife, nor approached a woman during her period.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Ezekiel 18:7">7</a> He has not been unkind to others, but has returned the borrower&#39;s collateral, has not robbed anyone by force, has shared his food with the hungry, and has given clothes to those without any.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Ezekiel 18:8">8</a> He who has not lent money at interest, nor taken extra for himself, who has avoided doing wrong, and has made fair decisions between people,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Ezekiel 18:9">9</a> Has followed my rules and has kept my decisions, acting honestly; he is righteous, he will certainly live, says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Ezekiel 18:10">10</a> If he has a son who steals, kills, and does any of these bad things,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Ezekiel 18:11">11</a> Someone who does none of those duties, but also eats on the mountains and dishonors his neighbor&#39;s wife,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Ezekiel 18:12">12</a> Has mistreated the poor and needy, has robbed by force, has not returned what was borrowed, and has looked up to idols, has done terrible things.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Ezekiel 18:13">13</a> Lent money for interest or made a profit from it: can he live? He won&#39;t live: he did all these bad things; he will certainly die, and it&#39;s his own fault.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Ezekiel 18:14">14</a> Now, look, if he has a son who sees all the sins his father has done, thinks about it, and does not do the same,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Ezekiel 18:15">15</a> Has not eaten on the mountains, nor looked up to the idols of Israel, has not dishonored another&#39;s wife,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Ezekiel 18:16">16</a> He has not hurt anyone, has not kept anything owed, has not robbed by force, but has given food to the hungry and has clothed those without clothes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Ezekiel 18:17">17</a> He who does not exploit the poor, who does not take interest or profit, who follows my laws and lives by my rules, will not die because of his father&#39;s sins; he will surely live.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Ezekiel 18:18">18</a> His father, because he hurt others, robbed his brother by force, and did bad things to his people, will also die for his sins.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Ezekiel 18:19">19</a> You ask, &quot;Why doesn&#39;t the son get punished for the father&#39;s sins?&quot; If the son follows what is legal and right, obeys all my rules, and does them, he will certainly live.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Ezekiel 18:20">20</a> The person who sins will die. A child won&#39;t be punished for a parent&#39;s wrongs, nor a parent for a child&#39;s. The good person&#39;s goodness will be their own, and the bad person&#39;s badness will be their own.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Ezekiel 18:21">21</a> If a bad person stops sinning and follows all God&#39;s laws, doing what is right and fair, they will live and not die.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Ezekiel 18:22">22</a> All the wrongs he did will not be brought up to him; by the good things he has done, he will live.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Ezekiel 18:23">23</a> Does God take pleasure in the death of the wicked? No, God wants them to turn from their evil ways and live.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Ezekiel 18:24">24</a> When someone good stops doing good, does wrong, and acts like a bad person, will they live? All the good they did won&#39;t count anymore. They will die because of their wrongs and sins.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Ezekiel 18:25">25</a> But you say, God&#39;s way isn&#39;t fair. Listen, people of Israel; isn&#39;t my way fair? Aren&#39;t your ways unfair?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Ezekiel 18:26">26</a> When a good person stops doing right and does wrong, and dies because of it, they will die because of the wrong they have done.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Ezekiel 18:27">27</a> When a bad person stops doing wrong and starts doing what is lawful and right, he will save his life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Ezekiel 18:28">28</a> Because he thinks about it and stops doing all the wrong things he did before, he will live and not die.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Ezekiel 18:29">29</a> The people of Israel say, &quot;God&#39;s ways are not fair.&quot; O people of Israel, aren&#39;t my ways fair? Aren&#39;t your ways unfair?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Ezekiel 18:30">30</a> So, I will judge you, people of Israel, each according to their actions, says God. Turn back and stop all your sins; then they won&#39;t destroy you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Ezekiel 18:31">31</a> Get rid of all the wrong things you have done, and create a new heart and a new spirit for yourself. Why should you die, people of Israel?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Ezekiel 18:32">32</a> I don&#39;t want anyone to die, says God. So change your ways and live.</p>
