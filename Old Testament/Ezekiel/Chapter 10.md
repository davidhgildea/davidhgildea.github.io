<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Ezekiel 10:1">1</a> I looked, and saw above the cherubim there was something like a blue sapphire stone above them, looking like a throne.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Ezekiel 10:2">2</a> He said to the man in linen clothes, &quot;Go between the wheels, under the cherub, take coals of fire from between the cherubs, and spread them over the city.&quot; He did this while I watched.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Ezekiel 10:3">3</a> The cherubim were on the right side of the house when the man entered, and a cloud filled the inner yard.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Ezekiel 10:4">4</a> Then God&#39;s glory rose up from the cherub and paused above the entrance of the temple; the temple became filled with the cloud, and the courtyard shone with the brightness of God&#39;s glory.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Ezekiel 10:5">5</a> The sound of the cherubim&#39;s wings could be heard all the way to the outer courtyard, like the voice of God when he speaks.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Ezekiel 10:6">6</a> When he gave the order to the man in linen clothes, &quot;Take fire from between the wheels, from between the cherubim,&quot; he went in and stood next to the wheels.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Ezekiel 10:7">7</a> One angel reached out from between the angels to the fire that was between them, took some, and put it into the hands of the person dressed in linen; who then took it and left.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Ezekiel 10:8">8</a> In the cherubs, there was something that looked like a man&#39;s hand below their wings.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Ezekiel 10:9">9</a> When I looked, I saw four wheels next to the cherubim, one wheel by each cherub, and the wheels looked like the color of a beryl stone.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Ezekiel 10:10">10</a> As for how they looked, all four of them were alike, like a wheel inside another wheel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Ezekiel 10:11">11</a> When they moved, they went on their four sides. They didn&#39;t turn when they moved, but followed the direction their head faced without turning as they went.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Ezekiel 10:12">12</a> Their whole body, backs, hands, wings, and the wheels were covered with eyes all around, even the wheels each of the four creatures had.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Ezekiel 10:13">13</a> The wheels were called &#39;wheel&#39; as I heard.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Ezekiel 10:14">14</a> Each one had four faces: the first was a cherub&#39;s face, the second a man&#39;s face, the third a lion&#39;s face, and the fourth an eagle&#39;s face.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Ezekiel 10:15">15</a> The cherubim rose up. This is the living being I saw by the Chebar River.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Ezekiel 10:16">16</a> When the cherubim moved, the wheels moved with them. And when the cherubim rose up from the ground with their wings, the wheels stayed close beside them without turning away.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Ezekiel 10:17">17</a> When they stood still, these also stood still; and when they rose up, these also rose up, because the spirit of the living being was in them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Ezekiel 10:18">18</a> God&#39;s glory left the entrance of the temple and hovered above the cherubim.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Ezekiel 10:19">19</a> The cherubim lifted their wings and rose from the ground before me: as they moved away, the wheels were next to them. Each one stopped at the entrance of the east gate of God&#39;s temple, and God&#39;s glory was above them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Ezekiel 10:20">20</a> This is the creature I saw by the river Chebar under Israel&#39;s God, and I knew they were cherubim.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Ezekiel 10:21">21</a> Each had four faces and four wings, and under their wings were what looked like human hands.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Ezekiel 10:22">22</a> The way their faces looked was the same as those I saw by the Chebar river; they looked the same and moved straight ahead.</p>
