<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Ezekiel 9:1">1</a> He also shouted loudly in my ears, saying, &quot;Tell those in charge of the city to come here, each with his weapon for destruction in hand.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Ezekiel 9:2">2</a> Look, six men came from the direction of the upper gate to the north, each with a weapon for killing in his hand; one of them was dressed in linen, with a writing kit at his side. They came in and stood by the bronze altar.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Ezekiel 9:3">3</a> God&#39;s bright presence, which had been over the cherubim, rose to the entrance of the temple. God then spoke to the man in linen clothes who had a writing kit at his side.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Ezekiel 9:4">4</a> God told him, &quot;Walk through the center of the city, through the middle of Jerusalem, and put a mark on the foreheads of the people who are upset and cry about all the bad things happening there.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Ezekiel 9:5">5</a> He told the others as I could hear, &quot;Follow him through the city and strike. Show no mercy and feel no pity.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Ezekiel 9:6">6</a> Kill all the old and young, girls, little children, and women, but do not hurt anyone with the mark; start at my holy place. Then they started with the elders in front of the house.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Ezekiel 9:7">7</a> He told them, &quot;Make the temple unclean and fill the courtyards with the bodies of the dead. Go out.&quot; So they went out and killed people in the city.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Ezekiel 9:8">8</a> While they were being killed and I was spared, I fell facedown, cried out, and said, &quot;Oh God! Will you wipe out the remaining people of Israel by pouring out your anger on Jerusalem?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Ezekiel 9:9">9</a> He told me, &quot;Israel and Judah&#39;s sin is very big, and the land is full of blood, and the city is full of wrongdoing: because they say, &#39;God has left the earth, and God does not see.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Ezekiel 9:10">10</a> I will not show mercy or feel sorry either; I will give them what they deserve, and they will feel the consequences of their actions.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Ezekiel 9:11">11</a> Look, the man in linen clothes with a writing kit at his side reported back, saying, &quot;I have done what you told me to do.&quot;</p>
