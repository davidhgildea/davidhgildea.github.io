<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Ezekiel 25:1">1</a> God spoke to me again, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Ezekiel 25:2">2</a> Human, look toward the Ammonites and speak against them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Ezekiel 25:3">3</a> Tell the Ammonites, listen to God&#39;s word: This is what God says, &quot;Because you mocked my sanctuary when it was defiled, mocked the land of Israel when it was empty, and mocked the people of Judah when they were taken captive.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Ezekiel 25:4">4</a> So, I will give you to the people from the east to own, and they will build their houses in you. They will eat your fruit and drink your milk.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Ezekiel 25:5">5</a> I will turn Rabbah into a place for camels to rest, and the Ammonites into a resting place for sheep; and you will know that I am God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Ezekiel 25:6">6</a> God says, because you clapped your hands, stomped your feet, and were happy in your heart with all your hate for the land of Israel;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Ezekiel 25:7">7</a> So I will reach out my hand against you, and give you as loot to the nations; I will cut you off from the peoples, and make you vanish from the countries: I will destroy you, and you will know that I am God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Ezekiel 25:8">8</a> God says, &quot;Because Moab and Seir claim, &#39;Look, the tribe of Judah is like other nations;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Ezekiel 25:9">9</a> So, I will expose Moab, starting from its cities on its borders, the best of the land, Bethjeshimoth, Baalmeon, and Kiriathaim.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Ezekiel 25:10">10</a> I will give the eastern lands to the men, including the Ammonites&#39; lands, so that no one will remember the Ammonites among the nations.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Ezekiel 25:11">11</a> I will punish Moab, and they will know that I am God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Ezekiel 25:12">12</a> God says: Because Edom acted against the people of Judah, seeking revenge and committing great wrongs against them,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Ezekiel 25:13">13</a> So God says: I will reach out my hand against Edom, and remove people and animals from it; I will make it empty from Teman, and those from Dedan will die by the sword.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Ezekiel 25:14">14</a> I will take revenge on Edom through my people Israel; they will act in Edom with my anger and fury; and they will understand my revenge, says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Ezekiel 25:15">15</a> God says: Because the Philistines acted for revenge with spiteful hearts, wanting to destroy because of their ancient hatred;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Ezekiel 25:16">16</a> So God says: Look, I will reach out my hand against the Philistines, I will wipe out the Cherethites, and remove the survivors of the seashore.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Ezekiel 25:17">17</a> I will punish them severely and angrily; and they will know that I am God when I take revenge on them.</p>
