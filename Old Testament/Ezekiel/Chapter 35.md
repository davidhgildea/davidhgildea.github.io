<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Ezekiel 35:1">1</a> Additionally, God spoke to me, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Ezekiel 35:2">2</a> Human, look toward Mount Seir and speak against it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Ezekiel 35:3">3</a> Tell the mountain, Seir, &quot;God says I am against you, I will reach out my hand against you, and make you very empty.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Ezekiel 35:4">4</a> I will destroy your cities, you will be empty, and you will know that I am God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Ezekiel 35:5">5</a> Because you have always hated them and killed the people of Israel with the sword when they were in trouble, when their wrongdoing ended.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Ezekiel 35:6">6</a> So, because I am alive, says God, I will make you face bloodshed, and bloodshed will follow you because you did not hate bloodshed, bloodshed will follow you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Ezekiel 35:7">7</a> I will make Mount Seir very empty and stop anyone from passing through or returning.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Ezekiel 35:8">8</a> I will cover his mountains with his dead bodies; dead people will fall on your hills, in your valleys, and in your rivers, killed by the sword.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Ezekiel 35:9">9</a> I will cause you to be forever ruined, and your cities will not be rebuilt: then you will understand that I am God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Ezekiel 35:10">10</a> Because you said, &quot;These two nations and countries will be mine, and we will take them,&quot; even though God was there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Ezekiel 35:11">11</a> So, because I am alive, says God, I will act just like you did in your anger and jealousy that came from your hate against them; and I will show who I am to them when I judge you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Ezekiel 35:12">12</a> You will know that I am God, and that I have heard all the insults you said against the mountains of Israel, saying, &quot;They are ruined and handed over to us to take.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Ezekiel 35:13">13</a> You have bragged against me with your mouths and spoken many words against me; I have heard them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Ezekiel 35:14">14</a> God says, when the whole world celebrates, I will leave you empty.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Ezekiel 35:15">15</a> Just as you were happy when the land of Israel was empty, I will do the same to you. You will be empty, Mount Seir, and all of Idumea, all of it. Then they will know that I am God.</p>
