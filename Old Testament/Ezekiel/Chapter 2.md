<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Ezekiel 2:1">1</a> He said to me, &quot;Human, stand on your feet, and I will talk to you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Ezekiel 2:2">2</a> The spirit came into me as he spoke to me and put me on my feet, so I could hear the one who was speaking to me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Ezekiel 2:3">3</a> He told me, &quot;Human, I am sending you to the people of Israel. They are a nation that has rebelled against me. Both they and their ancestors have sinned against me up to this very day.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Ezekiel 2:4">4</a> They are bold and stubborn children. I am sending you to them; you will tell them, &#39;This is what God says.&#39;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Ezekiel 2:5">5</a> Whether they listen or not (because they are stubborn), they will realize that a prophet has been with them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Ezekiel 2:6">6</a> You, human, don&#39;t be scared of them or their words, even if you&#39;re with thorns and live among scorpions. Don&#39;t fear what they say or get upset by their looks, even if they are defiant.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Ezekiel 2:7">7</a> You must tell them my words, whether they listen or not, because they are very rebellious.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Ezekiel 2:8">8</a> But you, human, listen to what I tell you; don&#39;t be rebellious like those rebels: open your mouth and eat what I give you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Ezekiel 2:9">9</a> When I looked, I saw a hand reaching out to me with a scroll in it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Ezekiel 2:10">10</a> He showed it to me; it had writing on the front and back, and it said there were sadness, crying, and terrible pain.</p>
