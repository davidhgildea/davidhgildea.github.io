<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Ezekiel 15:1">1</a> God spoke to me, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Ezekiel 15:2">2</a> Human, what makes a grapevine better than any other tree or a branch found in the forest?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Ezekiel 15:3">3</a> Can wood from it be used to make anything? Or can someone use a piece of it to hang things on?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Ezekiel 15:4">4</a> Look, it is thrown into the fire as fuel; the fire burns up both its ends, and its middle is charred. Can it be used for any task?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Ezekiel 15:5">5</a> Look, when it was whole, it was not fit for any work: how much less will it be fit for any work now, after the fire has eaten it, and it is burned?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Ezekiel 15:6">6</a> So this is what God says: Just as a vine among forest trees that I&#39;ve set aside to burn is given, so will I treat Jerusalem&#39;s people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Ezekiel 15:7">7</a> I will be against them; they will escape one fire, but another fire will consume them; and you will know that I am God when I am against them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Ezekiel 15:8">8</a> I will make the land empty, because they did wrong, says God.</p>
