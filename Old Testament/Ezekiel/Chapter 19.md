<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Ezekiel 19:1">1</a> Make a sad song for Israel&#39;s leaders.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Ezekiel 19:2">2</a> Ask, &quot;What is your mother? A lioness: she lay among lions, she raised her cubs with other lions.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Ezekiel 19:3">3</a> She raised one of her cubs; it became a young lion and learned to hunt; it ate people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Ezekiel 19:4">4</a> Other countries heard about him; he got caught in their trap, and they took him with chains to Egypt.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Ezekiel 19:5">5</a> When she saw her waiting was in vain and her hope was gone, she took another one of her young and raised it to be a young lion.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Ezekiel 19:6">6</a> He moved among lions, became a young lion, learned to hunt, and ate people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Ezekiel 19:7">7</a> He knew their empty homes and destroyed their cities; the land was empty and completely ruined by the sound of his roar.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Ezekiel 19:8">8</a> Nations from all around trapped him with their nets, and he was caught in their hole.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Ezekiel 19:9">9</a> They locked him up with chains and took him to the king of Babylon: they kept him in prison so his voice would not be heard on Israel&#39;s mountains anymore.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Ezekiel 19:10">10</a> Your mother is like a vine in your life, planted near water; she was productive and had many branches because of the plentiful water.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Ezekiel 19:11">11</a> She had sturdy branches fit for rulers&#39; scepters; she stood tall amidst the dense foliage, her height visible with her many branches.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Ezekiel 19:12">12</a> But she was yanked up angrily, thrown to the ground, and the east wind dried up her fruit; her strong branches were broken and dried up; the fire burned them up.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Ezekiel 19:13">13</a> And now it is planted in a desert, in a dry and parched land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Ezekiel 19:14">14</a> Fire came from one of her branch sticks and burned up her fruit. Now she has no strong branch to be a ruler&#39;s scepter. This is a sad song, and it will be used as a sad song.</p>
