<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Ezekiel 1:1">1</a> In the thirtieth year, on the fifth day of the fourth month, while I was with the captives by the Chebar River, the sky opened, and I saw visions from God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Ezekiel 1:2">2</a> On the fifth day of the month, during the fifth year of King Jehoiachin&#39;s capture,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Ezekiel 1:3">3</a> God&#39;s message came directly to Ezekiel the priest, Buzi&#39;s son, in the Chaldeans&#39; land by the Chebar river; and God&#39;s power was with him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Ezekiel 1:4">4</a> I looked and saw a storm coming from the north. It had a big cloud and fire swirling inside it. It was shining, and from the center, it glowed like amber in the fire.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Ezekiel 1:5">5</a> From within it came four living beings that looked like people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Ezekiel 1:6">6</a> Each one had four faces, and each one had four wings.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Ezekiel 1:7">7</a> Their feet were straight, and the bottoms of their feet were like a calf&#39;s hoof; they shone like polished bronze.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Ezekiel 1:8">8</a> They had human hands under their wings on all four sides, and all four had faces and wings.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Ezekiel 1:9">9</a> Their wings touched each other; they did not turn as they moved; each one went straight ahead.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Ezekiel 1:10">10</a> Each of them had a human face, and each had a lion&#39;s face on the right side. Each also had an ox&#39;s face on the left side, and each of them had an eagle&#39;s face.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Ezekiel 1:11">11</a> Their faces looked like this: their wings stretched up; each had two wings touching another&#39;s, and two covered their bodies.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Ezekiel 1:12">12</a> They all went straight ahead; wherever the spirit wanted to go, they went, without turning as they moved.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Ezekiel 1:13">13</a> The living creatures looked like glowing, burning coals and bright lamps moving up and down among them; the fire was bright and lightning flashed from the fire.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Ezekiel 1:14">14</a> The living creatures moved quickly back and forth, like a flash of lightning.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Ezekiel 1:15">15</a> As I watched the living beings, I saw one wheel on the ground next to the living beings, with its four sides.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Ezekiel 1:16">16</a> The wheels looked and were made like shiny yellow-green stones, and all four were alike; each one seemed to be a wheel inside another wheel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Ezekiel 1:17">17</a> When they moved, they went in four directions and did not turn as they moved.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Ezekiel 1:18">18</a> The rims of the wheels were tall and frightening, and they were covered with eyes all around the four of them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Ezekiel 1:19">19</a> When the living creatures moved, the wheels moved with them; and when the living creatures rose from the ground, the wheels also rose.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Ezekiel 1:20">20</a> Wherever the spirit wanted to go, they went; that&#39;s where their spirit aimed to go, and the wheels rose along with them, because the spirit of the living being was in the wheels.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Ezekiel 1:21">21</a> When they moved, these moved; when they stopped, these stopped; and when they rose from the ground, the wheels rose too, because the spirit of the living being was in the wheels.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Ezekiel 1:22">22</a> The appearance of the sky above the living creatures&#39; heads was like a frightening, clear crystal spread out over their heads.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Ezekiel 1:23">23</a> Under the sky, their wings were straight, facing each other. Each one had two wings covering one side of its body and two wings covering the other side.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Ezekiel 1:24">24</a> As they moved, I heard the sound of their wings, like the sound of mighty waters, like God&#39;s voice, a sound of talking, like the sound of an army: when they stopped, they lowered their wings.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Ezekiel 1:25">25</a> A voice came from above the sky when they stood still and lowered their wings.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Ezekiel 1:26">26</a> Above the sky above their heads was something that looked like a throne made from blue sapphire, and on that throne was a figure that looked like a man.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Ezekiel 1:27">27</a> I saw something that looked like amber, with fire inside and around it, from the middle up and from the middle down; it looked like fire and was bright all around.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Ezekiel 1:28">28</a> Like a rainbow in the clouds on a rainy day, so was the shining light around it. That was what the glory of God looked like. When I saw it, I fell on my face, and I heard someone speaking.</p>
