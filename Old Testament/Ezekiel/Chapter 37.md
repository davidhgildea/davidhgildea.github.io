<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Ezekiel 37:1">1</a> God&#39;s hand was on me, and he took me out in his spirit and put me down in the middle of a valley full of bones.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Ezekiel 37:2">2</a> God made me walk around them, and I saw a lot of bones in the valley; they were very dry.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Ezekiel 37:3">3</a> He asked me, &quot;Human, can these bones come to life?&quot; I replied, &quot;God, only you know.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Ezekiel 37:4">4</a> He told me again, &quot;Speak to these bones and say, &#39;You dry bones, listen to God&#39;s message.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Ezekiel 37:5">5</a> God says to these bones, &quot;Look, I will make breath come into you, and you will live.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Ezekiel 37:6">6</a> I will put tendons on you, make flesh grow on you, cover you with skin, and give you breath, so you will live; then you will understand that I am God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Ezekiel 37:7">7</a> I did as I was told: and as I spoke, there was a sound, and suddenly a shaking, and the bones moved and joined together, each bone connecting to its matching bone.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Ezekiel 37:8">8</a> When I looked, the tendons and flesh grew on them, and skin covered them, but they had no breath.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Ezekiel 37:9">9</a> Then he said to me, &quot;Speak to the wind, speak, human, and say to the wind, &#39;God says: Come from the four winds, O breath, and breathe on these dead ones, so they may live.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Ezekiel 37:10">10</a> I did as I was told, and breath entered them. They came to life and stood up on their feet—a very large army.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Ezekiel 37:11">11</a> He told me, &quot;Human, these bones represent all of Israel. Look, they say, &#39;Our bones are dried up, our hope is gone, we are completely cut off.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Ezekiel 37:12">12</a> So, tell them, &#39;This is what God says: Look, my people, I will open your graves and lift you out of them, and take you to the land of Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Ezekiel 37:13">13</a> You will understand that I am God when I open your graves and bring you out of them, my people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Ezekiel 37:14">14</a> I will put my spirit in you, you will live, and I will put you in your own land. Then you will know that I, God, have said and done this, says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Ezekiel 37:15">15</a> God spoke to me again, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Ezekiel 37:16">16</a> Also, you human, take one stick and write on it, &#39;For Judah and the Israelites associated with him.&#39; Then take another stick and write on it, &#39;For Joseph, which is for Ephraim, and for all the Israelite companions.&#39;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Ezekiel 37:17">17</a> Put them together, one to the other, into one piece; and they will be united in your hand.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Ezekiel 37:18">18</a> When your people&#39;s children talk to you and ask, &quot;Won&#39;t you explain what these things mean to us?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Ezekiel 37:19">19</a> Tell them, &quot;This is what God says: Look, I will take Joseph&#39;s stick, which is in Ephraim&#39;s hand, along with his companion tribes of Israel, and put them together with Judah&#39;s stick, making them one stick, and they will be one in my hand.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Ezekiel 37:20">20</a> Hold the sticks you wrote on in your hand for them to see.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Ezekiel 37:21">21</a> Tell them, &#39;This is what God says: Look, I will take the Israelites from the nations where they have gone, gather them from all around, and bring them back to their own land.&#39;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Ezekiel 37:22">22</a> I will make them one country in Israel&#39;s land on the mountains; and they will all have one king. They will no longer be two separate countries or split into two kingdoms ever again.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Ezekiel 37:23">23</a> They won&#39;t make themselves unclean with idols, hateful things, or sins. I will rescue them from all the places they sinned and make them clean. Then they will be my people, and I will be their God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Ezekiel 37:24">24</a> David my servant will be their king; they will all have one shepherd. They will follow my rules and keep and follow my laws.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Ezekiel 37:25">25</a> They will live in the land I gave to Jacob, my servant, where your ancestors lived. They, their children, and their grandchildren will live there forever, and David, my servant, will always be their leader.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Ezekiel 37:26">26</a> I will make a peace agreement with them that will last forever. I will settle them, increase their numbers, and put my holy place among them always.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Ezekiel 37:27">27</a> My tent will be with them; yes, I will be their God, and they will be my people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Ezekiel 37:28">28</a> The nations will understand that I, God, make Israel holy when my holy place is with them forever.</p>
