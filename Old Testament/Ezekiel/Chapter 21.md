<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Ezekiel 21:1">1</a> God&#39;s message came to me, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Ezekiel 21:2">2</a> Human, look toward Jerusalem, speak your message to the sacred places, and tell of coming troubles for the land of Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Ezekiel 21:3">3</a> Tell the land of Israel, God says: I am against you, and I will pull out my sword and cut down both good and bad people from you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Ezekiel 21:4">4</a> Since I will remove both good and bad people from you, my sword will be drawn and strike everyone from the south to the north.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Ezekiel 21:5">5</a> So that everyone may know that I, God, have taken out my sword from its cover: it will not be put back again.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Ezekiel 21:6">6</a> So, you human, sigh deeply with sadness; let everyone see your grief.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Ezekiel 21:7">7</a> When people ask you why you are sighing, you will say, &quot;Because of the news that is coming. Everyone&#39;s heart will melt, hands will go limp, spirits will lose courage, and knees will turn to water. Look, it&#39;s coming and will happen,&quot; says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Ezekiel 21:8">8</a> God spoke to me again, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Ezekiel 21:9">9</a> Human, prophesy and say, This is what God says: Say, A sword, a sword is sharpened and polished.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Ezekiel 21:10">10</a> It is sharpened for a terrible killing; it is polished to shine: should we then celebrate? It rejects the authority of my son, like any tree.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Ezekiel 21:11">11</a> He has made it ready to be used; this sword is sharp and polished to be given to the one who will kill.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Ezekiel 21:12">12</a> Weep and wail, human: it will happen to my people, it will happen to all the leaders of Israel: fears of war will be upon my people: so hit your leg in grief.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Ezekiel 21:13">13</a> It&#39;s a test, and what if the sword despises the stick? It won&#39;t exist anymore, says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Ezekiel 21:14">14</a> So, human, prophesy and clap your hands. Let the sword strike three times, the sword that kills the mighty; it is the sword that kills the great ones, reaching into their private rooms.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Ezekiel 21:15">15</a> I have placed the sword at all their gates so that they will be scared and their destruction will increase. Oh! It is sharpened and prepared for killing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Ezekiel 21:16">16</a> Go in any direction, to the right or to the left, wherever you decide to look.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Ezekiel 21:17">17</a> I will clap my hands together, and I will let my anger settle: I, God, have said it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Ezekiel 21:18">18</a> God spoke to me once more, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Ezekiel 21:19">19</a> You, too, must set up two roads for the king of Babylon&#39;s army to invade. Both will start from the same country. At the start of each road leading to the city, pick a spot for a signpost.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Ezekiel 21:20">20</a> Set a path for the sword to reach Rabbath of the Ammonites and Judah in fortified Jerusalem.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Ezekiel 21:21">21</a> The king of Babylon stopped at the crossroads, at the start of the two paths, to practice fortune-telling: he polished his arrows, he asked idols, he examined the liver.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Ezekiel 21:22">22</a> At his right side was the plan for Jerusalem: to set leaders, to begin the attack, to shout loudly, to place rams at the gates, to make a siege mound, and to construct a stronghold.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Ezekiel 21:23">23</a> They will see it as a false prediction, those who make promises; but their wrongdoing will be remembered, so they can be captured.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Ezekiel 21:24">24</a> So God says, because you have shown your sins and everyone sees your wrong acts, and because you are now remembered, you will be caught.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Ezekiel 21:25">25</a> You, evil and unholy ruler of Israel, your time has come when your wrongdoing will stop.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Ezekiel 21:26">26</a> God says, &quot;Take off the royal headband, remove the crown: things will change: lift up the lowly, and bring down the powerful.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Ezekiel 21:27">27</a> I will completely overturn it, and it will be gone, until the one who rightfully owns it comes; then I will give it to him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Ezekiel 21:28">28</a> You, human, tell them what God says about the Ammonites and their insults: &quot;The sword is drawn and ready for killing, polished to shine and destroy.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Ezekiel 21:29">29</a> While they show you false visions and tell you lies, to lead you to the fate of the wicked who are doomed, when their sin ends.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Ezekiel 21:30">30</a> Will I put it back in its holder? I will judge you where you were born, in your homeland.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Ezekiel 21:31">31</a> I will let out my anger on you, I will breathe on you with the fire of my anger, and give you to rough men who are good at destroying.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Ezekiel 21:32">32</a> You will be fuel for the fire; your blood will be spread across the land; you will be forgotten because I, God, have said so.</p>
