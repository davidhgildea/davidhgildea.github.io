<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Ezekiel 27:1">1</a> God spoke to me again and said,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Ezekiel 27:2">2</a> Now, you human, sing a sad song for Tyre.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Ezekiel 27:3">3</a> Tell the city of Tyre, you who are located by the sea gateway and trade with many islands, &quot;God says: O Tyre, you claim to be perfectly beautiful.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Ezekiel 27:4">4</a> Your borders are surrounded by the seas, your builders have made you very beautiful.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Ezekiel 27:5">5</a> They made all your ship planks from Senir&#39;s fir trees; they took Lebanon&#39;s cedars to make masts for you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Ezekiel 27:6">6</a> They made your oars from the Bashan oaks; your seats are carved ivory from the Ashurites, brought from the islands of Chittim.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Ezekiel 27:7">7</a> You used fine linen and embroidered fabric from Egypt for your sail; blue and purple from the Elishah islands were your covers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Ezekiel 27:8">8</a> The people of Zidon and Arvad sailed your ships; the skilled men of Tyre in you were your captains.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Ezekiel 27:9">9</a> The elders of Gebal and its skilled people were in you to seal your ships: all the sea&#39;s ships and their sailors were there to trade your goods.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Ezekiel 27:10">10</a> People from Persia, Lud, and Phut were in your army, your warriors: they carried shields and helmets; they made you look splendid.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Ezekiel 27:11">11</a> The soldiers from Arvad and your army were on your walls all around, and the warriors were in your towers. They put their shields on your walls all around; they made your city very beautiful.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Ezekiel 27:12">12</a> Tarshish traded with you because you had many riches; they exchanged silver, iron, tin, and lead for your goods.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Ezekiel 27:13">13</a> Javan, Tubal, and Meshech were your traders; they exchanged people and bronze items in your market.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Ezekiel 27:14">14</a> The people from Togarmah did business at your markets with horses, horsemen, and mules.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Ezekiel 27:15">15</a> The traders from Dedan traded with you; many islands gave you goods: they gave you ivory tusks and ebony as gifts.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Ezekiel 27:16">16</a> Syria traded with you because you made many goods; they exchanged emeralds, purple fabric, embroidered cloth, fine linen, coral, and agate at your markets.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Ezekiel 27:17">17</a> Judah and Israel were your traders; in your marketplace they exchanged wheat from Minnith, Pannag, honey, oil, and balm.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Ezekiel 27:18">18</a> Damascus traded with you because you made many goods, exchanging them for lots of riches, Helbon wine, and white wool.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Ezekiel 27:19">19</a> Dan and Javan traveled back and forth, busy in your markets: shiny iron, cassia, and sweet cane were among your goods.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Ezekiel 27:20">20</a> Dedan was your trader in fancy fabrics for carts.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Ezekiel 27:21">21</a> Arabia and Kedar&#39;s leaders traded with you in lambs, rams, and goats; these were your business partners.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Ezekiel 27:22">22</a> The traders from Sheba and Raamah were your traders; they traded at your markets with the best spices, precious stones, and gold.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Ezekiel 27:23">23</a> Haran, Canneh, and Eden, and the traders from Sheba, Asshur, and Chilmad were your traders.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Ezekiel 27:24">24</a> These were your traders with all kinds of goods, in blue fabrics, decorated cloth, and rich clothing in cedar chests tied with ropes among your items for sale.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Ezekiel 27:25">25</a> Tarshish&#39;s ships sang about you in your market; you were filled and made very glorious on the seas.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Ezekiel 27:26">26</a> Your rowers took you into deep waters: the east wind has shattered you in the middle of the seas.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Ezekiel 27:27">27</a> Your wealth, markets, goods, sailors, navigators, ship repairers, traders, soldiers, and everyone else with you will fall into the sea when you are destroyed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Ezekiel 27:28">28</a> The areas around will tremble when they hear your pilots&#39; shouts.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Ezekiel 27:29">29</a> Everyone who rows, the sailors, and all the sea captains will get off their ships and stand on the land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Ezekiel 27:30">30</a> They will raise their voices against you, cry loudly, throw dust on their heads and roll in ashes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Ezekiel 27:31">31</a> They will shave their heads for you, wear rough cloth as a sign of sadness, and cry for you with deep sorrow and loud cries.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Ezekiel 27:32">32</a> During their crying, they will mourn for you, saying, &quot;What city is like Tyre, like the ruined one in the sea?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Ezekiel 27:33">33</a> When your goods were sent out to sea, you supplied many people; you made the kings of the earth rich with your wealth and goods.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Ezekiel 27:34">34</a> When you are destroyed by the seas deep in the water, your goods and everyone with you will sink.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Ezekiel 27:35">35</a> Everyone living on the islands will be shocked at you, and their kings will be very scared; they will look worried.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Ezekiel 27:36">36</a> The traders among the people will scorn you; you will be feared, and never exist again.</p>
