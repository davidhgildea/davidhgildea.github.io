<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Daniel 1:1">1</a> In the third year that Jehoiakim was king of Judah, Nebuchadnezzar the king of Babylon came to Jerusalem and surrounded it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Daniel 1:2">2</a> God let Nebuchadnezzar capture Jehoiakim, the king of Judah, and some of God&#39;s house items. Nebuchadnezzar took them to Shinar, to his own god&#39;s temple, and placed them in his god&#39;s treasury.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Daniel 1:3">3</a> The king told Ashpenaz, the head of his servants, to bring some young Israelites from royal families, and young nobles.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Daniel 1:4">4</a> Children without flaws, good-looking, wise in all ways, clever in knowledge, understanding science, who could serve in the king&#39;s palace, and who could be taught the Chaldeans’ education and language.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Daniel 1:5">5</a> The king gave them a daily amount of his own food and wine to eat and drink. He did this for three years so they could be healthy enough to serve him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Daniel 1:6">6</a> Among these were Judah&#39;s descendants: Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Daniel 1:7">7</a> The head of the eunuchs gave them new names: he named Daniel, Belteshazzar; Hananiah was called Shadrach; Mishael was called Meshach; and Azariah was called Abednego.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Daniel 1:8">8</a> Daniel decided in his heart not to make himself unclean with the king&#39;s food or the wine he drank. So he asked the chief officer for permission not to make himself unclean.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Daniel 1:9">9</a> God made Daniel liked and loved by the chief eunuch.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Daniel 1:10">10</a> The official in charge of the servants told Daniel, &quot;I am afraid of the king who has decided what you should eat and drink. What if he sees you looking worse than the other young men your age? Then I could get in trouble with the king.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Daniel 1:11">11</a> Daniel spoke to Melzar, who was put in charge of Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah by the chief official.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Daniel 1:12">12</a> Test us for ten days: give us only vegetables to eat and water to drink.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Daniel 1:13">13</a> Let our faces be examined before you, and the faces of the kids who eat the king&#39;s food; and treat us as you see fit.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Daniel 1:14">14</a> He agreed to their request and tested them for ten days.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Daniel 1:15">15</a> After ten days, they looked healthier and better-fed than any of the young people who ate the king&#39;s food.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Daniel 1:16">16</a> So Melzar removed their share of meat and the wine they were to drink, and gave them vegetables instead.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Daniel 1:17">17</a> God gave these four young people knowledge and skill in all kinds of learning and wisdom, and Daniel could understand all kinds of visions and dreams.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Daniel 1:18">18</a> After the time set by the king to bring them in was over, the chief official presented them to King Nebuchadnezzar.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Daniel 1:19">19</a> The king spoke with them, but no one was like Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah, so they served the king.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Daniel 1:20">20</a> In every area of wisdom and knowledge where the king tested them, he saw they were ten times better than all the sorcerers and fortune-tellers in his whole kingdom.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Daniel 1:21">21</a> Daniel remained until King Cyrus&#39;s first year.</p>
