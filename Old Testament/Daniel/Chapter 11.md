<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Daniel 11:1">1</a> Also, in the first year of Darius the Mede, I stood by him to support and strengthen him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Daniel 11:2">2</a> I will now show you the truth. Look, three more kings will rise in Persia; and the fourth will be much richer than the others. With his wealth, he will cause everyone to fight against Greece.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Daniel 11:3">3</a> A powerful king will rise, who will rule with a large territory and do as he pleases.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Daniel 11:4">4</a> When he rises to power, his kingdom will break apart and spread to the four directions of the sky; it won&#39;t go to his descendants or remain as it was under his rule: for his kingdom will be uprooted and given to others, not his own family.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Daniel 11:5">5</a> The southern king will be powerful, as will one of his leaders; he will become stronger than the king and rule over a vast area.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Daniel 11:6">6</a> After some years, they will unite; the southern king&#39;s daughter will go to the northern king to form a pact. However, she won&#39;t keep her strength, nor will he or his strength last. She, along with her escorts, her father, and her supporter during those times, will be handed over.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Daniel 11:7">7</a> From her family&#39;s roots, one will rise to take power, bring an army, attack the northern king&#39;s stronghold, fight them, and win.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Daniel 11:8">8</a> He will also take their gods, leaders, and valuable silver and gold items as captives to Egypt; he will last longer than the king of the north.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Daniel 11:9">9</a> The king of the south will come into his kingdom and then go back to his own country.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Daniel 11:10">10</a> His sons will rise up and gather a large army. One will come for sure, sweep through and move on. Then he will go back, roused to attack his stronghold.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Daniel 11:11">11</a> The southern king will become very angry and will go out to fight the northern king. He will gather a large army, but his army will be defeated.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Daniel 11:12">12</a> When he removes many people, he will become proud; he will defeat thousands, but this will not make him stronger.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Daniel 11:13">13</a> The king from the north will come back, with a huge crowd bigger than before, and after some years, he will definitely come with a large army and lots of wealth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Daniel 11:14">14</a> During that time, many will oppose the southern king, and your people&#39;s rebels will rise to fulfill the prophecy, but they will fail.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Daniel 11:15">15</a> The northern king will come, build a siege ramp, and capture the strongest cities. The southern forces won&#39;t be able to resist, not even the best troops, nor will they have the power to stand against him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Daniel 11:16">16</a> Whoever attacks him will do as he pleases, and no one can stop him. He will stay in the beautiful country, which he will destroy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Daniel 11:17">17</a> He will determine to come with the full force of his kingdom, bringing with him those who are righteous; this is what he will do: and he will offer him a woman, intending to ruin her: but she will not support him or be on his side.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Daniel 11:18">18</a> After this, he will look toward the islands and conquer many: but a ruler will end the insults made by him, making the insults fall back on him without his own dishonor.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Daniel 11:19">19</a> He will look toward his country&#39;s strongholds, but he will trip, fall, and disappear.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Daniel 11:20">20</a> A tax collector will rise to power in the kingdom, but he will be destroyed soon after, not in anger or in war.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Daniel 11:21">21</a> A despicable person will rise to power where he was not given royal honors; yet he will gain control peacefully, using smooth talk.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Daniel 11:22">22</a> A flood will sweep away everything before him; it will destroy everything, even the leader of the covenant.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Daniel 11:23">23</a> After making an agreement, he will act dishonestly: he will rise to power with the help of a few people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Daniel 11:24">24</a> He will calmly enter the richest parts of the land; he will do things his ancestors never did, nor his ancestors before them; he will share the loot, goods, and wealth among them. Yes, he will plan his strategies against the strongholds, for a while.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Daniel 11:25">25</a> He will gather his strength and bravery to fight the southern king with a large army; but the southern king, with an even bigger and stronger army, will prepare for battle. Yet, he will fail because they will plan tricks against him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Daniel 11:26">26</a> Yes, those who eat his food will destroy him, and his army will be swept away: many will fall down dead.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Daniel 11:27">27</a> Both kings will plan evil and lie while eating together, but they will fail because the end will come at the set time.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Daniel 11:28">28</a> Then he will go back to his country with lots of wealth; but he will be against the sacred promise; he will do great things, and return to his country.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Daniel 11:29">29</a> At the set time, he will come back and move toward the south; but it won&#39;t be like before or after.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Daniel 11:30">30</a> Ships from Chittim will attack him; he will be upset, go back, and become angry at the sacred promise. He will act on this; he will go back and work with those who abandon the sacred promise.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Daniel 11:31">31</a> Forces will support him, they will corrupt the strong sanctuary, stop the regular offering, and set up the dreadful abomination.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Daniel 11:32">32</a> Those who do evil against the agreement will be corrupted by smooth talk, but the people who know their God will be strong and take action.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Daniel 11:33">33</a> People with knowledge will teach many, but they will face violence, fire, capture, and loss for a long time.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Daniel 11:34">34</a> When they fall, they will get a little help, but many will join them with smooth talk.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Daniel 11:35">35</a> Some wise people will stumble to test, cleanse, and refine them until the end time, because it&#39;s set for a specific time.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Daniel 11:36">36</a> The king will do as he wants; he will raise himself up and make himself greater than any god, and will say amazing things against the God of all gods, and will succeed until the anger is finished: because what is decided will happen.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Daniel 11:37">37</a> He will not respect his ancestors&#39; God, the love of women, or any god, because he will consider himself greater than all.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Daniel 11:38">38</a> He will honor the god of forces instead of his own god. He will honor a god his ancestors did not know with gold, silver, precious stones, and gifts.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="Daniel 11:39">39</a> He will act this way in the strongest fortresses with a foreign god, whom he will recognize and honor with glory: he will make them rulers over many and will distribute the land for profit.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="Daniel 11:40">40</a> At the end time, the southern king will attack him, and the northern king will come like a storm, with chariots, horsemen, and many ships. He will invade nations, sweeping through and conquering them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="Daniel 11:41">41</a> He will also go into the beautiful land, and many nations will be defeated: but Edom, Moab, and the leader of the Ammonites will escape from his power.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="Daniel 11:42">42</a> He will also reach out his hand over the nations, and Egypt will not escape.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v43"><a class="verse-num" href="#v43" aria-label="Daniel 11:43">43</a> He will control Egypt&#39;s gold and silver treasures and all its valuable items; Libyans and Ethiopians will follow him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v44"><a class="verse-num" href="#v44" aria-label="Daniel 11:44">44</a> News from the east and north will upset him; so he will attack angrily to destroy and kill many.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v45"><a class="verse-num" href="#v45" aria-label="Daniel 11:45">45</a> He will set up his royal tents between the seas at the beautiful holy mountain; but he will meet his end, and no one will help him.</p>
