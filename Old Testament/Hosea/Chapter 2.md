<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Hosea 2:1">1</a> Tell your brothers, &#39;My People,&#39; and your sisters, &#39;Loved One.&#39;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Hosea 2:2">2</a> Ask your mother to change her ways, for she is not my wife and I am not her husband. She must stop being unfaithful and not act like an adulterer anymore.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Hosea 2:3">3</a> So I don&#39;t leave her bare, put her back as she was when born, turn her into a desert, make her like dry land, and kill her with thirst.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Hosea 2:4">4</a> I will not show kindness to her children because they are children of unfaithfulness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Hosea 2:5">5</a> Their mother has acted like a prostitute: the one who gave birth to them has behaved disgracefully. She said, &quot;I will chase after my lovers who provide me with food, water, wool, linen, oil, and drinks.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Hosea 2:6">6</a> So, I will block your path with thorns and build a wall so she can&#39;t find her way.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Hosea 2:7">7</a> She will chase her lovers but not catch them; she will look for them but not find them. Then she will think, &#39;I will go back to my first husband because it was better for me then than now.&#39;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Hosea 2:8">8</a> She did not realize that I gave her grain, wine, and oil, and increased her silver and gold, which they used for Baal.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Hosea 2:9">9</a> So I will go back and take back my grain when it&#39;s time and my wine when it&#39;s due, and I will take back my wool and flax meant to cover her nakedness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Hosea 2:10">10</a> I will now show her bad behavior to her lovers, and no one can save her from me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Hosea 2:11">11</a> I will also make all her happiness stop, her holidays, her new moon celebrations, her rest days, and all her special festivals.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Hosea 2:12">12</a> I will ruin her grapevines and fig trees, which she claimed her lovers rewarded her with; I&#39;ll turn them into a wild area where wild animals will feed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Hosea 2:13">13</a> I will punish her for the times she worshiped Baal, when she burned incense to them, wore her earrings and jewelry, chased her lovers, and forgot me, says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Hosea 2:14">14</a> So, I will attract her, take her to the desert, and speak kindly to her.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Hosea 2:15">15</a> I will give her vineyards again, and the valley of Achor will be a door of hope. She will sing there like when she was young and when she left Egypt.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Hosea 2:16">16</a> On that day, God says, you will call me &#39;My Husband&#39; and not call me &#39;My Master&#39; anymore.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Hosea 2:17">17</a> I will remove the names of the Baals from her lips, and their names will not be remembered anymore.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Hosea 2:18">18</a> On that day, I will make a promise to them with the wild animals, the birds in the sky, and the insects on the ground: I will remove the bow, the sword, and war from the land, and they will rest in safety.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Hosea 2:19">19</a> I will promise to be yours forever; yes, I will promise to be yours with fairness, with justice, with kindness, and with compassion.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Hosea 2:20">20</a> I will promise myself to you faithfully, and you will know God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Hosea 2:21">21</a> On that day, I will listen, says God, I will hear the skies, and they will hear the land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Hosea 2:22">22</a> The earth will receive the grain, the wine, and the oil; and they will respond to Jezreel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Hosea 2:23">23</a> I will plant her for myself in the land; I will show love to those who were not loved; and I will tell those who were not my people, &#39;You are my people,&#39; and they will say, &#39;You are my God.&#39;</p>
