<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Hosea 8:1">1</a> Put the trumpet to your lips. An eagle swoops on God&#39;s house because they broke my agreement and disobeyed my laws.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Hosea 8:2">2</a> Israel will call out to me, &quot;My God, we know you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Hosea 8:3">3</a> Israel has rejected what is good; the enemy will chase after him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Hosea 8:4">4</a> They chose kings without my approval, made leaders I did not recognize, and from their silver and gold, they created idols to be destroyed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Hosea 8:5">5</a> Samaria, your calf has rejected you; I am angry with them. How long until they become innocent?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Hosea 8:6">6</a> It came from Israel: a craftsman made it; so it&#39;s not God. But the calf of Samaria will be smashed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Hosea 8:7">7</a> They planted wind and will get a storm; it won&#39;t grow stalks, blooms won&#39;t make flour: if it does yield, outsiders will eat it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Hosea 8:8">8</a> Israel is consumed; now they will be among the nations like an unwanted pot.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Hosea 8:9">9</a> They have gone to Assyria, like a wild donkey alone: Ephraim has paid for partners.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Hosea 8:10">10</a> Yes, even though they hired help from other nations, I will now bring them together, and they will feel some sadness because of the heavy demands of the king of leaders.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Hosea 8:11">11</a> Because Ephraim has made many altars for sinning, these altars will become a way for him to sin.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Hosea 8:12">12</a> I wrote to him about the important parts of my law, but they were seen as something odd.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Hosea 8:13">13</a> They offer meat for my offerings and eat it, but God does not accept them; now he will remember their sins and punish them: they will go back to Egypt.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Hosea 8:14">14</a> Israel has forgotten God and built temples; Judah has built many strong cities, but I will set fire to these cities, and it will destroy their palaces.</p>
