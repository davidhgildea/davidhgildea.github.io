<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Hosea 11:1">1</a> When Israel was young, I loved him and called my son out of Egypt.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Hosea 11:2">2</a> When they were called, they ignored it; they made sacrifices to false gods and burned incense to idols.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Hosea 11:3">3</a> I also taught Ephraim to walk, holding them by their arms; yet they did not realize that I healed them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Hosea 11:4">4</a> I led them with human ties, with ropes of love. Like one who eases the load on their jaws, I bent down to feed them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Hosea 11:5">5</a> He will not go back to Egypt, and the Assyrian will be his king, because they would not come back.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Hosea 11:6">6</a> The sword will stay in his cities, destroy his branches, and eat them up, because of their own plans.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Hosea 11:7">7</a> My people love to turn away from me; even when they are called to the Most High, no one praises Him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Hosea 11:8">8</a> How can I let you go, Ephraim? How can I hand you over, Israel? How can I make you like Admah? How can I treat you like Zeboim? My heart is changed inside me, and my compassion is stirred up.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Hosea 11:9">9</a> I will not act with fierce anger, nor come back to destroy Ephraim because I am God, not a human; the Holy One among you, and I will not come into the city.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Hosea 11:10">10</a> They will follow God; He will roar like a lion; when He roars, the children will shake with fear from the west.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Hosea 11:11">11</a> They will shake with fear like a bird from Egypt and like a dove from Assyria, and I will put them in their homes, says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Hosea 11:12">12</a> Ephraim surrounds me with lies, and Israel with deceit; but Judah still rules with God and is loyal with the holy people.</p>
