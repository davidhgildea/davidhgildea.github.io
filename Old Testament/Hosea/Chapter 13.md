<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Hosea 13:1">1</a> When Ephraim spoke with fear, he became a leader in Israel; but when he sinned with Baal, he died.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Hosea 13:2">2</a> They keep sinning more, making silver statues and idols they imagine, all made by craftsmen. They tell those who offer sacrifices to kiss these calf idols.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Hosea 13:3">3</a> So they will be like the morning cloud and like the early dew that goes away, like chaff blown away from the threshing floor by the wind, and like smoke from a chimney.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Hosea 13:4">4</a> I am God, who brought you out of Egypt, and you should not acknowledge any god except me because there is no one else who can save you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Hosea 13:5">5</a> I knew you in the desert, in the dry land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Hosea 13:6">6</a> As their fields were good, they ate and were satisfied; their hearts grew proud, so they forgot God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Hosea 13:7">7</a> So I will be like a lion to them, and like a leopard, I will watch over them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Hosea 13:8">8</a> I will confront them like a mother bear robbed of her cubs, and will tear open their heart; there I will eat them like a lion, and wild animals will rip them apart.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Hosea 13:9">9</a> Israel, you have ruined yourself; but your help is in me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Hosea 13:10">10</a> I will be your king. Where is another who can save you in all your cities? Where are your judges to whom you said, &quot;Give me a king and princes&quot;?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Hosea 13:11">11</a> I gave you a king because I was angry, and I took him away because I was furious.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Hosea 13:12">12</a> Ephraim&#39;s wrongdoing is tied up; his sin is hidden.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Hosea 13:13">13</a> He will feel pain like a woman giving birth: he is a foolish son, because he should not stay where babies are born for long.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Hosea 13:14">14</a> I will rescue them from the grave&#39;s power; I will save them from death: O death, I will bring your diseases; O grave, I will cause your ruin: I will not turn back.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Hosea 13:15">15</a> Although he may have success among his brothers, a strong wind will blow in from the desert, and this wind from God will dry up his water source and his well will run dry: all his valuable belongings will be ruined.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Hosea 13:16">16</a> Samaria will be ruined because she turned against God: people will die by the sword, babies will be smashed, and pregnant women will be cut open.</p>
