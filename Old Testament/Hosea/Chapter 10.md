<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Hosea 10:1">1</a> Israel is like a vine with no value; it produces fruit for itself. The more fruit it has, the more altars it builds; the better the land, the finer the idols.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Hosea 10:2">2</a> Their heart is not united; they will be seen as guilty: God will destroy their altars, God will ruin their statues.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Hosea 10:3">3</a> Now they will say, &quot;We have no king, because we did not fear God; what can a king do for us?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Hosea 10:4">4</a> They have said things, lying when they make a promise. So unfairness grows like poison plants in the field&#39;s rows.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Hosea 10:5">5</a> The people of Samaria will be scared because of the fake gods of Bethaven; they will be sad for them, and the priests who were happy about them will mourn, because their honor is gone.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Hosea 10:6">6</a> It will be taken to Assyria as a gift for King Jareb: Ephraim will feel shame, and Israel will be embarrassed by its own advice.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Hosea 10:7">7</a> Samaria&#39;s king is gone like foam on water.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Hosea 10:8">8</a> The worship sites of Aven, Israel&#39;s sin, will be ruined: thorns and thistles will grow on their altars; and people will ask the mountains to hide them and the hills to fall on them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Hosea 10:9">9</a> Israel, you have sinned since the time of Gibeah: there they remained: the fight in Gibeah against the wicked people did not defeat them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Hosea 10:10">10</a> I want to punish them, and people will come together against them when they get stuck in their own wrong ways.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Hosea 10:11">11</a> Ephraim is like a trained young cow that likes to crush grain; but I will put a yoke on her beautiful neck. I will make Ephraim work; Judah will plow and Jacob will break up the soil.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Hosea 10:12">12</a> Plant for yourselves the right way, gather with kindness; prepare your unused land: it&#39;s time to look for God, until He comes and showers you with the right way to live.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Hosea 10:13">13</a> You have planted evil, you have harvested sin; you have eaten the fruit of lies: because you trusted in your own path, in the strength of your warriors.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Hosea 10:14">14</a> So, your people will be in chaos, and all your strongholds will be ruined, just like when Shalman destroyed Betharbel on the day of war: the mother was shattered against her children.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Hosea 10:15">15</a> Bethel will do the same to you because of your great evil; by morning the king of Israel will be completely destroyed.</p>
