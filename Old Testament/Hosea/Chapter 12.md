<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Hosea 12:1">1</a> Ephraim chases the wind and follows the east wind: he keeps adding lies and destruction; they make agreements with the Assyrians, and oil is taken to Egypt.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Hosea 12:2">2</a> God also has an argument with Judah and will punish Jacob for his actions; He will repay him based on what he has done.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Hosea 12:3">3</a> In the womb, he grabbed his brother&#39;s heel, and with his strength, he struggled with God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Hosea 12:4">4</a> Yes, he had power over the angel and won; he cried and asked for mercy; he met him in Bethel, where he spoke with us.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Hosea 12:5">5</a> Even God of all; God is his name to remember.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Hosea 12:6">6</a> So turn to your God: practice kindness and fairness, and always depend on your God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Hosea 12:7">7</a> He is a trader with dishonest scales in his hands; he loves to cheat.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Hosea 12:8">8</a> Ephraim said, &quot;I have become rich and gained wealth. In all my work, no one will find any wrong that is sin in me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Hosea 12:9">9</a> I am God, who brought you out of Egypt, and I will make you live in tents again, like during the festival.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Hosea 12:10">10</a> I have spoken through the prophets, given many visions, and taught in parables through the prophets&#39; work.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Hosea 12:11">11</a> Is there wrongdoing in Gilead? Surely they are worthless; they offer bulls in Gilgal; yes, their altars are like piles of stones in the field rows.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Hosea 12:12">12</a> Jacob ran away to Syria, and Israel worked to marry a wife, and to get a wife he took care of sheep.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Hosea 12:13">13</a> God led Israel out of Egypt through a prophet, and by a prophet they were kept safe.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Hosea 12:14">14</a> Ephraim made God very angry, so God will make him face the consequences of his actions, and will make sure his shame remains on him.</p>
