<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Hosea 1:1">1</a> God&#39;s message came to Hosea, Beeri&#39;s son, when Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah were kings of Judah, and during Jeroboam, Joash&#39;s son, was king of Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Hosea 1:2">2</a> The start of God&#39;s message through Hosea. God told Hosea, &quot;Go, marry a woman who has been unfaithful, and have children with her, because the people have been very unfaithful, turning away from God.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Hosea 1:3">3</a> He went and married Gomer, daughter of Diblaim, who became pregnant and gave birth to a son for him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Hosea 1:4">4</a> God told him, &quot;Name the child Jezreel; soon I will punish Jehu&#39;s family for the bloodshed at Jezreel, and I will end the rule of Israel&#39;s kingdom.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Hosea 1:5">5</a> On that day, I will break Israel&#39;s bow in the Jezreel Valley.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Hosea 1:6">6</a> She became pregnant again and had a daughter. God told him, &quot;Name her Loruhamah, because I will no longer show mercy to Israel’s people; I will completely remove them.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Hosea 1:7">7</a> I will show kindness to Judah&#39;s family and save them through God, their God. I will not save them with bows, swords, battles, horses, or horsemen.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Hosea 1:8">8</a> After she finished breastfeeding Loruhamah, she became pregnant and gave birth to a son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Hosea 1:9">9</a> Then God said, &quot;Name him Not My People, because you are not my people, and I will not be your God.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Hosea 1:10">10</a> The children of Israel will be as many as the sand by the sea, too many to measure or count. And where they were told, &quot;You are not my people,&quot; there they will be called &quot;Children of the living God.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Hosea 1:11">11</a> The people of Judah and Israel will unite and choose one leader, and they will rise from the land, for the day of Jezreel will be great.</p>
