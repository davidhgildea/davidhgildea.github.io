<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Hosea 5:1">1</a> Listen, priests, pay attention, people of Israel, and hear this, royal house, because God will judge you. You have been traps at Mizpah and nets on Tabor.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Hosea 5:2">2</a> The rebels are skilled at killing, even though I have warned them all.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Hosea 5:3">3</a> I know Ephraim, and Israel is not hidden from me: because now, Ephraim, you are unfaithful, and Israel is unclean.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Hosea 5:4">4</a> They won&#39;t change their actions to return to God because a spirit of unfaithfulness is among them, and they don&#39;t know God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Hosea 5:5">5</a> Israel&#39;s pride shows clearly, so Israel and Ephraim will fall because of their sins; Judah will also fall with them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Hosea 5:6">6</a> They will go with their sheep and cattle to look for God, but they will not find him; he has pulled away from them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Hosea 5:7">7</a> They have betrayed God: they have had unusual children: now, within a month, they will be destroyed with all they have.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Hosea 5:8">8</a> Sound the horn in Gibeah and the trumpet in Ramah; shout loudly at Bethaven, follow you, Benjamin.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Hosea 5:9">9</a> Ephraim will be ruined on the day of punishment; I have told the tribes of Israel what will definitely happen.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Hosea 5:10">10</a> The leaders of Judah were like those who move boundaries; so I will pour out my anger on them like water.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Hosea 5:11">11</a> Ephraim is crushed and judged unfairly because he chose to follow wrong orders.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Hosea 5:12">12</a> So I will be to Ephraim like a moth, and to the house of Judah like decay.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Hosea 5:13">13</a> When Ephraim noticed his illness, and Judah noticed his injury, Ephraim went to the Assyrian, and sent a message to King Jareb: but he couldn&#39;t heal you or fix your injury.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Hosea 5:14">14</a> I will be like a lion to Ephraim and like a young lion to Judah&#39;s people. I will attack and leave, I will carry off, and no one can save them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Hosea 5:15">15</a> I will go back to my place until they admit their wrong and look for me. When they are in trouble, they will search for me earnestly.</p>
