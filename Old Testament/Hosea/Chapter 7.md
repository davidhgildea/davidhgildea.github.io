<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Hosea 7:1">1</a> When I wanted to heal Israel, Ephraim&#39;s sin and Samaria&#39;s evil were revealed because they lied, thieves broke in, and bands of robbers plundered outside.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Hosea 7:2">2</a> They don&#39;t think in their hearts that I remember all their bad actions: now what they&#39;ve done surrounds them; they are in my sight.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Hosea 7:3">3</a> They please the king with their bad deeds, and the leaders with their lies.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Hosea 7:4">4</a> They are all unfaithful, like an oven the baker heats and then stops stoking after mixing the dough until it rises.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Hosea 7:5">5</a> On our king&#39;s day, the leaders made him sick with lots of wine; he joined in with those who mock.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Hosea 7:6">6</a> They prepare their hearts like a hot oven while they wait in ambush. Their anger smolders all night and in the morning it blazes like a burning fire.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Hosea 7:7">7</a> They all burn with anger like an oven and have destroyed their rulers; all their kings have fallen, and none of them call out to me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Hosea 7:8">8</a> Ephraim has mixed himself with other people; Ephraim is like an uncooked cake.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Hosea 7:9">9</a> Outsiders have used up his energy, and he doesn&#39;t realize it; even with gray hair scattered on him, he still doesn&#39;t know.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Hosea 7:10">10</a> Israel&#39;s pride is shown openly, but they still don&#39;t turn back to God or look for Him, despite everything.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Hosea 7:11">11</a> Ephraim is like a foolish dove with no understanding: they turn to Egypt, they travel to Assyria.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Hosea 7:12">12</a> When they go, I will throw my net over them; I will pull them down like birds of the sky; I will punish them as they have been told.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Hosea 7:13">13</a> How terrible for them! They have run away from me. They will be destroyed because they have sinned against me. Even though I saved them, they have still told lies about me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Hosea 7:14">14</a> They haven&#39;t truly called out to God; instead, they wail on their beds. They gather together for grain and wine, but they turn against God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Hosea 7:15">15</a> Even though I made their arms strong, they still plan to do bad things against me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Hosea 7:16">16</a> They come back, but not to God; they are like a faulty bow; their leaders will die by the sword because of their angry words; this will make them a joke in Egypt.</p>
