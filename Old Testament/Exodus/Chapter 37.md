<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Exodus 37:1">1</a> Bezaleel made the ark from acacia wood: it was 2.5 cubits long, 1.5 cubits wide, and 1.5 cubits high.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Exodus 37:2">2</a> He covered it with pure gold inside and outside and put a gold border around it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Exodus 37:3">3</a> He made four gold rings for it, placing them at its four corners; two rings on one side and two on the other side.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Exodus 37:4">4</a> He made poles from acacia wood and covered them with gold.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Exodus 37:5">5</a> He put the poles through the rings on the sides of the chest to carry it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Exodus 37:6">6</a> He made the cover out of pure gold; it was three and a half feet long and two and a quarter feet wide.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Exodus 37:7">7</a> He made two gold cherubs from one piece of gold and put them on both ends of the mercy seat.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Exodus 37:8">8</a> One angel on one end here, and another angel on the other end there: the angels on both ends were made from the mercy seat.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Exodus 37:9">9</a> The cherubim spread their wings up high and covered the mercy seat with their wings, facing each other; their faces were toward the mercy seat.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Exodus 37:10">10</a> He made a table of acacia wood: it was two cubits long, one cubit wide, and one and a half cubits high.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Exodus 37:11">11</a> He covered it with pure gold and added a gold border around it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Exodus 37:12">12</a> He also made a border about a hand&#39;s breadth wide around it, and put a gold rim around the border.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Exodus 37:13">13</a> He made four gold rings and attached them to its four corners on the legs.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Exodus 37:14">14</a> Next to the edge were the rings, the spots for the poles to carry the table.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Exodus 37:15">15</a> He made the poles from acacia wood and covered them with gold to carry the table.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Exodus 37:16">16</a> He made the items for the table—his plates, spoons, cups, and lids to cover them—all out of pure gold.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Exodus 37:17">17</a> He made the lampstand of pure gold. The lampstand, its base and branches, its cups, buds, and blossoms, were all one piece.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Exodus 37:18">18</a> The lampstand has six branches coming from its sides; three branches come out from one side, and three branches come out from the other side.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Exodus 37:19">19</a> Each of the six branches has three cups shaped like almond flowers with buds and petals.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Exodus 37:20">20</a> The candlestick had four cups shaped like almonds, with buds and blossoms.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Exodus 37:21">21</a> Place a knob under two branches, and do the same for each pair of the six branches coming from the lampstand.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Exodus 37:22">22</a> Their buds and branches were all one piece; it was all made of pure gold that had been hammered into shape.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Exodus 37:23">23</a> He made seven lamps, lamp trimmers, and trays out of pure gold.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Exodus 37:24">24</a> He made it of a talent of pure gold, along with all its utensils.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Exodus 37:25">25</a> He made the altar for burning incense out of acacia wood. It was one cubit long and one cubit wide, square-shaped, and two cubits high with horns made from the same wood.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Exodus 37:26">26</a> He covered it with pure gold, the top, the sides all around, and its horns; he also made a gold border around it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Exodus 37:27">27</a> He made two gold rings below its molding, at its two corners, on both sides, to hold the poles for carrying it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Exodus 37:28">28</a> He made the poles from acacia wood and covered them with gold.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Exodus 37:29">29</a> He made the sacred oil for anointing and the pure, sweet-smelling incense, as a perfumer would.</p>
