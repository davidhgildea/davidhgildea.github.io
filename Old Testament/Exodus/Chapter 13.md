<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Exodus 13:1">1</a> God spoke to Moses, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Exodus 13:2">2</a> Set apart for me every firstborn, every first child or animal born among the Israelites: it belongs to me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Exodus 13:3">3</a> Moses told the people, &quot;Remember this day when you left Egypt, the place of slavery. God freed you with his powerful hand: do not eat bread with yeast.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Exodus 13:4">4</a> Today you left in the month of Abib.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Exodus 13:5">5</a> When God brings you to the land of the Canaanites, Hittites, Amorites, Hivites, and Jebusites, which he promised to your ancestors to give you, a land rich with milk and honey, you must do this service in this month.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Exodus 13:6">6</a> Eat bread without yeast for seven days, and on the seventh day there will be a celebration for God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Exodus 13:7">7</a> Eat bread without yeast for seven days; no bread with yeast should be with you, nor yeast anywhere in your living areas.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Exodus 13:8">8</a> On that day, you will explain to your son, saying, &quot;We do this because of what God did for me when I left Egypt.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Exodus 13:9">9</a> This will be a sign on your hand and a reminder on your forehead to keep God&#39;s law in your words, because God freed you from Egypt with great power.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Exodus 13:10">10</a> You should therefore follow this rule at the right time every year.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Exodus 13:11">11</a> When God brings you to the land of the Canaanites, as he promised you and your ancestors, and gives it to you,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Exodus 13:12">12</a> You must dedicate to God everything that is the first to come from a mother, and every firstborn male animal you have; these males belong to God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Exodus 13:13">13</a> You must trade a lamb for every firstborn donkey; if you choose not to trade, then you must kill the donkey. You must also trade for every firstborn child.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Exodus 13:14">14</a> When your son asks you in the future, &quot;What is this?&quot; you will tell him, &quot;God freed us from Egypt, where we were slaves, with a strong hand.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Exodus 13:15">15</a> When Pharaoh almost didn&#39;t let us leave, God killed all the firstborn in Egypt, both people and animals. That&#39;s why I sacrifice to God all male firstborn livestock, but I save my firstborn sons.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Exodus 13:16">16</a> It will be a sign on your hand and a reminder between your eyes because with strong hand, God brought us out of Egypt.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Exodus 13:17">17</a> When Pharaoh let the people go, God did not lead them through the Philistine land, even though it was close. God thought, &quot;Maybe the people will change their minds when they see war and go back to Egypt.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Exodus 13:18">18</a> God guided the people through the desert toward the Red Sea, and the Israelites left Egypt prepared for battle.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Exodus 13:19">19</a> Moses took Joseph&#39;s bones with him because Joseph had made the Israelites promise, saying, &quot;God will definitely come to help you, and you must take my bones from here with you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Exodus 13:20">20</a> They traveled from Succoth and camped at Etham, on the border of the wilderness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Exodus 13:21">21</a> God led them during the day with a cloud pillar to show the way; and at night with a fire pillar to give them light, so they could travel day and night.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Exodus 13:22">22</a> He did not remove the cloud pillar by day or the fire pillar by night from in front of the people.</p>
