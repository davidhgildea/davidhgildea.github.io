<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Exodus 20:1">1</a> God said all these words:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Exodus 20:2">2</a> I am God, your God, who brought you out from Egypt, from the place where you were slaves.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Exodus 20:3">3</a> You should not worship any other gods but me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Exodus 20:4">4</a> Do not make for yourself any carved idol, or any picture of anything in the sky above, or on the earth below, or in the water under the earth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Exodus 20:5">5</a> Do not bow down to them or serve them; for I, your God, am a jealous God, punishing the children for the sin of the parents to the third and fourth generation of those who hate me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Exodus 20:6">6</a> I show kindness to thousands who love me and follow my rules.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Exodus 20:7">7</a> Do not use God&#39;s name for no good reason; God will not let anyone who misuses his name go unpunished.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Exodus 20:8">8</a> Remember the day of rest, to keep it special.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Exodus 20:9">9</a> Work for six days and do all your tasks.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Exodus 20:10">10</a> The seventh day is a rest day for God. On that day, you or your son, daughter, servants, animals, or any guest in your home, should not do any work.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Exodus 20:11">11</a> In six days, God created the sky, the earth, the sea, and everything in them, and rested on the seventh day; therefore, God blessed the Sabbath day and made it holy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Exodus 20:12">12</a> Respect your father and your mother so you may live a long time in the land God gives you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Exodus 20:13">13</a> Do not kill.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Exodus 20:14">14</a> Do not cheat on your spouse.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Exodus 20:15">15</a> Do not steal.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Exodus 20:16">16</a> Do not tell lies about others.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Exodus 20:17">17</a> Do not want your neighbor&#39;s house, do not want your neighbor&#39;s wife, their worker, their helper, their ox, their donkey, or anything that belongs to your neighbor.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Exodus 20:18">18</a> Everyone saw the thunder, the lightning, the sound of the trumpet, and the mountain smoking; when they saw this, they moved back and stood far away.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Exodus 20:19">19</a> They said to Moses, &quot;You talk to us, and we will listen, but don&#39;t let God talk to us, or we might die.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Exodus 20:20">20</a> Moses told the people, &quot;Don&#39;t be afraid. God has come to test you, so that you will respect him and not sin.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Exodus 20:21">21</a> The people stayed far away, while Moses went close to the deep darkness where God was.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Exodus 20:22">22</a> God told Moses, &quot;Tell the people of Israel, &#39;You have seen that I have spoken to you from heaven.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Exodus 20:23">23</a> Do not make silver gods to be with me, and do not make gold gods for yourselves.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Exodus 20:24">24</a> Make an earth altar for me and sacrifice your burnt offerings and peace offerings, your sheep and cattle on it. Wherever I make my name known, I&#39;ll come to you and bless you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Exodus 20:25">25</a> If you make me an altar of stone, do not use cut stones: if you use tools on it, you make it unclean.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Exodus 20:26">26</a> Do not climb steps to my altar, so your nakedness is not exposed.</p>
