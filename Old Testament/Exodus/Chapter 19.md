<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Exodus 19:1">1</a> In the third month, after Israel&#39;s people left Egypt, they arrived in the Sinai desert on that same day.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Exodus 19:2">2</a> They left Rephidim, came to the Sinai desert, and set up camp in the wilderness; there Israel camped at the mountain.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Exodus 19:3">3</a> Moses went up to meet God, and God called to him from the mountain, &#39;This is what you must tell the people of Jacob and say to the Israelites:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Exodus 19:4">4</a> You saw what I did to the Egyptians, how I carried you on eagle&#39;s wings, and brought you to me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Exodus 19:5">5</a> So now, if you really listen to my voice and keep my agreement, you will be a special treasure for me above everyone because the whole earth is mine.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Exodus 19:6">6</a> You will be for me a kingdom of priests and a sacred nation. These are the words you must tell the people of Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Exodus 19:7">7</a> Moses came and gathered the leaders of the people, and told them all the words that God had commanded him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Exodus 19:8">8</a> All the people answered at once, saying, &quot;We will do everything God has said.&quot; Moses told God what the people said.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Exodus 19:9">9</a> God said to Moses, &quot;Look, I will come to you in a dense cloud so the people can hear me talking with you and will always trust you.&quot; Then Moses reported to God what the people had said.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Exodus 19:10">10</a> God told Moses, &quot;Go to the people, make them holy today and tomorrow, and have them wash their clothes.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Exodus 19:11">11</a> Be prepared for the third day because on that day God will come down where everyone can see on Mount Sinai.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Exodus 19:12">12</a> Set limits for the people all around, and say, &#39;Be careful not to go up the mountain or touch its edge. Anyone who touches the mountain will certainly be put to death.&#39;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Exodus 19:13">13</a> No one should touch it, or they must be stoned or shot. Whether animal or person, they must not survive. When the trumpet sounds a long blast, they can go up the mountain.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Exodus 19:14">14</a> Moses went down from the mountain to the people, made them holy, and they washed their clothes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Exodus 19:15">15</a> He told the people, &quot;Get ready for the third day and do not go near your wives.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Exodus 19:16">16</a> On the third morning, there were thunders and lightning, a thick cloud over the mountain, and a very loud trumpet sound; all the people in the camp shook with fear.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Exodus 19:17">17</a> Moses led the people out of the camp to meet God; and they stood at the lower part of the mountain.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Exodus 19:18">18</a> Mount Sinai was completely covered in smoke because God came down on it in fire, and the smoke rose like from a furnace, making the whole mountain shake a lot.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Exodus 19:19">19</a> When the trumpet sound grew long and louder, Moses spoke and God replied with a voice.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Exodus 19:20">20</a> God came down to the top of Mount Sinai and called Moses to the top; and Moses went up.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Exodus 19:21">21</a> God told Moses, &quot;Go down and warn the people not to force their way through to see God, or many of them will die.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Exodus 19:22">22</a> Let the priests who approach God make themselves pure, so God doesn&#39;t come forcefully upon them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Exodus 19:23">23</a> Moses said to God, &quot;The people can&#39;t come up to Mount Sinai because you told us to put limits around the mountain and make it holy.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Exodus 19:24">24</a> God told him, &quot;Go down, then come back up with Aaron. But the priests and people must not force their way up to God, or he will punish them.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Exodus 19:25">25</a> Moses went down to the people and spoke to them.</p>
