<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Exodus 40:1">1</a> God spoke to Moses, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Exodus 40:2">2</a> On the first day of the first month, you will set up the meeting tent&#39;s tabernacle.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Exodus 40:3">3</a> Place the Ark of the Covenant inside, and hang the curtain in front of the Ark.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Exodus 40:4">4</a> Bring in the table and arrange the items on it; then bring in the lampstand and light its lamps.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Exodus 40:5">5</a> Place the gold altar for the incense in front of the ark of the covenant and hang the curtain at the entrance of the tent.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Exodus 40:6">6</a> Place the altar for burnt offerings in front of the entrance to the tent where people meet with God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Exodus 40:7">7</a> Place the basin between the meeting tent and the altar, and fill it with water.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Exodus 40:8">8</a> You will set up the courtyard all around and put up the curtain at the courtyard entrance.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Exodus 40:9">9</a> You will take the anointing oil, and anoint the tent of worship, and everything in it, and make it holy, and all its containers: and it will be holy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Exodus 40:10">10</a> You will anoint the altar where offerings are burned, along with all its tools, and make the altar holy: it will be a very holy altar.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Exodus 40:11">11</a> Pour oil on the basin and its stand, and make it holy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Exodus 40:12">12</a> Bring Aaron and his sons to the entrance of the meeting tent and wash them with water.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Exodus 40:13">13</a> Dress Aaron in the holy clothes, anoint him, and make him holy, so he can serve me as a priest.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Exodus 40:14">14</a> Bring his sons and dress them in robes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Exodus 40:15">15</a> You will anoint them just as you anointed their father so they can serve me as priests. Their anointing will be for a priesthood that lasts forever, generation after generation.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Exodus 40:16">16</a> Moses did everything just as God had commanded him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Exodus 40:17">17</a> In the first month of the second year, on the first day, the tabernacle was set up.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Exodus 40:18">18</a> Moses set up the tent, secured its bases, put up its frames, inserted the crossbars, and raised its posts.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Exodus 40:19">19</a> He spread out the tent over the holy tent, and placed the tent&#39;s covering on top of it, just as God had instructed Moses.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Exodus 40:20">20</a> He took the testimony, placed it in the chest, attached the poles to the chest, and set the cover on top of the chest.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Exodus 40:21">21</a> He put the ark in the tent and hung the curtain to cover the ark of the covenant, just as God told Moses to do.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Exodus 40:22">22</a> He placed the table in the meeting tent, on the north side of the dwelling, outside the curtain.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Exodus 40:23">23</a> He arranged the bread on it before God, as God had instructed Moses.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Exodus 40:24">24</a> He placed the lampstand in the meeting tent opposite the table, on the south side of the tent.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Exodus 40:25">25</a> He lit the lamps before God as God had told Moses to do.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Exodus 40:26">26</a> He placed the gold altar in the meeting tent in front of the curtain.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Exodus 40:27">27</a> He burned sweet-smelling incense on it, just as God told Moses.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Exodus 40:28">28</a> He put up the curtain at the entrance of the tent.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Exodus 40:29">29</a> He placed the altar for burnt sacrifices near the entrance of the meeting tent&#39;s tabernacle and gave burnt and grain offerings on it, just as God told Moses.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Exodus 40:30">30</a> He placed the basin between the meeting tent and the altar and filled it with water for washing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Exodus 40:31">31</a> Moses, Aaron, and his sons washed their hands and feet there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Exodus 40:32">32</a> When they entered the meeting tent, and as they approached the altar, they washed; just as God told Moses.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Exodus 40:33">33</a> He built the courtyard around the tent and the altar, and put up the curtain for the courtyard entrance. So Moses completed the task.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Exodus 40:34">34</a> A cloud hid the meeting tent, and God&#39;s glory filled the dwelling place.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Exodus 40:35">35</a> Moses could not go into the meeting tent because the cloud stayed on it, and God&#39;s glory filled the holy tent.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Exodus 40:36">36</a> When the cloud lifted above the tent, the Israelites moved forward on their travels.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Exodus 40:37">37</a> But if the cloud did not rise, then they did not move until it did.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Exodus 40:38">38</a> God&#39;s cloud was over the tent during the day, and at night, there was fire in all the Israelites&#39; view during their travels.</p>
