<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Exodus 5:1">1</a> Later, Moses and Aaron went to Pharaoh and said, &quot;God of Israel says, &#39;Let my people go, so they can celebrate a festival for me in the desert.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Exodus 5:2">2</a> Pharaoh said, &quot;Who is God, that I should listen to him and let Israel go? I don&#39;t know God, and I will not let Israel go.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Exodus 5:3">3</a> They said, &quot;The God of the Hebrews has met with us. Please let us go on a three day trip into the desert to offer sacrifices to God, so he won&#39;t strike us with sickness or the sword.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Exodus 5:4">4</a> The king of Egypt asked Moses and Aaron, &quot;Why are you stopping the people from working? Go back to your tasks.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Exodus 5:5">5</a> Pharaoh said, &quot;Look, there are now many people in the land, and you let them stop working on their heavy tasks.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Exodus 5:6">6</a> On that day, Pharaoh ordered the slave drivers and their bosses, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Exodus 5:7">7</a> You will not provide straw to the people for brick-making anymore. Instead, let them get their own straw.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Exodus 5:8">8</a> You must give them the same number of bricks they made before; do not give them less. They are lazy, so they beg to worship their God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Exodus 5:9">9</a> Give the men more work to do so they work harder and ignore useless talk.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Exodus 5:10">10</a> The people&#39;s bosses and their helpers went out and told them, &quot;This is what Pharaoh says: I will not give you straw.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Exodus 5:11">11</a> Go, find straw wherever you can, but your work must not be lessened.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Exodus 5:12">12</a> The people spread out all over Egypt to collect stubble because they had no straw.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Exodus 5:13">13</a> The overseers rushed them, saying, &quot;Finish your work, your daily jobs, just as when you had straw.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Exodus 5:14">14</a> The Egyptian leaders beat the Israelite foremen they had appointed and asked, &quot;Why haven&#39;t you made as many bricks yesterday and today as before?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Exodus 5:15">15</a> The leaders of the Israelites went to the king and asked, &quot;Why are you treating us, your servants, like this?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Exodus 5:16">16</a> Your servants are not given straw, yet we are told to make bricks. Look, your servants are beaten, but it is your own people&#39;s fault.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Exodus 5:17">17</a> But he said, &quot;You are lazy, you are lazy; that&#39;s why you say, &#39;Let&#39;s go and make sacrifices to God.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Exodus 5:18">18</a> Go now and work; you will not be given straw, but you must still produce the same number of bricks.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Exodus 5:19">19</a> The leaders of the Israelites saw they were in trouble when they were told, &quot;You must not make fewer bricks each day.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Exodus 5:20">20</a> They met Moses and Aaron waiting for them as they left Pharaoh.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Exodus 5:21">21</a> They told them, &quot;God watch you and decide; because you&#39;ve made us hated by Pharaoh and his people, giving them a reason to kill us.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Exodus 5:22">22</a> Moses went back to God and said, &quot;God, why have you treated these people badly? Why did you send me?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Exodus 5:23">23</a> Since I went to the king to speak for you, he has treated these people badly; you have not rescued your people at all.</p>
