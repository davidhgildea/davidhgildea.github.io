<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Exodus 12:1">1</a> God spoke to Moses and Aaron in Egypt, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Exodus 12:2">2</a> This month will be the start of months for you: It will be your first month of the year.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Exodus 12:3">3</a> Tell all the people of Israel that on the tenth day of this month, each family must choose a lamb for their household.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Exodus 12:4">4</a> If the family is too small for a whole lamb, they must share with a nearby neighbor based on the number of people. Everyone should have enough lamb to eat.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Exodus 12:5">5</a> Choose a one-year-old male lamb without any defects, from either the sheep or the goats.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Exodus 12:6">6</a> You must keep it until the fourteenth day of the month. Then the whole community of Israel will slaughter it at twilight.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Exodus 12:7">7</a> They will take some of the blood and put it on the two side posts and above the door frame of the houses where they will eat it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Exodus 12:8">8</a> They will eat the meat that night, cooked over fire, along with bread without yeast and bitter herbs.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Exodus 12:9">9</a> Do not eat it raw or boiled in water, but roast it over a fire with its head, legs, and inner parts.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Exodus 12:10">10</a> Do not leave any of it until morning; whatever is left until morning, you must burn.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Exodus 12:11">11</a> Eat it ready to travel, with belts on, shoes on your feet, and staffs in hand. Eat quickly, it is God&#39;s Passover.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Exodus 12:12">12</a> Tonight, I will go through Egypt and strike down every firstborn, both people and animals, and I will judge all the gods of Egypt. I am God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Exodus 12:13">13</a> The blood will be a sign on the houses where you are. When I see the blood, I will pass over you. The plague will not touch you to destroy you when I strike Egypt.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Exodus 12:14">14</a> Remember this day and celebrate it as a feast to God for all generations; it is a forever feast by God&#39;s rule.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Exodus 12:15">15</a> Eat bread without yeast for seven days; on the first day, remove all yeast from your homes. Anyone who eats bread with yeast from the first to the seventh day will be separated from Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Exodus 12:16">16</a> On the first day, you will have a special gathering, and on the seventh day, another special gathering. You must not do any work on those days, except for preparing food that everyone needs to eat.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Exodus 12:17">17</a> You must celebrate the Feast of Unleavened Bread because on this very day I led your groups out of Egypt. So, you must keep this day as a lasting rule for all time in your generations.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Exodus 12:18">18</a> On the fourteenth day of the first month at evening, you must eat bread without yeast until the evening of the twenty-first day of the month.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Exodus 12:19">19</a> For seven days, you must not have any yeast in your homes. Anyone who eats anything with yeast will be separated from the community of Israel, whether they are a visitor or born in the country.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Exodus 12:20">20</a> You must not eat anything with yeast; in all the places you live, eat bread without yeast.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Exodus 12:21">21</a> Moses gathered all the Israelite leaders and said to them, &quot;Pick out and take a lamb for each family, and slaughter it for the Passover.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Exodus 12:22">22</a> Take a bunch of hyssop, dip it in the blood in the bowl, and touch the top and both sides of the doorframe with the blood in the bowl. Do not leave your house until morning.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Exodus 12:23">23</a> God will go through to hit the Egyptians; and when He sees the blood on the top and sides of the door frame, God will skip the door, and won&#39;t let the destroyer enter your homes to hit you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Exodus 12:24">24</a> You must always follow this rule, you and your children.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Exodus 12:25">25</a> When you arrive in the land that God will give you, as He promised, you must perform this service.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Exodus 12:26">26</a> When your children ask you, &quot;What does this service mean to you?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Exodus 12:27">27</a> You should say, &quot;It is the sacrifice of God&#39;s Passover, who passed over the houses of the children of Israel in Egypt, when he struck the Egyptians and saved our houses.&quot; And the people bowed their heads and worshiped.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Exodus 12:28">28</a> The Israelites left and did what God told Moses and Aaron to do.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Exodus 12:29">29</a> At midnight, God struck down every firstborn in Egypt, from Pharaoh&#39;s firstborn who sat on his throne to the firstborn of the prisoner in the dungeon; and every firstborn of the animals too.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Exodus 12:30">30</a> Pharaoh and all his officials and Egyptians got up during the night, and there was loud crying in Egypt because every house had someone who had died.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Exodus 12:31">31</a> During the night, he asked Moses and Aaron to come and told them, &quot;Get up, leave my people with all of you and the Israelites, and go worship God, as you wanted.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Exodus 12:32">32</a> Take your livestock and your cattle, as you mentioned, and leave; and give me a blessing too.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Exodus 12:33">33</a> The Egyptians strongly urged the people to hurry and leave the land because they said, &quot;We are all going to die.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Exodus 12:34">34</a> The people took their dough before it rose, their kneading bowls wrapped in their clothes on their shoulders.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Exodus 12:35">35</a> The Israelites did what Moses told them; they asked the Egyptians for silver and gold jewelry and clothes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Exodus 12:36">36</a> God made the Egyptians kind to the people, so they borrowed what they needed. And they took valuable things from the Egyptians.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Exodus 12:37">37</a> The Israelites traveled from Rameses to Succoth, with about six hundred thousand men walking, not counting children.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Exodus 12:38">38</a> A diverse group also went with them, along with flocks, herds, and many cattle.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="Exodus 12:39">39</a> They made flat bread without yeast from the dough they took from Egypt. It was unleavened because they had to leave Egypt quickly and had no time to wait or prepare food.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="Exodus 12:40">40</a> The time the Israelites lived in Egypt was 430 years.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="Exodus 12:41">41</a> On the very day after 430 years, all of God&#39;s people left Egypt.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="Exodus 12:42">42</a> This is a special night to honor God for leading them out of Egypt: this is the night for all Israelites to remember throughout their generations.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v43"><a class="verse-num" href="#v43" aria-label="Exodus 12:43">43</a> God told Moses and Aaron, &quot;This is the rule of the Passover: No outsider may eat it.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v44"><a class="verse-num" href="#v44" aria-label="Exodus 12:44">44</a> If you buy a man as a servant, after you circumcise him, he can eat of it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v45"><a class="verse-num" href="#v45" aria-label="Exodus 12:45">45</a> A foreigner and a paid worker shall not eat it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v46"><a class="verse-num" href="#v46" aria-label="Exodus 12:46">46</a> Eat it in one house; do not take any of the meat outside; and do not break any of its bones.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v47"><a class="verse-num" href="#v47" aria-label="Exodus 12:47">47</a> Every group in Israel must celebrate it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v48"><a class="verse-num" href="#v48" aria-label="Exodus 12:48">48</a> When a foreigner stays with you and wants to celebrate the Passover to God, have all his males circumcised. Then he can join in and celebrate. He will be like someone born in the country. No uncircumcised person may eat the Passover meal.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v49"><a class="verse-num" href="#v49" aria-label="Exodus 12:49">49</a> The same law applies to everyone born at home and to foreigners living with you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v50"><a class="verse-num" href="#v50" aria-label="Exodus 12:50">50</a> All the Israelites did as God told Moses and Aaron to do.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v51"><a class="verse-num" href="#v51" aria-label="Exodus 12:51">51</a> On that very day, God led the people of Israel out of Egypt, organized by their groups.</p>
