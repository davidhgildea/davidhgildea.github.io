<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Exodus 9:1">1</a> Then God told Moses, &quot;Go to Pharaoh and say to him, &#39;God of the Hebrews says, let my people go so they can worship me.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Exodus 9:2">2</a> If you refuse to let them go and still hold them,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Exodus 9:3">3</a> Look, God&#39;s power is on your livestock in the fields—on the horses, donkeys, camels, cows, and sheep. A severe disease will come.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Exodus 9:4">4</a> God will separate the livestock of Israel from the livestock of Egypt, and none of Israel&#39;s animals will die.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Exodus 9:5">5</a> God decided on a specific time and said, &quot;Tomorrow God will do this in the land.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Exodus 9:6">6</a> The next day, God did that, and all of Egypt&#39;s cattle died, but not one of Israel&#39;s cattle died.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Exodus 9:7">7</a> Pharaoh checked, and not one of the Israelites&#39; animals had died. But Pharaoh&#39;s heart was stubborn, and he did not let the people go.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Exodus 9:8">8</a> God told Moses and Aaron, &quot;Grab handfuls of furnace ashes, and have Moses toss them into the air where Pharaoh can see.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Exodus 9:9">9</a> It will turn into fine dust over all of Egypt, causing sores that burst into blisters on people and animals everywhere in Egypt.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Exodus 9:10">10</a> They took furnace ashes and stood before Pharaoh; Moses threw them into the air, and they caused sores to break out on people and animals.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Exodus 9:11">11</a> The magicians couldn&#39;t stay in front of Moses because of the sores, as the sores were on the magicians and all the Egyptians.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Exodus 9:12">12</a> God made Pharaoh&#39;s heart stubborn, and he didn&#39;t listen to them, just as God had told Moses.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Exodus 9:13">13</a> God told Moses, &quot;Get up early in the morning, stand in front of Pharaoh, and tell him, &#39;God, the God of the Hebrews, says, &quot;Let my people go, so they can worship me.&quot;&#39;”</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Exodus 9:14">14</a> I will now send all my diseases to you, your servants, and your people, so you will know there is no one like me in the whole world.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Exodus 9:15">15</a> I will now reach out my hand to strike you and your people with disease, and you will be removed from the earth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Exodus 9:16">16</a> I made you strong for this reason: to show my power through you and to spread my name all over the world.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Exodus 9:17">17</a> Are you still proud against my people and refuse to let them go?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Exodus 9:18">18</a> Look, tomorrow at this time I will make a very terrible hailstorm happen, like none that has been in Egypt since it was founded until now.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Exodus 9:19">19</a> Go now and bring in your animals and everything you have in the fields. If any person or animal is left outside and not brought to a safe place, they will be hit by hail and die.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Exodus 9:20">20</a> Anyone who respected God&#39;s message among Pharaoh&#39;s officials brought their servants and animals inside the houses.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Exodus 9:21">21</a> The person who ignored God&#39;s word left their workers and animals outside.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Exodus 9:22">22</a> God told Moses, &quot;Reach out your hand to the sky so that hail falls over all of Egypt—on people, animals, and every plant in the fields.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Exodus 9:23">23</a> Moses raised his staff to the sky, and God made thunder and hail, and fire moved along the ground; and God poured down hail on Egypt.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Exodus 9:24">24</a> There was hail and fire mixed with the hail, very terrible, like nothing ever seen in Egypt since it became a country.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Exodus 9:25">25</a> The hail hit all over Egypt, hurting people and animals outdoors, and it damaged all the plants and broke all the trees in the fields.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Exodus 9:26">26</a> In Goshen, where the Israelites lived, there was no hail.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Exodus 9:27">27</a> The king sent for Moses and Aaron, and said to them, I did wrong this time: God is right, and I and my people are bad.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Exodus 9:28">28</a> Ask God, because it&#39;s enough, to stop the powerful thunder and hail; then I&#39;ll let you go, and you won&#39;t have to stay longer.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Exodus 9:29">29</a> Moses said to him, &quot;Once I leave the city, I will raise my hands to God; the thunder will stop and there will be no more hail. This is to show you that the earth belongs to God.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Exodus 9:30">30</a> But I know that you and your servants do not yet fear God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Exodus 9:31">31</a> The flax and barley were destroyed because the barley had heads and the flax had buds.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Exodus 9:32">32</a> The wheat and the rye were not damaged because they had not yet grown.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Exodus 9:33">33</a> Moses left the city, away from Pharaoh, and reached out his hands to God. Then the thunder and hail stopped, and the rain no longer fell on the land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Exodus 9:34">34</a> When the king saw that the rain, hail, and thunder had stopped, he sinned even more and became more stubborn, along with his officials.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Exodus 9:35">35</a> Pharaoh&#39;s heart was stubborn, and he wouldn&#39;t let the Israelites go, just as God had said through Moses.</p>
