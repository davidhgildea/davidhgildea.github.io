<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Exodus 21:1">1</a> Here are the rules that you should give to them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Exodus 21:2">2</a> If you buy a Hebrew servant, he must work for six years; but in the seventh year, he will be set free without paying anything.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Exodus 21:3">3</a> If he entered alone, he will leave alone; if he was married, his wife will leave with him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Exodus 21:4">4</a> If his master gave him a wife, and she had sons or daughters; the wife and her children belong to her master, and he will leave alone.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Exodus 21:5">5</a> If the servant clearly says, &quot;I love my master, my wife, and my children; I don&#39;t want to be set free&quot;:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Exodus 21:6">6</a> The owner must take him to the authorities; he will take him to the door or doorpost. There, the owner must pierce his ear with a tool, and he will work for his owner always.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Exodus 21:7">7</a> If a man sells his daughter as a servant, she should not leave like the male servants.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Exodus 21:8">8</a> If her master is not happy with her and does not make her his wife, he must allow her to be bought back. He cannot sell her to foreigners because he was not honest with her.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Exodus 21:9">9</a> If he has promised her to his son, he should treat her like his own daughter.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Exodus 21:10">10</a> If he marries another woman, he must not reduce the first one&#39;s food, clothes, or marital rights.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Exodus 21:11">11</a> If he does not do these three things for her, then she can go free without paying anything.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Exodus 21:12">12</a> Whoever hits a person and kills them must be put to death.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Exodus 21:13">13</a> If a man doesn&#39;t plan to harm someone, but it happens by accident, I will choose a safe place for him to go.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Exodus 21:14">14</a> If someone deliberately attacks and kills another person, take them away from my altar so they can be put to death.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Exodus 21:15">15</a> Anyone who hits their father or mother must be put to death.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Exodus 21:16">16</a> Whoever steals a person and sells them, or is caught with them, must be put to death.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Exodus 21:17">17</a> Whoever curses their father or mother must be put to death.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Exodus 21:18">18</a> If men fight and one hits the other with a stone or his fist and the injured man doesn&#39;t die but has to stay in bed:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Exodus 21:19">19</a> If he gets up and walks around with a cane, then the one who hit him is not guilty. But he must pay for the injured person&#39;s lost time and make sure he is completely healed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Exodus 21:20">20</a> If someone hits their servant, male or female, with a rod and the servant dies, the owner must be punished.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Exodus 21:21">21</a> However, if the slave survives a day or two, no punishment will happen, because the slave is his owner&#39;s property.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Exodus 21:22">22</a> If men fight and injure a pregnant woman so that she gives birth prematurely but there is no serious harm, the man must be fined whatever the woman&#39;s husband demands and the court allows.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Exodus 21:23">23</a> If harm follows, then you must give a life for a life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Exodus 21:24">24</a> Give an eye for an eye, a tooth for a tooth, a hand for a hand, a foot for a foot.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Exodus 21:25">25</a> Burn for burn, cut for cut, bruise for bruise.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Exodus 21:26">26</a> If someone hurts their servant&#39;s eye and it is destroyed, they must set the servant free to make up for the lost eye.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Exodus 21:27">27</a> If he knocks out his male or female servant&#39;s tooth, he must let them go free because of the tooth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Exodus 21:28">28</a> If an ox hurts a man or a woman so they die, the ox must be stoned to death, and no one can eat its meat; but the owner of the ox is not to blame.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Exodus 21:29">29</a> If the ox had a habit of attacking with its horn before, and its owner was warned but did not keep it secure, and the ox killed a man or woman, the ox must be stoned and the owner must be put to death.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Exodus 21:30">30</a> If he must pay money, he must pay to save his life whatever amount is required.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Exodus 21:31">31</a> If the ox hurts a boy or a girl, the same rule will apply to the owner.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Exodus 21:32">32</a> If an ox injures a male or female servant, the owner must pay the servant&#39;s master thirty silver coins, and the ox must be killed with stones.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Exodus 21:33">33</a> If someone digs a hole and doesn&#39;t cover it and a cow or donkey falls into it,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Exodus 21:34">34</a> The person who owns the pit must pay for the damages and compensate the owner of the animals; the dead animal will then belong to the person who owned the pit.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Exodus 21:35">35</a> If one person&#39;s ox injures another&#39;s ox and it dies, they must sell the live ox and split the money, and they must also split the dead ox.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Exodus 21:36">36</a> If it&#39;s known that the ox often pushed before, and its owner didn&#39;t keep it secured, he must pay an ox for the ox; the dead ox will belong to him.</p>
