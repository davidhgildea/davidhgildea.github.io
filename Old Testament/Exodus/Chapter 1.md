<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Exodus 1:1">1</a> Here are the names of the people of Israel who went to Egypt; each one with his family joined Jacob.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Exodus 1:2">2</a> Reuben, Simeon, Levi, and Judah,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Exodus 1:3">3</a> Issachar, Zebulun, and Benjamin.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Exodus 1:4">4</a> Dan, Naphtali, Gad, and Asher.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Exodus 1:5">5</a> Everyone born from Jacob totaled seventy people; Joseph was already in Egypt.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Exodus 1:6">6</a> Joseph and all his brothers and everyone from that generation died.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Exodus 1:7">7</a> The Israelites grew many, became very strong and filled the land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Exodus 1:8">8</a> A new king came to power in Egypt who did not know about Joseph.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Exodus 1:9">9</a> He said to his people, &quot;Look, the Israelites are becoming too numerous and powerful for us.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Exodus 1:10">10</a> Let&#39;s be smart in handling them so they don&#39;t increase in number. If there&#39;s a war, they might join our enemies, fight us, and then leave this land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Exodus 1:11">11</a> So they put bosses over them to make them suffer with hard work. And they built storage cities for Pharaoh, Pithom and Raamses.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Exodus 1:12">12</a> The more they hurt them, the more they increased and became strong. And they were upset because of the children of Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Exodus 1:13">13</a> The Egyptians forced the Israelites to work very hard.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Exodus 1:14">14</a> They made their lives very hard with tough work, using mortar, bricks, and all sorts of field work; every task they forced them to do was done harshly.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Exodus 1:15">15</a> The king of Egypt talked to the Hebrew midwives, named Shiphrah and Puah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Exodus 1:16">16</a> He said, &quot;When you help the Hebrew women give birth and see them on the birthing stools; if it&#39;s a boy, you must kill him, but if it&#39;s a girl, she can live.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Exodus 1:17">17</a> The midwives respected God and did not follow the Egyptian king&#39;s orders; instead, they kept the baby boys alive.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Exodus 1:18">18</a> The king of Egypt called the midwives and asked them, &quot;Why have you done this and let the boy babies live?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Exodus 1:19">19</a> The midwives told the king, &quot;Hebrew women are not like Egyptian women; they are strong and give birth before the midwives arrive.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Exodus 1:20">20</a> So God was good to the midwives, and the people grew and became very strong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Exodus 1:21">21</a> Because the midwives respected God, he gave them homes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Exodus 1:22">22</a> Pharaoh ordered all his people, &quot;Throw every newborn boy into the river, but let every girl live.&quot;</p>
