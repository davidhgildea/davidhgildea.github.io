<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Exodus 34:1">1</a> God told Moses, &quot;Cut two stone tablets like the first ones, and I will write on them the words that were on the first tablets that you broke.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Exodus 34:2">2</a> Get ready in the morning, go up Mount Sinai, and meet me at the top of the mountain.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Exodus 34:3">3</a> No one should go up with you, and no one should be seen anywhere on the mountain; also, do not let sheep or cattle graze in front of that mountain.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Exodus 34:4">4</a> He cut two stone tablets like the first ones; Moses got up early in the morning, went up Mount Sinai as God told him, and carried the two stone tablets.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Exodus 34:5">5</a> God came down in the cloud, stood with him there, and announced his own name.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Exodus 34:6">6</a> God went by him and said, &quot;God, God, kind and forgiving, patient, full of love and truth.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Exodus 34:7">7</a> God shows mercy to thousands, forgives wrongdoing, rebellion, and sin, but does not ignore the guilty; He punishes the parents&#39; sins on the children and grandchildren to the third and fourth generation.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Exodus 34:8">8</a> Moses quickly bowed his head to the ground and worshiped.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Exodus 34:9">9</a> He said, &quot;If I have now found favor with you, God, please, go with us. This is a stubborn people. Forgive our wrongs and sins, and accept us as your own.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Exodus 34:10">10</a> He said, &quot;Look, I am making a promise: I will perform amazing acts for your people that have never been done anywhere on Earth or in any nation. All the people around you will see what God will do, and it will be an awesome thing that I&#39;ll do with you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Exodus 34:11">11</a> Pay attention to what I order you today: see, I will push out before you the Amorite, the Canaanite, the Hittite, the Perizzite, the Hivite, and the Jebusite.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Exodus 34:12">12</a> Be careful not to agree with the people living in the place you&#39;re going, so it doesn&#39;t become a trap for you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Exodus 34:13">13</a> You must destroy their altars, break their idols, and cut down their sacred places.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Exodus 34:14">14</a> You must not worship any other god, because God, who is named Jealous, is a jealous God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Exodus 34:15">15</a> Make sure you don&#39;t agree with the people living in the land, or they might follow their own gods, offer sacrifices to them, invite you, and then you eat from their offerings.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Exodus 34:16">16</a> If your sons marry their daughters, their daughters will chase after their own gods and will lead your sons to do the same.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Exodus 34:17">17</a> Do not make any metal gods for yourself.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Exodus 34:18">18</a> Celebrate the feast of bread without yeast. For seven days, eat bread without yeast as I told you, during the month of Abib, because that is when you left Egypt.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Exodus 34:19">19</a> Everything born first from a mother is mine, and every first male born from your livestock, whether cow or sheep.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Exodus 34:20">20</a> You must trade a lamb for the firstborn donkey. If you don&#39;t trade it, then you must kill the donkey. You must also buy back your firstborn sons. No one should come before me with nothing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Exodus 34:21">21</a> Work for six days, but rest on the seventh day; you should rest both when you plow and when you harvest.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Exodus 34:22">22</a> Celebrate the Feast of Weeks when you harvest the first wheat, and have a gathering festival at the end of the year.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Exodus 34:23">23</a> Three times a year, all your boys must go before God, the God of Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Exodus 34:24">24</a> I will force the nations out of your way and expand your territory. No one will want your land when you go to worship God three times a year.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Exodus 34:25">25</a> You must not offer the blood of my sacrifice with yeast; and do not let the passover feast sacrifice remain until morning.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Exodus 34:26">26</a> Bring the first of your land&#39;s early crops to God&#39;s house. Do not cook a young goat in its mother&#39;s milk.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Exodus 34:27">27</a> God said to Moses, &quot;Write these words down, because with these words I have made an agreement with you and with Israel.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Exodus 34:28">28</a> He stayed with God for forty days and nights without eating bread or drinking water. He wrote the Ten Commandments, the words of the agreement, on the tablets.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Exodus 34:29">29</a> When Moses came down from Mount Sinai with the two tablets of testimony in his hand, he did not know that his face was radiant because he had spoken with God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Exodus 34:30">30</a> When Aaron and all the Israelites saw Moses, his face was glowing and they were scared to go near him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Exodus 34:31">31</a> Moses called them, and Aaron and all the leaders came back to him, and Moses spoke with them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Exodus 34:32">32</a> Later, all the Israelites approached, and Moses told them everything God had said to him on Mount Sinai.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Exodus 34:33">33</a> After Moses finished talking with them, he covered his face with a veil.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Exodus 34:34">34</a> But when Moses went to speak with God, he removed the veil until he left. After leaving, he told the people of Israel what he was told to say.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Exodus 34:35">35</a> The Israelites saw that Moses&#39; face was glowing, and Moses covered his face with a veil until he went to talk with God again.</p>
