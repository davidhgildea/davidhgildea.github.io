<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Exodus 30:1">1</a> Make an altar for burning incense from acacia wood.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Exodus 30:2">2</a> The length will be one cubit and the width one cubit; it will be square. The height will be two cubits, and the horns will be part of it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Exodus 30:3">3</a> Cover it with pure gold—the top, sides, and horns—and make a gold border around it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Exodus 30:4">4</a> Make two gold rings for it below its molding on opposite sides to hold the poles used to carry it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Exodus 30:5">5</a> Make the poles from acacia wood and cover them with gold.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Exodus 30:6">6</a> Place it in front of the curtain near the Ark of the Agreement, before the cover above the Agreement, where I will meet with you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Exodus 30:7">7</a> Aaron must burn sweet-smelling incense there every morning: when he prepares the lamps, he must burn incense on it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Exodus 30:8">8</a> When Aaron lights the lamps in the evening, he must burn incense on it, a continuous offering of incense before God for all time to come.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Exodus 30:9">9</a> Do not burn any other incense, make burnt offerings, or grain offerings on it, nor pour a drink offering on it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Exodus 30:10">10</a> Aaron must make amends on its horns once a year using the blood from the sacrifice for sins. Every year he must do this for all your future generations. It is very holy to God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Exodus 30:11">11</a> God spoke to Moses and said,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Exodus 30:12">12</a> When you count the people of Israel, each one must pay God to save their life, so that no disease comes when you count them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Exodus 30:13">13</a> Everyone counted must give this: half a shekel based on the sanctuary&#39;s shekel (which is twenty gerahs). Half a shekel is the donation to God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Exodus 30:14">14</a> Everyone who is counted, age twenty and older, must give an offering to God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Exodus 30:15">15</a> Rich people should not give more, and poor people should not give less than half a shekel when they give an offering to God to atone for your souls.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Exodus 30:16">16</a> Take the atonement money from the Israelites and use it for the work of the meeting tent; it will remind the Israelites of God, who makes amends for your lives.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Exodus 30:17">17</a> God spoke to Moses and said,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Exodus 30:18">18</a> Make a basin of bronze with a bronze stand for washing. Place it between the tent of meeting and the altar, and fill it with water.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Exodus 30:19">19</a> Aaron and his sons must wash their hands and feet there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Exodus 30:20">20</a> When they enter the meeting tent, they must wash with water so they will not die; or when they approach the altar to serve, to burn a fire offering to God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Exodus 30:21">21</a> They must wash their hands and feet so they won&#39;t die. This rule will last forever for them, their children, and for all future generations.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Exodus 30:22">22</a> God spoke to Moses, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Exodus 30:23">23</a> Get the best spices for yourself: 500 shekels of pure myrrh, half as much sweet cinnamon, which is 250 shekels, and 250 shekels of sweet calamus.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Exodus 30:24">24</a> Five hundred shekels of cassia, by the holy shekel, and an hin of olive oil.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Exodus 30:25">25</a> Make a holy oil, a mixed ointment like a perfume maker&#39;s craft; it will be a sacred anointing oil.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Exodus 30:26">26</a> You will use the oil to anoint the tent where people meet and the chest that holds the laws.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Exodus 30:27">27</a> The table and all its dishes, the lampstand and its tools, and the altar for burning incense,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Exodus 30:28">28</a> The altar for burnt offerings with all its tools, and the washbasin with its base.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Exodus 30:29">29</a> Make these things holy, so they will be very holy; whatever touches them will become holy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Exodus 30:30">30</a> You will anoint Aaron and his sons, prepare them, so they can serve me as priests.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Exodus 30:31">31</a> Tell the people of Israel: This oil will be special for anointing, to be used for me by all your future generations.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Exodus 30:32">32</a> Do not pour it on human skin, and do not make anything similar to it. It is special, and it must be kept special for you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Exodus 30:33">33</a> Anyone who makes something similar, or who puts some on a non-Israelite, must be separated from their community.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Exodus 30:34">34</a> God told Moses, &quot;Take sweet spices, stacte, onycha, and galbanum; mix these sweet spices with pure frankincense, using equal weights of each.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Exodus 30:35">35</a> Make it a scented mixture, crafted by a perfumer, mixed well, clean and sacred.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Exodus 30:36">36</a> Grind some of it into fine powder and place it in front of the covenant box in the tent of meeting, where I will come to you. It shall be very holy for you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Exodus 30:37">37</a> Make the perfume as I have instructed you. Do not make any for yourselves using this recipe. It is special and only for God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Exodus 30:38">38</a> Anyone who makes perfume like that to smell it will be separated from their people.</p>
