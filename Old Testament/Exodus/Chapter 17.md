<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Exodus 17:1">1</a> The whole group of Israel&#39;s children traveled from the Sin desert, moving as God had told them, and they camped at Rephidim, but there was no water for them to drink.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Exodus 17:2">2</a> So the people argued with Moses and said, &quot;Give us water to drink.&quot; And Moses replied, &quot;Why are you arguing with me? Why are you testing God?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Exodus 17:3">3</a> The people were thirsty for water there, and they complained to Moses, saying, &quot;Why did you bring us out of Egypt to die of thirst, along with our children and animals?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Exodus 17:4">4</a> Moses cried out to God, saying, &quot;What should I do for these people? They are almost ready to stone me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Exodus 17:5">5</a> God told Moses, &quot;Go ahead of the people and bring some of Israel&#39;s elders with you; also, take the staff you used to strike the river in your hand and go.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Exodus 17:6">6</a> Look, I will be in front of you on the rock at Horeb; hit the rock, and water will come out so the people can drink. Moses did this with the Israelite elders watching.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Exodus 17:7">7</a> He named the place Massah and Meribah because the Israelites argued and tested God by asking, &quot;Is God with us or not?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Exodus 17:8">8</a> Amalek came and battled with Israel at Rephidim.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Exodus 17:9">9</a> Moses told Joshua, &quot;Pick some men for us, and go fight Amalek. Tomorrow I&#39;ll stand on the hilltop with God&#39;s staff in my hand.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Exodus 17:10">10</a> Joshua did what Moses told him, and fought Amalek. Moses, Aaron, and Hur went to the top of the hill.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Exodus 17:11">11</a> When Moses raised his hand, Israel won; when he lowered his hand, Amalek won.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Exodus 17:12">12</a> Moses&#39; hands were tired, so they placed a stone under him to sit on. Aaron and Hur helped hold up his hands, one on each side, and his hands stayed steady until sunset.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Exodus 17:13">13</a> Joshua defeated Amalek and his people with the sword.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Exodus 17:14">14</a> God told Moses, &quot;Write this down as a reminder in a book and tell it to Joshua: I will completely erase the memory of Amalek from the earth.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Exodus 17:15">15</a> Moses built an altar and named it The Lord Is My Banner.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Exodus 17:16">16</a> He said, because God has promised that God will fight Amalek forever, generation after generation.</p>
