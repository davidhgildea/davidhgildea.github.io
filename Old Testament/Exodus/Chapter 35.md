<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Exodus 35:1">1</a> Moses brought all the Israelites together and told them, &quot;These are the commands God has given for you to follow.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Exodus 35:2">2</a> Work for six days, but the seventh day is a holy day for you, a day of rest for God. Anyone who works on that day will be put to death.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Exodus 35:3">3</a> Do not start any fire in your homes on the Sabbath day.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Exodus 35:4">4</a> Moses spoke to all the Israelites, saying, &quot;This is what God has commanded.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Exodus 35:5">5</a> Bring a gift for God from among yourselves; anyone who wants to give can bring gold, silver, and bronze.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Exodus 35:6">6</a> And blue, purple, red, fine cloth, and goat hair.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Exodus 35:7">7</a> Red-dyed ram skins, other animal skins, and acacia wood.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Exodus 35:8">8</a> Oil for the lamp, spices for the anointing oil, and for the sweet smelling incense.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Exodus 35:9">9</a> And onyx stones, and stones for the ephod and breastplate.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Exodus 35:10">10</a> Everyone with a wise heart should come and make everything that God has commanded.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Exodus 35:11">11</a> God&#39;s tent, its covering, its clasps, its frames, its crossbars, its posts, and its bases.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Exodus 35:12">12</a> The chest, its poles, the lid, and the curtain that covers it,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Exodus 35:13">13</a> The table, its poles, all its tools, and the bread on display.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Exodus 35:14">14</a> The lampstand for the light, its accessories, its lamps, and the oil for the light.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Exodus 35:15">15</a> The altar for incense, its poles, the oil for anointing, the fragrant incense, and the curtain for the entrance to the tent.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Exodus 35:16">16</a> The altar for burning sacrifices, with its bronze grid, its poles, and all its tools, the washbasin and its base,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Exodus 35:17">17</a> The curtains of the courtyard, its posts, their bases, and the curtain for the entrance to the courtyard,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Exodus 35:18">18</a> The stakes of the tent, and the stakes of the yard, and their ropes,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Exodus 35:19">19</a> The special clothes for serving in the sacred space, the holy outfits for Aaron the priest, and the clothes for his sons to serve as priests.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Exodus 35:20">20</a> The whole group of Israel&#39;s people left from being with Moses.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Exodus 35:21">21</a> They came, everyone moved by their heart, and all who felt willing, and they brought God&#39;s offering for the work on the meeting tent, all its service, and the holy clothes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Exodus 35:22">22</a> Men and women came, all who wanted to help. They brought gold bracelets, earrings, rings, and other jewelry. Everyone who gave, gave gold gifts to God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Exodus 35:23">23</a> Everyone who had blue, purple, or scarlet cloth, fine linen, goats&#39; hair, red sheepskins, or leather brought them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Exodus 35:24">24</a> Everyone who gave silver or brass brought it to God, and anyone who had acacia wood for the work brought it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Exodus 35:25">25</a> All the skilled women spun thread with their hands and brought what they made, in blue, purple, scarlet, and fine linen.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Exodus 35:26">26</a> All the women who felt inspired and skilled spun goat hair.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Exodus 35:27">27</a> The leaders brought onyx stones and other gems for the priest&#39;s garment and chest piece.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Exodus 35:28">28</a> And spices, and oil for the lamp, and for the anointing oil, and for the fragrant incense.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Exodus 35:29">29</a> The people of Israel willingly gave offerings to God, each man and woman who wanted to contribute to all kinds of work, which God had instructed Moses to oversee.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Exodus 35:30">30</a> Moses told the people of Israel, &quot;Look, God has chosen Bezaleel, Uri&#39;s son, Hur&#39;s grandson, from the tribe of Judah by name.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Exodus 35:31">31</a> He has filled him with God&#39;s spirit, with wisdom, understanding, knowledge, and skill in all kinds of crafts.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Exodus 35:32">32</a> To create intricate designs and to work with gold, silver, and bronze.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Exodus 35:33">33</a> In shaping stones to place them, and in carving wood to create any kind of skilled work.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Exodus 35:34">34</a> God has given him the ability to teach, both him and Aholiab, Ahisamach&#39;s son from the tribe of Dan.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Exodus 35:35">35</a> He has given them skilled hearts to do every kind of work: engraving, expert crafting, and embroidering with blue, purple, scarlet thread and fine linen, and weaving. They can do any job and create intricate designs.</p>
