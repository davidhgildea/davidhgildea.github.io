<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Exodus 11:1">1</a> God told Moses, &quot;I will send one more plague on Pharaoh and Egypt. After that, he will let you leave this place. When he lets you go, he will really force you all out.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Exodus 11:2">2</a> Tell the people to ask their neighbors, both men and women, for silver and gold jewelry.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Exodus 11:3">3</a> God made the Egyptians like the people. Also, Moses was very important in Egypt to Pharaoh&#39;s officials and the people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Exodus 11:4">4</a> Moses said, &quot;God says, &#39;Around midnight, I will go through Egypt.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Exodus 11:5">5</a> Every first child in Egypt will die, from the Pharaoh&#39;s first child on his throne to the servant girl&#39;s first child working at the mill; and every first child of the animals too.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Exodus 11:6">6</a> A terrible scream will spread across Egypt like never before and will never be heard again.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Exodus 11:7">7</a> No dog will bark at any Israelite, person or animal, so you will see that God treats Israel differently from Egypt.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Exodus 11:8">8</a> All your servants will come to me and bow down, saying, &#39;Leave, with all who follow you.&#39; After that, I&#39;ll go. And he left Pharaoh, very angry.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Exodus 11:9">9</a> God told Moses, Pharaoh won&#39;t listen to you; this is so I can do many amazing things in Egypt.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Exodus 11:10">10</a> Moses and Aaron did all these miracles in front of Pharaoh, but God made Pharaoh&#39;s heart stubborn, so he wouldn&#39;t let the Israelites leave his country.</p>
