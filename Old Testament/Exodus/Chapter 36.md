<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Exodus 36:1">1</a> Bezaleel, Aholiab, and every skilled person whom God gave wisdom and understanding knew how to do all kinds of work for the sanctuary service, just as God had instructed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Exodus 36:2">2</a> Moses called Bezaleel, Aholiab, and every skilled person whom God had given wisdom, and everyone who felt moved to come and do the work.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Exodus 36:3">3</a> They took from Moses all the gifts the Israelites had brought for the sanctuary&#39;s service work. And every morning, they also brought voluntary gifts to him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Exodus 36:4">4</a> All the skilled people who did the work for the holy place stopped their own tasks and came.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Exodus 36:5">5</a> They said to Moses, &quot;The people are giving more than needed for the work that God ordered us to do.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Exodus 36:6">6</a> Moses ordered and they announced in the camp, &quot;No man or woman should make anything more for the sanctuary offering.&quot; So the people stopped bringing things.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Exodus 36:7">7</a> They had enough materials for all the work, and more than needed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Exodus 36:8">8</a> Every skilled worker among them who made the tabernacle created ten curtains from fine linen, blue, purple, and red fabric, with artistic designs of cherubs woven into them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Exodus 36:9">9</a> One curtain was twenty-eight cubits long and four cubits wide, and all the curtains were the same size.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Exodus 36:10">10</a> He joined five curtains together and then joined the other five curtains in the same way.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Exodus 36:11">11</a> He made blue loops on the edge of one curtain by the seam, and did the same on the edge of the other curtain where it joined the second.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Exodus 36:12">12</a> He made fifty loops on one curtain, and fifty loops on the edge of the second curtain to join them together. The loops connected one curtain to the other.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Exodus 36:13">13</a> He made fifty gold clasps and used them to attach the curtains together, so they became one tent.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Exodus 36:14">14</a> He made eleven goat hair curtains to cover the tent that was over the sanctuary.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Exodus 36:15">15</a> Each curtain was thirty units long and four units wide; all eleven curtains were the same size.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Exodus 36:16">16</a> He joined five curtains together, and six curtains together.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Exodus 36:17">17</a> He made fifty loops on the very end of one curtain for joining, and fifty loops on the edge of the second curtain to connect them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Exodus 36:18">18</a> He made fifty bronze clasps to connect the tent so it would be one piece.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Exodus 36:19">19</a> He made a red-dyed ram skin cover for the tent and another cover of badger skins on top.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Exodus 36:20">20</a> He made upright boards for the tabernacle out of acacia wood.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Exodus 36:21">21</a> A board was ten units long and one and a half units wide.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Exodus 36:22">22</a> Each board had two pegs set evenly apart: he did this for all the tabernacle&#39;s boards.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Exodus 36:23">23</a> He made panels for the tent; twenty panels for the south side facing south.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Exodus 36:24">24</a> He made forty silver bases for the twenty planks; each plank had two bases for its two projections.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Exodus 36:25">25</a> For the other side of the tent, facing north, he made twenty boards.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Exodus 36:26">26</a> They had forty silver bases; two bases under one plank, and two bases under another plank.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Exodus 36:27">27</a> He made six boards for the west side of the tabernacle.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Exodus 36:28">28</a> He made two boards for the corners of the tent on both sides.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Exodus 36:29">29</a> They were joined at the bottom and connected at the top to one ring. This was done for both of them at the two corners.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Exodus 36:30">30</a> There were eight boards; each had two silver sockets underneath, making sixteen sockets in total.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Exodus 36:31">31</a> He made poles of acacia wood; five for the panels on one side of the tent.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Exodus 36:32">32</a> Make five crossbars for the frames on the other side of the tent and five crossbars for the frames at the west end.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Exodus 36:33">33</a> He made the center bar to go through the boards from one end to the other.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Exodus 36:34">34</a> He covered the boards with gold, made their rings of gold for the bars, and covered the bars with gold.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Exodus 36:35">35</a> He made a curtain of blue, purple, and red, and quality linen, skillfully woven with angel designs.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Exodus 36:36">36</a> He made four pillars of acacia wood for it, covered them with gold, with gold hooks, and made four silver bases for them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Exodus 36:37">37</a> He made a curtain for the entrance of the tent with blue, purple, and red colors, and with finely twisted linen, that was sewn.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Exodus 36:38">38</a> It had five pillars with hooks, and he covered the tops and bands with gold; the five bases were bronze.</p>
