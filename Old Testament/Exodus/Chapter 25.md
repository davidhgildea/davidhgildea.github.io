<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Exodus 25:1">1</a> God spoke to Moses and said,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Exodus 25:2">2</a> Tell the people of Israel to give me an offering. You must accept the offering from anyone who gives it willingly from their heart.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Exodus 25:3">3</a> Give this as your offering: gold, silver, and bronze.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Exodus 25:4">4</a> And blue, purple, red, fine cloth, and goat hair.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Exodus 25:5">5</a> Red-dyed ram skins, badger skins, and acacia wood.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Exodus 25:6">6</a> Oil for the lamp, spices for the anointing oil, and for the sweet smelling incense.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Exodus 25:7">7</a> Onyx stones and stones for setting in the ephod and the breastplate.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Exodus 25:8">8</a> Let them build a holy place for me, so I can live with them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Exodus 25:9">9</a> Follow exactly the design I show you for the tent and for all the equipment, and make them that way.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Exodus 25:10">10</a> Make an ark from acacia wood: it should be 2.5 cubits long, 1.5 cubits wide, and 1.5 cubits high.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Exodus 25:11">11</a> Cover it with pure gold inside and out, and put a gold rim around it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Exodus 25:12">12</a> Make four gold rings for it and attach them to its four corners; two rings on one side and two on the other side.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Exodus 25:13">13</a> Make poles from acacia wood and cover them with gold.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Exodus 25:14">14</a> Put the poles through the rings on the sides of the chest to carry it with them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Exodus 25:15">15</a> The poles must remain in the rings of the chest; they must not be removed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Exodus 25:16">16</a> Put the laws I give you into the chest.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Exodus 25:17">17</a> Make a cover of pure gold, two and a half cubits long and one and a half cubits wide.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Exodus 25:18">18</a> Make two gold cherubs with hammered work at both ends of the cover.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Exodus 25:19">19</a> Make one angel on one end and another angel on the other end; you should make the angels from the same piece as the mercy seat, at its two ends. 

(Note: &quot;Globish&quot; typically refers to a simplified version of English designed to be understood by non-native speakers around the world, so the term &quot;cherub&quot; has been translated to &quot;angel&quot; for clarity.)</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Exodus 25:20">20</a> The cherubim will spread out their wings above, shielding the mercy seat with their wings, and they will face each other; the cherubim&#39;s faces should be toward the mercy seat.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Exodus 25:21">21</a> Put the mercy seat on top of the ark, and inside the ark, put the laws I will give you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Exodus 25:22">22</a> There I will meet with you, and I will talk to you from above the mercy seat, between the two cherubs on the ark of the testimony, about everything I command you for the people of Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Exodus 25:23">23</a> Also make a table from acacia wood: it should be two cubits long, one cubit wide, and one and a half cubits high.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Exodus 25:24">24</a> Cover it with pure gold and add a gold border around it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Exodus 25:25">25</a> Make a border one hand&#39;s width all around it, and put a gold rim around the border.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Exodus 25:26">26</a> Make four gold rings for it and attach them to its four corners at the four legs.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Exodus 25:27">27</a> The rings should be right next to the edge to hold the poles that carry the table.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Exodus 25:28">28</a> Make poles from acacia wood and cover them with gold so the table can be carried with them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Exodus 25:29">29</a> Make its plates, spoons, pitchers, and bowls for pouring. Make them all of pure gold.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Exodus 25:30">30</a> Put bread on the table in front of me always.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Exodus 25:31">31</a> Make a lampstand of pure gold. It should be crafted from hammered work: its base, arms, cups, buds, and blossoms must all be one piece.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Exodus 25:32">32</a> Six arms will extend from the sides; three arms of the lampstand from one side, and three arms of the lampstand from the other side.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Exodus 25:33">33</a> Each of the six branches coming out of the lampstand had three cups shaped like almond flowers, each with buds and petals.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Exodus 25:34">34</a> In the lampstands there will be four cups shaped like almonds, with their buds and blossoms.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Exodus 25:35">35</a> There will be a bud below two branches of it, a bud below another two branches, and a bud below two more branches, making six branches coming out of the lampstand.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Exodus 25:36">36</a> The buds and branches will be one piece with the lampstand, all made from solid gold.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Exodus 25:37">37</a> Make seven lamps for it, and set up the lamps so they shine light in front of it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Exodus 25:38">38</a> Its tongs and snuffdishes must be made of pure gold.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="Exodus 25:39">39</a> He must make it of a talent of pure gold, including all these utensils.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="Exodus 25:40">40</a> Make sure you make them just like the pattern you were shown on the mountain.</p>
