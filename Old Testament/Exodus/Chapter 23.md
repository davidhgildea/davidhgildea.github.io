<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Exodus 23:1">1</a> Do not spread false rumors or join hands with the wicked to be an unfair witness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Exodus 23:2">2</a> Do not follow a crowd to do wrong; nor should you agree with many to make unfair decisions.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Exodus 23:3">3</a> Do not favor a poor man in his case.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Exodus 23:4">4</a> If you find your enemy&#39;s ox or donkey lost, you must return it to him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Exodus 23:5">5</a> If you see your enemy&#39;s donkey lying down under its load, and you might avoid helping, you must help anyway.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Exodus 23:6">6</a> Do not treat the poor unfairly in legal matters.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Exodus 23:7">7</a> Stay away from lies and don&#39;t kill innocent or good people because I will not let the guilty go unpunished.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Exodus 23:8">8</a> Do not accept any gifts; gifts blind the wise and twist the words of the good people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Exodus 23:9">9</a> Do not treat foreigners badly because you know how it feels to be a foreigner, as you were foreigners in Egypt.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Exodus 23:10">10</a> For six years you should plant your land and collect its crops.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Exodus 23:11">11</a> In the seventh year, let the land rest and be still, so the poor can eat. Whatever they don&#39;t eat, the wild animals will. Do the same with your vineyard and olive grove.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Exodus 23:12">12</a> Work for six days, and rest on the seventh day, so your ox, donkey, your servant&#39;s child, and any foreigner can also rest and be refreshed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Exodus 23:13">13</a> Be careful to follow everything I have told you, and do not talk about other gods or even mention their names.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Exodus 23:14">14</a> Celebrate a feast for me three times a year.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Exodus 23:15">15</a> You must celebrate the Feast of Unleavened Bread by eating bread without yeast for seven days, as I have told you, during the set time in the month of Abib, because that is when you left Egypt. No one should come to see me without bringing something.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Exodus 23:16">16</a> Celebrate the Harvest Festival when you first collect crops from the fields, and the Gathering Festival at the year&#39;s end when you gather your work from the fields.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Exodus 23:17">17</a> Three times a year, all your men must come before God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Exodus 23:18">18</a> Do not give God a sacrifice mixed with yeast, and do not keep the fat from my offering until morning.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Exodus 23:19">19</a> Bring the first of your harvest to God&#39;s house. Do not cook a young goat in its mother&#39;s milk.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Exodus 23:20">20</a> Look, I am sending an Angel ahead of you to protect you on the journey and to lead you to the place I have prepared.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Exodus 23:21">21</a> Be careful around him, listen to what he says, and do not make him angry, because he will not forgive your wrongdoings; for he represents me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Exodus 23:22">22</a> If you really listen to his voice and do everything I say, then I will be against your enemies and oppose those who oppose you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Exodus 23:23">23</a> My angel will go ahead of you and lead you to the Amorites, Hittites, Perizzites, Canaanites, Hivites, and Jebusites, and I will eliminate them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Exodus 23:24">24</a> Do not bow down to their gods, do not serve them, or follow their ways; instead, you must completely destroy them and break their statues.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Exodus 23:25">25</a> You will serve God, and he will bless your food and your water; I will remove sickness from among you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Exodus 23:26">26</a> No animal will miscarry or be infertile in your land; I will allow you to live a full life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Exodus 23:27">27</a> I will make people afraid of you and destroy all the people you meet, and I will make all your enemies run away from you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Exodus 23:28">28</a> I will send hornets ahead of you to chase away the Hivite, the Canaanite, and the Hittite.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Exodus 23:29">29</a> I will not force them out from in front of you in one year; if I do, the land may become empty, and the wild animals may become too many for you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Exodus 23:30">30</a> Step by step, I will push them out before you, until you grow and take over the land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Exodus 23:31">31</a> I will define your borders from the Red Sea to the Philistine Sea, and from the desert to the river. I will give the people living there to you, and you will force them out.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Exodus 23:32">32</a> Do not make a treaty with them or their gods.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Exodus 23:33">33</a> They must not live in your land, or they will make you sin against me; if you worship their gods, it will certainly trap you.</p>
