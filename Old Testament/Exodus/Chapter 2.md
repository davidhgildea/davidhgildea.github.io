<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Exodus 2:1">1</a> A man from Levi&#39;s family went and married a woman from Levi&#39;s family.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Exodus 2:2">2</a> The woman became pregnant and had a baby boy. When she saw he was a fine child, she hid him for three months.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Exodus 2:3">3</a> When she couldn&#39;t hide her baby anymore, she made a basket from reeds, sealed it with tar and pitch, placed her child inside, and set it among the plants by the river&#39;s edge.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Exodus 2:4">4</a> His sister stood far away, to see what would happen to him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Exodus 2:5">5</a> Pharaoh&#39;s daughter went to bathe in the river; her attendants walked beside the river; when she saw the basket in the reeds, she sent her servant to get it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Exodus 2:6">6</a> When she opened it, she saw the baby crying, and she felt sorry for him, saying, &quot;This is one of the Hebrew babies.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Exodus 2:7">7</a> His sister asked Pharaoh&#39;s daughter, &quot;Should I go and find a Hebrew woman to nurse the baby for you?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Exodus 2:8">8</a> The princess told her, &quot;Go.&quot; So the girl went and called the baby&#39;s mother.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Exodus 2:9">9</a> Pharaoh&#39;s daughter told her, &quot;Take this baby and care for him for me, and I will pay you.&quot; So, the woman took the baby and cared for him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Exodus 2:10">10</a> The child grew up, and she took him to the Pharaoh&#39;s daughter, who adopted him as her son. She named him Moses, saying, &quot;I pulled him out of the water.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Exodus 2:11">11</a> In those days, when Moses was older, he went to his people and saw their hard work. He saw an Egyptian hitting a Hebrew, one of his own people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Exodus 2:12">12</a> He looked around, and seeing no one, he killed the Egyptian and hid him in the sand.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Exodus 2:13">13</a> On the second day he went out, he saw two Hebrew men fighting. He asked the one who was in the wrong, &quot;Why are you hitting your companion?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Exodus 2:14">14</a> He asked, &quot;Who made you our leader and judge? Do you plan to kill me like you killed the Egyptian?&quot; Moses was scared and thought, &quot;Everyone must know about it.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Exodus 2:15">15</a> When the king heard about this, he wanted to kill Moses. But Moses ran away from the king and lived in Midian, where he sat down by a well.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Exodus 2:16">16</a> The priest of Midian had seven daughters, and they came to draw water, filling the troughs to water their father&#39;s sheep.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Exodus 2:17">17</a> The shepherds arrived and chased them off, but Moses got up, helped the women, and gave their sheep water.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Exodus 2:18">18</a> When they returned to their father Reuel, he asked, &quot;Why have you come back so early today?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Exodus 2:19">19</a> They said, an Egyptian saved us from the shepherds and also got plenty of water for us, and gave it to the sheep.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Exodus 2:20">20</a> He asked his daughters, &quot;Where is he? Why did you leave the man behind? Invite him to eat bread with us.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Exodus 2:21">21</a> Moses was happy to live with the man and the man gave his daughter Zipporah to Moses as his wife.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Exodus 2:22">22</a> She gave birth to a son, and he named him Gershom, saying, &quot;I have been a foreigner in a foreign land.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Exodus 2:23">23</a> Over time, the king of Egypt died; the Israelites groaned because they were slaves, they cried out, and their cries reached God because of their slavery.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Exodus 2:24">24</a> God heard their cries, and God remembered his promise to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Exodus 2:25">25</a> God saw the Israelites and cared for them.</p>
