<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Exodus 24:1">1</a> He told Moses, &quot;Go up to God, you, Aaron, Nadab, Abihu, and seventy of Israel&#39;s elders, and worship from a distance.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Exodus 24:2">2</a> Only Moses will go close to God; others will not, nor will the people go up with him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Exodus 24:3">3</a> Moses told the people everything God said, including the laws. Everyone agreed and said, &quot;We will do everything God has told us.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Exodus 24:4">4</a> Moses wrote down all of God&#39;s words. Early in the morning, he got up, built an altar at the foot of the mountain, and set up twelve stones for the twelve tribes of Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Exodus 24:5">5</a> He sent young Israelites to present burnt offerings and sacrifice oxen as peace offerings to God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Exodus 24:6">6</a> Moses took half of the blood, put it in bowls, and sprinkled the other half on the altar.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Exodus 24:7">7</a> He took the book of the agreement and read it to the people. They said, &quot;We will do everything God has said and obey.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Exodus 24:8">8</a> Moses took the blood and sprinkled it on the people, saying, &quot;Look, this is the blood of the agreement God has made with you about all these words.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Exodus 24:9">9</a> Moses, Aaron, Nadab, Abihu, and seventy elders from Israel went up.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Exodus 24:10">10</a> They saw the God of Israel: under his feet was something like a floor made of sapphire, as clear as the sky itself.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Exodus 24:11">11</a> God did not harm the leaders of the Israelites; they saw God, and they ate and drank.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Exodus 24:12">12</a> God said to Moses, &quot;Come up to the mountain to me, and stay there. I will give you stone tablets, laws, and commands I have written, so you can teach them.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Exodus 24:13">13</a> Moses stood up with his helper Joshua and went up the mountain of God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Exodus 24:14">14</a> He told the elders, &quot;Stay here for us until we return to you. Aaron and Hur are with you; if anyone has any issues, they should go to them.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Exodus 24:15">15</a> Moses went up the mountain, and a cloud covered the mountain.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Exodus 24:16">16</a> God&#39;s brightness stayed on Mount Sinai, and a cloud hid it for six days. On the seventh day, He called to Moses from inside the cloud.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Exodus 24:17">17</a> The children of Israel saw the glory of God as a consuming fire on the mountaintop.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Exodus 24:18">18</a> Moses went into the cloud and climbed up the mountain, and he was on the mountain for forty days and nights.</p>
