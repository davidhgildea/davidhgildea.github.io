<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Exodus 10:1">1</a> God said to Moses, &quot;Go to the Pharaoh, because I have made his heart and his servants&#39; hearts stubborn, so I can display my miracles to them.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Exodus 10:2">2</a> So you can tell your son and grandson about what I did in Egypt, and the signs I showed them; so you will know that I am God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Exodus 10:3">3</a> Moses and Aaron went to Pharaoh and said, &quot;God of the Hebrews asks, &#39;How long will you not humble yourself before me? Let my people go to serve me.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Exodus 10:4">4</a> If you don&#39;t let my people go, tomorrow I will bring locusts into your land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Exodus 10:5">5</a> They will spread across the land so no one can see the ground. They will eat what&#39;s left after the hail and consume every tree that grows in your fields.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Exodus 10:6">6</a> They will fill your houses, the houses of your servants, and the houses of all Egyptians in a way your ancestors or their ancestors have never seen before. After saying this, he left Pharaoh&#39;s presence.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Exodus 10:7">7</a> Pharaoh&#39;s officials said to him, &quot;How long will this man trap us? Let the men go to worship their God. Don&#39;t you realize that Egypt is ruined?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Exodus 10:8">8</a> Moses and Aaron were taken back to Pharaoh, and he said to them, &quot;Go, worship your God. But who will go?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Exodus 10:9">9</a> Moses said, &quot;We will go with our young and old, our sons and daughters, and our animals; because we have to celebrate a feast for God.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Exodus 10:10">10</a> He told them, &quot;May God be with you, like I will let you and your children go. Be careful; danger is ahead of you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Exodus 10:11">11</a> No, only the men can go and worship God, as you asked. Then they were forced to leave Pharaoh&#39;s presence.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Exodus 10:12">12</a> God told Moses, &quot;Reach out your hand over Egypt to bring locusts, so they can cover the land and eat every plant, including what the hail has spared.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Exodus 10:13">13</a> Moses held out his stick over Egypt, and God sent an east wind across the land all day and night; when morning came, the east wind brought locusts.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Exodus 10:14">14</a> The locusts covered all of Egypt and settled everywhere. They were terrible; no other locusts like them had ever been seen before, nor would there ever be again.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Exodus 10:15">15</a> They spread over the whole earth, making the land dark; they ate every plant and all the fruit the hail had left. There was no green left on the trees or plants in the fields in Egypt.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Exodus 10:16">16</a> Then Pharaoh quickly called for Moses and Aaron; he said, &quot;I have sinned against your God and against you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Exodus 10:17">17</a> Please forgive my sin just this time, and ask God to stop this death from happening to me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Exodus 10:18">18</a> He left Pharaoh and prayed to God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Exodus 10:19">19</a> God sent a powerful west wind that blew the locusts away and into the Red Sea; not a single locust was left in Egypt.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Exodus 10:20">20</a> God made Pharaoh&#39;s heart stubborn, so he wouldn&#39;t let the Israelites go.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Exodus 10:21">21</a> God told Moses, &quot;Reach out your hand toward the sky so that darkness spreads over Egypt, a darkness that can be touched.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Exodus 10:22">22</a> Moses reached out his hand toward the sky, and there was a deep darkness over all of Egypt for three days.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Exodus 10:23">23</a> They couldn&#39;t see each other, and nobody left their spot for three days; but all the Israelites had light where they lived.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Exodus 10:24">24</a> The Pharaoh called Moses and said, &quot;Go and worship God; but leave your sheep and cattle behind. Take your children with you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Exodus 10:25">25</a> And Moses said, You must give us sacrifices and burnt offerings so we can offer them to God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Exodus 10:26">26</a> We will take our animals with us; not a single hoof will be left behind; we need them to worship God; and we don&#39;t know what we will need to worship God until we get there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Exodus 10:27">27</a> God made Pharaoh&#39;s heart stubborn, and he refused to let them go.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Exodus 10:28">28</a> Pharaoh said to him, &quot;Go away from me, be careful, and don&#39;t see me again; because the day you see my face, you will die.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Exodus 10:29">29</a> Moses said, &quot;You&#39;ve spoken well, I won&#39;t see you again.&quot;</p>
