<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Exodus 6:1">1</a> Then God told Moses, &quot;Now you will see what I will do to Pharaoh: he will let the people go because of my powerful action, and he will force them to leave his land.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Exodus 6:2">2</a> God spoke to Moses and told him, &quot;I am God.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Exodus 6:3">3</a> I showed myself to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob as God Almighty, but they did not know me as Jehovah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Exodus 6:4">4</a> I have also made a promise to them to give them the land of Canaan, where they traveled as outsiders.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Exodus 6:5">5</a> I have heard the cries of the Israelites, whom the Egyptians enslave; I remember my promise.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Exodus 6:6">6</a> Tell the people of Israel, I am God, and I will free you from the Egyptian&#39;s heavy work, and I will save you from their slavery, and I will rescue you with a powerful arm and with mighty acts of judgment.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Exodus 6:7">7</a> I will make you my people, and I will be your God. You will know that I am your God who frees you from the Egyptians&#39; heavy work.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Exodus 6:8">8</a> I will take you to the land that I promised to give to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, and I will give it to you as your inheritance: I am God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Exodus 6:9">9</a> Moses spoke this way to the Israelites, but they didn&#39;t listen to him because they were very sad and suffering from harsh slavery.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Exodus 6:10">10</a> God spoke to Moses and said,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Exodus 6:11">11</a> Go and talk to Pharaoh, the king of Egypt, and tell him to let the Israelites leave his country.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Exodus 6:12">12</a> Moses spoke to God, saying, &quot;Look, the people of Israel haven&#39;t listened to me; so how will Pharaoh listen to me, when I speak so poorly?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Exodus 6:13">13</a> God spoke to Moses and Aaron, and gave them instructions for the Israelites and Pharaoh, the king of Egypt, to lead the Israelites out of Egypt.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Exodus 6:14">14</a> These are the leaders of their families: Reuben, Israel&#39;s first son, had Hanoch, Pallu, Hezron, and Carmi; these are the Reuben families.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Exodus 6:15">15</a> Simeon&#39;s sons were Jemuel, Jamin, Ohad, Jachin, Zohar, and Shaul, whose mother was from Canaan. These are the Simeon families.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Exodus 6:16">16</a> These are the names of Levi&#39;s sons by generation: Gershon, Kohath, and Merari. Levi lived 137 years.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Exodus 6:17">17</a> Gershon&#39;s sons were Libni and Shimi, for each family group.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Exodus 6:18">18</a> Kohath&#39;s sons were Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel. Kohath lived for 133 years.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Exodus 6:19">19</a> Merari&#39;s sons were Mahali and Mushi. These were the Levi families by their generations.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Exodus 6:20">20</a> Amram married his aunt Jochebed; she gave birth to Aaron and Moses: Amram lived 137 years.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Exodus 6:21">21</a> Izhar&#39;s sons were Korah, Nepheg, and Zichri.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Exodus 6:22">22</a> Uzziel&#39;s sons were Mishael, Elzaphan, and Zithri.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Exodus 6:23">23</a> Aaron married Elisheba, Amminadab&#39;s daughter and Naashon&#39;s sister, and they had Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Exodus 6:24">24</a> Korah&#39;s sons were Assir, Elkanah, and Abiasaph; these are the Korahite family groups.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Exodus 6:25">25</a> Eleazar, Aaron&#39;s son, married one of Putiel&#39;s daughters; she gave birth to Phinehas: they are the leaders of the Levite family heads.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Exodus 6:26">26</a> These are Aaron and Moses, whom God told, &quot;Lead Israel&#39;s people out of Egypt with their groups.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Exodus 6:27">27</a> These are the ones who spoke to Pharaoh, the king of Egypt, to free the people of Israel from Egypt: they are Moses and Aaron.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Exodus 6:28">28</a> One day when God spoke to Moses in Egypt,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Exodus 6:29">29</a> God spoke to Moses and said, &quot;I am God; tell the Pharaoh, king of Egypt, everything I tell you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Exodus 6:30">30</a> Moses said to God, &quot;Look, I don&#39;t speak well, so how will Pharaoh listen to me?&quot;</p>
