<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 10:1">1</a> Dead flies make the perfumer&#39;s oil give off a bad smell; similarly, a little foolishness can ruin the respect of someone known for wisdom and honor.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 10:2">2</a> A wise person&#39;s heart leads them to do what is right; but a foolish person&#39;s heart leads them to do what is wrong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 10:3">3</a> Yes, when a fool walks on the road, he lacks wisdom, and he tells everyone that he is a fool.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 10:4">4</a> If a ruler gets angry with you, don&#39;t leave your position; staying calm can resolve major problems.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 10:5">5</a> I have seen a bad thing under the sun; it is a mistake that comes from the leader.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 10:6">6</a> Foolishness is honored, and the wealthy take the humble seats.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 10:7">7</a> I have seen workers on horses and rulers walking like workers on the ground.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 10:8">8</a> Anyone who digs a pit will fall into it; and whoever breaks through a hedge, a snake will bite him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 10:9">9</a> Whoever moves stones may get hurt; and whoever splits wood may be in danger from it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 10:10">10</a> If the tool is dull and its sharpness is not honed, then one must use more strength; however, wisdom helps to apply effort effectively.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 10:11">11</a> For sure, a snake will bite if it&#39;s not charmed; a chatterer is no different.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 10:12">12</a> A wise person&#39;s words are kind, but a fool&#39;s words will ruin him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 10:13">13</a> The first things he says are silly, and his final words are crazy and harmful.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 10:14">14</a> A fool talks too much and no one knows what will happen. Who can say what will come after him?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 10:15">15</a> The hard work of foolish people tires them all because they don&#39;t know how to get to the city.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 10:16">16</a> Trouble comes to a country when its leader is immature, and its rulers eat early in the day.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 10:17">17</a> Happy is the country when its king is from a noble family, and its leaders eat at the right time, for strength, and not to get drunk.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 10:18">18</a> Because of too much laziness, the building falls apart; and because of not using one&#39;s hands, the house leaks.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 10:19">19</a> A feast is for fun, and wine cheers us up, but money solves all problems.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 10:20">20</a> Do not curse the king, even to yourself; and do not curse the wealthy in your bedroom: because a bird might spread the news, and a creature with wings might reveal what you said.</p>
