<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 7:1">1</a> Having a good reputation is better than having expensive perfume, and the day someone dies is better than the day they are born.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 7:2">2</a> It is better to visit a place where people are sad because of death than a place where people are celebrating. This is because everyone will die someday, and the living should think about this.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 7:3">3</a> Sadness is better than laughing because a sad face can improve the heart.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 7:4">4</a> Wise people think about sad times; foolish people think about happy times.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 7:5">5</a> It is better to listen to the correction from someone smart than to enjoy the music of foolish people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 7:6">6</a> Like the noise of thorns burning under a pot, so is a fool&#39;s laugh: it&#39;s also meaningless.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 7:7">7</a> Oppression can make a smart person angry; and a bribe can ruin their character.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 7:8">8</a> The end of something is better than its start, and being patient is better than being proud.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 7:9">9</a> Don&#39;t be quick to get angry, because anger lives in the hearts of fools.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 7:10">10</a> Do not ask why the old days were better than now; asking this does not show wisdom.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 7:11">11</a> Having wisdom and an inheritance is good, and it benefits those who live under the sun.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 7:12">12</a> Wisdom protects us, and so does money, but the best thing about knowledge is that wisdom brings life to those who possess it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 7:13">13</a> Think about what God does: who can fix something if God made it bent?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 7:14">14</a> When times are good, be happy, but when times are bad, think: God has made one as well as the other, so that people can&#39;t predict anything about their future.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 7:15">15</a> In my empty days, I have seen everything: a good person dies despite being good, and a bad person lives long while being bad.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 7:16">16</a> Don&#39;t be too righteous or too wise; why ruin your life?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 7:17">17</a> Don&#39;t be too wicked or foolish; why die before it&#39;s your time?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 7:18">18</a> It is good for you to grasp this and not let go; because one who respects God will succeed in everything.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 7:19">19</a> Wisdom gives the wise more strength than ten strong men in the city.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 7:20">20</a> No one on earth is perfect; everyone does some good, but also makes mistakes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 7:21">21</a> Don&#39;t pay attention to everything people say, or you might hear your servant speaking badly of you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 7:22">22</a> Your heart knows that you have also cursed others many times.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 7:23">23</a> I have tested all these things with wisdom; I thought I could become wise, but it was out of my reach.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 7:24">24</a> What is far away and very deep, who can discover it?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 7:25">25</a> I set my heart to understand, to investigate, and to find wisdom and explanations, and to understand foolishness, senselessness, and madness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 7:26">26</a> I find a woman whose heart is full of traps and her hands are like chains more bitter than death. Someone who pleases God will avoid her, but a sinner will be caught by her.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 7:27">27</a> Look, this is what I have discovered, says the teacher, adding one thing to another to find the explanation.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 7:28">28</a> My soul still searches, but I do not find: I have found one man among a thousand, but not one woman among all.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 7:29">29</a> Look, I have found only this: God made people good, but they have come up with many schemes.</p>
