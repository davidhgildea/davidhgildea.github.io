<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 1:1">1</a> This is the Preacher&#39;s message, David&#39;s son and king in Jerusalem.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 1:2">2</a> Everything is meaningless, says the Teacher; everything is completely meaningless.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 1:3">3</a> What good does a person get from all the work they do under the sun?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 1:4">4</a> One generation goes, and another comes, but the earth lasts forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 1:5">5</a> The sun rises and sets, and quickly goes back to where it rose.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 1:6">6</a> The wind moves south, then turns north; it goes around and around, and comes back to its paths.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 1:7">7</a> All rivers flow into the sea, but the sea never fills up. The rivers go back to where they came from and start again.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 1:8">8</a> Everything is hard work; people can&#39;t express it: the eye is never happy with what it sees, nor the ear with what it hears.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 1:9">9</a> What has happened before will happen again; what has been done before will be done again; there&#39;s nothing new under the sun.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 1:10">10</a> Can we say about anything, &quot;Look, this is new&quot;? It has already existed long ago, before our time.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 1:11">11</a> People do not remember past things; and those to come will not be remembered by those who follow them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 1:12">12</a> I, the Teacher, was king over Israel in Jerusalem.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 1:13">13</a> I devoted myself to seek and understand by wisdom everything that happens on earth: this difficult task has been given by God to humanity to work on.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 1:14">14</a> I have seen everything people do on earth, and truly, everything is pointless and frustrating.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 1:15">15</a> What is twisted cannot be straightened; what is missing cannot be counted.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 1:16">16</a> I talked to myself, saying, &quot;Look, I have become very wealthy and have gained more wisdom than anyone before me in Jerusalem; yes, my heart has learned much about wisdom and knowledge.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 1:17">17</a> I devoted myself to understand wisdom, madness, and foolishness and realized that this too is frustrating.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 1:18">18</a> With much wisdom comes much sadness; gaining more knowledge leads to more sorrow.</p>
