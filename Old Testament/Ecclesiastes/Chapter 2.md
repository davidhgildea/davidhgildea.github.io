<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 2:1">1</a> I told myself, &quot;Come on, let&#39;s try pleasure and have fun.&quot; But this, too, turned out to be meaningless.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 2:2">2</a> I said about laughter, &quot;It&#39;s crazy,&quot; and about joy, &quot;What does it do?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 2:3">3</a> I decided to enjoy wine while keeping wise, and to embrace foolishness, until I could understand what is truly good for people to do throughout their lives.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 2:4">4</a> I made great projects; I built houses for myself; I planted vineyards for myself.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 2:5">5</a> I created gardens and orchards for myself, and in them I planted all types of fruit trees.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 2:6">6</a> I made pools of water to water the forest that grows trees.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 2:7">7</a> I got myself workers and maids, and had workers born in my house; I also had many herds of large and small animals, more than anyone in Jerusalem before me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 2:8">8</a> I collected silver and gold for myself, and special treasures from kings and regions. I got male and female singers, and all kinds of musical pleasures that people enjoy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 2:9">9</a> I became very great, more than anyone in Jerusalem before me; my wisdom stayed with me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 2:10">10</a> Whatever my eyes wanted, I did not keep from them. I did not deny my heart any joy; my heart was happy in all my work, and this was my reward from all my work.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 2:11">11</a> I looked at everything I had done and all the hard work I had put in, and I saw that it was all pointless and frustrating, and there was no real gain to be had on earth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 2:12">12</a> I looked to understand wisdom, madness, and folly: for what can a person do who follows the king? Only what has already been done.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 2:13">13</a> I saw that wisdom is better than foolishness, just as light is better than darkness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 2:14">14</a> A wise person uses their mind, but a fool lives without guidance. I also noticed that the same things happen to everyone.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 2:15">15</a> I thought to myself, &quot;What happens to fools will happen to me too. So why have I been so wise?&quot; I realized that this is also meaningless.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 2:16">16</a> People won&#39;t remember the wise more than the fool forever; because what exists now will be forgotten in the future. And the wise person dies just like the fool.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 2:17">17</a> So I disliked life because the work done under the sun troubled me; it all seemed pointless and frustrating.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 2:18">18</a> Yes, I hated all my hard work under the sun because I must leave it to the person who will come after me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 2:19">19</a> Who knows if he will be wise or foolish? Yet he will control all my hard work where I have been wise. This too is pointless.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 2:20">20</a> So, I lost hope over all the hard work I had done under the sun.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 2:21">21</a> There is a person who works with wisdom, knowledge, and fairness; but he will leave what he has worked for to another who hasn&#39;t worked for it. This too is meaningless and a big problem.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 2:22">22</a> What does a person gain from all their work and the troubles that make their heart worry, which they experience in their life under the sun?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 2:23">23</a> Every day is full of sadness and his work brings grief; even at night, his mind finds no rest. This too is meaningless.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 2:24">24</a> The best thing for a person is to eat, drink, and enjoy good things from their work. I saw that this too comes from God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 2:25">25</a> Who can eat or enjoy life more than I?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 2:26">26</a> God gives a good person wisdom, knowledge, and happiness, but gives a sinner hard work to collect and pile up wealth only to hand it over to the one who pleases God. This too is meaningless and frustrating.</p>
