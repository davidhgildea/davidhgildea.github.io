<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 3:1">1</a> Everything has its time, and there is a moment for every purpose under heaven.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 3:2">2</a> A time to be born, and a time to die; a time to plant, and a time to pull up what is planted.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 3:3">3</a> A time to kill, and a time to heal; a time to tear down, and a time to build up.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 3:4">4</a> A time to cry, and a time to laugh; a time to grieve, and a time to dance;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 3:5">5</a> A time to throw away stones, and a time to collect stones; a time to hug, and a time to avoid hugging.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 3:6">6</a> A time to gain, and a time to give up; a time to save, and a time to throw away;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 3:7">7</a> A time to tear, and a time to mend; a time to be quiet, and a time to talk;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 3:8">8</a> A time to love, and a time to hate; a time for war, and a time for peace.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 3:9">9</a> What does a person gain from their work?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 3:10">10</a> I have seen the hard work that God has given to people to do.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 3:11">11</a> God has made everything beautiful in its time. He also placed the world in their hearts, yet no one can fully understand what God does from start to finish.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 3:12">12</a> I understand that there is no real benefit for people except to be happy and do good during their lives.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 3:13">13</a> Everyone should eat, drink, and enjoy the results of their work—this is a gift from God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 3:14">14</a> I know that whatever God does will last forever. You can&#39;t add or take away from it. And God does it so people will respect Him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 3:15">15</a> What has happened is happening now; and what will happen has already happened; and God asks for what has occurred.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 3:16">16</a> I also saw under the sun the place of judgment, where there was evil, and the place of righteousness, where there was wrong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 3:17">17</a> I thought to myself, God will judge the good and the bad, because there is a right time for every plan and action.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 3:18">18</a> I thought to myself about people&#39;s lives, that God tests them so they can see that they are like animals.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 3:19">19</a> What happens to people happens to animals too. They all have the same fate: as one dies, so does the other. They all have the same spirit. People have no advantage over animals, for everything is meaningless.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 3:20">20</a> Everyone goes to the same place; everyone comes from dust, and everyone will turn to dust again.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 3:21">21</a> Who knows if the human spirit rises up and the animal spirit goes down into the earth?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 3:22">22</a> So I see that there is nothing better than for a person to be happy with their own work; that is their reward. Who can show them what will happen after they are gone?</p>
