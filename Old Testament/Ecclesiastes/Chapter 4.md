<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 4:1">1</a> I went back and saw all the unfair things happening on earth: I saw the tears of the oppressed, with no one to comfort them; the oppressors had the power, but the oppressed had no comforter.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 4:2">2</a> So I said that those who have already died are happier than those who are still alive.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 4:3">3</a> Yes, the one who has not yet been born is better off than both of them, for he has not seen the bad things done in the world.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 4:4">4</a> I saw that all hard work and success in doing good things can make people jealous of their neighbor. This is also meaningless and frustrating.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 4:5">5</a> The foolish person does nothing and harms themselves.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 4:6">6</a> It is better to have a little with peace than a lot with hard work and stress.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 4:7">7</a> Then I came back and saw that everything is pointless under the sun.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 4:8">8</a> There is only one person, without a companion; they have no children or siblings. Still, their work never ends; they are never happy with their wealth, and never ask, &quot;Who am I working for, and denying myself enjoyment?&quot; This too is meaningless, a miserable task.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 4:9">9</a> Two are better than one because they get a good return for their work.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 4:10">10</a> If they fall, one can help the other up, but pity the person who is alone and falls without someone to help them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 4:11">11</a> If two people lie together, they keep warm, but how can one stay warm alone?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 4:12">12</a> If one person can be defeated, two can stand strong against the enemy; a rope made of three strands is not easily snapped.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 4:13">13</a> A poor and smart child is better than an old and foolish king who won&#39;t listen to advice.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 4:14">14</a> He comes to rule after being in jail; even someone born in his kingdom may become poor.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 4:15">15</a> I thought about everyone alive who goes about under the sun, along with the next person who will take their place.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 4:16">16</a> People never stop coming, even those from before. Those who come later won&#39;t be happy with this. This too is pointless and frustrating.</p>
