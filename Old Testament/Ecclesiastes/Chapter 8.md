<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 8:1">1</a> Who is as wise as that person? And who understands the meaning of things? A person&#39;s wisdom lights up their face, and it softens the hardness of their expression.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 8:2">2</a> I advise you to obey the king&#39;s order, because of your promise to God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 8:3">3</a> Don&#39;t rush to leave his presence, and don&#39;t take part in wrong; for he does whatever he wants.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 8:4">4</a> Where a king&#39;s command is, there is power: and who can ask him, &quot;What are you doing?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 8:5">5</a> Anyone who follows the command will not experience anything bad; and a wise person understands the right time and decision.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 8:6">6</a> Every goal has its right time and way, so people often feel overwhelmed by troubles.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 8:7">7</a> He does not know what will happen: for who can tell him when it will be?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 8:8">8</a> No one has power over their spirit to keep it, and no one has control over the day of death. There is no release from that struggle; evil will not save those who are bound to it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 8:9">9</a> I have seen all this and given my heart to every deed done under the sun: there is a time when one person rules over another to their harm.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 8:10">10</a> I saw the bad people buried who went back and forth from the holy place, and they were forgotten in the city where they did these things; this is also meaningless.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 8:11">11</a> Because punishment for bad actions is not given quickly, people are encouraged to keep doing wrong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 8:12">12</a> Even if a sinner does wrong many times and lives a long life, I am sure that things will go well for those who respect God and show their respect for him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 8:13">13</a> Things will not go well for the wicked, and he will not live long, like a shadow, because he does not respect God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 8:14">14</a> There is a meaningless thing happening on earth: good people get what bad people deserve, and bad people get what good people deserve. I say this is also meaningless.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 8:15">15</a> I praised fun because the best thing for a person in life is to eat, drink, and enjoy themselves. This happiness will stay with them through their workdays, given by God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 8:16">16</a> When I devoted my heart to learn wisdom and to observe the activities done on the earth: (because there are those who do not get sleep day or night:)</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 8:17">17</a> I saw everything God has done, and we can&#39;t fully understand all that happens on earth. Even if we work hard to search for it, we won&#39;t find it. And even if a wise person thinks they can know it, they won&#39;t be able to discover it.</p>
