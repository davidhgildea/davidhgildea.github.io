<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 5:1">1</a> Be careful when you enter God&#39;s house, and be eager to listen rather than to offer the foolish sacrifice of those who don&#39;t realize they&#39;re doing wrong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 5:2">2</a> Don&#39;t speak without thinking, and don&#39;t be quick to say anything before God. God is in heaven and you are on earth, so use only a few words.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 5:3">3</a> Dreams come with lots of activity, and a fool is recognized by too many words.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 5:4">4</a> When you make a promise to God, do not delay in fulfilling it; for God does not like fools: fulfill the promise you made.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 5:5">5</a> It&#39;s better not to make a promise than to make one and not keep it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 5:6">6</a> Do not let your mouth lead you into sin, and don&#39;t tell the messenger it was a mistake. Why should God be angry with your words and ruin what you have done?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 5:7">7</a> In having many dreams and lots of words, there are also empty things: but respect God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 5:8">8</a> If you see the poor being oppressed, and unfairness in law and justice in the land, don&#39;t be surprised: because God, who is above the highest authorities, sees everything, and there are powers greater than them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 5:9">9</a> The good things the earth gives are for everyone; even the king depends on what the fields produce.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 5:10">10</a> Whoever loves money will never have enough; loving wealth brings no satisfaction. This is pointless too.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 5:11">11</a> As wealth grows, so do those who consume it. And what benefit is it to the owners except to look at it with their own eyes?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 5:12">12</a> A working person sleeps peacefully, whether they eat a little or a lot, but a rich person&#39;s wealth keeps them from sleeping well.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 5:13">13</a> I have seen a terrible problem on Earth: wealth hoarded by its owner to their harm.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 5:14">14</a> Those riches vanish by bad work: he has a son and has nothing to give him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 5:15">15</a> When he was born from his mother, he was naked, and he will go back the same way when he dies, taking nothing of his work with him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 5:16">16</a> This is also a terrible wrong: a person leaves the world just as they came into it. So what does someone gain from working for nothing?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 5:17">17</a> Throughout his life, he eats in the dark, feeling much sadness and anger because of his illness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 5:18">18</a> I have seen that it is good and proper for a person to eat, drink, and enjoy the benefits of their work throughout their life under the sun, the life that God has given them, because this is their share.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 5:19">19</a> Everyone whom God has given wealth and riches, and the ability to enjoy them, to take their share, and to find joy in their work; this is a gift from God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 5:20">20</a> He won&#39;t think much about his life&#39;s days, because God responds to him with joy in his heart.</p>
