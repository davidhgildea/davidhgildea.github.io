<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 6:1">1</a> I have seen a bad thing everywhere in the world, and it happens often to people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 6:2">2</a> A man whom God has given money, possessions, and respect, so he lacks nothing he wants, but if God doesn&#39;t let him enjoy these things and a stranger does instead, this is pointless and a terrible sickness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 6:3">3</a> If a man has a hundred children and lives a long time, but is not happy or buried when he dies, it is better to be born dead.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 6:4">4</a> He comes with emptiness, leaves in the dark, and his name will be shrouded in darkness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 6:5">5</a> He hasn&#39;t seen the sun or learned anything; he has more peace than the other.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 6:6">6</a> Even if a person lives two thousand years, they still do not experience true happiness. Don&#39;t we all end up in the same place after death?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 6:7">7</a> Everyone works to feed themselves, but no one is ever fully satisfied.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 6:8">8</a> What advantage does a wise person have over a fool? What does the poor person gain by knowing how to conduct themselves in life?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 6:9">9</a> Seeing what is in front of you is better than chasing your wishes. This, too, is pointless and frustrating.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 6:10">10</a> What has happened has already been named, and it is known that it is human; no one can argue with one who is stronger than themselves.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 6:11">11</a> With so many things that add to emptiness, how is a person improved?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Ecclesiastes 6:12">12</a> Who knows what is best for a person in life, during the meaningless days they pass like a shadow? Who can tell anyone what will happen after them under the sun?</p>
