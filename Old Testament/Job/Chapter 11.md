<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Job 11:1">1</a> Zophar from Naama replied, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Job 11:2">2</a> Shouldn&#39;t many words get a reply? And should a chatty person be right?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Job 11:3">3</a> Do your lies make people stay silent? When you mock, doesn&#39;t anyone make you feel ashamed?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Job 11:4">4</a> You have said, &quot;My teaching is pure, and I am clean in your sight.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Job 11:5">5</a> I wish God would speak and tell you off.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Job 11:6">6</a> God wants to show you the deep secrets of wisdom, which are twice as much as what exists. Understand then that God asks from you less than your wrongs deserve.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Job 11:7">7</a> Can you find out about God by searching? Can you fully understand the Almighty?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Job 11:8">8</a> It&#39;s as high as the sky; what can you do? It&#39;s deeper than the deepest place; what can you know?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Job 11:9">9</a> Its size is longer than the earth and wider than the sea.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Job 11:10">10</a> If he decides, closes in, or brings together, then who can stop him?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Job 11:11">11</a> For he knows foolish people: he sees evil too; won&#39;t he think about it?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Job 11:12">12</a> Empty-headed people want to seem wise, even though they are born as wild as a donkey&#39;s foal.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Job 11:13">13</a> If you get your heart ready and reach out your hands to him;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Job 11:14">14</a> If you hold wrongdoing in your hand, put it far from you, and do not let evil live in your homes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Job 11:15">15</a> You will be able to hold your head high without guilt; yes, you will be steady and without fear.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Job 11:16">16</a> You will forget your troubles, and think of them like water that has gone by.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Job 11:17">17</a> Your future will be brighter than midday: you will glow, you will be like the dawn.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Job 11:18">18</a> You will be safe because there is hope; yes, you will look around and lie down without fear.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Job 11:19">19</a> You will lie down without fear; many will come to ask for your favor.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Job 11:20">20</a> The wicked will lose their sight, won&#39;t get away, and their hope will vanish like a dying breath.</p>
