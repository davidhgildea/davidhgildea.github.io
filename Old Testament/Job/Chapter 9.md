<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Job 9:1">1</a> Then Job replied and spoke,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Job 9:2">2</a> I know it&#39;s true: but how can a person be right with God?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Job 9:3">3</a> If he argues with God, he cannot reply to even one out of a thousand questions.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Job 9:4">4</a> He is smart in his heart and very strong: who has resisted him and succeeded?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Job 9:5">5</a> God moves the mountains without their knowing and turns them over in anger.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Job 9:6">6</a> God shakes the earth from its place, making its pillars tremble.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Job 9:7">7</a> Who commands the sun not to rise and seals the stars.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Job 9:8">8</a> Who alone stretches out the sky and walks on the sea&#39;s waves.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Job 9:9">9</a> God made the stars Arcturus, Orion, and Pleiades and the hidden places of the south.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Job 9:10">10</a> God does great things beyond understanding and countless wonders.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Job 9:11">11</a> Look, he moves past me, but I don&#39;t see him; he goes by, but I don&#39;t notice him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Job 9:12">12</a> Look, when God takes something, who can stop Him? Who will ask Him, &quot;What are you doing?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Job 9:13">13</a> If God does not stop being angry, the prideful supporters must bow down to him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Job 9:14">14</a> How much less can I reply to God and carefully select my words to argue with Him?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Job 9:15">15</a> Even if I were right, I would not respond but ask for mercy from my judge.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Job 9:16">16</a> If I had called and he had answered me, I still wouldn&#39;t believe that he listened to my voice.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Job 9:17">17</a> He crushes me with a storm and gives me many wounds for no reason.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Job 9:18">18</a> He won&#39;t let me catch my breath, but fills me with sadness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Job 9:19">19</a> If I talk about power, God is powerful; and if about fairness, who can schedule a time for my case?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Job 9:20">20</a> If I claim to be right, my own words will show I&#39;m wrong: if I claim to be perfect, they will show I&#39;m not.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Job 9:21">21</a> Even if I were perfect, I still would not understand myself; I would hate my life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Job 9:22">22</a> I said this because God destroys both the good and the bad.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Job 9:23">23</a> If a disaster kills unexpectedly, God will mock when the innocent are tested.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Job 9:24">24</a> God has let wicked people control the earth; they hide the truth from its judges. If this isn&#39;t so, who else could it be?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Job 9:25">25</a> Now my days pass faster than a messenger: they rush by and see nothing good.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Job 9:26">26</a> They have gone by like fast ships, like an eagle swooping down on its food.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Job 9:27">27</a> If I decide to forget my troubles, to put aside my sadness, and to cheer myself up:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Job 9:28">28</a> I am scared of all my troubles; I know you will not find me innocent.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Job 9:29">29</a> If I am bad, why do I work for nothing?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Job 9:30">30</a> If I clean myself with snow and wash my hands very well;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Job 9:31">31</a> You will push me into a pit, and my own clothes will disgust me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Job 9:32">32</a> He is not a person like me that I could reply to him or that we could meet in court.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Job 9:33">33</a> There is no mediator between us to put a hand on both of us.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Job 9:34">34</a> Let him stop using his rod on me, so his fear won&#39;t scare me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Job 9:35">35</a> I would talk without being afraid of him; but that is not my situation.</p>
