<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Job 18:1">1</a> Bildad the Shuhite replied and said,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Job 18:2">2</a> How long until you stop talking? Pay attention, and then we will speak.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Job 18:3">3</a> Why are we treated like animals and considered disgusting to you?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Job 18:4">4</a> He hurts himself in his anger: should the world be abandoned for you? And should the stone be moved from its spot?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Job 18:5">5</a> Yes, the wicked&#39;s light will go out, and their fire&#39;s spark won&#39;t shine.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Job 18:6">6</a> The light in his tent will become dark, and his lamp will go out with him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Job 18:7">7</a> His strong steps will become tight, and his own plans will make him fall.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Job 18:8">8</a> He is trapped in a net by his own steps and walks on a trap.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Job 18:9">9</a> A trap will catch him by the foot, and a thief will defeat him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Job 18:10">10</a> A trap is set for him on the ground, and a snare is in his path.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Job 18:11">11</a> Fears will scare him from all around and make him run away.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Job 18:12">12</a> His strength will be weakened by hunger, and ruin will stand by his side.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Job 18:13">13</a> It will eat away the power of his skin; death&#39;s firstborn will consume his power.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Job 18:14">14</a> His trust will be pulled up from his home, and it will take him to the king of fears.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Job 18:15">15</a> It will live in its tent, for it is not its own; sulfur will be spread over its home.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Job 18:16">16</a> His roots will dry up below, and his branches will be cut off above.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Job 18:17">17</a> People will forget him on earth, and his name will not be spoken in public.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Job 18:18">18</a> He will be forced from light to darkness, and driven away from the world.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Job 18:19">19</a> He won&#39;t have a son or nephew among his people, or anyone left in his home.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Job 18:20">20</a> Those who follow him will be amazed at his time, just as those before were terrified.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Job 18:21">21</a> These are surely the homes of the evil, and this is where someone who does not know God lives.</p>
