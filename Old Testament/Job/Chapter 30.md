<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Job 30:1">1</a> Now, younger people mock me, people whose fathers I wouldn&#39;t have let sit with my sheepdogs.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Job 30:2">2</a> Yes, how could the strength of their hands help me, when they were worn out by old age?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Job 30:3">3</a> They were alone, suffering from hunger and need; they ran away to the empty, barren wilderness long ago.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Job 30:4">4</a> They gathered plants by the bushes and used juniper roots for food.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Job 30:5">5</a> They were forced out from where people lived; people shouted at them as they would at a thief.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Job 30:6">6</a> To live in valley cliffs, earth caves, and among the rocks.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Job 30:7">7</a> In the bushes they cried out; under the thorns they huddled together.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Job 30:8">8</a> They were kids of fools, yes, kids of lowly men; they were worse than dirt.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Job 30:9">9</a> Now I am their joke; yes, I am their mocking word.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Job 30:10">10</a> They hate me, they run away from me, and they don&#39;t hesitate to spit in my face.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Job 30:11">11</a> Because he has untied my rope and made me suffer, they have also removed the restraint in my presence.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Job 30:12">12</a> Young people gather on my right side; they knock me off my feet, and they build up paths to harm me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Job 30:13">13</a> They block my way, they increase my trouble, they have no helper.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Job 30:14">14</a> They attacked me like a flood breaking through: in my ruin, they overwhelmed me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Job 30:15">15</a> Fears attack me; they chase my soul like the wind: and my well-being disappears like a cloud.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Job 30:16">16</a> My soul is poured out inside me; days of suffering grip me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Job 30:17">17</a> At night, my bones hurt deeply, and my muscles get no rest.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Job 30:18">18</a> My sickness is so strong it changes my clothes; it wraps around me like my coat&#39;s collar.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Job 30:19">19</a> He has thrown me into the mud, and I have become like dust and ashes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Job 30:20">20</a> I call to you, but you don&#39;t hear me; I stand up, but you don&#39;t notice me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Job 30:21">21</a> You have become harsh to me; with your strong hand, you stand against me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Job 30:22">22</a> You lift me up to the wind; you make me ride on it, and you melt away my strength.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Job 30:23">23</a> I know you will lead me to death, to the place set for everyone who lives.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Job 30:24">24</a> However, he will not reach out to the grave when they call out as they perish.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Job 30:25">25</a> Didn&#39;t I cry for the person who was suffering? Wasn&#39;t I sad for the poor?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Job 30:26">26</a> When I hoped for good, evil came to me; and when I waited for light, darkness arrived.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Job 30:27">27</a> I felt deep turmoil inside and could not rest; my days of trouble came before I was ready.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Job 30:28">28</a> I walked sadly without the sun; I stood up and cried among the people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Job 30:29">29</a> I am like a brother to wild dragons, and a friend to owls.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Job 30:30">30</a> My skin is dark on me, and my bones are hot from burning.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Job 30:31">31</a> My harp now plays sad songs, and my flute sounds like the cries of those who are crying.</p>
