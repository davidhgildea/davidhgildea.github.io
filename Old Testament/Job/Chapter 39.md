<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Job 39:1">1</a> Do you know when the wild goats give birth? Can you tell when the deer are having their young?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Job 39:2">2</a> Can you count how many months they need? Or do you know when they give birth?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Job 39:3">3</a> They bend down, they give birth to their young, they let go of their pain.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Job 39:4">4</a> Their young are healthy, they grow up with grain; they leave and do not come back.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Job 39:5">5</a> Who set the wild donkey free? Who untied the ropes of the wild donkey?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Job 39:6">6</a> I made the wilderness his house and the empty land his home.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Job 39:7">7</a> He mocks the city&#39;s crowd and ignores the driver&#39;s shouts.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Job 39:8">8</a> The mountains are where his animals graze, and he looks for every plant that is green.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Job 39:9">9</a> Will the unicorn want to work for you or stay by your feeding trough?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Job 39:10">10</a> Can you tie the unicorn with a rope in the fields? Or will he plow the valleys behind you?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Job 39:11">11</a> Will you trust him because he is strong? Or will you rely on him to do your work?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Job 39:12">12</a> Will you trust him to bring back your crops and collect them into your barn?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Job 39:13">13</a> Did you give the beautiful wings to the peacocks? Or the wings and feathers to the ostrich?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Job 39:14">14</a> She leaves her eggs on the ground and keeps them warm with the dirt.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Job 39:15">15</a> And forgets that a foot might crush them, or a wild animal might destroy them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Job 39:16">16</a> She is cold toward her young, as if they were not hers; her hard work is pointless without concern.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Job 39:17">17</a> God has not given her wisdom, nor has he given her understanding.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Job 39:18">18</a> When she rises up, she looks down on the horse and its rider.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Job 39:19">19</a> Did you give the horse its strength? Did you make its neck strong like thunder?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Job 39:20">20</a> Can you scare him like a grasshopper? His strong breath is frightening.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Job 39:21">21</a> He digs in the valley, happy in his power; he moves towards the armed men.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Job 39:22">22</a> He laughs at fear, is not scared, and does not turn away from the sword.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Job 39:23">23</a> The arrows shake against him, the shining spear and the shield.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Job 39:24">24</a> He charges across the land with anger and fury; he does not think the trumpet sound is real.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Job 39:25">25</a> He says among the trumpets, &quot;Ha, ha&quot;; and he senses the battle from far away, the noise of the commanders, and the yelling.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Job 39:26">26</a> Does the hawk fly by your wisdom and spread its wings to the south?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Job 39:27">27</a> Does the eagle fly up on your order and build her nest up high?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Job 39:28">28</a> She lives and stays on the rock, at the top of the cliff, and in the secure spot.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Job 39:29">29</a> From there she looks for food, and her eyes see from far away.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Job 39:30">30</a> Her young ones also drink blood: and where there are dead bodies, there she is.</p>
