<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Job 17:1">1</a> My breath is bad, my days are over, the graves wait for me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Job 17:2">2</a> Aren&#39;t there people who mock me around? And doesn&#39;t my eye endure their insults?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Job 17:3">3</a> Lie down now, make a promise with me; who will agree and shake hands with me?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Job 17:4">4</a> You have kept their heart from understanding; so you will not lift them up.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Job 17:5">5</a> Whoever uses smooth talk with friends will find even their children&#39;s eyes will fail.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Job 17:6">6</a> He has made me a joke among the people; before, I was like a drum.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Job 17:7">7</a> My eyes are weak from sadness, and my whole body is like a shadow.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Job 17:8">8</a> Good people will be shocked at this, and the honest will stand up against the fake.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Job 17:9">9</a> Good people will stay on their path, and those with pure actions will become stronger and stronger.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Job 17:10">10</a> But all of you, come back now: I can&#39;t find one wise person among you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Job 17:11">11</a> My days are gone, my plans are shattered, and so are the hopes of my heart.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Job 17:12">12</a> They turn night into day: daylight ends quickly because of the dark.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Job 17:13">13</a> If I wait, the grave becomes my home; I have prepared my bed in the dark.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Job 17:14">14</a> I told decay, &quot;You are my father.&quot; To the worm, &quot;You are my mother and my sister.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Job 17:15">15</a> Where is my hope now? Who will see my hope?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Job 17:16">16</a> They will go to the gates of the grave when we all rest in the dust together.</p>
