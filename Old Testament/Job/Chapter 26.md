<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Job 26:1">1</a> But Job replied and spoke,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Job 26:2">2</a> How have you helped the person with no power? How can you save the arm that has no strength?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Job 26:3">3</a> How have you advised someone who lacks wisdom? And how have you fully explained the situation as it is?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Job 26:4">4</a> Who did you speak to? And who inspired your words?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Job 26:5">5</a> Dead things take shape beneath the waters, and those that live there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Job 26:6">6</a> God sees everything in hell, and nothing can hide destruction.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Job 26:7">7</a> He stretches out the north over empty space and hangs the earth on nothing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Job 26:8">8</a> He gathers water into his heavy clouds, and the clouds don&#39;t break under the weight.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Job 26:9">9</a> He covers his throne and puts a cloud over it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Job 26:10">10</a> He has set limits for the waters, until the time when day and night end.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Job 26:11">11</a> Heaven&#39;s pillars shake and are amazed at his scolding.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Job 26:12">12</a> He splits the sea with his strength, and with his wisdom, he defeats the arrogant.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Job 26:13">13</a> By his Spirit, he made the skies beautiful; his hand shaped the twisted snake.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Job 26:14">14</a> Look, these are some of his actions, but we hear only a small part about him. Who can grasp the greatness of his power?</p>
