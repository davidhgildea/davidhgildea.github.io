<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Job 13:1">1</a> I have seen all this with my eyes; my ears have heard and understood it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Job 13:2">2</a> I know what you know; I am not less than you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Job 13:3">3</a> I want to talk to God Almighty, and I wish to discuss with God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Job 13:4">4</a> You create lies and your healing is worthless.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Job 13:5">5</a> I wish you would all be completely quiet! It would be wise for you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Job 13:6">6</a> Listen to my arguments and pay attention to what I say.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Job 13:7">7</a> Will you speak wrongly for God and talk with lies for him?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Job 13:8">8</a> Will you favor him? Will you argue for God?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Job 13:9">9</a> Is it right for him to investigate you? Like one person teases another, do you tease him?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Job 13:10">10</a> God will certainly correct you if you unfairly favor some people in secret.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Job 13:11">11</a> Won&#39;t his greatness scare you? And his fear come on you?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Job 13:12">12</a> Your memories are like ashes, your forms like clay.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Job 13:13">13</a> Stay quiet, leave me be, so I can talk, and whatever happens, let it happen.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Job 13:14">14</a> Why do I risk my life and take my own life into my hands?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Job 13:15">15</a> Even if he kills me, I will still trust in God; but I will defend my own actions to him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Job 13:16">16</a> He will also be my savior, because a hypocrite will not come before Him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Job 13:17">17</a> Listen carefully to what I say and hear my explanation with your ears.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Job 13:18">18</a> Look, I have prepared my case; I know I will be proven right.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Job 13:19">19</a> Who will argue with me? For if I stay silent now, I will die.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Job 13:20">20</a> Please don&#39;t do two things to me; then I won&#39;t hide from you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Job 13:21">21</a> Stop punishing me, and don&#39;t scare me anymore.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Job 13:22">22</a> Then you call, and I will answer; or let me talk, and you reply to me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Job 13:23">23</a> What are my wrongs and sins? Show me what I have done wrong and my sin.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Job 13:24">24</a> Why do you hide your face and treat me like your enemy?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Job 13:25">25</a> Will you break a leaf blown back and forth? And will you chase the dry straw?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Job 13:26">26</a> You write harsh things against me and make me inherit the sins of my youth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Job 13:27">27</a> You put my feet in chains and watch all my ways closely; you leave a mark on the soles of my feet.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Job 13:28">28</a> He decays like something rotten, like clothing eaten by moths.</p>
