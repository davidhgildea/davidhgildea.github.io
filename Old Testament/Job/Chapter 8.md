<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Job 8:1">1</a> Bildad the Shuhite replied, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Job 8:2">2</a> How long will you keep talking like this? How long will your words blow like a strong wind?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Job 8:3">3</a> Does God twist fairness? Or does the Almighty twist what&#39;s right?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Job 8:4">4</a> If your children sinned against God, and he has rejected them for their wrongdoing;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Job 8:5">5</a> If you seek God early and pray to the All-powerful;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Job 8:6">6</a> If you were pure and honest, surely now God would act for you and make your righteous home successful.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Job 8:7">7</a> Even if you started small, your future will grow a lot.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Job 8:8">8</a> Please ask about the past, and get ready to learn from your ancestors.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Job 8:9">9</a> We have only been here for a short time and know very little, because our lives on earth are like a passing shadow.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Job 8:10">10</a> Won&#39;t they teach you, talk to you, and speak heartfelt words to you?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Job 8:11">11</a> Can reeds grow without mud? Can plants grow without water?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Job 8:12">12</a> While it is still green and not cut, it dries up before any other plant.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Job 8:13">13</a> The ways of all who forget God lead to nothing, and the fake person&#39;s hope will vanish.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Job 8:14">14</a> Those whose hope will get cut off, and who rely on something as weak as a spider&#39;s web.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Job 8:15">15</a> He will rely on his house, but it won&#39;t last; he will hold it tightly, but it won&#39;t remain.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Job 8:16">16</a> He thrives in the sunlight, and his branches spread out in his garden.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Job 8:17">17</a> His roots twist around the pile of rocks and reach into the stony ground.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Job 8:18">18</a> If he is destroyed from his place, then it will reject him, saying, I have never seen you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Job 8:19">19</a> Look, this is the happiness of his path, and from the ground others will rise.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Job 8:20">20</a> Look, God will not reject a good person, nor will he support those who do wrong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Job 8:21">21</a> Until he makes your mouth laugh and your lips shout with joy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Job 8:22">22</a> Those who hate you will be covered with shame; the home of the evil will become nothing.</p>
