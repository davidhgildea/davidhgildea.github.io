<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Job 10:1">1</a> I am tired of my life; I will not hold back my complaints; I will speak from my heart&#39;s bitterness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Job 10:2">2</a> I will tell God, &quot;Do not judge me; tell me why you argue with me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Job 10:3">3</a> Is it right for you to be unfair, to reject what you have created, and to favor the plans of the evil?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Job 10:4">4</a> Do you have human eyes? Or do you see as a person sees?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Job 10:5">5</a> Are your days like a human&#39;s days? Are your years like a person&#39;s years?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Job 10:6">6</a> You ask about my wrongs and search for my sins?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Job 10:7">7</a> You know I am not wicked; no one can rescue from your hand.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Job 10:8">8</a> Your hands made and shaped me completely; but now you are destroying me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Job 10:9">9</a> Please remember you created me from clay; will you turn me back into dust?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Job 10:10">10</a> Did you not pour me out like milk and thicken me like cheese?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Job 10:11">11</a> You dressed me with skin and flesh, and built me with bones and muscles.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Job 10:12">12</a> You have given me life and kindness, and your care has kept my spirit safe.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Job 10:13">13</a> You have kept these things hidden in your heart; I know you understand this.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Job 10:14">14</a> If I do wrong, you notice it, and you will not clear me of my guilt.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Job 10:15">15</a> If I am evil, trouble is upon me; and if I am good, I still can&#39;t hold my head high. I am totally confused; so see my suffering.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Job 10:16">16</a> It grows. You chase me like a fierce lion, and then you do amazing things to me again.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Job 10:17">17</a> You create new evidence against me and grow angrier with me; changes and conflict oppose me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Job 10:18">18</a> Why did you bring me out of the womb? I wish I had died before anyone saw me!</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Job 10:19">19</a> I would have been like I never existed; I would have been taken from birth to the grave.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Job 10:20">20</a> Aren&#39;t my days short? Stop and leave me be, so I can have a little comfort.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Job 10:21">21</a> Before I go to a place I cannot come back from, to the land of darkness and death&#39;s shadow;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Job 10:22">22</a> A land of total darkness, like the deepest darkness; a place of death&#39;s shadow, without any order, where even the light is dark.</p>
