<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Job 15:1">1</a> Eliphaz from Teman replied and said.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Job 15:2">2</a> Should a wise person speak empty words and fill themselves with useless thoughts?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Job 15:3">3</a> Should he argue with useless words or speak where he can do no good?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Job 15:4">4</a> Yes, you throw away respect and stop praying to God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Job 15:5">5</a> Your mouth speaks your wrongs, and you talk like a tricky person.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Job 15:6">6</a> Your own words condemn you, not me; your own lips speak against you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Job 15:7">7</a> Are you the first man who was born? Or were you created before the hills?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Job 15:8">8</a> Have you heard God&#39;s secret? And do you keep wisdom to yourself?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Job 15:9">9</a> What do you know that we don&#39;t? What do you understand that we don&#39;t have in us?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Job 15:10">10</a> With us are old men with gray hair, much older than your father.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Job 15:11">11</a> Is God&#39;s comfort unimportant to you? Is there something you are hiding?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Job 15:12">12</a> Why does your heart lead you astray? And what are your eyes signaling?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Job 15:13">13</a> Are you turning against God and letting such words come out of your mouth?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Job 15:14">14</a> Who is a person, that they can be pure? And someone born of a woman, that they can be right?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Job 15:15">15</a> Look, God does not fully trust his holy ones; even the skies are not pure in his eyes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Job 15:16">16</a> How much more disgusting and dirty is a person, who drinks up wrong like water?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Job 15:17">17</a> I will show you, listen to me; and I will tell you what I have seen.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Job 15:18">18</a> Wise people shared wisdom from their ancestors and did not hide it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Job 15:19">19</a> God gave the earth to them alone, and no outsider went among them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Job 15:20">20</a> The bad person suffers through all his days, and the years he will live are unknown to the bully.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Job 15:21">21</a> He hears terrifying noises: even when he&#39;s doing well, destruction will hit him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Job 15:22">22</a> He doesn&#39;t believe he will escape from darkness, and he is expected to meet the sword.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Job 15:23">23</a> He roams around looking for bread, asking, &quot;Where is it?&quot; He knows that times of trouble are close by.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Job 15:24">24</a> Worry and distress will scare him; they will overcome him, like a king prepared for battle.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Job 15:25">25</a> He reaches out his hand against God, and makes himself strong against the Almighty.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Job 15:26">26</a> He charges at him, right at his neck, on the thick parts of his shields.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Job 15:27">27</a> Because he covers his face with his fat and gets layers of fat on his sides.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Job 15:28">28</a> He lives in empty cities, in houses where no one lives, that are about to turn into ruins.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Job 15:29">29</a> He will not become rich, his wealth will not last, nor will he keep its success on earth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Job 15:30">30</a> He will not escape the darkness; the fire will wither his branches, and he will be gone with a puff of breath.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Job 15:31">31</a> Don&#39;t let the person who is fooled trust in things that have no value, because they will only get emptiness in return.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Job 15:32">32</a> It will happen before its time, and its sprout will not be green.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Job 15:33">33</a> He will lose his young grapes like a vine, and drop his blossoms like an olive tree.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Job 15:34">34</a> The group of fakes will be left empty, and fire will burn down the tents of the corrupt.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Job 15:35">35</a> They plan trouble, give birth to empty promises, and their hearts breed lies.</p>
