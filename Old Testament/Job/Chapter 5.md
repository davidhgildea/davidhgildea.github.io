<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Job 5:1">1</a> Call now; see if anyone will answer you; to which holy person will you turn?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Job 5:2">2</a> Anger kills the foolish person, and jealousy kills the naive one.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Job 5:3">3</a> I saw the fool grow strong, but quickly I said his home would fail.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Job 5:4">4</a> His children are not safe, they are beaten down at the city entrance, and no one is there to save them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Job 5:5">5</a> The hungry eat what they harvest and take it from the thorns, while thieves consume their wealth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Job 5:6">6</a> Trouble doesn&#39;t come from the dust, nor does problem grow from the ground.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Job 5:7">7</a> A person is born to face trouble, just like sparks rise up.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Job 5:8">8</a> I would ask God for help, and to God, I would entrust my problems.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Job 5:9">9</a> God does great things beyond understanding and countless wonders.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Job 5:10">10</a> God gives rain on the earth and sends water to the fields.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Job 5:11">11</a> To lift up those who are down and to raise those who grieve to a place of safety.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Job 5:12">12</a> He ruins the plans of the clever, so they can&#39;t succeed in what they do.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Job 5:13">13</a> God catches the clever in their tricks, and the plans of the deceitful rush to an end.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Job 5:14">14</a> They face darkness during the day and fumble at noon as if it were night.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Job 5:15">15</a> God saves the poor from violence, from being spoken against, and from the power of the strong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Job 5:16">16</a> So the poor have hope, and wrongdoing is silenced.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Job 5:17">17</a> Look, the person whom God corrects is happy, so do not hate God&#39;s discipline.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Job 5:18">18</a> God causes pain and then heals; He injures, but His hands also heal.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Job 5:19">19</a> God will rescue you from six disasters; even in seven, no harm will come to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Job 5:20">20</a> In hunger, God will save you from death, and in battle, from the sword&#39;s force.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Job 5:21">21</a> You will be protected from harmful words, and you won&#39;t fear disaster when it arrives.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Job 5:22">22</a> When disaster and hunger come, you will laugh; you won&#39;t be scared of the earth&#39;s wild animals.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Job 5:23">23</a> You will have an agreement with the stones of the field: and the wild animals will be peaceful with you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Job 5:24">24</a> You will know that your home will be safe; you will check on your place and will not do wrong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Job 5:25">25</a> You will also know that your descendants will be many and your children will cover the earth like grass.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Job 5:26">26</a> You will reach old age and die, just like corn is harvested when it&#39;s ready.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Job 5:27">27</a> Look, we have checked this, so it is true; listen to it, and understand it for your benefit.</p>
