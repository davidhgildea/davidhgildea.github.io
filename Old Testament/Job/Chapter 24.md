<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Job 24:1">1</a> Why, if God knows all time, do those who know him not recognize his days?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Job 24:2">2</a> Some people move boundary stones; they forcefully steal herds of animals and eat from them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Job 24:3">3</a> They chase away the orphan&#39;s donkey and take the widow&#39;s ox as collateral.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Job 24:4">4</a> They push the needy off the road; the world&#39;s poor all hide away.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Job 24:5">5</a> Look, like wild donkeys in the desert, they go out to work; they get up early to find food: the wild land provides food for them and their children.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Job 24:6">6</a> Everyone harvests their grain in the field, and they collect the wicked&#39;s grapes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Job 24:7">7</a> They make the naked sleep without clothes, so they have no cover when it&#39;s cold.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Job 24:8">8</a> They are soaked by the mountain rains and hug the rocks because they have no shelter.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Job 24:9">9</a> They snatch away the orphans and demand collateral from the poor.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Job 24:10">10</a> They make him go without clothes and take the bundle of grain from the hungry.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Job 24:11">11</a> They make oil inside their cities, press their grapes for wine, and still feel thirsty.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Job 24:12">12</a> People groan in the city, and the hurt souls cry out; but God does not charge them with wrongdoing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Job 24:13">13</a> They are among those who resist the light; they do not know its ways or stay on its paths.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Job 24:14">14</a> The killer gets up at dawn to kill the poor and needy, and at night he acts like a thief.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Job 24:15">15</a> The one who is unfaithful with their partner waits for night, thinking no one will see them, and covers their face.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Job 24:16">16</a> At night they break into houses they had picked out in the day; they do not know the light.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Job 24:17">17</a> Morning feels like the shadow of death to them; when recognized, they are terrified by this shadow of death.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Job 24:18">18</a> He is fast like the rivers; their land is cursed on the earth: he does not see the path of the vineyards.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Job 24:19">19</a> Dryness and warmth dry up the snow&#39;s water; just as the grave takes those who have sinned.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Job 24:20">20</a> The body will forget him; worms will eat him up; he will not be remembered; and evil will be broken like a tree.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Job 24:21">21</a> He treats the childless woman badly and does not help the widow.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Job 24:22">22</a> He pulls in the strong with his power: he stands up, and nobody can be sure of living.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Job 24:23">23</a> Even if someone feels safe and relaxed, God still watches their actions.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Job 24:24">24</a> They are raised up for a short time, but then they vanish and are brought down; they are removed just like everyone else, and cut off like the tops of the stalks of grain.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Job 24:25">25</a> If this is not true now, who will prove me wrong and show that my words are worthless?</p>
