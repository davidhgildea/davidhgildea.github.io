<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Job 6:1">1</a> But Job replied and spoke,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Job 6:2">2</a> I wish my sorrow could be fully measured, and my troubles put on scales at the same time.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Job 6:3">3</a> It&#39;s heavier than sea sand now; that&#39;s why I can&#39;t express my words.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Job 6:4">4</a> God&#39;s arrows are inside me, their poison drains my spirit; God&#39;s terrors line up against me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Job 6:5">5</a> Does the wild donkey call out when it has grass? Or does the ox moo over its feed?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Job 6:6">6</a> Can you eat tasteless food without salt? Or is there any flavor in egg whites?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Job 6:7">7</a> The things I didn&#39;t want to touch now feel like my sad food.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Job 6:8">8</a> I wish I could get what I ask for, and that God would give me what I deeply desire.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Job 6:9">9</a> I wish God would decide to crush me, release his grip, and end my life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Job 6:10">10</a> Then I would still be comforted. Even though I suffer, I would be brave. Let God not hold back, because I have not hidden the words of God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Job 6:11">11</a> What strength do I have to hope? What is my future that I should extend my life?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Job 6:12">12</a> Am I as strong as rocks? Is my body made of bronze?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Job 6:13">13</a> Isn&#39;t my help within me? And has wisdom completely left me?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Job 6:14">14</a> A friend should be kind to someone who is suffering, but he ignores respecting God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Job 6:15">15</a> My brothers have been as unreliable as a stream, like the water in streams that disappears.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Job 6:16">16</a> They are dark because of the ice, and snow is hidden in them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Job 6:17">17</a> When they get warm, they disappear; when it&#39;s hot, they are gone from where they were.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Job 6:18">18</a> Their paths have changed direction; they lead to nowhere and end in destruction.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Job 6:19">19</a> The soldiers from Tema searched, and the groups from Sheba waited for them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Job 6:20">20</a> They were confused because they had hope; they went there and felt embarrassed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Job 6:21">21</a> You are now worth nothing; you see my downfall, and you are scared.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Job 6:22">22</a> Did I ask you to bring something to me or to give me a gift from what you own?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Job 6:23">23</a> Save me from my enemy’s grasp, or rescue me from the clutches of the powerful?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Job 6:24">24</a> Instruct me, and I will be quiet; show me where I have made mistakes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Job 6:25">25</a> How powerful are true words! But what does your arguing prove?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Job 6:26">26</a> Do you think you can correct mere words when someone in despair speaks as if they are just wind?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Job 6:27">27</a> Yes, you overpower the orphans and trap your friend.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Job 6:28">28</a> So now, be satisfied and look at me; it is clear to you if I am lying.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Job 6:29">29</a> Please come back, don&#39;t think it&#39;s wrong; yes, come back, my goodness is in it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Job 6:30">30</a> Is there wrongdoing in my words? Can&#39;t I tell what is wrong?</p>
