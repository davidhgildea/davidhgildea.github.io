<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Job 33:1">1</a> So Job, please listen to what I say and pay attention to all my words.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Job 33:2">2</a> Look, now I have opened my mouth and my tongue has spoken inside it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Job 33:3">3</a> I will speak what is true and my words will clearly show what I know.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Job 33:4">4</a> God&#39;s spirit made me, and the Almighty&#39;s breath gave me life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Job 33:5">5</a> If you can answer me, arrange your words clearly and stand up.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Job 33:6">6</a> Look, I am just as you want, in place of God: I am made from clay too.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Job 33:7">7</a> You don&#39;t need to fear my terror, nor will my hand be hard on you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Job 33:8">8</a> You have definitely spoken where I could hear, and I have heard what you said.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Job 33:9">9</a> I am clean and have not wronged anyone, I am innocent; I have done nothing wrong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Job 33:10">10</a> Look, He finds reasons to accuse me; He considers me His enemy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Job 33:11">11</a> He puts my feet in shackles and watches all the ways I walk.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Job 33:12">12</a> Look, you are not right in this: I will tell you that God is greater than a human.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Job 33:13">13</a> Why do you argue with God? He doesn&#39;t need to explain anything he does.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Job 33:14">14</a> God speaks once, even twice, but people do not notice.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Job 33:15">15</a> In a dream, during the night, when people are in deep sleep and lying in bed half-asleep;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Job 33:16">16</a> Then he opens people&#39;s ears and gives them understanding.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Job 33:17">17</a> To make a person change their plan and keep them from being proud.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Job 33:18">18</a> He protects his soul from the pit and his life from dying by the sword.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Job 33:19">19</a> He suffers with pain on his bed, and his bones ache greatly.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Job 33:20">20</a> So he hates bread, and he refuses good food.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Job 33:21">21</a> His body wastes away, hidden from sight; and his bones, once unseen, now stick out.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Job 33:22">22</a> Yes, his soul is close to death, and his life nears those who destroy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Job 33:23">23</a> If a messenger is with him, an interpreter, one in a thousand, to show a person how to be right:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Job 33:24">24</a> Then he is kind to him and says, Save him from falling into the pit: I have found a payment.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Job 33:25">25</a> His body will be more refreshed than a child&#39;s: he will become young again.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Job 33:26">26</a> He will pray to God, and God will accept him; he will see God&#39;s face with happiness because God will give man what is right.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Job 33:27">27</a> He looks at people, and if anyone says, &quot;I have sinned, I have twisted what is right, and it did not benefit me;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Job 33:28">28</a> He will save his soul from falling into the pit, and his life will see the light.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Job 33:29">29</a> Look, God often does all these things with humans.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Job 33:30">30</a> To save his life from the grave, to enjoy the light of life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Job 33:31">31</a> Pay attention, Job, listen to me: be quiet, and I will talk.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Job 33:32">32</a> If you have something to say, reply: speak, because I want to clear you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Job 33:33">33</a> If not, listen to me: be quiet, and I will teach you wisdom.</p>
