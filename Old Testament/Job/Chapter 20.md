<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Job 20:1">1</a> Zophar from Naamath spoke and said,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Job 20:2">2</a> So my thoughts make me reply quickly.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Job 20:3">3</a> I have heard the criticism against me, and my insight makes me respond.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Job 20:4">4</a> Don&#39;t you know this from long ago, since the time humans were put on earth?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Job 20:5">5</a> The success of the wicked is brief, and the happiness of the dishonest lasts only a moment.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Job 20:6">6</a> Even if he becomes very important and his pride reaches the sky,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Job 20:7">7</a> He will die forever like his waste; those who saw him will ask, &quot;Where is he?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Job 20:8">8</a> He will vanish like a dream and not be found; he will be driven away like a night vision.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Job 20:9">9</a> The eye that saw him will never see him again; his place will no longer see him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Job 20:10">10</a> His kids will try to help the poor, and his hands will give back their things.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Job 20:11">11</a> His bones carry the sins of his youth, which will go down with him to the dirt.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Job 20:12">12</a> Even if wrongdoing tastes sweet in his mouth and he hides it under his tongue;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Job 20:13">13</a> Even if he doesn&#39;t hurt it but leaves it in his mouth without letting it go:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Job 20:14">14</a> The food in his stomach has changed; it is like snake poison inside him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Job 20:15">15</a> He has consumed wealth, and he will spit it out again: God will force it from his stomach.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Job 20:16">16</a> He will suck the snake&#39;s poison: the viper&#39;s tongue will kill him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Job 20:17">17</a> He will not see the rivers, the floods, or the streams of honey and cream.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Job 20:18">18</a> He must give back what he worked for and cannot keep it; he will return an amount equal to his wealth, but he will not be happy about it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Job 20:19">19</a> Because he hurt and left the poor; because he took a house by force that he did not build.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Job 20:20">20</a> He will not find peace within, nor keep what he wanted.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Job 20:21">21</a> All his food will be gone; so no one will search for his possessions.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Job 20:22">22</a> When he feels most secure, he will be in trouble; every hand of the wicked will attack him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Job 20:23">23</a> When he&#39;s about to eat, God will throw his anger at him and make it fall on him as he eats.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Job 20:24">24</a> He will run from the iron weapon, and a steel bow will shoot him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Job 20:25">25</a> It is pulled out, and comes from the body; yes, the shiny sword comes from his liver: fears are on him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Job 20:26">26</a> Darkness will hide in his hidden places; a fire not fanned will eat him up; things will go badly for anyone remaining in his tent.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Job 20:27">27</a> The sky will show his wrongs; and the ground will stand against him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Job 20:28">28</a> His wealth will disappear, and his possessions will be lost when God is angry.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Job 20:29">29</a> This is what a bad person gets from God, the share given to him by God.</p>
