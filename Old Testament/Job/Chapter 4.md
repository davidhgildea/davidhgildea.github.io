<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Job 4:1">1</a> Then Eliphaz from Teman replied,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Job 4:2">2</a> If we try to talk with you, will you be upset? But who can stop themselves from speaking?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Job 4:3">3</a> Look, you have taught many people, and you have given strength to tired hands.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Job 4:4">4</a> Your words have supported the one who was falling, and you have made weak knees strong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Job 4:5">5</a> Now it has happened to you, and you are weak; it affects you, and you are upset.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Job 4:6">6</a> Isn&#39;t this what you respect, what you trust, what you hope for, and how right you act?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Job 4:7">7</a> Please remember, who has ever died being innocent? Or when have the good people been destroyed?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Job 4:8">8</a> I have seen that those who do bad things and spread evil will get the same in return.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Job 4:9">9</a> With God&#39;s powerful breath, they are destroyed, and by his breath, they vanish.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Job 4:10">10</a> The lion&#39;s roar, the fierce lion&#39;s voice, and the young lions&#39; teeth are all broken.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Job 4:11">11</a> The old lion dies because it has no food, and the strong lion&#39;s cubs are spread out far away.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Job 4:12">12</a> Someone secretly told me something, and I heard a bit of it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Job 4:13">13</a> In thoughts from night visions, when deep sleep falls on people,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Job 4:14">14</a> I felt afraid and shook all over, making my bones tremble.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Job 4:15">15</a> Suddenly, a spirit moved in front of me, and the hair on my body stood up.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Job 4:16">16</a> It stood still, but I couldn&#39;t see its shape clearly: a figure was in front of my eyes, everything was quiet, and I heard a voice speaking.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Job 4:17">17</a> Can a human be more fair than God? Can someone be purer than the one who created him?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Job 4:18">18</a> Look, he doesn&#39;t trust his servants, and he accuses his angels of errors.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Job 4:19">19</a> How much less can those living in clay houses trust in themselves, when their very foundation is dust and they can be destroyed as easily as a moth?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Job 4:20">20</a> They are ruined from dawn to dusk; they vanish forever without anyone caring.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Job 4:21">21</a> Doesn&#39;t their greatness leave them? They die without wisdom.</p>
