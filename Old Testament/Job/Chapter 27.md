<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Job 27:1">1</a> Job kept on speaking his story, and said,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Job 27:2">2</a> God lives, who has taken away my justice; and the Almighty, who has troubled my soul.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Job 27:3">3</a> As long as I breathe, and God&#39;s breath is in my nose;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Job 27:4">4</a> I will not speak evil, nor will my tongue tell lies.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Job 27:5">5</a> I would never agree with you; I will keep my honesty until I die.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Job 27:6">6</a> I keep to my righteousness and will not give it up; my heart will not blame me as long as I live.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Job 27:7">7</a> Let my enemy be like the wicked, and the one who attacks me be like the unjust.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Job 27:8">8</a> What hope does a fake person have, even if they are rich, when God takes their life?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Job 27:9">9</a> Will God listen to him when he calls for help during trouble?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Job 27:10">10</a> Does he enjoy the Almighty? Will he constantly ask for God&#39;s help?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Job 27:11">11</a> I will teach you by God&#39;s power; I won&#39;t hide what is with the Almighty.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Job 27:12">12</a> Look, you have all seen it yourselves; so why do you continue to act so foolishly?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Job 27:13">13</a> This is what a bad person gets from God, and what bullies will get from the All-Powerful.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Job 27:14">14</a> If he has many children, they are for death, and his descendants will not have enough food.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Job 27:15">15</a> The ones left of him will be buried when they die, and his widows won&#39;t cry.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Job 27:16">16</a> Even if he piles up silver like dust and stores up clothes like dirt,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Job 27:17">17</a> He might make it ready, but good people will wear it, and those who did nothing wrong will share the money.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Job 27:18">18</a> He builds his house like a moth, and like a temporary shelter put up by a caretaker.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Job 27:19">19</a> The rich man will lie down but won&#39;t be picked up again; he opens his eyes, and he&#39;s gone.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Job 27:20">20</a> Fears grip him like water, a storm sweeps him away at night.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Job 27:21">21</a> The east wind takes him away, and he goes; a storm throws him out of his place.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Job 27:22">22</a> God will throw down on him and will not hold back; he really wants to escape from his power.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Job 27:23">23</a> People will clap their hands at him and boo him from his spot.</p>
