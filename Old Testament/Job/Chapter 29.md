<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Job 29:1">1</a> Job kept on speaking in his story, and said,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Job 29:2">2</a> I wish I were back in the old days when God took care of me;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Job 29:3">3</a> When his light glowed above me, and I walked in the dark by his light;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Job 29:4">4</a> When I was young and God&#39;s friendship was with me in my home;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Job 29:5">5</a> When God was still with me and my children were around me;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Job 29:6">6</a> When I washed my feet with cream, and the stones gave me streams of oil;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Job 29:7">7</a> When I went through the city to the gate, when I set up my seat in the public square!</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Job 29:8">8</a> The young men saw me and hid, while the older men stood up in respect.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Job 29:9">9</a> The leaders stopped speaking and put their hand over their mouth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Job 29:10">10</a> The important people stayed quiet, and their tongue stuck to the top of their mouth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Job 29:11">11</a> When people heard me, they praised me; when they saw me, they approved of me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Job 29:12">12</a> I helped the poor who called out, the orphan, and the person who had no one to help them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Job 29:13">13</a> The blessing of someone close to death came to me; and I made the widow&#39;s heart joyful.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Job 29:14">14</a> I wore what was right, and it covered me: my fairness was like a robe and a crown.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Job 29:15">15</a> I was eyes for the blind and feet for the lame.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Job 29:16">16</a> I was like a father to the poor and I investigated issues that I did not know about.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Job 29:17">17</a> I broke the wicked&#39;s jaw and took away the stolen goods from his teeth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Job 29:18">18</a> Then I said, I will die peacefully at home, and live as long as the grains of sand.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Job 29:19">19</a> My roots stretched out to the water, and the dew stayed on my branches all night.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Job 29:20">20</a> My honor was strong within me, and my strength was restored in my grip.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Job 29:21">21</a> People listened to me, waited, and stayed quiet for my advice.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Job 29:22">22</a> After I spoke, they did not reply; my words fell on them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Job 29:23">23</a> They waited for me like for the rain; and they opened their mouths wide for the late rain.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Job 29:24">24</a> If I smiled at them, they did not trust it; they did not make my happy face sad.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Job 29:25">25</a> I picked their path, sat as the leader, and lived like a king among the troops, comforting those who grieve.</p>
