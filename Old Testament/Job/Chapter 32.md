<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Job 32:1">1</a> These three men stopped answering Job because he thought he was right.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Job 32:2">2</a> Elihu, Barachel&#39;s son from the Buzite family, became very angry with Job because Job made himself look right instead of God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Job 32:3">3</a> God was also angry with Job&#39;s three friends because they could not find an answer but still blamed Job.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Job 32:4">4</a> Elihu waited until Job finished talking because the others were older than him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Job 32:5">5</a> When Elihu saw that these three men had no answer, he became very angry.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Job 32:6">6</a> Elihu, Barachel&#39;s son from the Buz family, replied, &quot;I am young, and you are old. That&#39;s why I was scared and didn&#39;t share my thoughts with you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Job 32:7">7</a> I said, &quot;Time should teach, and many years should bring wisdom.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Job 32:8">8</a> Humans have a spirit, and God&#39;s breath gives them insight.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Job 32:9">9</a> Powerful people are not always smart; old people do not always make good decisions.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Job 32:10">10</a> So I said, listen to me; I will also share my thoughts.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Job 32:11">11</a> I listened carefully to what you said; I heard your arguments while you found the right words to speak.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Job 32:12">12</a> Yes, I listened to you, and there was none among you who proved Job wrong or responded to his words.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Job 32:13">13</a> So you might not claim, we have discovered wisdom: it is God who brings him down, not people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Job 32:14">14</a> He has not spoken against me, and I won&#39;t reply to him with your words.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Job 32:15">15</a> They were surprised and stopped answering; they stopped talking.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Job 32:16">16</a> I waited because they were quiet, stood still, and didn&#39;t speak anymore.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Job 32:17">17</a> I said, I will give my answer, I will also share my opinion.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Job 32:18">18</a> I have a lot to say, and the spirit inside me compels me to speak.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Job 32:19">19</a> See, my stomach is like unopened wine; it feels like it&#39;s going to burst like new wineskins.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Job 32:20">20</a> I will talk to feel better: I will open my mouth and reply.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Job 32:21">21</a> Please don&#39;t let me show favoritism to anyone or use flattering titles for people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Job 32:22">22</a> I don&#39;t use flattering titles; if I did, my Creator would quickly end my life.</p>
