<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Job 12:1">1</a> Job answered, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Job 12:2">2</a> You must be the people, and wisdom will die with you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Job 12:3">3</a> I understand as much as you do; I am not less than you. Indeed, who doesn&#39;t know things like these?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Job 12:4">4</a> I am like someone mocked by their neighbor, who calls on God, and he answers: the honest, good person is made fun of.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Job 12:5">5</a> Someone who is about to fall is like a lamp that someone comfortable looks down on.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Job 12:6">6</a> Thieves live comfortably, and those who challenge God feel safe; into their hands, God gives plenty.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Job 12:7">7</a> Ask the animals, and they will teach you; ask the birds of the sky, and they will tell you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Job 12:8">8</a> Talk to the earth, it will teach you; and the fish in the sea will tell you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Job 12:9">9</a> Everyone knows that God has done all this.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Job 12:10">10</a> God holds the life of every creature and the breath of all people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Job 12:11">11</a> Doesn&#39;t the ear test words? And the mouth taste its food?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Job 12:12">12</a> Old people have wisdom; with many years comes understanding.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Job 12:13">13</a> He has wisdom and power; he gives advice and understanding.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Job 12:14">14</a> Look, he destroys, and it cannot be rebuilt; he imprisons a person, and there can be no escape.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Job 12:15">15</a> Look, he stops the waters, and they dry up; he also sends them out, and they flood the land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Job 12:16">16</a> He has power and knowledge: both the tricked and the trickster belong to him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Job 12:17">17</a> He leads advisors away as captives and turns judges into fools.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Job 12:18">18</a> He frees kings from their chains and ties a belt around their waists.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Job 12:19">19</a> He leads rulers away defeated, and overthrows the powerful.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Job 12:20">20</a> God takes away the speech of the reliable and removes the wisdom of the old.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Job 12:21">21</a> God shows scorn for rulers and reduces the power of the strong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Job 12:22">22</a> He reveals deep truths from darkness and brings the shadow of death into the light.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Job 12:23">23</a> He makes nations grow, and then destroys them; he expands nations, and then reduces them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Job 12:24">24</a> God removes the understanding of the world&#39;s leaders and makes them wander in a place without paths.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Job 12:25">25</a> They feel around in the dark with no light, and he causes them to stumble like a drunk person.</p>
