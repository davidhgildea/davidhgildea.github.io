<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Job 23:1">1</a> Then Job replied,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Job 23:2">2</a> Today my complaint is harsh; my suffering is worse than my groans.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Job 23:3">3</a> I wish I knew where to find God so I could go to where he is.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Job 23:4">4</a> I would explain my case to God and give many reasons to support it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Job 23:5">5</a> I want to know the words he would reply with, and understand what he would tell me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Job 23:6">6</a> Would he argue with me using his great power? No; instead, he would give me strength.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Job 23:7">7</a> There, good people could argue with God; so I would be saved forever from my judge.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Job 23:8">8</a> I look ahead, but he&#39;s not there; I look behind, but I can&#39;t see him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Job 23:9">9</a> On the left side, where he works, I cannot see him; he hides on the right side so I cannot find him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Job 23:10">10</a> God knows the path I follow; when he has tested me, I will come out like gold.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Job 23:11">11</a> I have followed his path without turning away.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Job 23:12">12</a> I have not turned away from what God commands; I value his words more than my daily bread.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Job 23:13">13</a> He has one plan, and no one can change it. Whatever he wants to do, he does.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Job 23:14">14</a> He does what he has planned for me, and he has many more things like this.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Job 23:15">15</a> So, when I feel God&#39;s presence, I get disturbed; when I think about it, I become scared of Him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Job 23:16">16</a> God makes my heart gentle, and the Almighty disturbs me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Job 23:17">17</a> Because I was not taken away before the darkness, nor has he hidden the darkness from my face.</p>
