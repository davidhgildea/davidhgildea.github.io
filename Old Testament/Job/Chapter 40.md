<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Job 40:1">1</a> God replied to Job, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Job 40:2">2</a> Can a person argue with God and teach Him? Anyone who corrects God should reply.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Job 40:3">3</a> Job replied to God and said,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Job 40:4">4</a> I realize I am worthless; what can I say to you? I will put my hand over my mouth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Job 40:5">5</a> I have spoken once; I won&#39;t reply again; even twice; but I&#39;ll say no more.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Job 40:6">6</a> God replied to Job from the storm and said,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Job 40:7">7</a> Prepare yourself like a man; I will question you, and you must answer me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Job 40:8">8</a> Will you cancel my decision? Will you say I am wrong so you can say you are right?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Job 40:9">9</a> Do you have an arm like God? Or can you thunder with a voice like him?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Job 40:10">10</a> Dress yourself now with greatness and honor; and put on glory and beauty.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Job 40:11">11</a> Throw out your anger and look at all the proud people; bring them down.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Job 40:12">12</a> Pay attention to everyone who is proud and make them humble; step on the evil ones where they stand.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Job 40:13">13</a> Put them all in the dust; cover their faces in darkness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Job 40:14">14</a> Then I will agree that your own power can save you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Job 40:15">15</a> Look at the behemoth, which I created with you; it eats grass like an ox.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Job 40:16">16</a> Look, his power is in his hips, and his strength is in his belly muscles.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Job 40:17">17</a> He swings his tail like a cedar tree; the muscles of his thighs are tightly woven.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Job 40:18">18</a> His bones are as hard as pieces of brass; his bones are like rods of iron.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Job 40:19">19</a> He is the greatest of God&#39;s creations; only his maker can threaten him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Job 40:20">20</a> The mountains give him food, and all the wild animals play there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Job 40:21">21</a> He lies under the shade trees, in the hiding place of the reeds and marshes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Job 40:22">22</a> The trees cast their shade over him; the willow trees by the stream surround him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Job 40:23">23</a> Look, he drinks up a river without rushing; he believes he can take the Jordan River into his mouth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Job 40:24">24</a> He sees it with his eyes; his nose detects traps.</p>
