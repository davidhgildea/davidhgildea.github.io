<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Job 7:1">1</a> Doesn&#39;t everyone have a set time to live on earth? Aren&#39;t their days like those of a worker?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Job 7:2">2</a> Like a worker who really wants rest, and like an employee eager for their pay.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Job 7:3">3</a> I am given months of emptiness and nights of trouble are set for me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Job 7:4">4</a> When I go to bed, I think, &quot;When will I get up and the night be over?&quot; I am restless until morning comes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Job 7:5">5</a> My body is covered with worms and dirt clumps; my skin is cracked and disgusting.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Job 7:6">6</a> My days go by faster than a weaver&#39;s tool, and they end without hope.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Job 7:7">7</a> Please remember my life is like the wind: I will not see happiness again.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Job 7:8">8</a> The person who has seen me won&#39;t see me again: you watch me, but I&#39;m gone.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Job 7:9">9</a> Just like a cloud disappears and is gone, so is the person who dies and will not rise again.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Job 7:10">10</a> He will not go back to his home, and his place will not recognize him anymore.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Job 7:11">11</a> So I won&#39;t hold back my words; I will speak because my spirit is in pain; I will express my soul&#39;s deep sorrow.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Job 7:12">12</a> Am I the sea, or a big fish, that you keep watch over me?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Job 7:13">13</a> When I say, &quot;My bed will comfort me, my sofa will soothe my worries,&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Job 7:14">14</a> You frighten me with dreams and scare me with visions.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Job 7:15">15</a> So my soul prefers suffocating, and death over my life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Job 7:16">16</a> I hate it; I don&#39;t want to live forever: leave me alone; my days are pointless.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Job 7:17">17</a> Who is a person that you make so important and think so much about them?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Job 7:18">18</a> You visit him every morning and test him every moment?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Job 7:19">19</a> How long will you not leave me, or give me a moment&#39;s peace?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Job 7:20">20</a> I have sinned; what should I do to you, God, protector of people? Why have you made me your target, so that I am a problem to myself?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Job 7:21">21</a> Why don&#39;t you forgive my wrong and remove my sin? For soon I will lie in the earth; you will look for me in the morning, but I won&#39;t be there.</p>
