<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Job 28:1">1</a> Surely there is a source for silver and a place where gold is refined.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Job 28:2">2</a> Iron comes from the ground, and copper is melted from the rock.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Job 28:3">3</a> He ends the darkness and explores every detail: the dark rocks and the shadow of death.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Job 28:4">4</a> The flood bursts out from where people live; the waters, once stepped in and forgotten, have dried up and disappeared from human presence.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Job 28:5">5</a> The earth gives us bread, and beneath it is heat like fire.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Job 28:6">6</a> Its rocks are where sapphires are found, and it has particles of gold.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Job 28:7">7</a> There is a path unknown to birds, and unseen by the vulture&#39;s eye.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Job 28:8">8</a> Young lions have not walked on it, nor has the strong lion crossed over it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Job 28:9">9</a> He reaches out his hand to the rock; he flips the mountains from their base.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Job 28:10">10</a> He carves out rivers in the rocks, and his eye sees every valuable thing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Job 28:11">11</a> He stops the rivers from flooding; and he brings hidden things into the light.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Job 28:12">12</a> Where can wisdom be found? And where is the place of understanding?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Job 28:13">13</a> No one knows its value; it cannot be found in the land of the living.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Job 28:14">14</a> The deep says, &#39;It&#39;s not in me,&#39; and the sea says, &#39;It&#39;s not with me.&#39;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Job 28:15">15</a> It can&#39;t be bought with gold, nor can you weigh silver to pay for it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Job 28:16">16</a> It can&#39;t be compared to Ophir&#39;s gold, valuable onyx, or sapphires.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Job 28:17">17</a> Gold and crystal are not its equal: you cannot swap it for any gold jewelry.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Job 28:18">18</a> People will not talk about coral or pearls; wisdom is worth more than rubies.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Job 28:19">19</a> Ethiopia&#39;s topaz won&#39;t match it, nor can it be compared to fine gold.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Job 28:20">20</a> Where does wisdom come from? And where can we find understanding?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Job 28:21">21</a> It is hidden from all living creatures and kept secret from the birds in the sky.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Job 28:22">22</a> Destruction and death say, &quot;We have heard of its fame with our ears.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Job 28:23">23</a> God understands its way and knows its place.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Job 28:24">24</a> He looks to the earth&#39;s ends and sees everything under the sky.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Job 28:25">25</a> To set the weight for the winds; and he measures the waters by amount.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Job 28:26">26</a> When he set rules for the rain and a path for the thunder&#39;s lightning:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Job 28:27">27</a> He saw it, explained it, got it ready, and thoroughly examined it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Job 28:28">28</a> To people he said, &quot;Look, respecting God is wisdom, and avoiding evil is understanding.&quot;</p>
