<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Job 41:1">1</a> Can you catch Leviathan with a fishhook or tie down its tongue with a rope?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Job 41:2">2</a> Can you put a hook in his nose or pierce his jaw with a spike?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Job 41:3">3</a> Will he beg you often? Will he speak gently to you?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Job 41:4">4</a> Will he agree with you? Will you make him your servant forever?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Job 41:5">5</a> Will you play with it like a bird? Or will you tie it up for your girls?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Job 41:6">6</a> Will friends feast on him? Will traders divide him up?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Job 41:7">7</a> Can you cover his skin with harpoons or his head with fishing spears?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Job 41:8">8</a> Touch him and remember the fight, do it no more.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Job 41:9">9</a> Look, his hope is pointless: won&#39;t someone be terrified just by seeing him?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Job 41:10">10</a> No one is brave enough to disturb him; who then can face me?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Job 41:11">11</a> Who has given to me first, so I need to pay him back? Everything under the whole sky is mine.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Job 41:12">12</a> I won&#39;t hide his body, his strength, or his good shape.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Job 41:13">13</a> Who can reach the edge of his clothing? Or who can approach him with a double bit bridle?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Job 41:14">14</a> Who can open the doors of his face? His teeth are terrifying all around.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Job 41:15">15</a> His scales are what he is proud of, tight and sealed closely.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Job 41:16">16</a> They are so close together that no air can fit between them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Job 41:17">17</a> They are connected to each other, they cling tightly, so they cannot be separated.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Job 41:18">18</a> When he sneezes, light shines, and his eyes are like the dawn&#39;s first light.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Job 41:19">19</a> From his mouth come shining lights, and sparks of fire jump out.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Job 41:20">20</a> Smoke comes out of his nose like from a boiling pot or kettle.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Job 41:21">21</a> His breath lights up coals, and flames come out of his mouth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Job 41:22">22</a> Strength stays in his neck, and sadness turns to joy in his presence.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Job 41:23">23</a> His skin is thick and tight; it is solid and does not move.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Job 41:24">24</a> His heart is as strong as a rock, yes, as hard as a lower grinding stone.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Job 41:25">25</a> When he stands up, the strong are scared: because of the terror, they try to make themselves clean.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Job 41:26">26</a> The warrior&#39;s sword won&#39;t succeed: neither the spear, the arrow, nor the armor.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Job 41:27">27</a> He thinks iron is like straw and brass is like rotten wood.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Job 41:28">28</a> Arrows cannot scare him; to him, slingshot stones turn to chaff.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Job 41:29">29</a> Arrows seem like straw to him; he laughs at the shaking of a spear.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Job 41:30">30</a> He lies on sharp rocks; he scatters spiky things on the mud.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Job 41:31">31</a> He makes the depths churn like a boiling pot; he makes the sea stir like a pot of ointment.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Job 41:32">32</a> He leaves a shining trail behind him; it seems like the depths have gray hair.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Job 41:33">33</a> On earth, there is no one like him; he is made without fear.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Job 41:34">34</a> He sees everything that is high up; he is a king over all the proud people.</p>
