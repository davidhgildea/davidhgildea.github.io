<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Job 2:1">1</a> Once more, a time came when God&#39;s children appeared before God, and Satan also arrived with them to present himself before God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Job 2:2">2</a> God asked Satan, &quot;Where did you come from?&quot; Satan replied to God, &quot;I&#39;ve been roaming around the earth and walking back and forth on it.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Job 2:3">3</a> God said to Satan, &quot;Have you noticed my servant Job? There&#39;s no one like him on earth. He&#39;s a perfect and honest man who respects God and stays away from evil. Yet he keeps his goodness, even though you convinced me to harm him for no reason.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Job 2:4">4</a> And Satan replied to God, &quot;Surely one will give everything they have to save their own life.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Job 2:5">5</a> Reach out now and hurt his bones and flesh, and he will surely curse you to your face.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Job 2:6">6</a> God said to Satan, &quot;Look, he is in your power, but spare his life.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Job 2:7">7</a> Satan left from being with God and gave Job painful sores from the bottom of his feet to the top of his head.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Job 2:8">8</a> He took a piece of broken pottery to scratch himself with and sat down in the ashes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Job 2:9">9</a> His wife said to him, &quot;Are you still holding on to your goodness? Curse God and die.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Job 2:10">10</a> He told her, &quot;You talk like a foolish woman. Should we accept only good from God and not trouble?&quot; Despite everything, Job did not say anything wrong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Job 2:11">11</a> When Job&#39;s three friends learned about all the bad things that had happened to him, each came from his own home: Eliphaz from Teman, Bildad from Shuah, and Zophar from Naamah. They had agreed to meet and go to sympathize with him and to comfort him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Job 2:12">12</a> When they looked up and didn&#39;t recognize him from far away, they cried out loud, tore their clothes, and threw dust on their heads, looking up to the sky.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Job 2:13">13</a> They sat with him on the ground for seven days and nights without speaking because they saw his sorrow was very deep.</p>
