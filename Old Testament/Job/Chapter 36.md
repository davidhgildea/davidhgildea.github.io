<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Job 36:1">1</a> Elihu continued and spoke,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Job 36:2">2</a> Let me speak a bit more, for I still have things to say about God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Job 36:3">3</a> I will bring my knowledge from far away and give credit for being right to my Creator.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Job 36:4">4</a> I speak the truth, and someone with perfect understanding is with you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Job 36:5">5</a> Look, God is powerful and does not reject anyone; He is strong and wise.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Job 36:6">6</a> He does not keep the wicked alive but gives justice to the poor.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Job 36:7">7</a> God never stops watching the good people; he sets them to rule with kings on thrones, making their power secure forever, and they are lifted high.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Job 36:8">8</a> If they are tied up in chains and caught in ropes of trouble;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Job 36:9">9</a> Then he shows them what they have done and the wrongs they have committed by going too far.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Job 36:10">10</a> God opens their ears to instruction and orders them to turn away from wrongdoing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Job 36:11">11</a> If they listen and work for God, they will live with success and enjoy their years.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Job 36:12">12</a> If they don&#39;t obey, they will die by the sword and will die without understanding.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Job 36:13">13</a> The insincere in heart store up anger; they don&#39;t cry out when he [God] restrains them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Job 36:14">14</a> They die young, and their life ends among the impure.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Job 36:15">15</a> He saves the needy in their trouble and listens to them when they are oppressed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Job 36:16">16</a> God would have taken you from a tight place to a spacious place with no tightness; your table would have been full of richness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Job 36:17">17</a> But you have carried out the punishment of the wicked: punishment and fairness catch up with you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Job 36:18">18</a> Be careful, because if God gets angry, he might punish you, and no large payment can save you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Job 36:19">19</a> Will he value your wealth? No, not gold or any strong army.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Job 36:20">20</a> Do not wish for the night when people are taken away from where they are.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Job 36:21">21</a> Be careful, don&#39;t accept wrongdoing because you prefer it over suffering.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Job 36:22">22</a> Look, God lifts up with His power: who can teach like Him?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Job 36:23">23</a> Who has directed his path? Or who can say, &quot;You have done wrong&quot;?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Job 36:24">24</a> Remember to praise God&#39;s work that people see.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Job 36:25">25</a> Everyone can see it; people can spot it from far away.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Job 36:26">26</a> Look, God is great, and we do not know him, and no one can understand how long he has existed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Job 36:27">27</a> He turns water into tiny drops; they fall as rain matching the mist.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Job 36:28">28</a> The clouds pour down and sprinkle plenty of water on people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Job 36:29">29</a> Can anyone understand how the clouds spread out, or the sounds from his home in the sky?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Job 36:30">30</a> Look, he spreads his light over it and covers the sea&#39;s depths.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Job 36:31">31</a> God judges the people by these things; he provides plenty of food.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Job 36:32">32</a> He covers the sun with clouds and orders them to block the light.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Job 36:33">33</a> The sound of it tells about it, the livestock also about the mist.</p>
