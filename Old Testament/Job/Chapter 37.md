<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Job 37:1">1</a> My heart shakes with fear and moves out of its place.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Job 37:2">2</a> Listen carefully to his voice and the noise that comes from his mouth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Job 37:3">3</a> He guides it under the whole sky, and his lightning to the ends of the earth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Job 37:4">4</a> After it, a voice roars: God thunders with his majestic voice, and does not hold back when his voice is heard.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Job 37:5">5</a> God thunders wonderfully with his voice; he does great things we cannot understand.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Job 37:6">6</a> He tells the snow to fall on the earth, and the same to the light rain and the heavy rain of his power.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Job 37:7">7</a> He closes the hands of every person so everyone can know His deeds.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Job 37:8">8</a> Then the animals go into their dens and stay there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Job 37:9">9</a> From the south comes a strong wind, and from the north comes cold.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Job 37:10">10</a> God&#39;s breath brings frost, and the surface of the waters becomes tight.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Job 37:11">11</a> God tires out the heavy cloud by adding water to it; he spreads his light cloud around.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Job 37:12">12</a> It moves as God plans, so they can do everything he tells them on the earth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Job 37:13">13</a> He makes it happen, whether for discipline, for his land, or for kindness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Job 37:14">14</a> Listen to this, Job: be still and think about God&#39;s amazing deeds.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Job 37:15">15</a> Do you know when God arranged them and made the light of his cloud shine?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Job 37:16">16</a> Do you know how the clouds stay balanced, the amazing things done by the one who has perfect knowledge?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Job 37:17">17</a> Your clothes become warm when God calms the earth with the south wind.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Job 37:18">18</a> Have you helped God spread out the sky, which is solid, like a mirror made of melted metal?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Job 37:19">19</a> Show us what to say to God; we don&#39;t know how to speak properly because we are confused.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Job 37:20">20</a> Should I tell God what I say? If someone speaks, they will certainly be overwhelmed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Job 37:21">21</a> People cannot see the bright light in the clouds, but the wind comes and clears them away.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Job 37:22">22</a> Clear skies come from the north; with God is awesome greatness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Job 37:23">23</a> About God, we cannot fully understand him: he is great in power, fair in judgment, and full of justice: he does not cause suffering.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Job 37:24">24</a> People fear God because he doesn&#39;t favor those who think they are wise.</p>
