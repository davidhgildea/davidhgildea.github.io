<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Job 34:1">1</a> Elihu replied and said,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Job 34:2">2</a> Listen to me, you wise people; pay attention, you who have understanding.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Job 34:3">3</a> The ear tests words like the mouth tastes food.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Job 34:4">4</a> Let&#39;s decide what is fair; let&#39;s understand together what is good.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Job 34:5">5</a> Job has said, &quot;I am righteous,&quot; but God has taken away my right to a fair judgment.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Job 34:6">6</a> Should I lie against what is right? My injury is incurable, though I did no wrong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Job 34:7">7</a> Who is like Job, who drinks up mockery as easily as water?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Job 34:8">8</a> Who spends time with those who do wrong and walks with evil people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Job 34:9">9</a> He has said, &quot;It benefits a person nothing to enjoy being with God.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Job 34:10">10</a> Listen to me, wise men: God would never do evil, and the All-Powerful would never act unjustly.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Job 34:11">11</a> God will repay a person for what they have done and give each one what their actions deserve.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Job 34:12">12</a> Yes, surely God will not act wrongly, nor will the Almighty twist justice.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Job 34:13">13</a> Who has put him in charge of the earth? Or who has arranged the whole world?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Job 34:14">14</a> If God decides to focus on a person, if he takes back that person&#39;s life and breath;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Job 34:15">15</a> Everyone will die at the same time, and humans will become dust again.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Job 34:16">16</a> If you understand now, listen to this: pay attention to what I am saying.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Job 34:17">17</a> Can someone who hates justice rule? Will you condemn the one who is completely fair?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Job 34:18">18</a> Is it right to tell a king, &quot;You are bad&quot; or to rulers, &quot;You are evil&quot;?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Job 34:19">19</a> How much less to someone who doesn&#39;t favor leaders or value the rich over the poor? For they are all created by God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Job 34:20">20</a> Suddenly they will die, the people will panic at midnight and vanish; and the powerful will be removed effortlessly.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Job 34:21">21</a> God watches over people&#39;s paths and sees everything they do.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Job 34:22">22</a> There is no darkness or shadow of death where the wrongdoers can hide.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Job 34:23">23</a> God will not give a person more than is fair, so that they would have to argue with God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Job 34:24">24</a> He will shatter countless strong men and replace them with others.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Job 34:25">25</a> So he knows what they do, and he turns things upside down at night, destroying them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Job 34:26">26</a> He strikes them as bad people for all to see.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Job 34:27">27</a> They turned away from God and did not think about any of His ways.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Job 34:28">28</a> So they make the poor&#39;s cries reach God, and he hears the cries of those who are suffering.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Job 34:29">29</a> When God brings peace, who can cause trouble? And when God hides, who can see him? It doesn&#39;t matter if it&#39;s a whole country or just one person.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Job 34:30">30</a> Let not the false person rule, so the people won&#39;t be trapped.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Job 34:31">31</a> It is right to tell God, &quot;I have accepted punishment, I will not sin again.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Job 34:32">32</a> Teach me what I do not see; if I have done wrong, I will not do it again.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Job 34:33">33</a> Should it go as you want? God will repay it, whether you refuse or choose; not me. So say what you know.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Job 34:34">34</a> Let people with understanding tell me, and let a wise person listen to me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Job 34:35">35</a> Job spoke without understanding, and his words lacked wisdom.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Job 34:36">36</a> I want Job to be tested to the limit because of how he answers for evil people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Job 34:37">37</a> He makes his sin worse by rebelling, applauds himself in our presence, and speaks many words against God.</p>
