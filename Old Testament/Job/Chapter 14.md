<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Job 14:1">1</a> A person born from a woman lives only a short time and has many problems.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Job 14:2">2</a> He blooms like a flower and then is trimmed. He also flees like a shadow and does not stay.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Job 14:3">3</a> Do you watch such a person and bring me to trial with you?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Job 14:4">4</a> Who can make a clean thing from an unclean one? No one.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Job 14:5">5</a> God has set the number of his days, and knows how many months he will live. You have decided his limits, which he cannot go beyond.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Job 14:6">6</a> Let him alone so he can have peace until he finishes his workday like a worker.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Job 14:7">7</a> A tree has hope because if it&#39;s cut down, it can grow back, and its new shoots won&#39;t stop.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Job 14:8">8</a> Even if its roots grow old in the earth and its stump dies in the ground;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Job 14:9">9</a> Even if it smells water, it will sprout and grow branches like a plant.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Job 14:10">10</a> When a person dies, they decay; when they stop breathing, where do they go?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Job 14:11">11</a> As the water disappears from the sea, and a riverbed becomes dry and empty:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Job 14:12">12</a> People lie down and do not rise again; until the sky is gone, they will not wake up or be woken from their sleep.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Job 14:13">13</a> I wish you would hide me in the grave and keep me hidden until your anger is over, then set a time to remember me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Job 14:14">14</a> If someone dies, will they live again? I will wait for all the days set for me until it is my turn to change.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Job 14:15">15</a> You will call, and I will answer you; you will long for what your hands have made.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Job 14:16">16</a> You count my steps now; are you not keeping watch on my sin?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Job 14:17">17</a> My wrong is locked in a bag, and you stitch up my sin.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Job 14:18">18</a> Mountains crumble and disappear, and rocks are moved from their spots.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Job 14:19">19</a> Water wears down stones; you wash away the soil&#39;s growth; you crush human hopes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Job 14:20">20</a> You always win against him, and he goes away; you change his face, and send him off.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Job 14:21">21</a> His sons achieve honor but he doesn&#39;t know it; they are humbled, yet he doesn&#39;t notice.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Job 14:22">22</a> His body will feel pain, and his heart will be sad.</p>
