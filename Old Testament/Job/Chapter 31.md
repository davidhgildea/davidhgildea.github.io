<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Job 31:1">1</a> I promised myself not to look with desire; so why should I think about a young woman?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Job 31:2">2</a> What does a person get from God above? And what does the Almighty give from high up?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Job 31:3">3</a> Isn&#39;t destruction meant for the wicked, and an unusual punishment for those who do wrong?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Job 31:4">4</a> Doesn&#39;t God see how I live and count every step I take?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Job 31:5">5</a> If I have lived with falsehood or hurried to deceive;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Job 31:6">6</a> Let me be measured in a fair scale so God can see my honesty.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Job 31:7">7</a> If I have gone off the path, and my heart followed what I saw, and if any stain has stuck to my hands;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Job 31:8">8</a> Let me plant seeds, but let someone else eat the harvest; yes, let my descendants be destroyed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Job 31:9">9</a> If my heart was misled by a woman, or if I waited secretly at my neighbor&#39;s door;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Job 31:10">10</a> Let my wife work for another man, and let other men be with her.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Job 31:11">11</a> This is a terrible crime, yes, it&#39;s a wrongdoing that the judges must punish.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Job 31:12">12</a> It is a fire that burns until everything is destroyed and would remove all my growth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Job 31:13">13</a> If I ignored my male or female servant&#39;s complaint against me;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Job 31:14">14</a> What should I do when God stands up? And when He comes to check, what will I say to Him?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Job 31:15">15</a> Didn&#39;t the same God who created me in the womb also create him? And didn&#39;t one shape both of us inside the womb?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Job 31:16">16</a> If I have denied the poor what they wanted, or made the widow&#39;s eyes weary;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Job 31:17">17</a> I did not eat my food alone and ignored the orphans; they ate with me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Job 31:18">18</a> Since I was young, he grew up with me like a father, and I&#39;ve guided her since I was in my mother&#39;s womb.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Job 31:19">19</a> If I have seen anyone die because they had no clothes, or any poor person without a blanket;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Job 31:20">20</a> If he didn&#39;t thank me or wasn&#39;t warmed by my sheep&#39;s wool;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Job 31:21">21</a> If I raised my hand against an orphan when I saw my support at the city gate:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Job 31:22">22</a> Let my arm drop off from my shoulder, and my arm be broken from its bone.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Job 31:23">23</a> I was scared of being destroyed by God, and because he is so great, I couldn&#39;t bear it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Job 31:24">24</a> If I have put my hope in gold or told pure gold, &#39;You are my trust;&#39;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Job 31:25">25</a> If I was happy because I was very rich and my own efforts brought me a lot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Job 31:26">26</a> If I looked at the sun shining or the moon moving brightly;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Job 31:27">27</a> My heart was secretly tempted, or my mouth kissed my hand.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Job 31:28">28</a> This would also be a sin that a judge must punish, because I would have denied God above.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Job 31:29">29</a> If I was happy when my enemy was destroyed, or felt proud when bad things happened to him:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Job 31:30">30</a> I have not let my mouth sin by cursing his life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Job 31:31">31</a> If the people in my home never said, &quot;We wish we had his meat!&quot; they are not content.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Job 31:32">32</a> The traveler did not sleep in the street; I opened my doors to them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Job 31:33">33</a> If I hid my wrongs like Adam, keeping my sins close to me:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Job 31:34">34</a> Was I scared of many people, or did the scorn of families frighten me, so that I stayed quiet and didn&#39;t go outside?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Job 31:35">35</a> I wish someone would listen to me! Look, what I want is for God to respond to me, and for my opponent to have written down his accusations.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Job 31:36">36</a> I would surely carry it on my shoulder and wear it like a crown.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Job 31:37">37</a> I would tell him how many steps I have taken; like a leader, I would approach him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Job 31:38">38</a> If my land cries out against me or its furrows also speak out;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="Job 31:39">39</a> If I have eaten its fruits without paying, or if I have caused the owners to lose their life:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="Job 31:40">40</a> Let weeds grow instead of wheat, and useless plants instead of barley. Job has finished speaking.</p>
