<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Job 1:1">1</a> In the land of Uz, there lived a man named Job. He was blameless and honest, feared God, and avoided evil.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Job 1:2">2</a> He had seven sons and three daughters.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Job 1:3">3</a> He also had seven thousand sheep, three thousand camels, five hundred pairs of oxen, five hundred female donkeys, and a huge family. So he was the richest man in the east.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Job 1:4">4</a> His sons had parties in their own homes, each on his special day; and they invited their three sisters to eat and drink with them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Job 1:5">5</a> When the feasting days were over, Job would make his children holy, get up early, and give burnt offerings for each one. He thought, &quot;Maybe my sons have done wrong and thought bad things about God.&quot; Job did this all the time.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Job 1:6">6</a> One day, the children of God gathered in front of God, and Satan joined them too.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Job 1:7">7</a> God asked Satan, &quot;Where do you come from?&quot; Satan answered God, &quot;I have been roaming around the earth, going back and forth on it.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Job 1:8">8</a> God said to Satan, &quot;Have you noticed my servant Job? There is no one like him on earth, a perfect and honest man who respects God and avoids evil.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Job 1:9">9</a> Then Satan replied to God, &quot;Does Job respect God for nothing?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Job 1:10">10</a> Haven&#39;t you protected him, his house, and everything he has on all sides? You have blessed what he does, and his wealth has grown in the land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Job 1:11">11</a> Reach out now and strike everything he owns, and he will surely insult you to your face.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Job 1:12">12</a> God said to Satan, &quot;Look, all he has is under your control; but do not hurt him.&quot; So Satan left God&#39;s presence.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Job 1:13">13</a> One day his sons and daughters were eating and drinking wine in the oldest brother&#39;s house.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Job 1:14">14</a> A messenger came to Job and said, &quot;The oxen were plowing and the donkeys were eating nearby.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Job 1:15">15</a> The Sabeans attacked and captured them; they killed the workers with swords; I alone escaped to tell you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Job 1:16">16</a> While he was still talking, another person came and said, &quot;Fire from God fell from the sky and burned up the sheep and the workers, and I&#39;m the only one who got away to tell you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Job 1:17">17</a> While he was still talking, another person came and said, &quot;The Chaldeans formed three groups, attacked the camels, took them, and also killed the workers with swords; only I have escaped to tell you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Job 1:18">18</a> While he was still talking, another one came and said, &quot;Your sons and daughters were eating and drinking wine in their oldest brother&#39;s house.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Job 1:19">19</a> Suddenly, a strong wind from the desert struck all four sides of the house, and it collapsed on the young men, and they died; I am the only one who got away to tell you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Job 1:20">20</a> Then Job stood up, tore his robe, shaved his head, fell to the ground, and worshiped.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Job 1:21">21</a> I was born with nothing, and I will die with nothing: God gave, and God has taken away; praise God&#39;s name.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Job 1:22">22</a> In all this, Job did not sin or blame God wrongly.</p>
