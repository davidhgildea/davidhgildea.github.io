<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Job 3:1">1</a> After this, Job opened his mouth and cursed his day.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Job 3:2">2</a> Job spoke and said,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Job 3:3">3</a> Let the day I was born disappear, and the night that said, &quot;A baby boy is conceived.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Job 3:4">4</a> Let that day be dark; may God not notice it from above, nor let light shine on it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Job 3:5">5</a> Let darkness and death&#39;s shadow mark it; let a cloud cover it; let the day&#39;s darkness scare it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Job 3:6">6</a> Let that night be dark; may it not be included among the days of the year or counted in the months.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Job 3:7">7</a> Look, let that night be alone, let no happy voice be heard in it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Job 3:8">8</a> Let those who curse the day do so, those who are prepared to wake up their sadness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Job 3:9">9</a> Let the stars of its dusk become dark; let it hope for light, yet find none; and let it not witness the break of dawn.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Job 3:10">10</a> Because it did not close the doors of my mother&#39;s womb or keep sadness from my eyes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Job 3:11">11</a> Why didn&#39;t I die at birth? Why didn&#39;t I stop breathing when I was born?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Job 3:12">12</a> Why did I have knees to sit on, or breasts to feed from?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Job 3:13">13</a> By now, I would be lying down in peace; I would be asleep and at rest.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Job 3:14">14</a> With leaders and advisors of the earth, who build ruined places for themselves;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Job 3:15">15</a> Or with leaders who had gold, who filled their homes with silver:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Job 3:16">16</a> Or I would have been like a baby born too early that never lived, as babies who never saw light.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Job 3:17">17</a> There the bad people stop causing trouble; and there the tired people rest.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Job 3:18">18</a> There the prisoners find peace; they do not hear the voice of the one who keeps them down.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Job 3:19">19</a> Both important and unimportant people are there; and the servant is no longer bound to their boss.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Job 3:20">20</a> Why is light given to those in misery, and life to those with bitter hearts?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Job 3:21">21</a> They want to die, but it doesn&#39;t happen; they search for death more eagerly than for hidden treasures.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Job 3:22">22</a> Who are very happy and glad when they find the grave?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Job 3:23">23</a> Why is light given to a person whose path is hidden, and whom God has surrounded?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Job 3:24">24</a> I sigh before I eat, and my loud cries flow like water.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Job 3:25">25</a> What I feared most has happened to me, and what I was scared of has come true.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Job 3:26">26</a> I was not safe, nor at rest, nor peaceful; still, trouble came.</p>
