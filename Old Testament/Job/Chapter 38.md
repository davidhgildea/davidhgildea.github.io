<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Job 38:1">1</a> Then God answered Job from the storm and said,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Job 38:2">2</a> Who is this who gives advice with words but has no understanding?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Job 38:3">3</a> Get ready like a man; I will question you, and you must answer me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Job 38:4">4</a> Where were you when I set the earth&#39;s foundations? Tell me if you understand.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Job 38:5">5</a> Who set its measurements, if you know? Or who stretched a measuring line across it?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Job 38:6">6</a> Where are its foundations fixed? Or who put its cornerstone in place?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Job 38:7">7</a> When the morning stars sang together and all of God&#39;s children shouted for joy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Job 38:8">8</a> Who closed in the sea with gates when it burst forth, as though coming out of the womb?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Job 38:9">9</a> When I wrapped it in clouds like clothes, and a thick dark blanket covered it,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Job 38:10">10</a> I broke open a place for it, and put in bars and doors.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Job 38:11">11</a> God said, &quot;You may come this far, but no farther; here your proud waves must stop.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Job 38:12">12</a> Have you ever ordered the morning to begin or shown the dawn its place?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Job 38:13">13</a> So it can grab the edges of the world and shake the bad people from it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Job 38:14">14</a> It changes like clay under a seal; and they settle into place like clothes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Job 38:15">15</a> The wicked are denied light, and their strong arm will be broken.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Job 38:16">16</a> Have you gone into the springs of the sea? Or have you walked looking for the depths?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Job 38:17">17</a> Have you seen the gates of death or the doors where death&#39;s shadow falls?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Job 38:18">18</a> Have you understood how wide the earth is? Tell me if you know everything about it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Job 38:19">19</a> Where is the place where light lives? And where is the home of darkness?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Job 38:20">20</a> You should take it to its limit and know the ways to its home.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Job 38:21">21</a> Do you know this because you were born then? Or because you have lived many days?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Job 38:22">22</a> Have you entered the storehouses of the snow? Or have you seen the storehouses of the hail?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Job 38:23">23</a> I have kept for a time of trouble, for a day of battle and war.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Job 38:24">24</a> How is light spread, or the east wind scattered over the earth?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Job 38:25">25</a> Who has made channels for the flood, or a path for thunder&#39;s lightning?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Job 38:26">26</a> To make it rain on the land where no one lives; on the desert where there is no one;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Job 38:27">27</a> To make the lonely, barren land fertile and to help young plants start to grow.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Job 38:28">28</a> Does the rain have a father? Or who has created the dewdrops?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Job 38:29">29</a> Who made ice come from the womb? Who created the frost from the heavens?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Job 38:30">30</a> The waters are covered like with a stone, and the surface of the deep water is solid like ice.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Job 38:31">31</a> Can you control the beautiful patterns of the stars or untie the ropes of the constellations?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Job 38:32">32</a> Can you make the constellations appear in their season? Or can you lead the Great Bear and its cubs?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Job 38:33">33</a> Do you know the laws of the sky? Can you establish their rule on the earth?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Job 38:34">34</a> Can you call out to the clouds to make them pour down water on you?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Job 38:35">35</a> Can you make lightning strike and have it report to you, &#39;Here we are&#39;?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Job 38:36">36</a> Who has placed wisdom inside us? Or who has given the heart understanding?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Job 38:37">37</a> Who can count the clouds wisely? Or who can hold back the rain from the sky?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Job 38:38">38</a> When the soil turns hard, and lumps stick tightly together?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="Job 38:39">39</a> Will you hunt food for the lion or satisfy the hunger of the young lions?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="Job 38:40">40</a> When they crouch in their lairs and stay hidden to ambush?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="Job 38:41">41</a> Who gives the raven its food? When its young cry out to God, they roam because they have no food.</p>
