<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Job 16:1">1</a> Then Job replied and spoke,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Job 16:2">2</a> I have heard many things like this: you are all terrible at comforting.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Job 16:3">3</a> Will empty talk ever stop? What makes you so bold to answer?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Job 16:4">4</a> I could talk like you if I were in your place. I could make many accusations against you and shake my head at you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Job 16:5">5</a> I would encourage you with my words, and my talking would ease your sadness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Job 16:6">6</a> Even if I talk, my sadness isn&#39;t lessened; and if I stay quiet, how am I comforted?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Job 16:7">7</a> But now he has made me tired: you have left all my friends alone.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Job 16:8">8</a> You have covered me with wrinkles, which stand as proof against me; my thinness is a testimony to my face.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Job 16:9">9</a> He violently attacks me in his anger, hating me; he grinds his teeth at me; my enemy glares at me with sharp eyes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Job 16:10">10</a> They opened their mouths wide at me; they slapped my face in insult; they joined together against me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Job 16:11">11</a> God has given me to the bad people, and put me into the hands of the evil ones.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Job 16:12">12</a> I was comfortable, but God has torn me apart; He has grabbed my neck and shattered me, and made me a target.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Job 16:13">13</a> His archers surround me, he splits my insides apart, and shows no mercy; he spills my bile on the ground.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Job 16:14">14</a> He shatters me, breaking me over and over; he charges at me like a giant.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Job 16:15">15</a> I have put rough cloth on my skin, and put my strength in the dirt.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Job 16:16">16</a> I cry so much my face is dirty, and my eyelids are dark like death.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Job 16:17">17</a> I have done nothing wrong, and my prayer is sincere.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Job 16:18">18</a> Earth, do not hide my blood, and do not let my cry go unheard.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Job 16:19">19</a> Even now, my witness is in heaven, and my evidence is up high.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Job 16:20">20</a> My friends mock me, but I cry to God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Job 16:21">21</a> I wish someone could talk to God for a person, just as someone argues for a friend.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Job 16:22">22</a> In a few years, I will go on a path from which I won&#39;t come back.</p>
