<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Job 19:1">1</a> Then Job replied and spoke,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Job 19:2">2</a> How long will you trouble me and crush me with your words?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Job 19:3">3</a> You have insulted me ten times; you&#39;re not ashamed that you&#39;ve been treating me like a stranger.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Job 19:4">4</a> If I have truly made a mistake, it stays with me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Job 19:5">5</a> If you really want to act superior to me and argue against my disgrace:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Job 19:6">6</a> Understand that God has defeated me and has surrounded me with his net.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Job 19:7">7</a> I shout because of injustice, but no one listens; I cry out, but there is no fairness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Job 19:8">8</a> God has blocked my path so I can&#39;t get through, and has made my ways dark.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Job 19:9">9</a> He has taken away my honor and removed the crown from my head.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Job 19:10">10</a> God has destroyed me on every side, and I am gone; and he has taken away my hope like a tree.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Job 19:11">11</a> He has also ignited his anger against me, and he considers me as one of his enemies.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Job 19:12">12</a> His soldiers gather, march against me, and camp around my tent.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Job 19:13">13</a> He has made my brothers stay away from me, and my friends have truly become strangers to me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Job 19:14">14</a> My relatives have let me down, and my close friends have ignored me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Job 19:15">15</a> Those who live in my house and my servants see me as a stranger; I am like a foreigner to them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Job 19:16">16</a> I called my worker, but he did not respond; I pleaded with him aloud.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Job 19:17">17</a> My wife is disgusted by my breath, even though I pleaded for the sake of my own children.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Job 19:18">18</a> Even young children looked down on me; when I stood up, they talked against me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Job 19:19">19</a> All my close friends hate me, and those I loved have turned against me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Job 19:20">20</a> My bones stick to my skin and flesh, and I barely escaped with my life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Job 19:21">21</a> Feel sorry for me, my friends, because God has struck me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Job 19:22">22</a> Why do you attack me like God does, and aren&#39;t happy with just hurting my body?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Job 19:23">23</a> I wish my words were written down! I wish they were recorded in a book!</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Job 19:24">24</a> They were carved with an iron tool and filled with lead in the rock forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Job 19:25">25</a> I know that my savior is alive, and he will stand on the earth on the last day.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Job 19:26">26</a> Even after my skin is eaten by worms and my body is gone, I will still see God with my own body.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Job 19:27">27</a> I will see God myself, my own eyes will look at him, and not a stranger&#39;s; even if I am wasting away inside.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Job 19:28">28</a> But you should ask, &quot;Why do we persecute him, when the heart of the problem lies within me?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Job 19:29">29</a> Be afraid of the sword: anger brings sword punishments, so you know there is a judgment.</p>
