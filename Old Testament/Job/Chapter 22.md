<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Job 22:1">1</a> Then Eliphaz from Teman replied,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Job 22:2">2</a> Can a person be useful to God, as a wise person is useful to themselves?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Job 22:3">3</a> Does it make God happy when you are good, or does He benefit when you live perfectly?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Job 22:4">4</a> Will God correct you because he fears you? Will he go to court with you?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Job 22:5">5</a> Isn&#39;t your evilness huge? And your sins endless?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Job 22:6">6</a> You took a promise from your brother for no reason and took clothes from those with none.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Job 22:7">7</a> You did not give water to the tired to drink, and you kept bread from the hungry.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Job 22:8">8</a> The strong person owned the land, and the respected person lived on it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Job 22:9">9</a> You sent widows away with nothing, and you have crushed the strength of orphans.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Job 22:10">10</a> So traps surround you, and sudden fear upsets you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Job 22:11">11</a> Or darkness, so you can&#39;t see, and lots of water covers you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Job 22:12">12</a> Isn&#39;t God in the highest part of heaven? And see how high the stars are!</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Job 22:13">13</a> And you ask, &quot;How does God know? Can he judge behind the dark cloud?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Job 22:14">14</a> Dense clouds hide God so he cannot be seen; and he moves in the sky&#39;s expanse.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Job 22:15">15</a> Have you noticed the old path that bad people have walked on?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Job 22:16">16</a> They were cut down too soon, and their foundation was washed away by a flood.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Job 22:17">17</a> They told God to go away: and what can God do for them?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Job 22:18">18</a> He filled their homes with good things, but I stay away from the plans of the wicked.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Job 22:19">19</a> Good people see it and are happy; those who are not guilty mock them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Job 22:20">20</a> Our resources are not destroyed, but the fire burns what&#39;s left of them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Job 22:21">21</a> Get to know God now and find peace; this will bring you goodness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Job 22:22">22</a> Please accept the teachings from God, and keep his words in your heart.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Job 22:23">23</a> If you come back to God, you will be restored, and you will keep sin far from your home.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Job 22:24">24</a> You will pile up gold like dust, and the gold of Ophir like pebbles from the streams.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Job 22:25">25</a> Yes, God will protect you, and you will have lots of silver.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Job 22:26">26</a> Then you will find joy in God and you can look up to Him with confidence.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Job 22:27">27</a> Pray to God, and he will listen to you, and you must keep your promises.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Job 22:28">28</a> You will decide on something, and it will happen for you: light will shine on your path.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Job 22:29">29</a> When people feel defeated, you should say, &quot;There is hope&quot;; and God will save the humble.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Job 22:30">30</a> He will save the land of the innocent; it is saved because of your clean hands.</p>
