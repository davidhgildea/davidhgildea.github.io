<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Job 35:1">1</a> Elihu spoke again and said,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Job 35:2">2</a> Do you think it&#39;s right to say, &quot;I am more righteous than God&quot;?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Job 35:3">3</a> You said, &quot;What benefit will it be to you?&quot; and, &quot;What will I gain if I am cleansed from my sin?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Job 35:4">4</a> I will answer you and your friends too.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Job 35:5">5</a> Look up at the sky and see; look at the clouds so high above you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Job 35:6">6</a> If you sin, what do you do against God? Or if your sins increase, what do you do to Him?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Job 35:7">7</a> If you are good, what do you give to God? Or what does He get from what you have?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Job 35:8">8</a> Your evil can harm someone like you; your good deeds can help people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Job 35:9">9</a> Because of many harsh acts, the victims cry out; they call for help because of the power of the strong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Job 35:10">10</a> But no one asks, &#39;Where is God my creator, who provides songs at night?&#39;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Job 35:11">11</a> Who teaches us more than the animals of the earth, and makes us wiser than the birds of the sky?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Job 35:12">12</a> They call out for help, but no one answers, because of the arrogance of wicked people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Job 35:13">13</a> God will not listen to emptiness, nor will the Almighty consider it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Job 35:14">14</a> Even if you say you can&#39;t see God, he is still the one to judge; so you should trust in him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Job 35:15">15</a> But now, God has come in anger because it&#39;s not as it should be; yet he doesn&#39;t realize how harsh it is.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Job 35:16">16</a> So Job speaks without reason; he talks a lot but without understanding.</p>
