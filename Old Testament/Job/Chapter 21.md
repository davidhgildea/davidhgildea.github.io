<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Job 21:1">1</a> Job replied,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Job 21:2">2</a> Listen carefully to what I say, and let this comfort you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Job 21:3">3</a> Let me speak, and after I have spoken, you can mock me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Job 21:4">4</a> Do I complain to people? If that&#39;s true, shouldn&#39;t I be upset?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Job 21:5">5</a> Listen to me, be amazed, and put your hand over your mouth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Job 21:6">6</a> When I think about it, I get scared, and my body starts to shake.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Job 21:7">7</a> Why do bad people live long, grow old, and become very strong?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Job 21:8">8</a> Their children are secure with them, and they can see their grandchildren.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Job 21:9">9</a> Their homes are safe and not afraid, and God&#39;s punishment does not touch them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Job 21:10">10</a> Their bull mates without failing; their cow gives birth and does not miscarry.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Job 21:11">11</a> They send out their young like a herd, and their kids dance.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Job 21:12">12</a> They play tambourines and harps, and are happy to hear the organ&#39;s music.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Job 21:13">13</a> They live in riches all their days, but suddenly they die.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Job 21:14">14</a> So they tell God to go away, because they don&#39;t want to know His ways.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Job 21:15">15</a> Who is God that we should serve Him? And what do we gain by praying to Him?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Job 21:16">16</a> Look, they have no control over their own welfare; I stay away from the plans of the wicked.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Job 21:17">17</a> How often is the wicked person&#39;s light extinguished! And how often does ruin fall upon them! God gives out sadness in his anger.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Job 21:18">18</a> They are like straw in the wind, and like husks that the storm blows away.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Job 21:19">19</a> God saves up the wrongs of someone for their children: He punishes them, and they will realize it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Job 21:20">20</a> He will see his own ruin and drink from God&#39;s anger.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Job 21:21">21</a> Why would he enjoy his house after he&#39;s gone if his life is cut short?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Job 21:22">22</a> Can anyone teach God knowledge, since He judges even the powerful?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Job 21:23">23</a> Someone dies in their prime, feeling completely peaceful and relaxed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Job 21:24">24</a> His chest is full of milk, and his bones are wet with marrow.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Job 21:25">25</a> Another person dies feeling bitter and never enjoys their food.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Job 21:26">26</a> They will all rest in the ground, and worms will cover them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Job 21:27">27</a> Look, I know what you are thinking and the plans you are wrongly making against me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Job 21:28">28</a> You ask, &quot;Where is the prince&#39;s house? And where do the wicked live?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Job 21:29">29</a> Have you not asked those who pass by? And do you not recognize the signs they know?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Job 21:30">30</a> The wicked are kept for the day of destruction; they will be taken to the day of anger.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Job 21:31">31</a> Who will tell him what he has done to his face? Who will pay him back for his actions?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Job 21:32">32</a> He will be taken to the grave and stay in the tomb.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Job 21:33">33</a> The lumps of earth in the valley will be dear to him, and everyone will follow him, just as countless have gone before him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Job 21:34">34</a> So how can you comfort me with nonsense, when your answers are full of lies?</p>
