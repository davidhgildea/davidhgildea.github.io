<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Kings 5:1">1</a> Hiram, the king of Tyre, sent his servants to Solomon because he heard they had made him king after his father. Hiram always loved David.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Kings 5:2">2</a> Solomon sent a message to Hiram, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Kings 5:3">3</a> You know my father David couldn&#39;t build a house for God because of the constant wars around him, until God defeated his enemies.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Kings 5:4">4</a> But now God has given me peace everywhere, with no enemy or bad things happening.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Kings 5:5">5</a> Look, I plan to build a house for the name of God, as God told my father David, saying, &quot;Your son, who I will put on your throne after you, will build a house for my name.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Kings 5:6">6</a> So now, tell them to cut cedar trees from Lebanon for me; my workers will work with yours: and I will pay your workers as you decide: because you know we have no one who can cut wood like the Sidonians.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Kings 5:7">7</a> When Hiram heard Solomon&#39;s words, he was very happy and said, &quot;Praise God today, for giving David a wise son to lead this great nation.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Kings 5:8">8</a> Hiram sent a message to Solomon, saying, &quot;I have thought about what you asked me for, and I will do everything you want with the cedar and fir wood.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Kings 5:9">9</a> My workers will bring them from Lebanon to the sea, and I&#39;ll transport them by sea on rafts to the place you choose for me. There, I&#39;ll unload them, and you&#39;ll pick them up. You&#39;ll fulfill my wish by providing food for my family.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Kings 5:10">10</a> Hiram gave Solomon all the cedar and fir trees he wanted.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Kings 5:11">11</a> Solomon gave Hiram 20,000 measures of wheat for his family&#39;s food and 20 measures of pure oil. Solomon did this for Hiram every year.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Kings 5:12">12</a> God gave Solomon wisdom as promised, and there was peace between Hiram and Solomon; they both formed a partnership.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 Kings 5:13">13</a> King Solomon gathered workers from all Israel; there were thirty thousand men.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="1 Kings 5:14">14</a> He sent them to Lebanon in groups of ten thousand each month: they spent one month in Lebanon and two months at home. Adoniram was in charge of them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="1 Kings 5:15">15</a> Solomon had seventy thousand carriers and eighty thousand stonecutters in the mountains.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="1 Kings 5:16">16</a> In addition to Solomon&#39;s main officers in charge of the work, there were 3,300 supervisors managing the workers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="1 Kings 5:17">17</a> The king ordered them to bring large, expensive cut stones to lay the house&#39;s foundation.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="1 Kings 5:18">18</a> Solomon&#39;s builders and Hiram&#39;s builders cut the stones, and the stonecutters helped. They got wood and stones ready to build the house.</p>
