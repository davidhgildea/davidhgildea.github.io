<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Kings 2:1">1</a> David&#39;s time to die was near, so he told his son Solomon,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Kings 2:2">2</a> I am going the same way everyone on earth goes: so be strong and act like a man.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Kings 2:3">3</a> Follow God&#39;s instructions, live by his rules, follow his commands, decisions, and teachings as written by Moses, so you may succeed in everything you do and wherever you go.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Kings 2:4">4</a> God will keep his promise to me, saying, &quot;If your children pay attention to their behavior and live truthfully with all their heart and soul, you will always have a descendant to rule over Israel.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Kings 2:5">5</a> You also know what Joab, son of Zeruiah, did to me and what he did to the two leaders of Israel&#39;s armies, to Abner, Ner&#39;s son, and to Amasa, Jether&#39;s son. He killed them and spilt blood as if at war even though it was peacetime. He stained his belt around his waist and his shoes on his feet with their blood.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Kings 2:6">6</a> So use your wisdom and don&#39;t let him die peacefully in old age.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Kings 2:7">7</a> Show kindness to Barzillai of Gilead&#39;s sons and let them eat at your table because they helped me when I ran away from your brother Absalom.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Kings 2:8">8</a> Look, you have with you Shimei, son of Gera, from the tribe of Benjamin in Bahurim. He cursed me terribly on the day I went to Mahanaim. But he later met me at the Jordan River, and I promised him in God&#39;s name, I would not kill him with the sword.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Kings 2:9">9</a> So now, don&#39;t consider him innocent: you&#39;re a smart man, you know what you should do to him; make sure he goes to his grave covered in blood.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Kings 2:10">10</a> David died and was buried in the city of David.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Kings 2:11">11</a> David ruled over Israel for forty years: seven years in Hebron and thirty-three years in Jerusalem.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Kings 2:12">12</a> Solomon sat on his father David&#39;s throne, and his reign became very strong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 Kings 2:13">13</a> Adonijah, Haggith&#39;s son, went to Bathsheba, Solomon&#39;s mother. She asked, &quot;Do you come in peace?&quot; He replied, &quot;Yes, in peace.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="1 Kings 2:14">14</a> He also said, &quot;I have something to tell you.&quot; And she replied, &quot;Go ahead.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="1 Kings 2:15">15</a> He said, &quot;You know I was supposed to be king, and all Israel looked to me to rule. But now, the kingdom has gone to my brother, because God gave it to him.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="1 Kings 2:16">16</a> I am asking you for one favor, please don&#39;t refuse me. She said to him, Go ahead.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="1 Kings 2:17">17</a> He said, &quot;Please talk to King Solomon (he won&#39;t refuse you) and ask him to let me marry Abishag the Shunammite.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="1 Kings 2:18">18</a> Bathsheba said, &quot;Okay; I will talk to the king for you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="1 Kings 2:19">19</a> Bathsheba went to King Solomon to talk about Adonijah. The king stood up to greet her, bowed to her, sat on his throne, and had a chair placed for his mother; she sat at his right side.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="1 Kings 2:20">20</a> She said, &quot;I have one small request; please don&#39;t say no to me.&quot; And the king answered, &quot;Go ahead, my mother, for I will not refuse you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="1 Kings 2:21">21</a> She said, &quot;Let Abishag from Shunem be given to your brother Adonijah as his wife.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="1 Kings 2:22">22</a> King Solomon answered his mother, &quot;Why do you request Abishag the Shunammite for Adonijah? You might as well ask for the kingdom for him since he&#39;s my older brother. And not just for him, but also for Abiathar the priest and Joab, son of Zeruiah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="1 Kings 2:23">23</a> King Solomon swore by God, &quot;May God do the same to me, and even more, if Adonijah hasn&#39;t said this and put his own life at risk.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="1 Kings 2:24">24</a> So now, because God is alive, who made me king and put me on my father David&#39;s throne, and has given me a home as he promised, Adonijah will be killed today.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="1 Kings 2:25">25</a> King Solomon sent Benaiah, Jehoiada&#39;s son, who attacked and killed him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="1 Kings 2:26">26</a> The king said to Abiathar the priest, &quot;Go to your lands in Anathoth, because you deserve to die. But I won&#39;t kill you now because you carried God&#39;s ark before my father David and suffered where he suffered.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="1 Kings 2:27">27</a> Solomon removed Abiathar from being a priest for God; this fulfilled what God had said about Eli&#39;s family in Shiloh.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="1 Kings 2:28">28</a> News reached Joab because he had supported Adonijah but not Absalom. Joab ran to God&#39;s tent and grabbed the corners of the altar.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="1 Kings 2:29">29</a> Someone told King Solomon that Joab ran away to God&#39;s tent and is now by the altar. Then Solomon sent Benaiah, Jehoiada&#39;s son, with the order: Go and kill him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="1 Kings 2:30">30</a> Benaiah went to God&#39;s tent and told him, &quot;The king says, &#39;Come out.&#39;&quot; But he replied, &quot;No, I&#39;ll die here.&quot; Then Benaiah told the king what Joab had said and how he responded.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="1 Kings 2:31">31</a> The king said to him, &quot;Do what he told you, attack him and bury him. This will remove the guilt of the innocent blood Joab spilled from me and my father&#39;s family.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="1 Kings 2:32">32</a> God will make him responsible for the blood he shed. He attacked and killed two men who were more good and fair than himself without my father David knowing it: Abner, Ner&#39;s son and leader of Israel&#39;s army, and Amasa, Jether&#39;s son and leader of Judah&#39;s army.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="1 Kings 2:33">33</a> Joab and his descendants will always be punished for the bloodshed, but David, his descendants, his family, and his throne will have eternal peace from God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="1 Kings 2:34">34</a> Benaiah, son of Jehoiada, went up, attacked him, and killed him. He was buried in his own home in the wilderness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="1 Kings 2:35">35</a> The king placed Benaiah, Jehoiada&#39;s son, in charge of the army, and Zadok the priest took Abiathar&#39;s position.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="1 Kings 2:36">36</a> The king sent for Shimei and told him, &quot;Build yourself a house in Jerusalem, live there, and do not go anywhere else.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="1 Kings 2:37">37</a> On the day you leave and cross Kidron Valley, you will know for sure that you will die; your own actions will be the cause.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="1 Kings 2:38">38</a> Shimei said to the king, &quot;The words are good: as you, the king, have said, so I, your servant, will do.&quot; And Shimei lived in Jerusalem for many days.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="1 Kings 2:39">39</a> After three years, two of Shimei&#39;s servants escaped to Achish, Maachah&#39;s son, the king of Gath. They informed Shimei, &quot;Look, your servants are in Gath.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="1 Kings 2:40">40</a> Shimei got up, put a saddle on his donkey, and went to Gath to see Achish to find his servants. Then Shimei went and brought his servants back from Gath.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="1 Kings 2:41">41</a> Someone told Solomon that Shimei had left Jerusalem for Gath and had returned.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="1 Kings 2:42">42</a> The king summoned Shimei and said to him, &quot;Didn&#39;t I make you promise by God and warn you, saying, &#39;Be sure of this: the day you leave and go anywhere, you will certainly die&#39;? And you said to me, &#39;What you said is good.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v43"><a class="verse-num" href="#v43" aria-label="1 Kings 2:43">43</a> Why didn&#39;t you keep the promise to God and follow the command I gave you?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v44"><a class="verse-num" href="#v44" aria-label="1 Kings 2:44">44</a> The king also told Shimei, &quot;You know all the wrong you kept secret in your heart that you did to my father David. So, God will make sure your wrongs come back on you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v45"><a class="verse-num" href="#v45" aria-label="1 Kings 2:45">45</a> King Solomon will be blessed, and David&#39;s throne will be set up for all time in God&#39;s presence.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v46"><a class="verse-num" href="#v46" aria-label="1 Kings 2:46">46</a> The king told Benaiah, Jehoiada&#39;s son, to kill him, and he did. So Solomon&#39;s rule was made secure.</p>
