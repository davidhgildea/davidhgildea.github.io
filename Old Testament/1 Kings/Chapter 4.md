<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Kings 4:1">1</a> King Solomon ruled over all Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Kings 4:2">2</a> God had leaders. Azariah, son of Zadok the priest, was one of them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Kings 4:3">3</a> Elihoreph and Ahiah, Shisha&#39;s sons, were secretaries; Jehoshaphat, Ahilud&#39;s son, was the historian.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Kings 4:4">4</a> Benaiah, son of Jehoiada, led the army; Zadok and Abiathar were the priests.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Kings 4:5">5</a> Azariah, Nathan&#39;s son, was in charge of the officers, and Zabud, Nathan&#39;s son, was the chief officer and the king&#39;s friend.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Kings 4:6">6</a> Ahishar was in charge of the household, and Adoniram, Abda&#39;s son, was responsible for collecting taxes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Kings 4:7">7</a> Solomon had twelve officers for all of Israel, who supplied food for the king and his household. Each one took a month per year to provide.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Kings 4:8">8</a> Their names are: Hur&#39;s son, in the Ephraim hills:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Kings 4:9">9</a> Dekar&#39;s son was over Makaz, Shaalbim, Bethshemesh, and Elonbethhanan.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Kings 4:10">10</a> Hesed&#39;s son was in charge of Aruboth; he also controlled Sochoh and the entire area of Hepher.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Kings 4:11">11</a> Abinadab&#39;s son, who was in charge of the Dor area, was married to Taphath, Solomon&#39;s daughter.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Kings 4:12">12</a> Baana, Ahilud&#39;s son, was responsible for Taanach, Megiddo, and all of Bethshean near Zartanah below Jezreel, stretching from Bethshean to Abelmeholah, and up to the area past Jokneam.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 Kings 4:13">13</a> Geber&#39;s son was in charge of Ramoth in Gilead and the towns of Jair, Manasseh&#39;s son, which are in Gilead. He also controlled the area of Argob in Bashan with sixty big cities that have walls and bronze gates.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="1 Kings 4:14">14</a> Ahinadab, Iddo&#39;s son, was in charge of Mahanaim.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="1 Kings 4:15">15</a> Ahimaaz lived in Naphtali and married Basmath, Solomon&#39;s daughter.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="1 Kings 4:16">16</a> Baanah, Hushai&#39;s son, was in Asher and Aloth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="1 Kings 4:17">17</a> Jehoshaphat, Paruah&#39;s son, was in Issachar.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="1 Kings 4:18">18</a> Shimei, son of Elah, was from the tribe of Benjamin.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="1 Kings 4:19">19</a> Geber, Uri&#39;s son, was in Gilead, Sihon the Amorite king&#39;s land, and Og the Bashan king&#39;s land; he was the only official there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="1 Kings 4:20">20</a> Judah and Israel had many people, like the sand by the sea, eating, drinking, and being happy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="1 Kings 4:21">21</a> Solomon ruled over all kingdoms from the river to the Philistine lands, and to Egypt&#39;s border: they gave gifts and served Solomon all his life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="1 Kings 4:22">22</a> Solomon&#39;s daily food supply was thirty measures of fine flour and sixty measures of grain.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="1 Kings 4:23">23</a> Ten fat oxen, twenty oxen from the fields, and a hundred sheep, plus deer, roebucks, fallow deer, and fat birds.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="1 Kings 4:24">24</a> He ruled over all the area by the river, from Tiphsah to Azzah, over all the kings by the river, and he had peace all around him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="1 Kings 4:25">25</a> Judah and Israel lived in safety, each person under their own vine and fig tree, from Dan to Beersheba, during all of Solomon&#39;s time.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="1 Kings 4:26">26</a> Solomon had 40,000 stalls for his chariot horses and 12,000 riders.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="1 Kings 4:27">27</a> Those officers supplied food for King Solomon and for everyone who came to King Solomon&#39;s table, every man during his month; they were missing nothing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="1 Kings 4:28">28</a> They brought barley and straw to the place where the officers were for the horses and camels, each person according to their responsibility.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="1 Kings 4:29">29</a> God gave Solomon great wisdom, deep understanding, and a big heart, as many as the sands on the seashore.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="1 Kings 4:30">30</a> Solomon&#39;s wisdom was greater than the wisdom of everyone in the eastern lands and all the wisdom of Egypt.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="1 Kings 4:31">31</a> He was smarter than everyone; smarter than Ethan the Ezrahite, Heman, Chalcol, and Darda, the sons of Mahol: and people everywhere knew about him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="1 Kings 4:32">32</a> He spoke three thousand wise sayings, and he had one thousand and five songs.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="1 Kings 4:33">33</a> He talked about trees, from the tall cedars of Lebanon to the small hyssop plants that grow in walls. He also talked about animals, birds, crawling creatures, and fish.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="1 Kings 4:34">34</a> People from everywhere came to hear Solomon&#39;s wisdom, including all the kings of the earth who had heard about it.</p>
