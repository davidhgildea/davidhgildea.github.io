<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Kings 17:1">1</a> Elijah from Tishbe in Gilead told Ahab, &quot;As the living God of Israel, before whom I stand, there won&#39;t be any dew or rain for these years, unless I say so.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Kings 17:2">2</a> God&#39;s message came to him, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Kings 17:3">3</a> Go away, turn east, and hide by the Cherith stream, in front of the Jordan.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Kings 17:4">4</a> You will drink from the stream, and I have told the ravens to bring you food there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Kings 17:5">5</a> So he went and did as God had said. He lived by the Cherith stream, which is near the Jordan.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Kings 17:6">6</a> The birds brought him bread and meat in the morning, and bread and meat in the evening; and he drank from the stream.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Kings 17:7">7</a> After some time, the stream dried up because there had been no rain in the country.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Kings 17:8">8</a> God&#39;s message came to him, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Kings 17:9">9</a> Get up, go to Zarephath in Sidon, and stay there. I have told a widow there to take care of you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Kings 17:10">10</a> He got up and went to Zarephath. As he arrived at the city entrance, he saw a widow picking up sticks. He asked her, “Please bring me a small amount of water in a container to drink.”</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Kings 17:11">11</a> As she went to get it, he called to her and said, &quot;Please bring me a piece of bread in your hand.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Kings 17:12">12</a> She said, &quot;As God is alive, I don&#39;t have a cake, only a little flour in a jar and a bit of oil in a jug. Look, I&#39;m picking up two sticks to cook it for me and my son, so we can eat it and then die.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 Kings 17:13">13</a> Elijah told her, &quot;Don&#39;t be afraid; go ahead and make what you said: but first, please make me a small cake and bring it to me, then make some for yourself and your son.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="1 Kings 17:14">14</a> God says the jar of flour won&#39;t run out, and the jug of oil won&#39;t dry up, until the day God sends rain on the land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="1 Kings 17:15">15</a> She went and did as Elijah said: she, he, and her family ate for many days.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="1 Kings 17:16">16</a> The jar of flour didn&#39;t run out, nor did the jug of oil dry up, just as God promised through Elijah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="1 Kings 17:17">17</a> After these events, the woman&#39;s son, who owned the house, became very ill, and he was so sick that he stopped breathing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="1 Kings 17:18">18</a> She asked Elijah, &quot;What do you want with me, man of God? Have you come to remind me of my sin and cause my son to die?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="1 Kings 17:19">19</a> He said to her, &quot;Give me your son.&quot; He took the boy from her arms, went to the upstairs room where he stayed, and laid him on his own bed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="1 Kings 17:20">20</a> He called out to God and said, &quot;My God, have you also caused harm to the widow I&#39;m staying with by causing her son to die?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="1 Kings 17:21">21</a> He lay on the child three times, called to God, and said, &quot;O God, please let the child&#39;s soul come back to him.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="1 Kings 17:22">22</a> God heard Elijah&#39;s voice, and the child&#39;s life returned to him, and he came back to life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="1 Kings 17:23">23</a> Elijah took the child, brought him from the room into the house, and gave him to his mother. Elijah said, &quot;Look, your son is alive.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="1 Kings 17:24">24</a> The woman said to Elijah, &quot;Now I know that you are a man of God, and that the word of God from your mouth is true.&quot;</p>
