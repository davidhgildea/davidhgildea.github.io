<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Kings 16:1">1</a> God&#39;s message came to Jehu, Hanani&#39;s son, against Baasha, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Kings 16:2">2</a> Because I lifted you from the dust and made you leader over my people Israel, but you followed Jeroboam&#39;s ways and caused my people Israel to sin, making me angry with their sins.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Kings 16:3">3</a> I will remove Baasha&#39;s descendants and his family; and will make your family like Jeroboam son of Nebat&#39;s family.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Kings 16:4">4</a> Dogs will eat those of Baasha who die in the city, and birds will eat those who die in the fields.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Kings 16:5">5</a> The other things Baasha did, his actions, and his power are written in the Israel kings&#39; records, right?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Kings 16:6">6</a> Baasha died like his ancestors and was buried in Tirzah. Then his son Elah became king after him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Kings 16:7">7</a> Through the prophet Jehu, son of Hanani, God&#39;s message came against Baasha and his family, for all the bad things he did that made God angry, like what Jeroboam did; and because Baasha killed him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Kings 16:8">8</a> In the twenty-sixth year of Asa&#39;s rule as king of Judah, Elah, Baasha&#39;s son, became king of Israel in Tirzah and ruled for two years.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Kings 16:9">9</a> Zimri, who led half the chariots, plotted against his master while he was getting drunk in his official Arza&#39;s house in Tirzah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Kings 16:10">10</a> Zimri went in, attacked him, and killed him in the twenty-seventh year of Asa, king of Judah&#39;s reign, and then took his place as ruler.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Kings 16:11">11</a> When he started to rule, as soon as he sat on his throne, he killed all of Baasha&#39;s family; he did not leave any male alive, not among his relatives or friends.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Kings 16:12">12</a> So Zimri killed all of Baasha&#39;s family, just as God had said through the prophet Jehu.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 Kings 16:13">13</a> Because of all the wrongs Baasha and his son Elah did, which caused them and Israel to sin and made God angry with their useless acts.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="1 Kings 16:14">14</a> The other things Elah did are written in the history book of Israel&#39;s kings, aren&#39;t they?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="1 Kings 16:15">15</a> In the twenty-seventh year of Asa, the king of Judah, Zimri ruled for seven days in Tirzah. Meanwhile, the people camped around Gibbethon, owned by the Philistines.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="1 Kings 16:16">16</a> The people camping heard that Zimri plotted against and killed the king, so all Israel made Omri, the army leader, their king that day in the camp.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="1 Kings 16:17">17</a> Omri left Gibbethon with all of Israel and they surrounded Tirzah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="1 Kings 16:18">18</a> When Zimri saw the city was captured, he went into the royal palace, set it on fire over himself, and died.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="1 Kings 16:19">19</a> Because of the bad things he did, seen by God, following Jeroboam&#39;s bad path, and leading Israel to do wrong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="1 Kings 16:20">20</a> The other things Zimri did and his betrayal are written in the book of the history of Israel&#39;s kings, aren&#39;t they?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="1 Kings 16:21">21</a> Then the people of Israel split into two groups: half wanted Tibni, son of Ginath, to be king, and the other half supported Omri.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="1 Kings 16:22">22</a> But the people who supported Omri were stronger than the ones who supported Tibni, son of Ginath. So Tibni died, and Omri became king.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="1 Kings 16:23">23</a> In the thirty-first year of Asa&#39;s rule as king of Judah, Omri started ruling over Israel for twelve years; he ruled six years in Tirzah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="1 Kings 16:24">24</a> He bought Samaria hill from Shemer for two silver talents, built on it, and named the city he built Samaria after Shemer, the hill&#39;s former owner.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="1 Kings 16:25">25</a> Omri did bad things in God&#39;s sight and was even worse than everyone before him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="1 Kings 16:26">26</a> He followed Jeroboam Nebat&#39;s path completely, repeating his sins that led Israel to do wrong, making God angry with their worthless actions.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="1 Kings 16:27">27</a> The other things Omri did and how strong he was can be found in the history book of Israel&#39;s kings, right?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="1 Kings 16:28">28</a> Omri died and was buried in Samaria; then his son Ahab became king after him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="1 Kings 16:29">29</a> In the thirty-eighth year of Asa&#39;s rule over Judah, Ahab, Omri&#39;s son, started to rule over Israel, and Ahab, Omri&#39;s son, ruled over Israel in Samaria for twenty-two years.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="1 Kings 16:30">30</a> Ahab, Omri&#39;s son, did more evil things in front of God than anyone before him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="1 Kings 16:31">31</a> It seemed like a small thing for him to follow Jeroboam Nebat&#39;s son&#39;s sins, so he married Jezebel, King Ethbaal of Zidon&#39;s daughter, and started to serve and worship Baal.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="1 Kings 16:32">32</a> He built an altar for Baal in the Baal temple he made in Samaria.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="1 Kings 16:33">33</a> Ahab built a shrine; Ahab made God, the God of Israel, angrier than any of the previous kings of Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="1 Kings 16:34">34</a> During Hiel the Bethelite&#39;s time, he rebuilt Jericho. He started the work at the cost of his oldest son Abiram&#39;s life, and finished it at the cost of his youngest son Segub&#39;s life. This happened as God had said through Joshua, Nun&#39;s son.</p>
