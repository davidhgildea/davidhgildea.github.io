<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Kings 20:1">1</a> Benhadad, the king of Syria, brought all his army together. He was joined by thirty-two kings, with their horses and chariots. He advanced and laid siege to Samaria and attacked it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Kings 20:2">2</a> He sent messengers to Ahab, the king of Israel, in the city, telling him, &quot;Benhadad says,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Kings 20:3">3</a> Your silver and your gold are mine; your wives and children, even the most beautiful, are mine too.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Kings 20:4">4</a> The king of Israel replied and said, &quot;My lord, O king, as you say, I am yours, and all that I have.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Kings 20:5">5</a> The messengers came back and said, &quot;Benhadad says, &#39;Even though I&#39;ve sent you a message demanding you give me your silver, gold, wives, and children...&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Kings 20:6">6</a> I will send my servants to you tomorrow at this time, and they will search your house and your servants&#39; houses. Whatever you like, they will take it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Kings 20:7">7</a> The king of Israel called all the leaders and said, &quot;Notice and see how this man wants to cause trouble. He asked for my wives, my children, my silver, and my gold, and I didn&#39;t refuse him.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Kings 20:8">8</a> All the leaders and everyone told him, &quot;Don&#39;t listen to him or agree.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Kings 20:9">9</a> So he told Benhadad&#39;s messengers, &quot;Tell my king I will do all he first asked of me, but I can&#39;t do this.&quot; The messengers left and reported back to him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Kings 20:10">10</a> Benhadad sent a message saying, &quot;May the gods punish me severely if there is enough dust in Samaria for all my followers to grab a handful.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Kings 20:11">11</a> The king of Israel replied, &quot;Tell him not to brag while putting on his armor like one who takes it off after battle.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Kings 20:12">12</a> When Ben-hadad heard this message while he was drinking with the kings in their tents, he told his servants to get ready for battle. So they prepared to attack the city.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 Kings 20:13">13</a> Look, a prophet came to Ahab, the king of Israel, and said, &quot;This is what God says: &#39;Have you seen this huge crowd? I will give them to you today, and you will know that I am God.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="1 Kings 20:14">14</a> Ahab asked, &quot;By whom?&quot; He replied, &quot;God says, &#39;By the young men of the province leaders.&#39;&quot; Then Ahab asked, &quot;Who will lead the attack?&quot; He answered, &quot;You will.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="1 Kings 20:15">15</a> He counted the young men from the provincial leaders, totaling 232; then he counted all the others, all the Israelites, and there were 7,000.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="1 Kings 20:16">16</a> They left at midday. But Benhadad and the thirty-two kings helping him were getting drunk in the tents.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="1 Kings 20:17">17</a> The young leaders&#39; soldiers went first. Then Benhadad was informed, &quot;Men have come out of Samaria.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="1 Kings 20:18">18</a> He said, &quot;If they come for peace, capture them alive; if they come for war, capture them alive.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="1 Kings 20:19">19</a> These young men, who were the leaders&#39; sons, left the city, along with the army that followed them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="1 Kings 20:20">20</a> Everyone killed their opponent, the Syrians ran away, Israel chased them, and King Benhadad of Syria escaped on a horse with his cavalry.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="1 Kings 20:21">21</a> The king of Israel went out, attacked the horses and chariots, and killed many Syrians.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="1 Kings 20:22">22</a> The prophet went to the king of Israel and told him, &quot;Go, get stronger, pay attention, and be careful with what you do. Because when the year comes around again, the king of Syria will attack you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="1 Kings 20:23">23</a> The king of Syria&#39;s servants told him, &quot;Their god is a god of the hills; that&#39;s why they were stronger than us. But if we fight them on the flat land, we will definitely be stronger than them.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="1 Kings 20:24">24</a> Do this: Remove the kings, every one from their positions, and appoint captains in their places.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="1 Kings 20:25">25</a> Count yourself an army, just like the one you lost, with the same number of horses and chariots. Then we will battle them on the flat land, and we will definitely be stronger. He listened to their advice and did it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="1 Kings 20:26">26</a> When the year came around again, Benhadad counted the Syrian troops and moved to Aphek to battle Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="1 Kings 20:27">27</a> The Israelites were counted, all showed up, and fought against them. The Israelites camped in front of them, looking like two small groups of young goats, while the Syrians covered the land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="1 Kings 20:28">28</a> A man of God came and spoke to the king of Israel, saying, &quot;This is what God says: &#39;Because the Syrians believe that God is only the God of the hills and not of the valleys, I will give you victory over this large army, and you will realize that I am God.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="1 Kings 20:29">29</a> They camped facing each other for seven days. On the seventh day, the fight began, and the Israelites killed one hundred thousand Syrian foot soldiers in one day.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="1 Kings 20:30">30</a> But the others ran away to Aphek, into the city. There, a wall collapsed on twenty-seven thousand of the remaining men. Benhadad also ran away and went into the city, to a room inside.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="1 Kings 20:31">31</a> His servants told him, &quot;We have heard that Israel&#39;s kings are kind. Please let us wear rough cloth around our waists and put ropes on our heads, and go to the king of Israel. Maybe he will spare your life.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="1 Kings 20:32">32</a> They wrapped rough cloth around their waists, placed ropes on their heads, went to the king of Israel, and said, &quot;Your servant Benhadad asks, please let me live.&quot; And he replied, &quot;Is he still alive? He is my brother.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="1 Kings 20:33">33</a> The men watched carefully to see if he would do something. When he gave a sign, they quickly took it. They said, &quot;Your brother Benhadad.&quot; He replied, &quot;Go and bring him.&quot; So Benhadad came to him, and he helped him into the chariot.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="1 Kings 20:34">34</a> Ben-hadad told him, &quot;I&#39;ll give back the cities my father took from your father; you can build your own neighborhoods in Damascus, like my father did in Samaria.&quot; Then Ahab replied, &quot;With this agreement, I&#39;ll let you go.&quot; So he made an agreement with him and let him go.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="1 Kings 20:35">35</a> A man from the group of prophets told his friend, speaking for God, &quot;Please hit me.&quot; But the man would not hit him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="1 Kings 20:36">36</a> He told him, &quot;Because you didn&#39;t listen to God, as soon as you leave me, a lion will kill you.&quot; And right after he left him, a lion found him and killed him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="1 Kings 20:37">37</a> He found another person and said, &quot;Please hit me.&quot; So the person hit him, injuring him in the process.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="1 Kings 20:38">38</a> The prophet left and waited for the king on the road, hiding his face with ashes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="1 Kings 20:39">39</a> As the king went by, he called out to the king: &quot;I went into the battle, and a man gave me another man and told me, &#39;Guard this man. If he goes missing, you&#39;ll have to die in his place or pay a talent of silver.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="1 Kings 20:40">40</a> While your servant was busy, he disappeared. The king of Israel said, &quot;That will be your punishment; you have chosen it yourself.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="1 Kings 20:41">41</a> He hurried to remove the ashes from his face, and the king of Israel recognized that he was one of the prophets.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="1 Kings 20:42">42</a> He told him, &quot;God says, because you released a man I wanted to destroy, your life will be taken for his life, and your people for his people.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v43"><a class="verse-num" href="#v43" aria-label="1 Kings 20:43">43</a> The king of Israel went home sad and upset, and arrived in Samaria.</p>
