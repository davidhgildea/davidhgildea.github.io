<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Kings 10:1">1</a> When the queen of Sheba heard about Solomon’s fame and his relationship with God, she came to test him with difficult questions.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Kings 10:2">2</a> She went to Jerusalem with a large group, camels carrying spices, lots of gold, and precious stones. When she met Solomon, she talked with him about everything on her mind.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Kings 10:3">3</a> Solomon answered all her questions; nothing was hidden from the king, which he did not explain to her.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Kings 10:4">4</a> When the queen of Sheba saw all of Solomon&#39;s wisdom and the house he built,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Kings 10:5">5</a> The food at his table, how his servants sat, how his aides served, their clothes, his cup-bearers, and the way he went up to God&#39;s house; it left her breathless.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Kings 10:6">6</a> She told the king, &quot;The stories I heard in my country about your deeds and your wisdom were true.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Kings 10:7">7</a> I didn&#39;t believe what was said until I came and saw it myself. And what I saw was far greater than I was told; your wisdom and wealth are more than the stories I heard.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Kings 10:8">8</a> Your men are happy, your servants who always stand before you and hear your wisdom are happy too.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Kings 10:9">9</a> God blesses you because he took pleasure in you and made you king of Israel; God&#39;s love for Israel is eternal, so he made you ruler to carry out fairness and justice.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Kings 10:10">10</a> She gave the king 120 talents of gold, lots of spices, and valuable stones. No one gave King Solomon as many spices as the Queen of Sheba did.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Kings 10:11">11</a> Hiram&#39;s ships, which carried gold from Ophir, also brought back many almug trees and valuable gems from Ophir.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Kings 10:12">12</a> The king used almug wood to make columns for God&#39;s house and the royal palace, and to make harps and stringed instruments for musicians. There had never been such almug wood before, and none has been seen since then.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 Kings 10:13">13</a> King Solomon gave the Queen of Sheba everything she wanted, beyond what he had already generously given her. Then she and her servants went back to her own country.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="1 Kings 10:14">14</a> Every year, Solomon received 666 talents of gold.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="1 Kings 10:15">15</a> He also received from the traders, spice sellers, all the kings of Arabia, and the country&#39;s leaders.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="1 Kings 10:16">16</a> King Solomon made 200 large shields of hammered gold; each shield used 600 shekels of gold.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="1 Kings 10:17">17</a> He made three hundred gold shields; each shield used three pounds of gold. The king placed them in the Lebanon Forest House.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="1 Kings 10:18">18</a> The king also made a big throne out of ivory and covered it with pure gold.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="1 Kings 10:19">19</a> The throne had six steps and a round top at the back. There were armrests on both sides of the seat with two lions standing next to the armrests.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="1 Kings 10:20">20</a> Twelve lions stood on each side of the six steps; nothing like this was made in any other kingdom.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="1 Kings 10:21">21</a> All of King Solomon&#39;s cups were made of gold, and everything in the Lebanon Forest House was pure gold; nothing was made of silver, as silver was not valued much in Solomon&#39;s time.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="1 Kings 10:22">22</a> The king had a fleet of ships for sailing to Tarshish that worked with Hiram&#39;s fleet. Every three years, Tarshish&#39;s ships came, bringing gold, silver, ivory, monkeys, and peacocks.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="1 Kings 10:23">23</a> King Solomon was richer and wiser than all other kings on earth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="1 Kings 10:24">24</a> Everyone in the world wanted to hear Solomon&#39;s wisdom that God had given him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="1 Kings 10:25">25</a> Everyone brought gifts, including silver and gold containers, clothes, weapons, spices, horses, and mules, each year.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="1 Kings 10:26">26</a> Solomon collected chariots and horsemen. He had 1,400 chariots and 12,000 horsemen. He placed them in chariot cities and with him in Jerusalem.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="1 Kings 10:27">27</a> The king made silver as common in Jerusalem as stones, and he made cedar trees as plentiful as sycamore trees in the lowlands.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="1 Kings 10:28">28</a> Solomon got horses from Egypt, and fine thread. The king&#39;s traders bought the thread at a cost.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="1 Kings 10:29">29</a> A chariot was brought from Egypt for six hundred pieces of silver, and a horse for one hundred and fifty pieces. This was done for all the Hittite kings and the Syrian kings, brought out by their agents.</p>
