<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Kings 18:1">1</a> After a long time, God&#39;s message came to Elijah in the third year, saying, &quot;Go, meet Ahab, and I will make it rain on the land.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Kings 18:2">2</a> Elijah went to meet Ahab, and there was a severe famine in Samaria.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Kings 18:3">3</a> Ahab asked Obadiah, his house manager, to come. (Obadiah deeply respected God:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Kings 18:4">4</a> When Jezebel killed God&#39;s prophets, Obadiah took one hundred prophets, hid them in groups of fifty in caves, and gave them bread and water.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Kings 18:5">5</a> Ahab told Obadiah, &quot;Go through the land to all the water springs and streams. Maybe we can find grass to keep the horses and mules alive so we don&#39;t lose all the animals.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Kings 18:6">6</a> They split the land to travel through it: Ahab went one way alone, and Obadiah went a different way alone.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Kings 18:7">7</a> As Obadiah was on the road, he met Elijah. He recognized him, bowed down to the ground, and asked, &quot;Are you Elijah, my master?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Kings 18:8">8</a> He replied, &quot;I am Elijah; go tell your master, &#39;Elijah is here.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Kings 18:9">9</a> He asked, &quot;What did I do wrong that you would let your servant be killed by Ahab?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Kings 18:10">10</a> As God, your God, is alive, there isn&#39;t any country or kingdom where my master hasn&#39;t looked for you. And when they said, &quot;He&#39;s not here,&quot; he made each country and kingdom promise they hadn&#39;t found you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Kings 18:11">11</a> Now you say, &quot;Go, tell your master, &#39;Look, Elijah is here.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Kings 18:12">12</a> When I leave you, God&#39;s Spirit will take you to a place I don&#39;t know. So when I tell Ahab and he can&#39;t find you, he will kill me. But I, your servant, have feared God since I was young.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 Kings 18:13">13</a> Didn&#39;t someone tell you, my lord, what I did when Jezebel killed God&#39;s prophets? I hid a hundred of God&#39;s prophets in caves, fifty in each, and gave them bread and water.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="1 Kings 18:14">14</a> You say, &quot;Go tell your master, &#39;Elijah is here,&#39;&quot; but he will kill me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="1 Kings 18:15">15</a> Elijah said, &quot;As the living God of all commands, whom I serve, I will definitely show myself to him today.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="1 Kings 18:16">16</a> Obadiah met Ahab and told him, and then Ahab went to meet Elijah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="1 Kings 18:17">17</a> When Ahab saw Elijah, Ahab said to him, &quot;Are you the one who troubles Israel?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="1 Kings 18:18">18</a> He replied, &quot;I have not caused trouble in Israel; it is you and your family because you have ignored God&#39;s commands and followed the Baals.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="1 Kings 18:19">19</a> So now, send a message and bring all of Israel to Mount Carmel, along with the 450 prophets of Baal and the 400 prophets of the groves who are fed by Jezebel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="1 Kings 18:20">20</a> Ahab sent a message to all the Israelites and brought the prophets to Mount Carmel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="1 Kings 18:21">21</a> Elijah came to everyone and asked, &quot;How long will you hesitate between two choices? If God is the true God, follow Him; but if Baal is, then follow him.&quot; But the people didn&#39;t say anything in reply.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="1 Kings 18:22">22</a> Elijah told the people, &quot;I am the only one left of God&#39;s prophets, but there are 450 prophets of Baal.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="1 Kings 18:23">23</a> So they should give us two young bulls. They can pick one, cut it up, and place it on the wood without setting it on fire. I&#39;ll prepare the other bull, put it on the wood, and also not light it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="1 Kings 18:24">24</a> Ask your gods for help, and I will ask God for help. The one who sends fire is the true God. And everyone agreed this was a good plan.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="1 Kings 18:25">25</a> Elijah said to Baal&#39;s prophets, &quot;Pick one bull for yourselves, prepare it first since you are many, and pray to your gods, but don&#39;t light a fire under it.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="1 Kings 18:26">26</a> They took the bull given to them, prepared it, and prayed to Baal from morning to noon, saying, &quot;O Baal, listen to us.&quot; But there was no reply or anyone who answered. And they jumped on the altar that was built.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="1 Kings 18:27">27</a> At noon, Elijah made fun of them and said, &quot;Shout louder; maybe your god is busy talking, away on a trip, taking a nap, or needs to wake up.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="1 Kings 18:28">28</a> They shouted loudly and cut themselves with knives and sharp tools, as was their custom, until blood flowed out on them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="1 Kings 18:29">29</a> After midday, they continued to prophesy until the evening sacrifice, but there was no voice, no answer, and no one paid attention.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="1 Kings 18:30">30</a> Elijah said to everyone, &quot;Come closer to me.&quot; So all the people came closer to him. Then he fixed God&#39;s altar that was damaged.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="1 Kings 18:31">31</a> Elijah took twelve stones, one for each tribe of Jacob&#39;s sons, because God had said, &quot;Your name will be Israel.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="1 Kings 18:32">32</a> He used the stones to build an altar for God and dug a ditch around it big enough to hold two measures of seed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="1 Kings 18:33">33</a> He arranged the wood, cut the bull into pieces, placed it on the wood, and said, &quot;Fill four barrels with water and pour it over the burnt offering and the wood.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="1 Kings 18:34">34</a> He said, &quot;Do it again.&quot; So they did it again. Then he said, &quot;Do it a third time.&quot; And they did it a third time.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="1 Kings 18:35">35</a> Water flowed around the altar, and the trench was also filled with water.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="1 Kings 18:36">36</a> When the time came to offer the evening sacrifice, the prophet Elijah stepped forward and prayed, &quot;God of Abraham, Isaac, and Israel, show everyone today that you are the true God in Israel, that I am your servant, and that I have followed your instructions.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="1 Kings 18:37">37</a> Listen to me, God, listen to me, so these people will know you are God, and that you have turned their hearts back to you again.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="1 Kings 18:38">38</a> Then God&#39;s fire came down and burned up the offering, the wood, the stones, the dust, and even dried up the water in the ditch.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="1 Kings 18:39">39</a> When everyone saw this, they fell down and said, &quot;God is the God; God is the God.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="1 Kings 18:40">40</a> Elijah said to them, &quot;Capture the prophets of Baal and don&#39;t let any of them get away.&quot; They captured them, and Elijah took them to the Kishon creek and killed them there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="1 Kings 18:41">41</a> Elijah told Ahab, &quot;Go up, eat and drink; because I hear the sound of a lot of rain coming.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="1 Kings 18:42">42</a> So Ahab went to eat and drink. Elijah climbed to the top of Carmel, lay down on the ground, and put his face between his knees.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v43"><a class="verse-num" href="#v43" aria-label="1 Kings 18:43">43</a> He told his servant, &quot;Go up now, look toward the sea.&quot; So he went up, looked, and said, &quot;There is nothing.&quot; Then he was told, &quot;Go back seven times.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v44"><a class="verse-num" href="#v44" aria-label="1 Kings 18:44">44</a> On the seventh time, he saw a small cloud rising from the sea, like a man&#39;s hand. He told the messenger to tell Ahab to get his chariot ready and go before the rain stopped him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v45"><a class="verse-num" href="#v45" aria-label="1 Kings 18:45">45</a> Meanwhile, the sky turned dark with clouds and wind, and a heavy rain fell. Ahab rode off to Jezreel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v46"><a class="verse-num" href="#v46" aria-label="1 Kings 18:46">46</a> God&#39;s power was with Elijah; he tied up his clothes and ran ahead of Ahab to the entrance of Jezreel.</p>
