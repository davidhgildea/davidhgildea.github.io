<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Kings 21:1">1</a> After these events, Naboth, from Jezreel, owned a vineyard next to Ahab&#39;s palace, the king of Samaria.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Kings 21:2">2</a> Ahab said to Naboth, &quot;Give me your vineyard so I can use it for a garden of herbs since it&#39;s close to my house. I&#39;ll give you a better vineyard in exchange, or if you prefer, I&#39;ll pay you its value in money.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Kings 21:3">3</a> Naboth said to Ahab, &quot;God won’t allow it. I can’t give you what I inherited from my ancestors.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Kings 21:4">4</a> Ahab went into his house feeling sad and upset because of what Naboth from Jezreel told him. Naboth had said, &quot;I won&#39;t give you my family&#39;s land.&quot; Ahab lay down on his bed, turned his face to the wall, and refused to eat.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Kings 21:5">5</a> Jezebel, his wife, came to him and asked, &quot;Why are you so sad that you&#39;re not eating?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Kings 21:6">6</a> He told her, &quot;I talked to Naboth from Jezreel. I offered to buy his vineyard or trade it for another one if he liked. But he said, &#39;I won&#39;t give you my vineyard.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Kings 21:7">7</a> Jezebel, his wife, said to him, &quot;Are you not ruling Israel? Get up, eat some bread, and cheer up; I will get you Naboth&#39;s vineyard.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Kings 21:8">8</a> She wrote letters using Ahab&#39;s name, sealed them with his seal, and sent them to the city elders and nobles who lived with Naboth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Kings 21:9">9</a> She wrote in the letters, &quot;Announce a fast, and put Naboth in a prominent place among the people.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Kings 21:10">10</a> Place two wicked men in front of him to accuse him, saying, &quot;You insulted God and the king.&quot; Then take him out and throw stones at him until he dies.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Kings 21:11">11</a> The men of the city, including the elders and leaders who lived there, did what Jezebel told them, following the instructions in her letters.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Kings 21:12">12</a> They announced a time without food and placed Naboth in a high position among the people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 Kings 21:13">13</a> Two bad men came and sat in front of him. They lied about Naboth in front of everyone, saying, &quot;Naboth insulted God and the king.&quot; So they took Naboth out of the city and threw stones at him until he died.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="1 Kings 21:14">14</a> They sent a message to Jezebel: &quot;Naboth has been stoned and is dead.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="1 Kings 21:15">15</a> When Jezebel found out that Naboth had been stoned to death, she told Ahab, &quot;Get up and take over Naboth the Jezreelite&#39;s vineyard, because he didn&#39;t sell it to you. Now that he&#39;s dead, it&#39;s yours.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="1 Kings 21:16">16</a> When Ahab learned that Naboth had died, he got up to go to Naboth the Jezreelite&#39;s vineyard to take it over.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="1 Kings 21:17">17</a> God&#39;s message came to Elijah the Tishbite, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="1 Kings 21:18">18</a> Get up, go meet Ahab, the king of Israel, in Samaria. Look, he is in Naboth&#39;s vineyard, where he has gone to take it over.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="1 Kings 21:19">19</a> You will tell him, &#39;God says, &quot;Have you murdered and also taken the land?&quot;&#39; And you will say to him, &#39;God says, &quot;In the spot where dogs licked Naboth&#39;s blood, dogs will lick your blood too.&quot;&#39;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="1 Kings 21:20">20</a> Ahab said to Elijah, &quot;Have you found me, my enemy?&quot; Elijah answered, &quot;Yes, I have found you because you have chosen to do evil in God&#39;s eyes.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="1 Kings 21:21">21</a> I will bring bad things on you, take away your future family, and destroy every male belonging to Ahab, whether they are free or trapped in Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="1 Kings 21:22">22</a> I will make your family like Jeroboam&#39;s family, the son of Nebat, and like Baasha&#39;s family, the son of Ahijah because you have made me angry and caused Israel to sin.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="1 Kings 21:23">23</a> God also said about Jezebel, &quot;Dogs will eat Jezebel by the wall of Jezreel.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="1 Kings 21:24">24</a> Anyone from Ahab&#39;s family who dies in the city will be eaten by dogs, and anyone who dies in the field will be eaten by birds.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="1 Kings 21:25">25</a> No one was like Ahab; he chose to do evil, watched by God, encouraged by his wife Jezebel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="1 Kings 21:26">26</a> He did terrible things by worshiping idols, just like the Amorites, whom God had driven out before the Israelites.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="1 Kings 21:27">27</a> When Ahab heard those words, he tore his clothes, put on sackcloth, fasted, lay in sackcloth, and walked quietly.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="1 Kings 21:28">28</a> God&#39;s message came to Elijah the Tishbite, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="1 Kings 21:29">29</a> Do you see how Ahab is humbling himself before me? Because he is doing this, I will not cause bad things to happen during his life; instead, I will make them happen during his son&#39;s life to his family.</p>
