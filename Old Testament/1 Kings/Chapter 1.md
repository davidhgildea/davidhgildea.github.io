<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Kings 1:1">1</a> King David was old and very weak; they put clothes on him, but he could not get warm.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Kings 1:2">2</a> So his servants told him, &quot;Let&#39;s find a young woman for our king. She will take care of him, lie close to him, and keep him warm.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Kings 1:3">3</a> They looked for a beautiful young woman all over Israel, found Abishag from Shunem, and took her to the king.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Kings 1:4">4</a> The young woman was very beautiful, took care of the king, and served him, but the king did not have intimate relations with her.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Kings 1:5">5</a> Adonijah, Haggith&#39;s son, boasted, &quot;I will become king.&quot; He organized chariots, riders, and fifty people to run ahead of him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Kings 1:6">6</a> His father never asked him why he acted that way and never made him unhappy. He was also very handsome, and he was born to his mother after Absalom.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Kings 1:7">7</a> He talked with Joab, Zeruiah&#39;s son, and Abiathar the priest; they supported Adonijah and helped him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Kings 1:8">8</a> But Zadok the priest, Benaiah son of Jehoiada, Nathan the prophet, Shimei, Rei, and David&#39;s strong soldiers did not support Adonijah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Kings 1:9">9</a> Adonijah killed sheep, oxen, and well-fed cattle by the stone of Zoheleth, near Enrogel, and invited all his brothers, the king&#39;s sons, and all the men of Judah who served the king.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Kings 1:10">10</a> Nathan the prophet, Benaiah, the strong soldiers, and Solomon his brother were not called by him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Kings 1:11">11</a> So Nathan said to Bathsheba, Solomon&#39;s mother, &quot;Have you heard that Adonijah, Haggith&#39;s son, has become king and David doesn&#39;t know about it?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Kings 1:12">12</a> Now, please come so I can give you advice to save your life and your son Solomon&#39;s life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 Kings 1:13">13</a> Go to King David and ask him, &quot;Didn&#39;t you, my lord the king, promise me that your son Solomon would be king after you and would sit on your throne? Why is Adonijah the king now?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="1 Kings 1:14">14</a> Look, while you are still talking to the king, I will come in after you and back up what you say.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="1 Kings 1:15">15</a> Bathsheba entered the king&#39;s room, and the king was very old; Abishag from Shunem served the king.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="1 Kings 1:16">16</a> Bathsheba bowed down to the king, and the king asked, &quot;What do you want?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="1 Kings 1:17">17</a> She said to him, &quot;Sir, you promised by God to your servant, &#39;Certainly, Solomon your son will be king after me, and he will sit on my throne.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="1 Kings 1:18">18</a> Now, Adonijah has become king, and you, my king, do not know about it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="1 Kings 1:19">19</a> He has killed many oxen, fat cows, and sheep, and invited all the king&#39;s sons, the priest Abiathar, and army commander Joab, but he did not invite your servant Solomon.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="1 Kings 1:20">20</a> And you, my king, all Israel is watching you, expecting you to say who will take your place as king after you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="1 Kings 1:21">21</a> If not, when the king, my lord, dies and joins his ancestors, my son Solomon and I will be treated as criminals.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="1 Kings 1:22">22</a> While she was still talking to the king, the prophet Nathan arrived.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="1 Kings 1:23">23</a> They told the king, &quot;Look, here is the prophet Nathan.&quot; And when Nathan came in, he bowed down to the king with his face to the ground.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="1 Kings 1:24">24</a> Nathan said, My lord, the king, have you said that Adonijah will be king after me and sit on my throne?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="1 Kings 1:25">25</a> Today he went down and killed many oxen, fat cows, and sheep. He invited all the king&#39;s sons, the army commanders, and the priest Abiathar. They are eating and drinking with him and shouting, &quot;Long live King Adonijah!&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="1 Kings 1:26">26</a> But me, your servant, and Zadok the priest, Benaiah son of Jehoiada, and your servant Solomon, he did not call.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="1 Kings 1:27">27</a> Did the king do this without telling me, who will sit on the throne after him?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="1 Kings 1:28">28</a> King David replied, &quot;Bring me Bathsheba.&quot; She came to see the king and stood in front of him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="1 Kings 1:29">29</a> The king promised and said, &quot;As God lives, who has saved me from all trouble,&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="1 Kings 1:30">30</a> Just as I promised you by the God of Israel, saying, &#39;Solomon your son will become king after me, and he will sit on my throne in my place,&#39; so I will definitely do today.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="1 Kings 1:31">31</a> Then Bathsheba knelt down, touched her forehead to the ground, showed deep respect to the king, and said, &quot;May my king, David, live forever.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="1 Kings 1:32">32</a> King David said, &quot;Get Zadok the priest, Nathan the prophet, and Benaiah, Jehoiada&#39;s son.&quot; They came to the king.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="1 Kings 1:33">33</a> The king also told them, &quot;Bring your lord&#39;s servants with you and have my son Solomon ride on my own mule, then take him to Gihon.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="1 Kings 1:34">34</a> Let Zadok the priest and Nathan the prophet make him king over Israel there; blow the trumpet and say, &quot;May God protect King Solomon.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="1 Kings 1:35">35</a> Then you will follow him, so he can come and sit on my throne; he will be king instead of me, and I have chosen him to rule over Israel and Judah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="1 Kings 1:36">36</a> Benaiah, son of Jehoiada, replied to the king, &quot;Yes, may God, the God of my king, agree.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="1 Kings 1:37">37</a> Just as God was with you, the king, may he also be with Solomon and make his rule even greater than your rule, King David.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="1 Kings 1:38">38</a> So Zadok the priest, Nathan the prophet, Benaiah son of Jehoiada, the Cherethites, and the Pelethites went down and made Solomon ride on King David&#39;s mule and took him to Gihon.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="1 Kings 1:39">39</a> Priest Zadok took a horn of oil from the tent and put it on Solomon. They sounded the trumpet; and everyone said, May God protect King Solomon.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="1 Kings 1:40">40</a> Everyone followed him, playing flutes and celebrating so loudly that the sound seemed to shake the ground.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="1 Kings 1:41">41</a> Adonijah and all his guests heard it when they finished eating. When Joab heard the trumpet, he asked, &quot;Why does the city sound so noisy and upset?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="1 Kings 1:42">42</a> While he was still talking, Jonathan, the son of Priest Abiathar, arrived; Adonijah said to him, &quot;Come in, for you are a brave man and bring good news.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v43"><a class="verse-num" href="#v43" aria-label="1 Kings 1:43">43</a> Jonathan replied to Adonijah, &quot;Truly, our king David has made Solomon the king.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v44"><a class="verse-num" href="#v44" aria-label="1 Kings 1:44">44</a> The king sent Zadok the priest, Nathan the prophet, Benaiah son of Jehoiada, the Cherethites, and the Pelethites with him, and they made him ride the king&#39;s mule.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v45"><a class="verse-num" href="#v45" aria-label="1 Kings 1:45">45</a> Zadok the priest and Nathan the prophet made him king at Gihon. They came up from there happy, and their joy filled the city. This is the sound you heard.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v46"><a class="verse-num" href="#v46" aria-label="1 Kings 1:46">46</a> Solomon sits on the throne of the kingdom.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v47"><a class="verse-num" href="#v47" aria-label="1 Kings 1:47">47</a> The king&#39;s helpers came to bless our lord King David, saying, &quot;May God make Solomon&#39;s name greater than yours, and his throne greater than your throne.&quot; Then the king bowed on the bed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v48"><a class="verse-num" href="#v48" aria-label="1 Kings 1:48">48</a> The king also said, &quot;Praise be to God of Israel, who has given someone to sit on my throne today, while I can still see it.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v49"><a class="verse-num" href="#v49" aria-label="1 Kings 1:49">49</a> All the guests with Adonijah became scared, stood up, and each went their own way.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v50"><a class="verse-num" href="#v50" aria-label="1 Kings 1:50">50</a> Adonijah was afraid of Solomon, so he got up, went and grabbed the corners of the altar.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v51"><a class="verse-num" href="#v51" aria-label="1 Kings 1:51">51</a> Someone told Solomon, &quot;Look, Adonijah is afraid of King Solomon. See, he is holding onto the altar horns and says, &#39;King Solomon must promise me today that he will not kill his servant with a sword.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v52"><a class="verse-num" href="#v52" aria-label="1 Kings 1:52">52</a> Solomon said, &quot;If he acts like a good man, not a single hair from his head will fall to the ground. But if he is found to be wicked, he will die.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v53"><a class="verse-num" href="#v53" aria-label="1 Kings 1:53">53</a> King Solomon sent for him, and they took him from the altar. He came and bowed down to King Solomon, and Solomon told him, &quot;Go to your home.&quot;</p>
