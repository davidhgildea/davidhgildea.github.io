<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Kings 9:1">1</a> When Solomon finished building God&#39;s house, his own palace, and everything he wanted to do,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Kings 9:2">2</a> God appeared to Solomon again, just as he had at Gibeon.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Kings 9:3">3</a> God said to him, &quot;I have heard your prayer and plea that you made before me. I have made this house holy, which you have built, to place my name there forever; and my eyes and heart will always be there.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Kings 9:4">4</a> If you walk before me like your father David did, with a pure heart and honesty, doing all I have told you and following my rules and decisions:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Kings 9:5">5</a> I will make sure your kingdom&#39;s throne in Israel lasts forever, just like I promised your father David, when I said, &quot;Your throne in Israel will always have a man to rule.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Kings 9:6">6</a> If you turn away from following me, you or your children, and do not obey my commands and laws I gave you, but follow and worship other gods:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Kings 9:7">7</a> I will remove Israel from the land I gave them; and this temple I made holy for my name, I will reject; and Israel will become an example and a common saying among all nations.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Kings 9:8">8</a> At this tall house, everyone passing by will be shocked and will hiss. They will ask, &quot;Why did God do this to the land and to this house?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Kings 9:9">9</a> They will say, &#39;Because they left God, who freed their ancestors from Egypt, and followed other gods, worshiping and serving them, that&#39;s why God let all this bad happen to them.&#39;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Kings 9:10">10</a> After twenty years, when Solomon finished building the two buildings, God&#39;s house and the king&#39;s house,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Kings 9:11">11</a> King Solomon gave King Hiram of Tyre twenty cities in Galilee because Hiram had provided Solomon with cedar and fir trees, and gold, as much as he wanted.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Kings 9:12">12</a> Hiram left Tyre to see the towns that Solomon gave him, but he did not like them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 Kings 9:13">13</a> He asked, &quot;What are these towns you&#39;ve given me, my brother?&quot; He named the area Cabul, a name it still has today.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="1 Kings 9:14">14</a> Hiram sent the king 120 talents of gold.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="1 Kings 9:15">15</a> This is why King Solomon collected taxes: to build God&#39;s house, his palace, Millo, the walls of Jerusalem, and the cities of Hazor, Megiddo, and Gezer.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="1 Kings 9:16">16</a> The king of Egypt, Pharaoh, attacked and captured Gezer, burned it, killed the Canaanites living there, and gave it as a gift to his daughter, Solomon&#39;s wife.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="1 Kings 9:17">17</a> Solomon built Gezer and lower Bethhoron.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="1 Kings 9:18">18</a> And Baalath, and Tadmor in the desert, in the country,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="1 Kings 9:19">19</a> Solomon had many storage cities, cities for his chariots, cities for his horsemen, and whatever he wanted to build in Jerusalem, Lebanon, and throughout his lands.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="1 Kings 9:20">20</a> And all the people remaining from the Amorites, Hittites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites, who were not Israelites,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="1 Kings 9:21">21</a> The children who remained in the land after them, whom the Israelites could not completely destroy, Solomon made those pay forced labor taxes that continue to this day.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="1 Kings 9:22">22</a> Solomon did not make slaves of the Israelites; instead, they were soldiers, servants, leaders, commanders, chariot officers, and horsemen.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="1 Kings 9:23">23</a> These were the top supervisors for Solomon&#39;s projects, 550, who managed the workers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="1 Kings 9:24">24</a> Pharaoh&#39;s daughter left David&#39;s city and went to the house that Solomon built for her; then he built Millo.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="1 Kings 9:25">25</a> Solomon made burnt sacrifices and peace sacrifices on the altar he made for God three times a year, and he burned incense on the altar in front of God. So he completed the temple.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="1 Kings 9:26">26</a> King Solomon built a fleet of ships at Eziongeber, near Eloth, on the coast of the Red Sea, in Edom&#39;s territory.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="1 Kings 9:27">27</a> Hiram sent his sailors, who knew the sea well, with Solomon&#39;s servants on the ships.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="1 Kings 9:28">28</a> They went to Ophir, got 420 talents of gold, and brought it to King Solomon.</p>
