<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Kings 3:1">1</a> Solomon formed an alliance with Pharaoh, the king of Egypt, and married Pharaoh&#39;s daughter. He brought her to the city of David until he finished building his palace, God&#39;s house, and the wall around Jerusalem.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Kings 3:2">2</a> People offered sacrifices on high places because no house for God&#39;s name had been built at that time.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Kings 3:3">3</a> Solomon loved God and followed his father David&#39;s laws, but he also offered sacrifices and burned incense on high hills.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Kings 3:4">4</a> The king went to Gibeon to make sacrifices, because that was the most important worship place. Solomon gave a thousand burnt offerings on that altar.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Kings 3:5">5</a> In Gibeon, God appeared to Solomon in a dream at night and said, &quot;Ask for whatever you want me to give you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Kings 3:6">6</a> Solomon said, &quot;You have shown great kindness to your servant, my father David, because he lived honestly and rightly, and was true-hearted with you; and you have kept this kindness for him by giving him a son to take his place as king, just like today.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Kings 3:7">7</a> God, you have made me king after my father David, but I am like a little child and do not know how to lead.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Kings 3:8">8</a> Your servant is among your chosen people, a great number that can&#39;t be counted.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Kings 3:9">9</a> Give me, your servant, a wise heart to govern your people and to know right from wrong, for who can govern this great people of yours?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Kings 3:10">10</a> God was happy that Solomon asked for this.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Kings 3:11">11</a> God said to him, because you asked for this, and did not ask for a long life, nor for wealth, nor for the death of your enemies, but for understanding to make the right decisions;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Kings 3:12">12</a> Look, I did what you asked for: I gave you a wise and understanding heart. There has been no one like you before, and there will be no one like you after.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 Kings 3:13">13</a> I have also given you what you did not ask for—wealth and honor—so no king will be like you all your life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="1 Kings 3:14">14</a> If you follow my ways, obey my laws and commands, like your father David did, I will make your life longer.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="1 Kings 3:15">15</a> Solomon woke up and realized it was a dream. He went to Jerusalem, stood in front of God&#39;s covenant chest, gave burnt offerings and peace offerings, and threw a big dinner for all his workers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="1 Kings 3:16">16</a> Two women who were prostitutes came to the king and stood before him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="1 Kings 3:17">17</a> One woman said, &quot;Oh sir, this woman and I live in the same house, and I gave birth to a baby while she was there.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="1 Kings 3:18">18</a> Three days after I gave birth, this woman also had her baby. We were alone in the house; just the two of us, with no one else there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="1 Kings 3:19">19</a> This woman&#39;s baby died at night because she lay on it too much.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="1 Kings 3:20">20</a> She got up at midnight, took my son while I, your servant, was sleeping, placed him in her arms, and put her dead baby in my arms.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="1 Kings 3:21">21</a> When I got up in the morning to feed my baby, I saw it was dead. But when I looked closely later, I saw it wasn&#39;t the son I had given birth to.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="1 Kings 3:22">22</a> The other woman said, &quot;No, the living child is mine and the dead one is yours.&quot; The first woman insisted, &quot;No, the dead child is yours and the living child is mine.&quot; This is how they argued before the king.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="1 Kings 3:23">23</a> Then the king said, &quot;One woman claims, &#39;This living boy is mine, and your son is dead,&#39; while the other says, &#39;No, your son is dead, and mine is alive.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="1 Kings 3:24">24</a> The king said, &quot;Bring me a sword.&quot; So they brought a sword to the king.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="1 Kings 3:25">25</a> The king said, &quot;Cut the living child in two, and give half to one and half to the other.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="1 Kings 3:26">26</a> The woman with the living baby spoke to the king because she deeply cared for her son and begged, &quot;Please, sir, give her the living baby, don&#39;t kill it.&quot; But the other woman said, &quot;It should belong to neither of us. Cut it in two.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="1 Kings 3:27">27</a> The king replied, &quot;Give her the baby that&#39;s alive, do not kill it; she&#39;s its mother.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="1 Kings 3:28">28</a> All of Israel heard about the king&#39;s decision and they respected the king because they realized God&#39;s wisdom was with him to make fair decisions.</p>
