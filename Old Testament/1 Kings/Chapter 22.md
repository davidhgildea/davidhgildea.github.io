<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Kings 22:1">1</a> They had peace for three years without fighting between Syria and Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Kings 22:2">2</a> In the third year, King Jehoshaphat of Judah went to see the king of Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Kings 22:3">3</a> The king of Israel said to his servants, &quot;Do you know that Ramoth in Gilead belongs to us, yet we do nothing to take it back from the king of Syria?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Kings 22:4">4</a> He said to Jehoshaphat, &quot;Will you join me in the fight at Ramoth-gilead?&quot; Jehoshaphat replied to the king of Israel, &quot;I am with you, my people are your people, and my horses are your horses.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Kings 22:5">5</a> Jehoshaphat said to the king of Israel, &quot;Please ask for God&#39;s guidance today.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Kings 22:6">6</a> The king of Israel brought together about four hundred prophets and asked them, &quot;Should I go to war against Ramothgilead or not?&quot; They replied, &quot;Go, for God will give it to the king.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Kings 22:7">7</a> Jehoshaphat asked, &quot;Isn&#39;t there a prophet of God here we can ask?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Kings 22:8">8</a> The king of Israel told Jehoshaphat, &quot;There is still one man, Micaiah son of Imlah, who can ask God for us. But I hate him because he never predicts anything good about me, only bad.&quot; Jehoshaphat replied, &quot;The king shouldn&#39;t say that.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Kings 22:9">9</a> The king of Israel called an officer and said, &quot;Quickly bring Micaiah, Imlah&#39;s son, here.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Kings 22:10">10</a> The king of Israel and Jehoshaphat, the king of Judah, were sitting on their thrones, dressed in their robes, at an open area by the gate of Samaria, while all the prophets spoke their prophecies in front of them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Kings 22:11">11</a> Zedekiah, Chenaanah&#39;s son, made iron horns and said, &quot;God says, &#39;With these, you will push the Syrians until they are defeated.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Kings 22:12">12</a> All the prophets said the same thing: &quot;Go to Ramothgilead and you will succeed, because God will give it to the king.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 Kings 22:13">13</a> The messenger who went to get Micaiah said to him, &quot;Look, all the prophets are telling the king good news with one voice. Please make your words like theirs and say something good.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="1 Kings 22:14">14</a> Micaiah said, &quot;As God lives, whatever God tells me, I will say.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="1 Kings 22:15">15</a> He went to the king. The king asked him, &quot;Micaiah, should we fight against Ramothgilead or not?&quot; He replied, &quot;Go and succeed, because God will give it to the king.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="1 Kings 22:16">16</a> The king said to him, &quot;How many times must I order you to tell me only the truth by God&#39;s name?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="1 Kings 22:17">17</a> He said, &quot;I saw all Israel spread out on the hills, like sheep without a shepherd. And God said, &#39;These people have no leader; let them each go back to their own home in peace.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="1 Kings 22:18">18</a> The king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, &quot;Didn&#39;t I say he would not predict anything good about me, but only bad?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="1 Kings 22:19">19</a> He said, &quot;Listen to God&#39;s message: I saw God sitting on his throne with all the heavenly beings standing around him on his right and on his left.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="1 Kings 22:20">20</a> God asked, &quot;Who will convince Ahab to attack Ramothgilead and die there?&quot; One suggested this way, and another suggested that way.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="1 Kings 22:21">21</a> A spirit came out, stood before God, and said, &quot;I will convince him.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="1 Kings 22:22">22</a> God asked him, &quot;How?&quot; He replied, &quot;I will go out and be a lying spirit in the mouths of all his prophets.&quot; God said, &quot;You will persuade him and succeed. Go out and do it.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="1 Kings 22:23">23</a> Now, look, God has placed a lying spirit in the mouths of all your prophets, and God has said bad things about you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="1 Kings 22:24">24</a> But Zedekiah, son of Chenaanah, came forward, hit Micaiah on the face, and said, &quot;How did God&#39;s Spirit leave me to talk to you?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="1 Kings 22:25">25</a> Micaiah said, &quot;Look, you will see on that day, when you go into a room to hide.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="1 Kings 22:26">26</a> The king of Israel said, &quot;Take Micaiah and take him back to Amon, the city&#39;s governor, and to Joash, the king&#39;s son.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="1 Kings 22:27">27</a> Say, &quot;The king orders, put this man in jail and give him only poor bread and water until I return safely.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="1 Kings 22:28">28</a> Micaiah said, &quot;If you come back safely, God has not spoken through me.&quot; And he said, &quot;Listen, everyone.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="1 Kings 22:29">29</a> The king of Israel and King Jehoshaphat of Judah went to Ramoth-gilead.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="1 Kings 22:30">30</a> The king of Israel told Jehoshaphat, &quot;I will hide who I am and go into the fight; but you wear your royal clothes.&quot; So the king of Israel disguised himself and joined the battle.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="1 Kings 22:31">31</a> The king of Syria ordered his thirty-two chariot commanders, saying, &quot;Do not fight with anyone, important or not, except the king of Israel.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="1 Kings 22:32">32</a> When the chariot commanders saw Jehoshaphat, they thought, &quot;This must be the king of Israel.&quot; So they went to attack him, and Jehoshaphat shouted for help.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="1 Kings 22:33">33</a> When the chariot commanders realized he was not the king of Israel, they stopped chasing him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="1 Kings 22:34">34</a> A man randomly shot an arrow and hit the king of Israel in the armor gaps. So he told his chariot driver, &quot;Turn around and take me out of the battle; I&#39;m hurt.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="1 Kings 22:35">35</a> The fight grew fiercer that day, and the king was propped up in his chariot facing the Syrians, and he died in the evening; blood flowed from his injury into the middle of the chariot.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="1 Kings 22:36">36</a> A message went through the camp as the sun was setting, saying, &quot;Everyone go to your own city and your own country.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="1 Kings 22:37">37</a> The king died and was taken to Samaria; they buried him there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="1 Kings 22:38">38</a> Someone washed the chariot at the Samaria pool, and dogs licked up his blood. They also washed his armor, just as God had said.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="1 Kings 22:39">39</a> The other things Ahab did, what he built, including the ivory house and cities, are they not recorded in the book of the history of Israel&#39;s kings?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="1 Kings 22:40">40</a> So Ahab died as his ancestors did; and his son Ahaziah became king after him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="1 Kings 22:41">41</a> Jehoshaphat, Asa&#39;s son, became king of Judah in the fourth year of Ahab&#39;s rule over Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="1 Kings 22:42">42</a> Jehoshaphat was 35 years old when he became king; he ruled for 25 years in Jerusalem. His mother&#39;s name was Azubah, and her father was Shilhi.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v43"><a class="verse-num" href="#v43" aria-label="1 Kings 22:43">43</a> He followed the example of his father Asa and did not turn away from it, doing what was right in God&#39;s eyes. However, the high places were not removed; the people still offered and burned incense there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v44"><a class="verse-num" href="#v44" aria-label="1 Kings 22:44">44</a> Jehoshaphat made peace with the king of Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v45"><a class="verse-num" href="#v45" aria-label="1 Kings 22:45">45</a> The other things Jehoshaphat did, his power, and his battles are written in the history book of Judah&#39;s kings, aren&#39;t they?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v46"><a class="verse-num" href="#v46" aria-label="1 Kings 22:46">46</a> He removed the remaining people of sinful practices left from his father Asa&#39;s time from the land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v47"><a class="verse-num" href="#v47" aria-label="1 Kings 22:47">47</a> At that time, Edom had no king; a deputy ruled instead.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v48"><a class="verse-num" href="#v48" aria-label="1 Kings 22:48">48</a> Jehoshaphat built ships to sail to Ophir for gold, but they never went because the ships were wrecked at Eziongeber.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v49"><a class="verse-num" href="#v49" aria-label="1 Kings 22:49">49</a> Ahaziah son of Ahab said to Jehoshaphat, &quot;Let my men go with your men on the ships.&quot; But Jehoshaphat did not agree.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v50"><a class="verse-num" href="#v50" aria-label="1 Kings 22:50">50</a> Jehoshaphat died like his ancestors and was buried with them in David&#39;s city. Jehoram, his son, became king after him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v51"><a class="verse-num" href="#v51" aria-label="1 Kings 22:51">51</a> Ahaziah, Ahab&#39;s son, became king of Israel in Samaria in the seventeenth year of Jehoshaphat&#39;s rule in Judah, and he ruled over Israel for two years.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v52"><a class="verse-num" href="#v52" aria-label="1 Kings 22:52">52</a> He did wrong in God&#39;s eyes, following the path of his father, his mother, and Jeroboam, Nebat&#39;s son, who led Israel into sin.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v53"><a class="verse-num" href="#v53" aria-label="1 Kings 22:53">53</a> He worshiped Baal and made him offerings, and angered God, just as his father had done.</p>
