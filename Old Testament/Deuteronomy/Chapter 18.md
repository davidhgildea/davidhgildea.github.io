<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Deuteronomy 18:1">1</a> The Levite priests and the whole Levi tribe won&#39;t have any property or share with Israel. They will eat the food given to God by fire and his share.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Deuteronomy 18:2">2</a> So they will not receive land among their brothers; God is their share, as he told them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Deuteronomy 18:3">3</a> The priest should get this from the people who give a sacrifice, whether it&#39;s a cow or sheep: they must give the priest the shoulder, both cheeks, and the stomach.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Deuteronomy 18:4">4</a> Give the first of your grain, wine, oil, and the first wool from your sheep to him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Deuteronomy 18:5">5</a> God has chosen him from all your tribes to serve in God&#39;s name, him and his sons forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Deuteronomy 18:6">6</a> If a Levite arrives from any of your towns throughout Israel where he has been staying, and he comes with all his heart&#39;s desire to the location God has chosen;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Deuteronomy 18:7">7</a> He will serve in God&#39;s name, like his fellow Levite priests who serve before God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Deuteronomy 18:8">8</a> They will have equal shares to eat, in addition to what comes from selling their family inheritance.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Deuteronomy 18:9">9</a> When you go into the land that God is giving you, do not learn to do the terrible things that those nations do.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Deuteronomy 18:10">10</a> No one among you should make their son or daughter go through fire, use magic, tell the future, cast spells, or be a witch.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Deuteronomy 18:11">11</a> A person who charms, talks to spirits, practices magic, or communicates with the dead.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Deuteronomy 18:12">12</a> Everyone who does these things is hated by God, and because of these hateful acts, God pushes them away from you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Deuteronomy 18:13">13</a> You should be without fault with God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Deuteronomy 18:14">14</a> The countries you will take over listened to fortune-tellers and diviners, but God has not allowed you to do that.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Deuteronomy 18:15">15</a> God will send a Prophet from among your own people, like me; you should listen to him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Deuteronomy 18:16">16</a> Just as you asked God at Horeb on the gathering day, saying, &quot;I don&#39;t want to hear God&#39;s voice again, nor see this big fire, so that I won&#39;t die.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Deuteronomy 18:17">17</a> God told me, &quot;They have said good things.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Deuteronomy 18:18">18</a> I will bring up a Prophet from among their brothers, like you, and will put my words in his mouth; he will tell them everything I command him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Deuteronomy 18:19">19</a> If anyone doesn&#39;t listen to my words that he speaks in my name, I will hold them accountable.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Deuteronomy 18:20">20</a> But the prophet who dares to say something in my name that I didn&#39;t tell him to say, or speaks in the name of other gods, will die.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Deuteronomy 18:21">21</a> If you think, &quot;How can we tell what words God hasn&#39;t spoken?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Deuteronomy 18:22">22</a> When a prophet claims to speak for God, if what they say doesn&#39;t happen, it is not a message from God. The prophet spoke arrogantly. You should not fear them.</p>
