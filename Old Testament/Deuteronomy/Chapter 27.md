<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Deuteronomy 27:1">1</a> Moses and the leaders of Israel told the people, &quot;Follow all the rules I give you today.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Deuteronomy 27:2">2</a> On the day you cross the Jordan into the land God is giving you, you must set up large stones and cover them with plaster.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Deuteronomy 27:3">3</a> Write all the words of this law on them when you cross over, so you can enter the land that God gives you, a land flowing with milk and honey, just as the God of your ancestors promised you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Deuteronomy 27:4">4</a> So when you cross the Jordan River, set up these stones I tell you about today on Mount Ebal and cover them with plaster.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Deuteronomy 27:5">5</a> There you will make an altar for God using stones. Do not use any iron tools on the stones.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Deuteronomy 27:6">6</a> Make God&#39;s altar with unbroken stones, and offer burnt sacrifices to God on it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Deuteronomy 27:7">7</a> Give peace offerings, eat them there, and be happy before God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Deuteronomy 27:8">8</a> You should write all the words of this law clearly on the stones.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Deuteronomy 27:9">9</a> Moses and the Levite priests spoke to all Israel, saying, &quot;Pay attention and listen, Israel; today you have become the people of God.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Deuteronomy 27:10">10</a> You must listen to God&#39;s voice and do what he commands, including his rules that I tell you today.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Deuteronomy 27:11">11</a> On that day, Moses gave the people these instructions:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Deuteronomy 27:12">12</a> These will stand on Mount Gerizim to bless the people when you have crossed the Jordan: Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, Joseph, and Benjamin.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Deuteronomy 27:13">13</a> These will stand on Mount Ebal to pronounce curses: Reuben, Gad, Asher, Zebulun, Dan, and Naphtali.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Deuteronomy 27:14">14</a> The Levites will speak out and say to all the Israelite men in a loud voice,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Deuteronomy 27:15">15</a> May the person who makes any carved or metal idol, which God hates, made by an artist, and hides it, be cursed. And all the people will agree and say, &quot;Amen.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Deuteronomy 27:16">16</a> May the one who disrespects his father or mother be cursed. And all the people shall agree, saying, &quot;Yes.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Deuteronomy 27:17">17</a> May the person who moves his neighbor&#39;s property marker be cursed. All the people shall agree by saying, &quot;Amen.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Deuteronomy 27:18">18</a> May the person who leads the blind astray be cursed. And everyone will agree by saying, &quot;Amen.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Deuteronomy 27:19">19</a> May the one who twists the justice due to foreigners, orphans, and widows be cursed. And let all the people say, &quot;So be it.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Deuteronomy 27:20">20</a> May the person who sleeps with his father&#39;s wife be cursed, for he shames his father. And all the people will agree by saying, Amen.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Deuteronomy 27:21">21</a> A person who sleeps with an animal is cursed. Everyone must agree to this by saying &quot;Amen.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Deuteronomy 27:22">22</a> Let the person who sleeps with his half-sister or stepsister be cursed, and everyone will agree by saying, &quot;Yes.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Deuteronomy 27:23">23</a> May the person who sleeps with his mother-in-law be cursed. And everyone will agree, saying, &quot;Yes.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Deuteronomy 27:24">24</a> Someone who secretly hurts their neighbor is cursed. And everyone will agree by saying, &quot;Yes.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Deuteronomy 27:25">25</a> May the person who takes money to kill an innocent be cursed. And everyone will agree.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Deuteronomy 27:26">26</a> Let the person who does not follow all the rules of these laws be cursed. And everyone will agree by saying, &quot;Amen.&quot;</p>
