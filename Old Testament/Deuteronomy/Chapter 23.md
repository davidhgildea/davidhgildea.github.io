<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Deuteronomy 23:1">1</a> Anyone with damaged testicles or a severed penis cannot join God&#39;s assembly.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Deuteronomy 23:2">2</a> A child born outside of marriage cannot join God&#39;s assembly; not even their descendants up to ten generations can join.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Deuteronomy 23:3">3</a> An Ammonite or Moabite may not join God&#39;s people; not even their descendants to the tenth generation can ever join.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Deuteronomy 23:4">4</a> Because they did not meet you with food and water when you left Egypt, and because they paid Balaam, son of Beor from Pethor in Mesopotamia, to curse you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Deuteronomy 23:5">5</a> But God didn&#39;t listen to Balaam; instead, God changed the curse into a blessing for you, because God loved you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Deuteronomy 23:6">6</a> Do not look for their peace or well-being at any time, forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Deuteronomy 23:7">7</a> Do not dislike an Edomite; he is your relative. Do not dislike an Egyptian; you were once a foreigner in his country.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Deuteronomy 23:8">8</a> The children born to them will be allowed to join God&#39;s community in their third generation.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Deuteronomy 23:9">9</a> When the army goes out against your enemies, stay away from all evil things.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Deuteronomy 23:10">10</a> If anyone among you becomes unclean because of something that happens to him at night, he must go outside the camp and not return.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Deuteronomy 23:11">11</a> When evening arrives, he must wash himself with water; and after sunset, he can return to the camp.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Deuteronomy 23:12">12</a> You must have a place outside the camp to which you can go as needed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Deuteronomy 23:13">13</a> You should carry a tool with your weapon. When you need to use the bathroom outside, dig a hole with it, do your business, then cover it up afterwards.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Deuteronomy 23:14">14</a> God walks in the middle of your camp to save you and to defeat your enemies ahead of you; so your camp must be sacred so that God does not see anything dirty in you and leave you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Deuteronomy 23:15">15</a> Do not return to their owner a servant who has run away from them to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Deuteronomy 23:16">16</a> He will live with you, in the place he picks within your gates, where he prefers: do not mistreat him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Deuteronomy 23:17">17</a> No woman of Israel shall be a prostitute, and no man of Israel shall be a male prostitute.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Deuteronomy 23:18">18</a> Do not bring money earned from a prostitute or from selling a dog to God&#39;s house to keep a promise, because both of these are disgusting to God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Deuteronomy 23:19">19</a> Do not charge your brother interest on money, food, or anything else that you lend with interest.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Deuteronomy 23:20">20</a> You may charge interest when you lend to a stranger, but not to your own people, so that God may bless everything you do in the land you are entering to own.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Deuteronomy 23:21">21</a> When you make a promise to God, you must not delay in fulfilling it, because God will definitely expect it from you; otherwise, it will be a sin for you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Deuteronomy 23:22">22</a> If you choose not to make a promise, you will not be sinning.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Deuteronomy 23:23">23</a> Do what you have said; fulfill your voluntary promise to God, just as you vowed with your own words.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Deuteronomy 23:24">24</a> When you go into your neighbor&#39;s vineyard, you can eat as many grapes as you want for your own enjoyment, but you must not take any away in a container.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Deuteronomy 23:25">25</a> When you go into your neighbor&#39;s grain field, you can pick ears by hand, but you must not cut your neighbor&#39;s grain with a sickle.</p>
