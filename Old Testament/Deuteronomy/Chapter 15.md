<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Deuteronomy 15:1">1</a> Every seven years, you must forgive debts.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Deuteronomy 15:2">2</a> This is how the release works: Everyone who has lent something to their neighbor must forgive the debt; they must not demand it back from their neighbor or relative, because it is known as God&#39;s time for forgiveness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Deuteronomy 15:3">3</a> You can require a foreigner to repay a debt, but you must let your own people go free without repayment.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Deuteronomy 15:4">4</a> Help those in need until no one is poor among you, because God will bless you greatly in the land God gives you as your own.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Deuteronomy 15:5">5</a> If you listen carefully to God&#39;s voice and follow all these commands I give you today.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Deuteronomy 15:6">6</a> God blesses you as he promised, and you will lend to many nations but not borrow; you will rule over many nations, but they won&#39;t rule over you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Deuteronomy 15:7">7</a> If there is a poor man from your people in any of your towns in the land God is giving you, do not be hard-hearted or close your hand to your poor brother.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Deuteronomy 15:8">8</a> You should open your hand generously to him and lend him enough for what he needs.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Deuteronomy 15:9">9</a> Be careful not to think evil thoughts, like &quot;The seventh year, the year to forgive debts, is near,&quot; and then look badly at your poor brother and give him nothing. If he calls out to God against you, it will be a sin for you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Deuteronomy 15:10">10</a> You must definitely give to him, and don&#39;t feel sad when you do because God will bless you in everything you do and in all your efforts.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Deuteronomy 15:11">11</a> The poor will always be in the land, so I command you to generously help your brother, the poor, and the needy in your land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Deuteronomy 15:12">12</a> If your brother, a Hebrew man or woman, is sold to you and works for you for six years, you must set them free in the seventh year.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Deuteronomy 15:13">13</a> When you set him free, do not send him away with nothing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Deuteronomy 15:14">14</a> You should generously provide for him from your flock, your grain, and your winepress. Give to him from the blessings God has given you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Deuteronomy 15:15">15</a> Remember you were a slave in Egypt, and God saved you; that&#39;s why I give you this command today.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Deuteronomy 15:16">16</a> If he tells you, &quot;I will not leave you,&quot; because he loves you and your household and is happy with you;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Deuteronomy 15:17">17</a> Then you will take a sharp tool and pierce his ear to the door, and he will be your servant forever. Do the same for your female servant.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Deuteronomy 15:18">18</a> It won&#39;t feel difficult for you when you set him free; for he was as good as two paid workers for you, working six years: and God will bless you in everything you do.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Deuteronomy 15:19">19</a> You must set apart for God all the firstborn male animals from your herds and flocks. Don&#39;t use the firstborn of your cow for work, and don&#39;t cut the wool of the firstborn of your sheep.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Deuteronomy 15:20">20</a> You will eat it in front of God every year at the place God will choose, you and your family.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Deuteronomy 15:21">21</a> If it has any flaws, like being lame or blind or having any other serious defect, do not offer it as a sacrifice to God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Deuteronomy 15:22">22</a> You should eat it inside your gates: both people who are unclean and those who are clean can eat it, just like they would eat a gazelle or a deer.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Deuteronomy 15:23">23</a> You must not eat the blood; you must pour it out on the ground like water.</p>
