<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Deuteronomy 21:1">1</a> If a dead person is found in the land that God is giving you, lying in the field, and nobody knows who killed him:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Deuteronomy 21:2">2</a> Then your leaders and judges will come out, and they will measure to the towns around where the person was killed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Deuteronomy 21:3">3</a> The closest city to the dead man, the city&#39;s leaders must take a cow that hasn&#39;t worked or pulled a plow.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Deuteronomy 21:4">4</a> The leaders of the city must take the cow to a rocky valley that has not been plowed or planted, and they must cut off the cow&#39;s head there in the valley.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Deuteronomy 21:5">5</a> The Levi priests should come forward; God has chosen them to serve Him and to give blessings in God&#39;s name. They will decide all arguments and cases.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Deuteronomy 21:6">6</a> All the leaders of the city closest to the man who was killed must wash their hands over the cow that is killed in the valley.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Deuteronomy 21:7">7</a> They will reply, &quot;Our hands did not kill this person, and our eyes did not see it.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Deuteronomy 21:8">8</a> Be kind, God, to your people Israel, whom you have saved, and do not blame them for the killing of innocent people. Then they will be forgiven for that blood.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Deuteronomy 21:9">9</a> Do what is right to clear yourself of any blame for innocent blood, just as God sees right.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Deuteronomy 21:10">10</a> When you go out to fight your enemies, and God gives them to you, and you take them as prisoners,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Deuteronomy 21:11">11</a> If you see a beautiful woman among the prisoners and want her as your wife;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Deuteronomy 21:12">12</a> You will bring her to your house, and she will shave her head and trim her nails.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Deuteronomy 21:13">13</a> She must take off her captive clothes, stay in your house, and cry for her parents for a month. After that, you can marry her, and she will be your wife.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Deuteronomy 21:14">14</a> If you no longer like her, you must let her go wherever she wants; do not sell her for money or treat her as an item to sell, because you have dishonored her.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Deuteronomy 21:15">15</a> If a man has two wives, one he loves and one he does not, and they both have children for him, the loved and the unloved; and if the oldest son is the son of the unloved wife:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Deuteronomy 21:16">16</a> When he gives his children what he owns, he must not favor the son of the woman he loves over his first son by the woman he does not love.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Deuteronomy 21:17">17</a> He must recognize the son from the unloved wife as the firstborn, giving him twice as much as he has because he was the father&#39;s first sign of strength; this son has the right of the firstborn.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Deuteronomy 21:18">18</a> If a man has a son who is stubborn and refuses to obey his father or his mother, and does not listen to them even after they correct him:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Deuteronomy 21:19">19</a> His father and mother will take him and bring him to the city elders at the town gate.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Deuteronomy 21:20">20</a> They will tell the city elders, &quot;Our son is stubborn and won&#39;t listen to us. He eats and drinks too much.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Deuteronomy 21:21">21</a> Everyone from his town must throw stones at him until he dies. This removes the evil from your community, and all of Israel will hear about it and be afraid.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Deuteronomy 21:22">22</a> If a man has done something that deserves death and he is to be executed, and you hang him on a tree:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Deuteronomy 21:23">23</a> Do not leave his body on the tree overnight; bury him that same day because anyone hung on a tree is under God&#39;s curse. Do this so that your land, which God is giving you as an inheritance, will not become unclean.</p>
