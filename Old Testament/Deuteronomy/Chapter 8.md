<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Deuteronomy 8:1">1</a> Follow all the commands I give you today so you can live, grow, and take over the land God promised to your ancestors.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Deuteronomy 8:2">2</a> Remember how God guided you through the wilderness for forty years, to humble and test you, and to see if you would follow His commands.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Deuteronomy 8:3">3</a> God made you humble, let you feel hunger, and gave you manna to eat, a food you and your ancestors didn&#39;t know. This was to teach you that people don&#39;t live by bread alone, but by everything God says.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Deuteronomy 8:4">4</a> Your clothes did not wear out and your feet did not swell during these forty years.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Deuteronomy 8:5">5</a> You should also think to yourself that just as a father disciplines his child, so God disciplines you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Deuteronomy 8:6">6</a> So you must follow God&#39;s commands, live as He wants, and respect Him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Deuteronomy 8:7">7</a> God is bringing you into a good land with streams, springs, and deep waters that flow in the valleys and hills.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Deuteronomy 8:8">8</a> A land with wheat, barley, grapevines, fig trees, and pomegranates; a land with olive oil and honey.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Deuteronomy 8:9">9</a> A place where you will eat bread without shortage, you will not lack anything; a place with iron stones and hills where you can dig out copper.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Deuteronomy 8:10">10</a> When you have eaten and are full, then you should thank God for the good land he has given you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Deuteronomy 8:11">11</a> Be careful not to forget God by not following his commands, rules, and laws that I give you today.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Deuteronomy 8:12">12</a> Be careful not to forget God when you have eaten your fill, built nice homes, and live in them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Deuteronomy 8:13">13</a> When your herds and flocks grow, and your silver and gold increase, and everything you own increases;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Deuteronomy 8:14">14</a> Then your heart may become proud, and you forget God who brought you out of Egypt, out of slavery.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Deuteronomy 8:15">15</a> Who guided you through the vast and scary desert with poisonous snakes and scorpions, and where there was no water; who made water come out of the hard rock for you;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Deuteronomy 8:16">16</a> God fed you in the desert with manna, unknown to your ancestors, to humble you and test you, to ultimately benefit you in the end.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Deuteronomy 8:17">17</a> And you think in your heart, My strength and the power of my hand have earned me this wealth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Deuteronomy 8:18">18</a> Remember God, because He gives you the strength to become wealthy, so that He can keep the promise He made to your ancestors, just as it is today.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Deuteronomy 8:19">19</a> If you forget God, follow other gods, serve and worship them, I warn you today that you will certainly be destroyed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Deuteronomy 8:20">20</a> Just like the countries God destroys before you, you too will die if you don&#39;t listen to God&#39;s voice.</p>
