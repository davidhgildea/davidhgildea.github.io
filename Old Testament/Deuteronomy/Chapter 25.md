<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Deuteronomy 25:1">1</a> If people have a dispute and go to court, the judges should decide the case, declare the innocent person right, and find the guilty person at fault.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Deuteronomy 25:2">2</a> If a bad person deserves punishment, the judge will make him lie down and be hit in front of him, as many times as his wrong act deserves.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Deuteronomy 25:3">3</a> He may be hit with up to forty lashes, but no more, so that your brother is not disgraced in your eyes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Deuteronomy 25:4">4</a> Do not cover the ox&#39;s mouth when it is working with the grain.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Deuteronomy 25:5">5</a> If two brothers live together and one dies without having children, his widow should not marry an outsider. Her husband&#39;s brother should marry her, fulfilling the brother-in-law&#39;s responsibility.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Deuteronomy 25:6">6</a> The first child she has will take his dead brother&#39;s place so his name will not be forgotten in Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Deuteronomy 25:7">7</a> If the man does not want to marry his brother&#39;s widow, she should go to the town leaders at the gate and say, &quot;My husband&#39;s brother won&#39;t continue his family name in Israel; he refuses his duty to me as my husband&#39;s brother.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Deuteronomy 25:8">8</a> The leaders of his town shall call him and talk to him; and if he insists and says, I don&#39;t want to marry her;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Deuteronomy 25:9">9</a> The brother&#39;s wife must come to him before the elders, take off his shoe, spit in his face, and say, &quot;This is what happens to a man who won&#39;t carry on his brother&#39;s family.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Deuteronomy 25:10">10</a> In Israel, people will call his family &#39;The House of the Man Whose Shoe Was Taken Off.&#39;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Deuteronomy 25:11">11</a> When men fight and one&#39;s wife tries to save her husband from the one hitting him by reaching out and grabbing his private parts:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Deuteronomy 25:12">12</a> Then you must cut off her hand and show her no pity.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Deuteronomy 25:13">13</a> You must not have different weights, big and small, in your bag.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Deuteronomy 25:14">14</a> Do not keep different sized measures, large and small, in your house.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Deuteronomy 25:15">15</a> You must use accurate and fair weights and measures so that you may live a long time in the land God is giving you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Deuteronomy 25:16">16</a> Everyone who does these things, and all who act unfairly, are hated by God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Deuteronomy 25:17">17</a> Remember what Amalek did to you on your journey after you left Egypt.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Deuteronomy 25:18">18</a> He attacked you on the journey, hitting all the weakest people at the back when you were tired and weak; he did not respect God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Deuteronomy 25:19">19</a> So when God has given you peace from all your enemies around, in the land God gives you to own, you must erase all memory of Amalek from under the sky; do not forget this.</p>
