<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Deuteronomy 34:1">1</a> Moses went up from the Moab plains to Mount Nebo, to the top of Pisgah, across from Jericho. God showed him all the land of Gilead, as far as Dan.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Deuteronomy 34:2">2</a> All Naphtali, Ephraim&#39;s land, Manasseh, and all Judah&#39;s land, up to the farthest sea,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Deuteronomy 34:3">3</a> To the south, the flat land of Jericho&#39;s valley, the city with palm trees, up to Zoar.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Deuteronomy 34:4">4</a> God said to him, &quot;This is the land that I promised to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. I said I would give it to your descendants. I have let you see it with your own eyes, but you will not cross over into it.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Deuteronomy 34:5">5</a> Moses, God&#39;s servant, died in Moab as God said.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Deuteronomy 34:6">6</a> He buried him in a valley in Moab, near Bethpeor, but no one knows his grave location to this day.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Deuteronomy 34:7">7</a> Moses was 120 years old when he died; his eyesight was still clear, and he was still strong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Deuteronomy 34:8">8</a> The Israelites cried for Moses in the Moab plains for thirty days; after that, the time for crying and grieving for Moses was over.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Deuteronomy 34:9">9</a> Joshua, son of Nun, was filled with the spirit of wisdom because Moses had put his hands on him; so, the Israelites listened to him and did what God had told Moses.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Deuteronomy 34:10">10</a> No prophet like Moses has since appeared in Israel, someone whom God knew personally.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Deuteronomy 34:11">11</a> In all the miracles and amazing things that God told him to do in Egypt, to Pharaoh, his officials, and his whole country,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Deuteronomy 34:12">12</a> And with all the power and amazing acts that Moses performed for all Israel to see.</p>
