<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Deuteronomy 11:1">1</a> So you must love God, follow his instructions, laws, decisions, and commands always.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Deuteronomy 11:2">2</a> Understand today: I am not talking to your children who have not known or seen how God disciplines, his greatness, his powerful hand, and his extended arm.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Deuteronomy 11:3">3</a> God&#39;s miracles and what he did in Egypt before Pharaoh, the king of Egypt, and his whole country;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Deuteronomy 11:4">4</a> God did to Egypt&#39;s army, their horses, and their chariots; how He made the Red Sea&#39;s water flood over them as they chased you, and how God has destroyed them up to today;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Deuteronomy 11:5">5</a> And what he did for you in the desert until you arrived here;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Deuteronomy 11:6">6</a> And what happened to Dathan and Abiram, Eliab&#39;s sons from the Reuben tribe: how the ground opened up and swallowed them, their families, their tents, and all their belongings in front of all Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Deuteronomy 11:7">7</a> But your eyes have seen all the great things God did.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Deuteronomy 11:8">8</a> So you must obey all the rules I give you today, so that you can be strong and take over the land you are going to live in.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Deuteronomy 11:9">9</a> So that you may live longer in the land, which God promised to your ancestors to give to them and their children, a land filled with milk and honey.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Deuteronomy 11:10">10</a> The land you are going to make your own is not like Egypt, where you came from. There you planted your seeds and used your feet to water them, like a herb garden.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Deuteronomy 11:11">11</a> The land where you are going to take over has hills and valleys and gets its water from the rain that comes from the sky.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Deuteronomy 11:12">12</a> A land that God takes care of: God&#39;s eyes are always on it, from the start of the year to the end of the year.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Deuteronomy 11:13">13</a> If you carefully listen to my commands that I give you today, to love God and serve Him with all your heart and soul, it will happen.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Deuteronomy 11:14">14</a> I will send rain to your land at the right time, both early and late rains, so you can collect your grain, wine, and oil.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Deuteronomy 11:15">15</a> I will make grass grow in your fields for your cattle so you can eat and be satisfied.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Deuteronomy 11:16">16</a> Be careful, don&#39;t let your heart be fooled, and don&#39;t turn away to serve other gods and bow down to them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Deuteronomy 11:17">17</a> If you make God angry, He might close the sky so it won&#39;t rain, and the ground won&#39;t grow food, and you might quickly die in the good land God is giving you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Deuteronomy 11:18">18</a> Keep these words of mine in your heart and soul, tie them on your hand as a reminder, and let them be like a band on your forehead.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Deuteronomy 11:19">19</a> Teach your children these things. Talk about them at home, when you travel, when you go to bed, and when you get up.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Deuteronomy 11:20">20</a> Write them on the door frames of your house and on your gates.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Deuteronomy 11:21">21</a> May your life and your children&#39;s lives be long in the land that God promised to your ancestors, like endless days in heaven on earth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Deuteronomy 11:22">22</a> If you carefully follow all these commands I give you, to do them, to love God, to walk in all his ways, and to hold fast to him;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Deuteronomy 11:23">23</a> God will force all these nations to leave before you, and you will take over greater and stronger nations than you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Deuteronomy 11:24">24</a> Every place where you step will belong to you: from the desert and Lebanon, from the Euphrates River to the farthest sea will be your border.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Deuteronomy 11:25">25</a> No one will be able to resist you: for God will make everyone fear and dread you wherever you go, just as he has promised you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Deuteronomy 11:26">26</a> Look, today I give you a choice between a blessing and a curse.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Deuteronomy 11:27">27</a> You will be blessed if you follow the commands of God that I give you today.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Deuteronomy 11:28">28</a> And a curse if you do not follow God&#39;s commandments, but turn away from the path I tell you today, to follow different gods, which you do not know.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Deuteronomy 11:29">29</a> When God brings you to the land you will take over, you should speak the blessings on Mount Gerizim and the curses on Mount Ebal.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Deuteronomy 11:30">30</a> Aren&#39;t they across the Jordan River, along the path where the sun sets, in the Canaanites&#39; land, near Gilgal and next to the Moreh fields?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Deuteronomy 11:31">31</a> You will cross the Jordan River to take over the land that God gives you, and you will own it and live there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Deuteronomy 11:32">32</a> Obey all the rules and decisions I am giving you today.</p>
