<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Deuteronomy 32:1">1</a> Listen, heavens, and I will talk; and listen, earth, to what I say.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Deuteronomy 32:2">2</a> My teaching will fall like rain, my words will settle like dew, like a light rain on new plants, and like showers on the grass.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Deuteronomy 32:3">3</a> I will tell everyone about God&#39;s name: say our God is great.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Deuteronomy 32:4">4</a> He is the Rock, His work is perfect; all His ways are fair. A God of truth, not doing wrong, just and upright is He.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Deuteronomy 32:5">5</a> They have made themselves bad; they are not like his children because they are a wrong and twisted group.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Deuteronomy 32:6">6</a> Are you repaying God like this, you foolish and senseless people? Isn&#39;t He your father who created you? Didn&#39;t He make and sustain you?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Deuteronomy 32:7">7</a> Think about the past and reflect on the years gone by. Ask your father, and he will teach you; talk to the older people, and they will explain it to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Deuteronomy 32:8">8</a> When God divided the lands among the nations, when he split up the people, he set the borders based on how many Israelites there were.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Deuteronomy 32:9">9</a> God&#39;s share is his people; Jacob is the group he has chosen to receive his inheritance.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Deuteronomy 32:10">10</a> He found him in a desert land, in a noisy empty wilderness; he led him around, taught him, and protected him like the pupil of his eye.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Deuteronomy 32:11">11</a> Like an eagle that stirs up its nest, hovers over its young, spreads its wings, takes them, and carries them on its wings.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Deuteronomy 32:12">12</a> God alone guided him, and there was no other god with him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Deuteronomy 32:13">13</a> God let him ride on the earth&#39;s high places, to eat the crops from the fields; God let him taste honey from the rock and oil from the hard stone.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Deuteronomy 32:14">14</a> Butter from cows, and sheep&#39;s milk, with lamb&#39;s fat, and Bashan-bred rams, and goats, with wheat&#39;s creamy richness; and you drank grape juice, pure and rich.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Deuteronomy 32:15">15</a> Jeshurun grew fat and rebelled; you became fat, you got thick, you were filled with fat; then you left God who created you, and didn&#39;t value the God who saved you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Deuteronomy 32:16">16</a> They made God jealous with foreign gods, and with hateful acts they made him angry.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Deuteronomy 32:17">17</a> They offered sacrifices to demons, not to God; to gods they did not know, to new gods that were recently made, that your ancestors did not fear.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Deuteronomy 32:18">18</a> You have forgotten the God who gave you life and ignored the one who made you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Deuteronomy 32:19">19</a> When God saw it, he hated them because they angered his sons and daughters.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Deuteronomy 32:20">20</a> He said, &quot;I will turn away from them and see what their future holds, because they are a difficult and unfaithful generation.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Deuteronomy 32:21">21</a> They made me jealous with things that are not God; they angered me with their useless actions. And I will make them jealous with those who are not a people; I will make them angry with a foolish nation.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Deuteronomy 32:22">22</a> My anger has started a fire that will burn to the deepest hell, destroy the earth and its crops, and set the mountain bases on fire.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Deuteronomy 32:23">23</a> I will bring many troubles upon them; I will use up my arrows against them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Deuteronomy 32:24">24</a> They will suffer from severe hunger, extreme heat, and harsh destruction. I will also let wild animals attack them, and snakes from the ground will poison them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Deuteronomy 32:25">25</a> Outside, the sword, and inside, fear, will kill the young man and the young woman, as well as the baby and the old man.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Deuteronomy 32:26">26</a> I said I would spread them out and make people forget them completely.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Deuteronomy 32:27">27</a> If I were not worried about our enemies&#39; anger, they might act wrongly and claim, &quot;We won by our own power, and God didn&#39;t do this.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Deuteronomy 32:28">28</a> They are a nation without guidance, and they have no understanding.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Deuteronomy 32:29">29</a> If only they were wise and understood this, so they would think about their future!</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Deuteronomy 32:30">30</a> How can one person chase a thousand, or two people make ten thousand run away, unless their protector has abandoned them and God has trapped them?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Deuteronomy 32:31">31</a> Their rock is not like our God, even our enemies agree.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Deuteronomy 32:32">32</a> Their vine comes from Sodom&#39;s vines, and Gomorrah&#39;s fields: their grapes are bitter, their bunches are sour.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Deuteronomy 32:33">33</a> Their wine is like dragon poison and very cruel snake venom.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Deuteronomy 32:34">34</a> Isn&#39;t this kept with me and sealed in my treasures?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Deuteronomy 32:35">35</a> I am in charge of revenge and payback; their feet will slip when the time comes: because their disaster day is near, and the things that will happen to them will come quickly.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Deuteronomy 32:36">36</a> God will judge his people and change his mind for his servants when he sees their strength is gone and no one is confined or remaining.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Deuteronomy 32:37">37</a> He will ask, &quot;Where are their gods, the rock they trusted in?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Deuteronomy 32:38">38</a> Who ate the best parts of your offerings and drank your wine gifts? Let them get up and help you; let them protect you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="Deuteronomy 32:39">39</a> Understand now that I am the only God; there is no other god beside me. I can cause death or give life; I can injure or heal, and no one can rescue from my power.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="Deuteronomy 32:40">40</a> I raise my hand to the sky and say, I will live forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="Deuteronomy 32:41">41</a> If I sharpen my shiny sword, and my hand grabs justice, I will give payback to my enemies and repay those who hate me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="Deuteronomy 32:42">42</a> I will make my arrows soaked with blood, and my sword will consume flesh; with the blood of those killed and prisoners, from the first acts of revenge against the enemy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v43"><a class="verse-num" href="#v43" aria-label="Deuteronomy 32:43">43</a> Celebrate, all you nations, with God&#39;s people, because He will take revenge for His servants&#39; blood, pay back His enemies, and be kind to His land and His people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v44"><a class="verse-num" href="#v44" aria-label="Deuteronomy 32:44">44</a> Moses came and told all the words of this song to the people, along with Hoshea, Nun&#39;s son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v45"><a class="verse-num" href="#v45" aria-label="Deuteronomy 32:45">45</a> Moses finished telling all these words to all Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v46"><a class="verse-num" href="#v46" aria-label="Deuteronomy 32:46">46</a> He told them, &quot;Pay close attention to everything I am telling you today. Teach your children to follow all the instructions of this law.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v47"><a class="verse-num" href="#v47" aria-label="Deuteronomy 32:47">47</a> This is not useless for you; it is your life. By doing this, you will live longer in the land you will own after crossing the Jordan.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v48"><a class="verse-num" href="#v48" aria-label="Deuteronomy 32:48">48</a> On that same day, God spoke to Moses, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v49"><a class="verse-num" href="#v49" aria-label="Deuteronomy 32:49">49</a> Go up to Mount Abarim, to Mount Nebo in Moab, across from Jericho; and look at the land of Canaan, which I am giving to the Israelites as their own.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v50"><a class="verse-num" href="#v50" aria-label="Deuteronomy 32:50">50</a> You will die on the mountain you are climbing and join your ancestors, just like your brother Aaron died on Mount Hor and joined his ancestors.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v51"><a class="verse-num" href="#v51" aria-label="Deuteronomy 32:51">51</a> Because you disobeyed me among the Israelites at Meribah-Kadesh, in the Zin desert; because you did not honor me in front of the Israelites.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v52"><a class="verse-num" href="#v52" aria-label="Deuteronomy 32:52">52</a> You will see the land in front of you, but you will not enter the land I am giving to the children of Israel.</p>
