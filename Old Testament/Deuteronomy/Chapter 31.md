<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Deuteronomy 31:1">1</a> Moses went and said these things to all Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Deuteronomy 31:2">2</a> He told them, &quot;Today I am 120 years old; I can&#39;t lead you anymore. God also told me, &#39;You will not cross this Jordan river.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Deuteronomy 31:3">3</a> God will go ahead of you and will destroy the nations so you can take their land; and Joshua will lead you, just as God has promised.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Deuteronomy 31:4">4</a> God will do to them what he did to Sihon and Og, the Amorite kings, and their land, which he destroyed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Deuteronomy 31:5">5</a> God will let you defeat them so you can follow all the rules I have given you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Deuteronomy 31:6">6</a> Be strong and brave, don&#39;t be scared or fear them, because God, your God, is with you; he will not leave you or abandon you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Deuteronomy 31:7">7</a> Moses called Joshua and told him in front of all Israel, &quot;Be strong and brave, for you will lead these people to the land God promised to give their ancestors, and you will help them take it as their own.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Deuteronomy 31:8">8</a> God goes before you; He will be with you, He will not leave you or forget you; do not be scared or worried.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Deuteronomy 31:9">9</a> Moses wrote down the law and gave it to the Levite priests who carried God&#39;s covenant chest, and to all the leaders of Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Deuteronomy 31:10">10</a> Moses told them, &quot;Every seven years, at the appointed time in the year of debt cancellation, during the Festival of Shelters,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Deuteronomy 31:11">11</a> When all Israel comes to meet God at the place He will choose, you must read this law to all Israel so they can hear it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Deuteronomy 31:12">12</a> Bring everyone together, men, women, and children, and any outsiders living with you, so they can listen, learn to respect God, and follow all the teachings of this law.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Deuteronomy 31:13">13</a> So that your children, who do not know anything, can listen and learn to respect God, as you live in the land you are going to take over across the Jordan.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Deuteronomy 31:14">14</a> God told Moses, &quot;Look, your time to die is near: call Joshua, and both of you come to the meeting tent, so I can give him instructions.&quot; Moses and Joshua went and stood in the meeting tent.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Deuteronomy 31:15">15</a> God appeared in the tent as a cloud pillar, and the cloud pillar stayed above the tent&#39;s entrance.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Deuteronomy 31:16">16</a> God said to Moses, &quot;Look, you will die just like your ancestors did; and these people will betray me and follow the foreign gods of the land they are entering. They will leave me and break the agreement I have made with them.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Deuteronomy 31:17">17</a> On that day, I&#39;ll get very angry with them, leave them, and turn away from them. They will be destroyed and face many bad things and troubles. Then they will realize and say, &quot;These bad things are happening because our God is not with us.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Deuteronomy 31:18">18</a> I will definitely hide my face on that day because of all the wrongs they have done, since they have turned to other gods.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Deuteronomy 31:19">19</a> Now, write down this song for yourselves and teach it to the people of Israel. Have them sing it, so it will stand as evidence against them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Deuteronomy 31:20">20</a> When I bring them to the land I promised their ancestors, a land rich with milk and honey, they will eat, be satisfied, become prosperous, then they will turn to other gods, worship them, anger me, and break our agreement.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Deuteronomy 31:21">21</a> When they face many bad things and troubles, this song will stand as evidence against them because it will not be forgotten by their descendants. I am aware of their thoughts even now, before they enter the land I promised.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Deuteronomy 31:22">22</a> Moses wrote this song that day and taught it to the people of Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Deuteronomy 31:23">23</a> God commanded Joshua, Nun&#39;s son, saying, &quot;Be strong and brave, for you will lead the people of Israel into the land I promised them, and I will be with you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Deuteronomy 31:24">24</a> When Moses finished writing the words of this law in a book,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Deuteronomy 31:25">25</a> Moses told the Levites who carried the covenant box of God, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Deuteronomy 31:26">26</a> Take this law book and place it beside the covenant box of God, so it can be a witness against you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Deuteronomy 31:27">27</a> I know you are rebellious and stubborn. Even while I am still alive with you today, you have rebelled against God; how much more will you do so after I die?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Deuteronomy 31:28">28</a> Bring to me all the tribe leaders and your officials so I can tell them these things and have heaven and earth witness against them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Deuteronomy 31:29">29</a> I know that after I die, you will become corrupt and leave the path I commanded. Bad things will happen to you later because you will do wrong, making God angry with what you do.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Deuteronomy 31:30">30</a> Moses spoke to all the Israelites the words of this song until it was finished.</p>
