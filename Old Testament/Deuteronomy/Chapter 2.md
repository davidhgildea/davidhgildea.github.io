<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Deuteronomy 2:1">1</a> We turned and traveled through the desert toward the Red Sea, as God told me to do, and we circled Mount Seir for many days.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Deuteronomy 2:2">2</a> God spoke to me, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Deuteronomy 2:3">3</a> You have traveled around this mountain long enough: now go north.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Deuteronomy 2:4">4</a> Tell the people: You will travel through the land of your relatives, the children of Esau, who live in Seir. They will fear you, so be careful.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Deuteronomy 2:5">5</a> Do not interfere with them; for I will not give you any of their land, not even enough to step on; because I have given Mount Seir to Esau as his own.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Deuteronomy 2:6">6</a> You should buy food from them with money, so you can eat; and you should also buy water from them with money, so you can drink.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Deuteronomy 2:7">7</a> God has blessed you in all you do; he has watched over you as you traveled through this vast desert. For forty years, God has been with you; you have not been without anything.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Deuteronomy 2:8">8</a> When we went past our relatives, Esau&#39;s descendants, who lived in Seir, taking the plain&#39;s road from Elath and Eziongaber, we changed direction and traveled through the Moab desert.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Deuteronomy 2:9">9</a> God told me, &quot;Do not bother the Moabites or fight with them, because I will not give you any of their land. I have given Ar to Lot&#39;s descendants to own.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Deuteronomy 2:10">10</a> Long ago, the Emims lived there, a large, numerous, and tall people, like the Anakims.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Deuteronomy 2:11">11</a> They were considered giants like the Anakims; however, the Moabites named them Emims.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Deuteronomy 2:12">12</a> The Horims also lived in Seir before; but Esau&#39;s descendants took their place after they defeated them and lived there instead; as Israel did in the land they owned, which God gave to them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Deuteronomy 2:13">13</a> I said, &quot;Get up and cross Zered Creek.&quot; So we crossed Zered Creek.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Deuteronomy 2:14">14</a> The journey from Kadeshbarnea to the Zered stream took us 38 years, until all the fighting men had died, just as God had promised them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Deuteronomy 2:15">15</a> Truly, God&#39;s hand was against them, to eliminate them from the group, until they were gone.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Deuteronomy 2:16">16</a> So it happened that when all the soldiers had died and were gone from the people,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Deuteronomy 2:17">17</a> God spoke to me and said,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Deuteronomy 2:18">18</a> Today, you will go through Ar, the land of Moab.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Deuteronomy 2:19">19</a> When you approach the land of the Ammonites, do not trouble them or fight with them, for I will not give you any of their land to own. I have given it to Lot&#39;s descendants to have as their own.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Deuteronomy 2:20">20</a> This land was also known as a home of giants: giants lived there long ago; and the Ammonites named them Zamzummims.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Deuteronomy 2:21">21</a> A large, numerous, and tall people, like the Anakim; but God destroyed them, and they took their place and lived there instead.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Deuteronomy 2:22">22</a> God did the same to the descendants of Esau who lived in Seir when he eliminated the Horims before them; they took over their land and have lived there to this day.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Deuteronomy 2:23">23</a> The Avims who lived in Hazerim, all the way to Azzah, were destroyed by the Caphtorims who came from Caphtor, and then the Caphtorims lived there instead.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Deuteronomy 2:24">24</a> Get up, start traveling, and cross the Arnon River. Look, I have placed Sihon the Amorite, king of Heshbon, and his land in your power. Start taking it over and fight him in battle.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Deuteronomy 2:25">25</a> Today, I will start making all nations under heaven fear and respect you. They will hear about you, shake with fear, and be very worried because of you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Deuteronomy 2:26">26</a> I sent messengers from the wilderness of Kedemoth to Sihon, the king of Heshbon, with a message of peace, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Deuteronomy 2:27">27</a> Let me go through your land: I will stay on the main road and won&#39;t turn right or left.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Deuteronomy 2:28">28</a> You will sell me food for money so I can eat, and you will give me water for money so I can drink; I will only walk through your land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Deuteronomy 2:29">29</a> Like the descendants of Esau living in Seir and the Moabites in Ar helped me, until I cross the Jordan into the land God is giving us.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Deuteronomy 2:30">30</a> But Sihon, the king of Heshbon, wouldn&#39;t let us go through his land: for God made his spirit stubborn and his heart tough, so He could give him to you, as you see today.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Deuteronomy 2:31">31</a> God said to me, &quot;Look, I have started to give Sihon and his land to you: start to take it over so you can own his land.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Deuteronomy 2:32">32</a> Sihon and all his people came out to fight against us at Jahaz.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Deuteronomy 2:33">33</a> God gave us victory over him; we defeated him, his sons, and all his people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Deuteronomy 2:34">34</a> We captured all his cities then, completely destroyed everyone—men, women, and children—in every city, leaving no survivors.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Deuteronomy 2:35">35</a> We only took the animals and the things from the cities we captured for ourselves.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Deuteronomy 2:36">36</a> From Aroer, next to the edge of the Arnon River, including the city near the river, all the way to Gilead, no city was too strong for us; God gave all of them to us.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Deuteronomy 2:37">37</a> You didn&#39;t go into the land of the Ammon people, the areas around the Jabbok River, the mountain cities, or any place God told us to stay away from.</p>
