<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Deuteronomy 26:1">1</a> When you enter the land God is giving you as an inheritance, and you take possession of it and live there;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Deuteronomy 26:2">2</a> You must take some of the first fruits from your harvest, from the land that God gives you, put them in a basket, and go to the place God chooses for his name to live.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Deuteronomy 26:3">3</a> You will go to the priest who is serving at that time, and tell him, &quot;I declare today to God that I have arrived in the land God promised to our ancestors to give to us.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Deuteronomy 26:4">4</a> The priest will take the basket from your hand and put it in front of God&#39;s altar.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Deuteronomy 26:5">5</a> You will say in front of God, &quot;My father was a struggling Syrian, and he went down to Egypt and stayed there with just a few people, and there he became a large, strong, and numerous nation.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Deuteronomy 26:6">6</a> The Egyptians treated us badly, hurt us, and forced us into harsh slavery.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Deuteronomy 26:7">7</a> When we cried to God of our ancestors, God heard us and saw our suffering, our hard work, and our oppression.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Deuteronomy 26:8">8</a> God led us out of Egypt with great power, with a strong arm, with fearsome acts, and with miraculous signs.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Deuteronomy 26:9">9</a> God has led us to this place and given us this land, a land rich with milk and honey.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Deuteronomy 26:10">10</a> And now, look, I have brought the first crops of the land that you, God, have given me. You will place them before God, and bow down before God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Deuteronomy 26:11">11</a> You will be happy with every good thing that God has given to you, your family, the Levite, and the foreigner living with you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Deuteronomy 26:12">12</a> When you finish taking a tenth part of all your crops every third year, the year for giving a tenth, and give it to the Levites, foreigners, orphans, and widows so they can have enough food in your towns and be satisfied.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Deuteronomy 26:13">13</a> Then you will say in front of God, &quot;I have removed the sacred things from my house, and I have given them to the Levite, the foreigner, the orphan, and the widow, just as you commanded me. I have not broken your commands, nor have I forgotten them.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Deuteronomy 26:14">14</a> I have not eaten any of it while sad, nor have I used any for impure purposes, nor have I given any for the dead. But I have listened to God&#39;s voice and done everything you commanded me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Deuteronomy 26:15">15</a> Look from your holy home in heaven and bless your people Israel and the land you gave us, like you promised our ancestors, a land filled with milk and honey.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Deuteronomy 26:16">16</a> Today, God has told you to follow these rules and decisions: so you must obey them with all your heart and with all your soul.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Deuteronomy 26:17">17</a> You have promised today that God is your God, and to follow his ways, keep his rules, commands, and decisions, and to listen to his voice.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Deuteronomy 26:18">18</a> Today, God has declared you to be his special people, just as he promised, and you should follow all his commands.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Deuteronomy 26:19">19</a> God will make you greater than any other nation He created, in praise, reputation, and honor, so you will be a special people dedicated to God, just as He promised.</p>
