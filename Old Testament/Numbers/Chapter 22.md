<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Numbers 22:1">1</a> The Israelites moved on and camped in the Moab plains near the Jordan River across from Jericho.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Numbers 22:2">2</a> Balak, Zippor&#39;s son, saw everything Israel did to the Amorites.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Numbers 22:3">3</a> Moab was very scared of the people because there were so many, and Moab was troubled because of the children of Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Numbers 22:4">4</a> Moab told Midian&#39;s leaders, &quot;This group will consume everything around us like an ox eats the field&#39;s grass.&quot; At that time, Balak, Zippor&#39;s son, was king of Moab.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Numbers 22:5">5</a> He sent messengers to Balaam, son of Beor, at Pethor by the river in his homeland, to invite him, saying, &quot;Look, a people has come out of Egypt; they cover the land and are settling near me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Numbers 22:6">6</a> Please come now and curse these people for me because they are too powerful. Maybe then I can defeat them and force them out of the land. I know that anyone you bless is blessed, and anyone you curse is cursed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Numbers 22:7">7</a> The leaders of Moab and Midian left with money for fortune-telling and went to Balaam. They told him what Balak had said.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Numbers 22:8">8</a> He told them, &quot;Stay here tonight, and I will give you an answer after I hear what God tells me.&quot; So the Moab leaders stayed with Balaam.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Numbers 22:9">9</a> God came to Balaam and asked, &quot;Who are these men with you?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Numbers 22:10">10</a> Balaam said to God, &quot;Balak, Zippor&#39;s son and Moab&#39;s king, has sent for me, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Numbers 22:11">11</a> Look, people have left Egypt and they are everywhere. Come and curse them for me; maybe then I can defeat them and drive them away.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Numbers 22:12">12</a> God told Balaam, &quot;Do not go with them; do not curse the people, because they are blessed.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Numbers 22:13">13</a> Balaam got up in the morning and told Balak&#39;s leaders, &quot;Go back to your country, because God will not let me go with you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Numbers 22:14">14</a> The Moab leaders got up, went to Balak, and said, &quot;Balaam won&#39;t come with us.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Numbers 22:15">15</a> Balak sent even more princes who were greater and more respected than the first ones.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Numbers 22:16">16</a> They went to Balaam and told him, &quot;Balak, Zippor&#39;s son, says, &#39;Please don&#39;t let anything stop you from coming to me.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Numbers 22:17">17</a> I will give you great honor and do what you ask: please come and curse these people for me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Numbers 22:18">18</a> Balaam replied to Balak&#39;s servants, &quot;Even if Balak gave me a house full of silver and gold, I can&#39;t ignore God&#39;s command to do anything less or more.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Numbers 22:19">19</a> Please stay here tonight, so I can find out what more God will tell me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Numbers 22:20">20</a> God came to Balaam at night and said, &quot;If the men come to call you, get up and go with them; but you must only do what I tell you to do.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Numbers 22:21">21</a> Balaam got up in the morning, put a saddle on his donkey, and left with the leaders of Moab.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Numbers 22:22">22</a> God became angry because Balaam went, and God&#39;s angel stood in the road to oppose him. Balaam was riding on his donkey, and he had two servants with him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Numbers 22:23">23</a> The donkey saw God&#39;s angel standing on the path with a sword in his hand, so the donkey left the path and went into the field. Balaam hit the donkey to make her go back on the path.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Numbers 22:24">24</a> An angel of God stood in a vineyard path with a wall on each side.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Numbers 22:25">25</a> When the donkey saw God&#39;s angel, she pushed against the wall, crushing Balaam&#39;s foot, and he hit her again.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Numbers 22:26">26</a> God&#39;s angel went on and stopped in a tight space where there was no room to move right or left.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Numbers 22:27">27</a> When the donkey saw God&#39;s angel, she lay down with Balaam on her; this made Balaam angry, and he hit the donkey with a stick.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Numbers 22:28">28</a> God let the donkey talk, and she asked Balaam, &quot;What did I do to you, that you have hit me three times?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Numbers 22:29">29</a> Balaam said to the donkey, &quot;You have made fun of me. If I had a sword in my hand, I would kill you right now.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Numbers 22:30">30</a> The donkey said to Balaam, &quot;Aren&#39;t I your donkey, the one you&#39;ve ridden on since you got me until today? Have I ever acted this way to you before?&quot; And he said, &quot;No.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Numbers 22:31">31</a> Then God let Balaam see, and he noticed God&#39;s angel standing in the path with a sword ready in his hand, so he bowed deeply and lay face down.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Numbers 22:32">32</a> The angel of God said to him, &quot;Why have you hit your donkey three times? Look, I came to oppose you because you are going the wrong way against my will.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Numbers 22:33">33</a> The donkey saw me and moved away from me three times. If she hadn&#39;t moved away, I would have surely killed you by now, but let her live.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Numbers 22:34">34</a> Balaam said to the angel of God, &quot;I have done wrong because I didn&#39;t know you were standing in my way. If you&#39;re unhappy, I&#39;ll go back.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Numbers 22:35">35</a> The angel of God told Balaam, &quot;Go with the men, but say only what I tell you to say.&quot; So Balaam went with Balak&#39;s leaders.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Numbers 22:36">36</a> When Balak found out that Balaam had arrived, he went to meet him at a city in Moab, near the edge of the Arnon River, at the farthest boundary.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Numbers 22:37">37</a> Balak said to Balaam, &quot;Didn&#39;t I send for you urgently to call you? Why didn&#39;t you come to me? Can&#39;t I reward you with honor?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Numbers 22:38">38</a> Balaam said to Balak, &quot;Look, I have come to you: can I just say anything? I will speak only what God gives me to say.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="Numbers 22:39">39</a> Balaam went with Balak to Kirjathhuzoth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="Numbers 22:40">40</a> Balak sacrificed oxen and sheep, and sent them to Balaam and to the leaders with him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="Numbers 22:41">41</a> The next day, Balak took Balaam to the high places of Baal so he could see all the people.</p>
