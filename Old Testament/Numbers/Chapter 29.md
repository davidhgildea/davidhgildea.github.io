<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Numbers 29:1">1</a> In the seventh month, on the first day, you will gather for a sacred meeting; you won&#39;t do any regular work: it&#39;s a day for you to blow trumpets.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Numbers 29:2">2</a> You must offer a burnt sacrifice that smells good to God; one young bull, one ram, and seven perfect lambs that are one year old.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Numbers 29:3">3</a> Their grain offering will be flour mixed with oil, three-tenths of an ephah for a bull and two-tenths for a ram.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Numbers 29:4">4</a> Give one-tenth of flour for each of the seven lambs.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Numbers 29:5">5</a> One young goat as a sin offering to ask for God&#39;s forgiveness for you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Numbers 29:6">6</a> In addition to the monthly burnt offering with its grain offering, the daily burnt offering with its grain offering, and their drink offerings, follow the usual rules to make a pleasing fire sacrifice to God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Numbers 29:7">7</a> On the tenth day of the seventh month, you must come together for a special meeting; you must humble yourselves and not do any work on that day.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Numbers 29:8">8</a> You must present a burnt offering to God as a pleasing aroma; one young bull, one ram, and seven one-year-old lambs with no defects.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Numbers 29:9">9</a> Their grain offering should be flour mixed with oil: three-tenths for a bull and two-tenths for a ram.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Numbers 29:10">10</a> For each lamb, give one-tenth of a bushel of grain, for all seven lambs.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Numbers 29:11">11</a> One young goat for a sin offering, along with the atonement sin offering, the regular burnt offering with its grain offering and their drink offerings.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Numbers 29:12">12</a> On the fifteenth day of the seventh month, you must gather for a holy meeting; you must not do any regular work, and you must celebrate a festival for God for seven days.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Numbers 29:13">13</a> You will present a burnt offering to God, a fire-made sacrifice with a pleasing aroma: thirteen young bulls, two rams, and fourteen one-year-old lambs, all without any defects.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Numbers 29:14">14</a> Their grain offering should be flour mixed with oil, 3 parts for each of the 13 bulls and 2 parts for each of the 2 rams.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Numbers 29:15">15</a> Give a tenth of an ephah of flour for each of the fourteen lambs.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Numbers 29:16">16</a> One goat for a sin offering, along with the regular burnt offering, its grain offering, and its drink offering.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Numbers 29:17">17</a> On the second day, give twelve young bulls, two rams, and fourteen perfect one-year-old lambs.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Numbers 29:18">18</a> Their grain offering and their drink offerings for the bulls, for the rams, and for the lambs must match their number, in the usual way.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Numbers 29:19">19</a> One young goat for a sin offering; in addition to the regular burnt offering, its grain offering, and their drink offerings.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Numbers 29:20">20</a> On the third day, they offered eleven bulls, two rams, and fourteen one-year-old lambs without any defects.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Numbers 29:21">21</a> Their grain offering and their drink offerings for the bulls, for the rams, and for the lambs, should be in line with their number, as is the custom.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Numbers 29:22">22</a> Offer one goat as a sin sacrifice in addition to the regular burnt offering with its grain and drink offerings.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Numbers 29:23">23</a> On the fourth day, ten bulls, two rams, and fourteen one-year-old lambs without any defects.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Numbers 29:24">24</a> Offer food and drink for the bulls, rams, and lambs in amounts that match their number, as is the custom.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Numbers 29:25">25</a> Offer one young goat for a sin sacrifice, in addition to the regular burnt offering, its grain offering, and its drink offering.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Numbers 29:26">26</a> On the fifth day, offer nine bulls, two rams, and fourteen one-year-old lambs without any defects.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Numbers 29:27">27</a> Their grain and drink offerings for the bulls, rams, and lambs must match their number, as required.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Numbers 29:28">28</a> Offer one goat for a sin offering, along with the regular burnt offering, its grain offering, and its drink offering.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Numbers 29:29">29</a> On the sixth day, eight bulls, two rams, and fourteen one-year-old lambs without any defects.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Numbers 29:30">30</a> Offer their grain and drink offerings for the bulls, rams, and lambs according to the number required, following the usual way.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Numbers 29:31">31</a> And one goat for a sin offering, in addition to the regular burnt offering, with its grain offering, and its drink offering.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Numbers 29:32">32</a> On the seventh day, seven bulls, two rams, and fourteen one-year-old lambs with no flaws.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Numbers 29:33">33</a> The food offerings and drink offerings for the bulls, rams, and lambs must match the number of animals, as required.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Numbers 29:34">34</a> And one goat for a sin offering; along with the regular burnt offering, its grain offering, and its drink offering.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Numbers 29:35">35</a> On the eighth day, have a special meeting and do not do any regular work.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Numbers 29:36">36</a> You must give God a burnt offering, a sacrifice burned with fire that smells good: one bull, one ram, and seven one-year-old lambs without any defects.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Numbers 29:37">37</a> Their grain offering and their drink offerings for the bull, for the ram, and for the lambs must match the number of animals, as required.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Numbers 29:38">38</a> And one goat for a sin offering, plus the regular burnt offering, its grain offering, and its drink offering.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="Numbers 29:39">39</a> During your special feasts, do these things for God, in addition to your promises and voluntary gifts, and also for your burnt, food, drink, and peace offerings.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="Numbers 29:40">40</a> Moses told the Israelites everything that God had commanded him.</p>
