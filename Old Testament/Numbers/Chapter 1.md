<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Numbers 1:1">1</a> God spoke to Moses in the Sinai wilderness, in the meeting tent, on the first day of the second month, in the second year after leaving Egypt, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Numbers 1:2">2</a> Count all the members of Israel&#39;s community by family groups, according to their fathers&#39; houses, and list the names of all the men one by one.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Numbers 1:3">3</a> Count everyone in Israel aged 20 and older who can fight in the army, you and Aaron must organize them by their military groups.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Numbers 1:4">4</a> You will have one man from each tribe with you; each one will be the leader of his family.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Numbers 1:5">5</a> Here are the names of the men who will be with you: from Reuben&#39;s tribe, Elizur son of Shedeur.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Numbers 1:6">6</a> From the tribe of Simeon: Shelumiel, son of Zurishaddai.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Numbers 1:7">7</a> From Judah came Nahshon, son of Amminadab.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Numbers 1:8">8</a> From the tribe of Issachar: Nethaneel, son of Zuar.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Numbers 1:9">9</a> From Zebulun: Eliab, son of Helon.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Numbers 1:10">10</a> From Joseph&#39;s descendants: From Ephraim, Elishama son of Ammihud; from Manasseh, Gamaliel son of Pedahzur.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Numbers 1:11">11</a> From the tribe of Benjamin: Abidan, son of Gideoni.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Numbers 1:12">12</a> From Dan: Ahiezer, son of Ammishaddai.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Numbers 1:13">13</a> From the Asher tribe, Pagiel son of Ocran.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Numbers 1:14">14</a> From Gad; Eliasaph son of Deuel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Numbers 1:15">15</a> From Naphtali; Ahira, Enan&#39;s son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Numbers 1:16">16</a> These were famous leaders of the group, chiefs of their family tribes, and in charge of thousands in Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Numbers 1:17">17</a> Moses and Aaron took these men whose names were listed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Numbers 1:18">18</a> They gathered the whole community on the first day of the second month. They listed their family lines by their fathers&#39; houses, by name, starting from age twenty and above, counting each man.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Numbers 1:19">19</a> As God told Moses, so he counted them in the Sinai wilderness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Numbers 1:20">20</a> The descendants of Reuben, Israel&#39;s oldest son, were organized by their generations, families, and fathers&#39; houses. The list included every man 20 years old and older who could fight in the war.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Numbers 1:21">21</a> The tribe of Reuben had 46,500 people counted.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Numbers 1:22">22</a> From Simeon&#39;s descendants, by their generations and family groups, according to their fathers&#39; houses, the men counted by name, each one over twenty years old and able to fight in war, were listed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Numbers 1:23">23</a> The tribe of Simeon had 59,300 people counted.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Numbers 1:24">24</a> From the descendants of Gad, grouped by their generations and family lines, and according to their fathers&#39; households, everyone 20 years old and older who could fight in war was counted.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Numbers 1:25">25</a> The total number of the tribe of Gad was 45,650.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Numbers 1:26">26</a> From the tribe of Judah, organized by their family lines and ancestors&#39; houses, counting each person by name, all the men aged twenty and above who could fight in the war.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Numbers 1:27">27</a> The census counted 74,600 people from the tribe of Judah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Numbers 1:28">28</a> From the tribe of Issachar came men who were listed by their family lines and fathers&#39; houses. Anyone 20 years old or older who could fight in the army was included by name.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Numbers 1:29">29</a> The tribe of Issachar had 54,400 people counted.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Numbers 1:30">30</a> From the tribe of Zebulun, listed by generations and families, from each father&#39;s house, everyone aged twenty or older who could go to war.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Numbers 1:31">31</a> The tribe of Zebulun had 57,400 people counted.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Numbers 1:32">32</a> From Joseph&#39;s descendants, specifically from Ephraim&#39;s line, they were counted by generations, family groups, and fathers&#39; houses, including everyone 20 years and older who could fight in wars.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Numbers 1:33">33</a> The count of people from the tribe of Ephraim was forty thousand and five hundred.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Numbers 1:34">34</a> From the descendants of Manasseh, according to their family lines and their fathers&#39; houses, following the list of names and counting those twenty years old and above, all those fit for military service.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Numbers 1:35">35</a> The tribe of Manasseh had 32,200 people counted.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Numbers 1:36">36</a> From Benjamin&#39;s descendants, by their family lines, according to their fathers&#39; houses, counting each name from age twenty and older, all who could go to war.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Numbers 1:37">37</a> The tribe of Benjamin had 35,400 people counted.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Numbers 1:38">38</a> From the Dan tribe, sorted by their generations, family groups, and fathers&#39; houses, all the men 20 years old and above who could go to war were counted by their names.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="Numbers 1:39">39</a> The count of the Dan tribe was 62,700 people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="Numbers 1:40">40</a> From the Asher family, according to their generations and family groups, counting by the men&#39;s houses, everyone 20 years old and above who could go to war.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="Numbers 1:41">41</a> The tribe of Asher had 41,500 people counted.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="Numbers 1:42">42</a> From Naphtali&#39;s descendants, by families and father&#39;s houses, counting by names, everyone 20 years and older who could fight in war.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v43"><a class="verse-num" href="#v43" aria-label="Numbers 1:43">43</a> The count of the tribe of Naphtali was 53,400.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v44"><a class="verse-num" href="#v44" aria-label="Numbers 1:44">44</a> These are the people counted by Moses, Aaron, and the twelve leaders of Israel, with one man representing each family.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v45"><a class="verse-num" href="#v45" aria-label="Numbers 1:45">45</a> All the Israelites counted by their families, from age twenty and up, who could fight in war, were included.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v46"><a class="verse-num" href="#v46" aria-label="Numbers 1:46">46</a> All those counted were 603,550.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v47"><a class="verse-num" href="#v47" aria-label="Numbers 1:47">47</a> The Levites from their ancestors&#39; tribe were not counted with them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v48"><a class="verse-num" href="#v48" aria-label="Numbers 1:48">48</a> God had spoken to Moses, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v49"><a class="verse-num" href="#v49" aria-label="Numbers 1:49">49</a> Do not count the tribe of Levi or include them in the census of the Israelites.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v50"><a class="verse-num" href="#v50" aria-label="Numbers 1:50">50</a> You should put the Levites in charge of the tent of testimony, all its equipment, and everything that belongs to it. They will carry the tent and all its equipment; they will take care of it and camp around the tent.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v51"><a class="verse-num" href="#v51" aria-label="Numbers 1:51">51</a> When it&#39;s time to move the tent, the Levites will take it apart, and when it&#39;s time to set up the tent, the Levites will put it together. Anyone else who comes close will be killed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v52"><a class="verse-num" href="#v52" aria-label="Numbers 1:52">52</a> The Israelites will set up their tents, each person near their own group, and each person by their own flag, among their units.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v53"><a class="verse-num" href="#v53" aria-label="Numbers 1:53">53</a> The Levites must camp around the tent of the covenant so that God&#39;s anger does not fall on the Israelites, and the Levites are responsible for looking after the tent of the covenant.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v54"><a class="verse-num" href="#v54" aria-label="Numbers 1:54">54</a> The Israelites did everything God told Moses to do.</p>
