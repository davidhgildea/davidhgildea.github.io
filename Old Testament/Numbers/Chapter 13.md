<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Numbers 13:1">1</a> God spoke to Moses, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Numbers 13:2">2</a> Send people to explore the land of Canaan, which I am giving to the Israelites. From each ancestral tribe, send one leader.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Numbers 13:3">3</a> Moses, following God&#39;s command, sent them from the Paran desert: all those men were leaders of the Israelites.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Numbers 13:4">4</a> Their names were these: from Reuben&#39;s tribe, Shammua, Zaccur&#39;s son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Numbers 13:5">5</a> From the tribe of Simeon, Shaphat, Hori&#39;s son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Numbers 13:6">6</a> Caleb, the son of Jephunneh, was from the tribe of Judah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Numbers 13:7">7</a> From the Issachar tribe, Igal, Joseph&#39;s son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Numbers 13:8">8</a> From the tribe of Ephraim, Oshea the son of Nun.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Numbers 13:9">9</a> From the tribe of Benjamin, Palti, Raphu&#39;s son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Numbers 13:10">10</a> From Zebulun&#39;s tribe, Gaddiel, Sodi&#39;s son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Numbers 13:11">11</a> From Joseph&#39;s tribe, that is, from Manasseh&#39;s tribe, Gaddi, Susi&#39;s son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Numbers 13:12">12</a> From the tribe of Dan, Ammiel son of Gemalli.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Numbers 13:13">13</a> From Asher&#39;s tribe, Sethur, Michael&#39;s son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Numbers 13:14">14</a> Nahbi, Vophsi&#39;s son, was from the tribe of Naphtali.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Numbers 13:15">15</a> From the tribe of Gad, Geuel son of Machi.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Numbers 13:16">16</a> These are the names of the men Moses sent to explore the land. And Moses named Oshea, Nun&#39;s son, Joshua.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Numbers 13:17">17</a> Moses sent them to explore the land of Canaan and told them, &quot;Go up from the south and climb into the mountains.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Numbers 13:18">18</a> Look at the land and what it&#39;s like, and the people living there, whether they are strong or weak, and if there are many or just a few.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Numbers 13:19">19</a> Find out about the land where they live, whether it is good or bad, and what kind of cities they live in, whether they are camps or fortified cities.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Numbers 13:20">20</a> Describe the land, if it&#39;s rich or poor, and if there are trees or not. Be brave and bring back some of the land&#39;s fruit. It was the season for early grapes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Numbers 13:21">21</a> They went and explored the land from the Zin desert to Rehob, near Hamath.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Numbers 13:22">22</a> They went up from the south and arrived at Hebron, where Ahiman, Sheshai, and Talmai, the descendants of Anak, lived. (Hebron was founded seven years before Zoan in Egypt.)</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Numbers 13:23">23</a> They reached Eshcol&#39;s stream, cut a grape branch with one bunch of grapes, carried it on a pole between two, and took pomegranates and figs.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Numbers 13:24">24</a> The place was named Eshcol Creek because the Israelites cut a bunch of grapes from there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Numbers 13:25">25</a> They came back from exploring the land after forty days.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Numbers 13:26">26</a> They went and returned to Moses, Aaron, and all the Israelites at the Paran desert, near Kadesh, and told them and everyone what they saw and showed them the fruit of the land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Numbers 13:27">27</a> They told him, &quot;We went to the land you sent us to, and it is full of milk and honey; this is its fruit.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Numbers 13:28">28</a> But the people living in the land are strong, and their cities have high walls and are very big. Also, we saw the descendants of Anak there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Numbers 13:29">29</a> The Amalekites live in the southern land; the Hittites, Jebusites, and Amorites live in the mountains; and the Canaanites live by the sea and near the Jordan river.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Numbers 13:30">30</a> Caleb calmed the people in front of Moses and said, &quot;Let&#39;s go right now and take the land; we can definitely conquer it.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Numbers 13:31">31</a> But the men who went with him said, &quot;We can&#39;t attack those people; they are stronger than us.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Numbers 13:32">32</a> They gave a bad report about the land they explored to the Israelites, saying, &quot;The land we went to check out is one that destroys its people, and everyone we saw there is very tall.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Numbers 13:33">33</a> We saw giants there, Anak&#39;s descendants, who were from the giants. We felt like grasshoppers to ourselves, and that&#39;s how we seemed to them.</p>
