<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Numbers 28:1">1</a> God spoke to Moses and said,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Numbers 28:2">2</a> Tell the people of Israel to give me my offerings and the bread for my burnt sacrifices, that please me, at the proper time.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Numbers 28:3">3</a> Tell them, &quot;This is the fire sacrifice you must give to God: two perfect lambs, one year old, each day, for a regular burnt offering.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Numbers 28:4">4</a> Offer one lamb in the morning and the other lamb in the evening.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Numbers 28:5">5</a> Use one-tenth of an ephah of flour and mix it with one-fourth of a hin of olive oil for a grain offering.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Numbers 28:6">6</a> This is a regular burnt offering set up at Mount Sinai to please God, a sacrifice burned for God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Numbers 28:7">7</a> Offer one-fourth of a hin of wine for each lamb as a drink offering in the sacred place for God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Numbers 28:8">8</a> Offer the second lamb in the evening with the same grain and drink offerings as in the morning; it will be a burnt offering with a pleasing aroma to God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Numbers 28:9">9</a> On the Sabbath, offer two lambs that are one year old and have no defects, along with two measures of flour mixed with oil for a grain offering, and the accompanying liquid offering.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Numbers 28:10">10</a> This is the special burnt offering for every Sabbath along with the regular burnt offering and its accompanying drink offering.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Numbers 28:11">11</a> At the start of each month, you must give a burnt offering to God: two young bulls, one ram, and seven lambs that are one year old and have no defects.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Numbers 28:12">12</a> Use three cups of flour mixed with oil for the grain offering for one bull and two cups of flour mixed with oil for the grain offering for one ram.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Numbers 28:13">13</a> For one sheep, mix a special amount of flour with oil to offer as food; it is a burnt offering, a pleasant smelling sacrifice to God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Numbers 28:14">14</a> Their drink gifts should be half a hin of wine for a bull, a third of a hin for a ram, and a quarter of a hin for a lamb. This is the monthly burnt offering for each month of the year.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Numbers 28:15">15</a> Offer a goat as a sin offering to God, in addition to the regular burnt offering and its drink offering.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Numbers 28:16">16</a> On the fourteenth day of the first month is God&#39;s Passover.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Numbers 28:17">17</a> On the fifteenth day of this month there is a festival; for seven days you must eat bread without yeast.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Numbers 28:18">18</a> On the first day, there will be a special gathering; you must not do any regular work on that day.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Numbers 28:19">19</a> You must offer a fire sacrifice to God; two young bulls, one ram, and seven one-year-old lambs without any defects.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Numbers 28:20">20</a> Offer their grain offering with oil mixed in: give three-tenths of an ephah for a bull and two-tenths for a ram.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Numbers 28:21">21</a> Give one-tenth of a measure of grain for each of the seven lambs.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Numbers 28:22">22</a> And one goat as a sin sacrifice to make amends for you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Numbers 28:23">23</a> You should give these along with the morning burnt offering that is given every day.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Numbers 28:24">24</a> Offer like this every day for seven days: food for a fire sacrifice that smells good to God. Give it along with the regular burnt offering and its drink offering.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Numbers 28:25">25</a> On the seventh day, you should gather together for a holy meeting; you should not do any regular work.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Numbers 28:26">26</a> On the day of the firstfruits, when you present a fresh grain offering to God after your weeks are done, you shall hold a special gathering; you shall not do any regular work.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Numbers 28:27">27</a> Give God a burnt offering that smells good: two young bulls, one ram, and seven one-year-old lambs.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Numbers 28:28">28</a> They offer flour mixed with oil as their food gift, three-tenths of an ephah for a bull, two-tenths for a ram.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Numbers 28:29">29</a> One portion of grain for each lamb, for all seven lambs.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Numbers 28:30">30</a> One young goat to make peace with God for you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Numbers 28:31">31</a> You should give these besides the regular burnt offering, its grain offering, and they must be without any defects, along with their drink offerings.</p>
