<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Numbers 31:1">1</a> God spoke to Moses, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Numbers 31:2">2</a> Get justice for the Israelites against the Midianites; then you will join your ancestors.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Numbers 31:3">3</a> Moses said to the people, &quot;Get some of you ready for battle, and attack the Midianites to take revenge for God.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Numbers 31:4">4</a> From every tribe of Israel, send a thousand people to fight in the war.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Numbers 31:5">5</a> From the thousands of Israel, a thousand from each tribe, twelve thousand ready for battle, were given.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Numbers 31:6">6</a> Moses sent a thousand men from each tribe to fight, including Phinehas, son of Eleazar the priest, with the holy items and trumpets to sound.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Numbers 31:7">7</a> They fought against the Midianites, as God told Moses; and they killed all the men.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Numbers 31:8">8</a> They killed the Midianite kings along with the others who were killed; the kings were Evi, Rekem, Zur, Hur, and Reba. They also killed Balaam, Beor&#39;s son, with the sword.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Numbers 31:9">9</a> The people of Israel captured all the women and children of Midian, and took all their cows, sheep, and possessions.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Numbers 31:10">10</a> They burned all the cities they lived in, and all their beautiful strongholds, with fire.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Numbers 31:11">11</a> They took everything they captured, including people and animals.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Numbers 31:12">12</a> They brought the prisoners, the captured goods, and the stolen items to Moses, to Eleazar the priest, and to the Israelites&#39; assembly at the camp on the Moab plains by the Jordan River, close to Jericho.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Numbers 31:13">13</a> Moses, Eleazar the priest, and all the leaders of the people went out of the camp to meet them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Numbers 31:14">14</a> Moses was angry with the army officers, the leaders of thousands and the leaders of hundreds, who returned from the battle.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Numbers 31:15">15</a> Moses asked them, &quot;Did you keep all the women alive?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Numbers 31:16">16</a> Look, these people led the Israelites, following Balaam&#39;s advice, to sin against God at Peor, and a disease spread among God&#39;s people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Numbers 31:17">17</a> Now, kill all the boys and every woman who has slept with a man.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Numbers 31:18">18</a> Keep alive all the young girls who have never slept with a man for yourselves.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Numbers 31:19">19</a> Stay outside the camp for seven days if you&#39;ve killed someone or touched a dead body. Clean yourselves and your prisoners on the third and seventh days.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Numbers 31:20">20</a> Clean all your clothes, everything made of skin, all goat hair items, and all wooden things.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Numbers 31:21">21</a> Priest Eleazar told the soldiers who fought in the battle, &quot;This is the rule of the law that God gave to Moses.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Numbers 31:22">22</a> Keep only the gold, silver, bronze, iron, tin, and lead.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Numbers 31:23">23</a> Everything that can withstand fire must be passed through it to become clean; however, it must also be purified with special water. But everything that cannot withstand fire must be passed through water.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Numbers 31:24">24</a> On the seventh day, wash your clothes, and then you will be clean. After that, you can return to the camp.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Numbers 31:25">25</a> God spoke to Moses, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Numbers 31:26">26</a> Count all the captured people and animals, you, Eleazar the priest, and the leading elders of the community.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Numbers 31:27">27</a> Split the loot into two parts: one for those who fought in the battle, and the other for the whole community.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Numbers 31:28">28</a> Collect a tax for God from the soldiers who fought in battle: one out of every five hundred people, cattle, donkeys, and sheep.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Numbers 31:29">29</a> Take some from their share and give it to priest Eleazar as a gift for God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Numbers 31:30">30</a> From the Israelites&#39; half, take one out of every fifty people and animals, including cattle, donkeys, and sheep, and give them to the Levites who are responsible for maintaining God&#39;s tent.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Numbers 31:31">31</a> Moses and Eleazar the priest did as God commanded Moses.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Numbers 31:32">32</a> The spoils, which were the remaining things captured by the soldiers, were six hundred and seventy-five thousand sheep.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Numbers 31:33">33</a> Seventy-two thousand cows,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Numbers 31:34">34</a> Sixty-one thousand donkeys.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Numbers 31:35">35</a> In total, thirty-two thousand women who had never slept with a man.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Numbers 31:36">36</a> And the half for those who went to battle was 337,500 sheep.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Numbers 31:37">37</a> God&#39;s share of the sheep was six hundred seventy-five.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Numbers 31:38">38</a> There were 36,000 cattle; God&#39;s share was 72.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="Numbers 31:39">39</a> There were thirty thousand and five hundred donkeys, and God&#39;s share was sixty-one.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="Numbers 31:40">40</a> There were 16,000 people; God&#39;s share was 32 people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="Numbers 31:41">41</a> Moses gave the special gift for God to Priest Eleazar, as God told Moses to do.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="Numbers 31:42">42</a> From the half belonging to the Israelites, which Moses separated from the soldiers,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v43"><a class="verse-num" href="#v43" aria-label="Numbers 31:43">43</a> The part that belonged to the people was 337,500 sheep.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v44"><a class="verse-num" href="#v44" aria-label="Numbers 31:44">44</a> Thirty-six thousand cattle,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v45"><a class="verse-num" href="#v45" aria-label="Numbers 31:45">45</a> Thirty thousand and five hundred donkeys.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v46"><a class="verse-num" href="#v46" aria-label="Numbers 31:46">46</a> And sixteen thousand people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v47"><a class="verse-num" href="#v47" aria-label="Numbers 31:47">47</a> From the Israelites&#39; half, Moses took one out of every fifty people and animals and gave them to the Levites, who looked after God&#39;s tent, as God told Moses.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v48"><a class="verse-num" href="#v48" aria-label="Numbers 31:48">48</a> The leaders in charge of a thousand soldiers, and the captains of hundreds, approached Moses.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v49"><a class="verse-num" href="#v49" aria-label="Numbers 31:49">49</a> They told Moses, &quot;Your servants have counted all the soldiers we are responsible for, and not one man is missing.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v50"><a class="verse-num" href="#v50" aria-label="Numbers 31:50">50</a> We have brought a gift for God, including gold jewelry like chains, bracelets, rings, earrings, and pendants, to ask forgiveness for our souls before God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v51"><a class="verse-num" href="#v51" aria-label="Numbers 31:51">51</a> Moses and Priest Eleazar took the gold from them, including all the crafted jewels.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v52"><a class="verse-num" href="#v52" aria-label="Numbers 31:52">52</a> All the gold given to God by the leaders of the thousands and hundreds was 16,750 shekels.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v53"><a class="verse-num" href="#v53" aria-label="Numbers 31:53">53</a> The soldiers had taken loot, each man for himself.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v54"><a class="verse-num" href="#v54" aria-label="Numbers 31:54">54</a> Moses and Eleazar the priest took the gold from the leaders of thousands and hundreds and brought it to the meeting tent as a reminder for the Israelites before God.</p>
