<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Numbers 21:1">1</a> When King Arad of Canaan who lived in the south heard that Israel was traveling by the spy&#39;s path, he attacked Israel and captured some of them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Numbers 21:2">2</a> Israel made a promise to God, saying, &quot;If you really help me defeat these people, then I will completely destroy their towns.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Numbers 21:3">3</a> God listened to Israel&#39;s voice, delivered the Canaanites, and they completely destroyed them and their cities; and he named the place Hormah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Numbers 21:4">4</a> They traveled from Mount Hor towards the Red Sea to go around the land of Edom; the people were very tired because of the journey.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Numbers 21:5">5</a> The people complained against God and Moses, asking, &quot;Why did you take us out of Egypt to die in the desert? There&#39;s no bread or water, and we hate this worthless food.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Numbers 21:6">6</a> God sent venomous snakes among the people, and they bit the people; and many of the Israelites died.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Numbers 21:7">7</a> So the people went to Moses and said, &quot;We have done wrong because we spoke against God and you. Ask God to remove the snakes from us.&quot; And Moses prayed for the people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Numbers 21:8">8</a> God told Moses, &quot;Make a fiery snake and put it on a pole: anyone who is bitten can look at it and live.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Numbers 21:9">9</a> Moses made a brass snake and put it on a pole. If anyone was bitten by a snake and looked at the brass snake, they lived.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Numbers 21:10">10</a> The Israelites moved on and camped at Oboth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Numbers 21:11">11</a> They traveled from Oboth and camped at Ijeabarim in the desert near Moab, facing the sunrise.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Numbers 21:12">12</a> From there they moved and camped in the Zared Valley.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Numbers 21:13">13</a> They moved from there and set up camp beyond the Arnon River in the desert that extends from the territory of the Amorites. The Arnon River marks the boundary between Moab and the Amorites.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Numbers 21:14">14</a> This is why it is written in the Book of God&#39;s Wars, what God did at the Red Sea and in the streams of Arnon.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Numbers 21:15">15</a> By the stream of the brooks that flow to Ar&#39;s home and run along the edge of Moab.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Numbers 21:16">16</a> From there, they traveled to Beer, the well where God told Moses to bring the people together so He could provide them with water.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Numbers 21:17">17</a> Then Israel sang this song: &quot;Come up, O well; sing to it.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Numbers 21:18">18</a> The leaders dug the well, the noble people dug it, following the lawgiver&#39;s orders, using their staffs. Then they traveled from the desert to Mattanah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Numbers 21:19">19</a> From Mattanah to Nahaliel, and from Nahaliel to Bamoth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Numbers 21:20">20</a> From Bamoth in the valley, in Moab&#39;s land, to Pisgah&#39;s peak, looking at Jeshimon.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Numbers 21:21">21</a> Israel sent messages to Sihon, the Amorite king, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Numbers 21:22">22</a> Let me go through your land: we won&#39;t enter the fields or vineyards; we won&#39;t drink well water; but we&#39;ll stay on the main road until we&#39;re beyond your borders.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Numbers 21:23">23</a> Sihon did not let Israel go through his land. Instead, Sihon brought all his people and went out to the desert to meet Israel. He reached Jahaz and fought against Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Numbers 21:24">24</a> Israel defeated him with the sword and took over his land from Arnon to Jabbok, up to the land of the Ammonites, because the Ammonites&#39; border was well-defended.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Numbers 21:25">25</a> Israel captured all these towns and lived in the Amorite towns, including Heshbon and its nearby settlements.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Numbers 21:26">26</a> Heshbon was King Sihon of the Amorites&#39; city. He fought the previous Moab king and took all his territory up to the Arnon River.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Numbers 21:27">27</a> People who use wise sayings tell us, &quot;Go to Heshbon, and let&#39;s rebuild Sihon&#39;s city.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Numbers 21:28">28</a> A fire has started in Heshbon, a flame from Sihon&#39;s city; it burned up Ar of Moab, and the rulers on Arnon&#39;s hills.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Numbers 21:29">29</a> Trouble comes to you, Moab! You are ruined, people of Chemosh: your god has let your surviving sons and daughters be taken captive by Sihon, king of the Amorites.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Numbers 21:30">30</a> We attacked them; Heshbon is destroyed up to Dibon, and we ruined them up to Nophah, which extends to Medeba.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Numbers 21:31">31</a> Israel lived in the Amorites&#39; land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Numbers 21:32">32</a> Moses sent spies to Jaazer, they captured its villages, and forced out the Amorites living there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Numbers 21:33">33</a> They turned and went toward Bashan, and Og, the king of Bashan, came out to fight them with all his people at Edrei.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Numbers 21:34">34</a> God said to Moses, &quot;Don&#39;t be afraid of him, for I have given him, his people, and his land into your control; and you will do to him what you did to Sihon, the Amorite king who lived in Heshbon.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Numbers 21:35">35</a> They struck him, his sons, and all his people until nobody was left alive; then they took over his land.</p>
