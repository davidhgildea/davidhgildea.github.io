<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Numbers 30:1">1</a> Moses told the leaders of the tribes about the children of Israel, saying, &quot;This is what God has commanded.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Numbers 30:2">2</a> If a person makes a promise to God, or takes an oath to commit themselves firmly, they must not go back on their word. They must do everything they said they would.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Numbers 30:3">3</a> If a young woman makes a promise to God while she is living at her father&#39;s home, and commits herself by that promise;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Numbers 30:4">4</a> If her father hears her promise or the obligation she has made for herself, and he says nothing, then all her promises and obligations will remain valid.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Numbers 30:5">5</a> But if her father forbids her when he hears about it, none of her promises or commitments will count. God will forgive her because her father forbade her.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Numbers 30:6">6</a> If she had a husband when she made a vow or said anything to bind herself by a promise;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Numbers 30:7">7</a> If her husband hears her vow and says nothing to her about it, then her vows and the pledge by which she obligated herself will stand.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Numbers 30:8">8</a> If her husband says no on the day he learns about it, her promise and the words she spoke that committed her will not stand. God will forgive her.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Numbers 30:9">9</a> Every promise a widow or a divorced woman makes, binding herself, must be kept by her.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Numbers 30:10">10</a> If she made a promise in her husband&#39;s home, or committed herself with a sworn agreement;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Numbers 30:11">11</a> When her husband heard it and said nothing to her and did not forbid her, then all her promises will count, and every agreement by which she committed herself will count.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Numbers 30:12">12</a> But if her husband canceled them on the day he learned about them; then whatever promises she made, or any pledge, will not be valid: her husband has canceled them; and God will forgive her.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Numbers 30:13">13</a> Every promise and every serious oath to deny oneself, her husband can confirm it, or her husband can cancel it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Numbers 30:14">14</a> If her husband says nothing to her from day to day, he agrees to all her promises or commitments. He makes them stand because he said nothing when he first heard them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Numbers 30:15">15</a> If he cancels her promises after hearing them, he must take responsibility for her wrong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Numbers 30:16">16</a> These are the rules that God told Moses, for a man and his wife, and for a father and his young daughter living in her father&#39;s house.</p>
