<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Numbers 12:1">1</a> Miriam and Aaron spoke against Moses because he married a woman from Ethiopia.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Numbers 12:2">2</a> They asked, &quot;Has God spoken only through Moses? Hasn&#39;t he also spoken through us?&quot; And God heard this.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Numbers 12:3">3</a> Moses was the humblest man on earth, more than anyone else.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Numbers 12:4">4</a> God spoke suddenly to Moses, Aaron, and Miriam, saying, &quot;You three come to the meeting tent.&quot; And the three of them went out.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Numbers 12:5">5</a> God came down in the cloud pillar, stood at the tabernacle entrance, and called Aaron and Miriam, and they both came out.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Numbers 12:6">6</a> He said, &quot;Listen to me: If there&#39;s a prophet with you, God will reveal himself in a vision and speak to him in a dream.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Numbers 12:7">7</a> My servant Moses is different; he is trustworthy in all my household.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Numbers 12:8">8</a> I will talk directly and clearly with him, not in riddles, and he will see a form of God. So why weren&#39;t you afraid to speak against my servant Moses?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Numbers 12:9">9</a> God became angry with them, and he left.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Numbers 12:10">10</a> The cloud moved away from the tent, and Miriam had a skin disease, white like snow. Aaron saw Miriam and she had the skin disease.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Numbers 12:11">11</a> Aaron said to Moses, &quot;Sorry, my lord, I ask you, don&#39;t blame us for the foolish things we have done and for our sins.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Numbers 12:12">12</a> Do not let her be like a newborn that is half dead with rotting flesh.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Numbers 12:13">13</a> Moses called out to God, saying, &quot;Please God, heal her now.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Numbers 12:14">14</a> God told Moses, &quot;If her father had spit in her face, wouldn&#39;t she feel shame for seven days? Keep her out of the camp for seven days, and after that, she can come back.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Numbers 12:15">15</a> Miriam had to stay outside the camp for seven days; the people did not move on until Miriam was allowed back in.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Numbers 12:16">16</a> Later, the people left Hazeroth and set up camp in the Paran wilderness.</p>
