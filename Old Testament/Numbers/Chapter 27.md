<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Numbers 27:1">1</a> Zelophehad&#39;s daughters came to Moses. Zelophehad was Hepher’s son, Gilead&#39;s son, Machir&#39;s son, and Manasseh&#39;s son from Joseph&#39;s family. His daughters were named Mahlah, Noah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Tirzah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Numbers 27:2">2</a> They stood before Moses, Eleazar the priest, the leaders, and the whole community at the entrance of the meeting tent, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Numbers 27:3">3</a> Our father died alone in the desert, not with those who turned against God with Korah; he died because of his own sin and had no sons.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Numbers 27:4">4</a> Why should our father&#39;s name disappear from his family because he has no son? Give us property among our father&#39;s brothers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Numbers 27:5">5</a> Moses took their case to God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Numbers 27:6">6</a> God spoke to Moses, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Numbers 27:7">7</a> Zelophehad&#39;s daughters are correct: you must give them property to inherit among their father&#39;s brothers, and you must give them their father&#39;s inheritance.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Numbers 27:8">8</a> Tell the people of Israel, if a man dies and has no son, then you must give his property to his daughter.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Numbers 27:9">9</a> If he has no daughter, then you must give his inheritance to his brothers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Numbers 27:10">10</a> If he has no brothers, then you should give his inheritance to his uncles.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Numbers 27:11">11</a> If his father has no brothers, give his property to the closest relative in his family, and they will own it. This will be a permanent law for the people of Israel, as God told Moses.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Numbers 27:12">12</a> God told Moses, &quot;Go up this mountain Abarim and look at the land I have given to the people of Israel.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Numbers 27:13">13</a> When you have seen it, you too will die as your brother Aaron did.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Numbers 27:14">14</a> You disobeyed my order in the Zin desert, during the people&#39;s argument, to show my holiness at the water in front of them: that is the water of Meribah in Kadesh in the Zin desert.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Numbers 27:15">15</a> Moses spoke to God, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Numbers 27:16">16</a> Let God, the God of everyone&#39;s spirit, choose someone to lead the group.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Numbers 27:17">17</a> Who can lead them and bring them back, so God&#39;s people won&#39;t be like sheep without a shepherd.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Numbers 27:18">18</a> God told Moses, &quot;Take Joshua, son of Nun, a man with the spirit, and put your hand on him.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Numbers 27:19">19</a> Place him in front of Priest Eleazar and all the people; and give him instructions while they watch.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Numbers 27:20">20</a> Give him some of your honor so that all the Israelite community will follow his lead.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Numbers 27:21">21</a> He will stand in front of priest Eleazar, who will seek advice for him by consulting the Urim before God. They will follow his commands to leave and to return, he and all the Israelites with him, as well as the whole community.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Numbers 27:22">22</a> Moses did what God told him: he took Joshua, put him in front of priest Eleazar, and in front of everyone gathered.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Numbers 27:23">23</a> He put his hands on him and gave him instructions, just as God had ordered through Moses.</p>
