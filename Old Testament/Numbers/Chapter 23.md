<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Numbers 23:1">1</a> Balaam said to Balak, &quot;Make me seven altars here, and get ready seven oxen and seven rams for me here.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Numbers 23:2">2</a> Balak did what Balaam said; Balak and Balaam sacrificed a bull and a ram on each altar.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Numbers 23:3">3</a> Balaam said to Balak, &quot;Stay by your burnt offering, and I&#39;ll go; maybe God will meet me. Whatever he shows me, I&#39;ll tell you.&quot; Then he went to a high place.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Numbers 23:4">4</a> God met Balaam, and Balaam said, &quot;I have set up seven altars and on each altar I have sacrificed a bull and a ram.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Numbers 23:5">5</a> God gave Balaam a message and said, &quot;Go back to Balak, and say this.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Numbers 23:6">6</a> He went back to him and saw him standing by his burnt offering with all the leaders of Moab.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Numbers 23:7">7</a> He began his message, saying, &quot;King Balak of Moab brought me from Aram, from the eastern mountains, and said, &#39;Come and curse Jacob for me; come and speak against Israel.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Numbers 23:8">8</a> How can I curse someone who God has not cursed? Or how can I condemn someone who God has not condemned?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Numbers 23:9">9</a> From the high rocks I see him, and from the hills I watch him: look, the people will live apart and will not be counted with other nations.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Numbers 23:10">10</a> Who can count Jacob&#39;s dust, or know the number of a quarter of Israel&#39;s people? I want to die like good people do, and end my life the same as theirs!</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Numbers 23:11">11</a> Balak said to Balaam, &quot;What have you done to me? I brought you to curse my enemies, but look, you have completely blessed them.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Numbers 23:12">12</a> He replied, &quot;Shouldn&#39;t I be careful to say what God has given me to speak?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Numbers 23:13">13</a> Balak said to him, &quot;Please come with me to another place. You can see only a part of the people from there, not all of them. Curse them for me from that place.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Numbers 23:14">14</a> He took him to Zophim&#39;s field, up on Pisgah peak, made seven altars, and sacrificed a bull and a ram on each one.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Numbers 23:15">15</a> He told Balak, &quot;Stay here by your burnt offering while I go to meet God over there.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Numbers 23:16">16</a> God met Balaam, gave him a message, and said, &quot;Go back to Balak and say this.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Numbers 23:17">17</a> When he arrived, he saw him standing by his burnt offering with Moab&#39;s leaders. And Balak asked, &quot;What has God said?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Numbers 23:18">18</a> He began his message, saying, &quot;Stand up, Balak, and listen; pay attention to me, son of Zippor:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Numbers 23:19">19</a> God is not a man who lies, or someone&#39;s son who changes his mind. If He says something, won&#39;t He do it? If He speaks, won&#39;t He make it happen?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Numbers 23:20">20</a> Look, I have been ordered to bless: God has given a blessing, and I cannot change it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Numbers 23:21">21</a> God has not seen any wrong in Jacob, nor has He noticed any evil in Israel; God is with him, and there is the sound of a king among them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Numbers 23:22">22</a> God led them out of Egypt; he has the power like a strong wild ox.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Numbers 23:23">23</a> There is no magic that can harm Jacob, nor any fortune-telling against Israel. Now it will be said about Jacob and Israel, &quot;Look at what God has done!&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Numbers 23:24">24</a> Look, the people will get up like a strong lion and raise themselves like a young lion: they will not rest until they have eaten their kill and drunk the blood of those they have defeated.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Numbers 23:25">25</a> Balak told Balaam, &quot;Don&#39;t curse or bless them at all.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Numbers 23:26">26</a> But Balaam replied to Balak, &quot;Didn&#39;t I tell you that I must do everything God says?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Numbers 23:27">27</a> Balak said to Balaam, &quot;Please come with me to another place; maybe from there you can ask God to curse them for me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Numbers 23:28">28</a> Balak took Balaam to the top of Peor, with a view over Jeshimon.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Numbers 23:29">29</a> Balaam said to Balak, &quot;Make seven altars for me here, and get ready seven bulls and seven rams for me here.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Numbers 23:30">30</a> Balak did what Balaam told him to do, and sacrificed a bull and a sheep on each altar.</p>
