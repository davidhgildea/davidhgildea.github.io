<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Numbers 25:1">1</a> Israel stayed in Shittim, and the people started to have sex with the women of Moab.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Numbers 25:2">2</a> They invited the people to the offerings for their gods, and the people ate and worshiped their gods.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Numbers 25:3">3</a> Israel worshiped Baal of Peor, and God became angry with Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Numbers 25:4">4</a> God told Moses, &quot;Take the leaders of the people and display them in front of God against the sun, so that God&#39;s intense anger can be calmed down from Israel.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Numbers 25:5">5</a> Moses told the judges of Israel, &quot;Each of you kill the men who followed Baal of Peor.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Numbers 25:6">6</a> Look, an Israelite man brought a Midianite woman to his brothers right in front of Moses and all the Israelites while they were crying at the entrance of the meeting tent.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Numbers 25:7">7</a> When Phinehas, who was Eleazar&#39;s son and Aaron the priest&#39;s grandson, saw this, he stood up from among the people and grabbed a spear.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Numbers 25:8">8</a> He followed the Israelite man into the tent and stabbed both of them, the man and the woman, in her belly. This stopped the plague among the Israelites.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Numbers 25:9">9</a> Twenty-four thousand people died in the plague.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Numbers 25:10">10</a> God spoke to Moses, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Numbers 25:11">11</a> Phinehas, Eleazar&#39;s son and Aaron the priest&#39;s grandson, stopped my anger against the Israelites by being passionately loyal to me among them, so I didn&#39;t destroy the Israelites in my jealousy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Numbers 25:12">12</a> So say, &#39;Look, I give him my promise of peace:&#39;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Numbers 25:13">13</a> He will have it, and his descendants too—the promise of a lasting priesthood; because he was eager for his God, and covered the sins of the Israelites.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Numbers 25:14">14</a> The Israelite man who was killed with the Midianite woman was named Zimri, son of Salu, a leader from a top family among the Simeonites.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Numbers 25:15">15</a> The Midianite woman who was killed was named Cozbi, Zur&#39;s daughter. Zur was a leader of a tribe and a ruling family in Midian.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Numbers 25:16">16</a> God spoke to Moses, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Numbers 25:17">17</a> Trouble the Midianites and strike them down.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Numbers 25:18">18</a> They trouble you with their tricks, which they used to deceive you about Peor, and about Cozbi, the Midianite prince&#39;s daughter and their sister, who was killed when the plague came because of Peor.</p>
