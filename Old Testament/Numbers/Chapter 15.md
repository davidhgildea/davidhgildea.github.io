<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Numbers 15:1">1</a> God spoke to Moses, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Numbers 15:2">2</a> Talk to the people of Israel and tell them, &quot;When you arrive in the land where you will live, which I am giving to you,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Numbers 15:3">3</a> I will make a fire offering to God, a burnt offering, or a sacrifice for a promise, or a voluntary offering, or during your special feasts, to please God, from the cattle or the sheep.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Numbers 15:4">4</a> The person who presents their offering to God must bring a grain offering of one-tenth of an ephah of flour mixed with one-fourth of a hin of oil.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Numbers 15:5">5</a> Prepare one-fourth of a quart of wine as a drink offering with the burnt offering or sacrifice for each lamb.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Numbers 15:6">6</a> For a ram, you should make a grain offering of two tenths of an ephah of flour mixed with one third of a hin of oil.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Numbers 15:7">7</a> For a drink offering, give one-third of a hin of wine as a sweet smell to God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Numbers 15:8">8</a> When you prepare a bull for a burnt offering, or as a sacrifice to keep a promise, or for peace offerings to God:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Numbers 15:9">9</a> He must bring a young bull with a grain offering of three tenths of an ephah of flour mixed with a half hin of oil.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Numbers 15:10">10</a> You should offer half a hin of wine as a drink offering to God, as a fire offering that smells sweet to Him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Numbers 15:11">11</a> Do this for one bull, one ram, one lamb, or one young goat.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Numbers 15:12">12</a> Prepare for each person according to the number you have.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Numbers 15:13">13</a> Everyone born in the country must follow these rules when they give a fire offering that smells good to God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Numbers 15:14">14</a> If a foreigner stays with you, or anyone else who lives with you through generations, and wants to make a fire offering that smells good to God, they should do it just like you do.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Numbers 15:15">15</a> One rule is for all of you in the congregation and for the foreigner staying with you, a lasting rule for all your generations: just like you, the foreigner will be before God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Numbers 15:16">16</a> One rule and one way will be for you and for the foreigner staying with you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Numbers 15:17">17</a> God spoke to Moses, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Numbers 15:18">18</a> Tell the people of Israel, when you enter the land I am taking you to,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Numbers 15:19">19</a> When you eat the bread from the land, you must give a portion as an offering to God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Numbers 15:20">20</a> You must give a piece of the first of your bread dough as a lifted offering: just as you lift the offering from the threshing floor, you must lift this one too.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Numbers 15:21">21</a> Give the first part of your dough to God as a special offering throughout your generations.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Numbers 15:22">22</a> If you have made a mistake and not followed all these commands that God gave to Moses,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Numbers 15:23">23</a> Do everything God told you through Moses, starting from when God gave Moses the instructions, for all future generations.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Numbers 15:24">24</a> If the people sin without knowing it, they must bring a young bull as a burnt offering to God, with its grain and drink offerings as usual, and a goat for a sin offering.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Numbers 15:25">25</a> The priest will ask God to forgive all of Israel&#39;s people, and they will be forgiven because they did not know what they were doing. They must bring their fire offering and sin offering to God because they did not know.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Numbers 15:26">26</a> The whole community of Israel&#39;s children and the foreigner living with them will be forgiven, because everyone made a mistake without knowing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Numbers 15:27">27</a> If someone sins without knowing, they must offer a one-year-old female goat as a sin offering.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Numbers 15:28">28</a> The priest will ask God to forgive the person who sins without knowing, and God will forgive him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Numbers 15:29">29</a> Everyone must follow the same rule for accidental wrongs—this applies to both the native-born Israelites and foreigners living with them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Numbers 15:30">30</a> But the person who deliberately does something wrong, whether they are a native or a foreigner, insults God, and that person will be separated from their community.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Numbers 15:31">31</a> Because he has looked down on God&#39;s word and broken his command, that person will be completely cut off; his wrongdoings will be on him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Numbers 15:32">32</a> While the Israelites were in the desert, they found a man collecting sticks on the Sabbath.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Numbers 15:33">33</a> The people who saw him picking up sticks took him to Moses, Aaron, and the whole community.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Numbers 15:34">34</a> They held him because it was not clear what should be done to him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Numbers 15:35">35</a> God told Moses, &quot;The man must be put to death: everyone must throw stones at him outside the camp.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Numbers 15:36">36</a> The whole group took him outside the camp and threw stones at him until he died, just as God had told Moses.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Numbers 15:37">37</a> God spoke to Moses, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Numbers 15:38">38</a> Tell the people of Israel to make tassels on the edges of their clothes for all time, and to add a blue ribbon on each tassel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="Numbers 15:39">39</a> It will be a tassel for you to look at and remember all of God&#39;s commands and follow them, and not to follow your own desires and sights, which lead you away.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="Numbers 15:40">40</a> Remember and follow all my commands, and be pure for your God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="Numbers 15:41">41</a> I am God, your God, who took you out of Egypt to be your God: I am God, your God.</p>
