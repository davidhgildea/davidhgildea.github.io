<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Numbers 26:1">1</a> After the plague, God spoke to Moses and Eleazar, Aaron&#39;s son the priest, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Numbers 26:2">2</a> Count all the Israelites who are 20 years old and above, from each family, who can fight in battles.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Numbers 26:3">3</a> Moses and the priest Eleazar talked to them on the plains of Moab by the Jordan River near Jericho, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Numbers 26:4">4</a> Count the people who are 20 years old and older; do as God told Moses and the Israelites when they left Egypt.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Numbers 26:5">5</a> Reuben, Israel&#39;s oldest son: Reuben&#39;s children; Hanoch, who started the Hanoch family; Pallu started the Pallu family.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Numbers 26:6">6</a> From Hezron comes the Hezronite family; from Carmi comes the Carmite family.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Numbers 26:7">7</a> These are the Reubenite families, and there were 43,730 of them counted.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Numbers 26:8">8</a> Pallu&#39;s sons were Eliab.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Numbers 26:9">9</a> Eliab&#39;s sons were Nemuel, Dathan, and Abiram. Dathan and Abiram were well-known among the people, who fought against Moses and Aaron with Korah&#39;s group when they opposed God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Numbers 26:10">10</a> The ground split open and swallowed them with Korah when they died, as fire consumed 250 men; and they became a warning.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Numbers 26:11">11</a> However, Korah&#39;s children did not die.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Numbers 26:12">12</a> Simeon&#39;s sons by their families: Nemuel&#39;s family, the Nemuelites; Jamin&#39;s family, the Jaminites; Jachin&#39;s family, the Jachinites.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Numbers 26:13">13</a> From Zerah comes the Zarhite family; from Shaul comes the Shaulite family.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Numbers 26:14">14</a> These are the Simeonite families, with a total of 22,200 people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Numbers 26:15">15</a> The Gad family groups included: the Zephon group called Zephonites, the Haggi group called Haggites, and the Shuni group called Shunites.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Numbers 26:16">16</a> From Ozni&#39;s family, the Oznites; from Eri&#39;s family, the Erites.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Numbers 26:17">17</a> From Arod comes the Arodite family; from Areli, the Areli family.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Numbers 26:18">18</a> These are the groups of Gad&#39;s descendants, counted at forty-five thousand.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Numbers 26:19">19</a> Judah&#39;s sons were Er and Onan, and they died in Canaan.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Numbers 26:20">20</a> The families of Judah&#39;s sons were: from Shelah, the Shelah family; from Perez, the Perez family; and from Zerah, the Zerah family.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Numbers 26:21">21</a> Pharez had sons: from Hezron came the Hezronite family; from Hamul came the Hamulite family.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Numbers 26:22">22</a> These are the families of Judah, and the count for them is 76,500.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Numbers 26:23">23</a> From the family of Issachar: Tola&#39;s descendants were the Tola family; Pua&#39;s descendants were the Pua family.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Numbers 26:24">24</a> From Jashub comes the Jashubite family; from Shimron comes the Shimronite family.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Numbers 26:25">25</a> These are the families of Issachar with their total of 64,300 people counted.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Numbers 26:26">26</a> The families of Zebulun&#39;s sons are: Sered&#39;s family the Sardites, Elon&#39;s family the Elonites, and Jahleel&#39;s family the Jahleelites.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Numbers 26:27">27</a> These are the Zebulun family groups and there are 60,500 people in them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Numbers 26:28">28</a> Joseph&#39;s sons and their families were Manasseh and Ephraim.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Numbers 26:29">29</a> From the descendants of Manasseh: from Machir&#39;s family came the Machirites, and Machir was the father of Gilead, from whom the Gileadites are descended.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Numbers 26:30">30</a> These are Gilead&#39;s sons: From Jeezer comes the Jeezerite family; from Helek comes the Helekite family.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Numbers 26:31">31</a> From the Asriel clan comes the Asrielites; from the Shechem clan comes the Shechemites.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Numbers 26:32">32</a> From Shemida came the Shemidaites, and from Hepher came the Hepherites.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Numbers 26:33">33</a> Zelophehad, Hepher&#39;s son, had no sons, only daughters: their names were Mahlah, Noah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Tirzah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Numbers 26:34">34</a> These are the groups of Manasseh, and the number in them is 52,700.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Numbers 26:35">35</a> Ephraim&#39;s sons and their families are: from Shuthelah, the Shuthelah family; from Becher, the Becher family; from Tahan, the Tahan family.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Numbers 26:36">36</a> Shuthelah had sons, and one was Eran, which started the family of the Eranites.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Numbers 26:37">37</a> These are the family groups of Ephraim&#39;s sons, numbering thirty-two thousand five hundred. These are Joseph&#39;s sons, grouped by families.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Numbers 26:38">38</a> Benjamin&#39;s sons by their families: Bela&#39;s family, the Belaites; Ashbel&#39;s family, the Ashbelites; Ahiram&#39;s family, the Ahiramites.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="Numbers 26:39">39</a> From Shupham comes the Shupham family; from Hupham comes the Hupham family.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="Numbers 26:40">40</a> Bela&#39;s sons were Ard and Naaman: from Ard came the Ardite family, and from Naaman came the Naamite family.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="Numbers 26:41">41</a> These are Benjamin&#39;s sons by their families: the counted number was 45,600.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="Numbers 26:42">42</a> These are the children of Dan by their families: from Shuham comes the Shuhamite family. These are the Dan families by their families.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v43"><a class="verse-num" href="#v43" aria-label="Numbers 26:43">43</a> All the Shuhamite families, when counted, totaled 64,400.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v44"><a class="verse-num" href="#v44" aria-label="Numbers 26:44">44</a> From Asher&#39;s descendants by their families: From Jimna, the Jimna family; from Jesui, the Jesui family; from Beriah, the Beriah family.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v45"><a class="verse-num" href="#v45" aria-label="Numbers 26:45">45</a> From Beriah&#39;s sons: Heber&#39;s descendants are the Heberites; Malchiel&#39;s descendants are the Malchielites.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v46"><a class="verse-num" href="#v46" aria-label="Numbers 26:46">46</a> Asher&#39;s daughter was named Sarah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v47"><a class="verse-num" href="#v47" aria-label="Numbers 26:47">47</a> These are the families of Asher&#39;s sons by their numbers; there were 53,400 of them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v48"><a class="verse-num" href="#v48" aria-label="Numbers 26:48">48</a> From Naphtali&#39;s sons by their families: Jahzeel&#39;s descendants, the Jahzeelites; Guni&#39;s descendants, the Gunites.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v49"><a class="verse-num" href="#v49" aria-label="Numbers 26:49">49</a> From Jezer comes the Jezerite family; from Shillem comes the Shillemite family.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v50"><a class="verse-num" href="#v50" aria-label="Numbers 26:50">50</a> Here are the family groups of Naphtali according to their own families: and the number of people in them was 45,400.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v51"><a class="verse-num" href="#v51" aria-label="Numbers 26:51">51</a> The children of Israel were counted as 603,730.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v52"><a class="verse-num" href="#v52" aria-label="Numbers 26:52">52</a> God spoke to Moses, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v53"><a class="verse-num" href="#v53" aria-label="Numbers 26:53">53</a> To these people, the land will be given as an inheritance, based on the number of names.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v54"><a class="verse-num" href="#v54" aria-label="Numbers 26:54">54</a> Give more land to those who have more people, and less land to those with fewer people; each group will get land based on its size.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v55"><a class="verse-num" href="#v55" aria-label="Numbers 26:55">55</a> Despite this, the land will be split up by drawing lots; each tribe will receive land based on their ancestors&#39; names.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v56"><a class="verse-num" href="#v56" aria-label="Numbers 26:56">56</a> The land will be divided by drawing lots, giving pieces to both the large and small groups.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v57"><a class="verse-num" href="#v57" aria-label="Numbers 26:57">57</a> These are the Levites counted by their families: from Gershon, the Gershonite family; from Kohath, the Kohathite family; from Merari, the Merarite family.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v58"><a class="verse-num" href="#v58" aria-label="Numbers 26:58">58</a> These are the Levite families: the Libnites, the Hebronites, the Mahlites, the Mushites, and the Korathites. Kohath was the father of Amram.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v59"><a class="verse-num" href="#v59" aria-label="Numbers 26:59">59</a> Amram&#39;s wife was named Jochebed, Levi&#39;s daughter, born to Levi in Egypt; and she gave birth to Aaron and Moses, and their sister Miriam for Amram.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v60"><a class="verse-num" href="#v60" aria-label="Numbers 26:60">60</a> Aaron had four sons named Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v61"><a class="verse-num" href="#v61" aria-label="Numbers 26:61">61</a> Nadab and Abihu died for offering unauthorized fire before God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v62"><a class="verse-num" href="#v62" aria-label="Numbers 26:62">62</a> There were 23,000 males one month old and older counted. They were not counted among the Israelites because they did not receive any land from the Israelites.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v63"><a class="verse-num" href="#v63" aria-label="Numbers 26:63">63</a> Moses and Priest Eleazar counted these people. They counted the Israelites on the Moab plains by the Jordan River near Jericho.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v64"><a class="verse-num" href="#v64" aria-label="Numbers 26:64">64</a> But none of the men counted by Moses and Aaron, the priest, when they counted the Israelites in the Sinai desert, were among these.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v65"><a class="verse-num" href="#v65" aria-label="Numbers 26:65">65</a> God had said they would definitely die in the wilderness. No man was left except Caleb, Jephunneh&#39;s son, and Joshua, Nun&#39;s son.</p>
