<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Numbers 17:1">1</a> God spoke to Moses, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Numbers 17:2">2</a> Talk to the people of Israel, and get a stick from each one based on their family leader’s house, making twelve sticks in total: write each man&#39;s name on his stick.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Numbers 17:3">3</a> Write Aaron&#39;s name on Levi&#39;s staff, because one staff represents the leader of their family.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Numbers 17:4">4</a> Put them in the meeting tent in front of the covenant, where I will meet you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Numbers 17:5">5</a> The man&#39;s stick I pick will flower, and I will stop the complaints of the Israelites against you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Numbers 17:6">6</a> Moses spoke to the Israelites, and each chief gave him a stick, one for each chief from their family groups, making twelve sticks total, including Aaron&#39;s stick among them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Numbers 17:7">7</a> Moses put the rods in front of God in the tent of meeting.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Numbers 17:8">8</a> The next day, Moses entered the tent of testimony and saw that Aaron&#39;s staff for the Levi family had sprouted, produced buds, flowered, and grown almonds.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Numbers 17:9">9</a> Moses brought all the rods from God to the Israelites, and they saw and each man took his own rod.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Numbers 17:10">10</a> God told Moses, &quot;Bring Aaron&#39;s staff back before the testimony to be saved as a sign for the rebels; and you will completely remove their complaints from me, so they do not die.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Numbers 17:11">11</a> Moses did exactly what God told him to do.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Numbers 17:12">12</a> The Israelites said to Moses, &quot;Look, we&#39;re going to die, we&#39;re all going to die.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Numbers 17:13">13</a> Anyone who comes close to God&#39;s tent will die: are we going to keep dying?</p>
