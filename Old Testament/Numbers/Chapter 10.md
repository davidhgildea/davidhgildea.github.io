<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Numbers 10:1">1</a> God spoke to Moses, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Numbers 10:2">2</a> Make two silver trumpets from one piece of metal to call the people together and to signal when it&#39;s time to move the camps.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Numbers 10:3">3</a> When they blow the horns, everyone must gather to you at the tent of meeting entrance.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Numbers 10:4">4</a> If they blow one trumpet, then the leaders, who are in charge of Israel&#39;s groups of a thousand, will come to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Numbers 10:5">5</a> When you sound an alarm, the camps on the east side should move ahead.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Numbers 10:6">6</a> When you sound the alarm a second time, the camps on the south side will start to travel; they will blow the alarm when they are to move.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Numbers 10:7">7</a> When it&#39;s time to gather the people, you should blow the trumpets, but do not sound an alarm.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Numbers 10:8">8</a> Aaron&#39;s sons, who are priests, will blow the trumpets; this will be a lasting rule for all your future families.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Numbers 10:9">9</a> If you fight in your land against the enemy who attacks you, then you must sound an alarm with the trumpets; and you will be thought of by God, and you will be rescued from your enemies.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Numbers 10:10">10</a> On happy days, special days, and the start of your months, blow trumpets over your burnt offerings and peace sacrifices as a reminder before your God: I am your God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Numbers 10:11">11</a> On the twentieth day of the second month in the second year, the cloud lifted from above the tent of the law.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Numbers 10:12">12</a> The Israelites left the Sinai desert, and the cloud settled in the Paran desert.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Numbers 10:13">13</a> They began their journey as God commanded through Moses.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Numbers 10:14">14</a> First, the group from the tribe of Judah marched with their flag, and Nahshon son of Amminadab led them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Numbers 10:15">15</a> The leader of Issachar&#39;s tribe&#39;s army was Nethaneel, Zuar&#39;s son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Numbers 10:16">16</a> Eliab, Helon&#39;s son, led the army of Zebulun&#39;s descendants.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Numbers 10:17">17</a> The tent was taken apart; and Gershon&#39;s and Merari&#39;s sons moved on, carrying the tent.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Numbers 10:18">18</a> Reuben&#39;s group marched with their banner, and Elizur, Shedeur&#39;s son, was in charge of their soldiers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Numbers 10:19">19</a> The leader of Simeon&#39;s tribe was Shelumiel, Zurishaddai&#39;s son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Numbers 10:20">20</a> Eliasaph, Deuel&#39;s son, was in charge of Gad tribe&#39;s soldiers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Numbers 10:21">21</a> The Kohathites moved ahead, carrying the sanctuary, and others set up the tent before they arrived.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Numbers 10:22">22</a> The group from Ephraim moved forward with their soldiers, and Elishama, Ammihud&#39;s son, was their leader.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Numbers 10:23">23</a> Gamaliel, Pedahzur&#39;s son, led the army of Manasseh&#39;s tribe.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Numbers 10:24">24</a> Abidan, Gideoni&#39;s son, led the army of Benjamin&#39;s tribe.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Numbers 10:25">25</a> The flag of Dan&#39;s camp moved ahead, which was at the back of all the groups, with Ahiezer, son of Ammishaddai, leading his people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Numbers 10:26">26</a> Pagiel, son of Ocran, was in charge of the group from the Asher tribe.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Numbers 10:27">27</a> Ahira, Enan&#39;s son, led the army of Naphtali&#39;s tribe.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Numbers 10:28">28</a> These were the travels of the Israelites by their groups when they moved on.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Numbers 10:29">29</a> Moses said to Hobab, Raguel the Midianite&#39;s son and Moses&#39; father-in-law, &quot;We are traveling to the place God promised to give us. Come with us, and we&#39;ll treat you well. God has promised good things for Israel.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Numbers 10:30">30</a> He said to him, &quot;I will not go; instead, I will go back to my own land and to my family.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Numbers 10:31">31</a> He said, &quot;Please don&#39;t leave us; you know where we can camp in the desert, and you can guide us.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Numbers 10:32">32</a> If you come with us, whatever good things God does for us, we will do the same for you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Numbers 10:33">33</a> They left God&#39;s mountain and traveled for three days. The chest of God&#39;s agreement led them during these three days to find a place to rest.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Numbers 10:34">34</a> God&#39;s cloud was over them during the day when they left the camp.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Numbers 10:35">35</a> When the ark moved, Moses said, &quot;Get up, God, make your enemies go away, and those who hate you run from you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Numbers 10:36">36</a> When it stopped, he said, &quot;Come back, God, to the many thousands of Israel.&quot;</p>
