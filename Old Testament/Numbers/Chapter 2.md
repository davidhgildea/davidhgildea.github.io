<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Numbers 2:1">1</a> God spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Numbers 2:2">2</a> Each man from Israel&#39;s tribes will camp under his own flag, near the banner of his family: they will set up their tents around the tent where the people meet.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Numbers 2:3">3</a> On the east side, where the sun comes up, the tribe of Judah will set up camp under their flag with their army: Nahshon son of Amminadab will lead them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Numbers 2:4">4</a> His army and the counted number of them were seventy-four thousand six hundred.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Numbers 2:5">5</a> The group camping next to them will be the Issachar tribe, with Nethaneel son of Zuar as the leader of the Issachar people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Numbers 2:6">6</a> His army and the counted men totaled fifty-four thousand four hundred.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Numbers 2:7">7</a> Next, the group from Zebulun: Eliab son of Helon will lead the Zebulun people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Numbers 2:8">8</a> His army, and the counted people in it, were fifty-seven thousand four hundred.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Numbers 2:9">9</a> In Judah&#39;s camp, 186,400 soldiers were counted. They will march first.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Numbers 2:10">10</a> On the south side, the flag of Reuben&#39;s camp will be set up for their military units: and Elizur son of Shedeur will lead Reuben&#39;s people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Numbers 2:11">11</a> His army, with everyone counted, was 46,500.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Numbers 2:12">12</a> The group camping next to him will be the Simeon tribe, and their leader will be Shelumiel, Zurishaddai&#39;s son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Numbers 2:13">13</a> His army, and the counted people in it, were fifty-nine thousand three hundred.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Numbers 2:14">14</a> The group from Gad: Eliasaph, Reuel&#39;s son, will lead Gad&#39;s descendants.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Numbers 2:15">15</a> His army, and the counted ones, were forty-five thousand six hundred and fifty.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Numbers 2:16">16</a> In Reuben&#39;s camp, 151,450 people were counted, organized in their groups. They will march second.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Numbers 2:17">17</a> Then the tent where people meet will move with the Levites&#39; camp in the middle of all the camps. As they camp, they will move, each person in their own spot under their flags.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Numbers 2:18">18</a> The west side will have Ephraim&#39;s camp flag, with their soldiers, and Elishama son of Ammihud will lead Ephraim&#39;s people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Numbers 2:19">19</a> His army and the counted men were forty-five thousand five hundred.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Numbers 2:20">20</a> The Manasseh tribe will be with him, and their leader will be Gamaliel, Pedahzur&#39;s son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Numbers 2:21">21</a> His army, and the counted number in it, were thirty-two thousand and two hundred.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Numbers 2:22">22</a> Then the tribe of Benjamin: and the leader of Benjamin&#39;s people will be Abidan, Gideoni&#39;s son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Numbers 2:23">23</a> His army, and the counted number of them, was thirty-five thousand four hundred.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Numbers 2:24">24</a> The army of Ephraim had 108,100 soldiers. They will march third.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Numbers 2:25">25</a> The flag of Dan&#39;s camp will be on the north side by their troops, and the leader of Dan&#39;s people will be Ahiezer, Ammishaddai&#39;s son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Numbers 2:26">26</a> His army and the counted number of them were 62,700.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Numbers 2:27">27</a> The group camping next to him will be the Asher tribe, and the Asher tribe leader will be Pagiel, Ocran&#39;s son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Numbers 2:28">28</a> His army, and the counted men, were forty-one thousand five hundred.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Numbers 2:29">29</a> The Naphtali tribe: Ahira, son of Enan, will be the leader of Naphtali&#39;s people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Numbers 2:30">30</a> His army, with those counted, was fifty-three thousand four hundred.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Numbers 2:31">31</a> In Dan&#39;s camp, there were 157,600 people counted. They will march last under their banners.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Numbers 2:32">32</a> These are the numbers of the Israelites counted by their family groups: all of those counted in the camps throughout their groups totaled 603,550.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Numbers 2:33">33</a> The Levites were not counted with the Israelites, as God told Moses.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Numbers 2:34">34</a> The Israelites did everything God told Moses: they camped under their flags and moved out grouped by family, following their ancestors&#39; houses.</p>
