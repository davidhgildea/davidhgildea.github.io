<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Numbers 24:1">1</a> When Balaam saw that God wanted to bless Israel, he did not look for magic spells like before, but instead he looked towards the desert.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Numbers 24:2">2</a> Balaam looked up and saw Israel camping by their tribes; then God&#39;s spirit came on him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Numbers 24:3">3</a> He began his message and said, Balaam, Beor&#39;s son, who can see clearly, said:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Numbers 24:4">4</a> He said, he heard God&#39;s words and saw a vision from God, fell into a trance with his eyes open.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Numbers 24:5">5</a> How beautiful are your tents, Jacob, and your shelters, Israel!</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Numbers 24:6">6</a> Valleys spread out, gardens by the river, trees of aloes that God planted, and cedar trees near the water.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Numbers 24:7">7</a> He will pour water from his buckets, and his offspring will thrive in many waters, and his king will be greater than Agag, and his kingdom will be lifted high.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Numbers 24:8">8</a> God led him out of Egypt; he has strength like a wild ox: he will defeat his enemy nations, break their bones, and shoot them with his arrows.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Numbers 24:9">9</a> He crouched, he lay down like a lion, and like a mighty lion: who will provoke him? Happy is the one who blesses you, and cursed is the one who curses you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Numbers 24:10">10</a> Balak became very angry at Balaam and clapped his hands. Then Balak said to Balaam, &quot;I asked you to curse my enemies, but you have completely blessed them three times instead.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Numbers 24:11">11</a> So now, go back home: I planned to give you high honor, but God has kept you from it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Numbers 24:12">12</a> Balaam said to Balak, &quot;Didn&#39;t I tell your messengers you sent to me, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Numbers 24:13">13</a> If Balak offered me a house full of money, I cannot ignore God&#39;s command and decide on my own to do good or bad; I will only say what God tells me to.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Numbers 24:14">14</a> Now I am going to my people. Come, and I will tell you what this people will do to your people in the future.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Numbers 24:15">15</a> He began his message and said: Balaam, Beor&#39;s son, the man with open eyes, speaks:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Numbers 24:16">16</a> He said, after hearing God&#39;s words and understanding the Most High&#39;s knowledge, he saw a vision of God while in a trance, yet his eyes were open.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Numbers 24:17">17</a> I will see him, but not soon. I will look at him, but he&#39;s not close. A star will come from Jacob, and a ruler will rise from Israel. He will defeat Moab&#39;s borders and destroy the descendants of Sheth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Numbers 24:18">18</a> Edom will belong to someone, and Seir will also belong to their enemies; Israel will act bravely.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Numbers 24:19">19</a> A ruler will come from Jacob&#39;s descendants; he will destroy the survivors of the city.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Numbers 24:20">20</a> When he saw Amalek, he told his story and said, Amalek was the first among nations, but in the end, he will be destroyed forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Numbers 24:21">21</a> He saw the Kenites, and began to tell a story, saying, &quot;Your home is strong, and you place your nest in a rock.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Numbers 24:22">22</a> The Kenite people will be destroyed, until Assyria takes you away as prisoners.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Numbers 24:23">23</a> He began his story and said, &quot;Oh no, who can survive when God does this!&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Numbers 24:24">24</a> Ships will come from the shores of Chittim and they will attack Asshur and Eber, and he too will be destroyed forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Numbers 24:25">25</a> Balaam got up, went back to his home, and Balak went on his way too.</p>
