<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Numbers 11:1">1</a> When the people complained, it upset God; God heard it, and became angry; God&#39;s fire burned among them, and it destroyed those on the edges of the camp.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Numbers 11:2">2</a> The people called out to Moses; and when Moses prayed to God, the fire went out.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Numbers 11:3">3</a> He named the place Taberah because God&#39;s fire had burned there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Numbers 11:4">4</a> The diverse crowd with them started to crave food, and the Israelites cried again, asking, &quot;Who will give us meat to eat?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Numbers 11:5">5</a> We remember eating fish for free in Egypt, along with cucumbers, melons, leeks, onions, and garlic.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Numbers 11:6">6</a> Now we feel empty inside; all we see is this manna and nothing else.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Numbers 11:7">7</a> The manna looked like coriander seed and was the same color as bdellium.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Numbers 11:8">8</a> The people collected the food and ground it in mills or crushed it in a bowl, then cooked it on pans to make cakes; it tasted like fresh oil.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Numbers 11:9">9</a> When the dew came down on the camp at night, the manna came down with it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Numbers 11:10">10</a> Moses heard the people crying in their families, each man at his tent door, and God became very angry; Moses was also upset.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Numbers 11:11">11</a> Moses said to God, &quot;Why have you made things hard for me, your servant? And why don&#39;t I have your favor, that you have put the responsibility of all these people on me?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Numbers 11:12">12</a> Did I give birth to all these people? Did I create them, that you tell me to carry them like a father carries a nursing baby, to the land you promised to their ancestors?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Numbers 11:13">13</a> Where can I get meat to give to all these people? They are crying to me, &#39;Give us meat so we can eat.&#39;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Numbers 11:14">14</a> I can&#39;t handle all these people by myself, it&#39;s too much for me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Numbers 11:15">15</a> If you treat me like this, please kill me now, if I have your favor; don&#39;t let me see my misery.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Numbers 11:16">16</a> God told Moses, &quot;Bring me seventy of Israel&#39;s elders, known to you as leaders and officials, to the meeting tent so they can stand with you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Numbers 11:17">17</a> I will come down and speak with you there. I will take some of the spirit that is on you and put it on them, so they can share the people&#39;s weight with you and you won&#39;t have to carry it alone.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Numbers 11:18">18</a> Tell the people to get ready for tomorrow, and you will eat meat. You cried to God for meat, saying, &quot;Who will give us meat to eat? We had it good in Egypt.&quot; So God will give you meat, and you will eat.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Numbers 11:19">19</a> You must not eat for one day, two days, five days, ten days, or twenty days.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Numbers 11:20">20</a> But even for a whole month, until you are sick of it because it comes out of your nose, and you hate it: you feel this way because you rejected God among you and cried in front of him, asking, &#39;Why did we leave Egypt?&#39;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Numbers 11:21">21</a> Moses said, &quot;The people I am with are 600,000 soldiers; and you have said, &#39;I will give them meat to eat for a whole month.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Numbers 11:22">22</a> Should we kill enough flocks and herds to feed them? Or should we gather all the fish from the sea for them to be satisfied?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Numbers 11:23">23</a> God said to Moses, &quot;Is God&#39;s power weak? You will now see if my promise to you happens or not.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Numbers 11:24">24</a> Moses went out, told the people what God said, gathered seventy elders, and placed them around the tent.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Numbers 11:25">25</a> God came down in a cloud, spoke to him, and shared his spirit with the seventy elders. When the spirit rested on them, they spoke God&#39;s message and did not stop.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Numbers 11:26">26</a> Two men stayed in the camp, one named Eldad and the other Medad. God&#39;s spirit was with them. They were listed to go to the tent but did not go. Instead, they spoke God&#39;s word in the camp.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Numbers 11:27">27</a> A young man ran and told Moses, &quot;Eldad and Medad are prophesying in the camp.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Numbers 11:28">28</a> Joshua, son of Nun, who served Moses, replied, &quot;Moses, tell them to stop.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Numbers 11:29">29</a> And Moses said to him, &quot;Are you jealous for me? I wish that all God&#39;s people were prophets and that God would give them his spirit!&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Numbers 11:30">30</a> Moses went into the camp with the elders of Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Numbers 11:31">31</a> A wind from God blew, and brought quails from the sea, dropping them near the camp, about a day&#39;s walk on one side and on the other side, all around the camp, and about two cubits deep on the ground.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Numbers 11:32">32</a> The people stood all day, all night, and the next day, collecting quails. The one who collected the least got ten baskets full. They laid them out around the camp.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Numbers 11:33">33</a> While the meat was still in their mouths, before they could chew it, God became very angry with the people, and God struck them with a terrible plague.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Numbers 11:34">34</a> He named that place Graves of Craving because there they buried the people who had strong desires.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Numbers 11:35">35</a> The people traveled from Kibrothhattaavah to Hazeroth and stayed there.</p>
