<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Numbers 33:1">1</a> These are the travels of the Israelites who left Egypt with their soldiers led by Moses and Aaron.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Numbers 33:2">2</a> Moses recorded where they traveled from as God instructed; these are their travels based on where they left.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Numbers 33:3">3</a> They left Rameses in the first month, on the fifteenth day; the day after Passover, the Israelites left boldly while all the Egyptians watched.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Numbers 33:4">4</a> The Egyptians buried all their firstborn whom God had struck down. God also punished their gods.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Numbers 33:5">5</a> The Israelites left Rameses and camped in Succoth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Numbers 33:6">6</a> They left Succoth and set up camp in Etham, at the border of the wilderness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Numbers 33:7">7</a> They left Etham, turned back to Pihahiroth near Baalzephon, and camped in front of Migdol.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Numbers 33:8">8</a> They left Pihahiroth, went through the sea to the desert, traveled for three days in the Etham desert, and camped at Marah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Numbers 33:9">9</a> They left Marah and arrived at Elim, where there were twelve water springs and seventy palm trees; they set up camp there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Numbers 33:10">10</a> They left Elim and camped by the Red Sea.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Numbers 33:11">11</a> They left the Red Sea and camped in the Sin Desert.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Numbers 33:12">12</a> They traveled from the wilderness of Sin and camped at Dophkah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Numbers 33:13">13</a> They left Dophkah and camped at Alush.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Numbers 33:14">14</a> They moved from Alush and camped at Rephidim where there was no water for the people to drink.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Numbers 33:15">15</a> They left Rephidim and camped in the Sinai desert.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Numbers 33:16">16</a> They left the Sinai Desert and camped at Kibroth Hattaavah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Numbers 33:17">17</a> They left Kibrothhattaavah and camped at Hazeroth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Numbers 33:18">18</a> They left Hazeroth and set up camp in Rithmah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Numbers 33:19">19</a> They left Rithmah and set up camp at Rimmonparez.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Numbers 33:20">20</a> They left Rimmonparez and camped at Libnah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Numbers 33:21">21</a> They moved from Libnah and camped at Rissah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Numbers 33:22">22</a> They traveled from Rissah and camped in Kehelathah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Numbers 33:23">23</a> They left Kehelathah and camped at Mount Shapher.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Numbers 33:24">24</a> They moved from Mount Shapher and camped in Haradah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Numbers 33:25">25</a> They moved from Haradah and set up camp in Makheloth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Numbers 33:26">26</a> They moved from Makheloth and camped at Tahath.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Numbers 33:27">27</a> They left Tahath and set up camp at Tarah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Numbers 33:28">28</a> They moved from Tarah and set up camp in Mithcah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Numbers 33:29">29</a> They left Mithcah and set up camp in Hashmonah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Numbers 33:30">30</a> They left Hashmonah and camped at Moseroth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Numbers 33:31">31</a> They left Moseroth and camped at Benejaakan.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Numbers 33:32">32</a> They moved from Benejaakan and camped at Horhagidgad.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Numbers 33:33">33</a> They traveled from Horhagidgad and camped at Jotbathah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Numbers 33:34">34</a> They moved from Jotbathah and set up camp at Ebronah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Numbers 33:35">35</a> They left Ebronah and camped at Eziongaber.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Numbers 33:36">36</a> They moved from Eziongaber and camped in the Zin Desert, near Kadesh.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Numbers 33:37">37</a> They moved from Kadesh and set up camp on Mount Hor, at the border of Edom.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Numbers 33:38">38</a> Aaron the priest went up to Mount Hor because God told him to, and he died there. It was forty years after the people of Israel left Egypt, on the first day of the fifth month.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="Numbers 33:39">39</a> Aaron was 123 years old when he died on Mount Hor.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="Numbers 33:40">40</a> The Canaanite King Arad, who lived in the southern part of Canaan, heard that the Israelites were coming.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="Numbers 33:41">41</a> They left Mount Hor and camped at Zalmonah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="Numbers 33:42">42</a> They left Zalmonah and set up camp in Punon.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v43"><a class="verse-num" href="#v43" aria-label="Numbers 33:43">43</a> They left Punon and camped at Oboth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v44"><a class="verse-num" href="#v44" aria-label="Numbers 33:44">44</a> They left Oboth and camped at Ije Abarim, on the edge of Moab.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v45"><a class="verse-num" href="#v45" aria-label="Numbers 33:45">45</a> They left Iim and set up camp in Dibongad.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v46"><a class="verse-num" href="#v46" aria-label="Numbers 33:46">46</a> They moved from Dibongad and camped at Almondiblathaim.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v47"><a class="verse-num" href="#v47" aria-label="Numbers 33:47">47</a> They moved from Almondiblathaim and camped in the Abarim mountains, near Nebo.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v48"><a class="verse-num" href="#v48" aria-label="Numbers 33:48">48</a> They left the Abarim mountains and camped in the Moab plains by the Jordan near Jericho.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v49"><a class="verse-num" href="#v49" aria-label="Numbers 33:49">49</a> They camped by the Jordan River, from Bethjesimoth to Abelshittim in the Moab plains.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v50"><a class="verse-num" href="#v50" aria-label="Numbers 33:50">50</a> God spoke to Moses on the plains of Moab by the Jordan River near Jericho, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v51"><a class="verse-num" href="#v51" aria-label="Numbers 33:51">51</a> Tell the people of Israel, when you cross the Jordan River into the land of Canaan.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v52"><a class="verse-num" href="#v52" aria-label="Numbers 33:52">52</a> Then you must force all the people of the land to leave, destroy all their images, melt down all their idols, and tear down all their worship places.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v53"><a class="verse-num" href="#v53" aria-label="Numbers 33:53">53</a> You will take over the land and live in it because I have given it to you to own.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v54"><a class="verse-num" href="#v54" aria-label="Numbers 33:54">54</a> You will divide the land by drawing lots to decide which family gets which part: give bigger parts to larger families and smaller parts to smaller families. Each person&#39;s land will be where their lot lands, and you will inherit land based on your ancestral tribes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v55"><a class="verse-num" href="#v55" aria-label="Numbers 33:55">55</a> If you do not force the people of the land to leave, the ones you allow to stay will cause you trouble, hurt your eyes, and bother you where you live.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v56"><a class="verse-num" href="#v56" aria-label="Numbers 33:56">56</a> I will do to you what I planned to do to them.</p>
