<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Numbers 36:1">1</a> The leaders of the clans in Gilead&#39;s family, who was Machir&#39;s son and Manasseh&#39;s grandson, both descendants of Joseph, approached and spoke to Moses and the heads of Israel&#39;s families.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Numbers 36:2">2</a> They said, God told my lord to divide the land among the Israelites by casting lots, and my lord was told by God to give our brother Zelophehad&#39;s daughters their inheritance.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Numbers 36:3">3</a> If they marry men from other Israelite tribes, their property will be moved from our ancestors&#39; tribe to their husbands&#39; tribe, reducing our inherited land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Numbers 36:4">4</a> When Israel&#39;s jubilee comes, their land will join the land of the tribe they have joined, and it will no longer be part of our ancestors&#39; tribal land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Numbers 36:5">5</a> Moses told the Israelites what God had said, and he agreed that the tribe of Joseph&#39;s sons had spoken wisely.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Numbers 36:6">6</a> God commands this about Zelophehad&#39;s daughters: They should marry whoever they want, but they must marry within their father&#39;s tribal family.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Numbers 36:7">7</a> The lands of Israel&#39;s tribes must not be passed from one tribe to another; each person from the tribes of Israel must hold on to the land inherited from their ancestors.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Numbers 36:8">8</a> Every daughter who has property in an Israelite tribe must marry someone from her father&#39;s tribe, so each Israelite man can keep his family inheritance.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Numbers 36:9">9</a> An inheritance must not pass from one tribe to another; each tribe of the children of Israel must hold on to its own inheritance.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Numbers 36:10">10</a> Just as God told Moses, the daughters of Zelophehad did the same.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Numbers 36:11">11</a> Mahlah, Tirzah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Noah, daughters of Zelophehad, married their cousins on their father&#39;s side.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Numbers 36:12">12</a> They married into the families of Manasseh&#39;s sons, Joseph&#39;s son, and their property stayed in their father&#39;s family tribe.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Numbers 36:13">13</a> These are the commands and rules that God gave through Moses to the people of Israel in the Moab plains by the Jordan River near Jericho.</p>
