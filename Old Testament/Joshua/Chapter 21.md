<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Joshua 21:1">1</a> The leaders of the Levite families approached Priest Eleazar, Joshua son of Nun, and the leaders of the other Israelite families.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Joshua 21:2">2</a> They talked to them at Shiloh in Canaan, saying, &quot;God told us through Moses to give us cities to live in, with the surrounding land for our animals.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Joshua 21:3">3</a> The Israelites gave the Levites these cities and the land around them from their own land, as God had commanded.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Joshua 21:4">4</a> The Kohathite families got their land by casting lots. Aaron&#39;s descendants, who were Levites, received thirteen cities from the territories of Judah, Simeon, and Benjamin.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Joshua 21:5">5</a> The remaining descendants of Kohath received ten cities by drawing lots from the tribes of Ephraim, Dan, and the half-tribe of Manasseh.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Joshua 21:6">6</a> Gershon&#39;s descendants received thirteen cities by lot from the tribes of Issachar, Asher, Naphtali, and from half of the tribe of Manasseh in Bashan.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Joshua 21:7">7</a> The Merari family members received twelve cities from the Reuben, Gad, and Zebulun tribes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Joshua 21:8">8</a> The people of Israel gave cities and surrounding land to the Levites by drawing lots, just as God ordered through Moses.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Joshua 21:9">9</a> They gave cities from the tribe of Judah and the tribe of Simeon, which are listed by name here.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Joshua 21:10">10</a> Aaron&#39;s descendants from the Kohathite clan, who were part of the Levi tribe, received this because they got the first share.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Joshua 21:11">11</a> They gave them the city of Arba, Anak&#39;s father&#39;s city, called Hebron, in Judah&#39;s hill country, and the surrounding suburbs.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Joshua 21:12">12</a> They gave the land around the city and its villages to Caleb, Jephunneh&#39;s son, for him to own.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Joshua 21:13">13</a> They gave Aaron&#39;s descendants Hebron, a safe city for someone who accidentally killed a person, and the area around it, plus Libnah and its surrounding area.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Joshua 21:14">14</a> Jattir and the surrounding area, Eshtemoa and the surrounding area,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Joshua 21:15">15</a> And Holon with the land around it, and Debir with the land around it,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Joshua 21:16">16</a> And Ain and its surrounding area, Juttah and its surrounding area, and Bethshemesh and its surrounding area; nine cities from those two tribes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Joshua 21:17">17</a> From Benjamin&#39;s tribe, the towns Gibeon and Geba with their surrounding areas.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Joshua 21:18">18</a> Anathoth and its surrounding area, and Almon and its surrounding area; four cities.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Joshua 21:19">19</a> Aaron&#39;s descendants, who were priests, had thirteen cities with the land around them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Joshua 21:20">20</a> The Kohathite families, the remaining Levites descended from Kohath, received towns as their share from the tribe of Ephraim.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Joshua 21:21">21</a> They gave them Shechem with its surrounding land in the hill country of Ephraim, to be a city of refuge for those who killed someone by accident, and Gezer with its surrounding land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Joshua 21:22">22</a> Kibzaim and its surrounding area, and Bethhoron and its surrounding area; four cities.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Joshua 21:23">23</a> From Dan&#39;s tribe, Eltekeh and its surrounding lands, Gibbethon and its surrounding lands,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Joshua 21:24">24</a> Aijalon and its surrounding land, Gathrimmon and its surrounding land; four cities.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Joshua 21:25">25</a> From the half tribe of Manasseh came the towns of Tanach and Gathrimmon, along with the land around them; in total, two towns.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Joshua 21:26">26</a> There were ten cities with their surrounding lands for the remaining families of Kohath&#39;s children.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Joshua 21:27">27</a> They gave the Gershonite Levites from the half-tribe of Manasseh the city of Golan in Bashan and its surrounding lands to be a safe place for those who killed someone by accident; they also gave them Beeshterah and its surrounding lands—two cities in total.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Joshua 21:28">28</a> From the tribe of Issachar, Kishon and its surrounding lands, Dabareh and its surrounding lands.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Joshua 21:29">29</a> Jarmuth with its surrounding lands, Engannim with its surrounding lands; four cities.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Joshua 21:30">30</a> From the Asher tribe, the town of Mishal and the land around it, and the town of Abdon and the land around it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Joshua 21:31">31</a> Helkath and its surrounding lands, and Rehob and its surrounding lands; four cities.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Joshua 21:32">32</a> From the Naphtali tribe, the city of Kedesh in Galilee and the surrounding area serve as a safe city for someone who killed another; also, the towns of Hammothdor and Kartan and their surrounding areas; three cities in total.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Joshua 21:33">33</a> The Gershonite family groups had thirteen cities with the land around them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Joshua 21:34">34</a> To Merari&#39;s descendants, the other Levite families, from Zebulun&#39;s tribe, they gave Jokneam and its surrounding land, also Kartah and its surrounding land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Joshua 21:35">35</a> Dimnah and its nearby lands, Nahalal and its nearby lands; four cities.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Joshua 21:36">36</a> From the tribe of Reuben, the towns Bezer and Jahazah and the areas around them,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Joshua 21:37">37</a> Kedemoth and the land around it, and Mephaath and the land around it; four cities.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Joshua 21:38">38</a> From the Gad tribe, the city Ramoth in Gilead and its surrounding land became a safe city for someone who killed another person by accident; and likewise, the city Mahanaim and its surrounding land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="Joshua 21:39">39</a> Heshbon and its surrounding lands, Jazer and its surrounding lands; in total, four cities.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="Joshua 21:40">40</a> All the cities given to Merari&#39;s descendants by their families, which were left from the Levite families, totaled twelve cities as their share.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="Joshua 21:41">41</a> The Levites had 48 towns within Israel&#39;s lands, each with surrounding land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="Joshua 21:42">42</a> Each of these cities had surrounding areas: that&#39;s how all these cities were.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v43"><a class="verse-num" href="#v43" aria-label="Joshua 21:43">43</a> God gave Israel all the land he promised to their ancestors; they took it over and lived there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v44"><a class="verse-num" href="#v44" aria-label="Joshua 21:44">44</a> God gave them peace on all sides, just as he promised their ancestors. None of their enemies could stand against them; God gave them victory over all their enemies.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v45"><a class="verse-num" href="#v45" aria-label="Joshua 21:45">45</a> Nothing good that God promised to the people of Israel failed; everything happened.</p>
