<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Joshua 14:1">1</a> These are the lands the Israelite people received in Canaan, which the priest Eleazar, Joshua son of Nun, and the tribe leaders gave to them as their share.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Joshua 14:2">2</a> Their land was given to them by drawing lots, as God had ordered through Moses, for the nine and a half tribes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Joshua 14:3">3</a> Moses had given land to two and a half tribes on the other side of the Jordan River, but he did not give any land to the Levites among them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Joshua 14:4">4</a> Joseph&#39;s children formed two groups, Manasseh and Ephraim; so they did not give the Levites a share of the land, only towns to live in, along with nearby lands for their animals and belongings.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Joshua 14:5">5</a> As God told Moses, the Israelites did, and they split the land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Joshua 14:6">6</a> The people of Judah went to Joshua at Gilgal. Caleb, the son of Jephunneh from the Kenaz tribe, said to him, &quot;You know what God told Moses about you and me at Kadesh Barnea.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Joshua 14:7">7</a> I was forty years old when Moses, God&#39;s servant, sent me from Kadeshbarnea to explore the land, and I reported back to him exactly what I felt.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Joshua 14:8">8</a> However, my companions who went with me caused the people to lose courage, but I fully followed God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Joshua 14:9">9</a> And Moses promised on that day, saying, The land where you walked will be your possession and your children&#39;s forever, because you have fully followed God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Joshua 14:10">10</a> And now, look, God has kept me alive, just as he promised, for forty-five years since God told Moses this when the Israelites were moving through the desert: and today, I am eighty-five years old.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Joshua 14:11">11</a> I am still as strong today as I was when Moses sent me out; my strength for battle, to advance and retreat, is unchanged.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Joshua 14:12">12</a> Now give me this mountain, which God talked about before; you heard how the giants lived there and the cities were big and strong. If God is with me, I will push them out, just as God promised.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Joshua 14:13">13</a> Joshua blessed Caleb, son of Jephunneh, and gave him Hebron as his inheritance.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Joshua 14:14">14</a> Hebron became Caleb son of Jephunneh the Kenezite&#39;s land because he always followed God of Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Joshua 14:15">15</a> Before, Hebron was called Kirjatharba, named after Arba, a great leader of the Anakims. And the land had peace from war.</p>
