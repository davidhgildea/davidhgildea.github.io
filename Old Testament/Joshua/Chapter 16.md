<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Joshua 16:1">1</a> Joseph&#39;s descendants received land extending from the Jordan at Jericho to the eastern waters of Jericho, reaching to the desert and then to Bethel hill.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Joshua 16:2">2</a> It goes from Bethel to Luz, and goes through to the edge of Archi to Ataroth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Joshua 16:3">3</a> It goes down west to Japhleti&#39;s border, to Bethhoron the lower border, and to Gezer; its western end is at the sea.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Joshua 16:4">4</a> Joseph&#39;s sons, Manasseh and Ephraim, received their share of the land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Joshua 16:5">5</a> The territory for the families of Ephraim&#39;s descendants was this: their land on the east reached from Atarothaddar to Upper Bethhoron.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Joshua 16:6">6</a> The border extended toward the sea to Michmethah on the north; it turned east to Taanathshiloh, going past it to Janohah on the east.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Joshua 16:7">7</a> It went from Janohah to Ataroth and Naarath, reached Jericho, and ended at the Jordan River.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Joshua 16:8">8</a> The boundary started at Tappuah, went west to the Kanah River, and ended at the sea. This was the land for the families of Ephraim&#39;s tribe.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Joshua 16:9">9</a> The towns set aside for the Ephraim tribe were within the land given to the Manasseh tribe, including all those towns and their surrounding villages.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Joshua 16:10">10</a> They did not force the Canaanites who lived in Gezer to leave, but the Canaanites still live among the people of Ephraim today, and they have to pay taxes.</p>
