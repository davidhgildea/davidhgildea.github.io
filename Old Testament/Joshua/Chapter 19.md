<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Joshua 19:1">1</a> The second choice was for the tribe of Simeon&#39;s descendants, according to their families. Their land was inside the land given to Judah&#39;s descendants.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Joshua 19:2">2</a> They received Beersheba, Sheba, and Moladah as their inheritance.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Joshua 19:3">3</a> And Hazarshual, Balah, and Azem.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Joshua 19:4">4</a> Eltolad, Bethul, and Hormah,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Joshua 19:5">5</a> Ziklag, Bethmarcaboth, and Hazarsusah,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Joshua 19:6">6</a> Bethlebaoth and Sharuhen; thirteen cities with their villages.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Joshua 19:7">7</a> Ain, Remmon, Ether, and Ashan; four cities with their surrounding villages.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Joshua 19:8">8</a> All the small towns near these cities, as far as Baalathbeer and Ramath in the south, were part of the land given to the Simeon tribe&#39;s families.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Joshua 19:9">9</a> The tribe of Simeon received their land from the allotment of the tribe of Judah because Judah&#39;s portion was too large for them, so Simeon&#39;s inheritance was within Judah&#39;s land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Joshua 19:10">10</a> The third share was for the families of Zebulun, and their territory reached to Sarid.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Joshua 19:11">11</a> Their boundary went up to the sea, through Maralah, to Dabbasheth, and to the river near Jokneam.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Joshua 19:12">12</a> It turned from Sarid to the east, towards the sunrise, to the edge of Chislothtabor, then continued to Daberath, and went up to Japhia.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Joshua 19:13">13</a> It moves on from there to the east toward Gittahhepher, to Ittahkazin, then turns toward Remmonmethoar until it reaches Neah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Joshua 19:14">14</a> The border goes around it on the north side to Hannathon, and ends in the Jiphthahel Valley.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Joshua 19:15">15</a> Kattath, Nahallal, Shimron, Idalah, and Bethlehem: twelve cities with their villages.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Joshua 19:16">16</a> This is what the families of Zebulun&#39;s children will receive as their inheritance: these cities and their surrounding villages.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Joshua 19:17">17</a> The fourth draw was for Issachar, for the families of Issachar&#39;s children.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Joshua 19:18">18</a> Their boundary went towards Jezreel, Chesulloth, and Shunem.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Joshua 19:19">19</a> And Haphraim, Shihon, and Anaharath.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Joshua 19:20">20</a> And Rabbith, Kishion, and Abez,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Joshua 19:21">21</a> And Remeth, Engannim, Enhaddah, and Bethpazzez;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Joshua 19:22">22</a> The border touches Tabor, Shahazimah, and Bethshemesh, ending at the Jordan River: sixteen cities and their villages.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Joshua 19:23">23</a> This is what the family groups of the tribe of Issachar receive as their inheritance: the towns and their surrounding villages.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Joshua 19:24">24</a> The fifth share was for the Asher tribe family groups.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Joshua 19:25">25</a> Their boundary included Helkath, Hali, Beten, and Achshaph.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Joshua 19:26">26</a> Alammelech, Amad, and Misheal; the land goes up to Carmel in the west and to Shihorlibnath.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Joshua 19:27">27</a> It turns toward the sunrise at Bethdagon, goes to Zebulun, to the Jiphthahel Valley north of Bethemek and Neiel, and ends at Cabul on the left.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Joshua 19:28">28</a> Hebron, Rehob, Hammon, Kanah, all the way to great Zidon;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Joshua 19:29">29</a> Then the border turns to Ramah and the fortified city of Tyre; the border turns to Hosah, ending at the sea from the border to Achzib.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Joshua 19:30">30</a> Ummah, Aphek, and Rehob too: twenty-two cities and their villages.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Joshua 19:31">31</a> This is what the Asher tribe&#39;s families will receive: These towns and their surrounding villages.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Joshua 19:32">32</a> The sixth share was given to the Naphtali people, each family group among them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Joshua 19:33">33</a> Their land extended from Heleph and Allon to Zaanannim, including Adami, Nekeb, and Jabneel, to Lakum, and ended at the Jordan River.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Joshua 19:34">34</a> Then the border turns west to Aznothtabor, goes from there to Hukkok, touches Zebulun on the south, Asher on the west, and Judah at the Jordan River to the east.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Joshua 19:35">35</a> The protected cities are Ziddim, Zer, Hammath, Rakkath, and Chinnereth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Joshua 19:36">36</a> Adamah, Ramah, and Hazor,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Joshua 19:37">37</a> Kedesh, Edrei, and Enhazor,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Joshua 19:38">38</a> Iron, Migdalel, Horem, Bethanath, and Bethshemesh; nineteen cities with their villages.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="Joshua 19:39">39</a> This is what the Naphtali tribe&#39;s families will receive as their inheritance: the towns and their surrounding villages.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="Joshua 19:40">40</a> The seventh share was picked for the Dan tribe families.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="Joshua 19:41">41</a> Their land included Zorah, Eshtaol, and Irshemesh.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="Joshua 19:42">42</a> And Shaalabbin, and Ajalon, and Jethlah,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v43"><a class="verse-num" href="#v43" aria-label="Joshua 19:43">43</a> Elon, Thimnathah, and Ekron,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v44"><a class="verse-num" href="#v44" aria-label="Joshua 19:44">44</a> Eltekeh, Gibbethon, and Baalath,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v45"><a class="verse-num" href="#v45" aria-label="Joshua 19:45">45</a> Jehud, Beneberak, and Gathrimmon,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v46"><a class="verse-num" href="#v46" aria-label="Joshua 19:46">46</a> And Mejarkon, and Rakkon, along with the territory facing Japho.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v47"><a class="verse-num" href="#v47" aria-label="Joshua 19:47">47</a> The land for Dan&#39;s descendants was too small, so they fought against Leshem, captured it, killed its people, took it over, lived there, and renamed Leshem as Dan after their ancestor Dan.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v48"><a class="verse-num" href="#v48" aria-label="Joshua 19:48">48</a> This is what the Dan tribe&#39;s families inherited: these cities and their villages.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v49"><a class="verse-num" href="#v49" aria-label="Joshua 19:49">49</a> After they finished dividing the land for inheritance by their regions, the people of Israel gave a share to Joshua, the son of Nun.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v50"><a class="verse-num" href="#v50" aria-label="Joshua 19:50">50</a> Following God&#39;s instructions, they gave him the city he requested, Timnathserah in the hills of Ephraim. He then rebuilt the city and lived there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v51"><a class="verse-num" href="#v51" aria-label="Joshua 19:51">51</a> These are the lands Eleazar the priest, Joshua son of Nun, and the tribal leaders of Israel&#39;s children divided as inheritances by drawing lots in Shiloh before God, at the entrance of the meeting tent. This is how they finished dividing the land.</p>
