<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Esther 6:1">1</a> That night the king could not sleep, so he ordered the record book of past events to be brought and read to him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Esther 6:2">2</a> It was recorded that Mordecai reported Bigthana and Teresh, two of the king&#39;s guards, who planned to attack King Ahasuerus.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Esther 6:3">3</a> The king asked, &quot;What honor and respect has Mordecai received for this?&quot; The king&#39;s servants who served him replied, &quot;Nothing has been done for him.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Esther 6:4">4</a> The king asked, &quot;Who is in the court?&quot; Just then, Haman arrived in the outer court of the king’s palace, hoping to talk to the king about hanging Mordecai on the gallows he had set up for him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Esther 6:5">5</a> The king&#39;s helpers told him, &quot;Look, Haman is standing in the courtyard.&quot; And the king said, &quot;Let him come in.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Esther 6:6">6</a> So Haman arrived. And the king asked him, &quot;What should be done for the man the king wants to honor?&quot; Haman thought to himself, &quot;Who would the king want to honor more than me?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Esther 6:7">7</a> Haman told the king, &quot;For the man the king likes to honor,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Esther 6:8">8</a> Bring the king&#39;s fancy clothes, the horse the king rides, and the royal crown that is placed on his head.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Esther 6:9">9</a> Give these clothes and horse to one of the king&#39;s top princes, so they can dress the man the king wants to honor. Then have him ride through the city streets while they announce, &quot;This is how the man the king wants to honor is treated.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Esther 6:10">10</a> The king told Haman, &quot;Hurry and take the clothes and the horse, as you suggested, and do the same for Mordecai the Jew, who sits at the palace entrance. Don&#39;t leave out anything you mentioned.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Esther 6:11">11</a> Haman took the clothes and the horse, dressed Mordecai, and led him on the horse through the city streets, announcing, &quot;This is how the man the king wants to honor will be treated.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Esther 6:12">12</a> Mordecai went back to the king&#39;s entrance. But Haman hurried to his home, sad, with his head covered.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Esther 6:13">13</a> Haman told his wife Zeresh and all his friends everything that had happened to him. Then his wise friends and his wife Zeresh said to him, &quot;If Mordecai is from the Jewish people, and you have started to lose to him, you will not win against him, but you will definitely lose to him.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Esther 6:14">14</a> While they were still chatting with him, the king&#39;s attendants arrived and quickly took Haman to the feast Esther had set up.</p>
