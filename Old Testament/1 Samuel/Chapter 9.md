<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Samuel 9:1">1</a> There was a man from the tribe of Benjamin named Kish. He was the son of Abiel, the grandson of Zeror, the great-grandson of Bechorath, and the great-great-grandson of Aphiah. He was a strong and influential man.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Samuel 9:2">2</a> He had a son named Saul, a fine young man and handsome. There was no one more handsome in Israel than him. He was taller than everyone else from the shoulders up.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Samuel 9:3">3</a> Kish, Saul&#39;s dad, lost his donkeys. Kish told Saul, his son, &quot;Take a servant with you, get up, and go find the donkeys.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Samuel 9:4">4</a> He went through the hills of Ephraim and the area of Shalisha, but they didn&#39;t find them. Then they went through the area of Shalim, and they weren&#39;t there either. He also went through the land of Benjamin, but still, they didn&#39;t find them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Samuel 9:5">5</a> When they reached the land of Zuph, Saul told his servant with him, &quot;Let&#39;s go back, or my father will stop worrying about the donkeys and start worrying about us.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Samuel 9:6">6</a> He told him, &quot;Look, in this city there&#39;s a man of God who is respected; everything he says happens for sure. Let&#39;s go there; maybe he can tell us the way we should take.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Samuel 9:7">7</a> Then Saul said to his servant, &quot;But if we go, what can we give the man? Our food is gone, and we don&#39;t have a gift for the man of God. What do we have?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Samuel 9:8">8</a> The servant told Saul again, &quot;Look, I have a quarter of a silver coin here. I will give it to the man of God to show us the way.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Samuel 9:9">9</a> Before in Israel, when someone wanted to ask God something, they would say, &quot;Let&#39;s go to the seer.&quot; What we now call a Prophet was once called a Seer.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Samuel 9:10">10</a> Saul told his servant, &quot;Good idea; let&#39;s go.&quot; So they set off towards the city where the man of God lived.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Samuel 9:11">11</a> As they climbed the hill to the city, they met young women going to get water and asked them, &quot;Is the prophet here?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Samuel 9:12">12</a> They replied, &quot;Yes, he is here; he&#39;s just ahead of you. Hurry now, because he arrived in the city today since there&#39;s a people&#39;s sacrifice happening today at the high place.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 Samuel 9:13">13</a> As soon as you arrive in the city, you will immediately find him before he goes up to eat at the high place, because the people won&#39;t eat until he arrives to bless the sacrifice. Then, those who are invited will eat. So go now; you will find him at this time.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="1 Samuel 9:14">14</a> They went up to the city, and as they arrived, they saw Samuel coming out to meet them on his way to the worship place.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="1 Samuel 9:15">15</a> God had told Samuel the day before Saul arrived, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="1 Samuel 9:16">16</a> Tomorrow at this time, I will send you a man from the land of Benjamin, and you will anoint him as leader over my people Israel, so he can save them from the Philistines; because I have heard my people&#39;s cry for help.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="1 Samuel 9:17">17</a> When Samuel saw Saul, God told him, &quot;Look at the man I told you about! He will rule over my people.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="1 Samuel 9:18">18</a> Saul came close to Samuel at the gate and asked, &quot;Please tell me where the prophet&#39;s house is.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="1 Samuel 9:19">19</a> Samuel replied to Saul, &quot;I am the prophet. Go ahead of me to the worship place; you will eat with me today, and tomorrow I will let you go and explain everything you are wondering about.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="1 Samuel 9:20">20</a> Don&#39;t worry about your donkeys that went missing three days ago; they&#39;ve been found. And who does all of Israel look up to? Isn&#39;t it you and your family?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="1 Samuel 9:21">21</a> Saul replied, &quot;Aren&#39;t I from Benjamin, the smallest tribe of Israel, and isn&#39;t my family the least important of all the families in the tribe of Benjamin? So why are you saying this to me?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="1 Samuel 9:22">22</a> Samuel took Saul and his servant to the dining room and had them sit in the best place among the about thirty invited guests.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="1 Samuel 9:23">23</a> Samuel told the cook, &quot;Bring the piece I gave you, the one I told you to keep aside.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="1 Samuel 9:24">24</a> The cook picked up the shoulder meat and everything on it, and placed it in front of Saul. Then Samuel said, &quot;Look, this is what&#39;s left! Put it in front of you and eat. It has been saved for you since I invited the people.&quot; So Saul ate with Samuel that day.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="1 Samuel 9:25">25</a> After they came down from the high place to the city, Samuel talked with Saul on the roof of the house.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="1 Samuel 9:26">26</a> They got up early. When the day began to dawn, Samuel called Saul to the rooftop and said, &quot;Get up, so I can send you off.&quot; Saul got up, and they both went outside, Saul and Samuel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="1 Samuel 9:27">27</a> As they were walking to the edge of the city, Samuel told Saul, &quot;Tell the servant to go ahead of us,&quot; (and he went ahead), &quot;but you stay here for a moment, so I can tell you what God has to say.&quot;</p>
