<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Samuel 27:1">1</a> David thought to himself, &quot;I will one day die by Saul&#39;s hand. The best thing I can do is quickly run away to the Philistine territory; then Saul will give up looking for me in Israel, and I will be safe from him.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Samuel 27:2">2</a> David got up and went with his six hundred men to Achish, son of Maoch, the king of Gath.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Samuel 27:3">3</a> David lived with Achish in Gath, along with his men and their families, including David&#39;s two wives, Ahinoam from Jezreel and Abigail, who was Nabal&#39;s widow, from Carmel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Samuel 27:4">4</a> Saul heard that David had escaped to Gath, so he stopped searching for him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Samuel 27:5">5</a> David said to Achish, &quot;If you now favor me, please give me a place in one of the country towns where I can live. Why should I, your servant, live in the capital city with you?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Samuel 27:6">6</a> That day, Achish gave him Ziklag, so Ziklag belongs to the kings of Judah to this day.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Samuel 27:7">7</a> David lived in the land of the Philistines for one year and four months.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Samuel 27:8">8</a> David and his men went up and attacked the Geshurites, the Gezrites, and the Amalekites, because these people had lived in the area from Shur to Egypt since ancient times.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Samuel 27:9">9</a> David attacked the land, killed everyone, took the sheep, cows, donkeys, camels, and clothes, then went back to Achish.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Samuel 27:10">10</a> Achish asked, &quot;Where did you go today?&quot; David answered, &quot;To the southern part of Judah, the southern area of the Jerahmeelites, and the southern territory of the Kenites.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Samuel 27:11">11</a> David did not let any man or woman live to go and report in Gath, thinking, &quot;They might inform against us, saying, &#39;This is what David does, and this will be his way as long as he stays in the land of the Philistines.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Samuel 27:12">12</a> Achish trusted David, thinking, &quot;He has made his people Israel completely hate him; so he will be my servant forever.&quot;</p>
