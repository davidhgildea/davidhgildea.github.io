<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Samuel 12:1">1</a> Samuel told all of Israel, &quot;Look, I have listened to what you said to me, and I have made a king for you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Samuel 12:2">2</a> Now look, the king leads you. I am old with gray hair, and my sons are with you. I have led you from my youth until now.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Samuel 12:3">3</a> Look, I am here: speak against me before God, and his chosen leader: Which ox or donkey have I taken? Who have I cheated or treated unfairly? From whom have I taken a bribe to look the other way? Tell me and I will give it back.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Samuel 12:4">4</a> They said, &quot;You have not cheated or mistreated us, nor have you taken anything from anyone.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Samuel 12:5">5</a> He told them, &quot;God is your witness, and his chosen one is also a witness today, that you found nothing wrong in what I&#39;ve done.&quot; And they replied, &quot;He is our witness.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Samuel 12:6">6</a> Samuel told the people, &quot;It is God who promoted Moses and Aaron, and who led your ancestors out of Egypt.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Samuel 12:7">7</a> Now stand still so I can tell you about all the good things God did for you and your ancestors.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Samuel 12:8">8</a> When Jacob arrived in Egypt, and your ancestors called out to God, then God sent Moses and Aaron who led your ancestors out of Egypt and settled them here.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Samuel 12:9">9</a> When they forgot God, he let Sisera, the army leader of Hazor, the Philistines, and the king of Moab capture them and fight against them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Samuel 12:10">10</a> They called out to God, saying, &quot;We have sinned by leaving God and serving Baal and Ashtaroth. Now save us from our enemies, and we will serve you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Samuel 12:11">11</a> God sent Jerubbaal, Bedan, Jephthah, and Samuel, and saved you from your enemies all around, and you lived in safety.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Samuel 12:12">12</a> When you saw that King Nahash of the Ammonites was attacking, you told me, &#39;No, we want a king to rule over us,&#39; even though God was already your king.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 Samuel 12:13">13</a> Now see the king you chose and wanted! God has made him your king.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="1 Samuel 12:14">14</a> If you respect God, serve Him, listen to His words, and don&#39;t disobey His commands, then both you and the king who rules over you will keep on following God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="1 Samuel 12:15">15</a> But if you don&#39;t listen to God&#39;s voice and disobey His command, then God will be against you, just as He was against your ancestors.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="1 Samuel 12:16">16</a> Now stand and watch this amazing thing that God will do before you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="1 Samuel 12:17">17</a> Isn&#39;t it wheat harvest today? I will call to God, and he will make thunder and rain come; so you will realize and see how bad your wrongdoings are, which you did before God by asking for a king.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="1 Samuel 12:18">18</a> So Samuel prayed to God, and God made thunder and rain that day; and all the people were very afraid of God and Samuel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="1 Samuel 12:19">19</a> And all the people told Samuel, &quot;Pray for us to God that we don&#39;t die, because we have made our sins worse by asking for a king.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="1 Samuel 12:20">20</a> Samuel told the people, &quot;Don&#39;t be afraid. You have done wrong, but do not stop following God. Instead, serve God with all your heart.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="1 Samuel 12:21">21</a> Do not turn away: if you do, you will chase worthless things that cannot help or save you, because they are useless.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="1 Samuel 12:22">22</a> God will not abandon his people for the sake of his great name: because it has pleased God to make you his people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="1 Samuel 12:23">23</a> Also, I would never sin against God by stopping my prayers for you. Instead, I will show you the good and right way to live.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="1 Samuel 12:24">24</a> Only be afraid of God, and serve Him honestly with all your heart: for think about the amazing things He has done for you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="1 Samuel 12:25">25</a> If you keep doing bad things, you and your king will be destroyed.</p>
