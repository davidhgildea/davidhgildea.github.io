<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Samuel 13:1">1</a> Saul was king for one year; after two years as king of Israel,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Samuel 13:2">2</a> Saul picked 3,000 Israeli soldiers; 2,000 stayed with Saul in Michmash and Bethel, 1,000 were with Jonathan in Gibeah of Benjamin, and he sent the others home.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Samuel 13:3">3</a> Jonathan attacked the Philistine outpost at Geba, and the Philistines found out. Saul sounded the trumpet all over the country, saying, &quot;Let the Israelites hear.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Samuel 13:4">4</a> Everyone in Israel heard that Saul had defeated a Philistine outpost, and that the Philistines hated Israel. So the people gathered to follow Saul to Gilgal.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Samuel 13:5">5</a> The Philistines came together to battle Israel with thirty thousand chariots, six thousand horsemen, and a huge crowd like the sand by the sea. They advanced and set up camp at Michmash, to the east of Bethaven.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Samuel 13:6">6</a> When the Israelite men realized they were in trouble because the people were distressed, they hid in caves, bushes, rocks, high areas, and pits.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Samuel 13:7">7</a> Some Hebrews crossed the Jordan River to Gad and Gilead, while Saul was still in Gilgal, and all the people with him were scared.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Samuel 13:8">8</a> He waited seven days, as Samuel had ordered, but Samuel did not come to Gilgal; and the people left him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Samuel 13:9">9</a> Saul said, &quot;Bring me a burnt offering and peace offerings.&quot; Then he gave the burnt offering.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Samuel 13:10">10</a> After he finished the burnt offering, Samuel arrived; Saul went out to greet him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Samuel 13:11">11</a> And Samuel asked, &quot;What have you done?&quot; Saul answered, &quot;I saw the people leaving me, you didn&#39;t arrive when you said you would, and the Philistines were uniting at Michmash.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Samuel 13:12">12</a> So I thought, &#39;The Philistines will soon attack me at Gilgal, and I haven&#39;t asked for God&#39;s help.&#39; So I felt I had to go ahead and offer a sacrifice.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 Samuel 13:13">13</a> Samuel said to Saul, &quot;You have acted foolishly; you did not follow God&#39;s command which He gave you. Because of this, God would have made your rule over Israel last forever, but now he will not.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="1 Samuel 13:14">14</a> But now your kingdom won&#39;t last. God has chosen a man who pleases Him, and God has told him to lead His people because you did not follow God&#39;s commands.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="1 Samuel 13:15">15</a> Samuel got up and went from Gilgal to Gibeah in Benjamin. Saul counted the people with him, about six hundred men.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="1 Samuel 13:16">16</a> Saul, his son Jonathan, and the people with them stayed in Gibeah in Benjamin&#39;s land, while the Philistines camped in Michmash.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="1 Samuel 13:17">17</a> The raiders came out of the Philistines&#39; camp in three groups: one group went toward the road to Ophrah, in Shual&#39;s territory.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="1 Samuel 13:18">18</a> One group went towards Bethhoron, and another went along the road that borders the valley of Zeboim, heading to the desert.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="1 Samuel 13:19">19</a> In all of Israel, no blacksmith could be found because the Philistines didn&#39;t want the Hebrews to make swords or spears.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="1 Samuel 13:20">20</a> All the Israelites went to the Philistines to sharpen their plows, cutting tools, axes, and hoes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="1 Samuel 13:21">21</a> They had a tool to sharpen the hoes, plows, forks, axes, and prods.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="1 Samuel 13:22">22</a> On the battle day, no one with Saul and Jonathan had a sword or spear, except for Saul and his son Jonathan.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="1 Samuel 13:23">23</a> The Philistine soldiers went to the Michmash path.</p>
