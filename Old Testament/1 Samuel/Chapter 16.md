<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Samuel 16:1">1</a> God said to Samuel, &quot;How long will you be sad for Saul, when I have decided he will not be king over Israel? Put oil in your horn and go. I will send you to Jesse of Bethlehem because I have chosen one of his sons to be king.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Samuel 16:2">2</a> And Samuel said, &quot;How can I go? If Saul hears it, he will kill me.&quot; And God said, &quot;Take a young cow with you and say, &#39;I have come to make a sacrifice to God.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Samuel 16:3">3</a> Invite Jesse to the offering, and I will tell you what to do: you shall anoint for me the one I point out to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Samuel 16:4">4</a> Samuel did as God said and went to Bethlehem. The town leaders shook with fear when he arrived and asked, &quot;Do you come in peace?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Samuel 16:5">5</a> He said, &quot;I&#39;ve come to offer a sacrifice to God peacefully. Get ready and join me in the sacrifice.&quot; He set Jesse and his sons apart as holy and invited them to the sacrifice.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Samuel 16:6">6</a> When they arrived, he saw Eliab and thought, &quot;Certainly, God&#39;s chosen one is here.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Samuel 16:7">7</a> But God told Samuel, Do not look at his face or how tall he is, because I have not chosen him. God does not see the way people see; people look at the outside, but God looks at the heart.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Samuel 16:8">8</a> Then Jesse called Abinadab and had him walk in front of Samuel. And he said, God has not chosen this one either.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Samuel 16:9">9</a> Then Jesse had Shammah walk by, but he said, &quot;God has not chosen this one either.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Samuel 16:10">10</a> Jesse had seven of his sons walk in front of Samuel. Samuel told Jesse, &quot;God has not chosen these.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Samuel 16:11">11</a> Samuel asked Jesse, &quot;Are all your children here?&quot; Jesse replied, &quot;The youngest is still out, taking care of the sheep.&quot; Then Samuel said to Jesse, &quot;Send for him; we won&#39;t sit down until he arrives.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Samuel 16:12">12</a> He sent for him and brought him in. He had red cheeks, a handsome face, and looked good. God said, &quot;Stand up, anoint him; this is the one.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 Samuel 16:13">13</a> Samuel took the oil horn and put oil on David in front of his brothers. God&#39;s Spirit came on David from that day on. Then Samuel got up and went to Ramah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="1 Samuel 16:14">14</a> God&#39;s Spirit left Saul, and a bad spirit from God troubled him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="1 Samuel 16:15">15</a> Saul&#39;s servants told him, &quot;Look, an evil spirit from God is troubling you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="1 Samuel 16:16">16</a> Please tell your servants here to find a skilled harp player. When the bad spirit from God comes on you, he can play and you will feel better.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="1 Samuel 16:17">17</a> Saul told his servants, &quot;Find a man who can play music well and bring him to me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="1 Samuel 16:18">18</a> A servant replied, &quot;Look, I&#39;ve seen Jesse of Bethlehem&#39;s son, who is skilled at playing music, a brave strong man, a fighter, smart in decision-making, good-looking, and God is with him.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="1 Samuel 16:19">19</a> So Saul sent messages to Jesse, saying, &quot;Send me your son David, who is with the sheep.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="1 Samuel 16:20">20</a> Jesse took a donkey loaded with bread, a bottle of wine, and a young goat, and sent them with his son David to Saul.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="1 Samuel 16:21">21</a> David went to Saul, stood in front of him, Saul loved him very much, and David became his armor carrier.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="1 Samuel 16:22">22</a> Saul sent a message to Jesse, saying, &quot;Please let David come and see me, because I like him.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="1 Samuel 16:23">23</a> When the evil spirit from God came upon Saul, David played the harp with his hand, and Saul felt better, and the evil spirit left him.</p>
