<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Samuel 3:1">1</a> The boy Samuel served God with Eli. In those times, God&#39;s messages were rare and visions were not common.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Samuel 3:2">2</a> At that time, when Eli was lying down in his spot and his eyes were starting to grow weak so he couldn&#39;t see;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Samuel 3:3">3</a> Before the lamp of God went out in God&#39;s temple, where God&#39;s ark was, and Samuel had gone to bed;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Samuel 3:4">4</a> God called Samuel, and he answered, &quot;I&#39;m here.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Samuel 3:5">5</a> He ran to Eli and said, &quot;Here I am; you called me.&quot; But Eli answered, &quot;I didn&#39;t call; go back to bed.&quot; So he went and lay down again.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Samuel 3:6">6</a> God called Samuel again, and Samuel got up and went to Eli and said, &quot;Here I am, you called me.&quot; But he replied, &quot;I didn&#39;t call you, my son; go back to sleep.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Samuel 3:7">7</a> Samuel did not yet know God, and God&#39;s word had not been shown to him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Samuel 3:8">8</a> God called Samuel a third time. He got up, went to Eli, and said, &quot;I&#39;m here; you called me.&quot; Then Eli realized that God had called the boy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Samuel 3:9">9</a> So Eli told Samuel, &quot;Go back to bed, and if he calls you, say, &#39;Speak, God; your servant is listening.&#39;&quot; Then Samuel went and lay down in his spot.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Samuel 3:10">10</a> God came, stood, and called as before, &quot;Samuel, Samuel.&quot; Then Samuel answered, &quot;Speak, for your servant is listening.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Samuel 3:11">11</a> God said to Samuel, &quot;Look, I will do something in Israel that will make everyone&#39;s ears who hears it ring.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Samuel 3:12">12</a> On that day, I will do everything I said about Eli&#39;s family: when I start, I will finish it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 Samuel 3:13">13</a> I have told him that I will always judge his family for the wrongs he knows about; because his sons acted badly, and he did not stop them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="1 Samuel 3:14">14</a> So I promised Eli&#39;s family that their wrongs will never be forgiven by sacrifice or offering.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="1 Samuel 3:15">15</a> Samuel lay until morning and opened the doors of God&#39;s house. Samuel was afraid to tell Eli about the vision.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="1 Samuel 3:16">16</a> Then Eli called Samuel and said, &quot;Samuel, my son.&quot; And he replied, &quot;I&#39;m here.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="1 Samuel 3:17">17</a> He asked, &quot;What did God tell you? Please, don&#39;t keep it from me. May God punish you if you hide anything he said from me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="1 Samuel 3:18">18</a> Samuel told him everything, and kept nothing from him. And he said, It is God: let him do what seems good to him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="1 Samuel 3:19">19</a> Samuel grew up, and God was with him, and made sure that all his words were effective.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="1 Samuel 3:20">20</a> Everyone in Israel, from Dan to Beersheba, understood that Samuel was confirmed as a prophet of God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="1 Samuel 3:21">21</a> God appeared again in Shiloh because He showed Himself to Samuel in Shiloh through God&#39;s message.</p>
