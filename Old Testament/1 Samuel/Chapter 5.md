<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Samuel 5:1">1</a> The Philistines captured God&#39;s chest and moved it from Ebenezer to Ashdod.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Samuel 5:2">2</a> When the Philistines captured the ark of God, they brought it into Dagon&#39;s temple and placed it next to Dagon.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Samuel 5:3">3</a> When the people of Ashdod got up the next morning, they saw Dagon had fallen face down on the ground before God&#39;s ark. They put Dagon back in his spot.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Samuel 5:4">4</a> When they got up the next morning, they saw that Dagon had fallen face down on the ground before God&#39;s ark; Dagon&#39;s head and both hands were cut off and lying on the doorstep, leaving only his body.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Samuel 5:5">5</a> So the priests of Dagon and anyone who enters Dagon&#39;s temple in Ashdod do not step on Dagon&#39;s doorway even now.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Samuel 5:6">6</a> God&#39;s hand was heavy on the people of Ashdod; he ruined them and struck them with tumors, both in Ashdod and its surrounding areas.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Samuel 5:7">7</a> When the people of Ashdod noticed this, they said, &quot;The ark of Israel&#39;s God can&#39;t stay with us because his power is too much for us and for Dagon, our god.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Samuel 5:8">8</a> So they called together all the Philistine rulers and asked, &quot;What should we do with the ark of the God of Israel?&quot; They decided, &quot;Take the ark of the God of Israel to Gath.&quot; Then they moved the ark of the God of Israel there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Samuel 5:9">9</a> And it happened that after they moved it, God brought a terrible disaster upon the city. He struck the people, young and old, and they developed tumors in their private areas.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Samuel 5:10">10</a> So they sent God&#39;s ark to Ekron. When God&#39;s ark arrived in Ekron, the people of Ekron shouted, &quot;They have brought the ark of Israel&#39;s God to us to kill us and our people.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Samuel 5:11">11</a> So they called all the leaders of the Philistines and said, &quot;Send back the ark of the God of Israel, so it can return to where it belongs and not kill us and our people.&quot; There was fatal trouble all over the city; God&#39;s power was very strong there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Samuel 5:12">12</a> The men who did not die were struck with tumors, and the city&#39;s cry reached up to heaven.</p>
