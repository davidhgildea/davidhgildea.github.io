<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Samuel 29:1">1</a> The Philistines brought all their armies together at Aphek, while the Israelites set up camp by a spring in Jezreel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Samuel 29:2">2</a> The leaders of the Philistines marched by hundreds and by thousands, while David and his men were at the back with Achish.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Samuel 29:3">3</a> The Philistine leaders asked, &quot;Why are these Hebrews here?&quot; Achish replied to the Philistine leaders, &quot;Isn&#39;t this David, the servant of Saul the king of Israel, who has been with me for some time now, and I have found nothing wrong with him up to today?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Samuel 29:4">4</a> The Philistine leaders were angry with him and said, &quot;Send this man back to the place you assigned him. He shouldn&#39;t fight with us, or he might turn against us during the battle. How could he win back his master&#39;s favor if not by taking the heads of our soldiers?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Samuel 29:5">5</a> Isn&#39;t this David, the one they celebrated in dances, saying, Saul killed thousands, but David killed tens of thousands?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Samuel 29:6">6</a> Then Achish spoke to David and said, &quot;As surely as God lives, you have been honest, and your actions with me in the army have been good; I&#39;ve seen nothing bad in you from when you first came to me until now. But the rulers don&#39;t like you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Samuel 29:7">7</a> So now, go back and leave in peace, so you don&#39;t upset the Philistine leaders.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Samuel 29:8">8</a> David asked Achish, &quot;What have I done wrong? What fault have you found in me, after all the time I&#39;ve been with you, that I can&#39;t go and fight the king&#39;s enemies?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Samuel 29:9">9</a> Achish replied to David, &quot;I know you are as good as an angel of God to me. But the Philistine leaders have said, &#39;He cannot join us in the fight.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Samuel 29:10">10</a> So get up early in the morning with your master&#39;s servants who are with you. Once it&#39;s early and light, go on your way.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Samuel 29:11">11</a> David and his men got up early in the morning to leave and go back to the land of the Philistines. The Philistines went to Jezreel.</p>
