<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Samuel 31:1">1</a> The Philistines battled Israel: the Israelites ran from the Philistines and many were killed on Mount Gilboa.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Samuel 31:2">2</a> The Philistines chased Saul and his sons closely; they killed Jonathan, Abinadab, and Melchishua, Saul&#39;s sons.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Samuel 31:3">3</a> The fight was very bad for Saul, and archers shot him; he was badly hurt by the archers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Samuel 31:4">4</a> Saul said to his helper, &quot;Pull out your sword and stab me with it; otherwise these enemies will come and kill me and treat me badly.&quot; But his helper wouldn&#39;t do it because he was very scared. So Saul took a sword and killed himself with it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Samuel 31:5">5</a> When his helper saw Saul was dead, he too fell on his sword and died with him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Samuel 31:6">6</a> Saul died, along with his three sons, his armor bearer, and all his men, on the same day.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Samuel 31:7">7</a> When the Israelites across the valley and the Jordan River saw that the Israelites were running away and that Saul and his sons had died, they left their towns and ran away; then the Philistines moved in and lived there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Samuel 31:8">8</a> The next day, when the Philistines came to take things from the dead bodies, they found Saul and his three sons dead on Mount Gilboa.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Samuel 31:9">9</a> They cut off his head, took his armor, and sent them throughout the land of the Philistines to show them at the temples of their gods and to the people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Samuel 31:10">10</a> They placed his armor in the temple of Ashtaroth and hung his body on the wall of Bethshan.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Samuel 31:11">11</a> When the people of Jabeshgilead heard what the Philistines did to Saul;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Samuel 31:12">12</a> All the brave men got up, traveled all night, took Saul&#39;s body and his sons&#39; bodies from the Bethshan wall, and went to Jabesh where they burned them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 Samuel 31:13">13</a> They collected their bones, buried them under a tree at Jabesh, and did not eat for seven days.</p>
