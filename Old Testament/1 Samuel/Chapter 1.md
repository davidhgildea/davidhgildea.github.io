<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Samuel 1:1">1</a> There was a man from Ramathaim, a town in the Ephraim hills, named Elkanah. He was Jeroham&#39;s son, Elihu&#39;s grandson, Tohu&#39;s great-grandson, and Zuph&#39;s great-great-grandson, from the tribe of Ephraim.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Samuel 1:2">2</a> He had two wives; one was named Hannah and the other Peninnah. Peninnah had children, but Hannah did not.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Samuel 1:3">3</a> This man went to his city every year to worship and offer sacrifices to God in Shiloh. Eli&#39;s two sons, Hophni and Phinehas, who were priests of God, were there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Samuel 1:4">4</a> When it was time for Elkanah to make an offering, he gave portions to his wife Peninnah and to all her sons and daughters.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Samuel 1:5">5</a> But he gave Hannah a special share because he loved her, even though God had closed her womb.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Samuel 1:6">6</a> Her enemy upset her a lot to make her worry because God had closed her womb.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Samuel 1:7">7</a> Every year, when she went to God&#39;s house, she upset her. So she cried and didn&#39;t eat.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Samuel 1:8">8</a> Elkanah, her husband, said to her, &quot;Hannah, why are you crying? Why aren&#39;t you eating? Why are you so sad? Aren&#39;t I more important to you than ten sons?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Samuel 1:9">9</a> Hannah stood up after eating in Shiloh and after drinking. Eli the priest was sitting on a chair by the temple doorpost of God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Samuel 1:10">10</a> She felt deep sadness, prayed to God, and cried hard.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Samuel 1:11">11</a> She made a promise and said, &quot;God of all, if you truly see my suffering and remember me, and not forget me, but give me a son, then I will dedicate him to God for all his life, and his hair will never be cut.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Samuel 1:12">12</a> As she kept praying to God, Eli noticed her mouth moving.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 Samuel 1:13">13</a> Hannah was praying silently; her lips moved but no sound came out, so Eli thought she was drunk.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="1 Samuel 1:14">14</a> Eli asked her, &quot;How long will you stay drunk? Stop drinking wine.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="1 Samuel 1:15">15</a> Hannah replied, &quot;No, sir, I am a woman with a sad heart. I haven&#39;t had wine or strong drink but have told all my troubles to God.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="1 Samuel 1:16">16</a> Don&#39;t consider me a wicked woman, for I have spoken from deep sadness and frustration up to now.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="1 Samuel 1:17">17</a> Eli answered and said, &quot;Go in peace, and may the God of Israel give you what you have asked from Him.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="1 Samuel 1:18">18</a> She said, &quot;I hope I have been good in your eyes.&quot; So the woman left, ate, and was not sad anymore.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="1 Samuel 1:19">19</a> They got up early in the morning, worshiped God, then went back to their home in Ramah. Elkanah was with his wife Hannah, and God remembered her.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="1 Samuel 1:20">20</a> So when the time came after Hannah became pregnant, she gave birth to a son and named him Samuel, because she had asked God for him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="1 Samuel 1:21">21</a> Elkanah and his family went to give God their yearly sacrifice and to fulfill his promise.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="1 Samuel 1:22">22</a> But Hannah didn&#39;t go; she told her husband, &quot;I won&#39;t go until the child is done nursing. Then I&#39;ll take him to appear before God, and he will stay there always.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="1 Samuel 1:23">23</a> Elkanah, her husband, said to her, &quot;Do what you think is best; stay until you have finished breastfeeding him; may God make sure His promise comes true.&quot; So the woman stayed and breastfed her son until she stopped.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="1 Samuel 1:24">24</a> After she stopped breastfeeding him, she brought him along with three young cows, some flour, and a bottle of wine to God&#39;s house in Shiloh; the boy was still little.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="1 Samuel 1:25">25</a> They killed a bull and brought the boy to Eli.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="1 Samuel 1:26">26</a> And she said, &quot;Oh sir, as you are alive, sir, I am the woman who was standing here with you, praying to God.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="1 Samuel 1:27">27</a> I prayed for this child, and God has granted what I asked of Him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="1 Samuel 1:28">28</a> So I have given him to God; for as long as he lives, he will be given to God. And he worshiped God there.</p>
