<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Samuel 24:1">1</a> When Saul came back from chasing the Philistines, he heard, &quot;Look, David is in the Engedi desert.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Samuel 24:2">2</a> Saul took three thousand picked men from all of Israel and went to look for David and his men on the cliffs where the wild goats live.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Samuel 24:3">3</a> He reached the sheep pens on the path, where there was a cave; Saul went inside to rest, and David with his men stayed further inside the cave.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Samuel 24:4">4</a> David&#39;s men said to him, &quot;This is the day God told you about, saying, &#39;I will give your enemy to you, and you can do what you think is right.&#39; &quot; Then David quietly cut a piece from Saul&#39;s robe.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Samuel 24:5">5</a> Later, David felt guilty because he had cut a piece of Saul&#39;s robe.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Samuel 24:6">6</a> He told his men, &quot;God won&#39;t let me do this to my master, God&#39;s chosen one, by harming him because he is chosen by God.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Samuel 24:7">7</a> David stopped his men with his words and did not let them attack Saul. But Saul left the cave and continued his journey.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Samuel 24:8">8</a> David got up later, left the cave, and called out to Saul, &quot;My king.&quot; When Saul turned around, David bowed down with his face to the ground.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Samuel 24:9">9</a> David said to Saul, &quot;Why do you listen to people who say, &#39;Look, David wants to harm you&#39;?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Samuel 24:10">10</a> Look, today your eyes saw that God has given you into my hands in the cave. Some urged me to kill you, but I spared you, saying I won&#39;t harm my master because he is God&#39;s chosen one.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Samuel 24:11">11</a> Also, my father, look, yes, look at the piece of your robe in my hand: because I cut the piece of your robe and did not kill you, understand and see that I have no bad intentions or guilt in my actions, and I have not done wrong against you; but still you chase after me to take my life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Samuel 24:12">12</a> God will judge between you and me, and God will take revenge for me on you, but I will not harm you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 Samuel 24:13">13</a> An old saying goes, evil comes from the evil, but I will not hurt you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="1 Samuel 24:14">14</a> Who is the king of Israel chasing? Who are you after? Just a dead dog, a flea.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="1 Samuel 24:15">15</a> So let God be the judge. Let God decide between you and me. Let God look at this, stand up for me, and save me from your power.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="1 Samuel 24:16">16</a> After David finished speaking to Saul, Saul asked, &quot;Is that your voice, my son David?&quot; Then Saul cried out loud with tears.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="1 Samuel 24:17">17</a> He told David, &quot;You are better than me because you have done good to me, even though I have done bad to you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="1 Samuel 24:18">18</a> Today you showed how you have been good to me; because when God gave me to you, you did not kill me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="1 Samuel 24:19">19</a> If someone finds their enemy, would they let them go safely? So, may God reward you well for what you did for me today.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="1 Samuel 24:20">20</a> And now, I know you will definitely become king, and that you will firmly hold the kingdom of Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="1 Samuel 24:21">21</a> Promise me now, by God, that you will not end my family line after I am gone, and that you will not erase my name from my father&#39;s house.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="1 Samuel 24:22">22</a> And David promised Saul. Then Saul went home while David and his men went to the fortress.</p>
