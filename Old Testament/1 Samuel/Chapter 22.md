<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Samuel 22:1">1</a> David left that place and went to the cave of Adullam. When his brothers and his father&#39;s family heard about it, they went down there to be with him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Samuel 22:2">2</a> Everyone in trouble, in debt, or unhappy gathered around him, and he became their leader. About four hundred men were with him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Samuel 22:3">3</a> David left and went to Mizpeh in Moab. He said to the king of Moab, &quot;Please let my father and mother stay with you until I find out what God will do for me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Samuel 22:4">4</a> He took them to the king of Moab, and they stayed with him as long as David was hiding.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Samuel 22:5">5</a> The prophet Gad told David, &quot;Don&#39;t stay in the stronghold; leave and go to the land of Judah.&quot; So David left and went to the Hareth forest.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Samuel 22:6">6</a> When Saul found out that David and his men had been located (Saul was staying in Gibeah, sitting under a tree in Ramah, holding his spear, with all his servants standing around him),</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Samuel 22:7">7</a> Saul said to his servants standing near him, &quot;Listen, you from Benjamin’s tribe. Will Jesse’s son give each of you fields and vineyards, and make you leaders over thousands and hundreds?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Samuel 22:8">8</a> You all have plotted against me, and no one told me that my son has allied with Jesse&#39;s son, and none of you feel sorry for me or informed me that my son has turned my servant against me, to ambush me as of today?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Samuel 22:9">9</a> Then Doeg the Edomite, who was in charge of Saul&#39;s servants, replied, &quot;I saw Jesse&#39;s son going to Nob, to meet Ahimelech, Ahitub&#39;s son.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Samuel 22:10">10</a> He asked God for help for him, gave him food, and gave him the sword of Goliath the Philistine.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Samuel 22:11">11</a> The king sent for Ahimelech the priest, Ahitub&#39;s son, and all his family who were priests in Nob, and they all came to the king.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Samuel 22:12">12</a> Saul said, &quot;Listen, you son of Ahitub.&quot; And he replied, &quot;I&#39;m here, my lord.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 Samuel 22:13">13</a> Saul asked, &quot;Why did you plot against me with Jesse&#39;s son by giving him bread, a sword, and asking God for help, so he could attack me as he does now?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="1 Samuel 22:14">14</a> Ahimelech replied to the king, &quot;And who is as loyal among all your servants as David, who is the king&#39;s son-in-law, does as you ask, and is respected in your household?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="1 Samuel 22:15">15</a> Did I start to ask God for him? No way: let the king not blame me or my family for anything, because I didn&#39;t know anything about this at all.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="1 Samuel 22:16">16</a> The king said, &quot;You will definitely die, Ahimelech, you and your whole family.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="1 Samuel 22:17">17</a> The king told his guards standing near him, &quot;Turn and kill God&#39;s priests because they support David and knew when he escaped but did not tell me.&quot; But the king&#39;s servants would not raise their hands to attack God&#39;s priests.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="1 Samuel 22:18">18</a> The king told Doeg, &quot;Attack the priests.&quot; So Doeg the Edomite attacked the priests and killed eighty-five men wearing linen clothes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="1 Samuel 22:19">19</a> He attacked Nob, the city of the priests, and killed with his sword all the men, women, children, babies, cows, donkeys, and sheep.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="1 Samuel 22:20">20</a> One of Ahimelech&#39;s sons, named Abiathar, got away and ran to join David.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="1 Samuel 22:21">21</a> Abiathar told David that Saul had killed God&#39;s priests.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="1 Samuel 22:22">22</a> David told Abiathar, &quot;I knew that day, when Doeg the Edomite was there, he would definitely tell Saul. I am responsible for the death of everyone in your father&#39;s family.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="1 Samuel 22:23">23</a> Stay with me, don&#39;t be afraid: whoever wants to hurt me wants to hurt you too, but you&#39;ll be safe with me.</p>
