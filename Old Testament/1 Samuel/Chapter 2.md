<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Samuel 2:1">1</a> Hannah prayed and said, &quot;My heart is happy in God, I am strong in God; my words are bold over my enemies because I am glad for your rescue.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Samuel 2:2">2</a> No one is as holy as God; no one else is like you; no other is as strong as our God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Samuel 2:3">3</a> Stop speaking so proudly and don&#39;t let arrogance come from your mouth; because God knows everything, and he judges what you do.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Samuel 2:4">4</a> The strong warriors&#39; bows are broken, and those who tripped are now strong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Samuel 2:5">5</a> People who had plenty now work for food; those who were hungry are not hungry anymore. So, the woman with no children has seven, and the woman with many children has grown weak.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Samuel 2:6">6</a> God can cause death or give life; He can send people to the grave and raise them up again.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Samuel 2:7">7</a> God makes some poor and others rich; He humbles some and elevates others.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Samuel 2:8">8</a> God raises the poor from the dust and lifts the needy from the trash heap to seat them with nobles and to give them a glorious throne. The earth&#39;s foundations belong to God, and he has placed the world on them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Samuel 2:9">9</a> God will protect his holy people, and the wicked will be quiet in the dark; no one will succeed by their own power.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Samuel 2:10">10</a> God&#39;s enemies will be shattered; He will thunder against them from heaven: God will judge the whole world; He will give power to his king and lift up the strength of his chosen one.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Samuel 2:11">11</a> Elkanah went home to Ramah, and the boy served God in the presence of Eli the priest.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Samuel 2:12">12</a> Eli&#39;s sons were bad men; they did not know God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 Samuel 2:13">13</a> When people offered a sacrifice, the priest&#39;s assistant would come with a three-pronged fork while the meat was boiling.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="1 Samuel 2:14">14</a> He put it into the pan, kettle, cauldron, or pot; whatever the meat hook pulled out, the priest kept for himself. This is what they did in Shiloh to all the Israelites who came there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="1 Samuel 2:15">15</a> Before they burned the fat, the priest&#39;s helper came and told the person offering the sacrifice, &quot;Give meat to cook for the priest; he doesn&#39;t want boiled meat from you, only uncooked.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="1 Samuel 2:16">16</a> If someone told him, &quot;Please burn the fat right away, and then take whatever you want,&quot; he would say, &quot;No, give it to me now; if you don&#39;t, I&#39;ll take it by force.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="1 Samuel 2:17">17</a> The young men&#39;s sin was very bad in God&#39;s sight because people hated God&#39;s offerings.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="1 Samuel 2:18">18</a> Samuel served in front of God as a child, wearing a linen garment.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="1 Samuel 2:19">19</a> His mother made him a small coat and brought it to him every year when she went with her husband to make the annual sacrifice.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="1 Samuel 2:20">20</a> Eli blessed Elkanah and his wife, saying, &quot;May God give you children from this woman to repay what was given to God.&quot; Then they went to their home.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="1 Samuel 2:21">21</a> God blessed Hannah, and she became pregnant, giving birth to three sons and two daughters. The boy Samuel grew up in the presence of God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="1 Samuel 2:22">22</a> Eli was very old and knew what his sons were doing to all of Israel; he knew they slept with the women who gathered at the entrance of the meeting tent.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="1 Samuel 2:23">23</a> He asked them, &quot;Why are you doing these bad things? Everyone is talking about your evil actions.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="1 Samuel 2:24">24</a> No, my sons; it is not a good thing I am told: you make God&#39;s people do wrong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="1 Samuel 2:25">25</a> If someone sins against another person, a judge will decide the case. But if someone sins against God, who will plead for them? Yet they did not listen to their father&#39;s warnings, for God intended to punish them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="1 Samuel 2:26">26</a> The boy Samuel grew up and was liked by both God and people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="1 Samuel 2:27">27</a> A man of God came to Eli and said to him, &quot;This is what God says: Did I clearly show myself to your father&#39;s family when they were in Egypt, in Pharaoh&#39;s palace?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="1 Samuel 2:28">28</a> Did I pick him from all Israel’s tribes to be my priest, to give sacrifices on my altar, to burn incense, and to wear the ephod in my presence? And did I give your father&#39;s family all the Israelites&#39; fire offerings?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="1 Samuel 2:29">29</a> Why do you disrespect my sacrifice and the offerings I ordered in my dwelling, and favor your sons over me, getting rich from the best parts of the offerings of Israel, my people?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="1 Samuel 2:30">30</a> So God, the God of Israel, says, &#39;I thought your family and your ancestors would always serve me, but now God declares, &#39;That will not happen because I will honor those who honor me, and I will not value those who look down on me.&#39;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="1 Samuel 2:31">31</a> Look, the time will come when I will end your family power, and no old man will remain in your family.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="1 Samuel 2:32">32</a> You will see a rival in my home, in all the riches God gives Israel, and you will never have an old man in your family.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="1 Samuel 2:33">33</a> The man from your family who I won&#39;t remove from serving at my altar will cause you sadness and pain, and all of your descendants will die young.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="1 Samuel 2:34">34</a> This will be a sign for you: your two sons, Hophni and Phinehas, will both die on the same day.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="1 Samuel 2:35">35</a> I will choose a loyal priest who will do what I want and think. I will give him a strong family, and he will serve my chosen one forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="1 Samuel 2:36">36</a> Everyone remaining in your family will approach him, bow down for some money and food, and will ask, &quot;Please give me a priest&#39;s job so I can have some bread to eat.&quot;</p>
