<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Samuel 19:1">1</a> Saul told his son Jonathan and all his servants to kill David.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Samuel 19:2">2</a> Jonathan, Saul&#39;s son, really liked David. Jonathan warned David, &quot;Saul, my father, wants to kill you. So please be careful and stay hidden in a secret place until morning.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Samuel 19:3">3</a> I will go out and stand next to my father in the field where you are, and I will talk to my father about you; and what I see, I will tell you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Samuel 19:4">4</a> Jonathan spoke well of David to his father Saul, and said to him, &quot;The king should not do wrong to his servant David; he has not sinned against you, and his actions towards you have been very good.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Samuel 19:5">5</a> He risked his life, killed the Philistine, and God saved all Israel; you saw it and were happy. So why would you sin and kill innocent David for no reason?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Samuel 19:6">6</a> Saul listened to what Jonathan said, and Saul promised, saying, &quot;As God lives, he will not be killed.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Samuel 19:7">7</a> Jonathan called David and showed him everything. Jonathan took David to Saul, and David was with Saul as before.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Samuel 19:8">8</a> War broke out again: David went and fought the Philistines, killed many, and they ran away from him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Samuel 19:9">9</a> An evil spirit from God came upon Saul as he sat in his house with his spear in hand, while David played music.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Samuel 19:10">10</a> Saul tried to hit David with a spear, aiming to pin him to the wall, but David dodged and the spear stuck in the wall. Then David ran away and got away that night.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Samuel 19:11">11</a> Saul sent people to David&#39;s house to watch and kill him in the morning. David&#39;s wife Michal warned him, &quot;If you don&#39;t escape tonight, you will be killed tomorrow.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Samuel 19:12">12</a> Michal helped David escape by lowering him from a window, and he ran away and got away.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 Samuel 19:13">13</a> Michal took an idol, placed it in the bed, put a goat hair pillow under its head, and covered it with a cloth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="1 Samuel 19:14">14</a> When Saul sent people to capture David, she said, &quot;He is sick.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="1 Samuel 19:15">15</a> Saul sent his messengers to check on David again, telling them, &quot;Bring him to me in his bed, so I can kill him.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="1 Samuel 19:16">16</a> When the messengers entered, they found an idol in the bed with a goat hair pillow as its headrest.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="1 Samuel 19:17">17</a> Saul asked Michal, &quot;Why did you trick me and let my enemy go so he could escape?&quot; Michal replied to Saul, &quot;He told me, &#39;Let me go; why should I kill you?&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="1 Samuel 19:18">18</a> David ran away, escaped, and went to Samuel in Ramah to tell him everything Saul had done to him. Then David and Samuel stayed in Naioth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="1 Samuel 19:19">19</a> Someone told Saul, &quot;Look, David is in Naioth at Ramah.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="1 Samuel 19:20">20</a> Saul sent people to capture David, but when they saw a group of prophets speaking God&#39;s messages and Samuel leading them, God&#39;s Spirit came on Saul&#39;s messengers, and they started to speak God&#39;s messages too.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="1 Samuel 19:21">21</a> When Saul heard, he sent more messengers, and they also spoke God&#39;s messages. Saul sent messengers a third time, and they spoke God&#39;s messages as well.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="1 Samuel 19:22">22</a> He also went to Ramah and arrived at a big well in Sechu. He asked, &quot;Where are Samuel and David?&quot; Someone replied, &quot;Look, they&#39;re at Naioth in Ramah.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="1 Samuel 19:23">23</a> He went to Naioth in Ramah, and God&#39;s Spirit was also upon him. He continued to speak God&#39;s messages until he reached Naioth in Ramah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="1 Samuel 19:24">24</a> He also took off his clothes, and before Samuel, he acted like a prophet. He lay there without clothes all day and night. That&#39;s why people ask, &quot;Is Saul also a prophet?&quot;</p>
