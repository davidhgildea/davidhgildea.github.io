<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Samuel 28:1">1</a> During that time, the Philistines prepared their armies for war to battle Israel. Achish told David, be certain that you and your men will join me in the fight.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Samuel 28:2">2</a> David said to Achish, &quot;You will see what I can do.&quot; And Achish said to David, &quot;So I will make you my bodyguard forever.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Samuel 28:3">3</a> Samuel had died, all Israel mourned for him, and they buried him in his hometown of Ramah. Saul had also removed all the mediums and fortune-tellers from the land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Samuel 28:4">4</a> The Philistines came together and camped in Shunem. Saul brought all of Israel together, and they camped in Gilboa.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Samuel 28:5">5</a> When Saul saw the Philistine army, he was scared, and his heart shook a lot.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Samuel 28:6">6</a> When Saul asked God, God did not answer him, not in dreams, not with Urim, and not through prophets.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Samuel 28:7">7</a> Saul said to his servants, &quot;Find me a woman who can speak with spirits, so I can go to her and ask for her help.&quot; His servants told him, &quot;There is a woman who can speak with spirits in Endor.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Samuel 28:8">8</a> Saul changed his clothes to hide who he was, and took two men with him. They went at night to a woman, and Saul asked her, &quot;Please use your spirit to tell me the future, and call up the person I ask for.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Samuel 28:9">9</a> The woman said to him, &quot;Look, you know what Saul did; he got rid of the people who talk to spirits and the wizards from the land. So why are you setting a trap for my life, to make me die?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Samuel 28:10">10</a> Saul promised her by God, saying, &quot;As God lives, you will not be punished for this.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Samuel 28:11">11</a> The woman asked, &quot;Who should I call for you?&quot; He replied, &quot;Call Samuel for me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Samuel 28:12">12</a> When the woman saw Samuel, she shouted loudly and said to Saul, &quot;Why did you trick me? You are Saul.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 Samuel 28:13">13</a> The king told her, &quot;Don&#39;t be scared. What did you see?&quot; The woman said to Saul, &quot;I saw spirits coming up from the ground.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="1 Samuel 28:14">14</a> He asked her, &quot;What does he look like?&quot; She answered, &quot;An old man is coming up, wearing a cloak.&quot; Then Saul knew it was Samuel, and he bowed down with his face to the ground.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="1 Samuel 28:15">15</a> Samuel asked Saul, &quot;Why have you disturbed me by bringing me back?&quot; Saul replied, &quot;I am deeply troubled because the Philistines are fighting me, and God has left me. He doesn&#39;t answer me anymore, not through prophets, nor in dreams. So, I&#39;ve called you to tell me what to do.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="1 Samuel 28:16">16</a> Then Samuel asked, &quot;Why do you ask me when God has left you and become your enemy?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="1 Samuel 28:17">17</a> God did what he said through me: God has taken the kingdom from you and given it to your neighbor, David.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="1 Samuel 28:18">18</a> Because you did not listen to God&#39;s voice or carry out his strong anger against Amalek, God has done this to you today.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="1 Samuel 28:19">19</a> God will also let Israel fall to the Philistines along with you: tomorrow you and your sons will join me: God will also let the army of Israel fall to the Philistines.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="1 Samuel 28:20">20</a> Saul immediately fell to the ground, very afraid because of Samuel&#39;s words. He had no strength because he had not eaten any food all day or night.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="1 Samuel 28:21">21</a> The woman approached Saul, noticed he was very troubled, and told him, &quot;Look, I, your servant, have followed your instructions. I risked my life and listened to what you told me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="1 Samuel 28:22">22</a> So now, please listen to me, your servant, and let me give you some food. Eat it and gain strength for your journey.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="1 Samuel 28:23">23</a> He refused, saying, &quot;I won&#39;t eat.&quot; But his servants and the woman insisted until he listened. Then he got up from the ground and sat on the bed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="1 Samuel 28:24">24</a> The woman had a healthy calf at home; she quickly slaughtered it, took flour, made dough, and baked bread without yeast from it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="1 Samuel 28:25">25</a> She gave it to Saul and his attendants; they ate, then got up and left that night.</p>
