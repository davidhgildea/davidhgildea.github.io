<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Samuel 21:1">1</a> David went to Nob to see Ahimelech the priest. Ahimelech was scared when he saw David and asked, &quot;Why are you alone and no one is with you?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Samuel 21:2">2</a> David told Ahimelech the priest, &quot;The king has given me a task and told me, &#39;Don&#39;t let anyone know about the task I&#39;m sending you on, or what I&#39;ve instructed you to do.&#39; I&#39;ve told my men to meet at a certain place.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Samuel 21:3">3</a> What do you have to give me? Please give me five loaves of bread or whatever you have now.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Samuel 21:4">4</a> The priest replied to David, &quot;I don&#39;t have any regular bread, only holy bread. The young men can have some if they have stayed away from women.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Samuel 21:5">5</a> David replied to the priest, &quot;Honestly, we have not been near women for three days since I left. The young men&#39;s belongings are clean, and even though the bread is ordinary, it is like it was made holy today.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Samuel 21:6">6</a> So the priest gave him sacred bread because only the special bread was there, which had been removed from God&#39;s presence to be replaced with fresh bread on the day it was taken out.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Samuel 21:7">7</a> A man who worked for Saul was there that day, held in God&#39;s presence; his name was Doeg, an Edomite, and he was the top shepherd working for Saul.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Samuel 21:8">8</a> David asked Ahimelech, &quot;Do you have a spear or sword here? I didn&#39;t bring mine because I had to hurry for the king&#39;s task.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Samuel 21:9">9</a> The priest said, &quot;Here is Goliath&#39;s sword wrapped in cloth behind the vest. If you want it, take it, because it&#39;s the only one here.&quot; David replied, &quot;There&#39;s none like it; give it to me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Samuel 21:10">10</a> David got up and ran away that day because he was afraid of Saul, and he went to Achish, the king of Gath.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Samuel 21:11">11</a> Achish&#39;s servants asked him, &quot;Isn&#39;t this David, the king of the country? Didn&#39;t people sing about him in their dances, saying, &#39;Saul has killed his thousands, and David his ten thousands&#39;?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Samuel 21:12">12</a> David remembered these words and was very afraid of Achish, the king of Gath.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 Samuel 21:13">13</a> He acted differently around them, pretended to be crazy, scratched on the gate doors, and let drool run down his beard.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="1 Samuel 21:14">14</a> Then Achish said to his servants, &quot;Look, you see the man is crazy: so why did you bring him to me?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="1 Samuel 21:15">15</a> Do I need crazy people, that you bring this man to act crazy in front of me? Should this man enter my house?</p>
