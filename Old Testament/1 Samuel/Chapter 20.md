<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Samuel 20:1">1</a> David ran away from Naioth in Ramah, went to Jonathan and asked, &quot;What have I done? What is my wrong? Why does your father want to kill me?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Samuel 20:2">2</a> He said to him, &quot;God won&#39;t let it happen; you won&#39;t die. Look, my father won&#39;t do anything, big or small, without telling me. And why would my father keep this from me? It&#39;s not true.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Samuel 20:3">3</a> David promised again and said, &quot;Your father knows well that you like me; and he says, &#39;Don&#39;t tell Jonathan, so he won&#39;t be sad.&#39; But as surely as God lives, and as you are alive, I am only a step away from death.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Samuel 20:4">4</a> Jonathan said to David, &quot;Whatever your heart wants, I will do it for you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Samuel 20:5">5</a> David said to Jonathan, &quot;Look, tomorrow is the new moon, and I&#39;m expected to eat with the king. But let me go and hide in the field until the evening of the third day.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Samuel 20:6">6</a> If your father notices I&#39;m gone, say, &quot;David really needed to go to Bethlehem, his hometown, because there&#39;s an annual family sacrifice there.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Samuel 20:7">7</a> If he says all is well, your servant will be at peace; but if he&#39;s really angry, then know he&#39;s planning something bad.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Samuel 20:8">8</a> So you should treat your servant well, because you have made a promise to God with me. But if I&#39;ve done something wrong, you should punish me yourself; why would you take me to your father?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Samuel 20:9">9</a> Jonathan said, &quot;Don&#39;t think that! If I knew for sure that my father planned to harm you, wouldn&#39;t I tell you?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Samuel 20:10">10</a> Then David asked Jonathan, &quot;Who will let me know? Or what if your father speaks to you harshly?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Samuel 20:11">11</a> Jonathan said to David, &quot;Come on, let&#39;s go to the field.&quot; So both of them went to the field.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Samuel 20:12">12</a> Jonathan said to David, &quot;God of Israel, when I talk to my father tomorrow or the day after, if he feels kindly toward David and I don&#39;t send you a message to let you know, then...&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 Samuel 20:13">13</a> God will deal with Jonathan severely if he doesn&#39;t, but if my father intends to harm you, I will tell you and let you leave safely. May God be with you as He was with my father.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="1 Samuel 20:14">14</a> While I am still alive, you must show me God&#39;s kindness, so I will not die.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="1 Samuel 20:15">15</a> But you must not stop being kind to my family forever, not even when God has removed all David&#39;s enemies from the earth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="1 Samuel 20:16">16</a> Jonathan made a promise with David&#39;s family, saying, &quot;Let God hold David&#39;s enemies responsible.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="1 Samuel 20:17">17</a> Jonathan made David promise again, because he loved him as much as he loved himself.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="1 Samuel 20:18">18</a> Jonathan said to David, &quot;Tomorrow is the new moon, and you will be missed because your seat will be empty.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="1 Samuel 20:19">19</a> After you&#39;ve stayed for three days, go down fast and go to the place where you hid before, and stay near the stone Ezel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="1 Samuel 20:20">20</a> I will shoot three arrows to the side, as if I were aiming at a target.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="1 Samuel 20:21">21</a> Look, I will send a boy, telling him, &#39;Go, look for the arrows.&#39; If I say to the boy, &#39;Look, the arrows are near you, get them,&#39; then you come back; it means you are safe and nothing is wrong; as God lives.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="1 Samuel 20:22">22</a> If I tell the young man, &#39;The arrows are past you,&#39; then leave, because God has sent you away.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="1 Samuel 20:23">23</a> About the issue we talked about, remember, God will be between you and me forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="1 Samuel 20:24">24</a> David hid in the field, and when the new moon arrived, the king sat down to eat.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="1 Samuel 20:25">25</a> The king sat on his throne as usual, next to the wall. Jonathan stood up, and Abner sat next to Saul, but David&#39;s spot was empty.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="1 Samuel 20:26">26</a> Saul said nothing that day because he thought, &quot;Something has happened to him; he&#39;s not pure; surely he&#39;s not pure.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="1 Samuel 20:27">27</a> The next day, the second day of the month, David&#39;s seat was empty. Saul asked his son Jonathan, &quot;Why hasn&#39;t Jesse&#39;s son come to eat, neither yesterday nor today?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="1 Samuel 20:28">28</a> Jonathan replied to Saul, &quot;David really asked for my permission to go to Bethlehem.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="1 Samuel 20:29">29</a> He said, &quot;Please let me go, because our family has a sacrifice in the city; my brother told me to come, and if you&#39;re okay with it, please let me go and see my brothers.&quot; That&#39;s why he didn&#39;t come to the king&#39;s table.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="1 Samuel 20:30">30</a> Saul got angry at Jonathan and said to him, &quot;You son of a stubborn and rebellious woman, don&#39;t I know you&#39;ve picked Jesse&#39;s son to your own shame and to the shame of your mother?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="1 Samuel 20:31">31</a> As long as Jesse&#39;s son lives, you will not secure your position or your kingdom. So send for him now, because he must die.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="1 Samuel 20:32">32</a> Jonathan answered his father Saul, &quot;Why should he be killed? What did he do?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="1 Samuel 20:33">33</a> Saul threw a spear at him to hit him: by this Jonathan knew that his father had decided to kill David.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="1 Samuel 20:34">34</a> Jonathan got up from the table very angry and didn&#39;t eat anything on the second day of the month because he was upset about David, since his father had embarrassed him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="1 Samuel 20:35">35</a> In the morning, Jonathan went to the field at the scheduled time with David and a young boy with him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="1 Samuel 20:36">36</a> He told his boy, &quot;Run and find the arrows I shoot.&quot; While the boy was running, he shot an arrow past him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="1 Samuel 20:37">37</a> When the boy reached the place where Jonathan had shot the arrow, Jonathan called out to him, &quot;Isn&#39;t the arrow farther than where you are?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="1 Samuel 20:38">38</a> Jonathan called out to the boy, &quot;Hurry, run fast, don&#39;t stop.&quot; And Jonathan&#39;s boy picked up the arrows and went back to him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="1 Samuel 20:39">39</a> The boy didn&#39;t know anything; only Jonathan and David knew about it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="1 Samuel 20:40">40</a> Jonathan gave his weapons to his young servant and said to him, &quot;Go, take them to the city.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="1 Samuel 20:41">41</a> As soon as the boy left, David got up from the southern side, bowed down with his face to the ground three times, and they hugged and cried together until David was overwhelmed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="1 Samuel 20:42">42</a> Jonathan told David, &quot;Go in peace, because we both promised in God&#39;s name, saying, &#39;God be between you and me, and between your descendants and my descendants forever.&#39;&quot; Then David left, and Jonathan went back to the city.</p>
