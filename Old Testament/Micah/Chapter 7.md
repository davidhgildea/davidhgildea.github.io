<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Micah 7:1">1</a> I am in trouble! I am like when people have picked the summer fruits, like the scraps left after grapes are picked: there is no bunch to eat; I really wanted the early ripe fruit.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Micah 7:2">2</a> The good person is gone from the world; no one is honest anymore. Everyone is setting traps to hurt others; they are all trying to catch their fellow man like hunting with a net.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Micah 7:3">3</a> They eagerly do bad things with both hands. The leader and the judge both want bribes; the powerful person tells of his evil wish, and so they plan it together.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Micah 7:4">4</a> Even the best person is like a prickly bush; the most honest is as sharp as a thorn fence. The time for your guards and punishment is coming; soon they will be confused.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Micah 7:5">5</a> Do not trust a friend or rely on a leader; be careful what you say around the one close to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Micah 7:6">6</a> The son disrespects his father, the daughter rebels against her mother, the daughter-in-law against her mother-in-law; a man&#39;s enemies are his own family members.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Micah 7:7">7</a> So I will look to God; I will wait for the God who saves me: my God will listen to me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Micah 7:8">8</a> Don&#39;t be happy over me, my enemy. If I fall down, I will get up; if I&#39;m in the dark, God will be my light.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Micah 7:9">9</a> I will accept God&#39;s anger because I sinned against him, until he stands up for me and makes things right for me. He will lead me into the light, and I will see his goodness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Micah 7:10">10</a> Then my enemy will see it, and she will be covered with shame, the one who asked me, &quot;Where is your God?&quot; I will see her then; she will be trampled like mud in the streets.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Micah 7:11">11</a> On the day when your walls are to be rebuilt, on that day the order will be ended.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Micah 7:12">12</a> On that day, he will come to you from Assyria, from strong cities, from the fortress to the river, and from sea to sea, and from mountain to mountain.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Micah 7:13">13</a> Despite this, the land will become empty because of the people living there, due to the results of their actions.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Micah 7:14">14</a> Take care of your people with your guidance, your special flock living alone in the forest, in the heart of Carmel: let them graze in Bashan and Gilead, like in the past.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Micah 7:15">15</a> Based on the number of days since you left Egypt, I will show him wonderful things.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Micah 7:16">16</a> Nations will see and be shocked by their own power; they will cover their mouths and be unable to hear.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Micah 7:17">17</a> They will lick the dust like snakes, they will come out of their holes like earthworms: they will be scared of our God and will be afraid because of you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Micah 7:18">18</a> Who is a God like you, forgiving sin and overlooking the wrongs of those who are left of your people? You do not stay angry forever because you enjoy being merciful.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Micah 7:19">19</a> He will come back, he will show us mercy; he will conquer our wrongs; and you will throw all their sins into the sea&#39;s depths.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Micah 7:20">20</a> You will show your faithfulness to Jacob and love to Abraham, as you promised our ancestors long ago.</p>
