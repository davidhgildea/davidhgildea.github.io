<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Micah 3:1">1</a> And I said, please listen, leaders of Jacob and rulers of Israel; isn&#39;t it your duty to know what is right?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Micah 3:2">2</a> Those who hate good and love evil, who tear off their skin and flesh from their bones.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Micah 3:3">3</a> They also eat my people&#39;s flesh, peel off their skin, break their bones, and cut them up like meat for cooking in a pot or caldron.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Micah 3:4">4</a> Then they will call out to God, but he will not listen to them: he will hide his face from them at that time because they have acted badly in their actions.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Micah 3:5">5</a> God says about the prophets who lead my people astray: they promise peace when they have food to eat, but they declare war against those who don&#39;t feed them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Micah 3:6">6</a> So you will have a night without visions; it will be dark for you, without divination. The sun will set on the prophets, and the day will turn dark for them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Micah 3:7">7</a> Then the seers will be embarrassed, and the diviners will be confused: yes, they will all cover their mouths; because there is no reply from God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Micah 3:8">8</a> I am truly filled with power by God&#39;s spirit, and with judgment and strength, to tell Jacob his wrongdoing, and to Israel his sin.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Micah 3:9">9</a> Please listen, leaders of Jacob&#39;s family and rulers of Israel, who hate fairness and twist what is right.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Micah 3:10">10</a> They construct Zion with violence, and Jerusalem with wrongdoing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Micah 3:11">11</a> Its leaders judge for bribes, its priests teach for a price, and its prophets tell the future for money. Yet they rely on God and say, &quot;Isn&#39;t God with us? No disaster will come upon us.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Micah 3:12">12</a> So, because of you, Zion will be plowed like a field, Jerusalem will turn into piles of rubble, and the temple hill will become like overgrown hilltops.</p>
