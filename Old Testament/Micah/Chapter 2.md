<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Micah 2:1">1</a> Trouble to those who plan bad things and do evil in their beds! When morning comes, they do it because they can.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Micah 2:2">2</a> They desire fields and seize them with force; and homes, and take them: so they crush a person and his family, even a person and his inheritance.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Micah 2:3">3</a> So God says: Look, I am planning a disaster against this family, which you cannot stop; you will not walk proudly, for this time is bad.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Micah 2:4">4</a> On that day someone will tell a story against you, mourn with deep sorrow, and say, &quot;We are completely ruined. He has altered what belonged to our people. How has he taken it away from me? While turning aside, he has split our land.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Micah 2:5">5</a> You will have no one to divide land by casting lots in God&#39;s assembly.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Micah 2:6">6</a> Don&#39;t prophesy, they tell the prophets: they must not prophesy, so they won&#39;t feel ashamed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Micah 2:7">7</a> You who are known as the family of Jacob, is God&#39;s spirit limited? Are these his actions? Don&#39;t my words benefit those who live rightly?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Micah 2:8">8</a> Recently, my people have turned into enemies: you take the coat and clothes from those who walk by peacefully, like people who dislike war.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Micah 2:9">9</a> You have forced the women of my people out of their nice homes, and you have taken my honor away from their children forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Micah 2:10">10</a> Get up and leave; this place is not your rest: it&#39;s unclean and will ruin you with terrible destruction.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Micah 2:11">11</a> If a man walks in the spirit and lies, saying, &quot;I will tell you about wine and strong drink,&quot; he will be considered a prophet by these people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Micah 2:12">12</a> I will definitely bring together all of you, Jacob; I will gather the leftovers of Israel; I will put them as the sheep in Bozrah, as a group in their pen: they will make a loud sound because there are so many people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Micah 2:13">13</a> The leader has gone ahead of them: they have smashed through, walked through the gate, and left by it: their king will lead them, with God at their front.</p>
