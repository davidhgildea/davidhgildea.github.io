<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Micah 6:1">1</a> Listen to what God says; stand up, argue your case before the mountains, and let the hills hear what you say.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Micah 6:2">2</a> Listen, mountains, to God&#39;s argument, and you solid grounds of the earth: for God has a dispute with his people, and he will speak to Israel about it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Micah 6:3">3</a> My people, what have I done to you? How have I tired you? Tell me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Micah 6:4">4</a> I brought you out of Egypt and saved you from slavery; and I sent Moses, Aaron, and Miriam ahead of you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Micah 6:5">5</a> My people, remember what King Balak of Moab planned and what Balaam son of Beor told him from Shittim to Gilgal; so you understand the goodness of God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Micah 6:6">6</a> How should I come before God and bow down to the High God? Should I come to Him with burnt offerings, with year-old calves?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Micah 6:7">7</a> Will God be happy with thousands of sheep, or with ten thousand gallons of oil? Should I offer my first child for my wrongs, the child of my body for the sin of my soul?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Micah 6:8">8</a> God has shown you, person, what is good. What does God ask of you but to act fairly, love kindness, and walk humbly with your God?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Micah 6:9">9</a> God&#39;s voice calls out to the city, and the wise person will recognize your name: listen to the punishment, and the one who has sent it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Micah 6:10">10</a> Are there still stolen riches in the house of the bad person, and the dishonest short measure that is hated?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Micah 6:11">11</a> Can I consider them honest when they use unfair scales and bags of false weights?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Micah 6:12">12</a> The rich people there are very violent, the people living there tell lies, and their speech is full of trickery.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Micah 6:13">13</a> I will make you sick by striking you and make you empty because of your sins.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Micah 6:14">14</a> You will eat but not be full; you will be thrown down within yourself; you will try to save, but you will not succeed; and what you do save, I will let be destroyed by the sword.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Micah 6:15">15</a> You will plant seeds, but you will not harvest crops; you will press olives, but you will not use the oil; you will make sweet wine, but you will not drink it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Micah 6:16">16</a> You follow Omri&#39;s laws and Ahab&#39;s family&#39;s deeds, and take their advice; so I will make you a wasteland, and your people will be mocked; therefore, you will suffer the shame of my people.</p>
