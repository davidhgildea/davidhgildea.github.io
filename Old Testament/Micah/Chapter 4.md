<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Micah 4:1">1</a> In the future, God&#39;s house on the mountain will be the most important, higher than all other hills, and people will come to it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Micah 4:2">2</a> Many nations will come and say, &quot;Let&#39;s go up to God&#39;s mountain, to the house of Jacob&#39;s God; He will teach us His ways, and we will follow His paths. For teachings will come from Zion, and God&#39;s word from Jerusalem.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Micah 4:3">3</a> He will judge among many peoples and correct powerful nations from far away; they will turn their swords into tools for farming, and their spears into tools for cutting branches. No country will fight against another, nor will they learn war anymore.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Micah 4:4">4</a> Everyone will sit under their own vine and fig tree without fear because God has promised it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Micah 4:5">5</a> Everyone will follow their own god, and we will follow our God forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Micah 4:6">6</a> On that day, God says, I will bring together those who limp, and I will collect those who have been scattered, and those whom I have hurt.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Micah 4:7">7</a> I will make those who limp a small surviving group, and those who were sent away a powerful nation: and God will rule over them on Mount Zion from now on, forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Micah 4:8">8</a> And you, tower of the sheep, the daughter of Zion&#39;s stronghold, power will come to you first; the kingdom will come to the daughter of Jerusalem.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Micah 4:9">9</a> Why are you crying out loud? Is there no king within you? Has your advisor died? For pains have gripped you like a woman in labor.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Micah 4:10">10</a> Feel pain and work hard to give birth, daughter of Zion, like a woman in childbirth: for now you will leave the city, you will live in the fields, and you will go all the way to Babylon; there you will be rescued; there God will save you from your enemies.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Micah 4:11">11</a> Now many countries are against you, saying, &quot;Let her become dirty, and let us see Zion.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Micah 4:12">12</a> But they do not know God&#39;s thoughts or understand his plan: for he will gather them like bundles of grain on the threshing floor.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Micah 4:13">13</a> Get up and crush, O daughter of Zion, for I will make your strength iron, and I will make your feet brass, and you will break many people into pieces, and I will dedicate their wealth to God, and their possessions to the God of the whole earth.</p>
