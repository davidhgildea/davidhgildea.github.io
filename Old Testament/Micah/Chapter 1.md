<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Micah 1:1">1</a> God&#39;s message that came to Micah of Morasheth during the time of Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah, the kings of Judah, was about Samaria and Jerusalem.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Micah 1:2">2</a> Listen, everyone; pay attention, O earth and everything in it: and let God be a witness against you, God from his holy place.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Micah 1:3">3</a> Look, God is coming from his place and will step down and walk on the earth&#39;s high places.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Micah 1:4">4</a> The mountains will melt beneath him, and the valleys will split open, like wax melting before a fire and like water rushing down a slope.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Micah 1:5">5</a> All this is because Jacob did wrong, and Israel&#39;s house sinned. What did Jacob do wrong? Wasn&#39;t it Samaria? And Judah&#39;s wrong places, aren&#39;t they Jerusalem?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Micah 1:6">6</a> So, I will turn Samaria into a pile of rubble in a field, like a cleared vineyard, and I will toss its stones into the valley and expose its foundations.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Micah 1:7">7</a> All its carved idols will be smashed, all its earnings will be burned up, and all its idols I will ruin. It collected its wealth by prostitution, and it will be used again for prostitution.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Micah 1:8">8</a> I will cry and yell, I will walk bare and naked: I will cry loudly like the dragons, and grieve like the owls.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Micah 1:9">9</a> Her injury can&#39;t be healed; it has reached Judah and even the entrance of my people in Jerusalem.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Micah 1:10">10</a> Do not announce it in Gath, do not cry at all; in the house of Aphrah cover yourself with dust.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Micah 1:11">11</a> Go away, you who live in Saphir, with your shame exposed. The people of Zaanan did not leave to mourn for Bethezel; they will get no support from you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Micah 1:12">12</a> The person from Maroth really hoped for something good, but bad things came from God to Jerusalem&#39;s gate.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Micah 1:13">13</a> You, who live in Lachish, hitch the chariot to the fast horse: you started the sin for Zion&#39;s people. Israel&#39;s wrongdoings were found in you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Micah 1:14">14</a> So you must give gifts to Moresheth of Gath; the towns of Achzib will deceive the kings of Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Micah 1:15">15</a> I will send an enemy to conquer you, people of Mareshah; the nobles of Israel will hide in the cave at Adullam.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Micah 1:16">16</a> Shave your head and cut off your hair for your precious children; make your bald spot bigger like an eagle, because they have been taken away from you as captives.</p>
