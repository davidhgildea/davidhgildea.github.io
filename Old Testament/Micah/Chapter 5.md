<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Micah 5:1">1</a> Get ready for battle, people of the city; we are under attack. They will strike the leader of Israel on the face with a stick.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Micah 5:2">2</a> Though you, Bethlehem Ephratah, are small among the clans of Judah, from you will come for me a ruler over Israel, whose origins are from long ago, from ancient times.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Micah 5:3">3</a> So he will let them go until the woman in labor gives birth; then, the rest of his brothers will come back to the people of Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Micah 5:4">4</a> He will stand and take care of the sheep with God&#39;s power, in the greatness of God&#39;s name; and they will live safely, because he will be honored everywhere on earth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Micah 5:5">5</a> This man will bring peace when the Assyrian invades our land; and if he marches into our palaces, we will fight against him with seven leaders and eight top officials.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Micah 5:6">6</a> They will destroy Assyria&#39;s land with the sword, and Nimrod&#39;s land at its borders: so he will save us from the Assyrian when he enters our land and walks inside our borders.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Micah 5:7">7</a> Jacob&#39;s remaining people will be among many, like dew from God, like rain on the grass, not depending on anyone or waiting for humans.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Micah 5:8">8</a> The remaining people of Jacob will live among the nations, with many people, like a lion among the forest animals, like a young lion among sheep. He will pass through, trample, and tear apart, and no one can rescue.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Micah 5:9">9</a> Your hand will rise against your enemies, and all your foes will be destroyed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Micah 5:10">10</a> On that day, God says, I will take away your horses and destroy your chariots.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Micah 5:11">11</a> I will destroy the cities in your land and knock down all your fortresses.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Micah 5:12">12</a> I will remove witchcraft from your hands, and you will no longer have fortune-tellers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Micah 5:13">13</a> I will destroy your idols and images from among you; you will no longer worship what your hands have made.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Micah 5:14">14</a> I will pull out your worship places from among you and I will destroy your cities.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Micah 5:15">15</a> I will carry out punishment with angry fury on the nations, the likes of which they have never heard.</p>
