# About the Simplified Bible

The **Simplified Bible** is the whole Bible, all 66 books of the Old and New Testaments, in plain, easy English. It is based on the **King James Version (KJV)** and keeps the KJV's books, chapters and verse numbers. Every chapter has audio narration and an illustration.

## Using the Simplified Bible
{: #quoting }

**Everyone is free to use the Simplified Bible as they wish**, for personal reading, study, teaching, churches, schools and sharing.

**Commercial projects must credit the Simplified Bible.** This includes books, apps, courses, products and any other work that is sold or earns money.

### How to cite

- **Short form:** John 3:16 (Simplified Bible)
- **Full form:** John 3:16, Simplified Bible (simplified.bible), <https://simplified.bible/New%20Testament/John/Chapter%203.html#v16>
- **Credit line:** Scripture quotations are from the Simplified Bible (simplified.bible).

### Linking to a verse

Every chapter has its own page, and every verse has its own link. Add `#v` and the verse number to the chapter's address:

`https://simplified.bible/New%20Testament/John/Chapter%203.html#v16` goes straight to John 3:16.

### Plain text

Each chapter is also available as plain text, one verse per line, for example [/text/john/3.txt](/text/john/3.txt). The complete Bible is available as a single file: [/text/simplified-bible.txt](/text/simplified-bible.txt). A summary for AI tools is at [/llms.txt](/llms.txt).

## The Simplified Bible app

The Simplified Bible is also available as an app for iPhone and Android, with the same text, audio and illustrations, for reading offline.
