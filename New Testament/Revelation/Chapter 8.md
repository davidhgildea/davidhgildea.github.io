<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Revelation 8:1">1</a> When he opened the seventh seal, there was quiet in heaven for about half an hour.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Revelation 8:2">2</a> I saw the seven angels standing before God; they were given seven trumpets.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Revelation 8:3">3</a> Another angel came and stood at the altar with a gold censer; he was given a lot of incense to offer along with all the saints&#39; prayers on the gold altar in front of the throne.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Revelation 8:4">4</a> The smoke from the incense, going up with the saints&#39; prayers, rose before God from the angel&#39;s hand.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Revelation 8:5">5</a> The angel took the incense burner, filled it with fire from the altar, and threw it onto the earth. Then there were voices, thunder, lightning, and an earthquake.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Revelation 8:6">6</a> The seven angels with the seven trumpets got ready to blow them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Revelation 8:7">7</a> The first angel blew his trumpet, and hail and fire mixed with blood fell to the earth. One third of the trees and all the green grass were burned up.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Revelation 8:8">8</a> The second angel blew his trumpet, and it looked like a huge mountain on fire was thrown into the sea, making a third of the sea turn into blood.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Revelation 8:9">9</a> A third of the living sea creatures died, and a third of the ships were destroyed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Revelation 8:10">10</a> The third angel blew his trumpet, and a large star fell from the sky. It was burning like a torch and dropped on a third of the rivers and on the water springs.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Revelation 8:11">11</a> The star is named Wormwood; a third of the waters turned to wormwood, and many people died from the bitter waters.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Revelation 8:12">12</a> The fourth angel blew his trumpet, and one third of the sun, one third of the moon, and one third of the stars were struck, so that a third of them turned dark. A third of the day lost its light, and the night was the same.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Revelation 8:13">13</a> I saw and heard an angel flying across the sky, shouting loudly, &quot;Trouble, trouble, trouble to the people on earth because of the trumpet sounds from the three other angels that are still to come!&quot;</p>
