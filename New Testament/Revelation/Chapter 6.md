<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Revelation 6:1">1</a> I saw the Lamb open a seal, and I heard what sounded like thunder. One of the four creatures said, &quot;Come and see.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Revelation 6:2">2</a> I saw a white horse, and its rider had a bow. He was given a crown and rode out to win many battles and gain victory.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Revelation 6:3">3</a> When he opened the second seal, I heard the second creature say, &quot;Come and see.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Revelation 6:4">4</a> Another red horse came out, and its rider was given the power to remove peace from the earth and make people kill each other. He was also given a large sword.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Revelation 6:5">5</a> When he opened the third seal, I heard the third creature say, &quot;Come and see.&quot; I looked, and there was a black horse, and the rider held weighing scales.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Revelation 6:6">6</a> I heard a voice among the four creatures say, &quot;A measure of wheat for a coin, and three measures of barley for a coin; but do not damage the oil and the wine.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Revelation 6:7">7</a> When he opened the fourth seal, I heard the fourth creature say, &quot;Come and see.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Revelation 6:8">8</a> I looked and saw a pale horse. The rider&#39;s name was Death, and Hell came with him. They were given power over a quarter of the world, to kill with the sword, hunger, death, and wild animals.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Revelation 6:9">9</a> When he opened the fifth seal, I saw under the altar the souls of those who had been killed because of God&#39;s message and their witness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Revelation 6:10">10</a> They shouted loudly, &quot;How long, God, holy and true, until you judge and punish those on the earth for our blood?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Revelation 6:11">11</a> White clothes were given to each of them; they were told to rest for a little while longer until the number of their fellow servants and brothers who were going to be killed like them was complete.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Revelation 6:12">12</a> I saw when he opened the sixth seal, there was a huge earthquake; the sun turned black like rough cloth, and the moon turned red like blood.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Revelation 6:13">13</a> The stars from the sky fell to the earth, like a fig tree drops its early figs when a strong wind shakes it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Revelation 6:14">14</a> The sky split like a rolled-up scroll, and all the mountains and islands shifted from their spots.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Revelation 6:15">15</a> The world&#39;s leaders, important people, wealthy individuals, top officers, powerful individuals, and all slaves and free people hid in caves and among the rocks of the mountains.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Revelation 6:16">16</a> They said to the mountains and rocks, &quot;Fall on us and hide us from the one on the throne and from the Lamb&#39;s anger.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Revelation 6:17">17</a> The great day of God&#39;s anger has come; who can stand against it?</p>
