<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Revelation 4:1">1</a> After this, I saw that a door in heaven was open: and the first sound I heard was like a trumpet speaking to me; it said, &quot;Come up here, and I will show you what must happen after this.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Revelation 4:2">2</a> Right away I was in the spirit, and I saw a throne in heaven with someone sitting on it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Revelation 4:3">3</a> The person sitting there looked like he was made of jasper and carnelian stones, and there was a rainbow encircling the throne, which appeared like an emerald.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Revelation 4:4">4</a> Around the throne were twenty-four chairs, and on the chairs I saw twenty-four elders sitting, dressed in white clothes, with gold crowns on their heads.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Revelation 4:5">5</a> From the throne came flashes of lightning, rumblings, and sounds; and seven flaming lamps were burning before the throne, which are the seven spirits of God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Revelation 4:6">6</a> In front of the throne, there was a clear glass sea like crystal. Around and at the center of the throne, there were four creatures covered with eyes in front and back.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Revelation 4:7">7</a> The first creature looked like a lion, the second one like a young cow, the third had a face like a person, and the fourth was like a flying eagle.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Revelation 4:8">8</a> Each of the four creatures had six wings and was covered with eyes inside. They never stop, day or night, saying, &quot;Holy, holy, holy, God Almighty, who was, and is, and is coming.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Revelation 4:9">9</a> When those creatures give praise, respect, and thanks to the one on the throne, who lives forever and ever,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Revelation 4:10">10</a> The twenty-four elders kneel before the one on the throne and worship the one who lives forever, and they throw their crowns in front of the throne, saying.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Revelation 4:11">11</a> You are worthy, God, to get glory, honor, and power because you created everything, and they were made for your pleasure.</p>
