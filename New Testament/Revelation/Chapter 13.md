<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Revelation 13:1">1</a> I stood on the sea&#39;s shore and saw a beast with seven heads and ten horns come up from the sea. It had ten crowns on its horns and names that insult God on its heads.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Revelation 13:2">2</a> The beast I saw was like a leopard, had feet like a bear, and a mouth like a lion; the dragon gave it his power, throne, and great authority.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Revelation 13:3">3</a> I saw one of the beast&#39;s heads as if it had been fatally wounded, but the deadly wound healed. Everyone in the world was amazed and followed the beast.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Revelation 13:4">4</a> They worshiped the dragon because it gave power to the beast: and they worshiped the beast, asking, &quot;Who is like the beast? Who can fight against him?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Revelation 13:5">5</a> He was given a mouth to speak big and disrespectful words; and he was allowed to use his power for 42 months.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Revelation 13:6">6</a> He spoke against God with insulting words, to disrespect His name, His dwelling, and those who live in heaven.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Revelation 13:7">7</a> He was allowed to fight against God&#39;s holy people and to defeat them. He was also given authority over all families, languages, and nations.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Revelation 13:8">8</a> Everyone living on Earth will worship him if their names are not in the Lamb&#39;s book of life, which was made before the world began.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Revelation 13:9">9</a> If anyone can hear, let them listen.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Revelation 13:10">10</a> Whoever takes someone captive will be taken captive; whoever kills with the sword must be killed with the sword. This calls for patient endurance and faithfulness from God&#39;s people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Revelation 13:11">11</a> I saw another beast rise from the earth; it had two horns like a lamb but spoke like a dragon.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Revelation 13:12">12</a> He uses all the power of the first beast in its presence, and makes the earth and its people worship the first beast, whose fatal wound was healed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Revelation 13:13">13</a> He performs great miracles, making fire come down from the sky to earth in front of people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Revelation 13:14">14</a> He tricks the people on earth with the miracles he could do in front of the beast, telling them to make an image of the beast that was hurt by a sword and yet lived.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Revelation 13:15">15</a> He could make the beast&#39;s image come alive, so it could talk and order the death of anyone not worshiping it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Revelation 13:16">16</a> He makes everyone, whether they are important or not, rich or poor, free or enslaved, have a mark on their right hand or forehead.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Revelation 13:17">17</a> No one could buy or sell unless they had the mark, the beast&#39;s name, or the number representing his name.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Revelation 13:18">18</a> Here is wisdom: Let the person with understanding calculate the beast&#39;s number, for it is a man&#39;s number; and the number is six hundred sixty-six.</p>
