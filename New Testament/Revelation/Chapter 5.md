<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Revelation 5:1">1</a> I saw in the right hand of the one sitting on the throne a book written inside and on the back, sealed with seven seals.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Revelation 5:2">2</a> I saw a powerful angel calling out loudly, &quot;Who is good enough to open the book and break its seals?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Revelation 5:3">3</a> No one in heaven, on earth, or under the earth could open the book or even look inside it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Revelation 5:4">4</a> I cried a lot because no one was worthy to open and read the book, or even to look at it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Revelation 5:5">5</a> One of the elders said to me, &quot;Don&#39;t cry: see, the Lion from the Judah tribe, the Root of David, has succeeded in opening the book and breaking its seven seals.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Revelation 5:6">6</a> I saw that in the center of the throne and the four creatures, and among the elders, there was a Lamb that looked as if it had been killed. It had seven horns and seven eyes, which are the seven spirits of God sent out into the whole world.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Revelation 5:7">7</a> He came and took the scroll from the right hand of the one sitting on the throne.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Revelation 5:8">8</a> When he took the scroll, the four creatures and the twenty-four elders bowed down before the Lamb. Each one had a harp and gold bowls filled with fragrances, which are the prayers of God&#39;s people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Revelation 5:9">9</a> They sang a new song, saying, &quot;You are worthy to take the book, and to open its seals: because you were killed, and have bought us for God with your blood, from every family, language, people, and country.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Revelation 5:10">10</a> You have made us kings and priests to serve our God, and we will rule over the earth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Revelation 5:11">11</a> I saw and heard many angels around the throne, the creatures, and the elders. There were millions and millions of them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Revelation 5:12">12</a> Shouting loudly, the Lamb who was killed is worthy to get power, wealth, wisdom, strength, honor, glory, and praise.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Revelation 5:13">13</a> Every creature in heaven, on earth, under the earth, in the sea, and everything in them, I heard saying, &quot;Blessing, honor, glory, and power be to the one on the throne, and to the Lamb forever and ever.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Revelation 5:14">14</a> The four creatures said, &quot;Amen.&quot; The twenty-four elders fell down and worshiped the one who lives forever and ever.</p>
