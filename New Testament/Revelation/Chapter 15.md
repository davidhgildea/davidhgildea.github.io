<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Revelation 15:1">1</a> I saw another amazing sign in heaven: seven angels with the last seven plagues, because with them God&#39;s anger is complete.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Revelation 15:2">2</a> I saw something like a glass sea mixed with fire, and those who had beaten the beast, its image, its mark, and the number of its name, were standing on the glass sea with God&#39;s harps.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Revelation 15:3">3</a> They sing Moses&#39; song, the servant of God, and the Lamb&#39;s song, saying, &quot;Your works are great and wonderful, God Almighty; your paths are right and true, you King of the holy.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Revelation 15:4">4</a> Who wouldn&#39;t fear you, God, and honor your name? You alone are holy. All countries will come to worship you because your fair decisions are clear to all.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Revelation 15:5">5</a> After that I saw, and look, the temple of the tent of the witness in heaven was opened.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Revelation 15:6">6</a> The seven angels left the temple with the seven plagues, dressed in clean white cloth, and wearing gold belts around their chests.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Revelation 15:7">7</a> One of the four creatures gave the seven angels seven gold bowls filled with God&#39;s anger, who lives forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Revelation 15:8">8</a> The temple was full of smoke because of God&#39;s glory and power, and no one could go into the temple until the seven angels&#39; seven plagues were finished.</p>
