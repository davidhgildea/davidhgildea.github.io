<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Revelation 1:1">1</a> This is the unveiling of Jesus Christ, which God gave to him to show his servants what must soon happen; and he made it known through his angel to his servant John.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Revelation 1:2">2</a> He testified about God&#39;s word, Jesus Christ&#39;s message, and everything he saw.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Revelation 1:3">3</a> Happy is the one who reads, and those who listen to the words of this prophecy, and follow what is written in it, because the time is near.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Revelation 1:4">4</a> John to the seven churches in Asia: May you have grace and peace from God, who is, who was, and who will come; and from the seven Spirits in front of his throne.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Revelation 1:5">5</a> From Jesus Christ, the trustworthy witness, the first to rise from the dead, and the ruler of earth&#39;s kings. He loved us and cleaned our sins with his blood.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Revelation 1:6">6</a> God has made us rulers and priests to serve God and his Father; may glory and power be his forever and ever. Amen.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Revelation 1:7">7</a> Look, he is coming with the clouds; everyone will see him, even those who hurt him; and all the people of the world will cry because of him. Yes, Amen.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Revelation 1:8">8</a> I am the First and the Last, the start and the end, says God, who is, was, and will come, the All-Powerful.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Revelation 1:9">9</a> I, John, who am also your brother, and share in your suffering, in God&#39;s kingdom and in the patience Jesus Christ taught, was on the island called Patmos because of God&#39;s message and to tell about Jesus Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Revelation 1:10">10</a> I was filled with the Spirit on God’s day and heard a loud voice behind me, like a trumpet.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Revelation 1:11">11</a> Saying, &quot;I am the Beginning and the End, the first and the last: and, what you see, write in a book, and send it to the seven churches in Asia; to Ephesus, Smyrna, Pergamum, Thyatira, Sardis, Philadelphia, and Laodicea.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Revelation 1:12">12</a> I turned to see who was speaking to me. When I turned, I saw seven gold lampstands.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Revelation 1:13">13</a> In the middle of the seven lampstands was someone like the Son of Man, dressed in a long robe, with a golden belt around his chest.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Revelation 1:14">14</a> His head and hair were white like wool, white as snow; and his eyes were like flames of fire.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Revelation 1:15">15</a> His feet were like polished bronze glowing as if in a fire, and his voice was like the noise of many waters.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Revelation 1:16">16</a> In his right hand he held seven stars, and a sharp double-edged sword came from his mouth. His face shone like the bright sun.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Revelation 1:17">17</a> When I saw him, I fell at his feet like I was dead. But he put his right hand on me and said, &quot;Don&#39;t be afraid; I am the first and the last.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Revelation 1:18">18</a> I am the one who lives, and was dead; look, I live forever, Amen; and I hold the keys to hell and death.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Revelation 1:19">19</a> Write what you have seen, what is now, and what will happen later.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Revelation 1:20">20</a> The secret of the seven stars you saw in my right hand and the seven gold lampstands is this: the seven stars are the messengers of the seven churches, and the seven lampstands you saw are the seven churches.</p>
