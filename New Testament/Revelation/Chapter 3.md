<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Revelation 3:1">1</a> Write to the angel of the church in Sardis: He who has the seven spirits of God and the seven stars says, I know your actions; you have a reputation for being alive, but you are dead.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Revelation 3:2">2</a> Stay alert and make stronger what is left, even though it is close to death, because your deeds are not yet complete in God&#39;s eyes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Revelation 3:3">3</a> Remember how you have received and heard; keep it firmly and turn away from your wrongs. If you do not stay alert, I will come like a thief, and you will not know when I will come to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Revelation 3:4">4</a> You have a few people in Sardis who have not dirtied their clothes; they will walk with me dressed in white because they are worthy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Revelation 3:5">5</a> Whoever overcomes will be dressed in white clothes; I will not erase their name from the book of life, but I will acknowledge their name before God and his angels.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Revelation 3:6">6</a> If you have an ear, listen to what the Spirit says to the churches.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Revelation 3:7">7</a> Write to the angel of the church in Philadelphia: The one who is holy and true, who holds David&#39;s key, who opens doors no one can close, and closes doors no one can open, says these things.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Revelation 3:8">8</a> I know what you do: see, I have placed an open door before you that no one can close. You have a bit of strength, have obeyed my message, and have not rejected my name.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Revelation 3:9">9</a> Look, I will make those from Satan&#39;s group, who claim to be Jews but are not and are lying, come and bow down before you and understand that I love you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Revelation 3:10">10</a> Because you have followed my teaching about endurance, I will also protect you from the time of testing that is going to happen to everyone on earth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Revelation 3:11">11</a> Look, I am coming soon. Keep what you have, so that no one will take your crown.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Revelation 3:12">12</a> Whoever wins, I will make them a strong supporter in the house of my God. They will never leave it. I will write on them the name of my God, the name of my God&#39;s city, the new Jerusalem that comes down from heaven from my God, and I will also write on them my new name.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Revelation 3:13">13</a> Anyone who has ears should listen to what the Spirit tells the churches.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Revelation 3:14">14</a> Write to the angel of the Laodicean church: The Amen, the reliable and truthful witness, the first of God&#39;s creation, says these things.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Revelation 3:15">15</a> I know what you do; you are not cold or hot. I wish you were either cold or hot.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Revelation 3:16">16</a> Because you are neither hot nor cold, but lukewarm, I will spit you out of my mouth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Revelation 3:17">17</a> Because you say, &quot;I am rich, have lots of things, and need nothing,&quot; you do not know that you are truly sad, pitiful, poor, blind, and naked.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Revelation 3:18">18</a> I advise you to buy from me gold refined by fire so you can be wealthy; and white clothes to wear, so your nakedness won&#39;t be seen; and put salve on your eyes, so you can see.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Revelation 3:19">19</a> I correct and discipline everyone I love. So be eager, and change your ways.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Revelation 3:20">20</a> Look, I am waiting at the door and knocking. If anyone hears my voice and opens the door, I will come in and have dinner with him, and he with me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Revelation 3:21">21</a> I will let those who win sit with me on my throne, just as I won and sat down with God on his throne.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Revelation 3:22">22</a> If you have an ear, listen to what the Spirit tells the churches.</p>
