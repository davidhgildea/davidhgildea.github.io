<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Luke 16:1">1</a> He also told his disciples, &quot;There was a rich man who had a manager accused of wasting his property.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Luke 16:2">2</a> He called him and asked, &quot;Why am I hearing these things about you? Explain your management because you might not be the manager anymore.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Luke 16:3">3</a> The manager thought to himself, &quot;What should I do? My master is taking away my job. I can&#39;t dig, and I&#39;m too ashamed to beg.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Luke 16:4">4</a> I have decided what to do so that when I lose my job, people will welcome me into their homes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Luke 16:5">5</a> He called each of his boss&#39;s debtors and asked the first one, &quot;How much do you owe my boss?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Luke 16:6">6</a> He said, &quot;A hundred barrels of oil.&quot; Then he told him, &quot;Take your bill, sit down fast, and write fifty.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Luke 16:7">7</a> Then he asked another, &quot;How much do you owe?&quot; And he replied, &quot;A hundred measures of wheat.&quot; So he told him, &quot;Take your bill and write eighty.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Luke 16:8">8</a> God praised the unfair manager, because he acted smartly: for the people of this world are smarter in their own time than the people of light.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Luke 16:9">9</a> I tell you, use your wealth to make friends for yourselves, so that when it&#39;s gone, they will welcome you into eternal homes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Luke 16:10">10</a> Someone faithful in small things is also faithful in big things; and someone unfair in small things is also unfair in big things.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Luke 16:11">11</a> So if you have not been reliable with dishonest wealth, who will trust you with true riches?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Luke 16:12">12</a> If you have not been trustworthy with someone else&#39;s property, who will give you property of your own?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Luke 16:13">13</a> A worker can&#39;t work for two bosses: he will hate one and like the other, or be loyal to one and not care for the other. You can&#39;t work for God and money at the same time.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Luke 16:14">14</a> The Pharisees, who loved money, heard all he said and made fun of him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Luke 16:15">15</a> He told them, &quot;You try to show people you are right, but God knows your hearts. What people value highly is disgusting to God.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Luke 16:16">16</a> Before John, there were the law and the prophets; now the kingdom of God is taught, and everyone is trying to enter it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Luke 16:17">17</a> It is simpler for heaven and earth to disappear than for the smallest part of God&#39;s law to be wrong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Luke 16:18">18</a> Anyone who divorces his wife and marries someone else is unfaithful, and anyone who marries a woman divorced from her husband is unfaithful.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Luke 16:19">19</a> There was a rich man who wore purple and fine linen and lived in luxury every day.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Luke 16:20">20</a> A poor man named Lazarus, who was covered in sores, was placed at his gate.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Luke 16:21">21</a> He wanted to eat the crumbs that fell from the rich man&#39;s table. Even dogs came and licked his wounds.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Luke 16:22">22</a> The poor man died and the angels took him to be with Abraham. The rich man also died and was buried.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Luke 16:23">23</a> In hell, he opened his eyes, suffering in pain, and saw Abraham far away with Lazarus close to him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Luke 16:24">24</a> He called out, &quot;Father Abraham, please have pity on me. Send Lazarus to dip his finger in water and cool my tongue, because I am suffering in this fire.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Luke 16:25">25</a> Abraham said, &quot;Son, remember that you had good things in your life, and Lazarus had bad things. But now he is comforted, and you are suffering.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Luke 16:26">26</a> Moreover, there is a big gap between us and you: so those who want to go from here to you can&#39;t, nor can anyone cross over to us from there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Luke 16:27">27</a> He then said, &quot;Please, father, send him to my father&#39;s house.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Luke 16:28">28</a> I have five brothers; he should warn them, so they won&#39;t come to this place of suffering.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Luke 16:29">29</a> Abraham says to him, &quot;They have Moses and the prophets; they should listen to them.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Luke 16:30">30</a> And he said, &quot;No, Father Abraham. But if someone from the dead goes to them, they will change their ways.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Luke 16:31">31</a> He told him, &quot;If they don&#39;t listen to Moses and the prophets, they won&#39;t be convinced even if someone rises from the dead.&quot;</p>
