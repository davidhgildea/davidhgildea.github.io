<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Luke 22:1">1</a> The time for the Feast of Unleavened Bread, also known as Passover, was near.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Luke 22:2">2</a> The high priests and teachers of the law wanted to find a way to kill him; they were afraid of the people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Luke 22:3">3</a> Satan entered into Judas, known as Iscariot, one of the twelve disciples.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Luke 22:4">4</a> He went and talked with the chief priests and officers about how he could hand him over to them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Luke 22:5">5</a> They were happy and agreed to pay him money.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Luke 22:6">6</a> He promised and looked for a chance to hand him over to them when the crowd was not around.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Luke 22:7">7</a> The day came for the bread without yeast when it was time for the Passover lamb to be sacrificed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Luke 22:8">8</a> He sent Peter and John, telling them, &quot;Go and get the Passover ready for us to eat.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Luke 22:9">9</a> They asked him, &quot;Where do you want us to prepare?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Luke 22:10">10</a> He told them, &quot;Look, when you go into the city, a man carrying a water jar will meet you; follow him into the house he enters.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Luke 22:11">11</a> You will tell the owner of the house, &#39;The Teacher asks you, where is the guest room where I can eat the Passover meal with my followers?&#39;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Luke 22:12">12</a> He will show you a big room upstairs that&#39;s ready: prepare there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Luke 22:13">13</a> They went and found everything just as he had told them, and they prepared the Passover meal.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Luke 22:14">14</a> When the time came, he sat down with the twelve apostles.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Luke 22:15">15</a> He said to them, &quot;I have really wanted to eat this Passover meal with you before I go through hard times.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Luke 22:16">16</a> I tell you, I won&#39;t eat it again until it comes true in God&#39;s kingdom.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Luke 22:17">17</a> He took the cup, thanked God, and said, &quot;Take this and share it among you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Luke 22:18">18</a> I tell you, I won&#39;t drink from the grapevine until God&#39;s kingdom comes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Luke 22:19">19</a> He took bread, thanked God, broke it, and gave it to them, saying, &quot;This is my body given for you; do this to remember me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Luke 22:20">20</a> After supper, Jesus took the cup and said, &quot;This cup represents the new agreement in my blood, poured out for you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Luke 22:21">21</a> Look, the hand of the one who will betray me is with mine on the table.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Luke 22:22">22</a> Truly, the Son of Man will go as planned, but how terrible it will be for the man who betrays him!</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Luke 22:23">23</a> They started to ask each other which one of them would do this.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Luke 22:24">24</a> There was also an argument among them about who should be considered the greatest.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Luke 22:25">25</a> He told them, &quot;The rulers of the nations rule over them, and those with power over the people are called good leaders.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Luke 22:26">26</a> But you must not be like that: instead, whoever is the greatest among you should be like the youngest, and whoever leads should be like someone who serves.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Luke 22:27">27</a> Who is more important, the one who sits and eats, or the one who serves? Isn&#39;t it the one who sits and eats? But I am with you as one who serves.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Luke 22:28">28</a> You are those who have stayed with me through my trials.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Luke 22:29">29</a> I give you a kingdom, just as my Father has given one to me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Luke 22:30">30</a> You may eat and drink at my table in my kingdom, and sit on thrones to judge the twelve tribes of Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Luke 22:31">31</a> God said, &quot;Simon, Simon, listen, Satan wants to take you, to shake you like wheat.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Luke 22:32">32</a> I have prayed for you that your faith will not fail; and when you turn back, help your brothers be strong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Luke 22:33">33</a> He said to him, &quot;Master, I am ready to follow you, even to jail and to death.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Luke 22:34">34</a> He said, &quot;I tell you, Peter, before the rooster crows today, you will deny three times that you know me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Luke 22:35">35</a> He asked them, &quot;When I sent you without money, bag, or sandals, did you need anything?&quot; And they answered, &quot;No.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Luke 22:36">36</a> He then told them, &quot;But now, if you have a purse, take it, and your bag too; if you don&#39;t have a sword, sell your clothes to buy one.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Luke 22:37">37</a> I tell you that what is written must happen to me. I must be counted among the wrongdoers, for what is said about me is coming to an end.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Luke 22:38">38</a> And they said, &quot;Master, look, here are two swords.&quot; And he replied, &quot;That&#39;s enough.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="Luke 22:39">39</a> He came out and went to the Mount of Olives, as he usually did, and his disciples followed him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="Luke 22:40">40</a> When he was there, he told them, &quot;Pray so you won&#39;t give in to temptation.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="Luke 22:41">41</a> He moved away from them about as far as you can throw a stone, kneeled down, and prayed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="Luke 22:42">42</a> Father, if you are willing, take this cup away from me: but let your will, not mine, be done.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v43"><a class="verse-num" href="#v43" aria-label="Luke 22:43">43</a> An angel from heaven appeared to him, giving him strength.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v44"><a class="verse-num" href="#v44" aria-label="Luke 22:44">44</a> Feeling very troubled, he prayed even harder, and his sweat was like big drops of blood falling to the ground.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v45"><a class="verse-num" href="#v45" aria-label="Luke 22:45">45</a> After praying, he went to his disciples and saw they were asleep because they were sad.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v46"><a class="verse-num" href="#v46" aria-label="Luke 22:46">46</a> He asked them, &quot;Why are you sleeping? Get up and pray so you don&#39;t give in to temptation.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v47"><a class="verse-num" href="#v47" aria-label="Luke 22:47">47</a> While he was still talking, a crowd appeared, and Judas, one of the twelve, led them to Jesus to kiss him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v48"><a class="verse-num" href="#v48" aria-label="Luke 22:48">48</a> But Jesus said to him, &quot;Judas, are you betraying the Son of Man with a kiss?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v49"><a class="verse-num" href="#v49" aria-label="Luke 22:49">49</a> When the people around him saw what was going to happen, they asked, &quot;Master, should we attack with our swords?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v50"><a class="verse-num" href="#v50" aria-label="Luke 22:50">50</a> One of them struck the high priest&#39;s servant and cut off his right ear.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v51"><a class="verse-num" href="#v51" aria-label="Luke 22:51">51</a> Jesus responded, &quot;Allow this for now.&quot; He then touched the man&#39;s ear and healed him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v52"><a class="verse-num" href="#v52" aria-label="Luke 22:52">52</a> Then Jesus said to the chief priests, the temple guards, and the elders who came to him, &quot;Did you come out with swords and clubs as if I were a thief?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v53"><a class="verse-num" href="#v53" aria-label="Luke 22:53">53</a> When I was with you every day in the temple, you did not try to hurt me. But now is your time, and darkness has power.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v54"><a class="verse-num" href="#v54" aria-label="Luke 22:54">54</a> They took him, led him, and brought him to the high priest&#39;s house. Peter followed from a distance.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v55"><a class="verse-num" href="#v55" aria-label="Luke 22:55">55</a> When they made a fire in the middle of the room and sat together, Peter sat with them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v56"><a class="verse-num" href="#v56" aria-label="Luke 22:56">56</a> A servant girl saw him sitting by the fire, stared at him, and said, &quot;This man was with him too.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v57"><a class="verse-num" href="#v57" aria-label="Luke 22:57">57</a> He denied knowing him, saying, &quot;Woman, I don&#39;t know him.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v58"><a class="verse-num" href="#v58" aria-label="Luke 22:58">58</a> After a short time, someone else saw Peter and said, &quot;You are one of them too.&quot; But Peter replied, &quot;No, I am not.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v59"><a class="verse-num" href="#v59" aria-label="Luke 22:59">59</a> About an hour later, another person strongly said, &quot;For sure, this man was with him too: because he is from Galilee.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v60"><a class="verse-num" href="#v60" aria-label="Luke 22:60">60</a> Peter said, &quot;I don&#39;t know what you&#39;re talking about.&quot; And right away, as he was still speaking, a rooster crowed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v61"><a class="verse-num" href="#v61" aria-label="Luke 22:61">61</a> God turned and looked at Peter. Then Peter remembered what God had told him: &quot;Before the rooster crows, you will deny me three times.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v62"><a class="verse-num" href="#v62" aria-label="Luke 22:62">62</a> Peter went out and cried a lot.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v63"><a class="verse-num" href="#v63" aria-label="Luke 22:63">63</a> The men who were holding Jesus made fun of him and hit him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v64"><a class="verse-num" href="#v64" aria-label="Luke 22:64">64</a> After they covered his eyes, they hit him in the face and asked, &quot;Guess who hit you?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v65"><a class="verse-num" href="#v65" aria-label="Luke 22:65">65</a> They said many other disrespectful things against him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v66"><a class="verse-num" href="#v66" aria-label="Luke 22:66">66</a> As soon as it was morning, the leaders and the head priests and the teachers of the law met together and took him into their meeting, asking,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v67"><a class="verse-num" href="#v67" aria-label="Luke 22:67">67</a> Are you the Messiah? Tell us. And he said to them, If I tell you, you will not believe.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v68"><a class="verse-num" href="#v68" aria-label="Luke 22:68">68</a> If I ask you too, you won&#39;t answer or let me go.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v69"><a class="verse-num" href="#v69" aria-label="Luke 22:69">69</a> From now on, the Son of Man will sit at the right side of God&#39;s power.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v70"><a class="verse-num" href="#v70" aria-label="Luke 22:70">70</a> They all asked, &quot;Are you the Son of God?&quot; He answered, &quot;You say that I am.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v71"><a class="verse-num" href="#v71" aria-label="Luke 22:71">71</a> They asked, &quot;Do we need more evidence? We&#39;ve heard it from his own mouth.&quot;</p>
