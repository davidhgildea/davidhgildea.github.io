<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Luke 6:1">1</a> On the second Saturday following the first, Jesus walked through the grain fields. His followers picked some heads of grain, rubbed them in their hands, and ate them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Luke 6:2">2</a> Some Pharisees asked them, &quot;Why are you doing what&#39;s not allowed on the Sabbath?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Luke 6:3">3</a> Jesus replied to them, &quot;Haven&#39;t you read what David did when he and his followers were hungry?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Luke 6:4">4</a> He entered God&#39;s house, took and ate the bread on display, and also gave it to his companions, though only priests are allowed to eat it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Luke 6:5">5</a> He told them that the Son of Man is also Lord of the Sabbath.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Luke 6:6">6</a> On another Sabbath, he went into the synagogue and taught. A man was there with a crippled right hand.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Luke 6:7">7</a> The teachers of the law and the Pharisees watched Jesus to see if he would heal on the Sabbath, so they could accuse him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Luke 6:8">8</a> But he knew what they were thinking and said to the man with the weak hand, &quot;Get up and stand here.&quot; And he got up and stood there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Luke 6:9">9</a> Jesus said to them, I have one question for you: Is it right to do good or bad on the Sabbath? To save a life or to destroy it?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Luke 6:10">10</a> He looked at them all and said to the man, &quot;Stretch out your hand.&quot; He did, and his hand was healed, just like the other one.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Luke 6:11">11</a> They were overcome with anger and discussed with each other what they should do to Jesus.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Luke 6:12">12</a> At that time, he went to a mountain to pray, and spent the whole night talking to God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Luke 6:13">13</a> When it became daytime, he called his disciples to him and out of them, he picked twelve, whom he also called apostles.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Luke 6:14">14</a> Simon, who was also called Peter, and his brother Andrew, James and John, Philip and Bartholomew,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Luke 6:15">15</a> Matthew and Thomas, James the son of Alphaeus, and Simon called the Zealot,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Luke 6:16">16</a> Judas, James&#39;s brother, and Judas Iscariot, who was the traitor.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Luke 6:17">17</a> He came down with them and stood on the flat land with his followers and a large crowd from all over Judea and Jerusalem, and from the coast of Tyre and Sidon. They came to listen to him and to be cured of their sicknesses.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Luke 6:18">18</a> People troubled by evil spirits were healed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Luke 6:19">19</a> The whole crowd wanted to touch him because healing power came from him and healed everyone.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Luke 6:20">20</a> He looked at his followers and said, &quot;Happy are you who are poor: for the kingdom of God belongs to you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Luke 6:21">21</a> Happy are you who are hungry now, because you will be satisfied. Happy are you who are crying now, because you will laugh.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Luke 6:22">22</a> You are blessed when people hate you, avoid you, insult you, and say you are evil because of the Son of man.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Luke 6:23">23</a> Be happy on that day and jump for joy, because your reward in heaven is big; their ancestors treated the prophets the same way.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Luke 6:24">24</a> But bad luck to you who are rich, because you have already gotten your comfort.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Luke 6:25">25</a> Trouble to you who are full, because you will be hungry. Trouble to you who laugh now, because you will be sad and cry.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Luke 6:26">26</a> Trouble comes to you when everyone says good things about you, because that&#39;s what they did for the fake prophets.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Luke 6:27">27</a> I tell you who are listening: Love your enemies, do good to those who hate you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Luke 6:28">28</a> Be kind to those who curse you, and pray for those who are mean to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Luke 6:29">29</a> If someone hits you on one cheek, let them hit the other one too; and if someone takes your jacket, don&#39;t stop them from taking your shirt as well.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Luke 6:30">30</a> Give to everyone who asks you, and if someone takes your belongings, don&#39;t ask for them back.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Luke 6:31">31</a> Do to others what you would want them to do to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Luke 6:32">32</a> If you love only those who love you, what reward do you get? Even sinners love those who love them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Luke 6:33">33</a> If you are good only to those who are good to you, what credit is that to you? Even sinners do that.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Luke 6:34">34</a> If you lend money to those you expect to pay you back, what credit is that to you? Even sinners lend to sinners, hoping to get back the same amount.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Luke 6:35">35</a> Love your enemies, do good, and lend without expecting anything back; your reward will be large, and you will be children of the Most High because God is kind to the ungrateful and wicked.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Luke 6:36">36</a> Be kind, just as your Father is kind.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Luke 6:37">37</a> Do not judge, and you won&#39;t be judged. Do not condemn, and you won&#39;t be condemned. Forgive, and you will be forgiven.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Luke 6:38">38</a> Give, and you will receive; you will be given a lot, packed tight and overflowing, into your arms. Because the same amount you give will be given back to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="Luke 6:39">39</a> He told them a story: If a blind person guides another blind person, won&#39;t they both fall into a hole?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="Luke 6:40">40</a> A student is not greater than their teacher, but anyone who is fully trained will be like their teacher.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="Luke 6:41">41</a> Why do you see the speck that is in your brother&#39;s eye, but do not notice the log that is in your own eye?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="Luke 6:42">42</a> How can you tell your brother, &#39;Brother, let me take out the speck in your eye,&#39; when you don&#39;t see the log in your own eye? You are acting fake. First, remove the log from your eye. Then you will see clearly to remove the speck from your brother&#39;s eye.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v43"><a class="verse-num" href="#v43" aria-label="Luke 6:43">43</a> A good tree does not produce bad fruit, and a bad tree does not produce good fruit.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v44"><a class="verse-num" href="#v44" aria-label="Luke 6:44">44</a> Every tree is recognized by its fruit. People don&#39;t pick figs from thorns, or grapes from bramble bushes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v45"><a class="verse-num" href="#v45" aria-label="Luke 6:45">45</a> A good person brings good things from the good in their heart, and a bad person brings bad things from the bad in their heart. What they have plenty of in their heart, their mouth talks about.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v46"><a class="verse-num" href="#v46" aria-label="Luke 6:46">46</a> Why do you call me &#39;Lord, Lord,&#39; and not do what I say?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v47"><a class="verse-num" href="#v47" aria-label="Luke 6:47">47</a> Anyone who comes to me, listens to what I say, and follows it, I will show you what they are like:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v48"><a class="verse-num" href="#v48" aria-label="Luke 6:48">48</a> He is like a person who built a house, dug deep, and set its base on rock: when the flood came, the water hit that house hard, but could not move it because it was built on rock.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v49"><a class="verse-num" href="#v49" aria-label="Luke 6:49">49</a> But the person who listens and doesn&#39;t act is like someone who built a house on the ground without a foundation. When the river hit it hard, the house quickly fell apart, and was completely destroyed.</p>
