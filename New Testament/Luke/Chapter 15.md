<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Luke 15:1">1</a> All the tax collectors and sinners came close to listen to him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Luke 15:2">2</a> The Pharisees and teachers complained, &quot;This man welcomes sinners and eats with them.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Luke 15:3">3</a> He told them this story:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Luke 15:4">4</a> Which one of you, if you have a hundred sheep and one gets lost, wouldn&#39;t leave the ninety-nine in the open country and go search for the lost one until you find it?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Luke 15:5">5</a> When he finds it, he puts it on his shoulders, happy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Luke 15:6">6</a> When he gets home, he invites his friends and neighbors over and says to them, &quot;Be happy with me because I found my lost sheep.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Luke 15:7">7</a> I tell you, there will be more joy in heaven for one sinner who turns from their wrong ways than for ninety-nine good people who don&#39;t need to change.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Luke 15:8">8</a> If a woman has ten silver coins and loses one, doesn&#39;t she light a lamp, clean the house, and look carefully until she finds it?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Luke 15:9">9</a> When she finds it, she calls her friends and neighbors and says, &quot;Be happy with me; I&#39;ve found the coin I lost.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Luke 15:10">10</a> I tell you, there is happiness among God&#39;s angels when one sinner decides to change their ways.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Luke 15:11">11</a> He said, &quot;A man had two sons.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Luke 15:12">12</a> The younger son said to his father, &quot;Father, give me my share of the property.&quot; So the father divided his wealth between them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Luke 15:13">13</a> Soon after, the younger son collected all he had, traveled to a distant land, and there squandered his wealth with wild living.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Luke 15:14">14</a> After he spent everything, a great famine hit that country, and he started to need things.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Luke 15:15">15</a> He went and worked for a local person, and that person sent him to his fields to take care of pigs.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Luke 15:16">16</a> He really wanted to fill his stomach with the pods that the pigs were eating, but no one gave him any.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Luke 15:17">17</a> When he realized his situation, he said, &quot;My father&#39;s workers have more than enough bread, while I am starving!&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Luke 15:18">18</a> I will get up and go to my father, and will say to him, Father, I&#39;ve done wrong against heaven and in your sight.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Luke 15:19">19</a> I am not worthy to be called your son; treat me like one of your paid workers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Luke 15:20">20</a> He got up and went to his father. When he was still far away, his father saw him, felt sorry for him, ran to him, hugged him, and kissed him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Luke 15:21">21</a> The son said to him, &quot;Father, I have done wrong against heaven and before you, and I am no longer good enough to be called your son.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Luke 15:22">22</a> But the father told his servants, Get the best coat and put it on him; and put a ring on his finger, and shoes on his feet.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Luke 15:23">23</a> Bring the fat calf here, kill it, let&#39;s eat, and be happy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Luke 15:24">24</a> My son was gone, and now he&#39;s back to life; he was lost, and now he&#39;s found. And they started to celebrate.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Luke 15:25">25</a> His older son was out in the fields. As he approached the house, he heard music and dancing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Luke 15:26">26</a> He asked one of the workers what was happening.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Luke 15:27">27</a> He told him, &quot;Your brother has come back, and your father has slaughtered the fattened calf because he got him back healthy and well.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Luke 15:28">28</a> He was angry and refused to go in, so his father came out and pleaded with him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Luke 15:29">29</a> He replied to his father, &quot;Look, I have served you for many years and never disobeyed your orders, yet you never gave me a goat to enjoy with my friends.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Luke 15:30">30</a> But when this son of yours came back, who wasted your money with prostitutes, you prepared a feast for him with the best calf.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Luke 15:31">31</a> He said to him, &quot;Son, you are always with me, and everything I have is yours.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Luke 15:32">32</a> It was right for us to celebrate and be happy: because your brother was dead, and now he is alive; he was lost, and now he is found.</p>
