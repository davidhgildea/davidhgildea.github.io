<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Luke 17:1">1</a> He told his followers, &quot;It&#39;s impossible to avoid problems, but bad things will happen to the person who causes them!&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Luke 17:2">2</a> It would be better for him to have a heavy stone tied around his neck and to be thrown into the sea than to hurt one of these little ones.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Luke 17:3">3</a> Be careful with yourselves: If your brother does wrong to you, correct him; and if he is sorry, forgive him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Luke 17:4">4</a> If he wrongs you seven times in a day, and seven times he comes back to you and says, &#39;I am sorry,&#39; you must forgive him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Luke 17:5">5</a> The apostles said to God, &quot;Make our faith grow.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Luke 17:6">6</a> And God said, &quot;If you have faith as small as a mustard seed, you could tell this mulberry tree, &#39;Be pulled out by the roots and planted in the sea,&#39; and it would listen to you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Luke 17:7">7</a> Which of you, if you have a servant plowing or taking care of the animals, will tell him as soon as he comes in from the field, &#39;Go and sit down to eat&#39;?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Luke 17:8">8</a> Won&#39;t you instead tell him, &#39;Prepare my dinner, put on your apron, and serve me while I eat and drink; after that, you can eat and drink&#39;?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Luke 17:9">9</a> Does he thank the servant for doing what was commanded? I don&#39;t think so.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Luke 17:10">10</a> In the same way, when you have done everything you were told to do, say, &#39;We are servants who have not earned any favor; we have only done our duty.&#39;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Luke 17:11">11</a> As he was going to Jerusalem, he went through the middle of Samaria and Galilee.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Luke 17:12">12</a> As he went into a village, ten men with leprosy met him and stood at a distance.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Luke 17:13">13</a> They raised their voices and said, &quot;Jesus, Teacher, please help us.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Luke 17:14">14</a> When he saw them, he told them, &quot;Go show yourselves to the priests.&quot; As they went, they were healed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Luke 17:15">15</a> When one of them saw he was healed, he came back praising God loudly.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Luke 17:16">16</a> He fell with his face to the ground at Jesus&#39; feet, thanking him, and he was a Samaritan.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Luke 17:17">17</a> Jesus replied, &quot;Weren&#39;t ten people cleansed? But where are the other nine?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Luke 17:18">18</a> No one else came back to thank God, only this foreigner.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Luke 17:19">19</a> He said to him, &quot;Get up, go on your way; your faith has healed you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Luke 17:20">20</a> When the Pharisees asked him when God&#39;s kingdom would come, he replied, &quot;God&#39;s kingdom doesn&#39;t come in a way you can see it.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Luke 17:21">21</a> No one will say, &#39;Look here!&#39; or &#39;Look there!&#39; because God&#39;s kingdom is inside you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Luke 17:22">22</a> He told the disciples, &quot;A time will come when you&#39;ll want to see just one of the days of the Son of man, but you won&#39;t be able to.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Luke 17:23">23</a> People might tell you, &#39;Look here!&#39; or &#39;Look there!&#39; Don&#39;t go or follow them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Luke 17:24">24</a> Just like lightning that flashes from one part of the sky and shines to the other part, so will the Son of Man be on his day.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Luke 17:25">25</a> First, he must go through a lot and be rejected by this generation.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Luke 17:26">26</a> Just like in Noah&#39;s time, it will also be in the days of the Son of Man.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Luke 17:27">27</a> They ate, they drank, they got married, they were given for marriage, until the day Noah went into the ark, and the flood came and wiped them all out.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Luke 17:28">28</a> Just like in Lot&#39;s time, people ate, drank, bought, sold, farmed, and built.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Luke 17:29">29</a> On the day Lot left Sodom, fire and sulfur rained from the sky and destroyed everyone.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Luke 17:30">30</a> It will be the same on the day when the Son of man is shown.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Luke 17:31">31</a> On that day, if someone is on the rooftop and their belongings are inside, they should not go down to get them. And if someone is in the field, they should not go back either.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Luke 17:32">32</a> Remember Lot&#39;s wife.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Luke 17:33">33</a> Anyone trying to save their life will lose it, and anyone who loses their life will keep it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Luke 17:34">34</a> I tell you, that night two men will be in one bed; one will be taken, and the other will be left.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Luke 17:35">35</a> Two women will be grinding grain together; one will be taken, and the other left behind.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Luke 17:36">36</a> Two people will be in the field; one will be taken, and the other will be left behind.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Luke 17:37">37</a> They replied, &quot;Where, Lord?&quot; He said to them, &quot;Wherever there is a dead body, that&#39;s where the eagles will gather.&quot;</p>
