<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Luke 13:1">1</a> At that time, some people told Jesus about the Galileans whose blood Pilate had mixed with their sacrifices.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Luke 13:2">2</a> Jesus answered them, &quot;Do you think that these Galileans were worse sinners than all the other Galileans because they suffered this way?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Luke 13:3">3</a> I tell you, no: but unless you change your ways, you will all die just as well.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Luke 13:4">4</a> Do you think those eighteen people the tower in Siloam fell on and killed were worse sinners than all the other people living in Jerusalem?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Luke 13:5">5</a> I tell you, no: but if you don&#39;t change your ways, you will all die just as they did.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Luke 13:6">6</a> He also told this story: A man had a fig tree in his vineyard; he looked for fruit on it but found none.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Luke 13:7">7</a> He said to the vineyard worker, &quot;Look, for three years I&#39;ve been looking for fruit on this fig tree and haven&#39;t found any. Cut it down; why should it waste the soil?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Luke 13:8">8</a> He replied, &quot;Master, leave it alone for one more year. I will dig around it and put fertilizer on it.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Luke 13:9">9</a> If it produces fruit, good; if not, then you should cut it down.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Luke 13:10">10</a> He was teaching in a synagogue on the Sabbath.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Luke 13:11">11</a> Look, there was a woman who had been sick for eighteen years, bent over and unable to straighten up at all.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Luke 13:12">12</a> When Jesus saw her, he called her over and said, &quot;Woman, you are freed from your sickness.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Luke 13:13">13</a> He touched her, and right away she could stand up straight and she praised God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Luke 13:14">14</a> The leader of the synagogue was upset because Jesus healed on the Sabbath. He told the people, &quot;There are six days to work, so come to be healed on those days, not on the Sabbath.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Luke 13:15">15</a> God answered him, &quot;You hypocrite! Doesn&#39;t each of you untie his ox or donkey from the stall on the Sabbath and lead it to drink water?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Luke 13:16">16</a> Shouldn&#39;t this woman, a daughter of Abraham, who has been tied by Satan for eighteen years, be set free from this bond on the Sabbath day?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Luke 13:17">17</a> After he said this, all his enemies felt ashamed, and everyone was happy because of the wonderful things he did.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Luke 13:18">18</a> He asked, &quot;What is God&#39;s kingdom like? What can I compare it to?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Luke 13:19">19</a> It is like a mustard seed that a man took and planted in his garden. It grew and became a big tree, and the birds made nests in its branches.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Luke 13:20">20</a> He said again, &quot;What can I compare God&#39;s kingdom to?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Luke 13:21">21</a> It is like yeast that a woman mixed into a large amount of flour until it spread through all of it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Luke 13:22">22</a> He traveled through towns and villages, teaching, and headed towards Jerusalem.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Luke 13:23">23</a> Someone asked him, &quot;Sir, are only a few people going to be saved?&quot; And he replied to them,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Luke 13:24">24</a> Try hard to go through the narrow door, because I tell you, many will try to enter and will not be able to.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Luke 13:25">25</a> When the owner of the house gets up and closes the door, and you start standing outside and knocking on the door, saying, &quot;Sir, Sir, let us in,&quot; he will answer, &quot;I don&#39;t know where you come from.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Luke 13:26">26</a> Then you will start to say, &quot;We ate and drank with you, and you taught in our streets.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Luke 13:27">27</a> But he will say, I tell you, I do not know you or where you come from; go away from me, all you who do wrong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Luke 13:28">28</a> You will cry and grind your teeth when you see Abraham, Isaac, Jacob, and all the prophets in God&#39;s kingdom, but you are thrown out.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Luke 13:29">29</a> People will come from the east, west, north, and south, and will sit in God&#39;s kingdom.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Luke 13:30">30</a> Look, some who are last will be first, and some who are first will be last.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Luke 13:31">31</a> That day some Pharisees came and said to him, &quot;Leave this place and go away, because Herod wants to kill you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Luke 13:32">32</a> He said to them, &quot;Go, tell that fox, &#39;Look, I drive out demons and heal people today and tomorrow, and on the third day I will complete my work.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Luke 13:33">33</a> I must go on my way today, tomorrow, and the next day, because it&#39;s not possible for a prophet to die outside of Jerusalem.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Luke 13:34">34</a> Jerusalem, Jerusalem, you who kill the prophets and stone those sent to you; how often I wanted to bring your children together, as a hen gathers her chicks under her wings, but you didn&#39;t want that!</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Luke 13:35">35</a> Look, your house is now empty: and I tell you, you won&#39;t see me until you say, &#39;Welcome is the one who comes in God&#39;s name.&#39;</p>
