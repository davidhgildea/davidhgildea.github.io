<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Luke 4:1">1</a> Jesus, filled with the Holy Spirit, came back from the Jordan River and was guided by the Spirit into the desert.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Luke 4:2">2</a> He was tempted by the devil for forty days. During that time, he ate nothing. After it ended, he felt hungry.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Luke 4:3">3</a> The devil said to him, &quot;If you are the Son of God, tell this stone to become bread.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Luke 4:4">4</a> Jesus replied, &quot;It is said, people do not live only by eating bread, but by every word from God.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Luke 4:5">5</a> The devil took him to a high mountain and showed him all the world&#39;s kingdoms in an instant.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Luke 4:6">6</a> The devil told him, &quot;I will give you all this power and its glory because it has been given to me, and I can give it to anyone I choose.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Luke 4:7">7</a> If you worship me, everything will be yours.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Luke 4:8">8</a> Jesus replied, &quot;Go away from me, Satan, because it is said, &#39;You must worship only God and serve him alone.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Luke 4:9">9</a> He took him to Jerusalem, put him on the highest point of the temple, and said, &quot;If you are the Son of God, throw yourself down from here.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Luke 4:10">10</a> It is written, God will command his angels to protect you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Luke 4:11">11</a> They will carry you in their hands so that you won&#39;t hit your foot on a stone.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Luke 4:12">12</a> Jesus replied, &quot;It is written, &#39;Do not test God.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Luke 4:13">13</a> After the devil finished all the tempting, he left him for a while.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Luke 4:14">14</a> Jesus came back to Galilee with the Spirit&#39;s power, and news about him spread through the whole area.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Luke 4:15">15</a> He taught in their gathering places, and everyone praised him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Luke 4:16">16</a> He went to Nazareth, where he grew up, and on the Sabbath, as he usually did, he entered the synagogue and stood up to read.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Luke 4:17">17</a> He received the book of the prophet Isaiah. When he opened the book, he found the section that said,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Luke 4:18">18</a> God&#39;s Spirit is on me, because he has chosen me to tell good news to the poor; he has sent me to fix broken hearts, to say to the captives &#39;You are free,&#39; and to help blind people see, to free those who have been hurt.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Luke 4:19">19</a> To announce God&#39;s year of favor.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Luke 4:20">20</a> He closed the book, gave it back to the assistant, and sat down. Everyone in the synagogue stared at him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Luke 4:21">21</a> He started to tell them, &quot;Today, this scripture has come true as you heard it.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Luke 4:22">22</a> Everyone listened to him and was amazed at the kind words he spoke. They asked, &quot;Isn&#39;t this Joseph&#39;s son?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Luke 4:23">23</a> He told them, &quot;You will definitely tell me this saying, &#39;Doctor, cure yourself:&#39; do here in your hometown what we heard you did in Capernaum.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Luke 4:24">24</a> He said, &quot;Truly I tell you, no prophet is welcomed in his hometown.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Luke 4:25">25</a> But I tell you truly, there were many widows in Israel during Elijah&#39;s time, when the sky was closed for three and a half years and there was a severe famine everywhere in the country.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Luke 4:26">26</a> Elijah was not sent to any of them but to a widow in the city of Sarepta in Sidon.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Luke 4:27">27</a> Many people with leprosy lived in Israel when Elisha was a prophet; but only Naaman the Syrian was healed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Luke 4:28">28</a> Everyone in the synagogue became very angry when they heard this.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Luke 4:29">29</a> They got up, pushed him out of the city, took him to the edge of the hill where their city was built, to throw him down.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Luke 4:30">30</a> He walked right through the crowd and went on his way.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Luke 4:31">31</a> He went down to Capernaum, a city in Galilee, and taught them on the Sabbath days.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Luke 4:32">32</a> They were amazed at his teaching, because his words had authority.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Luke 4:33">33</a> In the meeting place, there was a man with an evil spirit, and he shouted loudly.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Luke 4:34">34</a> Leave us alone; what do you want with us, Jesus of Nazareth? Have you come to destroy us? I know who you are; the Holy One of God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Luke 4:35">35</a> Jesus scolded the demon, saying, &quot;Be quiet and leave him.&quot; And after the demon threw the man down in front of everyone, it came out without injuring him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Luke 4:36">36</a> Everyone was amazed and said to each other, &quot;What kind of teaching is this? He gives orders to evil spirits with authority and power, and they leave.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Luke 4:37">37</a> News about him spread all over the surrounding region.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Luke 4:38">38</a> He got up from the synagogue and went into Simon’s house. Simon’s mother-in-law had a high fever, and they asked him to help her.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="Luke 4:39">39</a> He stood by her, told the fever to go away, and it did. Right away she got up and took care of them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="Luke 4:40">40</a> When the sun was going down, everyone with sick friends brought them to Jesus; he touched each one and healed them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="Luke 4:41">41</a> Evil spirits came out of many people, shouting, &quot;You are the Christ, the Son of God.&quot; But he scolded them and did not allow them to speak because they knew he was the Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="Luke 4:42">42</a> When day came, he left and went to a deserted place. The people looked for him, found him, and begged him not to leave them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v43"><a class="verse-num" href="#v43" aria-label="Luke 4:43">43</a> He told them, &quot;I must tell other cities about God&#39;s kingdom, too: that&#39;s why I was sent.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v44"><a class="verse-num" href="#v44" aria-label="Luke 4:44">44</a> He taught in the meeting places of Galilee.</p>
