<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Luke 14:1">1</a> As he entered a leading Pharisee&#39;s home to share a meal on the Sabbath, they observed him closely.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Luke 14:2">2</a> Look, there was a man in front of him with swelling in his body.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Luke 14:3">3</a> Jesus replied to the lawyers and Pharisees, asking, &quot;Is it allowed to heal on the Sabbath?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Luke 14:4">4</a> They stayed quiet. He took the man, healed him, and released him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Luke 14:5">5</a> He replied, &quot;If one of you has a donkey or an ox that falls into a hole, wouldn&#39;t you immediately help it out on the Sabbath day?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Luke 14:6">6</a> They could not reply to him after this.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Luke 14:7">7</a> He told a story to the invited guests when he noticed how they picked the best seats, and said to them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Luke 14:8">8</a> When you are invited by someone to a wedding, do not take the best seat; someone more important than you might have been invited by them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Luke 14:9">9</a> The person who invited you and the other man might come and say to you, &quot;Give your seat to this man,&quot; and then you will feel embarrassed to move to the least important place.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Luke 14:10">10</a> When you are invited, go and sit in the least important place so that the host might come to you and say, “Friend, move to a better seat.” Then you will be honored in front of the other guests.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Luke 14:11">11</a> Whoever makes himself important will be made humble; and the one who makes himself humble will be made important.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Luke 14:12">12</a> He also told the person who invited him, &quot;When you host a dinner or a meal, don&#39;t just invite your friends, family, or rich neighbors; otherwise, they might invite you back and repay you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Luke 14:13">13</a> When you have a feast, invite the poor, the hurt, the crippled, and the blind.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Luke 14:14">14</a> You will be blessed because they can&#39;t repay you; you will be repaid when good people are raised from death.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Luke 14:15">15</a> When someone eating with him heard this, he said to him, &quot;Happy is the one who will eat bread in God&#39;s kingdom.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Luke 14:16">16</a> He told him, &quot;A man prepared a big dinner and invited many people.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Luke 14:17">17</a> He sent his servant at dinner time to tell the invited guests, &#39;Come, everything is ready now.&#39;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Luke 14:18">18</a> They all unanimously started to give excuses. The first one told him, &quot;I have bought some land, and I really need to go see it; please excuse me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Luke 14:19">19</a> Another person said, &quot;I have bought five pairs of oxen and I&#39;m going to try them out. Please excuse me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Luke 14:20">20</a> Someone else said, &quot;I just got married, so I can&#39;t come.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Luke 14:21">21</a> The servant went and told his master what happened. The master, angry, said to his servant, &quot;Go fast into the city streets and paths, and bring here the poor, the injured, the lame, and the blind.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Luke 14:22">22</a> The servant said, &quot;Master, I did what you told me, and there&#39;s still space left.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Luke 14:23">23</a> God told the servant, &quot;Go out to the roads and fences, and make them come in, so my house will be full.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Luke 14:24">24</a> I tell you, none of those men invited will enjoy my dinner.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Luke 14:25">25</a> Lots of people followed him: then he turned around and spoke to them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Luke 14:26">26</a> If anyone comes to me and does not put aside love for their father, mother, spouse, children, siblings, and even their own life, they cannot be my student.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Luke 14:27">27</a> Whoever does not carry their own cross and follow me cannot be my disciple.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Luke 14:28">28</a> Which one of you, planning to build a tower, doesn&#39;t first sit down and figure out the cost to see if you have enough money to complete it?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Luke 14:29">29</a> So that if he lays the foundation and can&#39;t finish the building, everyone who sees it will make fun of him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Luke 14:30">30</a> He said, &quot;This person started to build but couldn&#39;t finish it.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Luke 14:31">31</a> What king, about to fight another king, doesn&#39;t sit first and think if he can face an army of twenty thousand with his ten thousand?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Luke 14:32">32</a> Otherwise, while the other is still far away, he sends messengers and asks for terms of peace.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Luke 14:33">33</a> Just as well, anyone of you who does not give up everything they have cannot be my follower.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Luke 14:34">34</a> Salt is good, but if salt loses its taste, how can it be made salty again?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Luke 14:35">35</a> It&#39;s not good for the soil or the manure pile; it&#39;s just thrown away. If someone can listen, they should listen.</p>
