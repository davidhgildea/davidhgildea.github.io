<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Luke 5:1">1</a> As the crowd gathered to listen to God&#39;s message, he stood by the Lake of Gennesaret.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Luke 5:2">2</a> He saw two boats by the lake, but the fishermen had left them and were cleaning their nets.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Luke 5:3">3</a> He went onto Simon&#39;s boat and asked Simon to push off a little from shore. Then he sat down and taught the crowd from the boat.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Luke 5:4">4</a> After he finished talking, he told Simon, &quot;Go out into the deep water and let down your nets to catch some fish.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Luke 5:5">5</a> Simon replied, &quot;Teacher, we worked hard all night and caught nothing, but because you say so, I will let down the net.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Luke 5:6">6</a> After they did this, they caught a large number of fish; the net tore.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Luke 5:7">7</a> They signaled to their friends in the other boat to come and help them. They came and both boats were so full that they started to sink.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Luke 5:8">8</a> When Simon Peter saw this, he fell at Jesus&#39; knees and said, &quot;Go away from me, because I am a sinful man, God.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Luke 5:9">9</a> He was amazed, and so was everyone with him, at the large number of fish they caught.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Luke 5:10">10</a> James and John, Zebedee&#39;s sons, who worked with Simon, were there too. Jesus told Simon, &quot;Don&#39;t be afraid; from now on you will catch people.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Luke 5:11">11</a> When they brought their boats to shore, they left everything and followed him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Luke 5:12">12</a> When Jesus was in a city, a man with leprosy saw him, fell on his face, and begged, &quot;Lord, if you are willing, you can make me clean.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Luke 5:13">13</a> He reached out his hand, touched him, and said, &quot;I&#39;m willing; be clean.&quot; Right away, the leprosy left him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Luke 5:14">14</a> He told him not to tell anyone, but to go show himself to the priest, and offer a cleansing sacrifice as Moses directed, as proof to them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Luke 5:15">15</a> But his fame spread even more, and large crowds gathered to listen and to be healed by him of their sicknesses.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Luke 5:16">16</a> He went to a quiet place and prayed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Luke 5:17">17</a> One day, as he was teaching, there were Pharisees and teachers of the law sitting nearby, from every town of Galilee, Judea, and Jerusalem. And God&#39;s power was there to heal them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Luke 5:18">18</a> Look, some people carried a man on a bed who was paralyzed. They tried to bring him in and place him in front of Jesus.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Luke 5:19">19</a> When they couldn&#39;t find a way to bring him in because of the crowd, they went up on the roof and lowered him on his mat through the tiles right in front of Jesus.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Luke 5:20">20</a> When he saw how much they believed, he said to him, &quot;Man, your sins are forgiven.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Luke 5:21">21</a> The teachers of the law and the Pharisees started to think, saying, &quot;Who is this that says bad things against God? Who can forgive sins except God alone?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Luke 5:22">22</a> When Jesus knew what they were thinking, he said to them, &quot;Why are you thinking these things in your hearts?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Luke 5:23">23</a> Which is easier to say, &#39;Your sins are forgiven,&#39; or to say, &#39;Get up and walk&#39;?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Luke 5:24">24</a> So you can know the Son of man has power on earth to forgive sins, he told the man with palsy, &quot;I tell you, get up, take your bed, and go home.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Luke 5:25">25</a> Right away, he stood up in front of everyone, picked up the mat he was lying on, and went home, praising God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Luke 5:26">26</a> Everyone was amazed, they praised God, and were very afraid, saying, &quot;We have seen amazing things today.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Luke 5:27">27</a> After this, he went out, saw a tax collector named Levi sitting at the tax office, and said to him, &quot;Follow me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Luke 5:28">28</a> He left everything, got up, and followed him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Luke 5:29">29</a> Levi held a big dinner at his home; many tax collectors and others ate with them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Luke 5:30">30</a> But their teachers and religious leaders complained about his followers, asking, &quot;Why do you eat and drink with tax collectors and bad people?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Luke 5:31">31</a> Jesus replied, &quot;Healthy people don&#39;t need a doctor; only the sick do.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Luke 5:32">32</a> I did not come to call good people, but sinners to turn away from their sins.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Luke 5:33">33</a> They asked him, &quot;Why do John&#39;s followers and the Pharisees&#39; disciples fast and pray often, but your followers eat and drink?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Luke 5:34">34</a> He asked them, &quot;Can you expect the wedding guests to fast while the groom is with them?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Luke 5:35">35</a> But a time will come when the groom will be taken from them, and then they will fast on those days.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Luke 5:36">36</a> He also told them a story: No one sews a patch from a new piece of clothing on an old one. If they do, the new piece will tear, and it will not match the old clothing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Luke 5:37">37</a> No one puts new wine into old bottles; otherwise, the new wine will break the bottles, spill out, and the bottles will be ruined.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Luke 5:38">38</a> New wine must go into new wineskins; then both stay good.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="Luke 5:39">39</a> Someone who has drunk old wine doesn&#39;t immediately want new wine. They think the old one is better.</p>
