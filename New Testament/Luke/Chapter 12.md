<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Luke 12:1">1</a> While a huge crowd was gathering and people were stepping on each other, he started to tell his disciples, &quot;Be careful of the Pharisees&#39; yeast, which is their fake behavior.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Luke 12:2">2</a> Everything hidden will be uncovered, and every secret will be known.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Luke 12:3">3</a> Whatever you have said in secret will be heard in the daylight, and what you have whispered in private rooms will be shouted from the rooftops.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Luke 12:4">4</a> I tell you, my friends, don&#39;t be scared of those who can kill the body but can&#39;t do anything more after that.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Luke 12:5">5</a> But I will tell you who to be afraid of: Be afraid of the one who, after killing, has the power to throw you into hell; yes, I am telling you, be afraid of him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Luke 12:6">6</a> Aren&#39;t five sparrows sold for two small coins, and God doesn&#39;t forget even one of them?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Luke 12:7">7</a> God knows the number of hairs on your head. So don&#39;t be afraid; you are worth more than many sparrows.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Luke 12:8">8</a> I tell you, if anyone acknowledges me before others, the Son of Man will also acknowledge them before God&#39;s angels.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Luke 12:9">9</a> Whoever denies me before others will be denied before God&#39;s angels.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Luke 12:10">10</a> Anyone who speaks against the Son of Man can be forgiven, but anyone who speaks against the Holy Spirit will not be forgiven.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Luke 12:11">11</a> When they take you to the synagogues, to leaders, and authorities, don&#39;t worry about how you will defend yourselves or what to say.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Luke 12:12">12</a> The Holy Spirit will teach you at that time what you should say.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Luke 12:13">13</a> Someone in the crowd said to him, &quot;Teacher, tell my brother to share the inheritance with me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Luke 12:14">14</a> He said to him, &quot;Person, who appointed me to be a judge or to split property between you?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Luke 12:15">15</a> He told them, &quot;Be careful and avoid greed: a person&#39;s life is not about having a lot of possessions.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Luke 12:16">16</a> He told them a story: A rich man&#39;s land produced a lot of crops.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Luke 12:17">17</a> He thought to himself, &quot;What should I do? I don&#39;t have enough space to store my crops.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Luke 12:18">18</a> He said, &quot;I will do this: I will tear down my barns and build bigger ones, and there I will store all my crops and possessions.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Luke 12:19">19</a> I will tell myself, &quot;You have plenty of things stored up for many years; relax, eat, drink, and have fun.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Luke 12:20">20</a> God said to him, &quot;You fool, tonight your life will be taken from you. Then who will get what you have prepared?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Luke 12:21">21</a> This is like someone who saves wealth for themselves but isn&#39;t generous towards God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Luke 12:22">22</a> He told his disciples, &quot;That&#39;s why I&#39;m telling you not to worry about your life, what you will eat; or about your body, what you will wear.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Luke 12:23">23</a> Life is more important than food, and the body is more important than clothes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Luke 12:24">24</a> Look at the ravens: they don&#39;t plant or harvest; they have no storage or barn; yet God feeds them. How much more valuable are you than birds?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Luke 12:25">25</a> Who among you can grow taller by worrying about it, even by a little bit?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Luke 12:26">26</a> If you can&#39;t do even the smallest task, why worry about the rest?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Luke 12:27">27</a> Look at the flowers and how they grow: they don&#39;t work hard or make cloth; but I tell you, not even Solomon in all his splendor was dressed as beautifully as one of these.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Luke 12:28">28</a> If God dresses the grass in the field today, which is thrown into the fire tomorrow, won&#39;t he give you clothes, you who have little faith?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Luke 12:29">29</a> Don&#39;t worry about what you will eat, drink, or be uncertain about.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Luke 12:30">30</a> All the countries in the world go after these things, and God knows you need them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Luke 12:31">31</a> Instead, look for God&#39;s kingdom, and all these things will be given to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Luke 12:32">32</a> Don&#39;t be afraid, small group; because God is happy to give you the kingdom.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Luke 12:33">33</a> Sell what you own and give to the poor; make for yourselves money bags that do not wear out, a treasure in heaven that never fails, where no thief can come near and no moth destroys.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Luke 12:34">34</a> Where you keep what you value most, that&#39;s where your heart will be too.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Luke 12:35">35</a> Be ready and keep your lamps lit.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Luke 12:36">36</a> Be like people waiting for their master to come back from a wedding, so when he arrives and knocks, they can open the door for him right away.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Luke 12:37">37</a> Happy are the servants who are found alert by God when he arrives; truly, I tell you, God will prepare himself, have them sit for a meal, and come out to serve them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Luke 12:38">38</a> If he comes during the second or third watch and finds them ready, those servants are blessed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="Luke 12:39">39</a> Know this: if the homeowner knew when the thief would come, he would have stayed awake and not let his house be broken into.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="Luke 12:40">40</a> So be ready too, because the Son of Man will come at a time you do not expect.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="Luke 12:41">41</a> Then Peter asked him, &quot;God, are you telling this story to us or to everyone?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="Luke 12:42">42</a> God asked, &quot;Who is the trustworthy and sensible manager that his master will put in charge of his household to give them their food at the right time?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v43"><a class="verse-num" href="#v43" aria-label="Luke 12:43">43</a> Happy is the servant whom his master finds working when he returns.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v44"><a class="verse-num" href="#v44" aria-label="Luke 12:44">44</a> I tell you the truth, he will put him in charge of everything he owns.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v45"><a class="verse-num" href="#v45" aria-label="Luke 12:45">45</a> But if that servant thinks, &#39;My master is taking a long time to come back,&#39; and starts to hit the other servants, and eats and drinks too much and gets drunk;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v46"><a class="verse-num" href="#v46" aria-label="Luke 12:46">46</a> The master of that servant will come on a day when he&#39;s not expecting him, at a time he doesn&#39;t know, and will punish him severely, assigning him a place with the disbelievers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v47"><a class="verse-num" href="#v47" aria-label="Luke 12:47">47</a> The servant who knew what his master wanted but didn&#39;t get ready or do what his master wanted will be punished severely.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v48"><a class="verse-num" href="#v48" aria-label="Luke 12:48">48</a> The person who did not know and did wrong will get a light punishment. When someone has been given a lot, a lot will be expected from them. And when people have been trusted with a lot, they will be asked to give even more.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v49"><a class="verse-num" href="#v49" aria-label="Luke 12:49">49</a> I have come to bring fire on the earth; and how I wish it was already burning!</p>

<p class="verse" id="v50"><a class="verse-num" href="#v50" aria-label="Luke 12:50">50</a> But I must go through a baptism; and I feel pressured until it&#39;s done!</p>

<p class="verse" id="v51"><a class="verse-num" href="#v51" aria-label="Luke 12:51">51</a> Do you think I came to bring peace on earth? I tell you, no; I came to divide.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v52"><a class="verse-num" href="#v52" aria-label="Luke 12:52">52</a> From now on, five people in one family will be split, three against two and two against three.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v53"><a class="verse-num" href="#v53" aria-label="Luke 12:53">53</a> A father will be against his son, and a son against his father; a mother will be against her daughter, and a daughter against her mother; a mother-in-law will be against her daughter-in-law, and a daughter-in-law against her mother-in-law.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v54"><a class="verse-num" href="#v54" aria-label="Luke 12:54">54</a> He also told the people, &quot;When you see a cloud coming from the west, right away you say, &#39;A rain is coming&#39;; and that&#39;s what happens.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v55"><a class="verse-num" href="#v55" aria-label="Luke 12:55">55</a> When you see the south wind, you say, &quot;It will be hot,&quot; and it happens.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v56"><a class="verse-num" href="#v56" aria-label="Luke 12:56">56</a> You pretenders, you can understand the look of the sky and the earth; but why can&#39;t you understand what&#39;s happening now?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v57"><a class="verse-num" href="#v57" aria-label="Luke 12:57">57</a> Yes, and why don&#39;t you judge for yourselves what is right?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v58"><a class="verse-num" href="#v58" aria-label="Luke 12:58">58</a> When you go to court with your opponent, try hard to settle the matter before you get there. If you don&#39;t, your opponent might drag you to the judge, the judge might hand you over to the guard, and the guard might put you in jail.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v59"><a class="verse-num" href="#v59" aria-label="Luke 12:59">59</a> I tell you, you will not leave there until you have paid the very last penny.</p>
