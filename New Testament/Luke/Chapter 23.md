<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Luke 23:1">1</a> The whole crowd got up and took him to Pilate.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Luke 23:2">2</a> They started to accuse him, saying, &quot;We caught this man corrupting the nation and telling people not to pay taxes to Caesar, claiming he is Christ, a king.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Luke 23:3">3</a> Pilate asked him, &quot;Are you the King of the Jews?&quot; He replied, &quot;You say so.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Luke 23:4">4</a> Pilate said to the religious leaders and the people, &quot;I find nothing wrong with this man.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Luke 23:5">5</a> They became even angrier, saying, &quot;He causes trouble among the people, teaching all over Judea, starting from Galilee to here.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Luke 23:6">6</a> When Pilate heard about Galilee, he asked if the man was from Galilee.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Luke 23:7">7</a> As soon as he found out that he was under Herod&#39;s authority, he sent him to Herod, who was also in Jerusalem then.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Luke 23:8">8</a> When Herod saw Jesus, he was very happy, because he had wanted to see him for a long time after hearing so much about him; he also hoped to see Jesus perform a miracle.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Luke 23:9">9</a> He asked him many questions, but he gave no answer.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Luke 23:10">10</a> The leading priests and teachers of the law stood and strongly accused him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Luke 23:11">11</a> Herod and his soldiers ignored Jesus, made fun of him, dressed him in a fancy robe, and sent him back to Pilate.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Luke 23:12">12</a> On that day, Pilate and Herod became friends; before that, they had been enemies.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Luke 23:13">13</a> Pilate gathered the chief priests, leaders, and the people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Luke 23:14">14</a> He said to them, &quot;You have brought this man to me as someone who corrupts the people. But look, I have questioned him in front of you and have found him not guilty of the accusations you make against him.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Luke 23:15">15</a> No, not even Herod, because I sent you to him, and look, he has done nothing that deserves death.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Luke 23:16">16</a> I will punish him and then let him go.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Luke 23:17">17</a> He had to release one prisoner to them during the feast.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Luke 23:18">18</a> They all shouted together, &quot;Get rid of this man and free Barabbas for us.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Luke 23:19">19</a> He was put in jail for causing a riot in the city and for murder.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Luke 23:20">20</a> So Pilate, wanting to let Jesus go, spoke to them again.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Luke 23:21">21</a> They shouted, &quot;Crucify him, crucify him.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Luke 23:22">22</a> He asked them a third time, &quot;What wrong has he done? I&#39;ve found no reason to kill him. So, I&#39;ll punish him and then release him.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Luke 23:23">23</a> They urgently shouted, wanting him to be crucified. And their voices, along with the chief priests&#39;, were stronger.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Luke 23:24">24</a> Pilate decided to do what they asked.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Luke 23:25">25</a> He freed the one they wanted who was in prison for causing trouble and murder, but he handed Jesus over to do what they wished.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Luke 23:26">26</a> As they took him away, they grabbed Simon from Cyrene, who was coming from the fields, and gave him the cross to carry behind Jesus.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Luke 23:27">27</a> A large crowd followed him, including women who mourned and cried for him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Luke 23:28">28</a> Jesus turned to them and said, &quot;Women of Jerusalem, don&#39;t cry for me, but cry for yourselves and your children.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Luke 23:29">29</a> Look, the time is coming when people will say, &quot;Happy are those who cannot have children, the wombs that never gave birth, and the breasts that never nursed.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Luke 23:30">30</a> Then they will start to tell the mountains, &quot;Fall on us,&quot; and to the hills, &quot;Hide us.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Luke 23:31">31</a> If this happens to a living tree, what will happen to a dead one?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Luke 23:32">32</a> Two other criminals were also led with him to be executed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Luke 23:33">33</a> When they reached the place known as Calvary, they nailed him to the cross along with the criminals, one on his right and the other on his left.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Luke 23:34">34</a> Jesus said, &quot;Father, forgive them, because they don&#39;t know what they&#39;re doing.&quot; They shared out his clothes by throwing dice.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Luke 23:35">35</a> The people watched. The leaders mocked him, saying, &quot;He saved others; let him save himself if he is the Messiah, the one chosen by God.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Luke 23:36">36</a> The soldiers also made fun of him, went up to him, and gave him vinegar.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Luke 23:37">37</a> They said, &quot;If you are the king of the Jews, save yourself.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Luke 23:38">38</a> A sign was also put above him with the words in Greek, Latin, and Hebrew, &quot;This is the King of the Jews.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="Luke 23:39">39</a> One of the criminals hanging beside Jesus insulted him, saying, &quot;If you are the Christ, save yourself and us.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="Luke 23:40">40</a> But the other one scolded him, saying, &quot;Don&#39;t you respect God since you are also being punished the same way?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="Luke 23:41">41</a> We are punished fairly, for we are getting what our actions deserve; but this man has done nothing wrong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="Luke 23:42">42</a> He told Jesus, &quot;Remember me when you come into your kingdom.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v43"><a class="verse-num" href="#v43" aria-label="Luke 23:43">43</a> Jesus said to him, &quot;I tell you the truth, today you will be with me in paradise.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v44"><a class="verse-num" href="#v44" aria-label="Luke 23:44">44</a> It was around noon, and darkness covered the whole earth until about three in the afternoon.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v45"><a class="verse-num" href="#v45" aria-label="Luke 23:45">45</a> The sun turned dark, and the temple&#39;s curtain ripped down the middle.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v46"><a class="verse-num" href="#v46" aria-label="Luke 23:46">46</a> When Jesus shouted loudly, he said, &quot;Father, I give my spirit to you.&quot; After saying this, he died.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v47"><a class="verse-num" href="#v47" aria-label="Luke 23:47">47</a> When the army officer saw what happened, he praised God, saying, &quot;This man was truly good.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v48"><a class="verse-num" href="#v48" aria-label="Luke 23:48">48</a> All the people who gathered to see what happened beat their chests and then went away.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v49"><a class="verse-num" href="#v49" aria-label="Luke 23:49">49</a> All his friends and the women who had followed him from Galilee stood at a distance watching these things.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v50"><a class="verse-num" href="#v50" aria-label="Luke 23:50">50</a> Look, there was a man named Joseph, who was a counselor; he was good and fair.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v51"><a class="verse-num" href="#v51" aria-label="Luke 23:51">51</a> He did not agree with their plan and action; he came from Arimathaea, a Jewish city, and he also waited for God&#39;s kingdom.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v52"><a class="verse-num" href="#v52" aria-label="Luke 23:52">52</a> This man went to Pilate and asked for Jesus&#39; body.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v53"><a class="verse-num" href="#v53" aria-label="Luke 23:53">53</a> He took the body down, wrapped it in cloth, and placed it in a tomb cut out of rock where no one had ever been laid before.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v54"><a class="verse-num" href="#v54" aria-label="Luke 23:54">54</a> That day was for getting ready, and the Sabbath was about to start.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v55"><a class="verse-num" href="#v55" aria-label="Luke 23:55">55</a> The women who had come with him from Galilee followed and saw the tomb and how his body was placed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v56"><a class="verse-num" href="#v56" aria-label="Luke 23:56">56</a> They went back and got spices and ointments ready. Then they rested on the Sabbath day, as the rule said.</p>
