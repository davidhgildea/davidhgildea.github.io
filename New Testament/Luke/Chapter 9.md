<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Luke 9:1">1</a> He gathered his twelve followers and gave them the power and authority to cast out all demons and to heal sicknesses.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Luke 9:2">2</a> He sent them to tell about God&#39;s kingdom and heal the sick.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Luke 9:3">3</a> He told them, &quot;Don&#39;t take anything for your trip, not a walking stick, not a bag, not bread, not money, and don&#39;t have two shirts each.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Luke 9:4">4</a> When you enter a house, stay there until you leave.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Luke 9:5">5</a> If anyone does not welcome you, leave that town and shake the dust off your feet as a warning to them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Luke 9:6">6</a> They left and traveled through the towns, sharing the good news and healing everywhere.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Luke 9:7">7</a> Herod the ruler heard about everything Jesus did. He was confused because some people said that John had come back to life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Luke 9:8">8</a> Some people thought Elijah had appeared, while others believed one of the past prophets had come back to life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Luke 9:9">9</a> Herod said, &quot;I beheaded John, but who is this I hear about?&quot; And he wanted to see him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Luke 9:10">10</a> The apostles came back and told Jesus everything they did. He took them to a quiet place in the desert near Bethsaida.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Luke 9:11">11</a> When the people found out, they followed him. He welcomed them, talked to them about God&#39;s kingdom, and healed those who needed healing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Luke 9:12">12</a> As the day was ending, the twelve disciples came to him and said, &quot;Send the crowd away so they can go to the nearby towns and countryside to find places to stay and get food, because we are in a remote place.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Luke 9:13">13</a> He said to them, &quot;You give them something to eat.&quot; But they replied, &quot;We only have five loaves of bread and two fish, unless we go and buy food for everyone.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Luke 9:14">14</a> There were about five thousand men. He told his disciples to have them sit down in groups of fifty.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Luke 9:15">15</a> They did as told and had everyone sit down.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Luke 9:16">16</a> He took the five loaves and two fish, looked up to heaven, blessed them, broke them, and gave them to the disciples to give to all the people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Luke 9:17">17</a> They ate, were all satisfied, and collected twelve baskets of leftover pieces.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Luke 9:18">18</a> While he was praying alone, his disciples joined him. He asked them, &quot;Who do people say I am?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Luke 9:19">19</a> They replied, &quot;John the Baptist; but some think Elijah; and others that a prophet from long ago has come back to life.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Luke 9:20">20</a> He asked them, &quot;But who do you say I am?&quot; Peter replied, &quot;The Messiah of God.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Luke 9:21">21</a> He strictly told them and ordered them not to tell anyone about that thing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Luke 9:22">22</a> The Son of Man must go through a lot of suffering, be turned away by the elders, the top priests, and the teachers of the law, be killed, and come back to life on the third day.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Luke 9:23">23</a> He told everyone, &quot;If anyone wants to follow me, they must forget about their own needs, carry their cross every day, and come with me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Luke 9:24">24</a> Whoever tries to keep their life will lose it, but whoever loses their life for me will save it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Luke 9:25">25</a> What good is it for a person to get the whole world, but lose themselves or be thrown away?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Luke 9:26">26</a> Whoever feels ashamed of me and my teachings, the Son of Man will feel ashamed of that person when he comes in his glory, his Father&#39;s glory, and the glory of the holy angels.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Luke 9:27">27</a> I tell you the truth, some standing here will not die before they see God&#39;s kingdom.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Luke 9:28">28</a> About eight days after saying this, he took Peter, John, and James and went up a mountain to pray.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Luke 9:29">29</a> While he was praying, his face changed, and his clothes became very white and shiny.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Luke 9:30">30</a> Two men, Moses and Elias, talked with him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Luke 9:31">31</a> Who appeared in glory and talked about his death that he would complete in Jerusalem.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Luke 9:32">32</a> Peter and his friends were very sleepy, but when they woke up, they saw his brightness and the two men with him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Luke 9:33">33</a> After they left him, Peter said to Jesus, &quot;Teacher, it&#39;s good for us to be here. Let&#39;s make three shelters: one for you, one for Moses, and one for Elijah,&quot; without understanding what he was saying.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Luke 9:34">34</a> While he was speaking, a cloud came and covered them, and they were afraid as they went into the cloud.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Luke 9:35">35</a> A voice came from the cloud, saying, &quot;This is my dear Son: listen to him.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Luke 9:36">36</a> After the voice ended, only Jesus was there. They kept quiet about it and didn’t tell anyone at that time what they had seen.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Luke 9:37">37</a> The next day, when they came down from the hill, many people met him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Luke 9:38">38</a> Look, a man in the crowd shouted, &quot;Teacher, please pay attention to my son because he is my only child.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="Luke 9:39">39</a> Suddenly, a spirit seizes him, and he cries out loudly; it shakes him violently, makes him foam at the mouth, hurts him badly, and only leaves after a struggle.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="Luke 9:40">40</a> I asked your followers to drive him out, but they couldn&#39;t.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="Luke 9:41">41</a> Jesus answered, &quot;You unbelieving and wrong-thinking people, how long must I stay with you and put up with you? Bring your son here.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="Luke 9:42">42</a> As he was still approaching, the devil knocked him down and attacked him. Jesus scolded the evil spirit, healed the boy, and gave him back to his father.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v43"><a class="verse-num" href="#v43" aria-label="Luke 9:43">43</a> Everyone was amazed at the great power of God. But as they were all wondering about everything Jesus did, he spoke to his disciples.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v44"><a class="verse-num" href="#v44" aria-label="Luke 9:44">44</a> Listen carefully to these words: the Son of Man will be handed over to people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v45"><a class="verse-num" href="#v45" aria-label="Luke 9:45">45</a> They did not understand what he meant, and it was kept secret from them so they could not realize it; and they were afraid to ask him about it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v46"><a class="verse-num" href="#v46" aria-label="Luke 9:46">46</a> They started to argue about which one of them was the most important.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v47"><a class="verse-num" href="#v47" aria-label="Luke 9:47">47</a> Jesus knew what they were thinking, so he took a child and had him stand next to him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v48"><a class="verse-num" href="#v48" aria-label="Luke 9:48">48</a> Jesus told them, &quot;Whoever welcomes this child in my name welcomes me; and whoever welcomes me also welcomes the one who sent me. The least one among you all will be the greatest.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v49"><a class="verse-num" href="#v49" aria-label="Luke 9:49">49</a> John replied, &quot;Teacher, we saw someone driving out demons using your name; we tried to stop him because he is not one of us.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v50"><a class="verse-num" href="#v50" aria-label="Luke 9:50">50</a> Jesus said to him, &quot;Don&#39;t stop him, because if he is not against us, he is with us.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v51"><a class="verse-num" href="#v51" aria-label="Luke 9:51">51</a> When the time came for him to be taken up to heaven, he firmly decided to go to Jerusalem.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v52"><a class="verse-num" href="#v52" aria-label="Luke 9:52">52</a> He sent messengers ahead of him, and they went to a Samaritan village to prepare for his arrival.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v53"><a class="verse-num" href="#v53" aria-label="Luke 9:53">53</a> They did not welcome him because he looked like he was headed for Jerusalem.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v54"><a class="verse-num" href="#v54" aria-label="Luke 9:54">54</a> When James and John, his followers, saw this, they asked, &quot;Do you want us to call down fire from heaven to destroy them, like Elijah did?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v55"><a class="verse-num" href="#v55" aria-label="Luke 9:55">55</a> He turned, corrected them, and said, &quot;You don&#39;t know what kind of spirit you belong to.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v56"><a class="verse-num" href="#v56" aria-label="Luke 9:56">56</a> The Son of man did not come to end people&#39;s lives, but to rescue them. Then they moved on to another town.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v57"><a class="verse-num" href="#v57" aria-label="Luke 9:57">57</a> As they traveled, a man said to him, &quot;Lord, I will follow you wherever you go.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v58"><a class="verse-num" href="#v58" aria-label="Luke 9:58">58</a> Jesus said to him, &quot;Foxes have holes, and birds in the sky have nests, but the Son of Man has no place to rest his head.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v59"><a class="verse-num" href="#v59" aria-label="Luke 9:59">59</a> He said to another, &quot;Follow me.&quot; But he replied, &quot;Please let me go and bury my father first.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v60"><a class="verse-num" href="#v60" aria-label="Luke 9:60">60</a> Jesus told him, &quot;Let the dead bury their own dead. Instead, go and spread the message of God&#39;s kingdom.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v61"><a class="verse-num" href="#v61" aria-label="Luke 9:61">61</a> Someone else said, &quot;God, I will follow you, but first let me say goodbye to those at my home.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v62"><a class="verse-num" href="#v62" aria-label="Luke 9:62">62</a> Jesus said to him, &quot;Anyone who starts to plow but keeps looking back isn&#39;t ready for God&#39;s kingdom.&quot;</p>
