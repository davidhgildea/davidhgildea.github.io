<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Luke 3:1">1</a> In the fifteenth year when Tiberius Caesar ruled, Pontius Pilate was the governor of Judaea, Herod ruled Galilee, his brother Philip ruled Ituraea and Trachonitis, and Lysanias ruled Abilene.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Luke 3:2">2</a> During the time Annas and Caiaphas were high priests, God spoke to John, Zacharias&#39;s son, in the desert.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Luke 3:3">3</a> He went through the whole region around the Jordan River, teaching about a baptism for repentance to forgive sins.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Luke 3:4">4</a> The book of the prophet Isaiah says, &quot;A voice calls out in the desert, &#39;Get ready for God&#39;s way, make a straight path for him.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Luke 3:5">5</a> Every low place will be filled, and every mountain and hill will be made low; the winding roads will be made straight, and the rough paths will be made smooth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Luke 3:6">6</a> Everyone will see how God saves.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Luke 3:7">7</a> He told the crowd coming to be baptized, &quot;You brood of snakes, who warned you to escape the coming anger?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Luke 3:8">8</a> Produce actions that show you have changed for the better, and don&#39;t think you can say, &#39;We have Abraham as our ancestor,&#39; because I tell you, God can turn these stones into descendants for Abraham.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Luke 3:9">9</a> The axe is now at the root of the trees: every tree that does not produce good fruit is cut down and thrown into the fire.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Luke 3:10">10</a> The people asked him, &quot;What should we do?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Luke 3:11">11</a> He answered and said to them, &quot;If you have two coats, give one to the person who has none; and if you have food, do the same.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Luke 3:12">12</a> Tax collectors also came to be baptized and asked him, &quot;Teacher, what should we do?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Luke 3:13">13</a> He told them, &quot;Don&#39;t take more than what you are told to.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Luke 3:14">14</a> The soldiers also asked him, &quot;What should we do?&quot; He told them, &quot;Don&#39;t hurt anyone, don&#39;t lie about others, and be happy with your pay.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Luke 3:15">15</a> As the people waited and wondered to themselves if John might be the Christ,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Luke 3:16">16</a> John replied to everyone, &quot;I baptize you in water, but someone more powerful than me is coming. I&#39;m not good enough to untie his shoes. He will baptize you with the Holy Spirit and with fire.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Luke 3:17">17</a> He holds a winnowing fan to clean his threshing area and he will bring the wheat into his barn, but he will burn the chaff with a fire that cannot be put out.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Luke 3:18">18</a> He preached many other things to encourage the people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Luke 3:19">19</a> But Herod the ruler, being corrected by him for taking Herodias, his brother Philip&#39;s wife, and for all the bad things Herod had done,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Luke 3:20">20</a> He also did this: he put John in jail.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Luke 3:21">21</a> After everyone was baptized, when Jesus was also baptized and was praying, the sky opened up.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Luke 3:22">22</a> The Holy Spirit came down in the form of a dove on him, and a voice from heaven said, &quot;You are my dear Son; I am happy with you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Luke 3:23">23</a> Jesus started to be about thirty years old, and people thought he was Joseph&#39;s son, who was Heli&#39;s son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Luke 3:24">24</a> He was the son of Matthat, who was Levi&#39;s son, who was Melchi&#39;s son, who was Janna&#39;s son, who was Joseph&#39;s son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Luke 3:25">25</a> He was the son of Mattathias, the son of Amos, the son of Naum, the son of Esli, the son of Nagge.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Luke 3:26">26</a> He was the son of Maath, the son of Mattathias, the son of Semei, the son of Joseph, the son of Juda.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Luke 3:27">27</a> He was the son of Joanna, the son of Rhesa, the son of Zerubbabel, the son of Shealtiel, the son of Neri.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Luke 3:28">28</a> He was the son of Melchi, the son of Addi, the son of Cosam, the son of Elmodam, and the son of Er.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Luke 3:29">29</a> He was the son of Jose, who was the son of Eliezer, who was the son of Jorim, who was the son of Matthat, who was the son of Levi.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Luke 3:30">30</a> He was the son of Simeon, the son of Juda, the son of Joseph, the son of Jonan, the son of Eliakim.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Luke 3:31">31</a> He was the son of Melea, the son of Menan, the son of Mattatha, the son of Nathan, the son of David.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Luke 3:32">32</a> He was the son of Jesse, who was the son of Obed, who was the son of Boaz, who was the son of Salmon, who was the son of Nahshon.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Luke 3:33">33</a> He was the son of Aminadab, who was the son of Aram, who was the son of Esrom, who was the son of Phares, who was the son of Judah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Luke 3:34">34</a> He was Jacob&#39;s son, who was Isaac&#39;s son, who was Abraham&#39;s son, who was Thara&#39;s son, who was Nachor&#39;s son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Luke 3:35">35</a> He was the son of Saruch, who was the son of Ragau, who was the son of Phalec, who was the son of Heber, who was the son of Sala.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Luke 3:36">36</a> He was the son of Cainan, who was the son of Arphaxad, who was the son of Shem, who was the son of Noah, who was the son of Lamech.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Luke 3:37">37</a> He was the son of Methuselah, who was the son of Enoch, who was the son of Jared, who was the son of Mahalaleel, who was the son of Cainan.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Luke 3:38">38</a> He was Enos&#39;s son, Seth&#39;s grandson, Adam&#39;s great-grandson, and God&#39;s descendant.</p>
