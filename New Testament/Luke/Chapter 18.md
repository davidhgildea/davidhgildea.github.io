<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Luke 18:1">1</a> He told them a story to show that they should always pray and never give up.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Luke 18:2">2</a> Someone said, &quot;In a city, there was a judge who did not fear God or care about people.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Luke 18:3">3</a> In that city, there was a widow; she approached him and said, &quot;Get justice for me from my opponent.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Luke 18:4">4</a> He didn&#39;t want to for some time, but later he thought, &quot;Even though I don&#39;t fear God or care about people;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Luke 18:5">5</a> Since this widow keeps bothering me, I will help her so she won&#39;t keep coming and wear me out.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Luke 18:6">6</a> God said, &quot;Listen to what the unfair judge says.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Luke 18:7">7</a> Won&#39;t God protect his chosen ones who cry out to him day and night, even though he is patient with them?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Luke 18:8">8</a> I tell you, God will quickly make things right for them. But when the Son of Man comes, will he find people with faith on the earth?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Luke 18:9">9</a> He told this story to some who were sure they were good people and looked down on everyone else:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Luke 18:10">10</a> Two men went to the temple to pray; one was a Pharisee, and the other was a tax collector.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Luke 18:11">11</a> The Pharisee stood up and prayed quietly to himself, &#39;God, I am thankful that I am not like other people—cheaters, unjust, unfaithful in marriage, or even like this tax collector.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Luke 18:12">12</a> I skip eating twice a week, I give part of everything I have.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Luke 18:13">13</a> The tax collector stood at a distance, wouldn&#39;t even look up to heaven, but hit his chest and said, &quot;God, have mercy on me, a sinner.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Luke 18:14">14</a> I tell you, this man went home right with God rather than the other: for everyone who lifts himself up will be put down, and he who lowers himself will be lifted up.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Luke 18:15">15</a> They also brought babies to him, so he could touch them. But when his followers saw this, they told them off.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Luke 18:16">16</a> Jesus called them to him and said, &quot;Let little children come to me, and don&#39;t stop them: for God&#39;s kingdom belongs to ones like these.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Luke 18:17">17</a> I tell you the truth, anyone who doesn&#39;t accept God&#39;s kingdom like a little child will never enter it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Luke 18:18">18</a> A leader asked him, &quot;Good Teacher, what must I do to have eternal life?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Luke 18:19">19</a> Jesus asked him, &quot;Why do you call me good? No one is good except God.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Luke 18:20">20</a> You know the commands: Don&#39;t cheat in marriage, don&#39;t kill, don&#39;t steal, don&#39;t lie about others, respect your father and mother.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Luke 18:21">21</a> He said, &quot;I have followed all these since I was young.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Luke 18:22">22</a> When Jesus heard this, he told him, &quot;You still need one thing: sell everything you own, give to the poor, and you will have treasure in heaven. Then come and follow me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Luke 18:23">23</a> When he heard this, he became very sad because he was very rich.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Luke 18:24">24</a> When Jesus saw how sad the man was, he said, &quot;It&#39;s really hard for rich people to get into God&#39;s kingdom!&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Luke 18:25">25</a> It is easier for a camel to pass through the hole of a needle than for a rich person to enter God&#39;s kingdom.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Luke 18:26">26</a> Those who heard this asked, &quot;Then who can be saved?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Luke 18:27">27</a> He said, What is impossible for people is possible with God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Luke 18:28">28</a> Then Peter said, &quot;Look, we have left everything and followed you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Luke 18:29">29</a> He told them, &quot;Truly, I tell you, no one who has left home, parents, brothers, wife, or children for God&#39;s kingdom,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Luke 18:30">30</a> Who will not get much more in this time, and in the future eternal life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Luke 18:31">31</a> He gathered the twelve disciples and said to them, &quot;Look, we&#39;re going to Jerusalem, and everything the prophets wrote about the Son of Man will happen.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Luke 18:32">32</a> He will be handed over to non-Jews, laughed at, badly treated, and spit on.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Luke 18:33">33</a> They will whip him, kill him, and on the third day, he will rise again.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Luke 18:34">34</a> They did not understand any of these things; the meaning was hidden from them, and they did not know what was said.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Luke 18:35">35</a> As he approached Jericho, a blind man was sitting by the road, begging.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Luke 18:36">36</a> Hearing the crowd go by, he asked what it was about.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Luke 18:37">37</a> They told him that Jesus from Nazareth is passing by.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Luke 18:38">38</a> He shouted, &quot;Jesus, son of David, please be kind to me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="Luke 18:39">39</a> The people in front told him to be quiet, but he shouted even louder, &quot;Son of David, have mercy on me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="Luke 18:40">40</a> Jesus stood up and ordered that the man be brought to him. When the man came close, Jesus asked him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="Luke 18:41">41</a> Asking, &quot;What do you want me to do for you?&quot; He replied, &quot;Sir, I want to see again.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="Luke 18:42">42</a> Jesus said to him, &quot;Get your sight back; your faith has healed you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v43"><a class="verse-num" href="#v43" aria-label="Luke 18:43">43</a> Right away he could see, and he followed him, praising God; and when all the people saw this, they also praised God.</p>
