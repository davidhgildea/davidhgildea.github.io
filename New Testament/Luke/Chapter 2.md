<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Luke 2:1">1</a> At that time, Caesar Augustus ordered that everyone in the world should be counted and taxed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Luke 2:2">2</a> This tax first happened when Cyrenius was the ruler of Syria.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Luke 2:3">3</a> Everyone went to register for taxes, each to their own city.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Luke 2:4">4</a> Joseph also left Galilee, from the town of Nazareth, to go to Judea, to the town called Bethlehem, the city of David, because he belonged to David&#39;s family line.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Luke 2:5">5</a> To register for taxes with Mary, his fianc&#233;e, who was pregnant.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Luke 2:6">6</a> While they were there, the time came for her to give birth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Luke 2:7">7</a> She gave birth to her first son, wrapped him in baby clothes, and put him in a feeding trough because there was no space for them in the guesthouse.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Luke 2:8">8</a> In that area, shepherds were in the fields, staying with their sheep at night.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Luke 2:9">9</a> Suddenly, an angel of God appeared to them, and God&#39;s brightness shone all around them: and they were very scared.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Luke 2:10">10</a> The angel told them, &quot;Don&#39;t be afraid; look, I have good news for you, a big joy that will be for everyone.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Luke 2:11">11</a> Today in David&#39;s city, a Savior is born for you, who is Christ the Lord.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Luke 2:12">12</a> This will be a sign for you; you will find a baby wrapped in cloths and lying in a feeding trough.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Luke 2:13">13</a> Suddenly, many angels from heaven appeared with the angel, all praising God and saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Luke 2:14">14</a> Praise God in the highest, and on earth peace, kindness to people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Luke 2:15">15</a> After the angels left and went to heaven, the shepherds said to each other, &quot;Let&#39;s go to Bethlehem and see what has happened that God has told us about.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Luke 2:16">16</a> They hurried and found Mary, Joseph, and the baby lying in a feeding trough.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Luke 2:17">17</a> After they saw it, they shared the message that was told to them about this child.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Luke 2:18">18</a> Everyone who heard it was amazed at what the shepherds told them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Luke 2:19">19</a> Mary remembered all these things and thought deeply about them in her heart.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Luke 2:20">20</a> The shepherds went back, praising and thanking God for everything they heard and saw, just as they had been told.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Luke 2:21">21</a> Eight days after the child was born, when it was time to circumcise him, he was named Jesus. This is the name the angel had given before he was born.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Luke 2:22">22</a> When her time of cleansing as required by Moses&#39; law was completed, they took him to Jerusalem to present him to God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Luke 2:23">23</a> The law of God says every first-born boy shall be considered sacred to God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Luke 2:24">24</a> To give a sacrifice as the law of God says, two turtledoves or two young pigeons.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Luke 2:25">25</a> Look, there was a man in Jerusalem named Simeon. This man was fair and devoted, looking forward to Israel&#39;s comfort, and the Holy Spirit was with him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Luke 2:26">26</a> God&#39;s Spirit told him he would not die before he saw God&#39;s promised Savior.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Luke 2:27">27</a> He was led by the Spirit to the temple. When Jesus&#39; parents came to do as the law required,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Luke 2:28">28</a> He picked him up, blessed God, and said.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Luke 2:29">29</a> God, now you let your servant go in peace, as you promised.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Luke 2:30">30</a> My eyes have seen your salvation.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Luke 2:31">31</a> You have made ready before all people;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Luke 2:32">32</a> A light to make the non-Jews see, and the honor of your people Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Luke 2:33">33</a> Joseph and his mother were amazed at what was said about him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Luke 2:34">34</a> Simeon blessed them and told Mary his mother, &quot;Look, this child is destined to cause many in Israel to fall and rise again; and to be a sign that will be opposed.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Luke 2:35">35</a> Yes, a sword will go through your own heart too, so that the secrets of many hearts will be shown.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Luke 2:36">36</a> Anna was a prophet, the daughter of Phanuel, from the Aser tribe. She was very old and had been married for seven years since she was young.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Luke 2:37">37</a> She was a widow of about 84 years old who never left the temple and served God with fasting and prayer day and night.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Luke 2:38">38</a> At that moment, she came in and thanked God too. She talked about him to everyone in Jerusalem waiting for salvation.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="Luke 2:39">39</a> After they had done everything the law of God required, they went back to Galilee, to their home town of Nazareth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="Luke 2:40">40</a> The child grew up, became strong in spirit, was full of wisdom, and God&#39;s favor was with him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="Luke 2:41">41</a> His parents went to Jerusalem every year for the Passover feast.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="Luke 2:42">42</a> When he was twelve, they went to Jerusalem for the festival as was their tradition.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v43"><a class="verse-num" href="#v43" aria-label="Luke 2:43">43</a> After the days were complete and they were going back, Jesus stayed in Jerusalem without Joseph and his mother knowing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v44"><a class="verse-num" href="#v44" aria-label="Luke 2:44">44</a> But they thought he was with the group, so they traveled for a day. Then they looked for him among their relatives and friends.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v45"><a class="verse-num" href="#v45" aria-label="Luke 2:45">45</a> When they didn&#39;t find him, they went back to Jerusalem to look for him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v46"><a class="verse-num" href="#v46" aria-label="Luke 2:46">46</a> Three days later, they found him in the temple. He was sitting with the teachers, listening to them and asking questions.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v47"><a class="verse-num" href="#v47" aria-label="Luke 2:47">47</a> Everyone who heard him was amazed at his understanding and replies.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v48"><a class="verse-num" href="#v48" aria-label="Luke 2:48">48</a> When they saw him, they were surprised. His mother asked him, &quot;Son, why have you treated us this way? Look, your father and I have been searching for you with heavy hearts.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v49"><a class="verse-num" href="#v49" aria-label="Luke 2:49">49</a> He asked them, &quot;Why were you looking for me? Didn&#39;t you know that I need to do my Father&#39;s work?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v50"><a class="verse-num" href="#v50" aria-label="Luke 2:50">50</a> They did not understand what he said to them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v51"><a class="verse-num" href="#v51" aria-label="Luke 2:51">51</a> He went with them to Nazareth and obeyed them. His mother remembered all these things in her heart.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v52"><a class="verse-num" href="#v52" aria-label="Luke 2:52">52</a> Jesus grew wiser and taller, and became liked by both God and people.</p>
