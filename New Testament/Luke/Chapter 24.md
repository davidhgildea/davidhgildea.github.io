<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Luke 24:1">1</a> On the first day of the week, very early in the morning, they went to the tomb with the spices they had made, and some others were with them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Luke 24:2">2</a> They saw that the stone was moved from the tomb&#39;s entrance.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Luke 24:3">3</a> They went in and did not find the body of Jesus.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Luke 24:4">4</a> While they were very confused about this, suddenly two men in bright clothes stood beside them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Luke 24:5">5</a> As they were scared and looked down at the ground, they asked them, &quot;Why are you looking for the living among the dead?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Luke 24:6">6</a> He is not here; he has risen. Remember what he told you when he was still in Galilee.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Luke 24:7">7</a> The Son of Man must be handed over to sinners, be crucified, and rise on the third day.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Luke 24:8">8</a> They remembered what he said.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Luke 24:9">9</a> They came back from the tomb and told everything to the eleven disciples and to all the others.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Luke 24:10">10</a> Mary Magdalene, Joanna, Mary the mother of James, and other women with them told the apostles these things.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Luke 24:11">11</a> What they said sounded like nonsense, and they did not believe it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Luke 24:12">12</a> Peter got up and ran to the tomb; bending over, he saw the linen wrappings lying alone, and he left, amazed at what had happened.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Luke 24:13">13</a> Two of them went to a village named Emmaus, about seven miles from Jerusalem, that day.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Luke 24:14">14</a> They talked about all these things that had happened.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Luke 24:15">15</a> While they were talking and discussing, Jesus himself came close and walked along with them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Luke 24:16">16</a> But they could not recognize him because their eyes were prevented.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Luke 24:17">17</a> He asked them, &quot;What are you discussing so intently as you walk along, looking so sad?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Luke 24:18">18</a> One of them, named Cleopas, replied, &quot;Are you the only visitor to Jerusalem who doesn&#39;t know the things that have happened here these days?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Luke 24:19">19</a> He asked them, &quot;What things?&quot; They answered, &quot;About Jesus from Nazareth, who was a powerful prophet in actions and words before God and all the people.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Luke 24:20">20</a> The leading priests and our leaders handed him over to be sentenced to death and crucified him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Luke 24:21">21</a> We hoped he was the one to save Israel, and what&#39;s more, today is the third day since this all happened.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Luke 24:22">22</a> Yes, some women from our group also surprised us; they were at the tomb early in the morning.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Luke 24:23">23</a> When they did not find his body, they came back saying they had seen a vision of angels who told them he was alive.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Luke 24:24">24</a> Some of us went to the tomb and found it just as the women had said, but we did not see him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Luke 24:25">25</a> Then he told them, &quot;You are foolish and slow to trust what the prophets have said:&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Luke 24:26">26</a> Shouldn&#39;t Christ have suffered these things and entered into his glory?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Luke 24:27">27</a> Starting with Moses and all the prophets, he explained to them what was said about himself in all the Scriptures.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Luke 24:28">28</a> They got close to the village where they were headed, and it seemed like he wanted to keep going.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Luke 24:29">29</a> But they urged him, saying, &quot;Stay with us, because it is almost night, and the day is almost over.&quot; So he went in to stay with them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Luke 24:30">30</a> While he was eating with them, he took bread, gave thanks, broke it, and gave it to them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Luke 24:31">31</a> Their eyes were opened, and they recognized him; then he disappeared from their sight.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Luke 24:32">32</a> They said to each other, &quot;Didn&#39;t our hearts feel warm as he talked to us on the road and explained the scriptures to us?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Luke 24:33">33</a> They got up that same hour, went back to Jerusalem, and found the eleven and those with them all together.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Luke 24:34">34</a> They said, &quot;The Lord has really risen and has shown himself to Simon.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Luke 24:35">35</a> They shared what happened on the road and how they recognized him when he broke the bread.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Luke 24:36">36</a> While they were talking, Jesus himself appeared among them and said to them, &quot;Peace to you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Luke 24:37">37</a> They were very scared and thought they had seen a ghost.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Luke 24:38">38</a> He asked them, &quot;Why are you worried? And why do you have doubts in your hearts?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="Luke 24:39">39</a> Look at my hands and my feet, it&#39;s really me. Touch me and see; a spirit doesn&#39;t have flesh and bones like you see I have.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="Luke 24:40">40</a> After saying this, he showed them his hands and feet.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="Luke 24:41">41</a> While they still couldn&#39;t believe because of their happiness, and were amazed, he asked them, &quot;Do you have any food?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="Luke 24:42">42</a> They gave him some cooked fish and honeycomb.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v43"><a class="verse-num" href="#v43" aria-label="Luke 24:43">43</a> He took it and ate it in front of them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v44"><a class="verse-num" href="#v44" aria-label="Luke 24:44">44</a> He told them, &quot;These are the words I told you when I was still with you: everything written about me in Moses&#39; law, the prophets, and the psalms must come true.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v45"><a class="verse-num" href="#v45" aria-label="Luke 24:45">45</a> He opened their minds so they could understand the scriptures.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v46"><a class="verse-num" href="#v46" aria-label="Luke 24:46">46</a> He told them, &quot;It is written that Christ must suffer and rise from the dead on the third day.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v47"><a class="verse-num" href="#v47" aria-label="Luke 24:47">47</a> People should be taught to change their ways and be forgiven for their sins in his name everywhere, starting from Jerusalem.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v48"><a class="verse-num" href="#v48" aria-label="Luke 24:48">48</a> You are witnesses of these things.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v49"><a class="verse-num" href="#v49" aria-label="Luke 24:49">49</a> Look, I will send you what my Father promised, but stay in the city of Jerusalem until you receive power from above.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v50"><a class="verse-num" href="#v50" aria-label="Luke 24:50">50</a> He led them to Bethany, raised his hands, and blessed them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v51"><a class="verse-num" href="#v51" aria-label="Luke 24:51">51</a> While he was blessing them, he left them and went up into heaven.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v52"><a class="verse-num" href="#v52" aria-label="Luke 24:52">52</a> They worshiped him and went back to Jerusalem feeling very happy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v53"><a class="verse-num" href="#v53" aria-label="Luke 24:53">53</a> They were always in the temple, praising and thanking God. Amen.</p>
