<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Matthew 4:1">1</a> Then Jesus was led by the Spirit into the desert to be tempted by the devil.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Matthew 4:2">2</a> After he fasted for forty days and nights, he felt hungry.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Matthew 4:3">3</a> When the tempter came to him, he said, &quot;If you are the Son of God, tell these stones to become bread.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Matthew 4:4">4</a> He replied, &quot;It is said, &#39;A person doesn&#39;t live only by eating bread, but by all words that come from God.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Matthew 4:5">5</a> Then the devil took him to the holy city and put him on the highest point of the temple.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Matthew 4:6">6</a> He said to him, &quot;If you are the Son of God, throw yourself down. It is said, &#39;God will command His angels to take care of you. They will lift you up in their hands, so you won&#39;t hit your foot on a stone.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Matthew 4:7">7</a> Jesus told him, &quot;It&#39;s also written, &#39;Don&#39;t test God.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Matthew 4:8">8</a> Once more, the devil took him to a very high mountain and showed him all the world&#39;s kingdoms and their splendor.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Matthew 4:9">9</a> He says to him, &quot;I will give you all these things if you bow down and worship me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Matthew 4:10">10</a> Jesus said to him, &quot;Go away, Satan: because it is written, You should worship God your God, and only serve him.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Matthew 4:11">11</a> Then the devil left him, and look, angels came and helped him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Matthew 4:12">12</a> When Jesus learned that John was put in jail, he went to Galilee.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Matthew 4:13">13</a> Leaving Nazareth, he went and lived in Capernaum by the sea, in the area of Zebulun and Naphtali.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Matthew 4:14">14</a> This was to fulfill what the prophet Isaiah had said:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Matthew 4:15">15</a> The land of Zabulon and the land of Naphtali, near the sea, across the Jordan River, Galilee where the non-Jews live;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Matthew 4:16">16</a> The people sitting in darkness have seen a great light, and those sitting in the area and shadow of death have had light shine on them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Matthew 4:17">17</a> From then on, Jesus started to preach, saying, &quot;Change your ways, for heaven&#39;s kingdom is near.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Matthew 4:18">18</a> Jesus, walking by the Sea of Galilee, saw two brothers, Simon called Peter, and his brother Andrew, throwing a net into the sea because they were fishermen.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Matthew 4:19">19</a> He said to them, &quot;Follow me, and I will teach you to bring people to me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Matthew 4:20">20</a> They immediately left their nets and followed him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Matthew 4:21">21</a> Moving on from there, he saw two other brothers, James and John, the sons of Zebedee. They were in a boat with their father Zebedee, fixing their nets. He called to them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Matthew 4:22">22</a> They quickly left the boat and their dad, and followed him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Matthew 4:23">23</a> Jesus traveled throughout Galilee, teaching in their places of worship, sharing the good news of the kingdom, and curing every kind of illness and disease among the people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Matthew 4:24">24</a> His fame spread all over Syria: and they brought to him all the sick with various diseases and pains, those with evil spirits, those who were insane, and those who were paralyzed; and he healed them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Matthew 4:25">25</a> Many people followed him from Galilee, Decapolis, Jerusalem, Judea, and the area across the Jordan River.</p>
