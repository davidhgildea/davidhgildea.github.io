<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Matthew 17:1">1</a> Six days later, Jesus took Peter, James, and John, his brother, up a high mountain by themselves.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Matthew 17:2">2</a> He changed form in front of them; his face shone like the sun, and his clothes became as white as light.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Matthew 17:3">3</a> Suddenly, Moses and Elijah appeared to them, talking with him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Matthew 17:4">4</a> Peter replied to Jesus, &quot;Teacher, it&#39;s great for us to be here. If you want, we can build three shelters: one for you, one for Moses, and one for Elijah.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Matthew 17:5">5</a> While he was still speaking, a bright cloud covered them. And a voice from the cloud said, &quot;This is my dear Son, who makes me happy; listen to him.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Matthew 17:6">6</a> When the followers heard this, they fell to the ground, very scared.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Matthew 17:7">7</a> Jesus came, touched them, and said, &quot;Get up, and do not be afraid.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Matthew 17:8">8</a> When they looked up, they saw no one except Jesus.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Matthew 17:9">9</a> As they came down from the mountain, Jesus instructed them, &quot;Don&#39;t tell anyone what you saw until the Son of man has risen from the dead.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Matthew 17:10">10</a> His followers asked him, &quot;Why do the teachers say that Elijah must come first?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Matthew 17:11">11</a> Jesus answered them, &quot;Elias will indeed come first and restore everything.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Matthew 17:12">12</a> I tell you, Elijah has already come, and they did not recognize him, but did to him whatever they wanted. In the same way, the Son of Man will suffer by their hands.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Matthew 17:13">13</a> Then the followers realized he was talking to them about John the Baptist.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Matthew 17:14">14</a> When they reached the crowd, a man came to him, knelt down before him, and said,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Matthew 17:15">15</a> God, please have mercy on my son; he has seizures and suffers terribly. Often he falls into the fire and into the water.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Matthew 17:16">16</a> I brought him to your followers, but they couldn&#39;t heal him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Matthew 17:17">17</a> Then Jesus replied, &quot;You have no faith and are all mixed up. How long must I stay with you? How long must I put up with you? Bring the boy to me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Matthew 17:18">18</a> Jesus told the evil spirit to leave, and it left the boy, who was healed from that moment.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Matthew 17:19">19</a> The disciples came to Jesus alone and asked, &quot;Why couldn&#39;t we drive it out?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Matthew 17:20">20</a> Jesus told them, &quot;It&#39;s because you don&#39;t believe. Truly, if you have faith even as small as a mustard seed, you can tell this mountain to move to another place and it will move. Nothing will be impossible for you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Matthew 17:21">21</a> However, this type can only come out through prayer and fasting.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Matthew 17:22">22</a> While they stayed in Galilee, Jesus told them, &quot;The Son of Man will be handed over to people.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Matthew 17:23">23</a> They will kill him, and on the third day, he will rise again. They were very sad.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Matthew 17:24">24</a> When they arrived in Capernaum, the people who collect the temple tax went to Peter and asked, &quot;Doesn&#39;t your teacher pay the tax?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Matthew 17:25">25</a> He said, &quot;Yes.&quot; When he entered the house, Jesus spoke first, asking, &quot;What do you think, Simon? Who do the kings of the earth collect taxes or fees from? Their own children or others?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Matthew 17:26">26</a> Peter said to him, &quot;From foreigners.&quot; Jesus said to him, &quot;Then the children are free.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Matthew 17:27">27</a> Still, we don&#39;t want to upset them, so go to the lake, throw in a line, and pull out the first fish you catch. Open its mouth and you&#39;ll find some money. Take it and pay them for both of us.</p>
