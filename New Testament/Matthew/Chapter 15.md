<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Matthew 15:1">1</a> Some teachers of the law and Pharisees from Jerusalem came to Jesus and asked,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Matthew 15:2">2</a> Why do your followers break the elders&#39; tradition? They don&#39;t wash their hands before eating bread.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Matthew 15:3">3</a> But he replied and said to them, &quot;Why do you also break God&#39;s command by following your tradition?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Matthew 15:4">4</a> God commanded, saying, Respect your father and mother; and, Anyone who speaks badly of their father or mother must be put to death.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Matthew 15:5">5</a> But you say, if anyone tells their father or mother, &#39;What you could have gained from me is a gift to God&#39;,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Matthew 15:6">6</a> If someone does not respect their father or mother, you say they are free from guilt. This way, you cancel out God&#39;s rule because of your tradition.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Matthew 15:7">7</a> You pretenders, Isaiah was right when he prophesied about you, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Matthew 15:8">8</a> These people come close to me with their words and honor me with their lips, but their hearts are far from me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Matthew 15:9">9</a> They worship me for nothing, teaching human rules as if they were God&#39;s teachings.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Matthew 15:10">10</a> He called the crowd and said to them, &quot;Listen and understand.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Matthew 15:11">11</a> What goes into the mouth does not make a person unclean; it&#39;s what comes out of the mouth that makes a person unclean.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Matthew 15:12">12</a> His disciples came and asked him, &quot;Do you know that the Pharisees were upset when they heard what you said?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Matthew 15:13">13</a> He replied, &quot;Every plant that my Father in heaven did not plant will be pulled out.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Matthew 15:14">14</a> Leave them; they are blind guides. If a blind person leads another blind person, both will fall into a hole.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Matthew 15:15">15</a> Peter replied and asked him, &quot;Explain this story to us.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Matthew 15:16">16</a> Jesus asked, &quot;Do you still not understand?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Matthew 15:17">17</a> Don&#39;t you understand that whatever goes into the mouth passes into the stomach and then is eliminated?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Matthew 15:18">18</a> The things that come out of the mouth start in the heart, and these make the person unclean.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Matthew 15:19">19</a> Evil thoughts, murders, cheating in marriage, sexual misconduct, stealing, lying, and insults all come from the heart.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Matthew 15:20">20</a> These things make a person unclean: but eating with unwashed hands does not make a person unclean.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Matthew 15:21">21</a> Jesus left that place and went to the region of Tyre and Sidon.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Matthew 15:22">22</a> Look, a Canaanite woman came from that area and called out to him, &quot;Please have mercy on me, Sir, son of David; my daughter is suffering terribly because of an evil spirit.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Matthew 15:23">23</a> He did not reply to her at all. His followers came and urged him, &quot;Tell her to go away; she keeps shouting at us.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Matthew 15:24">24</a> He replied, &quot;I am only sent to the lost sheep of Israel.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Matthew 15:25">25</a> She came and worshiped him, saying, &quot;Sir, help me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Matthew 15:26">26</a> But he replied, &quot;It&#39;s not right to take the children&#39;s bread and throw it to dogs.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Matthew 15:27">27</a> She said, &quot;Yes, God, but even the dogs eat the crumbs that fall from their owners&#39; table.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Matthew 15:28">28</a> Then Jesus replied to her, &quot;Woman, your faith is strong; let it happen for you as you wish.&quot; And her daughter was healed at that moment.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Matthew 15:29">29</a> Jesus left that place and went near the Sea of Galilee; He climbed a mountain and sat down there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Matthew 15:30">30</a> Many people came to him, bringing the lame, blind, mute, crippled, and many others, and placed them at Jesus&#39; feet; and he healed them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Matthew 15:31">31</a> The crowd was amazed to see those who couldn&#39;t speak talking, the injured healed, the lame walking, and the blind seeing; and they praised the God of Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Matthew 15:32">32</a> Jesus called his followers to him and said, &quot;I feel sorry for all the people because they have been with me for three days and have nothing to eat. I don&#39;t want to send them away hungry, or they might get weak on their journey.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Matthew 15:33">33</a> His followers asked him, &quot;Where can we get enough bread in this remote place to feed such a large crowd?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Matthew 15:34">34</a> Jesus asked them, &quot;How many loaves do you have?&quot; They replied, &quot;Seven, and a few small fish.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Matthew 15:35">35</a> He told the people to sit down on the ground.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Matthew 15:36">36</a> He took the seven loaves and the fish, thanked God, broke them, and gave them to his followers, and they gave them to the crowd.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Matthew 15:37">37</a> Everyone ate and was satisfied. They collected seven baskets full of leftover pieces.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Matthew 15:38">38</a> About four thousand men ate, not counting women and children.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="Matthew 15:39">39</a> He sent the crowd away, got into a boat, and went to the region of Magdala.</p>
