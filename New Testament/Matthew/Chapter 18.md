<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Matthew 18:1">1</a> The disciples came to Jesus then, asking, &quot;Who is the greatest in heaven&#39;s kingdom?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Matthew 18:2">2</a> Jesus called a child over and had him stand among them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Matthew 18:3">3</a> Truly, I tell you, if you don&#39;t change and become like little children, you won&#39;t enter the kingdom of heaven.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Matthew 18:4">4</a> Anyone who humbles themselves like this child is the greatest in heaven&#39;s kingdom.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Matthew 18:5">5</a> Anyone who welcomes a little child because of me is welcoming me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Matthew 18:6">6</a> But if anyone hurts one of these little ones who believe in me, it would be better for them to have a heavy stone tied around their neck and to be drowned in the deep sea.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Matthew 18:7">7</a> Trouble comes to the world because of wrongs; wrongs must happen, but trouble comes to the person who causes the wrong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Matthew 18:8">8</a> If your hand or foot causes you to sin, cut it off and throw it away. It&#39;s better to live with one hand or foot than to have two and be thrown into eternal fire.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Matthew 18:9">9</a> If your eye causes you to sin, take it out and throw it away. It&#39;s better to live with one eye than to have two eyes and be thrown into hell.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Matthew 18:10">10</a> Be careful not to look down on any of these little ones, because I tell you, their angels in heaven always see the face of my God in heaven.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Matthew 18:11">11</a> The Son of Man came to save what was lost.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Matthew 18:12">12</a> What do you think? If a man has a hundred sheep and one gets lost, won&#39;t he leave the ninety-nine and go to the mountains to look for the lost one?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Matthew 18:13">13</a> If he finds it, truly, I tell you, he is happier about that one sheep than about the ninety-nine that did not wander off.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Matthew 18:14">14</a> God in heaven does not want even one of these little ones to be lost.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Matthew 18:15">15</a> If your brother does wrong to you, go and point out his mistake, just between the two of you. If he listens to you, you have won your brother back.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Matthew 18:16">16</a> If he doesn&#39;t listen to you, bring one or two others with you, so that the truth of every word can be confirmed by two or three witnesses.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Matthew 18:17">17</a> If he ignores them, tell the church. But if he ignores the church, treat him like a stranger or a tax collector.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Matthew 18:18">18</a> I tell you the truth, whatever you bind on earth will be bound in heaven, and whatever you release on earth will be released in heaven.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Matthew 18:19">19</a> I tell you again, if two of you on earth agree about anything you ask for, it will be done for you by my Father in heaven.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Matthew 18:20">20</a> When two or three people meet in my name, I am there with them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Matthew 18:21">21</a> Peter came to him and asked, &quot;Lord, how many times should I forgive my brother when he sins against me? Up to seven times?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Matthew 18:22">22</a> Jesus said to him, &quot;I don&#39;t mean seven times, but seventy times seven.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Matthew 18:23">23</a> The kingdom of heaven is compared to a king who wanted to settle accounts with his servants.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Matthew 18:24">24</a> When he started to settle accounts, someone was brought to him who owed him ten thousand coins.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Matthew 18:25">25</a> Since he couldn&#39;t pay, his master ordered that he, his wife, his children, and everything he owned be sold to pay the debt.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Matthew 18:26">26</a> The servant knelt down and begged him, saying, &quot;Master, please wait, and I will pay you everything.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Matthew 18:27">27</a> The master of the servant felt sorry for him, set him free, and canceled his debt.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Matthew 18:28">28</a> The servant went out and found a fellow servant who owed him a small amount of money. He grabbed him and choked him, saying, &quot;Pay back what you owe me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Matthew 18:29">29</a> His coworker knelt down and begged him, saying, &quot;Please be patient with me, and I will pay you everything.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Matthew 18:30">30</a> He refused and put him in jail until he could pay back the money.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Matthew 18:31">31</a> When his fellow servants saw what happened, they were very sad and went to tell their master everything that happened.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Matthew 18:32">32</a> Then his master, after calling him, said to him, &#39;You evil servant, I forgave all your debt because you asked me to.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Matthew 18:33">33</a> Shouldn&#39;t you also have shown mercy to your fellow servant, just as I showed mercy to you?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Matthew 18:34">34</a> His master was angry and handed him over to the jailers until he could pay back everything he owed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Matthew 18:35">35</a> In the same way, my Father in heaven will treat you, unless you truly forgive everyone for the wrongs they have done to you.</p>
