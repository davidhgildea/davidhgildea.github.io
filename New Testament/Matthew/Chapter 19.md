<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Matthew 19:1">1</a> After Jesus finished speaking, he left Galilee and went to the area of Judea across the Jordan River.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Matthew 19:2">2</a> Large crowds followed him, and he healed them there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Matthew 19:3">3</a> The Pharisees came to him, trying to test him, and asked, &quot;Is it okay for a man to divorce his wife for any reason?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Matthew 19:4">4</a> He replied, &quot;Haven&#39;t you read that at the start, the Creator made them man and woman?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Matthew 19:5">5</a> And said, &quot;Because of this, a man will leave his father and mother and stick to his wife, and the two will become one body.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Matthew 19:6">6</a> So they are no longer two, but one body. What God has joined, let no one separate.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Matthew 19:7">7</a> They asked him, &quot;Then why did Moses tell us to give a divorce paper and send her away?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Matthew 19:8">8</a> He told them, Moses allowed you to divorce your wives because your hearts were hard, but it was not like that from the start.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Matthew 19:9">9</a> I tell you, if anyone divorces his wife for any reason other than unfaithfulness, and marries someone else, he is committing adultery. Whoever marries the divorced woman is also committing adultery.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Matthew 19:10">10</a> His followers said to him, &quot;If this is how it is between a man and his wife, it is better not to get married.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Matthew 19:11">11</a> He told them, &quot;Not everyone can accept this teaching, only those who are meant to.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Matthew 19:12">12</a> Some people are born unable to have children, some are made that way by others, and some choose to live without marriage for God&#39;s kingdom. Whoever can accept this should accept it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Matthew 19:13">13</a> People brought little children to him so he could lay his hands on them and pray, but the disciples scolded them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Matthew 19:14">14</a> Jesus said, Let the little children come to me, don&#39;t stop them, because heaven belongs to ones like these.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Matthew 19:15">15</a> He put his hands on them and left that place.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Matthew 19:16">16</a> Someone came and asked him, &quot;Good teacher, what must I do to get eternal life?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Matthew 19:17">17</a> He asked him, &quot;Why do you call me good? Only God is truly good. But if you want to have life, follow the commandments.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Matthew 19:18">18</a> He asked, &quot;Which ones?&quot; Jesus answered, &quot;Do not kill, do not cheat on your spouse, do not steal, do not lie about others,&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Matthew 19:19">19</a> Respect your father and mother, and love your neighbor as you love yourself.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Matthew 19:20">20</a> The young man said to him, &quot;I have kept all these things since I was young: what do I still need?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Matthew 19:21">21</a> Jesus said to him, &quot;If you want to be perfect, go sell what you own, give to the poor, and you will have treasure in heaven: then come and follow me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Matthew 19:22">22</a> But when the young man heard this, he left feeling sad because he had many things.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Matthew 19:23">23</a> Jesus told his disciples, &quot;I tell you the truth, it is hard for a rich man to enter the kingdom of heaven.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Matthew 19:24">24</a> I tell you again, it is easier for a camel to pass through the eye of a needle than for a rich person to enter God&#39;s kingdom.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Matthew 19:25">25</a> When his followers heard this, they were very surprised and asked, &quot;Then who can be rescued?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Matthew 19:26">26</a> Jesus looked at them and said, &quot;For people this is impossible, but for God everything is possible.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Matthew 19:27">27</a> Peter replied, &quot;Look, we&#39;ve left everything to follow you. What will we get for this?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Matthew 19:28">28</a> Jesus told them, &quot;Truly, I tell you, those who have followed me will, when the Son of Man sits on his glorious throne, also sit on twelve thrones to judge the twelve tribes of Israel.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Matthew 19:29">29</a> Everyone who has left homes, brothers, sisters, parents, spouse, children, or fields for my sake will get back much more and will have eternal life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Matthew 19:30">30</a> Many who are first will be last, and the last will be first.</p>
