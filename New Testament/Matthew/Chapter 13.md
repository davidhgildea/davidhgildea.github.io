<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Matthew 13:1">1</a> That day, Jesus left the house and sat by the sea.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Matthew 13:2">2</a> Large crowds gathered to him, so he got into a boat and sat down, while all the people stood on the beach.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Matthew 13:3">3</a> He told them many stories with hidden meanings, saying, &quot;Look, a farmer went out to plant seeds.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Matthew 13:4">4</a> As he was planting, some seeds fell by the path, and the birds came and ate them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Matthew 13:5">5</a> Some seeds fell on rocky ground where there wasn&#39;t much soil. They quickly grew because the soil wasn&#39;t deep.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Matthew 13:6">6</a> When the sun rose, they got burned up and since they had no roots, they dried out.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Matthew 13:7">7</a> Some seeds fell among thorns, and the thorns grew and choked them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Matthew 13:8">8</a> Some seeds fell on good soil and produced crops: some 100 times more, some 60 times more, and some 30 times more.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Matthew 13:9">9</a> Anyone who has ears to listen should listen.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Matthew 13:10">10</a> The disciples came and asked him, &quot;Why do you talk to the people using stories with hidden meanings?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Matthew 13:11">11</a> He replied, &quot;You are allowed to understand the secrets of heaven&#39;s kingdom, but others are not.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Matthew 13:12">12</a> Whoever has will get more and have plenty, but whoever does not have will lose even what they have.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Matthew 13:13">13</a> So I tell them stories because they look but don&#39;t really see, and they listen but don&#39;t truly hear or understand.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Matthew 13:14">14</a> In them, the prediction of Isaiah comes true, which says, &quot;You will hear but not understand; you will see but not recognize.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Matthew 13:15">15</a> This people’s heart has grown hard, their ears can barely hear, and they have shut their eyes; otherwise, they might see with their eyes, hear with their ears, understand with their heart, turn back, and be healed by me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Matthew 13:16">16</a> Your eyes are blessed because they see, and your ears are blessed because they hear.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Matthew 13:17">17</a> Truly, I tell you, many prophets and good people wanted to see what you see but did not see it, and to hear what you hear but did not hear it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Matthew 13:18">18</a> Listen to the story of the farmer who plants seeds.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Matthew 13:19">19</a> When someone hears about God&#39;s kingdom but doesn&#39;t understand it, the evil one comes and takes away what was planted in their heart. This is like the seed that falls on the path.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Matthew 13:20">20</a> But the one who gets the seed on rocky ground is the person who hears the message and immediately receives it with happiness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Matthew 13:21">21</a> He doesn&#39;t have a deep root inside, but lasts only a short time. When trouble or harassment comes because of the message, he quickly gets upset.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Matthew 13:22">22</a> The person who received the seed among thorns is the one who hears the message, but worries of life and the lure of wealth choke the message, making them unproductive.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Matthew 13:23">23</a> The person who receives the seed on good soil is the one who listens to the word and understands it; they also produce a harvest, yielding sometimes a hundred times, sometimes sixty, sometimes thirty.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Matthew 13:24">24</a> He told them another story, saying, &quot;The kingdom of heaven is like a man who planted good seeds in his field.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Matthew 13:25">25</a> While people were sleeping, his enemy came and scattered weeds among the wheat, then left.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Matthew 13:26">26</a> But when the plants grew and produced grain, the weeds also showed up.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Matthew 13:27">27</a> The workers of the owner came and asked him, &quot;Sir, didn&#39;t you plant good seeds in your field? Where did the weeds come from?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Matthew 13:28">28</a> He told them, &quot;An enemy did this.&quot; The servants asked him, &quot;Do you want us to go and collect them?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Matthew 13:29">29</a> He said, &quot;No, because if you pull up the weeds, you might also pull out the wheat.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Matthew 13:30">30</a> Let both grow until harvest time. At harvest, I will tell the workers, &quot;First collect the weeds and tie them to burn, but bring the wheat into my barn.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Matthew 13:31">31</a> He told them another story: &quot;God&#39;s kingdom is like a tiny mustard seed that a man planted in his field.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Matthew 13:32">32</a> This seed is really the smallest of all seeds, but when it grows, it becomes the largest of garden plants and turns into a tree, so that the birds can come and nest in its branches.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Matthew 13:33">33</a> He told them another story: The kingdom of heaven is like yeast that a woman mixed into a large amount of flour until it spread through all of it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Matthew 13:34">34</a> Jesus told all these things to the crowd using stories; he did not tell them anything without using a story.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Matthew 13:35">35</a> This was done so the prophet&#39;s words would come true, where he said, &quot;I will tell stories to explain things; I will share secrets hidden since the world began.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Matthew 13:36">36</a> Jesus sent the crowd home and went inside. His followers came to him and said, &quot;Explain the story of the weeds in the field to us.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Matthew 13:37">37</a> He replied to them, &quot;The one who plants the good seeds is the Son of Man.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Matthew 13:38">38</a> The field means the world; the good seeds represent the people of God&#39;s kingdom, while the weeds are the people of the evil one.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="Matthew 13:39">39</a> The enemy who planted the seeds is the devil; the harvest time is the end of the world; and the workers who harvest are the angels.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="Matthew 13:40">40</a> So just as weeds are collected and burned in a fire, it will be at the end of this world.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="Matthew 13:41">41</a> The Son of Man will send his angels, and they will remove from his kingdom everything that causes sin and all who do evil.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="Matthew 13:42">42</a> They will throw them into a fiery furnace, where there will be crying and grinding of teeth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v43"><a class="verse-num" href="#v43" aria-label="Matthew 13:43">43</a> The good people will shine like the sun in God&#39;s kingdom. If someone can listen, they should listen.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v44"><a class="verse-num" href="#v44" aria-label="Matthew 13:44">44</a> The kingdom of heaven is like treasure hidden in a field. When a person finds it, they hide it again. Out of happiness, they sell everything they own and buy that field.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v45"><a class="verse-num" href="#v45" aria-label="Matthew 13:45">45</a> The kingdom of heaven is like a merchant looking for fine pearls.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v46"><a class="verse-num" href="#v46" aria-label="Matthew 13:46">46</a> When he found a very valuable pearl, he went and sold everything he had, and bought it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v47"><a class="verse-num" href="#v47" aria-label="Matthew 13:47">47</a> The kingdom of heaven is like a net thrown into the sea that caught all kinds of fish.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v48"><a class="verse-num" href="#v48" aria-label="Matthew 13:48">48</a> When the net was full, they pulled it onto the shore. They sat down and put the good fish into containers, but threw the bad ones away.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v49"><a class="verse-num" href="#v49" aria-label="Matthew 13:49">49</a> At the world&#39;s end, the angels will come and separate the bad people from the good ones.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v50"><a class="verse-num" href="#v50" aria-label="Matthew 13:50">50</a> They will throw them into a fiery furnace, where there will be crying and grinding of teeth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v51"><a class="verse-num" href="#v51" aria-label="Matthew 13:51">51</a> Jesus asked them, &quot;Do you understand all these things?&quot; They answered him, &quot;Yes, Lord.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v52"><a class="verse-num" href="#v52" aria-label="Matthew 13:52">52</a> He told them, &quot;So, every teacher trained for the kingdom of heaven is like a homeowner who brings out new and old treasures from his storage.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v53"><a class="verse-num" href="#v53" aria-label="Matthew 13:53">53</a> After Jesus told these stories, he left that place.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v54"><a class="verse-num" href="#v54" aria-label="Matthew 13:54">54</a> When he returned to his hometown, he taught in their meeting place. People were amazed and asked, &quot;How did this man get such wisdom and the power to do great things?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v55"><a class="verse-num" href="#v55" aria-label="Matthew 13:55">55</a> Isn&#39;t this the son of the carpenter? Isn&#39;t his mother named Mary? And his brothers, James, Joses, Simon, and Judas?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v56"><a class="verse-num" href="#v56" aria-label="Matthew 13:56">56</a> Doesn&#39;t he have all his sisters here with us? So where did this man get all these things?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v57"><a class="verse-num" href="#v57" aria-label="Matthew 13:57">57</a> They were upset because of him. But Jesus told them, a prophet is respected everywhere except in his own town and home.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v58"><a class="verse-num" href="#v58" aria-label="Matthew 13:58">58</a> He did not do many powerful things there because they did not believe.</p>
