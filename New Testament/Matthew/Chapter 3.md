<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Matthew 3:1">1</a> In those days, John the Baptist came, teaching in the desert of Judea.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Matthew 3:2">2</a> Say you&#39;re sorry, because God&#39;s kingdom is very near.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Matthew 3:3">3</a> This is the one the prophet Isaiah talked about when he said, &quot;A voice calls out in the desert, &#39;Get the road ready for God, make a straight path for him.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Matthew 3:4">4</a> John wore clothes made of camel hair and a leather belt around his waist. He ate locusts and wild honey.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Matthew 3:5">5</a> People from Jerusalem, all of Judea, and the area around the Jordan River went to see him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Matthew 3:6">6</a> They were baptized by him in the Jordan River, admitting their sins.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Matthew 3:7">7</a> When he saw many Pharisees and Sadducees at his baptism, he said to them, &quot;You brood of snakes, who warned you to escape the coming anger?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Matthew 3:8">8</a> Produce actions that show you have changed for the better.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Matthew 3:9">9</a> Don&#39;t think to yourselves, &#39;We have Abraham as our father.&#39; I tell you, God can make children for Abraham from these stones.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Matthew 3:10">10</a> The axe is now at the tree roots; so any tree that doesn&#39;t grow good fruit is cut down and thrown into the fire.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Matthew 3:11">11</a> I baptize you with water to show you have changed your heart and life. But the one who comes after me is stronger than I am. I&#39;m not good enough to carry his sandals. He will baptize you with the Holy Spirit and with fire.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Matthew 3:12">12</a> He holds a winnowing fork to clean his threshing area and gather the wheat into his barn; but he will burn the chaff with a fire that cannot be put out.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Matthew 3:13">13</a> Jesus came from Galilee to the Jordan River to be baptized by John.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Matthew 3:14">14</a> John stopped him, saying, &quot;I should be baptized by you, and yet you come to me?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Matthew 3:15">15</a> Jesus replied, &quot;Let it happen now; this is how we do what is right.&quot; Then he allowed it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Matthew 3:16">16</a> When Jesus was baptized and came up from the water, the sky opened to him, and he saw God&#39;s Spirit come down like a dove and land on him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Matthew 3:17">17</a> A voice from heaven said, &quot;This is my dear Son, who makes me very happy.&quot;</p>
