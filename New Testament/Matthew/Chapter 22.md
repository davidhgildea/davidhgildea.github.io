<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Matthew 22:1">1</a> Jesus answered and told them another story using parables. He said,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Matthew 22:2">2</a> God&#39;s kingdom is like a king who arranged a wedding for his son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Matthew 22:3">3</a> He sent his servants to invite those who had been asked to the wedding, but they did not want to come.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Matthew 22:4">4</a> He sent more servants and said, &quot;Tell the invited guests, &#39;Look, I&#39;ve made my dinner ready: my oxen and fattened animals are butchered, and everything is set. Come to the wedding feast.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Matthew 22:5">5</a> But they ignored it and went away, one to his farm, another to his business.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Matthew 22:6">6</a> The remaining people took his servants, treated them badly, and killed them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Matthew 22:7">7</a> When the king heard this, he was angry. He sent his armies to kill those murderers and burn their city.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Matthew 22:8">8</a> He said to his servants, &quot;The wedding is ready, but the guests who were invited were not worthy.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Matthew 22:9">9</a> Go to the main roads and invite everyone you find to the wedding.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Matthew 22:10">10</a> The servants went out to the roads and brought back everyone they found, both bad and good, and the wedding hall was filled with guests.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Matthew 22:11">11</a> When the king entered to look at the guests, he noticed a man there who was not wearing wedding clothes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Matthew 22:12">12</a> He said to him, &quot;Friend, how did you come in here without a wedding outfit?&quot; And he had nothing to say.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Matthew 22:13">13</a> The king told his servants, &quot;Tie up his hands and feet, take him out, and throw him into the dark place, where there will be crying and grinding of teeth.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Matthew 22:14">14</a> Many are invited, but few are selected.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Matthew 22:15">15</a> Then the Pharisees went and planned how to trap him with his words.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Matthew 22:16">16</a> They sent their followers to him with the Herodians, saying, &quot;Teacher, we know you are honest and teach God&#39;s way truthfully. You don&#39;t favor anyone because you pay no attention to who they are.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Matthew 22:17">17</a> Tell us then, what do you think? Is it right to pay taxes to Caesar or not?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Matthew 22:18">18</a> But Jesus knew they were being evil and asked, &quot;Why are you trying to trick me, you fakers?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Matthew 22:19">19</a> Show me the tax money. And they brought him a coin.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Matthew 22:20">20</a> He asks them, &quot;Whose picture and name are on this?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Matthew 22:21">21</a> They said to him, &quot;Caesar&#39;s.&quot; Then he said to them, &quot;Give back to Caesar what belongs to Caesar, and to God what belongs to God.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Matthew 22:22">22</a> After hearing his words, they were amazed, then they left and went on their way.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Matthew 22:23">23</a> That day the Sadducees, who say there is no resurrection, came to him and asked him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Matthew 22:24">24</a> Teacher, Moses said, &quot;If a man dies without children, his brother should marry the widow and have children for his brother.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Matthew 22:25">25</a> Seven brothers were with us. The first brother died after he got married, and since he had no children, his wife became his brother&#39;s.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Matthew 22:26">26</a> The second one did the same, and the third, up to the seventh.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Matthew 22:27">27</a> Finally, the woman died too.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Matthew 22:28">28</a> So, at the resurrection, whose wife will she be, since all seven were married to her?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Matthew 22:29">29</a> Jesus answered them, &quot;You are mistaken, because you don&#39;t know the Scriptures or God&#39;s power.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Matthew 22:30">30</a> In the resurrection, people do not marry or are married off, but are like God&#39;s angels in heaven.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Matthew 22:31">31</a> But about rising from the dead, haven&#39;t you read what God told you, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Matthew 22:32">32</a> I am the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. God is not for the dead, but for the living.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Matthew 22:33">33</a> When the crowd heard this, they were amazed at his teaching.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Matthew 22:34">34</a> When the Pharisees heard that he had made the Sadducees quiet, they came together.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Matthew 22:35">35</a> One of them, a lawyer, asked him a question to test him, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Matthew 22:36">36</a> Teacher, what is the most important command in the law?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Matthew 22:37">37</a> Jesus told him, &quot;You should love God with all your heart, with all your soul, and with all your mind.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Matthew 22:38">38</a> This is the most important command.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="Matthew 22:39">39</a> The second command is similar: Love your neighbor the same way you love yourself.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="Matthew 22:40">40</a> All the laws and the teachings of the prophets are based on these two commandments.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="Matthew 22:41">41</a> While the Pharisees were together, Jesus asked them,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="Matthew 22:42">42</a> Asking, &quot;What do you think about the Messiah? Who&#39;s his father?&quot; They answered, &quot;David&#39;s son.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v43"><a class="verse-num" href="#v43" aria-label="Matthew 22:43">43</a> He asks them, &quot;So why does David, inspired by the Spirit, call him &#39;Lord,&#39; saying?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v44"><a class="verse-num" href="#v44" aria-label="Matthew 22:44">44</a> God said to my master, &quot;Sit at my right side until I make your enemies a place for your feet.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v45"><a class="verse-num" href="#v45" aria-label="Matthew 22:45">45</a> If David calls him Lord, how is he David&#39;s son?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v46"><a class="verse-num" href="#v46" aria-label="Matthew 22:46">46</a> No one could reply to him at all, nor did anyone dare to ask him more questions from that day on.</p>
