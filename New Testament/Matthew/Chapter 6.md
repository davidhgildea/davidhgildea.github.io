<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Matthew 6:1">1</a> Be careful not to do good things in front of others just to be noticed, because if you do, you won&#39;t get any reward from your Father in heaven.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Matthew 6:2">2</a> When you give to the needy, don&#39;t announce it loudly like hypocrites in the synagogues and streets for attention. Truly, they&#39;ve received their reward.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Matthew 6:3">3</a> When you give to those in need, don&#39;t let one hand know what the other is doing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Matthew 6:4">4</a> Give your help to others quietly, and God, who sees what is done in secret, will reward you openly.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Matthew 6:5">5</a> When you pray, don&#39;t be like the fakers who like to pray in public places just to be seen by others. Truly, they have gotten all they will get.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Matthew 6:6">6</a> When you pray, go into your room and close the door, then pray to your Father who is unseen; and your Father, who sees what&#39;s done in secret, will reward you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Matthew 6:7">7</a> When you pray, don&#39;t keep on repeating words with no meaning, like other people do. They believe they will be heard because they talk a lot.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Matthew 6:8">8</a> Do not be like them, because your Father knows what you need before you ask him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Matthew 6:9">9</a> Pray like this: Our Father in heaven, may your name be honored.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Matthew 6:10">10</a> Let your kingdom come, let your will be done on earth, just like it is in heaven.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Matthew 6:11">11</a> Give us today the food we need.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Matthew 6:12">12</a> Forgive us what we owe, as we forgive those who owe us.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Matthew 6:13">13</a> Do not let us be tempted, but save us from evil: for the kingdom, the power, and the glory are yours, forever. Amen.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Matthew 6:14">14</a> If you forgive people for what they&#39;ve done wrong, God in heaven will forgive you too.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Matthew 6:15">15</a> If you don&#39;t forgive others for their wrongs, God won&#39;t forgive your wrongs either.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Matthew 6:16">16</a> When you fast, don&#39;t be like the fakes who look sad to show others they are fasting. Truly, that&#39;s the only reward they will get.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Matthew 6:17">17</a> When you fast, put oil on your head and wash your face.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Matthew 6:18">18</a> Do not show others that you are fasting; only God, who is unseen, should know. And God, who sees what is done in secret, will reward you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Matthew 6:19">19</a> Do not store up treasures for yourself on earth, where moths and rust destroy, and where thieves break in and steal.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Matthew 6:20">20</a> Store up for yourselves treasures in heaven, where moths and rust cannot destroy, and where thieves do not break in and steal.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Matthew 6:21">21</a> Where you keep your treasure is where your heart will be too.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Matthew 6:22">22</a> Your eye is like a lamp for your body. If your eye is clear, your whole body will be bright.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Matthew 6:23">23</a> But if your eye is bad, your whole body will be full of darkness. If the light inside you is dark, how deep is that darkness!</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Matthew 6:24">24</a> A person can&#39;t serve two bosses: they will either hate one and love the other, or be loyal to one and not like the other. You can&#39;t serve God and money.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Matthew 6:25">25</a> So I tell you, don&#39;t worry about your life, what you will eat or drink; or about your body, what you will wear. Isn&#39;t life more than food and the body more than clothes?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Matthew 6:26">26</a> Look at the birds of the sky: they don&#39;t plant seeds, harvest, or store food in barns, but God feeds them. Aren&#39;t you worth more than they are?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Matthew 6:27">27</a> Who of you can add an inch to your height by worrying?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Matthew 6:28">28</a> Why worry about clothes? Look at the field lilies; they don&#39;t work or make cloth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Matthew 6:29">29</a> I tell you, even Solomon in all his splendor was not dressed as beautifully as one of these.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Matthew 6:30">30</a> So, if God dresses the grass in the field, which is here today and thrown into the fire tomorrow, won&#39;t he dress you even more, you with little faith?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Matthew 6:31">31</a> So don&#39;t worry, asking, &#39;What will we eat?&#39; or &#39;What will we drink?&#39; or &#39;What will we wear?&#39;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Matthew 6:32">32</a> The non-believers chase after all these things, but your Father in heaven knows you need them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Matthew 6:33">33</a> First, look for God&#39;s kingdom and do what is right; then, all these things will be given to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Matthew 6:34">34</a> Do not worry about tomorrow, for tomorrow will worry about itself. Each day has enough trouble of its own.</p>
