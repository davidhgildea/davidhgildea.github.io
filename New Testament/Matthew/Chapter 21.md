<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Matthew 21:1">1</a> When they got close to Jerusalem and reached Bethphage at the Mount of Olives, Jesus sent two disciples.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Matthew 21:2">2</a> Tell them, go to the village ahead of you, and at once you will find a donkey tied up, and a young donkey with her: untie them and bring them to me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Matthew 21:3">3</a> If anyone asks you about it, say, &#39;God needs them,&#39; and he will send them right away.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Matthew 21:4">4</a> This happened to fulfill what the prophet had said.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Matthew 21:5">5</a> Tell the people of Zion, look, your King is coming to you, humble, and riding on a donkey, and on a colt, the young of a donkey.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Matthew 21:6">6</a> The disciples went and did what Jesus told them to do.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Matthew 21:7">7</a> They brought the donkey and the young colt, put their cloaks on them, and Jesus sat on them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Matthew 21:8">8</a> A huge crowd spread their clothes on the road; others cut branches from the trees and scattered them on the road.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Matthew 21:9">9</a> The crowds going ahead and those following shouted, &quot;Hosanna to the son of David! He who comes in the name of God is blessed; Hosanna in the highest!&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Matthew 21:10">10</a> When he entered Jerusalem, the whole city was stirred up and asked, &quot;Who is this?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Matthew 21:11">11</a> The crowd said, &quot;This is Jesus, the prophet from Nazareth in Galilee.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Matthew 21:12">12</a> Jesus entered God&#39;s temple and drove out all who were selling and buying there. He flipped over the tables of the money changers and the chairs of those selling doves.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Matthew 21:13">13</a> He said to them, &quot;It is written, &#39;My house will be called a house of prayer,&#39; but you have turned it into a hideout for thieves.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Matthew 21:14">14</a> Blind and disabled people came to him in the temple, and he healed them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Matthew 21:15">15</a> When the religious leaders saw the amazing things he did, and heard the children shouting in the temple, &quot;Hosanna to the Son of David,&quot; they were very upset.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Matthew 21:16">16</a> And said to him, &quot;Do you hear what they are saying?&quot; And Jesus said to them, &quot;Yes; have you never read, &#39;From the mouths of children and babies you have made perfect praise&#39;?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Matthew 21:17">17</a> He left them, went out of the city to Bethany, and stayed there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Matthew 21:18">18</a> In the morning as he went back to the city, he was hungry.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Matthew 21:19">19</a> When he saw a fig tree by the road, he went to it but found no fruit, only leaves. Then he said to it, &quot;May you never grow fruit again.&quot; At once, the fig tree dried up.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Matthew 21:20">20</a> When the disciples saw it, they were amazed and said, &quot;How quickly has the fig tree dried up!&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Matthew 21:21">21</a> Jesus replied, &quot;I tell you the truth, if you believe and don&#39;t doubt, you can do not just what was done to the fig tree, but also tell this mountain to move and be thrown into the sea, and it will happen.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Matthew 21:22">22</a> When you pray and believe, anything you ask for, you will get.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Matthew 21:23">23</a> When he entered the temple, the religious leaders and elders approached him while he was teaching and asked, &quot;What gives you the right to do these things? Who gave you this authority?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Matthew 21:24">24</a> Jesus replied and said to them, &quot;I will ask you one question too, and if you answer me, I will tell you by what authority I do these things.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Matthew 21:25">25</a> Where did John&#39;s baptism come from? From God or from people? They thought, &quot;If we say, &#39;From God,&#39; he will ask us, &#39;Then why didn&#39;t you believe John?&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Matthew 21:26">26</a> But if we say, &#39;From people,&#39; we are afraid of the crowd, because everyone considers John a prophet.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Matthew 21:27">27</a> They replied to Jesus, &quot;We don&#39;t know.&quot; So he said to them, &quot;Then I won&#39;t tell you by what authority I do these things.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Matthew 21:28">28</a> What do you think? A man had two sons. He went to the first and said, &quot;Son, go work in my vineyard today.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Matthew 21:29">29</a> He replied, &quot;I won&#39;t.&quot; But later he changed his mind and went.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Matthew 21:30">30</a> He went to the second and said the same thing. The second one answered, &quot;I will go, sir,&quot; but he did not go.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Matthew 21:31">31</a> Which of the two did what his father wanted? They answered, &quot;The first.&quot; Jesus told them, &quot;I tell you the truth, the tax collectors and prostitutes will enter God&#39;s kingdom ahead of you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Matthew 21:32">32</a> John came to you living right, but you didn&#39;t believe him. Tax collectors and prostitutes did believe, and even after seeing this, you didn&#39;t change your minds to believe him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Matthew 21:33">33</a> Listen to another story: A man owned a house, planted a vineyard, put a fence around it, made a place to press grapes, built a watchtower, rented it to farmers, and then he went to a distant land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Matthew 21:34">34</a> When it was almost time to pick the fruit, he sent his workers to the farmers to collect the fruit.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Matthew 21:35">35</a> The farmers took his workers, beat one, killed another, and threw stones at another.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Matthew 21:36">36</a> He sent more servants than before, and they were treated the same way.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Matthew 21:37">37</a> Last of all, he sent them his son, thinking, &quot;They will respect my son.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Matthew 21:38">38</a> When the workers saw the son, they said to each other, &quot;This is the one who will inherit everything. Let&#39;s kill him and take his inheritance.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="Matthew 21:39">39</a> They grabbed him, threw him out of the vineyard, and killed him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="Matthew 21:40">40</a> When the owner of the vineyard comes, what will he do to those workers?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="Matthew 21:41">41</a> They tell him, God will terribly destroy those bad men, and will rent his vineyard to other farmers, who will give him the crops at the right times.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="Matthew 21:42">42</a> Jesus said to them, &quot;Have you never read in the scriptures that the stone the builders did not want became the cornerstone? This was done by God, and it is amazing to us?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v43"><a class="verse-num" href="#v43" aria-label="Matthew 21:43">43</a> I tell you, God&#39;s kingdom will be taken from you and given to a people who produce its fruits.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v44"><a class="verse-num" href="#v44" aria-label="Matthew 21:44">44</a> Anyone who falls on this stone will break, but if it falls on anyone, it will crush them into dust.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v45"><a class="verse-num" href="#v45" aria-label="Matthew 21:45">45</a> When the chief priests and Pharisees heard his stories, they understood he was talking about them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v46"><a class="verse-num" href="#v46" aria-label="Matthew 21:46">46</a> But when they tried to arrest him, they were afraid of the crowd, because the people believed he was a prophet.</p>
