<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Matthew 14:1">1</a> At that time, Herod the ruler heard about Jesus&#39;s fame.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Matthew 14:2">2</a> He told his servants, &quot;This is John the Baptist; he has risen from the dead, and that&#39;s why he can do powerful things.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Matthew 14:3">3</a> Herod arrested John, tied him up, and put him in jail because of Herodias, his brother Philip&#39;s wife.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Matthew 14:4">4</a> John told him, &quot;You should not have her.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Matthew 14:5">5</a> He wanted to kill him but was afraid of the people, because they thought he was a prophet.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Matthew 14:6">6</a> On Herod&#39;s birthday, Herodias&#39;s daughter danced for them and made Herod happy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Matthew 14:7">7</a> He promised with a promise to give her anything she asked for.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Matthew 14:8">8</a> Her mother had told her what to say, so she said, &quot;Give me John the Baptist&#39;s head on a plate right here.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Matthew 14:9">9</a> The king felt sad, but because of his promise and the guests at his table, he ordered that her request be granted.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Matthew 14:10">10</a> He sent someone and had John beheaded in the prison.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Matthew 14:11">11</a> They brought his head on a platter, gave it to the young woman, and she took it to her mother.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Matthew 14:12">12</a> His followers came, picked up the body, buried it, and then went to tell Jesus.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Matthew 14:13">13</a> When Jesus learned about it, he left by boat to a remote desert area. Hearing this, the people walked from the towns to follow him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Matthew 14:14">14</a> Jesus went out, saw many people, felt sorry for them, and healed their sick.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Matthew 14:15">15</a> In the evening, his followers came to him and said, &quot;This is a remote place, and it&#39;s getting late; tell the crowd to leave so they can go to the nearby towns and buy food for themselves.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Matthew 14:16">16</a> Jesus said to them, &quot;They don&#39;t need to leave; give them something to eat.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Matthew 14:17">17</a> They said to him, &quot;We only have five loaves of bread and two fish here.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Matthew 14:18">18</a> He said, &quot;Bring them here to me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Matthew 14:19">19</a> He told the people to sit on the grass, took the five loaves and two fish, looked up to the sky, said a blessing, broke the bread, and gave it to his followers, who then gave it to everyone.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Matthew 14:20">20</a> Everyone ate and was satisfied, and they gathered twelve baskets full of leftover pieces.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Matthew 14:21">21</a> About five thousand men ate, not counting women and children.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Matthew 14:22">22</a> Right away, Jesus made his disciples get into a boat and go ahead of him to the other side while he sent the crowds away.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Matthew 14:23">23</a> After he told the crowds to go, he went up a mountain by himself to pray. When night came, he was there alone.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Matthew 14:24">24</a> The boat was in the middle of the sea, being thrown about by waves, because the wind was against it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Matthew 14:25">25</a> In the last part of the night, Jesus went to them, walking on the water.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Matthew 14:26">26</a> When the disciples saw him walking on the water, they were scared and said, &quot;It&#39;s a ghost!&quot; and they screamed with fear.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Matthew 14:27">27</a> Right away Jesus spoke to them, saying, &quot;Be happy; it&#39;s me; don&#39;t be scared.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Matthew 14:28">28</a> Peter answered, &quot;Lord, if it&#39;s really you, tell me to come to you on the water.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Matthew 14:29">29</a> He said, &quot;Come.&quot; So Peter got out of the boat and walked on the water to go to Jesus.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Matthew 14:30">30</a> But when he saw the strong wind, he was scared; and starting to sink, he shouted, &quot;God, save me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Matthew 14:31">31</a> Right away, Jesus reached out his hand, grabbed him, and said, &quot;You with little faith, why did you doubt?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Matthew 14:32">32</a> When they got into the boat, the wind stopped.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Matthew 14:33">33</a> The people in the boat came to him and honored him, saying, &quot;Truly, you are the Son of God.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Matthew 14:34">34</a> After they crossed over, they arrived in the land of Gennesaret.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Matthew 14:35">35</a> When the people there recognized him, they spread the word throughout the region and brought all the sick to him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Matthew 14:36">36</a> They asked to touch just the edge of his clothes, and everyone who touched it was completely healed.</p>
