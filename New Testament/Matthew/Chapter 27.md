<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Matthew 27:1">1</a> When morning came, all the leading priests and elders planned together to kill Jesus.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Matthew 27:2">2</a> After they tied him up, they took him and handed him over to the governor, Pontius Pilate.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Matthew 27:3">3</a> Judas, who had betrayed Jesus, felt sorry when he saw Jesus was going to be punished. He returned the thirty silver coins to the religious leaders and elders.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Matthew 27:4">4</a> &quot;I have done wrong by betraying someone innocent,&quot; he said. &quot;That&#39;s your problem,&quot; they replied.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Matthew 27:5">5</a> He threw the silver coins in the temple, left, and went to hang himself.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Matthew 27:6">6</a> The chief priests took the silver and said, &quot;We can&#39;t put this in the treasury because it&#39;s blood money.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Matthew 27:7">7</a> They made a plan and used the money to buy the potter&#39;s field as a burial place for foreigners.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Matthew 27:8">8</a> That&#39;s why they call the field the Field of Blood to this day.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Matthew 27:9">9</a> Then what the prophet Jeremiah had said came true: &quot;They took the thirty silver coins, the price set on him by some of the people of Israel.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Matthew 27:10">10</a> They used the money to buy the potter&#39;s field, as God told me to do.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Matthew 27:11">11</a> Jesus stood in front of the governor, who asked him, &quot;Are you the king of the Jews?&quot; Jesus replied, &quot;You say so.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Matthew 27:12">12</a> When the leading priests and elders accused him, he did not respond.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Matthew 27:13">13</a> Pilate asked him, &quot;Don&#39;t you hear how many things they are accusing you of?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Matthew 27:14">14</a> He did not reply at all, which amazed the governor very much.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Matthew 27:15">15</a> At that festival, the governor usually freed a prisoner chosen by the people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Matthew 27:16">16</a> They had a well-known prisoner named Barabbas.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Matthew 27:17">17</a> So when they all came together, Pilate asked them, &quot;Who do you want me to set free for you? Barabbas, or Jesus who is called the Christ?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Matthew 27:18">18</a> He knew they had handed him over because they were jealous.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Matthew 27:19">19</a> When he sat on the judge&#39;s bench, his wife sent a message to him, saying, &quot;Don&#39;t get involved with that innocent man, because I had a lot of trouble in a dream about him today.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Matthew 27:20">20</a> The religious leaders and officials convinced the people to choose Barabbas and to get rid of Jesus.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Matthew 27:21">21</a> The leader answered and asked them, &quot;Which of the two do you want me to set free for you?&quot; They said, &quot;Barabbas.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Matthew 27:22">22</a> Pilate asked them, &quot;What should I do with Jesus who is called the Christ?&quot; They all answered, &quot;Crucify him.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Matthew 27:23">23</a> The governor asked, &quot;Why? What bad thing has he done?&quot; But they shouted louder, &quot;Crucify him.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Matthew 27:24">24</a> When Pilate realized he was getting nowhere and that a riot was starting, he washed his hands in front of the people and said, &quot;I am not responsible for this man&#39;s death; you deal with it.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Matthew 27:25">25</a> Then all the people answered, &quot;Let the responsibility for his death fall on us and our children.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Matthew 27:26">26</a> He set Barabbas free for them, and after he had whipped Jesus, he handed him over to be crucified.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Matthew 27:27">27</a> The governor&#39;s soldiers took Jesus to a big room and all the soldiers came together around him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Matthew 27:28">28</a> They took off his clothes and put a red robe on him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Matthew 27:29">29</a> After making a crown of thorns, they placed it on his head and a stick in his right hand. They knelt down in front of him and made fun of him, saying, &quot;Hello, King of the Jews!&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Matthew 27:30">30</a> They spat on him, took the stick, and hit him on the head.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Matthew 27:31">31</a> After mocking him, they removed his robe, dressed him in his own clothes, and took him to be crucified.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Matthew 27:32">32</a> As they were leaving, they met a man from Cyrene named Simon. They forced him to carry the cross.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Matthew 27:33">33</a> When they arrived at a place called Golgotha, which means a place of a skull,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Matthew 27:34">34</a> They gave him sour wine mixed with bitter herbs, and when he tasted it, he refused to drink.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Matthew 27:35">35</a> They nailed him to a cross and divided his clothes, throwing dice to decide who would get each piece. This happened to fulfill what the prophet said: &quot;They divided my clothes among them and cast lots for my garment.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Matthew 27:36">36</a> They sat down and watched him there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Matthew 27:37">37</a> They placed a sign above his head with the charge against him written on it: THIS IS JESUS THE KING OF THE JEWS.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Matthew 27:38">38</a> Two thieves were crucified with him, one on the right and one on the left.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="Matthew 27:39">39</a> People walking by insulted him, shaking their heads.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="Matthew 27:40">40</a> They said, &quot;You who would destroy the temple and rebuild it in three days, save yourself. If you are the Son of God, come down from the cross.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="Matthew 27:41">41</a> The religious leaders also made fun of him, along with the teachers of the law and the older leaders, and said,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="Matthew 27:42">42</a> He saved others; he cannot save himself. If he is Israel&#39;s king, let him come down from the cross now, and we will believe in him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v43"><a class="verse-num" href="#v43" aria-label="Matthew 27:43">43</a> He trusted in God; let God rescue him now, if He wants him: because he said, I am the Son of God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v44"><a class="verse-num" href="#v44" aria-label="Matthew 27:44">44</a> The robbers who were crucified with him also insulted him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v45"><a class="verse-num" href="#v45" aria-label="Matthew 27:45">45</a> From noon until three in the afternoon, there was darkness over the entire land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v46"><a class="verse-num" href="#v46" aria-label="Matthew 27:46">46</a> Around the ninth hour, Jesus shouted loudly, saying, Eli, Eli, why have you left me alone?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v47"><a class="verse-num" href="#v47" aria-label="Matthew 27:47">47</a> Some people standing there heard this and said, &quot;This man is calling for Elijah.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v48"><a class="verse-num" href="#v48" aria-label="Matthew 27:48">48</a> Right away one of them ran, took a sponge, filled it with sour wine, put it on a stick, and offered it to him to drink.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v49"><a class="verse-num" href="#v49" aria-label="Matthew 27:49">49</a> The others said, &quot;Leave it, let&#39;s see if Elijah comes to save him.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v50"><a class="verse-num" href="#v50" aria-label="Matthew 27:50">50</a> Jesus, after crying out loudly, died.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v51"><a class="verse-num" href="#v51" aria-label="Matthew 27:51">51</a> Look, the curtain in the temple tore in two from top to bottom; the ground shook, and the rocks split.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v52"><a class="verse-num" href="#v52" aria-label="Matthew 27:52">52</a> The graves opened, and many holy people who had died came back to life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v53"><a class="verse-num" href="#v53" aria-label="Matthew 27:53">53</a> They came out of the tombs after he was raised from the dead, and went into the holy city, where many people saw them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v54"><a class="verse-num" href="#v54" aria-label="Matthew 27:54">54</a> When the army officer and his companions guarding Jesus felt the earthquake and saw what happened, they were very afraid and said, &quot;This really was God&#39;s Son.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v55"><a class="verse-num" href="#v55" aria-label="Matthew 27:55">55</a> Many women were there watching from a distance, who had followed Jesus from Galilee, serving him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v56"><a class="verse-num" href="#v56" aria-label="Matthew 27:56">56</a> Included were Mary Magdalene, Mary the mother of James and Joseph, and the mother of Zebedee&#39;s sons.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v57"><a class="verse-num" href="#v57" aria-label="Matthew 27:57">57</a> When evening arrived, a wealthy man from Arimathaea called Joseph, who was also Jesus&#39; follower, came.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v58"><a class="verse-num" href="#v58" aria-label="Matthew 27:58">58</a> He went to Pilate and asked for Jesus&#39; body. Then Pilate ordered that the body be given to him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v59"><a class="verse-num" href="#v59" aria-label="Matthew 27:59">59</a> When Joseph got the body, he wrapped it in a clean cloth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v60"><a class="verse-num" href="#v60" aria-label="Matthew 27:60">60</a> He placed it in his own new grave that he had carved from the rock, and he rolled a big stone in front of the grave&#39;s entrance, then left.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v61"><a class="verse-num" href="#v61" aria-label="Matthew 27:61">61</a> Mary Magdalene and the other Mary were sitting across from the tomb.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v62"><a class="verse-num" href="#v62" aria-label="Matthew 27:62">62</a> The next day, after the preparation day, the chief priests and Pharisees met with Pilate.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v63"><a class="verse-num" href="#v63" aria-label="Matthew 27:63">63</a> They said, &quot;Sir, we recall that the deceiver claimed he would rise again three days after his death.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v64"><a class="verse-num" href="#v64" aria-label="Matthew 27:64">64</a> So, make the tomb secure until the third day, so that his followers cannot come at night, take him, and tell everyone, &#39;He has risen from the dead.&#39; This would make the last mistake worse than the first.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v65"><a class="verse-num" href="#v65" aria-label="Matthew 27:65">65</a> Pilate said to them, &quot;You have a guard; go and make it as secure as you know how.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v66"><a class="verse-num" href="#v66" aria-label="Matthew 27:66">66</a> They went and secured the tomb by sealing the stone and placing guards.</p>
