<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Matthew 1:1">1</a> This book lists Jesus Christ&#39;s ancestors. He was from David&#39;s and Abraham&#39;s family.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Matthew 1:2">2</a> Abraham had a son named Isaac; Isaac had a son named Jacob; and Jacob had sons, including Judas and his brothers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Matthew 1:3">3</a> Judas had Phares and Zara with Thamar; Phares had Esrom; and Esrom had Aram.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Matthew 1:4">4</a> Aram was the father of Aminadab; Aminadab was the father of Naasson; Naasson was the father of Salmon.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Matthew 1:5">5</a> Salmon fathered Boaz with Rahab; Boaz fathered Obed with Ruth; and Obed fathered Jesse.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Matthew 1:6">6</a> Jesse was the father of King David; and King David was the father of Solomon, whose mother had been Uriah&#39;s wife.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Matthew 1:7">7</a> Solomon had a son named Rehoboam; Rehoboam had a son named Abijah; and Abijah had a son named Asa.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Matthew 1:8">8</a> Asa fathered Jehoshaphat; Jehoshaphat fathered Joram; and Joram fathered Uzziah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Matthew 1:9">9</a> Ozias was the father of Joatham, Joatham was the father of Achaz, and Achaz was the father of Ezekias.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Matthew 1:10">10</a> Hezekiah fathered Manasseh; Manasseh fathered Amon; and Amon fathered Josiah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Matthew 1:11">11</a> Josias was the father of Jechonias and his brothers around the time they were taken to Babylon.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Matthew 1:12">12</a> After they were taken to Babylon, Jechonias became the father of Salathiel, and Salathiel became the father of Zorobabel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Matthew 1:13">13</a> Zerubbabel was the father of Abiud; Abiud was the father of Eliakim; and Eliakim was the father of Azor.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Matthew 1:14">14</a> Azor was the father of Sadoc; Sadoc was the father of Achim; and Achim was the father of Eliud.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Matthew 1:15">15</a> Eliud was the father of Eleazar, Eleazar was the father of Matthan, and Matthan was the father of Jacob.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Matthew 1:16">16</a> Jacob was the father of Joseph, who was married to Mary. Mary gave birth to Jesus, also known as Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Matthew 1:17">17</a> There are fourteen generations from Abraham to David, fourteen generations from David to the time they were taken to Babylon, and fourteen generations from then to Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Matthew 1:18">18</a> The birth of Jesus Christ happened like this: Before Mary, his mother, and Joseph were together as husband and wife, she was discovered to be pregnant by the Holy Spirit.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Matthew 1:19">19</a> Joseph, her husband, was a good man and did not want to embarrass her publicly, so he planned to break up with her quietly.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Matthew 1:20">20</a> As he was thinking about all this, an angel from God came to him in a dream and said, &quot;Joseph, descendant of David, do not be afraid to make Mary your wife because the baby in her is from the Holy Spirit.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Matthew 1:21">21</a> She will have a son, and you will name him Jesus, because he will save his people from their sins.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Matthew 1:22">22</a> All this happened to make true what the prophet had said from God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Matthew 1:23">23</a> Look, a young woman will have a baby, and she will give birth to a son. They will name him Emmanuel, which means God with us.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Matthew 1:24">24</a> Joseph woke up, did what God&#39;s angel told him to, and took Mary as his wife.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Matthew 1:25">25</a> He did not have marital relations with her until she gave birth to her first son; and he named him Jesus.</p>
