<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Matthew 9:1">1</a> He got into a boat, crossed over, and arrived at his own city.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Matthew 9:2">2</a> Look, they brought to Jesus a man with paralysis, lying on a bed. Jesus, seeing their faith, said to the paralyzed man, &quot;Young man, cheer up; your sins are forgiven.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Matthew 9:3">3</a> Some teachers of the law thought to themselves, &quot;This man is speaking against God.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Matthew 9:4">4</a> Jesus, aware of their thoughts, asked, &quot;Why do you think evil in your hearts?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Matthew 9:5">5</a> Which is simpler: to say &#39;Your sins are forgiven,&#39; or to say &#39;Get up and walk&#39;?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Matthew 9:6">6</a> I want you to know that the Son of Man has authority on earth to forgive sins, so he said to the man with paralysis, &quot;Get up, pick up your mat, and go home.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Matthew 9:7">7</a> He got up and went to his home.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Matthew 9:8">8</a> When the crowds saw this, they were amazed and praised God for giving such power to people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Matthew 9:9">9</a> As Jesus left that place, he saw a man called Matthew sitting at the tax office. He said to him, &quot;Follow me.&quot; Matthew got up and followed him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Matthew 9:10">10</a> As Jesus was eating in the house, many tax collectors and wrongdoers came and ate with him and his followers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Matthew 9:11">11</a> When the Pharisees saw this, they asked his disciples, &quot;Why does your teacher eat with tax collectors and sinners?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Matthew 9:12">12</a> When Jesus heard this, he said to them, &quot;Healthy people don&#39;t need a doctor, but sick people do.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Matthew 9:13">13</a> Go and understand this: I desire kindness, not offerings. I came not to call the good people, but sinners to turn away from their sins.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Matthew 9:14">14</a> John&#39;s followers came to Jesus and asked, &quot;Why do we and the Pharisees often fast, but your disciples do not fast?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Matthew 9:15">15</a> Jesus asked them, &quot;Can wedding guests be sad while the groom is with them? But a time will come when the groom will be taken away, and then they will fast.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Matthew 9:16">16</a> No one sews a piece of new fabric onto an old clothing because the patch will pull away from it, making the tear worse.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Matthew 9:17">17</a> People don&#39;t put new wine into old containers; otherwise, the containers will break, the wine will spill, and the containers will be ruined. Instead, they put new wine into new containers, and both stay good.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Matthew 9:18">18</a> While he was speaking to them, look, a leader came and bowed down to him, saying, &quot;My daughter has just died. But if you come and put your hand on her, she will live.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Matthew 9:19">19</a> Jesus got up, followed him, and his disciples did too.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Matthew 9:20">20</a> Look, a woman who had been sick with bleeding for twelve years came up behind him and touched the edge of his clothes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Matthew 9:21">21</a> She thought, &quot;If I can just touch his clothes, I will be healed.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Matthew 9:22">22</a> Jesus turned around, and when he saw her, he said, &quot;Daughter, be encouraged; your faith has healed you.&quot; And from that moment, the woman was healed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Matthew 9:23">23</a> When Jesus entered the leader&#39;s home and saw the musicians and the noisy crowd,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Matthew 9:24">24</a> He told them, &quot;Move aside: the girl is not dead, but sleeping.&quot; And they laughed at him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Matthew 9:25">25</a> After the crowd was sent outside, he went in, took her hand, and the girl got up.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Matthew 9:26">26</a> News of this spread throughout the entire area.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Matthew 9:27">27</a> When Jesus left that place, two blind men followed him, calling out, &quot;Son of David, have mercy on us.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Matthew 9:28">28</a> When he entered the house, the blind men approached him, and Jesus asked them, &quot;Do you believe I can do this?&quot; They answered, &quot;Yes, Lord.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Matthew 9:29">29</a> He touched their eyes and said, &quot;Let it happen for you as you believe.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Matthew 9:30">30</a> Their eyes were opened, and Jesus firmly told them, &quot;Make sure no one finds out about this.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Matthew 9:31">31</a> After they left, they told everyone about him everywhere in that area.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Matthew 9:32">32</a> As they left, they brought to Jesus a man who could not speak because he had a demon.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Matthew 9:33">33</a> When the devil was driven away, the person who could not talk spoke, and the crowds were amazed, saying, &quot;Nothing like this has ever been seen in Israel.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Matthew 9:34">34</a> The Pharisees said, He drives out evil spirits using the ruler of the evil spirits.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Matthew 9:35">35</a> Jesus traveled through all the cities and villages, teaching in their places of worship, spreading the good news of the kingdom, and curing every illness and disease among the people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Matthew 9:36">36</a> When he saw the crowds, he felt sorry for them because they were weak and scattered, like sheep without a shepherd.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Matthew 9:37">37</a> He said to his students, &quot;There really is a lot of crop, but there are not many workers.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Matthew 9:38">38</a> Ask God, who takes care of the harvest, to send workers for gathering it.</p>
