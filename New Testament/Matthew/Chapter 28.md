<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Matthew 28:1">1</a> At the end of the Sabbath, as the first day of the week was starting, Mary Magdalene and the other Mary went to see the tomb.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Matthew 28:2">2</a> Look, there was a big earthquake because an angel from God came down from heaven, moved the stone from the entrance, and sat on it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Matthew 28:3">3</a> His face shone like lightning, and his clothes were as white as snow.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Matthew 28:4">4</a> The guards were so afraid of him that they trembled and became like men who were dead.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Matthew 28:5">5</a> The angel said to the women, &quot;Don&#39;t be afraid. I know you are looking for Jesus, who was crucified.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Matthew 28:6">6</a> He is not here; he has risen, just as he said. Come and see where he was lying.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Matthew 28:7">7</a> Go fast, and tell his disciples he has risen from the dead. Look, he is going ahead of you to Galilee. You will see him there. I have told you now.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Matthew 28:8">8</a> They left the tomb quickly, feeling afraid yet very happy, and ran to tell his disciples.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Matthew 28:9">9</a> As they were going to tell his disciples, Jesus met them and said, &quot;Greetings.&quot; They came, took hold of his feet, and worshiped him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Matthew 28:10">10</a> Jesus told them, &quot;Don&#39;t be scared. Go and tell my brothers to go to Galilee, and they will see me there.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Matthew 28:11">11</a> As they were leaving, look, some of the guards went into the city and told the chief priests everything that had happened.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Matthew 28:12">12</a> When they met with the leaders and made their plan, they paid the soldiers a lot of money.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Matthew 28:13">13</a> Say, &quot;His followers came at night and took him while we were sleeping.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Matthew 28:14">14</a> If the governor hears about this, we will talk to him and protect you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Matthew 28:15">15</a> They took the money and did what they were told, and people still tell this story among the Jews today.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Matthew 28:16">16</a> Then the eleven followers went to Galilee, to a mountain where Jesus had told them to go.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Matthew 28:17">17</a> When they saw him, they worshiped him, but some were unsure.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Matthew 28:18">18</a> Jesus came and said to them, &quot;I have been given all authority in heaven and on earth.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Matthew 28:19">19</a> Go and teach all people, baptize them in the name of the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Matthew 28:20">20</a> Teach them to follow everything I have told you: and remember, I am with you always, to the end of time. Amen.</p>
