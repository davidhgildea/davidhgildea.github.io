<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Matthew 20:1">1</a> The kingdom of heaven is like a man who owns a house and went out early in the morning to find workers for his vineyard.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Matthew 20:2">2</a> After he made a deal with the workers for one penny each day, he sent them to his grape farm.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Matthew 20:3">3</a> He went out around nine in the morning and saw others standing around doing nothing in the market.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Matthew 20:4">4</a> He said to them, &quot;You go work in the vineyard too, and I will pay you what is fair.&quot; So they went.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Matthew 20:5">5</a> He went out again around six and nine in the morning and did the same thing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Matthew 20:6">6</a> Around eleven o&#39;clock, he went out, found others standing around doing nothing, and said to them, &#39;Why have you been standing here all day doing nothing?&#39;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Matthew 20:7">7</a> They tell him, &quot;No one has given us work.&quot; He says to them, &quot;Go work in the vineyard, and you will get what is fair.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Matthew 20:8">8</a> When evening came, the owner of the vineyard said to his manager, &quot;Call the workers, and pay them their wages, starting with the last ones hired to the first.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Matthew 20:9">9</a> When the workers who were hired around 5 p.m. came, each one got a penny.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Matthew 20:10">10</a> When the first workers came, they thought they would get more, but they also got one penny each.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Matthew 20:11">11</a> When they got it, they complained about the homeowner.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Matthew 20:12">12</a> You said, &#39;These people worked only one hour, but you&#39;ve treated them the same as us, who worked hard all day in the hot sun.&#39;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Matthew 20:13">13</a> He replied to one of them, &quot;Friend, I&#39;m not being unfair to you. Didn&#39;t you agree to work for a penny?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Matthew 20:14">14</a> Take what is yours and leave; I will give this last person the same as I gave you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Matthew 20:15">15</a> Isn&#39;t it okay for me to do what I want with what&#39;s mine? Are you jealous because I am generous?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Matthew 20:16">16</a> The last will be first, and the first will be last: many are called, but few are chosen.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Matthew 20:17">17</a> Jesus headed to Jerusalem and took the twelve disciples aside on the road and said to them,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Matthew 20:18">18</a> Look, we are going to Jerusalem; and the Son of Man will be handed over to the religious leaders and the teachers of the law, and they will sentence him to death.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Matthew 20:19">19</a> They will hand him over to foreigners to mock, whip, and crucify him; but on the third day, he will come back to life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Matthew 20:20">20</a> Zebedee&#39;s wife came to Jesus with her sons, bowing down to him, and asked him for something.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Matthew 20:21">21</a> He asked her, &quot;What do you want?&quot; She replied, &quot;Let my two sons sit with you in your kingdom, one on your right and the other on your left.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Matthew 20:22">22</a> But Jesus replied, &quot;You don&#39;t understand what you&#39;re asking. Can you handle the hard things I&#39;m going to face and go through the tough experience I will go through?&quot; They answered him, &quot;We can.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Matthew 20:23">23</a> He tells them, &quot;You will indeed drink from my cup and be baptized like I am. But to sit at my right or left side is not for me to give. That place is for those my Father has prepared it for.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Matthew 20:24">24</a> When the ten heard about it, they were very upset with the two brothers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Matthew 20:25">25</a> Jesus called them to him and said, &quot;You know that the leaders of other nations rule over them, and their powerful people have authority over them.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Matthew 20:26">26</a> In your group, it should not be like that. Instead, if someone wants to be important, they should serve you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Matthew 20:27">27</a> Whoever wants to be the most important among you must serve the rest.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Matthew 20:28">28</a> The Son of Man did not come to be served, but to serve, and to give his life to save many people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Matthew 20:29">29</a> As they left Jericho, a large crowd followed him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Matthew 20:30">30</a> Two blind men were sitting by the road. When they heard Jesus was coming by, they shouted, &quot;Please help us, Lord, Son of David!&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Matthew 20:31">31</a> The crowd told them to be quiet, but they shouted louder, &quot;Please have mercy on us, God, son of David.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Matthew 20:32">32</a> Jesus stopped, called them, and asked, &quot;What do you want me to do for you?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Matthew 20:33">33</a> They said to him, &quot;Lord, let our eyes be opened.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Matthew 20:34">34</a> Jesus felt sorry for them, touched their eyes, and right away they could see, and they followed him.</p>
