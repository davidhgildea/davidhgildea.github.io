<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Matthew 25:1">1</a> Then the kingdom of heaven will be like ten young women who took their lamps and went out to meet the groom.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Matthew 25:2">2</a> Five of them were smart, and five were foolish.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Matthew 25:3">3</a> The foolish ones took their lamps but didn&#39;t bring any oil.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Matthew 25:4">4</a> The smart ones took oil in containers along with their lamps.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Matthew 25:5">5</a> While the groom was delayed, they all dozed off and slept.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Matthew 25:6">6</a> At midnight someone shouted, &quot;Look, the groom is coming; go out to meet him.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Matthew 25:7">7</a> Then all those young women got up and made their lamps ready.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Matthew 25:8">8</a> The foolish ones said to the wise, &quot;Give us some of your oil; our lamps have gone out.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Matthew 25:9">9</a> The wise replied, &quot;No, there might not be enough for both us and you. Instead, go to those who sell oil and buy some for yourselves.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Matthew 25:10">10</a> While they were buying oil, the groom arrived; those prepared went with him to the wedding, and the door was closed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Matthew 25:11">11</a> Later, the other young women came too, saying, &quot;Sir, Sir, open the door for us.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Matthew 25:12">12</a> He replied, &quot;Truly, I tell you, I don&#39;t know you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Matthew 25:13">13</a> Stay alert, because you don&#39;t know the day or the time when the Son of Man will come.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Matthew 25:14">14</a> The kingdom of heaven is like a man going to a distant land, who called his servants and gave them his possessions.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Matthew 25:15">15</a> God gave one person five talents, another two, and another one, each according to their own ability; and then he left immediately.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Matthew 25:16">16</a> The person who got five talents went and did business with them and earned five more talents.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Matthew 25:17">17</a> The person who got two also made two more.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Matthew 25:18">18</a> The person who got one coin went, dug a hole in the ground, and hid his master&#39;s money.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Matthew 25:19">19</a> After a long time, the master of those servants came and settled accounts with them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Matthew 25:20">20</a> The person who got five talents came and brought five more, saying, God, you gave me five talents; look, I&#39;ve earned five more talents in addition.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Matthew 25:21">21</a> His master said to him, &quot;Good job, you&#39;re a good and loyal worker. You&#39;ve been loyal with a few things, I&#39;ll put you in charge of many things. Come and share your master&#39;s happiness.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Matthew 25:22">22</a> The person who got two talents also came and said, &quot;Master, you gave me two talents; look, I&#39;ve earned two more talents in addition.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Matthew 25:23">23</a> His master said to him, &quot;Well done, good and loyal servant; you have been loyal with a few things, I will put you in charge of many things: come and share your master&#39;s happiness.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Matthew 25:24">24</a> The person who got one talent came and said, &quot;Master, I knew you are a tough man, taking what you didn&#39;t plant and collecting what you didn&#39;t scatter.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Matthew 25:25">25</a> I was scared, so I went and buried your money in the ground. Look, here is what belongs to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Matthew 25:26">26</a> His master replied, &quot;You bad and lazy servant, you knew that I harvest where I haven&#39;t planted, and gather where I haven&#39;t scattered seed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Matthew 25:27">27</a> You should have put my money with the bankers, so when I came back I could have gotten it back with interest.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Matthew 25:28">28</a> So take the talent from him and give it to the one who has ten talents.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Matthew 25:29">29</a> Everyone who has something will receive more and have plenty. But the person who has nothing will lose even the little they have.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Matthew 25:30">30</a> Throw the useless servant into the deep darkness, where there will be crying and grinding of teeth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Matthew 25:31">31</a> When the Son of Man comes in his glory, with all the angels, he will sit on his glorious throne.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Matthew 25:32">32</a> Before him all nations will be gathered, and he will separate them from each other, just like a shepherd separates the sheep from the goats.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Matthew 25:33">33</a> He will put the sheep on his right side and the goats on his left.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Matthew 25:34">34</a> The King will say to those on his right side, &quot;Come, you who are blessed by my Father, receive the kingdom made ready for you since the world was made.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Matthew 25:35">35</a> I was hungry, and you gave me food; I was thirsty, and you gave me something to drink; I was a stranger, and you welcomed me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Matthew 25:36">36</a> I was naked, and you gave me clothes; I was sick, and you looked after me; I was in prison, and you came to see me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Matthew 25:37">37</a> The good people will answer, &quot;God, when did we see you hungry and feed you, or thirsty and give you something to drink?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Matthew 25:38">38</a> When did we see you as a stranger and welcome you, or without clothes and dress you?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="Matthew 25:39">39</a> When did we see you sick or in prison and come to you?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="Matthew 25:40">40</a> The King will reply, &#39;Truly, I tell you, whatever you did for one of the least of these brothers and sisters of mine, you did for me.&#39;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="Matthew 25:41">41</a> Then he will also say to those on the left, &#39;Go away from me, you cursed ones, into the eternal fire made for the devil and his angels.&#39;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="Matthew 25:42">42</a> I was hungry, and you gave me nothing to eat; I was thirsty, and you gave me nothing to drink.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v43"><a class="verse-num" href="#v43" aria-label="Matthew 25:43">43</a> I was a stranger, and you did not welcome me: without clothes, and you did not dress me: ill, and in jail, and you did not visit me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v44"><a class="verse-num" href="#v44" aria-label="Matthew 25:44">44</a> Then they will also answer, saying, &quot;God, when did we see you hungry, or thirsty, or a stranger, or without clothes, or sick, or in jail, and did not help you?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v45"><a class="verse-num" href="#v45" aria-label="Matthew 25:45">45</a> He will reply, &#39;I tell you, when you did not help one of the least important people, you did not help me.&#39;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v46"><a class="verse-num" href="#v46" aria-label="Matthew 25:46">46</a> These will go to eternal punishment, but the good people to eternal life.</p>
