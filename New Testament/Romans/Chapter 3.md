<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Romans 3:1">1</a> What advantage does the Jew have? Or what benefit comes from circumcision?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Romans 3:2">2</a> In many ways, mostly because God&#39;s messages were entrusted to them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Romans 3:3">3</a> What if some do not believe? Does their lack of belief cancel out God&#39;s faithfulness?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Romans 3:4">4</a> Absolutely not! Yes, God is truthful, and all people are liars; as the scripture says, &quot;So that you may be right in your words and win when you are judged.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Romans 3:5">5</a> If our wrongs show how right God is, what can we say? Is God wrong when He punishes? (I&#39;m speaking like a human.)</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Romans 3:6">6</a> Certainly not, because then how would God judge the world?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Romans 3:7">7</a> If God&#39;s truth has spread more because of my lie to his glory, then why am I still judged as a sinner?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Romans 3:8">8</a> And instead of being falsely accused, as some claim we say, &quot;Let&#39;s do bad things so that good may happen&quot;? Those who say this deserve to be punished.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Romans 3:9">9</a> So what? Are we better than them? Not at all: because we have already shown that both Jews and non-Jews are all guilty of sin.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Romans 3:10">10</a> It is written, no one is good, not even one.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Romans 3:11">11</a> No one understands; no one looks for God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Romans 3:12">12</a> Everyone has strayed from the right path; they have all become useless; not even one person does good.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Romans 3:13">13</a> Their throat is like an open grave; they lie with their tongues; there is snake venom under their lips.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Romans 3:14">14</a> Their mouth is full of curses and bitterness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Romans 3:15">15</a> Their feet quickly lead them to harm others.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Romans 3:16">16</a> Trouble and suffering are in their paths.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Romans 3:17">17</a> They do not know the way of peace.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Romans 3:18">18</a> They do not fear God at all.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Romans 3:19">19</a> We know that whatever the law says, it speaks to those who follow the law, so that no one can argue and the whole world is accountable to God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Romans 3:20">20</a> No one will be seen as right by God only by following the law&#39;s rules, because the law shows us what sin is.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Romans 3:21">21</a> Now, God&#39;s goodness is shown without the law, and the law and prophets tell us about it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Romans 3:22">22</a> God&#39;s righteousness comes through faith in Jesus Christ to all who believe, there is no difference.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Romans 3:23">23</a> Everyone has sinned and falls short of God&#39;s glory.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Romans 3:24">24</a> We are made right with God for free by His kindness because of the rescue that comes through Jesus Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Romans 3:25">25</a> God presented him as a sacrifice to forgive sins through faith in his blood, to show his righteousness by forgiving past sins because of God&#39;s patience.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Romans 3:26">26</a> I am telling now about God&#39;s goodness: so He can be fair and make right those who trust in Jesus.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Romans 3:27">27</a> Where can we boast, then? It is shut out. Through what rule? The rule of actions? No, but through the rule of trust.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Romans 3:28">28</a> We believe that a person is made right with God through faith, not by following the law&#39;s actions.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Romans 3:29">29</a> Is he only the God of the Jews? Isn&#39;t he also the God of the non-Jews? Yes, he is also the God of the non-Jews.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Romans 3:30">30</a> Since there is only one God, he will make both the circumcised right by faith and the uncircumcised through faith.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Romans 3:31">31</a> By having faith, do we cancel the law? No, we make the law stand.</p>
