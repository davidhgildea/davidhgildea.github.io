<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Romans 9:1">1</a> I speak the truth in Christ, I am not lying, my own conscience confirms it in the Holy Spirit.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Romans 9:2">2</a> I have great sadness and never-ending sorrow in my heart.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Romans 9:3">3</a> I could wish to be cut off from Christ for the sake of my brothers, my own family by blood.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Romans 9:4">4</a> They are Israelites, and to them belong the adoption as God&#39;s children, the glory, the agreements, the law, the worship of God, and the promises.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Romans 9:5">5</a> The fathers belong to them, and as a human, Christ came from them. He is above all, God forever praised. Amen.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Romans 9:6">6</a> It&#39;s not that God&#39;s message has failed. Not everyone from Israel truly belongs to Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Romans 9:7">7</a> Just because they are Abraham&#39;s descendants doesn&#39;t mean they are all his true children. Instead, &quot;Your true descendants will be through Isaac.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Romans 9:8">8</a> Those born from human desire are not God&#39;s children; only those born because of God&#39;s promise are considered true descendants.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Romans 9:9">9</a> This is the promised message: I will come at this time, and Sarah will have a son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Romans 9:10">10</a> Not only that, but when Rebecca became pregnant by our father Isaac;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Romans 9:11">11</a> Before the children were born and had done anything good or bad, God&#39;s plan was based on his choice, not their actions, but on his call.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Romans 9:12">12</a> Someone told her, &quot;The older will serve the younger.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Romans 9:13">13</a> It is written that I loved Jacob but did not love Esau.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Romans 9:14">14</a> What can we say? Is God unjust? No way.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Romans 9:15">15</a> God told Moses, &quot;I will show mercy to those I choose, and I will show compassion to those I choose.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Romans 9:16">16</a> It is not about one&#39;s own desire or effort, but about God who gives mercy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Romans 9:17">17</a> The Bible says to Pharaoh, I made you a leader for this reason: to show my power through you, so that my name would be known everywhere on earth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Romans 9:18">18</a> So God shows mercy to whom he chooses, and he makes stubborn those he decides.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Romans 9:19">19</a> You might then ask me, why does he still blame us? Who has been able to resist his will?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Romans 9:20">20</a> No, but human, who are you to argue with God? Can the creation ask the creator, Why did you make me like this?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Romans 9:21">21</a> Doesn&#39;t the potter have the right to make from the same lump of clay one pot for special use and another for common use?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Romans 9:22">22</a> What if God wanted to show his anger and power but was very patient with those who deserved punishment?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Romans 9:23">23</a> God wanted to show the wealth of his glory to those he had chosen for mercy, whom he had prepared for glory.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Romans 9:24">24</a> Even us, whom God has called, not only from the Jews but also from the non-Jews?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Romans 9:25">25</a> In Osee, God also says, &quot;I will name those &#39;my people&#39; who were not my people; and those &#39;loved one&#39; who were not loved.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Romans 9:26">26</a> It will happen that in the place where it was told to them, &quot;You are not my people,&quot; there they will be called &quot;children of the living God.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Romans 9:27">27</a> Isaiah also cries out about Israel, saying, even if the people of Israel are as many as the sand by the sea, only a small part will be saved.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Romans 9:28">28</a> God will complete the work and quickly do it with fairness; for God will carry out a brief task on the earth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Romans 9:29">29</a> As Isaiah said earlier, if God had not left us some descendants, we would have become like Sodom and Gomorrah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Romans 9:30">30</a> What can we say then? The non-Jews, who did not pursue being right with God, have achieved it through faith.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Romans 9:31">31</a> Israel, which chased the law of right living, did not reach the law of right living.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Romans 9:32">32</a> Why? Because they did not seek it with faith, but as if it could be earned by following the law. They tripped over that stone that makes people stumble.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Romans 9:33">33</a> It is written, &quot;Look, I put in Zion a stone that makes people fall, and a rock that makes them trip: but anyone who trusts in him will not feel shame.&quot;</p>
