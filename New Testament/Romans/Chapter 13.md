<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Romans 13:1">1</a> Everyone should obey those in authority, because all authority comes from God, and those who govern have been placed there by God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Romans 13:2">2</a> Anyone who fights against authority is fighting against what God has established, and those who resist will bring punishment on themselves.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Romans 13:3">3</a> Leaders are not to scare people doing good, but those doing bad. So if you don&#39;t want to be afraid of authority, do good things, and you will get their approval.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Romans 13:4">4</a> He serves God to do good to you. But if you do evil, be scared; he doesn&#39;t carry the sword for no reason: he serves God to punish those who do evil.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Romans 13:5">5</a> You must obey, not only to avoid punishment, but also to have a clear conscience.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Romans 13:6">6</a> You should pay taxes too because tax collectors are serving God by doing this important work.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Romans 13:7">7</a> Give everyone what you owe them: pay taxes to those you owe taxes, follow customs for those who deserve it, show respect to those who should be respected, and honor those who should be honored.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Romans 13:8">8</a> Don&#39;t owe anyone anything, except to love each other, because the one who loves another has done what the law requires.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Romans 13:9">9</a> Do not cheat in marriage, do not kill, do not steal, do not lie, do not desire what others have; all other rules are included in this one: Love others as much as you love yourself.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Romans 13:10">10</a> Love does no harm to others, so love fulfills the law.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Romans 13:11">11</a> Understand the time, that it is now important to wake up from sleep: for our rescue is closer than when we first believed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Romans 13:12">12</a> The night is almost over, the day is coming: so let&#39;s stop doing bad things, and start living in a good and right way.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Romans 13:13">13</a> Let&#39;s live well, as in daylight; not with wild parties and drinking, not with wild sex and unchecked desire, not with fighting and jealousy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Romans 13:14">14</a> Wear the ways of Jesus Christ, and don&#39;t make plans for self-indulgence to satisfy its desires.</p>
