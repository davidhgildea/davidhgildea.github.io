<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Romans 2:1">1</a> So, you can&#39;t be excused, person, whoever you are that judges others: because when you judge someone else, you are judging yourself, since you do the same things.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Romans 2:2">2</a> We are certain that God&#39;s judgment is true and fair against those who do such things.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Romans 2:3">3</a> Do you think, man, that if you judge others for doing certain things and then do them yourself, you will avoid God&#39;s judgment?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Romans 2:4">4</a> Do you look down on the wealth of his kindness, tolerance, and patience, not realizing that God&#39;s goodness is meant to lead you to change your ways?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Romans 2:5">5</a> By being stubborn and unwilling to change, you are storing up anger for yourself for the day when God will show his fair judgment.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Romans 2:6">6</a> God will give to each person according to what they have done.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Romans 2:7">7</a> To those who keep on doing good and seek glory, honor, and never-ending life, God will give eternal life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Romans 2:8">8</a> But for those who argue and don&#39;t follow the truth, but choose what&#39;s wrong, there will be anger and punishment.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Romans 2:9">9</a> Hard times and suffering will come to every person who does bad things, starting with the Jews and also to non-Jews.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Romans 2:10">10</a> But glory, honor, and peace to everyone who does good, first to the Jew and also to the non-Jew.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Romans 2:11">11</a> God does not show favoritism.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Romans 2:12">12</a> Everyone who sins without knowing God&#39;s law will still die, and those who sin knowing God&#39;s law will be judged by it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Romans 2:13">13</a> It&#39;s not just those who hear the law who are right with God, but those who follow the law will be considered right.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Romans 2:14">14</a> For when people who do not have God&#39;s law naturally follow what it says, even without the law, they are their own guide.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Romans 2:15">15</a> They show that God&#39;s law is written in their hearts, their own conscience agrees, and their thoughts either accuse them or tell them they are right.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Romans 2:16">16</a> On the day God judges people&#39;s secrets through Jesus Christ, following my good news.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Romans 2:17">17</a> Look, you are known as a Jew, rely on the law, and take pride in God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Romans 2:18">18</a> You know God&#39;s will, and you choose what is best, because you are taught by the law.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Romans 2:19">19</a> You are sure that you can guide the blind and be a light for those in the dark.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Romans 2:20">20</a> A teacher of the simple, a trainer of the young, who has the outline of knowledge and truth in the law.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Romans 2:21">21</a> So you, who teach others, do you not teach yourself? You who say people should not steal, do you steal?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Romans 2:22">22</a> You who say a person should not cheat in marriage, do you cheat? You who hate idols, do you disrespect holy things?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Romans 2:23">23</a> You who brag about the law, do you dishonor God by breaking the law?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Romans 2:24">24</a> Because of you, people who are not Jews speak badly about God, just like it is written.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Romans 2:25">25</a> Being circumcised is good if you follow the law, but if you break the law, your circumcision becomes worthless.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Romans 2:26">26</a> So if a person who is not circumcised follows the law&#39;s rules, won&#39;t that be just like being circumcised?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Romans 2:27">27</a> Won&#39;t the uncircumcised person by birth, if they follow the law, be able to judge you, who even with the written law and circumcision, break the law?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Romans 2:28">28</a> He is not a true Jew if he is only one outwardly, nor is true circumcision something external and physical.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Romans 2:29">29</a> A true Jew is one inside; and real circumcision is in the heart, by the spirit, not by written rules. Their praise comes not from people, but from God.</p>
