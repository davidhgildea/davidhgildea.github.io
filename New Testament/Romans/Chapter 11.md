<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Romans 11:1">1</a> So I ask, has God rejected his people? No way! For I am also an Israelite, a descendant of Abraham, from the tribe of Benjamin.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Romans 11:2">2</a> God has not rejected his people whom he knew before. Don&#39;t you know what the scripture says about Elijah? How he pleads with God against Israel, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Romans 11:3">3</a> God, they killed your prophets and destroyed your altars; I&#39;m alone and they want to kill me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Romans 11:4">4</a> What does God&#39;s reply to him say? I have kept seven thousand men for myself who have not kneeled to Baal&#39;s statue.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Romans 11:5">5</a> Even now, there are a few chosen by God&#39;s kindness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Romans 11:6">6</a> If it&#39;s by grace, then it&#39;s not by works; if it were, grace wouldn&#39;t be grace. But if it&#39;s by works, then it&#39;s not grace; otherwise, work wouldn&#39;t be work.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Romans 11:7">7</a> So what now? Israel did not get what it was looking for; only the chosen ones got it, and the others were unable to see.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Romans 11:8">8</a> As it is written, God has given them a sleepy spirit, eyes that cannot see, and ears that cannot hear, until this day.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Romans 11:9">9</a> David says, &quot;Let their table become a trap, a snare, a stumbling block, and a way to repay them.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Romans 11:10">10</a> Make their eyes dim so they cannot see and make them always stoop.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Romans 11:11">11</a> I ask then, did they trip so they would fall? Certainly not! Instead, by their mistake, salvation has come to the non-Jews, to make them jealous.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Romans 11:12">12</a> If their loss brings wealth to the world, and their decrease brings wealth to non-Jewish people, how much greater will their fullness be?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Romans 11:13">13</a> I talk to you non-Jews because I am the messenger to non-Jews, and I honor my work.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Romans 11:14">14</a> I hope to encourage my fellow people and save some of them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Romans 11:15">15</a> If rejecting them means the world is made right with God, what will accepting them be, if not life from the dead?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Romans 11:16">16</a> If the first piece of dough is holy, then the whole dough is holy; and if the root is holy, so are the branches.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Romans 11:17">17</a> If some of the branches were broken off and you, a wild olive tree, were grafted among them, sharing the root and richness of the olive tree.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Romans 11:18">18</a> Don&#39;t brag against the branches. But if you do brag, remember it&#39;s not you who supports the root, but the root supports you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Romans 11:19">19</a> You might say, &quot;The branches were broken off so I could be grafted in.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Romans 11:20">20</a> Because they didn&#39;t believe, they were removed, and you are supported by faith. Don&#39;t be proud, but be respectful.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Romans 11:21">21</a> If God did not spare the original branches, be careful or he might not spare you either.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Romans 11:22">22</a> See how kind and strict God is: He is strict to those who fell, but kind to you if you stay in His kindness. If not, you will be cut off too.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Romans 11:23">23</a> If they do not continue in their unbelief, they will be grafted in again because God can graft them in.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Romans 11:24">24</a> If you were cut from a wild olive tree and grafted, against nature, into a good olive tree, how much more will the natural branches be grafted into their own olive tree?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Romans 11:25">25</a> I don&#39;t want you, friends, to be unaware of this secret: part of Israel has been unable to see, and this will continue until all non-Jewish people have joined.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Romans 11:26">26</a> All Israel will be saved, just as it is written, &quot;The Savior will come from Zion and remove sin from Jacob.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Romans 11:27">27</a> This is my promise to them: when I will forgive their sins.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Romans 11:28">28</a> Regarding the good news, they are against you, but concerning God&#39;s choice, they are loved because of the ancestors.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Romans 11:29">29</a> God&#39;s gifts and calling cannot be taken back.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Romans 11:30">30</a> Before, you did not believe in God, but now you have received mercy because of their disbelief.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Romans 11:31">31</a> In the same way, they currently do not believe, so that by your kindness, they may also receive kindness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Romans 11:32">32</a> God has included everyone in disbelief so that he could be merciful to everyone.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Romans 11:33">33</a> Oh, how deep are God&#39;s riches, wisdom, and knowledge! How impossible it is to understand his decisions and ways!</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Romans 11:34">34</a> Who knows what God is thinking? Or who has ever given God advice?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Romans 11:35">35</a> Who has given something to God first so that God has to pay it back?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Romans 11:36">36</a> Everything comes from God, exists by His power, and is intended for His glory. May God be praised forever. Amen.</p>
