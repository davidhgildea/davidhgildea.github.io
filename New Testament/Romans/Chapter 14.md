<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Romans 14:1">1</a> Welcome those who are weak in faith, but don&#39;t argue over doubtful issues.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Romans 14:2">2</a> One person believes they can eat everything, but another, who is weak, eats only vegetables.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Romans 14:3">3</a> If someone eats, they shouldn&#39;t look down on someone who doesn&#39;t, and if someone doesn&#39;t eat, they shouldn&#39;t judge someone who does; because God has accepted them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Romans 14:4">4</a> Who are you to judge someone else&#39;s servant? They answer to their own master, and will either stand or fall. Yes, they will be supported, because God can make them stand.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Romans 14:5">5</a> One person values one day more than another; another values all days the same. Each person should be fully convinced in their own mind.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Romans 14:6">6</a> Someone who respects the day does so for God; and the one who does not respect the day also does it for God. The one who eats, eats for God and thanks God; and the one who does not eat, abstains for God and thanks God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Romans 14:7">7</a> None of us lives for ourselves alone, and no one dies for themselves alone.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Romans 14:8">8</a> Whether we live, we live for God; and whether we die, we die for God. So, whether we live or die, we belong to God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Romans 14:9">9</a> Christ died, came back to life, and revived so he could be the leader of both the dead and the living.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Romans 14:10">10</a> Why do you judge your brother? Or why do you think little of your brother? We will all stand before Christ&#39;s judgment seat.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Romans 14:11">11</a> It is written, &quot;I am alive,&quot; says God, &quot;and everyone will kneel to me and every tongue will admit to God.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Romans 14:12">12</a> So every one of us will explain our actions to God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Romans 14:13">13</a> Let&#39;s not judge each other anymore; instead, decide that no one should make their brother stumble or fall.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Romans 14:14">14</a> I understand and am convinced by Jesus that nothing is unclean by itself; but if someone considers something unclean, then for that person, it is unclean.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Romans 14:15">15</a> If the food you eat upsets your brother, you&#39;re not acting with love. Don&#39;t cause harm to him with your food, the one for whom Christ died.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Romans 14:16">16</a> Do not let anyone speak badly of your good actions.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Romans 14:17">17</a> God&#39;s kingdom is not about food and drink; it&#39;s about doing right, peace, and happiness in the Holy Spirit.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Romans 14:18">18</a> Whoever serves Christ in these things is pleasing to God and approved by people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Romans 14:19">19</a> Let&#39;s try to do things that create peace and help build each other up.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Romans 14:20">20</a> Do not ruin God&#39;s work for the sake of food. Everything is clean, but it is wrong for a person to eat anything that causes someone else to stumble.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Romans 14:21">21</a> It is not good to eat meat, drink wine, or do anything that makes your brother stumble, get upset, or become weak.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Romans 14:22">22</a> Do you have faith? Keep it between you and God. The person who does not feel guilty for what they approve of is happy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Romans 14:23">23</a> If someone eats but has doubts, they are wrong because their eating is not based on belief; anything not based on belief is wrong.</p>
