<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Romans 15:1">1</a> We who are strong should help those who are weak and not just make ourselves happy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Romans 15:2">2</a> Everyone should try to please their neighbor for their neighbor&#39;s good, to help them grow.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Romans 15:3">3</a> Even Christ did not please himself; instead, like it is written, the insults of those who insulted you fell on me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Romans 15:4">4</a> Whatever was written long ago was meant to teach us, so that through endurance and the encouragement from the scriptures, we might have hope.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Romans 15:5">5</a> May the God who gives patience and comfort help you to agree with each other as Christ Jesus taught.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Romans 15:6">6</a> So you can together with one mind and voice praise God, the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Romans 15:7">7</a> Welcome each other, just as Christ welcomed us to honor God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Romans 15:8">8</a> I say that Jesus Christ served the Jewish people to show God&#39;s truth and to fulfill the promises given to our ancestors.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Romans 15:9">9</a> So that non-Jews may praise God for his kindness, as it is written: &quot;Because of this, I will praise you among the non-Jews and sing about your name.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Romans 15:10">10</a> He says again, &quot;Be happy, you non-Jews, together with his people.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Romans 15:11">11</a> Praise God, all nations; and honor him, all peoples.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Romans 15:12">12</a> Esaias says again, a descendant of Jesse will come, and he will rule over the nations; the nations will put their trust in him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Romans 15:13">13</a> May the God of hope give you complete joy and peace as you trust in Him, so that you may overflow with hope by the power of the Holy Spirit.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Romans 15:14">14</a> I am confident, my friends, that you are good and have all the knowledge you need to advise each other.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Romans 15:15">15</a> Friends, I have written to you with confidence in some parts, to remind you, because of the grace God has given me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Romans 15:16">16</a> I am called to serve Jesus Christ for the non-Jewish people, sharing God&#39;s good news so that their dedication to God is welcomed, made holy by the Holy Spirit.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Romans 15:17">17</a> I can proudly talk about what I have done for God through Jesus Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Romans 15:18">18</a> I won&#39;t talk about what Christ hasn&#39;t done through me to bring the non-Jews to obey God with words and actions.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Romans 15:19">19</a> With powerful miracles and signs, by God&#39;s Spirit, I completely shared the good news of Christ from Jerusalem all the way around to Illyricum.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Romans 15:20">20</a> Yes, I have worked hard to share the good news where Christ was not known, so I wouldn&#39;t just add to what someone else had started.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Romans 15:21">21</a> But as it is written, those who did not know about him will see, and those who have not heard will understand.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Romans 15:22">22</a> This is why I have been prevented many times from visiting you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Romans 15:23">23</a> But now, having no more room here and having strongly wished for many years to visit you;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Romans 15:24">24</a> Whenever I travel to Spain, I will visit you because I hope to see you on my trip, and you can help me on my way there, if I can enjoy your company for a while first.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Romans 15:25">25</a> Now I am going to Jerusalem to help God&#39;s people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Romans 15:26">26</a> People from Macedonia and Achaia were happy to give some money to help the poor believers in Jerusalem.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Romans 15:27">27</a> They are truly happy to do it, and they owe it to them. Because if the non-Jews have shared in their spiritual blessings, then it is only right for them to help the Jews with material blessings.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Romans 15:28">28</a> So when I have finished this task and made sure they have received this contribution, I will visit you on my way to Spain.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Romans 15:29">29</a> I am confident that when I visit you, I will bring the full blessing of Christ&#39;s good news.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Romans 15:30">30</a> I ask you, friends, for the sake of Jesus Christ and the love of the Spirit, to join me in praying to God for me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Romans 15:31">31</a> I hope to be saved from the unbelievers in Judaea and that the help I bring to Jerusalem will be welcomed by God&#39;s people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Romans 15:32">32</a> I hope to come to you with joy, as God wants, and to be refreshed with you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Romans 15:33">33</a> May the God of peace be with everyone. Amen.</p>
