<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Romans 12:1">1</a> I ask you, friends, because of God&#39;s kindness, to offer your bodies as a living and holy sacrifice that pleases God, which is a sensible way to serve Him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Romans 12:2">2</a> Do not follow this world&#39;s ways: instead, change by renewing your mind to know God&#39;s good, pleasing, and perfect will.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Romans 12:3">3</a> I tell you, with the kindness given to me, to not think too highly of yourself, but to think sensibly, as God has given each of us a certain amount of faith.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Romans 12:4">4</a> Just as we have many parts in one body, and not all parts have the same function:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Romans 12:5">5</a> We, though many, are one body in Christ, and each one is a part of each other.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Romans 12:6">6</a> Since we have different gifts based on the grace given to us, if it is to prophesy, let us do so in proportion to our faith.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Romans 12:7">7</a> If we serve others, let&#39;s focus on our service; if we teach, let&#39;s focus on our teaching.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Romans 12:8">8</a> If you encourage others, do it earnestly. If you give, do it generously. If you lead, do it diligently. If you show kindness, do it happily.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Romans 12:9">9</a> Love sincerely. Hate what is evil; hold on to what is good.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Romans 12:10">10</a> Show genuine kindness and love to each other like brothers and sisters; give each other more honor.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Romans 12:11">11</a> Not lazy in work; passionate in spirit; serving God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Romans 12:12">12</a> Happy in hope; enduring in trouble; always praying.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Romans 12:13">13</a> Sharing with those in need; welcoming to others.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Romans 12:14">14</a> Be kind to those who are unkind to you; do not wish them harm.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Romans 12:15">15</a> Be happy with those who are happy, and cry with those who are crying.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Romans 12:16">16</a> Have the same attitude towards each other. Don&#39;t aim for high status, but be willing to associate with people of lower position. Don&#39;t think you know everything.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Romans 12:17">17</a> Don&#39;t pay back anyone with harm for harm. Do what is right in everyone&#39;s eyes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Romans 12:18">18</a> If you can, try to live in peace with everyone.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Romans 12:19">19</a> Dear friends, do not take revenge, but leave room for anger; for it is written, &quot;It is my right to avenge; I will pay back,&quot; says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Romans 12:20">20</a> If your enemy is hungry, give them food; if they are thirsty, give them water; because by doing this, you will make them feel ashamed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Romans 12:21">21</a> Do not let evil defeat you, but defeat evil by doing good.</p>
