<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Colossians 4:1">1</a> Employers, treat your workers fairly and equally, because you know you also have a Master in heaven.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Colossians 4:2">2</a> Keep praying, and stay alert when you do, with gratitude.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Colossians 4:3">3</a> Please pray for us too, that God will give us a chance to share Christ&#39;s secret message, for which I am in chains.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Colossians 4:4">4</a> So I can clearly explain it, as I should.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Colossians 4:5">5</a> Live wisely around those who are not believers, using your time well.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Colossians 4:6">6</a> Always speak kindly and thoughtfully, so you know how to reply to anyone.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Colossians 4:7">7</a> Tychicus will tell you all about how I&#39;m doing. He is a dear brother, a reliable helper, and someone who serves God with us.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Colossians 4:8">8</a> I sent him to you for this reason, so he can learn about your situation and give you comfort.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Colossians 4:9">9</a> Onesimus, a loyal and dear brother, who is one of you, will tell you everything that&#39;s happening here.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Colossians 4:10">10</a> Aristarchus, who is in prison with me, sends greetings to you, and so does Mark, Barnabas&#39;s cousin. You&#39;ve been told about him; if he comes to you, welcome him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Colossians 4:11">11</a> Jesus, called Justus, and others who are circumcised are the only ones working with me for God&#39;s kingdom and have comforted me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Colossians 4:12">12</a> Epaphras, who is one of you and serves Christ, greets you. He always works hard in prayer for you, so you can be fully mature and do all that God wants.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Colossians 4:13">13</a> I can tell you, he is very eager to help you and also those in Laodicea and Hierapolis.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Colossians 4:14">14</a> Luke, our dear doctor, and Demas, say hello to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Colossians 4:15">15</a> Greet the brothers and sisters in Laodicea, Nymphas, and the church that meets at his home.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Colossians 4:16">16</a> When you read this letter, make sure it is also read in the Laodicean church, and that you read the letter from Laodicea too.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Colossians 4:17">17</a> Tell Archippus: Pay attention to the service you were given by God and make sure you complete it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Colossians 4:18">18</a> I, Paul, am writing this greeting with my own hand. Remember that I am in chains. May God&#39;s kindness be with you. Amen.</p>
