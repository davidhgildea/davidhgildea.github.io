<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Colossians 3:1">1</a> If you have been raised with Christ, look for the things that are above, where Christ sits at God&#39;s right side.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Colossians 3:2">2</a> Focus on things above, not on earthly things.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Colossians 3:3">3</a> You are dead, and your life is hidden with Christ in God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Colossians 3:4">4</a> When Christ, who is our life, comes, you will also be with him in glory.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Colossians 3:5">5</a> So, make dead the parts of you that are earthly: sexual sin, impurity, strong lust, evil desires, and greed, which is like worshiping idols.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Colossians 3:6">6</a> Because of these things, God&#39;s anger comes upon the disobedient people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Colossians 3:7">7</a> You once lived this way when you followed these practices.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Colossians 3:8">8</a> Now you should also stop all these things: anger, rage, hate, insulting others, and dirty talk from your mouth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Colossians 3:9">9</a> Do not lie to each other, because you have taken off your old self and its behaviors.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Colossians 3:10">10</a> And have put on the new self, which is renewed in knowledge to be like God who created him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Colossians 3:11">11</a> There is no Greek or Jew, circumcised or not, barbarian, Scythian, slave or free; but Christ is everything and in everyone.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Colossians 3:12">12</a> Therefore, as God&#39;s chosen ones, holy and loved, put on a heart of compassion, kindness, humility, gentleness, and patience.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Colossians 3:13">13</a> Be patient with each other, and forgive each other. If someone has a complaint against anyone, forgive them just as Christ forgave you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Colossians 3:14">14</a> Above everything, wear love, which binds everything together in perfect unity.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Colossians 3:15">15</a> Let God&#39;s peace lead in your hearts, which you are called to as one group; and be thankful.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Colossians 3:16">16</a> Let Christ&#39;s message live in you fully, with all wisdom, and teach and warn each other with psalms, hymns, and spiritual songs, singing thankfully in your hearts to God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Colossians 3:17">17</a> In everything you say or do, do it all for Jesus, thanking God through him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Colossians 3:18">18</a> Wives, obey your husbands, as it is right with God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Colossians 3:19">19</a> Husbands, love your wives and don&#39;t be harsh with them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Colossians 3:20">20</a> Kids, listen to your parents in everything, because this makes God happy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Colossians 3:21">21</a> Parents, do not make your children angry, so they do not get discouraged.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Colossians 3:22">22</a> Workers, obey your earthly bosses in everything; not just when they&#39;re watching, like people who just want to please others, but with a true heart and respect for God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Colossians 3:23">23</a> Do everything with all your heart, as if you&#39;re doing it for God, not for people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Colossians 3:24">24</a> Understand that from God you will get the reward of what is kept for you in heaven, because you serve Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Colossians 3:25">25</a> Whoever does wrong will be paid back for their wrong, and everyone is treated the same.</p>
