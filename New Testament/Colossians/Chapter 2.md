<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Colossians 2:1">1</a> I want you to know about the big struggle I have for you, for those in Laodicea, and for all who have not met me in person.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Colossians 2:2">2</a> So that their hearts may be encouraged and united in love, leading to a complete confidence and understanding of God&#39;s secret plan, which is Christ himself.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Colossians 2:3">3</a> In this one, all the treasures of wisdom and knowledge are hidden.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Colossians 2:4">4</a> I say this so no one will trick you with persuasive words.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Colossians 2:5">5</a> Even though I am not there in person, I am with you in spirit, happy to see your discipline and firm faith in Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Colossians 2:6">6</a> Since you accepted Christ Jesus as Lord, continue to live in Him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Colossians 2:7">7</a> Grown deep in him and built up, strong in your faith as you were taught, filled with thankfulness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Colossians 2:8">8</a> Be careful that no one misleads you with philosophy and empty lies, following human traditions and worldly principles instead of Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Colossians 2:9">9</a> In him, the complete nature of God lives in a human form.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Colossians 2:10">10</a> You are made whole in him, who is the leader of all rulers and authorities.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Colossians 2:11">11</a> In Christ, you also were spiritually circumcised, not by a physical procedure but by stripping away the sinful nature&#39;s control over the body.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Colossians 2:12">12</a> In baptism, you were buried with him and also raised with him because you believe in God, who brought him back from the dead.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Colossians 2:13">13</a> You were dead because of your sins and because your flesh was not circumcised, but God made you alive with Christ and forgave all your sins.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Colossians 2:14">14</a> Erasing the written laws that were against us, which opposed us, and removed them, fixing them to his cross;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Colossians 2:15">15</a> He disarmed the rulers and authorities, displayed them publicly, and won a victory over them through it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Colossians 2:16">16</a> Don&#39;t let anyone criticize you about what you eat or drink, or about celebrating religious festivals, observing the new moon, or keeping the Sabbath.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Colossians 2:17">17</a> These are a preview of what will happen; but the real thing belongs to Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Colossians 2:18">18</a> Do not let anyone cheat you of your prize by pretending to be humble and worshipping angels, getting involved with things they have never seen, foolishly inflated by their human thinking.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Colossians 2:19">19</a> Not staying connected to the Head, from whom the whole body, supported and held together by its ligaments and sinews, grows as God causes it to grow.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Colossians 2:20">20</a> So if you have died with Christ and are free from the world&#39;s basic rules, why do you still follow its rules as if you belonged to the world?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Colossians 2:21">21</a> Do not touch; do not taste; do not handle;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Colossians 2:22">22</a> These are all destined to be used up and discarded, according to human rules and teachings.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Colossians 2:23">23</a> These things may seem wise because they require strong devotion, humility, and harsh treatment of the body, but they provide no help in controlling selfish desires.</p>
