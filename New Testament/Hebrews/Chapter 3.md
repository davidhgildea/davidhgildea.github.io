<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Hebrews 3:1">1</a> Therefore, holy brothers and sisters, who share in the call from heaven, think about Jesus, the messenger and high priest we profess.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Hebrews 3:2">2</a> He was loyal to God who chose him, just like Moses was loyal in every part of God&#39;s house.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Hebrews 3:3">3</a> This man was considered more glorious than Moses, because the one who builds the house is more honored than the house itself.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Hebrews 3:4">4</a> Every house is built by someone, but God is the one who built everything.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Hebrews 3:5">5</a> Moses was truly loyal in all God&#39;s house as a servant, to witness what would be said later.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Hebrews 3:6">6</a> But Christ is like a son in charge of his own home, and we are that home if we keep our courage and the joy of our hope strong until the end.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Hebrews 3:7">7</a> Therefore, (as God says, today if you listen to his voice,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Hebrews 3:8">8</a> Do not make your hearts hard as you did when you tested me in the desert.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Hebrews 3:9">9</a> When your ancestors tested me, they saw what I could do for forty years.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Hebrews 3:10">10</a> So I was upset with those people and said, &quot;They always go wrong in their hearts; they have never understood my ways.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Hebrews 3:11">11</a> I swore in my anger, they will not enter my place of rest.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Hebrews 3:12">12</a> Be careful, friends, so none of you have an evil, unbelieving heart that turns away from the living God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Hebrews 3:13">13</a> Encourage each other every day, while it is still called &quot;Today,&quot; so that none of you become stubborn because of sin&#39;s trickery.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Hebrews 3:14">14</a> We share in Christ if we keep our first confidence firm to the end.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Hebrews 3:15">15</a> Today, if you will listen to his voice, do not harden your hearts as you did during the rebellion.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Hebrews 3:16">16</a> Some who heard God&#39;s message made him angry, but not everyone who left Egypt with Moses did so.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Hebrews 3:17">17</a> But who made God upset for 40 years? Was it not those who sinned, whose bodies dropped dead in the desert?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Hebrews 3:18">18</a> And who did God promise would not enter his place of rest? It was those who did not believe.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Hebrews 3:19">19</a> So we understand they couldn&#39;t go in because they did not believe.</p>
