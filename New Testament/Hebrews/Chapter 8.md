<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Hebrews 8:1">1</a> Here&#39;s the main point of what we&#39;ve said: We have a high priest who sits at the right side of the throne of God in heaven.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Hebrews 8:2">2</a> A servant of the holy place and the real tent that God set up, not humans.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Hebrews 8:3">3</a> Every high priest is chosen to give gifts and sacrifices, so this man must have something to offer too.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Hebrews 8:4">4</a> If he were on earth, he wouldn&#39;t be a priest because there are already priests who give gifts following the law.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Hebrews 8:5">5</a> Those who serve are working with a copy and shadow of the things in heaven, just as Moses was warned by God when he was about to build the tent: God told him, &quot;Make sure you make everything according to the design shown to you on the mountain.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Hebrews 8:6">6</a> He now has a greater service because he is the mediator of a better agreement, which is based on better promises.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Hebrews 8:7">7</a> If the first agreement had been perfect, there would be no need to look for a second one.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Hebrews 8:8">8</a> Because he found faults with them, God says, &quot;Look, the time is coming when I will make a new agreement with the people of Israel and the people of Judah.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Hebrews 8:9">9</a> This is not like the agreement I made with their ancestors when I took their hand to guide them out of Egypt; they did not keep my agreement, so I ignored them, says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Hebrews 8:10">10</a> This is the agreement that I will set up with the people of Israel after that time, says God; I will place my rules in their minds and write them on their hearts. I will be their God, and they will be my people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Hebrews 8:11">11</a> They won&#39;t need to teach their neighbors or their brothers, saying, &#39;Know God,&#39; because everyone, from the least to the greatest, will know me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Hebrews 8:12">12</a> I will be kind to their wrongs, and I will not remember their sins and bad acts anymore.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Hebrews 8:13">13</a> By saying &quot;a new agreement,&quot; he has made the first one old. And what is getting old and wearing out will soon disappear.</p>
