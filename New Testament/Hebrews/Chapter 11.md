<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Hebrews 11:1">1</a> Faith is being sure of what we hope for and certain of what we do not see.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Hebrews 11:2">2</a> By it, the older people earned praise.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Hebrews 11:3">3</a> By believing, we know that God made the worlds with His command, so that what we see was not made from what we can see.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Hebrews 11:4">4</a> By trusting, Abel gave God a better offering than Cain, and it showed he was good, with God confirming his gifts; even after his death, he still speaks through it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Hebrews 11:5">5</a> Through faith, Enoch was taken up so he wouldn&#39;t experience death; and he was not found, because God had taken him up. Before he was taken up, it was known that he pleased God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Hebrews 11:6">6</a> Without faith, you cannot please God: whoever approaches God must believe that God exists and that he rewards those who earnestly search for him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Hebrews 11:7">7</a> Trusting in God, Noah, who was warned about things still unseen, built an ark out of fear to save his family; by doing this, he showed the world was wrong and became the rightful owner of the goodness that comes from faith.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Hebrews 11:8">8</a> Abraham trusted God, and when he was told to go to a land he would later get as his own, he obeyed and left, even though he didn&#39;t know where he was going.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Hebrews 11:9">9</a> Through trust, he lived as a foreigner in the promised land, staying in tents with Isaac and Jacob, who were also promised the same inheritance.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Hebrews 11:10">10</a> He was looking for a city with solid foundations, built by God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Hebrews 11:11">11</a> Sarah&#39;s faith gave her the power to have a child, even when she was too old, because she believed God who promised it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Hebrews 11:12">12</a> From just one man, almost dead, came as many descendants as there are stars in the sky or grains of sand by the sea—too many to count.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Hebrews 11:13">13</a> All these people died still believing what God had promised them. They did not receive what was promised, but they saw it all from a distance and welcomed it. They agreed that they were just visitors and travelers on earth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Hebrews 11:14">14</a> People who say these things make it clear that they are looking for a homeland.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Hebrews 11:15">15</a> Truly, if they had thought of the country they left, they could have found a chance to go back.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Hebrews 11:16">16</a> But now they want a better place, that is, a heavenly one. So, God is not ashamed to be called their God because he has made a city for them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Hebrews 11:17">17</a> Abraham, because of his faith, when tested, was willing to sacrifice Isaac. And this man, who was given God&#39;s promises, was ready to sacrifice his only son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Hebrews 11:18">18</a> God said your descendants will be named through Isaac.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Hebrews 11:19">19</a> Believing that God could bring him back to life, even from death; which is also how he figuratively got him back.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Hebrews 11:20">20</a> With trust, Isaac gave blessings to Jacob and Esau for their future.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Hebrews 11:21">21</a> In faith, when Jacob was near death, he gave a blessing to both of Joseph&#39;s sons and worshiped God, resting on his staff.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Hebrews 11:22">22</a> Joseph, trusting in God, talked about the Israelites leaving Egypt before he died, and gave instructions about what should be done with his bones.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Hebrews 11:23">23</a> Moses&#39; parents hid him for three months after he was born because they saw he was a special child and they were not scared of the king&#39;s order.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Hebrews 11:24">24</a> By faith, when Moses grew up, he chose not to be called Pharaoh&#39;s daughter&#39;s son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Hebrews 11:25">25</a> Choosing to endure hardship with God&#39;s people instead of enjoying short-lived sinful pleasures.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Hebrews 11:26">26</a> Valuing the shame of following Christ as greater wealth than the treasures of Egypt, because he looked forward to the reward.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Hebrews 11:27">27</a> Through trust, he left Egypt, not scared of the king&#39;s anger; because he stayed strong, as if seeing God who can&#39;t be seen.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Hebrews 11:28">28</a> By believing, he held the passover and put blood on the doorposts so the destroyer of the first children would not touch them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Hebrews 11:29">29</a> Through faith, they crossed the Red Sea on dry ground, but when the Egyptians tried to do the same, they drowned.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Hebrews 11:30">30</a> By believing, the walls of Jericho fell after people marched around them for seven days.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Hebrews 11:31">31</a> By trusting, Rahab the prostitute did not die with those who did not believe, when she welcomed the spies peacefully.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Hebrews 11:32">32</a> What more can I say? I don&#39;t have enough time to talk about Gideon, Barak, Samson, Jephthah, David, Samuel, and the prophets.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Hebrews 11:33">33</a> People with faith overcame kingdoms, did what is right, received what was promised, and shut lions&#39; mouths.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Hebrews 11:34">34</a> Put out fires, avoided being killed by swords, became strong from being weak, grew brave in battle, and made foreign armies run away.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Hebrews 11:35">35</a> Women got back their dead, raised to life again. Others were tortured and did not accept rescue, so they could have a better resurrection.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Hebrews 11:36">36</a> Others faced mean teasing and whippings, and were also put in chains and jail.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Hebrews 11:37">37</a> They were hit with stones, cut into pieces, tested, and killed with swords. They moved around in sheep and goat skins, poor, suffering, and in pain.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Hebrews 11:38">38</a> The world did not deserve them: they roamed through deserts, in mountains, and in holes and caves of the ground.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="Hebrews 11:39">39</a> Even though they were praised for their faith, they did not receive what God promised.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="Hebrews 11:40">40</a> God has prepared something better for us, so that they would not reach perfection without us.</p>
