<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Hebrews 6:1">1</a> So, moving beyond the basic teachings about Christ, let&#39;s grow towards complete understanding, without going over the beginning lessons about turning away from useless actions and believing in God again.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Hebrews 6:2">2</a> About the teaching of baptisms, the act of placing hands, the rising of the dead, and forever judgment.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Hebrews 6:3">3</a> We will do this if God allows it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Hebrews 6:4">4</a> It&#39;s impossible for those who once understood, enjoyed the gift from heaven, and shared in the Holy Spirit.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Hebrews 6:5">5</a> And have experienced the good message of God and the powers of the future world.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Hebrews 6:6">6</a> If they turn away again after knowing the truth, it&#39;s impossible to bring them back to change their ways; because they are like they are crucifying God&#39;s Son all over again, and shaming him publicly.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Hebrews 6:7">7</a> The land that soaks up the frequent rain and produces useful plants for the people who take care of it gets blessings from God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Hebrews 6:8">8</a> The plant that grows thorns and briers is useless, close to being cursed, and its fate is to be burned.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Hebrews 6:9">9</a> Dear friends, we are confident of better things in your case, things that go with being saved, even though we talk like this.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Hebrews 6:10">10</a> God is fair and won&#39;t forget the work and love you showed for His name by serving His holy people, and you still serve them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Hebrews 6:11">11</a> We want each of you to show the same hard work to be fully sure of your hope until the end.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Hebrews 6:12">12</a> Work hard and follow those who, with faith and patience, receive what God has promised.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Hebrews 6:13">13</a> When God promised Abraham, because there was no one greater to swear by, he swore by himself.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Hebrews 6:14">14</a> I will surely bless you and give you many descendants.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Hebrews 6:15">15</a> After waiting patiently, he received the promise.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Hebrews 6:16">16</a> People truly make promises by someone greater, and an oath ends all arguments for them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Hebrews 6:17">17</a> God, wanting to show the heirs of the promise his unchanging plan more clearly, guaranteed it with an oath.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Hebrews 6:18">18</a> Through two unchangeable things, where it is impossible for God to lie, we can have strong comfort, as we have run to take hold of the hope offered to us.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Hebrews 6:19">19</a> We have hope as an anchor for the soul, strong and secure, that reaches behind the curtain.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Hebrews 6:20">20</a> Jesus, our forerunner, has entered there for us and become a high priest forever, in the same way as Melchizedek.</p>
