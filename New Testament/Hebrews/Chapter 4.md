<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Hebrews 4:1">1</a> Let us be careful then that none of you fail to reach the rest that God promised.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Hebrews 4:2">2</a> The good news was told to us just like it was to them. But the message they heard didn&#39;t help them, because they didn&#39;t trust when they listened.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Hebrews 4:3">3</a> We who believe enter into rest, just as God said, &quot;I swore in my anger they would not enter my rest,&quot; even though the works were done since the beginning of the world.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Hebrews 4:4">4</a> He talked about the seventh day like this: God rested on the seventh day from all his work.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Hebrews 4:5">5</a> And in this place again, if they will enter into my peace.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Hebrews 4:6">6</a> So, it&#39;s clear some people will still enter, but those who first heard the message did not because they did not believe.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Hebrews 4:7">7</a> He sets a specific day, calling it &quot;Today,&quot; as David did after a long time, saying, &quot;If you hear God&#39;s voice today, do not make your hearts stubborn.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Hebrews 4:8">8</a> If Jesus had given them rest, he would not have spoken about another day later on.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Hebrews 4:9">9</a> So there is still a rest for God&#39;s people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Hebrews 4:10">10</a> Whoever enters into rest also stops working just like God did from his own.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Hebrews 4:11">11</a> Let us work hard to enter that rest so that no one will fall by following the same path of disbelief.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Hebrews 4:12">12</a> God&#39;s message is alive, strong, and sharper than any double-edged sword. It cuts deep, even separating the soul and spirit, joints and marrow, and it can tell what the heart thinks and wants.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Hebrews 4:13">13</a> No creature is hidden from God; everything is completely exposed to his view.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Hebrews 4:14">14</a> Since we have a great priest who has gone into heaven, Jesus the Son of God, let&#39;s keep strongly to our faith.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Hebrews 4:15">15</a> We don&#39;t have a high priest who can&#39;t understand our weaknesses; He was tempted in every way just like us, but He did not sin.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Hebrews 4:16">16</a> Let us then go confidently to God&#39;s throne of grace to receive mercy and find grace that will help us when we need it.</p>
