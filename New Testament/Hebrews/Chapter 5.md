<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Hebrews 5:1">1</a> Every high priest chosen from people is appointed for people in matters related to God, so he can present both gifts and sacrifices for sins.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Hebrews 5:2">2</a> Who can understand those who don&#39;t know, and those who have lost their way? Because that person also has weaknesses.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Hebrews 5:3">3</a> For this reason, he must offer sacrifices for sins for the people and also for himself.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Hebrews 5:4">4</a> No one takes this honor for themselves, but only those called by God, just like Aaron was.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Hebrews 5:5">5</a> Christ did not give himself the honor of becoming a high priest; but God, who told him, &quot;You are my Son, today I have become your father,&quot; did.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Hebrews 5:6">6</a> God also says in another place, &quot;You are a priest forever in the way of Melchizedek.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Hebrews 5:7">7</a> During his time on earth, when he prayed and pleaded with loud crying and tears to God, who could save him from death, he was heard because he respected God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Hebrews 5:8">8</a> Even though he was a son, he learned to obey through what he went through.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Hebrews 5:9">9</a> When he became perfect, he caused everlasting salvation for all who obey him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Hebrews 5:10">10</a> God chose him to be a high priest like Melchisedec.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Hebrews 5:11">11</a> We have much to say about this, but it is difficult because you are slow to listen.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Hebrews 5:12">12</a> By now you should be teachers, but you need someone to teach you the basics of God&#39;s message again. You&#39;re like babies who need milk, not solid food.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Hebrews 5:13">13</a> Everyone who drinks milk is not experienced in the teaching of right living because they are like a baby.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Hebrews 5:14">14</a> Solid food is for grown-ups, who have trained their senses to tell right from wrong through practice.</p>
