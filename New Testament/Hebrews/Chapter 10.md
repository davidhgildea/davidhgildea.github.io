<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Hebrews 10:1">1</a> The law has only a shadow of the good things that will come, not the real things themselves. So it can never make perfect those who come to God by the same sacrifices they offer continually every year.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Hebrews 10:2">2</a> If that were true, wouldn&#39;t the offerings have stopped? Because the worshippers would be cleansed and no longer feel guilty for their sins.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Hebrews 10:3">3</a> In those sacrifices, they recall sins every year.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Hebrews 10:4">4</a> Bull and goat blood cannot remove sins.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Hebrews 10:5">5</a> When he enters the world, he says, &quot;You did not want sacrifices and offerings, but you prepared a body for me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Hebrews 10:6">6</a> You did not find joy in burnt offerings and sacrifices for sin.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Hebrews 10:7">7</a> I said, &quot;Look, I am coming to do your will, God, as it is written about me in the book.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Hebrews 10:8">8</a> God did not want sacrifices, burnt offerings, or sin offerings, which are given according to the law.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Hebrews 10:9">9</a> He said, &quot;Look, I come to do your will, God. He removes the first to set up the second.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Hebrews 10:10">10</a> Through this will, we are made holy by the sacrifice of Jesus Christ&#39;s body, once for all.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Hebrews 10:11">11</a> Every priest stands every day to serve and often gives the same sacrifices that can never remove sins.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Hebrews 10:12">12</a> This man offered one sacrifice for sins forever and then sat down at God&#39;s right side.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Hebrews 10:13">13</a> From now on waiting until his enemies are turned into his footrest.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Hebrews 10:14">14</a> With one sacrifice, he has made perfect forever those who are made holy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Hebrews 10:15">15</a> The Holy Spirit is also a witness to us because he had previously said,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Hebrews 10:16">16</a> This is the promise I will make with them after that time, says God: I will place my laws in their hearts, and I will write them in their minds.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Hebrews 10:17">17</a> I will not remember their sins and wrongs anymore.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Hebrews 10:18">18</a> When these have been forgiven, there is no need for any more sacrifices for sin.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Hebrews 10:19">19</a> So, friends, we can confidently go into the most sacred place because of Jesus&#39; blood.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Hebrews 10:20">20</a> Through a new and living way he made for us, going through the curtain, which is his body.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Hebrews 10:21">21</a> We have a great priest in charge of God&#39;s house.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Hebrews 10:22">22</a> Let&#39;s come close with a true heart, fully sure of our faith, with hearts cleaned from bad thoughts, and bodies washed with clean water.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Hebrews 10:23">23</a> Let&#39;s firmly keep our faith without doubting; (because God who promised is trustworthy;)</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Hebrews 10:24">24</a> Let&#39;s think about each other to encourage love and good deeds.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Hebrews 10:25">25</a> Don&#39;t stop meeting together, as some do, but encourage each other, especially as you see the day coming closer.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Hebrews 10:26">26</a> If we choose to sin after knowing the truth, there is no more sacrifice left for sins.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Hebrews 10:27">27</a> A scary expectation of judgment and burning anger, which will consume the enemies.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Hebrews 10:28">28</a> Anyone who ignored Moses&#39; law was put to death without pity on the testimony of two or three people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Hebrews 10:29">29</a> How much worse punishment do you think will be given to someone who has stepped on the Son of God, treated the blood of the agreement that made him holy as nothing special, and insulted the Spirit of kindness?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Hebrews 10:30">30</a> We know the one who said, &quot;It is my job to take revenge; I will pay them back,&quot; says God. And again, &quot;God will judge his people.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Hebrews 10:31">31</a> Being in the power of the living God can be very frightening.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Hebrews 10:32">32</a> Remember the early days when, after you were enlightened, you faced many hard struggles.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Hebrews 10:33">33</a> You were publicly shamed and suffered, and you stood by those who were treated the same way.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Hebrews 10:34">34</a> You felt sorry for me in my chains, and happily accepted losing your things because you knew you have something better and lasting in heaven.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Hebrews 10:35">35</a> Do not give up your confidence; it will bring a great reward.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Hebrews 10:36">36</a> You need patience, so that after doing God&#39;s will, you can receive what was promised.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Hebrews 10:37">37</a> In just a short time, the one who will come, will come and won&#39;t delay.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Hebrews 10:38">38</a> The good person will live by trusting in God; but if someone turns away, God will not be pleased with that person.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="Hebrews 10:39">39</a> We are not those who turn back and are destroyed, but those who have faith and save their souls.</p>
