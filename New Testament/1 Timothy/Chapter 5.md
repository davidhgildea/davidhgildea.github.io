<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Timothy 5:1">1</a> Don&#39;t scold an older person, but ask him kindly like a father; treat the younger men like brothers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Timothy 5:2">2</a> Treat older women like mothers; younger women like sisters, with complete respect.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Timothy 5:3">3</a> Respect women who are truly alone without husbands.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Timothy 5:4">4</a> If a widow has children or grandchildren, they should first take care of their own family and repay their parents, because this pleases God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Timothy 5:5">5</a> A true widow who is alone puts her hope in God and prays day and night.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Timothy 5:6">6</a> A woman who enjoys life too much is like she&#39;s dead even when she&#39;s alive.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Timothy 5:7">7</a> Instruct them in these matters so they can be without fault.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Timothy 5:8">8</a> If someone does not take care of their family, especially those living at home, they have rejected the faith and are worse than someone who does not believe.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Timothy 5:9">9</a> Do not include a widow who is less than sixty years old and has been married to only one man.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Timothy 5:10">10</a> Known for good acts; if she has raised children, welcomed guests, washed believers&#39; feet, helped those in trouble, and eagerly done all kinds of good work.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Timothy 5:11">11</a> Do not accept the younger widows; when their desires grow strong, they want to marry again.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Timothy 5:12">12</a> They face punishment because they have abandoned their early belief.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 Timothy 5:13">13</a> They also learn to be lazy, moving from house to house; and they are not just lazy, but also gossips and meddlers, talking about things they should not.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="1 Timothy 5:14">14</a> I want younger women to get married, have children, manage their homes, and not give critics any reason to speak badly.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="1 Timothy 5:15">15</a> Some have already turned away following Satan.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="1 Timothy 5:16">16</a> If any believer, man or woman, has widows in their family, they should help them so that the church isn&#39;t burdened and can assist those who are truly in need.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="1 Timothy 5:17">17</a> Respect the leaders who guide well greatly, and give extra respect to those who work hard teaching God&#39;s message and beliefs.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="1 Timothy 5:18">18</a> The Bible says, you should not cover the ox&#39;s mouth when it is working with the grain. Also, a worker deserves to be paid for their work.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="1 Timothy 5:19">19</a> Do not accept a charge against an older person unless it is supported by two or three witnesses.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="1 Timothy 5:20">20</a> Tell everyone when someone does wrong so that others will be afraid to do the same.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="1 Timothy 5:21">21</a> I tell you before God, Jesus Christ, and the chosen angels, to follow these instructions fairly, treating everyone the same, without favoring anyone.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="1 Timothy 5:22">22</a> Don&#39;t quickly accuse anyone, and don&#39;t share in the wrongs of others; stay clean yourself.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="1 Timothy 5:23">23</a> Stop drinking only water, but have a little wine for your stomach and your frequent sicknesses.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="1 Timothy 5:24">24</a> Some people&#39;s sins are obvious, leading directly to judgment, while the sins of others become clear later.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="1 Timothy 5:25">25</a> Similarly, the good deeds of some people are clear from the start, and those that are not will still be seen.</p>
