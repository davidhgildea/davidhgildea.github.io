<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Timothy 3:1">1</a> It is true that if someone wants to be a leader in the church, they want to do a good thing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Timothy 3:2">2</a> A church leader must be without fault, married to one woman, watchful, clear-minded, well-behaved, welcoming to guests, and skilled in teaching.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Timothy 3:3">3</a> Doesn&#39;t drink too much, isn&#39;t violent, not chasing dirty money; but is patient, doesn&#39;t like to fight, and isn&#39;t greedy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Timothy 3:4">4</a> Someone who leads their own family well, keeping their children respectful and serious.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Timothy 3:5">5</a> If a man can&#39;t manage his own family, how can he take care of God&#39;s church?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Timothy 3:6">6</a> Someone new should not be given a high position, because if they become proud, they might be judged as the devil was.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Timothy 3:7">7</a> He must also have a good reputation among outsiders to avoid falling into disgrace and the devil&#39;s trap.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Timothy 3:8">8</a> Deacons must be serious, not lie, not drink too much wine, and not want dirty money.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Timothy 3:9">9</a> Keeping the secret of faith with a clear conscience.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Timothy 3:10">10</a> First, these should be tested; then if they are found faultless, they can serve as deacons.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Timothy 3:11">11</a> Their wives must be serious, not spreading rumors, clear-minded, and trustworthy in everything.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Timothy 3:12">12</a> Deacons should have one wife and manage their children and homes well.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 Timothy 3:13">13</a> Those who serve well as deacons gain a good standing for themselves and great confidence in their faith in Christ Jesus.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="1 Timothy 3:14">14</a> I write these things to you, hoping to visit you soon.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="1 Timothy 3:15">15</a> But if I&#39;m delayed, you will know how to act in God&#39;s house, which is the living God&#39;s church, the support and foundation of truth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="1 Timothy 3:16">16</a> There&#39;s no doubt, the secret of being good is great: God appeared in human form, proved right by the Spirit, seen by angels, told to outsiders, trusted in by people everywhere, taken up into greatness.</p>
