<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Timothy 2:1">1</a> I encourage, then, that above all, requests, prayers, asking for others, and thanks should be given for everyone.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Timothy 2:2">2</a> For rulers and all in power; so we can live quietly and peacefully with true faith and honesty.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Timothy 2:3">3</a> This is good and okay to God who saves us.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Timothy 2:4">4</a> God wants everyone to be saved and to understand the truth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Timothy 2:5">5</a> There is one God and one go-between for God and people, the man Christ Jesus.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Timothy 2:6">6</a> He gave himself as a ransom for everyone, to be shown at the right time.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Timothy 2:7">7</a> I am chosen to be a preacher and an apostle, speaking the truth in Christ without lying, and a teacher of the non-Jews in faith and truth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Timothy 2:8">8</a> I want men to pray everywhere, raising holy hands, without anger or doubt.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Timothy 2:9">9</a> Women should also dress simply and with modesty, acting with decency and self-control, not with fancy hairstyles, gold, pearls, or expensive clothes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Timothy 2:10">10</a> But (fitting for women who claim to honor God) by doing good deeds.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Timothy 2:11">11</a> Let the woman learn quietly and obey fully.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Timothy 2:12">12</a> I do not allow a woman to teach or have control over a man; she must be quiet.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 Timothy 2:13">13</a> Adam was created first, then Eve.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="1 Timothy 2:14">14</a> Adam was not tricked, but the woman was deceived and did wrong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="1 Timothy 2:15">15</a> She will be saved through having children, if they keep their faith, love, and holiness with good sense.</p>
