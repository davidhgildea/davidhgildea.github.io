<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Philippians 3:1">1</a> Friends, be happy in God. Writing the same things to you is not hard for me and it keeps you safe.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Philippians 3:2">2</a> Be careful of dogs, be careful of bad people, be careful of those who cut the body.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Philippians 3:3">3</a> We are the people who serve God with our hearts, find joy in Jesus, and do not trust in our own power.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Philippians 3:4">4</a> I could also trust in human ability. If anyone else thinks they can trust in human ability, I have even more reason to:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Philippians 3:5">5</a> Circumcised on the eighth day, from Israel&#39;s people, from the tribe of Benjamin, a Hebrew born from Hebrews; and concerning the law, a Pharisee.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Philippians 3:6">6</a> Eager to defend my beliefs, I attacked the church; but following the law&#39;s rules, I was faultless.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Philippians 3:7">7</a> The things that were valuable to me, I now consider worthless because of Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Philippians 3:8">8</a> Yes, I consider everything a loss compared to the great value of knowing Christ Jesus my Lord. For him, I&#39;ve lost all things and see them as trash, so that I can have Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Philippians 3:9">9</a> Be in unity with him, not with my own goodness from following the law, but with the goodness that comes through faith in Christ, the goodness from God that relies on faith.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Philippians 3:10">10</a> So I can know him, the power of his rising again, sharing in his sufferings, and becoming like him in his death.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Philippians 3:11">11</a> I hope somehow to be raised to life after death.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Philippians 3:12">12</a> It&#39;s not like I&#39;ve already succeeded or am already perfect, but I keep trying to reach what Jesus Christ chose me for.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Philippians 3:13">13</a> Friends, I don&#39;t consider that I have fully understood everything. But I do one thing: I forget what is behind and strive for what is ahead.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Philippians 3:14">14</a> I strive for the goal to win the prize that God calls us to through Christ Jesus.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Philippians 3:15">15</a> Let&#39;s think this way, those of us who are mature. And if you think differently about something, God will make that clear to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Philippians 3:16">16</a> Let&#39;s follow the same rules and think the same way for what we&#39;ve already achieved.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Philippians 3:17">17</a> Friends, follow my example together, and notice those who live as you have us as a model.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Philippians 3:18">18</a> Many people live in a way I often warned you about, and now I say it crying, they are against Christ&#39;s cross.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Philippians 3:19">19</a> Their end is ruin, their god is their appetite, they take pride in what should bring shame, and they focus on worldly things.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Philippians 3:20">20</a> Our citizenship is in heaven, and from there we eagerly await our Savior, Jesus Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Philippians 3:21">21</a> Who will transform our lowly body to be like his glorious body, using his power to bring everything under his control.</p>
