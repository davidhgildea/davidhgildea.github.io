<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Philippians 2:1">1</a> So, if there is any comfort from being united with Christ, if any love brings comfort, if we share in the Spirit, if there is any warmth and kindness,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Philippians 2:2">2</a> Make my joy complete by agreeing with each other, sharing the same love, and being united in thoughts and purpose.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Philippians 2:3">3</a> Do nothing out of fighting or empty pride; instead, with humble minds, value others more than yourselves.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Philippians 2:4">4</a> Don&#39;t just look out for your own interests, but also for the interests of others.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Philippians 2:5">5</a> Have the same mindset as Jesus Christ did.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Philippians 2:6">6</a> Even though he was like God, he did not see being equal with God as something to hold onto.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Philippians 2:7">7</a> He made himself humble, became a servant, and was born as a human.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Philippians 2:8">8</a> Being found as a man, he became humble and obeyed to the point of death, even dying on a cross.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Philippians 2:9">9</a> So God has lifted him high and given him a name greater than any other name.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Philippians 2:10">10</a> Everyone should kneel to honor Jesus&#39; name - those in heaven, on earth, and under the earth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Philippians 2:11">11</a> Everyone should agree that Jesus Christ is the Lord to honor God the Father.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Philippians 2:12">12</a> So, my dear friends, just as you have always followed, not only when I am with you but even more now that I&#39;m not, keep working on your own rescue with respect and care.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Philippians 2:13">13</a> God is the one who makes you want to do and actually do what pleases Him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Philippians 2:14">14</a> Do everything without complaining or arguing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Philippians 2:15">15</a> So that you may be pure and good, children of God, without fault, in a wicked and twisted world, where you shine like stars.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Philippians 2:16">16</a> Offering the message of life; so I can be joyful when Christ comes, knowing my efforts were not wasted.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Philippians 2:17">17</a> Yes, and if I am like a drink offering on the offering and service of your faith, I am happy and share joy with all of you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Philippians 2:18">18</a> For this reason, you too be glad and share my joy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Philippians 2:19">19</a> I hope Jesus will let me send Timothy to you soon, so I can feel better after learning how you are doing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Philippians 2:20">20</a> I have no one else who truly cares for your well-being.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Philippians 2:21">21</a> Everyone looks out for their own interests, not those of Jesus Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Philippians 2:22">22</a> You know how he has shown himself, like a son with his father, working with me to spread the good news.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Philippians 2:23">23</a> So I plan to send him to you soon, once I know what will happen with me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Philippians 2:24">24</a> I trust in God that I will also come soon.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Philippians 2:25">25</a> I thought it was important to send Epaphroditus to you – my brother, coworker, and fellow soldier – who is also your messenger and has helped me with my needs.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Philippians 2:26">26</a> He really missed you all and was very worried because you heard he was ill.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Philippians 2:27">27</a> He was really sick, almost dying, but God showed mercy on him; not just on him, but on me too, so I wouldn&#39;t have even more sadness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Philippians 2:28">28</a> I sent him with extra care so that when you see him again, you can be happy and I can be less sad.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Philippians 2:29">29</a> Welcome him in the name of God with joy; and respect such people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Philippians 2:30">30</a> He was near death because of his work for Christ, risking his life to help me when you could not.</p>
