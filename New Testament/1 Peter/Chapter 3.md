<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Peter 3:1">1</a> Wives, respect your husbands so that even if some do not believe the message, they may be convinced by their wives&#39; way of life without needing words.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Peter 3:2">2</a> As they see your pure way of life with respect.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Peter 3:3">3</a> Don&#39;t focus on decorating yourself by braiding your hair, wearing gold jewelry, or dressing in fine clothes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Peter 3:4">4</a> Let your inner self be gentle and calm, a lasting beauty precious to God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Peter 3:5">5</a> In the past, holy women who trusted in God also made themselves beautiful by accepting their husbands&#39; guidance.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Peter 3:6">6</a> Just like Sarah obeyed Abraham and called him lord, you are her daughters if you do good and are not frightened by anything scary.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Peter 3:7">7</a> Husbands, live with your wives wisely. Respect them as the weaker partner, sharing the gift of life together, so nothing will block your prayers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Peter 3:8">8</a> Finally, all of you should agree with each other, be kind to one another, love like brothers and sisters, be compassionate, and be polite.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Peter 3:9">9</a> Do not repay evil with evil, or insults with insults. Instead, give blessings, because you are called to inherit a blessing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Peter 3:10">10</a> To enjoy life and have good days, one should keep their tongue from evil and their lips from telling lies.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Peter 3:11">11</a> Avoid evil and do good; seek peace and follow it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Peter 3:12">12</a> God watches over good people, and He listens to their prayers, but He opposes those who do wrong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 Peter 3:13">13</a> Who would want to hurt you if you are doing good?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="1 Peter 3:14">14</a> If you suffer for doing right, you are blessed. Don&#39;t be scared or upset by their threats.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="1 Peter 3:15">15</a> Set apart God in your hearts and always be prepared to explain your hope to anyone who asks, doing so gently and respectfully.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="1 Peter 3:16">16</a> Keep a clear conscience so that those who speak badly of you as wrongdoers may feel ashamed for wrongly criticizing your good behavior as a follower of Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="1 Peter 3:17">17</a> It is better, if it is God&#39;s will, to suffer for doing good than for doing bad.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="1 Peter 3:18">18</a> Christ also suffered once for sins, the righteous for the unrighteous, to bring us to God. He was put to death in the body but made alive by the Spirit.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="1 Peter 3:19">19</a> He also went and spoke to the spirits in prison.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="1 Peter 3:20">20</a> People who did not obey long ago, when God patiently waited during Noah&#39;s time while the ark was being built, were saved by water, only a few, that is, eight people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="1 Peter 3:21">21</a> This symbol, baptism, now saves us too—not by cleaning our bodies from dirt, but by giving us a clear conscience toward God, because of Jesus Christ&#39;s resurrection.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="1 Peter 3:22">22</a> He has gone to heaven and sits at God&#39;s right side, with angels, authorities, and powers under his control.</p>
