<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Peter 2:1">1</a> So put away all meanness, trickery, pretending, jealousy, and talking about others badly.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Peter 2:2">2</a> Like new babies, crave pure spiritual milk so you can grow because of it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Peter 2:3">3</a> If you have experienced God&#39;s kindness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Peter 2:4">4</a> Come to Him like you would to a living stone, which people have rejected but which God has chosen and values highly.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Peter 2:5">5</a> You too, like living stones, are being made into a spiritual temple, a sacred group of priests, to give spiritual offerings that please God through Jesus Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Peter 2:6">6</a> The scripture says, &quot;Look, I put a very important and chosen cornerstone in Zion. Whoever believes in him will not be embarrassed.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Peter 2:7">7</a> So for you who believe, he is valuable, but for those who don&#39;t obey, the stone the builders rejected has become the cornerstone.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Peter 2:8">8</a> A stumbling block and a rock that makes them fall, even for those who stumble over the message because they do not obey: this is what they were destined for.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Peter 2:9">9</a> You are a special group, chosen to be kings&#39; priests and a holy nation, different from others; so you can show everyone how great God is, who brought you from darkness into his wonderful light.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Peter 2:10">10</a> Before, you were not a people, but now you are God&#39;s people; before, you did not receive mercy, but now you have received mercy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Peter 2:11">11</a> Dear friends, I urge you as outsiders and travelers, to avoid bodily desires, which fight against the soul.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Peter 2:12">12</a> Live well among non-believers so even if they accuse you of doing wrong, they will see your good deeds and honor God when he examines us.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 Peter 2:13">13</a> Obey all human laws for God&#39;s sake, whether they come from the king, who is highest.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="1 Peter 2:14">14</a> Or to leaders, as to those sent by God to punish those who do wrong and to praise those who do right.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="1 Peter 2:15">15</a> God wants you to do good so you can silence the foolish talk of ignorant people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="1 Peter 2:16">16</a> As free, but don&#39;t use your freedom to hide bad actions, instead live as God&#39;s servants.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="1 Peter 2:17">17</a> Respect everyone. Love your fellow believers. Be in awe of God. Respect the leader.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="1 Peter 2:18">18</a> Workers, obey your bosses with full respect; not just the kind and fair ones, but also the difficult ones.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="1 Peter 2:19">19</a> It is good if someone bears pain for doing right because of their conscience toward God, even if they suffer unfairly.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="1 Peter 2:20">20</a> What good is it if you endure punishment for doing wrong? But if you do right and still suffer for it, and endure it, this pleases God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="1 Peter 2:21">21</a> You were called to this because Christ suffered for us, giving us an example to follow his steps.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="1 Peter 2:22">22</a> He did not sin and no deceit was in his mouth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="1 Peter 2:23">23</a> When people insulted him, he did not insult back; when he was hurt, he did not threaten them but trusted in God who judges fairly.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="1 Peter 2:24">24</a> He carried our sins in his body on the cross so that we, free from sins, can live a good life. By his wounds, you have been healed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="1 Peter 2:25">25</a> You were like lost sheep; but now you have come back to the Shepherd and Guardian of your souls.</p>
