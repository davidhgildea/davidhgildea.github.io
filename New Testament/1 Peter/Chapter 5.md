<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Peter 5:1">1</a> I urge you, the older leaders among you, as I am also an older leader, a witness of Christ&#39;s sufferings, and one who will share in the glory to come.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Peter 5:2">2</a> Take care of God&#39;s flock that is with you, watch over them because you want to, not because you must, and not for money, but because you are eager to.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Peter 5:3">3</a> Not as bosses over God&#39;s people, but as examples to the group.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Peter 5:4">4</a> When the main Shepherd comes, you will get a crown of glory that never fades.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Peter 5:5">5</a> Young people, respect those who are older. Everyone should treat each other with respect and be humble, because God opposes the proud but gives grace to the humble.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Peter 5:6">6</a> So be humble under God&#39;s powerful hand, and he will lift you up when the time is right.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Peter 5:7">7</a> Give all your worries to God, because he cares for you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Peter 5:8">8</a> Stay alert and watchful, because your enemy the devil prowls around like a roaring lion, looking for someone to eat.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Peter 5:9">9</a> Stand firm against him by being strong in your faith, knowing that believers all over the world are going through the same kind of suffering.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Peter 5:10">10</a> God, full of all grace, who has called you to his eternal glory through Christ Jesus, will, after you have suffered for a little while, make you perfect, firm, strong, and steady.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Peter 5:11">11</a> May he have glory and power forever. Amen.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Peter 5:12">12</a> Through Silvanus, who I believe is a loyal friend to you, I&#39;ve written a short letter to encourage you and affirm that you are experiencing God&#39;s true kindness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 Peter 5:13">13</a> The church in Babylon, chosen with you, sends greetings; and so does Mark, my son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="1 Peter 5:14">14</a> Say hello to each other with a friendly kiss. May peace be with all of you who belong to Christ Jesus. Amen.</p>
