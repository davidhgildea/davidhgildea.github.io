<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Philemon 1:1">1</a> Paul, a prisoner for Jesus Christ, and Timothy, our brother, to Philemon, our dear friend and coworker.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Philemon 1:2">2</a> To our dear friend Apphia, and Archippus our comrade in arms, and the church that meets in your home:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Philemon 1:3">3</a> May you have kindness and peace from God our Father and Jesus Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Philemon 1:4">4</a> I thank God, always mentioning you in my prayers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Philemon 1:5">5</a> Hearing about your love and faith in Jesus, and for all God&#39;s people;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Philemon 1:6">6</a> May your faith become strong as you realize all the good things we have in Jesus.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Philemon 1:7">7</a> We are very happy and comforted by your love, because you, brother, have refreshed the hearts of God&#39;s holy people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Philemon 1:8">8</a> Although I could be confident in Christ to instruct you to do what is right,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Philemon 1:9">9</a> For love&#39;s sake, I prefer to ask you kindly, since I am Paul, an old man, and now also a prisoner for Jesus Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Philemon 1:10">10</a> I ask you for my son Onesimus, who became my child while I was in chains.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Philemon 1:11">11</a> Before, this was not useful to you, but now it is useful to both of us.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Philemon 1:12">12</a> I have sent him back to you, so please welcome him, as he is dear to me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Philemon 1:13">13</a> I wanted to keep him with me so he could help me while I am imprisoned for spreading the gospel, doing your work.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Philemon 1:14">14</a> I wouldn&#39;t do anything without your agreement, so your kindness is freely given, not forced.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Philemon 1:15">15</a> Maybe he left for a while so you could have him back forever.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Philemon 1:16">16</a> No longer as a servant, but more than a servant, a dear brother, especially to me, but even more to you, in person and in God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Philemon 1:17">17</a> If you consider me a partner, welcome him as you would me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Philemon 1:18">18</a> If he has wronged you or owes you anything, charge it to my account.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Philemon 1:19">19</a> I, Paul, wrote this with my own hand and will pay it back. But I won&#39;t remind you that you owe me your very self as well.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Philemon 1:20">20</a> Yes, brother, let me find joy in you through God: give me comfort and support in God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Philemon 1:21">21</a> Trusting in your willingness to follow, I wrote to you, knowing you will do even more than I ask.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Philemon 1:22">22</a> Please also get a room ready for me, because I believe that thanks to your prayers, I will be able to come to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Philemon 1:23">23</a> Epaphras, who is in prison with me for following Jesus, sends you greetings.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Philemon 1:24">24</a> Mark, Aristarchus, Demas, Luke, my fellow workers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Philemon 1:25">25</a> May the kindness of our Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit. Amen.</p>
