<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Ephesians 2:1">1</a> God made you alive even when you were dead because of your wrong actions and sins.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Ephesians 2:2">2</a> Before, you lived as the world does, following the ruler of the earthly realm, the spirit that is active in those who disobey.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Ephesians 2:3">3</a> We all once lived following our human desires, doing what our bodies and minds wanted. By nature, we were destined to face anger just like everyone else.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Ephesians 2:4">4</a> God, rich in mercy, because of his great love for us,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Ephesians 2:5">5</a> Even when we were dead in our wrongdoings, God made us alive with Christ, (because of kindness you are saved).</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Ephesians 2:6">6</a> God raised us up with Christ and seated us together in heaven with Jesus.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Ephesians 2:7">7</a> In future times, he could show us the great wealth of his kindness through Jesus Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Ephesians 2:8">8</a> You are saved by God&#39;s kindness through faith. This is not from yourselves; it&#39;s God&#39;s gift.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Ephesians 2:9">9</a> Not from what we do, so no one can brag.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Ephesians 2:10">10</a> We are what God has made us, created in Jesus to do good things, which God planned for us to do.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Ephesians 2:11">11</a> Remember that you were once non-Jews in the body, called &#39;Uncircumcised&#39; by those called &#39;Circumcised,&#39; which is done in the body by hand.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Ephesians 2:12">12</a> At that time, you were without Christ, outsiders to Israel&#39;s citizenship, unfamiliar with the promises&#39; agreements, without hope, and without God in the world.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Ephesians 2:13">13</a> Now in Christ Jesus, you who were once far away have been brought close by Christ&#39;s blood.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Ephesians 2:14">14</a> He is our peace, who made both groups one and tore down the dividing wall between us.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Ephesians 2:15">15</a> He ended the hostility with his own body, by ending the law with its rules. This was to create in himself one new humanity from two, making peace.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Ephesians 2:16">16</a> He aimed to bring both together in one body to God through the cross, by putting an end to their hostility that way.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Ephesians 2:17">17</a> He came and told you far away and those close about peace.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Ephesians 2:18">18</a> Through him, we both reach the Father by one Spirit.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Ephesians 2:19">19</a> So now you are not strangers or outsiders, but you are citizens together with God&#39;s people and members of God&#39;s family.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Ephesians 2:20">20</a> You are built on the foundation of the apostles and prophets, with Jesus Christ as the most important cornerstone.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Ephesians 2:21">21</a> Everyone in the building is joined together and grows into a holy temple for God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Ephesians 2:22">22</a> In whom you also are built together to be a home for God by the Spirit.</p>
