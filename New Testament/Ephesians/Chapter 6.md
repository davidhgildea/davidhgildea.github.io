<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Ephesians 6:1">1</a> Kids, listen to your parents because it is the right thing to do.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Ephesians 6:2">2</a> Respect your father and mother; this is the first commandment that comes with a promise.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Ephesians 6:3">3</a> So things go well for you, and you may live a long time on the earth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Ephesians 6:4">4</a> Parents, do not make your children angry, but raise them with care and teach them the ways of God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Ephesians 6:5">5</a> Workers, obey your earthly bosses with respect and sincerity, as if you were serving Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Ephesians 6:6">6</a> Do your work not just when being watched, to impress people, but as followers of Christ, do what God wants from the heart.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Ephesians 6:7">7</a> Serve with a good attitude, as if working for God, not people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Ephesians 6:8">8</a> Understanding that whatever good thing someone does, they will get the same from God, whether they are a slave or free.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Ephesians 6:9">9</a> Bosses, treat your workers well and don&#39;t threaten them, knowing that you both have the same Master in heaven, and He treats everyone equally.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Ephesians 6:10">10</a> Friends, be strong in God and in His mighty power.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Ephesians 6:11">11</a> Wear all of God&#39;s armor so you can stand against the devil&#39;s tricks.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Ephesians 6:12">12</a> We are not fighting against people, but against authorities, against forces, against world leaders of darkness, and against evil spirits in heavenly places.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Ephesians 6:13">13</a> So, put on all of God&#39;s armor to be strong against evil, and after you have done everything, to keep standing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Ephesians 6:14">14</a> So stand firm, with truth like a belt around your waist, and wearing righteousness as a chest armor.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Ephesians 6:15">15</a> Wear on your feet what will help you spread the good news of peace.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Ephesians 6:16">16</a> Above all, use the shield of belief to stop all the burning arrows of the evil one.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Ephesians 6:17">17</a> Wear the helmet of being saved, and use the sword of the Spirit, which is God&#39;s message.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Ephesians 6:18">18</a> Always pray with all kinds of prayers and requests in the Spirit, and stay alert with all perseverance and requests for all God&#39;s people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Ephesians 6:19">19</a> Please help me speak boldly and clearly to explain the gospel&#39;s secret.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Ephesians 6:20">20</a> I am God&#39;s ambassador, even though I am in chains. I pray to speak bravely, as I should.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Ephesians 6:21">21</a> So you can know about my situation and how I&#39;m doing, Tychicus, a dear brother and loyal servant, will tell you everything.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Ephesians 6:22">22</a> I sent him to you for this reason: so you can know about us and he can cheer you up.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Ephesians 6:23">23</a> May the brothers have peace and love with faith from God the Father and Jesus Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Ephesians 6:24">24</a> May grace be with everyone who truly loves our Lord Jesus Christ. Amen.</p>
