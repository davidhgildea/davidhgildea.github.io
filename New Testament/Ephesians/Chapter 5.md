<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Ephesians 5:1">1</a> So be like God, as beloved children.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Ephesians 5:2">2</a> Live in love, just as Christ loved us and gave himself for us, as a gift and sacrifice to God that is like a pleasing aroma.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Ephesians 5:3">3</a> Avoid sexual immorality, all impurity, or greed; don&#39;t even mention these things among you, as is proper for God&#39;s holy people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Ephesians 5:4">4</a> Avoid dirty talk, silly words, or jokes that are not appropriate, but instead, give thanks.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Ephesians 5:5">5</a> You know that no one who sleeps around, is unclean, or is greedy—which is worshiping wealth—has a place in Christ and God&#39;s kingdom.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Ephesians 5:6">6</a> Do not let anyone trick you with empty words, because God&#39;s anger comes on those who disobey because of these things.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Ephesians 5:7">7</a> So do not join in with them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Ephesians 5:8">8</a> You were once in darkness, but now you are in the light with God: live as children of light.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Ephesians 5:9">9</a> The Spirit&#39;s fruit is goodness, righteousness, and truth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Ephesians 5:10">10</a> Showing what pleases God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Ephesians 5:11">11</a> Do not join in the useless deeds of darkness, but instead correct them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Ephesians 5:12">12</a> It is embarrassing even to talk about the things they do secretly.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Ephesians 5:13">13</a> Everything that is shown to be wrong is revealed by the light, because anything that reveals is light.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Ephesians 5:14">14</a> So he says, &quot;Wake up, sleeper, get up from the dead, and Christ will shine on you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Ephesians 5:15">15</a> Make sure you live carefully, not like foolish people, but like wise ones.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Ephesians 5:16">16</a> Use time wisely, because these are bad days.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Ephesians 5:17">17</a> So don&#39;t be foolish, but understand what God wants.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Ephesians 5:18">18</a> Do not get drunk on wine, which leads to bad behavior; instead, be filled with the Spirit.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Ephesians 5:19">19</a> Talk to each other with psalms, hymns, and spiritual songs, sing and make music in your heart to God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Ephesians 5:20">20</a> Always give thanks for everything to God the Father in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Ephesians 5:21">21</a> Respect each other because you respect God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Ephesians 5:22">22</a> Wives, follow your own husbands, just as you do God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Ephesians 5:23">23</a> The husband is the leader of the wife, just as Christ is the leader of the church; and he is the one who saves it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Ephesians 5:24">24</a> So as the church obeys Christ, wives should also obey their husbands in everything.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Ephesians 5:25">25</a> Husbands, love your wives, just as Christ loved the people of the church and gave himself up for them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Ephesians 5:26">26</a> So he could make it holy and clean it by washing it with water and with the word.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Ephesians 5:27">27</a> He wanted to make the church glorious for himself, without any spots, wrinkles, or anything like that; it should be holy and perfect.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Ephesians 5:28">28</a> Men should love their wives as they love their own bodies. Someone who loves his wife loves himself.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Ephesians 5:29">29</a> No one ever hates their own body; instead, they feed and care for it, just like God does for the church.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Ephesians 5:30">30</a> We are parts of his body, his flesh, and his bones.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Ephesians 5:31">31</a> For this reason, a man will leave his parents and unite with his wife, and they will become one body.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Ephesians 5:32">32</a> This is a big mystery, but I am talking about Christ and the church.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Ephesians 5:33">33</a> Each of you should love your wife as yourself, and every wife should respect her husband.</p>
