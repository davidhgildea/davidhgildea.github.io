<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Ephesians 4:1">1</a> So I, a prisoner for God, ask you to live a life that reflects the calling you have received.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Ephesians 4:2">2</a> With complete humility and gentleness, with patience, tolerating each other in love;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Ephesians 4:3">3</a> Trying to maintain the unity of the Spirit through the bond of peace.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Ephesians 4:4">4</a> There is one body and one spirit, just as you are called to one hope when you were called.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Ephesians 4:5">5</a> One God, one belief, one baptism.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Ephesians 4:6">6</a> There is one God, the Father of everyone, who is over all, works through all, and lives in all of you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Ephesians 4:7">7</a> Each of us is given kindness by God as much as Christ has decided.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Ephesians 4:8">8</a> So he says, when he went up to the high place, he took prisoners and gave gifts to people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Ephesians 4:9">9</a> He went up, but before that, he went down to the lower parts of the earth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Ephesians 4:10">10</a> The one who came down is also the one who went up, way above all the skies, so he could be in everything.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Ephesians 4:11">11</a> He gave some to be apostles, others prophets, others evangelists, and others to be pastors and teachers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Ephesians 4:12">12</a> To improve God&#39;s people, for serving work, for building up Christ&#39;s group:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Ephesians 4:13">13</a> Until we all reach unity in faith and understanding of God&#39;s Son, becoming mature, achieving the full standard of Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Ephesians 4:14">14</a> We should no longer be like children, pushed around by every new teaching from deceitful people who are experts at tricking others.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Ephesians 4:15">15</a> Tell the truth with love, and grow in every way to become like Christ, who is the leader.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Ephesians 4:16">16</a> The whole body is closely joined and united by every supporting joint, and each part does its work, making the body grow and build itself up with love.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Ephesians 4:17">17</a> I say this and confirm it: from now on, don&#39;t live like the other non-Jews who follow their empty thoughts.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Ephesians 4:18">18</a> Their understanding is clouded, and they are cut off from God&#39;s life because they don&#39;t know any better and their hearts are blind.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Ephesians 4:19">19</a> They no longer feel guilty and have let themselves do indecent things, wanting more and more.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Ephesians 4:20">20</a> You have not learned about Christ in this way;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Ephesians 4:21">21</a> If you have heard Jesus and were taught by him, because truth is in Jesus.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Ephesians 4:22">22</a> Stop living the old way of life, which is ruined by seeking dishonest pleasures.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Ephesians 4:23">23</a> Let your mind&#39;s attitude become fresh and new.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Ephesians 4:24">24</a> Wear your new self, made like God in true goodness and holiness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Ephesians 4:25">25</a> So stop lying and always tell the truth to each other, because we are all part of the same group.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Ephesians 4:26">26</a> Get angry, but don&#39;t sin. Don&#39;t let the sun set while you are still angry.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Ephesians 4:27">27</a> Do not let the devil have a way in.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Ephesians 4:28">28</a> If someone has been stealing, they should stop. Instead, they should work hard and do good things with their hands, so they can share with others who are in need.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Ephesians 4:29">29</a> Don&#39;t let any bad words come out of your mouth, but only good words that help others become stronger, so that it can bring kindness to those who listen.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Ephesians 4:30">30</a> Do not make God&#39;s Holy Spirit sad, for He has marked you for the day of being saved.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Ephesians 4:31">31</a> Get rid of all bitterness, rage, anger, shouting, and mean talk, along with all spitefulness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Ephesians 4:32">32</a> Be kind to each other, be gentle, and forgive each other, just as God has forgiven you because of Christ.</p>
