<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Ephesians 3:1">1</a> Because of this, I Paul, imprisoned for serving Jesus Christ, am writing to you non-Jews.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Ephesians 3:2">2</a> If you know about the special role God&#39;s kindness has given me for your benefit:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Ephesians 3:3">3</a> He revealed the secret to me; as I briefly wrote before.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Ephesians 3:4">4</a> When you read this, you can understand my insight into the secret of Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Ephesians 3:5">5</a> In other times, this was not shown to people as it has now been made clear to his special messengers and prophets by the Spirit.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Ephesians 3:6">6</a> That non-Jewish people should also inherit, belong to the same group, and share in God&#39;s promise through Christ by the good news.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Ephesians 3:7">7</a> I became a servant because God gave me this gift by his power at work within me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Ephesians 3:8">8</a> God gave me, the least of all God&#39;s people, the grace to tell the non-Jews about the endless wealth that comes from Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Ephesians 3:9">9</a> To help everyone understand the shared secret that has been hidden in God since the world started, the God who made everything through Jesus Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Ephesians 3:10">10</a> The purpose is for the rulers and authorities in the heavens to learn about God’s many-sided wisdom through the church.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Ephesians 3:11">11</a> God made an everlasting plan which he fulfilled through Jesus Christ our Lord.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Ephesians 3:12">12</a> In him we are brave and can come close with confidence because of his faith.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Ephesians 3:13">13</a> I want you not to lose heart because of my struggles for you, which are for your honor.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Ephesians 3:14">14</a> For this reason, I kneel before the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Ephesians 3:15">15</a> The whole family in heaven and on earth gets its name from God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Ephesians 3:16">16</a> He asks God, with all His greatness, to make you strong inside by His Spirit.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Ephesians 3:17">17</a> May Christ live in your hearts through faith; and may you grow strong in love.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Ephesians 3:18">18</a> May understand with all God&#39;s people how wide, long, deep, and high it is.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Ephesians 3:19">19</a> To understand Christ&#39;s love, which is beyond knowledge, so that you may be completely filled with God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Ephesians 3:20">20</a> To the one who can do much more than all we ask or imagine, according to the power working within us,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Ephesians 3:21">21</a> May God receive glory in the church through Jesus Christ for all time, forever. Amen.</p>
