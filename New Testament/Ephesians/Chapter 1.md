<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Ephesians 1:1">1</a> Paul, sent by God to be an apostle of Jesus Christ, writes to God&#39;s holy people in Ephesus and to all who are faithful to Jesus Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Ephesians 1:2">2</a> May you have kindness and peace from God our Father and from Jesus Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Ephesians 1:3">3</a> God is great, the God and Father of Jesus Christ, who has given us every spiritual gift in heaven through Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Ephesians 1:4">4</a> God chose us in him before the world was made, so that we would be holy and without fault before him in love.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Ephesians 1:5">5</a> God planned for us to be his children through Jesus Christ, as he wanted.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Ephesians 1:6">6</a> To honor the greatness of his kindness, he made us welcomed through his love.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Ephesians 1:7">7</a> In him, we are set free by his blood, our sins forgiven, because of his great kindness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Ephesians 1:8">8</a> He has given us a lot of wisdom and understanding.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Ephesians 1:9">9</a> God revealed to us the secret of what he wants to do, which he decided by himself because it made him happy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Ephesians 1:10">10</a> In the time when everything is complete, he plans to unite all things in Christ, both in heaven and on earth, in him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Ephesians 1:11">11</a> In him, we also received an inheritance, because we were chosen as part of his plan who makes all things happen according to his own will.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Ephesians 1:12">12</a> We are meant to bring praise to his glory because we were the first to hope in Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Ephesians 1:13">13</a> In whom you also trusted, after you heard the true word, the good news of your salvation: in whom also after you believed, you were marked with the promised Holy Spirit.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Ephesians 1:14">14</a> It is the deposit guaranteeing our inheritance until God redeems what was bought, to the praise of his glory.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Ephesians 1:15">15</a> Because I too, when I heard about your belief in Jesus and your love for all of God&#39;s people,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Ephesians 1:16">16</a> I always give thanks for you and remember you in my prayers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Ephesians 1:17">17</a> May the God of Jesus Christ, the glorious Father, give you a spirit of wisdom and understanding to know him better.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Ephesians 1:18">18</a> May your mind&#39;s eye be opened, so you can understand the hope God calls you to, and how great his glorious inheritance is among the holy people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Ephesians 1:19">19</a> God&#39;s power for us who believe is very great, just like the mighty strength he has shown.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Ephesians 1:20">20</a> God showed this power in Christ when he raised him from the dead and placed him at his right side in heaven.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Ephesians 1:21">21</a> High above all rulers, authorities, strength, and lords, and every name known, not only in this world but also in the future one:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Ephesians 1:22">22</a> God has placed everything under his control and made him the leader of everything for the church.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Ephesians 1:23">23</a> This is his body, which is the complete presence of the one who fills everything completely.</p>
