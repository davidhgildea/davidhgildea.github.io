<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="2 Peter 1:1">1</a> Simon Peter, a servant and apostle of Jesus Christ, writes to those who share our valuable faith, given through the goodness of God and Jesus Christ our Savior.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="2 Peter 1:2">2</a> May grace and peace grow for you by knowing God and Jesus our Lord.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="2 Peter 1:3">3</a> God&#39;s divine power has given us everything we need for life and goodness, through knowing him who called us to glory and excellence.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="2 Peter 1:4">4</a> God has given us very great and valuable promises, so that through them you can share in God&#39;s nature, free from the world&#39;s corruption caused by human desires.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="2 Peter 1:5">5</a> Also, try your best and add goodness to your faith; and to goodness, add knowledge.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="2 Peter 1:6">6</a> Add self-control to your knowledge; and to self-control, add patience; and to patience, add devotion to God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="2 Peter 1:7">7</a> Add to being godly, kindness to others; and to kindness to others, love.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="2 Peter 1:8">8</a> If these things are in you and grow, they will make sure you are not empty or unproductive in knowing our Lord Jesus Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="2 Peter 1:9">9</a> But if someone doesn&#39;t have these qualities, they are blind, can&#39;t see far, and have forgotten they were cleansed from their past sins.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="2 Peter 1:10">10</a> So, my friends, work hard to confirm your calling and choice, because if you do these things, you will never stumble.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="2 Peter 1:11">11</a> This way, you will be given a rich welcome into the eternal kingdom of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="2 Peter 1:12">12</a> So I will always remind you of these things, even though you know them and are firm in the truth you have now.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="2 Peter 1:13">13</a> Yes, I believe it&#39;s right, as long as I&#39;m in this body, to remind you and keep you thinking.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="2 Peter 1:14">14</a> I know that soon I must leave this body, just as Jesus Christ has shown me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="2 Peter 1:15">15</a> I will try to make sure that after I die, you can always remember these things.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="2 Peter 1:16">16</a> We didn&#39;t follow cleverly made-up stories when we told you about the power and coming of our Lord Jesus Christ; we saw his greatness with our own eyes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="2 Peter 1:17">17</a> He got honor and glory from God the Father when a voice from the magnificent glory said to him, &quot;This is my dear Son, who makes me happy.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="2 Peter 1:18">18</a> We heard this voice from heaven when we were with him on the holy mountain.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="2 Peter 1:19">19</a> We also have a more certain message from the prophets. You do well to pay attention to it, like a light shining in a dark place, until the day breaks and the morning star rises in your hearts.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="2 Peter 1:20">20</a> Understand this: No prophecy in the Bible comes from someone&#39;s own explanation.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="2 Peter 1:21">21</a> The prophecy did not come long ago by human will, but holy men spoke from God as they were inspired by the Holy Spirit.</p>
