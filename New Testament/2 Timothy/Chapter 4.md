<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="2 Timothy 4:1">1</a> I command you before God and Jesus Christ, who will judge the living and the dead when he comes and begins his kingdom;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="2 Timothy 4:2">2</a> Share the message; always be ready, whether it&#39;s convenient or not; correct, scold, and encourage with patience and teaching.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="2 Timothy 4:3">3</a> A time will come when people won&#39;t accept true teaching; instead, they will follow their own desires and collect teachers who tell them what they want to hear.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="2 Timothy 4:4">4</a> They will stop listening to the truth and instead believe in made-up stories.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="2 Timothy 4:5">5</a> Stay alert in everything, be patient with hardships, do the work of a messenger of good news, and fully carry out your service.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="2 Timothy 4:6">6</a> I am ready to be sacrificed, and the time for me to leave is close.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="2 Timothy 4:7">7</a> I have fought well, I have completed my race, I have stayed true to my beliefs.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="2 Timothy 4:8">8</a> From now on, a crown of goodness is waiting for me, which God, the fair judge, will give me on that day; and not just to me, but to all who look forward to seeing Him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="2 Timothy 4:9">9</a> Make an effort to come to me soon.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="2 Timothy 4:10">10</a> Demas left me because he loved this world and has gone to Thessalonica; Crescens has gone to Galatia, and Titus to Dalmatia.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="2 Timothy 4:11">11</a> Only Luke is with me. Bring Mark with you, because he is helpful to me in the work.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="2 Timothy 4:12">12</a> I have sent Tychicus to Ephesus.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="2 Timothy 4:13">13</a> Bring the coat I left in Troas with Carpus when you come, and the books, but mainly the scrolls.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="2 Timothy 4:14">14</a> Alexander the metalworker caused me a lot of harm; may God repay him for what he has done.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="2 Timothy 4:15">15</a> Be careful of him too; he has strongly opposed our message.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="2 Timothy 4:16">16</a> At my first defense, no one was with me; everyone left me. I hope God will not hold it against them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="2 Timothy 4:17">17</a> Despite everything, God was with me and gave me strength, so that the message could be completely spread, and all non-Jewish people could hear it; and I was saved from the lion&#39;s mouth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="2 Timothy 4:18">18</a> God will rescue me from all evil deeds and keep me safe for his heavenly kingdom. To God be glory forever. Amen.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="2 Timothy 4:19">19</a> Say hello to Prisca and Aquila, and to the family of Onesiphorus.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="2 Timothy 4:20">20</a> Erastus stayed in Corinth, but I left Trophimus sick in Miletus.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="2 Timothy 4:21">21</a> Try hard to come before winter. Eubulus, Pudens, Linus, Claudia, and all the brothers send their greetings.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="2 Timothy 4:22">22</a> May Jesus Christ be with your spirit. May you have grace. Amen.</p>
