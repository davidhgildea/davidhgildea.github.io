<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="2 Timothy 2:1">1</a> You, my child, be strong in the grace that is in Jesus Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="2 Timothy 2:2">2</a> The teachings you have heard from me, in front of many witnesses, pass on to trustworthy people who can also teach others.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="2 Timothy 2:3">3</a> So, be strong like a good soldier of Jesus Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="2 Timothy 2:4">4</a> A person fighting in a war does not get involved with everyday life activities, so they can please the one who chose them to be a soldier.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="2 Timothy 2:5">5</a> If someone competes in contests, they are not awarded a crown unless they compete according to the rules.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="2 Timothy 2:6">6</a> The farmer who works hard should be the first to enjoy the fruits of his work.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="2 Timothy 2:7">7</a> Think about what I say, and may God help you understand everything.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="2 Timothy 2:8">8</a> Remember that Jesus Christ, who came from David&#39;s family, was brought back to life as told in my good news.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="2 Timothy 2:9">9</a> I face hardship as if I were a criminal, even to the point of being chained; yet God&#39;s message is not chained.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="2 Timothy 2:10">10</a> I put up with everything for the chosen people&#39;s sake, so they can also find the salvation in Christ Jesus that comes with eternal glory.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="2 Timothy 2:11">11</a> This saying is true: If we died with him, we will also live with him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="2 Timothy 2:12">12</a> If we go through hard times, we will also rule with him; if we reject him, he will also reject us.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="2 Timothy 2:13">13</a> If we don&#39;t believe, he remains faithful; he cannot contradict himself.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="2 Timothy 2:14">14</a> Remind them of these things and tell them in God&#39;s presence not to argue about words. It doesn&#39;t help anyone and ruins those who listen.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="2 Timothy 2:15">15</a> Make an effort to be acceptable to God, a worker with no need to be ashamed, properly understanding and teaching the true message.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="2 Timothy 2:16">16</a> Stay away from disrespectful and pointless talk; it leads to more wrong behavior.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="2 Timothy 2:17">17</a> Their teaching will spread like a disease. It includes Hymenaeus and Philetus.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="2 Timothy 2:18">18</a> They are wrong about the truth, claiming the resurrection has already happened, and they destroy the faith of some.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="2 Timothy 2:19">19</a> God&#39;s foundation stands firm, with this mark: God knows who belongs to Him. And anyone who calls on Christ&#39;s name should turn away from wrongdoing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="2 Timothy 2:20">20</a> In a big house, there are not just containers of gold and silver, but also of wood and clay; some are for special use, and some for everyday use.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="2 Timothy 2:21">21</a> If a person cleans himself from these things, he will be a vessel for honor, made holy, and ready for the owner&#39;s use, prepared for every good action.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="2 Timothy 2:22">22</a> Run away from young people&#39;s strong desires. But chase after living right, trusting, being kind, and peace with those who ask God for help with a clean heart.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="2 Timothy 2:23">23</a> Stay away from silly and ignorant questions, because they lead to arguments.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="2 Timothy 2:24">24</a> God&#39;s servant should not argue, but be kind to everyone, able to teach, and patient.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="2 Timothy 2:25">25</a> Gently teach those who disagree with themselves; if perhaps God will grant them a change of heart to recognize the truth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="2 Timothy 2:26">26</a> So they can escape the devil&#39;s trap, who has captured them to do his will.</p>
