<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="2 Timothy 3:1">1</a> Understand that in the last days, dangerous times will come.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="2 Timothy 3:2">2</a> People will love themselves, desire too much, brag, be arrogant, disrespect God, not obey parents, not be thankful, and not be respectful.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="2 Timothy 3:3">3</a> Unloving, promise-breakers, liars, without self-control, violent, haters of the good,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="2 Timothy 3:4">4</a> People who betray others, act without thinking, are full of themselves, and love fun more than they love God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="2 Timothy 3:5">5</a> They act religious but reject its true power. Stay away from these people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="2 Timothy 3:6">6</a> These people sneak into homes and take control of weak-willed women burdened with sins and swayed by various desires.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="2 Timothy 3:7">7</a> Always learning, but never able to understand the truth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="2 Timothy 3:8">8</a> Just as Jannes and Jambres opposed Moses, so these people also reject the truth: they are corrupt thinkers and fail to accept the faith.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="2 Timothy 3:9">9</a> They won&#39;t get any further: everyone will see their foolishness, just as others&#39; was seen.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="2 Timothy 3:10">10</a> You fully know my teaching, way of life, goal, faith, endurance, love, and patience.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="2 Timothy 3:11">11</a> Hard times and troubles that happened to me in Antioch, Iconium, and Lystra, the tough situations I went through: but God rescued me from them all.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="2 Timothy 3:12">12</a> Everyone who wants to live a good life in Christ Jesus will face troubles.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="2 Timothy 3:13">13</a> Bad people and tricksters will become more and more wicked, cheating others and being cheated themselves.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="2 Timothy 3:14">14</a> Keep following the teachings you have learned and are sure of, knowing who taught them to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="2 Timothy 3:15">15</a> From childhood, you have known the sacred writings that can teach you to be saved through faith in Christ Jesus.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="2 Timothy 3:16">16</a> All scripture comes from God&#39;s inspiration and is useful for teaching, for showing mistakes, for improving, and for training in right living.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="2 Timothy 3:17">17</a> So the person who serves God can be complete, fully equipped for every good action.</p>
