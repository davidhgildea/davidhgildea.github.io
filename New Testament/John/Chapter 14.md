<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="John 14:1">1</a> Don&#39;t be worried in your heart; you trust in God, trust also in me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="John 14:2">2</a> In my Father&#39;s house, there are many homes. If it wasn&#39;t true, I would have told you. I am going to get a place ready for you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="John 14:3">3</a> If I go and get a place ready for you, I will come back and take you with me; so you can be where I am too.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="John 14:4">4</a> You know where I&#39;m going and you know the way.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="John 14:5">5</a> Thomas said to him, &quot;Lord, we don&#39;t know where you are going; how can we know the way?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="John 14:6">6</a> Jesus said to him, &quot;I am the way, the truth, and the life. No one comes to the Father except through me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="John 14:7">7</a> If you had known me, you would have known my Father too. From now on, you do know him and have seen him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="John 14:8">8</a> Philip said to him, &quot;Lord, show us the Father, and that will be enough for us.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="John 14:9">9</a> Jesus said to him, &quot;Have I been with you for so long, and you still don&#39;t know me, Philip? Whoever has seen me has seen God; so why do you say, &#39;Show us God&#39;?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="John 14:10">10</a> Don&#39;t you believe that I am in God, and God is in me? The words I tell you are not just from me: it&#39;s God living in me who does the works.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="John 14:11">11</a> Trust that I am in God, and God is in me; if not, trust because of the things I have done.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="John 14:12">12</a> Truly, truly, I tell you, whoever believes in me will do the works I do, and they will do even greater works, because I am going to my Father.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="John 14:13">13</a> Whatever you ask for in my name, I will do it so that the Father may be honored through the Son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="John 14:14">14</a> If you ask for anything in my name, I will do it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="John 14:15">15</a> If you love me, follow my commands.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="John 14:16">16</a> I will ask God, and he will give you another Helper to be with you always.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="John 14:17">17</a> The Spirit of truth, whom the world cannot accept because it does not see or know him: but you know him, for he lives with you and will be in you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="John 14:18">18</a> I will not leave you alone: I will come to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="John 14:19">19</a> Soon, the world will not see me; but you will see me. Because I am alive, you will live too.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="John 14:20">20</a> On that day, you will understand that I am with my Father, and you are with me, and I am with you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="John 14:21">21</a> The one who has my commands and follows them truly loves me; and the one who loves me will be loved by my Father, and I will love him and show myself to him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="John 14:22">22</a> Judas, not Iscariot, asked him, &quot;Lord, how will you show yourself to us but not to the world?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="John 14:23">23</a> Jesus replied, &quot;If someone loves me, he will follow my teachings. Then my Father will love him, and we will come to him and live with him.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="John 14:24">24</a> Anyone who doesn&#39;t love me doesn&#39;t follow my teachings. The message you hear isn&#39;t mine, but it comes from the Father who sent me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="John 14:25">25</a> I have said these things to you while I am still with you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="John 14:26">26</a> The Helper, the Holy Spirit, whom God will send in my name, will teach you everything and remind you of all I have told you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="John 14:27">27</a> I leave you peace, my peace I give you. Not the way the world does, do I give to you. Don&#39;t let your heart be troubled or afraid.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="John 14:28">28</a> You have heard me say I will leave and come back to you. If you loved me, you would be happy because I am going to the Father, who is greater than me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="John 14:29">29</a> I have told you beforehand so that when it happens, you may believe.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="John 14:30">30</a> From now on, I won&#39;t talk much with you because the ruler of this world is coming, and he has no power over me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="John 14:31">31</a> I want the world to know I love God; I follow His commands. Let&#39;s get up and go.</p>
