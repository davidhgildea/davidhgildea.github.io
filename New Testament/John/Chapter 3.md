<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="John 3:1">1</a> A Pharisee named Nicodemus was a Jewish leader.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="John 3:2">2</a> He came to Jesus at night and said, &quot;Teacher, we know you have come from God because no one can do the miracles you do unless God is with him.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="John 3:3">3</a> Jesus replied, &quot;Truly, I tell you, unless a person is born again, they cannot see God&#39;s kingdom.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="John 3:4">4</a> Nicodemus said to him, &quot;How can an old man be born again? Can he go back into his mother&#39;s womb and be born a second time?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="John 3:5">5</a> Jesus replied, &quot;Truly, truly, I tell you, unless someone is born of water and the Spirit, they cannot enter God&#39;s kingdom.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="John 3:6">6</a> What comes from the body is body; and what comes from the Spirit is spirit.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="John 3:7">7</a> Don&#39;t be surprised when I tell you, you must be born again.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="John 3:8">8</a> The wind blows where it wants, and you hear the sound of it, but you can&#39;t tell where it comes from or where it goes: so it is with everyone born of the Spirit.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="John 3:9">9</a> Nicodemus replied and asked him, &quot;How can this happen?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="John 3:10">10</a> Jesus answered him, &quot;Are you a teacher of Israel and don&#39;t know these things?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="John 3:11">11</a> Truly, truly, I tell you, we talk about what we know and share what we have seen, but you do not accept our testimony.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="John 3:12">12</a> If I have spoken to you about things of this world and you do not believe, how will you believe if I speak to you about things of heaven?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="John 3:13">13</a> No one has gone up to heaven except the one who came down from heaven, the Son of Man who is in heaven.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="John 3:14">14</a> Just as Moses lifted up the snake in the desert, the Son of Man must also be lifted up.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="John 3:15">15</a> Everyone who believes in him will not die but have forever life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="John 3:16">16</a> God loved the world so much that he gave his only Son, so that everyone who believes in him will not die but have eternal life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="John 3:17">17</a> God did not send his Son into the world to judge it, but to save the world through him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="John 3:18">18</a> Whoever believes in him is not judged, but whoever does not believe is already judged, because they have not believed in the name of God&#39;s only Son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="John 3:19">19</a> This is the judgment: Light has come into the world, but people loved darkness more than light because their actions were bad.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="John 3:20">20</a> Everyone who does bad things hates the light and doesn&#39;t come to the light, so their actions won&#39;t be criticized.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="John 3:21">21</a> But the person who does what is true comes to the light, so that it can be clearly seen that their actions have been done through God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="John 3:22">22</a> After this, Jesus and his followers went to the area of Judea; there he stayed with them and performed baptisms.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="John 3:23">23</a> John was also baptizing in Aenon near Salim, because there was plenty of water there; so people came and were baptized.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="John 3:24">24</a> John had not yet been put in prison.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="John 3:25">25</a> A dispute about how to purify oneself arose between some of John&#39;s followers and the Jewish people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="John 3:26">26</a> They went to John and said, &quot;Teacher, the one you talked about on the other side of the Jordan River is now baptizing, and everyone is going to him.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="John 3:27">27</a> John replied, &quot;A person can only get what is given to them from heaven.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="John 3:28">28</a> You can testify that I said I am not the Christ, but I am sent ahead of him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="John 3:29">29</a> The one with the bride is the groom; but the groom&#39;s friend, who stands by and listens, is very happy to hear the groom&#39;s voice. So my happiness is complete.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="John 3:30">30</a> He must become greater, but I must become less.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="John 3:31">31</a> The one who comes from above is greater than all; the one who is from the earth belongs to the earth and talks about earthly things. The one who comes from heaven is greater than all.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="John 3:32">32</a> What he has seen and heard, he tells; but no one accepts what he says.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="John 3:33">33</a> The one who accepts what he has said shows that God is truthful.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="John 3:34">34</a> The one God sends speaks God&#39;s words because God gives the Spirit without limit to him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="John 3:35">35</a> God loves the Son and has given everything into his control.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="John 3:36">36</a> Anyone who believes in the Son has eternal life; but anyone who doesn&#39;t believe in the Son won&#39;t have life, and God&#39;s anger stays on them.</p>
