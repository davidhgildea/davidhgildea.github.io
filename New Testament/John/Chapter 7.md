<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="John 7:1">1</a> After this, Jesus stayed in Galilee because he did not want to be in Judea where the Jewish leaders wanted to kill him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="John 7:2">2</a> The Jewish festival of shelters was coming soon.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="John 7:3">3</a> His brothers told him, &quot;Leave this place and go to Judea, so your followers can see what you are doing.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="John 7:4">4</a> No one does things secretly if they want to be known publicly. If you do these things, let everyone see you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="John 7:5">5</a> His brothers did not believe in him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="John 7:6">6</a> Then Jesus told them, &quot;My time has not come yet, but your time is always here.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="John 7:7">7</a> The world cannot hate you; but it hates me because I say that its actions are evil.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="John 7:8">8</a> Go to this festival; I am not going yet because my time has not fully come.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="John 7:9">9</a> After he spoke to them, he stayed in Galilee.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="John 7:10">10</a> After his brothers left, he also went to the festival, not publicly, but in secret.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="John 7:11">11</a> During the festival, the Jewish people looked for him and asked, &quot;Where is he?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="John 7:12">12</a> Many people whispered about him: some said, &quot;He is good,&quot; while others said, &quot;No, he tricks the people.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="John 7:13">13</a> However, no one talked about him publicly because they were afraid of the Jewish leaders.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="John 7:14">14</a> During the festival, Jesus went to the temple and taught.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="John 7:15">15</a> The Jews were amazed and asked, &quot;How does this man know so much when he has never been taught?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="John 7:16">16</a> Jesus told them, &quot;My teaching is not my own, but belongs to the one who sent me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="John 7:17">17</a> If anyone chooses to do God&#39;s will, they will understand the teaching and know whether it comes from God or if I am speaking on my own.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="John 7:18">18</a> Someone who talks about themselves wants their own praise. But if a person seeks to honor the one who sent them, they are true, and there&#39;s nothing wrong in them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="John 7:19">19</a> Didn&#39;t Moses give you the law, but none of you follow it? Why do you want to kill me?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="John 7:20">20</a> The people replied, &quot;You are crazy: who is trying to kill you?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="John 7:21">21</a> Jesus replied to them, &quot;I did one thing, and you are all amazed.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="John 7:22">22</a> Moses gave you the practice of circumcision; (though it didn&#39;t start with Moses, but with the ancestors;) and you even circumcise a man on the Sabbath day.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="John 7:23">23</a> If a man is circumcised on the Sabbath so the law of Moses isn&#39;t broken, why are you mad at me for healing a man completely on the Sabbath?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="John 7:24">24</a> Do not judge by how things look, but judge with fair judgment.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="John 7:25">25</a> Some people from Jerusalem asked, &quot;Isn&#39;t this the man they want to kill?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="John 7:26">26</a> Look, he speaks openly, and no one says anything to him. Do the leaders really know that he is the Christ?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="John 7:27">27</a> We know where this man comes from, but when the Christ comes, no one will know where he is from.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="John 7:28">28</a> Jesus shouted in the temple as he was teaching, saying, &quot;You all know me, and you know where I&#39;m from. I didn&#39;t come on my own, but the one who sent me is real, and you don&#39;t know him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="John 7:29">29</a> I know him because I come from him, and he sent me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="John 7:30">30</a> They tried to seize him, but no one touched him because his time had not yet come.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="John 7:31">31</a> Many people believed in him and asked, &quot;When the Messiah comes, will he perform more miracles than this man has?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="John 7:32">32</a> The Pharisees heard the people whispering these things about him; so the Pharisees and the head priests sent guards to arrest him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="John 7:33">33</a> Jesus said to them, &quot;I will be with you a little longer, then I will go to the one who sent me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="John 7:34">34</a> You will look for me but won&#39;t find me; and you can&#39;t go where I am.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="John 7:35">35</a> Then the Jews said to each other, &quot;Where is he planning to go that we can&#39;t find him? Will he go to the scattered Jews among the non-Jews and teach the non-Jews?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="John 7:36">36</a> What does he mean by saying, &quot;You will look for me, but you won&#39;t find me: and you can&#39;t come where I am&quot;?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="John 7:37">37</a> On the final day, the big feast day, Jesus stood up and shouted, &quot;If anyone is thirsty, come to me and drink.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="John 7:38">38</a> Whoever believes in me, just as the scripture has said, streams of living water will flow from within them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="John 7:39">39</a> He said this about the Spirit, which believers in him would receive: The Spirit had not yet come, because Jesus had not yet been glorified.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="John 7:40">40</a> So when the people heard this, many said, &quot;Truly, this is the Prophet.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="John 7:41">41</a> Some people said, &quot;This is the Christ.&quot; But others asked, &quot;Does the Christ come from Galilee?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="John 7:42">42</a> Doesn&#39;t the scripture say that Christ will come from David&#39;s family, and from Bethlehem, the town where David lived?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v43"><a class="verse-num" href="#v43" aria-label="John 7:43">43</a> People were divided because of him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v44"><a class="verse-num" href="#v44" aria-label="John 7:44">44</a> Some of them wanted to seize him, but no one touched him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v45"><a class="verse-num" href="#v45" aria-label="John 7:45">45</a> The officers went to the chief priests and Pharisees, and they asked, &quot;Why didn&#39;t you bring him?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v46"><a class="verse-num" href="#v46" aria-label="John 7:46">46</a> The officers replied, &quot;No one ever spoke like this person.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v47"><a class="verse-num" href="#v47" aria-label="John 7:47">47</a> The Pharisees replied, &quot;Have you been tricked too?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v48"><a class="verse-num" href="#v48" aria-label="John 7:48">48</a> Have any leaders or Pharisees believed in him?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v49"><a class="verse-num" href="#v49" aria-label="John 7:49">49</a> But these people who do not know the law are cursed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v50"><a class="verse-num" href="#v50" aria-label="John 7:50">50</a> Nicodemus, who had visited Jesus at night and was one of the group, said to them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v51"><a class="verse-num" href="#v51" aria-label="John 7:51">51</a> Does our law judge anyone before it hears him and knows what he is doing?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v52"><a class="verse-num" href="#v52" aria-label="John 7:52">52</a> They replied to him, &quot;Are you from Galilee too? Look and see: no prophet comes from Galilee.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v53"><a class="verse-num" href="#v53" aria-label="John 7:53">53</a> Everyone went to their own home.</p>
