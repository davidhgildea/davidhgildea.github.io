<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="John 5:1">1</a> After this, the Jews had a festival, and Jesus went to Jerusalem.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="John 5:2">2</a> In Jerusalem near the sheep trading area, there is a pool named Bethesda in Hebrew, with five covered walkways.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="John 5:3">3</a> In this place, many sick people, blind, lame, and paralyzed, waited for the water to move.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="John 5:4">4</a> An angel sometimes went into the pool and stirred up the water. The first person to get in after the water was stirred was healed of any illness they had.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="John 5:5">5</a> A man was there who had been sick for thirty-eight years.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="John 5:6">6</a> When Jesus saw him lying there and knew he had been sick for a long time, he asked him, &quot;Do you want to get well?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="John 5:7">7</a> The weak man said to him, &quot;Sir, I have no one to help me into the pool when the water is stirred, and while I am trying to get there, someone else goes in before me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="John 5:8">8</a> Jesus said to him, &quot;Get up, pick up your mat, and walk.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="John 5:9">9</a> Right away, the man got well, picked up his mat, and walked. That day was the Sabbath.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="John 5:10">10</a> The Jewish people said to the man who was healed, &quot;It is the Sabbath day; you are not allowed to carry your bed.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="John 5:11">11</a> He replied, &quot;The one who healed me told me, &#39;Pick up your mat and walk.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="John 5:12">12</a> Then they asked him, &quot;Who is the man who told you, &#39;Pick up your mat and walk&#39;?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="John 5:13">13</a> The healed man did not know who had cured him, because Jesus had slipped away due to the crowd in that place.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="John 5:14">14</a> Later, Jesus found him in the temple and said to him, &quot;Look, you are healed. Don&#39;t sin again or something worse may happen to you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="John 5:15">15</a> The man left and told the Jews that it was Jesus who had healed him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="John 5:16">16</a> So the Jews were against Jesus and wanted to kill him because he did these things on the Sabbath.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="John 5:17">17</a> But Jesus replied, &quot;My Father has always been at work to this very day, and I too am working.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="John 5:18">18</a> So the Jews wanted even more to kill him, because he not only broke the Sabbath but also claimed that God was his Father, making himself equal to God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="John 5:19">19</a> Jesus replied to them, &quot;I tell you the truth, the Son can do nothing by himself. He only does what he sees God doing. Whatever God does, the Son does the same.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="John 5:20">20</a> God loves the Son and shows him everything he does: and he will show him even greater things than these, so that you will be amazed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="John 5:21">21</a> Just as God brings the dead back to life, the Son also gives life to whoever he chooses.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="John 5:22">22</a> God does not judge anyone, but has given all judgment to the Son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="John 5:23">23</a> Everyone should respect the Son just like they respect the Father. If someone doesn&#39;t respect the Son, they don&#39;t respect the Father who sent him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="John 5:24">24</a> Truly, truly, I tell you, whoever hears my word and believes in the one who sent me, has eternal life and will not be judged; but has moved from death to life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="John 5:25">25</a> Truly, truly, I tell you, the time is coming, and it is now, when the dead will hear the voice of the Son of God, and those who hear will live.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="John 5:26">26</a> Just as the Father has life in himself, so he has granted the Son to have life in himself.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="John 5:27">27</a> God has given him power to judge because he is the Son of Man.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="John 5:28">28</a> Don&#39;t be surprised: a time will come when all in the graves will hear his voice.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="John 5:29">29</a> People who have done good will rise to live; those who have done bad will rise to be judged.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="John 5:30">30</a> I can do nothing by myself; I make judgments based on what I hear, and my judgments are fair because I don&#39;t follow my own desires, but the desires of God who sent me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="John 5:31">31</a> If I speak for myself, my words are not true.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="John 5:32">32</a> Someone else speaks for me, and I know that what he says about me is true.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="John 5:33">33</a> You sent people to John, and he told the truth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="John 5:34">34</a> I don&#39;t accept praise from people, but I tell you this so you can be saved.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="John 5:35">35</a> He was a bright and shining light, and for a while you were happy to enjoy his light.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="John 5:36">36</a> I have proof stronger than John&#39;s: the tasks God gave me to complete, which I am doing, show that God has sent me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="John 5:37">37</a> God, who sent me, has testified about me. You have never heard his voice or seen his form.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="John 5:38">38</a> You don&#39;t keep God&#39;s message inside you, because you don&#39;t believe the one he sent.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="John 5:39">39</a> Look in the Bible; you believe it has the words for eternal life, and it tells about me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="John 5:40">40</a> You don&#39;t come to me to have life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="John 5:41">41</a> I do not seek praise from people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="John 5:42">42</a> But I know you do not have God&#39;s love in you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v43"><a class="verse-num" href="#v43" aria-label="John 5:43">43</a> I came in my Father&#39;s name, but you do not accept me. If someone else comes using his own name, you will accept him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v44"><a class="verse-num" href="#v44" aria-label="John 5:44">44</a> How can you believe when you accept praise from each other but don&#39;t look for the praise that comes only from God?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v45"><a class="verse-num" href="#v45" aria-label="John 5:45">45</a> Don&#39;t believe I will blame you before God: Moses, who you trust, is the one who accuses you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v46"><a class="verse-num" href="#v46" aria-label="John 5:46">46</a> If you had believed Moses, you would believe me because he wrote about me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v47"><a class="verse-num" href="#v47" aria-label="John 5:47">47</a> If you don&#39;t believe what he wrote, how will you believe what I say?</p>
