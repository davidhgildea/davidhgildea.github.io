<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="John 12:1">1</a> Six days before Passover, Jesus went to Bethany, where Lazarus lived, whom he had brought back to life after death.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="John 12:2">2</a> There they prepared a dinner for him; Martha served, while Lazarus was one of those who sat at the table with him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="John 12:3">3</a> Mary took a pound of very expensive spikenard perfume, put it on Jesus&#39; feet, and wiped his feet with her hair. The smell of the perfume filled the house.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="John 12:4">4</a> One of his followers, Judas Iscariot, the son of Simon, who would later betray him, said</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="John 12:5">5</a> Why wasn&#39;t this perfume sold for three hundred coins and given to the poor?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="John 12:6">6</a> He said this not because he cared for the poor, but because he was a thief, held the money bag, and took what was placed in it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="John 12:7">7</a> Jesus said, Leave her alone; she saved this for the day of my burial.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="John 12:8">8</a> You will always have poor people with you, but you will not always have me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="John 12:9">9</a> Many Jewish people knew he was there, so they came not just for Jesus, but also to see Lazarus whom he brought back to life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="John 12:10">10</a> The leading priests planned to kill Lazarus too.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="John 12:11">11</a> Because of him, many Jews left and believed in Jesus.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="John 12:12">12</a> The next day, many people who had come to the celebration heard that Jesus was heading to Jerusalem.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="John 12:13">13</a> They took palm tree branches, went out to meet him, and shouted, &quot;Hosanna: Blessed is the King of Israel who comes in God&#39;s name.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="John 12:14">14</a> Jesus found a young donkey and sat on it, as the scripture says.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="John 12:15">15</a> Don&#39;t be afraid, people of Zion: look, your King is coming, riding on a young donkey.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="John 12:16">16</a> At first, his disciples did not understand these things. But after Jesus was honored, they remembered that these things were written about him, and that they had happened to him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="John 12:17">17</a> The people with him when he called Lazarus out of his tomb and woke him from the dead, witnessed it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="John 12:18">18</a> The people came to meet him because they heard he had performed this miracle.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="John 12:19">19</a> The Pharisees then said to each other, &quot;Do you see that we are not succeeding at all? Look, everyone is following him.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="John 12:20">20</a> Some Greeks who were there to worship at the festival were among them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="John 12:21">21</a> This person came to Philip from Bethsaida in Galilee, and asked, &quot;Sir, we want to see Jesus.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="John 12:22">22</a> Philip came and told Andrew, and then Andrew and Philip told Jesus.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="John 12:23">23</a> Jesus replied to them, &quot;The time has come for the Son of man to be honored.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="John 12:24">24</a> Truly, truly, I tell you, unless a grain of wheat falls into the soil and dies, it stays single; but if it dies, it produces lots of seeds.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="John 12:25">25</a> Whoever loves their life will lose it; and whoever hates their life in this world will keep it for eternal life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="John 12:26">26</a> If someone serves me, he should follow me; and where I am, my servant will be too: if someone serves me, God will honor him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="John 12:27">27</a> My soul is troubled; what should I say? Father, rescue me from this time. Yet this is why I came to this moment.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="John 12:28">28</a> Father, make your name honored. Then a voice from heaven spoke, &quot;I have honored it, and I will honor it again.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="John 12:29">29</a> The people standing nearby who heard it said it thundered; others said an angel spoke to him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="John 12:30">30</a> Jesus replied, &quot;This voice was not for me, but for you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="John 12:31">31</a> The time has come to judge this world; now the ruler of this world will be thrown out.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="John 12:32">32</a> If I am raised up from the earth, I will attract everyone to me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="John 12:33">33</a> He said this to show what kind of death he was going to die.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="John 12:34">34</a> The people replied, &quot;We know from the law that Christ will live forever. So why do you say that the Son of Man must be raised up? Who is this Son of Man?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="John 12:35">35</a> Jesus told them, &quot;The light will be with you for just a short time. Walk while you have the light, before darkness overtakes you. Anyone walking in darkness doesn&#39;t know where they are going.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="John 12:36">36</a> While you have light, trust in it, so you can be children of light. After saying this, Jesus left and hid from them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="John 12:37">37</a> Even though he had performed many miracles in front of them, they still did not believe in him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="John 12:38">38</a> So that what the prophet Isaiah said would come true, he asked, &quot;God, who has believed what we said? And to whom has God shown his power?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="John 12:39">39</a> So they couldn&#39;t believe, because Isaiah had said before,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="John 12:40">40</a> God has made their eyes blind and their hearts hard; so they cannot see with their eyes or understand with their hearts, and turn to me so I can heal them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="John 12:41">41</a> Isaiah said these things when he saw God&#39;s glory and spoke about him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="John 12:42">42</a> Many leaders also believed in him, but they did not admit it because of the Pharisees; they were afraid of being expelled from the synagogue.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v43"><a class="verse-num" href="#v43" aria-label="John 12:43">43</a> They liked people&#39;s approval more than God&#39;s approval.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v44"><a class="verse-num" href="#v44" aria-label="John 12:44">44</a> Jesus cried out and said, &quot;Anyone who believes in me does not just believe in me, but in the one who sent me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v45"><a class="verse-num" href="#v45" aria-label="John 12:45">45</a> Whoever sees me sees the one who sent me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v46"><a class="verse-num" href="#v46" aria-label="John 12:46">46</a> I have come as a light into the world so that everyone who believes in me will not stay in darkness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v47"><a class="verse-num" href="#v47" aria-label="John 12:47">47</a> If anyone hears my words and does not believe, I do not judge them, because I did not come to judge the world, but to save it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v48"><a class="verse-num" href="#v48" aria-label="John 12:48">48</a> Whoever rejects me and does not accept my words has something that will judge them: the words I have spoken will be their judge on the last day.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v49"><a class="verse-num" href="#v49" aria-label="John 12:49">49</a> I don&#39;t speak for myself; the Father who sent me commanded me what to say and speak.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v50"><a class="verse-num" href="#v50" aria-label="John 12:50">50</a> I know that God&#39;s command leads to eternal life. So, whatever I say is just as the Father told me to say.</p>
