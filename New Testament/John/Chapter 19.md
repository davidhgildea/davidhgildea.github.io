<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="John 19:1">1</a> Then Pilate took Jesus and whipped him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="John 19:2">2</a> The soldiers twisted a thorn crown, put it on his head, and dressed him in a purple robe.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="John 19:3">3</a> They said, &quot;Hello, King of the Jews!&quot; and hit him with their hands.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="John 19:4">4</a> Pilate came out again and said to them, &quot;Look, I am bringing him out to you to show that I find no fault in him.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="John 19:5">5</a> Jesus came out wearing a crown of thorns and a purple robe. Pilate said to them, &quot;Look at the man!&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="John 19:6">6</a> So when the religious leaders and their helpers saw him, they shouted, &quot;Crucify him, crucify him!&quot; Pilate told them, &quot;You take him and crucify him, because I don&#39;t find him guilty.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="John 19:7">7</a> The Jews told him, &quot;We have a law, and according to our law, he should die because he claimed to be God&#39;s Son.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="John 19:8">8</a> When Pilate heard this, he became even more afraid.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="John 19:9">9</a> He went back into the court and asked Jesus, &quot;Where do you come from?&quot; But Jesus did not answer him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="John 19:10">10</a> Then Pilate said to him, &quot;Don&#39;t you talk to me? Don&#39;t you know that I have the power to crucify you and the power to set you free?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="John 19:11">11</a> Jesus answered, &quot;You would have no power over me if it weren&#39;t given to you from above. So the one who handed me over to you has a bigger sin.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="John 19:12">12</a> From then on, Pilate tried to set him free, but the Jews shouted, &quot;If you let this man go, you&#39;re not loyal to Caesar. Anyone who claims to be a king is against Caesar.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="John 19:13">13</a> When Pilate heard this, he brought Jesus outside and sat on the judge&#39;s bench at a place called the Pavement, which in Hebrew is Gabbatha.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="John 19:14">14</a> It was the time to get ready for the Passover, about six in the morning, when he said to the Jewish people, &quot;Look, here is your King!&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="John 19:15">15</a> But they shouted, &quot;Take him away, take him away, crucify him!&quot; Pilate said to them, &quot;Should I crucify your King?&quot; The chief priests answered, &quot;We have no king but Caesar.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="John 19:16">16</a> So he handed Jesus over to them to be crucified. They took Jesus and led him away.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="John 19:17">17</a> He carried his cross to a place known as Skull Hill, which in Hebrew is called Golgotha.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="John 19:18">18</a> They crucified him there with two others, one on each side, and Jesus in the middle.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="John 19:19">19</a> Pilate wrote a sign and put it on the cross. It said, &quot;Jesus of Nazareth, the King of the Jews.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="John 19:20">20</a> Many of the Jewish people read this title because the place where Jesus was crucified was close to the city. It was written in Hebrew, Greek, and Latin.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="John 19:21">21</a> The Jewish leaders told Pilate, &quot;Don&#39;t write &#39;The King of the Jews,&#39; but write that he claimed, &#39;I am King of the Jews.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="John 19:22">22</a> Pilate answered, &quot;What I&#39;ve written, I&#39;ve written.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="John 19:23">23</a> After the soldiers crucified Jesus, they took his clothes and divided them into four parts, giving each soldier a part. They also took his coat, which was seamless and woven in one piece from top to bottom.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="John 19:24">24</a> So they said to each other, &quot;Let&#39;s not tear it. Let&#39;s throw dice to see who gets it.&quot; This was to fulfill what was written: &quot;They divided my clothes among them and cast lots for my garment.&quot; And that&#39;s what the soldiers did.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="John 19:25">25</a> Near Jesus&#39; cross stood his mother, her sister, Mary married to Cleophas, and Mary Magdalene.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="John 19:26">26</a> When Jesus saw his mother and his close friend standing nearby, he said to his mother, &quot;Woman, here is your son!&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="John 19:27">27</a> Then he said to the follower, &quot;Here is your mother!&quot; And from that time, the follower took her into his home.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="John 19:28">28</a> After this, Jesus, knowing that everything was done and the scripture would be complete, said, &quot;I am thirsty.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="John 19:29">29</a> A jar full of sour wine was there; they soaked a sponge in the sour wine, put it on a stalk of hyssop, and held it up to his mouth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="John 19:30">30</a> When Jesus took the vinegar, he said, &quot;It is finished.&quot; Then he bowed his head and passed away.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="John 19:31">31</a> So the Jewish leaders, because it was the day to get ready, and since they did not want bodies to stay on the crosses during the Sabbath (which was a special day), asked Pilate to have their legs broken and the bodies removed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="John 19:32">32</a> Then the soldiers came and broke the legs of the first man and the other who was crucified with him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="John 19:33">33</a> But when they reached Jesus and saw he was already dead, they did not break his legs.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="John 19:34">34</a> A soldier stabbed his side with a spear, and blood and water flowed out right away.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="John 19:35">35</a> The one who saw it has testified, and his testimony is true: and he knows he&#39;s telling the truth, so you may believe.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="John 19:36">36</a> These things happened so that what was written would come true: None of his bones will be broken.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="John 19:37">37</a> Another part of the Bible says, &quot;They will look at the one they stabbed.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="John 19:38">38</a> After this, Joseph from Arimathaea, who followed Jesus but kept it secret because he was afraid of the Jewish leaders, asked Pilate for permission to take Jesus&#39;s body. Pilate allowed it, so he came and took Jesus&#39;s body away.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="John 19:39">39</a> Nicodemus, who first visited Jesus at night, also came, bringing about a hundred pounds of a mix of myrrh and aloes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="John 19:40">40</a> They took Jesus&#39; body and wrapped it in linen with spices, as is the Jewish way to bury.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="John 19:41">41</a> Near where he was crucified was a garden, and in it, a new tomb where no one had been laid before.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="John 19:42">42</a> They placed Jesus there because it was the Jewish day of preparation, and the tomb was close by.</p>
