<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="John 1:1">1</a> At the start, the Word existed, the Word was with God, and the Word was God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="John 1:2">2</a> He was with God from the start.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="John 1:3">3</a> God made everything; nothing was made without him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="John 1:4">4</a> In him was life, and that life was the light for everyone.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="John 1:5">5</a> The light shines in the dark, but the dark did not understand it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="John 1:6">6</a> God sent a man named John.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="John 1:7">7</a> He came as a witness to tell about the Light, so that everyone might believe through him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="John 1:8">8</a> He was not the Light, but came to testify about the Light.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="John 1:9">9</a> That was the real Light, giving light to everyone coming into the world.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="John 1:10">10</a> He was in the world, and he made the world, but the world did not know him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="John 1:11">11</a> He came to his own people, but they did not accept him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="John 1:12">12</a> But as many as accepted him, to them he gave the right to become God&#39;s children, even to those who believe in his name.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="John 1:13">13</a> They were born, not from human origin or human desire, but from God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="John 1:14">14</a> The Word became a human and lived with us, and we saw his glory, the glory of God&#39;s one and only Son, full of kindness and truth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="John 1:15">15</a> John gave witness about him, shouting, &quot;This is who I was talking about when I said, &#39;The one who comes after me ranks higher than me because he existed before me.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="John 1:16">16</a> From his fullness, we have all received one blessing after another.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="John 1:17">17</a> Moses gave the law, but Jesus Christ brought grace and truth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="John 1:18">18</a> No one has ever seen God, but the unique Son, who is close to the Father&#39;s heart, has made him known.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="John 1:19">19</a> This is John&#39;s testimony when the Jewish leaders sent priests and Levites from Jerusalem to ask him, &quot;Who are you?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="John 1:20">20</a> He admitted and did not deny; he admitted, I am not the Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="John 1:21">21</a> They asked him, &quot;What then? Are you Elijah?&quot; He said, &quot;I am not. Are you the prophet?&quot; He answered, &quot;No.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="John 1:22">22</a> They asked him, &quot;Who are you? We need to give an answer to those who sent us. What do you say about yourself?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="John 1:23">23</a> He said, &quot;I am someone shouting in the desert, &#39;Prepare the path for God,&#39; just as the prophet Isaiah said.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="John 1:24">24</a> The people who were sent came from the Pharisees.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="John 1:25">25</a> They asked him, &quot;Why do you baptize if you are not the Messiah, Elijah, or the prophet?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="John 1:26">26</a> John replied to them, &quot;I baptize with water, but there is someone among you who you do not recognize.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="John 1:27">27</a> He is the one who comes after me, but is greater than me, and I am not good enough to untie his sandal.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="John 1:28">28</a> These things happened in Bethabara across the Jordan River, where John was baptizing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="John 1:29">29</a> The next day John saw Jesus coming toward him and said, &quot;Look, the Lamb of God, who takes away the world&#39;s sin.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="John 1:30">30</a> This is the person I was talking about. A man comes after me who is more important than I am because he existed before me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="John 1:31">31</a> I did not know him, but I came to baptize with water so that he might be shown to Israel.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="John 1:32">32</a> John testified, saying, &quot;I saw the Spirit come down from heaven like a dove, and it stayed on him.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="John 1:33">33</a> I did not know him, but the one who sent me to baptize with water told me, &#39;The person you see the Spirit coming down on and staying with is the one who baptizes with the Holy Spirit.&#39;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="John 1:34">34</a> I saw and confirmed that this is God&#39;s Son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="John 1:35">35</a> The next day John was there again with two of his followers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="John 1:36">36</a> Seeing Jesus walking, he said, Look, the Lamb of God!</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="John 1:37">37</a> The two followers heard him speak, and they followed Jesus.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="John 1:38">38</a> Jesus turned around, saw them following, and asked, &quot;What are you looking for?&quot; They answered, &quot;Teacher, where are you staying?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="John 1:39">39</a> He said to them, &quot;Come and see.&quot; They came and saw where he lived and stayed with him that day; it was about four o&#39;clock in the afternoon.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="John 1:40">40</a> One of the two people who heard John and followed him was Andrew, Simon Peter&#39;s brother.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="John 1:41">41</a> He first finds his brother Simon and tells him, &quot;We have found the Messiah,&quot; which means Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="John 1:42">42</a> He took him to Jesus. When Jesus saw him, he said, &quot;You are Simon, son of Jonah. You will be called Cephas,&quot; which means a rock.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v43"><a class="verse-num" href="#v43" aria-label="John 1:43">43</a> The next day Jesus decided to go to Galilee, where he met Philip and said to him, &quot;Follow me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v44"><a class="verse-num" href="#v44" aria-label="John 1:44">44</a> Philip came from Bethsaida, the hometown of Andrew and Peter.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v45"><a class="verse-num" href="#v45" aria-label="John 1:45">45</a> Philip found Nathanael and told him, &quot;We have found the one Moses in the law, and the prophets wrote about, Jesus from Nazareth, Joseph&#39;s son.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v46"><a class="verse-num" href="#v46" aria-label="John 1:46">46</a> Nathanael asked him, &quot;Can anything good come from Nazareth?&quot; Philip answered, &quot;Come and see.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v47"><a class="verse-num" href="#v47" aria-label="John 1:47">47</a> Jesus saw Nathanael coming to him and said about him, &quot;Look, a true Israelite, who is not deceitful!&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v48"><a class="verse-num" href="#v48" aria-label="John 1:48">48</a> Nathanael asked him, &quot;How do you know me?&quot; Jesus replied, &quot;Before Philip called you, when you were under the fig tree, I saw you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v49"><a class="verse-num" href="#v49" aria-label="John 1:49">49</a> Nathanael answered him, &quot;Teacher, you are God&#39;s Son; you are the king of Israel.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v50"><a class="verse-num" href="#v50" aria-label="John 1:50">50</a> Jesus replied, &quot;Do you believe because I told you I saw you under the fig tree? You will see things even greater than that.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v51"><a class="verse-num" href="#v51" aria-label="John 1:51">51</a> He said to him, &quot;Truly, truly, I tell you, you will see heaven open and God&#39;s angels going up and down on the Son of Man.&quot;</p>
