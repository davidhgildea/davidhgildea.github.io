<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="John 15:1">1</a> I am the real vine, and my Father takes care of it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="John 15:2">2</a> Every branch in me that does not produce fruit, God removes. Every branch that does produce fruit, God cleans so it will make even more fruit.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="John 15:3">3</a> You are clean now because of the words I have spoken to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="John 15:4">4</a> Stay with me, and I with you. Just as a branch can&#39;t produce fruit alone without being part of the vine, you can&#39;t either unless you stay with me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="John 15:5">5</a> I am the vine, you are the branches. Anyone who remains in me, and I in them, will produce a lot of fruit. Without me, you can do nothing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="John 15:6">6</a> If someone does not remain in me, he is thrown away like a dead branch; people pick it up, throw it into the fire, and it burns.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="John 15:7">7</a> If you remain in me and my words stay in you, you can ask for anything you want, and it will be done for you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="John 15:8">8</a> In this, my Father is honored: when you produce a lot of fruit, then you are truly my followers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="John 15:9">9</a> Just as the Father loves me, I also love you. Stay in my love.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="John 15:10">10</a> If you follow my commands, you will remain in my love, just as I follow my Father&#39;s commands and remain in his love.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="John 15:11">11</a> I have told you these things so that my joy stays in you and your joy is complete.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="John 15:12">12</a> My command is this: Love each other as I have loved you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="John 15:13">13</a> No one has greater love than to give up their life for their friends.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="John 15:14">14</a> You are my friends if you do everything I tell you to do.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="John 15:15">15</a> From now on, I don&#39;t call you servants, because a servant doesn&#39;t know what their master is doing. Instead, I have called you friends, because everything I learned from my Father, I have told you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="John 15:16">16</a> You did not choose me, but I chose you and appointed you to go and produce lasting fruit. So whatever you ask of God in my name, he will give to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="John 15:17">17</a> I instruct you to love each other.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="John 15:18">18</a> If the world hates you, know that it hated me before it hated you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="John 15:19">19</a> If you belonged to the world, the world would love you as its own. But you do not belong to the world, because I have chosen you out of the world. That&#39;s why the world hates you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="John 15:20">20</a> Remember what I told you: A servant isn&#39;t above their master. If they have mistreated me, they will mistreat you too; if they have followed my teachings, they will follow yours as well.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="John 15:21">21</a> They will do all these things to you because of me, since they do not know the one who sent me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="John 15:22">22</a> If I hadn&#39;t come and spoken to them, they wouldn&#39;t be guilty of sin; but now they can&#39;t hide their sin.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="John 15:23">23</a> Whoever hates me also hates my Father.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="John 15:24">24</a> If I had not done works among them that no one else did, they would not be guilty of sin. But now they have seen and hated both me and my Father.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="John 15:25">25</a> This happens so that what is written in their law comes true: They hated me for no reason.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="John 15:26">26</a> But when the Helper comes, whom I will send to you from God, the Spirit of truth who goes out from God, he will tell about me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="John 15:27">27</a> You will also testify because you have been with me from the start.</p>
