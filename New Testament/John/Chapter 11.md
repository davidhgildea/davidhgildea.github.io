<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="John 11:1">1</a> A man named Lazarus from the town of Bethany, where Mary and her sister Martha lived, was sick.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="John 11:2">2</a> Mary, who put perfume on Jesus and dried his feet with her hair, had a brother named Lazarus who was ill.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="John 11:3">3</a> So his sisters sent a message to him, saying, &quot;Lord, look, the one you love is sick.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="John 11:4">4</a> When Jesus heard this, he said, &quot;This illness will not lead to death. It is for God&#39;s glory, so that God&#39;s Son may be honored through it.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="John 11:5">5</a> Jesus loved Martha, her sister, and Lazarus.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="John 11:6">6</a> After hearing that he was sick, he stayed two more days in the same place he was.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="John 11:7">7</a> After that, he said to his disciples, &quot;Let&#39;s go back to Judea.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="John 11:8">8</a> His followers asked him, &quot;Teacher, recently the Jews wanted to stone you; and you&#39;re going back there?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="John 11:9">9</a> Jesus answered, &quot;Isn&#39;t there twelve hours in a day? If someone walks during the day, they won&#39;t stumble because they see the world&#39;s light.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="John 11:10">10</a> If someone walks at night, they trip because there&#39;s no light inside them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="John 11:11">11</a> He said these things, and then he told them, &quot;Our friend Lazarus is sleeping; but I am going to wake him up.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="John 11:12">12</a> Then his followers said, &quot;Master, if he is sleeping, he will get better.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="John 11:13">13</a> However, Jesus was talking about his death; but they thought he was talking about resting by sleeping.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="John 11:14">14</a> Jesus told them clearly, &quot;Lazarus is dead.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="John 11:15">15</a> I am happy for you that I wasn&#39;t there, so you might believe; but let&#39;s go to him now.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="John 11:16">16</a> Then Thomas, also known as Didymus, told the other disciples, &quot;Let&#39;s go too, so we can die with him.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="John 11:17">17</a> When Jesus arrived, he found that the man had been in the tomb for four days.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="John 11:18">18</a> Bethany was close to Jerusalem, about fifteen furlongs away.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="John 11:19">19</a> Many Jews visited Martha and Mary to comfort them about their brother.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="John 11:20">20</a> Martha, when she heard Jesus was coming, went to meet him; but Mary stayed in the house.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="John 11:21">21</a> Martha said to Jesus, &quot;Lord, if you had been here, my brother would not have died.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="John 11:22">22</a> I know that even now, whatever you ask from God, He will give it to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="John 11:23">23</a> Jesus said to her, Your brother will live again.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="John 11:24">24</a> Martha said to him, &quot;I know he will rise again in the resurrection on the last day.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="John 11:25">25</a> Jesus told her, &quot;I am the one who brings people back to life, and I am life itself. Anyone who believes in me will live, even if they die.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="John 11:26">26</a> Anyone who lives and believes in me will never die. Do you believe this?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="John 11:27">27</a> She said to him, &quot;Yes, Lord: I believe that you are the Christ, the Son of God, who was to come into the world.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="John 11:28">28</a> After saying this, she left and quietly told her sister Mary, &quot;The Teacher has arrived and is asking for you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="John 11:29">29</a> As soon as she heard that, she got up fast and went to him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="John 11:30">30</a> Jesus had not entered the town yet; he was still in the place where Martha met him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="John 11:31">31</a> The Jews who were with her in the house, comforting her, saw Mary get up quickly and leave. They followed her, saying, &quot;She is going to the tomb to cry.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="John 11:32">32</a> When Mary arrived where Jesus was and saw him, she fell at his feet and said, &quot;Lord, if you had been here, my brother would not have died.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="John 11:33">33</a> When Jesus saw her crying and the Jews who came with her crying too, he felt deeply moved and was upset.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="John 11:34">34</a> And asked, &quot;Where have you placed him?&quot; They replied, &quot;Sir, come and see.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="John 11:35">35</a> Jesus cried.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="John 11:36">36</a> Then the Jewish people said, &quot;Look how much he cared for him!&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="John 11:37">37</a> Some of them asked, &quot;Couldn&#39;t this man, who made blind people see, have stopped this man from dying?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="John 11:38">38</a> Jesus, feeling deep sadness again, came to the tomb. It was a cave with a stone placed over the entrance.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="John 11:39">39</a> Jesus said, &quot;Remove the stone.&quot; Martha, the sister of the man who had died, said to him, &quot;God, by now there will be a bad smell because he has been dead for four days.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="John 11:40">40</a> Jesus said to her, &quot;Didn&#39;t I tell you that if you believed, you would see God&#39;s glory?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="John 11:41">41</a> They removed the stone from where the dead person was placed. Jesus looked up and said, &quot;Father, I thank you for hearing me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="John 11:42">42</a> I know you always hear me, but I said this for the people here, so they will believe you sent me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v43"><a class="verse-num" href="#v43" aria-label="John 11:43">43</a> After he said this, he shouted, &quot;Lazarus, come out!&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v44"><a class="verse-num" href="#v44" aria-label="John 11:44">44</a> The dead man came out, tied up at the hands and feet with burial cloths, and his face was wrapped in a cloth. Jesus told them, &quot;Untie him and let him go.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v45"><a class="verse-num" href="#v45" aria-label="John 11:45">45</a> Many Jews who visited Mary and saw what Jesus did started to believe in him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v46"><a class="verse-num" href="#v46" aria-label="John 11:46">46</a> Some of them went to the Pharisees and told them what Jesus had done.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v47"><a class="verse-num" href="#v47" aria-label="John 11:47">47</a> The chief priests and Pharisees met and said, &quot;What should we do? This man performs many miracles.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v48"><a class="verse-num" href="#v48" aria-label="John 11:48">48</a> If we leave him alone, everyone will believe in him, and the Romans will come and take away our positions and our country.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v49"><a class="verse-num" href="#v49" aria-label="John 11:49">49</a> One of them, named Caiaphas, who was the high priest that year, told them, &quot;You don&#39;t understand anything.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v50"><a class="verse-num" href="#v50" aria-label="John 11:50">50</a> We must realize it&#39;s better for us that one man dies for the people, so the whole nation doesn&#39;t get destroyed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v51"><a class="verse-num" href="#v51" aria-label="John 11:51">51</a> He didn&#39;t say this on his own; as the high priest that year, he predicted that Jesus would die for the nation.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v52"><a class="verse-num" href="#v52" aria-label="John 11:52">52</a> Not just for that nation, but also to unite all of God&#39;s children who were spread out.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v53"><a class="verse-num" href="#v53" aria-label="John 11:53">53</a> From that day on, they planned together to kill him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v54"><a class="verse-num" href="#v54" aria-label="John 11:54">54</a> So Jesus didn&#39;t walk openly among the Jews anymore. Instead, he went to a region near the wilderness, to a city named Ephraim, and stayed there with his followers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v55"><a class="verse-num" href="#v55" aria-label="John 11:55">55</a> The Jewish Passover was soon to happen, and many people left the countryside to go to Jerusalem before the Passover to make themselves clean.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v56"><a class="verse-num" href="#v56" aria-label="John 11:56">56</a> They looked for Jesus and asked each other as they stood in the temple, &quot;What do you think, will he not come to the celebration?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v57"><a class="verse-num" href="#v57" aria-label="John 11:57">57</a> The chief priests and the Pharisees had ordered that if anyone knew where he was, they should tell so they could arrest him.</p>
