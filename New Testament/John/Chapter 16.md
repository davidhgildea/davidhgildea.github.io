<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="John 16:1">1</a> I have told you these things so you won&#39;t be upset.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="John 16:2">2</a> They will throw you out of the synagogues; yes, a time is coming when anyone who kills you will think they are serving God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="John 16:3">3</a> They will do these things to you because they do not know the Father or me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="John 16:4">4</a> I have told you these things so that when the time comes, you will remember that I warned you. I didn&#39;t tell you this from the start because I was with you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="John 16:5">5</a> Now I am going to the one who sent me, and none of you is asking me, &quot;Where are you going?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="John 16:6">6</a> Because I told you this, sadness has filled your heart.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="John 16:7">7</a> I&#39;m telling you the truth: It&#39;s better for you that I leave. If I don&#39;t go, the Helper won&#39;t come to you. But if I go, I&#39;ll send him to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="John 16:8">8</a> When he comes, he will show the world their sin, what is right, and how they are judged.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="John 16:9">9</a> About sin, because they do not believe in me;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="John 16:10">10</a> About being right with God, because I am going to my Father, and you won&#39;t see me again;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="John 16:11">11</a> About judgment, because the leader of this world has been judged.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="John 16:12">12</a> I have many more things to tell you, but you can&#39;t handle them now.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="John 16:13">13</a> However, when the Spirit of truth comes, he will lead you to all truth. He won&#39;t speak on his own but will say what he hears, and he will show you what is to come.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="John 16:14">14</a> He will honor me because he will take from what is mine and show it to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="John 16:15">15</a> Everything that the Father has is mine; that&#39;s why I said He will take what&#39;s mine and show it to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="John 16:16">16</a> Soon you will not see me, but then soon after, you will see me again because I am going to the Father.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="John 16:17">17</a> Some of his followers asked each other, &quot;What does he mean by saying, &#39;In a little while, you won&#39;t see me, but then after a little while, you will see me,&#39; and &#39;Because I am going to the Father&#39;?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="John 16:18">18</a> So they asked, &quot;What does he mean by &#39;a little while&#39;? We don&#39;t understand what he is saying.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="John 16:19">19</a> Jesus knew they wanted to ask him questions. He said to them, &quot;Are you wondering about what I meant when I said, &#39;Soon you won&#39;t see me, and then after a little while, you will see me again&#39;?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="John 16:20">20</a> Truly, truly, I tell you, you will cry and be sad, while the world will be happy. You will feel sorrow, but your sorrow will become joy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="John 16:21">21</a> A woman feels pain when she is giving birth because her time has arrived; but once the baby is born, she forgets the pain because she is happy that a baby has come into the world.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="John 16:22">22</a> You are sad now, but I will see you again, and you will be happy, and no one will take away your happiness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="John 16:23">23</a> On that day you will not ask me anything. Truly, I tell you, whatever you ask God in my name, he will give to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="John 16:24">24</a> Until now, you have asked for nothing in my name; ask and you will receive, so that your happiness may be complete.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="John 16:25">25</a> I have told you these things in riddles, but a time will come when I will not use riddles. Instead, I will tell you clearly about God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="John 16:26">26</a> On that day you will ask in my name, and I am not saying that I will ask God for you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="John 16:27">27</a> God loves you himself because you have loved me and believed that I came from God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="John 16:28">28</a> I came from the Father and entered the world; now I leave the world and return to the Father.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="John 16:29">29</a> His followers said to him, &quot;Look, now you are speaking clearly and not using difficult sayings.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="John 16:30">30</a> We are now certain that you know everything and don&#39;t need anyone to question you. This makes us believe that you came from God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="John 16:31">31</a> Jesus answered them, &quot;Do you now believe?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="John 16:32">32</a> Soon, you will all go your separate ways and leave me alone, but I am not really alone because God is with me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="John 16:33">33</a> I told you these things so you can have peace in me. You will face trouble in the world, but stay positive; I have beaten the world.</p>
