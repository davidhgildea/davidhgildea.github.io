<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 4:1">1</a> We ask you, friends, and encourage you in Jesus&#39; name, to live as we taught you, so you can please God and keep growing in it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 4:2">2</a> You know the commands we gave you from Jesus.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 4:3">3</a> God wants you to be holy and avoid sexual immorality.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 4:4">4</a> Each of you should know how to live with your body in a way that is holy and respectful.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 4:5">5</a> Do not follow strong sexual desires, like the non-believers who do not know God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 4:6">6</a> Let no one take advantage of or cheat their fellow in any matter; for God punishes all such acts, as we have already warned and confirmed to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 4:7">7</a> God has not called us to be unclean, but to be holy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 4:8">8</a> So, if someone disrespects, they don&#39;t disrespect people but God, who has also given us his Holy Spirit.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 4:9">9</a> Regarding loving others like brothers, you don&#39;t need me to write to you, because God has taught you to love each other.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 4:10">10</a> You are showing love to all God&#39;s people in Macedonia, but we urge you, friends, to do so more and more.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 4:11">11</a> Work hard and mind your own business, just as we told you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 4:12">12</a> Live honestly with those outside your group, so you won&#39;t need anything from others.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 4:13">13</a> I want you to understand, friends, about those who have died, so you won&#39;t be sad like others who have no hope.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 4:14">14</a> If we believe that Jesus died and came back to life, then God will also bring with him those who have died believing in Jesus.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 4:15">15</a> We tell you, by God&#39;s message, that those of us who are alive and left until God&#39;s return will not go ahead of those who have died.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 4:16">16</a> God will come down from heaven with a shout, with the archangel&#39;s voice, and with God&#39;s trumpet: and those who believed in Christ and have died will come back to life first.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 4:17">17</a> We who are alive and left will be lifted up with them in the clouds to meet God in the sky, and we will always be with God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 4:18">18</a> Therefore, encourage each other with these words.</p>
