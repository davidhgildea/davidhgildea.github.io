<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 1:1">1</a> Paul, Silvanus, and Timothy, to the Thessalonian church in God and Jesus Christ: may you have grace and peace from God our Father and Jesus Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 1:2">2</a> We always thank God for all of you, remembering you in our prayers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 1:3">3</a> We constantly remember your faith in action, love&#39;s hard work, and hopeful patience in our Lord Jesus Christ, before God our Father.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 1:4">4</a> Dear friends, know that God has chosen you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 1:5">5</a> Our good news didn&#39;t come to you just by words, but also with power, God&#39;s Spirit, and strong confidence; as you know how we acted among you for your benefit.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 1:6">6</a> You followed our example and God&#39;s, accepting the message in great trouble yet with Holy Spirit&#39;s joy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 1:7">7</a> So you became examples to all the believers in Macedonia and Achaia.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 1:8">8</a> From you, the message of God has spread not only in Macedonia and Achaia but also everywhere your faith in God is known; so we don&#39;t need to say anything more.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 1:9">9</a> People tell how we came to you, and how you turned to God from idols to serve the true and living God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 1:10">10</a> To wait for his Son from heaven, whom God raised from the dead, Jesus who saves us from the coming anger.</p>
