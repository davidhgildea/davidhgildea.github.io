<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 5:1">1</a> About the times and dates, friends, you don&#39;t need me to write to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 5:2">2</a> You know very well that the day of God will come like a thief in the night.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 5:3">3</a> When people say, &quot;Peace and safety,&quot; then sudden ruin will hit them, like labor pains on a pregnant woman, and they won&#39;t escape.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 5:4">4</a> But friends, you are not in the dark, so that the day should surprise you like a thief.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 5:5">5</a> You are all children of light and of the day; we are not of the night or of darkness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 5:6">6</a> So let&#39;s not sleep like others; instead, let&#39;s stay alert and clear-headed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 5:7">7</a> People who sleep, sleep at night; and those who get drunk, get drunk at night.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 5:8">8</a> Let us who live in the light be clear-minded, wearing faith and love as a protective vest, and the hope of being saved as a helmet.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 5:9">9</a> God has not chosen us for anger, but to gain salvation through our Lord Jesus Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 5:10">10</a> He died for us so that, awake or asleep, we might live with him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 5:11">11</a> Encourage each other and build each other up, just as you are already doing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 5:12">12</a> We ask you, friends, to recognize those who work hard among you, who guide you in the Lord, and who warn you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 5:13">13</a> Value them greatly with love because of their work and live peacefully with each other.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 5:14">14</a> We urge you, friends, to warn those who are not following the rules, encourage those who are discouraged, help those who are weak, and be patient with everyone.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 5:15">15</a> Make sure no one pays back wrong for wrong to anyone; but always try to do what is good, for each other and for everyone else.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 5:16">16</a> Be happy always.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 5:17">17</a> Keep praying all the time.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 5:18">18</a> In everything, be thankful: because this is what God wants for you in Christ Jesus.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 5:19">19</a> Do not put out the Spirit&#39;s fire.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 5:20">20</a> Do not look down on prophecies.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 5:21">21</a> Test everything; keep what is good.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 5:22">22</a> Stay away from anything that looks evil.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 5:23">23</a> May the God of peace make you completely holy, and I hope God keeps your spirit, soul, and body without fault until our Lord Jesus Christ comes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 5:24">24</a> God, who calls you, is faithful and will do what he promises.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 5:25">25</a> Brothers and sisters, pray for us.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 5:26">26</a> Say hello to all the brothers and sisters with a holy kiss.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 5:27">27</a> I ask you seriously in God&#39;s name to make sure this letter is read to all the believers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 5:28">28</a> May the kindness of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you. Amen.</p>
