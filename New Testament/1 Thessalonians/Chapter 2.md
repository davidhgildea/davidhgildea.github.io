<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 2:1">1</a> You know, friends, that our visit to you was not a waste.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 2:2">2</a> Even though we had already suffered and were badly treated in Philippi, as you know, we were brave in our faith to tell you God&#39;s good news, despite strong opposition.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 2:3">3</a> Our encouragement did not come from lies, impurity, or trickery.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 2:4">4</a> God trusted us with the good news, so we speak to please not people, but God who tests our hearts.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 2:5">5</a> We never used flattery or were greedy, and you know it; God saw it all.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 2:6">6</a> We did not seek praise from people, not from you or anyone else, even though we could have made demands as Christ&#39;s apostles.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 2:7">7</a> We were kind to you, just like a nurse who takes care of her children.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 2:8">8</a> We really wanted to share with you, not only God&#39;s message, but also our own lives because you were so important to us.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 2:9">9</a> Brothers and sisters, you remember our hard work and struggle: because we worked day and night and didn&#39;t want to be a burden to any of you, we shared with you God&#39;s message.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 2:10">10</a> You are witnesses, and God too, how we acted holy, justly, and without blame among you who believe.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 2:11">11</a> You know how we encouraged, comforted, and urged each of you, like a father does his children.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 2:12">12</a> You should live in a way that honors God, who invites you to His kingdom and splendor.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 2:13">13</a> We always thank God because when you accepted the message you heard from us, you didn&#39;t think of it as human words but as God&#39;s truth, which also powerfully works in those who believe.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 2:14">14</a> You, brothers and sisters, followed the example of God&#39;s churches in Judea that are in Christ Jesus: you also suffered from your own people, just as they did from the Jews.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 2:15">15</a> They killed both Jesus and their own prophets and have persecuted us; they do not please God and oppose everyone.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 2:16">16</a> Stopping us from talking to the non-Jews so they can be saved, to make their sins fully complete: because God&#39;s anger has fully come upon them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 2:17">17</a> We, brothers, were separated from you for a little while in person, not in spirit, and tried very hard to see you because we really wanted to.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 2:18">18</a> We wanted to come to you, I, Paul, tried more than once; but Satan stopped us.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 2:19">19</a> What is our hope, joy, or reason to be proud? Aren&#39;t you with our Lord Jesus Christ when he comes?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="1 Thessalonians 2:20">20</a> You are our pride and happiness.</p>
