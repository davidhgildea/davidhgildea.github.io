<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 John 3:1">1</a> Look at how much love God has given us, so we can be called God&#39;s children. That&#39;s why the world doesn&#39;t recognize us, because it didn&#39;t recognize him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 John 3:2">2</a> Friends, now we are children of God, and it&#39;s not yet clear what we will become. But we know that when he comes, we will be like him because we will see him as he truly is.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 John 3:3">3</a> Everyone who has this hope in him makes himself clean, just as God is clean.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 John 3:4">4</a> Anyone who sins also breaks the law, because sin is breaking the law.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 John 3:5">5</a> You know he appeared to remove our sins, and he has no sin.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 John 3:6">6</a> Anyone who stays true to God does not sin; anyone who sins has not seen or known God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 John 3:7">7</a> Kids, don&#39;t let anyone trick you: if someone does what is right, they are good, just like God is good.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 John 3:8">8</a> Anyone who sins belongs to the devil because the devil has been sinning from the start. The Son of God came to destroy the devil&#39;s work.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 John 3:9">9</a> Anyone born of God does not keep on sinning because God&#39;s nature stays in them; they can&#39;t keep sinning because they are born of God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 John 3:10">10</a> This shows who are God&#39;s children and who are the devil&#39;s children: anyone who does not act righteously is not from God, nor is the one who does not love their brother.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 John 3:11">11</a> This is the message you have heard from the start: we should love each other.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 John 3:12">12</a> Unlike Cain, who belonged to the evil one and killed his brother. And why did he kill him? Because his actions were bad, and his brother&#39;s were good.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 John 3:13">13</a> Do not be surprised, my friends, if the world hates you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="1 John 3:14">14</a> We understand that we have moved from death to life because we love our fellow believers. Whoever does not love their sibling remains in death.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="1 John 3:15">15</a> Anyone who hates their brother or sister is a killer, and you know that no killer has eternal life in them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="1 John 3:16">16</a> We understand God&#39;s love because He gave up His life for us, and we should be willing to give up our lives for others.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="1 John 3:17">17</a> If someone has what they need in this world and sees their brother in need but does not care for him, how can God&#39;s love be in that person?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="1 John 3:18">18</a> My dear children, let&#39;s not love just with words or speech, but with actions and truth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="1 John 3:19">19</a> We know we belong to the truth, and we can calm our hearts before God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="1 John 3:20">20</a> If we feel guilty, God is bigger than our feelings and knows everything.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="1 John 3:21">21</a> Dear friends, if we don&#39;t feel guilty, we have trust in God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="1 John 3:22">22</a> Whatever we ask for, we get from God because we follow his commands and do what makes him happy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="1 John 3:23">23</a> This is God&#39;s command: we must believe in his Son, Jesus Christ, and love each other as he told us to.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="1 John 3:24">24</a> Whoever follows his commands lives in God, and God in him. This is how we know God remains in us: by the Spirit he gave us.</p>
