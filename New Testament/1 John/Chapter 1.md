<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 John 1:1">1</a> What was there from the start, which we have heard, seen with our own eyes, and touched with our hands, is about the message of life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 John 1:2">2</a> The life appeared, we have seen it, and we tell you about it. We show you the eternal life that was with God and was shown to us.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 John 1:3">3</a> We tell you what we&#39;ve seen and heard so you can join us. Our friendship is with God and his Son Jesus Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 John 1:4">4</a> We write these things to you so that your joy can be complete.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 John 1:5">5</a> We heard a message from him and now tell you: God is light, with no darkness at all.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 John 1:6">6</a> If we claim to have a close relationship with God but live in the wrong way, we are lying and not living by the truth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 John 1:7">7</a> But if we live in the truth, as he is in the truth, we share a bond with each other, and the blood of Jesus Christ his Son cleans us from all sin.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 John 1:8">8</a> If we claim we have no sin, we lie to ourselves, and we don&#39;t have the truth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 John 1:9">9</a> If we admit our sins, God is loyal and fair to forgive us our sins, and to make us clean from all wrongdoing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 John 1:10">10</a> If we say we haven&#39;t sinned, we call God a liar, and his message isn&#39;t in us.</p>
