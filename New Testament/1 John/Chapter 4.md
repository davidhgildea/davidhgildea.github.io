<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 John 4:1">1</a> Dear friends, do not believe all spirits, but test the spirits to see if they are from God, for many false prophets have spread throughout the world.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 John 4:2">2</a> You know God&#39;s Spirit if it says Jesus Christ came as a human.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 John 4:3">3</a> Any spirit that does not admit Jesus Christ has come as a human is not from God. This is the spirit of the enemy of Christ you have heard about, which is already in the world.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 John 4:4">4</a> You belong to God, little children, and have defeated them: because the one in you is greater than the one in the world.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 John 4:5">5</a> They belong to the world, so they talk about worldly things, and the world listens to them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 John 4:6">6</a> We belong to God: anyone who knows God listens to us; anyone who does not belong to God does not listen to us. This is how we know the Spirit of truth and the spirit of falsehood.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 John 4:7">7</a> Dear friends, let&#39;s love each other, because love comes from God. Everyone who loves is God&#39;s child and understands God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 John 4:8">8</a> Whoever does not love does not know God, because God is love.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 John 4:9">9</a> God showed his love for us by sending his one and only Son into the world so we could have life through him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 John 4:10">10</a> Love is this: not that we loved God, but that he loved us and sent his Son to be the sacrifice for our sins.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 John 4:11">11</a> Dear friends, if God loved us so much, we should also love each other.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 John 4:12">12</a> No one has ever seen God. If we love each other, God lives in us and his love becomes complete in us.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 John 4:13">13</a> We know we live in God and God lives in us because he has given us his Spirit.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="1 John 4:14">14</a> We have seen and tell others that God sent his Son to save the world.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="1 John 4:15">15</a> Anyone who says Jesus is the Son of God lives with God, and God lives with him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="1 John 4:16">16</a> We know and trust God&#39;s love for us. God is love; anyone living in love lives in God, and God lives in them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="1 John 4:17">17</a> Our love is made complete by this, so that we can be confident on judgment day: because we are like him in this world.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="1 John 4:18">18</a> Love has no fear; true love gets rid of fear because fear brings pain. Someone who is afraid doesn&#39;t have complete love.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="1 John 4:19">19</a> We love God because God loved us first.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="1 John 4:20">20</a> If someone says, &quot;I love God,&quot; but hates his brother, he is lying. If he doesn&#39;t love his brother who he can see, how can he love God who he cannot see?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="1 John 4:21">21</a> We have this rule from God: Anyone who loves God should love their brother too.</p>
