<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="James 3:1">1</a> My friends, don&#39;t all try to be teachers, because we know that we will be judged more strictly.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="James 3:2">2</a> We all make many mistakes. If someone does not make mistakes when they speak, they are perfect and can control their whole body too.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="James 3:3">3</a> Look, we put pieces in horse&#39;s mouths so they follow us; we guide their whole body.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="James 3:4">4</a> Look at the ships too. Even though they are big and strong winds push them, a tiny rudder can steer them wherever the pilot chooses.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="James 3:5">5</a> The tongue is small, yet it talks about big things. See how a small fire can start something so huge!</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="James 3:6">6</a> The tongue is like a fire; it is full of wrong. It can spoil your whole life and is itself set on fire by hell.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="James 3:7">7</a> Every kind of animal, bird, snake, and sea creature can be tamed and has been tamed by humans.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="James 3:8">8</a> No one can control the tongue; it is a restless evil, filled with deadly poison.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="James 3:9">9</a> We use our words to bless God, the Father, and with those same words, we curse people who are made to look like God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="James 3:10">10</a> From the same mouth come both blessings and curses. My brothers, this should not be the case.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="James 3:11">11</a> Can a spring pour out both fresh and bitter water from the same opening?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="James 3:12">12</a> My friends, can a fig tree produce olives, or a grapevine produce figs? Just as a spring can&#39;t give both salty and fresh water.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="James 3:13">13</a> Who is wise and has knowledge among you? Let them show their good actions with a humble attitude that comes from wisdom.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="James 3:14">14</a> If you feel bitter jealousy and conflict in your hearts, don&#39;t boast or deny the truth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="James 3:15">15</a> This wisdom does not come from heaven but is from the earth, appealing to our senses, and evil.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="James 3:16">16</a> Where jealousy and conflict exist, confusion and all kinds of wrong actions are found.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="James 3:17">17</a> The wisdom from above is first pure, then peaceful, kind, willing to listen, full of mercy and good actions, fair, and sincere.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="James 3:18">18</a> The good results of right living are planted in peace by those who work for peace.</p>
