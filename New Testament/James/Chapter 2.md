<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="James 2:1">1</a> My brothers and sisters, do not combine faith in our Lord Jesus Christ, who is glorious, with favoritism.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="James 2:2">2</a> If a man wearing a gold ring and fine clothes comes into your meeting, and a poor man in dirty clothes also comes in;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="James 2:3">3</a> You favor the one in fancy clothes, telling him, &quot;Sit here in a good spot,&quot; while you tell the poor person, &quot;Stand there,&quot; or, &quot;Sit by my feet.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="James 2:4">4</a> Aren&#39;t you showing favoritism and judging with wrong thoughts?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="James 2:5">5</a> Listen, my dear friends, hasn&#39;t God chosen the world&#39;s poor to be rich in faith and to inherit the kingdom he promised to those who love him?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="James 2:6">6</a> You have looked down on the poor. Don&#39;t rich people bully you and drag you to court?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="James 2:7">7</a> Aren&#39;t they insulting the honorable name you bear?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="James 2:8">8</a> If you follow the important rule in the scripture, &quot;Love your neighbor as yourself,&quot; you are doing well.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="James 2:9">9</a> If you show favoritism, you sin and break the law.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="James 2:10">10</a> Anyone who follows all of God&#39;s laws but fails in one aspect is guilty of breaking them all.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="James 2:11">11</a> For the one who said, &quot;Do not cheat in marriage,&quot; also said, &quot;Do not kill.&quot; So if you don&#39;t cheat but you do kill, you are still breaking the law.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="James 2:12">12</a> Talk and act like people who will be judged by the law of freedom.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="James 2:13">13</a> He will be judged without mercy if he has not been merciful; but mercy triumphs over judgment.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="James 2:14">14</a> What good is it, my friends, if someone claims to have faith but doesn&#39;t act on it? Can faith alone save him?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="James 2:15">15</a> If a brother or sister has no clothes and lacks everyday food,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="James 2:16">16</a> If one of you tells them, &quot;Go in peace, stay warm and well fed,&quot; but doesn’t give them what their body needs, what good is it?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="James 2:17">17</a> Likewise, if faith does not have actions, it is dead by itself.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="James 2:18">18</a> Someone might say, &quot;You have faith, and I do things.&quot; Show me your faith without doing anything, and I&#39;ll show you my faith by what I do.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="James 2:19">19</a> You believe there is one God; that&#39;s good: even demons believe that, and they shake with fear.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="James 2:20">20</a> Do you want to understand, foolish person, that faith without actions is useless?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="James 2:21">21</a> Wasn&#39;t Abraham our father proved right by his actions when he was about to sacrifice his son Isaac on the altar?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="James 2:22">22</a> Do you see how faith worked together with his actions, and through actions faith was made complete?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="James 2:23">23</a> The scripture came true that says, Abraham trusted God, and it was counted to him as being right with God: and he was named God&#39;s Friend.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="James 2:24">24</a> You see then that a person is made right by what they do and not by faith alone.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="James 2:25">25</a> Rahab, the prostitute, was also accepted for her actions when she took in the messengers and sent them off by a different path, right?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="James 2:26">26</a> Just like a body without its spirit is dead, so is faith without actions.</p>
