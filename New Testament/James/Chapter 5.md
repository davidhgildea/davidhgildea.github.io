<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="James 5:1">1</a> Listen now, you rich people, cry and wail because of the troubles that are going to happen to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="James 5:2">2</a> Your wealth has gone bad, and your clothes have been eaten by moths.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="James 5:3">3</a> Your gold and silver have corroded, and their corrosion will be evidence against you and eat your flesh like fire. You have stored up wealth for the last days.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="James 5:4">4</a> Look, the pay of the workers who harvested your fields, which you unfairly kept from them, is calling out; and their cries have reached God&#39;s ears.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="James 5:5">5</a> You have enjoyed life on Earth and lived recklessly; you have filled your hearts, as if it were a day to kill animals for feasting.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="James 5:6">6</a> You condemned and killed the righteous person; he did not fight against you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="James 5:7">7</a> So, my friends, wait patiently for the Lord to come. Think about farmers who wait for their valuable crops to grow. They wait patiently for the rain in the fall and spring.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="James 5:8">8</a> Be patient too; make your hearts strong because God&#39;s coming is near.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="James 5:9">9</a> Do not hold grudges against each other, brothers, or you may be judged: see, the judge is right at the door.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="James 5:10">10</a> Look at the prophets who spoke for God as examples of those who suffered and were patient.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="James 5:11">11</a> Look, we think those who endure are happy. You have heard about Job&#39;s patience, and you know how God&#39;s story ended; God is very compassionate and kind.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="James 5:12">12</a> My friends, don&#39;t make promises using the name of heaven or earth or anything else. Just say &quot;yes&quot; when you mean yes, and &quot;no&quot; when you mean no, so you won&#39;t be judged.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="James 5:13">13</a> If any of you are having trouble, pray. If anyone is happy, sing songs of praise.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="James 5:14">14</a> If anyone among you is sick, they should call for the church leaders to pray over them and anoint them with oil in God&#39;s name.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="James 5:15">15</a> The faithful prayer will heal the sick, God will lift him up, and if he has sinned, he will be forgiven.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="James 5:16">16</a> Admit your mistakes to each other, and pray for each other, so you can be healed. The powerful and earnest prayer of a good person has great impact.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="James 5:17">17</a> Elijah was a person with feelings like ours, and he prayed hard for no rain: and it did not rain on the land for three years and six months.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="James 5:18">18</a> He prayed once more, and the sky sent rain, and the land produced its crops.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="James 5:19">19</a> Friends, if anyone of you wanders away from the truth and someone brings them back,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="James 5:20">20</a> Let him understand that one who turns a sinner from the wrong path will save a soul from death and cover many sins.</p>
