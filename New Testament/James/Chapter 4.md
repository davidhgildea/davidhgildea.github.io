<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="James 4:1">1</a> Where do your wars and battles with each other come from? Do they not come from your desires that battle within you?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="James 4:2">2</a> You want things but don&#39;t have them. You kill and are jealous but still cannot get what you want. You argue and fight but don&#39;t have because you don&#39;t ask.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="James 4:3">3</a> You ask but don&#39;t receive because you ask with wrong motives, wanting only to satisfy your desires.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="James 4:4">4</a> You unfaithful men and women, don&#39;t you know that loving this world means being God&#39;s enemy? So if anyone chooses to be the world&#39;s friend, they become God&#39;s enemy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="James 4:5">5</a> Do you think the scripture says without reason that the spirit in us longs for what others have?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="James 4:6">6</a> He gives more kindness. So he says, God opposes the proud, but gives kindness to the humble.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="James 4:7">7</a> Obey God, then. Stand up against the devil, and he will run away from you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="James 4:8">8</a> Come close to God, and He will come close to you. Wash your hands, you sinners, and make your hearts pure, you who are unsure.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="James 4:9">9</a> Feel sad, cry, and weep: may your laughter become crying, and your happiness become sadness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="James 4:10">10</a> Lower yourselves before God, and he will raise you up.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="James 4:11">11</a> Do not speak badly about each other, friends. Whoever talks badly about a friend and judges them, speaks against the rules and judges the rules. But if you judge the rules, you are not following them but judging them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="James 4:12">12</a> There is one lawmaker, who can save and destroy: who are you to judge someone else?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="James 4:13">13</a> Listen, those who say, &quot;Today or tomorrow we will go to this city, stay there for a year, trade, and make a profit&quot;:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="James 4:14">14</a> You don&#39;t know what will happen tomorrow. What is your life? It is like a mist that appears for a short while and then disappears.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="James 4:15">15</a> You should say, &quot;If God wants, we will live and do this or that.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="James 4:16">16</a> But now you are happy with your bragging: all this kind of happiness is bad.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="James 4:17">17</a> So if someone knows what is good to do and does not do it, for them it is wrong.</p>
