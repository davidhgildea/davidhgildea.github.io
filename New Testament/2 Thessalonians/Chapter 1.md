<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="2 Thessalonians 1:1">1</a> Paul, Silvanus, and Timothy, to the church of the Thessalonians in God our Father and Jesus Christ:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="2 Thessalonians 1:2">2</a> May you have grace and peace from God our Father and Jesus Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="2 Thessalonians 1:3">3</a> We must always thank God for you, brothers, because it is right. Your faith is growing a lot, and the love you all have for each other is increasing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="2 Thessalonians 1:4">4</a> We are proud of you among God&#39;s churches because of your patience and faith during all the persecutions and troubles you go through.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="2 Thessalonians 1:5">5</a> This clearly shows God&#39;s fair judgment, so that you can be seen as fit for God&#39;s kingdom, which you are also suffering for.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="2 Thessalonians 1:6">6</a> God thinks it is fair to give trouble to those who trouble you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="2 Thessalonians 1:7">7</a> If you are troubled, find rest with us when the Lord Jesus comes from heaven with his powerful angels.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="2 Thessalonians 1:8">8</a> With fiery flames, punishing those who do not know God and do not follow the teachings of our Lord Jesus Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="2 Thessalonians 1:9">9</a> They will be punished with never-ending ruin, away from God and his mighty glory.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="2 Thessalonians 1:10">10</a> When he comes to receive glory in his holy people and to be marveled at among all who have believed (because you believed our testimony to you) on that day.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="2 Thessalonians 1:11">11</a> So we always pray for you that God will think you worthy of his call, and will complete every good thing you desire and your work of faith with strength.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="2 Thessalonians 1:12">12</a> May our Lord Jesus Christ&#39;s name be honored through you, and you through him, by the kindness of our God and the Lord Jesus Christ.</p>
