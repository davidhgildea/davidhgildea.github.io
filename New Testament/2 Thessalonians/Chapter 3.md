<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="2 Thessalonians 3:1">1</a> Friends, please pray for us, so that God&#39;s message can spread freely and be honored, just like with you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="2 Thessalonians 3:2">2</a> So that we can be rescued from people who are unfair and evil: not everyone has faith.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="2 Thessalonians 3:3">3</a> God is faithful and will make you strong and protect you from evil.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="2 Thessalonians 3:4">4</a> We trust in God about you, that you are and will continue to follow the instructions we give you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="2 Thessalonians 3:5">5</a> May God guide your hearts to love God and to wait patiently for Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="2 Thessalonians 3:6">6</a> We tell you, friends, speak for Jesus Christ and stay away from anyone who does not follow the teachings we gave and does not act right.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="2 Thessalonians 3:7">7</a> You know how you should imitate us: we did not act improperly among you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="2 Thessalonians 3:8">8</a> We did not eat anyone&#39;s bread without paying; we worked hard day and night so we would not be a burden to any of you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="2 Thessalonians 3:9">9</a> Not because we lack the authority, but to be an example for you to imitate us.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="2 Thessalonians 3:10">10</a> Even when we were with you, we told you: If anyone won&#39;t work, they shouldn&#39;t eat.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="2 Thessalonians 3:11">11</a> We hear that some among you are acting out of line, not working at all, but meddling in others&#39; business.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="2 Thessalonians 3:12">12</a> We tell and encourage those people, by Jesus Christ, to work calmly and eat their own food.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="2 Thessalonians 3:13">13</a> Friends, don&#39;t get tired of doing good.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="2 Thessalonians 3:14">14</a> If anyone does not follow our instructions in this letter, take note of that person and do not associate with him, so he may feel ashamed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="2 Thessalonians 3:15">15</a> Don&#39;t see him as an enemy, but warn him as a brother.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="2 Thessalonians 3:16">16</a> May God, the Lord of peace, give you peace at all times and in every way. May God be with all of you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="2 Thessalonians 3:17">17</a> This is my greeting in every letter, all written by me, Paul.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="2 Thessalonians 3:18">18</a> May the kindness of our Lord Jesus Christ be with everyone. Amen.</p>
