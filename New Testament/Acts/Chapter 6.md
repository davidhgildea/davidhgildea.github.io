<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Acts 6:1">1</a> During that time, as the number of followers grew, the Greek-speaking followers complained against the Hebrew-speaking followers because their widows were not being helped every day.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Acts 6:2">2</a> The twelve gathered all the disciples and said, it isn&#39;t right for us to stop teaching God&#39;s word to help with meals.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Acts 6:3">3</a> Brothers, choose seven men from among you who are known to be honest, filled with the Holy Spirit and wise, so we can put them in charge of this task.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Acts 6:4">4</a> We will keep praying and serving the message of God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Acts 6:5">5</a> Everyone liked the idea, so they picked Stephen, a man full of faith and the Holy Spirit, Philip, Prochorus, Nicanor, Timon, Parmenas, and Nicolas from Antioch, who had converted to their faith.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Acts 6:6">6</a> They presented these men to the apostles, and after praying, the apostles placed their hands on them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Acts 6:7">7</a> God&#39;s message spread; the group of followers grew a lot in Jerusalem; and many priests started to follow the faith.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Acts 6:8">8</a> Stephen, full of trust and strength, performed amazing deeds and miracles among the people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Acts 6:9">9</a> Some people from the synagogue called the synagogue of the Freedmen, along with Cyrenians, Alexandrians, and those from Cilicia and Asia, started to argue with Stephen.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Acts 6:10">10</a> They could not argue against the wisdom and spirit he spoke with.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Acts 6:11">11</a> They influenced some people to lie, who said, &quot;We heard him say disrespectful things about Moses and about God.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Acts 6:12">12</a> They roused the people, the elders, and the teachers of the law, then they attacked him, seized him, and took him to the council.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Acts 6:13">13</a> They found lying witnesses who said, &quot;This man never stops speaking against this holy place and the law.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Acts 6:14">14</a> We heard him claim that this Jesus from Nazareth will destroy this place and will alter the traditions that Moses gave us.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Acts 6:15">15</a> Everyone in the council stared at him and saw his face shining like an angel&#39;s.</p>
