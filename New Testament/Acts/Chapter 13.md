<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Acts 13:1">1</a> In the Antioch church, there were some prophets and teachers like Barnabas, Simeon also known as Niger, Lucius from Cyrene, Manaen who grew up with Herod the ruler, and Saul.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Acts 13:2">2</a> While they served God and went without food, the Holy Spirit said, &quot;Set apart Barnabas and Saul for the job I have chosen them for.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Acts 13:3">3</a> After they fasted, prayed, and put their hands on them, they sent them off.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Acts 13:4">4</a> They were sent by the Holy Spirit and went to Seleucia; from there they sailed to Cyprus.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Acts 13:5">5</a> When they were in Salamis, they shared God&#39;s message in the Jewish synagogues and John helped them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Acts 13:6">6</a> After they traveled across the island to Paphos, they met a magician who was a fake prophet and a Jew named Barjesus.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Acts 13:7">7</a> He was with the governor of the region, Sergius Paulus, a wise man. He asked for Barnabas and Saul because he wanted to hear God&#39;s message.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Acts 13:8">8</a> Elymas the magician tried to stop them and wanted to keep the governor from believing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Acts 13:9">9</a> Then Saul, also known as Paul, filled with the Holy Spirit, looked straight at him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Acts 13:10">10</a> And said, &quot;Oh, you are very tricky and evil, you child of the devil and enemy of everything good, will you not stop twisting the true paths of God?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Acts 13:11">11</a> Now, look, God&#39;s power is on you, and you will be blind and not see the sun for a while. Suddenly, he was covered in a fog and darkness, and he walked around looking for someone to guide him by the hand.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Acts 13:12">12</a> When the governor saw what happened, he believed and was amazed at God&#39;s teaching.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Acts 13:13">13</a> When Paul and his friends sailed from Paphos, they arrived at Perga in Pamphylia. John left them and went back to Jerusalem.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Acts 13:14">14</a> After leaving Perga, they arrived in Antioch in Pisidia, entered the synagogue on the Sabbath, and sat down.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Acts 13:15">15</a> After the reading of the law and the prophets, the synagogue leaders sent a message to them, saying, &quot;Friends, if you have any encouraging message for the people, please speak.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Acts 13:16">16</a> Paul stood up, waved his hand, and said, &quot;People of Israel and all who respect God, listen to me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Acts 13:17">17</a> God chose our ancestors, honored the people while they lived as foreigners in Egypt, and with great power led them out of there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Acts 13:18">18</a> For forty years God put up with their behavior in the desert.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Acts 13:19">19</a> After he defeated seven nations in Canaan, he gave their land to them by drawing lots.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Acts 13:20">20</a> After that, he gave them leaders for about 450 years, until Samuel the prophet.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Acts 13:21">21</a> Later they wanted a king, so God gave them Saul, son of Kish, from the tribe of Benjamin, for forty years.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Acts 13:22">22</a> After removing him, God made David their king and testified about him, saying, &quot;I have found David, Jesse&#39;s son, a man who shares my desires and will do everything I want.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Acts 13:23">23</a> God promised to bring Israel a Savior, Jesus, from this man&#39;s descendants.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Acts 13:24">24</a> Before Jesus came, John told all the people of Israel to turn away from their sins and be baptized.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Acts 13:25">25</a> As John finished his work, he asked, &quot;Who do you think I am? I am not the one. But look, someone is coming after me, and I&#39;m not good enough to untie his sandals.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Acts 13:26">26</a> Friends and descendants of Abraham, and all who honor God, this message of salvation is for you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Acts 13:27">27</a> The people living in Jerusalem and their leaders didn&#39;t recognize him or understand what the prophets said, even though they read their words every Sabbath. By condemning him, they made these prophecies come true.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Acts 13:28">28</a> Even though they could not prove he deserved to die, they still asked Pilate to have him killed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Acts 13:29">29</a> After completing all that was written about him, they removed him from the cross and placed him in a tomb.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Acts 13:30">30</a> God raised him from the dead.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Acts 13:31">31</a> He was seen for many days by those who went with him from Galilee to Jerusalem, who now tell the people about him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Acts 13:32">32</a> We share with you good news about the promise given to our ancestors.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Acts 13:33">33</a> God has done what he promised our ancestors by raising Jesus from the dead, as it&#39;s written in the second psalm: &quot;You are my Son; today I have become your father.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Acts 13:34">34</a> About raising him from the dead, never to decay again, he said, I will give you the faithful blessings promised to David.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Acts 13:35">35</a> That&#39;s why it is said in another psalm, &quot;You will not let your Holy One decay.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Acts 13:36">36</a> David served his time by God&#39;s will, then he died, was buried with his ancestors, and his body decayed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Acts 13:37">37</a> But he, who was raised by God, did not decay.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Acts 13:38">38</a> Let it be clear to you then, friends, that through this man the forgiveness of sins is announced to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="Acts 13:39">39</a> By him, all who believe are made right with God from everything that the law of Moses could not make right.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="Acts 13:40">40</a> Be careful, so what the prophets said doesn&#39;t happen to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="Acts 13:41">41</a> Look, you who scoff, be amazed and vanish: because I am doing something in your days, something you will never believe, even if someone explains it to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="Acts 13:42">42</a> After the Jews left the synagogue, the non-Jews asked to hear these teachings the following Sabbath.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v43"><a class="verse-num" href="#v43" aria-label="Acts 13:43">43</a> After the meeting ended, many Jews and believers followed Paul and Barnabas. They talked to them and encouraged them to keep living in God&#39;s kindness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v44"><a class="verse-num" href="#v44" aria-label="Acts 13:44">44</a> On the next Sabbath, nearly the whole city gathered to hear God&#39;s message.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v45"><a class="verse-num" href="#v45" aria-label="Acts 13:45">45</a> When the Jews saw the crowds, they felt jealous and spoke against what Paul was saying, arguing and insulting God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v46"><a class="verse-num" href="#v46" aria-label="Acts 13:46">46</a> Paul and Barnabas became brave and said, &quot;It was important to speak God&#39;s word to you first. But since you reject it and think you&#39;re not worthy of eternal life, now we will speak to other people.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v47"><a class="verse-num" href="#v47" aria-label="Acts 13:47">47</a> God has instructed us, saying, I have made you a light for the non-Jews, so that you may bring salvation to the farthest places of the world.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v48"><a class="verse-num" href="#v48" aria-label="Acts 13:48">48</a> When the non-Jews heard this, they were happy and praised God&#39;s message. All those chosen for eternal life believed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v49"><a class="verse-num" href="#v49" aria-label="Acts 13:49">49</a> God&#39;s message spread all over the area.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v50"><a class="verse-num" href="#v50" aria-label="Acts 13:50">50</a> The Jews convinced the religious and respected women and the city&#39;s leaders to persecute Paul and Barnabas and forced them to leave their region.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v51"><a class="verse-num" href="#v51" aria-label="Acts 13:51">51</a> They brushed the dust from their feet as a warning to them and went to Iconium.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v52"><a class="verse-num" href="#v52" aria-label="Acts 13:52">52</a> The followers were filled with happiness and the Holy Spirit.</p>
