<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Acts 20:1">1</a> After the disturbance stopped, Paul gathered the disciples, hugged them, and left to go to Macedonia.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Acts 20:2">2</a> After he traveled through those areas and encouraged the people a lot, he went to Greece.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Acts 20:3">3</a> He stayed there three months. When the Jews planned to catch him as he prepared to sail to Syria, he decided to go back through Macedonia.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Acts 20:4">4</a> Going with him to Asia were Sopater from Berea; from Thessalonica, Aristarchus and Secundus; Gaius from Derbe; Timothy; and from Asia, Tychicus and Trophimus.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Acts 20:5">5</a> Those who went ahead waited for us in Troas.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Acts 20:6">6</a> We left Philippi after the Festival of Unleavened Bread and reached Troas in five days, where we stayed for seven days.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Acts 20:7">7</a> On the first day of the week, when the followers met to share a meal, Paul spoke to them, planning to leave the next day. He kept talking until midnight.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Acts 20:8">8</a> There were many lamps in the upstairs room where they met.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Acts 20:9">9</a> A young man named Eutychus was sitting in a window and fell into a deep sleep while Paul was speaking for a long time. He fell asleep, dropped from the third story, and was picked up dead.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Acts 20:10">10</a> Paul went down, hugged him, and said, &quot;Don&#39;t worry; he&#39;s still alive.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Acts 20:11">11</a> After he returned, shared food, ate, and talked for many hours until morning, he left.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Acts 20:12">12</a> They brought the young man back alive, and they were very comforted.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Acts 20:13">13</a> We went ahead to the ship and sailed to Assos to pick up Paul, as he had planned to join us there on foot.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Acts 20:14">14</a> When he joined us at Assos, we brought him aboard and sailed to Mitylene.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Acts 20:15">15</a> We sailed from there and reached Chios the next day; the following day we went to Samos and stopped at Trogyllium, and the day after, we arrived at Miletus.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Acts 20:16">16</a> Paul decided to pass by Ephesus because he did not want to stay in Asia. He hurried to try to be in Jerusalem for Pentecost.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Acts 20:17">17</a> From Miletus, he sent a message to Ephesus and invited the church leaders.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Acts 20:18">18</a> When they arrived, he told them, &quot;You are aware, from the first day I arrived in Asia, how I have always acted with you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Acts 20:19">19</a> Serving God with complete humility, and with many tears and trials that happened to me because of the Jews&#39; secret plots.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Acts 20:20">20</a> I held back nothing useful for you, but showed and taught you openly and in homes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Acts 20:21">21</a> Telling Jews and Greeks to turn to God and believe in our Lord Jesus Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Acts 20:22">22</a> And now, I go to Jerusalem, tied by the spirit, not knowing what will happen to me there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Acts 20:23">23</a> The Holy Spirit warns me in every city that chains and troubles are waiting for me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Acts 20:24">24</a> These things do not bother me, and I do not consider my life important to myself, so I can complete my work happily, and the service I received from Lord Jesus, to share the good news of God&#39;s kindness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Acts 20:25">25</a> Now I know that all of you, whom I have taught about God&#39;s kingdom, will not see me again.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Acts 20:26">26</a> Today, I tell you that I am innocent of harming anyone.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Acts 20:27">27</a> I have not avoided telling you everything God wants you to know.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Acts 20:28">28</a> Be careful to look after yourselves and everyone in your group, because the Holy Spirit has made you leaders to care for God&#39;s church that he bought with his blood.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Acts 20:29">29</a> I know that after I leave, fierce wolves will come among you and not be kind to the sheep.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Acts 20:30">30</a> From among you, some will come, saying wrong things to lead followers to themselves.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Acts 20:31">31</a> So be alert and remember that for three years I never stopped warning each of you, day and night, with tears.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Acts 20:32">32</a> Now, friends, I entrust you to God and to his kind message, which can strengthen you and provide you with a share among all who are made holy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Acts 20:33">33</a> I have not desired anyone&#39;s money, gold, or clothes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Acts 20:34">34</a> Yes, you know that these hands have worked for my needs and for those with me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Acts 20:35">35</a> I have shown you in every way, by working hard you must help the weak and remember what Jesus said, &quot;It is better to give than to get.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Acts 20:36">36</a> After saying this, he knelt down and prayed with everyone.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Acts 20:37">37</a> They all cried hard, hugged Paul, and kissed him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Acts 20:38">38</a> They were very sad because he said they would never see him again. And they went with him to the boat.</p>
