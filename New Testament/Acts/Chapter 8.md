<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Acts 8:1">1</a> Saul agreed to his death. There was a big persecution against the church in Jerusalem; and everyone was scattered across Judaea and Samaria, except the apostles.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Acts 8:2">2</a> Faithful people took Stephen to bury him and cried a lot for him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Acts 8:3">3</a> Saul caused great trouble for the church, going into houses and dragging out men and women to put them in jail.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Acts 8:4">4</a> So those who were spread out went all around telling God&#39;s message.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Acts 8:5">5</a> Philip went to the city of Samaria and told the people about Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Acts 8:6">6</a> The people unanimously paid attention to what Philip said, listening and watching the miracles he performed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Acts 8:7">7</a> Many people with evil spirits shouted loudly as the spirits left them. And many who could not move or walk were healed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Acts 8:8">8</a> The city was filled with great happiness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Acts 8:9">9</a> A man named Simon lived in that city before. He practiced magic, amazing the people of Samaria, and claimed to be someone important.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Acts 8:10">10</a> Everyone listened to him closely, from the least important to the most important, saying, &quot;This man has great power from God.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Acts 8:11">11</a> They respected him because for a long time he had amazed them with his magic.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Acts 8:12">12</a> When people believed what Philip was saying about God&#39;s kingdom and Jesus Christ, they got baptized, both men and women.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Acts 8:13">13</a> Simon also believed and after his baptism, he stayed with Philip, amazed by the miracles and signs he saw.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Acts 8:14">14</a> When the apostles in Jerusalem learned that Samaria had accepted God&#39;s word, they sent Peter and John to them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Acts 8:15">15</a> When they arrived, they prayed for them so they could receive the Holy Spirit.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Acts 8:16">16</a> Because none of them had received the Holy Spirit yet; they had only been baptized in the name of Jesus.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Acts 8:17">17</a> They placed their hands on them and they received the Holy Spirit.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Acts 8:18">18</a> When Simon noticed that the Holy Spirit was given after the apostles laid their hands on people, he offered them money.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Acts 8:19">19</a> Give me this power too, so anyone I touch can receive the Holy Spirit.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Acts 8:20">20</a> Peter told him, &quot;May your money be lost with you, because you believed that God&#39;s gift can be bought with money.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Acts 8:21">21</a> You have no part or share in this matter, because your heart is not right with God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Acts 8:22">22</a> Turn away from your evil ways and ask God to forgive the thoughts of your heart.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Acts 8:23">23</a> I see that you are full of bitterness and trapped in sin.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Acts 8:24">24</a> Simon answered, &quot;Please pray to God for me so that none of the things you talked about will happen to me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Acts 8:25">25</a> After they told about God and shared his message, they went back to Jerusalem and spoke about Jesus in many Samaritan towns.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Acts 8:26">26</a> God&#39;s messenger spoke to Philip, saying, &quot;Get up and go south on the road from Jerusalem to Gaza, which is in the desert.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Acts 8:27">27</a> He got up and went, and there was a man from Ethiopia, a high-ranking official under Queen Candace of the Ethiopians, who was in charge of all her wealth and had come to Jerusalem to worship.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Acts 8:28">28</a> He was coming back, sitting in his chariot, and reading the prophet Isaiah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Acts 8:29">29</a> Then the Spirit told Philip, &quot;Go close and join this chariot.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Acts 8:30">30</a> Philip ran to him, heard him reading the prophet Isaiah, and asked, &quot;Do you understand what you are reading?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Acts 8:31">31</a> He asked, &quot;How can I understand unless someone explains it to me?&quot; So he invited Philip to come up and sit beside him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Acts 8:32">32</a> The part of the scripture he read said, &quot;He was led like a sheep to be killed, and like a quiet lamb before someone cutting its wool, he did not open his mouth.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Acts 8:33">33</a> In his low state, he was denied justice: and who can speak of his descendants? For his life is taken from the earth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Acts 8:34">34</a> The eunuch asked Philip, &quot;Please, who is the prophet talking about? Himself or someone else?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Acts 8:35">35</a> Then Philip started speaking and, using that part of the Bible, told him about Jesus.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Acts 8:36">36</a> As they traveled, they found some water. The eunuch said, &quot;Look, here&#39;s water. What&#39;s stopping me from getting baptized?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Acts 8:37">37</a> Philip said, &quot;If you believe with all your heart, you can.&quot; And he replied, &quot;I believe Jesus Christ is God&#39;s Son.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Acts 8:38">38</a> He told the chariot to stop. Then both Philip and the eunuch went down into the water, and Philip baptized him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="Acts 8:39">39</a> After they came up from the water, God&#39;s Spirit took Philip away, and the eunuch didn&#39;t see him again but continued on his journey happily.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="Acts 8:40">40</a> Philip appeared in Azotus, and as he traveled, he shared the message in all the cities until he reached Caesarea.</p>
