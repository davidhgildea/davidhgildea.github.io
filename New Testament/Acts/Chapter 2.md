<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Acts 2:1">1</a> When Pentecost Day arrived, they were all together in one place.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Acts 2:2">2</a> Suddenly a loud sound like a strong wind came from heaven and filled the whole house where they were sitting.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Acts 2:3">3</a> They saw something like flames of fire that were split in two touching each person.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Acts 2:4">4</a> Everyone was filled with the Holy Spirit and started to speak in different languages, as the Spirit helped them to speak.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Acts 2:5">5</a> In Jerusalem, there were Jews, religious men from every country in the world, living there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Acts 2:6">6</a> When news of this spread, a large crowd gathered, and they were amazed because each person heard them speaking in their own language.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Acts 2:7">7</a> Everyone was amazed and surprised, asking each other, &quot;Look, aren&#39;t all these people who are speaking from Galilee?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Acts 2:8">8</a> How can we each hear them speaking in our own native language?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Acts 2:9">9</a> People from Parthia, Media, Elam, Mesopotamia, Judea, Cappadocia, Pontus, and Asia,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Acts 2:10">10</a> Phrygia, Pamphylia, Egypt, areas of Libya near Cyrene, visitors from Rome, Jews, and new believers,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Acts 2:11">11</a> People from Crete and Arabia, we hear them telling in our own languages the amazing deeds of God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Acts 2:12">12</a> They were all surprised and confused, asking each other, &quot;What does this mean?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Acts 2:13">13</a> Others making fun said, These men have drunk too much wine.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Acts 2:14">14</a> But Peter stood up with the eleven, raised his voice, and said to them, &quot;You men of Judaea and everyone in Jerusalem, listen to me and hear my words:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Acts 2:15">15</a> They are not drunk as you think, since it is only nine in the morning.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Acts 2:16">16</a> This is what the prophet Joel talked about.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Acts 2:17">17</a> In the last days, God says, I will give my Spirit to everyone: your sons and daughters will tell of the future, your young men will have visions, and your old men will dream dreams.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Acts 2:18">18</a> I will pour out my Spirit on my servants and maidens in those days, and they will prophesy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Acts 2:19">19</a> I will show amazing things in the sky above and signs on the earth below; there will be blood, fire, and clouds of smoke.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Acts 2:20">20</a> The sun will turn dark, and the moon will turn red, before the great and important day of God comes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Acts 2:21">21</a> Whoever calls on God&#39;s name will be saved.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Acts 2:22">22</a> People of Israel, listen to this: Jesus from Nazareth was a man shown to be from God to you through miracles, wonders, and signs, which God did through him among you, as you know.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Acts 2:23">23</a> God planned for him to be handed over to you, and you took him and had evil men kill him by nailing him to a cross.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Acts 2:24">24</a> God raised him up, freeing him from the grip of death, because it was not possible for death to keep holding him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Acts 2:25">25</a> David says about him: &quot;I always see God before me. He is at my right side so I will not be shaken.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Acts 2:26">26</a> So my heart was happy, and my tongue was joyful; and even my body will rest with hope.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Acts 2:27">27</a> Because you will not leave my soul in hell, nor will you let your Holy One decay.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Acts 2:28">28</a> You have shown me the paths of life; you will fill me with joy by being with me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Acts 2:29">29</a> Friends, I want to talk openly to you about our ancestor David. He died, was buried, and his tomb is still here today.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Acts 2:30">30</a> Because he was a prophet and knew that God had promised with a strong promise that a descendant from his own body would become the Christ and sit on his throne.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Acts 2:31">31</a> He foresaw this and spoke about Christ being raised from the dead. His soul wasn&#39;t left in the grave, and his body didn&#39;t decay.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Acts 2:32">32</a> God raised Jesus, and we all saw it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Acts 2:33">33</a> So, God raised him to His right side and after getting the Holy Spirit as promised by the Father, he has poured out what you now see and hear.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Acts 2:34">34</a> David did not go up to heaven, but he said, &quot;God told my lord, &#39;Sit at my right side.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Acts 2:35">35</a> Until I make your enemies your footstool.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Acts 2:36">36</a> So all the people of Israel should clearly understand that God made Jesus, whom you crucified, both Lord and Messiah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Acts 2:37">37</a> When they heard this, their hearts felt troubled, and they said to Peter and the other apostles, &quot;Friends, what should we do?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Acts 2:38">38</a> Peter said to them, &quot;Change your ways, and be baptized each one of you in Jesus Christ&#39;s name to have your sins forgiven, and you will receive the gift of the Holy Spirit.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="Acts 2:39">39</a> The promise is for you, your children, and everyone far away, as many as God will call.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="Acts 2:40">40</a> He strongly urged with many other words, &quot;Save yourselves from this wicked generation.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="Acts 2:41">41</a> People who happily accepted the message were baptized. That day, about three thousand people joined them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="Acts 2:42">42</a> They stayed devoted to the teachings of the apostles, to sharing with each other, to eating meals together, and to praying.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v43"><a class="verse-num" href="#v43" aria-label="Acts 2:43">43</a> Everyone felt afraid, and the apostles performed many miracles and signs.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v44"><a class="verse-num" href="#v44" aria-label="Acts 2:44">44</a> Everyone who believed was together and shared everything.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v45"><a class="verse-num" href="#v45" aria-label="Acts 2:45">45</a> They sold their belongings and shared with everyone, giving to each as they needed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v46"><a class="verse-num" href="#v46" aria-label="Acts 2:46">46</a> They met together every day in the temple, and went from home to home to share meals. They ate happily and with sincere hearts.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v47"><a class="verse-num" href="#v47" aria-label="Acts 2:47">47</a> Praising God and being liked by everyone. And God added to the group every day those who would be saved.</p>
