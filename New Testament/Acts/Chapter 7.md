<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Acts 7:1">1</a> Then the high priest asked, &quot;Is this true?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Acts 7:2">2</a> He said, &quot;Listen, men and fathers, the glorious God appeared to our ancestor Abraham in Mesopotamia before he lived in Haran.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Acts 7:3">3</a> God told him, &quot;Leave your country and your relatives, and go to the land I will show you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Acts 7:4">4</a> He left the land of the Chaldeans, lived in Haran, and after his father died, God moved him to this land where you live now.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Acts 7:5">5</a> God did not give him any land to own, not even a place to step on. But God promised to give it to him and to his children after him, even though he had no child yet.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Acts 7:6">6</a> God said in this way: That his descendants would live in a foreign land; and they would be made slaves and treated badly for four hundred years.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Acts 7:7">7</a> God said: I will judge the nation where they are slaves, and afterwards, they will come out and worship me here.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Acts 7:8">8</a> God made a promise with Abraham about circumcision: So Abraham had a son, Isaac, and circumcised him on the eighth day; Isaac had Jacob; and Jacob had twelve sons who became leaders.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Acts 7:9">9</a> The family leaders, jealous of Joseph, sold him as a slave to Egypt; but God stayed with him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Acts 7:10">10</a> God saved him from all his troubles, gave him favor and wisdom before Pharaoh, king of Egypt, and Pharaoh made him ruler over Egypt and his whole house.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Acts 7:11">11</a> A famine spread over all of Egypt and Canaan, bringing much suffering, and our ancestors couldn&#39;t find food.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Acts 7:12">12</a> When Jacob found out there was grain in Egypt, he sent our ancestors there first.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Acts 7:13">13</a> Joseph revealed his identity to his brothers a second time, and his family became known to Pharaoh.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Acts 7:14">14</a> Joseph sent for his father Jacob and all his relatives, 75 people in total.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Acts 7:15">15</a> Jacob went to Egypt and he and our ancestors died there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Acts 7:16">16</a> They were taken to Shechem and placed in the tomb that Abraham purchased from the sons of Hamor, Shechem&#39;s father.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Acts 7:17">17</a> As the time for God&#39;s promise to Abraham came near, the people increased and became many in Egypt.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Acts 7:18">18</a> A new king came to power who did not know about Joseph.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Acts 7:19">19</a> This person tricked our relatives and treated our ancestors badly, making them throw out their babies so they would not survive.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Acts 7:20">20</a> During this time, Moses was born, was very beautiful, and was cared for in his father&#39;s home for three months.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Acts 7:21">21</a> When he was thrown away, Pharaoh&#39;s daughter picked him up and cared for him as her own son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Acts 7:22">22</a> Moses knew all the wisdom of the Egyptians and was powerful in speaking and acting.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Acts 7:23">23</a> When he was 40 years old, he felt the urge to see his fellow Israelites.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Acts 7:24">24</a> He saw someone being treated unfairly, so he defended him, took revenge for the one who was hurt, and struck down the Egyptian.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Acts 7:25">25</a> He thought his brothers would realize that God would use him to save them, but they didn&#39;t understand.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Acts 7:26">26</a> The next day he appeared to them as they were fighting, wanting to reconcile them, saying, &quot;Friends, you are brothers; why are you hurting each other?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Acts 7:27">27</a> The person who wronged his neighbor pushed him away, asking, &quot;Who made you our ruler and judge?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Acts 7:28">28</a> Will you kill me like you killed the Egyptian yesterday?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Acts 7:29">29</a> Moses ran away after hearing this and lived as a foreigner in the land of Midian, where he had two sons.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Acts 7:30">30</a> After forty years passed, he saw an angel of God in a fire inside a bush in the desert near Mount Sinai.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Acts 7:31">31</a> When Moses saw it, he was amazed at the sight. As he went closer to look, God&#39;s voice spoke to him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Acts 7:32">32</a> I am the God of your ancestors, the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. Moses shook with fear and did not dare to look.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Acts 7:33">33</a> God said to him, &quot;Take off your shoes because the place where you are standing is holy ground.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Acts 7:34">34</a> I have seen my people&#39;s suffering in Egypt, heard their cries, and have come to rescue them. Now go, I will send you to Egypt.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Acts 7:35">35</a> They rejected Moses, asking, &quot;Who made you a leader and judge?&quot; But God chose him to be their leader and rescuer, with the help of the angel who met him in the bush.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Acts 7:36">36</a> God led them out after showing them amazing things and signs in Egypt, at the Red Sea, and in the desert for forty years.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Acts 7:37">37</a> This is Moses, who told the Israelites, &quot;God will bring up a prophet like me from among your brothers; you must listen to him.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Acts 7:38">38</a> This is the one who was with the angel in the church in the wilderness, on Mount Sinai, and with our ancestors: he received the living messages to give to us.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="Acts 7:39">39</a> Our ancestors did not want to obey him. Instead, they pushed him away and wanted to return to Egypt in their hearts.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="Acts 7:40">40</a> Tell Aaron to make us gods to lead us: for this Moses who took us out of Egypt, we don&#39;t know what happened to him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="Acts 7:41">41</a> They made a calf back then, gave offerings to the idol and were happy with what they had done.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="Acts 7:42">42</a> God let them worship the stars of the sky. As the prophets wrote, &quot;People of Israel, did you bring me animal sacrifices for forty years in the desert?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v43"><a class="verse-num" href="#v43" aria-label="Acts 7:43">43</a> Yes, you carried the tent of Moloch and the star of your god Remphan, idols you made to worship them: and I will take you away beyond Babylon.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v44"><a class="verse-num" href="#v44" aria-label="Acts 7:44">44</a> Our ancestors had the tent of testimony in the desert, as God commanded, telling Moses to make it like the design he had seen.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v45"><a class="verse-num" href="#v45" aria-label="Acts 7:45">45</a> Our ancestors, including Joshua, took over the land of the nations, which God had cleared away for them, all the way until the time of David.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v46"><a class="verse-num" href="#v46" aria-label="Acts 7:46">46</a> He found favor with God and wanted to build a home for the God of Jacob.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v47"><a class="verse-num" href="#v47" aria-label="Acts 7:47">47</a> Solomon built a house for God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v48"><a class="verse-num" href="#v48" aria-label="Acts 7:48">48</a> However, the highest does not live in buildings made by hands, as the prophet says.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v49"><a class="verse-num" href="#v49" aria-label="Acts 7:49">49</a> Heaven is where I sit as king, and the earth is a place to rest my feet. What kind of home could you build for me? What place could be my resting spot?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v50"><a class="verse-num" href="#v50" aria-label="Acts 7:50">50</a> Didn&#39;t my hand make all these things?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v51"><a class="verse-num" href="#v51" aria-label="Acts 7:51">51</a> You are stubborn and closed in your heart and ears, you always resist the Holy Spirit, just as your ancestors did.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v52"><a class="verse-num" href="#v52" aria-label="Acts 7:52">52</a> Which prophets did your ancestors not attack? They killed those who told about the arrival of the Righteous One, whom you have now betrayed and murdered.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v53"><a class="verse-num" href="#v53" aria-label="Acts 7:53">53</a> They received the law given through angels but have not followed it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v54"><a class="verse-num" href="#v54" aria-label="Acts 7:54">54</a> When they heard this, they felt deep pain in their hearts and gritted their teeth at him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v55"><a class="verse-num" href="#v55" aria-label="Acts 7:55">55</a> He, filled with the Holy Spirit, looked intently into heaven and saw God&#39;s glory, with Jesus standing at God&#39;s right side.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v56"><a class="verse-num" href="#v56" aria-label="Acts 7:56">56</a> And said, Look, I see the sky opened, and the Son of man standing at God&#39;s right side.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v57"><a class="verse-num" href="#v57" aria-label="Acts 7:57">57</a> They shouted loudly, covered their ears, and all rushed at him together.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v58"><a class="verse-num" href="#v58" aria-label="Acts 7:58">58</a> They threw him out of the city and stoned him. The witnesses left their coats near a young man named Saul.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v59"><a class="verse-num" href="#v59" aria-label="Acts 7:59">59</a> They threw stones at Stephen, who called out to God, saying, &quot;Lord Jesus, take my spirit.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v60"><a class="verse-num" href="#v60" aria-label="Acts 7:60">60</a> He kneeled and shouted, &quot;God, do not hold this sin against them.&quot; After he said this, he passed away.</p>
