<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Acts 24:1">1</a> Five days later, the high priest Ananias came down with some elders and a speaker named Tertullus, who accused Paul to the governor.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Acts 24:2">2</a> When he was brought out, Tertullus started to accuse him, saying, &quot;Because of you we have much peace, and good things are done for our nation by your care.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Acts 24:3">3</a> We always accept it everywhere, most respected Felix, with great thanks.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Acts 24:4">4</a> But so I don&#39;t bother you too much, I ask you kindly to listen to us for just a moment.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Acts 24:5">5</a> We have found this man to be a troublemaker, stirring up rebellion among Jews everywhere, and the leader of the Nazarene group.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Acts 24:6">6</a> He also tried to disrespect the temple; we caught him and wanted to judge him by our law.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Acts 24:7">7</a> The commander Lysias came and with strong force took him from us.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Acts 24:8">8</a> Telling his accusers to appear before you, so you can find out about all the things we are accusing him of by questioning him yourself.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Acts 24:9">9</a> The Jews agreed, saying that this was true.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Acts 24:10">10</a> Then Paul, when the governor signaled him to speak, replied, &quot;Knowing that you have been a judge for this nation for many years, I happily defend myself.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Acts 24:11">11</a> I want you to understand that I went up to Jerusalem to worship only twelve days ago.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Acts 24:12">12</a> They did not find me arguing with anyone at the temple, or stirring up the crowd, either in the synagogues or in the city.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Acts 24:13">13</a> They cannot show proof of what they accuse me of now.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Acts 24:14">14</a> I admit to you that I worship the God of my ancestors in the way they call heresy, believing in everything written in the law and the prophets.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Acts 24:15">15</a> Have hope in God, as they do, that the dead will rise, both good and bad people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Acts 24:16">16</a> I always try to keep a clear conscience before God and everyone.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Acts 24:17">17</a> After many years, I came to bring help and gifts to my people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Acts 24:18">18</a> Some Jews from Asia saw me clean in the temple, without a crowd or commotion.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Acts 24:19">19</a> They should have been here to accuse me in front of you, if they had any charge against me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Acts 24:20">20</a> Or let these people here tell if they found any wrong in me when I was before the council.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Acts 24:21">21</a> I am on trial before you today just because I said, while standing among them, that the dead are raised.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Acts 24:22">22</a> When Felix heard this, knowing more about the way, he postponed the decision, saying, &quot;When Captain Lysias comes, I will fully examine your case.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Acts 24:23">23</a> He ordered a captain to guard Paul, allow him freedom, and let his friends visit and help him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Acts 24:24">24</a> After some time, Felix and his wife Drusilla, who was Jewish, arrived. He called for Paul to listen to him talk about faith in Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Acts 24:25">25</a> As he talked about being right, self-control, and the future judgment, Felix became scared and said, &quot;Leave now; when I find the right time, I will send for you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Acts 24:26">26</a> He also hoped that Paul would give him money to release him, so he sent for him more often and talked with him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Acts 24:27">27</a> Two years later, Porcius Festus replaced Felix. Felix, wanting to please the Jews, kept Paul in chains.</p>
