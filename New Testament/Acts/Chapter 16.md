<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Acts 16:1">1</a> He went to Derbe and Lystra. There, he met a follower named Timothy, whose mother was a Jewish believer, but whose father was Greek.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Acts 16:2">2</a> He had a good reputation among the believers in Lystra and Iconium.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Acts 16:3">3</a> Paul wanted him to travel with him, so he circumcised him because of the Jews in that area, since they all knew his father was Greek.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Acts 16:4">4</a> As they traveled through the towns, they gave out the rules to follow that the apostles and elders in Jerusalem had decided on.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Acts 16:5">5</a> The churches were strengthened in their faith and grew in number every day.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Acts 16:6">6</a> After they traveled through Phrygia and Galatia, the Holy Spirit did not allow them to preach the word in Asia.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Acts 16:7">7</a> After they arrived in Mysia, they tried to go to Bithynia, but the Spirit did not allow them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Acts 16:8">8</a> They went through Mysia and came to Troas.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Acts 16:9">9</a> Paul had a vision at night; a Macedonian man stood there, asking him, &quot;Please come to Macedonia and help us.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Acts 16:10">10</a> After seeing the vision, we immediately tried to go to Macedonia, confident that God had called us to share the good news with them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Acts 16:11">11</a> So we sailed away from Troas and went directly to Samothrace, and the following day to Neapolis.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Acts 16:12">12</a> From there we went to Philippi, the main city of that part of Macedonia and a colony. We stayed in that city for several days.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Acts 16:13">13</a> On the Sabbath, we left the city to a riverside where people often prayed; we sat down and talked with the women who came there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Acts 16:14">14</a> A woman named Lydia, who sold purple cloth and lived in Thyatira, worshipped God and listened to us because God opened her heart to pay attention to what Paul said.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Acts 16:15">15</a> After she and her family were baptized, she asked us, &quot;If you think I am loyal to God, please come to my home and stay.&quot; And she insisted we go.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Acts 16:16">16</a> As we were going to pray, we met a girl with a spirit of telling the future, who made a lot of money for her owners by predicting things.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Acts 16:17">17</a> She followed Paul and us, shouting, &quot;These men are servants of the most high God, who show us how to be saved.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Acts 16:18">18</a> She did this for many days. But Paul, upset, turned and told the spirit, &quot;I order you in Jesus Christ&#39;s name to leave her.&quot; And it left her that hour.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Acts 16:19">19</a> When her owners realized they could no longer make money from her, they grabbed Paul and Silas and dragged them to the authorities in the public square.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Acts 16:20">20</a> They brought them to the officials, saying, &quot;These men are Jews and are causing a lot of trouble in our city.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Acts 16:21">21</a> They teach practices that we, as Romans, cannot accept or follow.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Acts 16:22">22</a> The crowd stood up together against them. The officials tore their clothes and ordered them to be beaten.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Acts 16:23">23</a> After they whipped them many times, they threw them into jail and told the jailer to watch them carefully.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Acts 16:24">24</a> The guard, given this order, put them into the innermost cell and clamped their feet in wooden blocks.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Acts 16:25">25</a> At midnight, Paul and Silas prayed and sang songs to God, and the other prisoners listened to them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Acts 16:26">26</a> Suddenly, there was a big earthquake that shook the prison&#39;s foundations: right away, all the doors opened, and everyone&#39;s chains came loose.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Acts 16:27">27</a> When the prison guard woke up and saw the doors open, he took out his sword to kill himself because he thought the prisoners had escaped.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Acts 16:28">28</a> Paul shouted loudly, &quot;Don&#39;t hurt yourself; we are all still here.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Acts 16:29">29</a> He asked for a light, jumped in, shook with fear, and knelt before Paul and Silas.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Acts 16:30">30</a> He led them out and asked, &quot;Sirs, what should I do to be saved?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Acts 16:31">31</a> They said, &quot;Trust in Jesus Christ, and you and your family will be saved.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Acts 16:32">32</a> They told him and everyone in his house what God had said.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Acts 16:33">33</a> He took them at night, cleaned their wounds, and he and everyone with him were immediately baptized.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Acts 16:34">34</a> When he brought them into his home, he gave them food, and was very happy, trusting in God with everyone in his home.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Acts 16:35">35</a> When daylight came, the officers told the guards, &quot;Release those men.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Acts 16:36">36</a> The prison guard told Paul, &quot;The officials have sent word to release you: so now you can leave and go peacefully.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Acts 16:37">37</a> But Paul said to them, &quot;They beat us in public without a trial, even though we are Roman citizens. They threw us in jail, and now they want to send us away secretly? No, certainly not. Let them come and take us out themselves.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Acts 16:38">38</a> The officers reported these things to the officials, and they became afraid when they learned that the men were Romans.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="Acts 16:39">39</a> They came and asked them, then led them out, and asked them to leave the city.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="Acts 16:40">40</a> They left the prison, went to Lydia&#39;s home, saw the other believers, cheered them up, and then left.</p>
