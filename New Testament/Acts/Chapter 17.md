<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Acts 17:1">1</a> After they traveled through Amphipolis and Apollonia, they arrived in Thessalonica, where there was a Jewish synagogue.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Acts 17:2">2</a> Paul, as he usually did, met with them and for three Saturdays discussed the scriptures with them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Acts 17:3">3</a> Explaining and proving that Christ had to suffer and rise from the dead; and that this Jesus, who I tell you about, is the Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Acts 17:4">4</a> Some believed and joined Paul and Silas, including many god-fearing Greeks and several influential women.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Acts 17:5">5</a> But the Jews who did not believe, jealous as they were, gathered some wicked men from the streets, formed a mob, started a riot in the city, attacked Jason&#39;s house, and tried to bring them out to the crowd.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Acts 17:6">6</a> When they couldn&#39;t find them, they dragged Jason and some other followers to the city authorities, shouting, &quot;These people who have been causing trouble all over the world have now come here too.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Acts 17:7">7</a> Jason has welcomed them, and they all act against Caesar&#39;s laws, claiming there is another king named Jesus.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Acts 17:8">8</a> They upset the people and city leaders when they heard this.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Acts 17:9">9</a> After they got a promise from Jason and the other person, they released them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Acts 17:10">10</a> The brothers quickly sent Paul and Silas away at night to the city of Berea, and when they arrived, they went into the Jewish synagogue.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Acts 17:11">11</a> They were more open-minded than the people in Thessalonica because they eagerly accepted the message and examined the scriptures every day to see if it was true.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Acts 17:12">12</a> So many people believed, including respected Greek women and quite a few men.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Acts 17:13">13</a> When the Thessalonica Jews learned that Paul was spreading God&#39;s message in Berea, they went there and caused trouble among the people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Acts 17:14">14</a> Right away, the followers sent Paul to the sea, while Silas and Timothy stayed there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Acts 17:15">15</a> The people guiding Paul took him to Athens. After telling Silas and Timothy to hurry and join him, they left.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Acts 17:16">16</a> While Paul was waiting for them in Athens, he felt deeply troubled when he saw that the city was full of idol worship.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Acts 17:17">17</a> So he argued in the church with the Jews and with the religious people, and every day in the town center with those who spent time with him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Acts 17:18">18</a> Some Epicurean and Stoic philosophers met him. Some asked, &quot;What is this chatterer trying to say?&quot; Others said, &quot;He seems to talk about foreign gods,&quot; because he was telling them about Jesus and the resurrection.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Acts 17:19">19</a> They took him to Areopagus and asked, &quot;Can we find out what this new teaching you speak of is?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Acts 17:20">20</a> You are telling us about some new and strange things, and we want to understand what they mean.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Acts 17:21">21</a> The people of Athens and the visitors there only used their time to talk about or listen to the latest news.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Acts 17:22">22</a> Paul stood in the middle of Mars Hill and said, &quot;People of Athens, I see that you are very religious in every way.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Acts 17:23">23</a> As I walked by and saw your religious statues, I noticed one altar with the words, &#39;To the Unknown God.&#39; You worship without knowing; this is the God I&#39;m telling you about.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Acts 17:24">24</a> God, who created the world and everything in it, is the ruler of heaven and earth and does not live in buildings made by people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Acts 17:25">25</a> God is not served by human hands, as if He needed anything, because He gives life, breath, and everything to everyone.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Acts 17:26">26</a> God made all people from one family to live everywhere on earth, and set the times and limits of where they live.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Acts 17:27">27</a> They should look for God, hoping they might reach out to find him, because he is not far from any of us.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Acts 17:28">28</a> In him we exist, move, and are alive; as some of your poets have said, we are his children too.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Acts 17:29">29</a> Since we are God&#39;s children, we should not believe that God is like gold, silver, or stone, shaped by skill and human design.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Acts 17:30">30</a> In the past, God overlooked such ignorance, but now He commands everyone everywhere to change their ways.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Acts 17:31">31</a> God has set a day when he will fairly judge everyone by the man he has chosen, and he has proven this to everyone by raising him from the dead.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Acts 17:32">32</a> When they heard about the dead coming back to life, some laughed, and others said, &quot;We will listen to you talk about this again later.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Acts 17:33">33</a> So Paul left them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Acts 17:34">34</a> However, some men stuck with him and believed. Among them was Dionysius the Areopagite, a woman named Damaris, and others with them.</p>
