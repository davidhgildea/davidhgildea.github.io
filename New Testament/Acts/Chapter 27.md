<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Acts 27:1">1</a> When it was decided that we would sail to Italy, they handed Paul and some other prisoners over to a man named Julius, who was a captain in Augustus&#39; army.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Acts 27:2">2</a> We got on a boat from Adramyttium and set sail to pass by the coasts of Asia, with a man named Aristarchus from Thessalonica in Macedonia joining us.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Acts 27:3">3</a> The next day we arrived at Sidon. Julius kindly treated Paul and allowed him to visit his friends to rest.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Acts 27:4">4</a> After we left there, we sailed near Cyprus because the winds were against us.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Acts 27:5">5</a> After crossing the sea near Cilicia and Pamphylia, we arrived at Myra, a city in Lycia.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Acts 27:6">6</a> The officer found a ship from Alexandria going to Italy and put us on it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Acts 27:7">7</a> After sailing slowly for many days and barely passing Cnidus because the wind was against us, we sailed below Crete, near Salmone.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Acts 27:8">8</a> After barely getting past it, they reached a place called Fair Havens, near the city of Lasea.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Acts 27:9">9</a> After a lot of time had passed, and sailing had become risky because the time of fasting was over, Paul warned them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Acts 27:10">10</a> He said to them, &quot;Friends, I see that this trip will bring harm and much loss, not just to the cargo and the ship, but to our lives too.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Acts 27:11">11</a> The officer trusted the ship&#39;s captain and owner more than what Paul said.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Acts 27:12">12</a> Since the harbor wasn&#39;t good for staying the winter, most people suggested leaving to try to reach Phoenix and spend the winter there; it&#39;s a harbor on Crete, facing southwest and northwest.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Acts 27:13">13</a> When the south wind gently blew, thinking they had achieved their goal, they left and sailed near Crete.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Acts 27:14">14</a> Soon after, a strong storm called Euroclydon came up against it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Acts 27:15">15</a> When the ship was trapped and couldn&#39;t sail into the wind, we let it go wherever it was pushed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Acts 27:16">16</a> Sailing close to an island named Clauda, we worked hard to secure the lifeboat.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Acts 27:17">17</a> After they lifted it, they supported the ship with ropes. Worried about hitting the sandbanks, they lowered the sails and let the wind carry them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Acts 27:18">18</a> We were greatly shaken by a storm, so the next day they threw cargo overboard to lighten the ship.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Acts 27:19">19</a> On the third day, we threw the ship&#39;s equipment overboard with our own hands.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Acts 27:20">20</a> When the sun and stars did not show for many days and a big storm was upon us, we lost all hope of being saved.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Acts 27:21">21</a> But after not eating for a long time, Paul stood up among them and said, &quot;Friends, you should have listened to me and not left Crete, then we could have avoided this trouble and loss.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Acts 27:22">22</a> I encourage you to stay positive because no one will lose their life, only the ship will be lost.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Acts 27:23">23</a> Last night, God&#39;s angel, who I belong to and serve, stood by me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Acts 27:24">24</a> &quot;God says, &#39;Don&#39;t be afraid, Paul. You will be taken to Caesar, and God has granted the safety of everyone sailing with you.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Acts 27:25">25</a> So, friends, cheer up: for I trust God, that it will happen just as I was told.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Acts 27:26">26</a> We have to land on a certain island.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Acts 27:27">27</a> On the fourteenth night, as we were still being tossed around in the sea near Adria, around midnight the sailors thought we were getting close to land.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Acts 27:28">28</a> They measured and found it was twenty fathoms deep. After going a bit further, they measured again and it was fifteen fathoms deep.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Acts 27:29">29</a> Afraid we might hit rocks, they threw four anchors from the back of the ship and hoped for daylight.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Acts 27:30">30</a> As the sailors were trying to escape from the ship by lowering the lifeboat into the sea, pretending they were going to drop anchors from the front of the ship,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Acts 27:31">31</a> Paul told the officer and soldiers, &quot;If these men don&#39;t stay on the ship, you won&#39;t be saved.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Acts 27:32">32</a> Then the soldiers cut the ropes of the boat and let it drift away.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Acts 27:33">33</a> As the day began, Paul urged everyone to eat, saying, &quot;Today is the fourteenth day you&#39;ve waited and fasted without eating anything.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Acts 27:34">34</a> So I ask you to eat some food, because it is good for you. Not even a single hair from your heads will be lost.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Acts 27:35">35</a> After saying this, he took some bread, thanked God in front of everyone, broke it, and started to eat.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Acts 27:36">36</a> Then they all felt encouraged and ate some food.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Acts 27:37">37</a> There were 276 of us on the ship.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Acts 27:38">38</a> After they ate enough, they made the ship lighter by throwing the wheat into the sea.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="Acts 27:39">39</a> When daylight came, they did not recognize the land, but they saw a bay with a beach and decided to try to drive the ship onto it if they could.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="Acts 27:40">40</a> After they lifted the anchors, they let themselves go on the sea, released the steering ropes, raised the main sail to catch the wind, and headed for the shore.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="Acts 27:41">41</a> They crashed the ship where two seas came together; the front stuck firm and wouldn&#39;t move, while the back part broke from the strong waves.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="Acts 27:42">42</a> The soldiers decided to kill the prisoners to prevent any from swimming away and escaping.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v43"><a class="verse-num" href="#v43" aria-label="Acts 27:43">43</a> The officer, wanting to save Paul, stopped them from their plan. He ordered those who could swim to jump into the sea and go to shore first.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v44"><a class="verse-num" href="#v44" aria-label="Acts 27:44">44</a> The others held onto planks or pieces of the ship, and this way, everyone safely reached shore.</p>
