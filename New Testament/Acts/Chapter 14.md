<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Acts 14:1">1</a> In Iconium, they both went into the Jewish synagogue, and spoke so well that many Jews and Greeks believed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Acts 14:2">2</a> But the Jews who did not believe upset the non-Jews and turned their thoughts against the believers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Acts 14:3">3</a> They stayed there a long time, speaking bravely about God, who showed that their message of kindness was true by letting them perform miracles and wonders.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Acts 14:4">4</a> The people in the city were split: some sided with the Jews, and some with the apostles.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Acts 14:5">5</a> When both the non-Jews and the Jews with their leaders attacked them to abuse and stone them,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Acts 14:6">6</a> They realized it and ran away to the cities of Lystra and Derbe in Lycaonia, and to the surrounding area.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Acts 14:7">7</a> There they shared the good news.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Acts 14:8">8</a> In Lystra, there was a man who couldn&#39;t use his feet. He had been unable to walk since he was born.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Acts 14:9">9</a> Paul spoke and a man listened; he looked straight at him, saw he believed, and could be healed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Acts 14:10">10</a> Spoke loudly, &quot;Get up on your feet.&quot; And he jumped up and walked.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Acts 14:11">11</a> When the people saw what Paul did, they shouted in the Lycaonian language, &quot;The gods have come down to us looking like humans.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Acts 14:12">12</a> People named Barnabas as Jupiter and Paul as Mercury because Paul was the main speaker.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Acts 14:13">13</a> The priest of Jupiter from their city brought bulls and wreaths to the gates and wanted to offer a sacrifice with everyone.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Acts 14:14">14</a> When the apostles, Barnabas and Paul, heard this, they tore their clothes and ran into the crowd, shouting.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Acts 14:15">15</a> And asking, &quot;Friends, why do you do these things? We are just like you, feeling the same things, and we tell you to stop worshipping these worthless things and turn to the real God who created the sky, the land, the sea, and everything in them.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Acts 14:16">16</a> In the past, God allowed all nations to follow their own paths.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Acts 14:17">17</a> But God didn&#39;t leave without evidence, doing good, giving us rain from the sky, and seasons of bounty, filling our lives with food and joy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Acts 14:18">18</a> With these words, they barely stopped the people from offering sacrifices to them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Acts 14:19">19</a> Some Jews from Antioch and Iconium arrived and convinced the people to stone Paul. They thought he was dead, so they dragged him out of the city.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Acts 14:20">20</a> Even though the followers were standing around him, he got up, went into the city, and the next day he left with Barnabas to Derbe.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Acts 14:21">21</a> After they shared the good news with that city and taught many people, they went back to Lystra, Iconium, and Antioch.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Acts 14:22">22</a> Strengthening the believers and encouraging them to stay true to the faith, and saying that we must go through many hard times to enter God&#39;s kingdom.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Acts 14:23">23</a> After they chose leaders for each church and prayed with fasting, they trusted these leaders to God, in whom they believed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Acts 14:24">24</a> After they traveled through Pisidia, they arrived in Pamphylia.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Acts 14:25">25</a> After they shared God&#39;s message in Perga, they traveled to Attalia.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Acts 14:26">26</a> From there, they sailed to Antioch, where they had been entrusted to God&#39;s care for the task they completed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Acts 14:27">27</a> When they arrived and brought the church together, they told everyone what God had done with them and how He had made it possible for non-Jews to believe.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Acts 14:28">28</a> They stayed there a long time with the followers.</p>
