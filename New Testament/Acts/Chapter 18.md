<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Acts 18:1">1</a> After this, Paul left Athens and went to Corinth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Acts 18:2">2</a> They met a Jew named Aquila from Pontus, who had just arrived from Italy with his wife Priscilla. This was after Emperor Claudius had ordered all Jews to leave Rome. They went to see them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Acts 18:3">3</a> Because he had the same job, he stayed with them and worked, since they were all tentmakers by trade.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Acts 18:4">4</a> He discussed in the meeting place every Saturday, convincing both the Jews and the non-Jews.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Acts 18:5">5</a> When Silas and Timothy arrived from Macedonia, Paul was deeply moved and told the Jews that Jesus was the Messiah.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Acts 18:6">6</a> When they argued and said disrespectful things about God, he shook his clothes and told them, &quot;Your actions are your own responsibility; I am not guilty. From now on, I will go to non-Jewish people.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Acts 18:7">7</a> He left that place and went into the house of a man named Justus, who worshiped God, and his house was right next to the synagogue.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Acts 18:8">8</a> Crispus, the main leader of the synagogue, believed in God with his whole family; and many people in Corinth, after listening, also believed and were baptized.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Acts 18:9">9</a> One night God spoke to Paul in a dream, &quot;Don&#39;t be scared, but talk and don&#39;t stop speaking.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Acts 18:10">10</a> I am with you, and no one will attack you to harm you, for I have many people in this city.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Acts 18:11">11</a> He stayed there one and a half years, sharing God&#39;s message with them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Acts 18:12">12</a> When Gallio was the governor of Achaia, the Jews united against Paul and took him to the court.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Acts 18:13">13</a> He is saying, &quot;This man is convincing people to worship God in a way that is against the law.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Acts 18:14">14</a> When Paul was about to speak, Gallio told the Jews, &quot;If this were a case of crime or bad behavior, Jews, it would make sense for me to listen to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Acts 18:15">15</a> If it&#39;s about words, names, and your law, you handle it; I won&#39;t judge these things.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Acts 18:16">16</a> He forced them away from the judge&#39;s bench.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Acts 18:17">17</a> Then all the Greeks grabbed Sosthenes, the head of the synagogue, and hit him in front of the judge&#39;s bench. But Gallio did not care about any of those things.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Acts 18:18">18</a> Paul stayed there for a long time, then said goodbye to the brothers, sailed to Syria with Priscilla and Aquila, and he cut his hair at Cenchrea because of a promise.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Acts 18:19">19</a> He went to Ephesus, left them there, but went into the synagogue and talked with the Jews.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Acts 18:20">20</a> When they asked him to stay longer with them, he did not agree.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Acts 18:21">21</a> He said goodbye, telling them, &quot;I really must celebrate this upcoming festival in Jerusalem: but I plan to come back to you, if God allows.&quot; Then he left from Ephesus.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Acts 18:22">22</a> When he arrived at Caesarea, after going up to greet the church, he went down to Antioch.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Acts 18:23">23</a> After staying there for a while, he left and traveled through the regions of Galatia and Phrygia, encouraging all the followers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Acts 18:24">24</a> A Jewish man named Apollos, from Alexandria, who was an excellent speaker and very knowledgeable in the scriptures, went to Ephesus.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Acts 18:25">25</a> This man learned about God&#39;s way; he was passionate, and he talked and taught about God&#39;s teachings with care, knowing only John&#39;s baptism.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Acts 18:26">26</a> He started to speak confidently in the synagogue. When Aquila and Priscilla heard him, they welcomed him and explained God&#39;s way to him more clearly.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Acts 18:27">27</a> When he wanted to go to Achaia, the believers sent letters asking the followers there to welcome him. When he arrived, he helped those who through God&#39;s kindness had believed a lot.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Acts 18:28">28</a> He strongly persuaded the Jews in public, using the scriptures to show that Jesus was the Messiah.</p>
