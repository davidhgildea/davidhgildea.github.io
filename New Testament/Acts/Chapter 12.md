<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Acts 12:1">1</a> At that time, King Herod started to bother some people from the church.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Acts 12:2">2</a> He killed James, John&#39;s brother, with a sword.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Acts 12:3">3</a> Seeing that it made the Jews happy, he decided to arrest Peter too. (It was the time of the Feast of Unleavened Bread.)</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Acts 12:4">4</a> After arresting him, he put him in jail and handed him over to four groups of four soldiers to guard him, planning to present him to the people after Passover.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Acts 12:5">5</a> Peter was kept in jail, but the church constantly prayed to God for him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Acts 12:6">6</a> When Herod planned to bring him out, that night Peter was sleeping between two soldiers, tied with two chains, and guards by the door were watching the jail.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Acts 12:7">7</a> An angel from God came to him, and a light shone in the jail. The angel hit Peter on the side and lifted him up, saying, &quot;Get up fast.&quot; And his chains fell off his hands.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Acts 12:8">8</a> The angel told him, &quot;Get dressed and put on your sandals.&quot; He did that. Then the angel said, &quot;Put on your coat and follow me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Acts 12:9">9</a> He went out and followed, not knowing the angel had really done this; he thought he was seeing a vision.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Acts 12:10">10</a> After they passed the first and second guard posts, they reached the iron gate leading to the city; it opened by itself for them. They went out, walked down one street, and right away the angel left him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Acts 12:11">11</a> When Peter realized what happened, he said, &quot;Now I&#39;m certain that God has sent his angel and rescued me from Herod&#39;s control and from what the Jewish people expected.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Acts 12:12">12</a> After thinking about it, he went to Mary&#39;s house, the mother of John also called Mark, where many people were meeting and praying.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Acts 12:13">13</a> While Peter knocked at the gate door, a young woman named Rhoda came to listen.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Acts 12:14">14</a> When she recognized Peter&#39;s voice, she didn&#39;t open the gate because she was so happy, but she ran inside and told everyone that Peter was at the gate.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Acts 12:15">15</a> They told her, &quot;You&#39;re crazy.&quot; But she kept saying it was true. So they said, &quot;It must be his angel.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Acts 12:16">16</a> Peter kept knocking, and when they opened the door and saw him, they were amazed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Acts 12:17">17</a> But he, signaling to them with his hand to be quiet, told them how God had helped him escape from jail. He said, &quot;Go tell these things to James and the others.&quot; Then he left and went to a different place.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Acts 12:18">18</a> As soon as it was morning, the soldiers were very upset about what had happened to Peter.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Acts 12:19">19</a> When Herod couldn&#39;t find him, he questioned the guards and ordered them to be killed. Then he left Judea, went to Caesarea, and stayed there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Acts 12:20">20</a> Herod was very unhappy with the people of Tyre and Sidon. But they joined together and went to him, and after winning over Blastus, the king&#39;s servant, they asked for peace because their land depended on the king&#39;s land for food.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Acts 12:21">21</a> On a certain day, Herod, dressed in his royal clothes, sat on his throne and spoke to the people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Acts 12:22">22</a> The people shouted, &quot;This is the voice of a god, not a human.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Acts 12:23">23</a> Right away, God&#39;s angel struck him down because he didn&#39;t honor God: so worms ate him, and he died.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Acts 12:24">24</a> God&#39;s word spread and increased widely.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Acts 12:25">25</a> Barnabas and Saul came back from Jerusalem after completing their work and brought John Mark with them.</p>
