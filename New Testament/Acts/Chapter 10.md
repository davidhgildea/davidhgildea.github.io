<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Acts 10:1">1</a> In Caesarea there was a man named Cornelius, a captain of the Italian group.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Acts 10:2">2</a> A religious man who respected God with his whole family, gave lots of charity to others, and always prayed to God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Acts 10:3">3</a> In a clear vision around three in the afternoon, he saw an angel of God come to him and say, &quot;Cornelius.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Acts 10:4">4</a> When he saw him, he was scared and asked, &quot;What is it, God?&quot; And God answered, &quot;Your prayers and gifts to the poor have been remembered by God.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Acts 10:5">5</a> Now, send people to Joppa and ask for a man named Simon, also known as Peter.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Acts 10:6">6</a> He is staying with Simon, a leather worker, whose house is by the sea. He will tell you what you should do.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Acts 10:7">7</a> After the angel who spoke to Cornelius left, he summoned two of his servants and a loyal soldier who served him regularly.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Acts 10:8">8</a> After he explained everything to them, he sent them to Joppa.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Acts 10:9">9</a> The next day, as they were traveling and getting close to the city, Peter went up to the rooftop to pray around noon.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Acts 10:10">10</a> He became very hungry and wanted to eat, but as they were preparing the food, he fell into a deep dream.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Acts 10:11">11</a> He saw heaven open and a vessel coming down to him, like a large sheet tied at the four corners, being lowered to the ground.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Acts 10:12">12</a> In it were all kinds of four-legged animals, wild creatures, crawling things, and birds of the sky.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Acts 10:13">13</a> A voice came to him saying, &quot;Get up, Peter; kill and eat.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Acts 10:14">14</a> Peter said, &quot;No, God; I have never eaten anything not pure or clean.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Acts 10:15">15</a> The voice spoke to him a second time, &quot;Do not call anything impure that God has made clean.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Acts 10:16">16</a> This happened three times, and then the container was taken back up into heaven.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Acts 10:17">17</a> While Peter was wondering what his vision meant, the men Cornelius sent asked for Simon&#39;s house and were standing at the gate.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Acts 10:18">18</a> They called and asked if Simon, also known as Peter, was staying there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Acts 10:19">19</a> As Peter was thinking about the vision, the Spirit told him, &quot;Look, three men are looking for you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Acts 10:20">20</a> Get up, go down, and go with them without doubting, because I have sent them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Acts 10:21">21</a> Then Peter went to the men who Cornelius sent to him and said, &quot;Look, I&#39;m the one you&#39;re looking for. Why have you come?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Acts 10:22">22</a> They said, &quot;Cornelius, a fair officer, who respects God and is well spoken of by all the Jewish people, was told by God through a holy angel to invite you to his home and listen to what you have to say.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Acts 10:23">23</a> He invited them in to stay. The next day Peter left with them, and some believers from Joppa went along.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Acts 10:24">24</a> The next day they arrived in Caesarea. Cornelius was expecting them and had gathered his relatives and close friends.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Acts 10:25">25</a> As Peter entered, Cornelius met him, knelt before him, and honored him as if he were God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Acts 10:26">26</a> Peter helped him up, saying, &quot;Get up; I am just a man like you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Acts 10:27">27</a> As he chatted with him, he entered and saw many people had gathered.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Acts 10:28">28</a> He told them, &quot;You are aware that it&#39;s not allowed for a Jewish man to associate with or visit someone from a different nation. However, God has shown me that I should not consider any person impure or unclean.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Acts 10:29">29</a> So I came to you without arguing, as soon as I was called. Why did you call for me?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Acts 10:30">30</a> Cornelius said, &quot;Four days ago, I was fasting until this time. At three in the afternoon, I was praying in my home, when suddenly a man in shining clothes stood in front of me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Acts 10:31">31</a> Cornelius, your prayer is heard, and your acts of charity are remembered before God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Acts 10:32">32</a> Go to Joppa and ask for Simon, also known as Peter, who is staying at the home of Simon the leatherworker by the sea. When he arrives, he will talk to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Acts 10:33">33</a> I sent for you right away; you did right to come. Now we&#39;re all here before God to listen to everything God has told you to tell us.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Acts 10:34">34</a> Then Peter began to speak, &quot;I truly understand that God does not show favoritism to anyone.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Acts 10:35">35</a> In every country, anyone who respects God and does what is right is accepted by Him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Acts 10:36">36</a> God sent a message to the people of Israel, telling them about peace through Jesus Christ: (he is the ruler of all.)</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Acts 10:37">37</a> You know the message that spread through all Judea, starting in Galilee after the baptism John preached.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Acts 10:38">38</a> God gave Jesus of Nazareth the Holy Spirit and power. He went around doing good and healing all who were under the devil&#39;s control, because God was with him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="Acts 10:39">39</a> We saw everything he did in the Jewish lands and in Jerusalem. They killed him and hung him on a tree.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="Acts 10:40">40</a> God raised him up on the third day and showed him openly.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="Acts 10:41">41</a> Not to everyone, but to chosen witnesses before God, to us who ate and drank with him after he came back to life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="Acts 10:42">42</a> He told us to speak to the people and to say that he is the one chosen by God to judge the living and the dead.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v43"><a class="verse-num" href="#v43" aria-label="Acts 10:43">43</a> Every prophet agrees that anyone who believes in him will have their sins forgiven through his name.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v44"><a class="verse-num" href="#v44" aria-label="Acts 10:44">44</a> While Peter was still speaking, the Holy Spirit came upon all who heard the message.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v45"><a class="verse-num" href="#v45" aria-label="Acts 10:45">45</a> The believers who were Jews were amazed because the Holy Spirit was given to the non-Jewish people who had come with Peter.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v46"><a class="verse-num" href="#v46" aria-label="Acts 10:46">46</a> They heard them speaking in different languages and praising God. Then Peter replied.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v47"><a class="verse-num" href="#v47" aria-label="Acts 10:47">47</a> Can anyone stop these people from being baptized with water? They have received the Holy Spirit just like us.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v48"><a class="verse-num" href="#v48" aria-label="Acts 10:48">48</a> He ordered them to be baptized in God&#39;s name. Then they asked him to stay for some days.</p>
