<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Acts 26:1">1</a> Then Agrippa told Paul, &quot;You may now speak for yourself.&quot; So Paul reached out his hand and began to explain his side.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Acts 26:2">2</a> I am happy, King Agrippa, because today I get to explain to you everything the Jews accuse me of.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Acts 26:3">3</a> I know you&#39;re skilled in all Jewish customs and issues, so I ask you to listen to me patiently.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Acts 26:4">4</a> Since I was young, I have lived in my own country, in Jerusalem, and all the Jews know how I have lived.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Acts 26:5">5</a> Those who have known me for a long time would say that I strictly followed our religion&#39;s rules as a Pharisee.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Acts 26:6">6</a> I am here on trial because of the hope in the promise God made to our ancestors.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Acts 26:7">7</a> Our twelve tribes hope to receive what God promised, as they serve God constantly, day and night. For this hope, King Agrippa, I am being accused by the Jews.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Acts 26:8">8</a> Why is it hard for you to believe that God can bring the dead back to life?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Acts 26:9">9</a> I truly believed that I should do many things against Jesus of Nazareth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Acts 26:10">10</a> I also did this in Jerusalem: I locked up many of God&#39;s holy people in prison, with the chief priests&#39; permission; and when they were killed, I was against them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Acts 26:11">11</a> I often punished them in every synagogue and forced them to speak against God; I was very angry with them and chased them even to foreign cities.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Acts 26:12">12</a> While I was going to Damascus with the chief priests&#39; permission and orders,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Acts 26:13">13</a> At noon, O king, I saw a light from the sky brighter than the sun shining all around me and those traveling with me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Acts 26:14">14</a> When we all fell to the ground, I heard a voice saying to me in Hebrew, &quot;Saul, Saul, why are you persecuting me? It&#39;s hard for you to fight against what&#39;s right.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Acts 26:15">15</a> And I asked, &quot;Who are you, God?&quot; And he replied, &quot;I am Jesus, whom you are persecuting.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Acts 26:16">16</a> Stand up and stay on your feet. I have shown myself to you to appoint you as a servant and a witness of what you have seen and what I will show you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Acts 26:17">17</a> Saving you from the people and from the non-Jews, to whom I now send you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Acts 26:18">18</a> To make them see, and to change them from darkness to light, and from Satan&#39;s control to God, so they can have their sins forgiven and share in the blessings with those made holy by trusting in me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Acts 26:19">19</a> So, King Agrippa, I followed the vision from heaven.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Acts 26:20">20</a> God first revealed himself to the people of Damascus, then in Jerusalem, all over Judaea, and to non-Jewish people, telling them to change their ways, turn to God, and do things that show they are sorry for their sins.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Acts 26:21">21</a> For these reasons, the Jews grabbed me in the temple and tried to kill me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Acts 26:22">22</a> So, with God&#39;s help I am still here today, telling everyone, both important and not, only what the prophets and Moses said would happen.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Acts 26:23">23</a> Christ had to suffer, be the first to rise from the dead, and bring light to the people and the non-Jews.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Acts 26:24">24</a> As he defended himself, Festus shouted, &quot;Paul, you&#39;re out of your mind; your great learning is driving you insane.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Acts 26:25">25</a> He replied, &quot;I am not crazy, respected Festus; I am speaking words of truth and seriousness.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Acts 26:26">26</a> The king knows about these things, and I can speak openly to him: I am sure he is aware of them, because this was not done secretly.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Acts 26:27">27</a> King Agrippa, do you believe the prophets? I know that you believe.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Acts 26:28">28</a> Agrippa said to Paul, &quot;You almost convince me to become a Christian.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Acts 26:29">29</a> Paul said, &quot;I wish to God that not only you but also everyone listening today could be almost exactly like me, except for being in chains.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Acts 26:30">30</a> After he said this, the king stood up, along with the governor and Bernice, and everyone sitting with them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Acts 26:31">31</a> After they left, they said to each other, &quot;This man hasn&#39;t done anything to deserve death or imprisonment.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Acts 26:32">32</a> Agrippa said to Festus, &quot;This man could have been freed if he had not asked to go to Caesar.&quot;</p>
