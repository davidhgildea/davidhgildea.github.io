<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Acts 11:1">1</a> The apostles and believers in Judea heard that non-Jews also accepted God&#39;s message.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Acts 11:2">2</a> When Peter arrived in Jerusalem, those who were circumcised argued with him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Acts 11:3">3</a> You are accused of going to uncircumcised men and eating with them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Acts 11:4">4</a> But Peter explained the story from the start, and told it to them step by step, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Acts 11:5">5</a> I was in Joppa praying when I fell into a trance and saw a vision. I saw something like a large sheet coming down from heaven, held by its four corners, and it came down to me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Acts 11:6">6</a> I looked closely and saw animals with four feet, wild creatures, crawling things, and birds of the air.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Acts 11:7">7</a> I heard a voice telling me, &quot;Get up, Peter; kill and eat.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Acts 11:8">8</a> But I said, &quot;No, God, because I have never eaten anything that is not pure or clean.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Acts 11:9">9</a> The voice from heaven told me again, &quot;What God has made clean, do not call ordinary.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Acts 11:10">10</a> This happened three times, and then everything was taken back up into heaven.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Acts 11:11">11</a> Look, right away three men who had been sent to me from Caesarea arrived at the house where I was staying.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Acts 11:12">12</a> The Spirit told me to go with them without doubting. Also, these six friends came with me, and we went into the man&#39;s house.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Acts 11:13">13</a> He showed us how he saw an angel in his house who stood and said to him, &quot;Send men to Joppa and ask for Simon, also known as Peter.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Acts 11:14">14</a> Someone will tell you messages by which you and your whole family will be saved.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Acts 11:15">15</a> As I started to talk, the Holy Spirit came on them, just like it did on us at first.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Acts 11:16">16</a> I remembered what God said: John baptized with water, but you will be baptized with the Holy Spirit.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Acts 11:17">17</a> Since God gave them the same gift as he gave us who believed in Jesus Christ, who was I to oppose God?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Acts 11:18">18</a> When they heard this, they became quiet and praised God, saying, &quot;So God has also given to non-Jews the chance to turn from their sins and receive life.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Acts 11:19">19</a> Those forced away by the troubles starting with Stephen went to places like Phoenicia, Cyprus, and Antioch, telling God&#39;s message only to Jews.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Acts 11:20">20</a> Some of them were from Cyprus and Cyrene, and when they arrived in Antioch, they spoke to the Greeks, telling them about Jesus.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Acts 11:21">21</a> God helped them, and many people believed and turned to God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Acts 11:22">22</a> News about these events reached the church in Jerusalem, so they sent Barnabas to go to Antioch.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Acts 11:23">23</a> When he arrived and saw God&#39;s grace, he was happy and encouraged everyone to stay loyal to God with a determined heart.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Acts 11:24">24</a> He was a good man, filled with the Holy Spirit and faith, and many people joined God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Acts 11:25">25</a> Barnabas went to Tarsus to look for Saul.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Acts 11:26">26</a> When he found him, he took him to Antioch. For a whole year, they met with the church and taught many people. It was in Antioch that the disciples were first called Christians.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Acts 11:27">27</a> During this time, prophets traveled from Jerusalem to Antioch.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Acts 11:28">28</a> One of them, named Agabus, stood up and showed by the Spirit that a big famine would happen all over the world, and it did happen when Claudius Caesar was in power.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Acts 11:29">29</a> The followers decided to send help to their fellow believers in Judaea, each one giving as much as they could.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Acts 11:30">30</a> They did this and sent it to the leaders through Barnabas and Saul.</p>
