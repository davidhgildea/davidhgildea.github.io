<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="2 John 1:1">1</a> I, an older leader, write to the chosen woman and her children, whom I love because we know the truth. Not only I love them, but all who know the truth do too.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="2 John 1:2">2</a> We live by the truth, and it will always be with us.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="2 John 1:3">3</a> May grace, mercy, and peace be with you from God the Father and from Jesus Christ, the Father&#39;s Son, in truth and love.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="2 John 1:4">4</a> I was very happy to see some of your children living in truth, just as we were commanded by God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="2 John 1:5">5</a> Now I ask you, lady, not as if I&#39;m writing a new rule to you, but the one we&#39;ve had from the start: that we should love each other.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="2 John 1:6">6</a> Love means following his commands. Keep the command you’ve known from the start: follow it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="2 John 1:7">7</a> Many tricksters have come into the world who do not admit that Jesus Christ has come as a human. Such a person is a liar and against Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="2 John 1:8">8</a> Take care that we don&#39;t lose what we have worked for, but that we get the full reward.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="2 John 1:9">9</a> Anyone who sins and does not follow Christ&#39;s teaching does not have God. Whoever follows Christ&#39;s teaching has God the Father and the Son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="2 John 1:10">10</a> If anyone comes to you and does not teach this message, do not welcome them into your home or wish them well.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="2 John 1:11">11</a> Anyone who wishes him well shares in his wrong actions.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="2 John 1:12">12</a> I have a lot to tell you, but I don&#39;t want to do it by writing. I hope to visit you soon and talk in person, so we can both be completely joyful.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="2 John 1:13">13</a> Your chosen sister&#39;s children say hello to you. Amen.</p>
