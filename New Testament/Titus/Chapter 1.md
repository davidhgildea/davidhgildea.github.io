<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Titus 1:1">1</a> Paul, a servant of God and an apostle of Jesus Christ, follows the faith of God&#39;s chosen ones and recognizes the truth that leads to a godly life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Titus 1:2">2</a> Hoping for eternal life, which God, who cannot lie, promised before the world started.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Titus 1:3">3</a> God showed his message at the right time through preaching, which was given to me by the order of God our Savior.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Titus 1:4">4</a> To Titus, my true son in our shared faith: May you have kindness, compassion, and peace from God the Father and Jesus Christ our Savior.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Titus 1:5">5</a> I left you in Crete so you could fix what was needed and appoint leaders in each town, as I told you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Titus 1:6">6</a> If anyone is without fault, married to one wife, and has trustworthy children who are not accused of being wild or disobedient.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Titus 1:7">7</a> A leader in the church must be without fault, as God&#39;s manager; not stubborn, not quick to anger, not addicted to alcohol, not violent, not greedy for money.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Titus 1:8">8</a> But someone who welcomes guests, likes good people, is self-controlled, fair, holy, and disciplined;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Titus 1:9">9</a> Sticking to the true message as taught to him, so he can use solid teaching to encourage and prove wrong those who disagree.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Titus 1:10">10</a> There are many who do not obey, who talk for no good reason and trick others, especially those who insist on circumcision.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Titus 1:11">11</a> These people who confuse entire families by teaching wrong things for dishonest gain must be silenced.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Titus 1:12">12</a> One of their own prophets said, &#39;People from Crete are always lying, they&#39;re bad creatures, and they are lazy.&#39;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Titus 1:13">13</a> This testimony is true. So correct them firmly, so they will have a strong faith.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Titus 1:14">14</a> Do not pay attention to Jewish stories or human rules that turn people away from the truth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Titus 1:15">15</a> To the pure, everything is pure; but to those who are spoiled and don&#39;t believe, nothing is pure because their minds and hearts are spoiled.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Titus 1:16">16</a> They claim to know God, but their actions deny Him, being hateful, disobedient, and useless for any good work.</p>
