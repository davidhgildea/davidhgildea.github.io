<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Titus 2:1">1</a> Teach what matches with good teaching.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Titus 2:2">2</a> Older men should be self-controlled, serious, wise, strong in faith, loving, and patient.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Titus 2:3">3</a> Older women should also act in a way that fits with a holy life, not spreading rumors, not drinking too much, and teaching what is good.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Titus 2:4">4</a> To help young women be sensible, love their husbands, and love their children.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Titus 2:5">5</a> To be wise, pure, take care of their homes, kind, and listen to their husbands, so God&#39;s message is not spoken against.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Titus 2:6">6</a> Advise young men also to be self-controlled.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Titus 2:7">7</a> In everything, be an example of good actions: teach with honesty, seriousness, and truthfulness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Titus 2:8">8</a> Speak clearly and rightly so no one can find fault with you, and those who disagree might feel embarrassed because they can&#39;t say anything bad about you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Titus 2:9">9</a> Tell workers to listen to their bosses, try hard to make them happy in everything, and not talk back.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Titus 2:10">10</a> Do not steal, but show complete loyalty, so that in everything you may make the teachings of God our Savior attractive.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Titus 2:11">11</a> God&#39;s grace that offers salvation has been shown to everyone.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Titus 2:12">12</a> This teaches us to say no to ungodly ways and worldly desires, and to live self-controlled, upright, and godly lives in this world.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Titus 2:13">13</a> Waiting for the happy hope and the wonderful coming of the great God and our Savior Jesus Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Titus 2:14">14</a> He gave himself for us, so he could rescue us from all wrongs and clean us for himself, to be his special people who are eager to do good.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Titus 2:15">15</a> Talk about these things, encourage others, and correct them with full authority. Don&#39;t let anyone look down on you.</p>
