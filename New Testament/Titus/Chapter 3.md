<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Titus 3:1">1</a> Remind them to follow rulers and authorities, to listen to leaders, and to be prepared for all good actions.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Titus 3:2">2</a> Don&#39;t say bad things about anyone, avoid fighting, be kind, and show humbleness to everyone.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Titus 3:3">3</a> We were once foolish, disobedient, misled, chasing many desires and pleasures, living with meanness and jealousy, full of hate, and hating each other.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Titus 3:4">4</a> But then God our Savior showed us kindness and love.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Titus 3:5">5</a> We are not saved by our good deeds, but by God&#39;s kindness. He saved us by giving us a new birth and a fresh start through the Holy Spirit.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Titus 3:6">6</a> He gave to us generously through Jesus Christ our Savior.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Titus 3:7">7</a> Being made right with God by His kindness, we should become inheritors based on the hope of everlasting life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Titus 3:8">8</a> This true saying I want you to always confirm: those who trust in God should work hard to do good. These actions are good and helpful to people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Titus 3:9">9</a> Stay away from silly questions, family histories, arguments, and fights about the law; they are useless and pointless.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Titus 3:10">10</a> Turn away from someone who keeps causing divisions after being warned once and then again.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Titus 3:11">11</a> Understand that someone like that has gone wrong, is sinning, and has judged himself guilty.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Titus 3:12">12</a> When I send Artemas or Tychicus to you, make sure to join me in Nicopolis, because I plan to stay there during the winter.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Titus 3:13">13</a> Take care to help Zenas the lawyer and Apollos on their journey carefully, so they lack nothing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Titus 3:14">14</a> Let ours also learn to do good deeds for important needs, so they are not unproductive.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Titus 3:15">15</a> Everyone with me says hello to you. Say hi to those who love us because of our shared faith. May kindness be with all of you. Goodbye.</p>
