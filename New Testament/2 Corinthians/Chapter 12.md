<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="2 Corinthians 12:1">1</a> It&#39;s not really good for me to boast, but I will talk about visions and messages from God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="2 Corinthians 12:2">2</a> Fourteen years ago, I knew a man connected to Christ. I don&#39;t know if he was in his body or out of it, but God knows. He was taken up to the third heaven.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="2 Corinthians 12:3">3</a> I knew a man, but I&#39;m not sure if it was in the body or out of the body; only God knows.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="2 Corinthians 12:4">4</a> He was taken up to paradise and heard words that cannot be spoken, which no one is allowed to say.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="2 Corinthians 12:5">5</a> I will boast about someone like that, but I won&#39;t boast about myself, except about my weaknesses.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="2 Corinthians 12:6">6</a> Even though I might want to boast, I won&#39;t be foolish, because I will speak the truth. But I hold back so no one will think more of me than what they see or hear about me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="2 Corinthians 12:7">7</a> So I wouldn&#39;t become too proud because of the great revelations, I was given a pain in my body, a messenger from Satan to beat me down, so I wouldn&#39;t become too proud.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="2 Corinthians 12:8">8</a> I asked God three times to take it away from me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="2 Corinthians 12:9">9</a> He told me, &quot;My kindness is enough for you: my power works best in weakness.&quot; So I will most happily take pride in my weaknesses, so that Christ&#39;s power can work on me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="2 Corinthians 12:10">10</a> So I am happy with weaknesses, insults, hard times, persecutions, and troubles for Christ; because when I am weak, then I am strong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="2 Corinthians 12:11">11</a> I have been foolish to boast, but you made me do it. You should have praised me because I am not less than the top apostles, even though I am nothing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="2 Corinthians 12:12">12</a> The true marks of an apostle were shown to you with great patience, and through signs, wonders, and powerful acts.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="2 Corinthians 12:13">13</a> What way were you less than other churches, except that I was not a burden to you? Please forgive me for this.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="2 Corinthians 12:14">14</a> Look, I am prepared to visit you for the third time, and I won&#39;t be a burden to you. I don&#39;t want your things, but I want you. Children shouldn&#39;t save up for their parents, but parents should save up for their children.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="2 Corinthians 12:15">15</a> I will happily give everything for you, even if the more I love you, the less you love me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="2 Corinthians 12:16">16</a> Even though I did not burden you, I was clever and tricked you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="2 Corinthians 12:17">17</a> Did I profit from you through anyone I sent to you?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="2 Corinthians 12:18">18</a> I asked Titus, and sent a friend with him. Did Titus take advantage of you? Didn&#39;t we act in the same way? Didn&#39;t we follow the same path?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="2 Corinthians 12:19">19</a> Do you think we are making excuses to you? We are speaking in front of God as we are in Christ: but everything we do, dear friends, is to help you grow.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="2 Corinthians 12:20">20</a> I&#39;m worried that when I come, I may not find you as I wish, and I may be not as you wish. I&#39;m concerned there might be arguments, jealousy, anger, conflicts, gossip, arrogance, and disorder.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="2 Corinthians 12:21">21</a> So when I return, I hope my God does not make me sad in front of you because I may have to mourn for many who have sinned but not changed their ways, or stopped their impure and immoral acts.</p>
