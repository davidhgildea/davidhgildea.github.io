<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="2 Corinthians 4:1">1</a> Because we are given this service and have received kindness, we do not lose heart.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="2 Corinthians 4:2">2</a> We have turned away from secret and shameful ways; we do not use trickery, nor do we distort God&#39;s message. Instead, by clearly showing the truth, we prove ourselves to everyone&#39;s conscience in God&#39;s presence.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="2 Corinthians 4:3">3</a> If our good news is hidden, it&#39;s hidden from those who are lost.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="2 Corinthians 4:4">4</a> The god of this world has made the minds of unbelievers so they can&#39;t see. This way, the bright Good News about Christ, who is like God, doesn&#39;t shine on them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="2 Corinthians 4:5">5</a> We do not speak about ourselves, but we speak about Jesus Christ as the Lord; and we are your servants because of Jesus.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="2 Corinthians 4:6">6</a> God, who made light shine out of darkness, has shone in our hearts to give us the understanding of God&#39;s glory shown in Jesus Christ&#39;s face.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="2 Corinthians 4:7">7</a> We hold this treasure in clay jars, so that the power shown is God&#39;s and not ours.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="2 Corinthians 4:8">8</a> We face troubles on all sides but are not crushed; we are confused but not hopeless.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="2 Corinthians 4:9">9</a> Chased, but not abandoned; knocked down, but not ruined;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="2 Corinthians 4:10">10</a> Always carrying in our body the death of Jesus, so that the life of Jesus may also be shown in our body.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="2 Corinthians 4:11">11</a> We who are alive are always facing death for Jesus so that Jesus&#39; life can also be shown in our dying bodies.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="2 Corinthians 4:12">12</a> So death is active in us, but life is in you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="2 Corinthians 4:13">13</a> We have the same spirit of faith, just as it is written, &quot;I believed, and so I spoke.&quot; We also believe, and so we speak.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="2 Corinthians 4:14">14</a> Understanding that God, who raised Jesus, will also raise us with Jesus, and will bring us together with you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="2 Corinthians 4:15">15</a> Everything is for you, so that God&#39;s great kindness can spread to more people, making them grateful and honoring God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="2 Corinthians 4:16">16</a> We don&#39;t lose heart, for even if our bodies are failing, our true self is renewed every day.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="2 Corinthians 4:17">17</a> Our small troubles, which last only a short time, are earning us a much greater and lasting glory.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="2 Corinthians 4:18">18</a> We focus not on what can be seen, but on what cannot be seen. What is seen is temporary; what is unseen is forever.</p>
