<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="2 Corinthians 9:1">1</a> Regarding helping God&#39;s people, it&#39;s unnecessary for me to write to you about it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="2 Corinthians 9:2">2</a> I know how eager you are, and I have been bragging about you to the Macedonians, telling them that Achaia has been ready since last year; and your enthusiasm has inspired many others.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="2 Corinthians 9:3">3</a> I sent the friends to make sure our praise of you wasn&#39;t empty, so you would be prepared as I told you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="2 Corinthians 9:4">4</a> We don&#39;t want to be embarrassed if some Macedonians come with me and see that you&#39;re not ready, after I&#39;ve been so sure of you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="2 Corinthians 9:5">5</a> I felt it was important to encourage our friends to visit you first and arrange the generous gift you had promised so it will be ready as a willing gift, not as something forced.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="2 Corinthians 9:6">6</a> I say this: a person who plants a little will harvest a little, and a person who plants a lot will harvest a lot.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="2 Corinthians 9:7">7</a> Each person should give what they have decided in their heart to give. They should not give with regret or because they feel forced. God loves someone who gives happily.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="2 Corinthians 9:8">8</a> God can give you plenty of grace so that you always have enough for yourselves and can give plenty to every good cause.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="2 Corinthians 9:9">9</a> It is written, &quot;He has shared widely; he has given to the poor: his goodness lasts forever.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="2 Corinthians 9:10">10</a> The one who provides seeds for the farmer also gives you bread to eat, will make your planted seeds grow, and will make your good deeds produce more.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="2 Corinthians 9:11">11</a> Becoming rich in every way to give generously, leading us to thank God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="2 Corinthians 9:12">12</a> This service not only meets the needs of God&#39;s people, but also overflows with many thanks to God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="2 Corinthians 9:13">13</a> Through this service, they praise God because you follow Christ&#39;s teaching and because you freely share with them and everyone else.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="2 Corinthians 9:14">14</a> They pray for you and deeply care because of the great grace God has given you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="2 Corinthians 9:15">15</a> Thank God for his amazing gift.</p>
