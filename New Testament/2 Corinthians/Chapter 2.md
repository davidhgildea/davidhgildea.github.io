<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="2 Corinthians 2:1">1</a> I decided not to visit you again while feeling sad.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="2 Corinthians 2:2">2</a> If I make you sad, who can cheer me up except the one I’ve made sad?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="2 Corinthians 2:3">3</a> I wrote this to you so that when I come, I won&#39;t be sad about those I should be happy about, trusting that what makes me happy also makes all of you happy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="2 Corinthians 2:4">4</a> I wrote to you with a heavy heart and many tears, not to make you sad, but to show you how much I love you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="2 Corinthians 2:5">5</a> If someone has caused sadness, they haven&#39;t deeply saddened me, but only to a small extent, so as not to weigh you all down too much.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="2 Corinthians 2:6">6</a> This punishment, given by many, is enough for that person.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="2 Corinthians 2:7">7</a> So instead you should forgive and comfort him, so he isn&#39;t overcome with too much sadness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="2 Corinthians 2:8">8</a> So I urge you to show him your love clearly.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="2 Corinthians 2:9">9</a> I wrote for this reason too, so I could see if you would follow all instructions.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="2 Corinthians 2:10">10</a> If you forgive anyone, I also forgive them. If I have forgiven anything, I did it for you in the way of Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="2 Corinthians 2:11">11</a> So Satan won&#39;t outsmart us: we know his tricks.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="2 Corinthians 2:12">12</a> When I went to Troas to tell people about Christ, God opened an opportunity for me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="2 Corinthians 2:13">13</a> I could not rest because I did not find my brother Titus. So, I said goodbye and went to Macedonia.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="2 Corinthians 2:14">14</a> Thank you to God, who always leads us to victory in Christ and shows the value of his knowledge through us everywhere.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="2 Corinthians 2:15">15</a> We are like a pleasing fragrance of Christ to God, among those who are being saved and those who are perishing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="2 Corinthians 2:16">16</a> To some, we smell like death leading to more death; to others, we smell like life leading to more life. Who is fit for such tasks?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="2 Corinthians 2:17">17</a> We are not like some who misuse God&#39;s words. We speak with honesty, as from God and in God&#39;s presence, we speak in Christ.</p>
