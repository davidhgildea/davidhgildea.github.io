<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="2 Corinthians 10:1">1</a> I, Paul, ask you kindly because of Christ&#39;s humility and gentleness. When with you I may seem weak, but when away I am bold with you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="2 Corinthians 10:2">2</a> I ask you, so that I don&#39;t have to be too confident and firm when I&#39;m with you, like I plan to be with some people who believe we live by human standards.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="2 Corinthians 10:3">3</a> Even though we live in human bodies, we do not fight with human strength.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="2 Corinthians 10:4">4</a> Our fight&#39;s tools are not physical, but they are powerful through God to destroy strong barriers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="2 Corinthians 10:5">5</a> Throwing down ideas and every proud obstacle that rises against knowing God, and making every thought obey Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="2 Corinthians 10:6">6</a> Being ready to punish all disobedience when your obedience is complete.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="2 Corinthians 10:7">7</a> Do you judge by how things look on the outside? If anyone is confident that they belong to Christ, they should remind themselves that just as they are Christ&#39;s, we are also Christ&#39;s.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="2 Corinthians 10:8">8</a> Even if I were to talk more about our power, which God has given us to help you, not to hurt you, I would not be ashamed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="2 Corinthians 10:9">9</a> So it doesn&#39;t look like I&#39;m trying to scare you with my letters.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="2 Corinthians 10:10">10</a> People say his letters are strong and forceful, but in person he is unimpressive, and his speaking is not good.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="2 Corinthians 10:11">11</a> Let someone think this: the way we are in our letters when not there, we will act the same when we are there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="2 Corinthians 10:12">12</a> We don&#39;t dare to count ourselves as one of those, or compare ourselves to those who praise themselves. But those who measure and compare themselves by their own standards are not wise.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="2 Corinthians 10:13">13</a> We won&#39;t brag about things beyond our limits, but we will boast according to the limits of the work God assigned to us, which extends to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="2 Corinthians 10:14">14</a> We don&#39;t stretch ourselves beyond our limits, as if we couldn&#39;t reach you. We have come to you too, to preach the good news about Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="2 Corinthians 10:15">15</a> We don&#39;t brag about work that isn&#39;t ours or what others have done. Instead, we hope that as your faith grows, we can do much more with you, as our role allows.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="2 Corinthians 10:16">16</a> To spread the good news in places far beyond you, without bragging about work already done by others.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="2 Corinthians 10:17">17</a> If someone wants to boast, they should boast about God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="2 Corinthians 10:18">18</a> It&#39;s not the one who praises himself who is approved, but the one whom God praises.</p>
