<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="2 Corinthians 7:1">1</a> So with these promises, dear friends, let&#39;s clean ourselves from every dirty thing of body and spirit, making our holiness complete by respecting God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="2 Corinthians 7:2">2</a> Accept us; we have not wronged anyone, we have not corrupted anyone, we have not cheated anyone.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="2 Corinthians 7:3">3</a> I am not saying this to blame you: I have already told you that you are so dear to us, we would happily live or die with you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="2 Corinthians 7:4">4</a> I speak to you with great confidence, and I take great pride in you. I am filled with comfort and am very happy, even during our troubles.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="2 Corinthians 7:5">5</a> When we arrived in Macedonia, we had no peace. We faced problems everywhere; there were conflicts outside and fears inside.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="2 Corinthians 7:6">6</a> God, who comforts the discouraged, cheered us up with Titus&#39;s arrival.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="2 Corinthians 7:7">7</a> Not just his arrival, but also the comfort he got from you made me happy, as he shared your strong desire, your sadness, and your warm feelings for me; this made me even happier.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="2 Corinthians 7:8">8</a> Even though my letter made you sad, I don&#39;t regret it, even though I did feel sorry before. I see now that the letter made you sad, but only for a little while.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="2 Corinthians 7:9">9</a> I&#39;m glad not because you were upset, but because your sadness led to change. Your sorrow was for the right reasons, so it didn&#39;t harm you because of us.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="2 Corinthians 7:10">10</a> Feeling truly sorry for our wrongs leads to a change of heart that brings us to be saved, and we won&#39;t regret it; but worldly sadness only leads to death.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="2 Corinthians 7:11">11</a> Look at how seriously you took your sadness, leading to earnestness, a desire to clear yourselves, anger at wrong, fear of doing wrong, strong longing to do right, passion for truth, and a determination to make things right! You have shown that you are innocent in this matter.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="2 Corinthians 7:12">12</a> I wrote to you not because of the person who did wrong, or the person who was wronged, but to show you our concern for you before God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="2 Corinthians 7:13">13</a> So we were cheered up by your encouragement and even more happy about Titus&#39;s joy, because you all made him feel better.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="2 Corinthians 7:14">14</a> If I have praised you to him, I am not embarrassed; just as everything we told you was true, our praises to Titus about you are also true.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="2 Corinthians 7:15">15</a> His deep feelings for you are very strong as he remembers how all of you obeyed and welcomed him with respect and nervousness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="2 Corinthians 7:16">16</a> I am happy because I trust you in everything.</p>
