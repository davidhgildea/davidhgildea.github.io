<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="2 Corinthians 6:1">1</a> We, as partners with God, urge you not to take God&#39;s kindness for nothing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="2 Corinthians 6:2">2</a> God says, &quot;I have listened to you at the right time, and on the day of rescue, I have helped you. Look, now is the right time; now is the day of rescue.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="2 Corinthians 6:3">3</a> Not causing trouble in anything, so that no one can criticize our service.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="2 Corinthians 6:4">4</a> We show we are God&#39;s servants in everything by being patient, during troubles, when we need things, and in hard times.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="2 Corinthians 6:5">5</a> Through beatings, jail time, riots, hard work, sleepless nights, and times without food;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="2 Corinthians 6:6">6</a> Through purity, understanding, patience, kindness, the Holy Spirit, and genuine love,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="2 Corinthians 6:7">7</a> By the true message, by God&#39;s power, by the right ways to act on both sides,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="2 Corinthians 6:8">8</a> Through praise and criticism, by bad and good reputation: seen as liars, but truthful.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="2 Corinthians 6:9">9</a> Although unknown, we are well recognized; although we face death, see, we live; although disciplined, we are not put to death.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="2 Corinthians 6:10">10</a> Feeling sad but always happy; being poor but making many rich; owning nothing but having everything.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="2 Corinthians 6:11">11</a> People of Corinth, we speak openly to you; our hearts are full of love for you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="2 Corinthians 6:12">12</a> You are not limited by us, but you are limited by your own feelings.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="2 Corinthians 6:13">13</a> Now, to repay me in the same way, (I&#39;m speaking to you as my children,) you should also open your hearts to us.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="2 Corinthians 6:14">14</a> Don&#39;t team up with those who don&#39;t believe: what do good and bad have in common? Or what can light share with darkness?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="2 Corinthians 6:15">15</a> What agreement does Christ have with Belial? Or what does a believer have in common with an unbeliever?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="2 Corinthians 6:16">16</a> What deal does God&#39;s temple have with idols? You are the living God&#39;s temple. God said, I will live in them and be with them; I will be their God, and they will be my people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="2 Corinthians 6:17">17</a> So leave them and be different, says God, and do not touch dirty things; then I will accept you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="2 Corinthians 6:18">18</a> I will be a Father to you, and you will be my sons and daughters, says God Almighty.</p>
