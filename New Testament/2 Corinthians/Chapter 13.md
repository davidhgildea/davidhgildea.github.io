<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="2 Corinthians 13:1">1</a> I am coming to you for the third time. Every word must be proven true by two or three witnesses.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="2 Corinthians 13:2">2</a> I warned you before, and now I&#39;m warning you again as if I were there with you, even though I&#39;m not with you now. I&#39;m writing both to those who have sinned before and to everyone else. When I come back, I won&#39;t hold back.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="2 Corinthians 13:3">3</a> Since you look for a sign that Christ speaks through me, he is not weak towards you but strong within you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="2 Corinthians 13:4">4</a> Even though he was killed because he was weak, he now lives by God&#39;s power. We too are weak as he was, but with him, we will live by God&#39;s power for you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="2 Corinthians 13:5">5</a> Check if you really have faith; test yourselves. Don&#39;t you realize that Jesus Christ is in you unless you fail the test?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="2 Corinthians 13:6">6</a> But I hope you will understand that we are not failures.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="2 Corinthians 13:7">7</a> I pray to God that you will not do wrong; not to show we are good, but so you do what is right, even if we seem to fail.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="2 Corinthians 13:8">8</a> We can&#39;t act against the truth, only for it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="2 Corinthians 13:9">9</a> We are happy when we are weak and you are strong; we truly want you to be perfect.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="2 Corinthians 13:10">10</a> So I write these things while I&#39;m not with you, so that when I am with you, I won&#39;t have to be too strict, using the authority that God gave me to build up, not tear down.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="2 Corinthians 13:11">11</a> Friends, goodbye. Aim to be flawless, be encouraged, agree with each other, live peacefully; and the God of love and peace will be with you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="2 Corinthians 13:12">12</a> Say hello to each other with a sacred kiss.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="2 Corinthians 13:13">13</a> All the holy people send you greetings.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="2 Corinthians 13:14">14</a> May the kindness of Jesus Christ, the love of God, and the sharing of the Holy Spirit, be with everyone. Amen.</p>
