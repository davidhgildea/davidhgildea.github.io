<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="2 Corinthians 3:1">1</a> Are we starting to praise ourselves again? Do we need, like some people, letters of recommendation to or from you?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="2 Corinthians 3:2">2</a> You are our letter, written in our hearts, known and read by everyone.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="2 Corinthians 3:3">3</a> It is clear that you are Christ&#39;s letter, delivered by us, not written with ink but with the Spirit of the living God, not on stone tablets but on the human heart&#39;s living pages.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="2 Corinthians 3:4">4</a> We have this confidence in God through Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="2 Corinthians 3:5">5</a> We are not capable on our own to consider anything as coming from ourselves; our ability comes from God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="2 Corinthians 3:6">6</a> God has also made us skilled servants of the new agreement; not by the written laws, but by the spirit: because the written laws can cause death, but the spirit gives life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="2 Corinthians 3:7">7</a> But if the service of death, written on stones, was so bright that the people of Israel could not look straight at Moses&#39;s face because of its shine, which was passing away:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="2 Corinthians 3:8">8</a> How could serving in the Spirit not be more glorious?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="2 Corinthians 3:9">9</a> For if the service that brings condemnation is glorious, the service that brings righteousness is far more glorious.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="2 Corinthians 3:10">10</a> Even what used to be glorious has no glory now compared to the greater glory.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="2 Corinthians 3:11">11</a> If something that ends was great, then what stays is even greater.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="2 Corinthians 3:12">12</a> Since we have this hope, we speak very clearly.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="2 Corinthians 3:13">13</a> Unlike Moses, who covered his face with a veil to prevent the Israelites from seeing the end of the fading glory.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="2 Corinthians 3:14">14</a> Their minds were closed. Even today, the same covering stays when they read the old covenant, not removed because it is taken away through Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="2 Corinthians 3:15">15</a> But even now, when people read about Moses, their hearts are covered like with a veil.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="2 Corinthians 3:16">16</a> When people turn to God, the veil will be removed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="2 Corinthians 3:17">17</a> Now God is that Spirit, and where God&#39;s Spirit is, there is freedom.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="2 Corinthians 3:18">18</a> We all, with uncovered faces, look at God&#39;s glory as if looking in a mirror and are transformed to become more like Him, from one degree of glory to another, by God&#39;s Spirit.</p>
