<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Galatians 4:1">1</a> I tell you, an heir is no different from a servant while he is still a child, even if he owns everything.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Galatians 4:2">2</a> But is under teachers and managers until the time set by the father.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Galatians 4:3">3</a> Just like that, when we were young, we were trapped under the world&#39;s basic rules.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Galatians 4:4">4</a> When the right time came, God sent his Son, born of a woman, born under the law.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Galatians 4:5">5</a> To free those who were under the law, so that we might be accepted as God&#39;s children.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Galatians 4:6">6</a> Because you are children, God has sent the spirit of his Son into your hearts, calling out, &quot;Daddy, Father.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Galatians 4:7">7</a> So you are not a servant any longer, but a son; and if a son, then God&#39;s heir through Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Galatians 4:8">8</a> But back then, when you didn&#39;t know God, you worshiped things that are not really gods.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Galatians 4:9">9</a> But now, knowing God, or actually being known by God, why do you go back to the weak and poor basic things you want to be slaves to again?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Galatians 4:10">10</a> You keep track of days, months, seasons, and years.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Galatians 4:11">11</a> I am worried about you, in case my hard work for you was for nothing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Galatians 4:12">12</a> Friends, I urge you, be like me, because I am like you: you have done me no wrong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Galatians 4:13">13</a> You know that despite my physical weakness, I first shared the good news with you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Galatians 4:14">14</a> You did not look down on or turn away from my physical illness. Instead, you welcomed me as if I were an angel of God, just like Jesus Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Galatians 4:15">15</a> Where is the happiness you talked about? I know that if you could, you would have given me your own eyes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Galatians 4:16">16</a> Have I become your enemy by telling you the truth?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Galatians 4:17">17</a> They are eagerly trying to influence you, but not in a good way; indeed, they want to shut you out so that you will try to win their approval.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Galatians 4:18">18</a> It is good to always be energetically supportive of a good cause, not just when I am with you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Galatians 4:19">19</a> My dear children, I am working hard for you again, like a mother giving birth, until you truly become like Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Galatians 4:20">20</a> I want to be with you now and change how I speak because I&#39;m unsure about you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Galatians 4:21">21</a> Ask yourselves, you who want to follow the law, don&#39;t you listen to what the law says?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Galatians 4:22">22</a> It is written that Abraham had two sons, one with a slave woman and the other with a free woman.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Galatians 4:23">23</a> The child of the slave woman was born in the ordinary way, but the child of the free woman was born because of God&#39;s promise.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Galatians 4:24">24</a> These things represent two agreements; one from Mount Sinai, leading to slavery, which is Hagar.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Galatians 4:25">25</a> This Hagar represents Mount Sinai in Arabia and corresponds to the current Jerusalem, for she and her children live in slavery.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Galatians 4:26">26</a> Jerusalem above is free and is our mother.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Galatians 4:27">27</a> It is written, &quot;Celebrate, you who cannot have children; shout out loud, you who do not go through labor pain, because the woman who is alone will have many more children than the woman with a husband.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Galatians 4:28">28</a> We, brothers and sisters, are like Isaac, children of God&#39;s promise.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Galatians 4:29">29</a> But like before, the person born in a normal human way made life hard for the person born with God&#39;s Spirit, and it&#39;s still like that now.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Galatians 4:30">30</a> What does the scripture say? Send away the slave woman and her child, because the slave woman&#39;s child will not share the inheritance with the free woman&#39;s child.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Galatians 4:31">31</a> So, friends, we are not the children of a slave woman, but of the free woman.</p>
