<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Galatians 6:1">1</a> Brothers, if someone makes a mistake, those of you who are spiritual should help them with gentleness. Think about yourself, so you are not tempted too.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Galatians 6:2">2</a> Help each other with your troubles, and in this way, you obey the law of Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Galatians 6:3">3</a> If someone thinks they are important when they are not, they are fooling themselves.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Galatians 6:4">4</a> Let everyone test their own actions, then they can take pride in themselves without comparing to someone else.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Galatians 6:5">5</a> Each person must carry their own load.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Galatians 6:6">6</a> Let the person who learns from the teachings share with their teacher in every good way.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Galatians 6:7">7</a> Don&#39;t be fooled; no one can mock God: whatever a person plants, that&#39;s what they will harvest.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Galatians 6:8">8</a> Whoever plants for selfish reasons will harvest ruin, but whoever plants for the Spirit will harvest eternal life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Galatians 6:9">9</a> Let&#39;s not get tired of doing good, because at the right time we will get a reward if we don&#39;t give up.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Galatians 6:10">10</a> Whenever we can, we should help everyone, especially those who share our faith.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Galatians 6:11">11</a> You see how big a letter I have written to you with my own hand.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Galatians 6:12">12</a> Many people want to look good outwardly, so they pressure you to get circumcised, just to avoid being persecuted for following Christ&#39;s teachings.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Galatians 6:13">13</a> Those who are circumcised don&#39;t follow the law; they just want you to be circumcised so they can boast about what they did to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Galatians 6:14">14</a> I would never boast except about the cross of Jesus Christ, which makes the world dead to me and me dead to the world.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Galatians 6:15">15</a> In Christ Jesus, being circumcised or not doesn&#39;t matter, but being a new person does.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Galatians 6:16">16</a> Everyone who follows this rule will have peace and mercy, as will all of God&#39;s people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Galatians 6:17">17</a> From now on let no one cause me trouble, because I have the scars of Jesus on my body.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Galatians 6:18">18</a> Friends, may the kindness of our Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit. Amen.</p>
