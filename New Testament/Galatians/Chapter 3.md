<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Galatians 3:1">1</a> Silly Galatians, who tricked you into not following the truth, when you clearly saw Jesus Christ shown as crucified before you?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Galatians 3:2">2</a> I want to know this from you: Did you receive the Spirit by following the law, or by believing what you heard?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Galatians 3:3">3</a> Are you so foolish? After starting with the Spirit, are you now trying to become perfect by human effort?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Galatians 3:4">4</a> Did you go through all this for nothing? If it was indeed for nothing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Galatians 3:5">5</a> So, the one who gives you the Spirit and does miracles among you, does he do it because you follow the law, or because you believe what you heard?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Galatians 3:6">6</a> Abraham trusted God, and it was considered as righteousness for him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Galatians 3:7">7</a> Understand then that those who have faith are Abraham&#39;s children.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Galatians 3:8">8</a> The Bible, knowing that God would accept non-Jews because of their faith, told Abraham the good news in advance: &quot;All peoples will be blessed through you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Galatians 3:9">9</a> So those who have faith are blessed together with Abraham, who was faithful.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Galatians 3:10">10</a> Everyone who relies on following the law is under a curse, because it is said, &quot;Cursed is anyone who does not keep doing everything written in the law.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Galatians 3:11">11</a> It&#39;s clear that no one can be seen as right by God just by following the law, for a person who is right will live by faith.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Galatians 3:12">12</a> The law does not depend on faith; rather, a person who follows the rules will have life by them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Galatians 3:13">13</a> Christ has saved us from the law&#39;s curse by becoming a curse for us, because it is written, &quot;Everyone who hangs on a tree is cursed.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Galatians 3:14">14</a> So Abraham&#39;s blessing could come to people of all nations through Jesus Christ; and so we could receive what the Spirit promised by believing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Galatians 3:15">15</a> Friends, I&#39;m speaking in human terms; even with a human agreement, once it&#39;s been made official, no one cancels or adds to it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Galatians 3:16">16</a> God made promises to Abraham and his one descendant, who is Christ, not to many descendants.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Galatians 3:17">17</a> I tell you this: the agreement God made in Christ long ago cannot be canceled by the law that came 430 years later. It cannot erase the promise.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Galatians 3:18">18</a> If the inheritance comes from the law, it is not based on a promise; but God gave it to Abraham through a promise.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Galatians 3:19">19</a> Why then do we have the law? It was put in place for when people do wrong, until the one promised would come; and it was set up by God through messengers with a go-between.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Galatians 3:20">20</a> A go-between does not just represent one person, but God is one.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Galatians 3:21">21</a> Is the law against God&#39;s promises? No way: because if there were a law that could give life, then being right with God would truly come from the law.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Galatians 3:22">22</a> The Bible has shown everyone is under sin, so that the promise could be given through faith in Jesus Christ to those who believe.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Galatians 3:23">23</a> Before we had faith, we were guarded by the law, locked up until the faith that was to be revealed later.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Galatians 3:24">24</a> The law was our teacher to lead us to Christ, so that we could be made right with God by trusting in Him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Galatians 3:25">25</a> After faith arrives, we are no longer under a teacher.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Galatians 3:26">26</a> You are all God&#39;s children through faith in Christ Jesus.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Galatians 3:27">27</a> All of you who were baptized into Christ have clothed yourselves with Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Galatians 3:28">28</a> In Christ Jesus, everyone is the same—no Jew or Greek, no slave or free person, no male or female.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Galatians 3:29">29</a> If you belong to Christ, then you are descendants of Abraham and will inherit what was promised.</p>
