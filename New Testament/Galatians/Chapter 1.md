<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Galatians 1:1">1</a> Paul, an apostle not chosen by people or through a person, but by Jesus Christ and God who brought him back from the dead.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Galatians 1:2">2</a> Me and all the friends here send greetings to the churches in Galatia.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Galatians 1:3">3</a> May you have kindness and peace from God the Father and from our Lord Jesus Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Galatians 1:4">4</a> He gave himself for our sins so he could save us from this evil world, as God our Father wanted.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Galatians 1:5">5</a> May glory be to God forever and ever. Amen.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Galatians 1:6">6</a> I am amazed that you are quickly turning away from God who called you by Christ&#39;s grace to a different good news.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Galatians 1:7">7</a> This is not really a different gospel, but some are troubling you, wanting to twist Christ&#39;s good news.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Galatians 1:8">8</a> Even if we or an angel from heaven tell you a different good news than what we already told you, that person should be considered cursed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Galatians 1:9">9</a> I told you before, and I say it again, if anyone teaches a different message than what you received, they should be rejected.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Galatians 1:10">10</a> Am I now trying to win over people or God? Or am I trying to please people? If I were still trying to please people, I wouldn&#39;t be Christ&#39;s servant.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Galatians 1:11">11</a> I assure you, friends, that the good news I shared was not made by people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Galatians 1:12">12</a> I did not get it from any person, nor was I taught it, but Jesus Christ revealed it to me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Galatians 1:13">13</a> You have heard about how I lived long ago, following the Jewish religion, and how I persecuted God&#39;s church greatly and tried to destroy it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Galatians 1:14">14</a> I advanced in the Jewish faith more than many peers in my country, being much more passionate about the traditions of my ancestors.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Galatians 1:15">15</a> But when God, who set me apart from birth and called me by his kindness,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Galatians 1:16">16</a> To show his Son through me, so I could tell about him to non-believers; I did not immediately consult with any person.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Galatians 1:17">17</a> I did not go to Jerusalem to see those who were apostles before me; instead, I went to Arabia and then came back to Damascus.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Galatians 1:18">18</a> Three years later, I went to Jerusalem to meet Peter and stayed with him for fifteen days.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Galatians 1:19">19</a> I did not see any other apostles, only James, the brother of the Lord.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Galatians 1:20">20</a> I am telling you the truth, I am not lying, and God knows this.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Galatians 1:21">21</a> Next, I went to the areas of Syria and Cilicia.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Galatians 1:22">22</a> The churches in Judea that belonged to Christ did not know me by face.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Galatians 1:23">23</a> They had only heard that the one who used to persecute us now preaches the faith he once tried to destroy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Galatians 1:24">24</a> They praised God because of me.</p>
