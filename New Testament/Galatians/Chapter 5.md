<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Galatians 5:1">1</a> Stay strong in the freedom that Christ has given us, and don&#39;t get tied up again in slavery.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Galatians 5:2">2</a> Listen, I, Paul, tell you that if you get circumcised, Christ will be of no benefit to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Galatians 5:3">3</a> I tell every circumcised man that he must follow the entire law.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Galatians 5:4">4</a> Christ no longer benefits you, whoever of you try to be right with God by the law; you have lost God&#39;s favor.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Galatians 5:5">5</a> We wait for the hope of being right with God through faith with the Spirit&#39;s help.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Galatians 5:6">6</a> In Jesus Christ, neither being circumcised nor not being circumcised matters, but having faith that shows itself through love does.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Galatians 5:7">7</a> You were running well; who stopped you from following the truth?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Galatians 5:8">8</a> This belief does not come from God who calls you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Galatians 5:9">9</a> A small amount of yeast spreads through all the dough.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Galatians 5:10">10</a> I trust you in God to think the same. But anyone who causes you trouble will face their own punishment.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Galatians 5:11">11</a> And friends, if I still tell people to be circumcised, why am I still being persecuted? Then the problem with the cross would be gone.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Galatians 5:12">12</a> I wish those upsetting you were gone.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Galatians 5:13">13</a> Friends, you are called to be free; but do not use your freedom to indulge selfish desires, instead serve each other with love.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Galatians 5:14">14</a> All the law is summed up in one command: Love your neighbor as yourself.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Galatians 5:15">15</a> If you hurt and attack each other, be careful that you don&#39;t destroy each other.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Galatians 5:16">16</a> I say this: Live by the Spirit, and you will not satisfy the desires of the body.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Galatians 5:17">17</a> The body has desires against the Spirit, and the Spirit has desires against the body. They are opposed to each other, so you cannot do what you want.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Galatians 5:18">18</a> But if the Spirit leads you, you are not bound by the law.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Galatians 5:19">19</a> The actions that come from our human nature are clear. They are: being unfaithful in marriage, having sex without being married, being impure, and wanting forbidden pleasures.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Galatians 5:20">20</a> Worshiping idols, using magic, hating, fighting, wanting to be better than others, anger, arguing, dividing people, and wrong beliefs,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Galatians 5:21">21</a> Jealousy, killings, getting drunk, wild parties, and things like these: I warn you now, as I did before, that people who do such things will not receive God&#39;s kingdom.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Galatians 5:22">22</a> The Spirit&#39;s fruit is love, happiness, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, and faith.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Galatians 5:23">23</a> Gentleness, self-control: there is no law against these things.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Galatians 5:24">24</a> Those who belong to Christ have put to death their human desires and wants.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Galatians 5:25">25</a> If we live by the Spirit, let&#39;s also follow the Spirit&#39;s lead.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Galatians 5:26">26</a> Let&#39;s not seek empty praise, causing each other trouble or being jealous of each other.</p>
