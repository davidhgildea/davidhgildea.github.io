<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Mark 10:1">1</a> He got up from there and went to the region of Judea across the Jordan. People came to him again, and as usual, he taught them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Mark 10:2">2</a> The Pharisees went to him and asked, &quot;Is it okay for a man to divorce his wife?&quot; to test him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Mark 10:3">3</a> He replied and asked them, &quot;What did Moses tell you to do?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Mark 10:4">4</a> They said, Moses allowed to write a divorce paper, and to send her away.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Mark 10:5">5</a> Jesus answered them, &quot;Because your hearts were hard, he gave you this rule.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Mark 10:6">6</a> Since the start of creation, God made them as man and woman.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Mark 10:7">7</a> A man will leave his father and mother and be united with his wife.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Mark 10:8">8</a> The two will become one body, so they are not two anymore, but one body.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Mark 10:9">9</a> So what God has united, let no one separate.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Mark 10:10">10</a> In the house, his disciples questioned him once more about that topic.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Mark 10:11">11</a> He tells them, &quot;Anyone who divorces his wife and marries another woman is unfaithful to her.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Mark 10:12">12</a> If a woman divorces her husband and marries another man, she cheats on him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Mark 10:13">13</a> People brought little children to Jesus so he could touch them. But his followers scolded those who brought the children.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Mark 10:14">14</a> But when Jesus saw this, he was very upset and said to them, &quot;Let the children come to me, do not stop them: for the kingdom of God belongs to such as these.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Mark 10:15">15</a> Truly I tell you, anyone who doesn&#39;t accept the kingdom of God like a small child will not get in.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Mark 10:16">16</a> He picked them up, touched them with his hands, and gave them his blessing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Mark 10:17">17</a> As he started on his way, someone ran up, knelt before him, and asked, &quot;Good Teacher, what must I do to get eternal life?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Mark 10:18">18</a> Jesus asked him, &quot;Why do you call me good? No one is good except God alone.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Mark 10:19">19</a> You know the commands: Do not cheat on your partner, do not kill, do not steal, do not lie, do not cheat others, respect your father and mother.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Mark 10:20">20</a> He replied, &quot;Teacher, I have followed all these since I was young.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Mark 10:21">21</a> Jesus looked at him with love and said, &quot;You&#39;re missing one thing: go, sell everything you own, and give the money to the poor. Then you will have riches in heaven. Come, carry the cross, and follow me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Mark 10:22">22</a> He felt sad when he heard this and left feeling upset because he owned a lot.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Mark 10:23">23</a> Jesus looked around and said to his followers, &quot;How difficult it is for those with wealth to enter God&#39;s kingdom!&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Mark 10:24">24</a> The followers were amazed at his words. But Jesus spoke again, and said to them, &quot;Children, how difficult it is for those who rely on their wealth to get into God&#39;s kingdom!&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Mark 10:25">25</a> It&#39;s simpler for a camel to pass through a needle&#39;s eye than for a rich person to enter God&#39;s kingdom.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Mark 10:26">26</a> They were extremely surprised and asked each other, &quot;Then who can be saved?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Mark 10:27">27</a> Jesus looked at them and said, &quot;For people it&#39;s impossible, but not for God; with God all things are possible.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Mark 10:28">28</a> Then Peter started to say to him, &quot;Look, we have left everything and followed you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Mark 10:29">29</a> Jesus replied, &quot;I tell you the truth, no one who has left home, brothers, sisters, father, mother, wife, children, or fields for me and the good news.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Mark 10:30">30</a> He will get a hundred times more now in this time: homes, brothers, sisters, mothers, children, and fields, along with troubles; and in the future world, eternal life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Mark 10:31">31</a> Many who are first will be last, and the last will be first.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Mark 10:32">32</a> They were traveling to Jerusalem. Jesus was leading them. They were amazed and scared as they followed him. He gathered the twelve disciples and started to explain what would happen to him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Mark 10:33">33</a> Look, we are going to Jerusalem; and the Son of man will be handed over to the chief priests and the teachers of the law; they will find him guilty of death and will hand him over to the foreigners.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Mark 10:34">34</a> They will make fun of him, whip him, spit on him, and kill him. But on the third day, he will come back to life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Mark 10:35">35</a> James and John, Zebedee&#39;s sons, came to Jesus and said, &quot;Teacher, we want you to do whatever we ask.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Mark 10:36">36</a> He asked them, &quot;What do you want me to do for you?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Mark 10:37">37</a> They asked him, &quot;Let us sit with you in your glory, one on your right side and the other on your left side.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Mark 10:38">38</a> But Jesus told them, You don&#39;t understand what you&#39;re asking. Can you drink the cup I drink? And be baptized with the baptism I am baptized with?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="Mark 10:39">39</a> They said to him, &quot;We can.&quot; And Jesus told them, &quot;You will truly drink the cup I drink from; and you will be baptized with the baptism I am baptized with.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="Mark 10:40">40</a> But to sit at my right or left side isn&#39;t for me to give; it will be given to those it&#39;s ready for.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="Mark 10:41">41</a> When the ten other disciples heard this, they became very upset with James and John.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="Mark 10:42">42</a> Jesus called them over and said, &quot;You know that the ones who are considered rulers over the people have power over them, and the important people have authority over them.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v43"><a class="verse-num" href="#v43" aria-label="Mark 10:43">43</a> However, among you it should be different: whoever wants to be important must serve you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v44"><a class="verse-num" href="#v44" aria-label="Mark 10:44">44</a> Anyone who wants to be the most important among you must serve everyone.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v45"><a class="verse-num" href="#v45" aria-label="Mark 10:45">45</a> Even the Son of man did not come to be served, but to serve, and to give his life to save many people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v46"><a class="verse-num" href="#v46" aria-label="Mark 10:46">46</a> They arrived at Jericho. As he was leaving Jericho with his followers and many people, a blind man named Bartimaeus, Timaeus&#39;s son, was sitting by the road asking for money.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v47"><a class="verse-num" href="#v47" aria-label="Mark 10:47">47</a> When he heard it was Jesus from Nazareth, he started shouting, &quot;Jesus, son of David, please have mercy on me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v48"><a class="verse-num" href="#v48" aria-label="Mark 10:48">48</a> Many people told him to be quiet, but he shouted even louder, &quot;Son of David, have mercy on me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v49"><a class="verse-num" href="#v49" aria-label="Mark 10:49">49</a> Jesus stopped and told them to call the man. They called the blind man, saying, &quot;Cheer up, get up; he is calling you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v50"><a class="verse-num" href="#v50" aria-label="Mark 10:50">50</a> He threw off his coat, got up, and went to Jesus.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v51"><a class="verse-num" href="#v51" aria-label="Mark 10:51">51</a> Jesus answered, &quot;What do you want me to do for you?&quot; The blind man said, &quot;I want to see again.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v52"><a class="verse-num" href="#v52" aria-label="Mark 10:52">52</a> Jesus said to him, &quot;Go; your faith has healed you.&quot; Right away, he could see, and he followed Jesus on the path.</p>
