<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Mark 12:1">1</a> He started to tell them stories. A man planted a vineyard, put a fence around it, made a pit for the grape juice, built a watchtower, rented it to farmers, and then he went away to another place.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Mark 12:2">2</a> During the right time, he sent a worker to the farmers to collect some of the vineyard&#39;s fruit from them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Mark 12:3">3</a> They grabbed him, hit him, and sent him away with nothing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Mark 12:4">4</a> He sent them another servant, but they threw stones at him, hurt his head, and sent him away badly treated.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Mark 12:5">5</a> He sent another, whom they killed, and many more, beating some and killing others.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Mark 12:6">6</a> Still having one beloved son, he sent him to them last, thinking, They will respect my son.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Mark 12:7">7</a> But the farmers said to each other, &quot;This is the heir; come, let&#39;s kill him, and the inheritance will be ours.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Mark 12:8">8</a> They seized him, killed him, and threw him out of the vineyard.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Mark 12:9">9</a> What will the owner of the vineyard do then? He will come and destroy the farmers, and will give the vineyard to others.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Mark 12:10">10</a> Have you not read this part of the Scripture: The stone the builders did not want is now the most important stone.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Mark 12:11">11</a> This was what God did, and it is amazing to us.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Mark 12:12">12</a> They tried to arrest him but were afraid of the crowd because they knew the story was about them, so they left him and went away.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Mark 12:13">13</a> Some Pharisees and Herodians were sent to trap him by what he said.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Mark 12:14">14</a> When they arrived, they said to him, &quot;Teacher, we know you are honest and don&#39;t favor anyone, because you don&#39;t judge by appearances but teach God&#39;s way truthfully: Is it right to pay taxes to Caesar or not?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Mark 12:15">15</a> Should we pay, or shouldn&#39;t we? But knowing they were fake, he said to them, &quot;Why are you testing me? Show me a coin so I can look at it.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Mark 12:16">16</a> They brought it to him. He asked them, &quot;Whose picture and title is this?&quot; They answered, &quot;Caesar&#39;s.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Mark 12:17">17</a> Jesus replied, &quot;Give to the leader what belongs to the leader, and to God what belongs to God.&quot; They were amazed at him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Mark 12:18">18</a> The Sadducees, who say there is no resurrection, came to him and asked him, saying,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Mark 12:19">19</a> Teacher, Moses told us that if a man dies and leaves a wife but no children, his brother should marry the widow and have children for his brother.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Mark 12:20">20</a> There were seven brothers. The first married but died without having children.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Mark 12:21">21</a> The second brother married her and then died, leaving no children; and the same happened with the third brother.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Mark 12:22">22</a> All seven men married her and had no children. Finally, the woman also died.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Mark 12:23">23</a> In the resurrection, when they come back to life, whose wife will she be among them? Because all seven were married to her.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Mark 12:24">24</a> Jesus answered them, &quot;Aren&#39;t you mistaken because you don’t know the scriptures or God&#39;s power?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Mark 12:25">25</a> When they rise from the dead, they don&#39;t marry or are married off; they are like the angels in heaven.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Mark 12:26">26</a> About the dead, that they come back to life: haven&#39;t you read in Moses&#39; book, how in the bush God spoke to him, saying, I am the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Mark 12:27">27</a> He is not the God of those who are dead, but the God of those who are alive; you are making a big mistake.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Mark 12:28">28</a> A teacher of the law came forward. After hearing them discuss, and noticing Jesus had given good answers, he asked, &quot;What is the most important commandment?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Mark 12:29">29</a> Jesus replied, &quot;The most important commandment is, &#39;Listen, Israel: God is our only Lord.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Mark 12:30">30</a> You should love God with all your heart, all your soul, all your mind, and all your strength: this is the first commandment.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Mark 12:31">31</a> The second command is this: Love your neighbor as yourself. No other commandment is greater than these.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Mark 12:32">32</a> The teacher said to him, &quot;Good, Teacher, you have spoken the truth: there is only one God, and no one else but Him.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Mark 12:33">33</a> To love God with all your heart, mind, soul, and strength, and to love others as you love yourself, is more important than any burnt offerings and sacrifices.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Mark 12:34">34</a> When Jesus saw he answered wisely, he said to him, &quot;You are close to God&#39;s kingdom.&quot; After that, no one dared to ask him more questions.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Mark 12:35">35</a> Jesus replied while teaching in the temple, &quot;Why do the teachers say that the Messiah is David&#39;s son?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Mark 12:36">36</a> David himself, inspired by the Holy Spirit, said, &quot;God told my lord, &#39;Sit at my right side until I make your enemies a place to rest your feet.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Mark 12:37">37</a> David himself calls him Lord; so how is he his son? And the people liked listening to him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Mark 12:38">38</a> He taught them, &quot;Be careful of the teachers of the law who like to wear long robes and enjoy being greeted with respect in the marketplaces.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="Mark 12:39">39</a> The best seats in the meeting places, and the places of honor at banquets:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="Mark 12:40">40</a> They eat up widows&#39; homes and pretend to be pious with long prayers; they will face stricter punishment.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="Mark 12:41">41</a> Jesus sat opposite the money box and watched how people put money into it; many rich people gave a lot.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="Mark 12:42">42</a> A poor widow came and put in two small coins, worth a little.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v43"><a class="verse-num" href="#v43" aria-label="Mark 12:43">43</a> He called his followers and told them, &quot;I truly tell you, this poor widow has given more than anyone else who has donated to the treasury.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v44"><a class="verse-num" href="#v44" aria-label="Mark 12:44">44</a> Everyone else gave from their wealth, but she, in her poverty, gave everything she had to live on.</p>
