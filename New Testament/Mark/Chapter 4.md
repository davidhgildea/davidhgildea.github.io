<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Mark 4:1">1</a> He started teaching by the sea again, and so many people came to him that he got into a boat and sat on the water, while all the people stayed on the shore.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Mark 4:2">2</a> He taught them many things using stories and explained his teachings to them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Mark 4:3">3</a> Listen; Look, a farmer went out to plant seeds.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Mark 4:4">4</a> As he was planting seeds, some dropped on the path, and birds flew down and ate them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Mark 4:5">5</a> Some seeds fell on rocky soil where there wasn&#39;t much dirt; they quickly grew because the soil was shallow.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Mark 4:6">6</a> When the sun rose, it burned the plant, and because the plant had no roots, it dried up.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Mark 4:7">7</a> Some seeds fell among thorns, and when the thorns grew, they choked the seeds so they produced no fruit.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Mark 4:8">8</a> Some seeds fell on good soil and grew, making more seeds. They produced thirty, sixty, or even a hundred times more.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Mark 4:9">9</a> He told them, &quot;If anyone can hear, listen carefully.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Mark 4:10">10</a> When he was alone, the people with the twelve disciples asked him about the parable.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Mark 4:11">11</a> He told them, &quot;You are given the knowledge of God&#39;s kingdom&#39;s secret, but to outsiders, everything is told in stories.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Mark 4:12">12</a> They can look but not see, and listen but not understand, so they won&#39;t turn back and be forgiven for their wrongs.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Mark 4:13">13</a> He asked them, &quot;Don&#39;t you understand this story? How then will you understand any story?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Mark 4:14">14</a> The farmer plants the message.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Mark 4:15">15</a> These are the ones near the path where the message is planted; but as soon as they hear it, Satan comes quickly and removes the message that was planted in their hearts.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Mark 4:16">16</a> These are also the ones planted on rocky soil; they hear the message and quickly accept it with joy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Mark 4:17">17</a> They have no strong root, and only last for a while. When trouble or persecution comes because of the message, they quickly get upset.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Mark 4:18">18</a> These are the people whose seed fell among thorns; they are those who hear the message,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Mark 4:19">19</a> Worries about life, the trickery of wealth, and wanting other things crowd out God&#39;s message, making it unproductive.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Mark 4:20">20</a> These are the ones planted on good soil: they hear the message, accept it, and produce crops—some thirty times what was sown, some sixty times, and some a hundred times.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Mark 4:21">21</a> He asked them, &quot;Do you bring a candle to hide it under a bowl or a bed? Shouldn&#39;t it be placed on a candle holder?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Mark 4:22">22</a> Nothing hidden will stay that way; what was secret will become known.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Mark 4:23">23</a> If anyone can hear, let them listen.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Mark 4:24">24</a> He told them, &quot;Be careful what you listen to: the way you give to others will be the way you get from others, and if you listen well, you will receive even more.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Mark 4:25">25</a> Whoever has will receive more, and whoever does not have, even what they have will be taken away.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Mark 4:26">26</a> He said, “God&#39;s kingdom is like when a man plants seeds in the soil.”</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Mark 4:27">27</a> He sleeps and wakes, day and night, and the seed sprouts and grows, but he doesn&#39;t know how.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Mark 4:28">28</a> The earth produces its own crops: first the plant, then the head of grain, then the fully developed grain in the head.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Mark 4:29">29</a> But when the fruit is ready, right away he uses the sickle, because it&#39;s time to harvest.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Mark 4:30">30</a> He asked, &quot;What can we compare God&#39;s kingdom to? What example can we use to describe it?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Mark 4:31">31</a> It is like a tiny mustard seed that, when planted in the ground, is smaller than all the other seeds in the ground.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Mark 4:32">32</a> When it&#39;s planted, it grows and becomes bigger than all the plants, and it spreads out big branches so birds can rest in its shade.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Mark 4:33">33</a> Using many stories like these, he shared his message with them, as much as they could understand.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Mark 4:34">34</a> He always taught using stories, but when alone with his followers, he explained everything.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Mark 4:35">35</a> That same day, when evening came, he said to them, &quot;Let&#39;s go across to the other side.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Mark 4:36">36</a> After sending the crowd away, they went with him on the boat just as he was. There were also other small boats with him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Mark 4:37">37</a> A big storm came with strong winds, and the waves hit the boat until it was full of water.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Mark 4:38">38</a> He was at the back of the boat, sleeping on a cushion. They woke him and said, &quot;Teacher, don&#39;t you care if we drown?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="Mark 4:39">39</a> He got up, told the wind to stop, and said to the sea, &quot;Quiet, be calm.&quot; Then the wind stopped, and it was very calm.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="Mark 4:40">40</a> He asked them, &quot;Why are you so scared? Why don&#39;t you have faith?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="Mark 4:41">41</a> They were very afraid and asked each other, &quot;Who is this man that even the wind and the sea listen to him?&quot;</p>
