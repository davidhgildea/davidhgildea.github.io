<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Mark 7:1">1</a> The Pharisees and some teachers of the Law who were from Jerusalem gathered around him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Mark 7:2">2</a> When they saw some of his followers eating bread with dirty hands, they criticized them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Mark 7:3">3</a> The Pharisees and all the Jews do not eat unless they often wash their hands, following the elders&#39; tradition.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Mark 7:4">4</a> When they return from the market, they do not eat unless they wash. They also follow many other rules, like washing cups, pots, metal bowls, and tables.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Mark 7:5">5</a> The Pharisees and teachers of the law asked Jesus, &quot;Why don&#39;t your disciples follow the elders&#39; traditions, but eat bread with dirty hands?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Mark 7:6">6</a> He replied, &quot;Isaiah was right when he prophesied about you fakes, as it is written, &#39;These people show me respect with their words, but their hearts are not with me.&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Mark 7:7">7</a> They worship me for nothing, teaching human rules as if they were God&#39;s teachings.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Mark 7:8">8</a> You ignore God&#39;s command to follow human traditions, like washing pots and cups, and you do many other similar things.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Mark 7:9">9</a> He told them, &quot;You completely ignore God&#39;s command to follow your own tradition.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Mark 7:10">10</a> Moses said, Respect your father and mother; and anyone who speaks badly of their father or mother must be put to death.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Mark 7:11">11</a> But you say, if someone tells their father or mother, &#39;It is Corban&#39;—which means &#39;a gift&#39;—so you cannot benefit from me; then they don’t have to help.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Mark 7:12">12</a> And you don&#39;t let him do anything for his father or mother anymore.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Mark 7:13">13</a> You make God&#39;s word useless with your tradition that you&#39;ve passed down, and you do many things like that.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Mark 7:14">14</a> When he gathered everyone around, he told them, &quot;Listen to me, all of you, and understand:&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Mark 7:15">15</a> Nothing outside a person can make them unclean by going into them. Rather, it is what comes out of a person that makes them unclean.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Mark 7:16">16</a> If anyone can hear, let them listen.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Mark 7:17">17</a> After he went into the house away from the crowd, his followers asked him about the story.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Mark 7:18">18</a> He asked them, &quot;Do you also not understand? Don&#39;t you realize that nothing entering a person from outside can make them unclean?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Mark 7:19">19</a> It doesn&#39;t go into his heart but into his stomach and then out of the body, cleaning out all foods.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Mark 7:20">20</a> He said, What comes out of a person is what makes them unclean.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Mark 7:21">21</a> From inside, from people&#39;s hearts, come bad thoughts, cheating in marriage, sexual sins, and killings.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Mark 7:22">22</a> Stealing, wanting what others have, bad behavior, lying, inappropriate desires, looking to harm others, speaking against God, thinking too highly of oneself, acting without sense.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Mark 7:23">23</a> All these bad things start inside a person and make them unclean.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Mark 7:24">24</a> He got up from there, went to the area near Tyre and Sidon, went into a house, and did not want anyone to know it, but he could not stay hidden.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Mark 7:25">25</a> A woman whose daughter had an evil spirit heard about him, went to him, and knelt down.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Mark 7:26">26</a> The woman was Greek, from Syria-Phoenicia. She begged Jesus to drive the demon out of her daughter.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Mark 7:27">27</a> Jesus said to her, &quot;Let the children eat first; it&#39;s not right to take the children&#39;s food and give it to the dogs.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Mark 7:28">28</a> She replied, &quot;Yes, God, even the dogs under the table eat the children&#39;s crumbs.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Mark 7:29">29</a> He told her, &quot;Because of your words, go; the evil spirit has left your daughter.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Mark 7:30">30</a> When she got home, she saw that the demon had left, and her daughter was lying on the bed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Mark 7:31">31</a> Leaving the region of Tyre and Sidon, he went to the Sea of Galilee, passing through the area of Decapolis.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Mark 7:32">32</a> They brought to him a person who was deaf and had trouble speaking, and they asked him to touch him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Mark 7:33">33</a> He took the man away from the crowd, put his fingers in the man&#39;s ears, spit, and touched the man&#39;s tongue.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Mark 7:34">34</a> Looking up to heaven, he sighed and said to him, &quot;Ephphatha,&quot; which means, &quot;Be opened.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Mark 7:35">35</a> Right away his ears could hear, his tongue was freed, and he spoke clearly.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Mark 7:36">36</a> He told them not to tell anyone, but the more he insisted, the more they spread the news.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Mark 7:37">37</a> They were extremely amazed and said, &quot;He does everything well: he makes the deaf hear and the mute speak.&quot;</p>
