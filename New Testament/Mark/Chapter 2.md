<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Mark 2:1">1</a> He went back to Capernaum after a few days, and people heard that he was at home.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Mark 2:2">2</a> Right away, many people came together, so much that there was no space for them, not even near the door, and he shared God&#39;s message with them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Mark 2:3">3</a> They came to him, carrying a man paralyzed by sickness, who was carried by four people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Mark 2:4">4</a> When they couldn&#39;t get close to him because of the crowd, they opened up the roof above where he was. After they made a hole, they lowered the mat where the person with paralysis was lying.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Mark 2:5">5</a> When Jesus saw how much they believed, he said to the man who could not move, &quot;Son, your sins are forgiven.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Mark 2:6">6</a> Some teachers were sitting there, thinking to themselves.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Mark 2:7">7</a> Why does this man speak such insults? Who can forgive sins except God alone?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Mark 2:8">8</a> Right away, when Jesus knew in his spirit that they were thinking this way inside, he asked them, &quot;Why are you thinking these things in your hearts?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Mark 2:9">9</a> Is it easier to tell someone with paralysis, &quot;Your sins are forgiven,&quot; or to say, &quot;Get up, take your mat, and walk?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Mark 2:10">10</a> So you may know that the Son of man has the power on earth to forgive sins, he said to the sick man with paralysis.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Mark 2:11">11</a> I tell you, get up, take your bed, and go home.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Mark 2:12">12</a> Right away he got up, picked up his mat, and walked out in front of everyone. They were all so amazed, they praised God, saying, &quot;We&#39;ve never seen anything like this.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Mark 2:13">13</a> He went out again by the sea, and all the people came to him, and he taught them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Mark 2:14">14</a> As he walked by, he saw Levi, Alphaeus&#39; son, sitting at the tax booth and told him, &quot;Follow me.&quot; Levi got up and followed him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Mark 2:15">15</a> As Jesus was eating in his house, many tax collectors and wrongdoers were also eating with him and his followers, because there were a lot of them, and they followed him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Mark 2:16">16</a> When the teachers of the law and Pharisees saw him eating with tax collectors and sinners, they asked his followers, &quot;Why does he eat and drink with tax collectors and sinners?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Mark 2:17">17</a> When Jesus heard this, he said to them, &quot;Healthy people don&#39;t need a doctor, but sick people do. I didn&#39;t come to call the good, but sinners to change their ways.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Mark 2:18">18</a> John&#39;s followers and the Pharisees often fast, and they came and asked him, &quot;Why do John&#39;s followers and the Pharisees fast, but your followers do not fast?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Mark 2:19">19</a> Jesus asked them, &quot;Can wedding guests fast while the groom is with them? As long as they have the groom, they can&#39;t fast.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Mark 2:20">20</a> But a time will come when the bridegroom will be taken from them, and then they will fast on those days.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Mark 2:21">21</a> No one sews a new piece of cloth onto an old piece of clothing. If they do, the new patch pulls away from the old and the tear gets bigger.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Mark 2:22">22</a> No one puts new wine into old bottles; otherwise, the new wine will burst the bottles, the wine will spill out, and the bottles will be ruined. But new wine should be put into new bottles.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Mark 2:23">23</a> He walked through the cornfields on the Sabbath, and as they walked, his followers started to pick corn ears.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Mark 2:24">24</a> The Pharisees asked him, &quot;Look, why do they do things on the Sabbath day that are not allowed?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Mark 2:25">25</a> He asked them, &quot;Have you never read what David did when he was in need and hungry, along with his companions?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Mark 2:26">26</a> He entered God&#39;s house when Abiathar was high priest, ate the sacred bread, which only priests can legally eat, and shared it with his companions.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Mark 2:27">27</a> He said to them, &quot;The sabbath was made for people, not people for the sabbath.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Mark 2:28">28</a> So, the Son of Man is also the ruler of the Sabbath.</p>
