<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Mark 9:1">1</a> He told them, &quot;Truly, I tell you, some standing here won&#39;t die before they see God&#39;s kingdom come with power.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Mark 9:2">2</a> Six days later, Jesus took Peter, James, and John up a tall mountain alone. There, he changed in appearance before them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Mark 9:3">3</a> His clothes became very bright and white, whiter than anyone in the world could make them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Mark 9:4">4</a> Elias and Moses appeared to them and were talking with Jesus.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Mark 9:5">5</a> Peter replied to Jesus, &quot;Teacher, it&#39;s good that we are here. Let&#39;s make three shelters: one for you, one for Moses, and one for Elijah.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Mark 9:6">6</a> He did not know what to say because they were very afraid.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Mark 9:7">7</a> A cloud covered them, and a voice from the cloud said, &quot;This is my dear Son: listen to him.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Mark 9:8">8</a> Suddenly, when they looked around, they saw no one else except Jesus with them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Mark 9:9">9</a> As they came down from the mountain, he told them not to tell anyone what they had seen until the Son of Man had risen from the dead.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Mark 9:10">10</a> They kept those words to themselves, wondering what &quot;rising from the dead&quot; meant.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Mark 9:11">11</a> They asked him, &quot;Why do the teachers say that Elijah must come first?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Mark 9:12">12</a> He replied, &quot;Elijah truly comes first to restore everything; but it is written that the Son of Man must endure many hardships and be ignored.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Mark 9:13">13</a> I tell you, Elijah has already come, and they did to him whatever they wanted, just as it is written about him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Mark 9:14">14</a> When he came to his disciples, he saw a large crowd around them and the scribes asking them questions.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Mark 9:15">15</a> Right away, when the people saw him, they were very surprised and ran up to greet him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Mark 9:16">16</a> He asked the teachers, &quot;What are you discussing with them?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Mark 9:17">17</a> Someone in the crowd answered, &quot;Teacher, I brought you my son who has a mute spirit.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Mark 9:18">18</a> Wherever it takes him, it throws him into fits. He foams at the mouth, grinds his teeth, and becomes weak. I asked your followers to drive it out, but they couldn&#39;t.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Mark 9:19">19</a> He replied, &quot;You untrusting people, how much longer must I stay with you? How much longer must I put up with you? Bring the boy to me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Mark 9:20">20</a> They brought the boy to Jesus, and as soon as the evil spirit saw Jesus, it threw the boy into a fit. He fell to the ground, rolling around and foaming at the mouth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Mark 9:21">21</a> He asked his father, &quot;How long has he had this?&quot; His father replied, &quot;Since he was a child.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Mark 9:22">22</a> Many times it has thrown him into the fire and water to kill him. But if you can do anything, have pity on us and help.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Mark 9:23">23</a> Jesus told him, &quot;If you can believe, everything is possible for the one who believes.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Mark 9:24">24</a> Right away, the child&#39;s father cried out with tears, &quot;God, I believe; help me with my doubt.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Mark 9:25">25</a> When Jesus saw the crowd running to join them, he told off the evil spirit, saying, &quot;You silent and hearingless spirit, I command you, come out of him and never go back into him again.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Mark 9:26">26</a> The spirit screamed, hurt him badly, and left him. He looked like he was dead, so many people said, &quot;He is dead.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Mark 9:27">27</a> Jesus took his hand, helped him up, and he stood.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Mark 9:28">28</a> When he entered the house, his followers asked him in private, &quot;Why couldn&#39;t we drive it out?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Mark 9:29">29</a> He told them, &quot;This kind can only go out through prayer and fasting.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Mark 9:30">30</a> They left that place, traveled through Galilee, and he didn&#39;t want anyone to know about it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Mark 9:31">31</a> He taught his followers, telling them, &quot;The Son of Man will be handed over to people, and they will kill him; but after he is dead, he will rise on the third day.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Mark 9:32">32</a> But they did not understand what he meant, and were scared to ask him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Mark 9:33">33</a> He came to Capernaum, and in the house, he asked them, &quot;What were you arguing about on the road?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Mark 9:34">34</a> But they kept quiet because on the way they had argued about who should be the most important.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Mark 9:35">35</a> He sat down, called the twelve, and said to them, &quot;If anyone wants to be first, they must be last of all and serve everyone.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Mark 9:36">36</a> He picked up a child and placed him among them. Holding him in his arms, he said to them,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Mark 9:37">37</a> Anyone who welcomes a child like this because of me is also welcoming me. And anyone who welcomes me is not just welcoming me but also the one who sent me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Mark 9:38">38</a> John replied, &quot;Teacher, we saw someone driving out demons using your name, and he doesn&#39;t join us. So we stopped him because he doesn&#39;t join us.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="Mark 9:39">39</a> Jesus said, Do not stop him, because if someone does a miracle in my name, he will not easily say bad things about me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="Mark 9:40">40</a> Anyone who is not against us is with us.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="Mark 9:41">41</a> Anyone who gives you a drink of water because you follow Christ will truly not miss out on their reward.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="Mark 9:42">42</a> Whoever hurts one of these little ones who believe in me, it would be better for them to have a heavy stone tied around their neck and to be thrown into the sea.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v43"><a class="verse-num" href="#v43" aria-label="Mark 9:43">43</a> If your hand causes you to sin, cut it off. It&#39;s better to live with one hand than to have two hands and go to hell, where the fire never goes out.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v44"><a class="verse-num" href="#v44" aria-label="Mark 9:44">44</a> Their worms never die, and the fire never goes out.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v45"><a class="verse-num" href="#v45" aria-label="Mark 9:45">45</a> If your foot causes you to sin, cut it off: it is better for you to enter life with a limp than to have two feet and be thrown into hell, where the fire never goes out.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v46"><a class="verse-num" href="#v46" aria-label="Mark 9:46">46</a> Their worms never die, and the fire never goes out.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v47"><a class="verse-num" href="#v47" aria-label="Mark 9:47">47</a> If your eye causes you to sin, take it out. It is better to go into God&#39;s kingdom with one eye than to have two eyes and be thrown into hell fire.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v48"><a class="verse-num" href="#v48" aria-label="Mark 9:48">48</a> Where their worm does not die, and the fire is not put out.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v49"><a class="verse-num" href="#v49" aria-label="Mark 9:49">49</a> Everyone will be purified with fire, and every offering will be seasoned with salt.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v50"><a class="verse-num" href="#v50" aria-label="Mark 9:50">50</a> Salt is good, but if the salt becomes tasteless, how can you make it salty again? Keep salt within you and live peacefully with each other.</p>
