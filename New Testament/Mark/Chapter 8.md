<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Mark 8:1">1</a> During that time, there was a large crowd with nothing to eat, so Jesus called his followers to him and said to them,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Mark 8:2">2</a> I feel sorry for all these people because they have been with me for three days and have nothing to eat.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Mark 8:3">3</a> If I send them home hungry, they might collapse on the way, because some of them have come from a long distance.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Mark 8:4">4</a> And his followers asked him, &quot;Where can anyone get enough bread to feed these people in this remote place?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Mark 8:5">5</a> He asked them, &quot;How many loaves do you have?&quot; And they said, &quot;Seven.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Mark 8:6">6</a> He told the people to sit on the ground. He took the seven loaves, thanked God, broke them, and gave them to his disciples to give to the people; and the disciples gave the bread to the people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Mark 8:7">7</a> They had a few little fish; he gave thanks for them and told them to put these before the people too.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Mark 8:8">8</a> So they ate, were satisfied, and gathered seven baskets of leftover pieces.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Mark 8:9">9</a> About four thousand people had eaten, and he sent them away.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Mark 8:10">10</a> Right away he got into a boat with his followers and went to the area of Dalmanutha.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Mark 8:11">11</a> The Pharisees came out and started to question him, wanting a sign from heaven to test him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Mark 8:12">12</a> He sighed deeply and said, &quot;Why does this generation look for a sign? Truly, I tell you, no sign will be given to this generation.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Mark 8:13">13</a> He left them, got back into the boat, and went to the opposite shore.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Mark 8:14">14</a> The followers had forgotten to bring bread and had only one loaf with them on the boat.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Mark 8:15">15</a> He warned them, &quot;Be careful and watch out for the Pharisees&#39; yeast and Herod&#39;s yeast.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Mark 8:16">16</a> They talked to each other, saying, &quot;It&#39;s because we don&#39;t have bread.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Mark 8:17">17</a> When Jesus realized this, he asked them, &quot;Why are you worried about not having bread? Don&#39;t you see or understand? Is your heart still hard?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Mark 8:18">18</a> You have eyes but don&#39;t see, and ears but don&#39;t hear? Don&#39;t you remember?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Mark 8:19">19</a> When I divided the five loaves for five thousand people, how many baskets of leftover pieces did you gather? They answered him, &quot;Twelve.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Mark 8:20">20</a> When you fed the four thousand, how many full baskets of leftovers did you gather? They said, &quot;Seven.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Mark 8:21">21</a> He asked them, &quot;Why don&#39;t you understand?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Mark 8:22">22</a> He came to Bethsaida; people brought a blind man to him and asked him to touch the man.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Mark 8:23">23</a> He took the blind man&#39;s hand, guided him out of the town, spat on his eyes, put his hands on him, and asked if he could see anything.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Mark 8:24">24</a> He looked up and said, &quot;I see people like walking trees.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Mark 8:25">25</a> Then he put his hands on the man&#39;s eyes again, and told him to look up. The man&#39;s sight returned, and he could see everyone clearly.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Mark 8:26">26</a> He sent him home with the instruction, &quot;Don&#39;t go into the town or tell anyone there.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Mark 8:27">27</a> Jesus and his followers went to the towns of Caesarea Philippi. On the way, he asked them, &quot;Who do people say I am?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Mark 8:28">28</a> They replied, &quot;John the Baptist; but some say Elijah; and others, one of the prophets.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Mark 8:29">29</a> He asks them, &quot;But who do you say I am?&quot; And Peter answers, &quot;You are the Christ.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Mark 8:30">30</a> He told them not to tell anyone about him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Mark 8:31">31</a> He started to teach them that the Son of Man had to go through a lot of suffering, be turned away by the elders, the main priests, and the writers, be killed, and then come back to life after three days.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Mark 8:32">32</a> He spoke those words clearly. Peter took him aside and started to correct him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Mark 8:33">33</a> When he turned around and saw his followers, he scolded Peter, saying, &quot;Get away from me, Satan; you do not care about what God wants, but what people want.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Mark 8:34">34</a> When he gathered the crowd and his students, he told them, &quot;If anyone wants to follow me, they must forget about their own desires, carry their own cross, and come with me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Mark 8:35">35</a> Anyone who wants to save their life will lose it, but anyone who loses their life for me and the good news will save it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Mark 8:36">36</a> What good is it for someone to have everything in the world but lose their own life?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Mark 8:37">37</a> What can a person offer to buy back their life?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Mark 8:38">38</a> Anyone who is ashamed of me and my words in this unfaithful and sinful time, I, the Son of Man, will also be ashamed of when I come with the glory of my Father and the holy angels.</p>
