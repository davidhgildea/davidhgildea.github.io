<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Mark 16:1">1</a> After the Sabbath ended, Mary Magdalene, Mary the mother of James, and Salome bought sweet spices to anoint him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Mark 16:2">2</a> Early on the first day of the week, they went to the tomb at sunrise.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Mark 16:3">3</a> They asked each other, &quot;Who will move the stone away from the tomb&#39;s entrance for us?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Mark 16:4">4</a> When they looked, they saw the stone was moved aside because it was very big.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Mark 16:5">5</a> When they went into the tomb, they saw a young man sitting on the right side, wearing a long white robe, and they were scared.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Mark 16:6">6</a> He tells them, &quot;Don&#39;t be scared. You are looking for Jesus of Nazareth, who was crucified. He has risen; he is not here. Look at the place where they put him.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Mark 16:7">7</a> Go and tell his followers and Peter that he is going ahead of you to Galilee; there you will see him, just as he told you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Mark 16:8">8</a> They quickly left the tomb, shaking and amazed. They didn&#39;t say anything to anyone because they were afraid.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Mark 16:9">9</a> When Jesus rose early on the first day of the week, he first appeared to Mary Magdalene, who he had freed from seven demons.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Mark 16:10">10</a> She went and told those who had been with him while they were sad and crying.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Mark 16:11">11</a> When they heard she had seen him alive, they did not believe it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Mark 16:12">12</a> Later, he showed himself in a different way to two of them as they were walking into the countryside.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Mark 16:13">13</a> They went and told the others, but the others did not believe them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Mark 16:14">14</a> Later, he showed up to the eleven disciples while they were eating and scolded them for their lack of faith and stubbornness, because they didn&#39;t believe those who had seen him after he came back to life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Mark 16:15">15</a> He told them, &quot;Go into the whole world and tell the good news to everyone.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Mark 16:16">16</a> Anyone who believes and is baptized will be saved; but anyone who does not believe will be condemned.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Mark 16:17">17</a> People who believe will have these signs: They will drive out demons in my name; they will speak in new languages.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Mark 16:18">18</a> They will pick up snakes; and if they drink something poisonous, it will not harm them; they will touch the sick, and they will get better.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Mark 16:19">19</a> After the Lord spoke to them, he went up to heaven and sat at the right side of God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Mark 16:20">20</a> They went out and shared the message everywhere, with God helping them, and showed that it was true by the miracles that happened. Amen.</p>
