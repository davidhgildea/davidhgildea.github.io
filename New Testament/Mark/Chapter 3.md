<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Mark 3:1">1</a> He went into the synagogue again, and there was a man there with a crippled hand.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Mark 3:2">2</a> They watched to see if he would heal the man on the Sabbath so they could accuse him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Mark 3:3">3</a> He said to the man with the crippled hand, &quot;Stand up.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Mark 3:4">4</a> He asked them, &quot;Is it right to do good on the Sabbath, or to do bad? To save a life, or to kill?&quot; But they were silent.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Mark 3:5">5</a> When he saw them with anger, upset because their hearts were hard, he told the man, &quot;Stretch out your hand.&quot; He did so, and his hand was healed and became like the other one.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Mark 3:6">6</a> The Pharisees left and immediately planned with the Herodians how to get rid of him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Mark 3:7">7</a> Jesus went away to the sea with his disciples, and a large crowd from Galilee and Judea followed him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Mark 3:8">8</a> From Jerusalem, from Idumaea, from across the Jordan, and from around Tyre and Sidon, a large crowd came to him when they heard about the amazing things he did.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Mark 3:9">9</a> He told his disciples to have a small boat ready for him to keep the crowd from crowding him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Mark 3:10">10</a> He had healed so many people that they crowded around him, hoping to touch him, all those who were sick with diseases.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Mark 3:11">11</a> Evil spirits, when they saw him, fell down before him, and shouted, &quot;You are the Son of God.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Mark 3:12">12</a> He strongly ordered them not to tell anyone about him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Mark 3:13">13</a> He went up a mountain and called those he chose, and they went to him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Mark 3:14">14</a> He chose twelve to stay with him and to send them out to teach.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Mark 3:15">15</a> To have the ability to cure diseases and drive out demons.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Mark 3:16">16</a> Simon was nicknamed Peter.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Mark 3:17">17</a> James, Zebedee&#39;s son, and John, James&#39; brother, were named by him Sons of Thunder.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Mark 3:18">18</a> Andrew, Philip, Bartholomew, Matthew, Thomas, James son of Alphaeus, Thaddaeus, and Simon the Canaanite,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Mark 3:19">19</a> Judas Iscariot, who betrayed him, was also there; and they entered a house.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Mark 3:20">20</a> The crowd gathered again, so much that they couldn&#39;t even eat.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Mark 3:21">21</a> When his friends heard this, they came to take control of him because they said, &quot;He is out of his mind.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Mark 3:22">22</a> The teachers of the law who came from Jerusalem said, &quot;He has Beelzebub, and by the ruler of demons he drives out demons.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Mark 3:23">23</a> He called them over and told them using simple stories, &quot;How can Satan drive out Satan?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Mark 3:24">24</a> If a kingdom fights within itself, that kingdom cannot last.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Mark 3:25">25</a> If a house is split within, that house can&#39;t last.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Mark 3:26">26</a> If Satan fights against himself and is split apart, he cannot last, but will come to an end.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Mark 3:27">27</a> No one can go into a strong man&#39;s house and take his things unless he first ties up the strong man; then he can rob his house.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Mark 3:28">28</a> I tell you the truth, all sins and insults against God will be forgiven for people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Mark 3:29">29</a> Whoever speaks against the Holy Spirit will not be forgiven and is at risk of never-ending punishment.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Mark 3:30">30</a> They said he has a bad spirit.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Mark 3:31">31</a> His brothers and mother came and, standing outside, they sent someone in to call him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Mark 3:32">32</a> A crowd sat around him, and they told him, &quot;Look, your mother and your brothers outside are looking for you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Mark 3:33">33</a> He replied to them, &quot;Who is my mother or my brothers?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Mark 3:34">34</a> He looked around at those sitting nearby and said, &quot;Look, here are my mother and my brothers!&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Mark 3:35">35</a> Anyone who does what God wants is like my brother, my sister, and my mother.</p>
