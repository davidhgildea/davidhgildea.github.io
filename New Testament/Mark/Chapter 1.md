<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Mark 1:1">1</a> The start of the good news about Jesus Christ, God&#39;s Son;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Mark 1:2">2</a> The prophets wrote, &quot;Look, I will send my messenger ahead of you to get things ready for you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Mark 1:3">3</a> A voice cries out in the desert, &quot;Get ready for God&#39;s coming, make a straight path for him.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Mark 1:4">4</a> John baptized in the desert and taught about baptism for forgiveness of sins.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Mark 1:5">5</a> Everyone from the area of Judaea and the city of Jerusalem went to him, and they were all baptized by him in the Jordan River while admitting their sins.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Mark 1:6">6</a> John wore camel hair clothes with a leather belt around his waist and ate locusts and wild honey.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Mark 1:7">7</a> He preached, &quot;Someone more powerful than me is coming after me, and I&#39;m not even worthy to bend down and untie his shoes.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Mark 1:8">8</a> I have baptized you with water; but he will baptize you with the Holy Spirit.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Mark 1:9">9</a> During that time, Jesus traveled from Nazareth in Galilee and John baptized him in the Jordan River.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Mark 1:10">10</a> Right after coming up from the water, he saw the sky open, and the Spirit like a dove coming down on him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Mark 1:11">11</a> A voice from heaven said, &quot;You are my dear Son, and I am very pleased with you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Mark 1:12">12</a> Right away, the spirit led him into the desert.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Mark 1:13">13</a> He was in the desert for forty days, tempted by Satan, with wild animals, and angels helped him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Mark 1:14">14</a> After John was put in jail, Jesus went to Galilee and preached the good news about God&#39;s kingdom.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Mark 1:15">15</a> They are saying, &quot;The right time has come, and God&#39;s kingdom is near: change your ways and trust in the good news.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Mark 1:16">16</a> As he walked by the Sea of Galilee, he saw Simon and his brother Andrew throwing a net into the sea because they were fishermen.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Mark 1:17">17</a> Jesus said to them, &quot;Follow me, and I will make you become fishers of people.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Mark 1:18">18</a> Right away they left their nets and followed him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Mark 1:19">19</a> After he went a little further from there, he saw James, Zebedee&#39;s son, and John his brother, who were in the boat fixing their nets.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Mark 1:20">20</a> Immediately he called them, and they left their father Zebedee in the boat with the hired workers, and followed him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Mark 1:21">21</a> They went to Capernaum; immediately on the Sabbath, he went into the meeting place and taught.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Mark 1:22">22</a> They were amazed at his teaching because he spoke with authority, not like the teachers of the law.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Mark 1:23">23</a> In their meeting place, there was a man with an evil spirit; and he shouted,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Mark 1:24">24</a> They said, &quot;Leave us alone; what do you want with us, Jesus of Nazareth? Have you come to destroy us? I know who you are, the Holy One of God.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Mark 1:25">25</a> Jesus scolded him, saying, &quot;Be quiet and come out of him.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Mark 1:26">26</a> When the evil spirit had hurt him and shouted loudly, it left him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Mark 1:27">27</a> Everyone was amazed and asked each other, &quot;What is this? A new teaching? He gives orders to evil spirits with power, and they listen to him.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Mark 1:28">28</a> Right away, his fame spread all over the area around Galilee.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Mark 1:29">29</a> Right after leaving the synagogue, they went into Simon and Andrew&#39;s house, along with James and John.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Mark 1:30">30</a> Simon&#39;s mother-in-law was in bed with a fever, and they quickly told Jesus about her.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Mark 1:31">31</a> He came, took her hand, helped her stand; at once the fever was gone, and she served them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Mark 1:32">32</a> In the evening, when the sun set, they brought to him all who were sick and those who had demons.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Mark 1:33">33</a> Everyone in the city came together at the door.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Mark 1:34">34</a> He healed many who were sick with different diseases and drove out many demons; he did not let the demons talk because they knew who he was.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Mark 1:35">35</a> In the morning, long before sunrise, he got up, left the house, went to a quiet place, and prayed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Mark 1:36">36</a> Simon and his companions went after him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Mark 1:37">37</a> When they found him, they told him, &quot;Everyone is looking for you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Mark 1:38">38</a> He said to them, &quot;Let&#39;s go to the nearby towns so I can preach there too; because that&#39;s why I came.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="Mark 1:39">39</a> He taught in their meeting places all over Galilee and drove out demons.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="Mark 1:40">40</a> A man with leprosy came to him, asking for help, knelt down, and said, &quot;If you want to, you can make me clean.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="Mark 1:41">41</a> Jesus, feeling sorry for him, reached out, touched him, and said, &quot;I want to; be clean.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="Mark 1:42">42</a> Right after he spoke, the leprosy left him, and he was clean.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v43"><a class="verse-num" href="#v43" aria-label="Mark 1:43">43</a> He strictly told him and immediately sent him away.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v44"><a class="verse-num" href="#v44" aria-label="Mark 1:44">44</a> He said to him, &quot;Don&#39;t tell anyone, but go and show yourself to the priest and offer what Moses told you to for your cleansing, as proof to them.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v45"><a class="verse-num" href="#v45" aria-label="Mark 1:45">45</a> He left and started to spread the news widely, so much that Jesus couldn&#39;t go into the town publicly but stayed in remote areas. And people came to him from everywhere.</p>
