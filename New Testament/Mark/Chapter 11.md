<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Mark 11:1">1</a> When they got close to Jerusalem, near Bethphage and Bethany, by the Mount of Olives, he sent two of his followers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Mark 11:2">2</a> He says to them, &quot;Go into the village ahead of you, and as soon as you enter, you will see a young donkey tied up that no one has ridden. Untie it and bring it here.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Mark 11:3">3</a> If anyone asks you why you are doing this, tell them God needs it, and he will send it right away.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Mark 11:4">4</a> They went, found the young donkey tied up outside by a door at a crossroads, and untied it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Mark 11:5">5</a> Some people standing there asked them, &quot;What are you doing, untying the colt?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Mark 11:6">6</a> They told them just as Jesus had instructed, and they let them go.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Mark 11:7">7</a> They brought the young donkey to Jesus, put their clothes on it, and he sat on it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Mark 11:8">8</a> Many people spread their clothes on the road, while others cut branches from the trees and scattered them on the road.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Mark 11:9">9</a> Those who were ahead and those who followed shouted, &quot;Hosanna; blessed is the one who comes in God&#39;s name.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Mark 11:10">10</a> Let us praise the kingdom of our father David, that comes in God&#39;s name: Hosanna in the highest.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Mark 11:11">11</a> Jesus went into Jerusalem and the temple. After looking at everything and evening came, he left for Bethany with his twelve disciples.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Mark 11:12">12</a> The next day, after they left Bethany, he felt hungry.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Mark 11:13">13</a> Seeing a fig tree with leaves from a distance, he went to see if he could find any fruit on it. But when he got there, he found only leaves, because it was not the season for figs.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Mark 11:14">14</a> Jesus replied, &quot;From now on, no one will eat your fruit again.&quot; His disciples heard this.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Mark 11:15">15</a> They came to Jerusalem: Jesus entered the temple and started to drive out those who were buying and selling there. He flipped over the tables of the money changers and the benches of those selling doves.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Mark 11:16">16</a> He did not allow anyone to carry items through the temple.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Mark 11:17">17</a> He taught them, &quot;Isn&#39;t it written, my house will be known as a place of prayer for all nations? But you have turned it into a hideout for thieves.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Mark 11:18">18</a> The teachers of the law and the leading priests heard what Jesus said and looked for a way to kill him. They were afraid of him because everyone was amazed at his teaching.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Mark 11:19">19</a> When evening came, he left the city.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Mark 11:20">20</a> In the morning, when they walked by, they saw that the fig tree had withered from the roots.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Mark 11:21">21</a> Peter remembered and said to him, &quot;Teacher, look, the fig tree you cursed has withered.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Mark 11:22">22</a> Jesus replied, &quot;Trust in God.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Mark 11:23">23</a> I tell you the truth, anyone who tells this mountain to move and fall into the sea, and does not doubt but believes what they say will happen, it will be done for them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Mark 11:24">24</a> So I tell you, whatever you ask for in prayer, believe you have received it, and it will be yours.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Mark 11:25">25</a> When you stand and pray, forgive anyone you have something against, so that God in heaven may forgive your wrongs.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Mark 11:26">26</a> If you don&#39;t forgive others, God in heaven won&#39;t forgive your wrongs.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Mark 11:27">27</a> They return to Jerusalem: and while he is walking in the temple, the chief priests, the teachers of the law, and the elders come to him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Mark 11:28">28</a> Tell him, &quot;What authority allows you to do these actions? And who gave you this authority?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Mark 11:29">29</a> Jesus replied, &quot;I have one question for you. Answer me, and I&#39;ll tell you by what authority I do these things.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Mark 11:30">30</a> Was John&#39;s baptism from God or from people? Tell me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Mark 11:31">31</a> They thought to themselves, saying, &quot;If we say, &#39;From heaven,&#39; He will ask, &#39;Then why didn&#39;t you believe him?&#39;&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Mark 11:32">32</a> But if we say, &quot;From people,&quot; they are afraid of the crowd, because everyone believed John was truly a prophet.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Mark 11:33">33</a> They replied to Jesus, &quot;We don&#39;t know.&quot; And Jesus said to them, &quot;Then I won&#39;t tell you with what authority I do these things.&quot;</p>
