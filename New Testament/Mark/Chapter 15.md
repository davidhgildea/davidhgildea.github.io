<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="Mark 15:1">1</a> Early in the morning, the chief priests met with the elders and teachers of the law and the whole council. They tied up Jesus, took Him away, and handed Him over to Pilate.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="Mark 15:2">2</a> Pilate asked him, &quot;Are you the King of the Jews?&quot; He replied, &quot;You say that I am.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="Mark 15:3">3</a> The religious leaders accused him of many things, but he did not reply.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="Mark 15:4">4</a> Pilate asked him another time, &quot;Aren&#39;t you going to answer? See how many accusations they are making against you.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="Mark 15:5">5</a> But Jesus still did not answer anything, so Pilate was amazed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="Mark 15:6">6</a> At the festival, he set free for them any one prisoner they wanted.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="Mark 15:7">7</a> There was a man named Barabbas, who was in prison with others who had started a rebellion, and he had killed someone in that rebellion.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="Mark 15:8">8</a> The crowd loudly started to ask him to do what he always did for them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="Mark 15:9">9</a> Pilate answered them, &quot;Do you want me to release the King of the Jews to you?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="Mark 15:10">10</a> He knew the chief priests had handed him over because they were jealous.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="Mark 15:11">11</a> The leading priests convinced the crowd to ask for Barabbas to be released instead.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="Mark 15:12">12</a> Pilate answered and asked them again, &quot;What do you want me to do to the one you call the King of the Jews?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="Mark 15:13">13</a> They shouted again, &quot;Crucify him.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="Mark 15:14">14</a> Then Pilate asked them, &quot;Why, what wrong has he done?&quot; But they shouted even louder, &quot;Crucify him.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="Mark 15:15">15</a> So Pilate, wanting to please the crowd, let Barabbas go and handed Jesus over to be nailed to a cross after he had whipped him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="Mark 15:16">16</a> The soldiers took him inside the building named Praetorium and gathered all the troops there.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="Mark 15:17">17</a> They dressed him in purple, made a thorn crown, and placed it on his head.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="Mark 15:18">18</a> They started to greet him, saying, &quot;Hello, King of the Jews!&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="Mark 15:19">19</a> They hit him on the head with a stick, spat on him, and kneeling down, pretended to worship him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="Mark 15:20">20</a> After they made fun of him, they took off the purple robe, dressed him in his own clothes, and took him out to nail him to a cross.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="Mark 15:21">21</a> They forced Simon from Cyrene, who was just passing by from the fields and was father to Alexander and Rufus, to carry his cross.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="Mark 15:22">22</a> They took him to a place called Golgotha, which means Skull Place.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="Mark 15:23">23</a> They offered him wine mixed with myrrh, but he did not take it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="Mark 15:24">24</a> After they nailed him to the cross, they divided his clothes by casting lots to decide what each one would get.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="Mark 15:25">25</a> It was nine in the morning when they nailed him to the cross.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="Mark 15:26">26</a> The sign of his charge was written above: THE KING OF THE JEWS.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="Mark 15:27">27</a> They crucified two thieves with him, one on his right and the other on his left.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="Mark 15:28">28</a> The scripture that says, &quot;He was counted among the lawbreakers,&quot; came true.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="Mark 15:29">29</a> People walking by insulted him, shaking their heads and saying, &quot;Oh, you who would destroy the temple and rebuild it in three days,&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="Mark 15:30">30</a> Save yourself and come down from the cross.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="Mark 15:31">31</a> The leading priests also made fun of him, talking with the teachers of the law, saying, &quot;He saved others, but he can&#39;t save himself.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="Mark 15:32">32</a> Let the King of Israel, Christ, come down from the cross now, so we can see and believe. Those who were on the crosses next to him insulted him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="Mark 15:33">33</a> When it was noon, the whole country became dark until three in the afternoon.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="Mark 15:34">34</a> At three in the afternoon, Jesus shouted loudly, &quot;Eloi, Eloi, lama sabachthani?&quot; which means, &quot;My God, my God, why have you left me alone?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="Mark 15:35">35</a> Some people standing nearby heard this and said, &quot;Look, he&#39;s calling for Elijah.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="Mark 15:36">36</a> Someone ran and soaked a sponge with vinegar, put it on a stick, and offered it to him to drink, saying, &quot;Wait; let&#39;s see if Elijah will come to save him.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="Mark 15:37">37</a> Jesus shouted loudly and then died.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="Mark 15:38">38</a> The curtain in the temple tore in two from top to bottom.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="Mark 15:39">39</a> When the army officer standing in front of him saw that he shouted like this and then died, he said, &quot;Truly, this man was God&#39;s Son.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="Mark 15:40">40</a> Women were also watching from far away, including Mary Magdalene, Mary the mother of James the younger and Joses, and Salome.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="Mark 15:41">41</a> She, while in Galilee, followed him and helped him; and many other women who went with him to Jerusalem did the same.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="Mark 15:42">42</a> Now when evening came, since it was the time to get ready, which is the day before the Sabbath,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v43"><a class="verse-num" href="#v43" aria-label="Mark 15:43">43</a> Joseph from Arimathaea, a respected leader who was also looking forward to God&#39;s kingdom, went bravely to Pilate and asked for Jesus&#39; body.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v44"><a class="verse-num" href="#v44" aria-label="Mark 15:44">44</a> Pilate was surprised that he was already dead. He called the centurion to ask if he had been dead for some time.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v45"><a class="verse-num" href="#v45" aria-label="Mark 15:45">45</a> When he learned it from the officer, he gave the body to Joseph.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v46"><a class="verse-num" href="#v46" aria-label="Mark 15:46">46</a> He bought good cloth, took him down, wrapped him in it, put him in a tomb cut from rock, and rolled a stone to the tomb&#39;s entrance.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v47"><a class="verse-num" href="#v47" aria-label="Mark 15:47">47</a> Mary Magdalene and Mary, Joses&#39;s mother, saw where he was placed.</p>
