<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Corinthians 8:1">1</a> About food given to idols, we understand that we all have knowledge. Knowledge makes one proud, but love builds up.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Corinthians 8:2">2</a> If someone thinks they know something, they don&#39;t know it as well as they should.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Corinthians 8:3">3</a> If anyone loves God, that person is known by God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Corinthians 8:4">4</a> About eating food given to idols, we understand that an idol means nothing in the world, and there is only one God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Corinthians 8:5">5</a> Even though some are known as gods, whether in heaven or on earth (and there are many gods and many lords),</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Corinthians 8:6">6</a> For us, there is only one God, the Father, from whom everything comes, and we live for him; and there is one Lord, Jesus Christ, through whom everything is made, and through whom we live.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Corinthians 8:7">7</a> However, not everyone knows this: some people still think of the meat as connected to the idol and eat it with that belief. Because their understanding is weak, it makes them feel guilty.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Corinthians 8:8">8</a> Eating food does not make us closer to God; if we eat, we are not better; if we don&#39;t eat, we are not worse.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Corinthians 8:9">9</a> But be careful that your freedom does not cause those who are weak to stumble.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Corinthians 8:10">10</a> If someone sees you with knowledge eating in the temple of an idol, won&#39;t a person with a weak conscience feel encouraged to eat food offered to idols?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Corinthians 8:11">11</a> Will your knowledge cause your weak brother to be lost, for whom Christ died?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Corinthians 8:12">12</a> But when you do wrong to your brothers and sisters, hurting their weak understanding, you also do wrong to Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 Corinthians 8:13">13</a> If food causes my brother to sin, I will not eat meat as long as the world exists, so I don&#39;t make my brother sin.</p>
