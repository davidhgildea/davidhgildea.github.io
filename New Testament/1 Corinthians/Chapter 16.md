<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Corinthians 16:1">1</a> About the money being gathered for God&#39;s people, do as I told the churches in Galatia to do.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Corinthians 16:2">2</a> On the first day of the week, each of you should set aside some money in keeping with how much God has blessed you, so that we won&#39;t need to collect money when I arrive.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Corinthians 16:3">3</a> When I arrive, whoever you choose and recommend in your letters, I will send them to take your gift to Jerusalem.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Corinthians 16:4">4</a> If it&#39;s right that I go too, they will go with me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Corinthians 16:5">5</a> I will come to you after I travel through Macedonia because I am going through Macedonia.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Corinthians 16:6">6</a> I might stay and spend the winter with you, so you can help me continue my trip wherever I go.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Corinthians 16:7">7</a> I won&#39;t visit you now as I travel; but I hope to stay with you for a bit, if God allows.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Corinthians 16:8">8</a> But I will stay in Ephesus until Pentecost.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Corinthians 16:9">9</a> A big and powerful opportunity has come to me, and there are many opponents.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Corinthians 16:10">10</a> If Timothy comes, make sure he can be with you without fear, because he is doing God&#39;s work, just like I am.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Corinthians 16:11">11</a> Don&#39;t let anyone look down on him. Send him off in peace so he can come to me, because I am expecting him with the others.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Corinthians 16:12">12</a> About our friend Apollos, I really wanted him to visit you with the others, but he did not want to come right now. He will come when he has a good time.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 Corinthians 16:13">13</a> Stay alert, stand firm in your belief, be brave and strong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="1 Corinthians 16:14">14</a> Do everything with love.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="1 Corinthians 16:15">15</a> I ask you, friends, you know Stephanas&#39;s family was the first to believe in Achaia, and they have dedicated themselves to serving God&#39;s people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="1 Corinthians 16:16">16</a> Submit to such people, and to everyone who works and labors with us.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="1 Corinthians 16:17">17</a> I am happy Stephanas, Fortunatus, and Achaicus came because they have provided what you could not.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="1 Corinthians 16:18">18</a> They have uplifted my spirit and yours; so recognize those like them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="1 Corinthians 16:19">19</a> The churches in Asia send their greetings to you. Aquila and Priscilla send a warm greeting in God, along with the church that meets in their home.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="1 Corinthians 16:20">20</a> All the friends send their greetings. Greet each other with a sacred kiss.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="1 Corinthians 16:21">21</a> I, Paul, greet you with my own hand.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="1 Corinthians 16:22">22</a> If someone does not love the Lord Jesus Christ, let that person be cursed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="1 Corinthians 16:23">23</a> May the kindness of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="1 Corinthians 16:24">24</a> My love is with all of you in Christ Jesus. Amen.</p>
