<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Corinthians 5:1">1</a> People often say that sexual immorality is happening among you, the kind not even non-believers talk about, like someone living with his stepmother.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Corinthians 5:2">2</a> You have become arrogant instead of mourning, so that the one who did this should be removed from among you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Corinthians 5:3">3</a> Although I am not there in person, I am with you in spirit and have already made my decision about the person who did this.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Corinthians 5:4">4</a> In the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, when you gather and my spirit, with the power of our Lord Jesus Christ,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Corinthians 5:5">5</a> To hand someone over to Satan to destroy their flesh so that their spirit can be saved on the day of Jesus.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Corinthians 5:6">6</a> Your boasting is not good. Don&#39;t you know that a little yeast works through the whole batch of dough?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Corinthians 5:7">7</a> Get rid of the old yeast, so you can be a fresh batch of dough, since you are without yeast. Just as Christ, our Passover lamb, has been offered for us.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Corinthians 5:8">8</a> Let&#39;s celebrate the festival not with old yeast or with yeast of evil and badness, but with bread without yeast that is pure and true.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Corinthians 5:9">9</a> I wrote to you in a letter not to associate with sexually immoral people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Corinthians 5:10">10</a> Don&#39;t completely avoid people in this world who sin sexually, are greedy, cheat others, or worship idols; if you did, you would have to leave this world.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Corinthians 5:11">11</a> I have told you not to associate with anyone who claims to be a brother but is sexually immoral, greedy, worships idols, insults others, drinks too much, or cheats people. Do not even eat with such a person.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Corinthians 5:12">12</a> Why should I judge those outside? Don&#39;t you judge those inside?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 Corinthians 5:13">13</a> God judges those who are outside. So remove the evil person from among you.</p>
