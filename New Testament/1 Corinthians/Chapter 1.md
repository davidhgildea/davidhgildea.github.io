<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Corinthians 1:1">1</a> Paul, chosen as a messenger of Jesus Christ by God&#39;s will, and our brother Sosthenes,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Corinthians 1:2">2</a> To God&#39;s church in Corinth, to those made holy in Christ Jesus, called to be God&#39;s people, along with all who call on the name of our Lord Jesus Christ everywhere: theirs and ours.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Corinthians 1:3">3</a> May you have kindness and peace from God our Father and from Jesus Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Corinthians 1:4">4</a> I always thank God for you because of His kindness given to you through Jesus Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Corinthians 1:5">5</a> In every way, you have been made rich by God, in all your speaking and in all your understanding.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Corinthians 1:6">6</a> Just as the message about Christ was proven true among you:</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Corinthians 1:7">7</a> So you lack no gift as you wait for our Lord Jesus Christ to come.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Corinthians 1:8">8</a> God will also strengthen you to the end, so that you may be without fault on the day of our Lord Jesus Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Corinthians 1:9">9</a> God is trustworthy, and He called you to join in the life with His Son, Jesus Christ, our Lord.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Corinthians 1:10">10</a> I ask you, friends, in the name of Jesus Christ, to all agree and not be divided; be completely united with the same thoughts and the same opinions.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Corinthians 1:11">11</a> I have been told by Chloe&#39;s people that there are arguments among you, my brothers and sisters.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Corinthians 1:12">12</a> I tell you, everyone says, &quot;I follow Paul,&quot; or &quot;I follow Apollos,&quot; or &quot;I follow Cephas,&quot; or &quot;I follow Christ.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 Corinthians 1:13">13</a> Is Christ split into parts? Was Paul put on the cross for you? Or were you baptized in Paul&#39;s name?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="1 Corinthians 1:14">14</a> I thank God I baptized none of you except Crispus and Gaius.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="1 Corinthians 1:15">15</a> So no one could claim that I baptized using my own name.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="1 Corinthians 1:16">16</a> I also baptized Stephanas&#39;s family, but I don&#39;t know if I baptized anyone else.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="1 Corinthians 1:17">17</a> Christ did not send me to baptize, but to share the good news; not with fancy words, so the work of Christ on the cross is not made useless.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="1 Corinthians 1:18">18</a> Talking about the cross seems silly to those who are dying, but to us who are saved, it shows God&#39;s power.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="1 Corinthians 1:19">19</a> It is written, I will ruin the wisdom of the wise, and I will make the knowledge of the smart people worthless.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="1 Corinthians 1:20">20</a> Where is the wise? Where is the writer? Where is the debater of this age? Hasn&#39;t God made the world&#39;s wisdom look foolish?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="1 Corinthians 1:21">21</a> Because the world did not know God through its own wisdom, God chose to save those who believe through the simple message of preaching.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="1 Corinthians 1:22">22</a> Jews ask for a sign, and Greeks look for wisdom.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="1 Corinthians 1:23">23</a> We tell people about Christ, who was nailed to a cross. This causes Jewish people to trip and seem silly to Greek people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="1 Corinthians 1:24">24</a> But for those who are called, both Jews and non-Jews, Christ is God&#39;s power and God&#39;s wisdom.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="1 Corinthians 1:25">25</a> Even what seems foolish in God is wiser than human wisdom, and what seems weak in God is stronger than human strength.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="1 Corinthians 1:26">26</a> You see, friends, that not many wise by human standards, not many powerful, not many of high status are chosen.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="1 Corinthians 1:27">27</a> God chose the foolish things in the world to confuse the wise; God chose the weak things in the world to confuse the strong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="1 Corinthians 1:28">28</a> God has chosen the lowly and despised things of the world, even things that are not, to make nothing of the things that are.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="1 Corinthians 1:29">29</a> No one should boast before God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="1 Corinthians 1:30">30</a> Through Christ Jesus, you have received from God wisdom, righteousness, holiness, and freedom from sin.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="1 Corinthians 1:31">31</a> So, as it is written, &quot;If anyone wants to boast, they should boast about God.&quot;</p>
