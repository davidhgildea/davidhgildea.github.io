<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Corinthians 15:1">1</a> Friends, I tell you the good news I gave you, which you accepted and still follow.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Corinthians 15:2">2</a> You are saved by this too, if you remember what I told you, unless your belief was for nothing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Corinthians 15:3">3</a> I first told you what I was also told: Christ died for our sins as the writings said.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Corinthians 15:4">4</a> He was buried and rose again on the third day, just as the scriptures said.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Corinthians 15:5">5</a> He was seen by Cephas, then by the twelve.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Corinthians 15:6">6</a> After that, more than five hundred followers saw him at one time; most of them are still alive today, though some have died.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Corinthians 15:7">7</a> After that, James saw him; then all the apostles did.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Corinthians 15:8">8</a> Lastly, he was seen by me too, like someone born at the wrong time.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Corinthians 15:9">9</a> I am the least of the apostles and not worthy to be called an apostle because I persecuted God&#39;s church.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Corinthians 15:10">10</a> Because of God&#39;s kindness, I am who I am, and his kindness to me was not wasted; I worked harder than all of them, yet it was not me, but God&#39;s kindness with me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Corinthians 15:11">11</a> So whether it was I or they, this is what we preach, and this is what you believed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Corinthians 15:12">12</a> If we preach that Christ rose from the dead, why do some of you say that the dead are not raised?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 Corinthians 15:13">13</a> If the dead are not raised, then Christ has not been raised.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="1 Corinthians 15:14">14</a> If Christ has not been raised, then our teaching is useless, and so is your faith.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="1 Corinthians 15:15">15</a> Yes, and we are proven liars about God; because we claimed that God brought Christ back to life, which He didn&#39;t do if it&#39;s true that the dead don&#39;t come back to life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="1 Corinthians 15:16">16</a> If the dead don&#39;t rise, then Christ has not been raised either.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="1 Corinthians 15:17">17</a> If Christ has not been raised, your faith is useless; you are still guilty of your sins.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="1 Corinthians 15:18">18</a> Then those who have died believing in Christ are also lost.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="1 Corinthians 15:19">19</a> If we only hope in Christ for this life, we are the most unhappy of all people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="1 Corinthians 15:20">20</a> Now Christ has risen from the dead and is the first to rise from those who have died.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="1 Corinthians 15:21">21</a> Death came through a person, and the raising of the dead also came through a person.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="1 Corinthians 15:22">22</a> Just as everyone dies because of Adam, so everyone will be given life because of Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="1 Corinthians 15:23">23</a> Everyone will be raised to life in their own turn: Christ was the first; then, when he comes, those who belong to him will be raised to life.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="1 Corinthians 15:24">24</a> The end comes when he hands over the kingdom to God, the Father, after he ends all control, authority, and power.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="1 Corinthians 15:25">25</a> He must rule until he has defeated all his enemies.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="1 Corinthians 15:26">26</a> The final enemy to be destroyed is death.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="1 Corinthians 15:27">27</a> God has placed everything under his control, but when it is said that everything is under him, it&#39;s clear that this does not include God who put everything under his control.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="1 Corinthians 15:28">28</a> When everything is under his control, then the Son will also be under the one who gave him power over everything, so that God will be everything to everyone.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="1 Corinthians 15:29">29</a> What will those who are baptized for the dead do if the dead are not raised at all? Why are they baptized for the dead?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="1 Corinthians 15:30">30</a> Why do we risk our lives every hour?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="1 Corinthians 15:31">31</a> I assure you by the happiness I have in Christ Jesus our God, I face death every day.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="1 Corinthians 15:32">32</a> If I fought wild beasts in Ephesus just as people do, what good is it to me if the dead don&#39;t rise again? Let&#39;s eat and drink, because tomorrow we will die.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="1 Corinthians 15:33">33</a> Don&#39;t be fooled: bad company ruins good character.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="1 Corinthians 15:34">34</a> Wake up to what is right, and do not sin; because some do not know God: I say this to make you ashamed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="1 Corinthians 15:35">35</a> Someone might ask, &quot;How are the dead brought back to life? And what kind of body will they have?&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="1 Corinthians 15:36">36</a> You fool, what you plant won&#39;t come to life unless it dies first.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="1 Corinthians 15:37">37</a> When you plant, you do not plant the full-grown plant, but just a seed, maybe wheat or another kind.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="1 Corinthians 15:38">38</a> God gives it a body as he wants, and to every seed its own body.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="1 Corinthians 15:39">39</a> Not all living beings have the same body: humans have one kind, animals another, fish another, and birds another.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="1 Corinthians 15:40">40</a> There are heavenly bodies and earthly bodies: the beauty of the heavenly is one kind, and the beauty of the earthly is another kind.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v41"><a class="verse-num" href="#v41" aria-label="1 Corinthians 15:41">41</a> The sun has one kind of beauty, the moon another, and the stars another; and each star is different in its beauty.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v42"><a class="verse-num" href="#v42" aria-label="1 Corinthians 15:42">42</a> The resurrection of the dead is like this: What is buried decays, but what is raised cannot decay.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v43"><a class="verse-num" href="#v43" aria-label="1 Corinthians 15:43">43</a> It is planted with shame; it is raised with honor: it is planted weak; it is raised strong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v44"><a class="verse-num" href="#v44" aria-label="1 Corinthians 15:44">44</a> A physical body is planted; a spiritual body is raised. There is a physical body, and there is a spiritual body.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v45"><a class="verse-num" href="#v45" aria-label="1 Corinthians 15:45">45</a> The first man, Adam, became a living being; the last Adam became a life-giving spirit.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v46"><a class="verse-num" href="#v46" aria-label="1 Corinthians 15:46">46</a> The natural life comes first, not the spiritual one; the spiritual life comes after.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v47"><a class="verse-num" href="#v47" aria-label="1 Corinthians 15:47">47</a> The first man comes from the earth and is made of earth; the second man comes from heaven and is the Lord.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v48"><a class="verse-num" href="#v48" aria-label="1 Corinthians 15:48">48</a> People from the earth are like the earth; those from heaven are like heaven.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v49"><a class="verse-num" href="#v49" aria-label="1 Corinthians 15:49">49</a> We have carried the likeness of the earthly, and we will also carry the likeness of the heavenly.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v50"><a class="verse-num" href="#v50" aria-label="1 Corinthians 15:50">50</a> I tell you, friends, that our physical bodies cannot enter God&#39;s kingdom; and what is perishable cannot become imperishable.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v51"><a class="verse-num" href="#v51" aria-label="1 Corinthians 15:51">51</a> Look, I tell you a secret; not all of us will die, but we will all be changed.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v52"><a class="verse-num" href="#v52" aria-label="1 Corinthians 15:52">52</a> In a moment, in a quick blink, when the final trumpet sounds, the trumpet will blow, the dead will rise never to die again, and we will be different.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v53"><a class="verse-num" href="#v53" aria-label="1 Corinthians 15:53">53</a> This body that can be destroyed must become one that cannot be destroyed, and this body that can die must become one that cannot die.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v54"><a class="verse-num" href="#v54" aria-label="1 Corinthians 15:54">54</a> When what can decay becomes undying, and what can die becomes immortal, then the saying that is written will happen: Death is overcome by triumph.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v55"><a class="verse-num" href="#v55" aria-label="1 Corinthians 15:55">55</a> Death, where is your sting? Grave, where is your victory?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v56"><a class="verse-num" href="#v56" aria-label="1 Corinthians 15:56">56</a> Death hurts because of sin; and sin is powerful because of the law.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v57"><a class="verse-num" href="#v57" aria-label="1 Corinthians 15:57">57</a> Thank God, who gives us victory through our Lord Jesus Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v58"><a class="verse-num" href="#v58" aria-label="1 Corinthians 15:58">58</a> So, my dear friends, remain firm, unshaken, always doing plenty in God’s work, because you know that what you do for God is never useless.</p>
