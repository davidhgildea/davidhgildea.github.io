<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Corinthians 14:1">1</a> Chase after love and want spiritual gifts, especially to prophesy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Corinthians 14:2">2</a> When someone speaks in a language no one knows, they are not speaking to people but to God, because no one can understand them. However, they are saying secret things in their spirit.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Corinthians 14:3">3</a> The person who prophesies speaks to people to build them up, encourage them, and give them comfort.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Corinthians 14:4">4</a> Anyone who speaks in a language not known improves himself; but one who prophesies builds up the church.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Corinthians 14:5">5</a> I wish you could all speak in different languages, but it&#39;s better to share God&#39;s messages. The one sharing God&#39;s messages is more helpful than one speaking in languages, unless they explain it, so the church can learn and grow.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Corinthians 14:6">6</a> Now, friends, if I come to you speaking in different languages, how will it help you unless I bring you some revelation, knowledge, prophecy, or teaching?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Corinthians 14:7">7</a> Even lifeless things that make noise, like pipes or harps, need to make different sounds. If they don&#39;t, how can you tell what is being played?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Corinthians 14:8">8</a> If the trumpet makes a confusing sound, who will get ready for the fight?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Corinthians 14:9">9</a> In the same way, if you don&#39;t speak clear words with your tongue, how will anyone know what you&#39;re saying? Otherwise, you&#39;re just talking to the air.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Corinthians 14:10">10</a> In the world, there might be many different sounds, and each one has its own meaning.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Corinthians 14:11">11</a> So if I don&#39;t understand what someone is saying, I am like a foreigner to the speaker, and the speaker is like a foreigner to me.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Corinthians 14:12">12</a> In the same way, since you are eager for spiritual gifts, try to be the best at building up the church.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 Corinthians 14:13">13</a> So, if someone speaks in a language others don&#39;t know, they should pray for the ability to explain it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="1 Corinthians 14:14">14</a> If I pray in a language I don&#39;t know, my spirit prays but I can&#39;t understand it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="1 Corinthians 14:15">15</a> So what should be done? I will pray with my spirit, but I will also pray with my mind. I will sing with my spirit, but I will also sing with my mind.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="1 Corinthians 14:16">16</a> If you give thanks with your spirit, how can someone who doesn&#39;t know what you&#39;re saying join in and say &quot;Amen&quot; to your prayer of thanks?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="1 Corinthians 14:17">17</a> You truly give thanks well, but the other person is not helped.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="1 Corinthians 14:18">18</a> I thank God, I speak in different languages more than all of you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="1 Corinthians 14:19">19</a> In church, I prefer to say five words that I understand to teach others, rather than ten thousand words in a language nobody knows.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="1 Corinthians 14:20">20</a> Friends, don&#39;t be childish in understanding. Yet in bad intentions be like children, but in understanding be adults.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="1 Corinthians 14:21">21</a> The law says, &quot;I will speak to these people through people with different languages and lips, but even then, they will not listen to me,&quot; says God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="1 Corinthians 14:22">22</a> So, speaking in different languages is a sign for unbelievers, not believers, while prophesying is for believers, not unbelievers.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="1 Corinthians 14:23">23</a> So if the whole church meets in one place and everyone starts speaking in different languages, and people who don&#39;t understand or don&#39;t believe come in, won&#39;t they think you are crazy?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="1 Corinthians 14:24">24</a> If everyone prophesies and an unbeliever or someone who doesn&#39;t know about this comes in, they are convinced by everyone and judged by everyone.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="1 Corinthians 14:25">25</a> The secrets of his heart become clear; he falls down on his face to worship God, saying truly God is with you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="1 Corinthians 14:26">26</a> So, friends, when you meet, each of you brings a song, a teaching, a language, a revelation, or an explanation. Make sure that all of these build up the group.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="1 Corinthians 14:27">27</a> If someone speaks in a language not known, they should do so two or three at a time, taking turns; and there should be one person to explain it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="1 Corinthians 14:28">28</a> If there is no interpreter, he should remain quiet in the church; he can speak to himself and to God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="1 Corinthians 14:29">29</a> Let two or three prophets speak, and let the others evaluate.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="1 Corinthians 14:30">30</a> If something is shown to another person sitting nearby, the first person should stop talking.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="1 Corinthians 14:31">31</a> You can all give messages from God one at a time, so everyone can learn and be encouraged.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="1 Corinthians 14:32">32</a> The prophets&#39; spirits are under the prophets&#39; control.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="1 Corinthians 14:33">33</a> God does not create confusion, but peace, in all the churches of the holy people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="1 Corinthians 14:34">34</a> Women should keep quiet in churches: they are not allowed to speak, but must be submissive, as the law also says.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="1 Corinthians 14:35">35</a> If they want to learn something, they should ask their husbands at home, because it&#39;s shameful for women to talk in the church.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="1 Corinthians 14:36">36</a> Did God&#39;s message originate with you? Or was it given to only you?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="1 Corinthians 14:37">37</a> If someone believes they are a prophet or spiritual, they should accept that what I am writing to you are God&#39;s commands.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="1 Corinthians 14:38">38</a> If someone does not know, let them not know.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="1 Corinthians 14:39">39</a> Friends, want to prophesy and do not stop others from speaking in different tongues.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="1 Corinthians 14:40">40</a> Do everything properly and in an organized way.</p>
