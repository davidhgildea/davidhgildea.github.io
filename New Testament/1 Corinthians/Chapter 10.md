<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Corinthians 10:1">1</a> My friends, I want you to know that all our ancestors were under the cloud and all went through the sea.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Corinthians 10:2">2</a> They were all baptized as followers of Moses in the cloud and in the sea.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Corinthians 10:3">3</a> Everyone ate the same spiritual food.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Corinthians 10:4">4</a> Everyone drank the same spiritual drink because they drank from the spiritual rock that went with them, and that rock was Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Corinthians 10:5">5</a> God was not happy with many of them because they were defeated in the wilderness.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Corinthians 10:6">6</a> These things happened to them as examples for us, so we wouldn&#39;t desire bad things like they did.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Corinthians 10:7">7</a> Do not worship idols like some of them did; as the Scripture says, &quot;The people ate and drank, then got up to have fun.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Corinthians 10:8">8</a> Let&#39;s not have sex outside of marriage, like some of them did, and in one day twenty-three thousand died.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Corinthians 10:9">9</a> Let&#39;s not test Christ, as some of them did and were killed by snakes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Corinthians 10:10">10</a> Do not complain as some of them did and were killed by the destroyer.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Corinthians 10:11">11</a> All these things happened to them as examples and are written to warn us, who are living in the last days.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Corinthians 10:12">12</a> So, if someone thinks they are standing firm, they should be careful not to fall.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 Corinthians 10:13">13</a> No temptation has come to you that isn&#39;t normal for people. But God can be trusted not to let you be tempted more than you can handle. He will also give you a way out so you can endure it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="1 Corinthians 10:14">14</a> So, my dear friends, run away from worshipping idols.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="1 Corinthians 10:15">15</a> I talk to you as if you are wise; think about what I&#39;m saying.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="1 Corinthians 10:16">16</a> The cup we give thanks for, isn&#39;t it sharing in Christ&#39;s blood? The bread we break, isn&#39;t it sharing in Christ&#39;s body?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="1 Corinthians 10:17">17</a> We are many, but we are one loaf of bread and one body, because we all share that one loaf.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="1 Corinthians 10:18">18</a> Look at the people of Israel: aren&#39;t those who eat the sacrifices sharing in the altar?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="1 Corinthians 10:19">19</a> What am I saying, then? That an idol is worth anything, or that food offered to idols means anything?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="1 Corinthians 10:20">20</a> I say that what non-Jews sacrifice, they offer to evil spirits, not to God, and I don&#39;t want you to be associated with evil spirits.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="1 Corinthians 10:21">21</a> You cannot drink from God&#39;s cup and from the cup of demons; you cannot share in God&#39;s table and the table of demons.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="1 Corinthians 10:22">22</a> Are we making God jealous? Are we stronger than God?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="1 Corinthians 10:23">23</a> I can do anything, but not everything is good for me: I can do anything, but not everything is helpful to others.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="1 Corinthians 10:24">24</a> Don&#39;t just look out for your own interests, but look out for the interests of others too.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="1 Corinthians 10:25">25</a> Eat anything sold in the market, without worrying about it for the sake of your conscience.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="1 Corinthians 10:26">26</a> The Earth belongs to God, and everything in it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="1 Corinthians 10:27">27</a> If someone who doesn&#39;t believe invites you to a dinner and you want to go, eat whatever they serve you without asking questions, to keep a clear conscience.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="1 Corinthians 10:28">28</a> If someone tells you, &quot;This food was sacrificed to idols,&quot; don&#39;t eat it for the sake of the person who mentioned it and for conscience. Because the world belongs to God, and everything in it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="1 Corinthians 10:29">29</a> I mean your conscience should consider not just yourself, but others too: for why should my freedom be limited by someone else&#39;s conscience?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="1 Corinthians 10:30">30</a> If I have received a gift from God and am thankful for it, why do people criticize me for it?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="1 Corinthians 10:31">31</a> No matter what you eat, drink, or do, do everything to honor God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="1 Corinthians 10:32">32</a> Do not offend anyone, not Jews, not non-Jews, nor God&#39;s church.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="1 Corinthians 10:33">33</a> I try to please everyone in every way, not looking for my own benefit, but for the good of many so that they may be saved.</p>
