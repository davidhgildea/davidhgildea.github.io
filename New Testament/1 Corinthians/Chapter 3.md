<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Corinthians 3:1">1</a> Brothers and sisters, I could not talk to you as spiritual people but as worldly, as if to babies in Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Corinthians 3:2">2</a> I have given you milk, not solid food, because until now you were not ready for it, and even now you cannot handle it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Corinthians 3:3">3</a> You are still worldly because you have jealousy, arguments, and divisions among you. Doesn&#39;t this show that you are worldly and behave like ordinary people?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Corinthians 3:4">4</a> When one of you says, &quot;I follow Paul,&quot; and another, &quot;I follow Apollos,&quot; aren&#39;t you acting like mere humans?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Corinthians 3:5">5</a> Who then is Paul, and who is Apollos, but servants through whom you believed, as God gave to each person?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Corinthians 3:6">6</a> I planted, Apollos watered, but God made it grow.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Corinthians 3:7">7</a> So the one who plants or the one who waters is not important, but God who makes things grow is.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Corinthians 3:8">8</a> The person who plants and the person who waters are working together, and each will get a reward based on their own work.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Corinthians 3:9">9</a> We work together with God: you are God&#39;s field, you are God&#39;s construction.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Corinthians 3:10">10</a> God has given me grace, so like a skilled architect, I laid a foundation for others to build on. But everyone should be careful about how they build on it.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Corinthians 3:11">11</a> No one can lay any other foundation than the one already laid, which is Jesus Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Corinthians 3:12">12</a> If anyone builds on this foundation with gold, silver, precious stones, wood, hay, or straw,</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 Corinthians 3:13">13</a> Everyone&#39;s work will become clear: the day will show it because fire will reveal it; and the fire will test what kind of work each person has done.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="1 Corinthians 3:14">14</a> If someone&#39;s work lasts which they have built on it, they will get a reward.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="1 Corinthians 3:15">15</a> If someone&#39;s work is burned up, they will lose it: but they themselves will be saved, but only as if through fire.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="1 Corinthians 3:16">16</a> Don&#39;t you know that you are God&#39;s temple and that God&#39;s Spirit lives in you?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="1 Corinthians 3:17">17</a> If anyone ruins God&#39;s temple, God will ruin that person; because God&#39;s temple is sacred, and that temple is you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="1 Corinthians 3:18">18</a> Do not trick yourself. If someone thinks they are wise by this world&#39;s standards, they should become like they know nothing, so they can truly become wise.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="1 Corinthians 3:19">19</a> The wisdom of this world is silly to God. It is said, He catches the clever in their tricks.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="1 Corinthians 3:20">20</a> God knows the thoughts of the smart people, that they are useless.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="1 Corinthians 3:21">21</a> So don&#39;t boast about following people. Everything belongs to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="1 Corinthians 3:22">22</a> Whether Paul, Apollos, Cephas, the world, life, death, things now, or things in the future; all belong to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="1 Corinthians 3:23">23</a> You belong to Christ; and Christ belongs to God.</p>
