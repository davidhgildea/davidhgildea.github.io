<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Corinthians 2:1">1</a> When I came to you, friends, I did not use fancy talking or great wisdom to tell you about God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Corinthians 2:2">2</a> I decided to focus only on knowing about Jesus Christ and his crucifixion when I was with you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Corinthians 2:3">3</a> I was with you feeling weak, scared, and shaking a lot.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Corinthians 2:4">4</a> My speaking and preaching weren&#39;t with fancy words of human wisdom, but with clear evidence of the Spirit and power.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Corinthians 2:5">5</a> Your faith should not be based on human wisdom, but on God&#39;s power.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Corinthians 2:6">6</a> We talk about deep understanding with the mature, but it&#39;s not the knowledge of this world or its leaders, which will all end.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Corinthians 2:7">7</a> We talk about God&#39;s wisdom that is secret and hidden, wisdom that God decided before the world began for our glory.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Corinthians 2:8">8</a> The rulers of this world did not understand this; if they had, they would not have crucified the glorious Lord.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Corinthians 2:9">9</a> People have not seen, heard, or even imagined what God has ready for those who love Him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Corinthians 2:10">10</a> God has shown these things to us through his Spirit, because the Spirit explores everything, even the deep things of God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Corinthians 2:11">11</a> Who knows what a person thinks except their own spirit? Just like that, no one knows God&#39;s thoughts except God&#39;s Spirit.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Corinthians 2:12">12</a> We have received, not the world&#39;s spirit, but the spirit from God; so we can know what God has freely given us.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 Corinthians 2:13">13</a> We speak about these things not with words taught by human wisdom, but with those taught by the Holy Spirit, explaining spiritual truths with the help of the Spirit.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="1 Corinthians 2:14">14</a> A person without the Spirit does not accept what comes from God&#39;s Spirit, because it seems foolish to them. They cannot understand it because it must be understood in a spiritual way.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="1 Corinthians 2:15">15</a> The spiritual person can assess everything, but no one can judge him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="1 Corinthians 2:16">16</a> Who has understood God&#39;s thoughts, that they could teach Him? But we think like Christ.</p>
