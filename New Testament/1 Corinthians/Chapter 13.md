<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Corinthians 13:1">1</a> If I speak in human and angel languages but don&#39;t have love, I am only a loud horn or a clanging cymbal.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Corinthians 13:2">2</a> Even if I can prophesy, understand all secrets, know everything, and have enough faith to move mountains, but don&#39;t have love, I am nothing.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Corinthians 13:3">3</a> Even if I give away everything I own to help the poor, and even if I sacrifice my body, but do not have love, it does me no good.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Corinthians 13:4">4</a> Love is patient and kind; love does not envy; love does not boast and is not proud.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Corinthians 13:5">5</a> Doesn&#39;t act improperly, doesn&#39;t seek its own benefit, isn&#39;t easily angered, doesn&#39;t think of doing wrong.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Corinthians 13:6">6</a> Does not delight in wrong, but celebrates the truth.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Corinthians 13:7">7</a> Bears everything, believes everything, hopes for everything, endures everything.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Corinthians 13:8">8</a> Love never ends. But if there are prophecies, they will stop; if there are languages, they will end; if there is knowledge, it will go away.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Corinthians 13:9">9</a> We know some things and we tell about the future as much as we know.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Corinthians 13:10">10</a> When the perfect thing comes, the imperfect things will disappear.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Corinthians 13:11">11</a> When I was a child, I talked like a child, I thought like a child, I reasoned like a child. But when I grew up, I stopped doing childish things.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Corinthians 13:12">12</a> Now we see unclearly, as in a mirror; but then we will see clearly, face to face. Now I know only a little; but then I will fully understand, just as I am fully understood.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 Corinthians 13:13">13</a> Now faith, hope, and love remain, these three; but the most important of these is love.</p>
