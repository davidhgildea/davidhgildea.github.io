<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Corinthians 7:1">1</a> About what you wrote to me: It&#39;s good for a man not to be with a woman.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Corinthians 7:2">2</a> To avoid wrong sexual acts, every man should have a wife and every woman should have a husband.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Corinthians 7:3">3</a> The husband should be kind to his wife, and the wife should be kind to her husband.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Corinthians 7:4">4</a> A wife does not control her own body, but her husband does; and in the same way, a husband does not control his own body, but his wife does.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Corinthians 7:5">5</a> Do not cheat each other, unless you both agree to stop having sex for a while to focus on fasting and praying. Then come back together so that Satan won&#39;t tempt you because you can&#39;t control yourselves.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Corinthians 7:6">6</a> I say this as a suggestion, not as an order.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Corinthians 7:7">7</a> I wish that all people were just as I am. But each person has their own special gift from God, one in this way, and another in that way.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Corinthians 7:8">8</a> I tell unmarried people and widows that it&#39;s good for them to stay single, like I am.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Corinthians 7:9">9</a> If they can&#39;t control themselves, they should get married, because it&#39;s better to marry than to have strong desires.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Corinthians 7:10">10</a> To the married, I give this order (not me, but God): A wife should not leave her husband.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Corinthians 7:11">11</a> If she leaves, she should stay single or get back with her husband; and the husband shouldn&#39;t divorce his wife.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Corinthians 7:12">12</a> I say this, not God: If a man has a wife who does not believe, but she agrees to live with him, he should not leave her.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 Corinthians 7:13">13</a> If a woman has a husband who does not believe, but he wants to live with her, she should not leave him.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="1 Corinthians 7:14">14</a> The husband who does not believe is made holy through his wife, and the wife who does not believe is made holy through her husband; otherwise your children would be impure, but now they are holy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="1 Corinthians 7:15">15</a> If someone does not believe and wants to leave, let them leave. A brother or sister is not bound in such situations: God wants us to live in peace.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="1 Corinthians 7:16">16</a> Wife, how do you know if you can save your husband? Or husband, how do you know if you can save your wife?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="1 Corinthians 7:17">17</a> But as God has given to each person, as God has called each one, let them live that way. This is what I instruct in all churches.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="1 Corinthians 7:18">18</a> If a man is already circumcised when he is called, he should not try to reverse it. If he is uncircumcised when called, he should not get circumcised.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="1 Corinthians 7:19">19</a> Circumcision doesn&#39;t matter, and not being circumcised doesn&#39;t matter, but obeying God&#39;s commands does matter.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="1 Corinthians 7:20">20</a> Everyone should remain in the life situation they were in when God called them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="1 Corinthians 7:21">21</a> Are you called while a servant? Don&#39;t worry about it: but if you can become free, choose that instead.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="1 Corinthians 7:22">22</a> When someone serves and follows God, they become free, and a person who is free but follows Christ becomes His servant.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="1 Corinthians 7:23">23</a> You were bought at a price; do not become slaves to people.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="1 Corinthians 7:24">24</a> Friends, stay as you are when God calls you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="1 Corinthians 7:25">25</a> About unmarried women, I don&#39;t have a direct order from God, but I offer my opinion as someone who God has shown kindness to be trustworthy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="1 Corinthians 7:26">26</a> I believe that, given our current troubles, it is good for a person to stay as they are.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="1 Corinthians 7:27">27</a> Are you married? Don&#39;t try to get a divorce. Are you divorced? Don&#39;t look for a wife.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="1 Corinthians 7:28">28</a> If you get married, you haven&#39;t sinned; and if an unmarried woman gets married, she hasn&#39;t sinned. However, they will have problems in life. But I want to spare you this.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="1 Corinthians 7:29">29</a> I tell you, friends, time is running out. It&#39;s important that those with wives should live as if they had none.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="1 Corinthians 7:30">30</a> People who cry should act as if they&#39;re not crying; those who are happy should act as if they&#39;re not happy; and those who buy things should act as if they don&#39;t own them.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="1 Corinthians 7:31">31</a> People should use this world without misusing it, because this world&#39;s way will disappear.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="1 Corinthians 7:32">32</a> I want you to be free from worries. A single person cares about how to please God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="1 Corinthians 7:33">33</a> But a married person cares about worldly things, how to make their spouse happy.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="1 Corinthians 7:34">34</a> A wife and a virgin are different. The single woman cares about God&#39;s work, to be pure in body and spirit, but the married woman cares about worldly things, to please her husband.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v35"><a class="verse-num" href="#v35" aria-label="1 Corinthians 7:35">35</a> I say this for your benefit, not to trap you, but to promote what is proper, and so you can focus on God without distraction.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v36"><a class="verse-num" href="#v36" aria-label="1 Corinthians 7:36">36</a> If a man believes he is acting improperly towards his fianc&#233;e, if she is past her prime age and it is necessary, he can do as he chooses, he does not sin: they should get married.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v37"><a class="verse-num" href="#v37" aria-label="1 Corinthians 7:37">37</a> The person who is firm in their decision, not forced but in control of their desire, and decides to stay unmarried, does well.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v38"><a class="verse-num" href="#v38" aria-label="1 Corinthians 7:38">38</a> So the person who helps her get married does good, but the person who does not help her get married does better.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v39"><a class="verse-num" href="#v39" aria-label="1 Corinthians 7:39">39</a> The wife must follow the law while her husband is alive; but if her husband dies, she is free to marry anyone she wants, as long as it is with a believer.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v40"><a class="verse-num" href="#v40" aria-label="1 Corinthians 7:40">40</a> But she is happier if she stays as she is, in my opinion, and I believe I have God&#39;s Spirit.</p>
