<p class="verse" id="v1"><a class="verse-num" href="#v1" aria-label="1 Corinthians 11:1">1</a> Follow me, just as I follow Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v2"><a class="verse-num" href="#v2" aria-label="1 Corinthians 11:2">2</a> I am happy, friends, that you remember me in everything and follow the teachings just as I gave them to you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v3"><a class="verse-num" href="#v3" aria-label="1 Corinthians 11:3">3</a> I want you to understand that Christ leads every man, the man leads the woman, and God leads Christ.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v4"><a class="verse-num" href="#v4" aria-label="1 Corinthians 11:4">4</a> Every man who prays or speaks God&#39;s words with his head covered shames his head.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v5"><a class="verse-num" href="#v5" aria-label="1 Corinthians 11:5">5</a> Every woman who prays or speaks God&#39;s messages with her head not covered dishonors her head; it&#39;s just like if she had her head shaved.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v6"><a class="verse-num" href="#v6" aria-label="1 Corinthians 11:6">6</a> If a woman does not wear a covering, she should cut her hair. But if it is shameful for a woman to have short hair or be shaved, she should cover her head.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v7"><a class="verse-num" href="#v7" aria-label="1 Corinthians 11:7">7</a> A man should not cover his head because he is made in God&#39;s image and reflects God&#39;s glory. But a woman reflects a man&#39;s glory.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v8"><a class="verse-num" href="#v8" aria-label="1 Corinthians 11:8">8</a> The man does not come from the woman, but the woman comes from the man.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v9"><a class="verse-num" href="#v9" aria-label="1 Corinthians 11:9">9</a> The man was not created for the woman, but the woman for the man.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v10"><a class="verse-num" href="#v10" aria-label="1 Corinthians 11:10">10</a> For this reason, a woman should have a symbol of authority on her head, because of the angels.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v11"><a class="verse-num" href="#v11" aria-label="1 Corinthians 11:11">11</a> Still, neither is man complete without woman, nor woman without man, in God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v12"><a class="verse-num" href="#v12" aria-label="1 Corinthians 11:12">12</a> Just as a woman comes from a man, so does a man come through a woman; but everything comes from God.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v13"><a class="verse-num" href="#v13" aria-label="1 Corinthians 11:13">13</a> Think for yourselves: is it proper for a woman to pray to God with her head uncovered?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v14"><a class="verse-num" href="#v14" aria-label="1 Corinthians 11:14">14</a> Doesn&#39;t nature itself show you that if a man has long hair, it is a disgrace to him?</p>

<p class="verse" id="v15"><a class="verse-num" href="#v15" aria-label="1 Corinthians 11:15">15</a> If a woman has long hair, it is her pride, because her hair is given to her as a covering.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v16"><a class="verse-num" href="#v16" aria-label="1 Corinthians 11:16">16</a> If anyone likes to argue, we don&#39;t have that habit, and neither do God&#39;s churches.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v17"><a class="verse-num" href="#v17" aria-label="1 Corinthians 11:17">17</a> In telling you this, I do not praise you, because when you meet, it&#39;s not for the good but for the bad.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v18"><a class="verse-num" href="#v18" aria-label="1 Corinthians 11:18">18</a> First, when you meet as a church, I&#39;ve heard there are splits among you, and I believe it somewhat.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v19"><a class="verse-num" href="#v19" aria-label="1 Corinthians 11:19">19</a> There must also be false beliefs among you, so that those who are true can be clearly seen.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v20"><a class="verse-num" href="#v20" aria-label="1 Corinthians 11:20">20</a> When you meet together in one place, it is not to eat God&#39;s supper.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v21"><a class="verse-num" href="#v21" aria-label="1 Corinthians 11:21">21</a> When they eat, some people go first and take their own meal, leaving others hungry while some have too much to drink.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v22"><a class="verse-num" href="#v22" aria-label="1 Corinthians 11:22">22</a> Do you not have homes to eat and drink in? Or do you look down on God&#39;s church and embarrass those who have nothing? What should I say to you? Should I commend you for this? I do not commend you.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v23"><a class="verse-num" href="#v23" aria-label="1 Corinthians 11:23">23</a> I got from God what I also passed on to you: on the night Jesus was betrayed, he took bread.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v24"><a class="verse-num" href="#v24" aria-label="1 Corinthians 11:24">24</a> After thanking God, he broke the bread and said, &quot;Take and eat; this is my body given for you. Do this to remember me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v25"><a class="verse-num" href="#v25" aria-label="1 Corinthians 11:25">25</a> In the same way, he took the cup after supper and said, &quot;This cup is the new agreement in my blood; do this whenever you drink it, to remember me.&quot;</p>

<p class="verse" id="v26"><a class="verse-num" href="#v26" aria-label="1 Corinthians 11:26">26</a> Every time you eat this bread and drink this cup, you show the death of God until he comes.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v27"><a class="verse-num" href="#v27" aria-label="1 Corinthians 11:27">27</a> So anyone who eats this bread and drinks this cup of God in a wrong way will be guilty of sinning against God&#39;s body and blood.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v28"><a class="verse-num" href="#v28" aria-label="1 Corinthians 11:28">28</a> A person should check themselves before they eat the bread and drink from the cup.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v29"><a class="verse-num" href="#v29" aria-label="1 Corinthians 11:29">29</a> Anyone who eats and drinks without respecting the body of God eats and drinks judgment on themselves.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v30"><a class="verse-num" href="#v30" aria-label="1 Corinthians 11:30">30</a> Because of this, many of you are weak and ill, and a number have died.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v31"><a class="verse-num" href="#v31" aria-label="1 Corinthians 11:31">31</a> If we judge ourselves, we won&#39;t be judged by others.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v32"><a class="verse-num" href="#v32" aria-label="1 Corinthians 11:32">32</a> When God corrects us, it&#39;s so we won&#39;t be punished along with the world.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v33"><a class="verse-num" href="#v33" aria-label="1 Corinthians 11:33">33</a> So, my friends, when you gather to eat, wait for each other.</p>

<p class="verse" id="v34"><a class="verse-num" href="#v34" aria-label="1 Corinthians 11:34">34</a> If anyone is hungry, they should eat at home so that when you meet, it doesn&#39;t lead to judgment. I will take care of the other matters when I arrive.</p>
