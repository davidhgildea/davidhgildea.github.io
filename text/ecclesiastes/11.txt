Ecclesiastes 11:1 Throw your bread on the water, and you will find it again after a long time.
Ecclesiastes 11:2 Share with seven, or even eight, because you do not know what troubles may come on the earth.
Ecclesiastes 11:3 If clouds are full, they pour rain on the earth. If a tree falls south or north, it stays where it fell.
Ecclesiastes 11:4 If you wait for perfect weather, you won't plant seeds; if you look at the clouds, you won't harvest crops.
Ecclesiastes 11:5 You don't know how the spirit comes to the body, or how a baby's bones grow inside a pregnant woman: just like that, you don't understand how God, who makes everything, works.
Ecclesiastes 11:6 Plant your seeds in the morning, and don't stop working in the evening. You can't tell if one will succeed, the other, or if both will be equally good.
Ecclesiastes 11:7 Surely light is sweet, and it's nice for the eyes to see the sun.
Ecclesiastes 11:8 If a person lives for many years and enjoys them, he should still remember there will be many dark days. Everything that happens is meaningless.
Ecclesiastes 11:9 Be happy, young person, while you are young. Let your heart give you joy in your youth. Follow your heart and what you see with your eyes, but remember that God will judge you for all these things.
Ecclesiastes 11:10 So take away sadness from your heart and stop doing bad things with your body, because being young is not so important.
