Ecclesiastes 4:1 I went back and saw all the unfair things happening on earth: I saw the tears of the oppressed, with no one to comfort them; the oppressors had the power, but the oppressed had no comforter.
Ecclesiastes 4:2 So I said that those who have already died are happier than those who are still alive.
Ecclesiastes 4:3 Yes, the one who has not yet been born is better off than both of them, for he has not seen the bad things done in the world.
Ecclesiastes 4:4 I saw that all hard work and success in doing good things can make people jealous of their neighbor. This is also meaningless and frustrating.
Ecclesiastes 4:5 The foolish person does nothing and harms themselves.
Ecclesiastes 4:6 It is better to have a little with peace than a lot with hard work and stress.
Ecclesiastes 4:7 Then I came back and saw that everything is pointless under the sun.
Ecclesiastes 4:8 There is only one person, without a companion; they have no children or siblings. Still, their work never ends; they are never happy with their wealth, and never ask, "Who am I working for, and denying myself enjoyment?" This too is meaningless, a miserable task.
Ecclesiastes 4:9 Two are better than one because they get a good return for their work.
Ecclesiastes 4:10 If they fall, one can help the other up, but pity the person who is alone and falls without someone to help them.
Ecclesiastes 4:11 If two people lie together, they keep warm, but how can one stay warm alone?
Ecclesiastes 4:12 If one person can be defeated, two can stand strong against the enemy; a rope made of three strands is not easily snapped.
Ecclesiastes 4:13 A poor and smart child is better than an old and foolish king who won't listen to advice.
Ecclesiastes 4:14 He comes to rule after being in jail; even someone born in his kingdom may become poor.
Ecclesiastes 4:15 I thought about everyone alive who goes about under the sun, along with the next person who will take their place.
Ecclesiastes 4:16 People never stop coming, even those from before. Those who come later won't be happy with this. This too is pointless and frustrating.
