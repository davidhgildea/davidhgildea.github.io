Ecclesiastes 7:1 Having a good reputation is better than having expensive perfume, and the day someone dies is better than the day they are born.
Ecclesiastes 7:2 It is better to visit a place where people are sad because of death than a place where people are celebrating. This is because everyone will die someday, and the living should think about this.
Ecclesiastes 7:3 Sadness is better than laughing because a sad face can improve the heart.
Ecclesiastes 7:4 Wise people think about sad times; foolish people think about happy times.
Ecclesiastes 7:5 It is better to listen to the correction from someone smart than to enjoy the music of foolish people.
Ecclesiastes 7:6 Like the noise of thorns burning under a pot, so is a fool's laugh: it's also meaningless.
Ecclesiastes 7:7 Oppression can make a smart person angry; and a bribe can ruin their character.
Ecclesiastes 7:8 The end of something is better than its start, and being patient is better than being proud.
Ecclesiastes 7:9 Don't be quick to get angry, because anger lives in the hearts of fools.
Ecclesiastes 7:10 Do not ask why the old days were better than now; asking this does not show wisdom.
Ecclesiastes 7:11 Having wisdom and an inheritance is good, and it benefits those who live under the sun.
Ecclesiastes 7:12 Wisdom protects us, and so does money, but the best thing about knowledge is that wisdom brings life to those who possess it.
Ecclesiastes 7:13 Think about what God does: who can fix something if God made it bent?
Ecclesiastes 7:14 When times are good, be happy, but when times are bad, think: God has made one as well as the other, so that people can't predict anything about their future.
Ecclesiastes 7:15 In my empty days, I have seen everything: a good person dies despite being good, and a bad person lives long while being bad.
Ecclesiastes 7:16 Don't be too righteous or too wise; why ruin your life?
Ecclesiastes 7:17 Don't be too wicked or foolish; why die before it's your time?
Ecclesiastes 7:18 It is good for you to grasp this and not let go; because one who respects God will succeed in everything.
Ecclesiastes 7:19 Wisdom gives the wise more strength than ten strong men in the city.
Ecclesiastes 7:20 No one on earth is perfect; everyone does some good, but also makes mistakes.
Ecclesiastes 7:21 Don't pay attention to everything people say, or you might hear your servant speaking badly of you.
Ecclesiastes 7:22 Your heart knows that you have also cursed others many times.
Ecclesiastes 7:23 I have tested all these things with wisdom; I thought I could become wise, but it was out of my reach.
Ecclesiastes 7:24 What is far away and very deep, who can discover it?
Ecclesiastes 7:25 I set my heart to understand, to investigate, and to find wisdom and explanations, and to understand foolishness, senselessness, and madness.
Ecclesiastes 7:26 I find a woman whose heart is full of traps and her hands are like chains more bitter than death. Someone who pleases God will avoid her, but a sinner will be caught by her.
Ecclesiastes 7:27 Look, this is what I have discovered, says the teacher, adding one thing to another to find the explanation.
Ecclesiastes 7:28 My soul still searches, but I do not find: I have found one man among a thousand, but not one woman among all.
Ecclesiastes 7:29 Look, I have found only this: God made people good, but they have come up with many schemes.
