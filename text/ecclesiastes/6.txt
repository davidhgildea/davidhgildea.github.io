Ecclesiastes 6:1 I have seen a bad thing everywhere in the world, and it happens often to people.
Ecclesiastes 6:2 A man whom God has given money, possessions, and respect, so he lacks nothing he wants, but if God doesn't let him enjoy these things and a stranger does instead, this is pointless and a terrible sickness.
Ecclesiastes 6:3 If a man has a hundred children and lives a long time, but is not happy or buried when he dies, it is better to be born dead.
Ecclesiastes 6:4 He comes with emptiness, leaves in the dark, and his name will be shrouded in darkness.
Ecclesiastes 6:5 He hasn't seen the sun or learned anything; he has more peace than the other.
Ecclesiastes 6:6 Even if a person lives two thousand years, they still do not experience true happiness. Don't we all end up in the same place after death?
Ecclesiastes 6:7 Everyone works to feed themselves, but no one is ever fully satisfied.
Ecclesiastes 6:8 What advantage does a wise person have over a fool? What does the poor person gain by knowing how to conduct themselves in life?
Ecclesiastes 6:9 Seeing what is in front of you is better than chasing your wishes. This, too, is pointless and frustrating.
Ecclesiastes 6:10 What has happened has already been named, and it is known that it is human; no one can argue with one who is stronger than themselves.
Ecclesiastes 6:11 With so many things that add to emptiness, how is a person improved?
Ecclesiastes 6:12 Who knows what is best for a person in life, during the meaningless days they pass like a shadow? Who can tell anyone what will happen after them under the sun?
