Ezekiel 19:1 Make a sad song for Israel's leaders.
Ezekiel 19:2 Ask, "What is your mother? A lioness: she lay among lions, she raised her cubs with other lions."
Ezekiel 19:3 She raised one of her cubs; it became a young lion and learned to hunt; it ate people.
Ezekiel 19:4 Other countries heard about him; he got caught in their trap, and they took him with chains to Egypt.
Ezekiel 19:5 When she saw her waiting was in vain and her hope was gone, she took another one of her young and raised it to be a young lion.
Ezekiel 19:6 He moved among lions, became a young lion, learned to hunt, and ate people.
Ezekiel 19:7 He knew their empty homes and destroyed their cities; the land was empty and completely ruined by the sound of his roar.
Ezekiel 19:8 Nations from all around trapped him with their nets, and he was caught in their hole.
Ezekiel 19:9 They locked him up with chains and took him to the king of Babylon: they kept him in prison so his voice would not be heard on Israel's mountains anymore.
Ezekiel 19:10 Your mother is like a vine in your life, planted near water; she was productive and had many branches because of the plentiful water.
Ezekiel 19:11 She had sturdy branches fit for rulers' scepters; she stood tall amidst the dense foliage, her height visible with her many branches.
Ezekiel 19:12 But she was yanked up angrily, thrown to the ground, and the east wind dried up her fruit; her strong branches were broken and dried up; the fire burned them up.
Ezekiel 19:13 And now it is planted in a desert, in a dry and parched land.
Ezekiel 19:14 Fire came from one of her branch sticks and burned up her fruit. Now she has no strong branch to be a ruler's scepter. This is a sad song, and it will be used as a sad song.
