Ezekiel 11:1 The spirit picked me up and took me to the east entrance of God's house, facing east. There, at the entrance gate, I saw twenty-five men. Among them were Jaazaniah, Azur's son, and Pelatiah, Benaiah's son, who were leaders of the people.
Ezekiel 11:2 He said to me, "Human, these are the people who plan trouble and give evil advice in this city."
Ezekiel 11:3 They say, "It's not close; let's build houses: this city is the pot, and we are the meat."
Ezekiel 11:4 So tell them what will happen, speak out, human.
Ezekiel 11:5 God's Spirit came on me and He said, "Speak: This is what God says; you, the people of Israel, have said these things, and I know every thought that comes into your minds."
Ezekiel 11:6 You have increased the number of your dead in this city, and you have filled its streets with the dead.
Ezekiel 11:7 So God says: "The people you killed and left in the city are like meat, and the city is like a pot. But I will take you out of the city."
Ezekiel 11:8 You have been afraid of the sword; and I will bring a sword upon you, says God.
Ezekiel 11:9 I will take you out of there, give you to foreigners, and carry out punishments on you.
Ezekiel 11:10 You will die by the sword; I will judge you at Israel's border; and you will know that I am God.
Ezekiel 11:11 This city won't be your cooking pot, nor will you be the meat inside it; instead, I will judge you at Israel's border.
Ezekiel 11:12 You will understand that I am God because you have not followed my laws or done what I said but have acted like the foreigners around you.
Ezekiel 11:13 When I was prophesying, Pelatiah, Benaiah's son, died. I fell face down, shouted loudly, and said, "Ah God! Are you going to completely destroy what's left of Israel?"
Ezekiel 11:14 God spoke to me again, saying,
Ezekiel 11:15 Human, your relatives, your own family, and all the people of Israel are those to whom the people of Jerusalem have said, "Stay away from God: this land is ours to own."
Ezekiel 11:16 So say, God says this: Even though I sent them away among other nations, and I have spread them across different lands, I will still be like a small place of safety for them in the lands where they go.
Ezekiel 11:17 So say this: God says, "I will bring you together from the people, gather you from the countries where you were spread out, and I will give you the land of Israel."
Ezekiel 11:18 They will go there and remove all its disgusting things and all its horrors.
Ezekiel 11:19 I will give them one heart, and put a new spirit in you; I will remove the heart of stone from their body, and give them a heart of flesh.
Ezekiel 11:20 So they can follow my rules, obey my commands, and do them: and they will be my people, and I will be their God.
Ezekiel 11:21 But as for those who follow their wicked hearts and disgusting actions, I will make sure their actions come back on them, says God.
Ezekiel 11:22 Then the cherubim lifted their wings, and the wheels were beside them; and God's glory was above them.
Ezekiel 11:23 God's glory rose from the center of the city and rested on the mountain to the east of the city.
Ezekiel 11:24 Later, the spirit lifted me up. In a vision from God's spirit, I was taken to the land of the exiles in Chaldea. There, the vision I saw left me.
Ezekiel 11:25 Then I told the captives everything that God had shown me.
