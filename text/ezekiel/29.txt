Ezekiel 29:1 In the tenth year, on the tenth month, on the twelfth day, God's message came to me, saying,
Ezekiel 29:2 Human, look toward Pharaoh, the king of Egypt, speak against him and all of Egypt.
Ezekiel 29:3 Say this: God says, "Look, I am against you, Pharaoh, king of Egypt, the great monster lying in the rivers, who claims, 'The river is mine; I made it for myself.'"
Ezekiel 29:4 I will put hooks in your jaws, make the fish of your rivers cling to your scales, pull you out from your rivers, and all the fish will stick to your scales.
Ezekiel 29:5 I will abandon you in the wilderness, along with all the fish from your rivers. You will lie in the fields, not collected or gathered. I have made you food for the animals of the land and the birds in the sky.
Ezekiel 29:6 Everyone in Egypt will understand that I am God, because they were like a weak stick to the people of Israel.
Ezekiel 29:7 When they grabbed your hand, you snapped and tore their shoulder; when they leaned on you, you broke and caused their hips to freeze.
Ezekiel 29:8 So God says: "Look, I will bring a sword against you and will wipe out both people and animals from you."
Ezekiel 29:9 Egypt will become empty and ruined; and they will understand that I am God: because he claimed, "The river is mine, and I made it."
Ezekiel 29:10 Look, I am against you and your rivers, and I will turn Egypt into complete ruins, from the tower of Syene to the border of Ethiopia.
Ezekiel 29:11 No person or animal will walk through it, and no one will live there for forty years.
Ezekiel 29:12 I will turn Egypt into a barren land among other barren countries, and its cities will be ruined for forty years along with other destroyed cities. I will spread the Egyptians among different nations and scatter them across various countries.
Ezekiel 29:13 God says, "After forty years, I will bring the Egyptians back together from the places where they were spread out."
Ezekiel 29:14 I will end Egypt's captivity and make them return to Pathros, their homeland; there, they will be a lowly kingdom.
Ezekiel 29:15 It will become the weakest of the kingdoms; it will not rise above the nations anymore because I will make them smaller, so they won't rule over the nations again.
Ezekiel 29:16 The people of Israel will no longer rely on what makes them sin, and they will realize that I am God.
Ezekiel 29:17 In the twenty-seventh year, on the first day of the first month, God's message came to me.
Ezekiel 29:18 Man, King Nebuchadnezzar of Babylon made his army work hard attacking Tyre: they lost their hair and injured their shoulders, but he and his army got nothing from Tyre for their effort.
Ezekiel 29:19 So God says, "Look, I will give Egypt to Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon. He will take their people, their wealth, and their goods as payment for his army."
Ezekiel 29:20 I have rewarded him with the land of Egypt for his work, because they worked for me, says God.
Ezekiel 29:21 On that day, I will make Israel's power grow, and I will let you speak freely among them; then they will know that I am God.
