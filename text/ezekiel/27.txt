Ezekiel 27:1 God spoke to me again and said,
Ezekiel 27:2 Now, you human, sing a sad song for Tyre.
Ezekiel 27:3 Tell the city of Tyre, you who are located by the sea gateway and trade with many islands, "God says: O Tyre, you claim to be perfectly beautiful."
Ezekiel 27:4 Your borders are surrounded by the seas, your builders have made you very beautiful.
Ezekiel 27:5 They made all your ship planks from Senir's fir trees; they took Lebanon's cedars to make masts for you.
Ezekiel 27:6 They made your oars from the Bashan oaks; your seats are carved ivory from the Ashurites, brought from the islands of Chittim.
Ezekiel 27:7 You used fine linen and embroidered fabric from Egypt for your sail; blue and purple from the Elishah islands were your covers.
Ezekiel 27:8 The people of Zidon and Arvad sailed your ships; the skilled men of Tyre in you were your captains.
Ezekiel 27:9 The elders of Gebal and its skilled people were in you to seal your ships: all the sea's ships and their sailors were there to trade your goods.
Ezekiel 27:10 People from Persia, Lud, and Phut were in your army, your warriors: they carried shields and helmets; they made you look splendid.
Ezekiel 27:11 The soldiers from Arvad and your army were on your walls all around, and the warriors were in your towers. They put their shields on your walls all around; they made your city very beautiful.
Ezekiel 27:12 Tarshish traded with you because you had many riches; they exchanged silver, iron, tin, and lead for your goods.
Ezekiel 27:13 Javan, Tubal, and Meshech were your traders; they exchanged people and bronze items in your market.
Ezekiel 27:14 The people from Togarmah did business at your markets with horses, horsemen, and mules.
Ezekiel 27:15 The traders from Dedan traded with you; many islands gave you goods: they gave you ivory tusks and ebony as gifts.
Ezekiel 27:16 Syria traded with you because you made many goods; they exchanged emeralds, purple fabric, embroidered cloth, fine linen, coral, and agate at your markets.
Ezekiel 27:17 Judah and Israel were your traders; in your marketplace they exchanged wheat from Minnith, Pannag, honey, oil, and balm.
Ezekiel 27:18 Damascus traded with you because you made many goods, exchanging them for lots of riches, Helbon wine, and white wool.
Ezekiel 27:19 Dan and Javan traveled back and forth, busy in your markets: shiny iron, cassia, and sweet cane were among your goods.
Ezekiel 27:20 Dedan was your trader in fancy fabrics for carts.
Ezekiel 27:21 Arabia and Kedar's leaders traded with you in lambs, rams, and goats; these were your business partners.
Ezekiel 27:22 The traders from Sheba and Raamah were your traders; they traded at your markets with the best spices, precious stones, and gold.
Ezekiel 27:23 Haran, Canneh, and Eden, and the traders from Sheba, Asshur, and Chilmad were your traders.
Ezekiel 27:24 These were your traders with all kinds of goods, in blue fabrics, decorated cloth, and rich clothing in cedar chests tied with ropes among your items for sale.
Ezekiel 27:25 Tarshish's ships sang about you in your market; you were filled and made very glorious on the seas.
Ezekiel 27:26 Your rowers took you into deep waters: the east wind has shattered you in the middle of the seas.
Ezekiel 27:27 Your wealth, markets, goods, sailors, navigators, ship repairers, traders, soldiers, and everyone else with you will fall into the sea when you are destroyed.
Ezekiel 27:28 The areas around will tremble when they hear your pilots' shouts.
Ezekiel 27:29 Everyone who rows, the sailors, and all the sea captains will get off their ships and stand on the land.
Ezekiel 27:30 They will raise their voices against you, cry loudly, throw dust on their heads and roll in ashes.
Ezekiel 27:31 They will shave their heads for you, wear rough cloth as a sign of sadness, and cry for you with deep sorrow and loud cries.
Ezekiel 27:32 During their crying, they will mourn for you, saying, "What city is like Tyre, like the ruined one in the sea?"
Ezekiel 27:33 When your goods were sent out to sea, you supplied many people; you made the kings of the earth rich with your wealth and goods.
Ezekiel 27:34 When you are destroyed by the seas deep in the water, your goods and everyone with you will sink.
Ezekiel 27:35 Everyone living on the islands will be shocked at you, and their kings will be very scared; they will look worried.
Ezekiel 27:36 The traders among the people will scorn you; you will be feared, and never exist again.
