Ezekiel 30:1 God spoke to me again, saying,
Ezekiel 30:2 Human, speak God's message: Cry out, 'What a terrible day!'
Ezekiel 30:3 The day is coming soon, even God's day is close, a dark day; it will be the time for non-believers.
Ezekiel 30:4 A sword will come to Egypt, and Ethiopia will be in great pain when people are killed in Egypt, their crowds taken, and their strong bases destroyed.
Ezekiel 30:5 Ethiopia, Libya, Lydia, all mixed peoples, Chub, and allies will fall by the sword together.
Ezekiel 30:6 God says, "Those who support Egypt will fall; their great power will be brought low. They will fall by the sword from the stronghold of Syene," says God.
Ezekiel 30:7 They will be empty among empty lands, and her cities will be among ruined cities.
Ezekiel 30:8 They will know that I am God when I start a fire in Egypt and all her allies are ruined.
Ezekiel 30:9 On that day, I will send messengers in ships to scare the unprepared Ethiopians, and they will feel great pain like in the time of Egypt, because look, it's coming.
Ezekiel 30:10 God says, I will also end the large number of people in Egypt by the hand of Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon.
Ezekiel 30:11 He, his people, and the fiercest from different nations will come to destroy the land. They will attack Egypt with their swords and fill the land with the dead.
Ezekiel 30:12 I will dry up the rivers and hand the land over to the wicked; I will turn the land empty, along with everything in it, through strangers. I, God, have said this.
Ezekiel 30:13 God says, I will destroy the idols and stop their images in Noph; there will be no more a prince in Egypt, and I will bring fear to Egypt.
Ezekiel 30:14 I will make Pathros empty, and I will start a fire in Zoan, and I will carry out judgements in No.
Ezekiel 30:15 I will pour out my anger on Sin, the fortress of Egypt; and I will destroy the many people of No.
Ezekiel 30:16 I will start a fire in Egypt: Sin will be in great pain, No will be torn apart, and Noph will suffer every day.
Ezekiel 30:17 The young men of Aven and Pibeseth will die by the sword, and these cities will be taken captive.
Ezekiel 30:18 In Tehaphnehes too, the day will turn dark when I break Egypt's chains. Its proud power will end, a cloud will cover it, and its towns will be taken captive.
Ezekiel 30:19 I will carry out punishments in Egypt, and they will understand that I am God.
Ezekiel 30:20 In the eleventh year, on the first day of the first month, God's message came to me.
Ezekiel 30:21 Human, I have made Pharaoh, the king of Egypt, weak; his power will not be restored, he won't be healed or able to use his strength to fight.
Ezekiel 30:22 So God says: I am against the king of Egypt, Pharaoh. I will break his powerful arms, both the strong one and the already broken one, and make him drop his sword.
Ezekiel 30:23 I will spread the Egyptians among different nations and scatter them throughout the countries.
Ezekiel 30:24 I will make the king of Babylon strong and give him my sword, but I will break the power of Pharaoh, and he will moan in front of him like someone fatally injured.
Ezekiel 30:25 I will give strength to Babylon's king, while Egypt's pharaoh will lose power; they will realize I am God when I give my sword to Babylon's king to use against Egypt.
Ezekiel 30:26 I will spread the Egyptians among the nations and scatter them in different countries; then they will understand that I am God.
