1 Timothy 5:1 Don't scold an older person, but ask him kindly like a father; treat the younger men like brothers.
1 Timothy 5:2 Treat older women like mothers; younger women like sisters, with complete respect.
1 Timothy 5:3 Respect women who are truly alone without husbands.
1 Timothy 5:4 If a widow has children or grandchildren, they should first take care of their own family and repay their parents, because this pleases God.
1 Timothy 5:5 A true widow who is alone puts her hope in God and prays day and night.
1 Timothy 5:6 A woman who enjoys life too much is like she's dead even when she's alive.
1 Timothy 5:7 Instruct them in these matters so they can be without fault.
1 Timothy 5:8 If someone does not take care of their family, especially those living at home, they have rejected the faith and are worse than someone who does not believe.
1 Timothy 5:9 Do not include a widow who is less than sixty years old and has been married to only one man.
1 Timothy 5:10 Known for good acts; if she has raised children, welcomed guests, washed believers' feet, helped those in trouble, and eagerly done all kinds of good work.
1 Timothy 5:11 Do not accept the younger widows; when their desires grow strong, they want to marry again.
1 Timothy 5:12 They face punishment because they have abandoned their early belief.
1 Timothy 5:13 They also learn to be lazy, moving from house to house; and they are not just lazy, but also gossips and meddlers, talking about things they should not.
1 Timothy 5:14 I want younger women to get married, have children, manage their homes, and not give critics any reason to speak badly.
1 Timothy 5:15 Some have already turned away following Satan.
1 Timothy 5:16 If any believer, man or woman, has widows in their family, they should help them so that the church isn't burdened and can assist those who are truly in need.
1 Timothy 5:17 Respect the leaders who guide well greatly, and give extra respect to those who work hard teaching God's message and beliefs.
1 Timothy 5:18 The Bible says, you should not cover the ox's mouth when it is working with the grain. Also, a worker deserves to be paid for their work.
1 Timothy 5:19 Do not accept a charge against an older person unless it is supported by two or three witnesses.
1 Timothy 5:20 Tell everyone when someone does wrong so that others will be afraid to do the same.
1 Timothy 5:21 I tell you before God, Jesus Christ, and the chosen angels, to follow these instructions fairly, treating everyone the same, without favoring anyone.
1 Timothy 5:22 Don't quickly accuse anyone, and don't share in the wrongs of others; stay clean yourself.
1 Timothy 5:23 Stop drinking only water, but have a little wine for your stomach and your frequent sicknesses.
1 Timothy 5:24 Some people's sins are obvious, leading directly to judgment, while the sins of others become clear later.
1 Timothy 5:25 Similarly, the good deeds of some people are clear from the start, and those that are not will still be seen.
