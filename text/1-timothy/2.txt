1 Timothy 2:1 I encourage, then, that above all, requests, prayers, asking for others, and thanks should be given for everyone.
1 Timothy 2:2 For rulers and all in power; so we can live quietly and peacefully with true faith and honesty.
1 Timothy 2:3 This is good and okay to God who saves us.
1 Timothy 2:4 God wants everyone to be saved and to understand the truth.
1 Timothy 2:5 There is one God and one go-between for God and people, the man Christ Jesus.
1 Timothy 2:6 He gave himself as a ransom for everyone, to be shown at the right time.
1 Timothy 2:7 I am chosen to be a preacher and an apostle, speaking the truth in Christ without lying, and a teacher of the non-Jews in faith and truth.
1 Timothy 2:8 I want men to pray everywhere, raising holy hands, without anger or doubt.
1 Timothy 2:9 Women should also dress simply and with modesty, acting with decency and self-control, not with fancy hairstyles, gold, pearls, or expensive clothes.
1 Timothy 2:10 But (fitting for women who claim to honor God) by doing good deeds.
1 Timothy 2:11 Let the woman learn quietly and obey fully.
1 Timothy 2:12 I do not allow a woman to teach or have control over a man; she must be quiet.
1 Timothy 2:13 Adam was created first, then Eve.
1 Timothy 2:14 Adam was not tricked, but the woman was deceived and did wrong.
1 Timothy 2:15 She will be saved through having children, if they keep their faith, love, and holiness with good sense.
