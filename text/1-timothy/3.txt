1 Timothy 3:1 It is true that if someone wants to be a leader in the church, they want to do a good thing.
1 Timothy 3:2 A church leader must be without fault, married to one woman, watchful, clear-minded, well-behaved, welcoming to guests, and skilled in teaching.
1 Timothy 3:3 Doesn't drink too much, isn't violent, not chasing dirty money; but is patient, doesn't like to fight, and isn't greedy.
1 Timothy 3:4 Someone who leads their own family well, keeping their children respectful and serious.
1 Timothy 3:5 If a man can't manage his own family, how can he take care of God's church?
1 Timothy 3:6 Someone new should not be given a high position, because if they become proud, they might be judged as the devil was.
1 Timothy 3:7 He must also have a good reputation among outsiders to avoid falling into disgrace and the devil's trap.
1 Timothy 3:8 Deacons must be serious, not lie, not drink too much wine, and not want dirty money.
1 Timothy 3:9 Keeping the secret of faith with a clear conscience.
1 Timothy 3:10 First, these should be tested; then if they are found faultless, they can serve as deacons.
1 Timothy 3:11 Their wives must be serious, not spreading rumors, clear-minded, and trustworthy in everything.
1 Timothy 3:12 Deacons should have one wife and manage their children and homes well.
1 Timothy 3:13 Those who serve well as deacons gain a good standing for themselves and great confidence in their faith in Christ Jesus.
1 Timothy 3:14 I write these things to you, hoping to visit you soon.
1 Timothy 3:15 But if I'm delayed, you will know how to act in God's house, which is the living God's church, the support and foundation of truth.
1 Timothy 3:16 There's no doubt, the secret of being good is great: God appeared in human form, proved right by the Spirit, seen by angels, told to outsiders, trusted in by people everywhere, taken up into greatness.
