Deuteronomy 32:1 Listen, heavens, and I will talk; and listen, earth, to what I say.
Deuteronomy 32:2 My teaching will fall like rain, my words will settle like dew, like a light rain on new plants, and like showers on the grass.
Deuteronomy 32:3 I will tell everyone about God's name: say our God is great.
Deuteronomy 32:4 He is the Rock, His work is perfect; all His ways are fair. A God of truth, not doing wrong, just and upright is He.
Deuteronomy 32:5 They have made themselves bad; they are not like his children because they are a wrong and twisted group.
Deuteronomy 32:6 Are you repaying God like this, you foolish and senseless people? Isn't He your father who created you? Didn't He make and sustain you?
Deuteronomy 32:7 Think about the past and reflect on the years gone by. Ask your father, and he will teach you; talk to the older people, and they will explain it to you.
Deuteronomy 32:8 When God divided the lands among the nations, when he split up the people, he set the borders based on how many Israelites there were.
Deuteronomy 32:9 God's share is his people; Jacob is the group he has chosen to receive his inheritance.
Deuteronomy 32:10 He found him in a desert land, in a noisy empty wilderness; he led him around, taught him, and protected him like the pupil of his eye.
Deuteronomy 32:11 Like an eagle that stirs up its nest, hovers over its young, spreads its wings, takes them, and carries them on its wings.
Deuteronomy 32:12 God alone guided him, and there was no other god with him.
Deuteronomy 32:13 God let him ride on the earth's high places, to eat the crops from the fields; God let him taste honey from the rock and oil from the hard stone.
Deuteronomy 32:14 Butter from cows, and sheep's milk, with lamb's fat, and Bashan-bred rams, and goats, with wheat's creamy richness; and you drank grape juice, pure and rich.
Deuteronomy 32:15 Jeshurun grew fat and rebelled; you became fat, you got thick, you were filled with fat; then you left God who created you, and didn't value the God who saved you.
Deuteronomy 32:16 They made God jealous with foreign gods, and with hateful acts they made him angry.
Deuteronomy 32:17 They offered sacrifices to demons, not to God; to gods they did not know, to new gods that were recently made, that your ancestors did not fear.
Deuteronomy 32:18 You have forgotten the God who gave you life and ignored the one who made you.
Deuteronomy 32:19 When God saw it, he hated them because they angered his sons and daughters.
Deuteronomy 32:20 He said, "I will turn away from them and see what their future holds, because they are a difficult and unfaithful generation."
Deuteronomy 32:21 They made me jealous with things that are not God; they angered me with their useless actions. And I will make them jealous with those who are not a people; I will make them angry with a foolish nation.
Deuteronomy 32:22 My anger has started a fire that will burn to the deepest hell, destroy the earth and its crops, and set the mountain bases on fire.
Deuteronomy 32:23 I will bring many troubles upon them; I will use up my arrows against them.
Deuteronomy 32:24 They will suffer from severe hunger, extreme heat, and harsh destruction. I will also let wild animals attack them, and snakes from the ground will poison them.
Deuteronomy 32:25 Outside, the sword, and inside, fear, will kill the young man and the young woman, as well as the baby and the old man.
Deuteronomy 32:26 I said I would spread them out and make people forget them completely.
Deuteronomy 32:27 If I were not worried about our enemies' anger, they might act wrongly and claim, "We won by our own power, and God didn't do this."
Deuteronomy 32:28 They are a nation without guidance, and they have no understanding.
Deuteronomy 32:29 If only they were wise and understood this, so they would think about their future!
Deuteronomy 32:30 How can one person chase a thousand, or two people make ten thousand run away, unless their protector has abandoned them and God has trapped them?
Deuteronomy 32:31 Their rock is not like our God, even our enemies agree.
Deuteronomy 32:32 Their vine comes from Sodom's vines, and Gomorrah's fields: their grapes are bitter, their bunches are sour.
Deuteronomy 32:33 Their wine is like dragon poison and very cruel snake venom.
Deuteronomy 32:34 Isn't this kept with me and sealed in my treasures?
Deuteronomy 32:35 I am in charge of revenge and payback; their feet will slip when the time comes: because their disaster day is near, and the things that will happen to them will come quickly.
Deuteronomy 32:36 God will judge his people and change his mind for his servants when he sees their strength is gone and no one is confined or remaining.
Deuteronomy 32:37 He will ask, "Where are their gods, the rock they trusted in?"
Deuteronomy 32:38 Who ate the best parts of your offerings and drank your wine gifts? Let them get up and help you; let them protect you.
Deuteronomy 32:39 Understand now that I am the only God; there is no other god beside me. I can cause death or give life; I can injure or heal, and no one can rescue from my power.
Deuteronomy 32:40 I raise my hand to the sky and say, I will live forever.
Deuteronomy 32:41 If I sharpen my shiny sword, and my hand grabs justice, I will give payback to my enemies and repay those who hate me.
Deuteronomy 32:42 I will make my arrows soaked with blood, and my sword will consume flesh; with the blood of those killed and prisoners, from the first acts of revenge against the enemy.
Deuteronomy 32:43 Celebrate, all you nations, with God's people, because He will take revenge for His servants' blood, pay back His enemies, and be kind to His land and His people.
Deuteronomy 32:44 Moses came and told all the words of this song to the people, along with Hoshea, Nun's son.
Deuteronomy 32:45 Moses finished telling all these words to all Israel.
Deuteronomy 32:46 He told them, "Pay close attention to everything I am telling you today. Teach your children to follow all the instructions of this law."
Deuteronomy 32:47 This is not useless for you; it is your life. By doing this, you will live longer in the land you will own after crossing the Jordan.
Deuteronomy 32:48 On that same day, God spoke to Moses, saying,
Deuteronomy 32:49 Go up to Mount Abarim, to Mount Nebo in Moab, across from Jericho; and look at the land of Canaan, which I am giving to the Israelites as their own.
Deuteronomy 32:50 You will die on the mountain you are climbing and join your ancestors, just like your brother Aaron died on Mount Hor and joined his ancestors.
Deuteronomy 32:51 Because you disobeyed me among the Israelites at Meribah-Kadesh, in the Zin desert; because you did not honor me in front of the Israelites.
Deuteronomy 32:52 You will see the land in front of you, but you will not enter the land I am giving to the children of Israel.
