Deuteronomy 8:1 Follow all the commands I give you today so you can live, grow, and take over the land God promised to your ancestors.
Deuteronomy 8:2 Remember how God guided you through the wilderness for forty years, to humble and test you, and to see if you would follow His commands.
Deuteronomy 8:3 God made you humble, let you feel hunger, and gave you manna to eat, a food you and your ancestors didn't know. This was to teach you that people don't live by bread alone, but by everything God says.
Deuteronomy 8:4 Your clothes did not wear out and your feet did not swell during these forty years.
Deuteronomy 8:5 You should also think to yourself that just as a father disciplines his child, so God disciplines you.
Deuteronomy 8:6 So you must follow God's commands, live as He wants, and respect Him.
Deuteronomy 8:7 God is bringing you into a good land with streams, springs, and deep waters that flow in the valleys and hills.
Deuteronomy 8:8 A land with wheat, barley, grapevines, fig trees, and pomegranates; a land with olive oil and honey.
Deuteronomy 8:9 A place where you will eat bread without shortage, you will not lack anything; a place with iron stones and hills where you can dig out copper.
Deuteronomy 8:10 When you have eaten and are full, then you should thank God for the good land he has given you.
Deuteronomy 8:11 Be careful not to forget God by not following his commands, rules, and laws that I give you today.
Deuteronomy 8:12 Be careful not to forget God when you have eaten your fill, built nice homes, and live in them.
Deuteronomy 8:13 When your herds and flocks grow, and your silver and gold increase, and everything you own increases;
Deuteronomy 8:14 Then your heart may become proud, and you forget God who brought you out of Egypt, out of slavery.
Deuteronomy 8:15 Who guided you through the vast and scary desert with poisonous snakes and scorpions, and where there was no water; who made water come out of the hard rock for you;
Deuteronomy 8:16 God fed you in the desert with manna, unknown to your ancestors, to humble you and test you, to ultimately benefit you in the end.
Deuteronomy 8:17 And you think in your heart, My strength and the power of my hand have earned me this wealth.
Deuteronomy 8:18 Remember God, because He gives you the strength to become wealthy, so that He can keep the promise He made to your ancestors, just as it is today.
Deuteronomy 8:19 If you forget God, follow other gods, serve and worship them, I warn you today that you will certainly be destroyed.
Deuteronomy 8:20 Just like the countries God destroys before you, you too will die if you don't listen to God's voice.
