Deuteronomy 2:1 We turned and traveled through the desert toward the Red Sea, as God told me to do, and we circled Mount Seir for many days.
Deuteronomy 2:2 God spoke to me, saying,
Deuteronomy 2:3 You have traveled around this mountain long enough: now go north.
Deuteronomy 2:4 Tell the people: You will travel through the land of your relatives, the children of Esau, who live in Seir. They will fear you, so be careful.
Deuteronomy 2:5 Do not interfere with them; for I will not give you any of their land, not even enough to step on; because I have given Mount Seir to Esau as his own.
Deuteronomy 2:6 You should buy food from them with money, so you can eat; and you should also buy water from them with money, so you can drink.
Deuteronomy 2:7 God has blessed you in all you do; he has watched over you as you traveled through this vast desert. For forty years, God has been with you; you have not been without anything.
Deuteronomy 2:8 When we went past our relatives, Esau's descendants, who lived in Seir, taking the plain's road from Elath and Eziongaber, we changed direction and traveled through the Moab desert.
Deuteronomy 2:9 God told me, "Do not bother the Moabites or fight with them, because I will not give you any of their land. I have given Ar to Lot's descendants to own."
Deuteronomy 2:10 Long ago, the Emims lived there, a large, numerous, and tall people, like the Anakims.
Deuteronomy 2:11 They were considered giants like the Anakims; however, the Moabites named them Emims.
Deuteronomy 2:12 The Horims also lived in Seir before; but Esau's descendants took their place after they defeated them and lived there instead; as Israel did in the land they owned, which God gave to them.
Deuteronomy 2:13 I said, "Get up and cross Zered Creek." So we crossed Zered Creek.
Deuteronomy 2:14 The journey from Kadeshbarnea to the Zered stream took us 38 years, until all the fighting men had died, just as God had promised them.
Deuteronomy 2:15 Truly, God's hand was against them, to eliminate them from the group, until they were gone.
Deuteronomy 2:16 So it happened that when all the soldiers had died and were gone from the people,
Deuteronomy 2:17 God spoke to me and said,
Deuteronomy 2:18 Today, you will go through Ar, the land of Moab.
Deuteronomy 2:19 When you approach the land of the Ammonites, do not trouble them or fight with them, for I will not give you any of their land to own. I have given it to Lot's descendants to have as their own.
Deuteronomy 2:20 This land was also known as a home of giants: giants lived there long ago; and the Ammonites named them Zamzummims.
Deuteronomy 2:21 A large, numerous, and tall people, like the Anakim; but God destroyed them, and they took their place and lived there instead.
Deuteronomy 2:22 God did the same to the descendants of Esau who lived in Seir when he eliminated the Horims before them; they took over their land and have lived there to this day.
Deuteronomy 2:23 The Avims who lived in Hazerim, all the way to Azzah, were destroyed by the Caphtorims who came from Caphtor, and then the Caphtorims lived there instead.
Deuteronomy 2:24 Get up, start traveling, and cross the Arnon River. Look, I have placed Sihon the Amorite, king of Heshbon, and his land in your power. Start taking it over and fight him in battle.
Deuteronomy 2:25 Today, I will start making all nations under heaven fear and respect you. They will hear about you, shake with fear, and be very worried because of you.
Deuteronomy 2:26 I sent messengers from the wilderness of Kedemoth to Sihon, the king of Heshbon, with a message of peace, saying,
Deuteronomy 2:27 Let me go through your land: I will stay on the main road and won't turn right or left.
Deuteronomy 2:28 You will sell me food for money so I can eat, and you will give me water for money so I can drink; I will only walk through your land.
Deuteronomy 2:29 Like the descendants of Esau living in Seir and the Moabites in Ar helped me, until I cross the Jordan into the land God is giving us.
Deuteronomy 2:30 But Sihon, the king of Heshbon, wouldn't let us go through his land: for God made his spirit stubborn and his heart tough, so He could give him to you, as you see today.
Deuteronomy 2:31 God said to me, "Look, I have started to give Sihon and his land to you: start to take it over so you can own his land."
Deuteronomy 2:32 Sihon and all his people came out to fight against us at Jahaz.
Deuteronomy 2:33 God gave us victory over him; we defeated him, his sons, and all his people.
Deuteronomy 2:34 We captured all his cities then, completely destroyed everyone—men, women, and children—in every city, leaving no survivors.
Deuteronomy 2:35 We only took the animals and the things from the cities we captured for ourselves.
Deuteronomy 2:36 From Aroer, next to the edge of the Arnon River, including the city near the river, all the way to Gilead, no city was too strong for us; God gave all of them to us.
Deuteronomy 2:37 You didn't go into the land of the Ammon people, the areas around the Jabbok River, the mountain cities, or any place God told us to stay away from.
