Deuteronomy 34:1 Moses went up from the Moab plains to Mount Nebo, to the top of Pisgah, across from Jericho. God showed him all the land of Gilead, as far as Dan.
Deuteronomy 34:2 All Naphtali, Ephraim's land, Manasseh, and all Judah's land, up to the farthest sea,
Deuteronomy 34:3 To the south, the flat land of Jericho's valley, the city with palm trees, up to Zoar.
Deuteronomy 34:4 God said to him, "This is the land that I promised to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. I said I would give it to your descendants. I have let you see it with your own eyes, but you will not cross over into it."
Deuteronomy 34:5 Moses, God's servant, died in Moab as God said.
Deuteronomy 34:6 He buried him in a valley in Moab, near Bethpeor, but no one knows his grave location to this day.
Deuteronomy 34:7 Moses was 120 years old when he died; his eyesight was still clear, and he was still strong.
Deuteronomy 34:8 The Israelites cried for Moses in the Moab plains for thirty days; after that, the time for crying and grieving for Moses was over.
Deuteronomy 34:9 Joshua, son of Nun, was filled with the spirit of wisdom because Moses had put his hands on him; so, the Israelites listened to him and did what God had told Moses.
Deuteronomy 34:10 No prophet like Moses has since appeared in Israel, someone whom God knew personally.
Deuteronomy 34:11 In all the miracles and amazing things that God told him to do in Egypt, to Pharaoh, his officials, and his whole country,
Deuteronomy 34:12 And with all the power and amazing acts that Moses performed for all Israel to see.
