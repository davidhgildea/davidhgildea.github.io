2 Chronicles 17:1 Jehoshaphat, his son, became king after him and made himself strong against Israel.
2 Chronicles 17:2 He put troops in all the protected cities of Judah, and put guards in Judah's land and in the cities of Ephraim that his father Asa had captured.
2 Chronicles 17:3 God was with Jehoshaphat because he followed the good ways of his ancestor David and did not worship Baalim.
2 Chronicles 17:4 He looked for guidance from his father's God and lived by His rules, not following Israel's actions.
2 Chronicles 17:5 God made his kingdom strong; everyone from Judah gave gifts to Jehoshaphat; and he became very rich and honored.
2 Chronicles 17:6 His heart became proud following God's ways; he also removed the worship sites and sacred trees from Judah.
2 Chronicles 17:7 In the third year of his rule, he sent his leaders, Benhail, Obadiah, Zechariah, Nethaneel, and Michaiah, to teach in the towns of Judah.
2 Chronicles 17:8 He sent Levites with them, including Shemaiah, Nethaniah, Zebadiah, Asahel, Shemiramoth, Jonathan, Adonijah, Tobijah, Tobadonijah, and also Elishama and Jehoram, who were priests.
2 Chronicles 17:9 They taught in Judah with God's law book and traveled to all Judah's cities to teach the people.
2 Chronicles 17:10 The fear of God spread to all the neighboring kingdoms around Judah, so they did not go to war against Jehoshaphat.
2 Chronicles 17:11 Some Philistines gave Jehoshaphat gifts and silver, and the Arabians gave him 7,700 rams and 7,700 male goats.
2 Chronicles 17:12 Jehoshaphat became very powerful; he built strongholds and storage cities in Judah.
2 Chronicles 17:13 He was very busy in the towns of Judah, and the soldiers, strong and brave, were in Jerusalem.
2 Chronicles 17:14 Here are the numbers for each family group: From Judah, the leaders of thousands; Adnah was the top leader, and with him were 300,000 brave soldiers.
2 Chronicles 17:15 Next to him stood Jehohanan the leader, with 280,000 men.
2 Chronicles 17:16 Next to him was Amasiah, son of Zichri, who volunteered to serve God; he was accompanied by two hundred thousand brave warriors.
2 Chronicles 17:17 From the tribe of Benjamin, Eliada, a brave warrior, was there with 200,000 armed men carrying bows and shields.
2 Chronicles 17:18 Next to him was Jehozabad, with him one hundred and eighty thousand men ready for battle.
2 Chronicles 17:19 These served the king, along with those the king placed in the protected cities all over Judah.
