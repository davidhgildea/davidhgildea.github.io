Romans 2:1 So, you can't be excused, person, whoever you are that judges others: because when you judge someone else, you are judging yourself, since you do the same things.
Romans 2:2 We are certain that God's judgment is true and fair against those who do such things.
Romans 2:3 Do you think, man, that if you judge others for doing certain things and then do them yourself, you will avoid God's judgment?
Romans 2:4 Do you look down on the wealth of his kindness, tolerance, and patience, not realizing that God's goodness is meant to lead you to change your ways?
Romans 2:5 By being stubborn and unwilling to change, you are storing up anger for yourself for the day when God will show his fair judgment.
Romans 2:6 God will give to each person according to what they have done.
Romans 2:7 To those who keep on doing good and seek glory, honor, and never-ending life, God will give eternal life.
Romans 2:8 But for those who argue and don't follow the truth, but choose what's wrong, there will be anger and punishment.
Romans 2:9 Hard times and suffering will come to every person who does bad things, starting with the Jews and also to non-Jews.
Romans 2:10 But glory, honor, and peace to everyone who does good, first to the Jew and also to the non-Jew.
Romans 2:11 God does not show favoritism.
Romans 2:12 Everyone who sins without knowing God's law will still die, and those who sin knowing God's law will be judged by it.
Romans 2:13 It's not just those who hear the law who are right with God, but those who follow the law will be considered right.
Romans 2:14 For when people who do not have God's law naturally follow what it says, even without the law, they are their own guide.
Romans 2:15 They show that God's law is written in their hearts, their own conscience agrees, and their thoughts either accuse them or tell them they are right.
Romans 2:16 On the day God judges people's secrets through Jesus Christ, following my good news.
Romans 2:17 Look, you are known as a Jew, rely on the law, and take pride in God.
Romans 2:18 You know God's will, and you choose what is best, because you are taught by the law.
Romans 2:19 You are sure that you can guide the blind and be a light for those in the dark.
Romans 2:20 A teacher of the simple, a trainer of the young, who has the outline of knowledge and truth in the law.
Romans 2:21 So you, who teach others, do you not teach yourself? You who say people should not steal, do you steal?
Romans 2:22 You who say a person should not cheat in marriage, do you cheat? You who hate idols, do you disrespect holy things?
Romans 2:23 You who brag about the law, do you dishonor God by breaking the law?
Romans 2:24 Because of you, people who are not Jews speak badly about God, just like it is written.
Romans 2:25 Being circumcised is good if you follow the law, but if you break the law, your circumcision becomes worthless.
Romans 2:26 So if a person who is not circumcised follows the law's rules, won't that be just like being circumcised?
Romans 2:27 Won't the uncircumcised person by birth, if they follow the law, be able to judge you, who even with the written law and circumcision, break the law?
Romans 2:28 He is not a true Jew if he is only one outwardly, nor is true circumcision something external and physical.
Romans 2:29 A true Jew is one inside; and real circumcision is in the heart, by the spirit, not by written rules. Their praise comes not from people, but from God.
