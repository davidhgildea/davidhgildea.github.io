Romans 3:1 What advantage does the Jew have? Or what benefit comes from circumcision?
Romans 3:2 In many ways, mostly because God's messages were entrusted to them.
Romans 3:3 What if some do not believe? Does their lack of belief cancel out God's faithfulness?
Romans 3:4 Absolutely not! Yes, God is truthful, and all people are liars; as the scripture says, "So that you may be right in your words and win when you are judged."
Romans 3:5 If our wrongs show how right God is, what can we say? Is God wrong when He punishes? (I'm speaking like a human.)
Romans 3:6 Certainly not, because then how would God judge the world?
Romans 3:7 If God's truth has spread more because of my lie to his glory, then why am I still judged as a sinner?
Romans 3:8 And instead of being falsely accused, as some claim we say, "Let's do bad things so that good may happen"? Those who say this deserve to be punished.
Romans 3:9 So what? Are we better than them? Not at all: because we have already shown that both Jews and non-Jews are all guilty of sin.
Romans 3:10 It is written, no one is good, not even one.
Romans 3:11 No one understands; no one looks for God.
Romans 3:12 Everyone has strayed from the right path; they have all become useless; not even one person does good.
Romans 3:13 Their throat is like an open grave; they lie with their tongues; there is snake venom under their lips.
Romans 3:14 Their mouth is full of curses and bitterness.
Romans 3:15 Their feet quickly lead them to harm others.
Romans 3:16 Trouble and suffering are in their paths.
Romans 3:17 They do not know the way of peace.
Romans 3:18 They do not fear God at all.
Romans 3:19 We know that whatever the law says, it speaks to those who follow the law, so that no one can argue and the whole world is accountable to God.
Romans 3:20 No one will be seen as right by God only by following the law's rules, because the law shows us what sin is.
Romans 3:21 Now, God's goodness is shown without the law, and the law and prophets tell us about it.
Romans 3:22 God's righteousness comes through faith in Jesus Christ to all who believe, there is no difference.
Romans 3:23 Everyone has sinned and falls short of God's glory.
Romans 3:24 We are made right with God for free by His kindness because of the rescue that comes through Jesus Christ.
Romans 3:25 God presented him as a sacrifice to forgive sins through faith in his blood, to show his righteousness by forgiving past sins because of God's patience.
Romans 3:26 I am telling now about God's goodness: so He can be fair and make right those who trust in Jesus.
Romans 3:27 Where can we boast, then? It is shut out. Through what rule? The rule of actions? No, but through the rule of trust.
Romans 3:28 We believe that a person is made right with God through faith, not by following the law's actions.
Romans 3:29 Is he only the God of the Jews? Isn't he also the God of the non-Jews? Yes, he is also the God of the non-Jews.
Romans 3:30 Since there is only one God, he will make both the circumcised right by faith and the uncircumcised through faith.
Romans 3:31 By having faith, do we cancel the law? No, we make the law stand.
