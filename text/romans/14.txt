Romans 14:1 Welcome those who are weak in faith, but don't argue over doubtful issues.
Romans 14:2 One person believes they can eat everything, but another, who is weak, eats only vegetables.
Romans 14:3 If someone eats, they shouldn't look down on someone who doesn't, and if someone doesn't eat, they shouldn't judge someone who does; because God has accepted them.
Romans 14:4 Who are you to judge someone else's servant? They answer to their own master, and will either stand or fall. Yes, they will be supported, because God can make them stand.
Romans 14:5 One person values one day more than another; another values all days the same. Each person should be fully convinced in their own mind.
Romans 14:6 Someone who respects the day does so for God; and the one who does not respect the day also does it for God. The one who eats, eats for God and thanks God; and the one who does not eat, abstains for God and thanks God.
Romans 14:7 None of us lives for ourselves alone, and no one dies for themselves alone.
Romans 14:8 Whether we live, we live for God; and whether we die, we die for God. So, whether we live or die, we belong to God.
Romans 14:9 Christ died, came back to life, and revived so he could be the leader of both the dead and the living.
Romans 14:10 Why do you judge your brother? Or why do you think little of your brother? We will all stand before Christ's judgment seat.
Romans 14:11 It is written, "I am alive," says God, "and everyone will kneel to me and every tongue will admit to God."
Romans 14:12 So every one of us will explain our actions to God.
Romans 14:13 Let's not judge each other anymore; instead, decide that no one should make their brother stumble or fall.
Romans 14:14 I understand and am convinced by Jesus that nothing is unclean by itself; but if someone considers something unclean, then for that person, it is unclean.
Romans 14:15 If the food you eat upsets your brother, you're not acting with love. Don't cause harm to him with your food, the one for whom Christ died.
Romans 14:16 Do not let anyone speak badly of your good actions.
Romans 14:17 God's kingdom is not about food and drink; it's about doing right, peace, and happiness in the Holy Spirit.
Romans 14:18 Whoever serves Christ in these things is pleasing to God and approved by people.
Romans 14:19 Let's try to do things that create peace and help build each other up.
Romans 14:20 Do not ruin God's work for the sake of food. Everything is clean, but it is wrong for a person to eat anything that causes someone else to stumble.
Romans 14:21 It is not good to eat meat, drink wine, or do anything that makes your brother stumble, get upset, or become weak.
Romans 14:22 Do you have faith? Keep it between you and God. The person who does not feel guilty for what they approve of is happy.
Romans 14:23 If someone eats but has doubts, they are wrong because their eating is not based on belief; anything not based on belief is wrong.
