2 Corinthians 6:1 We, as partners with God, urge you not to take God's kindness for nothing.
2 Corinthians 6:2 God says, "I have listened to you at the right time, and on the day of rescue, I have helped you. Look, now is the right time; now is the day of rescue."
2 Corinthians 6:3 Not causing trouble in anything, so that no one can criticize our service.
2 Corinthians 6:4 We show we are God's servants in everything by being patient, during troubles, when we need things, and in hard times.
2 Corinthians 6:5 Through beatings, jail time, riots, hard work, sleepless nights, and times without food;
2 Corinthians 6:6 Through purity, understanding, patience, kindness, the Holy Spirit, and genuine love,
2 Corinthians 6:7 By the true message, by God's power, by the right ways to act on both sides,
2 Corinthians 6:8 Through praise and criticism, by bad and good reputation: seen as liars, but truthful.
2 Corinthians 6:9 Although unknown, we are well recognized; although we face death, see, we live; although disciplined, we are not put to death.
2 Corinthians 6:10 Feeling sad but always happy; being poor but making many rich; owning nothing but having everything.
2 Corinthians 6:11 People of Corinth, we speak openly to you; our hearts are full of love for you.
2 Corinthians 6:12 You are not limited by us, but you are limited by your own feelings.
2 Corinthians 6:13 Now, to repay me in the same way, (I'm speaking to you as my children,) you should also open your hearts to us.
2 Corinthians 6:14 Don't team up with those who don't believe: what do good and bad have in common? Or what can light share with darkness?
2 Corinthians 6:15 What agreement does Christ have with Belial? Or what does a believer have in common with an unbeliever?
2 Corinthians 6:16 What deal does God's temple have with idols? You are the living God's temple. God said, I will live in them and be with them; I will be their God, and they will be my people.
2 Corinthians 6:17 So leave them and be different, says God, and do not touch dirty things; then I will accept you.
2 Corinthians 6:18 I will be a Father to you, and you will be my sons and daughters, says God Almighty.
