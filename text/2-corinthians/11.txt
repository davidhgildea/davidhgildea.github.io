2 Corinthians 11:1 I wish you could put up with my foolishness just a bit; please do bear with me.
2 Corinthians 11:2 I am very protective of you with a pure jealousy: because I have promised you to one husband, so I can present you as a pure virgin to Christ.
2 Corinthians 11:3 I am worried that, just as the snake tricked Eve with its cleverness, your thoughts might be led away from the easy and pure understanding of Christ.
2 Corinthians 11:4 If someone comes to you and preaches a different Jesus, one we didn't teach about, or if you get a different spirit, one you haven't received, or a different good news, one you haven't accepted, you might put up with it.
2 Corinthians 11:5 I believe I was not at all inferior to the top apostles.
2 Corinthians 11:6 Even if I speak simply, it doesn't mean I lack knowledge; we have clearly shown you this in everything.
2 Corinthians 11:7 Did I do wrong by lowering myself so you could be lifted up, because I shared God's message with you for free?
2 Corinthians 11:8 I took support from other churches to serve you.
2 Corinthians 11:9 When I was with you and in need, I did not burden anyone. The friends who came from Macedonia gave me what I needed. I have done everything to not be a burden to you and will continue to do so.
2 Corinthians 11:10 Christ's truth is in me, no one will stop my boasting in Achaia.
2 Corinthians 11:11 Why? Is it because I don't love you? God knows.
2 Corinthians 11:12 I will keep doing what I am doing to prevent those looking for an opportunity from boasting; they should be seen just as we are.
2 Corinthians 11:13 These people are fake apostles and dishonest workers who pretend to be Christ's apostles.
2 Corinthians 11:14 Do not be surprised; for Satan himself pretends to be an angel of light.
2 Corinthians 11:15 So it's not surprising if his servants also disguise themselves as servants of what is right; their end will match their actions.
2 Corinthians 11:16 I'll say it again, don't think I'm a fool; but if you do, at least accept me as a fool so I can do a little bragging.
2 Corinthians 11:17 What I am saying, I do not say as God would want but rather, I speak foolishly, as if I am confident and proud.
2 Corinthians 11:18 Since many boast about physical achievements, I will boast too.
2 Corinthians 11:19 You put up with fools easily because you think you are wise.
2 Corinthians 11:20 You put up with it when someone enslaves you, when someone eats up your resources, when someone takes from you, when someone boasts above you, or when someone hits you in the face.
2 Corinthians 11:21 I talk about being shamed, like we were weak. But if anyone is confident—though it sounds silly—I am confident too.
2 Corinthians 11:22 Are they Hebrews? I am too. Are they Israelites? I am too. Are they descendants of Abraham? I am too.
2 Corinthians 11:23 Are they serving Christ? (I talk like a foolish person) I serve even more. I've worked harder, been whipped more, jailed more, and faced death often.
2 Corinthians 11:24 I was whipped with 39 lashes by the Jews five times.
2 Corinthians 11:25 I was beaten with rods three times, once I was stoned, three times I was shipwrecked, and I spent a night and a day in the ocean.
2 Corinthians 11:26 Often traveling, facing dangers from floods, robbers, my own people, foreigners, city dangers, wilderness risks, sea threats, and among those pretending to be friends.
2 Corinthians 11:27 Feeling tired and pain, staying awake a lot, being hungry and thirsty, fasting many times, feeling cold and without enough clothes.
2 Corinthians 11:28 Apart from outside matters, I face daily the concern for all the churches.
2 Corinthians 11:29 Who is weak without me feeling weak? Who is upset without me being angry?
2 Corinthians 11:30 If I must boast, I will boast about my weaknesses.
2 Corinthians 11:31 God, the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who is forever blessed, knows I am not lying.
2 Corinthians 11:32 In Damascus, the governor working for King Aretas guarded the city with soldiers because he wanted to arrest me.
2 Corinthians 11:33 Through a window in a basket, I was lowered down the wall and got away from him.
