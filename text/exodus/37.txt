Exodus 37:1 Bezaleel made the ark from acacia wood: it was 2.5 cubits long, 1.5 cubits wide, and 1.5 cubits high.
Exodus 37:2 He covered it with pure gold inside and outside and put a gold border around it.
Exodus 37:3 He made four gold rings for it, placing them at its four corners; two rings on one side and two on the other side.
Exodus 37:4 He made poles from acacia wood and covered them with gold.
Exodus 37:5 He put the poles through the rings on the sides of the chest to carry it.
Exodus 37:6 He made the cover out of pure gold; it was three and a half feet long and two and a quarter feet wide.
Exodus 37:7 He made two gold cherubs from one piece of gold and put them on both ends of the mercy seat.
Exodus 37:8 One angel on one end here, and another angel on the other end there: the angels on both ends were made from the mercy seat.
Exodus 37:9 The cherubim spread their wings up high and covered the mercy seat with their wings, facing each other; their faces were toward the mercy seat.
Exodus 37:10 He made a table of acacia wood: it was two cubits long, one cubit wide, and one and a half cubits high.
Exodus 37:11 He covered it with pure gold and added a gold border around it.
Exodus 37:12 He also made a border about a hand's breadth wide around it, and put a gold rim around the border.
Exodus 37:13 He made four gold rings and attached them to its four corners on the legs.
Exodus 37:14 Next to the edge were the rings, the spots for the poles to carry the table.
Exodus 37:15 He made the poles from acacia wood and covered them with gold to carry the table.
Exodus 37:16 He made the items for the table—his plates, spoons, cups, and lids to cover them—all out of pure gold.
Exodus 37:17 He made the lampstand of pure gold. The lampstand, its base and branches, its cups, buds, and blossoms, were all one piece.
Exodus 37:18 The lampstand has six branches coming from its sides; three branches come out from one side, and three branches come out from the other side.
Exodus 37:19 Each of the six branches has three cups shaped like almond flowers with buds and petals.
Exodus 37:20 The candlestick had four cups shaped like almonds, with buds and blossoms.
Exodus 37:21 Place a knob under two branches, and do the same for each pair of the six branches coming from the lampstand.
Exodus 37:22 Their buds and branches were all one piece; it was all made of pure gold that had been hammered into shape.
Exodus 37:23 He made seven lamps, lamp trimmers, and trays out of pure gold.
Exodus 37:24 He made it of a talent of pure gold, along with all its utensils.
Exodus 37:25 He made the altar for burning incense out of acacia wood. It was one cubit long and one cubit wide, square-shaped, and two cubits high with horns made from the same wood.
Exodus 37:26 He covered it with pure gold, the top, the sides all around, and its horns; he also made a gold border around it.
Exodus 37:27 He made two gold rings below its molding, at its two corners, on both sides, to hold the poles for carrying it.
Exodus 37:28 He made the poles from acacia wood and covered them with gold.
Exodus 37:29 He made the sacred oil for anointing and the pure, sweet-smelling incense, as a perfumer would.
