Exodus 26:1 Make the tabernacle with ten curtains of fine linen, blue, purple, and red, and with skilled work of cherubim on them.
Exodus 26:2 One curtain should be twenty-eight cubits long and four cubits wide, and all the curtains should be the same size.
Exodus 26:3 The five curtains must be joined together, and the other five curtains must also be joined together.
Exodus 26:4 Make blue loops along the edge of one curtain at the side where it connects, and do the same on the far edge of the other curtain where it connects to the second one.
Exodus 26:5 Make fifty loops on one curtain and fifty loops on the edge of the second curtain so they can fasten together.
Exodus 26:6 Make fifty gold clasps, and join the curtains together with the clasps to form one tent.
Exodus 26:7 Make eleven curtains out of goat hair for the tent's covering.
Exodus 26:8 One curtain should be thirty units long and four units wide, and all eleven curtains should be the same size.
Exodus 26:9 You will connect five curtains on their own, and six curtains on their own, and fold the sixth curtain double at the front of the tent.
Exodus 26:10 Make fifty loops on the edge of the outer curtain in the set, and fifty loops on the edge of the second curtain that connects to it.
Exodus 26:11 Make fifty brass clasps, put them in the loops, and fasten the tent together so it becomes one piece.
Exodus 26:12 The leftover part of the tent curtains, the half-piece that's left, should cover the back of the dwelling.
Exodus 26:13 Add one foot of cloth on each side of the tent's remaining length so it hangs over the sides of the dwelling place to cover it.
Exodus 26:14 Make a red-dyed ram skin cover for the tent, and put a badger skin cover over it.
Exodus 26:15 Make upright wooden boards for the tent from acacia wood.
Exodus 26:16 Each board must be ten cubits long and one and a half cubits wide.
Exodus 26:17 Each board must have two pegs, lined up with each other; do this for all the boards of the shelter.
Exodus 26:18 Make twenty boards for the tabernacle on the south side facing south.
Exodus 26:19 Make forty silver bases for the twenty planks; put two bases under each plank for its two projections.
Exodus 26:20 For the north side of the tent, there will be twenty boards.
Exodus 26:21 They had forty silver bases; each board was supported by two bases.
Exodus 26:22 Make six boards for the west side of the tent.
Exodus 26:23 Make two frames for the corners of the shelter on both sides.
Exodus 26:24 They should be joined together at the bottom and joined at the top to one ring. This is how both will be for the two corners.
Exodus 26:25 There will be eight boards with their bases of silver, sixteen bases in total; two bases under each board.
Exodus 26:26 Make bars from acacia wood; five for the boards on one side of the tent.
Exodus 26:27 Use five bars for one side of the tent, and five bars for the other side of the tent, on the tent's west side.
Exodus 26:28 The center bar inside the panels will stretch from one end to the other.
Exodus 26:29 Cover the boards with gold and make gold rings to hold the bars, then cover the bars with gold too.
Exodus 26:30 Set up the tent as you were shown on the mountain.
Exodus 26:31 Make a curtain of blue, purple, and red, and finely twisted linen with skilled work, decorated with cherubs.
Exodus 26:32 Hang the curtain on four wooden posts covered with gold, with gold hooks, and set them in four silver bases.
Exodus 26:33 Hang the curtain under the clasps, so you can place the ark of the covenant behind the curtain. The curtain will separate the holy place from the most holy place.
Exodus 26:34 Place the mercy seat on top of the ark of the covenant inside the most holy room.
Exodus 26:35 Place the table outside the curtain, and put the lampstand across from the table on the south side of the tent. Place the table on the north side.
Exodus 26:36 Make a curtain for the tent door using blue, purple, red, and good quality linen, decorated with embroidery.
Exodus 26:37 Make five wooden posts and cover them with gold. Their hooks will be gold, and make five bronze bases for them.
