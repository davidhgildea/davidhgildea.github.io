Exodus 36:1 Bezaleel, Aholiab, and every skilled person whom God gave wisdom and understanding knew how to do all kinds of work for the sanctuary service, just as God had instructed.
Exodus 36:2 Moses called Bezaleel, Aholiab, and every skilled person whom God had given wisdom, and everyone who felt moved to come and do the work.
Exodus 36:3 They took from Moses all the gifts the Israelites had brought for the sanctuary's service work. And every morning, they also brought voluntary gifts to him.
Exodus 36:4 All the skilled people who did the work for the holy place stopped their own tasks and came.
Exodus 36:5 They said to Moses, "The people are giving more than needed for the work that God ordered us to do."
Exodus 36:6 Moses ordered and they announced in the camp, "No man or woman should make anything more for the sanctuary offering." So the people stopped bringing things.
Exodus 36:7 They had enough materials for all the work, and more than needed.
Exodus 36:8 Every skilled worker among them who made the tabernacle created ten curtains from fine linen, blue, purple, and red fabric, with artistic designs of cherubs woven into them.
Exodus 36:9 One curtain was twenty-eight cubits long and four cubits wide, and all the curtains were the same size.
Exodus 36:10 He joined five curtains together and then joined the other five curtains in the same way.
Exodus 36:11 He made blue loops on the edge of one curtain by the seam, and did the same on the edge of the other curtain where it joined the second.
Exodus 36:12 He made fifty loops on one curtain, and fifty loops on the edge of the second curtain to join them together. The loops connected one curtain to the other.
Exodus 36:13 He made fifty gold clasps and used them to attach the curtains together, so they became one tent.
Exodus 36:14 He made eleven goat hair curtains to cover the tent that was over the sanctuary.
Exodus 36:15 Each curtain was thirty units long and four units wide; all eleven curtains were the same size.
Exodus 36:16 He joined five curtains together, and six curtains together.
Exodus 36:17 He made fifty loops on the very end of one curtain for joining, and fifty loops on the edge of the second curtain to connect them.
Exodus 36:18 He made fifty bronze clasps to connect the tent so it would be one piece.
Exodus 36:19 He made a red-dyed ram skin cover for the tent and another cover of badger skins on top.
Exodus 36:20 He made upright boards for the tabernacle out of acacia wood.
Exodus 36:21 A board was ten units long and one and a half units wide.
Exodus 36:22 Each board had two pegs set evenly apart: he did this for all the tabernacle's boards.
Exodus 36:23 He made panels for the tent; twenty panels for the south side facing south.
Exodus 36:24 He made forty silver bases for the twenty planks; each plank had two bases for its two projections.
Exodus 36:25 For the other side of the tent, facing north, he made twenty boards.
Exodus 36:26 They had forty silver bases; two bases under one plank, and two bases under another plank.
Exodus 36:27 He made six boards for the west side of the tabernacle.
Exodus 36:28 He made two boards for the corners of the tent on both sides.
Exodus 36:29 They were joined at the bottom and connected at the top to one ring. This was done for both of them at the two corners.
Exodus 36:30 There were eight boards; each had two silver sockets underneath, making sixteen sockets in total.
Exodus 36:31 He made poles of acacia wood; five for the panels on one side of the tent.
Exodus 36:32 Make five crossbars for the frames on the other side of the tent and five crossbars for the frames at the west end.
Exodus 36:33 He made the center bar to go through the boards from one end to the other.
Exodus 36:34 He covered the boards with gold, made their rings of gold for the bars, and covered the bars with gold.
Exodus 36:35 He made a curtain of blue, purple, and red, and quality linen, skillfully woven with angel designs.
Exodus 36:36 He made four pillars of acacia wood for it, covered them with gold, with gold hooks, and made four silver bases for them.
Exodus 36:37 He made a curtain for the entrance of the tent with blue, purple, and red colors, and with finely twisted linen, that was sewn.
Exodus 36:38 It had five pillars with hooks, and he covered the tops and bands with gold; the five bases were bronze.
