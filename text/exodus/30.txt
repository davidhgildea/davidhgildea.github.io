Exodus 30:1 Make an altar for burning incense from acacia wood.
Exodus 30:2 The length will be one cubit and the width one cubit; it will be square. The height will be two cubits, and the horns will be part of it.
Exodus 30:3 Cover it with pure gold—the top, sides, and horns—and make a gold border around it.
Exodus 30:4 Make two gold rings for it below its molding on opposite sides to hold the poles used to carry it.
Exodus 30:5 Make the poles from acacia wood and cover them with gold.
Exodus 30:6 Place it in front of the curtain near the Ark of the Agreement, before the cover above the Agreement, where I will meet with you.
Exodus 30:7 Aaron must burn sweet-smelling incense there every morning: when he prepares the lamps, he must burn incense on it.
Exodus 30:8 When Aaron lights the lamps in the evening, he must burn incense on it, a continuous offering of incense before God for all time to come.
Exodus 30:9 Do not burn any other incense, make burnt offerings, or grain offerings on it, nor pour a drink offering on it.
Exodus 30:10 Aaron must make amends on its horns once a year using the blood from the sacrifice for sins. Every year he must do this for all your future generations. It is very holy to God.
Exodus 30:11 God spoke to Moses and said,
Exodus 30:12 When you count the people of Israel, each one must pay God to save their life, so that no disease comes when you count them.
Exodus 30:13 Everyone counted must give this: half a shekel based on the sanctuary's shekel (which is twenty gerahs). Half a shekel is the donation to God.
Exodus 30:14 Everyone who is counted, age twenty and older, must give an offering to God.
Exodus 30:15 Rich people should not give more, and poor people should not give less than half a shekel when they give an offering to God to atone for your souls.
Exodus 30:16 Take the atonement money from the Israelites and use it for the work of the meeting tent; it will remind the Israelites of God, who makes amends for your lives.
Exodus 30:17 God spoke to Moses and said,
Exodus 30:18 Make a basin of bronze with a bronze stand for washing. Place it between the tent of meeting and the altar, and fill it with water.
Exodus 30:19 Aaron and his sons must wash their hands and feet there.
Exodus 30:20 When they enter the meeting tent, they must wash with water so they will not die; or when they approach the altar to serve, to burn a fire offering to God.
Exodus 30:21 They must wash their hands and feet so they won't die. This rule will last forever for them, their children, and for all future generations.
Exodus 30:22 God spoke to Moses, saying,
Exodus 30:23 Get the best spices for yourself: 500 shekels of pure myrrh, half as much sweet cinnamon, which is 250 shekels, and 250 shekels of sweet calamus.
Exodus 30:24 Five hundred shekels of cassia, by the holy shekel, and an hin of olive oil.
Exodus 30:25 Make a holy oil, a mixed ointment like a perfume maker's craft; it will be a sacred anointing oil.
Exodus 30:26 You will use the oil to anoint the tent where people meet and the chest that holds the laws.
Exodus 30:27 The table and all its dishes, the lampstand and its tools, and the altar for burning incense,
Exodus 30:28 The altar for burnt offerings with all its tools, and the washbasin with its base.
Exodus 30:29 Make these things holy, so they will be very holy; whatever touches them will become holy.
Exodus 30:30 You will anoint Aaron and his sons, prepare them, so they can serve me as priests.
Exodus 30:31 Tell the people of Israel: This oil will be special for anointing, to be used for me by all your future generations.
Exodus 30:32 Do not pour it on human skin, and do not make anything similar to it. It is special, and it must be kept special for you.
Exodus 30:33 Anyone who makes something similar, or who puts some on a non-Israelite, must be separated from their community.
Exodus 30:34 God told Moses, "Take sweet spices, stacte, onycha, and galbanum; mix these sweet spices with pure frankincense, using equal weights of each."
Exodus 30:35 Make it a scented mixture, crafted by a perfumer, mixed well, clean and sacred.
Exodus 30:36 Grind some of it into fine powder and place it in front of the covenant box in the tent of meeting, where I will come to you. It shall be very holy for you.
Exodus 30:37 Make the perfume as I have instructed you. Do not make any for yourselves using this recipe. It is special and only for God.
Exodus 30:38 Anyone who makes perfume like that to smell it will be separated from their people.
