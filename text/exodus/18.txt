Exodus 18:1 When Jethro, the religious leader of Midian and Moses' father-in-law, heard about everything God had done for Moses and the people of Israel, and how God had led Israel out of Egypt;
Exodus 18:2 Jethro, Moses' father-in-law, brought back Zipporah, Moses' wife, after she had been sent away.
Exodus 18:3 She had two sons. The first was named Gershom, because he said, "I have been a foreigner in another country."
Exodus 18:4 The other's name was Eliezer, for he said, "My father's God was my helper and saved me from Pharaoh's sword."
Exodus 18:5 Jethro, Moses' father-in-law, came with his sons and wife to Moses in the desert, where he camped at God's mountain.
Exodus 18:6 He said to Moses, "I, your father-in-law Jethro, have come to you with your wife and her two sons."
Exodus 18:7 Moses went out to meet his father-in-law, bowed down, and kissed him; they asked about each other's well-being and then went into the tent.
Exodus 18:8 Moses told his father-in-law everything that God had done to Pharaoh and the Egyptians for Israel, all their troubles on the journey, and how God saved them.
Exodus 18:9 Jethro was happy about all the good things God did for Israel, saving them from the Egyptians.
Exodus 18:10 Jethro said, "Praise God, who has saved you from the Egyptians and Pharaoh, and has rescued the people from the Egyptians' control."
Exodus 18:11 Now I understand that God is greater than all gods, because where they acted with arrogance, He was superior.
Exodus 18:12 Jethro, Moses' father-in-law, brought a burnt offering and sacrifices to God. Aaron and all the leaders of Israel came to eat with him in God's presence.
Exodus 18:13 The next day, Moses took his place to make decisions for the people, and they stood around Moses from morning to evening.
Exodus 18:14 When Moses' father-in-law saw everything he was doing for the people, he asked, "What are you doing to the people? Why do you sit alone while everyone waits around you from morning to evening?"
Exodus 18:15 Moses told his father-in-law, "The people come to me to ask about God."
Exodus 18:16 When they have an issue, they come to me; and I decide between them, and I teach them God's rules and laws.
Exodus 18:17 Moses' father-in-law said to him, "What you are doing is not good."
Exodus 18:18 You will definitely become exhausted, both you and these people with you, because this task is too much for you; you can't do it all by yourself.
Exodus 18:19 Listen to me, I will advise you, and God will be with you: You be the people's representative before God, and bring their disputes to God.
Exodus 18:20 You should teach them the rules and laws, and show them the path they must follow, and the work they must do.
Exodus 18:21 Choose from all the people capable men who respect God, honest men who dislike greed, and appoint them as leaders over groups of one thousand, one hundred, fifty, and ten.
Exodus 18:22 Let them decide for the people at all times. They will handle big issues with you, but they will handle small ones themselves. This will make things easier for you, and they will share the work with you.
Exodus 18:23 If you do this and if God tells you to, then you will be able to keep going, and all these people will go home peacefully.
Exodus 18:24 Moses listened to his father-in-law and did everything he said.
Exodus 18:25 Moses picked skilled men from all Israel and appointed them as leaders of the people: chiefs over thousands, hundreds, fifties, and tens.
Exodus 18:26 They judged the people at all times: they took difficult cases to Moses, but they decided every small issue themselves.
Exodus 18:27 Moses allowed his father-in-law to leave, and he returned to his own country.
