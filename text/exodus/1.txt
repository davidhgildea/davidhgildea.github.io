Exodus 1:1 Here are the names of the people of Israel who went to Egypt; each one with his family joined Jacob.
Exodus 1:2 Reuben, Simeon, Levi, and Judah,
Exodus 1:3 Issachar, Zebulun, and Benjamin.
Exodus 1:4 Dan, Naphtali, Gad, and Asher.
Exodus 1:5 Everyone born from Jacob totaled seventy people; Joseph was already in Egypt.
Exodus 1:6 Joseph and all his brothers and everyone from that generation died.
Exodus 1:7 The Israelites grew many, became very strong and filled the land.
Exodus 1:8 A new king came to power in Egypt who did not know about Joseph.
Exodus 1:9 He said to his people, "Look, the Israelites are becoming too numerous and powerful for us."
Exodus 1:10 Let's be smart in handling them so they don't increase in number. If there's a war, they might join our enemies, fight us, and then leave this land.
Exodus 1:11 So they put bosses over them to make them suffer with hard work. And they built storage cities for Pharaoh, Pithom and Raamses.
Exodus 1:12 The more they hurt them, the more they increased and became strong. And they were upset because of the children of Israel.
Exodus 1:13 The Egyptians forced the Israelites to work very hard.
Exodus 1:14 They made their lives very hard with tough work, using mortar, bricks, and all sorts of field work; every task they forced them to do was done harshly.
Exodus 1:15 The king of Egypt talked to the Hebrew midwives, named Shiphrah and Puah.
Exodus 1:16 He said, "When you help the Hebrew women give birth and see them on the birthing stools; if it's a boy, you must kill him, but if it's a girl, she can live."
Exodus 1:17 The midwives respected God and did not follow the Egyptian king's orders; instead, they kept the baby boys alive.
Exodus 1:18 The king of Egypt called the midwives and asked them, "Why have you done this and let the boy babies live?"
Exodus 1:19 The midwives told the king, "Hebrew women are not like Egyptian women; they are strong and give birth before the midwives arrive."
Exodus 1:20 So God was good to the midwives, and the people grew and became very strong.
Exodus 1:21 Because the midwives respected God, he gave them homes.
Exodus 1:22 Pharaoh ordered all his people, "Throw every newborn boy into the river, but let every girl live."
