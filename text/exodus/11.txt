Exodus 11:1 God told Moses, "I will send one more plague on Pharaoh and Egypt. After that, he will let you leave this place. When he lets you go, he will really force you all out."
Exodus 11:2 Tell the people to ask their neighbors, both men and women, for silver and gold jewelry.
Exodus 11:3 God made the Egyptians like the people. Also, Moses was very important in Egypt to Pharaoh's officials and the people.
Exodus 11:4 Moses said, "God says, 'Around midnight, I will go through Egypt.'"
Exodus 11:5 Every first child in Egypt will die, from the Pharaoh's first child on his throne to the servant girl's first child working at the mill; and every first child of the animals too.
Exodus 11:6 A terrible scream will spread across Egypt like never before and will never be heard again.
Exodus 11:7 No dog will bark at any Israelite, person or animal, so you will see that God treats Israel differently from Egypt.
Exodus 11:8 All your servants will come to me and bow down, saying, 'Leave, with all who follow you.' After that, I'll go. And he left Pharaoh, very angry.
Exodus 11:9 God told Moses, Pharaoh won't listen to you; this is so I can do many amazing things in Egypt.
Exodus 11:10 Moses and Aaron did all these miracles in front of Pharaoh, but God made Pharaoh's heart stubborn, so he wouldn't let the Israelites leave his country.
