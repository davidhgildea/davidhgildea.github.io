Exodus 20:1 God said all these words:
Exodus 20:2 I am God, your God, who brought you out from Egypt, from the place where you were slaves.
Exodus 20:3 You should not worship any other gods but me.
Exodus 20:4 Do not make for yourself any carved idol, or any picture of anything in the sky above, or on the earth below, or in the water under the earth.
Exodus 20:5 Do not bow down to them or serve them; for I, your God, am a jealous God, punishing the children for the sin of the parents to the third and fourth generation of those who hate me.
Exodus 20:6 I show kindness to thousands who love me and follow my rules.
Exodus 20:7 Do not use God's name for no good reason; God will not let anyone who misuses his name go unpunished.
Exodus 20:8 Remember the day of rest, to keep it special.
Exodus 20:9 Work for six days and do all your tasks.
Exodus 20:10 The seventh day is a rest day for God. On that day, you or your son, daughter, servants, animals, or any guest in your home, should not do any work.
Exodus 20:11 In six days, God created the sky, the earth, the sea, and everything in them, and rested on the seventh day; therefore, God blessed the Sabbath day and made it holy.
Exodus 20:12 Respect your father and your mother so you may live a long time in the land God gives you.
Exodus 20:13 Do not kill.
Exodus 20:14 Do not cheat on your spouse.
Exodus 20:15 Do not steal.
Exodus 20:16 Do not tell lies about others.
Exodus 20:17 Do not want your neighbor's house, do not want your neighbor's wife, their worker, their helper, their ox, their donkey, or anything that belongs to your neighbor.
Exodus 20:18 Everyone saw the thunder, the lightning, the sound of the trumpet, and the mountain smoking; when they saw this, they moved back and stood far away.
Exodus 20:19 They said to Moses, "You talk to us, and we will listen, but don't let God talk to us, or we might die."
Exodus 20:20 Moses told the people, "Don't be afraid. God has come to test you, so that you will respect him and not sin."
Exodus 20:21 The people stayed far away, while Moses went close to the deep darkness where God was.
Exodus 20:22 God told Moses, "Tell the people of Israel, 'You have seen that I have spoken to you from heaven.'"
Exodus 20:23 Do not make silver gods to be with me, and do not make gold gods for yourselves.
Exodus 20:24 Make an earth altar for me and sacrifice your burnt offerings and peace offerings, your sheep and cattle on it. Wherever I make my name known, I'll come to you and bless you.
Exodus 20:25 If you make me an altar of stone, do not use cut stones: if you use tools on it, you make it unclean.
Exodus 20:26 Do not climb steps to my altar, so your nakedness is not exposed.
