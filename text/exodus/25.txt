Exodus 25:1 God spoke to Moses and said,
Exodus 25:2 Tell the people of Israel to give me an offering. You must accept the offering from anyone who gives it willingly from their heart.
Exodus 25:3 Give this as your offering: gold, silver, and bronze.
Exodus 25:4 And blue, purple, red, fine cloth, and goat hair.
Exodus 25:5 Red-dyed ram skins, badger skins, and acacia wood.
Exodus 25:6 Oil for the lamp, spices for the anointing oil, and for the sweet smelling incense.
Exodus 25:7 Onyx stones and stones for setting in the ephod and the breastplate.
Exodus 25:8 Let them build a holy place for me, so I can live with them.
Exodus 25:9 Follow exactly the design I show you for the tent and for all the equipment, and make them that way.
Exodus 25:10 Make an ark from acacia wood: it should be 2.5 cubits long, 1.5 cubits wide, and 1.5 cubits high.
Exodus 25:11 Cover it with pure gold inside and out, and put a gold rim around it.
Exodus 25:12 Make four gold rings for it and attach them to its four corners; two rings on one side and two on the other side.
Exodus 25:13 Make poles from acacia wood and cover them with gold.
Exodus 25:14 Put the poles through the rings on the sides of the chest to carry it with them.
Exodus 25:15 The poles must remain in the rings of the chest; they must not be removed.
Exodus 25:16 Put the laws I give you into the chest.
Exodus 25:17 Make a cover of pure gold, two and a half cubits long and one and a half cubits wide.
Exodus 25:18 Make two gold cherubs with hammered work at both ends of the cover.
Exodus 25:19 Make one angel on one end and another angel on the other end; you should make the angels from the same piece as the mercy seat, at its two ends. 

(Note: "Globish" typically refers to a simplified version of English designed to be understood by non-native speakers around the world, so the term "cherub" has been translated to "angel" for clarity.)
Exodus 25:20 The cherubim will spread out their wings above, shielding the mercy seat with their wings, and they will face each other; the cherubim's faces should be toward the mercy seat.
Exodus 25:21 Put the mercy seat on top of the ark, and inside the ark, put the laws I will give you.
Exodus 25:22 There I will meet with you, and I will talk to you from above the mercy seat, between the two cherubs on the ark of the testimony, about everything I command you for the people of Israel.
Exodus 25:23 Also make a table from acacia wood: it should be two cubits long, one cubit wide, and one and a half cubits high.
Exodus 25:24 Cover it with pure gold and add a gold border around it.
Exodus 25:25 Make a border one hand's width all around it, and put a gold rim around the border.
Exodus 25:26 Make four gold rings for it and attach them to its four corners at the four legs.
Exodus 25:27 The rings should be right next to the edge to hold the poles that carry the table.
Exodus 25:28 Make poles from acacia wood and cover them with gold so the table can be carried with them.
Exodus 25:29 Make its plates, spoons, pitchers, and bowls for pouring. Make them all of pure gold.
Exodus 25:30 Put bread on the table in front of me always.
Exodus 25:31 Make a lampstand of pure gold. It should be crafted from hammered work: its base, arms, cups, buds, and blossoms must all be one piece.
Exodus 25:32 Six arms will extend from the sides; three arms of the lampstand from one side, and three arms of the lampstand from the other side.
Exodus 25:33 Each of the six branches coming out of the lampstand had three cups shaped like almond flowers, each with buds and petals.
Exodus 25:34 In the lampstands there will be four cups shaped like almonds, with their buds and blossoms.
Exodus 25:35 There will be a bud below two branches of it, a bud below another two branches, and a bud below two more branches, making six branches coming out of the lampstand.
Exodus 25:36 The buds and branches will be one piece with the lampstand, all made from solid gold.
Exodus 25:37 Make seven lamps for it, and set up the lamps so they shine light in front of it.
Exodus 25:38 Its tongs and snuffdishes must be made of pure gold.
Exodus 25:39 He must make it of a talent of pure gold, including all these utensils.
Exodus 25:40 Make sure you make them just like the pattern you were shown on the mountain.
