Exodus 40:1 God spoke to Moses, saying,
Exodus 40:2 On the first day of the first month, you will set up the meeting tent's tabernacle.
Exodus 40:3 Place the Ark of the Covenant inside, and hang the curtain in front of the Ark.
Exodus 40:4 Bring in the table and arrange the items on it; then bring in the lampstand and light its lamps.
Exodus 40:5 Place the gold altar for the incense in front of the ark of the covenant and hang the curtain at the entrance of the tent.
Exodus 40:6 Place the altar for burnt offerings in front of the entrance to the tent where people meet with God.
Exodus 40:7 Place the basin between the meeting tent and the altar, and fill it with water.
Exodus 40:8 You will set up the courtyard all around and put up the curtain at the courtyard entrance.
Exodus 40:9 You will take the anointing oil, and anoint the tent of worship, and everything in it, and make it holy, and all its containers: and it will be holy.
Exodus 40:10 You will anoint the altar where offerings are burned, along with all its tools, and make the altar holy: it will be a very holy altar.
Exodus 40:11 Pour oil on the basin and its stand, and make it holy.
Exodus 40:12 Bring Aaron and his sons to the entrance of the meeting tent and wash them with water.
Exodus 40:13 Dress Aaron in the holy clothes, anoint him, and make him holy, so he can serve me as a priest.
Exodus 40:14 Bring his sons and dress them in robes.
Exodus 40:15 You will anoint them just as you anointed their father so they can serve me as priests. Their anointing will be for a priesthood that lasts forever, generation after generation.
Exodus 40:16 Moses did everything just as God had commanded him.
Exodus 40:17 In the first month of the second year, on the first day, the tabernacle was set up.
Exodus 40:18 Moses set up the tent, secured its bases, put up its frames, inserted the crossbars, and raised its posts.
Exodus 40:19 He spread out the tent over the holy tent, and placed the tent's covering on top of it, just as God had instructed Moses.
Exodus 40:20 He took the testimony, placed it in the chest, attached the poles to the chest, and set the cover on top of the chest.
Exodus 40:21 He put the ark in the tent and hung the curtain to cover the ark of the covenant, just as God told Moses to do.
Exodus 40:22 He placed the table in the meeting tent, on the north side of the dwelling, outside the curtain.
Exodus 40:23 He arranged the bread on it before God, as God had instructed Moses.
Exodus 40:24 He placed the lampstand in the meeting tent opposite the table, on the south side of the tent.
Exodus 40:25 He lit the lamps before God as God had told Moses to do.
Exodus 40:26 He placed the gold altar in the meeting tent in front of the curtain.
Exodus 40:27 He burned sweet-smelling incense on it, just as God told Moses.
Exodus 40:28 He put up the curtain at the entrance of the tent.
Exodus 40:29 He placed the altar for burnt sacrifices near the entrance of the meeting tent's tabernacle and gave burnt and grain offerings on it, just as God told Moses.
Exodus 40:30 He placed the basin between the meeting tent and the altar and filled it with water for washing.
Exodus 40:31 Moses, Aaron, and his sons washed their hands and feet there.
Exodus 40:32 When they entered the meeting tent, and as they approached the altar, they washed; just as God told Moses.
Exodus 40:33 He built the courtyard around the tent and the altar, and put up the curtain for the courtyard entrance. So Moses completed the task.
Exodus 40:34 A cloud hid the meeting tent, and God's glory filled the dwelling place.
Exodus 40:35 Moses could not go into the meeting tent because the cloud stayed on it, and God's glory filled the holy tent.
Exodus 40:36 When the cloud lifted above the tent, the Israelites moved forward on their travels.
Exodus 40:37 But if the cloud did not rise, then they did not move until it did.
Exodus 40:38 God's cloud was over the tent during the day, and at night, there was fire in all the Israelites' view during their travels.
