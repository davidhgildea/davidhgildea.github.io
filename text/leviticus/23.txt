Leviticus 23:1 God spoke to Moses, saying,
Leviticus 23:2 Tell the people of Israel, tell them about God's festivals that you must announce as sacred gatherings; these are my festivals.
Leviticus 23:3 Work for six days, but the seventh day is a rest day, a special gathering; do no work on that day: it is God's rest day, everywhere you live.
Leviticus 23:4 These are God's special feasts, the sacred meetings you must announce at the right times.
Leviticus 23:5 On the fourteenth day of the first month, in the evening, is God's Passover.
Leviticus 23:6 On the fifteenth day of that month is God's Feast of Unleavened Bread: for seven days you must eat bread without yeast.
Leviticus 23:7 On the first day, you should gather for a special meeting; you must not do any regular work.
Leviticus 23:8 For seven days, you must present a burnt offering to God. On the seventh day, there is a special gathering; you must not do any regular work on that day.
Leviticus 23:9 God spoke to Moses and said,
Leviticus 23:10 Tell the people of Israel, when you enter the land I am giving you and harvest its crops, bring the first bundle of your harvest to the priest.
Leviticus 23:11 He will present the bundle of grain to God for you to be accepted; the next day after the Sabbath, the priest will do this presentation.
Leviticus 23:12 On that day when you present the sheaf, offer a one-year-old male lamb with no defects as a burnt sacrifice to God.
Leviticus 23:13 Its grain offering shall be two-tenths of an ephah of fine flour mixed with oil, burned as a pleasing aroma to God; and its drink offering shall be wine, one-quarter of a hin.
Leviticus 23:14 Do not eat bread, roasted grain, or fresh grain until the same day you bring an offering to God. This rule will last forever for all your descendants wherever you live.
Leviticus 23:15 Count seven full weeks from the day after the Sabbath when you brought the bundle of grain to wave as an offering.
Leviticus 23:16 Count fifty days until the day after the seventh Sabbath; then give a new grain offering to God.
Leviticus 23:17 From your homes, bring two loaves of bread made from two-tenths of an ephah of fine flour, baked with yeast, as an offering to God.
Leviticus 23:18 You must give seven perfect lambs that are one year old, one young cow, and two rams with the bread as a burnt sacrifice to God. Include grain and drink offerings. It will be a fire offering, a pleasing aroma to God.
Leviticus 23:19 You must offer one young goat as a sin offering and two one-year-old lambs as a peace offering.
Leviticus 23:20 The priest will move them and the bread of the first harvest up and down as a special offering in front of God, along with the two lambs. These are holy for God and are for the priest.
Leviticus 23:21 You must announce on that very day, it should be a special gathering for you: do not do any regular work: this rule will last forever where you live, for all your family to follow.
Leviticus 23:22 When you harvest your land, do not completely clear the corners of your field, nor pick up leftovers. Leave them for the poor and the foreigner. I am God your God.
Leviticus 23:23 God spoke to Moses, saying,
Leviticus 23:24 Tell the people of Israel: On the first day of the seventh month, you must have a day of rest, a special time to remember with trumpet blasts, a sacred gathering.
Leviticus 23:25 Do not do any regular work on that day, but you must present a fire offering to God.
Leviticus 23:26 God spoke to Moses, saying,
Leviticus 23:27 On the tenth day of the seventh month, you will have a day to make amends. It will be a special meeting for you. You must show sorrow and give a burnt offering to God.
Leviticus 23:28 Do no work on that day, because it is a day to make up for your sins before God.
Leviticus 23:29 Anyone who does not show their sorrow on that day will be separated from their people.
Leviticus 23:30 If anyone works on that day, I will remove that person from their community.
Leviticus 23:31 You must not do any work; this rule will last forever, for all your future family wherever you live.
Leviticus 23:32 This day will be a day of rest for you, and you should humbly reflect on yourselves. Starting on the evening of the ninth day of the month, from one evening to the next, you will observe your day of rest.
Leviticus 23:33 God spoke to Moses, saying,
Leviticus 23:34 Tell the people of Israel, on the fifteenth day of the seventh month, there will be the Feast of Tabernacles to honor God for seven days.
Leviticus 23:35 On the first day, you must have a special gathering; do not do any regular work on that day.
Leviticus 23:36 For seven days, give a fire offering to God. On the eighth day, gather for a special meeting and give another fire offering to God. It is an important gathering, and you must not do any regular work on that day.
Leviticus 23:37 These are God's special celebrations that you must announce as sacred gatherings, where you present offerings made with fire to God, including burnt offerings, grain offerings, sacrifices, and drink offerings, each on its proper day.
Leviticus 23:38 Apart from God's Sabbaths, your gifts, all your promises, and all the voluntary offerings you give to God.
Leviticus 23:39 On the fifteenth day of the seventh month, after you collect the crops, celebrate a festival for God for seven days. The first day is a rest day, and the eighth day is also a rest day.
Leviticus 23:40 On the first day, grab branches from nice trees, palm trees, thick trees, and willow trees by the stream; and be happy in front of God for seven days.
Leviticus 23:41 You will celebrate a festival for God for seven days each year. This will be a permanent law for all your future generations: you must observe it in the seventh month.
Leviticus 23:42 Live in shelters for seven days; every native-born Israelite must live in shelters.
Leviticus 23:43 I want your future families to know that I had the Israelites live in temporary shelters when I led them out of Egypt. I am God, your God.
Leviticus 23:44 Moses told the Israelites about God's festivals.
