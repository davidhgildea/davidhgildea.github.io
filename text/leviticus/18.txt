Leviticus 18:1 God spoke to Moses, saying,
Leviticus 18:2 Tell the people of Israel, 'I am God, your God.'
Leviticus 18:3 Do not follow the customs of Egypt where you lived, nor the customs of Canaan where I am taking you. Do not follow their rules.
Leviticus 18:4 Follow my rules and keep my laws, and live by them. I am God, your God.
Leviticus 18:5 You must follow my rules and decisions: if a person does this, they will have a good life by them: I am God.
Leviticus 18:6 None of you should come close to a relative to see them naked: I am God.
Leviticus 18:7 Do not see your father's or your mother's naked body; she is your mother, so do not look at her nakedness.
Leviticus 18:8 Do not see your father's wife undressed; she is your father's privacy.
Leviticus 18:9 Do not see the private parts of your sister, either your father's daughter or your mother's daughter, whether she was born in the same house or elsewhere.
Leviticus 18:10 Do not see the private parts of your son's daughter or your daughter's daughter; their privacy is your privacy.
Leviticus 18:11 Do not have sexual relations with your stepsister, the daughter of your father's wife; she is your sister.
Leviticus 18:12 Do not see your father's sister without clothes; she is your father's close relative.
Leviticus 18:13 Do not see your aunt undressed; she is your mother's close relative.
Leviticus 18:14 Do not see your uncle's nakedness or be close to his wife; she is your aunt.
Leviticus 18:15 Do not see your daughter-in-law without clothes; she is your son's wife; do not see her without clothes.
Leviticus 18:16 Do not see your brother's wife without clothes; it is like seeing your brother without clothes.
Leviticus 18:17 Do not see a woman and her daughter or her granddaughter naked; it is wrong because they are close relatives.
Leviticus 18:18 Do not marry your wife's sister while your wife is alive, to cause her trouble by having both of them.
Leviticus 18:19 Also, do not go near a woman to see her without clothes while she is set apart during her monthly period.
Leviticus 18:20 Do not have sex with your neighbor's wife and make yourself impure.
Leviticus 18:21 Do not let any of your children be burned in sacrifice to Molech, and do not dishonor your God's name: I am God.
Leviticus 18:22 Do not have sexual relations with a man as one does with a woman; it is disgusting.
Leviticus 18:23 Do not have sexual relations with any animal; it makes you unclean. A woman must not offer herself to an animal to have sexual relations with it; this is a perversion.
Leviticus 18:24 Do not make yourselves unclean with any of these actions; for by all these things, the nations I am driving out before you have become unclean.
Leviticus 18:25 The land is dirty: so I punish its wrongs, and the land throws out its people.
Leviticus 18:26 You must follow my laws and decisions, and must not do any of these disgusting things—neither anyone from your people nor any foreigner living with you.
Leviticus 18:27 The people who lived in the land before you did all these terrible things, and they made the land unclean.
Leviticus 18:28 So that the land does not vomit you out when you make it dirty, as it vomited out the nations before you.
Leviticus 18:29 Anyone who does any of these disgusting things will be separated from their community.
Leviticus 18:30 So you must follow my rules and not do any of these disgusting things that were done before you, so you do not become unclean by them: I am God, your God.
