Leviticus 24:1 God spoke to Moses, saying,
Leviticus 24:2 Tell the Israelites to bring you pure, crushed olive oil for the lamps so they will keep burning all the time.
Leviticus 24:3 Aaron must keep the lamps burning from evening until morning before God inside the tent of meeting. This is to be a lasting rule for all your descendants.
Leviticus 24:4 He will arrange the lamps on the clean lampstand before God all the time.
Leviticus 24:5 Take good flour and make twelve cakes; each cake should have two-tenths of an ephah of flour.
Leviticus 24:6 Place them in two lines, six in each line, on the clean table before God.
Leviticus 24:7 Put pure frankincense on each row so it will be on the bread as a reminder, an offering to God.
Leviticus 24:8 Every Sabbath, he should arrange it before God always, as it is an eternal promise from the people of Israel.
Leviticus 24:9 Aaron and his sons will own it, and they will eat it in a sacred place. It is very holy to them as part of God's offerings made with fire, a rule to follow always.
Leviticus 24:10 The son of an Israeli woman and an Egyptian man went out among the Israelis, and he and an Israeli man fought in the camp.
Leviticus 24:11 The son of the Israelite woman spoke against God's name and cursed. They took him to Moses; his mother was Shelomith, daughter of Dibri from the Dan tribe.
Leviticus 24:12 They kept him in custody to find out what God wanted.
Leviticus 24:13 God spoke to Moses, saying,
Leviticus 24:14 Take the person who cursed outside the camp; everyone who heard them must touch their head, and then everyone in the assembly must throw stones at them.
Leviticus 24:15 You will tell the people of Israel, if anyone curses their God, they will be guilty of sin.
Leviticus 24:16 Anyone who speaks against God’s name must be put to death. The whole community must stone them. It doesn't matter if they are a foreigner or born in the land; if they speak against God’s name, they must be put to death.
Leviticus 24:17 Whoever kills a person must surely be put to death.
Leviticus 24:18 If someone kills an animal, they must replace it; one animal for another.
Leviticus 24:19 If a person hurts their neighbor, they should be hurt in the same way.
Leviticus 24:20 Injury for injury, eye for eye, tooth for tooth: if someone hurts another, the same hurt will be given to them.
Leviticus 24:21 If someone kills an animal, they must replace it; if someone kills a person, they must be put to death.
Leviticus 24:22 You will have one law, for both the foreigner and the native-born, because I am your God.
Leviticus 24:23 Moses told the Israelites to take the person who cursed outside the camp and throw stones at him. The Israelites did what God told Moses.
