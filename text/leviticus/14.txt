Leviticus 14:1 God spoke to Moses and said,
Leviticus 14:2 This will be the rule for the person with leprosy on the day they are cleansed: They must be taken to the priest.
Leviticus 14:3 The priest will go outside the camp; the priest will look, and if the leprosy sores are healed in the person with leprosy,
Leviticus 14:4 The priest will order to get for the person to be cleaned two live, clean birds, cedar wood, red fabric, and hyssop.
Leviticus 14:5 The priest will order that one bird be killed in a clay pot over flowing water.
Leviticus 14:6 Take the live bird, the cedar wood, the red yarn, and the hyssop, and dip them and the live bird in the blood of the bird that was killed over flowing water.
Leviticus 14:7 He must sprinkle the person to be cleansed from leprosy seven times, declare him clean, and release the live bird into the open field.
Leviticus 14:8 The person who needs to be clean must wash their clothes, shave off all their hair, and bathe in water to become clean. After this, they can return to the camp but must stay outside their tent for seven days.
Leviticus 14:9 On the seventh day, he must shave all the hair from his head, beard, and eyebrows. He must shave off all his hair, wash his clothes, and bathe in water to be clean.
Leviticus 14:10 On the eighth day, he must bring two male lambs with no defects, one female lamb less than a year old with no defects, some fine flour mixed with oil for a grain offering, and some oil.
Leviticus 14:11 The priest who cleans the man will bring him and his offerings to God at the entrance of the meeting tent.
Leviticus 14:12 The priest will take one male lamb and give it as a guilt offering, along with the oil, and lift them up as an offering before God.
Leviticus 14:13 He must kill the lamb where he kills the sin and burnt offerings, in the holy place. Like the sin offering belongs to the priest, so does the guilt offering. It is very holy.
Leviticus 14:14 The priest will take some of the blood from the guilt offering and put it on the top of the right ear, the right thumb, and the right big toe of the person who is being cleansed.
Leviticus 14:15 The priest will take some of the oil and pour it into his own left hand.
Leviticus 14:16 The priest will dip his right finger in the oil in his left hand and sprinkle the oil with his finger seven times in front of God.
Leviticus 14:17 The priest will put the rest of the oil from his hand on the right ear, the right thumb, and the right big toe of the person being cleansed, on top of the blood from the guilt offering.
Leviticus 14:18 The priest will pour the rest of the oil in his hand on the head of the person being cleansed, and will ask God to forgive him.
Leviticus 14:19 The priest will offer the sin sacrifice, and make amends for the one being cleansed from his impurity; then he will sacrifice the burnt offering.
Leviticus 14:20 The priest will offer the burnt and grain offerings on the altar, and the priest will make amends for him, so he will be clean.
Leviticus 14:21 If he is poor and can't afford much, he must bring one lamb as a guilt offering to be waved to ask for forgiveness for him, and a small amount of fine flour mixed with oil for a food offering, plus a small jug of oil.
Leviticus 14:22 Bring two doves or two young pigeons, whichever you can afford. One will be for a sin offering and the other for a burnt offering.
Leviticus 14:23 He must bring them to the priest at the entrance of the meeting tent for his cleansing on the eighth day, before God.
Leviticus 14:24 The priest will take the lamb for the guilt offering and the container of oil, and then wave them as an offering before God.
Leviticus 14:25 He must kill the lamb for the guilt offering. The priest will take some of the lamb's blood and put it on the right ear, right thumb, and right big toe of the person being made clean.
Leviticus 14:26 The priest will pour some oil into his left hand.
Leviticus 14:27 The priest will use his right finger to flick some of the oil in his left hand seven times in front of God.
Leviticus 14:28 The priest will put some of the oil from his hand on the top of the right ear, the right thumb, and the big toe of the right foot of the person being cleansed, where the blood from the guilt offering was put.
Leviticus 14:29 The priest will put the remaining oil on the head of the person who needs to be made clean to atone for him before God.
Leviticus 14:30 He must give either one dove or one young pigeon, whatever he can afford.
Leviticus 14:31 He should bring what he can afford: one for a sin offering and the other for a burnt offering, along with a grain offering. The priest will then make atonement for the person to be cleansed before God.
Leviticus 14:32 This is the rule for someone who has leprosy but cannot afford the things needed for their cleansing.
Leviticus 14:33 God spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying,
Leviticus 14:34 When you enter the land of Canaan, which I am giving to you as your own, and I allow a leprosy disease in a house in your land;
Leviticus 14:35 The homeowner should go and say to the priest, "I think there might be a disease in the house."
Leviticus 14:36 The priest will order the house to be emptied before the priest enters to inspect the disease, so that everything inside won't become unclean; then the priest will go in to examine the house.
Leviticus 14:37 He will examine the disease, and, look, if the disease in the house's walls has green or red hollow streaks that appear deeper than the surface;
Leviticus 14:38 Then the priest will leave the house, go to the door, and close the house for seven days.
Leviticus 14:39 On the seventh day, the priest will return and check the house. If the disease has spread on the walls,
Leviticus 14:40 Then the priest will order to remove the stones with the disease and throw them into a dirty place outside the city.
Leviticus 14:41 He must have the house scraped all around inside, and the scraped-off dust must be dumped outside the city in a dirty place.
Leviticus 14:42 They will get new stones to replace the old ones, and use fresh mortar to cover the house.
Leviticus 14:43 If the disease returns and spreads in the house after the stones are removed, the house is scraped, and it is plastered;
Leviticus 14:44 The priest will come to check, and if the disease has spread in the house, it is a harmful leprosy in the house; it is unclean.
Leviticus 14:45 He will tear down the house, its stones, wood, and all its plaster, and take them out of the city to a dirty place.
Leviticus 14:46 Anyone who enters the house while it is closed up will be unclean until evening.
Leviticus 14:47 Anyone who sleeps in the house must wash their clothes; and anyone who eats in the house must wash their clothes.
Leviticus 14:48 If the priest enters, sees that the disease has not spread after the house was plastered, then he will declare the house clean because the disease is healed.
Leviticus 14:49 He must use two birds, cedar wood, scarlet thread, and hyssop to clean the house.
Leviticus 14:50 He will kill one of the birds over fresh water in a clay pot.
Leviticus 14:51 He must take the cedar wood, hyssop, scarlet cloth, and live bird, dip them into the blood of the dead bird and fresh water, and sprinkle the house seven times.
Leviticus 14:52 He will clean the house with the bird's blood, with flowing water, with the live bird, with cedar wood, with hyssop, and with scarlet fabric.
Leviticus 14:53 He must release the live bird outside the city into the open fields to make up for any wrongs against the house, and then it will be clean.
Leviticus 14:54 This rule is for every kind of leprosy and skin disease.
Leviticus 14:55 For the disease of clothes or a house,
Leviticus 14:56 For swelling, for sores, and for white patches:
Leviticus 14:57 To teach when something is not clean and when it is clean: this is the rule about leprosy.
