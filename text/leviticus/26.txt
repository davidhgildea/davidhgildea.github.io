Leviticus 26:1 Do not make any idols or carved images for yourselves, do not set up any sculpted stone figures in your land to bow down to, because I am God, your God.
Leviticus 26:2 You must keep my rest days and respect my holy place: I am God.
Leviticus 26:3 If you follow my rules and obey my commands, and follow through with them;
Leviticus 26:4 I will send rain at the right time, and the land will produce crops, and the trees will bear fruit.
Leviticus 26:5 Your grain grinding will last until grape picking, and grape picking will last until planting time. You will have plenty of bread to eat and live safely in your land.
Leviticus 26:6 I will bring peace to the land, and you will rest without fear; I will remove dangerous animals, and no war will enter your land.
Leviticus 26:7 You will chase your enemies, and they will fall in front of you by the sword.
Leviticus 26:8 Five of you will chase a hundred, and a hundred of you will chase ten thousand; your enemies will fall by your sword.
Leviticus 26:9 I will care for you, make you fruitful, increase your numbers, and keep my promise to you.
Leviticus 26:10 You will eat from your stored food and make room for the new by using up the old.
Leviticus 26:11 I will put my tent among you, and I will not reject you.
Leviticus 26:12 I will live with you, be your God, and you will be my people.
Leviticus 26:13 I am God, who brought you out of Egypt so you wouldn't be slaves; I broke the chains of your burden, so you could walk freely.
Leviticus 26:14 But if you don't listen to me and don't follow all these commands;
Leviticus 26:15 If you ignore my laws or hate my rules, and so do not follow all my commands, but break my agreement:
Leviticus 26:16 I will also do this to you: I will send terror, wasting sickness, and fever that will blind you and make your hearts sad. You will plant seeds for nothing because your enemies will eat your crops.
Leviticus 26:17 I will be against you, and you will be killed by your enemies. Those who hate you will rule over you, and you will run away even when no one is chasing you.
Leviticus 26:18 If you still don't listen to me after all this, I will punish you seven times more for your sins.
Leviticus 26:19 I will break your power's pride; I will make your sky like iron and your land like brass.
Leviticus 26:20 You will use all your energy for nothing: your land won't produce crops and the trees won't give fruit.
Leviticus 26:21 If you act against me and don't listen to me, I will give you seven times more trouble for your sins.
Leviticus 26:22 I will also send wild animals to attack you, take your children, kill your livestock, and reduce your numbers; your roads will be empty.
Leviticus 26:23 If you don't change your ways because of these things but continue to oppose me;
Leviticus 26:24 Then I will also go against you and punish you seven times more for your sins.
Leviticus 26:25 I will send a sword against you to punish you for breaking my agreement. When you are together in your cities, I will bring disease among you, and you will be given over to your enemies.
Leviticus 26:26 When I make your bread supply scarce, ten women will bake your bread in one oven, and they will give you your bread in measured amounts: you will eat but still be hungry.
Leviticus 26:27 If you still don't listen to me and choose to go against my ways;
Leviticus 26:28 Then I will act against you in anger; and I will punish you seven times for your sins.
Leviticus 26:29 You will eat the flesh of your sons, and you will eat the flesh of your daughters.
Leviticus 26:30 I will destroy your worship places, cut down your statues, throw your dead bodies on your idols' dead bodies, and I will hate you.
Leviticus 26:31 I will turn your cities into ruins, destroy your holy places, and I will not enjoy the scent of your offerings.
Leviticus 26:32 I will make the land empty, and your enemies living there will be shocked by it.
Leviticus 26:33 I will spread you among the nations, and I will chase you with a sword: and your land will be empty, and your cities ruined.
Leviticus 26:34 The land will have its rest days while it's empty, and you are in your enemies' land; the land will rest and have its rest days.
Leviticus 26:35 As long as it is empty, it will rest; because it didn't rest during your Sabbaths when you lived on it.
Leviticus 26:36 I will make those of you who survive feel weak in heart in their enemies' lands; even the sound of a blowing leaf will scare them. They will run away as if running from a sword and they will fall, even though no one is chasing them.
Leviticus 26:37 They will stumble over each other as if fleeing from a sword, even when no one is chasing them; and you will be unable to resist your enemies.
Leviticus 26:38 You will die in foreign lands, and your enemies' land will consume you.
Leviticus 26:39 Those of you who survive will waste away in your enemies' lands because of your sins and your ancestors' sins.
Leviticus 26:40 If they admit their wrongs, and their ancestors' wrongs, and their betrayal against me, and that they have also acted against me;
Leviticus 26:41 I have opposed them and led them into their enemies' land. If their stubborn hearts are humble and they accept their punishment,
Leviticus 26:42 Then I will remember my promise to Jacob, to Isaac, and to Abraham, and I will remember the land.
Leviticus 26:43 The land will be abandoned by them and will rest during its sabbaths while it's empty without them; and they will accept their punishment for their sins because they hated my laws and their hearts rejected my rules.
Leviticus 26:44 Even then, when they are in their enemies' land, I won't reject them or hate them enough to completely destroy them and break my promise to them, because I am their God.
Leviticus 26:45 But for their sake, I will remember the agreement with their ancestors, who I freed from Egypt for all to see, so I could be their God: I am God.
Leviticus 26:46 These are the rules, decisions, and laws that God gave to the Israelites at Mount Sinai through Moses.
