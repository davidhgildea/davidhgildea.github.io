1 Corinthians 7:1 About what you wrote to me: It's good for a man not to be with a woman.
1 Corinthians 7:2 To avoid wrong sexual acts, every man should have a wife and every woman should have a husband.
1 Corinthians 7:3 The husband should be kind to his wife, and the wife should be kind to her husband.
1 Corinthians 7:4 A wife does not control her own body, but her husband does; and in the same way, a husband does not control his own body, but his wife does.
1 Corinthians 7:5 Do not cheat each other, unless you both agree to stop having sex for a while to focus on fasting and praying. Then come back together so that Satan won't tempt you because you can't control yourselves.
1 Corinthians 7:6 I say this as a suggestion, not as an order.
1 Corinthians 7:7 I wish that all people were just as I am. But each person has their own special gift from God, one in this way, and another in that way.
1 Corinthians 7:8 I tell unmarried people and widows that it's good for them to stay single, like I am.
1 Corinthians 7:9 If they can't control themselves, they should get married, because it's better to marry than to have strong desires.
1 Corinthians 7:10 To the married, I give this order (not me, but God): A wife should not leave her husband.
1 Corinthians 7:11 If she leaves, she should stay single or get back with her husband; and the husband shouldn't divorce his wife.
1 Corinthians 7:12 I say this, not God: If a man has a wife who does not believe, but she agrees to live with him, he should not leave her.
1 Corinthians 7:13 If a woman has a husband who does not believe, but he wants to live with her, she should not leave him.
1 Corinthians 7:14 The husband who does not believe is made holy through his wife, and the wife who does not believe is made holy through her husband; otherwise your children would be impure, but now they are holy.
1 Corinthians 7:15 If someone does not believe and wants to leave, let them leave. A brother or sister is not bound in such situations: God wants us to live in peace.
1 Corinthians 7:16 Wife, how do you know if you can save your husband? Or husband, how do you know if you can save your wife?
1 Corinthians 7:17 But as God has given to each person, as God has called each one, let them live that way. This is what I instruct in all churches.
1 Corinthians 7:18 If a man is already circumcised when he is called, he should not try to reverse it. If he is uncircumcised when called, he should not get circumcised.
1 Corinthians 7:19 Circumcision doesn't matter, and not being circumcised doesn't matter, but obeying God's commands does matter.
1 Corinthians 7:20 Everyone should remain in the life situation they were in when God called them.
1 Corinthians 7:21 Are you called while a servant? Don't worry about it: but if you can become free, choose that instead.
1 Corinthians 7:22 When someone serves and follows God, they become free, and a person who is free but follows Christ becomes His servant.
1 Corinthians 7:23 You were bought at a price; do not become slaves to people.
1 Corinthians 7:24 Friends, stay as you are when God calls you.
1 Corinthians 7:25 About unmarried women, I don't have a direct order from God, but I offer my opinion as someone who God has shown kindness to be trustworthy.
1 Corinthians 7:26 I believe that, given our current troubles, it is good for a person to stay as they are.
1 Corinthians 7:27 Are you married? Don't try to get a divorce. Are you divorced? Don't look for a wife.
1 Corinthians 7:28 If you get married, you haven't sinned; and if an unmarried woman gets married, she hasn't sinned. However, they will have problems in life. But I want to spare you this.
1 Corinthians 7:29 I tell you, friends, time is running out. It's important that those with wives should live as if they had none.
1 Corinthians 7:30 People who cry should act as if they're not crying; those who are happy should act as if they're not happy; and those who buy things should act as if they don't own them.
1 Corinthians 7:31 People should use this world without misusing it, because this world's way will disappear.
1 Corinthians 7:32 I want you to be free from worries. A single person cares about how to please God.
1 Corinthians 7:33 But a married person cares about worldly things, how to make their spouse happy.
1 Corinthians 7:34 A wife and a virgin are different. The single woman cares about God's work, to be pure in body and spirit, but the married woman cares about worldly things, to please her husband.
1 Corinthians 7:35 I say this for your benefit, not to trap you, but to promote what is proper, and so you can focus on God without distraction.
1 Corinthians 7:36 If a man believes he is acting improperly towards his fiancée, if she is past her prime age and it is necessary, he can do as he chooses, he does not sin: they should get married.
1 Corinthians 7:37 The person who is firm in their decision, not forced but in control of their desire, and decides to stay unmarried, does well.
1 Corinthians 7:38 So the person who helps her get married does good, but the person who does not help her get married does better.
1 Corinthians 7:39 The wife must follow the law while her husband is alive; but if her husband dies, she is free to marry anyone she wants, as long as it is with a believer.
1 Corinthians 7:40 But she is happier if she stays as she is, in my opinion, and I believe I have God's Spirit.
