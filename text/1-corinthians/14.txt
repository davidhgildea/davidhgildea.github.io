1 Corinthians 14:1 Chase after love and want spiritual gifts, especially to prophesy.
1 Corinthians 14:2 When someone speaks in a language no one knows, they are not speaking to people but to God, because no one can understand them. However, they are saying secret things in their spirit.
1 Corinthians 14:3 The person who prophesies speaks to people to build them up, encourage them, and give them comfort.
1 Corinthians 14:4 Anyone who speaks in a language not known improves himself; but one who prophesies builds up the church.
1 Corinthians 14:5 I wish you could all speak in different languages, but it's better to share God's messages. The one sharing God's messages is more helpful than one speaking in languages, unless they explain it, so the church can learn and grow.
1 Corinthians 14:6 Now, friends, if I come to you speaking in different languages, how will it help you unless I bring you some revelation, knowledge, prophecy, or teaching?
1 Corinthians 14:7 Even lifeless things that make noise, like pipes or harps, need to make different sounds. If they don't, how can you tell what is being played?
1 Corinthians 14:8 If the trumpet makes a confusing sound, who will get ready for the fight?
1 Corinthians 14:9 In the same way, if you don't speak clear words with your tongue, how will anyone know what you're saying? Otherwise, you're just talking to the air.
1 Corinthians 14:10 In the world, there might be many different sounds, and each one has its own meaning.
1 Corinthians 14:11 So if I don't understand what someone is saying, I am like a foreigner to the speaker, and the speaker is like a foreigner to me.
1 Corinthians 14:12 In the same way, since you are eager for spiritual gifts, try to be the best at building up the church.
1 Corinthians 14:13 So, if someone speaks in a language others don't know, they should pray for the ability to explain it.
1 Corinthians 14:14 If I pray in a language I don't know, my spirit prays but I can't understand it.
1 Corinthians 14:15 So what should be done? I will pray with my spirit, but I will also pray with my mind. I will sing with my spirit, but I will also sing with my mind.
1 Corinthians 14:16 If you give thanks with your spirit, how can someone who doesn't know what you're saying join in and say "Amen" to your prayer of thanks?
1 Corinthians 14:17 You truly give thanks well, but the other person is not helped.
1 Corinthians 14:18 I thank God, I speak in different languages more than all of you.
1 Corinthians 14:19 In church, I prefer to say five words that I understand to teach others, rather than ten thousand words in a language nobody knows.
1 Corinthians 14:20 Friends, don't be childish in understanding. Yet in bad intentions be like children, but in understanding be adults.
1 Corinthians 14:21 The law says, "I will speak to these people through people with different languages and lips, but even then, they will not listen to me," says God.
1 Corinthians 14:22 So, speaking in different languages is a sign for unbelievers, not believers, while prophesying is for believers, not unbelievers.
1 Corinthians 14:23 So if the whole church meets in one place and everyone starts speaking in different languages, and people who don't understand or don't believe come in, won't they think you are crazy?
1 Corinthians 14:24 If everyone prophesies and an unbeliever or someone who doesn't know about this comes in, they are convinced by everyone and judged by everyone.
1 Corinthians 14:25 The secrets of his heart become clear; he falls down on his face to worship God, saying truly God is with you.
1 Corinthians 14:26 So, friends, when you meet, each of you brings a song, a teaching, a language, a revelation, or an explanation. Make sure that all of these build up the group.
1 Corinthians 14:27 If someone speaks in a language not known, they should do so two or three at a time, taking turns; and there should be one person to explain it.
1 Corinthians 14:28 If there is no interpreter, he should remain quiet in the church; he can speak to himself and to God.
1 Corinthians 14:29 Let two or three prophets speak, and let the others evaluate.
1 Corinthians 14:30 If something is shown to another person sitting nearby, the first person should stop talking.
1 Corinthians 14:31 You can all give messages from God one at a time, so everyone can learn and be encouraged.
1 Corinthians 14:32 The prophets' spirits are under the prophets' control.
1 Corinthians 14:33 God does not create confusion, but peace, in all the churches of the holy people.
1 Corinthians 14:34 Women should keep quiet in churches: they are not allowed to speak, but must be submissive, as the law also says.
1 Corinthians 14:35 If they want to learn something, they should ask their husbands at home, because it's shameful for women to talk in the church.
1 Corinthians 14:36 Did God's message originate with you? Or was it given to only you?
1 Corinthians 14:37 If someone believes they are a prophet or spiritual, they should accept that what I am writing to you are God's commands.
1 Corinthians 14:38 If someone does not know, let them not know.
1 Corinthians 14:39 Friends, want to prophesy and do not stop others from speaking in different tongues.
1 Corinthians 14:40 Do everything properly and in an organized way.
