1 Corinthians 12:1 About spiritual gifts, friends, I don't want you to be unaware.
1 Corinthians 12:2 You know that before, as non-Jews, you followed silent idols wherever you were taken.
1 Corinthians 12:3 I want you to know that no one led by God's Spirit says Jesus is cursed, and no one can say Jesus is Lord without the Holy Spirit.
1 Corinthians 12:4 There are different kinds of gifts, but the same Spirit.
1 Corinthians 12:5 There are different ways to serve, but the same God.
1 Corinthians 12:6 There are different ways of working, but it is the same God who does all the work in everyone.
1 Corinthians 12:7 The Spirit shows itself in each person for the common good.
1 Corinthians 12:8 One person receives wisdom from the Spirit; another gets knowledge from the same Spirit.
1 Corinthians 12:9 To one person the Spirit gives strong faith; to another person the same Spirit gives the power to heal.
1 Corinthians 12:10 One is given the power to perform miracles; another can prophesy; another can distinguish between spirits; another can speak in different languages; and yet another can interpret those languages.
1 Corinthians 12:11 But the same Spirit does all these things, giving to each person different gifts as he chooses.
1 Corinthians 12:12 Just as a body is one unit but has many parts, and all these parts make up one body, it is the same with Christ.
1 Corinthians 12:13 Through one Spirit, we were all baptized into one body, no matter if we are Jews or non-Jews, slaves or free; and we were all given the same Spirit to drink.
1 Corinthians 12:14 The body is not just one part, but many parts.
1 Corinthians 12:15 If the foot says, "Since I am not the hand, I don't belong to the body," does that mean it's not part of the body?
1 Corinthians 12:16 If the ear says, "Because I'm not the eye, I don't belong to the body," does that mean it's not part of the body?
1 Corinthians 12:17 If the whole body were an eye, how could we hear? If it were all ear, how could we smell?
1 Corinthians 12:18 God has placed each part of the body as he wanted.
1 Corinthians 12:19 If they were all one part, where would the body be?
1 Corinthians 12:20 Now there are many parts, but only one body.
1 Corinthians 12:21 The eye can't tell the hand, "I don't need you," and the head can't tell the feet, "I don't need you."
1 Corinthians 12:22 No, even those parts of the body that seem weaker are important.
1 Corinthians 12:23 We give more honor to the parts of the body we think are less worthy, and our less attractive parts get more attention to look better.
1 Corinthians 12:24 Our attractive parts don't need anything, but God has put the body together, giving extra honor to the parts that were lacking.
1 Corinthians 12:25 The body should have no divide; but its parts should care equally for each other.
1 Corinthians 12:26 If one part hurts, all parts hurt with it; if one part is honored, all parts are happy with it.
1 Corinthians 12:27 You are now the body of Christ, each one of you a part of it.
1 Corinthians 12:28 God has placed in the church first apostles, then prophets, next teachers, then those who do miracles, followed by those with healing gifts, helpers, leaders, and different kinds of language speakers.
1 Corinthians 12:29 Are all messengers? Are all prophets? Are all teachers? Does everyone perform miracles?
1 Corinthians 12:30 Does everyone have healing gifts? Does everyone speak in different languages? Does everyone explain what is said?
1 Corinthians 12:31 Desire greatly the best gifts; but I will show you an even better way.
