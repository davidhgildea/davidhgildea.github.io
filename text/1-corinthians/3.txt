1 Corinthians 3:1 Brothers and sisters, I could not talk to you as spiritual people but as worldly, as if to babies in Christ.
1 Corinthians 3:2 I have given you milk, not solid food, because until now you were not ready for it, and even now you cannot handle it.
1 Corinthians 3:3 You are still worldly because you have jealousy, arguments, and divisions among you. Doesn't this show that you are worldly and behave like ordinary people?
1 Corinthians 3:4 When one of you says, "I follow Paul," and another, "I follow Apollos," aren't you acting like mere humans?
1 Corinthians 3:5 Who then is Paul, and who is Apollos, but servants through whom you believed, as God gave to each person?
1 Corinthians 3:6 I planted, Apollos watered, but God made it grow.
1 Corinthians 3:7 So the one who plants or the one who waters is not important, but God who makes things grow is.
1 Corinthians 3:8 The person who plants and the person who waters are working together, and each will get a reward based on their own work.
1 Corinthians 3:9 We work together with God: you are God's field, you are God's construction.
1 Corinthians 3:10 God has given me grace, so like a skilled architect, I laid a foundation for others to build on. But everyone should be careful about how they build on it.
1 Corinthians 3:11 No one can lay any other foundation than the one already laid, which is Jesus Christ.
1 Corinthians 3:12 If anyone builds on this foundation with gold, silver, precious stones, wood, hay, or straw,
1 Corinthians 3:13 Everyone's work will become clear: the day will show it because fire will reveal it; and the fire will test what kind of work each person has done.
1 Corinthians 3:14 If someone's work lasts which they have built on it, they will get a reward.
1 Corinthians 3:15 If someone's work is burned up, they will lose it: but they themselves will be saved, but only as if through fire.
1 Corinthians 3:16 Don't you know that you are God's temple and that God's Spirit lives in you?
1 Corinthians 3:17 If anyone ruins God's temple, God will ruin that person; because God's temple is sacred, and that temple is you.
1 Corinthians 3:18 Do not trick yourself. If someone thinks they are wise by this world's standards, they should become like they know nothing, so they can truly become wise.
1 Corinthians 3:19 The wisdom of this world is silly to God. It is said, He catches the clever in their tricks.
1 Corinthians 3:20 God knows the thoughts of the smart people, that they are useless.
1 Corinthians 3:21 So don't boast about following people. Everything belongs to you.
1 Corinthians 3:22 Whether Paul, Apollos, Cephas, the world, life, death, things now, or things in the future; all belong to you.
1 Corinthians 3:23 You belong to Christ; and Christ belongs to God.
