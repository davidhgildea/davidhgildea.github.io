1 Corinthians 11:1 Follow me, just as I follow Christ.
1 Corinthians 11:2 I am happy, friends, that you remember me in everything and follow the teachings just as I gave them to you.
1 Corinthians 11:3 I want you to understand that Christ leads every man, the man leads the woman, and God leads Christ.
1 Corinthians 11:4 Every man who prays or speaks God's words with his head covered shames his head.
1 Corinthians 11:5 Every woman who prays or speaks God's messages with her head not covered dishonors her head; it's just like if she had her head shaved.
1 Corinthians 11:6 If a woman does not wear a covering, she should cut her hair. But if it is shameful for a woman to have short hair or be shaved, she should cover her head.
1 Corinthians 11:7 A man should not cover his head because he is made in God's image and reflects God's glory. But a woman reflects a man's glory.
1 Corinthians 11:8 The man does not come from the woman, but the woman comes from the man.
1 Corinthians 11:9 The man was not created for the woman, but the woman for the man.
1 Corinthians 11:10 For this reason, a woman should have a symbol of authority on her head, because of the angels.
1 Corinthians 11:11 Still, neither is man complete without woman, nor woman without man, in God.
1 Corinthians 11:12 Just as a woman comes from a man, so does a man come through a woman; but everything comes from God.
1 Corinthians 11:13 Think for yourselves: is it proper for a woman to pray to God with her head uncovered?
1 Corinthians 11:14 Doesn't nature itself show you that if a man has long hair, it is a disgrace to him?
1 Corinthians 11:15 If a woman has long hair, it is her pride, because her hair is given to her as a covering.
1 Corinthians 11:16 If anyone likes to argue, we don't have that habit, and neither do God's churches.
1 Corinthians 11:17 In telling you this, I do not praise you, because when you meet, it's not for the good but for the bad.
1 Corinthians 11:18 First, when you meet as a church, I've heard there are splits among you, and I believe it somewhat.
1 Corinthians 11:19 There must also be false beliefs among you, so that those who are true can be clearly seen.
1 Corinthians 11:20 When you meet together in one place, it is not to eat God's supper.
1 Corinthians 11:21 When they eat, some people go first and take their own meal, leaving others hungry while some have too much to drink.
1 Corinthians 11:22 Do you not have homes to eat and drink in? Or do you look down on God's church and embarrass those who have nothing? What should I say to you? Should I commend you for this? I do not commend you.
1 Corinthians 11:23 I got from God what I also passed on to you: on the night Jesus was betrayed, he took bread.
1 Corinthians 11:24 After thanking God, he broke the bread and said, "Take and eat; this is my body given for you. Do this to remember me."
1 Corinthians 11:25 In the same way, he took the cup after supper and said, "This cup is the new agreement in my blood; do this whenever you drink it, to remember me."
1 Corinthians 11:26 Every time you eat this bread and drink this cup, you show the death of God until he comes.
1 Corinthians 11:27 So anyone who eats this bread and drinks this cup of God in a wrong way will be guilty of sinning against God's body and blood.
1 Corinthians 11:28 A person should check themselves before they eat the bread and drink from the cup.
1 Corinthians 11:29 Anyone who eats and drinks without respecting the body of God eats and drinks judgment on themselves.
1 Corinthians 11:30 Because of this, many of you are weak and ill, and a number have died.
1 Corinthians 11:31 If we judge ourselves, we won't be judged by others.
1 Corinthians 11:32 When God corrects us, it's so we won't be punished along with the world.
1 Corinthians 11:33 So, my friends, when you gather to eat, wait for each other.
1 Corinthians 11:34 If anyone is hungry, they should eat at home so that when you meet, it doesn't lead to judgment. I will take care of the other matters when I arrive.
