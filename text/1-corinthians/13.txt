1 Corinthians 13:1 If I speak in human and angel languages but don't have love, I am only a loud horn or a clanging cymbal.
1 Corinthians 13:2 Even if I can prophesy, understand all secrets, know everything, and have enough faith to move mountains, but don't have love, I am nothing.
1 Corinthians 13:3 Even if I give away everything I own to help the poor, and even if I sacrifice my body, but do not have love, it does me no good.
1 Corinthians 13:4 Love is patient and kind; love does not envy; love does not boast and is not proud.
1 Corinthians 13:5 Doesn't act improperly, doesn't seek its own benefit, isn't easily angered, doesn't think of doing wrong.
1 Corinthians 13:6 Does not delight in wrong, but celebrates the truth.
1 Corinthians 13:7 Bears everything, believes everything, hopes for everything, endures everything.
1 Corinthians 13:8 Love never ends. But if there are prophecies, they will stop; if there are languages, they will end; if there is knowledge, it will go away.
1 Corinthians 13:9 We know some things and we tell about the future as much as we know.
1 Corinthians 13:10 When the perfect thing comes, the imperfect things will disappear.
1 Corinthians 13:11 When I was a child, I talked like a child, I thought like a child, I reasoned like a child. But when I grew up, I stopped doing childish things.
1 Corinthians 13:12 Now we see unclearly, as in a mirror; but then we will see clearly, face to face. Now I know only a little; but then I will fully understand, just as I am fully understood.
1 Corinthians 13:13 Now faith, hope, and love remain, these three; but the most important of these is love.
