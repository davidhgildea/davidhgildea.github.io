Joel 3:1 For, look, in those days and at that time, when I bring back the people of Judah and Jerusalem,
Joel 3:2 I will gather all nations and take them to the Valley of Jehoshaphat. There, I will argue for my people and my land, Israel, because they have been scattered among the nations and my land has been divided.
Joel 3:3 They have drawn lots for my people, traded a boy for a prostitute, and sold a girl for wine to drink.
Joel 3:4 Yes, and what do you have to do with me, O Tyre, Zidon, and all the coasts of Palestine? Will you pay me back? If you pay me back, I will quickly return your payment on your own head.
Joel 3:5 Because you took my silver and gold, and brought my precious things into your temples:
Joel 3:6 You have sold the people of Judah and Jerusalem to the Greeks, so you could send them far from their land.
Joel 3:7 I will lift them from the place where you sold them and bring the consequences back on you.
Joel 3:8 I will sell your sons and daughters to the people of Judah, and they will sell them to the Sabeans, a distant nation, because God has said so.
Joel 3:9 Tell this to the nations: Get ready for battle, wake up the strong fighters, let all the soldiers come close; let them move forward.
Joel 3:10 Turn your farming tools into swords and your cutting tools into spears; let those who feel weak say, I am strong.
Joel 3:11 Come together and join, all you nations, and gather around: call your strong ones to come down, God.
Joel 3:12 Let the nations wake up and come to the Valley of Jehoshaphat; for there I will sit to judge all the surrounding nations.
Joel 3:13 Use the sickle, the crops are ready: come, gather them; the vat is full, it's overflowing because there is so much evil.
Joel 3:14 Many people, many people in the valley of choice: for God's day is close in the valley of choice.
Joel 3:15 The sun and the moon will become dark, and the stars will stop shining.
Joel 3:16 God will roar from Zion and shout from Jerusalem; the sky and land will tremble. But God will be a safe place for his people, and a stronghold for the people of Israel.
Joel 3:17 You will know that I am God living in Zion, my holy mountain. Then Jerusalem will be a holy city, and no strangers will pass through it anymore.
Joel 3:18 On that day, the mountains will drip with sweet wine, the hills will overflow with milk, all of Judah's streams will run with water, and a spring will come out of God's house and water the valley of Shittim.
Joel 3:19 Egypt will become ruined, and Edom will turn into a barren wasteland because of the harm done to the people of Judah, since they have spilled the blood of the innocent in their country.
Joel 3:20 Judah will live forever, and Jerusalem will last from one generation to the next.
Joel 3:21 I will purify their blood that I haven't purified before, because God lives in Zion.
