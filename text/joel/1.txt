Joel 1:1 God's message that came to Joel, Pethuel's son.
Joel 1:2 Listen, old men, and all who live in the land, pay attention. Has anything like this happened in your time or in your ancestors' time?
Joel 1:3 Tell your children about it, let them tell their children, and those children tell the next generation.
Joel 1:4 What the palmerworm did not eat, the locust did; and what the locust did not eat, the cankerworm did; and what the cankerworm did not eat, the caterpillar did.
Joel 1:5 Wake up, you who drink too much, and cry; scream, all of you wine lovers, because the fresh wine is taken away from your lips.
Joel 1:6 A nation has invaded my land, powerful and countless, with teeth like a lion's, and with the fangs of a mighty lion.
Joel 1:7 He has destroyed my vine and stripped my fig tree. He has completely bared it and thrown it aside; its branches are now white.
Joel 1:8 Mourn like a young woman wrapped in rough cloth for the husband of her young days.
Joel 1:9 The food offering and the drink offering are stopped at God's house; the priests, God's servants, are sad.
Joel 1:10 The crops are gone, the earth is sad; the grain is ruined, the new wine is gone, the oil is failing.
Joel 1:11 Feel ashamed, you farmers; cry out, you vine growers, because the wheat and barley are ruined; the field crops are destroyed.
Joel 1:12 The grapevine has dried up, and the fig tree is weak; the pomegranate tree, the palm tree, and the apple tree, all the trees in the fields have dried up because happiness has faded away from people.
Joel 1:13 Get ready and cry, priests; wail, servants of the altar; come, spend the night in mourning, servants of my God, because the grain and drink offerings are no longer brought to your God's temple.
Joel 1:14 Set a special time to fast, call a serious meeting, bring together the leaders and everyone who lives in the land to God's house, and pray to God.
Joel 1:15 Oh no, the day is coming! It's the day of God, and it will bring destruction like a disaster from the powerful one.
Joel 1:16 Isn't the food taken away from us, along with joy and happiness from God's house?
Joel 1:17 The seeds have rotted under the dirt, the storehouses are empty, the barns are ruined; because the grain has dried up.
Joel 1:18 The animals cry out in pain! The herds of cows are confused because there is no grass to eat; even the sheep are left with nothing.
Joel 1:19 God, I will call out to you: because the fire has eaten up the wild land's grass, and the flame has burned every tree in the fields.
Joel 1:20 The wild animals cry out to you because the water streams are dry, and fire has burned the wild grasslands.
