2 Timothy 2:1 You, my child, be strong in the grace that is in Jesus Christ.
2 Timothy 2:2 The teachings you have heard from me, in front of many witnesses, pass on to trustworthy people who can also teach others.
2 Timothy 2:3 So, be strong like a good soldier of Jesus Christ.
2 Timothy 2:4 A person fighting in a war does not get involved with everyday life activities, so they can please the one who chose them to be a soldier.
2 Timothy 2:5 If someone competes in contests, they are not awarded a crown unless they compete according to the rules.
2 Timothy 2:6 The farmer who works hard should be the first to enjoy the fruits of his work.
2 Timothy 2:7 Think about what I say, and may God help you understand everything.
2 Timothy 2:8 Remember that Jesus Christ, who came from David's family, was brought back to life as told in my good news.
2 Timothy 2:9 I face hardship as if I were a criminal, even to the point of being chained; yet God's message is not chained.
2 Timothy 2:10 I put up with everything for the chosen people's sake, so they can also find the salvation in Christ Jesus that comes with eternal glory.
2 Timothy 2:11 This saying is true: If we died with him, we will also live with him.
2 Timothy 2:12 If we go through hard times, we will also rule with him; if we reject him, he will also reject us.
2 Timothy 2:13 If we don't believe, he remains faithful; he cannot contradict himself.
2 Timothy 2:14 Remind them of these things and tell them in God's presence not to argue about words. It doesn't help anyone and ruins those who listen.
2 Timothy 2:15 Make an effort to be acceptable to God, a worker with no need to be ashamed, properly understanding and teaching the true message.
2 Timothy 2:16 Stay away from disrespectful and pointless talk; it leads to more wrong behavior.
2 Timothy 2:17 Their teaching will spread like a disease. It includes Hymenaeus and Philetus.
2 Timothy 2:18 They are wrong about the truth, claiming the resurrection has already happened, and they destroy the faith of some.
2 Timothy 2:19 God's foundation stands firm, with this mark: God knows who belongs to Him. And anyone who calls on Christ's name should turn away from wrongdoing.
2 Timothy 2:20 In a big house, there are not just containers of gold and silver, but also of wood and clay; some are for special use, and some for everyday use.
2 Timothy 2:21 If a person cleans himself from these things, he will be a vessel for honor, made holy, and ready for the owner's use, prepared for every good action.
2 Timothy 2:22 Run away from young people's strong desires. But chase after living right, trusting, being kind, and peace with those who ask God for help with a clean heart.
2 Timothy 2:23 Stay away from silly and ignorant questions, because they lead to arguments.
2 Timothy 2:24 God's servant should not argue, but be kind to everyone, able to teach, and patient.
2 Timothy 2:25 Gently teach those who disagree with themselves; if perhaps God will grant them a change of heart to recognize the truth.
2 Timothy 2:26 So they can escape the devil's trap, who has captured them to do his will.
