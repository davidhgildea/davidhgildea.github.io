2 Timothy 3:1 Understand that in the last days, dangerous times will come.
2 Timothy 3:2 People will love themselves, desire too much, brag, be arrogant, disrespect God, not obey parents, not be thankful, and not be respectful.
2 Timothy 3:3 Unloving, promise-breakers, liars, without self-control, violent, haters of the good,
2 Timothy 3:4 People who betray others, act without thinking, are full of themselves, and love fun more than they love God.
2 Timothy 3:5 They act religious but reject its true power. Stay away from these people.
2 Timothy 3:6 These people sneak into homes and take control of weak-willed women burdened with sins and swayed by various desires.
2 Timothy 3:7 Always learning, but never able to understand the truth.
2 Timothy 3:8 Just as Jannes and Jambres opposed Moses, so these people also reject the truth: they are corrupt thinkers and fail to accept the faith.
2 Timothy 3:9 They won't get any further: everyone will see their foolishness, just as others' was seen.
2 Timothy 3:10 You fully know my teaching, way of life, goal, faith, endurance, love, and patience.
2 Timothy 3:11 Hard times and troubles that happened to me in Antioch, Iconium, and Lystra, the tough situations I went through: but God rescued me from them all.
2 Timothy 3:12 Everyone who wants to live a good life in Christ Jesus will face troubles.
2 Timothy 3:13 Bad people and tricksters will become more and more wicked, cheating others and being cheated themselves.
2 Timothy 3:14 Keep following the teachings you have learned and are sure of, knowing who taught them to you.
2 Timothy 3:15 From childhood, you have known the sacred writings that can teach you to be saved through faith in Christ Jesus.
2 Timothy 3:16 All scripture comes from God's inspiration and is useful for teaching, for showing mistakes, for improving, and for training in right living.
2 Timothy 3:17 So the person who serves God can be complete, fully equipped for every good action.
