Revelation 6:1 I saw the Lamb open a seal, and I heard what sounded like thunder. One of the four creatures said, "Come and see."
Revelation 6:2 I saw a white horse, and its rider had a bow. He was given a crown and rode out to win many battles and gain victory.
Revelation 6:3 When he opened the second seal, I heard the second creature say, "Come and see."
Revelation 6:4 Another red horse came out, and its rider was given the power to remove peace from the earth and make people kill each other. He was also given a large sword.
Revelation 6:5 When he opened the third seal, I heard the third creature say, "Come and see." I looked, and there was a black horse, and the rider held weighing scales.
Revelation 6:6 I heard a voice among the four creatures say, "A measure of wheat for a coin, and three measures of barley for a coin; but do not damage the oil and the wine."
Revelation 6:7 When he opened the fourth seal, I heard the fourth creature say, "Come and see."
Revelation 6:8 I looked and saw a pale horse. The rider's name was Death, and Hell came with him. They were given power over a quarter of the world, to kill with the sword, hunger, death, and wild animals.
Revelation 6:9 When he opened the fifth seal, I saw under the altar the souls of those who had been killed because of God's message and their witness.
Revelation 6:10 They shouted loudly, "How long, God, holy and true, until you judge and punish those on the earth for our blood?"
Revelation 6:11 White clothes were given to each of them; they were told to rest for a little while longer until the number of their fellow servants and brothers who were going to be killed like them was complete.
Revelation 6:12 I saw when he opened the sixth seal, there was a huge earthquake; the sun turned black like rough cloth, and the moon turned red like blood.
Revelation 6:13 The stars from the sky fell to the earth, like a fig tree drops its early figs when a strong wind shakes it.
Revelation 6:14 The sky split like a rolled-up scroll, and all the mountains and islands shifted from their spots.
Revelation 6:15 The world's leaders, important people, wealthy individuals, top officers, powerful individuals, and all slaves and free people hid in caves and among the rocks of the mountains.
Revelation 6:16 They said to the mountains and rocks, "Fall on us and hide us from the one on the throne and from the Lamb's anger."
Revelation 6:17 The great day of God's anger has come; who can stand against it?
