Song of Solomon 3:1 At night in my bed, I searched for the one my soul loves: I searched for him, but did not find him.
Song of Solomon 3:2 I will get up now and walk through the city, in the streets and squares, looking for the one my heart loves. I looked for him but did not find him.
Song of Solomon 3:3 The guards walking through the city found me. I asked them, "Have you seen the one I love deeply?"
Song of Solomon 3:4 I had just left them when I found the one my heart loves. I held him tightly and wouldn't let go until I brought him to my mother's home, to the room of the one who gave birth to me.
Song of Solomon 3:5 I ask you, women of Jerusalem, by the deer and the wild does, do not disturb or wake my love until he wants to.
Song of Solomon 3:6 Who is this coming from the desert like smoke columns, scented with myrrh and incense, with all the spice seller's powders?
Song of Solomon 3:7 Look at his bed, which belongs to Solomon; sixty brave men surround it, the brave ones of Israel.
Song of Solomon 3:8 They all carry swords, skilled in fighting: each man has his sword on his leg because of fear at night.
Song of Solomon 3:9 King Solomon built himself a carriage from the wood of Lebanon.
Song of Solomon 3:10 He made its pillars of silver, its base of gold, its cover of purple, and its middle was decorated with love, for the women of Jerusalem.
Song of Solomon 3:11 Go out, daughters of Zion, and see King Solomon wearing the crown his mother gave him on his wedding day, and on the day he was very happy.
