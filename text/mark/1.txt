Mark 1:1 The start of the good news about Jesus Christ, God's Son;
Mark 1:2 The prophets wrote, "Look, I will send my messenger ahead of you to get things ready for you."
Mark 1:3 A voice cries out in the desert, "Get ready for God's coming, make a straight path for him."
Mark 1:4 John baptized in the desert and taught about baptism for forgiveness of sins.
Mark 1:5 Everyone from the area of Judaea and the city of Jerusalem went to him, and they were all baptized by him in the Jordan River while admitting their sins.
Mark 1:6 John wore camel hair clothes with a leather belt around his waist and ate locusts and wild honey.
Mark 1:7 He preached, "Someone more powerful than me is coming after me, and I'm not even worthy to bend down and untie his shoes."
Mark 1:8 I have baptized you with water; but he will baptize you with the Holy Spirit.
Mark 1:9 During that time, Jesus traveled from Nazareth in Galilee and John baptized him in the Jordan River.
Mark 1:10 Right after coming up from the water, he saw the sky open, and the Spirit like a dove coming down on him.
Mark 1:11 A voice from heaven said, "You are my dear Son, and I am very pleased with you."
Mark 1:12 Right away, the spirit led him into the desert.
Mark 1:13 He was in the desert for forty days, tempted by Satan, with wild animals, and angels helped him.
Mark 1:14 After John was put in jail, Jesus went to Galilee and preached the good news about God's kingdom.
Mark 1:15 They are saying, "The right time has come, and God's kingdom is near: change your ways and trust in the good news."
Mark 1:16 As he walked by the Sea of Galilee, he saw Simon and his brother Andrew throwing a net into the sea because they were fishermen.
Mark 1:17 Jesus said to them, "Follow me, and I will make you become fishers of people."
Mark 1:18 Right away they left their nets and followed him.
Mark 1:19 After he went a little further from there, he saw James, Zebedee's son, and John his brother, who were in the boat fixing their nets.
Mark 1:20 Immediately he called them, and they left their father Zebedee in the boat with the hired workers, and followed him.
Mark 1:21 They went to Capernaum; immediately on the Sabbath, he went into the meeting place and taught.
Mark 1:22 They were amazed at his teaching because he spoke with authority, not like the teachers of the law.
Mark 1:23 In their meeting place, there was a man with an evil spirit; and he shouted,
Mark 1:24 They said, "Leave us alone; what do you want with us, Jesus of Nazareth? Have you come to destroy us? I know who you are, the Holy One of God."
Mark 1:25 Jesus scolded him, saying, "Be quiet and come out of him."
Mark 1:26 When the evil spirit had hurt him and shouted loudly, it left him.
Mark 1:27 Everyone was amazed and asked each other, "What is this? A new teaching? He gives orders to evil spirits with power, and they listen to him."
Mark 1:28 Right away, his fame spread all over the area around Galilee.
Mark 1:29 Right after leaving the synagogue, they went into Simon and Andrew's house, along with James and John.
Mark 1:30 Simon's mother-in-law was in bed with a fever, and they quickly told Jesus about her.
Mark 1:31 He came, took her hand, helped her stand; at once the fever was gone, and she served them.
Mark 1:32 In the evening, when the sun set, they brought to him all who were sick and those who had demons.
Mark 1:33 Everyone in the city came together at the door.
Mark 1:34 He healed many who were sick with different diseases and drove out many demons; he did not let the demons talk because they knew who he was.
Mark 1:35 In the morning, long before sunrise, he got up, left the house, went to a quiet place, and prayed.
Mark 1:36 Simon and his companions went after him.
Mark 1:37 When they found him, they told him, "Everyone is looking for you."
Mark 1:38 He said to them, "Let's go to the nearby towns so I can preach there too; because that's why I came."
Mark 1:39 He taught in their meeting places all over Galilee and drove out demons.
Mark 1:40 A man with leprosy came to him, asking for help, knelt down, and said, "If you want to, you can make me clean."
Mark 1:41 Jesus, feeling sorry for him, reached out, touched him, and said, "I want to; be clean."
Mark 1:42 Right after he spoke, the leprosy left him, and he was clean.
Mark 1:43 He strictly told him and immediately sent him away.
Mark 1:44 He said to him, "Don't tell anyone, but go and show yourself to the priest and offer what Moses told you to for your cleansing, as proof to them."
Mark 1:45 He left and started to spread the news widely, so much that Jesus couldn't go into the town publicly but stayed in remote areas. And people came to him from everywhere.
