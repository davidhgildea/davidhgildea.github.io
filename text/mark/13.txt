Mark 13:1 As he left the temple, one of his disciples said to him, "Teacher, look at the size of these stones and these buildings!"
Mark 13:2 Jesus answered him, "Do you see these large buildings? Not one stone will be left on top of another; every one will be thrown down."
Mark 13:3 As he was sitting on the Mount of Olives across from the temple, Peter, James, John, and Andrew spoke to him in private.
Mark 13:4 Tell us, when will these things happen? And what will be the sign that all these things are about to be completed?
Mark 13:5 Jesus replied to them, saying, "Be careful so that no one tricks you."
Mark 13:6 Many will come using my name, claiming they are Christ, and will trick many people.
Mark 13:7 When you hear about wars and talk of wars, don't be worried: these things must happen, but it's not the end yet.
Mark 13:8 One country will fight against another, and one kingdom against another. There will be earthquakes in various places, and there will be famines and troubles. These are the first signs of suffering.
Mark 13:9 Be careful: people will hand you over to local courts; you will be whipped in synagogues; and you will stand before leaders and rulers because of me, to prove them wrong.
Mark 13:10 The good news has to be shared with all countries first.
Mark 13:11 When they take you and turn you over, don't worry ahead of time about what to say. Just say what is given to you at that time, because it's not you speaking, but the Holy Spirit.
Mark 13:12 Now a brother will betray his brother to death, and a father his son; children will turn against their parents and have them killed.
Mark 13:13 Everyone will hate you because of me, but if you keep going until the end, you will be saved.
Mark 13:14 When you see the terrible thing that causes destruction, as the prophet Daniel talked about, in a place it should not be, (the reader should pay attention,) then those in Judea should run away to the hills.
Mark 13:15 If you are on the roof, do not go down into the house or enter to take anything out.
Mark 13:16 And let the person in the field not go back to get their coat.
Mark 13:17 But it will be hard for pregnant women and those nursing babies in those days!
Mark 13:18 Pray that you don't have to run away during winter.
Mark 13:19 In those days, there will be suffering unlike anything since God created the world, and there will never be anything like it again.
Mark 13:20 If God had not made those days shorter, no one would survive. But for the sake of the chosen ones, whom he has picked, he has made the days shorter.
Mark 13:21 If anyone tells you, "Look, here's Christ," or "There he is," don't believe it.
Mark 13:22 Fake saviors and false prophets will appear and perform miracles and wonders to trick people, if they could, even those chosen by God.
Mark 13:23 Be careful: I have already told you everything.
Mark 13:24 But in those days, after the suffering ends, the sun will become dark, and the moon will not shine.
Mark 13:25 The stars from the sky will fall, and the forces in the sky will be shaken.
Mark 13:26 People will see the Son of Man coming in the clouds with great power and glory.
Mark 13:27 Then he will send his angels to collect his chosen people from everywhere on earth to the farthest parts of heaven.
Mark 13:28 Learn from the story of the fig tree: When its branch gets soft and grows leaves, you know that summer is close.
Mark 13:29 In the same way, when you see these things happening, understand that it is close, right at the door.
Mark 13:30 I truly tell you, this generation will not disappear until all these things happen.
Mark 13:31 The sky and the earth will disappear, but my words will never go away.
Mark 13:32 No one knows the day or hour, not even the angels in heaven, nor the Son, only the Father.
Mark 13:33 Be careful, stay alert, and pray, because you do not know when the time will come.
Mark 13:34 The Son of Man is like someone who went on a long trip, left his home, gave his workers power, each one a job, and told the doorkeeper to stay alert.
Mark 13:35 Stay alert, because you don't know when the owner of the house will come back; it could be in the evening, at midnight, when the rooster crows, or in the morning.
Mark 13:36 Be careful that he doesn't come suddenly and find you asleep.
Mark 13:37 I tell you and everyone: Stay alert.
