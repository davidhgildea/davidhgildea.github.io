Mark 4:1 He started teaching by the sea again, and so many people came to him that he got into a boat and sat on the water, while all the people stayed on the shore.
Mark 4:2 He taught them many things using stories and explained his teachings to them.
Mark 4:3 Listen; Look, a farmer went out to plant seeds.
Mark 4:4 As he was planting seeds, some dropped on the path, and birds flew down and ate them.
Mark 4:5 Some seeds fell on rocky soil where there wasn't much dirt; they quickly grew because the soil was shallow.
Mark 4:6 When the sun rose, it burned the plant, and because the plant had no roots, it dried up.
Mark 4:7 Some seeds fell among thorns, and when the thorns grew, they choked the seeds so they produced no fruit.
Mark 4:8 Some seeds fell on good soil and grew, making more seeds. They produced thirty, sixty, or even a hundred times more.
Mark 4:9 He told them, "If anyone can hear, listen carefully."
Mark 4:10 When he was alone, the people with the twelve disciples asked him about the parable.
Mark 4:11 He told them, "You are given the knowledge of God's kingdom's secret, but to outsiders, everything is told in stories."
Mark 4:12 They can look but not see, and listen but not understand, so they won't turn back and be forgiven for their wrongs.
Mark 4:13 He asked them, "Don't you understand this story? How then will you understand any story?"
Mark 4:14 The farmer plants the message.
Mark 4:15 These are the ones near the path where the message is planted; but as soon as they hear it, Satan comes quickly and removes the message that was planted in their hearts.
Mark 4:16 These are also the ones planted on rocky soil; they hear the message and quickly accept it with joy.
Mark 4:17 They have no strong root, and only last for a while. When trouble or persecution comes because of the message, they quickly get upset.
Mark 4:18 These are the people whose seed fell among thorns; they are those who hear the message,
Mark 4:19 Worries about life, the trickery of wealth, and wanting other things crowd out God's message, making it unproductive.
Mark 4:20 These are the ones planted on good soil: they hear the message, accept it, and produce crops—some thirty times what was sown, some sixty times, and some a hundred times.
Mark 4:21 He asked them, "Do you bring a candle to hide it under a bowl or a bed? Shouldn't it be placed on a candle holder?"
Mark 4:22 Nothing hidden will stay that way; what was secret will become known.
Mark 4:23 If anyone can hear, let them listen.
Mark 4:24 He told them, "Be careful what you listen to: the way you give to others will be the way you get from others, and if you listen well, you will receive even more."
Mark 4:25 Whoever has will receive more, and whoever does not have, even what they have will be taken away.
Mark 4:26 He said, “God's kingdom is like when a man plants seeds in the soil.”
Mark 4:27 He sleeps and wakes, day and night, and the seed sprouts and grows, but he doesn't know how.
Mark 4:28 The earth produces its own crops: first the plant, then the head of grain, then the fully developed grain in the head.
Mark 4:29 But when the fruit is ready, right away he uses the sickle, because it's time to harvest.
Mark 4:30 He asked, "What can we compare God's kingdom to? What example can we use to describe it?"
Mark 4:31 It is like a tiny mustard seed that, when planted in the ground, is smaller than all the other seeds in the ground.
Mark 4:32 When it's planted, it grows and becomes bigger than all the plants, and it spreads out big branches so birds can rest in its shade.
Mark 4:33 Using many stories like these, he shared his message with them, as much as they could understand.
Mark 4:34 He always taught using stories, but when alone with his followers, he explained everything.
Mark 4:35 That same day, when evening came, he said to them, "Let's go across to the other side."
Mark 4:36 After sending the crowd away, they went with him on the boat just as he was. There were also other small boats with him.
Mark 4:37 A big storm came with strong winds, and the waves hit the boat until it was full of water.
Mark 4:38 He was at the back of the boat, sleeping on a cushion. They woke him and said, "Teacher, don't you care if we drown?"
Mark 4:39 He got up, told the wind to stop, and said to the sea, "Quiet, be calm." Then the wind stopped, and it was very calm.
Mark 4:40 He asked them, "Why are you so scared? Why don't you have faith?"
Mark 4:41 They were very afraid and asked each other, "Who is this man that even the wind and the sea listen to him?"
