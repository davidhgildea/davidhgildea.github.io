Mark 7:1 The Pharisees and some teachers of the Law who were from Jerusalem gathered around him.
Mark 7:2 When they saw some of his followers eating bread with dirty hands, they criticized them.
Mark 7:3 The Pharisees and all the Jews do not eat unless they often wash their hands, following the elders' tradition.
Mark 7:4 When they return from the market, they do not eat unless they wash. They also follow many other rules, like washing cups, pots, metal bowls, and tables.
Mark 7:5 The Pharisees and teachers of the law asked Jesus, "Why don't your disciples follow the elders' traditions, but eat bread with dirty hands?"
Mark 7:6 He replied, "Isaiah was right when he prophesied about you fakes, as it is written, 'These people show me respect with their words, but their hearts are not with me.'"
Mark 7:7 They worship me for nothing, teaching human rules as if they were God's teachings.
Mark 7:8 You ignore God's command to follow human traditions, like washing pots and cups, and you do many other similar things.
Mark 7:9 He told them, "You completely ignore God's command to follow your own tradition."
Mark 7:10 Moses said, Respect your father and mother; and anyone who speaks badly of their father or mother must be put to death.
Mark 7:11 But you say, if someone tells their father or mother, 'It is Corban'—which means 'a gift'—so you cannot benefit from me; then they don’t have to help.
Mark 7:12 And you don't let him do anything for his father or mother anymore.
Mark 7:13 You make God's word useless with your tradition that you've passed down, and you do many things like that.
Mark 7:14 When he gathered everyone around, he told them, "Listen to me, all of you, and understand:"
Mark 7:15 Nothing outside a person can make them unclean by going into them. Rather, it is what comes out of a person that makes them unclean.
Mark 7:16 If anyone can hear, let them listen.
Mark 7:17 After he went into the house away from the crowd, his followers asked him about the story.
Mark 7:18 He asked them, "Do you also not understand? Don't you realize that nothing entering a person from outside can make them unclean?"
Mark 7:19 It doesn't go into his heart but into his stomach and then out of the body, cleaning out all foods.
Mark 7:20 He said, What comes out of a person is what makes them unclean.
Mark 7:21 From inside, from people's hearts, come bad thoughts, cheating in marriage, sexual sins, and killings.
Mark 7:22 Stealing, wanting what others have, bad behavior, lying, inappropriate desires, looking to harm others, speaking against God, thinking too highly of oneself, acting without sense.
Mark 7:23 All these bad things start inside a person and make them unclean.
Mark 7:24 He got up from there, went to the area near Tyre and Sidon, went into a house, and did not want anyone to know it, but he could not stay hidden.
Mark 7:25 A woman whose daughter had an evil spirit heard about him, went to him, and knelt down.
Mark 7:26 The woman was Greek, from Syria-Phoenicia. She begged Jesus to drive the demon out of her daughter.
Mark 7:27 Jesus said to her, "Let the children eat first; it's not right to take the children's food and give it to the dogs."
Mark 7:28 She replied, "Yes, God, even the dogs under the table eat the children's crumbs."
Mark 7:29 He told her, "Because of your words, go; the evil spirit has left your daughter."
Mark 7:30 When she got home, she saw that the demon had left, and her daughter was lying on the bed.
Mark 7:31 Leaving the region of Tyre and Sidon, he went to the Sea of Galilee, passing through the area of Decapolis.
Mark 7:32 They brought to him a person who was deaf and had trouble speaking, and they asked him to touch him.
Mark 7:33 He took the man away from the crowd, put his fingers in the man's ears, spit, and touched the man's tongue.
Mark 7:34 Looking up to heaven, he sighed and said to him, "Ephphatha," which means, "Be opened."
Mark 7:35 Right away his ears could hear, his tongue was freed, and he spoke clearly.
Mark 7:36 He told them not to tell anyone, but the more he insisted, the more they spread the news.
Mark 7:37 They were extremely amazed and said, "He does everything well: he makes the deaf hear and the mute speak."
