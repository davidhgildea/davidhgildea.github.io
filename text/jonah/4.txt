Jonah 4:1 Jonah was very upset by this, and he became angry.
Jonah 4:2 He prayed to God and said, "I knew this would happen, God. That's why I ran away to Tarshish. I knew you are kind and merciful, not easily angered, full of love, and you change your mind about punishing.
Jonah 4:3 So now, God, please take my life from me; it's better for me to die than to live.
Jonah 4:4 Then God asked, "Is it right for you to be angry?"
Jonah 4:5 Jonah left the city, sat on its east side, built himself a shelter, and sat in its shade to see what would happen to the city.
Jonah 4:6 God made a plant grow over Jonah to give him shade and help him feel better. Jonah was very happy about the plant.
Jonah 4:7 God made a worm come up the next morning, and it attacked the plant so that it dried up.
Jonah 4:8 When the sun rose, God sent a strong east wind; the sun blazed on Jonah's head so much that he became weak, wished to die, and thought, "It's better for me to die than to live."
Jonah 4:9 God asked Jonah, "Is it right for you to be angry about the plant?" And he answered, "Yes, I am right to be angry enough to die."
Jonah 4:10 God said, "You feel sorry for the plant you didn't work for or make grow. It appeared overnight and died overnight."
Jonah 4:11 Shouldn't I have mercy on Nineveh, that large city, with more than 120,000 people who do not know right from left, and many animals too?
