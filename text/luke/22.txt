Luke 22:1 The time for the Feast of Unleavened Bread, also known as Passover, was near.
Luke 22:2 The high priests and teachers of the law wanted to find a way to kill him; they were afraid of the people.
Luke 22:3 Satan entered into Judas, known as Iscariot, one of the twelve disciples.
Luke 22:4 He went and talked with the chief priests and officers about how he could hand him over to them.
Luke 22:5 They were happy and agreed to pay him money.
Luke 22:6 He promised and looked for a chance to hand him over to them when the crowd was not around.
Luke 22:7 The day came for the bread without yeast when it was time for the Passover lamb to be sacrificed.
Luke 22:8 He sent Peter and John, telling them, "Go and get the Passover ready for us to eat."
Luke 22:9 They asked him, "Where do you want us to prepare?"
Luke 22:10 He told them, "Look, when you go into the city, a man carrying a water jar will meet you; follow him into the house he enters."
Luke 22:11 You will tell the owner of the house, 'The Teacher asks you, where is the guest room where I can eat the Passover meal with my followers?'
Luke 22:12 He will show you a big room upstairs that's ready: prepare there.
Luke 22:13 They went and found everything just as he had told them, and they prepared the Passover meal.
Luke 22:14 When the time came, he sat down with the twelve apostles.
Luke 22:15 He said to them, "I have really wanted to eat this Passover meal with you before I go through hard times."
Luke 22:16 I tell you, I won't eat it again until it comes true in God's kingdom.
Luke 22:17 He took the cup, thanked God, and said, "Take this and share it among you."
Luke 22:18 I tell you, I won't drink from the grapevine until God's kingdom comes.
Luke 22:19 He took bread, thanked God, broke it, and gave it to them, saying, "This is my body given for you; do this to remember me."
Luke 22:20 After supper, Jesus took the cup and said, "This cup represents the new agreement in my blood, poured out for you."
Luke 22:21 Look, the hand of the one who will betray me is with mine on the table.
Luke 22:22 Truly, the Son of Man will go as planned, but how terrible it will be for the man who betrays him!
Luke 22:23 They started to ask each other which one of them would do this.
Luke 22:24 There was also an argument among them about who should be considered the greatest.
Luke 22:25 He told them, "The rulers of the nations rule over them, and those with power over the people are called good leaders."
Luke 22:26 But you must not be like that: instead, whoever is the greatest among you should be like the youngest, and whoever leads should be like someone who serves.
Luke 22:27 Who is more important, the one who sits and eats, or the one who serves? Isn't it the one who sits and eats? But I am with you as one who serves.
Luke 22:28 You are those who have stayed with me through my trials.
Luke 22:29 I give you a kingdom, just as my Father has given one to me.
Luke 22:30 You may eat and drink at my table in my kingdom, and sit on thrones to judge the twelve tribes of Israel.
Luke 22:31 God said, "Simon, Simon, listen, Satan wants to take you, to shake you like wheat."
Luke 22:32 I have prayed for you that your faith will not fail; and when you turn back, help your brothers be strong.
Luke 22:33 He said to him, "Master, I am ready to follow you, even to jail and to death."
Luke 22:34 He said, "I tell you, Peter, before the rooster crows today, you will deny three times that you know me."
Luke 22:35 He asked them, "When I sent you without money, bag, or sandals, did you need anything?" And they answered, "No."
Luke 22:36 He then told them, "But now, if you have a purse, take it, and your bag too; if you don't have a sword, sell your clothes to buy one."
Luke 22:37 I tell you that what is written must happen to me. I must be counted among the wrongdoers, for what is said about me is coming to an end.
Luke 22:38 And they said, "Master, look, here are two swords." And he replied, "That's enough."
Luke 22:39 He came out and went to the Mount of Olives, as he usually did, and his disciples followed him.
Luke 22:40 When he was there, he told them, "Pray so you won't give in to temptation."
Luke 22:41 He moved away from them about as far as you can throw a stone, kneeled down, and prayed.
Luke 22:42 Father, if you are willing, take this cup away from me: but let your will, not mine, be done.
Luke 22:43 An angel from heaven appeared to him, giving him strength.
Luke 22:44 Feeling very troubled, he prayed even harder, and his sweat was like big drops of blood falling to the ground.
Luke 22:45 After praying, he went to his disciples and saw they were asleep because they were sad.
Luke 22:46 He asked them, "Why are you sleeping? Get up and pray so you don't give in to temptation."
Luke 22:47 While he was still talking, a crowd appeared, and Judas, one of the twelve, led them to Jesus to kiss him.
Luke 22:48 But Jesus said to him, "Judas, are you betraying the Son of Man with a kiss?"
Luke 22:49 When the people around him saw what was going to happen, they asked, "Master, should we attack with our swords?"
Luke 22:50 One of them struck the high priest's servant and cut off his right ear.
Luke 22:51 Jesus responded, "Allow this for now." He then touched the man's ear and healed him.
Luke 22:52 Then Jesus said to the chief priests, the temple guards, and the elders who came to him, "Did you come out with swords and clubs as if I were a thief?"
Luke 22:53 When I was with you every day in the temple, you did not try to hurt me. But now is your time, and darkness has power.
Luke 22:54 They took him, led him, and brought him to the high priest's house. Peter followed from a distance.
Luke 22:55 When they made a fire in the middle of the room and sat together, Peter sat with them.
Luke 22:56 A servant girl saw him sitting by the fire, stared at him, and said, "This man was with him too."
Luke 22:57 He denied knowing him, saying, "Woman, I don't know him."
Luke 22:58 After a short time, someone else saw Peter and said, "You are one of them too." But Peter replied, "No, I am not."
Luke 22:59 About an hour later, another person strongly said, "For sure, this man was with him too: because he is from Galilee."
Luke 22:60 Peter said, "I don't know what you're talking about." And right away, as he was still speaking, a rooster crowed.
Luke 22:61 God turned and looked at Peter. Then Peter remembered what God had told him: "Before the rooster crows, you will deny me three times."
Luke 22:62 Peter went out and cried a lot.
Luke 22:63 The men who were holding Jesus made fun of him and hit him.
Luke 22:64 After they covered his eyes, they hit him in the face and asked, "Guess who hit you?"
Luke 22:65 They said many other disrespectful things against him.
Luke 22:66 As soon as it was morning, the leaders and the head priests and the teachers of the law met together and took him into their meeting, asking,
Luke 22:67 Are you the Messiah? Tell us. And he said to them, If I tell you, you will not believe.
Luke 22:68 If I ask you too, you won't answer or let me go.
Luke 22:69 From now on, the Son of Man will sit at the right side of God's power.
Luke 22:70 They all asked, "Are you the Son of God?" He answered, "You say that I am."
Luke 22:71 They asked, "Do we need more evidence? We've heard it from his own mouth."
