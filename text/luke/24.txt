Luke 24:1 On the first day of the week, very early in the morning, they went to the tomb with the spices they had made, and some others were with them.
Luke 24:2 They saw that the stone was moved from the tomb's entrance.
Luke 24:3 They went in and did not find the body of Jesus.
Luke 24:4 While they were very confused about this, suddenly two men in bright clothes stood beside them.
Luke 24:5 As they were scared and looked down at the ground, they asked them, "Why are you looking for the living among the dead?"
Luke 24:6 He is not here; he has risen. Remember what he told you when he was still in Galilee.
Luke 24:7 The Son of Man must be handed over to sinners, be crucified, and rise on the third day.
Luke 24:8 They remembered what he said.
Luke 24:9 They came back from the tomb and told everything to the eleven disciples and to all the others.
Luke 24:10 Mary Magdalene, Joanna, Mary the mother of James, and other women with them told the apostles these things.
Luke 24:11 What they said sounded like nonsense, and they did not believe it.
Luke 24:12 Peter got up and ran to the tomb; bending over, he saw the linen wrappings lying alone, and he left, amazed at what had happened.
Luke 24:13 Two of them went to a village named Emmaus, about seven miles from Jerusalem, that day.
Luke 24:14 They talked about all these things that had happened.
Luke 24:15 While they were talking and discussing, Jesus himself came close and walked along with them.
Luke 24:16 But they could not recognize him because their eyes were prevented.
Luke 24:17 He asked them, "What are you discussing so intently as you walk along, looking so sad?"
Luke 24:18 One of them, named Cleopas, replied, "Are you the only visitor to Jerusalem who doesn't know the things that have happened here these days?"
Luke 24:19 He asked them, "What things?" They answered, "About Jesus from Nazareth, who was a powerful prophet in actions and words before God and all the people."
Luke 24:20 The leading priests and our leaders handed him over to be sentenced to death and crucified him.
Luke 24:21 We hoped he was the one to save Israel, and what's more, today is the third day since this all happened.
Luke 24:22 Yes, some women from our group also surprised us; they were at the tomb early in the morning.
Luke 24:23 When they did not find his body, they came back saying they had seen a vision of angels who told them he was alive.
Luke 24:24 Some of us went to the tomb and found it just as the women had said, but we did not see him.
Luke 24:25 Then he told them, "You are foolish and slow to trust what the prophets have said:"
Luke 24:26 Shouldn't Christ have suffered these things and entered into his glory?
Luke 24:27 Starting with Moses and all the prophets, he explained to them what was said about himself in all the Scriptures.
Luke 24:28 They got close to the village where they were headed, and it seemed like he wanted to keep going.
Luke 24:29 But they urged him, saying, "Stay with us, because it is almost night, and the day is almost over." So he went in to stay with them.
Luke 24:30 While he was eating with them, he took bread, gave thanks, broke it, and gave it to them.
Luke 24:31 Their eyes were opened, and they recognized him; then he disappeared from their sight.
Luke 24:32 They said to each other, "Didn't our hearts feel warm as he talked to us on the road and explained the scriptures to us?"
Luke 24:33 They got up that same hour, went back to Jerusalem, and found the eleven and those with them all together.
Luke 24:34 They said, "The Lord has really risen and has shown himself to Simon."
Luke 24:35 They shared what happened on the road and how they recognized him when he broke the bread.
Luke 24:36 While they were talking, Jesus himself appeared among them and said to them, "Peace to you."
Luke 24:37 They were very scared and thought they had seen a ghost.
Luke 24:38 He asked them, "Why are you worried? And why do you have doubts in your hearts?"
Luke 24:39 Look at my hands and my feet, it's really me. Touch me and see; a spirit doesn't have flesh and bones like you see I have.
Luke 24:40 After saying this, he showed them his hands and feet.
Luke 24:41 While they still couldn't believe because of their happiness, and were amazed, he asked them, "Do you have any food?"
Luke 24:42 They gave him some cooked fish and honeycomb.
Luke 24:43 He took it and ate it in front of them.
Luke 24:44 He told them, "These are the words I told you when I was still with you: everything written about me in Moses' law, the prophets, and the psalms must come true."
Luke 24:45 He opened their minds so they could understand the scriptures.
Luke 24:46 He told them, "It is written that Christ must suffer and rise from the dead on the third day."
Luke 24:47 People should be taught to change their ways and be forgiven for their sins in his name everywhere, starting from Jerusalem.
Luke 24:48 You are witnesses of these things.
Luke 24:49 Look, I will send you what my Father promised, but stay in the city of Jerusalem until you receive power from above.
Luke 24:50 He led them to Bethany, raised his hands, and blessed them.
Luke 24:51 While he was blessing them, he left them and went up into heaven.
Luke 24:52 They worshiped him and went back to Jerusalem feeling very happy.
Luke 24:53 They were always in the temple, praising and thanking God. Amen.
