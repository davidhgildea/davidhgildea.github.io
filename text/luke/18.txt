Luke 18:1 He told them a story to show that they should always pray and never give up.
Luke 18:2 Someone said, "In a city, there was a judge who did not fear God or care about people."
Luke 18:3 In that city, there was a widow; she approached him and said, "Get justice for me from my opponent."
Luke 18:4 He didn't want to for some time, but later he thought, "Even though I don't fear God or care about people;
Luke 18:5 Since this widow keeps bothering me, I will help her so she won't keep coming and wear me out.
Luke 18:6 God said, "Listen to what the unfair judge says."
Luke 18:7 Won't God protect his chosen ones who cry out to him day and night, even though he is patient with them?
Luke 18:8 I tell you, God will quickly make things right for them. But when the Son of Man comes, will he find people with faith on the earth?
Luke 18:9 He told this story to some who were sure they were good people and looked down on everyone else:
Luke 18:10 Two men went to the temple to pray; one was a Pharisee, and the other was a tax collector.
Luke 18:11 The Pharisee stood up and prayed quietly to himself, 'God, I am thankful that I am not like other people—cheaters, unjust, unfaithful in marriage, or even like this tax collector.
Luke 18:12 I skip eating twice a week, I give part of everything I have.
Luke 18:13 The tax collector stood at a distance, wouldn't even look up to heaven, but hit his chest and said, "God, have mercy on me, a sinner."
Luke 18:14 I tell you, this man went home right with God rather than the other: for everyone who lifts himself up will be put down, and he who lowers himself will be lifted up.
Luke 18:15 They also brought babies to him, so he could touch them. But when his followers saw this, they told them off.
Luke 18:16 Jesus called them to him and said, "Let little children come to me, and don't stop them: for God's kingdom belongs to ones like these."
Luke 18:17 I tell you the truth, anyone who doesn't accept God's kingdom like a little child will never enter it.
Luke 18:18 A leader asked him, "Good Teacher, what must I do to have eternal life?"
Luke 18:19 Jesus asked him, "Why do you call me good? No one is good except God."
Luke 18:20 You know the commands: Don't cheat in marriage, don't kill, don't steal, don't lie about others, respect your father and mother.
Luke 18:21 He said, "I have followed all these since I was young."
Luke 18:22 When Jesus heard this, he told him, "You still need one thing: sell everything you own, give to the poor, and you will have treasure in heaven. Then come and follow me."
Luke 18:23 When he heard this, he became very sad because he was very rich.
Luke 18:24 When Jesus saw how sad the man was, he said, "It's really hard for rich people to get into God's kingdom!"
Luke 18:25 It is easier for a camel to pass through the hole of a needle than for a rich person to enter God's kingdom.
Luke 18:26 Those who heard this asked, "Then who can be saved?"
Luke 18:27 He said, What is impossible for people is possible with God.
Luke 18:28 Then Peter said, "Look, we have left everything and followed you."
Luke 18:29 He told them, "Truly, I tell you, no one who has left home, parents, brothers, wife, or children for God's kingdom,
Luke 18:30 Who will not get much more in this time, and in the future eternal life.
Luke 18:31 He gathered the twelve disciples and said to them, "Look, we're going to Jerusalem, and everything the prophets wrote about the Son of Man will happen."
Luke 18:32 He will be handed over to non-Jews, laughed at, badly treated, and spit on.
Luke 18:33 They will whip him, kill him, and on the third day, he will rise again.
Luke 18:34 They did not understand any of these things; the meaning was hidden from them, and they did not know what was said.
Luke 18:35 As he approached Jericho, a blind man was sitting by the road, begging.
Luke 18:36 Hearing the crowd go by, he asked what it was about.
Luke 18:37 They told him that Jesus from Nazareth is passing by.
Luke 18:38 He shouted, "Jesus, son of David, please be kind to me."
Luke 18:39 The people in front told him to be quiet, but he shouted even louder, "Son of David, have mercy on me."
Luke 18:40 Jesus stood up and ordered that the man be brought to him. When the man came close, Jesus asked him.
Luke 18:41 Asking, "What do you want me to do for you?" He replied, "Sir, I want to see again."
Luke 18:42 Jesus said to him, "Get your sight back; your faith has healed you."
Luke 18:43 Right away he could see, and he followed him, praising God; and when all the people saw this, they also praised God.
