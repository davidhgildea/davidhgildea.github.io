Luke 23:1 The whole crowd got up and took him to Pilate.
Luke 23:2 They started to accuse him, saying, "We caught this man corrupting the nation and telling people not to pay taxes to Caesar, claiming he is Christ, a king."
Luke 23:3 Pilate asked him, "Are you the King of the Jews?" He replied, "You say so."
Luke 23:4 Pilate said to the religious leaders and the people, "I find nothing wrong with this man."
Luke 23:5 They became even angrier, saying, "He causes trouble among the people, teaching all over Judea, starting from Galilee to here."
Luke 23:6 When Pilate heard about Galilee, he asked if the man was from Galilee.
Luke 23:7 As soon as he found out that he was under Herod's authority, he sent him to Herod, who was also in Jerusalem then.
Luke 23:8 When Herod saw Jesus, he was very happy, because he had wanted to see him for a long time after hearing so much about him; he also hoped to see Jesus perform a miracle.
Luke 23:9 He asked him many questions, but he gave no answer.
Luke 23:10 The leading priests and teachers of the law stood and strongly accused him.
Luke 23:11 Herod and his soldiers ignored Jesus, made fun of him, dressed him in a fancy robe, and sent him back to Pilate.
Luke 23:12 On that day, Pilate and Herod became friends; before that, they had been enemies.
Luke 23:13 Pilate gathered the chief priests, leaders, and the people.
Luke 23:14 He said to them, "You have brought this man to me as someone who corrupts the people. But look, I have questioned him in front of you and have found him not guilty of the accusations you make against him."
Luke 23:15 No, not even Herod, because I sent you to him, and look, he has done nothing that deserves death.
Luke 23:16 I will punish him and then let him go.
Luke 23:17 He had to release one prisoner to them during the feast.
Luke 23:18 They all shouted together, "Get rid of this man and free Barabbas for us."
Luke 23:19 He was put in jail for causing a riot in the city and for murder.
Luke 23:20 So Pilate, wanting to let Jesus go, spoke to them again.
Luke 23:21 They shouted, "Crucify him, crucify him."
Luke 23:22 He asked them a third time, "What wrong has he done? I've found no reason to kill him. So, I'll punish him and then release him."
Luke 23:23 They urgently shouted, wanting him to be crucified. And their voices, along with the chief priests', were stronger.
Luke 23:24 Pilate decided to do what they asked.
Luke 23:25 He freed the one they wanted who was in prison for causing trouble and murder, but he handed Jesus over to do what they wished.
Luke 23:26 As they took him away, they grabbed Simon from Cyrene, who was coming from the fields, and gave him the cross to carry behind Jesus.
Luke 23:27 A large crowd followed him, including women who mourned and cried for him.
Luke 23:28 Jesus turned to them and said, "Women of Jerusalem, don't cry for me, but cry for yourselves and your children."
Luke 23:29 Look, the time is coming when people will say, "Happy are those who cannot have children, the wombs that never gave birth, and the breasts that never nursed."
Luke 23:30 Then they will start to tell the mountains, "Fall on us," and to the hills, "Hide us."
Luke 23:31 If this happens to a living tree, what will happen to a dead one?
Luke 23:32 Two other criminals were also led with him to be executed.
Luke 23:33 When they reached the place known as Calvary, they nailed him to the cross along with the criminals, one on his right and the other on his left.
Luke 23:34 Jesus said, "Father, forgive them, because they don't know what they're doing." They shared out his clothes by throwing dice.
Luke 23:35 The people watched. The leaders mocked him, saying, "He saved others; let him save himself if he is the Messiah, the one chosen by God."
Luke 23:36 The soldiers also made fun of him, went up to him, and gave him vinegar.
Luke 23:37 They said, "If you are the king of the Jews, save yourself."
Luke 23:38 A sign was also put above him with the words in Greek, Latin, and Hebrew, "This is the King of the Jews."
Luke 23:39 One of the criminals hanging beside Jesus insulted him, saying, "If you are the Christ, save yourself and us."
Luke 23:40 But the other one scolded him, saying, "Don't you respect God since you are also being punished the same way?"
Luke 23:41 We are punished fairly, for we are getting what our actions deserve; but this man has done nothing wrong.
Luke 23:42 He told Jesus, "Remember me when you come into your kingdom."
Luke 23:43 Jesus said to him, "I tell you the truth, today you will be with me in paradise."
Luke 23:44 It was around noon, and darkness covered the whole earth until about three in the afternoon.
Luke 23:45 The sun turned dark, and the temple's curtain ripped down the middle.
Luke 23:46 When Jesus shouted loudly, he said, "Father, I give my spirit to you." After saying this, he died.
Luke 23:47 When the army officer saw what happened, he praised God, saying, "This man was truly good."
Luke 23:48 All the people who gathered to see what happened beat their chests and then went away.
Luke 23:49 All his friends and the women who had followed him from Galilee stood at a distance watching these things.
Luke 23:50 Look, there was a man named Joseph, who was a counselor; he was good and fair.
Luke 23:51 He did not agree with their plan and action; he came from Arimathaea, a Jewish city, and he also waited for God's kingdom.
Luke 23:52 This man went to Pilate and asked for Jesus' body.
Luke 23:53 He took the body down, wrapped it in cloth, and placed it in a tomb cut out of rock where no one had ever been laid before.
Luke 23:54 That day was for getting ready, and the Sabbath was about to start.
Luke 23:55 The women who had come with him from Galilee followed and saw the tomb and how his body was placed.
Luke 23:56 They went back and got spices and ointments ready. Then they rested on the Sabbath day, as the rule said.
