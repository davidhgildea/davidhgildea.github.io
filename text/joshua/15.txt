Joshua 15:1 This was the tribe of Judah's family land; it reached to Edom's border and Zin desert in the south, which was the farthest part of the southern border.
Joshua 15:2 Their southern boundary started at the edge of the Salt Sea, stretching towards the south.
Joshua 15:3 It went to the south toward Maalehacrabbim, then to Zin, and up the south to Kadeshbarnea, moved on to Hezron, up to Adar, and turned toward Karkaa.
Joshua 15:4 From there it went to Azmon, and ended at the river of Egypt; the southern boundary ended at the sea.
Joshua 15:5 The eastern border reached the Salt Sea, up to the end of the Jordan River. And the northern border started at the sea inlet, at the far end of the Jordan.
Joshua 15:6 The border rose to Bethhogla, went north of Betharabah, and rose again to the stone of Bohan, Reuben's son.
Joshua 15:7 The border went up to Debir from Achor Valley, then north, facing Gilgal, near the ascent to Adummim, on the river's south side. The border reached Enshemesh's waters and ended at Enrogel.
Joshua 15:8 The boundary went up through Hinnom Valley to the southern area of the Jebusite city, which is Jerusalem. Then it went up to the mountain peak facing Hinnom Valley to the west, at the end of the Rephaim Valley to the north.
Joshua 15:9 The border went from the hilltop to Nephtoah's water spring, then to the towns of Mount Ephron, and reached Baalah, known as Kirjathjearim.
Joshua 15:10 The border went from Baalah west to Mount Seir, then ran by the side of Mount Jearim, or Chesalon, to the north, continued down to Bethshemesh, and stretched toward Timnah.
Joshua 15:11 The boundary extended to the north side of Ekron, then it went to Shicron, passed by Mount Baalah, and reached Jabneel. The boundary ended at the sea.
Joshua 15:12 The western border reached the big sea and its shoreline. This is the surrounding boundary for the families of Judah's descendants.
Joshua 15:13 God gave Caleb, Jephunneh's son, a place among Judah's people as God told Joshua, including Hebron, the city of Arba, Anak's father.
Joshua 15:14 Caleb forced out the three sons of Anak: Sheshai, Ahiman, and Talmai.
Joshua 15:15 He went from there to the people of Debir, which used to be called Kirjathsepher.
Joshua 15:16 Caleb said, Whoever defeats Kirjathsepher and captures it, I will give him my daughter Achsah as his wife.
Joshua 15:17 Othniel, Kenaz's son and Caleb's brother, captured the city, so he was given Achsah, his daughter, as his wife.
Joshua 15:18 As she came to him, she persuaded him to ask her father for a field. She got off her donkey, and Caleb asked her, "What do you want?"
Joshua 15:19 She replied, "Please give me a special gift; you have given me land in the south; now also give me water springs." So he gave her the upper and lower springs.
Joshua 15:20 This is what the family of the tribe of Judah's descendants will inherit.
Joshua 15:21 The farthest towns of Judah's tribe near Edom's southern border were Kabzeel, Eder, and Jagur.
Joshua 15:22 And Kinah, Dimonah, and Adadah,
Joshua 15:23 Kedesh, Hazor, and Ithnan.
Joshua 15:24 Ziph, Telem, Bealoth,
Joshua 15:25 Hazor, Hadattah, Kerioth, and Hezron, which is also called Hazor,
Joshua 15:26 Amam, Shema, and Moladah.
Joshua 15:27 And Hazargaddah, Heshmon, and Bethpalet,
Joshua 15:28 And Hazarshual, Beersheba, and Bizjothjah,
Joshua 15:29 Baalah, Iim, and Azem.
Joshua 15:30 Eltolad, Chesil, and Hormah,
Joshua 15:31 Ziklag, Madmannah, and Sansannah.
Joshua 15:32 Lebaoth, Shilhim, Ain, and Rimmon: there are 29 cities in total with their villages.
Joshua 15:33 In the valley were Eshtaol, Zoreah, and Ashnah.
Joshua 15:34 Zanoah, Engannim, Tappuah, and Enam,
Joshua 15:35 Jarmuth, Adullam, Socoh, and Azekah,
Joshua 15:36 Sharaim, Adithaim, Gederah, and Gederothaim; fourteen cities and their surrounding villages.
Joshua 15:37 Zenan, Hadashah, and Migdalgad,
Joshua 15:38 And Dilean, Mizpeh, and Joktheel,
Joshua 15:39 Lachish, Bozkath, and Eglon.
Joshua 15:40 And Cabbon, Lahmam, and Kithlish,
Joshua 15:41 Gederoth, Bethdagon, Naamah, and Makkedah; sixteen cities with their surrounding villages.
Joshua 15:42 Libnah, Ether, and Ashan.
Joshua 15:43 Jiphtah, Ashnah, and Nezib,
Joshua 15:44 Keilah, Achzib, and Mareshah, along with their surrounding villages, make nine cities.
Joshua 15:45 Ekron, with its towns and villages:
Joshua 15:46 From Ekron to the sea, all the area near Ashdod along with its villages.
Joshua 15:47 Ashdod with its towns and villages, Gaza with its towns and villages, to the River of Egypt, and the great sea and its border.
Joshua 15:48 In the mountains were Shamir, Jattir, and Socoh.
Joshua 15:49 And Dannah, and the city of Dannah, which is Debir,
Joshua 15:50 Anab, Eshtemoh, and Anim,
Joshua 15:51 Goshen, Holon, and Giloh; eleven cities and their surrounding villages.
Joshua 15:52 Arab, Dumah, and Eshan.
Joshua 15:53 And Janum, Beth-tappuah, and Aphekah,
Joshua 15:54 Humtah, Kirjatharba or Hebron, and Zior; nine cities and their surrounding villages.
Joshua 15:55 Maon, Carmel, Ziph, and Juttah
Joshua 15:56 And Jezreel, Jokdeam, and Zanoah,
Joshua 15:57 Cain, Gibeah, and Timnah; ten cities with their areas.
Joshua 15:58 Halhul, Bethzur, and Gedor.
Joshua 15:59 And Maarath, Bethanoth, and Eltekon; six towns with their surrounding areas.
Joshua 15:60 Kirjathbaal, also known as Kirjathjearim, and Rabbah; two cities with their surrounding small towns.
Joshua 15:61 In the desert, Betharabah, Middin, and Secacah,
Joshua 15:62 Nibshan, Salt City, Engedi; six cities and their villages.
Joshua 15:63 The people of Judah couldn't make the Jebusites, who lived in Jerusalem, leave; so the Jebusites live with the people of Judah in Jerusalem until now.
