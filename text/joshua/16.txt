Joshua 16:1 Joseph's descendants received land extending from the Jordan at Jericho to the eastern waters of Jericho, reaching to the desert and then to Bethel hill.
Joshua 16:2 It goes from Bethel to Luz, and goes through to the edge of Archi to Ataroth.
Joshua 16:3 It goes down west to Japhleti's border, to Bethhoron the lower border, and to Gezer; its western end is at the sea.
Joshua 16:4 Joseph's sons, Manasseh and Ephraim, received their share of the land.
Joshua 16:5 The territory for the families of Ephraim's descendants was this: their land on the east reached from Atarothaddar to Upper Bethhoron.
Joshua 16:6 The border extended toward the sea to Michmethah on the north; it turned east to Taanathshiloh, going past it to Janohah on the east.
Joshua 16:7 It went from Janohah to Ataroth and Naarath, reached Jericho, and ended at the Jordan River.
Joshua 16:8 The boundary started at Tappuah, went west to the Kanah River, and ended at the sea. This was the land for the families of Ephraim's tribe.
Joshua 16:9 The towns set aside for the Ephraim tribe were within the land given to the Manasseh tribe, including all those towns and their surrounding villages.
Joshua 16:10 They did not force the Canaanites who lived in Gezer to leave, but the Canaanites still live among the people of Ephraim today, and they have to pay taxes.
