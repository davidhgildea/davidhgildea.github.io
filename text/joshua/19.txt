Joshua 19:1 The second choice was for the tribe of Simeon's descendants, according to their families. Their land was inside the land given to Judah's descendants.
Joshua 19:2 They received Beersheba, Sheba, and Moladah as their inheritance.
Joshua 19:3 And Hazarshual, Balah, and Azem.
Joshua 19:4 Eltolad, Bethul, and Hormah,
Joshua 19:5 Ziklag, Bethmarcaboth, and Hazarsusah,
Joshua 19:6 Bethlebaoth and Sharuhen; thirteen cities with their villages.
Joshua 19:7 Ain, Remmon, Ether, and Ashan; four cities with their surrounding villages.
Joshua 19:8 All the small towns near these cities, as far as Baalathbeer and Ramath in the south, were part of the land given to the Simeon tribe's families.
Joshua 19:9 The tribe of Simeon received their land from the allotment of the tribe of Judah because Judah's portion was too large for them, so Simeon's inheritance was within Judah's land.
Joshua 19:10 The third share was for the families of Zebulun, and their territory reached to Sarid.
Joshua 19:11 Their boundary went up to the sea, through Maralah, to Dabbasheth, and to the river near Jokneam.
Joshua 19:12 It turned from Sarid to the east, towards the sunrise, to the edge of Chislothtabor, then continued to Daberath, and went up to Japhia.
Joshua 19:13 It moves on from there to the east toward Gittahhepher, to Ittahkazin, then turns toward Remmonmethoar until it reaches Neah.
Joshua 19:14 The border goes around it on the north side to Hannathon, and ends in the Jiphthahel Valley.
Joshua 19:15 Kattath, Nahallal, Shimron, Idalah, and Bethlehem: twelve cities with their villages.
Joshua 19:16 This is what the families of Zebulun's children will receive as their inheritance: these cities and their surrounding villages.
Joshua 19:17 The fourth draw was for Issachar, for the families of Issachar's children.
Joshua 19:18 Their boundary went towards Jezreel, Chesulloth, and Shunem.
Joshua 19:19 And Haphraim, Shihon, and Anaharath.
Joshua 19:20 And Rabbith, Kishion, and Abez,
Joshua 19:21 And Remeth, Engannim, Enhaddah, and Bethpazzez;
Joshua 19:22 The border touches Tabor, Shahazimah, and Bethshemesh, ending at the Jordan River: sixteen cities and their villages.
Joshua 19:23 This is what the family groups of the tribe of Issachar receive as their inheritance: the towns and their surrounding villages.
Joshua 19:24 The fifth share was for the Asher tribe family groups.
Joshua 19:25 Their boundary included Helkath, Hali, Beten, and Achshaph.
Joshua 19:26 Alammelech, Amad, and Misheal; the land goes up to Carmel in the west and to Shihorlibnath.
Joshua 19:27 It turns toward the sunrise at Bethdagon, goes to Zebulun, to the Jiphthahel Valley north of Bethemek and Neiel, and ends at Cabul on the left.
Joshua 19:28 Hebron, Rehob, Hammon, Kanah, all the way to great Zidon;
Joshua 19:29 Then the border turns to Ramah and the fortified city of Tyre; the border turns to Hosah, ending at the sea from the border to Achzib.
Joshua 19:30 Ummah, Aphek, and Rehob too: twenty-two cities and their villages.
Joshua 19:31 This is what the Asher tribe's families will receive: These towns and their surrounding villages.
Joshua 19:32 The sixth share was given to the Naphtali people, each family group among them.
Joshua 19:33 Their land extended from Heleph and Allon to Zaanannim, including Adami, Nekeb, and Jabneel, to Lakum, and ended at the Jordan River.
Joshua 19:34 Then the border turns west to Aznothtabor, goes from there to Hukkok, touches Zebulun on the south, Asher on the west, and Judah at the Jordan River to the east.
Joshua 19:35 The protected cities are Ziddim, Zer, Hammath, Rakkath, and Chinnereth.
Joshua 19:36 Adamah, Ramah, and Hazor,
Joshua 19:37 Kedesh, Edrei, and Enhazor,
Joshua 19:38 Iron, Migdalel, Horem, Bethanath, and Bethshemesh; nineteen cities with their villages.
Joshua 19:39 This is what the Naphtali tribe's families will receive as their inheritance: the towns and their surrounding villages.
Joshua 19:40 The seventh share was picked for the Dan tribe families.
Joshua 19:41 Their land included Zorah, Eshtaol, and Irshemesh.
Joshua 19:42 And Shaalabbin, and Ajalon, and Jethlah,
Joshua 19:43 Elon, Thimnathah, and Ekron,
Joshua 19:44 Eltekeh, Gibbethon, and Baalath,
Joshua 19:45 Jehud, Beneberak, and Gathrimmon,
Joshua 19:46 And Mejarkon, and Rakkon, along with the territory facing Japho.
Joshua 19:47 The land for Dan's descendants was too small, so they fought against Leshem, captured it, killed its people, took it over, lived there, and renamed Leshem as Dan after their ancestor Dan.
Joshua 19:48 This is what the Dan tribe's families inherited: these cities and their villages.
Joshua 19:49 After they finished dividing the land for inheritance by their regions, the people of Israel gave a share to Joshua, the son of Nun.
Joshua 19:50 Following God's instructions, they gave him the city he requested, Timnathserah in the hills of Ephraim. He then rebuilt the city and lived there.
Joshua 19:51 These are the lands Eleazar the priest, Joshua son of Nun, and the tribal leaders of Israel's children divided as inheritances by drawing lots in Shiloh before God, at the entrance of the meeting tent. This is how they finished dividing the land.
