Joshua 21:1 The leaders of the Levite families approached Priest Eleazar, Joshua son of Nun, and the leaders of the other Israelite families.
Joshua 21:2 They talked to them at Shiloh in Canaan, saying, "God told us through Moses to give us cities to live in, with the surrounding land for our animals."
Joshua 21:3 The Israelites gave the Levites these cities and the land around them from their own land, as God had commanded.
Joshua 21:4 The Kohathite families got their land by casting lots. Aaron's descendants, who were Levites, received thirteen cities from the territories of Judah, Simeon, and Benjamin.
Joshua 21:5 The remaining descendants of Kohath received ten cities by drawing lots from the tribes of Ephraim, Dan, and the half-tribe of Manasseh.
Joshua 21:6 Gershon's descendants received thirteen cities by lot from the tribes of Issachar, Asher, Naphtali, and from half of the tribe of Manasseh in Bashan.
Joshua 21:7 The Merari family members received twelve cities from the Reuben, Gad, and Zebulun tribes.
Joshua 21:8 The people of Israel gave cities and surrounding land to the Levites by drawing lots, just as God ordered through Moses.
Joshua 21:9 They gave cities from the tribe of Judah and the tribe of Simeon, which are listed by name here.
Joshua 21:10 Aaron's descendants from the Kohathite clan, who were part of the Levi tribe, received this because they got the first share.
Joshua 21:11 They gave them the city of Arba, Anak's father's city, called Hebron, in Judah's hill country, and the surrounding suburbs.
Joshua 21:12 They gave the land around the city and its villages to Caleb, Jephunneh's son, for him to own.
Joshua 21:13 They gave Aaron's descendants Hebron, a safe city for someone who accidentally killed a person, and the area around it, plus Libnah and its surrounding area.
Joshua 21:14 Jattir and the surrounding area, Eshtemoa and the surrounding area,
Joshua 21:15 And Holon with the land around it, and Debir with the land around it,
Joshua 21:16 And Ain and its surrounding area, Juttah and its surrounding area, and Bethshemesh and its surrounding area; nine cities from those two tribes.
Joshua 21:17 From Benjamin's tribe, the towns Gibeon and Geba with their surrounding areas.
Joshua 21:18 Anathoth and its surrounding area, and Almon and its surrounding area; four cities.
Joshua 21:19 Aaron's descendants, who were priests, had thirteen cities with the land around them.
Joshua 21:20 The Kohathite families, the remaining Levites descended from Kohath, received towns as their share from the tribe of Ephraim.
Joshua 21:21 They gave them Shechem with its surrounding land in the hill country of Ephraim, to be a city of refuge for those who killed someone by accident, and Gezer with its surrounding land.
Joshua 21:22 Kibzaim and its surrounding area, and Bethhoron and its surrounding area; four cities.
Joshua 21:23 From Dan's tribe, Eltekeh and its surrounding lands, Gibbethon and its surrounding lands,
Joshua 21:24 Aijalon and its surrounding land, Gathrimmon and its surrounding land; four cities.
Joshua 21:25 From the half tribe of Manasseh came the towns of Tanach and Gathrimmon, along with the land around them; in total, two towns.
Joshua 21:26 There were ten cities with their surrounding lands for the remaining families of Kohath's children.
Joshua 21:27 They gave the Gershonite Levites from the half-tribe of Manasseh the city of Golan in Bashan and its surrounding lands to be a safe place for those who killed someone by accident; they also gave them Beeshterah and its surrounding lands—two cities in total.
Joshua 21:28 From the tribe of Issachar, Kishon and its surrounding lands, Dabareh and its surrounding lands.
Joshua 21:29 Jarmuth with its surrounding lands, Engannim with its surrounding lands; four cities.
Joshua 21:30 From the Asher tribe, the town of Mishal and the land around it, and the town of Abdon and the land around it.
Joshua 21:31 Helkath and its surrounding lands, and Rehob and its surrounding lands; four cities.
Joshua 21:32 From the Naphtali tribe, the city of Kedesh in Galilee and the surrounding area serve as a safe city for someone who killed another; also, the towns of Hammothdor and Kartan and their surrounding areas; three cities in total.
Joshua 21:33 The Gershonite family groups had thirteen cities with the land around them.
Joshua 21:34 To Merari's descendants, the other Levite families, from Zebulun's tribe, they gave Jokneam and its surrounding land, also Kartah and its surrounding land.
Joshua 21:35 Dimnah and its nearby lands, Nahalal and its nearby lands; four cities.
Joshua 21:36 From the tribe of Reuben, the towns Bezer and Jahazah and the areas around them,
Joshua 21:37 Kedemoth and the land around it, and Mephaath and the land around it; four cities.
Joshua 21:38 From the Gad tribe, the city Ramoth in Gilead and its surrounding land became a safe city for someone who killed another person by accident; and likewise, the city Mahanaim and its surrounding land.
Joshua 21:39 Heshbon and its surrounding lands, Jazer and its surrounding lands; in total, four cities.
Joshua 21:40 All the cities given to Merari's descendants by their families, which were left from the Levite families, totaled twelve cities as their share.
Joshua 21:41 The Levites had 48 towns within Israel's lands, each with surrounding land.
Joshua 21:42 Each of these cities had surrounding areas: that's how all these cities were.
Joshua 21:43 God gave Israel all the land he promised to their ancestors; they took it over and lived there.
Joshua 21:44 God gave them peace on all sides, just as he promised their ancestors. None of their enemies could stand against them; God gave them victory over all their enemies.
Joshua 21:45 Nothing good that God promised to the people of Israel failed; everything happened.
