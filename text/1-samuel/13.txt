1 Samuel 13:1 Saul was king for one year; after two years as king of Israel,
1 Samuel 13:2 Saul picked 3,000 Israeli soldiers; 2,000 stayed with Saul in Michmash and Bethel, 1,000 were with Jonathan in Gibeah of Benjamin, and he sent the others home.
1 Samuel 13:3 Jonathan attacked the Philistine outpost at Geba, and the Philistines found out. Saul sounded the trumpet all over the country, saying, "Let the Israelites hear."
1 Samuel 13:4 Everyone in Israel heard that Saul had defeated a Philistine outpost, and that the Philistines hated Israel. So the people gathered to follow Saul to Gilgal.
1 Samuel 13:5 The Philistines came together to battle Israel with thirty thousand chariots, six thousand horsemen, and a huge crowd like the sand by the sea. They advanced and set up camp at Michmash, to the east of Bethaven.
1 Samuel 13:6 When the Israelite men realized they were in trouble because the people were distressed, they hid in caves, bushes, rocks, high areas, and pits.
1 Samuel 13:7 Some Hebrews crossed the Jordan River to Gad and Gilead, while Saul was still in Gilgal, and all the people with him were scared.
1 Samuel 13:8 He waited seven days, as Samuel had ordered, but Samuel did not come to Gilgal; and the people left him.
1 Samuel 13:9 Saul said, "Bring me a burnt offering and peace offerings." Then he gave the burnt offering.
1 Samuel 13:10 After he finished the burnt offering, Samuel arrived; Saul went out to greet him.
1 Samuel 13:11 And Samuel asked, "What have you done?" Saul answered, "I saw the people leaving me, you didn't arrive when you said you would, and the Philistines were uniting at Michmash."
1 Samuel 13:12 So I thought, 'The Philistines will soon attack me at Gilgal, and I haven't asked for God's help.' So I felt I had to go ahead and offer a sacrifice.
1 Samuel 13:13 Samuel said to Saul, "You have acted foolishly; you did not follow God's command which He gave you. Because of this, God would have made your rule over Israel last forever, but now he will not."
1 Samuel 13:14 But now your kingdom won't last. God has chosen a man who pleases Him, and God has told him to lead His people because you did not follow God's commands.
1 Samuel 13:15 Samuel got up and went from Gilgal to Gibeah in Benjamin. Saul counted the people with him, about six hundred men.
1 Samuel 13:16 Saul, his son Jonathan, and the people with them stayed in Gibeah in Benjamin's land, while the Philistines camped in Michmash.
1 Samuel 13:17 The raiders came out of the Philistines' camp in three groups: one group went toward the road to Ophrah, in Shual's territory.
1 Samuel 13:18 One group went towards Bethhoron, and another went along the road that borders the valley of Zeboim, heading to the desert.
1 Samuel 13:19 In all of Israel, no blacksmith could be found because the Philistines didn't want the Hebrews to make swords or spears.
1 Samuel 13:20 All the Israelites went to the Philistines to sharpen their plows, cutting tools, axes, and hoes.
1 Samuel 13:21 They had a tool to sharpen the hoes, plows, forks, axes, and prods.
1 Samuel 13:22 On the battle day, no one with Saul and Jonathan had a sword or spear, except for Saul and his son Jonathan.
1 Samuel 13:23 The Philistine soldiers went to the Michmash path.
