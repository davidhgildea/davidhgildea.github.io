1 Samuel 19:1 Saul told his son Jonathan and all his servants to kill David.
1 Samuel 19:2 Jonathan, Saul's son, really liked David. Jonathan warned David, "Saul, my father, wants to kill you. So please be careful and stay hidden in a secret place until morning."
1 Samuel 19:3 I will go out and stand next to my father in the field where you are, and I will talk to my father about you; and what I see, I will tell you.
1 Samuel 19:4 Jonathan spoke well of David to his father Saul, and said to him, "The king should not do wrong to his servant David; he has not sinned against you, and his actions towards you have been very good."
1 Samuel 19:5 He risked his life, killed the Philistine, and God saved all Israel; you saw it and were happy. So why would you sin and kill innocent David for no reason?
1 Samuel 19:6 Saul listened to what Jonathan said, and Saul promised, saying, "As God lives, he will not be killed."
1 Samuel 19:7 Jonathan called David and showed him everything. Jonathan took David to Saul, and David was with Saul as before.
1 Samuel 19:8 War broke out again: David went and fought the Philistines, killed many, and they ran away from him.
1 Samuel 19:9 An evil spirit from God came upon Saul as he sat in his house with his spear in hand, while David played music.
1 Samuel 19:10 Saul tried to hit David with a spear, aiming to pin him to the wall, but David dodged and the spear stuck in the wall. Then David ran away and got away that night.
1 Samuel 19:11 Saul sent people to David's house to watch and kill him in the morning. David's wife Michal warned him, "If you don't escape tonight, you will be killed tomorrow."
1 Samuel 19:12 Michal helped David escape by lowering him from a window, and he ran away and got away.
1 Samuel 19:13 Michal took an idol, placed it in the bed, put a goat hair pillow under its head, and covered it with a cloth.
1 Samuel 19:14 When Saul sent people to capture David, she said, "He is sick."
1 Samuel 19:15 Saul sent his messengers to check on David again, telling them, "Bring him to me in his bed, so I can kill him."
1 Samuel 19:16 When the messengers entered, they found an idol in the bed with a goat hair pillow as its headrest.
1 Samuel 19:17 Saul asked Michal, "Why did you trick me and let my enemy go so he could escape?" Michal replied to Saul, "He told me, 'Let me go; why should I kill you?'"
1 Samuel 19:18 David ran away, escaped, and went to Samuel in Ramah to tell him everything Saul had done to him. Then David and Samuel stayed in Naioth.
1 Samuel 19:19 Someone told Saul, "Look, David is in Naioth at Ramah."
1 Samuel 19:20 Saul sent people to capture David, but when they saw a group of prophets speaking God's messages and Samuel leading them, God's Spirit came on Saul's messengers, and they started to speak God's messages too.
1 Samuel 19:21 When Saul heard, he sent more messengers, and they also spoke God's messages. Saul sent messengers a third time, and they spoke God's messages as well.
1 Samuel 19:22 He also went to Ramah and arrived at a big well in Sechu. He asked, "Where are Samuel and David?" Someone replied, "Look, they're at Naioth in Ramah."
1 Samuel 19:23 He went to Naioth in Ramah, and God's Spirit was also upon him. He continued to speak God's messages until he reached Naioth in Ramah.
1 Samuel 19:24 He also took off his clothes, and before Samuel, he acted like a prophet. He lay there without clothes all day and night. That's why people ask, "Is Saul also a prophet?"
