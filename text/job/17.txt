Job 17:1 My breath is bad, my days are over, the graves wait for me.
Job 17:2 Aren't there people who mock me around? And doesn't my eye endure their insults?
Job 17:3 Lie down now, make a promise with me; who will agree and shake hands with me?
Job 17:4 You have kept their heart from understanding; so you will not lift them up.
Job 17:5 Whoever uses smooth talk with friends will find even their children's eyes will fail.
Job 17:6 He has made me a joke among the people; before, I was like a drum.
Job 17:7 My eyes are weak from sadness, and my whole body is like a shadow.
Job 17:8 Good people will be shocked at this, and the honest will stand up against the fake.
Job 17:9 Good people will stay on their path, and those with pure actions will become stronger and stronger.
Job 17:10 But all of you, come back now: I can't find one wise person among you.
Job 17:11 My days are gone, my plans are shattered, and so are the hopes of my heart.
Job 17:12 They turn night into day: daylight ends quickly because of the dark.
Job 17:13 If I wait, the grave becomes my home; I have prepared my bed in the dark.
Job 17:14 I told decay, "You are my father." To the worm, "You are my mother and my sister."
Job 17:15 Where is my hope now? Who will see my hope?
Job 17:16 They will go to the gates of the grave when we all rest in the dust together.
