Job 40:1 God replied to Job, saying,
Job 40:2 Can a person argue with God and teach Him? Anyone who corrects God should reply.
Job 40:3 Job replied to God and said,
Job 40:4 I realize I am worthless; what can I say to you? I will put my hand over my mouth.
Job 40:5 I have spoken once; I won't reply again; even twice; but I'll say no more.
Job 40:6 God replied to Job from the storm and said,
Job 40:7 Prepare yourself like a man; I will question you, and you must answer me.
Job 40:8 Will you cancel my decision? Will you say I am wrong so you can say you are right?
Job 40:9 Do you have an arm like God? Or can you thunder with a voice like him?
Job 40:10 Dress yourself now with greatness and honor; and put on glory and beauty.
Job 40:11 Throw out your anger and look at all the proud people; bring them down.
Job 40:12 Pay attention to everyone who is proud and make them humble; step on the evil ones where they stand.
Job 40:13 Put them all in the dust; cover their faces in darkness.
Job 40:14 Then I will agree that your own power can save you.
Job 40:15 Look at the behemoth, which I created with you; it eats grass like an ox.
Job 40:16 Look, his power is in his hips, and his strength is in his belly muscles.
Job 40:17 He swings his tail like a cedar tree; the muscles of his thighs are tightly woven.
Job 40:18 His bones are as hard as pieces of brass; his bones are like rods of iron.
Job 40:19 He is the greatest of God's creations; only his maker can threaten him.
Job 40:20 The mountains give him food, and all the wild animals play there.
Job 40:21 He lies under the shade trees, in the hiding place of the reeds and marshes.
Job 40:22 The trees cast their shade over him; the willow trees by the stream surround him.
Job 40:23 Look, he drinks up a river without rushing; he believes he can take the Jordan River into his mouth.
Job 40:24 He sees it with his eyes; his nose detects traps.
