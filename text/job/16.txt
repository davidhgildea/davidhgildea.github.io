Job 16:1 Then Job replied and spoke,
Job 16:2 I have heard many things like this: you are all terrible at comforting.
Job 16:3 Will empty talk ever stop? What makes you so bold to answer?
Job 16:4 I could talk like you if I were in your place. I could make many accusations against you and shake my head at you.
Job 16:5 I would encourage you with my words, and my talking would ease your sadness.
Job 16:6 Even if I talk, my sadness isn't lessened; and if I stay quiet, how am I comforted?
Job 16:7 But now he has made me tired: you have left all my friends alone.
Job 16:8 You have covered me with wrinkles, which stand as proof against me; my thinness is a testimony to my face.
Job 16:9 He violently attacks me in his anger, hating me; he grinds his teeth at me; my enemy glares at me with sharp eyes.
Job 16:10 They opened their mouths wide at me; they slapped my face in insult; they joined together against me.
Job 16:11 God has given me to the bad people, and put me into the hands of the evil ones.
Job 16:12 I was comfortable, but God has torn me apart; He has grabbed my neck and shattered me, and made me a target.
Job 16:13 His archers surround me, he splits my insides apart, and shows no mercy; he spills my bile on the ground.
Job 16:14 He shatters me, breaking me over and over; he charges at me like a giant.
Job 16:15 I have put rough cloth on my skin, and put my strength in the dirt.
Job 16:16 I cry so much my face is dirty, and my eyelids are dark like death.
Job 16:17 I have done nothing wrong, and my prayer is sincere.
Job 16:18 Earth, do not hide my blood, and do not let my cry go unheard.
Job 16:19 Even now, my witness is in heaven, and my evidence is up high.
Job 16:20 My friends mock me, but I cry to God.
Job 16:21 I wish someone could talk to God for a person, just as someone argues for a friend.
Job 16:22 In a few years, I will go on a path from which I won't come back.
