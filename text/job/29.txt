Job 29:1 Job kept on speaking in his story, and said,
Job 29:2 I wish I were back in the old days when God took care of me;
Job 29:3 When his light glowed above me, and I walked in the dark by his light;
Job 29:4 When I was young and God's friendship was with me in my home;
Job 29:5 When God was still with me and my children were around me;
Job 29:6 When I washed my feet with cream, and the stones gave me streams of oil;
Job 29:7 When I went through the city to the gate, when I set up my seat in the public square!
Job 29:8 The young men saw me and hid, while the older men stood up in respect.
Job 29:9 The leaders stopped speaking and put their hand over their mouth.
Job 29:10 The important people stayed quiet, and their tongue stuck to the top of their mouth.
Job 29:11 When people heard me, they praised me; when they saw me, they approved of me.
Job 29:12 I helped the poor who called out, the orphan, and the person who had no one to help them.
Job 29:13 The blessing of someone close to death came to me; and I made the widow's heart joyful.
Job 29:14 I wore what was right, and it covered me: my fairness was like a robe and a crown.
Job 29:15 I was eyes for the blind and feet for the lame.
Job 29:16 I was like a father to the poor and I investigated issues that I did not know about.
Job 29:17 I broke the wicked's jaw and took away the stolen goods from his teeth.
Job 29:18 Then I said, I will die peacefully at home, and live as long as the grains of sand.
Job 29:19 My roots stretched out to the water, and the dew stayed on my branches all night.
Job 29:20 My honor was strong within me, and my strength was restored in my grip.
Job 29:21 People listened to me, waited, and stayed quiet for my advice.
Job 29:22 After I spoke, they did not reply; my words fell on them.
Job 29:23 They waited for me like for the rain; and they opened their mouths wide for the late rain.
Job 29:24 If I smiled at them, they did not trust it; they did not make my happy face sad.
Job 29:25 I picked their path, sat as the leader, and lived like a king among the troops, comforting those who grieve.
