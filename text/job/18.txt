Job 18:1 Bildad the Shuhite replied and said,
Job 18:2 How long until you stop talking? Pay attention, and then we will speak.
Job 18:3 Why are we treated like animals and considered disgusting to you?
Job 18:4 He hurts himself in his anger: should the world be abandoned for you? And should the stone be moved from its spot?
Job 18:5 Yes, the wicked's light will go out, and their fire's spark won't shine.
Job 18:6 The light in his tent will become dark, and his lamp will go out with him.
Job 18:7 His strong steps will become tight, and his own plans will make him fall.
Job 18:8 He is trapped in a net by his own steps and walks on a trap.
Job 18:9 A trap will catch him by the foot, and a thief will defeat him.
Job 18:10 A trap is set for him on the ground, and a snare is in his path.
Job 18:11 Fears will scare him from all around and make him run away.
Job 18:12 His strength will be weakened by hunger, and ruin will stand by his side.
Job 18:13 It will eat away the power of his skin; death's firstborn will consume his power.
Job 18:14 His trust will be pulled up from his home, and it will take him to the king of fears.
Job 18:15 It will live in its tent, for it is not its own; sulfur will be spread over its home.
Job 18:16 His roots will dry up below, and his branches will be cut off above.
Job 18:17 People will forget him on earth, and his name will not be spoken in public.
Job 18:18 He will be forced from light to darkness, and driven away from the world.
Job 18:19 He won't have a son or nephew among his people, or anyone left in his home.
Job 18:20 Those who follow him will be amazed at his time, just as those before were terrified.
Job 18:21 These are surely the homes of the evil, and this is where someone who does not know God lives.
