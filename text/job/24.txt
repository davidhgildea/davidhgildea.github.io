Job 24:1 Why, if God knows all time, do those who know him not recognize his days?
Job 24:2 Some people move boundary stones; they forcefully steal herds of animals and eat from them.
Job 24:3 They chase away the orphan's donkey and take the widow's ox as collateral.
Job 24:4 They push the needy off the road; the world's poor all hide away.
Job 24:5 Look, like wild donkeys in the desert, they go out to work; they get up early to find food: the wild land provides food for them and their children.
Job 24:6 Everyone harvests their grain in the field, and they collect the wicked's grapes.
Job 24:7 They make the naked sleep without clothes, so they have no cover when it's cold.
Job 24:8 They are soaked by the mountain rains and hug the rocks because they have no shelter.
Job 24:9 They snatch away the orphans and demand collateral from the poor.
Job 24:10 They make him go without clothes and take the bundle of grain from the hungry.
Job 24:11 They make oil inside their cities, press their grapes for wine, and still feel thirsty.
Job 24:12 People groan in the city, and the hurt souls cry out; but God does not charge them with wrongdoing.
Job 24:13 They are among those who resist the light; they do not know its ways or stay on its paths.
Job 24:14 The killer gets up at dawn to kill the poor and needy, and at night he acts like a thief.
Job 24:15 The one who is unfaithful with their partner waits for night, thinking no one will see them, and covers their face.
Job 24:16 At night they break into houses they had picked out in the day; they do not know the light.
Job 24:17 Morning feels like the shadow of death to them; when recognized, they are terrified by this shadow of death.
Job 24:18 He is fast like the rivers; their land is cursed on the earth: he does not see the path of the vineyards.
Job 24:19 Dryness and warmth dry up the snow's water; just as the grave takes those who have sinned.
Job 24:20 The body will forget him; worms will eat him up; he will not be remembered; and evil will be broken like a tree.
Job 24:21 He treats the childless woman badly and does not help the widow.
Job 24:22 He pulls in the strong with his power: he stands up, and nobody can be sure of living.
Job 24:23 Even if someone feels safe and relaxed, God still watches their actions.
Job 24:24 They are raised up for a short time, but then they vanish and are brought down; they are removed just like everyone else, and cut off like the tops of the stalks of grain.
Job 24:25 If this is not true now, who will prove me wrong and show that my words are worthless?
