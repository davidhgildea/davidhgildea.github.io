Job 37:1 My heart shakes with fear and moves out of its place.
Job 37:2 Listen carefully to his voice and the noise that comes from his mouth.
Job 37:3 He guides it under the whole sky, and his lightning to the ends of the earth.
Job 37:4 After it, a voice roars: God thunders with his majestic voice, and does not hold back when his voice is heard.
Job 37:5 God thunders wonderfully with his voice; he does great things we cannot understand.
Job 37:6 He tells the snow to fall on the earth, and the same to the light rain and the heavy rain of his power.
Job 37:7 He closes the hands of every person so everyone can know His deeds.
Job 37:8 Then the animals go into their dens and stay there.
Job 37:9 From the south comes a strong wind, and from the north comes cold.
Job 37:10 God's breath brings frost, and the surface of the waters becomes tight.
Job 37:11 God tires out the heavy cloud by adding water to it; he spreads his light cloud around.
Job 37:12 It moves as God plans, so they can do everything he tells them on the earth.
Job 37:13 He makes it happen, whether for discipline, for his land, or for kindness.
Job 37:14 Listen to this, Job: be still and think about God's amazing deeds.
Job 37:15 Do you know when God arranged them and made the light of his cloud shine?
Job 37:16 Do you know how the clouds stay balanced, the amazing things done by the one who has perfect knowledge?
Job 37:17 Your clothes become warm when God calms the earth with the south wind.
Job 37:18 Have you helped God spread out the sky, which is solid, like a mirror made of melted metal?
Job 37:19 Show us what to say to God; we don't know how to speak properly because we are confused.
Job 37:20 Should I tell God what I say? If someone speaks, they will certainly be overwhelmed.
Job 37:21 People cannot see the bright light in the clouds, but the wind comes and clears them away.
Job 37:22 Clear skies come from the north; with God is awesome greatness.
Job 37:23 About God, we cannot fully understand him: he is great in power, fair in judgment, and full of justice: he does not cause suffering.
Job 37:24 People fear God because he doesn't favor those who think they are wise.
