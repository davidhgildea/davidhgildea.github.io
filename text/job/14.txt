Job 14:1 A person born from a woman lives only a short time and has many problems.
Job 14:2 He blooms like a flower and then is trimmed. He also flees like a shadow and does not stay.
Job 14:3 Do you watch such a person and bring me to trial with you?
Job 14:4 Who can make a clean thing from an unclean one? No one.
Job 14:5 God has set the number of his days, and knows how many months he will live. You have decided his limits, which he cannot go beyond.
Job 14:6 Let him alone so he can have peace until he finishes his workday like a worker.
Job 14:7 A tree has hope because if it's cut down, it can grow back, and its new shoots won't stop.
Job 14:8 Even if its roots grow old in the earth and its stump dies in the ground;
Job 14:9 Even if it smells water, it will sprout and grow branches like a plant.
Job 14:10 When a person dies, they decay; when they stop breathing, where do they go?
Job 14:11 As the water disappears from the sea, and a riverbed becomes dry and empty:
Job 14:12 People lie down and do not rise again; until the sky is gone, they will not wake up or be woken from their sleep.
Job 14:13 I wish you would hide me in the grave and keep me hidden until your anger is over, then set a time to remember me.
Job 14:14 If someone dies, will they live again? I will wait for all the days set for me until it is my turn to change.
Job 14:15 You will call, and I will answer you; you will long for what your hands have made.
Job 14:16 You count my steps now; are you not keeping watch on my sin?
Job 14:17 My wrong is locked in a bag, and you stitch up my sin.
Job 14:18 Mountains crumble and disappear, and rocks are moved from their spots.
Job 14:19 Water wears down stones; you wash away the soil's growth; you crush human hopes.
Job 14:20 You always win against him, and he goes away; you change his face, and send him off.
Job 14:21 His sons achieve honor but he doesn't know it; they are humbled, yet he doesn't notice.
Job 14:22 His body will feel pain, and his heart will be sad.
