Job 7:1 Doesn't everyone have a set time to live on earth? Aren't their days like those of a worker?
Job 7:2 Like a worker who really wants rest, and like an employee eager for their pay.
Job 7:3 I am given months of emptiness and nights of trouble are set for me.
Job 7:4 When I go to bed, I think, "When will I get up and the night be over?" I am restless until morning comes.
Job 7:5 My body is covered with worms and dirt clumps; my skin is cracked and disgusting.
Job 7:6 My days go by faster than a weaver's tool, and they end without hope.
Job 7:7 Please remember my life is like the wind: I will not see happiness again.
Job 7:8 The person who has seen me won't see me again: you watch me, but I'm gone.
Job 7:9 Just like a cloud disappears and is gone, so is the person who dies and will not rise again.
Job 7:10 He will not go back to his home, and his place will not recognize him anymore.
Job 7:11 So I won't hold back my words; I will speak because my spirit is in pain; I will express my soul's deep sorrow.
Job 7:12 Am I the sea, or a big fish, that you keep watch over me?
Job 7:13 When I say, "My bed will comfort me, my sofa will soothe my worries,"
Job 7:14 You frighten me with dreams and scare me with visions.
Job 7:15 So my soul prefers suffocating, and death over my life.
Job 7:16 I hate it; I don't want to live forever: leave me alone; my days are pointless.
Job 7:17 Who is a person that you make so important and think so much about them?
Job 7:18 You visit him every morning and test him every moment?
Job 7:19 How long will you not leave me, or give me a moment's peace?
Job 7:20 I have sinned; what should I do to you, God, protector of people? Why have you made me your target, so that I am a problem to myself?
Job 7:21 Why don't you forgive my wrong and remove my sin? For soon I will lie in the earth; you will look for me in the morning, but I won't be there.
