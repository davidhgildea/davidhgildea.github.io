Job 19:1 Then Job replied and spoke,
Job 19:2 How long will you trouble me and crush me with your words?
Job 19:3 You have insulted me ten times; you're not ashamed that you've been treating me like a stranger.
Job 19:4 If I have truly made a mistake, it stays with me.
Job 19:5 If you really want to act superior to me and argue against my disgrace:
Job 19:6 Understand that God has defeated me and has surrounded me with his net.
Job 19:7 I shout because of injustice, but no one listens; I cry out, but there is no fairness.
Job 19:8 God has blocked my path so I can't get through, and has made my ways dark.
Job 19:9 He has taken away my honor and removed the crown from my head.
Job 19:10 God has destroyed me on every side, and I am gone; and he has taken away my hope like a tree.
Job 19:11 He has also ignited his anger against me, and he considers me as one of his enemies.
Job 19:12 His soldiers gather, march against me, and camp around my tent.
Job 19:13 He has made my brothers stay away from me, and my friends have truly become strangers to me.
Job 19:14 My relatives have let me down, and my close friends have ignored me.
Job 19:15 Those who live in my house and my servants see me as a stranger; I am like a foreigner to them.
Job 19:16 I called my worker, but he did not respond; I pleaded with him aloud.
Job 19:17 My wife is disgusted by my breath, even though I pleaded for the sake of my own children.
Job 19:18 Even young children looked down on me; when I stood up, they talked against me.
Job 19:19 All my close friends hate me, and those I loved have turned against me.
Job 19:20 My bones stick to my skin and flesh, and I barely escaped with my life.
Job 19:21 Feel sorry for me, my friends, because God has struck me.
Job 19:22 Why do you attack me like God does, and aren't happy with just hurting my body?
Job 19:23 I wish my words were written down! I wish they were recorded in a book!
Job 19:24 They were carved with an iron tool and filled with lead in the rock forever.
Job 19:25 I know that my savior is alive, and he will stand on the earth on the last day.
Job 19:26 Even after my skin is eaten by worms and my body is gone, I will still see God with my own body.
Job 19:27 I will see God myself, my own eyes will look at him, and not a stranger's; even if I am wasting away inside.
Job 19:28 But you should ask, "Why do we persecute him, when the heart of the problem lies within me?"
Job 19:29 Be afraid of the sword: anger brings sword punishments, so you know there is a judgment.
