Job 4:1 Then Eliphaz from Teman replied,
Job 4:2 If we try to talk with you, will you be upset? But who can stop themselves from speaking?
Job 4:3 Look, you have taught many people, and you have given strength to tired hands.
Job 4:4 Your words have supported the one who was falling, and you have made weak knees strong.
Job 4:5 Now it has happened to you, and you are weak; it affects you, and you are upset.
Job 4:6 Isn't this what you respect, what you trust, what you hope for, and how right you act?
Job 4:7 Please remember, who has ever died being innocent? Or when have the good people been destroyed?
Job 4:8 I have seen that those who do bad things and spread evil will get the same in return.
Job 4:9 With God's powerful breath, they are destroyed, and by his breath, they vanish.
Job 4:10 The lion's roar, the fierce lion's voice, and the young lions' teeth are all broken.
Job 4:11 The old lion dies because it has no food, and the strong lion's cubs are spread out far away.
Job 4:12 Someone secretly told me something, and I heard a bit of it.
Job 4:13 In thoughts from night visions, when deep sleep falls on people,
Job 4:14 I felt afraid and shook all over, making my bones tremble.
Job 4:15 Suddenly, a spirit moved in front of me, and the hair on my body stood up.
Job 4:16 It stood still, but I couldn't see its shape clearly: a figure was in front of my eyes, everything was quiet, and I heard a voice speaking.
Job 4:17 Can a human be more fair than God? Can someone be purer than the one who created him?
Job 4:18 Look, he doesn't trust his servants, and he accuses his angels of errors.
Job 4:19 How much less can those living in clay houses trust in themselves, when their very foundation is dust and they can be destroyed as easily as a moth?
Job 4:20 They are ruined from dawn to dusk; they vanish forever without anyone caring.
Job 4:21 Doesn't their greatness leave them? They die without wisdom.
