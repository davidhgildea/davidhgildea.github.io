Job 13:1 I have seen all this with my eyes; my ears have heard and understood it.
Job 13:2 I know what you know; I am not less than you.
Job 13:3 I want to talk to God Almighty, and I wish to discuss with God.
Job 13:4 You create lies and your healing is worthless.
Job 13:5 I wish you would all be completely quiet! It would be wise for you.
Job 13:6 Listen to my arguments and pay attention to what I say.
Job 13:7 Will you speak wrongly for God and talk with lies for him?
Job 13:8 Will you favor him? Will you argue for God?
Job 13:9 Is it right for him to investigate you? Like one person teases another, do you tease him?
Job 13:10 God will certainly correct you if you unfairly favor some people in secret.
Job 13:11 Won't his greatness scare you? And his fear come on you?
Job 13:12 Your memories are like ashes, your forms like clay.
Job 13:13 Stay quiet, leave me be, so I can talk, and whatever happens, let it happen.
Job 13:14 Why do I risk my life and take my own life into my hands?
Job 13:15 Even if he kills me, I will still trust in God; but I will defend my own actions to him.
Job 13:16 He will also be my savior, because a hypocrite will not come before Him.
Job 13:17 Listen carefully to what I say and hear my explanation with your ears.
Job 13:18 Look, I have prepared my case; I know I will be proven right.
Job 13:19 Who will argue with me? For if I stay silent now, I will die.
Job 13:20 Please don't do two things to me; then I won't hide from you.
Job 13:21 Stop punishing me, and don't scare me anymore.
Job 13:22 Then you call, and I will answer; or let me talk, and you reply to me.
Job 13:23 What are my wrongs and sins? Show me what I have done wrong and my sin.
Job 13:24 Why do you hide your face and treat me like your enemy?
Job 13:25 Will you break a leaf blown back and forth? And will you chase the dry straw?
Job 13:26 You write harsh things against me and make me inherit the sins of my youth.
Job 13:27 You put my feet in chains and watch all my ways closely; you leave a mark on the soles of my feet.
Job 13:28 He decays like something rotten, like clothing eaten by moths.
