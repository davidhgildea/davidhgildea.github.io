Job 11:1 Zophar from Naama replied, saying,
Job 11:2 Shouldn't many words get a reply? And should a chatty person be right?
Job 11:3 Do your lies make people stay silent? When you mock, doesn't anyone make you feel ashamed?
Job 11:4 You have said, "My teaching is pure, and I am clean in your sight."
Job 11:5 I wish God would speak and tell you off.
Job 11:6 God wants to show you the deep secrets of wisdom, which are twice as much as what exists. Understand then that God asks from you less than your wrongs deserve.
Job 11:7 Can you find out about God by searching? Can you fully understand the Almighty?
Job 11:8 It's as high as the sky; what can you do? It's deeper than the deepest place; what can you know?
Job 11:9 Its size is longer than the earth and wider than the sea.
Job 11:10 If he decides, closes in, or brings together, then who can stop him?
Job 11:11 For he knows foolish people: he sees evil too; won't he think about it?
Job 11:12 Empty-headed people want to seem wise, even though they are born as wild as a donkey's foal.
Job 11:13 If you get your heart ready and reach out your hands to him;
Job 11:14 If you hold wrongdoing in your hand, put it far from you, and do not let evil live in your homes.
Job 11:15 You will be able to hold your head high without guilt; yes, you will be steady and without fear.
Job 11:16 You will forget your troubles, and think of them like water that has gone by.
Job 11:17 Your future will be brighter than midday: you will glow, you will be like the dawn.
Job 11:18 You will be safe because there is hope; yes, you will look around and lie down without fear.
Job 11:19 You will lie down without fear; many will come to ask for your favor.
Job 11:20 The wicked will lose their sight, won't get away, and their hope will vanish like a dying breath.
