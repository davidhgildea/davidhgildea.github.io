Job 22:1 Then Eliphaz from Teman replied,
Job 22:2 Can a person be useful to God, as a wise person is useful to themselves?
Job 22:3 Does it make God happy when you are good, or does He benefit when you live perfectly?
Job 22:4 Will God correct you because he fears you? Will he go to court with you?
Job 22:5 Isn't your evilness huge? And your sins endless?
Job 22:6 You took a promise from your brother for no reason and took clothes from those with none.
Job 22:7 You did not give water to the tired to drink, and you kept bread from the hungry.
Job 22:8 The strong person owned the land, and the respected person lived on it.
Job 22:9 You sent widows away with nothing, and you have crushed the strength of orphans.
Job 22:10 So traps surround you, and sudden fear upsets you.
Job 22:11 Or darkness, so you can't see, and lots of water covers you.
Job 22:12 Isn't God in the highest part of heaven? And see how high the stars are!
Job 22:13 And you ask, "How does God know? Can he judge behind the dark cloud?"
Job 22:14 Dense clouds hide God so he cannot be seen; and he moves in the sky's expanse.
Job 22:15 Have you noticed the old path that bad people have walked on?
Job 22:16 They were cut down too soon, and their foundation was washed away by a flood.
Job 22:17 They told God to go away: and what can God do for them?
Job 22:18 He filled their homes with good things, but I stay away from the plans of the wicked.
Job 22:19 Good people see it and are happy; those who are not guilty mock them.
Job 22:20 Our resources are not destroyed, but the fire burns what's left of them.
Job 22:21 Get to know God now and find peace; this will bring you goodness.
Job 22:22 Please accept the teachings from God, and keep his words in your heart.
Job 22:23 If you come back to God, you will be restored, and you will keep sin far from your home.
Job 22:24 You will pile up gold like dust, and the gold of Ophir like pebbles from the streams.
Job 22:25 Yes, God will protect you, and you will have lots of silver.
Job 22:26 Then you will find joy in God and you can look up to Him with confidence.
Job 22:27 Pray to God, and he will listen to you, and you must keep your promises.
Job 22:28 You will decide on something, and it will happen for you: light will shine on your path.
Job 22:29 When people feel defeated, you should say, "There is hope"; and God will save the humble.
Job 22:30 He will save the land of the innocent; it is saved because of your clean hands.
