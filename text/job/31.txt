Job 31:1 I promised myself not to look with desire; so why should I think about a young woman?
Job 31:2 What does a person get from God above? And what does the Almighty give from high up?
Job 31:3 Isn't destruction meant for the wicked, and an unusual punishment for those who do wrong?
Job 31:4 Doesn't God see how I live and count every step I take?
Job 31:5 If I have lived with falsehood or hurried to deceive;
Job 31:6 Let me be measured in a fair scale so God can see my honesty.
Job 31:7 If I have gone off the path, and my heart followed what I saw, and if any stain has stuck to my hands;
Job 31:8 Let me plant seeds, but let someone else eat the harvest; yes, let my descendants be destroyed.
Job 31:9 If my heart was misled by a woman, or if I waited secretly at my neighbor's door;
Job 31:10 Let my wife work for another man, and let other men be with her.
Job 31:11 This is a terrible crime, yes, it's a wrongdoing that the judges must punish.
Job 31:12 It is a fire that burns until everything is destroyed and would remove all my growth.
Job 31:13 If I ignored my male or female servant's complaint against me;
Job 31:14 What should I do when God stands up? And when He comes to check, what will I say to Him?
Job 31:15 Didn't the same God who created me in the womb also create him? And didn't one shape both of us inside the womb?
Job 31:16 If I have denied the poor what they wanted, or made the widow's eyes weary;
Job 31:17 I did not eat my food alone and ignored the orphans; they ate with me.
Job 31:18 Since I was young, he grew up with me like a father, and I've guided her since I was in my mother's womb.
Job 31:19 If I have seen anyone die because they had no clothes, or any poor person without a blanket;
Job 31:20 If he didn't thank me or wasn't warmed by my sheep's wool;
Job 31:21 If I raised my hand against an orphan when I saw my support at the city gate:
Job 31:22 Let my arm drop off from my shoulder, and my arm be broken from its bone.
Job 31:23 I was scared of being destroyed by God, and because he is so great, I couldn't bear it.
Job 31:24 If I have put my hope in gold or told pure gold, 'You are my trust;'
Job 31:25 If I was happy because I was very rich and my own efforts brought me a lot;
Job 31:26 If I looked at the sun shining or the moon moving brightly;
Job 31:27 My heart was secretly tempted, or my mouth kissed my hand.
Job 31:28 This would also be a sin that a judge must punish, because I would have denied God above.
Job 31:29 If I was happy when my enemy was destroyed, or felt proud when bad things happened to him:
Job 31:30 I have not let my mouth sin by cursing his life.
Job 31:31 If the people in my home never said, "We wish we had his meat!" they are not content.
Job 31:32 The traveler did not sleep in the street; I opened my doors to them.
Job 31:33 If I hid my wrongs like Adam, keeping my sins close to me:
Job 31:34 Was I scared of many people, or did the scorn of families frighten me, so that I stayed quiet and didn't go outside?
Job 31:35 I wish someone would listen to me! Look, what I want is for God to respond to me, and for my opponent to have written down his accusations.
Job 31:36 I would surely carry it on my shoulder and wear it like a crown.
Job 31:37 I would tell him how many steps I have taken; like a leader, I would approach him.
Job 31:38 If my land cries out against me or its furrows also speak out;
Job 31:39 If I have eaten its fruits without paying, or if I have caused the owners to lose their life:
Job 31:40 Let weeds grow instead of wheat, and useless plants instead of barley. Job has finished speaking.
