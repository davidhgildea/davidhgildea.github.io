Job 9:1 Then Job replied and spoke,
Job 9:2 I know it's true: but how can a person be right with God?
Job 9:3 If he argues with God, he cannot reply to even one out of a thousand questions.
Job 9:4 He is smart in his heart and very strong: who has resisted him and succeeded?
Job 9:5 God moves the mountains without their knowing and turns them over in anger.
Job 9:6 God shakes the earth from its place, making its pillars tremble.
Job 9:7 Who commands the sun not to rise and seals the stars.
Job 9:8 Who alone stretches out the sky and walks on the sea's waves.
Job 9:9 God made the stars Arcturus, Orion, and Pleiades and the hidden places of the south.
Job 9:10 God does great things beyond understanding and countless wonders.
Job 9:11 Look, he moves past me, but I don't see him; he goes by, but I don't notice him.
Job 9:12 Look, when God takes something, who can stop Him? Who will ask Him, "What are you doing?"
Job 9:13 If God does not stop being angry, the prideful supporters must bow down to him.
Job 9:14 How much less can I reply to God and carefully select my words to argue with Him?
Job 9:15 Even if I were right, I would not respond but ask for mercy from my judge.
Job 9:16 If I had called and he had answered me, I still wouldn't believe that he listened to my voice.
Job 9:17 He crushes me with a storm and gives me many wounds for no reason.
Job 9:18 He won't let me catch my breath, but fills me with sadness.
Job 9:19 If I talk about power, God is powerful; and if about fairness, who can schedule a time for my case?
Job 9:20 If I claim to be right, my own words will show I'm wrong: if I claim to be perfect, they will show I'm not.
Job 9:21 Even if I were perfect, I still would not understand myself; I would hate my life.
Job 9:22 I said this because God destroys both the good and the bad.
Job 9:23 If a disaster kills unexpectedly, God will mock when the innocent are tested.
Job 9:24 God has let wicked people control the earth; they hide the truth from its judges. If this isn't so, who else could it be?
Job 9:25 Now my days pass faster than a messenger: they rush by and see nothing good.
Job 9:26 They have gone by like fast ships, like an eagle swooping down on its food.
Job 9:27 If I decide to forget my troubles, to put aside my sadness, and to cheer myself up:
Job 9:28 I am scared of all my troubles; I know you will not find me innocent.
Job 9:29 If I am bad, why do I work for nothing?
Job 9:30 If I clean myself with snow and wash my hands very well;
Job 9:31 You will push me into a pit, and my own clothes will disgust me.
Job 9:32 He is not a person like me that I could reply to him or that we could meet in court.
Job 9:33 There is no mediator between us to put a hand on both of us.
Job 9:34 Let him stop using his rod on me, so his fear won't scare me.
Job 9:35 I would talk without being afraid of him; but that is not my situation.
