Job 39:1 Do you know when the wild goats give birth? Can you tell when the deer are having their young?
Job 39:2 Can you count how many months they need? Or do you know when they give birth?
Job 39:3 They bend down, they give birth to their young, they let go of their pain.
Job 39:4 Their young are healthy, they grow up with grain; they leave and do not come back.
Job 39:5 Who set the wild donkey free? Who untied the ropes of the wild donkey?
Job 39:6 I made the wilderness his house and the empty land his home.
Job 39:7 He mocks the city's crowd and ignores the driver's shouts.
Job 39:8 The mountains are where his animals graze, and he looks for every plant that is green.
Job 39:9 Will the unicorn want to work for you or stay by your feeding trough?
Job 39:10 Can you tie the unicorn with a rope in the fields? Or will he plow the valleys behind you?
Job 39:11 Will you trust him because he is strong? Or will you rely on him to do your work?
Job 39:12 Will you trust him to bring back your crops and collect them into your barn?
Job 39:13 Did you give the beautiful wings to the peacocks? Or the wings and feathers to the ostrich?
Job 39:14 She leaves her eggs on the ground and keeps them warm with the dirt.
Job 39:15 And forgets that a foot might crush them, or a wild animal might destroy them.
Job 39:16 She is cold toward her young, as if they were not hers; her hard work is pointless without concern.
Job 39:17 God has not given her wisdom, nor has he given her understanding.
Job 39:18 When she rises up, she looks down on the horse and its rider.
Job 39:19 Did you give the horse its strength? Did you make its neck strong like thunder?
Job 39:20 Can you scare him like a grasshopper? His strong breath is frightening.
Job 39:21 He digs in the valley, happy in his power; he moves towards the armed men.
Job 39:22 He laughs at fear, is not scared, and does not turn away from the sword.
Job 39:23 The arrows shake against him, the shining spear and the shield.
Job 39:24 He charges across the land with anger and fury; he does not think the trumpet sound is real.
Job 39:25 He says among the trumpets, "Ha, ha"; and he senses the battle from far away, the noise of the commanders, and the yelling.
Job 39:26 Does the hawk fly by your wisdom and spread its wings to the south?
Job 39:27 Does the eagle fly up on your order and build her nest up high?
Job 39:28 She lives and stays on the rock, at the top of the cliff, and in the secure spot.
Job 39:29 From there she looks for food, and her eyes see from far away.
Job 39:30 Her young ones also drink blood: and where there are dead bodies, there she is.
