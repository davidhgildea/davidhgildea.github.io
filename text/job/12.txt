Job 12:1 Job answered, saying,
Job 12:2 You must be the people, and wisdom will die with you.
Job 12:3 I understand as much as you do; I am not less than you. Indeed, who doesn't know things like these?
Job 12:4 I am like someone mocked by their neighbor, who calls on God, and he answers: the honest, good person is made fun of.
Job 12:5 Someone who is about to fall is like a lamp that someone comfortable looks down on.
Job 12:6 Thieves live comfortably, and those who challenge God feel safe; into their hands, God gives plenty.
Job 12:7 Ask the animals, and they will teach you; ask the birds of the sky, and they will tell you.
Job 12:8 Talk to the earth, it will teach you; and the fish in the sea will tell you.
Job 12:9 Everyone knows that God has done all this.
Job 12:10 God holds the life of every creature and the breath of all people.
Job 12:11 Doesn't the ear test words? And the mouth taste its food?
Job 12:12 Old people have wisdom; with many years comes understanding.
Job 12:13 He has wisdom and power; he gives advice and understanding.
Job 12:14 Look, he destroys, and it cannot be rebuilt; he imprisons a person, and there can be no escape.
Job 12:15 Look, he stops the waters, and they dry up; he also sends them out, and they flood the land.
Job 12:16 He has power and knowledge: both the tricked and the trickster belong to him.
Job 12:17 He leads advisors away as captives and turns judges into fools.
Job 12:18 He frees kings from their chains and ties a belt around their waists.
Job 12:19 He leads rulers away defeated, and overthrows the powerful.
Job 12:20 God takes away the speech of the reliable and removes the wisdom of the old.
Job 12:21 God shows scorn for rulers and reduces the power of the strong.
Job 12:22 He reveals deep truths from darkness and brings the shadow of death into the light.
Job 12:23 He makes nations grow, and then destroys them; he expands nations, and then reduces them.
Job 12:24 God removes the understanding of the world's leaders and makes them wander in a place without paths.
Job 12:25 They feel around in the dark with no light, and he causes them to stumble like a drunk person.
