Job 6:1 But Job replied and spoke,
Job 6:2 I wish my sorrow could be fully measured, and my troubles put on scales at the same time.
Job 6:3 It's heavier than sea sand now; that's why I can't express my words.
Job 6:4 God's arrows are inside me, their poison drains my spirit; God's terrors line up against me.
Job 6:5 Does the wild donkey call out when it has grass? Or does the ox moo over its feed?
Job 6:6 Can you eat tasteless food without salt? Or is there any flavor in egg whites?
Job 6:7 The things I didn't want to touch now feel like my sad food.
Job 6:8 I wish I could get what I ask for, and that God would give me what I deeply desire.
Job 6:9 I wish God would decide to crush me, release his grip, and end my life.
Job 6:10 Then I would still be comforted. Even though I suffer, I would be brave. Let God not hold back, because I have not hidden the words of God.
Job 6:11 What strength do I have to hope? What is my future that I should extend my life?
Job 6:12 Am I as strong as rocks? Is my body made of bronze?
Job 6:13 Isn't my help within me? And has wisdom completely left me?
Job 6:14 A friend should be kind to someone who is suffering, but he ignores respecting God.
Job 6:15 My brothers have been as unreliable as a stream, like the water in streams that disappears.
Job 6:16 They are dark because of the ice, and snow is hidden in them.
Job 6:17 When they get warm, they disappear; when it's hot, they are gone from where they were.
Job 6:18 Their paths have changed direction; they lead to nowhere and end in destruction.
Job 6:19 The soldiers from Tema searched, and the groups from Sheba waited for them.
Job 6:20 They were confused because they had hope; they went there and felt embarrassed.
Job 6:21 You are now worth nothing; you see my downfall, and you are scared.
Job 6:22 Did I ask you to bring something to me or to give me a gift from what you own?
Job 6:23 Save me from my enemy’s grasp, or rescue me from the clutches of the powerful?
Job 6:24 Instruct me, and I will be quiet; show me where I have made mistakes.
Job 6:25 How powerful are true words! But what does your arguing prove?
Job 6:26 Do you think you can correct mere words when someone in despair speaks as if they are just wind?
Job 6:27 Yes, you overpower the orphans and trap your friend.
Job 6:28 So now, be satisfied and look at me; it is clear to you if I am lying.
Job 6:29 Please come back, don't think it's wrong; yes, come back, my goodness is in it.
Job 6:30 Is there wrongdoing in my words? Can't I tell what is wrong?
