Job 34:1 Elihu replied and said,
Job 34:2 Listen to me, you wise people; pay attention, you who have understanding.
Job 34:3 The ear tests words like the mouth tastes food.
Job 34:4 Let's decide what is fair; let's understand together what is good.
Job 34:5 Job has said, "I am righteous," but God has taken away my right to a fair judgment.
Job 34:6 Should I lie against what is right? My injury is incurable, though I did no wrong.
Job 34:7 Who is like Job, who drinks up mockery as easily as water?
Job 34:8 Who spends time with those who do wrong and walks with evil people.
Job 34:9 He has said, "It benefits a person nothing to enjoy being with God."
Job 34:10 Listen to me, wise men: God would never do evil, and the All-Powerful would never act unjustly.
Job 34:11 God will repay a person for what they have done and give each one what their actions deserve.
Job 34:12 Yes, surely God will not act wrongly, nor will the Almighty twist justice.
Job 34:13 Who has put him in charge of the earth? Or who has arranged the whole world?
Job 34:14 If God decides to focus on a person, if he takes back that person's life and breath;
Job 34:15 Everyone will die at the same time, and humans will become dust again.
Job 34:16 If you understand now, listen to this: pay attention to what I am saying.
Job 34:17 Can someone who hates justice rule? Will you condemn the one who is completely fair?
Job 34:18 Is it right to tell a king, "You are bad" or to rulers, "You are evil"?
Job 34:19 How much less to someone who doesn't favor leaders or value the rich over the poor? For they are all created by God.
Job 34:20 Suddenly they will die, the people will panic at midnight and vanish; and the powerful will be removed effortlessly.
Job 34:21 God watches over people's paths and sees everything they do.
Job 34:22 There is no darkness or shadow of death where the wrongdoers can hide.
Job 34:23 God will not give a person more than is fair, so that they would have to argue with God.
Job 34:24 He will shatter countless strong men and replace them with others.
Job 34:25 So he knows what they do, and he turns things upside down at night, destroying them.
Job 34:26 He strikes them as bad people for all to see.
Job 34:27 They turned away from God and did not think about any of His ways.
Job 34:28 So they make the poor's cries reach God, and he hears the cries of those who are suffering.
Job 34:29 When God brings peace, who can cause trouble? And when God hides, who can see him? It doesn't matter if it's a whole country or just one person.
Job 34:30 Let not the false person rule, so the people won't be trapped.
Job 34:31 It is right to tell God, "I have accepted punishment, I will not sin again."
Job 34:32 Teach me what I do not see; if I have done wrong, I will not do it again.
Job 34:33 Should it go as you want? God will repay it, whether you refuse or choose; not me. So say what you know.
Job 34:34 Let people with understanding tell me, and let a wise person listen to me.
Job 34:35 Job spoke without understanding, and his words lacked wisdom.
Job 34:36 I want Job to be tested to the limit because of how he answers for evil people.
Job 34:37 He makes his sin worse by rebelling, applauds himself in our presence, and speaks many words against God.
