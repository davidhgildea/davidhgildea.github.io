Job 30:1 Now, younger people mock me, people whose fathers I wouldn't have let sit with my sheepdogs.
Job 30:2 Yes, how could the strength of their hands help me, when they were worn out by old age?
Job 30:3 They were alone, suffering from hunger and need; they ran away to the empty, barren wilderness long ago.
Job 30:4 They gathered plants by the bushes and used juniper roots for food.
Job 30:5 They were forced out from where people lived; people shouted at them as they would at a thief.
Job 30:6 To live in valley cliffs, earth caves, and among the rocks.
Job 30:7 In the bushes they cried out; under the thorns they huddled together.
Job 30:8 They were kids of fools, yes, kids of lowly men; they were worse than dirt.
Job 30:9 Now I am their joke; yes, I am their mocking word.
Job 30:10 They hate me, they run away from me, and they don't hesitate to spit in my face.
Job 30:11 Because he has untied my rope and made me suffer, they have also removed the restraint in my presence.
Job 30:12 Young people gather on my right side; they knock me off my feet, and they build up paths to harm me.
Job 30:13 They block my way, they increase my trouble, they have no helper.
Job 30:14 They attacked me like a flood breaking through: in my ruin, they overwhelmed me.
Job 30:15 Fears attack me; they chase my soul like the wind: and my well-being disappears like a cloud.
Job 30:16 My soul is poured out inside me; days of suffering grip me.
Job 30:17 At night, my bones hurt deeply, and my muscles get no rest.
Job 30:18 My sickness is so strong it changes my clothes; it wraps around me like my coat's collar.
Job 30:19 He has thrown me into the mud, and I have become like dust and ashes.
Job 30:20 I call to you, but you don't hear me; I stand up, but you don't notice me.
Job 30:21 You have become harsh to me; with your strong hand, you stand against me.
Job 30:22 You lift me up to the wind; you make me ride on it, and you melt away my strength.
Job 30:23 I know you will lead me to death, to the place set for everyone who lives.
Job 30:24 However, he will not reach out to the grave when they call out as they perish.
Job 30:25 Didn't I cry for the person who was suffering? Wasn't I sad for the poor?
Job 30:26 When I hoped for good, evil came to me; and when I waited for light, darkness arrived.
Job 30:27 I felt deep turmoil inside and could not rest; my days of trouble came before I was ready.
Job 30:28 I walked sadly without the sun; I stood up and cried among the people.
Job 30:29 I am like a brother to wild dragons, and a friend to owls.
Job 30:30 My skin is dark on me, and my bones are hot from burning.
Job 30:31 My harp now plays sad songs, and my flute sounds like the cries of those who are crying.
