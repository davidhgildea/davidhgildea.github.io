Job 20:1 Zophar from Naamath spoke and said,
Job 20:2 So my thoughts make me reply quickly.
Job 20:3 I have heard the criticism against me, and my insight makes me respond.
Job 20:4 Don't you know this from long ago, since the time humans were put on earth?
Job 20:5 The success of the wicked is brief, and the happiness of the dishonest lasts only a moment.
Job 20:6 Even if he becomes very important and his pride reaches the sky,
Job 20:7 He will die forever like his waste; those who saw him will ask, "Where is he?"
Job 20:8 He will vanish like a dream and not be found; he will be driven away like a night vision.
Job 20:9 The eye that saw him will never see him again; his place will no longer see him.
Job 20:10 His kids will try to help the poor, and his hands will give back their things.
Job 20:11 His bones carry the sins of his youth, which will go down with him to the dirt.
Job 20:12 Even if wrongdoing tastes sweet in his mouth and he hides it under his tongue;
Job 20:13 Even if he doesn't hurt it but leaves it in his mouth without letting it go:
Job 20:14 The food in his stomach has changed; it is like snake poison inside him.
Job 20:15 He has consumed wealth, and he will spit it out again: God will force it from his stomach.
Job 20:16 He will suck the snake's poison: the viper's tongue will kill him.
Job 20:17 He will not see the rivers, the floods, or the streams of honey and cream.
Job 20:18 He must give back what he worked for and cannot keep it; he will return an amount equal to his wealth, but he will not be happy about it.
Job 20:19 Because he hurt and left the poor; because he took a house by force that he did not build.
Job 20:20 He will not find peace within, nor keep what he wanted.
Job 20:21 All his food will be gone; so no one will search for his possessions.
Job 20:22 When he feels most secure, he will be in trouble; every hand of the wicked will attack him.
Job 20:23 When he's about to eat, God will throw his anger at him and make it fall on him as he eats.
Job 20:24 He will run from the iron weapon, and a steel bow will shoot him.
Job 20:25 It is pulled out, and comes from the body; yes, the shiny sword comes from his liver: fears are on him.
Job 20:26 Darkness will hide in his hidden places; a fire not fanned will eat him up; things will go badly for anyone remaining in his tent.
Job 20:27 The sky will show his wrongs; and the ground will stand against him.
Job 20:28 His wealth will disappear, and his possessions will be lost when God is angry.
Job 20:29 This is what a bad person gets from God, the share given to him by God.
