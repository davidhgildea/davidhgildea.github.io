Job 36:1 Elihu continued and spoke,
Job 36:2 Let me speak a bit more, for I still have things to say about God.
Job 36:3 I will bring my knowledge from far away and give credit for being right to my Creator.
Job 36:4 I speak the truth, and someone with perfect understanding is with you.
Job 36:5 Look, God is powerful and does not reject anyone; He is strong and wise.
Job 36:6 He does not keep the wicked alive but gives justice to the poor.
Job 36:7 God never stops watching the good people; he sets them to rule with kings on thrones, making their power secure forever, and they are lifted high.
Job 36:8 If they are tied up in chains and caught in ropes of trouble;
Job 36:9 Then he shows them what they have done and the wrongs they have committed by going too far.
Job 36:10 God opens their ears to instruction and orders them to turn away from wrongdoing.
Job 36:11 If they listen and work for God, they will live with success and enjoy their years.
Job 36:12 If they don't obey, they will die by the sword and will die without understanding.
Job 36:13 The insincere in heart store up anger; they don't cry out when he [God] restrains them.
Job 36:14 They die young, and their life ends among the impure.
Job 36:15 He saves the needy in their trouble and listens to them when they are oppressed.
Job 36:16 God would have taken you from a tight place to a spacious place with no tightness; your table would have been full of richness.
Job 36:17 But you have carried out the punishment of the wicked: punishment and fairness catch up with you.
Job 36:18 Be careful, because if God gets angry, he might punish you, and no large payment can save you.
Job 36:19 Will he value your wealth? No, not gold or any strong army.
Job 36:20 Do not wish for the night when people are taken away from where they are.
Job 36:21 Be careful, don't accept wrongdoing because you prefer it over suffering.
Job 36:22 Look, God lifts up with His power: who can teach like Him?
Job 36:23 Who has directed his path? Or who can say, "You have done wrong"?
Job 36:24 Remember to praise God's work that people see.
Job 36:25 Everyone can see it; people can spot it from far away.
Job 36:26 Look, God is great, and we do not know him, and no one can understand how long he has existed.
Job 36:27 He turns water into tiny drops; they fall as rain matching the mist.
Job 36:28 The clouds pour down and sprinkle plenty of water on people.
Job 36:29 Can anyone understand how the clouds spread out, or the sounds from his home in the sky?
Job 36:30 Look, he spreads his light over it and covers the sea's depths.
Job 36:31 God judges the people by these things; he provides plenty of food.
Job 36:32 He covers the sun with clouds and orders them to block the light.
Job 36:33 The sound of it tells about it, the livestock also about the mist.
