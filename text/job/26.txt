Job 26:1 But Job replied and spoke,
Job 26:2 How have you helped the person with no power? How can you save the arm that has no strength?
Job 26:3 How have you advised someone who lacks wisdom? And how have you fully explained the situation as it is?
Job 26:4 Who did you speak to? And who inspired your words?
Job 26:5 Dead things take shape beneath the waters, and those that live there.
Job 26:6 God sees everything in hell, and nothing can hide destruction.
Job 26:7 He stretches out the north over empty space and hangs the earth on nothing.
Job 26:8 He gathers water into his heavy clouds, and the clouds don't break under the weight.
Job 26:9 He covers his throne and puts a cloud over it.
Job 26:10 He has set limits for the waters, until the time when day and night end.
Job 26:11 Heaven's pillars shake and are amazed at his scolding.
Job 26:12 He splits the sea with his strength, and with his wisdom, he defeats the arrogant.
Job 26:13 By his Spirit, he made the skies beautiful; his hand shaped the twisted snake.
Job 26:14 Look, these are some of his actions, but we hear only a small part about him. Who can grasp the greatness of his power?
