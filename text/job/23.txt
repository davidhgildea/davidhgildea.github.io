Job 23:1 Then Job replied,
Job 23:2 Today my complaint is harsh; my suffering is worse than my groans.
Job 23:3 I wish I knew where to find God so I could go to where he is.
Job 23:4 I would explain my case to God and give many reasons to support it.
Job 23:5 I want to know the words he would reply with, and understand what he would tell me.
Job 23:6 Would he argue with me using his great power? No; instead, he would give me strength.
Job 23:7 There, good people could argue with God; so I would be saved forever from my judge.
Job 23:8 I look ahead, but he's not there; I look behind, but I can't see him.
Job 23:9 On the left side, where he works, I cannot see him; he hides on the right side so I cannot find him.
Job 23:10 God knows the path I follow; when he has tested me, I will come out like gold.
Job 23:11 I have followed his path without turning away.
Job 23:12 I have not turned away from what God commands; I value his words more than my daily bread.
Job 23:13 He has one plan, and no one can change it. Whatever he wants to do, he does.
Job 23:14 He does what he has planned for me, and he has many more things like this.
Job 23:15 So, when I feel God's presence, I get disturbed; when I think about it, I become scared of Him.
Job 23:16 God makes my heart gentle, and the Almighty disturbs me.
Job 23:17 Because I was not taken away before the darkness, nor has he hidden the darkness from my face.
