Job 28:1 Surely there is a source for silver and a place where gold is refined.
Job 28:2 Iron comes from the ground, and copper is melted from the rock.
Job 28:3 He ends the darkness and explores every detail: the dark rocks and the shadow of death.
Job 28:4 The flood bursts out from where people live; the waters, once stepped in and forgotten, have dried up and disappeared from human presence.
Job 28:5 The earth gives us bread, and beneath it is heat like fire.
Job 28:6 Its rocks are where sapphires are found, and it has particles of gold.
Job 28:7 There is a path unknown to birds, and unseen by the vulture's eye.
Job 28:8 Young lions have not walked on it, nor has the strong lion crossed over it.
Job 28:9 He reaches out his hand to the rock; he flips the mountains from their base.
Job 28:10 He carves out rivers in the rocks, and his eye sees every valuable thing.
Job 28:11 He stops the rivers from flooding; and he brings hidden things into the light.
Job 28:12 Where can wisdom be found? And where is the place of understanding?
Job 28:13 No one knows its value; it cannot be found in the land of the living.
Job 28:14 The deep says, 'It's not in me,' and the sea says, 'It's not with me.'
Job 28:15 It can't be bought with gold, nor can you weigh silver to pay for it.
Job 28:16 It can't be compared to Ophir's gold, valuable onyx, or sapphires.
Job 28:17 Gold and crystal are not its equal: you cannot swap it for any gold jewelry.
Job 28:18 People will not talk about coral or pearls; wisdom is worth more than rubies.
Job 28:19 Ethiopia's topaz won't match it, nor can it be compared to fine gold.
Job 28:20 Where does wisdom come from? And where can we find understanding?
Job 28:21 It is hidden from all living creatures and kept secret from the birds in the sky.
Job 28:22 Destruction and death say, "We have heard of its fame with our ears."
Job 28:23 God understands its way and knows its place.
Job 28:24 He looks to the earth's ends and sees everything under the sky.
Job 28:25 To set the weight for the winds; and he measures the waters by amount.
Job 28:26 When he set rules for the rain and a path for the thunder's lightning:
Job 28:27 He saw it, explained it, got it ready, and thoroughly examined it.
Job 28:28 To people he said, "Look, respecting God is wisdom, and avoiding evil is understanding."
