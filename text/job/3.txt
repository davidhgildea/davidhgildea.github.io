Job 3:1 After this, Job opened his mouth and cursed his day.
Job 3:2 Job spoke and said,
Job 3:3 Let the day I was born disappear, and the night that said, "A baby boy is conceived."
Job 3:4 Let that day be dark; may God not notice it from above, nor let light shine on it.
Job 3:5 Let darkness and death's shadow mark it; let a cloud cover it; let the day's darkness scare it.
Job 3:6 Let that night be dark; may it not be included among the days of the year or counted in the months.
Job 3:7 Look, let that night be alone, let no happy voice be heard in it.
Job 3:8 Let those who curse the day do so, those who are prepared to wake up their sadness.
Job 3:9 Let the stars of its dusk become dark; let it hope for light, yet find none; and let it not witness the break of dawn.
Job 3:10 Because it did not close the doors of my mother's womb or keep sadness from my eyes.
Job 3:11 Why didn't I die at birth? Why didn't I stop breathing when I was born?
Job 3:12 Why did I have knees to sit on, or breasts to feed from?
Job 3:13 By now, I would be lying down in peace; I would be asleep and at rest.
Job 3:14 With leaders and advisors of the earth, who build ruined places for themselves;
Job 3:15 Or with leaders who had gold, who filled their homes with silver:
Job 3:16 Or I would have been like a baby born too early that never lived, as babies who never saw light.
Job 3:17 There the bad people stop causing trouble; and there the tired people rest.
Job 3:18 There the prisoners find peace; they do not hear the voice of the one who keeps them down.
Job 3:19 Both important and unimportant people are there; and the servant is no longer bound to their boss.
Job 3:20 Why is light given to those in misery, and life to those with bitter hearts?
Job 3:21 They want to die, but it doesn't happen; they search for death more eagerly than for hidden treasures.
Job 3:22 Who are very happy and glad when they find the grave?
Job 3:23 Why is light given to a person whose path is hidden, and whom God has surrounded?
Job 3:24 I sigh before I eat, and my loud cries flow like water.
Job 3:25 What I feared most has happened to me, and what I was scared of has come true.
Job 3:26 I was not safe, nor at rest, nor peaceful; still, trouble came.
