Job 8:1 Bildad the Shuhite replied, saying,
Job 8:2 How long will you keep talking like this? How long will your words blow like a strong wind?
Job 8:3 Does God twist fairness? Or does the Almighty twist what's right?
Job 8:4 If your children sinned against God, and he has rejected them for their wrongdoing;
Job 8:5 If you seek God early and pray to the All-powerful;
Job 8:6 If you were pure and honest, surely now God would act for you and make your righteous home successful.
Job 8:7 Even if you started small, your future will grow a lot.
Job 8:8 Please ask about the past, and get ready to learn from your ancestors.
Job 8:9 We have only been here for a short time and know very little, because our lives on earth are like a passing shadow.
Job 8:10 Won't they teach you, talk to you, and speak heartfelt words to you?
Job 8:11 Can reeds grow without mud? Can plants grow without water?
Job 8:12 While it is still green and not cut, it dries up before any other plant.
Job 8:13 The ways of all who forget God lead to nothing, and the fake person's hope will vanish.
Job 8:14 Those whose hope will get cut off, and who rely on something as weak as a spider's web.
Job 8:15 He will rely on his house, but it won't last; he will hold it tightly, but it won't remain.
Job 8:16 He thrives in the sunlight, and his branches spread out in his garden.
Job 8:17 His roots twist around the pile of rocks and reach into the stony ground.
Job 8:18 If he is destroyed from his place, then it will reject him, saying, I have never seen you.
Job 8:19 Look, this is the happiness of his path, and from the ground others will rise.
Job 8:20 Look, God will not reject a good person, nor will he support those who do wrong.
Job 8:21 Until he makes your mouth laugh and your lips shout with joy.
Job 8:22 Those who hate you will be covered with shame; the home of the evil will become nothing.
