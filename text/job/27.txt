Job 27:1 Job kept on speaking his story, and said,
Job 27:2 God lives, who has taken away my justice; and the Almighty, who has troubled my soul.
Job 27:3 As long as I breathe, and God's breath is in my nose;
Job 27:4 I will not speak evil, nor will my tongue tell lies.
Job 27:5 I would never agree with you; I will keep my honesty until I die.
Job 27:6 I keep to my righteousness and will not give it up; my heart will not blame me as long as I live.
Job 27:7 Let my enemy be like the wicked, and the one who attacks me be like the unjust.
Job 27:8 What hope does a fake person have, even if they are rich, when God takes their life?
Job 27:9 Will God listen to him when he calls for help during trouble?
Job 27:10 Does he enjoy the Almighty? Will he constantly ask for God's help?
Job 27:11 I will teach you by God's power; I won't hide what is with the Almighty.
Job 27:12 Look, you have all seen it yourselves; so why do you continue to act so foolishly?
Job 27:13 This is what a bad person gets from God, and what bullies will get from the All-Powerful.
Job 27:14 If he has many children, they are for death, and his descendants will not have enough food.
Job 27:15 The ones left of him will be buried when they die, and his widows won't cry.
Job 27:16 Even if he piles up silver like dust and stores up clothes like dirt,
Job 27:17 He might make it ready, but good people will wear it, and those who did nothing wrong will share the money.
Job 27:18 He builds his house like a moth, and like a temporary shelter put up by a caretaker.
Job 27:19 The rich man will lie down but won't be picked up again; he opens his eyes, and he's gone.
Job 27:20 Fears grip him like water, a storm sweeps him away at night.
Job 27:21 The east wind takes him away, and he goes; a storm throws him out of his place.
Job 27:22 God will throw down on him and will not hold back; he really wants to escape from his power.
Job 27:23 People will clap their hands at him and boo him from his spot.
