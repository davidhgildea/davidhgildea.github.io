Job 38:1 Then God answered Job from the storm and said,
Job 38:2 Who is this who gives advice with words but has no understanding?
Job 38:3 Get ready like a man; I will question you, and you must answer me.
Job 38:4 Where were you when I set the earth's foundations? Tell me if you understand.
Job 38:5 Who set its measurements, if you know? Or who stretched a measuring line across it?
Job 38:6 Where are its foundations fixed? Or who put its cornerstone in place?
Job 38:7 When the morning stars sang together and all of God's children shouted for joy.
Job 38:8 Who closed in the sea with gates when it burst forth, as though coming out of the womb?
Job 38:9 When I wrapped it in clouds like clothes, and a thick dark blanket covered it,
Job 38:10 I broke open a place for it, and put in bars and doors.
Job 38:11 God said, "You may come this far, but no farther; here your proud waves must stop."
Job 38:12 Have you ever ordered the morning to begin or shown the dawn its place?
Job 38:13 So it can grab the edges of the world and shake the bad people from it.
Job 38:14 It changes like clay under a seal; and they settle into place like clothes.
Job 38:15 The wicked are denied light, and their strong arm will be broken.
Job 38:16 Have you gone into the springs of the sea? Or have you walked looking for the depths?
Job 38:17 Have you seen the gates of death or the doors where death's shadow falls?
Job 38:18 Have you understood how wide the earth is? Tell me if you know everything about it.
Job 38:19 Where is the place where light lives? And where is the home of darkness?
Job 38:20 You should take it to its limit and know the ways to its home.
Job 38:21 Do you know this because you were born then? Or because you have lived many days?
Job 38:22 Have you entered the storehouses of the snow? Or have you seen the storehouses of the hail?
Job 38:23 I have kept for a time of trouble, for a day of battle and war.
Job 38:24 How is light spread, or the east wind scattered over the earth?
Job 38:25 Who has made channels for the flood, or a path for thunder's lightning?
Job 38:26 To make it rain on the land where no one lives; on the desert where there is no one;
Job 38:27 To make the lonely, barren land fertile and to help young plants start to grow.
Job 38:28 Does the rain have a father? Or who has created the dewdrops?
Job 38:29 Who made ice come from the womb? Who created the frost from the heavens?
Job 38:30 The waters are covered like with a stone, and the surface of the deep water is solid like ice.
Job 38:31 Can you control the beautiful patterns of the stars or untie the ropes of the constellations?
Job 38:32 Can you make the constellations appear in their season? Or can you lead the Great Bear and its cubs?
Job 38:33 Do you know the laws of the sky? Can you establish their rule on the earth?
Job 38:34 Can you call out to the clouds to make them pour down water on you?
Job 38:35 Can you make lightning strike and have it report to you, 'Here we are'?
Job 38:36 Who has placed wisdom inside us? Or who has given the heart understanding?
Job 38:37 Who can count the clouds wisely? Or who can hold back the rain from the sky?
Job 38:38 When the soil turns hard, and lumps stick tightly together?
Job 38:39 Will you hunt food for the lion or satisfy the hunger of the young lions?
Job 38:40 When they crouch in their lairs and stay hidden to ambush?
Job 38:41 Who gives the raven its food? When its young cry out to God, they roam because they have no food.
