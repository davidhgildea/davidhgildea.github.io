Job 10:1 I am tired of my life; I will not hold back my complaints; I will speak from my heart's bitterness.
Job 10:2 I will tell God, "Do not judge me; tell me why you argue with me."
Job 10:3 Is it right for you to be unfair, to reject what you have created, and to favor the plans of the evil?
Job 10:4 Do you have human eyes? Or do you see as a person sees?
Job 10:5 Are your days like a human's days? Are your years like a person's years?
Job 10:6 You ask about my wrongs and search for my sins?
Job 10:7 You know I am not wicked; no one can rescue from your hand.
Job 10:8 Your hands made and shaped me completely; but now you are destroying me.
Job 10:9 Please remember you created me from clay; will you turn me back into dust?
Job 10:10 Did you not pour me out like milk and thicken me like cheese?
Job 10:11 You dressed me with skin and flesh, and built me with bones and muscles.
Job 10:12 You have given me life and kindness, and your care has kept my spirit safe.
Job 10:13 You have kept these things hidden in your heart; I know you understand this.
Job 10:14 If I do wrong, you notice it, and you will not clear me of my guilt.
Job 10:15 If I am evil, trouble is upon me; and if I am good, I still can't hold my head high. I am totally confused; so see my suffering.
Job 10:16 It grows. You chase me like a fierce lion, and then you do amazing things to me again.
Job 10:17 You create new evidence against me and grow angrier with me; changes and conflict oppose me.
Job 10:18 Why did you bring me out of the womb? I wish I had died before anyone saw me!
Job 10:19 I would have been like I never existed; I would have been taken from birth to the grave.
Job 10:20 Aren't my days short? Stop and leave me be, so I can have a little comfort.
Job 10:21 Before I go to a place I cannot come back from, to the land of darkness and death's shadow;
Job 10:22 A land of total darkness, like the deepest darkness; a place of death's shadow, without any order, where even the light is dark.
