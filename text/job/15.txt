Job 15:1 Eliphaz from Teman replied and said.
Job 15:2 Should a wise person speak empty words and fill themselves with useless thoughts?
Job 15:3 Should he argue with useless words or speak where he can do no good?
Job 15:4 Yes, you throw away respect and stop praying to God.
Job 15:5 Your mouth speaks your wrongs, and you talk like a tricky person.
Job 15:6 Your own words condemn you, not me; your own lips speak against you.
Job 15:7 Are you the first man who was born? Or were you created before the hills?
Job 15:8 Have you heard God's secret? And do you keep wisdom to yourself?
Job 15:9 What do you know that we don't? What do you understand that we don't have in us?
Job 15:10 With us are old men with gray hair, much older than your father.
Job 15:11 Is God's comfort unimportant to you? Is there something you are hiding?
Job 15:12 Why does your heart lead you astray? And what are your eyes signaling?
Job 15:13 Are you turning against God and letting such words come out of your mouth?
Job 15:14 Who is a person, that they can be pure? And someone born of a woman, that they can be right?
Job 15:15 Look, God does not fully trust his holy ones; even the skies are not pure in his eyes.
Job 15:16 How much more disgusting and dirty is a person, who drinks up wrong like water?
Job 15:17 I will show you, listen to me; and I will tell you what I have seen.
Job 15:18 Wise people shared wisdom from their ancestors and did not hide it.
Job 15:19 God gave the earth to them alone, and no outsider went among them.
Job 15:20 The bad person suffers through all his days, and the years he will live are unknown to the bully.
Job 15:21 He hears terrifying noises: even when he's doing well, destruction will hit him.
Job 15:22 He doesn't believe he will escape from darkness, and he is expected to meet the sword.
Job 15:23 He roams around looking for bread, asking, "Where is it?" He knows that times of trouble are close by.
Job 15:24 Worry and distress will scare him; they will overcome him, like a king prepared for battle.
Job 15:25 He reaches out his hand against God, and makes himself strong against the Almighty.
Job 15:26 He charges at him, right at his neck, on the thick parts of his shields.
Job 15:27 Because he covers his face with his fat and gets layers of fat on his sides.
Job 15:28 He lives in empty cities, in houses where no one lives, that are about to turn into ruins.
Job 15:29 He will not become rich, his wealth will not last, nor will he keep its success on earth.
Job 15:30 He will not escape the darkness; the fire will wither his branches, and he will be gone with a puff of breath.
Job 15:31 Don't let the person who is fooled trust in things that have no value, because they will only get emptiness in return.
Job 15:32 It will happen before its time, and its sprout will not be green.
Job 15:33 He will lose his young grapes like a vine, and drop his blossoms like an olive tree.
Job 15:34 The group of fakes will be left empty, and fire will burn down the tents of the corrupt.
Job 15:35 They plan trouble, give birth to empty promises, and their hearts breed lies.
