Job 41:1 Can you catch Leviathan with a fishhook or tie down its tongue with a rope?
Job 41:2 Can you put a hook in his nose or pierce his jaw with a spike?
Job 41:3 Will he beg you often? Will he speak gently to you?
Job 41:4 Will he agree with you? Will you make him your servant forever?
Job 41:5 Will you play with it like a bird? Or will you tie it up for your girls?
Job 41:6 Will friends feast on him? Will traders divide him up?
Job 41:7 Can you cover his skin with harpoons or his head with fishing spears?
Job 41:8 Touch him and remember the fight, do it no more.
Job 41:9 Look, his hope is pointless: won't someone be terrified just by seeing him?
Job 41:10 No one is brave enough to disturb him; who then can face me?
Job 41:11 Who has given to me first, so I need to pay him back? Everything under the whole sky is mine.
Job 41:12 I won't hide his body, his strength, or his good shape.
Job 41:13 Who can reach the edge of his clothing? Or who can approach him with a double bit bridle?
Job 41:14 Who can open the doors of his face? His teeth are terrifying all around.
Job 41:15 His scales are what he is proud of, tight and sealed closely.
Job 41:16 They are so close together that no air can fit between them.
Job 41:17 They are connected to each other, they cling tightly, so they cannot be separated.
Job 41:18 When he sneezes, light shines, and his eyes are like the dawn's first light.
Job 41:19 From his mouth come shining lights, and sparks of fire jump out.
Job 41:20 Smoke comes out of his nose like from a boiling pot or kettle.
Job 41:21 His breath lights up coals, and flames come out of his mouth.
Job 41:22 Strength stays in his neck, and sadness turns to joy in his presence.
Job 41:23 His skin is thick and tight; it is solid and does not move.
Job 41:24 His heart is as strong as a rock, yes, as hard as a lower grinding stone.
Job 41:25 When he stands up, the strong are scared: because of the terror, they try to make themselves clean.
Job 41:26 The warrior's sword won't succeed: neither the spear, the arrow, nor the armor.
Job 41:27 He thinks iron is like straw and brass is like rotten wood.
Job 41:28 Arrows cannot scare him; to him, slingshot stones turn to chaff.
Job 41:29 Arrows seem like straw to him; he laughs at the shaking of a spear.
Job 41:30 He lies on sharp rocks; he scatters spiky things on the mud.
Job 41:31 He makes the depths churn like a boiling pot; he makes the sea stir like a pot of ointment.
Job 41:32 He leaves a shining trail behind him; it seems like the depths have gray hair.
Job 41:33 On earth, there is no one like him; he is made without fear.
Job 41:34 He sees everything that is high up; he is a king over all the proud people.
