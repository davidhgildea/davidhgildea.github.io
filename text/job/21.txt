Job 21:1 Job replied,
Job 21:2 Listen carefully to what I say, and let this comfort you.
Job 21:3 Let me speak, and after I have spoken, you can mock me.
Job 21:4 Do I complain to people? If that's true, shouldn't I be upset?
Job 21:5 Listen to me, be amazed, and put your hand over your mouth.
Job 21:6 When I think about it, I get scared, and my body starts to shake.
Job 21:7 Why do bad people live long, grow old, and become very strong?
Job 21:8 Their children are secure with them, and they can see their grandchildren.
Job 21:9 Their homes are safe and not afraid, and God's punishment does not touch them.
Job 21:10 Their bull mates without failing; their cow gives birth and does not miscarry.
Job 21:11 They send out their young like a herd, and their kids dance.
Job 21:12 They play tambourines and harps, and are happy to hear the organ's music.
Job 21:13 They live in riches all their days, but suddenly they die.
Job 21:14 So they tell God to go away, because they don't want to know His ways.
Job 21:15 Who is God that we should serve Him? And what do we gain by praying to Him?
Job 21:16 Look, they have no control over their own welfare; I stay away from the plans of the wicked.
Job 21:17 How often is the wicked person's light extinguished! And how often does ruin fall upon them! God gives out sadness in his anger.
Job 21:18 They are like straw in the wind, and like husks that the storm blows away.
Job 21:19 God saves up the wrongs of someone for their children: He punishes them, and they will realize it.
Job 21:20 He will see his own ruin and drink from God's anger.
Job 21:21 Why would he enjoy his house after he's gone if his life is cut short?
Job 21:22 Can anyone teach God knowledge, since He judges even the powerful?
Job 21:23 Someone dies in their prime, feeling completely peaceful and relaxed.
Job 21:24 His chest is full of milk, and his bones are wet with marrow.
Job 21:25 Another person dies feeling bitter and never enjoys their food.
Job 21:26 They will all rest in the ground, and worms will cover them.
Job 21:27 Look, I know what you are thinking and the plans you are wrongly making against me.
Job 21:28 You ask, "Where is the prince's house? And where do the wicked live?"
Job 21:29 Have you not asked those who pass by? And do you not recognize the signs they know?
Job 21:30 The wicked are kept for the day of destruction; they will be taken to the day of anger.
Job 21:31 Who will tell him what he has done to his face? Who will pay him back for his actions?
Job 21:32 He will be taken to the grave and stay in the tomb.
Job 21:33 The lumps of earth in the valley will be dear to him, and everyone will follow him, just as countless have gone before him.
Job 21:34 So how can you comfort me with nonsense, when your answers are full of lies?
