Job 33:1 So Job, please listen to what I say and pay attention to all my words.
Job 33:2 Look, now I have opened my mouth and my tongue has spoken inside it.
Job 33:3 I will speak what is true and my words will clearly show what I know.
Job 33:4 God's spirit made me, and the Almighty's breath gave me life.
Job 33:5 If you can answer me, arrange your words clearly and stand up.
Job 33:6 Look, I am just as you want, in place of God: I am made from clay too.
Job 33:7 You don't need to fear my terror, nor will my hand be hard on you.
Job 33:8 You have definitely spoken where I could hear, and I have heard what you said.
Job 33:9 I am clean and have not wronged anyone, I am innocent; I have done nothing wrong.
Job 33:10 Look, He finds reasons to accuse me; He considers me His enemy.
Job 33:11 He puts my feet in shackles and watches all the ways I walk.
Job 33:12 Look, you are not right in this: I will tell you that God is greater than a human.
Job 33:13 Why do you argue with God? He doesn't need to explain anything he does.
Job 33:14 God speaks once, even twice, but people do not notice.
Job 33:15 In a dream, during the night, when people are in deep sleep and lying in bed half-asleep;
Job 33:16 Then he opens people's ears and gives them understanding.
Job 33:17 To make a person change their plan and keep them from being proud.
Job 33:18 He protects his soul from the pit and his life from dying by the sword.
Job 33:19 He suffers with pain on his bed, and his bones ache greatly.
Job 33:20 So he hates bread, and he refuses good food.
Job 33:21 His body wastes away, hidden from sight; and his bones, once unseen, now stick out.
Job 33:22 Yes, his soul is close to death, and his life nears those who destroy.
Job 33:23 If a messenger is with him, an interpreter, one in a thousand, to show a person how to be right:
Job 33:24 Then he is kind to him and says, Save him from falling into the pit: I have found a payment.
Job 33:25 His body will be more refreshed than a child's: he will become young again.
Job 33:26 He will pray to God, and God will accept him; he will see God's face with happiness because God will give man what is right.
Job 33:27 He looks at people, and if anyone says, "I have sinned, I have twisted what is right, and it did not benefit me;"
Job 33:28 He will save his soul from falling into the pit, and his life will see the light.
Job 33:29 Look, God often does all these things with humans.
Job 33:30 To save his life from the grave, to enjoy the light of life.
Job 33:31 Pay attention, Job, listen to me: be quiet, and I will talk.
Job 33:32 If you have something to say, reply: speak, because I want to clear you.
Job 33:33 If not, listen to me: be quiet, and I will teach you wisdom.
