Micah 7:1 I am in trouble! I am like when people have picked the summer fruits, like the scraps left after grapes are picked: there is no bunch to eat; I really wanted the early ripe fruit.
Micah 7:2 The good person is gone from the world; no one is honest anymore. Everyone is setting traps to hurt others; they are all trying to catch their fellow man like hunting with a net.
Micah 7:3 They eagerly do bad things with both hands. The leader and the judge both want bribes; the powerful person tells of his evil wish, and so they plan it together.
Micah 7:4 Even the best person is like a prickly bush; the most honest is as sharp as a thorn fence. The time for your guards and punishment is coming; soon they will be confused.
Micah 7:5 Do not trust a friend or rely on a leader; be careful what you say around the one close to you.
Micah 7:6 The son disrespects his father, the daughter rebels against her mother, the daughter-in-law against her mother-in-law; a man's enemies are his own family members.
Micah 7:7 So I will look to God; I will wait for the God who saves me: my God will listen to me.
Micah 7:8 Don't be happy over me, my enemy. If I fall down, I will get up; if I'm in the dark, God will be my light.
Micah 7:9 I will accept God's anger because I sinned against him, until he stands up for me and makes things right for me. He will lead me into the light, and I will see his goodness.
Micah 7:10 Then my enemy will see it, and she will be covered with shame, the one who asked me, "Where is your God?" I will see her then; she will be trampled like mud in the streets.
Micah 7:11 On the day when your walls are to be rebuilt, on that day the order will be ended.
Micah 7:12 On that day, he will come to you from Assyria, from strong cities, from the fortress to the river, and from sea to sea, and from mountain to mountain.
Micah 7:13 Despite this, the land will become empty because of the people living there, due to the results of their actions.
Micah 7:14 Take care of your people with your guidance, your special flock living alone in the forest, in the heart of Carmel: let them graze in Bashan and Gilead, like in the past.
Micah 7:15 Based on the number of days since you left Egypt, I will show him wonderful things.
Micah 7:16 Nations will see and be shocked by their own power; they will cover their mouths and be unable to hear.
Micah 7:17 They will lick the dust like snakes, they will come out of their holes like earthworms: they will be scared of our God and will be afraid because of you.
Micah 7:18 Who is a God like you, forgiving sin and overlooking the wrongs of those who are left of your people? You do not stay angry forever because you enjoy being merciful.
Micah 7:19 He will come back, he will show us mercy; he will conquer our wrongs; and you will throw all their sins into the sea's depths.
Micah 7:20 You will show your faithfulness to Jacob and love to Abraham, as you promised our ancestors long ago.
