Isaiah 34:1 Come close, all you countries, to listen; and pay attention, all you peoples: let the earth and everything on it hear; the world and all that comes out of it.
Isaiah 34:2 God is angry with all nations and furious with their armies; he has completely destroyed them and handed them over to be killed.
Isaiah 34:3 Their dead bodies will be thrown away, and they will smell bad. The mountains will flow with their blood.
Isaiah 34:4 Every star in the sky will disappear, and the sky will close up like a scroll. All the stars will fall, like leaves drop from a grapevine, and like figs drop from a fig tree.
Isaiah 34:5 My sword will be ready in the sky; it will fall on Edom and the people I have decided to punish, for punishment.
Isaiah 34:6 God's sword is covered with blood, it is thick with fat, from the blood of lambs and goats, and from the fat of ram's kidneys: because God has a sacrifice in Bozrah and a big killing in the land of Idumea.
Isaiah 34:7 The unicorns will come down with them, and the young bulls with the grown bulls; their land will be wet with blood, and their soil rich with fat.
Isaiah 34:8 It is the day of God's revenge, and the time to pay back for the dispute over Zion.
Isaiah 34:9 Its rivers will turn into tar, its soil into sulfur, and its ground will burn like tar.
Isaiah 34:10 It will not stop burning day or night; its smoke will rise forever. It will remain empty from one generation to another; no one will ever pass through it again.
Isaiah 34:11 The cormorant and bittern will live there; the owl and raven will make it their home. God will mark it with lines of chaos and stones of emptiness.
Isaiah 34:12 They will invite its nobles to rule, but no one will be there, and all its leaders will become nothing.
Isaiah 34:13 Thorns will grow in her palaces, nettles and brambles in her strongholds; it will be a home for dragons and a place for owls.
Isaiah 34:14 Desert animals will meet with island creatures, and goat-demons will call to each other; the night bird will also settle there and find a resting place.
Isaiah 34:15 The great owl will build its nest there, lay eggs, hatch them, and protect them under its wings; vultures will also meet there, each with its partner.
Isaiah 34:16 Look in God's book and read: none of these will be missing, none will be without a partner, because God has ordered it, and his spirit has brought them together.
Isaiah 34:17 He has decided their share by casting lots, and his hand has distributed it to them with measuring lines: they will own it forever, and they will live there from one generation to the next.
