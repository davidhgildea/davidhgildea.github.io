Isaiah 29 — Simplified Bible (simplified.bible)

Isaiah 29:1 Sadness to Ariel, the city where David lived! Keep adding year after year; continue the sacrifices.
Isaiah 29:2 I will trouble Ariel, and there will be sadness and pain; and Ariel will be to me as Ariel.
Isaiah 29:3 I will surround you with my camp, attack you with a siege mound, and build towers to besiege you.
Isaiah 29:4 You will be brought low, and you will speak from the ground, and your words will be soft from the dust, and your voice will be like a ghost from the ground, and your words will be a whisper from the dust.
Isaiah 29:5 Many strangers among you will be like tiny dust, and many cruel people will vanish like chaff blown away, and all this will happen quickly, in a moment.
Isaiah 29:6 You will be visited by God with thunder, earthquake, loud noise, storm, tempest, and flames of consuming fire.
Isaiah 29:7 The crowds from all countries battling Ariel, and all attacking her and her defenses, making her suffer, will be like a dream seen in the night.
Isaiah 29:8 It will be like a hungry person who dreams of eating, but wakes up still hungry, or like a thirsty person who dreams of drinking, but wakes up thirsty and weak. This is how it will be for all the nations that fight against God's holy mountain.
Isaiah 29:9 Wait and be amazed; shout out loud, they act drunk, but not from wine; they stumble, but not from strong drink.
Isaiah 29:10 God has made you fall into a deep sleep and has shut your eyes. The prophets and leaders, the seers, he has covered.
Isaiah 29:11 The whole vision is like a sealed book to you. When it is given to someone who can read and they are asked, "Please read this," they say, "I can't, because it is sealed."
Isaiah 29:12 The book is given to someone who can't read, and they are asked, "Please read this," but he answers, "I can't read."
Isaiah 29:13 So God said, "Although these people come close to me with their words and honor me with their lips, their hearts are far from me, and their fear of me is just a human command they've been taught."
Isaiah 29:14 So, I will do an amazing thing for these people, a truly amazing thing that will amaze them: their wise people will lose their wisdom, and their smart people will not be able to understand.
Isaiah 29:15 Trouble awaits those who try to hide their plans from God; they do their deeds in secret and think, "Who sees us? Who knows what we're doing?"
Isaiah 29:16 Thinking you can twist things will be seen as silly as clay questioning the potter: Can what is made say to the one who made it, "You didn't make me"? Or can a creation say to its creator, "You don't know what you're doing"?
Isaiah 29:17 Won't it soon happen that Lebanon becomes a fertile land, and the fertile land will be considered like a forest?
Isaiah 29:18 On that day, the deaf will hear the words of the book, and the blind will see, even from darkness.
Isaiah 29:19 Humble people will find more joy in God, and the needy will celebrate the Holy One of Israel.
Isaiah 29:20 The dreadful one is defeated, the mocker is destroyed, and all who look for evil are stopped.
Isaiah 29:21 They make a person guilty because of a single word, set traps for those who correct in public, and twist the truth to harm the innocent for no reason.
Isaiah 29:22 So God, who saved Abraham, says about Jacob's family: Jacob won't be ashamed now, nor will his face turn pale.
Isaiah 29:23 But when he sees his children, my creation, around him, they will honor my name, respect the Holy One of Jacob, and fear the God of Israel.
Isaiah 29:24 Those who were confused will understand, and those who complained will learn what to believe.
