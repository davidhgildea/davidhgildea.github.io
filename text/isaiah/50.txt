Isaiah 50:1 God says, "Where is your mother's divorce paper that I sent away? Who is the one I sold you to because of my debts? Look, because of your wrongs you sold yourselves, and because of your mistakes your mother was sent away.
Isaiah 50:2 Why, when I arrived, was no one there? When I called, why did no one respond? Is my power lessened, so that I can't save? Or do I lack the strength to rescue? Look, at my command I dry up the sea, I turn rivers into deserts; their fish rot because there is no water, and they die of thirst.
Isaiah 50:3 I make the skies dark, and I cover them with sackcloth.
Isaiah 50:4 God has given me the tongue of someone who is educated, so I can say the right thing at the right time to those who are tired: he wakes me up every morning, he makes my ear ready to listen like someone who is educated.
Isaiah 50:5 God has opened my ear, and I was not rebellious, nor did I turn back.
Isaiah 50:6 I let people beat my back and pull hair from my face without hiding from shame or spit.
Isaiah 50:7 God will help me, so I won't be confused. I am determined, and I know I won't be ashamed.
Isaiah 50:8 God who declares me right is close; who will argue with me? Let's face each other: who is against me? Let them approach me.
Isaiah 50:9 Look, God will help me; who can declare me guilty? See, they will all wear out like clothes; moths will consume them.
Isaiah 50:10 Who among you respects God, listens to his servant, walks in darkness without light? Let him trust in God's name and rely on his God.
Isaiah 50:11 Look, all who start a fire and surround yourselves with sparks: go ahead and walk in the light of your fire and the sparks you've made. This is what you'll get from me; you will lay down in sadness.
