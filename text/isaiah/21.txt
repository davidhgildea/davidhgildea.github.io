Isaiah 21:1 The heavy message about the sea desert. Like storms in the south move through; it comes from the desert, from a scary land.
Isaiah 21:2 A terrible vision is told to me; the cheater cheats, and the destroyer destroys. Attack, Elam: surround, Media; I have ended all its groaning.
Isaiah 21:3 My waist is full of pain; cramps have gripped me, like a woman in labor. I was bent over when I heard it; I was shocked when I saw it.
Isaiah 21:4 My heart beat fast, and fear scared me: the night that was once joyful for me has become a time of fear.
Isaiah 21:5 Set the table, stay alert in the tower, eat and drink: get up, leaders, and get the shield ready.
Isaiah 21:6 God told me, "Go, put a guard on duty, and let him report what he sees."
Isaiah 21:7 He saw a chariot with two horsemen, a donkey chariot, and a camel chariot; and he listened carefully with great attention.
Isaiah 21:8 He shouted, "A lion! Sir, I'm always on the lookout during the day, and I stay at my guard post all night."
Isaiah 21:9 Look, here comes a chariot with two riders. He replied, "Babylon has fallen, has fallen; and all the idols of its gods are shattered on the ground."
Isaiah 21:10 My people, whom I will thresh and the grain of my threshing floor: what I've heard from God, the God of Israel, I have told you.
Isaiah 21:11 The heavy message of Dumah. Someone from Seir calls to me, “Guard, how much of the night is left? Guard, how much of the night is left?”
Isaiah 21:12 The guard said, "Morning is coming, and also the night. If you want to ask, then ask. Come back, come."
Isaiah 21:13 The heavy task is on Arabia. You will stay in the woods in Arabia, you traveling groups of Dedanites.
Isaiah 21:14 The people of Tema brought water to the thirsty and gave bread to the fugitives.
Isaiah 21:15 They ran away from the swords, the pulled-out sword, the bent bow, and the harshness of war.
Isaiah 21:16 God told me that within one year, like the time a hired worker counts, all Kedar's pride will disappear.
Isaiah 21:17 The remaining archers from the strong warriors of Kedar's descendants will decrease because God of Israel has said so.
