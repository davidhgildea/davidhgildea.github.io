Isaiah 23:1 The weight of Tyre’s troubles. Cry out, ships of Tarshish; for it is destroyed, with no homes or way to enter: from the land of Chittim, they learn of it.
Isaiah 23:2 Stay calm, people of the island; you whom the sea-traveling merchants of Sidon have filled up.
Isaiah 23:3 By large waters, the crops of the Nile, the river's yield, bring her income; she is a marketplace for nations.
Isaiah 23:4 Feel embarrassed, O Sidon, because the sea has claimed, even its mighty waves, saying, I do not struggle in labor, nor give birth to children, and I do not raise young men or nurture young women.
Isaiah 23:5 Just like the news about Egypt hurt them, they will be very upset by the news about Tyre.
Isaiah 23:6 Go to Tarshish; cry out, you people of the island.
Isaiah 23:7 Is this your happy city, which is very old? It will walk far away to stay.
Isaiah 23:8 Who has planned this against Tyre, the city known for crowns, where the merchants are like leaders, and the traders respected throughout the world?
Isaiah 23:9 God has planned it, to tarnish all pride and to disgrace all respected people on earth.
Isaiah 23:10 Go through your land like a river, O daughter of Tarshish: you have no more power.
Isaiah 23:11 He reached out his hand over the sea and disturbed nations: God has ordered to destroy the merchant city and its strong defenses.
Isaiah 23:12 He said, "You won't be happy anymore, you mistreated young woman of Sidon. Get up, go over to Cyprus; even there you won't find peace."
Isaiah 23:13 Look at the land of the Chaldeans; these people did not exist until the Assyrian built it for the desert dwellers: they built its towers, they built its palaces; and it was destroyed.
Isaiah 23:14 Cry out, you ships of Tarshish: because your power is destroyed.
Isaiah 23:15 On that day, Tyre will be forgotten for seventy years, the length of one king's reign. After seventy years are over, Tyre will return to life like a prostitute.
Isaiah 23:16 Play the harp, walk around the town, forgotten woman; play lovely tunes, sing a lot of songs, so you will be remembered.
Isaiah 23:17 After seventy years, God will return to Tyre, and it will start earning money again by interacting with all the world's kingdoms on the earth.
Isaiah 23:18 Her goods and earnings will be sacred to God; they will not be stored or hoarded. Her goods will provide enough food and lasting clothes for those living in God’s presence.
