Isaiah 24:1 Look, God will make the earth empty, destroy it, flip it over, and scatter its people.
Isaiah 24:2 And it will happen to the people just like the priest; to the servant like his master; to the maid like her mistress; to the buyer like the seller; to the lender like the borrower; to the one who charges interest just like the one who pays it.
Isaiah 24:3 The land will be completely emptied and totally ruined because God has said this.
Isaiah 24:4 The earth is sad and withers, the world weakens and withers, the proud people of the earth become weak.
Isaiah 24:5 The earth is polluted because of its people; they've broken the laws, changed the rules, and violated the eternal agreement.
Isaiah 24:6 So the curse has consumed the earth, and those living in it are left empty; that's why the people of the earth are scorched, with only a few men remaining.
Isaiah 24:7 The new wine is sad, the vine is weak, and all the happy people sigh.
Isaiah 24:8 The sound of tambourines stops, the noise of celebrators ends, the happiness of the harp stops.
Isaiah 24:9 They won't enjoy wine with singing; hard liquor will taste bad to those who drink it.
Isaiah 24:10 The city of chaos is destroyed: every house is closed so that no one can enter.
Isaiah 24:11 People in the streets cry for wine; all happiness has faded, the land's joy has disappeared.
Isaiah 24:12 The city is left in ruins, and the gate is struck with devastation.
Isaiah 24:13 When this happens across the land among the people, it will be like the shaking of an olive tree and like picking the last grapes after the harvest is over.
Isaiah 24:14 They will raise their voices, they will sing about God's greatness, they will shout loud from the sea.
Isaiah 24:15 So praise God when you are in trouble, and honor the name of the God of Israel in the islands of the sea.
Isaiah 24:16 We have heard songs of praise from the farthest parts of the earth, singing glory to the good people. But I cried out, 'I am so weak, so weak, how terrible for me! The cheaters have cheated; yes, the cheaters have cheated a lot.'
Isaiah 24:17 Fear, a pit, and a trap are coming for you, people of the earth.
Isaiah 24:18 It will happen that the person running away from the sound of terror will fall into a hole; and the one who gets out of the hole will be caught in a trap. This is because the skies above are open, and the earth's foundations are trembling.
Isaiah 24:19 The earth is completely shattered, the earth is totally melted, the earth is shaken a lot.
Isaiah 24:20 The earth will sway back and forth like a drunk person and will be moved like a hut; its wrongdoing will weigh it down, and it will fall, never to get up again.
Isaiah 24:21 On that day, God will punish the powerful beings above and the kings on the earth.
Isaiah 24:22 They will be brought together like captives in a hole and locked up in jail, and after a long time, they will be checked on.
Isaiah 24:23 Then the moon will be embarrassed, and the sun ashamed, when God rules on Mount Zion and in Jerusalem, and before his elders with glory.
