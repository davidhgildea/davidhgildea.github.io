Colossians 3:1 If you have been raised with Christ, look for the things that are above, where Christ sits at God's right side.
Colossians 3:2 Focus on things above, not on earthly things.
Colossians 3:3 You are dead, and your life is hidden with Christ in God.
Colossians 3:4 When Christ, who is our life, comes, you will also be with him in glory.
Colossians 3:5 So, make dead the parts of you that are earthly: sexual sin, impurity, strong lust, evil desires, and greed, which is like worshiping idols.
Colossians 3:6 Because of these things, God's anger comes upon the disobedient people.
Colossians 3:7 You once lived this way when you followed these practices.
Colossians 3:8 Now you should also stop all these things: anger, rage, hate, insulting others, and dirty talk from your mouth.
Colossians 3:9 Do not lie to each other, because you have taken off your old self and its behaviors.
Colossians 3:10 And have put on the new self, which is renewed in knowledge to be like God who created him.
Colossians 3:11 There is no Greek or Jew, circumcised or not, barbarian, Scythian, slave or free; but Christ is everything and in everyone.
Colossians 3:12 Therefore, as God's chosen ones, holy and loved, put on a heart of compassion, kindness, humility, gentleness, and patience.
Colossians 3:13 Be patient with each other, and forgive each other. If someone has a complaint against anyone, forgive them just as Christ forgave you.
Colossians 3:14 Above everything, wear love, which binds everything together in perfect unity.
Colossians 3:15 Let God's peace lead in your hearts, which you are called to as one group; and be thankful.
Colossians 3:16 Let Christ's message live in you fully, with all wisdom, and teach and warn each other with psalms, hymns, and spiritual songs, singing thankfully in your hearts to God.
Colossians 3:17 In everything you say or do, do it all for Jesus, thanking God through him.
Colossians 3:18 Wives, obey your husbands, as it is right with God.
Colossians 3:19 Husbands, love your wives and don't be harsh with them.
Colossians 3:20 Kids, listen to your parents in everything, because this makes God happy.
Colossians 3:21 Parents, do not make your children angry, so they do not get discouraged.
Colossians 3:22 Workers, obey your earthly bosses in everything; not just when they're watching, like people who just want to please others, but with a true heart and respect for God.
Colossians 3:23 Do everything with all your heart, as if you're doing it for God, not for people.
Colossians 3:24 Understand that from God you will get the reward of what is kept for you in heaven, because you serve Christ.
Colossians 3:25 Whoever does wrong will be paid back for their wrong, and everyone is treated the same.
