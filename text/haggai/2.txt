Haggai 2:1 In the seventh month, on the twenty-first day, God's message came through the prophet Haggai, saying,
Haggai 2:2 Talk to Zerubbabel, son of Shealtiel, the leader of Judah, and to Joshua, son of Josedech, the high priest, and to the rest of the people, and say,
Haggai 2:3 Who among you saw this temple when it was first built? And what do you think of it now? Doesn't it seem like nothing compared to before?
Haggai 2:4 Be strong now, Zerubbabel, says God; and be strong, Joshua, son of Josedech, the high priest; and be strong, all you people of the land, says God, and work: because I am with you, says God.
Haggai 2:5 Based on the promise I made to you when you left Egypt, my spirit stays with you: do not be afraid.
Haggai 2:6 God says: After a little while, I will shake the sky, the earth, the sea, and the land.
Haggai 2:7 I will shake all nations, and what every nation wants will come. I will fill this house with glory, says God.
Haggai 2:8 The silver and the gold belong to me, says God.
Haggai 2:9 The splendor of this new house will be greater than the old one, says God, and here I will bring peace, says God.
Haggai 2:10 On the 24th day of the ninth month, in the second year of King Darius, God's message came through the prophet Haggai, saying,
Haggai 2:11 God says; Now ask the priests about the law and tell them,
Haggai 2:12 If someone carries sacred meat in their clothes and their clothes touch bread, soup, wine, oil, or any food, will it become sacred? The priests said no.
Haggai 2:13 Then Haggai asked, "If someone unclean from a dead body touches any of these, does it become unclean?" And the priests replied, "It will become unclean."
Haggai 2:14 Haggai replied, "This is how these people and this nation are before God, and so is everything they do; even their offerings are unclean."
Haggai 2:15 Please think about this day and the past, before any stone was set on another in God's temple.
Haggai 2:16 Since that time, when someone went to a pile of twenty measures, there were only ten; when someone went to the wine vat to get fifty containers, there were only twenty.
Haggai 2:17 I struck you with blight, mildew, and hail, which ruined your work; but you still didn't turn to me, says God.
Haggai 2:18 Think about this day and from now on, from the 24th day of the ninth month, since the day the foundation of God's temple was set, think about it.
Haggai 2:19 Is the seed still in the barn? Yes, the grapevine, fig tree, pomegranate, and olive tree haven't produced fruit yet. But from today, I will bless you.
Haggai 2:20 Once more, on the twenty-fourth day of the month, God's message came to Haggai.
Haggai 2:21 Tell Zerubbabel, the leader of Judah, I will shake the sky and the ground.
Haggai 2:22 I will topple the rulers of nations, destroy the power of foreign kingdoms, overturn their war chariots and riders; the horses and riders will fall, each killed by his comrade's sword.
Haggai 2:23 On that day, says God, I will choose you, Zerubbabel, my servant, the son of Shealtiel, says God, and I will make you like a seal ring, because I have chosen you, says God.
