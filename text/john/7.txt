John 7 — Simplified Bible (simplified.bible)

John 7:1 After this, Jesus stayed in Galilee because he did not want to be in Judea where the Jewish leaders wanted to kill him.
John 7:2 The Jewish festival of shelters was coming soon.
John 7:3 His brothers told him, "Leave this place and go to Judea, so your followers can see what you are doing."
John 7:4 No one does things secretly if they want to be known publicly. If you do these things, let everyone see you.
John 7:5 His brothers did not believe in him.
John 7:6 Then Jesus told them, "My time has not come yet, but your time is always here."
John 7:7 The world cannot hate you; but it hates me because I say that its actions are evil.
John 7:8 Go to this festival; I am not going yet because my time has not fully come.
John 7:9 After he spoke to them, he stayed in Galilee.
John 7:10 After his brothers left, he also went to the festival, not publicly, but in secret.
John 7:11 During the festival, the Jewish people looked for him and asked, "Where is he?"
John 7:12 Many people whispered about him: some said, "He is good," while others said, "No, he tricks the people."
John 7:13 However, no one talked about him publicly because they were afraid of the Jewish leaders.
John 7:14 During the festival, Jesus went to the temple and taught.
John 7:15 The Jews were amazed and asked, "How does this man know so much when he has never been taught?"
John 7:16 Jesus told them, "My teaching is not my own, but belongs to the one who sent me."
John 7:17 If anyone chooses to do God's will, they will understand the teaching and know whether it comes from God or if I am speaking on my own.
John 7:18 Someone who talks about themselves wants their own praise. But if a person seeks to honor the one who sent them, they are true, and there's nothing wrong in them.
John 7:19 Didn't Moses give you the law, but none of you follow it? Why do you want to kill me?
John 7:20 The people replied, "You are crazy: who is trying to kill you?"
John 7:21 Jesus replied to them, "I did one thing, and you are all amazed."
John 7:22 Moses gave you the practice of circumcision; (though it didn't start with Moses, but with the ancestors;) and you even circumcise a man on the Sabbath day.
John 7:23 If a man is circumcised on the Sabbath so the law of Moses isn't broken, why are you mad at me for healing a man completely on the Sabbath?
John 7:24 Do not judge by how things look, but judge with fair judgment.
John 7:25 Some people from Jerusalem asked, "Isn't this the man they want to kill?"
John 7:26 Look, he speaks openly, and no one says anything to him. Do the leaders really know that he is the Christ?
John 7:27 We know where this man comes from, but when the Christ comes, no one will know where he is from.
John 7:28 Jesus shouted in the temple as he was teaching, saying, "You all know me, and you know where I'm from. I didn't come on my own, but the one who sent me is real, and you don't know him.
John 7:29 I know him because I come from him, and he sent me.
John 7:30 They tried to seize him, but no one touched him because his time had not yet come.
John 7:31 Many people believed in him and asked, "When the Messiah comes, will he perform more miracles than this man has?"
John 7:32 The Pharisees heard the people whispering these things about him; so the Pharisees and the head priests sent guards to arrest him.
John 7:33 Jesus said to them, "I will be with you a little longer, then I will go to the one who sent me."
John 7:34 You will look for me but won't find me; and you can't go where I am.
John 7:35 Then the Jews said to each other, "Where is he planning to go that we can't find him? Will he go to the scattered Jews among the non-Jews and teach the non-Jews?"
John 7:36 What does he mean by saying, "You will look for me, but you won't find me: and you can't come where I am"?
John 7:37 On the final day, the big feast day, Jesus stood up and shouted, "If anyone is thirsty, come to me and drink."
John 7:38 Whoever believes in me, just as the scripture has said, streams of living water will flow from within them.
John 7:39 He said this about the Spirit, which believers in him would receive: The Spirit had not yet come, because Jesus had not yet been glorified.
John 7:40 So when the people heard this, many said, "Truly, this is the Prophet."
John 7:41 Some people said, "This is the Christ." But others asked, "Does the Christ come from Galilee?"
John 7:42 Doesn't the scripture say that Christ will come from David's family, and from Bethlehem, the town where David lived?
John 7:43 People were divided because of him.
John 7:44 Some of them wanted to seize him, but no one touched him.
John 7:45 The officers went to the chief priests and Pharisees, and they asked, "Why didn't you bring him?"
John 7:46 The officers replied, "No one ever spoke like this person."
John 7:47 The Pharisees replied, "Have you been tricked too?"
John 7:48 Have any leaders or Pharisees believed in him?
John 7:49 But these people who do not know the law are cursed.
John 7:50 Nicodemus, who had visited Jesus at night and was one of the group, said to them.
John 7:51 Does our law judge anyone before it hears him and knows what he is doing?
John 7:52 They replied to him, "Are you from Galilee too? Look and see: no prophet comes from Galilee."
John 7:53 Everyone went to their own home.
