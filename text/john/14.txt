John 14 — Simplified Bible (simplified.bible)

John 14:1 Don't be worried in your heart; you trust in God, trust also in me.
John 14:2 In my Father's house, there are many homes. If it wasn't true, I would have told you. I am going to get a place ready for you.
John 14:3 If I go and get a place ready for you, I will come back and take you with me; so you can be where I am too.
John 14:4 You know where I'm going and you know the way.
John 14:5 Thomas said to him, "Lord, we don't know where you are going; how can we know the way?"
John 14:6 Jesus said to him, "I am the way, the truth, and the life. No one comes to the Father except through me."
John 14:7 If you had known me, you would have known my Father too. From now on, you do know him and have seen him.
John 14:8 Philip said to him, "Lord, show us the Father, and that will be enough for us."
John 14:9 Jesus said to him, "Have I been with you for so long, and you still don't know me, Philip? Whoever has seen me has seen God; so why do you say, 'Show us God'?"
John 14:10 Don't you believe that I am in God, and God is in me? The words I tell you are not just from me: it's God living in me who does the works.
John 14:11 Trust that I am in God, and God is in me; if not, trust because of the things I have done.
John 14:12 Truly, truly, I tell you, whoever believes in me will do the works I do, and they will do even greater works, because I am going to my Father.
John 14:13 Whatever you ask for in my name, I will do it so that the Father may be honored through the Son.
John 14:14 If you ask for anything in my name, I will do it.
John 14:15 If you love me, follow my commands.
John 14:16 I will ask God, and he will give you another Helper to be with you always.
John 14:17 The Spirit of truth, whom the world cannot accept because it does not see or know him: but you know him, for he lives with you and will be in you.
John 14:18 I will not leave you alone: I will come to you.
John 14:19 Soon, the world will not see me; but you will see me. Because I am alive, you will live too.
John 14:20 On that day, you will understand that I am with my Father, and you are with me, and I am with you.
John 14:21 The one who has my commands and follows them truly loves me; and the one who loves me will be loved by my Father, and I will love him and show myself to him.
John 14:22 Judas, not Iscariot, asked him, "Lord, how will you show yourself to us but not to the world?"
John 14:23 Jesus replied, "If someone loves me, he will follow my teachings. Then my Father will love him, and we will come to him and live with him."
John 14:24 Anyone who doesn't love me doesn't follow my teachings. The message you hear isn't mine, but it comes from the Father who sent me.
John 14:25 I have said these things to you while I am still with you.
John 14:26 The Helper, the Holy Spirit, whom God will send in my name, will teach you everything and remind you of all I have told you.
John 14:27 I leave you peace, my peace I give you. Not the way the world does, do I give to you. Don't let your heart be troubled or afraid.
John 14:28 You have heard me say I will leave and come back to you. If you loved me, you would be happy because I am going to the Father, who is greater than me.
John 14:29 I have told you beforehand so that when it happens, you may believe.
John 14:30 From now on, I won't talk much with you because the ruler of this world is coming, and he has no power over me.
John 14:31 I want the world to know I love God; I follow His commands. Let's get up and go.
