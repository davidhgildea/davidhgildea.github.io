John 11:1 A man named Lazarus from the town of Bethany, where Mary and her sister Martha lived, was sick.
John 11:2 Mary, who put perfume on Jesus and dried his feet with her hair, had a brother named Lazarus who was ill.
John 11:3 So his sisters sent a message to him, saying, "Lord, look, the one you love is sick."
John 11:4 When Jesus heard this, he said, "This illness will not lead to death. It is for God's glory, so that God's Son may be honored through it."
John 11:5 Jesus loved Martha, her sister, and Lazarus.
John 11:6 After hearing that he was sick, he stayed two more days in the same place he was.
John 11:7 After that, he said to his disciples, "Let's go back to Judea."
John 11:8 His followers asked him, "Teacher, recently the Jews wanted to stone you; and you're going back there?"
John 11:9 Jesus answered, "Isn't there twelve hours in a day? If someone walks during the day, they won't stumble because they see the world's light."
John 11:10 If someone walks at night, they trip because there's no light inside them.
John 11:11 He said these things, and then he told them, "Our friend Lazarus is sleeping; but I am going to wake him up."
John 11:12 Then his followers said, "Master, if he is sleeping, he will get better."
John 11:13 However, Jesus was talking about his death; but they thought he was talking about resting by sleeping.
John 11:14 Jesus told them clearly, "Lazarus is dead."
John 11:15 I am happy for you that I wasn't there, so you might believe; but let's go to him now.
John 11:16 Then Thomas, also known as Didymus, told the other disciples, "Let's go too, so we can die with him."
John 11:17 When Jesus arrived, he found that the man had been in the tomb for four days.
John 11:18 Bethany was close to Jerusalem, about fifteen furlongs away.
John 11:19 Many Jews visited Martha and Mary to comfort them about their brother.
John 11:20 Martha, when she heard Jesus was coming, went to meet him; but Mary stayed in the house.
John 11:21 Martha said to Jesus, "Lord, if you had been here, my brother would not have died."
John 11:22 I know that even now, whatever you ask from God, He will give it to you.
John 11:23 Jesus said to her, Your brother will live again.
John 11:24 Martha said to him, "I know he will rise again in the resurrection on the last day."
John 11:25 Jesus told her, "I am the one who brings people back to life, and I am life itself. Anyone who believes in me will live, even if they die.
John 11:26 Anyone who lives and believes in me will never die. Do you believe this?
John 11:27 She said to him, "Yes, Lord: I believe that you are the Christ, the Son of God, who was to come into the world."
John 11:28 After saying this, she left and quietly told her sister Mary, "The Teacher has arrived and is asking for you."
John 11:29 As soon as she heard that, she got up fast and went to him.
John 11:30 Jesus had not entered the town yet; he was still in the place where Martha met him.
John 11:31 The Jews who were with her in the house, comforting her, saw Mary get up quickly and leave. They followed her, saying, "She is going to the tomb to cry."
John 11:32 When Mary arrived where Jesus was and saw him, she fell at his feet and said, "Lord, if you had been here, my brother would not have died."
John 11:33 When Jesus saw her crying and the Jews who came with her crying too, he felt deeply moved and was upset.
John 11:34 And asked, "Where have you placed him?" They replied, "Sir, come and see."
John 11:35 Jesus cried.
John 11:36 Then the Jewish people said, "Look how much he cared for him!"
John 11:37 Some of them asked, "Couldn't this man, who made blind people see, have stopped this man from dying?"
John 11:38 Jesus, feeling deep sadness again, came to the tomb. It was a cave with a stone placed over the entrance.
John 11:39 Jesus said, "Remove the stone." Martha, the sister of the man who had died, said to him, "God, by now there will be a bad smell because he has been dead for four days."
John 11:40 Jesus said to her, "Didn't I tell you that if you believed, you would see God's glory?"
John 11:41 They removed the stone from where the dead person was placed. Jesus looked up and said, "Father, I thank you for hearing me."
John 11:42 I know you always hear me, but I said this for the people here, so they will believe you sent me.
John 11:43 After he said this, he shouted, "Lazarus, come out!"
John 11:44 The dead man came out, tied up at the hands and feet with burial cloths, and his face was wrapped in a cloth. Jesus told them, "Untie him and let him go."
John 11:45 Many Jews who visited Mary and saw what Jesus did started to believe in him.
John 11:46 Some of them went to the Pharisees and told them what Jesus had done.
John 11:47 The chief priests and Pharisees met and said, "What should we do? This man performs many miracles."
John 11:48 If we leave him alone, everyone will believe in him, and the Romans will come and take away our positions and our country.
John 11:49 One of them, named Caiaphas, who was the high priest that year, told them, "You don't understand anything."
John 11:50 We must realize it's better for us that one man dies for the people, so the whole nation doesn't get destroyed.
John 11:51 He didn't say this on his own; as the high priest that year, he predicted that Jesus would die for the nation.
John 11:52 Not just for that nation, but also to unite all of God's children who were spread out.
John 11:53 From that day on, they planned together to kill him.
John 11:54 So Jesus didn't walk openly among the Jews anymore. Instead, he went to a region near the wilderness, to a city named Ephraim, and stayed there with his followers.
John 11:55 The Jewish Passover was soon to happen, and many people left the countryside to go to Jerusalem before the Passover to make themselves clean.
John 11:56 They looked for Jesus and asked each other as they stood in the temple, "What do you think, will he not come to the celebration?"
John 11:57 The chief priests and the Pharisees had ordered that if anyone knew where he was, they should tell so they could arrest him.
