John 15:1 I am the real vine, and my Father takes care of it.
John 15:2 Every branch in me that does not produce fruit, God removes. Every branch that does produce fruit, God cleans so it will make even more fruit.
John 15:3 You are clean now because of the words I have spoken to you.
John 15:4 Stay with me, and I with you. Just as a branch can't produce fruit alone without being part of the vine, you can't either unless you stay with me.
John 15:5 I am the vine, you are the branches. Anyone who remains in me, and I in them, will produce a lot of fruit. Without me, you can do nothing.
John 15:6 If someone does not remain in me, he is thrown away like a dead branch; people pick it up, throw it into the fire, and it burns.
John 15:7 If you remain in me and my words stay in you, you can ask for anything you want, and it will be done for you.
John 15:8 In this, my Father is honored: when you produce a lot of fruit, then you are truly my followers.
John 15:9 Just as the Father loves me, I also love you. Stay in my love.
John 15:10 If you follow my commands, you will remain in my love, just as I follow my Father's commands and remain in his love.
John 15:11 I have told you these things so that my joy stays in you and your joy is complete.
John 15:12 My command is this: Love each other as I have loved you.
John 15:13 No one has greater love than to give up their life for their friends.
John 15:14 You are my friends if you do everything I tell you to do.
John 15:15 From now on, I don't call you servants, because a servant doesn't know what their master is doing. Instead, I have called you friends, because everything I learned from my Father, I have told you.
John 15:16 You did not choose me, but I chose you and appointed you to go and produce lasting fruit. So whatever you ask of God in my name, he will give to you.
John 15:17 I instruct you to love each other.
John 15:18 If the world hates you, know that it hated me before it hated you.
John 15:19 If you belonged to the world, the world would love you as its own. But you do not belong to the world, because I have chosen you out of the world. That's why the world hates you.
John 15:20 Remember what I told you: A servant isn't above their master. If they have mistreated me, they will mistreat you too; if they have followed my teachings, they will follow yours as well.
John 15:21 They will do all these things to you because of me, since they do not know the one who sent me.
John 15:22 If I hadn't come and spoken to them, they wouldn't be guilty of sin; but now they can't hide their sin.
John 15:23 Whoever hates me also hates my Father.
John 15:24 If I had not done works among them that no one else did, they would not be guilty of sin. But now they have seen and hated both me and my Father.
John 15:25 This happens so that what is written in their law comes true: They hated me for no reason.
John 15:26 But when the Helper comes, whom I will send to you from God, the Spirit of truth who goes out from God, he will tell about me.
John 15:27 You will also testify because you have been with me from the start.
