John 12:1 Six days before Passover, Jesus went to Bethany, where Lazarus lived, whom he had brought back to life after death.
John 12:2 There they prepared a dinner for him; Martha served, while Lazarus was one of those who sat at the table with him.
John 12:3 Mary took a pound of very expensive spikenard perfume, put it on Jesus' feet, and wiped his feet with her hair. The smell of the perfume filled the house.
John 12:4 One of his followers, Judas Iscariot, the son of Simon, who would later betray him, said
John 12:5 Why wasn't this perfume sold for three hundred coins and given to the poor?
John 12:6 He said this not because he cared for the poor, but because he was a thief, held the money bag, and took what was placed in it.
John 12:7 Jesus said, Leave her alone; she saved this for the day of my burial.
John 12:8 You will always have poor people with you, but you will not always have me.
John 12:9 Many Jewish people knew he was there, so they came not just for Jesus, but also to see Lazarus whom he brought back to life.
John 12:10 The leading priests planned to kill Lazarus too.
John 12:11 Because of him, many Jews left and believed in Jesus.
John 12:12 The next day, many people who had come to the celebration heard that Jesus was heading to Jerusalem.
John 12:13 They took palm tree branches, went out to meet him, and shouted, "Hosanna: Blessed is the King of Israel who comes in God's name."
John 12:14 Jesus found a young donkey and sat on it, as the scripture says.
John 12:15 Don't be afraid, people of Zion: look, your King is coming, riding on a young donkey.
John 12:16 At first, his disciples did not understand these things. But after Jesus was honored, they remembered that these things were written about him, and that they had happened to him.
John 12:17 The people with him when he called Lazarus out of his tomb and woke him from the dead, witnessed it.
John 12:18 The people came to meet him because they heard he had performed this miracle.
John 12:19 The Pharisees then said to each other, "Do you see that we are not succeeding at all? Look, everyone is following him."
John 12:20 Some Greeks who were there to worship at the festival were among them.
John 12:21 This person came to Philip from Bethsaida in Galilee, and asked, "Sir, we want to see Jesus."
John 12:22 Philip came and told Andrew, and then Andrew and Philip told Jesus.
John 12:23 Jesus replied to them, "The time has come for the Son of man to be honored."
John 12:24 Truly, truly, I tell you, unless a grain of wheat falls into the soil and dies, it stays single; but if it dies, it produces lots of seeds.
John 12:25 Whoever loves their life will lose it; and whoever hates their life in this world will keep it for eternal life.
John 12:26 If someone serves me, he should follow me; and where I am, my servant will be too: if someone serves me, God will honor him.
John 12:27 My soul is troubled; what should I say? Father, rescue me from this time. Yet this is why I came to this moment.
John 12:28 Father, make your name honored. Then a voice from heaven spoke, "I have honored it, and I will honor it again."
John 12:29 The people standing nearby who heard it said it thundered; others said an angel spoke to him.
John 12:30 Jesus replied, "This voice was not for me, but for you."
John 12:31 The time has come to judge this world; now the ruler of this world will be thrown out.
John 12:32 If I am raised up from the earth, I will attract everyone to me.
John 12:33 He said this to show what kind of death he was going to die.
John 12:34 The people replied, "We know from the law that Christ will live forever. So why do you say that the Son of Man must be raised up? Who is this Son of Man?"
John 12:35 Jesus told them, "The light will be with you for just a short time. Walk while you have the light, before darkness overtakes you. Anyone walking in darkness doesn't know where they are going."
John 12:36 While you have light, trust in it, so you can be children of light. After saying this, Jesus left and hid from them.
John 12:37 Even though he had performed many miracles in front of them, they still did not believe in him.
John 12:38 So that what the prophet Isaiah said would come true, he asked, "God, who has believed what we said? And to whom has God shown his power?"
John 12:39 So they couldn't believe, because Isaiah had said before,
John 12:40 God has made their eyes blind and their hearts hard; so they cannot see with their eyes or understand with their hearts, and turn to me so I can heal them.
John 12:41 Isaiah said these things when he saw God's glory and spoke about him.
John 12:42 Many leaders also believed in him, but they did not admit it because of the Pharisees; they were afraid of being expelled from the synagogue.
John 12:43 They liked people's approval more than God's approval.
John 12:44 Jesus cried out and said, "Anyone who believes in me does not just believe in me, but in the one who sent me."
John 12:45 Whoever sees me sees the one who sent me.
John 12:46 I have come as a light into the world so that everyone who believes in me will not stay in darkness.
John 12:47 If anyone hears my words and does not believe, I do not judge them, because I did not come to judge the world, but to save it.
John 12:48 Whoever rejects me and does not accept my words has something that will judge them: the words I have spoken will be their judge on the last day.
John 12:49 I don't speak for myself; the Father who sent me commanded me what to say and speak.
John 12:50 I know that God's command leads to eternal life. So, whatever I say is just as the Father told me to say.
