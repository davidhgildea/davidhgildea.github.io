John 10:1 Truly, truly, I tell you, anyone who does not enter the sheep pen by the gate but climbs in by another way is a thief and a robber.
John 10:2 The one who comes in through the door is the shepherd of the sheep.
John 10:3 The gatekeeper opens the gate for him, and the sheep listen to his voice. He calls his own sheep by name and leads them out.
John 10:4 When he leads his sheep out, he walks ahead of them, and the sheep follow him because they recognize his voice.
John 10:5 They will not follow a stranger but will run away from him because they do not recognize a stranger's voice.
John 10:6 Jesus told them this story, but they did not understand what he was saying to them.
John 10:7 Jesus told them again, "Truly, I tell you, I am the gate for the sheep."
John 10:8 Everyone who came before me were thieves and robbers, but the sheep did not listen to them.
John 10:9 I am the door. If anyone enters through me, they will be saved. They will come in and go out and find food.
John 10:10 The thief comes only to steal, kill, and destroy. I have come so that they can have life, and have it fully.
John 10:11 I am the good shepherd; the good shepherd gives his life for the sheep.
John 10:12 But the person who works only for pay and is not the shepherd, who does not own the sheep, sees the wolf coming, leaves the sheep, runs away, and the wolf catches and scatters the sheep.
John 10:13 The worker runs away because he is just a worker and does not care about the sheep.
John 10:14 I am the good shepherd; I know my sheep and my sheep know me.
John 10:15 Just as the Father knows me, I also know the Father; and I give up my life for the sheep.
John 10:16 I have other sheep that are not from this pen. I must bring them too, and they will listen to my voice; then there will be one flock and one shepherd.
John 10:17 So my Father loves me because I give up my life and then take it back.
John 10:18 No one takes it from me; I give it up by my choice. I have the power to give it up, and I have the power to take it back. This order I got from my Father.
John 10:19 So the Jews were divided again because of these words.
John 10:20 Many people said, "He is possessed by a demon and is crazy; why do you listen to him?"
John 10:21 Others said, "These aren't the words of someone with an evil spirit. Can an evil spirit make blind people see?"
John 10:22 It was winter during the Feast of Dedication in Jerusalem.
John 10:23 Jesus walked in the temple on Solomon's porch.
John 10:24 The Jews gathered around him and asked, "How long will you keep us guessing? If you are the Christ, tell us clearly."
John 10:25 Jesus replied, "I told you, but you did not believe. The things I do, done in my Father's name, show who I am."
John 10:26 But you don't believe because you are not my sheep, as I told you.
John 10:27 My sheep listen to me, I know them, and they follow me.
John 10:28 I give them forever life; they will never die, and no one can take them from my hand.
John 10:29 My Father, who gave them to me, is greater than everyone; and no one can take them from my Father's hand.
John 10:30 My Father and I are the same.
John 10:31 The Jews picked up stones once more to throw at him.
John 10:32 Jesus answered them, "I have shown you many good things from God; for which of those good things do you want to punish me with stones?"
John 10:33 The Jews replied, "We're not going to stone you for doing good things; we're doing it for disrespecting God, because you, a man, claim to be God."
John 10:34 Jesus replied, "Isn't it written in your law, 'I said, you are gods'?"
John 10:35 If he named them gods, to whom God's message came, and the scripture cannot be changed;
John 10:36 Are you saying that the one whom God has made holy and sent into the world is speaking blasphemy because I said, 'I am God's Son'?
John 10:37 If I don't do what my Father does, don't believe me.
John 10:38 But if I do them, even if you don't believe me, believe the things I do. Then you will understand and believe that God is in me, and I am in God.
John 10:39 So they tried again to seize him, but he got away from them.
John 10:40 He went back to the area beyond the Jordan River, where John first baptized people, and stayed there.
John 10:41 Many people came to him and said, "John didn't perform any miracles, but everything John said about this man was true."
John 10:42 Many people there believed in him.
