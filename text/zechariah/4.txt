Zechariah 4:1 The angel who was speaking with me came back and woke me up like someone being woken from sleep.
Zechariah 4:2 He asked me, "What do you see?" And I answered, "I see a golden lampstand with a bowl at the top and seven lights on it, with seven tubes going to the lights."
Zechariah 4:3 Two olive trees were next to it, one on the right side of the bowl and one on the left side.
Zechariah 4:4 So I replied to the angel speaking with me, asking, "What are these, sir?"
Zechariah 4:5 The angel speaking with me replied and asked, "Do you not know what these are?" And I answered, "No, sir."
Zechariah 4:6 He replied to me, "This is what God tells Zerubbabel: 'Not by strength or by force, but by my spirit,' says God."
Zechariah 4:7 Who are you, great mountain? Before Zerubbabel, you will become flat land: and he will present the top stone amid shouts of 'Grace, grace to it!'
Zechariah 4:8 Also, God spoke to me, saying,
Zechariah 4:9 Zerubbabel's hands have started building this house; his hands will also complete it; and you will know that God has sent me to you.
Zechariah 4:10 Who has looked down on small beginnings? They will celebrate and see the measuring line in Zerubbabel's hand along with the seven; they represent God's eyes, which move throughout the entire world.
Zechariah 4:11 I answered and asked him, "What are these two olive trees on the right and left sides of the lampstand?"
Zechariah 4:12 I replied again and asked him, "What are these two olive branches that pour out golden oil through the two gold pipes?"
Zechariah 4:13 He answered and asked, "Do you not know what these are?" And I said, "No, sir."
Zechariah 4:14 He said, "These are the two chosen ones who stand by God of the whole earth."
