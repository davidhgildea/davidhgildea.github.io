Zechariah 2:1 I looked up once more, and saw a man holding a measuring tape.
Zechariah 2:2 Then I asked, "Where are you going?" And he answered me, "To measure Jerusalem, to find out how wide and how long it is."
Zechariah 2:3 The angel who was speaking with me left, and another angel came out to meet him.
Zechariah 2:4 He said to him, "Go quickly, tell the young man, 'Jerusalem will be filled with people and animals, and it won't need walls.'"
Zechariah 2:5 I, says God, will be like a protective wall of fire around her, and I will be the honor within her.
Zechariah 2:6 Come, come, leave the land of the north, says God, for I have scattered you in all directions like the four winds of the sky, says God.
Zechariah 2:7 Save yourself, Zion, who lives among the people of Babylon.
Zechariah 2:8 God, the Lord of all, says this: After the glory, he sent me to the nations that robbed you. Because whoever harms you, harms the apple of his eye.
Zechariah 2:9 Look, I will wave my hand at them, and they will become loot for their own slaves. Then you will know that God has sent me.
Zechariah 2:10 Sing and be happy, daughter of Zion; for look, I am coming and I will live among you, says God.
Zechariah 2:11 Many countries will join with God on that day and will be my people. I will live among you, and you will realize that God has sent me to you.
Zechariah 2:12 God will take Judah as his own in the sacred land and will choose Jerusalem once more.
Zechariah 2:13 Everyone be quiet before God, for he has risen from his sacred home.
