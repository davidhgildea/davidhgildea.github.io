Proverbs 17:1 It is better to have a little with peace than a house full of food with fighting.
Proverbs 17:2 A smart servant will be in charge instead of a son who brings shame, and will share in the family inheritance.
Proverbs 17:3 A melting pot is for silver, and a furnace is for gold, but God tests the heart.
Proverbs 17:4 A bad person listens to lies, and a liar pays attention to hurtful words.
Proverbs 17:5 Anyone who mocks the poor insults God, and those who are happy when others suffer will be punished.
Proverbs 17:6 Grandchildren are a joy to the elderly; and children take pride in their parents.
Proverbs 17:7 Good words are not fitting for a fool; even less are lies suitable for a leader.
Proverbs 17:8 A gift is like a valuable gem to the person who has it: wherever it goes, it succeeds.
Proverbs 17:9 Whoever forgives an offense shows love; but one who keeps talking about the issue pushes friends apart.
Proverbs 17:10 A correction is understood better by a wise person than a hundred beatings are by a fool.
Proverbs 17:11 A bad person looks for trouble, so a harsh messenger will be sent to him.
Proverbs 17:12 It's better to meet a mother bear who has lost her cubs than a fool doing something stupid.
Proverbs 17:13 Anyone who pays back good with evil will always have evil in their home.
Proverbs 17:14 Strife starts like a water leak, so stop arguing before it gets worse.
Proverbs 17:15 Anyone who says the bad person is right, and anyone who says the good person is wrong, both are hated by God.
Proverbs 17:16 Why does a fool have money to buy wisdom when he has no desire for it?
Proverbs 17:17 A friend always loves, and a brother is there for tough times.
Proverbs 17:18 A person without good sense shakes hands in agreement, promising to pay for his friend.
Proverbs 17:19 Someone who loves fighting loves to sin; and one who makes their entrance grand is looking for ruin.
Proverbs 17:20 Someone with a crooked heart finds no good, and a person with a twisted tongue gets into trouble.
Proverbs 17:21 Whoever has a foolish child brings sorrow to themselves; a fool's parent has no happiness.
Proverbs 17:22 A happy heart is good for you like medicine, but a sad spirit dries up the bones.
Proverbs 17:23 A bad person takes a bribe secretly to twist fair decisions.
Proverbs 17:24 The wise person keeps wisdom in view, but a fool's gaze wanders to the ends of the earth.
Proverbs 17:25 A silly son makes his father sad and his mother bitter.
Proverbs 17:26 It is wrong to punish the innocent, and it is not good to hit leaders for doing right.
Proverbs 17:27 Someone with knowledge uses few words: a person with understanding has a calm spirit.
Proverbs 17:28 Even a fool seems wise if they stay silent, and a person who keeps their mouth shut is thought to be smart.
