Proverbs 24:1 Do not be jealous of bad people, nor wish to spend time with them.
Proverbs 24:2 Their heart plans to destroy, and their lips speak of trouble.
Proverbs 24:3 A house is built with wisdom and set up with understanding.
Proverbs 24:4 With knowledge, the rooms will be filled with all valuable and beautiful treasures.
Proverbs 24:5 A smart person is powerful; yes, a person who knows a lot gets even stronger.
Proverbs 24:6 With smart advice, you will plan your battles well; having many advisors brings safety.
Proverbs 24:7 Wisdom is too much for a fool; he doesn't speak in public.
Proverbs 24:8 Anyone who plans to do bad will be known as a troublemaker.
Proverbs 24:9 Thinking foolish thoughts is wrong, and people hate a mocker.
Proverbs 24:10 If you give up when trouble comes, you don't have much strength.
Proverbs 24:11 If you don't save those who are being led to death, and those about to be killed;
Proverbs 24:12 If you say, "We didn't know about it," doesn't God, who knows what's in your heart, see it? And the one who watches over you, doesn't He know it? Won't He repay everyone for what they've done?
Proverbs 24:13 My son, eat honey because it is good; and the honeycomb, which tastes sweet to you.
Proverbs 24:14 Understanding wisdom will be good for you. When you find it, you will be rewarded, and your hopes will not be dashed.
Proverbs 24:15 Don't set a trap, wicked person, at the home of the good person; don't destroy where they rest.
Proverbs 24:16 A good person may fall down seven times but gets up again, while the bad will stumble into trouble.
Proverbs 24:17 Do not be happy when your enemy falls, and do not feel glad when they trip.
Proverbs 24:18 So that God does not see it and get unhappy, causing him to stop being angry with that person.
Proverbs 24:19 Don't worry about bad people, and don't be jealous of the wicked.
Proverbs 24:20 Evil people won't be rewarded; the light of the wicked will be extinguished.
Proverbs 24:21 My son, respect God and the king, and do not associate with those who like to rebel.
Proverbs 24:22 Their disaster will come quickly; and who knows the destruction that will come upon them both?
Proverbs 24:23 Wise people know this too: It is not good to favor some people over others when making a decision.
Proverbs 24:24 Anyone who tells the wicked they are right will be cursed by people and despised by nations.
Proverbs 24:25 Those who correct others will find joy, and blessings will come to them.
Proverbs 24:26 Everyone likes someone who gives the right answer.
Proverbs 24:27 Get your work ready outside, and make it ready for yourself in the field; then build your house.
Proverbs 24:28 Do not accuse your neighbor without reason, and do not lie.
Proverbs 24:29 Do not say, "I will treat him as he treated me; I will give the man what he deserves for what he did."
Proverbs 24:30 I passed by the lazy person's field and the vineyard of someone who lacks sense.
Proverbs 24:31 Look, it was all covered with thorns, and nettles had spread over its surface, and its stone wall was torn down.
Proverbs 24:32 I saw it, thought about it carefully, observed it, and learned a lesson.
Proverbs 24:33 Just a bit more sleep, a bit more rest, a bit more of hugging your arms to sleep.
Proverbs 24:34 Your poverty will come like a traveler; your need like an armed man.
