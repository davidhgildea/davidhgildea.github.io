Proverbs 6:1 My son, if you promise to pay for your friend, if you have shaken hands with a stranger,
Proverbs 6:2 You are trapped by what you said, caught by your own words.
Proverbs 6:3 Do this now, my child, and save yourself, when you are in your friend's power; go, lower yourself, and make peace with your friend.
Proverbs 6:4 Do not let your eyes fall asleep, or your eyelids get drowsy.
Proverbs 6:5 Escape like a deer from the hunter, and like a bird from the person who catches birds.
Proverbs 6:6 Look at the ant, you lazy person; watch how it does things and learn.
Proverbs 6:7 Without a leader, watcher, or ruler,
Proverbs 6:8 She gets her food in the summer and collects her food at harvest time.
Proverbs 6:9 How long will you sleep, you lazy person? When will you get up from your sleep?
Proverbs 6:10 Just a bit more sleep, a bit more rest, a bit more folding of the hands to rest.
Proverbs 6:11 Your poverty will come like a traveler, and your need like an armed person.
Proverbs 6:12 A bad person, an evil man, speaks with a twisted mouth.
Proverbs 6:13 He signals with his eyes, he communicates with his feet, he instructs with his fingers.
Proverbs 6:14 He has evil plans in his heart, he always thinks of harm; he spreads conflict.
Proverbs 6:15 So his trouble will come quickly; he will be quickly destroyed with no cure.
Proverbs 6:16 God hates these six things; indeed, seven are detestable to Him:
Proverbs 6:17 An arrogant stare, a deceitful speech, and hands that harm the innocent,
Proverbs 6:18 A heart that plans evil ideas, feet that quickly run to trouble,
Proverbs 6:19 A person who lies as a witness and someone who causes arguments among brothers.
Proverbs 6:20 My child, follow your father's instruction and do not ignore your mother's teaching.
Proverbs 6:21 Keep them always in your heart, and wrap them around your neck.
Proverbs 6:22 When you go, it will guide you; when you sleep, it will watch over you; and when you wake up, it will speak to you.
Proverbs 6:23 The commandment is a lamp, the law is light, and corrections that teach are the path to life.
Proverbs 6:24 To protect you from a bad woman, from the smooth talk of a stranger.
Proverbs 6:25 Do not desire her beauty in your heart; do not let her capture you with her eyes.
Proverbs 6:26 A loose woman can reduce a man to poverty, and a cheating wife can endanger a valuable life.
Proverbs 6:27 Can someone hold fire close and not burn their clothes?
Proverbs 6:28 Can someone walk on hot coals without burning their feet?
Proverbs 6:29 Anyone who sleeps with his neighbor's wife or touches her will not be innocent.
Proverbs 6:30 People do not hate a thief if he steals to feed himself when he is hungry.
Proverbs 6:31 If he is caught, he must repay seven times what he took; he must give everything he owns.
Proverbs 6:32 Anyone who cheats with a woman is unwise; doing this harms his own life.
Proverbs 6:33 He will get hurt and disgrace, and his shame will never go away.
Proverbs 6:34 Jealousy makes a man very angry, so he won't show mercy when he takes revenge.
Proverbs 6:35 He will not accept a payment; nor will he be satisfied, even if you give many gifts.
