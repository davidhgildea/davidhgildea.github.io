Proverbs 25:1 These are also sayings of Solomon, which the men of Hezekiah, king of Judah, wrote down.
Proverbs 25:2 God finds glory in hiding things; a king's honor is to discover them.
Proverbs 25:3 The sky is high, the earth is deep, and a king’s thoughts are hard to understand.
Proverbs 25:4 Remove the impurities from the silver, and a vessel for the silversmith will be made.
Proverbs 25:5 Remove the bad people from the king's presence, and his rule will be secure and good.
Proverbs 25:6 Do not push yourself forward in front of the king, and do not stand where important people are.
Proverbs 25:7 It is better to be invited to come up higher than to be told to move down in front of a leader you have seen.
Proverbs 25:8 Don't rush to argue, or you won't know what to do when your neighbor embarrasses you.
Proverbs 25:9 Talk about your problem with your neighbor directly, and don't tell someone else their secret.
Proverbs 25:10 So the listener won't embarrass you, and your bad reputation won't disappear.
Proverbs 25:11 The right word at the right time is like golden apples in a silver setting.
Proverbs 25:12 Like a gold earring or a fine gold decoration, so is wise advice to someone who listens.
Proverbs 25:13 Like the coolness of snow at harvest time, a trustworthy messenger is to those who send him; he revives the spirits of his employers.
Proverbs 25:14 Anyone who brags about a fake gift is like clouds and wind that bring no rain.
Proverbs 25:15 With patient persistence, a ruler can be convinced, and gentle speech can overcome strong resistance.
Proverbs 25:16 Have you found honey? Eat only as much as you need, so you don't overeat and throw it up.
Proverbs 25:17 Stop visiting your neighbor's house too much, or he will get tired of you and start to dislike you.
Proverbs 25:18 A person who lies about others is as harmful as a club, a sword, or a sharp arrow.
Proverbs 25:19 Trusting someone who is not loyal when there is trouble is like having a broken tooth or a sprained foot.
Proverbs 25:20 Like someone who takes off a coat on a cold day or pours vinegar on soda, is a person who sings songs to a sad heart.
Proverbs 25:21 If your enemy is hungry, give him food; if he is thirsty, give him water.
Proverbs 25:22 You will make him feel guilty, like putting hot coals on his head, and God will reward you.
Proverbs 25:23 The north wind blows rain away; just like an angry look stops mean gossip.
Proverbs 25:24 It's better to live in a small corner on the roof than in a big house with a quarrelsome woman.
Proverbs 25:25 Like cold water to a thirsty person, good news from a far place is so refreshing.
Proverbs 25:26 A good person bending before the bad is like a dirty water source or a spoiled stream.
Proverbs 25:27 Eating too much honey is not good, and seeking your own praise is not really praise.
Proverbs 25:28 A person who can't control their emotions is like a city with broken walls and no defense.
