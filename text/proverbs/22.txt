Proverbs 22:1 Having a good reputation is better than having a lot of money, and being liked is better than having silver and gold.
Proverbs 22:2 Rich and poor people come together; God made all of them.
Proverbs 22:3 A wise person sees danger and hides, but the naive keep going and suffer for it.
Proverbs 22:4 By being humble and respecting God come wealth, respect, and life.
Proverbs 22:5 Thorns and traps are in the path of the stubborn; the one who protects their own soul stays away from them.
Proverbs 22:6 Teach a child how to live right, and even when old, they won't stray from it.
Proverbs 22:7 The rich rule the poor, and the borrower becomes the lender's servant.
Proverbs 22:8 The person who does wrong will get useless results; their anger will not succeed.
Proverbs 22:9 The person with a generous eye will be blessed, because he shares his food with the needy.
Proverbs 22:10 Remove the mocker, and fighting will end; yes, conflict and insults will stop.
Proverbs 22:11 Someone who loves a pure heart and speaks kindly will be a friend to the king.
Proverbs 22:12 God's eyes protect knowledge and He overthrows the words of the wrongdoer.
Proverbs 22:13 The lazy person says, "There is a lion outside, I will be killed in the streets."
Proverbs 22:14 The words of unfamiliar women are like a deep hole: a man disliked by God will fall into it.
Proverbs 22:15 A child's heart holds foolishness; but strict discipline removes it far from them.
Proverbs 22:16 Whoever mistreats the poor to get more wealth, and gives to the rich, will definitely become poor.
Proverbs 22:17 Listen closely and hear the wise words, and take my knowledge to heart.
Proverbs 22:18 It is good if you remember these things in your heart; they will be ready on your lips.
Proverbs 22:19 I have told you today so you can trust in God, just you.
Proverbs 22:20 I have written to you excellent things about advice and knowledge.
Proverbs 22:21 I want you to clearly understand the true words so you can give true answers to those who ask you.
Proverbs 22:22 Do not steal from the poor because they are poor, and do not make life hard for those who are suffering at the city gate.
Proverbs 22:23 God will defend their cause and ruin those who have ruined them.
Proverbs 22:24 Do not be friends with someone who is always angry, and do not go with someone who has a bad temper.
Proverbs 22:25 So you won't learn his ways and trap your soul.
Proverbs 22:26 Don't be someone who shakes hands in agreement, or who guarantees to pay someone else's debts.
Proverbs 22:27 If you have nothing to pay, why should he take your bed from under you?
Proverbs 22:28 Do not move the old boundary markers set by your ancestors.
Proverbs 22:29 Do you see a person who works hard in their work? They will be in the presence of rulers; they will not be in the presence of ordinary people.
