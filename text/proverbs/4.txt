Proverbs 4:1 Listen, children, to a father's teaching, and pay attention to gain wisdom.
Proverbs 4:2 I give you good teaching, do not abandon my rules.
Proverbs 4:3 I was my father's child, gentle and the only one loved by my mother.
Proverbs 4:4 He also taught me and told me, "Keep my words in your heart: follow my rules, and you will live."
Proverbs 4:5 Gain wisdom, gain insight: don't forget; don't turn away from what I say.
Proverbs 4:6 Do not abandon her, and she will protect you: love her, and she will take care of you.
Proverbs 4:7 Wisdom is the most important; so get wisdom, and with all you have, get understanding.
Proverbs 4:8 Honor her, and she will lift you up; she will give you respect when you hold her tightly.
Proverbs 4:9 She will give you a beautiful crown to make you look good; you will receive a glorious crown from her.
Proverbs 4:10 Listen, my child, and accept what I say; then you will live a long life.
Proverbs 4:11 I have taught you in the way of wisdom; I have guided you on right paths.
Proverbs 4:12 When you go, your steps will not be tight; and when you run, you will not trip.
Proverbs 4:13 Hold on to teaching tightly; don't let it go: protect it, because it is your life.
Proverbs 4:14 Don't follow the path of the wicked or walk in the way of bad people.
Proverbs 4:15 Stay away, do not go near it, turn from it and keep going.
Proverbs 4:16 They can't sleep until they do wrong; they miss sleep if they haven't made someone stumble.
Proverbs 4:17 They eat the food of evil and drink the wine of violence.
Proverbs 4:18 The way of good people is like the sunrise, getting brighter and brighter until full daylight.
Proverbs 4:19 Wicked people live in darkness and don't understand what makes them fall.
Proverbs 4:20 My son, listen to my words; pay attention to what I say.
Proverbs 4:21 Always keep them in your sight; store them deep in your heart.
Proverbs 4:22 They give life to those who find them and health to everyone's body.
Proverbs 4:23 Protect your heart carefully, because from it come the important things in life.
Proverbs 4:24 Stop talking in a twisted way, and keep dishonest words far from you.
Proverbs 4:25 Keep your eyes looking directly forward, and your gaze straight ahead of you.
Proverbs 4:26 Think carefully about where you walk, and make sure all your actions are steady.
Proverbs 4:27 Do not turn right or left: keep away from evil.
