Proverbs 12:1 Anyone who loves learning loves knowledge, but someone who hates correction is foolish.
Proverbs 12:2 A good person gains God's favor, but God will condemn a person with evil plans.
Proverbs 12:3 Evil actions won't make a person successful, but good people will remain strong and secure.
Proverbs 12:4 A good woman is her husband's pride, but a woman who brings shame is like decay in his bones.
Proverbs 12:5 Good people think right thoughts, but the advice of the wicked is to lie.
Proverbs 12:6 Wicked people's words are like traps set to hurt others, but honest people will be saved by what they say.
Proverbs 12:7 Evil people are defeated and gone, but the home of the good will last.
Proverbs 12:8 People will praise a man for his wisdom, but someone with a bad heart will be disliked.
Proverbs 12:9 Someone who is looked down on but has a servant is better off than someone who acts important but has no food.
Proverbs 12:10 A good person cares for their animal's life, but even the kindness of a bad person is harsh.
Proverbs 12:11 Whoever works their land will have plenty of food, but whoever chases empty dreams lacks wisdom.
Proverbs 12:12 Bad people want what other bad people have, but good people produce good results.
Proverbs 12:13 Evil people get caught by their own words, but the good get out of trouble.
Proverbs 12:14 A person will be happy with the good things they say, and they will get back what their own work deserves.
Proverbs 12:15 A fool thinks they are always right, but a wise person listens to advice.
Proverbs 12:16 A foolish person quickly shows anger, but a wise person hides embarrassment.
Proverbs 12:17 The person who speaks the truth shows what is right, but a liar tells lies.
Proverbs 12:18 Some people speak sharp words like a sword's stab, but wise people's words bring healing.
Proverbs 12:19 Truthful words will last forever, but lies last only a moment.
Proverbs 12:20 People who plan evil have lies in their hearts, but those who advise peace find joy.
Proverbs 12:21 No harm will come to the good person, but the bad will be filled with trouble.
Proverbs 12:22 God hates lying lips, but loves those who speak the truth.
Proverbs 12:23 A wise person keeps knowledge to themselves, but a fool's heart shouts out silliness.
Proverbs 12:24 Hard workers will be leaders, but lazy people will be servants.
Proverbs 12:25 Sadness in a person's heart weighs them down: but a kind word cheers them up.
Proverbs 12:26 A good person is better than their neighbor, but the wicked are led astray by their own ways.
Proverbs 12:27 The lazy person doesn't cook what they catch hunting, but a hardworking person's wealth is valuable.
Proverbs 12:28 Living right brings life: there is no death on that path.
