Proverbs 20:1 Wine can mock you, strong drink can cause trouble, and anyone fooled by it is not smart.
Proverbs 20:2 Being afraid of a king is like hearing a lion roar: whoever makes him angry harms themselves.
Proverbs 20:3 It is honorable for a person to avoid arguments, but a foolish person keeps interfering.
Proverbs 20:4 The lazy person won't plow because it's cold; so they will have to beg during harvest time and will get nothing.
Proverbs 20:5 Advice in a person's heart is deep; but a wise person can bring it out.
Proverbs 20:6 Many people brag about how good they are, but finding someone faithful is hard.
Proverbs 20:7 A good person lives honestly, and their children are fortunate because of them.
Proverbs 20:8 A king sitting on the judgment throne chases away all evil with his look.
Proverbs 20:9 Who can claim, I have cleaned my heart, I am free from my sin?
Proverbs 20:10 Different weights and different measures, both are hated by God.
Proverbs 20:11 A child's actions show if their behavior is good and if what they do is right.
Proverbs 20:12 God made both the ear that hears and the eye that sees.
Proverbs 20:13 Don't love sleeping too much, or you will become poor; wake up, and you will have enough food to eat.
Proverbs 20:14 It's no good, it's no good, says the buyer; but when he leaves, then he brags.
Proverbs 20:15 There is gold and many rubies, but wise words are a valuable treasure.
Proverbs 20:16 Take his clothes if he promises for a stranger; get a security from him for an unknown woman.
Proverbs 20:17 Food gained by cheating may taste good, but later it will feel like eating rocks.
Proverbs 20:18 Every plan is set by advice: and with wise guidance, you should fight a battle.
Proverbs 20:19 Someone who spreads gossip tells secrets; so don't associate with a person who talks too much.
Proverbs 20:20 Anyone who curses their father or mother will have their light snuffed out in complete darkness.
Proverbs 20:21 Getting an inheritance quickly at first may not lead to a blessing in the end.
Proverbs 20:22 Do not say, "I will pay back evil;" but wait for God, and He will rescue you.
Proverbs 20:23 Different weights that cheat are disgusting to God; a dishonest scale is bad.
Proverbs 20:24 A person's paths are directed by God; so how can a person understand their own way?
Proverbs 20:25 A trap is set for the person who eats sacred things and then asks questions after making promises.
Proverbs 20:26 A wise king spreads out the bad people and rolls justice over them.
Proverbs 20:27 A person's spirit is like God's light, shining deep inside them.
Proverbs 20:28 Kindness and truth protect the king; his rule is supported by kindness.
Proverbs 20:29 Young men are honored for their strength, and older men are respected for their gray hair.
Proverbs 20:30 A bruise cleans out badness: in the same way, punishment cleans the inside of a person.
