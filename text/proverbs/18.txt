Proverbs 18:1 A person with strong desires sets themselves apart, searches, and gets involved with all kinds of knowledge.
Proverbs 18:2 A fool does not enjoy learning, only showing off their own opinions.
Proverbs 18:3 When evil people come, disrespect also comes, and with it comes shame and blame.
Proverbs 18:4 A person's words are like deep water, and wisdom is like a flowing stream.
Proverbs 18:5 It is not right to favor the wicked or to deny justice to the righteous.
Proverbs 18:6 A foolish person's words lead to arguments, and their mouth invites punishment.
Proverbs 18:7 A foolish person's words cause their ruin, and their lips trap their own life.
Proverbs 18:8 A gossip's words hurt like wounds; they reach deep inside a person.
Proverbs 18:9 The person who is lazy in their work is just like someone who wastes a lot.
Proverbs 18:10 God's name is like a strong tower: the good people run to it and are safe.
Proverbs 18:11 A rich person's money is like a protective city, and in their mind, it's like a tall wall.
Proverbs 18:12 Before ruin, a person's heart is proud, but before getting honor, there must be humility.
Proverbs 18:13 If someone replies to a topic without listening first, it is foolish and embarrassing for them.
Proverbs 18:14 A person's spirit can endure sickness; but who can live with a broken spirit?
Proverbs 18:15 A careful person's heart gains knowledge; a wise person's ear looks for knowledge.
Proverbs 18:16 A person's gift opens the way and brings them to meet important people.
Proverbs 18:17 Someone who speaks first in his argument appears right, until another comes and examines him.
Proverbs 18:18 Drawing lots stops fights and decides between powerful people.
Proverbs 18:19 An upset brother is tougher to win back than a fortified city, and their arguments are as secure as the locks on a fortress.
Proverbs 18:20 A person will be content with what they say; they will get enough from what they speak.
Proverbs 18:21 Life and death lie in what we say: those who enjoy talking will face the results.
Proverbs 18:22 Whoever finds a wife finds something good and gets favor from God.
Proverbs 18:23 The poor beg kindly, but the rich reply harshly.
Proverbs 18:24 A person with friends should act friendly, and some friends are more loyal than a brother.
