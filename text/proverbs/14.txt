Proverbs 14:1 A smart woman builds up her home, but a foolish one tears it down by herself.
Proverbs 14:2 Someone who lives rightly respects God, but a person with corrupt ways despises Him.
Proverbs 14:3 Foolish people speak with pride which brings punishment, but wise people's words keep them safe.
Proverbs 14:4 Where there are no oxen, the feeding trough is empty; but a strong ox brings a big harvest.
Proverbs 14:5 A trustworthy person won't lie, but a liar will tell lies.
Proverbs 14:6 Someone who mocks others looks for wisdom but does not find it; yet knowledge comes easily to someone who has understanding.
Proverbs 14:7 Leave the company of a foolish person when you realize they do not speak wise words.
Proverbs 14:8 Smart people know their own path; but foolish people trick others.
Proverbs 14:9 Fools laugh at doing wrong, but God's favor is with the good people.
Proverbs 14:10 The heart understands its own sadness; and an outsider does not share its happiness.
Proverbs 14:11 The wicked person's house will be destroyed, but the good person's home will thrive.
Proverbs 14:12 A path may seem right to a person, but it ends in death.
Proverbs 14:13 Even when laughing, the heart can be sad; and joy can finish with sadness.
Proverbs 14:14 Someone who turns away in heart will have to live with their own choices, and a good person will be content with themselves.
Proverbs 14:15 A naive person believes everything, but a wise one thinks carefully about their steps.
Proverbs 14:16 A wise person respects God and avoids evil, but a fool gets angry and feels sure of himself.
Proverbs 14:17 Someone who gets angry quickly acts foolishly, and a person who plans evil is disliked.
Proverbs 14:18 Simple people gain foolishness, but careful ones are rewarded with knowledge.
Proverbs 14:19 Bad people bow down to good people; and the wicked stand at the doors of those who are right.
Proverbs 14:20 Poor people are disliked even by their neighbors, but rich people have many friends.
Proverbs 14:21 Whoever looks down on their neighbor does wrong, but the one who is kind to the poor is happy.
Proverbs 14:22 Aren't those who plan evil wrong? But kindness and honesty will be for those who plan good.
Proverbs 14:23 In every kind of hard work there is benefit, but just talking leads to poverty.
Proverbs 14:24 Wise people's wealth is their crown, but foolish people's foolishness is just that.
Proverbs 14:25 A truthful person saves lives, but a liar tells lies.
Proverbs 14:26 Respecting God brings true security; his children will have a safe place.
Proverbs 14:27 Being afraid to disobey God is a source of life, helping to avoid deadly traps.
Proverbs 14:28 A king is honored with a lot of people, but a prince will be ruined without them.
Proverbs 14:29 Someone patient in anger has much understanding, but a quick-tempered person shows foolishness.
Proverbs 14:30 A healthy heart is the life of the body, but jealousy is the decay of the bones.
Proverbs 14:31 Whoever mistreats the poor insults God, but the one who respects God is kind to the poor.
Proverbs 14:32 The bad person is pushed away because of his badness: but the good person has hope even when he dies.
Proverbs 14:33 Wisdom stays in the heart of a person with understanding, but a fool's thoughts become known.
Proverbs 14:34 Being good lifts up a country, but sin shames any nation.
Proverbs 14:35 The king likes a smart servant, but he's angry at one who brings disgrace.
