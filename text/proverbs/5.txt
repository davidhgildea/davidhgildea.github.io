Proverbs 5:1 My son, listen to my wisdom, and pay attention to my understanding.
Proverbs 5:2 So you will value good judgment and your words will hold wisdom.
Proverbs 5:3 A strange woman's lips are sweet like honey, and her words are smoother than oil.
Proverbs 5:4 But her end is as bitter as a bitter plant and sharp like a sword with two edges.
Proverbs 5:5 Her feet lead to death; her steps reach hell.
Proverbs 5:6 Be careful thinking about the way of life; its paths are shifting, and you can't fully understand them.
Proverbs 5:7 Listen to me now, children, and do not turn away from what I say.
Proverbs 5:8 Stay far away from her, and don't go near her house door.
Proverbs 5:9 Don't let others take your honor or your life be controlled by the cruel.
Proverbs 5:10 Be careful that outsiders do not take your riches, and that your hard work does not end up in a foreigner's home.
Proverbs 5:11 You will be sad in the end, when your body is wasted away.
Proverbs 5:12 I say, "Why did I hate to be corrected, and why did my heart reject discipline?"
Proverbs 5:13 I did not listen to my teachers or pay attention to those who taught me.
Proverbs 5:14 I was close to being completely evil when I was among the people and the group.
Proverbs 5:15 Drink water from your own tank, and flowing water from your own well.
Proverbs 5:16 Let your springs flow freely, and streams of water fill the streets.
Proverbs 5:17 Keep them for yourself alone, and not for others with you.
Proverbs 5:18 May your source of life be blessed; be happy with the wife you married when you were young.
Proverbs 5:19 Let her be like a caring deer and a delightful doe; let her breasts always satisfy you, and always be captivated by her love.
Proverbs 5:20 Why would you, my son, be enchanted by a woman you don't know and hug someone else's wife?
Proverbs 5:21 God sees all that people do, and he thinks about everywhere they go.
Proverbs 5:22 The wicked person will be caught by their own wrongdoings and held fast by the ropes of their sins.
Proverbs 5:23 He will die for lack of teaching, and because of his great foolishness, he will lose his way.
