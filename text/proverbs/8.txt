Proverbs 8:1 Doesn't wisdom call out? And understanding raise her voice?
Proverbs 8:2 She stands at the top of high places, beside the roads, at the crossroads.
Proverbs 8:3 She calls out at the city gates, where people enter, at the doors.
Proverbs 8:4 I call to you, men; my voice is for all people.
Proverbs 8:5 You simple people, learn to be wise; and you fools, learn to understand.
Proverbs 8:6 Listen, for I will talk about great things; and when I open my lips, I will say what is right.
Proverbs 8:7 I will speak only truth; I hate to say anything wicked.
Proverbs 8:8 Everything I say is right; there's nothing wrong or twisted in my words.
Proverbs 8:9 Everything is clear to someone who understands, and correct for those who gain knowledge.
Proverbs 8:10 Accept my teaching instead of silver, and wisdom rather than the finest gold.
Proverbs 8:11 Wisdom is better than jewels; nothing you want can equal it.
Proverbs 8:12 I, Wisdom, live with good sense, and I discover knowledge of clever designs.
Proverbs 8:13 Being afraid of God means hating evil: I hate pride, arrogance, evil behavior, and lying speech.
Proverbs 8:14 I give advice and true wisdom; I am insight; I have power.
Proverbs 8:15 Through me, kings rule and leaders make fair laws.
Proverbs 8:16 Through me, leaders govern, and nobles, including all the earth's judges.
Proverbs 8:17 I love those who love me; and those who look for me early will find me.
Proverbs 8:18 Wealth and honor are with me; yes, lasting wealth and fairness.
Proverbs 8:19 My fruit is more valuable than gold, even pure gold; and my earnings are better than the best silver.
Proverbs 8:20 I guide on the path of what is right, through the ways of fair decisions.
Proverbs 8:21 I want to help those who love me get wealth, and I will make their treasures full.
Proverbs 8:22 God chose me at the start of His path, before His ancient works.
Proverbs 8:23 I was chosen from forever, from the start, before the earth existed.
Proverbs 8:24 Before the oceans existed, I was born; before there were springs filled with water.
Proverbs 8:25 Before the mountains were in place, before the hills I was born.
Proverbs 8:26 Before God made the earth, the fields, or the highest dust particles of the world.
Proverbs 8:27 When he made the sky, I was there: when he drew a circle on the deep:
Proverbs 8:28 When he made the clouds up high: when he made the deep springs strong:
Proverbs 8:29 When he told the sea its limits, so the waters would not disobey his command, when he set the earth's foundations:
Proverbs 8:30 I was with God, like someone raised with him; I was his joy every day, always happy in his presence.
Proverbs 8:31 I celebrated in the world he made; I enjoyed being with people.
Proverbs 8:32 Listen to me now, children: happy are those who follow my teachings.
Proverbs 8:33 Listen to advice, be smart, and don't ignore it.
Proverbs 8:34 Happy is the person who listens to me, keeping watch at my doors every day, waiting by the doorframes of my house.
Proverbs 8:35 Whoever finds me finds life and will receive favor from God.
Proverbs 8:36 Whoever sins against me hurts their own soul; all who hate me love death.
