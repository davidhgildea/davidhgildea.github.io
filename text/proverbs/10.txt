Proverbs 10:1 Solomon's proverbs. A smart son makes his father happy, but a silly son saddens his mother.
Proverbs 10:2 Money gained by wrongdoings is useless: but being right with God saves from death.
Proverbs 10:3 God will not let good people go hungry; but he will keep the wicked from getting what they want.
Proverbs 10:4 The person who is lazy becomes poor, but the hard worker becomes rich.
Proverbs 10:5 Someone who gathers crops in summer is smart, but someone who sleeps during the harvest brings shame.
Proverbs 10:6 Blessings are on the head of the fair, but the wicked's mouth is covered with violence.
Proverbs 10:7 People remember good people with happiness, but the names of bad people will be forgotten.
Proverbs 10:8 A wise person listens to instructions, but a foolish talker will fail.
Proverbs 10:9 Anyone who lives rightly walks safely; but those who follow crooked paths will be exposed.
Proverbs 10:10 Someone who winks causes trouble, but a talking fool will fail.
Proverbs 10:11 A good person's words give life, but the evil hide behind violent talk.
Proverbs 10:12 Hatred causes fights, but love forgives all wrongs.
Proverbs 10:13 Someone with understanding speaks wise words, but a person without understanding gets punished.
Proverbs 10:14 Wise people save knowledge, but a fool's words lead to trouble.
Proverbs 10:15 A rich person's money is like a protective city; a poor person's ruin is their lack of wealth.
Proverbs 10:16 The work of good people leads to life; the actions of bad people lead to sin.
Proverbs 10:17 Someone who follows advice is on the path to life, but someone who ignores correction goes wrong.
Proverbs 10:18 Someone who hides hate with lies, and someone who spreads false rumors, is foolish.
Proverbs 10:19 When people talk a lot, they often sin. But if someone keeps quiet, they are smart.
Proverbs 10:20 The words of a good person are like valuable silver; a bad person's thoughts have little value.
Proverbs 10:21 The words of good people help many, but fools die because they lack wisdom.
Proverbs 10:22 God's blessing brings wealth, without any sadness added to it.
Proverbs 10:23 A fool enjoys causing trouble, but a wise person has understanding.
Proverbs 10:24 What the wicked fear will happen to them, but what the good people want will be given to them.
Proverbs 10:25 Like a tornado that goes by, the bad person disappears; but the good person stands strong forever.
Proverbs 10:26 Like vinegar hurts teeth and smoke stings the eyes, so a lazy person troubles those who ask for their help.
Proverbs 10:27 Respecting God extends life, but the wicked will have shorter lives.
Proverbs 10:28 Righteous people's hope will bring joy, but the wicked's desires will vanish.
Proverbs 10:29 God's way gives strength to the good, but will destroy those who do wrong.
Proverbs 10:30 Good people will always be safe, but the bad will not stay on earth.
Proverbs 10:31 The just person's mouth speaks wisdom, but the deceitful tongue will be silenced.
Proverbs 10:32 The good person's words are right, but the bad person's words are twisted.
