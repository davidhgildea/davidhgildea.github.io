Proverbs 27:1 Don't brag about tomorrow; you don't know what the day will bring.
Proverbs 27:2 Let someone else praise you, not your own mouth; an outsider, not your own lips.
Proverbs 27:3 A rock is heavy, and sand is weighty, but a fool's anger is heavier than both.
Proverbs 27:4 Anger is harsh, and temper is wild, but who can face jealousy?
Proverbs 27:5 Public correction is better than hidden love.
Proverbs 27:6 A friend's wounds can be trusted, but an enemy's kisses are lies.
Proverbs 27:7 A satisfied person despises honey; but to a hungry person, even bitter food is sweet.
Proverbs 27:8 Like a bird that strays from its nest, so is a person who strays from their home.
Proverbs 27:9 Lotion and fragrance make the heart happy, just as a friend's sincere advice is sweet.
Proverbs 27:10 Don't abandon your friend or your father's friend, and don't go to your brother's house when you're in trouble; a close neighbor is better than a distant brother.
Proverbs 27:11 My son, be smart and make me happy, so I can reply to the one who criticizes me.
Proverbs 27:12 A wise person sees trouble ahead and hides; but the foolish keep going and suffer for it.
Proverbs 27:13 Take his coat if he promises to pay for someone he doesn't know, and get something from him to guarantee payment for a woman he's not familiar with.
Proverbs 27:14 If someone blesses their friend with a loud voice early in the morning, it will seem like a curse.
Proverbs 27:15 Constant dripping on a rainy day and a nagging woman are the same.
Proverbs 27:16 Anyone who hides her is like trying to hide the wind, and their actions will reveal themselves, just like the scent of perfume on their hand.
Proverbs 27:17 Iron makes iron sharp; so a person makes their friend's face bright.
Proverbs 27:18 Whoever takes care of the fig tree will eat its fruit; just as someone who serves their master well will be respected.
Proverbs 27:19 Just as water reflects a face back to a face, so one person's heart reflects another person's heart.
Proverbs 27:20 Hell and destruction are never full, just like a person's eyes are never satisfied.
Proverbs 27:21 Just as a pot is for melting silver and a furnace is for purifying gold, so a person is tested by being praised.
Proverbs 27:22 Even if you grind a fool in a bowl with grain using a crushing tool, his foolishness will not leave him.
Proverbs 27:23 Work hard to understand the condition of your sheep, and pay close attention to your cattle.
Proverbs 27:24 Wealth does not last forever: and will a crown be passed down through every generation?
Proverbs 27:25 The hay shows up, the young grass appears, and mountain herbs are collected.
Proverbs 27:26 Use sheep for clothes, and goats to buy a field.
Proverbs 27:27 You will have enough goat's milk for your food, your family's food, and to support your servant girls.
