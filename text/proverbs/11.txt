Proverbs 11:1 Using dishonest scales is disgusting to God, but accurate weights make him happy.
Proverbs 11:2 When you are proud, you will be embarrassed, but if you are humble, you will be wise.
Proverbs 11:3 The honesty of good people will lead them, but the dishonesty of the unfaithful will ruin them.
Proverbs 11:4 Money is no help on the day of anger, but doing right saves from death.
Proverbs 11:5 The goodness of a flawless person guides their path, but the evil ones will stumble because of their own evil.
Proverbs 11:6 The goodness of honest people will save them, but wrongdoers will be caught by their own bad actions.
Proverbs 11:7 When a bad person dies, their expectations end; and the hopes of unfair people disappear.
Proverbs 11:8 The good person is saved from trouble, and the bad one takes their place.
Proverbs 11:9 A fake person with their words can hurt others, but the right people will be saved by knowing the truth.
Proverbs 11:10 When things go well for good people, the city is happy; and when bad people die, there are cheers.
Proverbs 11:11 The city flourishes when good people bless it, but it falls apart when evil people talk against it.
Proverbs 11:12 Someone without wisdom looks down on their neighbor, but a person who understands stays calm.
Proverbs 11:13 Someone who gossips tells secrets, but a trustworthy person keeps them hidden.
Proverbs 11:14 Without advice, people fail; with many advisers, they are safe.
Proverbs 11:15 Someone who promises to pay for another will suffer, but whoever refuses to promise will be safe.
Proverbs 11:16 A kind woman keeps respect, and powerful men keep wealth.
Proverbs 11:17 A kind person benefits their own soul, but a cruel one harms themselves.
Proverbs 11:18 Bad people do tricky things, but those who do right will get a real reward.
Proverbs 11:19 Doing right leads to life, but chasing evil leads to death.
Proverbs 11:20 People with twisted hearts are hated by God, but those who follow a straight path bring him joy.
Proverbs 11:21 Even if the wicked join forces, they will not escape punishment, but the descendants of the righteous will be saved.
Proverbs 11:22 Like a gold ring in a pig's nose, so is a beautiful woman who lacks good judgment.
Proverbs 11:23 Righteous people desire only good things, but the wicked expect anger.
Proverbs 11:24 Some give freely and gain more; others keep too much and become poor.
Proverbs 11:25 A generous person will prosper, and whoever refreshes others will be refreshed themselves.
Proverbs 11:26 Someone who keeps corn for himself will be cursed by people, but the one who sells it will be blessed.
Proverbs 11:27 Whoever looks for good finds kindness, but whoever searches for trouble, it will find them.
Proverbs 11:28 Whoever trusts in their wealth will fail, but the good person will thrive like a tree branch.
Proverbs 11:29 Whoever causes trouble in their home will gain nothing, and the foolish person will serve the wise-hearted.
Proverbs 11:30 Righteous people produce life-giving fruit; whoever brings others to the right path is wise.
Proverbs 11:31 Look, good people will be rewarded on earth; even more, so will the bad and the sinful.
