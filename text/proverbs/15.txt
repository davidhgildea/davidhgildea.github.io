Proverbs 15:1 A gentle reply calms anger, but harsh words cause tempers to rise.
Proverbs 15:2 The wise person uses knowledge correctly, but a fool's mouth spills out nonsense.
Proverbs 15:3 God sees everything everywhere, watching both the bad and the good.
Proverbs 15:4 A healthy tongue is like a tree of life, but a twisted tongue can break the spirit.
Proverbs 15:5 A foolish person hates his father's teaching, but someone who listens to correction is wise.
Proverbs 15:6 In the home of the good person, there is a lot of value, but the earnings of the bad person bring problems.
Proverbs 15:7 The wise spread knowledge with their words, but the foolish do not.
Proverbs 15:8 God hates the wicked's offerings, but loves the good person's prayers.
Proverbs 15:9 Evil ways disgust God, but he loves those who seek to do what is right.
Proverbs 15:10 Correction is hard for someone who leaves the right path, and if you hate being corrected, you will die.
Proverbs 15:11 God sees everything in death and destruction, so he surely knows human hearts.
Proverbs 15:12 Someone who mocks others doesn't like being corrected; they won't seek advice from the wise.
Proverbs 15:13 A happy heart makes a smiling face, but a sad heart crushes the spirit.
Proverbs 15:14 A person with understanding seeks knowledge, but fools enjoy saying silly things.
Proverbs 15:15 Every day is bad for the suffering person, but a happy heart always feels like a feast.
Proverbs 15:16 It is better to have a small amount with respect for God than to have a lot of wealth with problems.
Proverbs 15:17 A meal of vegetables with love is better than a big cooked ox with hate.
Proverbs 15:18 An angry person causes fights, but one who is patient calms down arguments.
Proverbs 15:19 The lazy person's path is full of thorns, but the good person's path is smooth.
Proverbs 15:20 A smart son makes his father happy, but a silly man looks down on his mother.
Proverbs 15:21 A foolish person enjoys being unwise, but a smart person acts with integrity.
Proverbs 15:22 Without advice, plans fail; but with many advisers, they succeed.
Proverbs 15:23 A person feels happy with the reply they give; and a timely word, how wonderful it is!
Proverbs 15:24 The path to life leads upward for the wise to keep them from going down to the grave.
Proverbs 15:25 God will destroy the proud person's house, but he will protect the widow's land.
Proverbs 15:26 Wicked people's thoughts disgust God, but pure people's words are nice.
Proverbs 15:27 A person who wants to get rich quick will cause problems for their family, but someone who refuses bribes will have a good life.
Proverbs 15:28 The good person thinks carefully before answering, but the wicked person spills out evil words.
Proverbs 15:29 God is far from the bad people, but he listens to the prayers of the good people.
Proverbs 15:30 Bright eyes make the heart happy, and good news gives the body strength.
Proverbs 15:31 The ear that listens to life's corrections stays with the wise.
Proverbs 15:32 Whoever ignores advice hates themselves, but someone who listens to correction gains understanding.
Proverbs 15:33 Respecting God teaches wisdom; before getting honor, you must be humble.
