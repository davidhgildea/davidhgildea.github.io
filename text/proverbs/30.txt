Proverbs 30:1 Agur, Jakeh's son, said this prophecy: he told Ithiel, and also Ithiel and Ucal.
Proverbs 30:2 I am surely less wise than anyone, and I lack a man's understanding.
Proverbs 30:3 I have not learned wisdom, nor do I know about the holy.
Proverbs 30:4 Who has gone up to heaven and come down? Who has held the wind in his hands? Who has wrapped up the waters in a cloak? Who has set up all the earth's edges? What's his name, and what's his son's name, if you know it?
Proverbs 30:5 Every word from God is pure; he protects those who trust in him.
Proverbs 30:6 Do not add to his words, or God will correct you, and you will be shown to be a liar.
Proverbs 30:7 I ask you for two things; don't refuse me before I die.
Proverbs 30:8 Keep me away from useless things and dishonesty; don't make me poor or rich; give me just the food I need.
Proverbs 30:9 So I don't become too full, forget you, and ask, "Who is God?" or become poor, steal, and disrespect my God's name.
Proverbs 30:10 Do not blame someone's servant to their boss, or they might curse you and you'll be found at fault.
Proverbs 30:11 A generation curses their father and doesn't bless their mother.
Proverbs 30:12 Some people think they are clean, but they are not free from their dirt.
Proverbs 30:13 A group of people exist who are very proud; they look at others with arrogance.
Proverbs 30:14 Some people are like sword teeth and knife jaws, they eat up the poor and needy from the world and among people.
Proverbs 30:15 The leech has two daughters that cry, "Give, give." There are three things never full, even four that never say, "Enough":
Proverbs 30:16 The grave, the empty womb, the dry earth, and the fire that never says 'Enough.'
Proverbs 30:17 The eye that makes fun of its father, and hates to listen to its mother, the ravens of the valley will pluck it out, and young eagles will eat it.
Proverbs 30:18 There are three things that are amazing to me, yes, four things I don't understand:
Proverbs 30:19 How an eagle flies in the sky; how a snake moves on a rock; how a ship sails in the sea; and how a man acts with a woman.
Proverbs 30:20 This is how an unfaithful woman acts: she eats, cleans her mouth, and says, I've done nothing wrong.
Proverbs 30:21 The earth is troubled by three things, and there are four it can't stand:
Proverbs 30:22 When a servant becomes a leader and a fool gets plenty to eat.
Proverbs 30:23 A disliked woman when she gets married; and a servant girl who inherits from her lady.
Proverbs 30:24 There are four small things on earth, but they are very wise:
Proverbs 30:25 Ants are not strong, yet they gather their food in the summer.
Proverbs 30:26 The rock badgers are weak creatures, yet they make their homes in the rocks.
Proverbs 30:27 The locusts don't have a king, but they all go out in groups.
Proverbs 30:28 The spider uses its hands to make a web and lives in king's homes.
Proverbs 30:29 Three things move gracefully, indeed, four look good as they go:
Proverbs 30:30 A lion, the strongest among animals, doesn't turn away from anything.
Proverbs 30:31 A greyhound; a male goat also; and a king, who cannot be opposed.
Proverbs 30:32 If you acted foolishly by being proud, or if you planned something bad, put your hand over your mouth.
Proverbs 30:33 When you stir milk, you make butter, and when you squeeze a nose, you get blood; so when you force anger, you cause fighting.
