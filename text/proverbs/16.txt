Proverbs 16:1 A person plans in their heart and speaks with their tongue, but God decides both.
Proverbs 16:2 A person thinks all their ways are right, but God judges their motives.
Proverbs 16:3 Give your work to God, and your plans will succeed.
Proverbs 16:4 God made everything for his own purpose, even the bad people for the day of trouble.
Proverbs 16:5 Everyone with a proud heart is hated by God; even if they unite, they will not avoid punishment.
Proverbs 16:6 Through kindness and honesty, wrong is made right; and by respecting God, people avoid evil.
Proverbs 16:7 When a person's actions please God, even his enemies live peacefully with him.
Proverbs 16:8 It is better to have a little with fairness than to have a lot of money without justice.
Proverbs 16:9 A person plans their path, but God guides their steps.
Proverbs 16:10 A king's words are God's words: he is fair in his decisions.
Proverbs 16:11 A fair scale and balance belong to God: every weight in the bag is made by him.
Proverbs 16:12 Kings hate doing evil: a kingdom is built on fairness.
Proverbs 16:13 Kings enjoy honest words; they value those who speak the truth.
Proverbs 16:14 A king's anger is like a messenger of death, but a wise person can calm it down.
Proverbs 16:15 The king's smiling face brings life; his kindness is like a refreshing rain cloud.
Proverbs 16:16 It is much better to gain wisdom than gold, and gaining understanding is more valuable than getting silver.
Proverbs 16:17 The right path for good people is to avoid evil; those who follow their path protect their life.
Proverbs 16:18 Pride leads to ruin, and arrogance comes before a fall.
Proverbs 16:19 It is better to be humble with the poor than to share the riches with the proud.
Proverbs 16:20 Anyone who deals with a problem wisely will succeed; and a person who trusts in God will be happy.
Proverbs 16:21 Smart people are known for their good judgment, and kind words help others learn.
Proverbs 16:22 Understanding is a source of life to those who have it, but teaching fools is useless.
Proverbs 16:23 A wise person's heart guides their words and gives knowledge to what they say.
Proverbs 16:24 Nice words are like honey, sweet to the heart, and good for the body.
Proverbs 16:25 A path may seem right to someone, but it can end in death.
Proverbs 16:26 The person who works, works for themselves; their own hunger forces them to do it.
Proverbs 16:27 A wicked person stirs up trouble, and their words burn like a fire.
Proverbs 16:28 A stubborn person causes conflict, and a gossip breaks apart close friends.
Proverbs 16:29 A rough person tricks their friend and guides them down a bad path.
Proverbs 16:30 He closes his eyes to plan bad things; when he moves his lips, he causes trouble.
Proverbs 16:31 Grey hair is a mark of honor when it comes from living a good life.
Proverbs 16:32 Someone who stays calm is stronger than a powerful person, and controlling your emotions is better than conquering a city.
Proverbs 16:33 People throw lots to make a decision, but God decides the outcome.
