Proverbs 23:1 When you sit down to eat with a ruler, think carefully about what is in front of you.
Proverbs 23:2 If you love to eat too much, it’s better to put a knife to your own throat.
Proverbs 23:3 Don't want his tasty food; it's tricky to eat.
Proverbs 23:4 Do not work to become rich; stop relying on your own knowledge.
Proverbs 23:5 Will you focus on what is not? For sure, wealth grows wings and flies off like an eagle toward the sky.
Proverbs 23:6 Do not eat the food of someone with a greedy heart, and don't crave their fancy dishes.
Proverbs 23:7 As a person thinks in their heart, so are they: They say to you, "Eat and drink," but their heart is not with you.
Proverbs 23:8 You will throw up the food you ate and waste your nice words.
Proverbs 23:9 Don't talk to a fool; they will scorn the wisdom in what you say.
Proverbs 23:10 Do not move the old boundary stones; and do not go into the fields of orphans.
Proverbs 23:11 Their protector is strong; he will defend their case against you.
Proverbs 23:12 Open your heart to learning, and listen to wise words.
Proverbs 23:13 Do not avoid disciplining the child: if you punish him with a rod, he will not die.
Proverbs 23:14 You should discipline him with a rod, and save his soul from death.
Proverbs 23:15 My son, if your heart is wise, then my heart will be happy, even mine.
Proverbs 23:16 Yes, my heart will be glad when your lips say what is right.
Proverbs 23:17 Do not envy wrongdoers, but always respect God.
Proverbs 23:18 For sure, there is an end; and your hope will not be destroyed.
Proverbs 23:19 Listen, my child, be smart, and lead your heart on the right path.
Proverbs 23:20 Don't hang out with heavy drinkers or those who eat meat to excess.
Proverbs 23:21 Drunk and overeaters will become poor, and being too sleepy will dress a person in rags.
Proverbs 23:22 Listen to your father who gave you life, and do not disrespect your mother when she gets old.
Proverbs 23:23 Buy the truth and do not sell it; also buy wisdom, teaching, and insight.
Proverbs 23:24 The good person's parent will be very happy; and the one who has a wise child will be joyful because of them.
Proverbs 23:25 Your father and your mother will be happy, and the woman who gave birth to you will be joyful.
Proverbs 23:26 My child, give me your heart, and let your eyes watch my ways.
Proverbs 23:27 A prostitute is like a deep hole; and a foreign woman is like a tight trap.
Proverbs 23:28 She waits like a hunter for her target and leads to more people doing wrong.
Proverbs 23:29 Who has sadness? Who has pain? Who has fights? Who has pointless talk? Who has injuries for no reason? Who has red eyes?
Proverbs 23:30 Those who stay long at the wine; those who go to look for mixed wine.
Proverbs 23:31 Don't look at the wine when it's red, when it sparkles in the cup, when it goes down smoothly.
Proverbs 23:32 In the end, it bites like a snake and poisons like a viper.
Proverbs 23:33 You will see strange women, and your heart will say twisted things.
Proverbs 23:34 Yes, you will be like someone lying down in the middle of the sea, or like someone lying on the top of a sail mast.
Proverbs 23:35 You will say, "They hit me, but I wasn't hurt; they beat me, but I didn't feel it. When will I wake up? I will go back to it again."
