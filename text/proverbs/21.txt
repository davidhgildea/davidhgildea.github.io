Proverbs 21:1 The king's mind is under God's control, like water channels; He directs it wherever He chooses.
Proverbs 21:2 Everyone thinks their own way is right, but God knows what's in their hearts.
Proverbs 21:3 God prefers fairness and justice over sacrifices.
Proverbs 21:4 A haughty look, a proud heart, and the evil doings of the wicked are all sinful.
Proverbs 21:5 Careful thinkers plan to be rich; but those who rush can expect to be poor.
Proverbs 21:6 Getting riches by lying is useless and leads to death.
Proverbs 21:7 The stolen goods of bad people will ruin them because they won't act fairly.
Proverbs 21:8 People's ways can be twisted and odd, but those who are pure do what is right.
Proverbs 21:9 It's better to live in a corner of the roof than with a quarrelsome woman in a big house.
Proverbs 21:10 A bad person wants to do wrong, and he doesn't care about his neighbor.
Proverbs 21:11 When the mocker is punished, the naive become smart; and when the smart are taught, they gain knowledge.
Proverbs 21:12 The good person thinks carefully about the home of the bad, but God brings down the bad because of their evil ways.
Proverbs 21:13 Anyone who ignores the cry of the poor will also cry out and not be heard.
Proverbs 21:14 Giving a gift quietly can calm down anger; a hidden bribe can ease great fury.
Proverbs 21:15 Good people find joy in being fair, but those who do wrong will meet with ruin.
Proverbs 21:16 The person who leaves the path of understanding will stay among the dead.
Proverbs 21:17 Someone who loves fun will be poor; a person who loves wine and oil won't get rich.
Proverbs 21:18 Bad people will take the place of good people, and those who do wrong will take the place of the honest.
Proverbs 21:19 It is better to live in the desert than with a quarrelsome and angry woman.
Proverbs 21:20 A wise person has valuable treasure and oil at home; but a foolish person wastes it all.
Proverbs 21:21 Those who pursue what is right and kind find life, goodness, and respect.
Proverbs 21:22 A wise person climbs into a strong city and breaks down the city's strong defenses.
Proverbs 21:23 Whoever controls their speech protects themselves from problems.
Proverbs 21:24 His name is Proud and Arrogant, someone who acts with angry pride.
Proverbs 21:25 The lazy person's wants destroy him, because he won't work with his hands.
Proverbs 21:26 He always wants more all day long, but a good person gives freely without holding back.
Proverbs 21:27 God hates the wicked's offering; it's even worse when they offer it with evil intentions.
Proverbs 21:28 A liar will fail, but a person who listens will always speak the truth.
Proverbs 21:29 A bad person makes their face stubborn, but a good person stays on the right path.
Proverbs 21:30 No wisdom, understanding, or advice can succeed against God.
Proverbs 21:31 People get horses ready for battle day, but God gives safety.
