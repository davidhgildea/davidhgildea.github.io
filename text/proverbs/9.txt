Proverbs 9:1 Wisdom built her house; she carved out its seven pillars.
Proverbs 9:2 She has killed her animals; she has mixed her wine; she has also set her table.
Proverbs 9:3 She has sent out her servant girls; she calls out from the city's highest spots.
Proverbs 9:4 If you are simple, come this way; anyone lacking understanding, she says to him,
Proverbs 9:5 Come, eat my bread, and drink the wine I have mixed.
Proverbs 9:6 Leave the foolish behind and live; walk on the path of wisdom.
Proverbs 9:7 The person who corrects a mocker invites insult, and the one who tells a wicked person they are wrong risks being shamed.
Proverbs 9:8 Don't correct a mocker, or he will dislike you; advise a wise person, and he will appreciate you.
Proverbs 9:9 Guide a smart person, and they will become smarter; show a fair person, and their knowledge will grow.
Proverbs 9:10 Being in awe of God is the start of wisdom, and knowing what is sacred leads to understanding.
Proverbs 9:11 With my help, your life will last longer, and you'll have more years to live.
Proverbs 9:12 If you are wise, you will benefit yourself: but if you mock, you alone will suffer.
Proverbs 9:13 A silly woman is loud: she is naive, and knows nothing.
Proverbs 9:14 She sits at her house door, on a chair in the city's high spots.
Proverbs 9:15 To invite people who are traveling straight on their paths:
Proverbs 9:16 If you are simple, come here; if you lack understanding, she says to you.
Proverbs 9:17 Taken water tastes good, and hidden bread is nice to eat.
Proverbs 9:18 He doesn't know that the dead are there; her guests are in the depths of the grave.
