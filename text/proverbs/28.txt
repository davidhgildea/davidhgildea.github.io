Proverbs 28:1 Bad people run away even when no one chases them, but good people are as brave as a lion.
Proverbs 28:2 When a country sins, it has many rulers, but a wise and knowledgeable man will help it last longer.
Proverbs 28:3 A poor person who mistreats other poor people is like a rainstorm that destroys all the crops.
Proverbs 28:4 Those who ignore God's laws praise the bad people, but those who follow the laws stand against them.
Proverbs 28:5 Bad people do not understand what is right, but those who look for God understand everything.
Proverbs 28:6 It's better to be poor and honest than to be dishonest and rich.
Proverbs 28:7 Whoever follows the law is a wise child, but he who hangs out with wild people brings shame to his parent.
Proverbs 28:8 Whoever makes money through charging too much interest and unfair profit will end up collecting wealth for someone who cares for the poor.
Proverbs 28:9 Whoever ignores the law, even their prayers are detestable.
Proverbs 28:10 Anyone who leads good people to do wrong will fall into their own trap, but the honest will get good things.
Proverbs 28:11 The rich man thinks he is smart, but the poor with insight can see through him.
Proverbs 28:12 When good people are happy, there is great joy; but when bad people gain power, others go into hiding.
Proverbs 28:13 Whoever hides their sins won't succeed, but the one who admits and stops them will receive mercy.
Proverbs 28:14 The person who always respects God is happy, but someone who becomes stubborn will get into trouble.
Proverbs 28:15 Like a loud lion and a wild bear, so is a bad leader to the poor.
Proverbs 28:16 A ruler who lacks understanding is also very harsh, but someone who hates greed will live a long time.
Proverbs 28:17 Anyone who hurts another person must run away to a deep hole; nobody should help him.
Proverbs 28:18 Anyone who lives in a good way will be safe, but someone who chooses to do wrong will suddenly fail.
Proverbs 28:19 The one who works their land will have lots of bread, but the one who chases after worthless people will have plenty of poverty.
Proverbs 28:20 A loyal person will have many blessings, but someone rushing to get rich won't stay guiltless.
Proverbs 28:21 Showing favoritism is wrong: a person might sin just for a small piece of food.
Proverbs 28:22 Someone who rushes to get rich has bad intentions and does not realize that they will become poor.
Proverbs 28:23 Someone who corrects a person will later be appreciated more than someone who uses words to flatter.
Proverbs 28:24 Anyone who steals from their father or mother and says it's not wrong is a friend of someone who destroys.
Proverbs 28:25 A person with a proud heart causes conflict, but one who trusts in God will prosper.
Proverbs 28:26 Anyone who trusts only in themselves is foolish, but someone who acts wisely will be safe.
Proverbs 28:27 Whoever gives to the poor will not be in need, but one who ignores them will face many troubles.
Proverbs 28:28 When bad people gain power, others hide; but when they die, the good grow in number.
