Proverbs 1:1 These are wise sayings from Solomon, David's son, who was the king of Israel.
Proverbs 1:2 To understand wisdom and teaching; to grasp the meaning of insightful words;
Proverbs 1:3 To learn wisdom, fairness, right decisions, and equality;
Proverbs 1:4 To give cleverness to the simple, to the young person knowledge and good judgment.
Proverbs 1:5 A wise person listens and learns more; someone who understands will find good advice.
Proverbs 1:6 To grasp a wise saying and its meaning, the words of the wise, and their riddles.
Proverbs 1:7 Respecting God is the start of understanding, but fools hate learning and guidance.
Proverbs 1:8 My child, listen to your father's teaching and do not ignore your mother's rules.
Proverbs 1:9 They will be like a beautiful crown on your head and a necklace around your neck.
Proverbs 1:10 My son, if sinners tempt you, do not agree.
Proverbs 1:11 If they say, "Come with us, let's hide and wait to hurt someone innocent for no reason,"
Proverbs 1:12 Let's consume them fully alive as the grave does; completely, like those who descend into the pit.
Proverbs 1:13 We will find valuable treasures; we will fill our homes with loot.
Proverbs 1:14 Join us and share our money.
Proverbs 1:15 My son, do not go along with them; keep your foot from following their path.
Proverbs 1:16 Their feet rush to do bad things, and they hurry to spill blood.
Proverbs 1:17 Surely, setting a net where a bird can see it is useless.
Proverbs 1:18 They set a trap for themselves; they hide and wait to take their own lives.
Proverbs 1:19 Greedy people's ways take life from those they harm.
Proverbs 1:20 Wisdom shouts outside; she raises her voice in the streets:
Proverbs 1:21 She calls out in the busiest places, at the city gates she speaks her words, saying,
Proverbs 1:22 How long will you who are naive love being simple-minded? And how long will mockers enjoy mocking and fools hate understanding?
Proverbs 1:23 Listen to my correction: I will share my spirit with you and teach you my words.
Proverbs 1:24 I called, but you refused; I reached out my hand, but no one paid attention.
Proverbs 1:25 You have ignored all my advice and did not want my correction.
Proverbs 1:26 I will laugh when trouble comes for you; I will make fun when you are afraid.
Proverbs 1:27 When your fear arrives like ruins, and your destruction sweeps in like a storm; when distress and suffering fall upon you.
Proverbs 1:28 They will call for me, but I won't respond; they will look for me, but won't find me.
Proverbs 1:29 They hated knowledge and did not choose to respect God.
Proverbs 1:30 They rejected my advice and ignored all my warnings.
Proverbs 1:31 So they will eat the results of their actions and be full of their own schemes.
Proverbs 1:32 Rejecting knowledge will kill the naive, and the success of fools will ruin them.
Proverbs 1:33 Whoever listens to me will live safely and be at peace, without fear of harm.
