Proverbs 26:1 Like snow in summer and rain during harvest, honor does not suit a fool.
Proverbs 26:2 Just like a bird that wanders or a swallow that flies, a curse without cause won't come.
Proverbs 26:3 A whip for the horse, a harness for the donkey, and a stick for the fool's back.
Proverbs 26:4 Do not answer a fool in the same foolish way, or you will be just like him.
Proverbs 26:5 Reply to a fool as his nonsense deserves, so he doesn't think he's wise.
Proverbs 26:6 Sending a message through a fool is like cutting off your own feet and bringing trouble on yourself.
Proverbs 26:7 A disabled person's legs are not the same; similarly, a wise saying sounds wrong when a foolish person says it.
Proverbs 26:8 Like tying a stone in a sling, so is giving honor to a fool.
Proverbs 26:9 Like a thorn sticks in a drunk person's hand, a wise saying is misunderstood by fools.
Proverbs 26:10 The great God who made everything rewards both the fool and those who do wrong.
Proverbs 26:11 Like a dog that goes back to its vomit, a fool repeats his foolish actions.
Proverbs 26:12 Do you see a person who thinks they are very wise? A fool has more chance of finding wisdom than that person.
Proverbs 26:13 The lazy person says, "There is a lion on the road; a lion is in the streets."
Proverbs 26:14 Just as a door swings on its hinges, a lazy person turns over in bed.
Proverbs 26:15 The lazy person hides his hand in his pocket; it bothers him to lift it to his mouth again.
Proverbs 26:16 The lazy person thinks he is smarter than seven people who give good reasons.
Proverbs 26:17 Someone who interferes in a fight that's not their own is like someone who grabs a dog by its ears.
Proverbs 26:18 Like a crazy person who throws torches, arrows, and death,
Proverbs 26:19 This is like the person who tricks their friend and then says, "I was only joking!"
Proverbs 26:20 When there's no wood, the fire goes out; just as when there's no gossip, arguments stop.
Proverbs 26:21 Just as coals heat coals and wood fuels fire, a quarrelsome person starts arguments.
Proverbs 26:22 A gossip's words hurt like wounds, reaching deep inside a person.
Proverbs 26:23 Hot words and a bad heart are like a broken pot covered with cheap silver.
Proverbs 26:24 The one who hates hides it with his words and keeps lies inside himself.
Proverbs 26:25 When he talks nicely, don't trust him: because his heart hides seven horrible things.
Proverbs 26:26 Who hides hate with lies, their evil will be exposed to everyone.
Proverbs 26:27 Anyone who digs a hole will fall into it; and if someone rolls a stone, it will roll back on them.
Proverbs 26:28 A dishonest speaker dislikes those they hurt; and smooth talk can lead to destruction.
