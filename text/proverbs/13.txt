Proverbs 13:1 A smart son listens to his father's teaching, but someone who mocks does not listen to correction.
Proverbs 13:2 A person will enjoy benefits from the good things they say, but those who break rules will experience harm.
Proverbs 13:3 Someone who controls their words protects their life, but someone who talks too much will find trouble.
Proverbs 13:4 The lazy person wants things but has nothing, while the hardworking person will prosper.
Proverbs 13:5 A good person hates lying, but an evil person is disgusting and will be shamed.
Proverbs 13:6 Goodness protects the honest person, but evil brings down the wrongdoer.
Proverbs 13:7 Some people act rich but have nothing; others act poor but have great wealth.
Proverbs 13:8 A man's wealth is his life's ransom; but the poor do not hear harsh words.
Proverbs 13:9 Good people shine brightly, but the light of the wicked will go out.
Proverbs 13:10 Pride leads to arguments, but wisdom is with those who take good advice.
Proverbs 13:11 Money earned unfairly will decrease, but those who work hard will gain more.
Proverbs 13:12 When you must wait for something you hope for, it makes you feel sad; but when you get what you want, it makes you very happy.
Proverbs 13:13 Anyone who ignores God's word will be destroyed, but the one who respects the command will be rewarded.
Proverbs 13:14 The teachings of the wise are like a fountain of life, helping to avoid death's traps.
Proverbs 13:15 Clear understanding brings approval; but the path of wrongdoers is difficult.
Proverbs 13:16 A wise person acts with understanding, but a fool shows his foolishness.
Proverbs 13:17 A bad messenger gets into trouble, but a reliable envoy brings wellness.
Proverbs 13:18 Being poor and ashamed will happen to someone who ignores teaching, but the one who listens to correction will be respected.
Proverbs 13:19 When you get what you want, it feels good inside, but fools hate to stop doing bad things.
Proverbs 13:20 Someone who spends time with wise people will become wise, but someone who is friends with fools will get into trouble.
Proverbs 13:21 Evil follows those who sin, but good will be given to those who do right.
Proverbs 13:22 A good person leaves wealth for their grandchildren, and a sinner's riches will go to those who are right.
Proverbs 13:23 The poor person's farm has plenty of food, but some is wasted because of bad decisions.
Proverbs 13:24 Someone who doesn't discipline their child dislikes them, but one who loves their child corrects them early on.
Proverbs 13:25 Good people eat and are satisfied, but the wicked will be left wanting.
