Proverbs 29:1 Anyone who keeps making the same mistakes and refuses to accept correction will suddenly be ruined beyond repair.
Proverbs 29:2 When good people lead, everyone is happy; but when bad people rule, everyone is sad.
Proverbs 29:3 Anyone who loves wisdom makes their father happy, but someone who hangs out with prostitutes wastes their money.
Proverbs 29:4 A king makes a country strong by fair judgement, but someone who takes bribes ruins it.
Proverbs 29:5 Someone who praises their neighbor too much is setting a trap for them.
Proverbs 29:6 When a bad person does wrong, they get caught in a trap, but a good person is happy and sings.
Proverbs 29:7 Good people think about the needs of the poor; but bad people don't care to know them.
Proverbs 29:8 Mocking people trap a city, but wise people calm anger.
Proverbs 29:9 If a wise person argues with a foolish one, whether they get angry or laugh, there is no peace.
Proverbs 29:10 Violent people dislike those who are honest, but righteous people care about their well-being.
Proverbs 29:11 A foolish person says everything they think; but a wise person keeps it to themselves for later.
Proverbs 29:12 If a leader listens to lies, all their followers become bad.
Proverbs 29:13 The poor person and the liar come across each other; God gives light to both of their eyes.
Proverbs 29:14 The king who judges the poor fairly, his rule will last forever.
Proverbs 29:15 Discipline and correction lead to wisdom, but a child left alone brings shame to his mother.
Proverbs 29:16 When more wicked people exist, wrongdoing grows, but good people will see them fail.
Proverbs 29:17 Discipline your child, and they will bring you peace; they will bring joy to your heart.
Proverbs 29:18 Where there is no guidance, people fail; but those who follow the rules will be happy.
Proverbs 29:19 A worker won't change just by talking to them; even if they get it, they won't respond.
Proverbs 29:20 Do you see someone who talks without thinking? A fool has more hope than him.
Proverbs 29:21 The one who raises his servant with care from a young age will eventually have him as his own son.
Proverbs 29:22 A mad person causes trouble, and someone very angry commits many sins.
Proverbs 29:23 A person's pride will cause their downfall, but respect will support those who are humble.
Proverbs 29:24 Anyone who partners with a thief hates themselves: they hear cursing but do not report it.
Proverbs 29:25 Being afraid of people can trap you, but trusting in God keeps you safe.
Proverbs 29:26 Many people try to win the leader's approval; but God gives each person's decision.
Proverbs 29:27 A dishonest person disgusts the honest, and someone who lives rightly is hated by the wicked.
