Proverbs 31:1 The sayings of King Lemuel, the teaching his mother gave him.
Proverbs 31:2 What should I say, my son? What, child of my body? What, child of my promises?
Proverbs 31:3 Do not give your strength to women, or your paths to what ruins kings.
Proverbs 31:4 Kings should not drink wine, Lemuel, nor should leaders drink strong alcohol.
Proverbs 31:5 They might drink, forget the rules, and twist the justice due to those who are suffering.
Proverbs 31:6 Give hard liquor to someone who is dying, and wine to those who are very sad.
Proverbs 31:7 Let him drink and forget his lack of money, and not recall his troubles anymore.
Proverbs 31:8 Speak up for those who cannot speak, in the defense of all who are headed for ruin.
Proverbs 31:9 Speak up, make fair decisions, and defend the rights of the poor and needy.
Proverbs 31:10 Who can find a good woman? Her value is much higher than jewels.
Proverbs 31:11 Her husband's heart trusts her deeply, so he lacks nothing of value.
Proverbs 31:12 She will treat him well and never badly all her life.
Proverbs 31:13 She looks for wool and flax and works eagerly with her hands.
Proverbs 31:14 She is like the traders' boats; she gets her food from far away.
Proverbs 31:15 She gets up while it's still night, and provides food for her family, and shares with her servant girls.
Proverbs 31:16 She thinks about a field, buys it, and with her own work, she plants a vineyard.
Proverbs 31:17 She wraps her waist with power and makes her arms strong.
Proverbs 31:18 She sees that her goods are valuable; her lamp does not go out at night.
Proverbs 31:19 She puts her hands to the spindle and holds the distaff.
Proverbs 31:20 She extends her hand to the poor; yes, she offers her hands to those in need.
Proverbs 31:21 She is not worried about the cold for her family, because they all wear warm red clothes.
Proverbs 31:22 She makes herself patterned blankets; her clothes are silk and purple.
Proverbs 31:23 Her husband is respected in the community where he meets with the local leaders.
Proverbs 31:24 She makes good linen cloth and sells it; she also delivers belts to the shopkeeper.
Proverbs 31:25 She wears strength and respect; she will be glad in the future.
Proverbs 31:26 She speaks wisely and kindly advice is on her tongue.
Proverbs 31:27 She takes good care of her family and does not waste time doing nothing.
Proverbs 31:28 Her children stand up and call her blessed; her husband too, and he praises her.
Proverbs 31:29 Many women have been good, but you are the best of them all.
Proverbs 31:30 Being liked for charm is misleading, and beauty is shallow: but a woman who respects God will be honored.
Proverbs 31:31 Give her the rewards of her work, and let her actions praise her publicly.
