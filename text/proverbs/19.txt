Proverbs 19:1 It is better to be poor and honest than to be a liar and foolish.
Proverbs 19:2 It's not good for a person to lack knowledge, and those who hurry with their feet do wrong.
Proverbs 19:3 A person's foolishness twists their path, and their heart blames God.
Proverbs 19:4 Having money brings many friends; but being poor pushes people away.
Proverbs 19:5 A liar will be punished, and someone who tells lies will not go free.
Proverbs 19:6 Many will ask a ruler for favors, and everyone is a friend to a person who gives gifts.
Proverbs 19:7 All the poor person's brothers dislike him, and even more, his friends stay away from him. He tries to talk to them, but they are not there for him.
Proverbs 19:8 The person who gains wisdom loves themselves; the one who holds on to understanding will discover what is good.
Proverbs 19:9 Someone who lies as a witness will not go free, and those who tell lies will be destroyed.
Proverbs 19:10 Enjoyment doesn't suit a fool, and it's even worse when a servant becomes the boss of nobles.
Proverbs 19:11 A wise person controls their anger; and their honor is to forgive a wrong.
Proverbs 19:12 A king's anger is like a lion's roar; but his kindness is like morning dew on the grass.
Proverbs 19:13 A foolish son brings trouble to his father; a quarreling wife is like constant dripping.
Proverbs 19:14 Homes and wealth are gifts from parents, but a wise wife comes from God.
Proverbs 19:15 Laziness leads to deep sleep; a person who does not work will go hungry.
Proverbs 19:16 Whoever obeys the command protects their life; but whoever ignores their ways will die.
Proverbs 19:17 Whoever shows kindness to the poor lends to God; and God will repay what they have given.
Proverbs 19:18 Discipline your child while there is still hope, and do not give in because of their tears.
Proverbs 19:19 A person with much anger will face consequences: if you save him, you will have to do it again.
Proverbs 19:20 Listen to advice and accept teaching so you can be wise in the future.
Proverbs 19:21 People have many plans in their heart, but God's advice will last.
Proverbs 19:22 A person's kindness makes them desirable, and someone who is poor is better than one who lies.
Proverbs 19:23 Respecting God leads to life; those who do so will live content and untouched by harm.
Proverbs 19:24 A lazy person hides their hand in their pocket and won't even bring it to their mouth.
Proverbs 19:25 Punish a mocker, and the naive will learn caution; correct a wise person, and they will gain knowledge.
Proverbs 19:26 Someone who uses up his father's resources and drives his mother away brings shame and disgrace as a child.
Proverbs 19:27 Stop listening, my son, to teaching that leads you away from true knowledge.
Proverbs 19:28 A dishonest witness mocks fairness, and the words of the wicked are greedy for evil.
Proverbs 19:29 Punishments are ready for mockers, and beatings for the backs of fools.
