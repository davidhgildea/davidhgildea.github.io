Zephaniah 2:1 Come together, yes, come together, unwanted nation.
Zephaniah 2:2 Before the command comes out, before the day flies away like husks, before God's strong anger hits you, before the day of God's anger arrives.
Zephaniah 2:3 Look for God, all you humble people of the earth, who have followed his laws; look for doing right and being humble: perhaps you will be kept safe on the day of God's anger.
Zephaniah 2:4 Gaza will be abandoned, Ashkelon will be ruined, they will force Ashdod out at midday, and Ekron will be destroyed.
Zephaniah 2:5 Sorry for you, people by the sea, Cherethite nation! God's message is against you; Canaan, land of the Philistines, I will destroy you so no one will live there.
Zephaniah 2:6 The seashore will become homes and huts for shepherds, and pens for sheep.
Zephaniah 2:7 The land will be for Judah's survivors; they will graze there. At night, they will rest in Ashkelon's homes because God will care for them and end their exile.
Zephaniah 2:8 I have heard Moab's insults and Ammon's children's taunts, as they insult my people and boast against their land.
Zephaniah 2:9 So I promise, as I am alive, says God, Moab will become like Sodom and the Ammonites like Gomorrah—a place of weeds and salt pits, a land forever empty. The survivors of my people will take their things, and those left of my people will own their land.
Zephaniah 2:10 They will receive this for their arrogance because they insulted and acted superior to God's people.
Zephaniah 2:11 God will be fearsome to them: for he will starve all the earth's gods; and people will worship him, each from their own place, including all the islands of the nations.
Zephaniah 2:12 You people of Ethiopia will also be killed by my sword.
Zephaniah 2:13 He will reach out his hand to the north to ruin Assyria; he will turn Nineveh into ruins, dry as a desert.
Zephaniah 2:14 Animals will rest inside the city, creatures from every country: birds like pelicans and herons will nest in the high beams; their sounds will fill the windows; ruins will be at the doorways because God will reveal the woodwork.
Zephaniah 2:15 This happy city lived without worries, and thought to herself, "I am alone and there's no one like me." But look how it's now empty, a spot where animals rest! Everyone who goes by will hiss and shake their head.
