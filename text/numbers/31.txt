Numbers 31:1 God spoke to Moses, saying,
Numbers 31:2 Get justice for the Israelites against the Midianites; then you will join your ancestors.
Numbers 31:3 Moses said to the people, "Get some of you ready for battle, and attack the Midianites to take revenge for God."
Numbers 31:4 From every tribe of Israel, send a thousand people to fight in the war.
Numbers 31:5 From the thousands of Israel, a thousand from each tribe, twelve thousand ready for battle, were given.
Numbers 31:6 Moses sent a thousand men from each tribe to fight, including Phinehas, son of Eleazar the priest, with the holy items and trumpets to sound.
Numbers 31:7 They fought against the Midianites, as God told Moses; and they killed all the men.
Numbers 31:8 They killed the Midianite kings along with the others who were killed; the kings were Evi, Rekem, Zur, Hur, and Reba. They also killed Balaam, Beor's son, with the sword.
Numbers 31:9 The people of Israel captured all the women and children of Midian, and took all their cows, sheep, and possessions.
Numbers 31:10 They burned all the cities they lived in, and all their beautiful strongholds, with fire.
Numbers 31:11 They took everything they captured, including people and animals.
Numbers 31:12 They brought the prisoners, the captured goods, and the stolen items to Moses, to Eleazar the priest, and to the Israelites' assembly at the camp on the Moab plains by the Jordan River, close to Jericho.
Numbers 31:13 Moses, Eleazar the priest, and all the leaders of the people went out of the camp to meet them.
Numbers 31:14 Moses was angry with the army officers, the leaders of thousands and the leaders of hundreds, who returned from the battle.
Numbers 31:15 Moses asked them, "Did you keep all the women alive?"
Numbers 31:16 Look, these people led the Israelites, following Balaam's advice, to sin against God at Peor, and a disease spread among God's people.
Numbers 31:17 Now, kill all the boys and every woman who has slept with a man.
Numbers 31:18 Keep alive all the young girls who have never slept with a man for yourselves.
Numbers 31:19 Stay outside the camp for seven days if you've killed someone or touched a dead body. Clean yourselves and your prisoners on the third and seventh days.
Numbers 31:20 Clean all your clothes, everything made of skin, all goat hair items, and all wooden things.
Numbers 31:21 Priest Eleazar told the soldiers who fought in the battle, "This is the rule of the law that God gave to Moses."
Numbers 31:22 Keep only the gold, silver, bronze, iron, tin, and lead.
Numbers 31:23 Everything that can withstand fire must be passed through it to become clean; however, it must also be purified with special water. But everything that cannot withstand fire must be passed through water.
Numbers 31:24 On the seventh day, wash your clothes, and then you will be clean. After that, you can return to the camp.
Numbers 31:25 God spoke to Moses, saying,
Numbers 31:26 Count all the captured people and animals, you, Eleazar the priest, and the leading elders of the community.
Numbers 31:27 Split the loot into two parts: one for those who fought in the battle, and the other for the whole community.
Numbers 31:28 Collect a tax for God from the soldiers who fought in battle: one out of every five hundred people, cattle, donkeys, and sheep.
Numbers 31:29 Take some from their share and give it to priest Eleazar as a gift for God.
Numbers 31:30 From the Israelites' half, take one out of every fifty people and animals, including cattle, donkeys, and sheep, and give them to the Levites who are responsible for maintaining God's tent.
Numbers 31:31 Moses and Eleazar the priest did as God commanded Moses.
Numbers 31:32 The spoils, which were the remaining things captured by the soldiers, were six hundred and seventy-five thousand sheep.
Numbers 31:33 Seventy-two thousand cows,
Numbers 31:34 Sixty-one thousand donkeys.
Numbers 31:35 In total, thirty-two thousand women who had never slept with a man.
Numbers 31:36 And the half for those who went to battle was 337,500 sheep.
Numbers 31:37 God's share of the sheep was six hundred seventy-five.
Numbers 31:38 There were 36,000 cattle; God's share was 72.
Numbers 31:39 There were thirty thousand and five hundred donkeys, and God's share was sixty-one.
Numbers 31:40 There were 16,000 people; God's share was 32 people.
Numbers 31:41 Moses gave the special gift for God to Priest Eleazar, as God told Moses to do.
Numbers 31:42 From the half belonging to the Israelites, which Moses separated from the soldiers,
Numbers 31:43 The part that belonged to the people was 337,500 sheep.
Numbers 31:44 Thirty-six thousand cattle,
Numbers 31:45 Thirty thousand and five hundred donkeys.
Numbers 31:46 And sixteen thousand people.
Numbers 31:47 From the Israelites' half, Moses took one out of every fifty people and animals and gave them to the Levites, who looked after God's tent, as God told Moses.
Numbers 31:48 The leaders in charge of a thousand soldiers, and the captains of hundreds, approached Moses.
Numbers 31:49 They told Moses, "Your servants have counted all the soldiers we are responsible for, and not one man is missing."
Numbers 31:50 We have brought a gift for God, including gold jewelry like chains, bracelets, rings, earrings, and pendants, to ask forgiveness for our souls before God.
Numbers 31:51 Moses and Priest Eleazar took the gold from them, including all the crafted jewels.
Numbers 31:52 All the gold given to God by the leaders of the thousands and hundreds was 16,750 shekels.
Numbers 31:53 The soldiers had taken loot, each man for himself.
Numbers 31:54 Moses and Eleazar the priest took the gold from the leaders of thousands and hundreds and brought it to the meeting tent as a reminder for the Israelites before God.
