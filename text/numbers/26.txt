Numbers 26:1 After the plague, God spoke to Moses and Eleazar, Aaron's son the priest, saying,
Numbers 26:2 Count all the Israelites who are 20 years old and above, from each family, who can fight in battles.
Numbers 26:3 Moses and the priest Eleazar talked to them on the plains of Moab by the Jordan River near Jericho, saying,
Numbers 26:4 Count the people who are 20 years old and older; do as God told Moses and the Israelites when they left Egypt.
Numbers 26:5 Reuben, Israel's oldest son: Reuben's children; Hanoch, who started the Hanoch family; Pallu started the Pallu family.
Numbers 26:6 From Hezron comes the Hezronite family; from Carmi comes the Carmite family.
Numbers 26:7 These are the Reubenite families, and there were 43,730 of them counted.
Numbers 26:8 Pallu's sons were Eliab.
Numbers 26:9 Eliab's sons were Nemuel, Dathan, and Abiram. Dathan and Abiram were well-known among the people, who fought against Moses and Aaron with Korah's group when they opposed God.
Numbers 26:10 The ground split open and swallowed them with Korah when they died, as fire consumed 250 men; and they became a warning.
Numbers 26:11 However, Korah's children did not die.
Numbers 26:12 Simeon's sons by their families: Nemuel's family, the Nemuelites; Jamin's family, the Jaminites; Jachin's family, the Jachinites.
Numbers 26:13 From Zerah comes the Zarhite family; from Shaul comes the Shaulite family.
Numbers 26:14 These are the Simeonite families, with a total of 22,200 people.
Numbers 26:15 The Gad family groups included: the Zephon group called Zephonites, the Haggi group called Haggites, and the Shuni group called Shunites.
Numbers 26:16 From Ozni's family, the Oznites; from Eri's family, the Erites.
Numbers 26:17 From Arod comes the Arodite family; from Areli, the Areli family.
Numbers 26:18 These are the groups of Gad's descendants, counted at forty-five thousand.
Numbers 26:19 Judah's sons were Er and Onan, and they died in Canaan.
Numbers 26:20 The families of Judah's sons were: from Shelah, the Shelah family; from Perez, the Perez family; and from Zerah, the Zerah family.
Numbers 26:21 Pharez had sons: from Hezron came the Hezronite family; from Hamul came the Hamulite family.
Numbers 26:22 These are the families of Judah, and the count for them is 76,500.
Numbers 26:23 From the family of Issachar: Tola's descendants were the Tola family; Pua's descendants were the Pua family.
Numbers 26:24 From Jashub comes the Jashubite family; from Shimron comes the Shimronite family.
Numbers 26:25 These are the families of Issachar with their total of 64,300 people counted.
Numbers 26:26 The families of Zebulun's sons are: Sered's family the Sardites, Elon's family the Elonites, and Jahleel's family the Jahleelites.
Numbers 26:27 These are the Zebulun family groups and there are 60,500 people in them.
Numbers 26:28 Joseph's sons and their families were Manasseh and Ephraim.
Numbers 26:29 From the descendants of Manasseh: from Machir's family came the Machirites, and Machir was the father of Gilead, from whom the Gileadites are descended.
Numbers 26:30 These are Gilead's sons: From Jeezer comes the Jeezerite family; from Helek comes the Helekite family.
Numbers 26:31 From the Asriel clan comes the Asrielites; from the Shechem clan comes the Shechemites.
Numbers 26:32 From Shemida came the Shemidaites, and from Hepher came the Hepherites.
Numbers 26:33 Zelophehad, Hepher's son, had no sons, only daughters: their names were Mahlah, Noah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Tirzah.
Numbers 26:34 These are the groups of Manasseh, and the number in them is 52,700.
Numbers 26:35 Ephraim's sons and their families are: from Shuthelah, the Shuthelah family; from Becher, the Becher family; from Tahan, the Tahan family.
Numbers 26:36 Shuthelah had sons, and one was Eran, which started the family of the Eranites.
Numbers 26:37 These are the family groups of Ephraim's sons, numbering thirty-two thousand five hundred. These are Joseph's sons, grouped by families.
Numbers 26:38 Benjamin's sons by their families: Bela's family, the Belaites; Ashbel's family, the Ashbelites; Ahiram's family, the Ahiramites.
Numbers 26:39 From Shupham comes the Shupham family; from Hupham comes the Hupham family.
Numbers 26:40 Bela's sons were Ard and Naaman: from Ard came the Ardite family, and from Naaman came the Naamite family.
Numbers 26:41 These are Benjamin's sons by their families: the counted number was 45,600.
Numbers 26:42 These are the children of Dan by their families: from Shuham comes the Shuhamite family. These are the Dan families by their families.
Numbers 26:43 All the Shuhamite families, when counted, totaled 64,400.
Numbers 26:44 From Asher's descendants by their families: From Jimna, the Jimna family; from Jesui, the Jesui family; from Beriah, the Beriah family.
Numbers 26:45 From Beriah's sons: Heber's descendants are the Heberites; Malchiel's descendants are the Malchielites.
Numbers 26:46 Asher's daughter was named Sarah.
Numbers 26:47 These are the families of Asher's sons by their numbers; there were 53,400 of them.
Numbers 26:48 From Naphtali's sons by their families: Jahzeel's descendants, the Jahzeelites; Guni's descendants, the Gunites.
Numbers 26:49 From Jezer comes the Jezerite family; from Shillem comes the Shillemite family.
Numbers 26:50 Here are the family groups of Naphtali according to their own families: and the number of people in them was 45,400.
Numbers 26:51 The children of Israel were counted as 603,730.
Numbers 26:52 God spoke to Moses, saying,
Numbers 26:53 To these people, the land will be given as an inheritance, based on the number of names.
Numbers 26:54 Give more land to those who have more people, and less land to those with fewer people; each group will get land based on its size.
Numbers 26:55 Despite this, the land will be split up by drawing lots; each tribe will receive land based on their ancestors' names.
Numbers 26:56 The land will be divided by drawing lots, giving pieces to both the large and small groups.
Numbers 26:57 These are the Levites counted by their families: from Gershon, the Gershonite family; from Kohath, the Kohathite family; from Merari, the Merarite family.
Numbers 26:58 These are the Levite families: the Libnites, the Hebronites, the Mahlites, the Mushites, and the Korathites. Kohath was the father of Amram.
Numbers 26:59 Amram's wife was named Jochebed, Levi's daughter, born to Levi in Egypt; and she gave birth to Aaron and Moses, and their sister Miriam for Amram.
Numbers 26:60 Aaron had four sons named Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar.
Numbers 26:61 Nadab and Abihu died for offering unauthorized fire before God.
Numbers 26:62 There were 23,000 males one month old and older counted. They were not counted among the Israelites because they did not receive any land from the Israelites.
Numbers 26:63 Moses and Priest Eleazar counted these people. They counted the Israelites on the Moab plains by the Jordan River near Jericho.
Numbers 26:64 But none of the men counted by Moses and Aaron, the priest, when they counted the Israelites in the Sinai desert, were among these.
Numbers 26:65 God had said they would definitely die in the wilderness. No man was left except Caleb, Jephunneh's son, and Joshua, Nun's son.
