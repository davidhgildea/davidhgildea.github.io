Numbers 10:1 God spoke to Moses, saying,
Numbers 10:2 Make two silver trumpets from one piece of metal to call the people together and to signal when it's time to move the camps.
Numbers 10:3 When they blow the horns, everyone must gather to you at the tent of meeting entrance.
Numbers 10:4 If they blow one trumpet, then the leaders, who are in charge of Israel's groups of a thousand, will come to you.
Numbers 10:5 When you sound an alarm, the camps on the east side should move ahead.
Numbers 10:6 When you sound the alarm a second time, the camps on the south side will start to travel; they will blow the alarm when they are to move.
Numbers 10:7 When it's time to gather the people, you should blow the trumpets, but do not sound an alarm.
Numbers 10:8 Aaron's sons, who are priests, will blow the trumpets; this will be a lasting rule for all your future families.
Numbers 10:9 If you fight in your land against the enemy who attacks you, then you must sound an alarm with the trumpets; and you will be thought of by God, and you will be rescued from your enemies.
Numbers 10:10 On happy days, special days, and the start of your months, blow trumpets over your burnt offerings and peace sacrifices as a reminder before your God: I am your God.
Numbers 10:11 On the twentieth day of the second month in the second year, the cloud lifted from above the tent of the law.
Numbers 10:12 The Israelites left the Sinai desert, and the cloud settled in the Paran desert.
Numbers 10:13 They began their journey as God commanded through Moses.
Numbers 10:14 First, the group from the tribe of Judah marched with their flag, and Nahshon son of Amminadab led them.
Numbers 10:15 The leader of Issachar's tribe's army was Nethaneel, Zuar's son.
Numbers 10:16 Eliab, Helon's son, led the army of Zebulun's descendants.
Numbers 10:17 The tent was taken apart; and Gershon's and Merari's sons moved on, carrying the tent.
Numbers 10:18 Reuben's group marched with their banner, and Elizur, Shedeur's son, was in charge of their soldiers.
Numbers 10:19 The leader of Simeon's tribe was Shelumiel, Zurishaddai's son.
Numbers 10:20 Eliasaph, Deuel's son, was in charge of Gad tribe's soldiers.
Numbers 10:21 The Kohathites moved ahead, carrying the sanctuary, and others set up the tent before they arrived.
Numbers 10:22 The group from Ephraim moved forward with their soldiers, and Elishama, Ammihud's son, was their leader.
Numbers 10:23 Gamaliel, Pedahzur's son, led the army of Manasseh's tribe.
Numbers 10:24 Abidan, Gideoni's son, led the army of Benjamin's tribe.
Numbers 10:25 The flag of Dan's camp moved ahead, which was at the back of all the groups, with Ahiezer, son of Ammishaddai, leading his people.
Numbers 10:26 Pagiel, son of Ocran, was in charge of the group from the Asher tribe.
Numbers 10:27 Ahira, Enan's son, led the army of Naphtali's tribe.
Numbers 10:28 These were the travels of the Israelites by their groups when they moved on.
Numbers 10:29 Moses said to Hobab, Raguel the Midianite's son and Moses' father-in-law, "We are traveling to the place God promised to give us. Come with us, and we'll treat you well. God has promised good things for Israel."
Numbers 10:30 He said to him, "I will not go; instead, I will go back to my own land and to my family."
Numbers 10:31 He said, "Please don't leave us; you know where we can camp in the desert, and you can guide us."
Numbers 10:32 If you come with us, whatever good things God does for us, we will do the same for you.
Numbers 10:33 They left God's mountain and traveled for three days. The chest of God's agreement led them during these three days to find a place to rest.
Numbers 10:34 God's cloud was over them during the day when they left the camp.
Numbers 10:35 When the ark moved, Moses said, "Get up, God, make your enemies go away, and those who hate you run from you."
Numbers 10:36 When it stopped, he said, "Come back, God, to the many thousands of Israel."
