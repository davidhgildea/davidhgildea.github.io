Numbers 32:1 The Reubenites and Gadites had many animals. When they saw the land of Jazer and Gilead, they noticed it was good for animals.
Numbers 32:2 The descendants of Gad and Reuben came and spoke to Moses, Eleazar the priest, and the leaders of the people, saying,
Numbers 32:3 Ataroth, Dibon, Jazer, Nimrah, Heshbon, Elealeh, Shebam, Nebo, and Beon.
Numbers 32:4 Even the land that God struck down before Israel's people is good for livestock, and your servants have livestock.
Numbers 32:5 So they said, if we have gained your favor, let this land be given to your servants as property, and do not take us across the Jordan.
Numbers 32:6 Moses said to Gad's and Reuben's descendants, "Will your brothers go to fight while you stay here?"
Numbers 32:7 Why are you making the Israelites not want to go to the land that God has given them?
Numbers 32:8 Your ancestors did this when I sent them from Kadesh Barnea to explore the land.
Numbers 32:9 When they came to the Eshcol Valley and saw the land, they made the Israelites' hearts lose courage so they would not enter the land God had given them.
Numbers 32:10 God became very angry then, and he promised, saying,
Numbers 32:11 No one who came from Egypt, age twenty or older, will see the land I promised to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, because they did not fully follow me.
Numbers 32:12 Protect Caleb son of Jephunneh the Kenezite, and Joshua son of Nun, because they have completely followed God.
Numbers 32:13 God became angry with Israel and made them wander in the desert for forty years, until all the people who had done wrong in God's eyes had died.
Numbers 32:14 Look, you have taken your fathers' place as a group of sinful people, making God even angrier with Israel.
Numbers 32:15 If you turn away from God, he will leave you in the desert, and you will ruin everyone.
Numbers 32:16 They came to him and said, "We will make places for our sheep and towns for our children here."
Numbers 32:17 We will go armed ahead of the Israelites until they reach their land. Our families will live in protected cities because of the land's people.
Numbers 32:18 We won't go back to our homes until each Israelite has received their share of the land.
Numbers 32:19 We will not own land with them across the Jordan, nor beyond it, because our inheritance has been given to us on this side of the Jordan to the east.
Numbers 32:20 And Moses said to them, "If you do this, if you go ready for battle before God,
Numbers 32:21 All of you, ready for battle, will cross the Jordan River before God until he has forced his enemies out of his way.
Numbers 32:22 When the land is conquered for God, then you can go back home. You will be innocent before God and before Israel, and this land will be yours under God's watch.
Numbers 32:23 If you don't do this, you have sinned against God, and your sin will become known.
Numbers 32:24 Make towns for your children and pens for your sheep; and do what you said you would.
Numbers 32:25 The children of Gad and Reuben said to Moses, "Your servants will do what you command."
Numbers 32:26 Our children, our wives, our herds, and all our animals will stay in the cities of Gilead.
Numbers 32:27 Your servants will cross over, each man ready for war, before God to fight, as my lord says.
Numbers 32:28 Moses told Priest Eleazar, Joshua, Nun's son, and the main leaders of Israel's tribes about them.
Numbers 32:29 Moses told them, "If the Gadites and the Reubenites cross the Jordan River with you, ready to fight in front of God, and the land is conquered, then you should give them the land of Gilead to own."
Numbers 32:30 If they won't cross over fully armed to help you, they must settle among you in Canaan.
Numbers 32:31 The children of Gad and the children of Reuben replied, "We will do as God has told your servants."
Numbers 32:32 We will go across armed in front of God to the land of Canaan, so the land we inherit on this side of the Jordan River will belong to us.
Numbers 32:33 Moses gave the Gadites, the Reubenites, and half of the Manasseh tribe the lands and cities, including the territories of the kingdoms of Sihon and Og.
Numbers 32:34 The Gad people built Dibon, Ataroth, and Aroer.
Numbers 32:35 And Atroth, Shophan, Jaazer, and Jogbehah,
Numbers 32:36 Bethnimrah and Bethharan were protected cities with sheep pens.
Numbers 32:37 Reuben's descendants built Heshbon, Elealeh, and Kirjathaim.
Numbers 32:38 They renamed Nebo, Baalmeon, and Shibmah, and gave new names to the cities they built.
Numbers 32:39 Machir's descendants, who were Manasseh's children, went to Gilead, captured it, and forced out the Amorites living there.
Numbers 32:40 Moses gave Gilead to Machir, Manasseh's son, and he lived there.
Numbers 32:41 Jair, who was from Manasseh's family, captured the small towns and named them Havothjair.
Numbers 32:42 Nobah went and captured Kenath and its surrounding villages, and renamed it Nobah, like his own name.
