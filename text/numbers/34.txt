Numbers 34:1 And God spoke to Moses, saying,
Numbers 34:2 Tell the Israelites, when you enter the land of Canaan, this is the land that will be your inheritance, the land of Canaan and its borders.
Numbers 34:3 Your southern area will start from the Zin wilderness, going by Edom's edge, and your southern boundary will be the farthest shore of the Salt Sea to the east.
Numbers 34:4 Your boundary will go from the south up to the Akrabbim Scarp, continue to Zin, and start out from the south to Kadesh Barnea. Then it will move on to Hazar Addar and over to Azmon.
Numbers 34:5 The border will go around from Azmon to the river of Egypt, and it will end at the sea.
Numbers 34:6 Your western border will be the big sea: this is your western edge.
Numbers 34:7 Your northern boundary will start from the Great Sea and go to Mount Hor.
Numbers 34:8 From Mount Hor, you will mark your boundary to the entrance of Hamath; and the end of the boundary will be at Zedad.
Numbers 34:9 The border will continue to Ziphron, and it will end at Hazarenan: this will be your northern boundary.
Numbers 34:10 Mark your eastern border from Hazarenan to Shepham.
Numbers 34:11 The border will extend from Shepham to Riblah, east of Ain; it will go down and stretch to the eastern shore of the Sea of Galilee.
Numbers 34:12 The boundary will go down to the Jordan River, and end at the Salt Sea. This will be your land, including all its borders.
Numbers 34:13 Moses told the Israelites, "This is the land that you will receive as your inheritance, which God has instructed to be given to the nine and a half tribes."
Numbers 34:14 The Reuben family tribe and the Gad family tribe have gotten their land, and half of the Manasseh family tribe has gotten theirs too.
Numbers 34:15 The two and a half tribes got their land east of the Jordan River by Jericho, toward the sunrise.
Numbers 34:16 God spoke to Moses, saying,
Numbers 34:17 Here are the names of the men who will divide the land for you: Eleazar the priest and Joshua, Nun's son.
Numbers 34:18 You will choose one leader from each tribe to divide the land as an inheritance.
Numbers 34:19 The men's names are as follows: From Judah's tribe, Caleb son of Jephunneh.
Numbers 34:20 From the Simeon tribe, Shemuel, Ammihud's son.
Numbers 34:21 From the tribe of Benjamin, Elidad, Chislon's son.
Numbers 34:22 The leader from the tribe of Dan's descendants was Bukki, Jogli's son.
Numbers 34:23 The leader of Joseph's descendants from the Manasseh tribe was Hanniel, Ephod's son.
Numbers 34:24 The leader of the Ephraim tribe, Kemuel son of Shiphtan.
Numbers 34:25 The leader from the Zebulun people's group, Elizaphan, Parnach's son.
Numbers 34:26 The leader from Issachar's tribe was Paltiel, Azzan's son.
Numbers 34:27 The leader from the Asher tribe, Ahihud, Shelomi's son.
Numbers 34:28 The leader from Naphtali's tribe, the son of Ammihud, was Pedahel.
Numbers 34:29 These are the people God told to divide the land for the Israelites to inherit in Canaan.
