Numbers 22:1 The Israelites moved on and camped in the Moab plains near the Jordan River across from Jericho.
Numbers 22:2 Balak, Zippor's son, saw everything Israel did to the Amorites.
Numbers 22:3 Moab was very scared of the people because there were so many, and Moab was troubled because of the children of Israel.
Numbers 22:4 Moab told Midian's leaders, "This group will consume everything around us like an ox eats the field's grass." At that time, Balak, Zippor's son, was king of Moab.
Numbers 22:5 He sent messengers to Balaam, son of Beor, at Pethor by the river in his homeland, to invite him, saying, "Look, a people has come out of Egypt; they cover the land and are settling near me."
Numbers 22:6 Please come now and curse these people for me because they are too powerful. Maybe then I can defeat them and force them out of the land. I know that anyone you bless is blessed, and anyone you curse is cursed.
Numbers 22:7 The leaders of Moab and Midian left with money for fortune-telling and went to Balaam. They told him what Balak had said.
Numbers 22:8 He told them, "Stay here tonight, and I will give you an answer after I hear what God tells me." So the Moab leaders stayed with Balaam.
Numbers 22:9 God came to Balaam and asked, "Who are these men with you?"
Numbers 22:10 Balaam said to God, "Balak, Zippor's son and Moab's king, has sent for me, saying,
Numbers 22:11 Look, people have left Egypt and they are everywhere. Come and curse them for me; maybe then I can defeat them and drive them away.
Numbers 22:12 God told Balaam, "Do not go with them; do not curse the people, because they are blessed."
Numbers 22:13 Balaam got up in the morning and told Balak's leaders, "Go back to your country, because God will not let me go with you."
Numbers 22:14 The Moab leaders got up, went to Balak, and said, "Balaam won't come with us."
Numbers 22:15 Balak sent even more princes who were greater and more respected than the first ones.
Numbers 22:16 They went to Balaam and told him, "Balak, Zippor's son, says, 'Please don't let anything stop you from coming to me.'"
Numbers 22:17 I will give you great honor and do what you ask: please come and curse these people for me.
Numbers 22:18 Balaam replied to Balak's servants, "Even if Balak gave me a house full of silver and gold, I can't ignore God's command to do anything less or more."
Numbers 22:19 Please stay here tonight, so I can find out what more God will tell me.
Numbers 22:20 God came to Balaam at night and said, "If the men come to call you, get up and go with them; but you must only do what I tell you to do."
Numbers 22:21 Balaam got up in the morning, put a saddle on his donkey, and left with the leaders of Moab.
Numbers 22:22 God became angry because Balaam went, and God's angel stood in the road to oppose him. Balaam was riding on his donkey, and he had two servants with him.
Numbers 22:23 The donkey saw God's angel standing on the path with a sword in his hand, so the donkey left the path and went into the field. Balaam hit the donkey to make her go back on the path.
Numbers 22:24 An angel of God stood in a vineyard path with a wall on each side.
Numbers 22:25 When the donkey saw God's angel, she pushed against the wall, crushing Balaam's foot, and he hit her again.
Numbers 22:26 God's angel went on and stopped in a tight space where there was no room to move right or left.
Numbers 22:27 When the donkey saw God's angel, she lay down with Balaam on her; this made Balaam angry, and he hit the donkey with a stick.
Numbers 22:28 God let the donkey talk, and she asked Balaam, "What did I do to you, that you have hit me three times?"
Numbers 22:29 Balaam said to the donkey, "You have made fun of me. If I had a sword in my hand, I would kill you right now."
Numbers 22:30 The donkey said to Balaam, "Aren't I your donkey, the one you've ridden on since you got me until today? Have I ever acted this way to you before?" And he said, "No."
Numbers 22:31 Then God let Balaam see, and he noticed God's angel standing in the path with a sword ready in his hand, so he bowed deeply and lay face down.
Numbers 22:32 The angel of God said to him, "Why have you hit your donkey three times? Look, I came to oppose you because you are going the wrong way against my will."
Numbers 22:33 The donkey saw me and moved away from me three times. If she hadn't moved away, I would have surely killed you by now, but let her live.
Numbers 22:34 Balaam said to the angel of God, "I have done wrong because I didn't know you were standing in my way. If you're unhappy, I'll go back."
Numbers 22:35 The angel of God told Balaam, "Go with the men, but say only what I tell you to say." So Balaam went with Balak's leaders.
Numbers 22:36 When Balak found out that Balaam had arrived, he went to meet him at a city in Moab, near the edge of the Arnon River, at the farthest boundary.
Numbers 22:37 Balak said to Balaam, "Didn't I send for you urgently to call you? Why didn't you come to me? Can't I reward you with honor?"
Numbers 22:38 Balaam said to Balak, "Look, I have come to you: can I just say anything? I will speak only what God gives me to say."
Numbers 22:39 Balaam went with Balak to Kirjathhuzoth.
Numbers 22:40 Balak sacrificed oxen and sheep, and sent them to Balaam and to the leaders with him.
Numbers 22:41 The next day, Balak took Balaam to the high places of Baal so he could see all the people.
