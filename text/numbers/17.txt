Numbers 17:1 God spoke to Moses, saying,
Numbers 17:2 Talk to the people of Israel, and get a stick from each one based on their family leader’s house, making twelve sticks in total: write each man's name on his stick.
Numbers 17:3 Write Aaron's name on Levi's staff, because one staff represents the leader of their family.
Numbers 17:4 Put them in the meeting tent in front of the covenant, where I will meet you.
Numbers 17:5 The man's stick I pick will flower, and I will stop the complaints of the Israelites against you.
Numbers 17:6 Moses spoke to the Israelites, and each chief gave him a stick, one for each chief from their family groups, making twelve sticks total, including Aaron's stick among them.
Numbers 17:7 Moses put the rods in front of God in the tent of meeting.
Numbers 17:8 The next day, Moses entered the tent of testimony and saw that Aaron's staff for the Levi family had sprouted, produced buds, flowered, and grown almonds.
Numbers 17:9 Moses brought all the rods from God to the Israelites, and they saw and each man took his own rod.
Numbers 17:10 God told Moses, "Bring Aaron's staff back before the testimony to be saved as a sign for the rebels; and you will completely remove their complaints from me, so they do not die."
Numbers 17:11 Moses did exactly what God told him to do.
Numbers 17:12 The Israelites said to Moses, "Look, we're going to die, we're all going to die."
Numbers 17:13 Anyone who comes close to God's tent will die: are we going to keep dying?
