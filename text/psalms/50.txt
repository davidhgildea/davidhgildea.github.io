Psalms 50:1 The powerful God has spoken and called to the earth from where the sun rises to where it sets.
Psalms 50:2 From Zion, the perfect place of beauty, God has shone forth.
Psalms 50:3 God will come and will not be silent. A fire will burn before Him, and it will be very stormy around Him.
Psalms 50:4 He will call to the skies above and the earth below to judge his people.
Psalms 50:5 Bring together those who are devoted to me, those who have made a promise to me through sacrifice.
Psalms 50:6 The skies will show how right and good He is, because God is the judge. Pause and think about that.
Psalms 50:7 Listen, my people, and I will speak; Israel, listen, and I will witness against you: I am God, your God.
Psalms 50:8 I will not scold you for your sacrifices or burnt offerings, which have always been before me.
Psalms 50:9 I will not take any bulls from your house or goats from your pens.
Psalms 50:10 Every animal in the forest is mine, and the cattle on a thousand hills belong to me.
Psalms 50:11 I know all the birds of the mountains, and the wild animals of the fields are mine.
Psalms 50:12 If I were hungry, I wouldn't tell you because the world is mine and everything in it.
Psalms 50:13 Will I eat bull meat, or drink goat blood?
Psalms 50:14 Give thanks to God; and keep your promises to the Most High.
Psalms 50:15 Ask for my help when you have trouble: I will save you, and you will praise me.
Psalms 50:16 God says to the wicked, "Why do you talk about my laws or say you follow my agreement?"
Psalms 50:17 You hate advice and ignore what I say.
Psalms 50:18 When you saw a thief, you agreed with him, and you joined in with adulterers.
Psalms 50:19 You use your mouth for evil and your tongue tells lies.
Psalms 50:20 You sit and talk against your brother; you spread lies about your own mother's son.
Psalms 50:21 You have done these things, and I stayed quiet; you thought I was just like you: but I will correct you and show you clearly.
Psalms 50:22 Think about this, you who forget God, or I will tear you apart, and no one will save you.
Psalms 50:23 Whoever offers praise honors me, and those who live rightly will see God's salvation.
