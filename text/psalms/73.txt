Psalms 73:1 God is truly good to Israel and to those who have a pure heart.
Psalms 73:2 But as for me, I almost lost my footing; my steps nearly slipped.
Psalms 73:3 I felt jealous of foolish people when I saw the success of the wicked.
Psalms 73:4 In their death, they face no struggles, and they remain strong.
Psalms 73:5 They don't have troubles like other people; they aren't afflicted like everyone else.
Psalms 73:6 So pride surrounds them like a chain; violence covers them like clothes.
Psalms 73:7 They have puffy eyes from being rich: they own more than anyone could desire.
Psalms 73:8 They are bad, talking badly about making others suffer. They talk with great pride.
Psalms 73:9 They speak against God above, and their words spread across the world.
Psalms 73:10 So his people come back here, and they are given plenty to drink.
Psalms 73:11 And they ask, "Does God know? Does the Most High have knowledge?"
Psalms 73:12 Look, these are the wicked, who succeed in life; they grow rich.
Psalms 73:13 I truly cleaned my heart for nothing, and washed my hands being innocent.
Psalms 73:14 All day I suffer and am punished each morning.
Psalms 73:15 If I decide to speak like this, I realize I would upset your children's generation.
Psalms 73:16 When I tried to understand this, it was too hard for me.
Psalms 73:17 Until I went into God's sanctuary; then I understood their fate.
Psalms 73:18 You truly put them in risky places; you threw them down to ruin.
Psalms 73:19 How suddenly they are ruined! They are completely destroyed by fears.
Psalms 73:20 Like a dream when someone wakes up; so God, when you wake up, you will look down on their appearance.
Psalms 73:21 My heart was sad, and I felt a sharp pain inside.
Psalms 73:22 I was foolish and did not understand; I was like an animal before you.
Psalms 73:23 I am always with you; you hold my right hand.
Psalms 73:24 You will guide me with your advice, and later take me to heaven.
Psalms 73:25 Who else do I have in heaven but you? And on earth, I want no one but you.
Psalms 73:26 My body and heart may grow weak, but God is the strong source of my heart and my share forever.
Psalms 73:27 Those far from you will die; you destroy all who are unfaithful to you.
Psalms 73:28 It is good for me to get close to God. I trust in God so I can tell about all your deeds.
