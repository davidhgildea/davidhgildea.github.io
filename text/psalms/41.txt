Psalms 41:1 Happy is the one who thinks about the poor; God will save him when trouble comes.
Psalms 41:2 God will protect him and keep him alive; he will be happy on earth, and you will not let his enemies defeat him.
Psalms 41:3 God will give him strength when he is sick in bed; you will take care of him during his illness.
Psalms 41:4 I said, "God, be kind to me: make my soul better; for I have done wrong against you."
Psalms 41:5 My enemies say bad things about me, asking, "When will he die and his name be forgotten?"
Psalms 41:6 If he comes to visit, he speaks nonsense; his heart collects evil thoughts, and when he leaves, he spreads them.
Psalms 41:7 Everyone who hates me talks secretly against me; they plan to harm me.
Psalms 41:8 They say a terrible sickness sticks to him; and now that he's down, he will not get up again.
Psalms 41:9 Yes, my close friend whom I trusted, who shared my food, has turned against me.
Psalms 41:10 But you, God, be kind to me, and lift me up, so I can pay them back.
Psalms 41:11 I know you favor me because my enemy hasn't defeated me.
Psalms 41:12 As for me, you support me in my honesty, and place me in your presence forever.
Psalms 41:13 Praise God of Israel forever and ever. Amen.
