Psalms 91:1 Whoever lives in the hidden place of the Most High will stay safe under the protection of the Almighty.
Psalms 91:2 I will say about God, He is my safe place and strong shelter: my God; in Him I will trust.
Psalms 91:3 God will surely save you from the hunter's trap and from deadly disease.
Psalms 91:4 God will protect you like a bird covers its young with feathers; you will find safety under his wings: his faithfulness will be your defense and protector.
Psalms 91:5 Do not be afraid of dangers at night or arrows that fly during the day.
Psalms 91:6 Not for the sickness that moves in the dark; not for the ruin that destroys at midday.
Psalms 91:7 A thousand will fall near you, and ten thousand by your side, but it will not come close to you.
Psalms 91:8 You will only watch and see what happens to the wicked.
Psalms 91:9 Because you have made God, who is my protection, even the highest place, your home.
Psalms 91:10 No harm will happen to you, nor will any sickness come close to your home.
Psalms 91:11 God will send his angels to look after you and guard you wherever you go.
Psalms 91:12 They will carry you in their hands, so you won't hurt your foot on a stone.
Psalms 91:13 You will walk on the lion and snake; you will step on the young lion and the dragon.
Psalms 91:14 Because he loves me, I will rescue him. I will protect him because he knows my name.
Psalms 91:15 He will call to me, and I will reply; I will be with him in trouble, save him, and give him honor.
Psalms 91:16 I will give him a long life and show him how I can save.
