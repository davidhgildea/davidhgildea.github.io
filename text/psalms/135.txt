Psalms 135:1 Praise God. Praise the name of God; praise him, all you servants of God.
Psalms 135:2 You who stand in God's house, in the courts of our God's house.
Psalms 135:3 Praise God because God is good. Sing praises to His name because it is nice.
Psalms 135:4 God has chosen Jacob for himself, and Israel as his special treasure.
Psalms 135:5 I know that God is great and that our Lord is greater than all other gods.
Psalms 135:6 Whatever God wanted to do, he did in heaven and on earth, in the seas and in all deep places.
Psalms 135:7 God makes the mist rise from the earth's edges; he creates lightning for the rain; he brings the wind from his storerooms.
Psalms 135:8 Who struck down the firstborn of Egypt, both of humans and animals.
Psalms 135:9 God sent signs and miracles into the middle of Egypt, upon Pharaoh, and upon all his servants.
Psalms 135:10 God defeated large nations and killed powerful kings.
Psalms 135:11 Sihon, the king of the Amorites, Og, the king of Bashan, and all the kingdoms of Canaan.
Psalms 135:12 God gave their land as a heritage, a heritage to his people Israel.
Psalms 135:13 Your name, O God, lasts forever; and people will remember you, O God, through all generations.
Psalms 135:14 God will judge his people, and he will change his mind about his servants.
Psalms 135:15 The gods of other nations are made of silver and gold and are crafted by human hands.
Psalms 135:16 They have mouths but do not talk; they have eyes but do not see;
Psalms 135:17 They have ears, but they don't hear; they also don't breathe through their mouths.
Psalms 135:18 Those who make idols become like them; so does everyone who trusts in them.
Psalms 135:19 Praise God, O people of Israel; praise God, O family of Aaron.
Psalms 135:20 Praise God, O family of Levi: all who respect God, praise God.
Psalms 135:21 May God, who lives in Jerusalem, be blessed. Praise God.
