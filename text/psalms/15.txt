Psalms 15:1 God, who will live in your tent? Who will stay on your sacred mountain?
Psalms 15:2 The person who lives rightly, does what is just, and tells the truth in their heart.
Psalms 15:3 He who does not talk badly about others with his tongue, does not harm his neighbor, and does not insult his neighbor.
Psalms 15:4 He looks down on those who are bad, but respects those who respect God. He keeps his promises, even when it costs him.
Psalms 15:5 Someone who does not lend money for interest or take bribes to hurt the innocent will always stand firm.
