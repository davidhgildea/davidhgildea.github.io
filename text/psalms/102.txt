Psalms 102:1 Listen to my prayer, God, and hear my call for help.
Psalms 102:2 Do not hide from me when I am in trouble; listen to me: answer quickly when I call you.
Psalms 102:3 My days vanish like smoke, and my bones are as burned out as a fireplace.
Psalms 102:4 My heart is struck down and dried up like grass; I even forget to eat my food.
Psalms 102:5 Because of my crying, my bones stick to my skin.
Psalms 102:6 I am like a pelican in the wild: I am like an owl in the desert.
Psalms 102:7 I watch and am like a lone sparrow on the rooftop.
Psalms 102:8 My enemies insult me all day; those who hate me are always plotting against me.
Psalms 102:9 I have eaten ashes as if they were bread and mixed my drink with tears.
Psalms 102:10 Because you were angry and upset, you picked me up and then threw me down.
Psalms 102:11 My days are like a fading shadow; I am wilting like grass.
Psalms 102:12 But you, God, will last forever; and you will be remembered by all generations.
Psalms 102:13 You will stand up and be kind to Zion because it is time to help her; yes, the exact time has arrived.
Psalms 102:14 Your servants enjoy her stones and care for her dust.
Psalms 102:15 So the nations will respect God's name, and all the earth's rulers your greatness.
Psalms 102:16 When God rebuilds Zion, he will show his greatness.
Psalms 102:17 God will pay attention to the poor's prayer and won't reject it.
Psalms 102:18 This will be recorded for future generations: and the people yet to be made will praise God.
Psalms 102:19 God has looked down from his holy place above; from heaven he watched over the earth.
Psalms 102:20 To listen to the cries of the prisoners; to free those who are set to die;
Psalms 102:21 To tell of God's name in Zion, and his praise in Jerusalem;
Psalms 102:22 When the people and the kingdoms come together to serve God.
Psalms 102:23 God made me weaker on my journey; He cut my life shorter.
Psalms 102:24 I said, O my God, do not take me away when my life is half done; your years last through all generations.
Psalms 102:25 Long ago, you created the earth's foundation, and the skies are made by your hands.
Psalms 102:26 They will be destroyed, but you will last. Yes, they will all wear out like clothes; you will change them like an outfit, and they will be changed.
Psalms 102:27 You remain the same, and your years will never end.
Psalms 102:28 Your servants' children will live on, and their descendants will be secure in your presence.
