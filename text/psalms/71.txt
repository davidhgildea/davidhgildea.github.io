Psalms 71:1 In you, God, I place my trust: may I never be confused.
Psalms 71:2 Save me by your goodness, and help me escape. Listen to me and rescue me.
Psalms 71:3 Be my strong home where I can always go; you ordered my rescue because you are my rock and my safe place.
Psalms 71:4 Save me, my God, from the wicked, from the unjust and cruel person.
Psalms 71:5 You are my hope, God; you have been my trust since I was young.
Psalms 71:6 You have supported me since birth; you brought me out of my mother's body: I will always praise you.
Psalms 71:7 I am a surprise to many; but you are my strong shelter.
Psalms 71:8 May my mouth always be full of your praise and honor.
Psalms 71:9 Don't reject me when I get old; don't leave me when my strength is gone.
Psalms 71:10 My enemies talk about me; and those who want to hurt me plan together.
Psalms 71:11 They say, "God has left him: chase him and catch him, for no one will save him."
Psalms 71:12 God, don't be far from me; God, hurry to help me.
Psalms 71:13 Let those who are against me be confused and destroyed; let those who want to harm me be covered with shame and disgrace.
Psalms 71:14 I will always have hope and will praise you more and more.
Psalms 71:15 I will always talk about your goodness and your help, because I can't count them.
Psalms 71:16 I will go with God's strength; I will talk about your righteousness, yours alone.
Psalms 71:17 God, you have taught me since I was young, and until now I have told of your wonderful deeds.
Psalms 71:18 Even now when I am old and have gray hair, God, do not leave me alone until I have told this generation about your strength and shown all those yet to come your power.
Psalms 71:19 Your goodness, O God, is so great, you have done amazing things: O God, there is no one like you!
Psalms 71:20 You, who have shown me great and hard troubles, will make me live again, and will lift me up from the deep places of the earth.
Psalms 71:21 You will make me greater and give me comfort on all sides.
Psalms 71:22 I will praise you with the lyre for your faithfulness, my God; I will sing to you with the harp, Holy One of Israel.
Psalms 71:23 I will be very happy as I sing to you; my soul, which you have saved, will rejoice.
Psalms 71:24 My tongue will speak of your goodness all day long because those who want to harm me are confused and ashamed.
