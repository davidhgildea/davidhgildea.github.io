Psalms 25:1 To you, God, I lift up my soul.
Psalms 25:2 My God, I trust in you; don't let me be embarrassed, don't let my enemies win over me.
Psalms 25:3 Yes, let no one who looks to you be embarrassed; let those who sin without reason feel shame.
Psalms 25:4 Show me your ways, God; teach me your paths.
Psalms 25:5 Guide me in your truth and teach me, for you are the God who saves me; I wait for you all day long.
Psalms 25:6 God, remember your tender mercy and love, for they have always been.
Psalms 25:7 Don't remember the wrongs I did when I was young, or my mistakes. Please, for your goodness, remember me with your mercy, God.
Psalms 25:8 God is good and fair, so he shows sinners the right path.
Psalms 25:9 God will guide the gentle in making fair decisions; and God will teach them his path.
Psalms 25:10 All of God's ways are kind and true for those who follow his agreement and his teachings.
Psalms 25:11 For your name's sake, God, forgive my sin; for it is very big.
Psalms 25:12 Who is the man that respects God? God will show him the path to choose.
Psalms 25:13 He will live in peace, and his children will inherit the land.
Psalms 25:14 God shares His secrets with those who respect Him; He will show them His agreement.
Psalms 25:15 I always look to God; He will free my feet from the trap.
Psalms 25:16 Turn to me and be kind to me; I am lonely and in trouble.
Psalms 25:17 My heart's troubles have grown; please rescue me from my distress.
Psalms 25:18 See my suffering and my pain; and forgive all my wrongs.
Psalms 25:19 Think about my enemies; they are numerous; and they hate me fiercely.
Psalms 25:20 Protect my life, and save me; do not let me be disappointed, because I trust in you.
Psalms 25:21 Let honesty and goodness protect me; I wait for you.
Psalms 25:22 Save Israel, God, from all his problems.
