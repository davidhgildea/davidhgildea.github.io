Psalms 11:1 I trust in God; why do you tell me to run away like a bird to the mountains?
Psalms 11:2 Look, the evil ones pull back their bows, they put their arrows on the strings to secretly shoot at the good-hearted.
Psalms 11:3 If the base is ruined, what can good people do?
Psalms 11:4 God is in his holy temple; God's throne is in heaven. He watches and judges everyone.
Psalms 11:5 God tests the good people, but hates the evil ones who love violence.
Psalms 11:6 God will rain traps, fire, and sulfur on the wicked, along with a terrible storm: this will be what fills their cup.
Psalms 11:7 God loves good people; He sees those who are honest.
