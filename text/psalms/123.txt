Psalms 123:1 I look up to you, God in the heavens.
Psalms 123:2 Just like servants watch their masters' hands, and a girl watches her mistress' hand, we watch for God until He shows us kindness.
Psalms 123:3 God, have mercy on us, have mercy on us; we are very much filled with scorn.
Psalms 123:4 Our soul is deeply filled with scorn from those who are at peace and with the contempt of the proud.
