Psalms 115:1 Not for us, God, not for us, but for your name give glory, because of your kindness and your truth.
Psalms 115:2 Why should other nations ask, "Where is their God now?"
Psalms 115:3 Our God is in heaven: he does whatever he wants.
Psalms 115:4 Their idols are made of silver and gold; they are made by human hands.
Psalms 115:5 They have mouths but don't speak; they have eyes but don't see.
Psalms 115:6 They have ears but cannot hear, and they have noses but cannot smell.
Psalms 115:7 They have hands but do not touch; they have feet but do not walk; they do not make sounds with their throats.
Psalms 115:8 Those who make idols are like them; so is everyone who trusts in them.
Psalms 115:9 Israel, trust in God: He is their help and shield.
Psalms 115:10 Family of Aaron, trust in God; He is their help and protector.
Psalms 115:11 If you respect God, trust in God: He is their help and protector.
Psalms 115:12 God has remembered us; He will bless us; He will bless Israel's people; He will bless Aaron's family.
Psalms 115:13 God will bless those who respect Him, no matter if they are unimportant or important.
Psalms 115:14 God will make you and your children more and more prosperous.
Psalms 115:15 You are blessed by God who made the sky and the earth.
Psalms 115:16 The skies, and even the highest skies, belong to God; but He has given the earth to people.
Psalms 115:17 Dead people cannot praise God, nor can anyone who goes into the quietness of death.
Psalms 115:18 We will praise God now and forever. Praise God.
