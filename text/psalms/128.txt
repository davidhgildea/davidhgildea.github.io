Psalms 128:1 Happy is everyone who respects God and follows His paths.
Psalms 128:2 You will enjoy the work of your hands: you will be happy, and things will go well for you.
Psalms 128:3 Your wife will be like a vine full of fruit next to your house; your children will be like young olive trees around your table.
Psalms 128:4 See, this is how the man who respects God will be blessed.
Psalms 128:5 God will bless you from Zion; and you will enjoy Jerusalem's goodness all your life.
Psalms 128:6 Yes, you will see your grandchildren, and there will be peace in Israel.
