Psalms 129:1 They have troubled me often since I was young, let Israel say now.
Psalms 129:2 They have troubled me often since I was young, but they have not beaten me.
Psalms 129:3 People plowed on my back; they made their cuts long.
Psalms 129:4 God is good: he has broken the ropes of the evil ones.
Psalms 129:5 Let all who hate Zion be confused and driven away.
Psalms 129:6 Let them be like the grass on the rooftops, which dries up before it can grow.
Psalms 129:7 The mower doesn't fill his hand, nor does the one who ties bundles fill his arms.
Psalms 129:8 Those who pass by don't say, "God's blessing be on you; we bless you in God's name."
