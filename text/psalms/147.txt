Psalms 147:1 Praise God: because it is good to sing to our God; it is enjoyable; and praise looks good.
Psalms 147:2 God builds up Jerusalem; he brings together Israel's scattered people.
Psalms 147:3 He heals those with a broken heart, and wraps their wounds.
Psalms 147:4 He counts the number of the stars; he calls each one by its name.
Psalms 147:5 Our God is great and very powerful; his wisdom has no end.
Psalms 147:6 God lifts up the humble; he throws the wicked down to the ground.
Psalms 147:7 Sing to God with thanks; play the harp to praise our God.
Psalms 147:8 God covers the sky with clouds, gets rain ready for the earth, and makes grass grow on the mountains.
Psalms 147:9 He gives food to the animals and feeds the crying young ravens.
Psalms 147:10 God does not enjoy the power of the horse; he does not like the strength of a person's legs.
Psalms 147:11 God finds joy in those who respect him and in those who trust in his kindness.
Psalms 147:12 Praise God, O Jerusalem; praise your God, O Zion.
Psalms 147:13 God has made your city gates strong; He has blessed your children inside your walls.
Psalms 147:14 He brings peace to your land and gives you the best wheat.
Psalms 147:15 He sends out his command to the earth: his message spreads very fast.
Psalms 147:16 He gives snow like wool; he spreads the frost like ashes.
Psalms 147:17 He throws his ice like small pieces: who can withstand his cold?
Psalms 147:18 He sends out his word and melts them; he makes his wind blow, and the waters flow.
Psalms 147:19 He shows his words to Jacob, his laws and decisions to Israel.
Psalms 147:20 God has not treated any other nation like this; they have not understood his laws. Praise God.
