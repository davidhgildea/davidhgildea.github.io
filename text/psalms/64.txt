Psalms 64:1 Listen to me, God, when I pray; keep me safe from my enemies' fear.
Psalms 64:2 Protect me from the hidden plans of the evil; from the rebellion of those who do wrong.
Psalms 64:3 They sharpen their tongues like swords and aim their words like arrows.
Psalms 64:4 They shoot from hiding at the innocent; they shoot suddenly and are not afraid.
Psalms 64:5 They boost each other in a bad plan: they talk about setting traps secretly; they say, Who will see us?
Psalms 64:6 They look for sins; they carry out a thorough search: everyone's secret thoughts and heart are hidden deep.
Psalms 64:7 God will shoot them with an arrow; they will be quickly wounded.
Psalms 64:8 They will be trapped by their own words, and everyone who sees them will run away.
Psalms 64:9 Everyone will be afraid and will tell about what God has done; they will think carefully about his actions.
Psalms 64:10 Good people will be happy with God, trust in him, and all who are true in heart will celebrate.
