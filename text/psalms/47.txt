Psalms 47:1 Clap your hands, everyone; shout to God with happy voices.
Psalms 47:2 God is supreme and awesome; he is a mighty ruler over the whole earth.
Psalms 47:3 He will bring the people under our control, and the nations under our feet.
Psalms 47:4 God will pick our land for us, the best land of Jacob whom He loved. Pause.
Psalms 47:5 God has ascended with cheers; God with the blast of a trumpet.
Psalms 47:6 Praise God, praise him; sing to our King, praise him.
Psalms 47:7 God is the king of the whole earth: sing praises with insight.
Psalms 47:8 God rules over the nations; God sits on His holy throne.
Psalms 47:9 The leaders of the nations come together, including the people who worship the God of Abraham: all the defenders of the world are under God's power: He is highly honored.
