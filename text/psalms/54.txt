Psalms 54:1 God, save me because of your name and decide about me with your power.
Psalms 54:2 Listen to my prayer, God; pay attention to what I say.
Psalms 54:3 Foreigners have attacked me, and bullies chase my life; they do not consider God. Pause.
Psalms 54:4 Look, God helps me; God supports those who keep me safe.
Psalms 54:5 God will pay back my enemies with evil and destroy them because you are truthful.
Psalms 54:6 I will gladly offer sacrifices to you: I will praise your name, God, because it is good.
Psalms 54:7 God has saved me from all my troubles, and I have seen my enemies get what they deserved.
