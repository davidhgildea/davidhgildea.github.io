Psalms 3:1 God, so many are making trouble for me! Many are turning against me.
Psalms 3:2 Many say about me, "God won't save him." (Pause)
Psalms 3:3 But you, God, are a protector for me; my honor, and the one who lifts my head high.
Psalms 3:4 I called to God with my voice, and He listened to me from His holy mountain. Selah.
Psalms 3:5 I lay down and slept; I woke up because God supported me.
Psalms 3:6 I will not fear thousands of people surrounding me, who are against me.
Psalms 3:7 Get up, God; save me, my God: because you have hit all my enemies on the cheek; you have broken the teeth of the wicked.
Psalms 3:8 God gives salvation; your blessing rests on your people. Pause.
