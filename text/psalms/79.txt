Psalms 79:1 God, foreigners have entered your land; they have made your holy temple dirty; they have turned Jerusalem into ruins.
Psalms 79:2 They gave your servants' dead bodies as food to the birds of the sky, and your holy people's flesh to the earth's animals.
Psalms 79:3 They poured out their blood like water all around Jerusalem, and no one was there to bury them.
Psalms 79:4 We have become a disgrace to our neighbors, mocked and laughed at by those around us.
Psalms 79:5 How long, God? Will you be angry always? Will your jealousy burn like fire?
Psalms 79:6 Show your anger to the nations that don't know you, and to the kingdoms that don't call on your name.
Psalms 79:7 They have destroyed Jacob and ruined his home.
Psalms 79:8 God, don't remember our past sins. Quickly show us your compassion, because we are very weak.
Psalms 79:9 Help us, God who saves us, for the sake of your name's glory; rescue us and take away our sins for your name's sake.
Psalms 79:10 Why should other nations ask, "Where is their God?" Let it be known to these nations that you avenge the spilled blood of your servants.
Psalms 79:11 Let the groans of the captive reach you; save those sentenced to die by your great power.
Psalms 79:12 Give back to our neighbors seven times the insults they have given you, God.
Psalms 79:13 We, your people and sheep of your pasture, will always thank you; we will praise you for all generations.
