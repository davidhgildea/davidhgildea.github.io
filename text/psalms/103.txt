Psalms 103:1 Praise God, O my soul, and everything in me, praise his holy name.
Psalms 103:2 Praise God, my soul, and don't forget all the good things he does.
Psalms 103:3 God forgives all your sins; God heals all your sicknesses.
Psalms 103:4 God saves your life from destruction; God blesses you with kindness and compassion.
Psalms 103:5 God fills your hunger with good things, making you feel young again like an eagle.
Psalms 103:6 God carries out what is right and fair for all who are mistreated.
Psalms 103:7 He showed his ways to Moses, his deeds to the Israelites.
Psalms 103:8 God is kind and good, slow to get angry, and full of mercy.
Psalms 103:9 God won't always scold us, nor stay angry forever.
Psalms 103:10 God has not treated us as our sins deserve; nor has He repaid us based on our wrongdoings.
Psalms 103:11 Just as the sky is high above the ground, so is God's kindness to those who respect Him.
Psalms 103:12 Just as the east is far from the west, so God has taken our wrongs far away from us.
Psalms 103:13 Just as a father shows kindness to his children, so God shows kindness to those who respect him.
Psalms 103:14 God knows how we are made; He remembers that we are like dust.
Psalms 103:15 A person's life is like grass: they grow like a field flower.
Psalms 103:16 The wind blows over it, and it disappears; and its place will not recognize it anymore.
Psalms 103:17 God's kindness lasts forever for those who respect Him, and His goodness extends to their grandchildren.
Psalms 103:18 Those who follow God's promise and remember to obey his rules.
Psalms 103:19 God has set up his throne in the sky; and his kingdom controls everything.
Psalms 103:20 Praise God, you strong angels who obey his commands and listen to what he says.
Psalms 103:21 Praise God, all his armies; you who serve him and do what he wants.
Psalms 103:22 Praise God, all his creations everywhere he rules: praise God, my inner self.
