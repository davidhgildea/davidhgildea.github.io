Psalms 21:1 The king will be happy because of your power, God; he will be very glad because you save him!
Psalms 21:2 You have granted him what his heart wanted, and you have not denied what he asked with his lips. Pause.
Psalms 21:3 You go before him with good blessings; you place a crown of pure gold on his head.
Psalms 21:4 He asked you for life, and you gave it to him, a long life that lasts forever and ever.
Psalms 21:5 God's glory is great because of your saving help: you have given him honor and greatness.
Psalms 21:6 You have made him forever blessed; you have filled him with joy by your presence.
Psalms 21:7 The king trusts in God, and with the kindness of the Most High, he will not be shaken.
Psalms 21:8 Your hand will discover all your enemies: your strong hand will find those who hate you.
Psalms 21:9 You will make them like a blazing furnace when you are angry: God will consume them in his fury, and the fire will eat them up.
Psalms 21:10 You will destroy their fruit from the earth and their offspring among people.
Psalms 21:11 They planned to harm you: they thought of a wicked plan, which they cannot carry out.
Psalms 21:12 So you will make them run away when you prepare to shoot your arrows at them.
Psalms 21:13 Let yourself be lifted up, God, by your own power; then we will sing and praise your strength.
