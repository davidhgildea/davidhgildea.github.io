Psalms 74:1 God, why have you rejected us forever? Why is your anger burning against the sheep of your field?
Psalms 74:2 Remember your people whom you long ago bought; the tribe you saved, which is your heritage; this Mount Zion where you live.
Psalms 74:3 Walk toward the endless ruins; see all the harm the enemy has done in the holy place.
Psalms 74:4 Your enemies shout in your meeting places; they put up their flags as symbols.
Psalms 74:5 A man was known for how much he used axes on large trees.
Psalms 74:6 But now they smash its carved art all at once with axes and hammers.
Psalms 74:7 They have thrown fire into your holy place; they have polluted it by tearing down where your name lives to the ground.
Psalms 74:8 In their minds, they planned, "Let's wipe them out as one group; they have burned down all of God's meeting places in the country."
Psalms 74:9 We no longer see our signs; there is no prophet left, and no one among us knows how long this will last.
Psalms 74:10 God, how long will the enemy insult us? Will the foe disrespect your name forever?
Psalms 74:11 Why do you pull back your hand, even your strong hand? Take it out from inside your coat.
Psalms 74:12 God has always been my King, saving people all over the world.
Psalms 74:13 You split the sea with your power; you crushed the sea monsters' heads in the waters.
Psalms 74:14 You crushed the heads of the sea monster and gave it as food to the people living in the desert.
Psalms 74:15 You split open fountains and streams; you dried up powerful rivers.
Psalms 74:16 The day is yours, the night is also yours; you have made the light and the sun.
Psalms 74:17 You have set all the earth's boundaries: you have made summer and winter.
Psalms 74:18 Remember that the enemy has insulted you, God, and that the foolish people have disrespected your name.
Psalms 74:19 Do not give your gentle one's life to many evil people; never forget your poor people's group.
Psalms 74:20 Pay attention to the agreement; the world's dark places are full of cruel homes.
Psalms 74:21 God, don't let the oppressed go back feeling ashamed; let the poor and needy praise your name.
Psalms 74:22 Get up, God, defend your own case: Remember how the foolish person insults you every day.
Psalms 74:23 Do not ignore the voice of your enemies: the noise of those who oppose you grows stronger all the time.
