Psalms 34:1 I will always bless God; I will constantly praise him with my words.
Psalms 34:2 I will proudly speak of God; the humble will hear and be happy.
Psalms 34:3 Let's praise God together and lift up his name.
Psalms 34:4 I looked for God, and he listened to me, and saved me from all my worries.
Psalms 34:5 They looked at him and found brightness; their faces showed no shame.
Psalms 34:6 This man in need cried out, and God heard him, and rescued him from all his troubles.
Psalms 34:7 God's angel camps around those who respect him and saves them.
Psalms 34:8 Try and discover that God is good: happy is the person who trusts in Him.
Psalms 34:9 Respect God, all you who are his holy people, because those who respect him will have everything they need.
Psalms 34:10 Young lions may go hungry, but those who look for God will not lack anything good.
Psalms 34:11 Come, children, listen to me: I will teach you to reverence God.
Psalms 34:12 Who is the person who wants a long life to enjoy good things?
Psalms 34:13 Control your tongue from evil, and keep your lips from speaking deceit.
Psalms 34:14 Turn away from bad things, and do good things; look for peace, and keep trying to get it.
Psalms 34:15 God watches over good people, and he listens to their calls for help.
Psalms 34:16 God is against those who do evil, to remove any memory of them from the earth.
Psalms 34:17 Good people cry out, and God hears them, and saves them from all their troubles.
Psalms 34:18 God is close to those with a broken heart and saves those with a humble spirit.
Psalms 34:19 Righteous people have many troubles, but God saves them from every one.
Psalms 34:20 He protects all his bones; not even one is broken.
Psalms 34:21 Wrongdoing will kill the bad people; those who dislike good people will end up alone.
Psalms 34:22 God saves the souls of his servants, and none who trust in him will be abandoned.
