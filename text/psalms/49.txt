Psalms 49:1 Listen to this, everyone; pay attention, all who live in the world:
Psalms 49:2 Everyone, both unimportant and important, both rich and poor, together.
Psalms 49:3 I will speak wise words; my heart will think deeply with understanding.
Psalms 49:4 I will listen carefully to a story; I will explain my mysterious idea with music on the harp.
Psalms 49:5 Why should I be afraid on bad days, when the wrongs I've done surround me?
Psalms 49:6 Those who trust in their money and brag about how much they have;
Psalms 49:7 No one can save their brother by paying a ransom to God for him.
Psalms 49:8 To save a life is costly, and it never stops.
Psalms 49:9 He should live forever and not decay.
Psalms 49:10 He sees that wise people die, just like fools and senseless people, and they leave their riches to others.
Psalms 49:11 They think their homes will last forever and their places will be there for all time; they name their lands after themselves.
Psalms 49:12 Even though a person is respected, they do not last; they are like the animals that die.
Psalms 49:13 Their path is foolish, but their descendants praise their words. Pause.
Psalms 49:14 They are put in the grave like sheep; death will eat them up; in the morning, the good will rule over them; their beauty will fade away in the grave, far from their home.
Psalms 49:15 God will rescue my life from the grip of death because he will take me in.
Psalms 49:16 Don't be afraid when someone becomes rich, when their house gains more wealth.
Psalms 49:17 When he dies, he takes nothing with him; his fame does not follow him.
Psalms 49:18 While he was alive, he praised himself; and people will compliment you if you are good to yourself.
Psalms 49:19 He will join his ancestors in death; they will never again see the light of life.
Psalms 49:20 A person with honor, who lacks understanding, is like animals that die.
