Psalms 94:1 God, who owns revenge, show yourself.
Psalms 94:2 Rise up, you judge of the earth: give the proud what they deserve.
Psalms 94:3 God, how long will the wicked win?
Psalms 94:4 How long will they keep on speaking harsh words and all the wrongdoers brag about themselves?
Psalms 94:5 They crush your people, God, and harm your cherished ones.
Psalms 94:6 They kill the widow and the foreigner, and they murder the orphan.
Psalms 94:7 They say, God won't see, and the God of Jacob won't pay attention.
Psalms 94:8 Listen, you senseless people: and you fools, when will you become wise?
Psalms 94:9 The one who made the ear, can't he hear? The one who created the eye, can't he see?
Psalms 94:10 The one who punishes nations, won't he correct? The one who teaches people knowledge, won't he know?
Psalms 94:11 God knows what people think, and that it is meaningless.
Psalms 94:12 Happy is the person you correct, God, and teach from your law.
Psalms 94:13 So you can give him peace from hard times until a trap is made for the evil.
Psalms 94:14 God will not reject His people or abandon His inheritance.
Psalms 94:15 Justice will come back to what is right, and all the honest-hearted will support it.
Psalms 94:16 Who will stand up for me against those who do evil? Or who will stand up for me against those who commit wrongs?
Psalms 94:17 If God hadn't helped me, I would have been very close to complete silence.
Psalms 94:18 When I said, "I'm slipping," your kindness, God, supported me.
Psalms 94:19 When I have many worries, your comforts make my soul happy.
Psalms 94:20 Can a throne of evil be your partner, making trouble through its laws?
Psalms 94:21 They join together against the good person and blame the innocent.
Psalms 94:22 God is my protector, and He is the strong rock where I am safe.
Psalms 94:23 He will make them suffer for their sins and will completely destroy them because of their evil; yes, our God will wipe them out.
