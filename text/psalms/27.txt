Psalms 27:1 God is my light and my rescue; who should I fear? God is the power of my life; who should I be scared of?
Psalms 27:2 When the bad people, my enemies, attacked me to destroy me, they tripped and fell.
Psalms 27:3 Even if an army surrounds me, I won't be afraid; even if I am threatened by war, I will remain confident.
Psalms 27:4 I want one thing from God and I will look for it: to live in God's house all my life, to see how beautiful God is, and to learn in his holy place.
Psalms 27:5 When trouble comes, God will protect me in His shelter; He will keep me safe in His tent and lift me onto a rock.
Psalms 27:6 Now I will hold my head high above my enemies around me. So I will give joyful sacrifices in his tent; I will sing and praise God.
Psalms 27:7 Listen, God, when I call out loud: be kind to me and respond to me.
Psalms 27:8 When you said, "Look for me," my heart responded, "I will look for you, God."
Psalms 27:9 Don't hide from me; don't turn me away in anger. You have been my help; don't leave or abandon me, God who saves me.
Psalms 27:10 When my father and my mother leave me, then God will take care of me.
Psalms 27:11 Teach me your way, God, and guide me on a clear path because of my enemies.
Psalms 27:12 Do not give me up to my enemies' wishes; false witnesses have stood against me, speaking cruelty.
Psalms 27:13 I would have lost hope if I didn't believe I would see God's goodness while I'm alive.
Psalms 27:14 Wait for God; be brave, and He will make your heart strong; I say, wait for God.
