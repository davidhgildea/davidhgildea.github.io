Psalms 24:1 The earth belongs to God, and everything in it; the world, and everyone who lives in it.
Psalms 24:2 He founded the earth on the seas and set it firm on the waters.
Psalms 24:3 Who will go up to God's hill? Or who will stand in his sacred place?
Psalms 24:4 The person with clean hands and a pure heart, who has not chased after worthless things or lied under oath.
Psalms 24:5 He will be blessed by God and given righteousness by the God who saves him.
Psalms 24:6 These people seek God; they look for your presence, O Jacob. Pause.
Psalms 24:7 Raise your heads, you gates; open up, you ageless doors, so the glorious King can enter.
Psalms 24:8 Who is this glorious king? God, strong and powerful, God mighty in war.
Psalms 24:9 Raise your heads, you gates; lift up, you ancient doors, so the glorious King can enter.
Psalms 24:10 Who is this glorious king? God, the ruler of all, is the glorious king. Pause and think about this.
