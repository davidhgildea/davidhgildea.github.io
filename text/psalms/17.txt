Psalms 17:1 Listen to me, God, hear my cry, pay attention to my prayer that comes from honest lips.
Psalms 17:2 Let your decision come from your presence; let your eyes see what is fair.
Psalms 17:3 You have tested my heart; you have visited me at night; you have examined me and will find no wrong; I have decided that my mouth will not sin.
Psalms 17:4 About what people do, following what you have said, I have stayed away from the harmful ways.
Psalms 17:5 Guide my steps on your paths, so I don't slip.
Psalms 17:6 I have called to you, because you will listen to me, God: turn your ear to me, and listen to what I say.
Psalms 17:7 Show your wonderful love, O God, who saves with your right hand those who trust in you from their enemies.
Psalms 17:8 Protect me like you would your most cherished possession; hide me in the safety of your wings.
Psalms 17:9 Save me from the wicked who attack me, from my deadly enemies who surround me.
Psalms 17:10 They are surrounded by their own fat; they speak with proud mouths.
Psalms 17:11 They have surrounded us wherever we go. They watch us closely, looking to the ground.
Psalms 17:12 Like a lion hungry for its catch, and like a young lion hiding and waiting.
Psalms 17:13 Stand up, God, let him be defeated and pushed down: save me from the evil one who is your weapon.
Psalms 17:14 You save me from people, God, from worldly people who get their share in this life, whose stomach you fill with your treasures: they have many children and leave what's left to their little ones.
Psalms 17:15 I will see your face in fairness; I will be content when I wake up to be like you.
