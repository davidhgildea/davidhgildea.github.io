Psalms 66:1 Celebrate happily before God, all you lands.
Psalms 66:2 Sing to honor God's name; make your praises of him wonderful.
Psalms 66:3 Tell God, "How awesome are your deeds! Because of your great power, your enemies will come to you in submission."
Psalms 66:4 Everyone on earth will worship you and sing to you; they will sing about your name. Pause.
Psalms 66:5 Come and see what God has done; he is awesome in his actions among people.
Psalms 66:6 He turned the sea into dry land; they walked through the river on foot; there we were happy in him.
Psalms 66:7 He rules forever by his power; his eyes watch the nations: rebellious people should not lift themselves up. Pause.
Psalms 66:8 Praise our God, everyone, and let his praise be heard.
Psalms 66:9 God keeps us alive and does not let us stumble.
Psalms 66:10 God, you have tested us; you have refined us like silver.
Psalms 66:11 You caught us in a trap; you put a heavy burden on our backs.
Psalms 66:12 You let people walk over us; we went through fire and water, but you brought us to a place of abundance.
Psalms 66:13 I will enter your house with offerings; I will fulfill my promises to you.
Psalms 66:14 What I said with my lips and spoke with my mouth when I was in trouble.
Psalms 66:15 I will give you burnt offerings of young animals and the scent of rams; I will present bulls and goats. Pause and think about that.
Psalms 66:16 Come and listen, all who respect God, and I will tell what he did for my life.
Psalms 66:17 I called out to God with my mouth, and I praised him with my tongue.
Psalms 66:18 If I keep sin in my heart, God will not listen to me.
Psalms 66:19 God truly heard me; he listened to my prayer.
Psalms 66:20 Praise God, who did not ignore my prayer or withdraw his kindness from me.
