Psalms 136:1 Give thanks to God, for He is good; His love lasts forever.
Psalms 136:2 Give thanks to the God of all: for his kindness lasts forever.
Psalms 136:3 Give thanks to the God of gods: for his kindness lasts forever.
Psalms 136:4 To the one who alone does great miracles: because his kindness lasts forever.
Psalms 136:5 He made the skies using wisdom: His kindness lasts forever.
Psalms 136:6 To the one who spread the earth over the waters: because his kindness lasts forever.
Psalms 136:7 He made the big lights: His kindness lasts forever.
Psalms 136:8 The sun rules by day: for his kindness lasts forever.
Psalms 136:9 The moon and stars govern the night; God's kindness lasts forever.
Psalms 136:10 To the one who struck down Egypt's firstborn: because his kindness lasts forever.
Psalms 136:11 God led Israel out from among them because his kindness lasts forever.
Psalms 136:12 With a powerful hand and an extended arm: because his kindness lasts forever.
Psalms 136:13 He split the Red Sea in two: His love lasts forever.
Psalms 136:14 God led Israel through it because his kindness lasts forever.
Psalms 136:15 God overthrew Pharaoh and his army in the Red Sea because His kindness lasts forever.
Psalms 136:16 To the one who guided his people through the desert: because his kindness lasts forever.
Psalms 136:17 He struck down mighty kings: for his love lasts forever.
Psalms 136:18 He killed great kings: because His kindness lasts forever:
Psalms 136:19 Give thanks to God for defeating Sihon, king of the Amorites; His kindness lasts forever.
Psalms 136:20 And Og, the king of Bashan, because God's kindness lasts forever.
Psalms 136:21 God gave their land as a heritage because his mercy lasts forever.
Psalms 136:22 It's a gift to Israel, his servant, because God's kindness lasts forever.
Psalms 136:23 God remembered us when we were helpless, because his kindness lasts forever.
Psalms 136:24 God has saved us from our enemies because his kindness lasts forever.
Psalms 136:25 God gives food to all creatures; His kindness lasts forever.
Psalms 136:26 Give thanks to the God of heaven because his kindness lasts forever.
