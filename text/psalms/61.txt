Psalms 61:1 Listen to my cry, God; pay attention to my prayer.
Psalms 61:2 From the farthest part of the earth, I will call to you when I am feeling too weak: take me to the place that is stronger than I am.
Psalms 61:3 You have been a safe place for me and a strong tower against the enemy.
Psalms 61:4 I will live in your tent forever: I will find shelter under your wings. Pause.
Psalms 61:5 God, you have heard my promises; you have given me the inheritance of those who respect your name.
Psalms 61:6 You will extend the king's life, and his years will be like many generations.
Psalms 61:7 He will live with God forever: please make ready kindness and honesty to protect him.
Psalms 61:8 I will praise your name forever and keep my promises every day.
