Psalms 29:1 Give to God, you powerful ones, give God glory and power.
Psalms 29:2 Give God the honor that belongs to His name; worship God in the beauty of being holy.
Psalms 29:3 God's voice is over the waters; the glorious God thunders; God is over the vast waters.
Psalms 29:4 God's voice is strong; God's voice is majestic.
Psalms 29:5 God's voice breaks the cedars; yes, God breaks the cedars of Lebanon.
Psalms 29:6 He makes them leap like a calf; Lebanon and Sirion like a young wild ox.
Psalms 29:7 God's voice splits the flames of fire.
Psalms 29:8 God's voice shakes the desert; God shakes the Kadesh desert.
Psalms 29:9 God's voice makes the deer give birth and uncovers the forests; in his temple, everyone talks about his greatness.
Psalms 29:10 God sits above the flood; yes, God reigns as King forever.
Psalms 29:11 God will give his people strength; God will bless his people with peace.
