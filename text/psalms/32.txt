Psalms 32:1 Happy is the person whose wrongdoings are forgiven, and whose sins are hidden.
Psalms 32:2 Happy is the person whom God does not blame for wrongdoing, and who is honest in their heart.
Psalms 32:3 When I stayed quiet, my bones grew weak because I groaned all day long.
Psalms 32:4 Day and night your hand was hard on me: my energy became like the dryness of summer.
Psalms 32:5 I admit my sin to you, and I haven't hidden my wrongs. I decided to tell you about my sins, and you forgave me. Selah.
Psalms 32:6 Every good person will pray to you when you can be found. In times of great floods, they will not reach him.
Psalms 32:7 You are my hiding place; you will protect me from trouble; you will surround me with songs of rescue. Pause.
Psalms 32:8 I will teach you and show you the way to go; I will guide you with my eye.
Psalms 32:9 Do not be like the horse or mule that have no understanding and need a bit and bridle to be controlled, or they will not come to you.
Psalms 32:10 The wicked will have many troubles, but if you trust in God, you will be surrounded by mercy.
Psalms 32:11 Be happy in God, and be joyful, you good people: and shout happily, all you who are honest in heart.
