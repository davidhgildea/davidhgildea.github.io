Psalms 57:1 Have mercy on me, God, have mercy on me, for my soul trusts in you; yes, under the protection of your wings I will find shelter until these disasters have passed.
Psalms 57:2 I will call out to God above all; to God who does everything for me.
Psalms 57:3 God will send help from heaven to save me from my enemies' insults. God will show his kindness and truth.
Psalms 57:4 My soul is with lions; I'm surrounded by people burning with anger, men whose teeth are like spears and arrows, and whose tongues are as sharp as swords.
Psalms 57:5 God, be honored high above the skies; let your greatness be over the whole earth.
Psalms 57:6 They set a trap for me; I am deeply troubled. They dug a hole in my path, but they have fallen into it themselves. Pause.
Psalms 57:7 My heart is steady, God, my heart is steady; I will sing and praise you.
Psalms 57:8 Wake up, my honor; wake up, stringed instruments and harp: I will wake up early.
Psalms 57:9 I will praise you, God, among the people; I will sing to you among the nations.
Psalms 57:10 Your kindness reaches to the sky, and your truth goes up to the clouds.
Psalms 57:11 God, be lifted high above the heavens; let your glory be over all the earth.
