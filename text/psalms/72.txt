Psalms 72:1 God, give the king your justice, and your righteousness to the king's son.
Psalms 72:2 He will judge your people fairly and give fair decisions for your poor.
Psalms 72:3 The mountains will give peace to the people, and the small hills will do it by being fair.
Psalms 72:4 He will judge the poor fairly, save the children of the needy, and crush the oppressor.
Psalms 72:5 People will respect you as long as the sun and moon last, for all time to come.
Psalms 72:6 He will come down like rain on cut grass, like showers that soak the earth.
Psalms 72:7 During his time, good people will thrive; and there will be plenty of peace as long as the moon lasts.
Psalms 72:8 He will rule from one sea to another, and from the river to the ends of the earth.
Psalms 72:9 People living in the wild will kneel before him, and his enemies will lick the ground.
Psalms 72:10 The kings of Tarshish and the islands will bring gifts; the kings of Sheba and Seba will present offerings.
Psalms 72:11 Yes, all kings will bow down to him; all countries will serve him.
Psalms 72:12 God will save the needy when they call out; the poor too, and those with no one to help them.
Psalms 72:13 He will help the poor and needy and save the lives of those in need.
Psalms 72:14 He will save them from lies and harm, and their lives will be valuable to him.
Psalms 72:15 He will live, and he will receive gold from Sheba; people will always pray for him, and he will be praised every day.
Psalms 72:16 A small amount of grain will grow in the land, even on the mountains; its produce will sway like the trees of Lebanon, and the city people will thrive like the grass on the ground.
Psalms 72:17 His name will last forever; it will continue as long as the sun shines. People will be blessed through him; all countries will praise him.
Psalms 72:18 Praise be to God, the God of Israel, who alone does wonderful things.
Psalms 72:19 May his wonderful name be blessed forever; may the whole earth be full of his glory. Amen, Amen.
Psalms 72:20 David, Jesse's son, has finished his prayers.
