Psalms 118:1 Give thanks to God; for he is good: his kindness lasts forever.
Psalms 118:2 Let Israel say now, God's kindness lasts forever.
Psalms 118:3 Let Aaron's family now say, God's kindness lasts forever.
Psalms 118:4 Let those who respect God say, his kindness lasts forever.
Psalms 118:5 I called to God when I was in trouble; God answered me and put me in a spacious place.
Psalms 118:6 God is with me; I will not be afraid: what can anyone do to me?
Psalms 118:7 God supports me along with those who help me; therefore, I will see my wishes fulfilled against those who hate me.
Psalms 118:8 It is better to trust in God than to rely on people.
Psalms 118:9 It is better to trust in God than to rely on leaders.
Psalms 118:10 All countries surrounded me, but with God's help, I will defeat them.
Psalms 118:11 They surrounded me; yes, they surrounded me: but in God's name, I will defeat them.
Psalms 118:12 They surrounded me like bees; they were put out like a thorn fire: because in the name of God I will defeat them.
Psalms 118:13 You pushed me hard to make me fall, but God helped me.
Psalms 118:14 God is my strength and song, and has become my salvation.
Psalms 118:15 People are celebrating and being saved in the homes of the good; God's powerful hand does great things.
Psalms 118:16 God's right hand is lifted high; God's right hand does mighty things.
Psalms 118:17 I will not die, but live, and tell of what God has done.
Psalms 118:18 God has punished me a lot, but he hasn't let me die.
Psalms 118:19 Open the right way for me: I will enter and thank God.
Psalms 118:20 This gate belongs to God, and the good people will go in through it.
Psalms 118:21 I will praise you because you have heard me and have become my salvation.
Psalms 118:22 The stone that the builders rejected has become the most important cornerstone.
Psalms 118:23 This is what God has done; it is wonderful to us.
Psalms 118:24 This is the day that God has made; we will celebrate and be happy in it.
Psalms 118:25 Please save us now, I ask you, God; God, I ask you, bring us success now.
Psalms 118:26 Happy is the one who comes in God's name: we have given you blessings from God's house.
Psalms 118:27 God is Lord, who has given us light; tie the offering with ropes, all the way to the altar's corners.
Psalms 118:28 You are my God, and I will praise you; you are my God, I will lift you up.
Psalms 118:29 Give thanks to God, for he is good; his kindness lasts forever.
