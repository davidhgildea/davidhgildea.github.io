Psalms 89:1 I will sing about God's kindness forever; with my words, I will tell everyone about your faithfulness through all times.
Psalms 89:2 I have said that kindness will last forever; you will set your faithfulness firmly in the skies.
Psalms 89:3 I have made a promise with my chosen, I have sworn to David my servant.
Psalms 89:4 I will make your descendants last forever, and build your kingdom for all generations. Pause and think.
Psalms 89:5 The sky will praise your miracles, God: your faithfulness too, among the holy people.
Psalms 89:6 Who in heaven can compare to God? Who among the powerful is like God?
Psalms 89:7 God should be greatly respected by his holy people and revered by all who are around him.
Psalms 89:8 God of all, who is as mighty as you? Who matches your faithfulness?
Psalms 89:9 You control the furious sea: when its waves rise, you calm them.
Psalms 89:10 You have shattered Rahab like someone killed; you have spread out your enemies with your powerful arm.
Psalms 89:11 The skies and the earth belong to you; you created the world and everything in it.
Psalms 89:12 You created the north and the south: Tabor and Hermon will be happy in your name.
Psalms 89:13 You have a powerful arm; your hand is strong and your right hand is lifted high.
Psalms 89:14 Fairness and right decisions support your rule; kindness and honesty will be ahead of you.
Psalms 89:15 Happy are the people who know the joyful sound: they will walk, God, in the light of your presence.
Psalms 89:16 They will be happy all day because of you, God, and your goodness will lift them up.
Psalms 89:17 For you are the source of their power; and by your kindness, we are made strong.
Psalms 89:18 God is our protector; and the Holy One of Israel is our king.
Psalms 89:19 You spoke in a vision to your holy one and said, I have given help to someone strong; I have raised up a chosen one from the people.
Psalms 89:20 I have chosen David as my servant; I have anointed him with my sacred oil.
Psalms 89:21 I will support him with my hand; my arm will also make him strong.
Psalms 89:22 The enemy will not force him to pay; nor will the wicked person hurt him.
Psalms 89:23 I will defeat his enemies in front of him and trouble those who hate him.
Psalms 89:24 My loyalty and kindness will stay with him, and through my power, his strength will be lifted up.
Psalms 89:25 I will give him power over the sea and control over the rivers.
Psalms 89:26 He will call out to me, 'You are my father, my God, and the rock who saves me.'
Psalms 89:27 I will also make him my most important child, greater than all the rulers of the world.
Psalms 89:28 I will always show him kindness, and my agreement with him will always be strong.
Psalms 89:29 I will make his descendants last forever, and his throne will endure as long as the sky.
Psalms 89:30 If his children ignore my rules and do not follow my decisions;
Psalms 89:31 If they do not follow my laws and do not obey my commands;
Psalms 89:32 I will punish their sins with a rod and their wrongdoings with whipping.
Psalms 89:33 I will not completely remove my kindness from him, nor let my faithfulness end.
Psalms 89:34 I will not break my promise or change what I have said.
Psalms 89:35 I have sworn by my holiness that I will not lie to David.
Psalms 89:36 His descendants will last forever, and his rule will be like the sun in my presence.
Psalms 89:37 It will last forever like the moon, and be a reliable witness in the sky. Pause.
Psalms 89:38 But you have rejected and despised, you have been angry with your chosen one.
Psalms 89:39 You have broken the agreement with your servant; you have disrespected his crown by throwing it to the ground.
Psalms 89:40 You have torn down all his fences; you have destroyed his strong defenses.
Psalms 89:41 Everyone who goes by robs him; he's a disgrace to his neighbors.
Psalms 89:42 You gave strength to his foes; you made all his enemies happy.
Psalms 89:43 You have also dulled the edge of his sword, and have not allowed him to stand strong in battle.
Psalms 89:44 You have ended his glory and thrown his throne down to the ground.
Psalms 89:45 You have cut short the days of his youth; you have wrapped him in disgrace. Pause.
Psalms 89:46 How long, God? Will you hide yourself forever? Will your anger keep burning like fire?
Psalms 89:47 Remember how brief my life is; why have you created everyone for no purpose?
Psalms 89:48 Who is the person that will live and not die? Can he save himself from the power of the grave? Pause and think.
Psalms 89:49 God, where are your past acts of love, which you promised to David in your faithfulness?
Psalms 89:50 Remember, God, how your servants are insulted; I carry the insults of many peoples deep in my heart.
Psalms 89:51 Your enemies have insulted you, God; they have insulted the path your chosen one has walked.
Psalms 89:52 May God be praised always. So be it, and so be it.
