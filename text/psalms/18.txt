Psalms 18:1 I will love you, God, my strength.
Psalms 18:2 God is my rock, my fortress, and my rescuer; my God gives me strength and I trust in Him; He protects me, saves me, and is like a high tower to me.
Psalms 18:3 I will call on God, who deserves praise: so I will be saved from my enemies.
Psalms 18:4 Death's pains surrounded me, and the rush of wicked people scared me.
Psalms 18:5 Hell's pains surrounded me; death's traps stopped me.
Psalms 18:6 In my trouble, I called to God and shouted for help. He heard my voice from his home, and my call reached his ears.
Psalms 18:7 The earth shook and quaked; the hill bases moved and shook because God was angry.
Psalms 18:8 Smoke went up from his nose, and fire from his mouth burned everything; it lit up coals.
Psalms 18:9 He bent the sky down and came down; darkness was under his feet.
Psalms 18:10 He rode on a cherub and flew; he soared on the wind's wings.
Psalms 18:11 He made darkness his hiding place; around him were dark waters and heavy clouds in the sky.
Psalms 18:12 As the light shone in front of him, his dark clouds moved away, with hailstones and burning coals.
Psalms 18:13 God thundered in the sky, and the Most High shouted; there were hailstones and coals of fire.
Psalms 18:14 Yes, God shot his arrows and spread them out; he sent lightning and confused them.
Psalms 18:15 The water paths were shown, and the world's bases were uncovered when you, God, scolded, at the puff of your breath.
Psalms 18:16 From above, God sent for me; he rescued me from deep waters.
Psalms 18:17 God saved me from my powerful enemy and from those who hated me, because they were too strong for me.
Psalms 18:18 They stopped me on the day of my trouble, but God was my support.
Psalms 18:19 God led me out into a wide open space; he saved me because he was pleased with me.
Psalms 18:20 God rewarded me for doing right; he paid me back because my hands were clean.
Psalms 18:21 I have followed God's paths and not turned away from my God to do wrong.
Psalms 18:22 I kept all his rules in mind, and I did not ignore his teachings.
Psalms 18:23 I was also honest before God, and I kept myself from doing wrong.
Psalms 18:24 So God rewarded me because of my good behavior and because I kept my hands clean in his view.
Psalms 18:25 You are kind to those who are kind, and you are honest to those who are honest.
Psalms 18:26 You are pure with the pure, and you act tricky with the tricky.
Psalms 18:27 You will save the suffering people but will humble the proud.
Psalms 18:28 You will light my lamp; God will brighten my darkness.
Psalms 18:29 With your help, I have charged through an army; and with my God, I have jumped over a wall.
Psalms 18:30 God's way is perfect. His words are tested and true. He protects all who trust in Him.
Psalms 18:31 Who is God besides the Lord? Or who is a stronghold besides our God?
Psalms 18:32 God gives me strength and makes my path perfect.
Psalms 18:33 God makes my feet as steady as a deer's, and places me on high ground.
Psalms 18:34 God teaches my hands to fight, so that I can break a steel bow with my arms.
Psalms 18:35 You have given me the shield of your salvation; your right hand has supported me, and your kindness has made me strong.
Psalms 18:36 You have made my path wide so I didn't fall.
Psalms 18:37 I chased my enemies and caught them. I did not stop until they were defeated.
Psalms 18:38 I have hurt them so they can't get up; they have fallen beneath my feet.
Psalms 18:39 You have given me strength for the fight; you have made those who attacked me surrender to me.
Psalms 18:40 You have also let me defeat my enemies; so I can destroy those who hate me.
Psalms 18:41 They called for help, but no one saved them; even when they called to God, he did not answer.
Psalms 18:42 I crushed them into dust blown away by the wind; I threw them out like mud in the streets.
Psalms 18:43 You have rescued me from fighting among the people; you have made me the leader of nations; people I did not know will now serve me.
Psalms 18:44 When they hear about me, they will obey me: foreigners will yield to me.
Psalms 18:45 Foreigners will grow weak and be scared from their hidden spots.
Psalms 18:46 God lives; my protector is blessed; let the God who saves me be honored.
Psalms 18:47 God avenges me and makes people submit to me.
Psalms 18:48 God saves me from my enemies; you raise me above my attackers; you save me from violent people.
Psalms 18:49 So I will thank you, God, among the nations, and sing praises to your name.
Psalms 18:50 He gives great rescue to his king and shows kindness to his chosen one, to David and his descendants forever.
