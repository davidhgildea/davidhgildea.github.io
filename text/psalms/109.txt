Psalms 109:1 God of my praise, do not stay silent.
Psalms 109:2 Wicked and deceitful people have opened their mouths against me; they have lied about me.
Psalms 109:3 They surrounded me with hateful words and attacked me for no reason.
Psalms 109:4 Even though I show love, they are my enemies; but I still pray.
Psalms 109:5 They have paid me back bad for good, and hate for my love.
Psalms 109:6 Put an evil person in charge of him, and let Satan be by his side.
Psalms 109:7 When he is judged, let him be found guilty, and may his prayer be counted as sin.
Psalms 109:8 May his time be short; may someone else replace him in his job.
Psalms 109:9 May his children have no father, and his wife be a widow.
Psalms 109:10 May his children wander and beg, always seeking food from ruined places.
Psalms 109:11 Let the thief take everything he owns; and let outsiders take what he has worked for.
Psalms 109:12 Let no one show him kindness, and no one care for his orphaned children.
Psalms 109:13 Let his descendants disappear; and let their name be erased in the next generation.
Psalms 109:14 Let God remember his fathers' wrongs and not erase his mother's sins.
Psalms 109:15 Keep them always before God, so that he may erase the memory of them from the earth.
Psalms 109:16 Because he did not remember to show mercy but chased after the poor and needy, intending to kill those whose hearts were crushed.
Psalms 109:17 Since he liked to curse, let curses come to him; since he did not enjoy blessing, let it be far from him.
Psalms 109:18 He wore curses like clothes, so let them soak into him like water and like oil into his bones.
Psalms 109:19 May it be to him like the clothes he wears and the belt he always has on.
Psalms 109:20 Let this be the payback for my enemies from God, and for those who say bad things about me.
Psalms 109:21 Help me, God, for the sake of your name; since your mercy is good, rescue me.
Psalms 109:22 I am poor and in need, and my heart is hurt inside me.
Psalms 109:23 I have vanished like a shadow when it fades; I am shaken back and forth like a locust.
Psalms 109:24 My knees are weak from not eating, and I've lost weight.
Psalms 109:25 I also became someone they insulted; when they saw me, they shook their heads.
Psalms 109:26 Help me, God my God; save me because of your kindness.
Psalms 109:27 So they will understand this is your work; that you, God, have done it.
Psalms 109:28 Let them curse, but you bless: when they stand up, let them be embarrassed; but let your servant be happy.
Psalms 109:29 Let my enemies be covered with shame and wrap themselves in their confusion like a cloak.
Psalms 109:30 I will praise God a lot with my words; yes, I will praise him in front of many people.
Psalms 109:31 He will stand by the poor to protect them from those who judge them wrongly.
