Psalms 37:1 Don't worry about wrongdoers, and don't be jealous of those who do evil.
Psalms 37:2 They will quickly be chopped down like grass and dry up like green plants.
Psalms 37:3 Trust in God, and do good; then you will live safely in the land, and you will have plenty to eat.
Psalms 37:4 Enjoy being close to God, and he will give you what your heart wants.
Psalms 37:5 Trust your path to God; believe in him, and he will make it happen.
Psalms 37:6 He will make your goodness shine like the light, and your fair decisions like the midday sun.
Psalms 37:7 Trust in God and wait calmly for Him; don't worry about those who succeed in their bad plans.
Psalms 37:8 Stop being angry, leave behind rage; do not worry yourself at all to do wrong.
Psalms 37:9 Those who do wrong will be removed, but those who wait for God will receive the earth.
Psalms 37:10 Soon the wicked will be gone; you will look for them, but they won't be there.
Psalms 37:11 The gentle will inherit the land and enjoy great peace.
Psalms 37:12 The bad person makes plans against the good person and shows anger by grinding his teeth at him.
Psalms 37:13 God will laugh at him because He sees that his time is coming.
Psalms 37:14 Evil people have pulled out their swords and aimed their bows to knock down the poor and needy, and to kill those who live honestly.
Psalms 37:15 Their swords will stab their own hearts, and their bows will snap.
Psalms 37:16 A small amount that a good person has is better than the wealth of many bad people.
Psalms 37:17 The power of the bad will be broken, but God supports the good.
Psalms 37:18 God knows the days of the good people, and their inheritance will last forever.
Psalms 37:19 They will not feel shame when times are bad; and during famine, they will have enough.
Psalms 37:20 But the bad people will die, and God's enemies will vanish like lamb fat in fire; they will disappear like smoke.
Psalms 37:21 The wicked borrow and do not repay, but the good are kind and give.
Psalms 37:22 Those blessed by God will inherit the land; those cursed by Him will be destroyed.
Psalms 37:23 God guides a good person's path and finds joy in their journey.
Psalms 37:24 If he falls, he will not be completely defeated because God supports him with his hand.
Psalms 37:25 I was once young, and now I'm old; but I've never seen a good person abandoned, or their children having to beg for food.
Psalms 37:26 He is always kind and gives to others; and his children are blessed.
Psalms 37:27 Turn away from bad things and do good things; live forever.
Psalms 37:28 God loves fairness and does not abandon his faithful people; they are protected forever, but the descendants of the wicked will be destroyed.
Psalms 37:29 Good people will receive the land and live there always.
Psalms 37:30 The righteous person's mouth speaks wisdom, and their tongue discusses fairness.
Psalms 37:31 God's law is in his heart; he will not slip.
Psalms 37:32 The bad person watches the good person and tries to kill him.
Psalms 37:33 God will not let him be defeated or declare him guilty when he is on trial.
Psalms 37:34 Wait for God, follow His path, and He will raise you up to receive the land; when the wicked are removed, you will see it.
Psalms 37:35 I have seen evil people with much power, growing strong like a healthy tree.
Psalms 37:36 He died, and suddenly he was gone. I looked for him, but he could not be found.
Psalms 37:37 Notice the good person, and see the honest one: for a peaceful end awaits that person.
Psalms 37:38 Wrongdoers will all be destroyed: the future of the wicked will end.
Psalms 37:39 God saves good people; he is their protector when trouble comes.
Psalms 37:40 God will help them and rescue them; He will save them from the wicked because they rely on Him.
