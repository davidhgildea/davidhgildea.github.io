Psalms 104:1 Praise God, my soul. O my God, you are very great; you are dressed in glory and splendor.
Psalms 104:2 You cover yourself with light like clothes: you spread out the skies like a curtain.
Psalms 104:3 He sets the beams for his rooms in the waters. He makes the clouds his carriage. He walks on the wings of the wind.
Psalms 104:4 God makes his angels spirits and his servants like a blazing fire.
Psalms 104:5 Who set the earth's foundations so it would never move.
Psalms 104:6 You covered it with the deep like a garment; the waters stood over the mountains.
Psalms 104:7 At your scolding they ran away; at the sound of your thunder they hurried off.
Psalms 104:8 They climb the mountains; they go down into the valleys to the place you have created for them.
Psalms 104:9 You have set a limit they cannot cross; they cannot come back to cover the earth.
Psalms 104:10 He sends streams into the valleys; they flow between the hills.
Psalms 104:11 They provide water for every animal in the field; the wild donkeys satisfy their thirst.
Psalms 104:12 The birds of the sky will live there, singing in the branches.
Psalms 104:13 He gives water to the hills from his home; the earth has all it needs from what you have done.
Psalms 104:14 God makes grass grow for the cows, and plants for people to use, so they can get food from the ground.
Psalms 104:15 Wine cheers people's hearts, oil makes their faces glow, and bread gives them strength.
Psalms 104:16 God's trees are full of juice; the cedars of Lebanon, which he has planted.
Psalms 104:17 Birds build their nests there; the stork makes its home in the fir trees.
Psalms 104:18 High hills give wild goats safety; rocks give shelter to hyraxes.
Psalms 104:19 God made the moon to mark the seasons; the sun knows when to set.
Psalms 104:20 You make darkness, and it becomes night: when all the forest animals come out.
Psalms 104:21 Young lions roar for their food and look to God for it.
Psalms 104:22 The sun rises, they come together, and lie down in their dens.
Psalms 104:23 A person goes out to work and works until evening.
Psalms 104:24 God, how many are your works! In wisdom, you made them all: the earth is filled with your wealth.
Psalms 104:25 This big, vast sea is home to countless creeping things, both tiny and huge creatures.
Psalms 104:26 Ships sail there; the great sea creature you made plays in the waters.
Psalms 104:27 They all wait for you to give them their food at the right time.
Psalms 104:28 You give to them, and they collect; you open your hand, and they are satisfied with good things.
Psalms 104:29 When you hide your face, they are scared; when you take their breath away, they die and turn back to dust.
Psalms 104:30 You send out your spirit, and they are created; you renew the earth's surface.
Psalms 104:31 God's glory will last forever; God will be happy with his creations.
Psalms 104:32 God looks at the earth, and it shakes; he touches the mountains, and they smoke.
Psalms 104:33 I will sing to God as long as I live: I will praise my God as long as I exist.
Psalms 104:34 I will enjoy thinking about God: I will be happy with God.
Psalms 104:35 Let the sinners vanish from the earth, and the wicked exist no more. Praise God, my soul. Praise God.
