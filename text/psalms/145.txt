Psalms 145:1 I will praise you, my God, the king; and I will bless your name always and forever.
Psalms 145:2 I will bless you every day; I will praise your name forever and ever.
Psalms 145:3 God is great and deserves much praise; no one can understand how great he is.
Psalms 145:4 One generation will tell the next about your works and talk about your powerful deeds.
Psalms 145:5 I will talk about the glorious honor of your majesty and your amazing works.
Psalms 145:6 People will talk about how powerful your amazing acts are, and I will tell others about your greatness.
Psalms 145:7 They will speak often of your great kindness and sing about your fairness.
Psalms 145:8 God is kind and caring; patient and very merciful.
Psalms 145:9 God is good to everyone, and his compassion is on all he has made.
Psalms 145:10 Everything you have made will praise you, God; and your holy people will bless you.
Psalms 145:11 They will talk about the greatness of your kingdom and discuss your power.
Psalms 145:12 To show people his powerful works and the splendid beauty of his kingdom.
Psalms 145:13 Your kingdom lasts forever, and your rule continues through all time.
Psalms 145:14 God supports all who fall and lifts up all who are bent over.
Psalms 145:15 Everyone looks to you, and you give them their food at the right time.
Psalms 145:16 You open your hand and satisfy the needs of every living creature.
Psalms 145:17 God is fair in all he does, and good in all his actions.
Psalms 145:18 God is close to everyone who calls on him, to all who call on him honestly.
Psalms 145:19 He will fulfill the wishes of those who respect him; he will listen to their cries and save them.
Psalms 145:20 God protects everyone who loves Him, but He will destroy all the wicked.
Psalms 145:21 I will praise God with my words; may everyone honor his holy name always and forever.
