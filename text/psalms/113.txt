Psalms 113:1 Praise God. All you who serve God, praise God's name.
Psalms 113:2 God's name is blessed from now on and forever.
Psalms 113:3 From sunrise to sunset, God's name should be praised.
Psalms 113:4 God is greater than all countries, and his greatness is higher than the skies.
Psalms 113:5 Who is like God, our God, who lives on high?
Psalms 113:6 God bows down to look at the things in heaven and on earth.
Psalms 113:7 He lifts the poor from the ground and raises the needy from the dirt.
Psalms 113:8 So he can place him with leaders, with his people's leaders.
Psalms 113:9 God helps the woman with no children to have a family and be happy with her kids. Praise God.
