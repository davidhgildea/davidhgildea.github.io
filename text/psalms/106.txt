Psalms 106:1 Praise God. Give thanks to God, for he is good and his kindness lasts forever.
Psalms 106:2 Who can speak of God's powerful deeds? Who can show all the praise he deserves?
Psalms 106:3 Happy are those who follow what is just, and the one who does what is right all the time.
Psalms 106:4 Remember me, God, with the kindness you show to your people: come to save me.
Psalms 106:5 So I can see the good things for your chosen ones, be happy with your people's joy, and celebrate with your heritage.
Psalms 106:6 We have done wrong like our ancestors, we have acted badly, we have behaved wickedly.
Psalms 106:7 Our ancestors did not understand your miracles in Egypt; they did not remember your many acts of kindness but angered you at the sea, at the Red Sea.
Psalms 106:8 Yet he saved them for his name's sake, to show his great power.
Psalms 106:9 He scolded the Red Sea, and it dried up; so he guided them through the deep parts as if through a desert.
Psalms 106:10 He saved them from the one who hated them and rescued them from their enemy.
Psalms 106:11 The water covered their enemies: not even one survived.
Psalms 106:12 Then they believed what he said; they praised him.
Psalms 106:13 They quickly forgot what he had done; they did not wait for his advice.
Psalms 106:14 They desired too much in the wilderness and tested God in the desert.
Psalms 106:15 He granted their wish, but made their life empty.
Psalms 106:16 They were jealous of Moses in the camp, and of Aaron, God's holy one.
Psalms 106:17 The ground opened up and swallowed Dathan and then closed over Abiram's group.
Psalms 106:18 A fire started among them; the flame consumed the wicked.
Psalms 106:19 They created a calf in Horeb and worshipped the metal statue.
Psalms 106:20 So they turned their honor into the likeness of a cow that eats grass.
Psalms 106:21 They forgot God their savior who did great things in Egypt.
Psalms 106:22 Amazing things in the land of Egypt, and scary events by the Red Sea.
Psalms 106:23 So he said he would end them, but Moses, his chosen one, stood before him to stop his anger, or he would have ended them.
Psalms 106:24 Yes, they looked down on the beautiful land and did not trust what he said.
Psalms 106:25 But they complained in their tents and did not listen to God's voice.
Psalms 106:26 So he raised his hand against them, to knock them down in the desert.
Psalms 106:27 To destroy their children among different countries and spread them out across various lands.
Psalms 106:28 They also followed Baalpeor and ate offerings for the dead.
Psalms 106:29 Their actions angered God, and a plague spread among them.
Psalms 106:30 Phinehas stood up and carried out justice; and so the disease was stopped.
Psalms 106:31 God considered that as true goodness forever for all people to come.
Psalms 106:32 They also made him angry at the Waters of Strife, which caused trouble for Moses because of them.
Psalms 106:33 Because they angered him, he spoke without thinking.
Psalms 106:34 They did not wipe out the nations, which God had told them to do.
Psalms 106:35 But they mixed with other nations and adopted their ways.
Psalms 106:36 They worshiped their idols, which became a trap for them.
Psalms 106:37 Yes, they sacrificed their sons and daughters to demons.
Psalms 106:38 They killed innocent people, including their own sons and daughters, whom they offered to Canaan's idols, and the land was tainted with blood.
Psalms 106:39 They became unclean by what they did and were unfaithful with their own ideas.
Psalms 106:40 God became very angry with his people and came to detest his own chosen ones.
Psalms 106:41 He let other nations defeat them, and their enemies ruled over them.
Psalms 106:42 Their enemies pressured them, and they came under their control.
Psalms 106:43 God saved them many times, but they angered him with their advice and were punished for their sins.
Psalms 106:44 God noticed their suffering when he heard their call for help.
Psalms 106:45 God remembered his promise to them and felt sorry for them because of his great kindness.
Psalms 106:46 He also made all who took them captive feel sorry for them.
Psalms 106:47 Save us, God, and bring us back from among the nations, to thank your holy name and to celebrate your praise.
Psalms 106:48 Let's praise the God of Israel forever and ever; and let everyone say, "Yes." Praise God.
