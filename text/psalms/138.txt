Psalms 138:1 I will praise you with all my heart; I will sing praises to you before other gods.
Psalms 138:2 I will worship facing your holy temple and thank your name for your kindness and truth; because you have made your word greater than your name.
Psalms 138:3 On the day I called out, you answered me and made my soul strong.
Psalms 138:4 Every king on earth will praise you, God, when they hear what you say.
Psalms 138:5 Yes, they will sing about God's paths: for God's glory is great.
Psalms 138:6 Even though God is great, he cares for the humble, but he keeps his distance from the proud.
Psalms 138:7 Even when I walk through trouble, you will bring me back to life. You will act against my enemies' anger, and your power will save me.
Psalms 138:8 God will complete what is important to me; Your kindness, God, lasts forever; do not abandon what Your hands have made.
