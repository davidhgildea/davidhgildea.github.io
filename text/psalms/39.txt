Psalms 39:1 I decided to watch how I act, so I won't sin with what I say; I will control my words when bad people are around me.
Psalms 39:2 I stayed quiet, didn't even speak what's good; my sadness grew.
Psalms 39:3 I felt a strong burning in my heart as I thought deeply; then I began to speak.
Psalms 39:4 God, let me know my life's end and how many days I have; let me realize how short-lived I am.
Psalms 39:5 Look, you have made my life very short, and my lifetime seems like nothing to you. Truly, even at his healthiest, every person is completely fleeting.
Psalms 39:6 Everyone goes through life putting on a show for no reason; they worry for nothing. They pile up wealth, without knowing who will end up with it.
Psalms 39:7 And now, God, what do I wait for? My hope is in you.
Psalms 39:8 Save me from all my wrongdoings; don't let the foolish shame me.
Psalms 39:9 I was silent, I did not open my mouth; because you did it.
Psalms 39:10 Take away your punishment; I am worn out from your strong hand.
Psalms 39:11 When you correct people for their wrongs with scolding, you cause their beauty to fade away like a moth: truly, all people are fleeting. Pause and think.
Psalms 39:12 Listen to my prayer, God, and hear my cry; do not ignore my tears: for I am a stranger to you, and a traveler, just like my ancestors were.
Psalms 39:13 Please give me rest so I can get strong again before I leave and disappear.
