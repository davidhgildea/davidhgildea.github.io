Psalms 10:1 Why do you stand far away, God? Why do you hide yourself in times of trouble?
Psalms 10:2 In his pride, the wicked chases the poor: may they get caught in the traps they've set.
Psalms 10:3 The wicked brags about what they want and praises greedy people, who God hates.
Psalms 10:4 Bad people, because of their proud faces, will not look for God. God is not in any of their thoughts.
Psalms 10:5 His ways are always harsh; your decisions are too high for him to see: he sneers at all his enemies.
Psalms 10:6 He thinks to himself, I won't be shaken; I'll never have trouble.
Psalms 10:7 His mouth is full of curses, lies, and trickery; under his tongue is trouble and nonsense.
Psalms 10:8 He sits in the hidden spots of the towns, secretly killing the innocent; his eyes are sneakingly watching the poor.
Psalms 10:9 He hides quietly like a lion in its den, waiting to trap the poor; he catches the poor by pulling them into his net.
Psalms 10:10 He bends down and acts humble so that the poor may be defeated by his powerful followers.
Psalms 10:11 He thinks to himself, "God has forgotten; He hides His face and will never see it."
Psalms 10:12 Get up, God; lift your hand. Don't forget the humble.
Psalms 10:13 Why does the wicked disrespect God? He thinks to himself, You won't hold him accountable.
Psalms 10:14 You have seen it; for you watch trouble and spite, to repay it with your hand: the poor trust in you; you are the helper of the orphans.
Psalms 10:15 Break the power of the wicked and the wrongdoer; search for their evil until you find none.
Psalms 10:16 God is King forever and ever; the nations have vanished from his land.
Psalms 10:17 God, you have heard the desires of the humble: you will make their hearts ready, you will listen to them.
Psalms 10:18 To make fair decisions for orphans and those suffering, so that no person can bully them again.
