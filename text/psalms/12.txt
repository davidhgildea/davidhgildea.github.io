Psalms 12:1 Help, God; for good people are gone; the loyal ones are missing among humans.
Psalms 12:2 They say meaningless things to each other; they speak with flattering lips and a double heart.
Psalms 12:3 God will stop all flattering lips and the tongue that speaks proud words.
Psalms 12:4 Those who say, "We will succeed with our words; our lips belong to us. Who rules over us?"
Psalms 12:5 Because poor people are hurt and needy people sigh, I will get up now, says God; I will save them from those who blow against them.
Psalms 12:6 God's words are pure: like silver refined in a clay furnace, made pure seven times.
Psalms 12:7 You will protect them, God; you will keep them safe from this generation forever.
Psalms 12:8 Evil people are everywhere when the worst ones get power.
