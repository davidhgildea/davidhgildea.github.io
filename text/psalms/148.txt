Psalms 148:1 Praise God. Praise God from the sky: praise him up high.
Psalms 148:2 Praise him, all his angels; praise him, all his armies.
Psalms 148:3 Praise God, sun and moon; praise Him, all you shining stars.
Psalms 148:4 Praise God, you highest heavens and the waters above the sky.
Psalms 148:5 Let them praise God's name because when he gave the order, they were made.
Psalms 148:6 God has set them up forever; He has given an order that will never end.
Psalms 148:7 Praise God from the earth, you sea creatures, and all ocean depths.
Psalms 148:8 Fire and hail, snow and clouds; stormy wind carrying out God's command:
Psalms 148:9 Mountains and all hills; trees with fruit and all cedars.
Psalms 148:10 Animals, all livestock; creatures that crawl, and birds that fly:
Psalms 148:11 Earth's kings and everyone, leaders and all earth's judges:
Psalms 148:12 Young people and girls; elderly people and children:
Psalms 148:13 Let them praise God's name, for only His name is wonderful; His majesty is higher than earth and heaven.
Psalms 148:14 He also lifts up the strength of his people, the honor of all his holy ones; even the children of Israel, a people close to him. Praise God.
