Psalms 33:1 Be happy with God, you good people, because it's good for those who are right to give praise.
Psalms 33:2 Praise God with the harp: sing to him with the psaltery and a ten-stringed instrument.
Psalms 33:3 Sing a new song to God; play well with loud sounds.
Psalms 33:4 God's message is true; everything he does is honest.
Psalms 33:5 He loves what is right and fair; the world is filled with God's kindness.
Psalms 33:6 God made the heavens by his word; and all the stars with his breath.
Psalms 33:7 He brings the sea waters together like a pile; he stores the depths in storerooms.
Psalms 33:8 Let everyone on earth fear God; let all the people of the world be amazed by him.
Psalms 33:9 He spoke, and it happened; he gave an order, and it lasted.
Psalms 33:10 God makes the plans of nations fail; He causes the schemes of people to have no effect.
Psalms 33:11 God's plan lasts forever, his thoughts reach through all generations.
Psalms 33:12 Happy is the country that has God as its God; and the people God has picked to be His own.
Psalms 33:13 God looks from heaven; he sees all humans.
Psalms 33:14 From where he lives, he watches everyone on earth.
Psalms 33:15 He shapes everyone's heart the same; he looks at all they do.
Psalms 33:16 No king is saved by having a big army; a strong man is not rescued by his great power.
Psalms 33:17 A horse is not reliable for protection; it cannot save anyone by its great power.
Psalms 33:18 Look, God watches over those who respect him, those who wait for his kindness.
Psalms 33:19 To save them from death and to sustain them in times of hunger.
Psalms 33:20 Our soul waits for God: He is our help and our protector.
Psalms 33:21 Our heart will be happy in God because we have trusted in his holy name.
Psalms 33:22 God, let your kindness be with us, just as we trust in you.
