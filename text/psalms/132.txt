Psalms 132:1 God, remember David and all his hardships.
Psalms 132:2 He promised to God and made a vow to the powerful God of Jacob.
Psalms 132:3 I will not enter my home or go to my bed;
Psalms 132:4 I will not let my eyes sleep or my eyelids close.
Psalms 132:5 Until I find a place for God, a home for the powerful God of Jacob.
Psalms 132:6 We heard about it in Ephratah and found it in the forest fields.
Psalms 132:7 We will enter his tents: we will worship at his footrest.
Psalms 132:8 Get up, God, and go to your resting place; you and the chest that shows your power.
Psalms 132:9 May your priests wear goodness, and may your holy people yell happily.
Psalms 132:10 For your servant David's sake, do not reject your chosen one.
Psalms 132:11 God has truly promised David; he will not break his promise; I will put one of your descendants on your throne.
Psalms 132:12 If your children follow my agreement and the lessons I give them, their children will rule on your throne forever.
Psalms 132:13 God has chosen Zion; he wants it as his home.
Psalms 132:14 This is where I will rest forever; I will live here because I want it.
Psalms 132:15 I will greatly bless her food supply; I will fill her needy with bread.
Psalms 132:16 I will dress her priests in salvation, and her holy people will loudly celebrate with joy.
Psalms 132:17 I will make David's power grow there; I have set up a light for my chosen one.
Psalms 132:18 I will cover his enemies with shame, but his own crown will thrive.
