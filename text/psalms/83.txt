Psalms 83:1 God, do not be silent; do not stay quiet, God.
Psalms 83:2 Look, your enemies are causing chaos: and those who hate you have become arrogant.
Psalms 83:3 They have made sneaky plans against your people and plotted against those you protect.
Psalms 83:4 They said, "Come, let's destroy them as a nation, so no one remembers the name Israel anymore."
Psalms 83:5 They have agreed together to join forces against you.
Psalms 83:6 The tents of Edom, and the Ishmaelites; of Moab, and the Hagarenes;
Psalms 83:7 Gebal, Ammon, and Amalek; the Philistines and the people of Tyre;
Psalms 83:8 Assur has also teamed up with them; they have helped Lot's descendants. Pause.
Psalms 83:9 Treat them like you did the Midianites and like you did Sisera and Jabin at the Kishon River.
Psalms 83:10 They died at Endor and became like manure on the ground.
Psalms 83:11 Make their leaders like Oreb and Zeeb; make all their princes like Zebah and Zalmunna.
Psalms 83:12 They said, "Let's take God's houses for ourselves."
Psalms 83:13 My God, make them like a spinning wheel; like dry plants before the wind.
Psalms 83:14 Just as fire burns wood, and flames set the mountains ablaze;
Psalms 83:15 Chase them with your wind and scare them with your storm.
Psalms 83:16 Make them feel ashamed so that they will look for you, God.
Psalms 83:17 May they be confused and troubled forever; yes, may they be embarrassed and die.
Psalms 83:18 People will know that you, named God, are the highest above all the earth.
