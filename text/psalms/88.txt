Psalms 88:1 God, who saves me, I have called out to you day and night.
Psalms 88:2 Let my prayer reach you: listen to my call for help.
Psalms 88:3 My soul is filled with troubles, and my life is close to ending.
Psalms 88:4 I am grouped with those who descend into the pit; I am like a man without strength.
Psalms 88:5 Free among the dead, like those killed that lie in the grave, whom you no longer remember: and they are separated from your care.
Psalms 88:6 You have put me in the deepest pit, in dark places, in the depths.
Psalms 88:7 Your anger weighs heavily on me, and you have hit me with all your waves. Pause.
Psalms 88:8 You have pushed my friends far away from me; you have made me disgusting to them: I am trapped and can't get out.
Psalms 88:9 My eye cries because I am suffering: God, I call to you every day, I reach out my hands to you.
Psalms 88:10 Will you show wonders to the dead? Will the dead rise and praise you? Pause.
Psalms 88:11 Can anyone speak of your kindness in death, or of your faithfulness in ruin?
Psalms 88:12 Can your miracles be known in the dark? Can your goodness be known in the land of forgetting?
Psalms 88:13 I have cried out to you, God, and in the morning my prayer will come before you.
Psalms 88:14 God, why do you reject me? Why do you hide your face from me?
Psalms 88:15 I have been suffering and close to death since I was young; I am overwhelmed by your fears.
Psalms 88:16 Your fierce anger has swept over me; your fears have isolated me.
Psalms 88:17 They surrounded me every day like water; they all closed in on me together.
Psalms 88:18 You have pushed my loved ones and friends far away from me, and made my companions disappear into darkness.
