Psalms 107:1 Give thanks to God, because He is good; His love lasts forever.
Psalms 107:2 Let those saved by God say so, whom he has saved from the enemy's power.
Psalms 107:3 God brought them together from the east, west, north, and south.
Psalms 107:4 They roamed the desert on a lonely path and found no city to live in.
Psalms 107:5 They were hungry and thirsty, and they felt weak deep inside.
Psalms 107:6 They called out to God in their trouble, and he rescued them from their distress.
Psalms 107:7 He guided them on the right path so they could reach a city to live in.
Psalms 107:8 I wish people would thank God for his kindness and for the amazing things he does for humans!
Psalms 107:9 God satisfies the longing person and fills the hungry person with good things.
Psalms 107:10 Those who sit in darkness and near death, trapped in suffering and chains;
Psalms 107:11 They disobeyed God's words and scorned the advice of the Most High.
Psalms 107:12 So God made them weak with hard work; they stumbled, and no one was there to help them.
Psalms 107:13 They called out to God in their trouble, and he rescued them from their problems.
Psalms 107:14 He led them from darkness and the shadow of death, and broke their chains apart.
Psalms 107:15 I wish people would thank God for his kindness and his amazing deeds for humanity!
Psalms 107:16 He has smashed the bronze gates and cut through the iron bars.
Psalms 107:17 Fools suffer because they rebel and do wrong.
Psalms 107:18 Their soul hates all kinds of food; and they are close to dying.
Psalms 107:19 Then they call to God in their trouble, and he saves them from their distresses.
Psalms 107:20 He sent his message, healed them, and saved them from their troubles.
Psalms 107:21 I wish people would thank God for His kindness and for His amazing deeds to humans!
Psalms 107:22 Let them offer thanksgiving sacrifices and joyfully talk about what God has done.
Psalms 107:23 Those who sail on the sea in ships, doing work on the vast waters;
Psalms 107:24 They see God's deeds and his amazing works in the ocean.
Psalms 107:25 He commands, and a stormy wind rises, lifting the waves.
Psalms 107:26 They rise up to the sky, they sink down to the deep: their spirit is overwhelmed by distress.
Psalms 107:27 They sway back and forth, and stumble like a drunk person, and don't know what to do.
Psalms 107:28 Then they call out to God in their trouble, and he saves them from their distresses.
Psalms 107:29 He turns the storm into a calm so that the waves become still.
Psalms 107:30 Then they are happy because they are calm; so he takes them to the place they wanted to reach.
Psalms 107:31 I wish people would thank God for his kindness and his amazing deeds for all people!
Psalms 107:32 Let them praise God among the people, and honor him in the gathering of the elders.
Psalms 107:33 He turns rivers into deserts and springs of water into dry land.
Psalms 107:34 A productive land becomes empty because of the evil of those who live there.
Psalms 107:35 He turns the desert into pools of water and parched land into springs.
Psalms 107:36 There he lets the hungry live, so they can build a city to live in.
Psalms 107:37 Plant the fields and grow vineyards to produce a harvest of fruit.
Psalms 107:38 He blesses them, and they increase a lot; he does not let their animals become fewer.
Psalms 107:39 They are reduced and weakened by hardship, trouble, and sadness.
Psalms 107:40 He shows disrespect to rulers and makes them walk in the desert, where there are no paths.
Psalms 107:41 God lifts the poor from trouble and gives them many children like a herd of sheep.
Psalms 107:42 Good people will see it and be happy, while all wrongdoers will be silent.
Psalms 107:43 Anyone who is wise and pays attention to these things will understand God's kindness.
