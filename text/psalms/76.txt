Psalms 76:1 God is known in Judah; His name is great in Israel.
Psalms 76:2 God's tent is in Salem, and his home is in Zion.
Psalms 76:3 There he broke the arrows, the shield, the sword, and the battle. Pause.
Psalms 76:4 You are more splendid and wonderful than the mountains of prey.
Psalms 76:5 The brave are ruined, they have slept their last sleep; and none of the strong found their strength.
Psalms 76:6 When you scold, God of Jacob, both chariots and horses fall into a deep sleep.
Psalms 76:7 You, yes you, are to be feared: and who can stand before you when you are angry?
Psalms 76:8 You made your judgment heard from heaven; the earth was afraid and became quiet.
Psalms 76:9 When God stood up to judge and to rescue all the gentle people on earth. Pause.
Psalms 76:10 Human anger will end up praising you; you will hold back the rest of the anger.
Psalms 76:11 Promise and give to God: everyone around Him should bring gifts to the one who should be respected.
Psalms 76:12 He will end the power of rulers; he is fearsome to the kings of the world.
