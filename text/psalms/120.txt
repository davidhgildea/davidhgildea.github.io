Psalms 120:1 When I was in trouble, I called out to God, and he listened to me.
Psalms 120:2 God, save me from lies and a tricky tongue.
Psalms 120:3 What will you get? What will happen to you, you lying tongue?
Psalms 120:4 Strong arrows of the powerful, with burning coals.
Psalms 120:5 I am sad because I live in Mesech and stay in the tents of Kedar.
Psalms 120:6 My soul has lived a long time with someone who hates peace.
Psalms 120:7 I want peace, but when I talk, they want war.
