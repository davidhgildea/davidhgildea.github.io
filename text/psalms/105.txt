Psalms 105:1 Give thanks to God; call on his name; tell people about what he has done.
Psalms 105:2 Sing to him, sing songs to him; talk about all his amazing works.
Psalms 105:3 Be proud of his holy name; let the hearts of those who seek God be happy.
Psalms 105:4 Look for God and his power; always try to see him.
Psalms 105:5 Remember the amazing things God has done, his miracles, and the decisions he has spoken.
Psalms 105:6 Descendants of Abraham, his servant, and children of Jacob, his chosen one.
Psalms 105:7 He is our God; His decisions are everywhere on earth.
Psalms 105:8 God has always remembered his promise, the command he gave for thousands of generations.
Psalms 105:9 God made this promise to Abraham and swore an oath to Isaac.
Psalms 105:10 God made it a law for Jacob and an eternal covenant for Israel.
Psalms 105:11 I will give you the land of Canaan, your inheritance.
Psalms 105:12 They were only a few people, very few, and foreigners there.
Psalms 105:13 As they moved from one nation to another and from one kingdom to a different group of people;
Psalms 105:14 God did not let anyone harm them; indeed, he corrected kings because of them.
Psalms 105:15 God says, "Do not hurt the people I have chosen, and do not harm my messengers."
Psalms 105:16 He caused a famine in the land; he broke the support of bread.
Psalms 105:17 God sent a man ahead of them, named Joseph, who was sold as a servant.
Psalms 105:18 They hurt his feet with chains; he was put in irons.
Psalms 105:19 Until the time that his word came true, God's promise tested him.
Psalms 105:20 The king sent for him and set him free; the leader of the people released him.
Psalms 105:21 He made him the master of his home and the leader of all he owned.
Psalms 105:22 To tie up his leaders as he wishes; and to give his wise men knowledge.
Psalms 105:23 Israel went to Egypt; Jacob stayed in Ham's land.
Psalms 105:24 He made his people grow in number greatly; and made them stronger than their enemies.
Psalms 105:25 He made them hate his people and treat his servants with deceit.
Psalms 105:26 God sent Moses, his servant, and Aaron, whom he chose.
Psalms 105:27 They showed his miracles to them, and amazing things in Ham's land.
Psalms 105:28 God sent darkness and it became dark, but they did not rebel against his word.
Psalms 105:29 He turned their water into blood and killed their fish.
Psalms 105:30 Their land had many frogs, even in their kings' rooms.
Psalms 105:31 He spoke, and different types of flies and lice appeared throughout their lands.
Psalms 105:32 He sent them ice instead of rain, and fire that burned in their land.
Psalms 105:33 He struck down their grapevines and fig trees, and broke the trees in their territory.
Psalms 105:34 He spoke, and locusts and caterpillars came in endless numbers.
Psalms 105:35 They ate all the plants in the land and consumed the crops from their soil.
Psalms 105:36 He also struck down every firstborn in their land, the most powerful of all.
Psalms 105:37 He also led them out with silver and gold, and all of them were strong and healthy.
Psalms 105:38 Egypt was happy when they left because they were afraid of them.
Psalms 105:39 He put a cloud as a cover and a fire to shine at night.
Psalms 105:40 The people requested, and he provided birds, and filled them with food from heaven.
Psalms 105:41 God opened the rock, and water rushed out; it flowed in the dry areas like a river.
Psalms 105:42 God remembered his sacred promise to his servant Abraham.
Psalms 105:43 He led his people out with joy, and his chosen ones with happiness.
Psalms 105:44 God gave them the lands of the nations, and they received the work of the peoples.
Psalms 105:45 So they could follow his rules and obey his laws. Praise God.
