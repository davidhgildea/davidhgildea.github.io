Psalms 108:1 God, my heart is steady; I will sing and praise you with all my heart.
Psalms 108:2 Wake up, stringed instruments and harp: I will wake up early.
Psalms 108:3 I will praise you, God, among the people; and I will sing praises to you among the nations.
Psalms 108:4 Your kindness is vast, beyond the skies, and your truth goes up to the clouds.
Psalms 108:5 God, be lifted up higher than the heavens; let your glory be over all the earth.
Psalms 108:6 Help your loved ones be rescued: save them with your power, and respond to me.
Psalms 108:7 God has spoken in his sacred place; I will be happy, I will split Shechem, and measure out the valley of Succoth.
Psalms 108:8 Gilead belongs to me; Manasseh is mine; Ephraim is my strong leader; Judah makes my laws.
Psalms 108:9 Moab is my washbasin; I will toss my shoe over Edom; I will be victorious over Philistia.
Psalms 108:10 Who will take me into the fortified city? Who will guide me into Edom?
Psalms 108:11 Won't you, God, who have rejected us, come with our armies?
Psalms 108:12 Help us in trouble, because human help is useless.
Psalms 108:13 With God's help, we will be brave; He will defeat our enemies.
