Psalms 8:1 God, our Lord, how wonderful is your name all over the earth! You have put your greatness higher than the sky.
Psalms 8:2 God has given strength through the words of children and babies to silence the enemy and the avenger.
Psalms 8:3 When I look at your skies, the creation of your hands, the moon and the stars you set in place;
Psalms 8:4 What is a person that you think of him? And a human that you visit him?
Psalms 8:5 You made him a little less than the angels and crowned him with glory and honor.
Psalms 8:6 You made him ruler over everything you created; you put everything under his control.
Psalms 8:7 Every sheep and cow, and the wild animals of the field.
Psalms 8:8 Birds in the sky, fish in the sea, and everything that moves through the sea paths.
Psalms 8:9 God, our Master, how wonderful is your name throughout the earth!
