Psalms 48:1 God is great, and deserves high praise in the city of our God, on his holy mountain.
Psalms 48:2 Mount Zion is beautifully placed, the joy of the entire world, on the north side, the city of the great King.
Psalms 48:3 God is known in her strong buildings as a place of safety.
Psalms 48:4 Look, the kings gathered and went by together.
Psalms 48:5 They saw it and were amazed; they were distressed and hurried away.
Psalms 48:6 They were very afraid and in pain like a woman giving birth.
Psalms 48:7 You break the ships of Tarshish with an east wind.
Psalms 48:8 We have heard and now we see in the city of God, the city of our God: God will make it last forever. Pause and think about that.
Psalms 48:9 We have thought of your kindness, God, in the middle of your temple.
Psalms 48:10 God, your name reaches to the ends of the earth, just like your praise; your right hand is filled with fairness.
Psalms 48:11 Let Mount Zion be happy, let Judah's daughters be joyful, because of your decisions.
Psalms 48:12 Walk around Zion, circle it, and count its towers.
Psalms 48:13 Pay close attention to her strong walls, look carefully at her palaces; so you can tell the next generation about them.
Psalms 48:14 This God is our God forever: He will guide us until we die.
