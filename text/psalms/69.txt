Psalms 69:1 Help me, God; the waters have come up to my soul.
Psalms 69:2 I am sinking in deep mud where I can't stand; I've come into deep waters where the floods sweep over me.
Psalms 69:3 I am tired from crying; my throat is dry; my eyes are weak as I wait for my God.
Psalms 69:4 Those who hate me for no reason outnumber the hairs on my head; those who attack me without cause are strong. Yet I gave back what I didn't steal.
Psalms 69:5 God, you know my foolishness; my sins are not hidden from you.
Psalms 69:6 God, don't let those who wait for you be embarrassed because of me. Don't let those who look for you be confused because of me, God of Israel.
Psalms 69:7 I have faced insults and shame for you; my face is hidden by disgrace.
Psalms 69:8 I have become a stranger to my brothers, and a foreigner to my mother's children.
Psalms 69:9 My passion for your house has consumed me; the insults aimed at you have fallen on me.
Psalms 69:10 When I cried and disciplined myself with fasting, it became my shame.
Psalms 69:11 I also made sackcloth my clothing, and I turned into a saying among them.
Psalms 69:12 Those who sit at the gate talk about me; and I am the song of drunk people.
Psalms 69:13 As for me, I pray to you, God, at the right time: O God, because of your great mercy, listen to me and save me with your faithful help.
Psalms 69:14 Save me from the mud and don't let me drown; rescue me from those who hate me and from the deep waters.
Psalms 69:15 Don't let the floodwaters overwhelm me, nor let the depths swallow me, and don't let the pit close over me.
Psalms 69:16 Listen to me, God, because your kindness is good; turn to me because you are very merciful.
Psalms 69:17 Do not hide from me, your servant; I am in trouble: listen to me quickly.
Psalms 69:18 Come close to my soul and save it; rescue me because of my enemies.
Psalms 69:19 You know of my disgrace, my shame, and my dishonor: all my enemies are in front of you.
Psalms 69:20 Insults have broken my heart; I am very sad. I hoped for someone to show sympathy, but nobody did; I looked for comforters, but found none.
Psalms 69:21 They also gave me bitter food to eat; and when I was thirsty, they gave me vinegar to drink.
Psalms 69:22 May their table turn into a trap before them, and let what should have been for their good become a snare.
Psalms 69:23 Make their eyes so dark that they cannot see; and keep their backs always shaking.
Psalms 69:24 Pour out your anger on them, and let your fierce anger catch them.
Psalms 69:25 Let their home be empty; and let no one live in their tents.
Psalms 69:26 They chase after the one you have struck, and they talk to hurt those you have wounded.
Psalms 69:27 Add wrong upon their wrongs, and don't let them share in your goodness.
Psalms 69:28 Remove them from the book of life, and do not list them with the good people.
Psalms 69:29 I am poor and sad: God, let your salvation lift me up.
Psalms 69:30 I will praise God with a song and honor him with thanks.
Psalms 69:31 This will make God happier than a cow or a bull with horns and hooves.
Psalms 69:32 The humble will see this and be happy, and the hearts of those who seek God will be filled with life.
Psalms 69:33 God hears the poor and does not scorn his captives.
Psalms 69:34 Let the sky and earth praise God, the seas, and all that moves in them.
Psalms 69:35 God will rescue Zion and rebuild the towns of Judah so people can live there and own it.
Psalms 69:36 His servants' descendants will inherit the land, and those who love his name will live there.
