Psalms 68:1 May God stand up, may his enemies scatter; may those who hate him run away from him.
Psalms 68:2 Like smoke is blown away, blow them away; as wax melts before fire, let the wicked vanish in God's presence.
Psalms 68:3 Let good people be happy; let them celebrate in front of God; yes, let them be very joyful.
Psalms 68:4 Sing to God, sing praises to His name; praise Him who rides through the skies, who is called JAH, and be happy in His presence.
Psalms 68:5 God is a protector of orphans and a defender of widows in his sacred home.
Psalms 68:6 God places lonely people into families; he frees the chained; but the rebellious live in a barren place.
Psalms 68:7 God, when you went out before your people, when you walked through the desert; pause and think about that.
Psalms 68:8 The earth trembled, the skies poured down rain before God: even Mount Sinai shook before God, the God of Israel.
Psalms 68:9 God, you sent a lot of rain, which strengthened your people when they were tired.
Psalms 68:10 Your people have lived there; God, you have provided for the needy with your goodness.
Psalms 68:11 God gave the message: many people spread it.
Psalms 68:12 Army rulers ran away quickly; the woman who stayed home shared the loot.
Psalms 68:13 Even if you lay among the ashes, you will be like a dove with silver wings and golden feathers.
Psalms 68:14 When God spread out kings there, it was as white as snow on Salmon.
Psalms 68:15 God's hill is like the hill of Bashan; a tall hill like the hill of Bashan.
Psalms 68:16 Why do you jump, you tall mountains? This is the mountain where God chooses to live; yes, God will live there forever.
Psalms 68:17 God has twenty thousand chariots and many angels; God is with them, as at Sinai, in the sacred place.
Psalms 68:18 You went up high, you took prisoners: you got gifts for people, even for those against you, so that God could live with them.
Psalms 68:19 God blesses us every day with good things, the God who saves us. Pause and think about this.
Psalms 68:20 Our God is the God who saves us; and God controls life and death.
Psalms 68:21 God will hurt the heads of his enemies and the hairy heads of those who keep sinning.
Psalms 68:22 God said, "I will bring them back from Bashan, I will bring my people back from the depths of the sea."
Psalms 68:23 So your foot will step in your enemies' blood, and your dogs will lick it too.
Psalms 68:24 People have seen how you move, O God; they've seen how my God, my King, moves in the holy place.
Psalms 68:25 Singers led the way, musicians followed; young women with drums were among them.
Psalms 68:26 Praise God together, everyone; praise the Lord, O descendants of Israel.
Psalms 68:27 Benjamin's group with their leader, Judah's chiefs and their advisors, Zebulun's leaders, and Naphtali's leaders are there.
Psalms 68:28 Your God has ordered your power: make us stronger, God, for what you have done for us.
Psalms 68:29 Because of your temple in Jerusalem, kings will bring you gifts.
Psalms 68:30 Tell off the group of soldiers, the many strong ones, and the young ones of the nations, until they all give in with silver gifts: send away the people who enjoy fighting.
Psalms 68:31 Leaders will come from Egypt; Ethiopia will quickly reach out to God.
Psalms 68:32 Sing to God, you kingdoms of the earth; sing praises to God; pause and think.
Psalms 68:33 To the one who rides on the highest heavens, which existed long ago, listen, he sends out his voice, a powerful voice.
Psalms 68:34 Give God credit for being strong: He is most excellent above Israel, and his power is in the skies.
Psalms 68:35 God, you are awesome from your holy places. The God of Israel gives strength and power to his people. God is blessed.
