Psalms 80:1 Listen, Shepherd of Israel, you who guide Joseph like sheep; you who sit above the angels, show your light.
Psalms 80:2 Before the tribes of Ephraim, Benjamin, and Manasseh, wake up your power and come save us.
Psalms 80:3 Bring us back, God, make your face bright; then we will be saved.
Psalms 80:4 God of all, how long will you stay angry when your people pray to you?
Psalms 80:5 You feed them with tears for bread; and make them drink tears in abundance.
Psalms 80:6 You make us fight with our neighbors, and our enemies laugh at us.
Psalms 80:7 Bring us back, God of all, make your face shine on us, and we will be saved.
Psalms 80:8 You brought a vine from Egypt; you drove out the nations and planted it.
Psalms 80:9 You prepared space for it, made its roots grow deep, and it spread throughout the land.
Psalms 80:10 The hills were hidden by its shadow, and its branches were like the great cedars.
Psalms 80:11 She stretched her branches out to the sea and her limbs to the river.
Psalms 80:12 Why have you broken down her walls, so that everyone who goes by can pick from her?
Psalms 80:13 The wild pig from the forest destroys it, and the wild animal of the field eats it up.
Psalms 80:14 Please come back, God of power. Look from heaven, see, and take care of this vine.
Psalms 80:15 The vineyard your right hand planted and the shoot you strengthened for yourself.
Psalms 80:16 It is burned with fire, it is chopped down: they die by the scolding of your face.
Psalms 80:17 Let your hand be on the man you support, on the human being you have strengthened for your purpose.
Psalms 80:18 We won't turn away from you; give us life, and we will call on your name.
Psalms 80:19 Bring us back, God of all, make your face shine on us; then we will be saved.
