Psalms 137:1 By the rivers of Babylon, there we sat and cried when we thought of Zion.
Psalms 137:2 We hung our harps on the willow trees there.
Psalms 137:3 There, our captors asked us for a song; and our tormentors asked for joy, saying, "Sing us one of the Zion songs."
Psalms 137:4 How can we sing God's song in a foreign place?
Psalms 137:5 If I forget you, Jerusalem, may my right hand lose its skill.
Psalms 137:6 If I don't remember you, may my tongue stick to the top of my mouth; if I don't value Jerusalem more than my greatest happiness.
Psalms 137:7 God, remember the people of Edom and what they did when Jerusalem fell; they said, "Tear it down, tear it down, to its very foundation."
Psalms 137:8 O daughter of Babylon, doomed to destruction; happy will he be who repays you for what you have done to us.
Psalms 137:9 He will be happy who takes your babies and smashes them against the rocks.
