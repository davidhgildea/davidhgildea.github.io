Psalms 77:1 I called out to God loudly; I called to God, and he listened to me.
Psalms 77:2 On the day I was in trouble, I looked for God. My pain continued all night and did not stop, and my heart would not be comforted.
Psalms 77:3 I thought of God and felt upset; I complained, and my spirit felt crushed. Pause.
Psalms 77:4 You keep me awake: I am so upset that I can't talk.
Psalms 77:5 I have thought about the old days, the years from long ago.
Psalms 77:6 I remember my night song; I talk with my heart; my spirit searches carefully.
Psalms 77:7 Will God reject us forever? Will he never show favor again?
Psalms 77:8 Has his kindness completely disappeared forever? Will his promise never come true?
Psalms 77:9 Has God forgotten to be kind? Has he in anger closed off his compassion? Pause and think about that.
Psalms 77:10 And I thought, "This is my weakness, but I will remember the years of God's powerful works."
Psalms 77:11 I will remember what God has done; I will surely remember the amazing things you did long ago.
Psalms 77:12 I will think about all your works and talk about what you have done.
Psalms 77:13 Your path, God, is in the holy place: who is as mighty as our God?
Psalms 77:14 You are the God who performs miracles; you have shown your power to the people.
Psalms 77:15 With your strong arm, you rescued your people, the descendants of Jacob and Joseph. Pause.
Psalms 77:16 The waters saw you, God, the waters saw you; they were scared: even the deep waters were disturbed.
Psalms 77:17 The clouds released water; the sky made noise; your arrows flew everywhere.
Psalms 77:18 Your thunder's sound was in the sky; the lightning lit up the world; the ground quaked and shook.
Psalms 77:19 Your way is in the sea, your path is in the big waters, and no one knows your footsteps.
Psalms 77:20 You led your people like sheep with Moses and Aaron guiding them.
