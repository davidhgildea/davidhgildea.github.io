Psalms 51:1 Be kind to me, God, because of your love; because of your great compassion, wipe away my wrongdoings.
Psalms 51:2 Clean me completely from my wrongs, and wash away all my sins.
Psalms 51:3 I admit my wrongs, and my sin is always in my sight.
Psalms 51:4 I have sinned only against you and done this wrong in your view: so you are right when you speak and fair when you judge.
Psalms 51:5 I was born sinful, and my mother became pregnant with me in sin.
Psalms 51:6 You want honesty deep inside us; in our secret hearts, you teach us wisdom.
Psalms 51:7 Clean me with hyssop, and I will be clean: wash me, and I will be whiter than snow.
Psalms 51:8 Let me hear joy and happiness, so the bones you have crushed can be happy.
Psalms 51:9 Turn away from my sins and erase all my wrongs.
Psalms 51:10 Make my heart clean, God; and give me a new, good spirit inside.
Psalms 51:11 Do not push me away from you; and do not take your holy spirit from me.
Psalms 51:12 Give me back the happiness of your salvation; and support me with a generous spirit.
Psalms 51:13 Then I will teach wrongdoers your paths, and sinners will turn back to you.
Psalms 51:14 Save me from being guilty of causing death, O God, the God who saves me, and my tongue will sing loudly of your goodness.
Psalms 51:15 God, open my lips; and my mouth will speak your praise.
Psalms 51:16 You do not want sacrifices, or I would give them; you are not pleased with burnt offerings.
Psalms 51:17 God values a broken spirit: a heart that is truly sorry, God, you will not reject.
Psalms 51:18 Do what makes you happy for Zion: rebuild Jerusalem's walls.
Psalms 51:19 Then you will be happy with the right sacrifices, with burnt offerings and complete burnt offerings; then they will present bulls on your altar.
