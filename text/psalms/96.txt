Psalms 96:1 Sing a new song to God; let everyone on earth sing to God.
Psalms 96:2 Sing to God, praise His name; show His salvation every day.
Psalms 96:3 Tell everyone about God's greatness and the amazing things he does for all people.
Psalms 96:4 God is great and deserves much praise; we should respect Him more than all other gods.
Psalms 96:5 All the gods of other nations are just idols, but God created the skies.
Psalms 96:6 Honor and greatness are in front of him; power and beauty are in his holy place.
Psalms 96:7 Give to God, all families of the people, give to God glory and strength.
Psalms 96:8 Give God the honor that fits his name; bring a gift and enter his courts.
Psalms 96:9 Worship God in the beauty of being holy; let all the earth respect him.
Psalms 96:10 Tell the nations that God rules: the earth is firm and cannot be shaken; he will judge everyone fairly.
Psalms 96:11 Let the sky be happy, and the earth be joyful; let the sea and everything in it make loud sounds.
Psalms 96:12 Let the field be happy, and everything in it; then all the trees of the forest will celebrate.
Psalms 96:13 Before God, for he comes to judge the earth: he will judge the world fairly and the people with truth.
