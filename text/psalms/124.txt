Psalms 124:1 If God hadn't been with us, let Israel say so now.
Psalms 124:2 If God had not been with us when people attacked us:
Psalms 124:3 Then they would have swallowed us alive, when their anger flared up against us.
Psalms 124:4 Then the waters would have drowned us, the stream would have swept over us.
Psalms 124:5 Then the high waters would have swept over us.
Psalms 124:6 Praise God, who has not let us become victims to their teeth.
Psalms 124:7 Our soul has escaped like a bird from the hunter's trap; the trap is broken, and we have escaped.
Psalms 124:8 Our help comes from God, who created the sky and the earth.
