Psalms 126:1 When God freed the captives of Zion, we were like those who dream.
Psalms 126:2 Our mouths were full of laughter and our tongues sang with joy. Then the nations said, "God has done great things for them."
Psalms 126:3 God has done great things for us; we are happy about it.
Psalms 126:4 Bring us back from being captives, God, like streams in the desert.
Psalms 126:5 Those who plant with tears will harvest with joy.
Psalms 126:6 The one who goes out and cries while carrying valuable seeds will surely come back with joy, bringing his harvest with him.
