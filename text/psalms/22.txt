Psalms 22:1 My God, my God, why have you left me alone? Why are you so far from saving me and from hearing my cries?
Psalms 22:2 God, I call out for help during the day, but you do not hear me; and at night, I don't stay quiet.
Psalms 22:3 But you are holy, O you who are praised by Israel.
Psalms 22:4 Our ancestors trusted in you; they trusted, and you rescued them.
Psalms 22:5 They called to you and were saved; they trusted in you and were not disappointed.
Psalms 22:6 I am like a worm, not a man; scorned by everyone and looked down on by people.
Psalms 22:7 Everyone who sees me mocks me; they sneer and shake their heads, saying,
Psalms 22:8 He trusted in God to rescue him; let God save him since he takes pleasure in him.
Psalms 22:9 You are the one who brought me out of the womb; you made me feel safe as I nursed at my mother's breasts.
Psalms 22:10 From birth I was placed in your care; you have been my God since my mother carried me.
Psalms 22:11 Stay close to me, because trouble is near and no one is here to help.
Psalms 22:12 Many strong bulls have surrounded me; powerful bulls of Bashan have encircled me.
Psalms 22:13 They opened their mouths wide at me, like a hungry, roaring lion.
Psalms 22:14 I am drained like water, and all my bones are disjointed; my heart is like melted wax within me.
Psalms 22:15 My strength has dried up like a clay pot; my tongue sticks to my gums; and you have brought me to the edge of death.
Psalms 22:16 Dogs surround me; a group of evil people has trapped me; they have pierced my hands and feet.
Psalms 22:17 I can count all my bones; they just look and stare at me.
Psalms 22:18 They divide my clothes among themselves and gamble for my outfit.
Psalms 22:19 But don't be far from me, God; hurry to help me, my strength.
Psalms 22:20 Save my life from the sword; rescue me from the dog's grip.
Psalms 22:21 Rescue me from the lion's mouth; You have answered me from the wild oxen's horns.
Psalms 22:22 I will tell my brothers your name; I will praise you among the people gathered together.
Psalms 22:23 You who respect God, praise him; all of you from Jacob's family, honor him; and respect him, everyone from Israel's family.
Psalms 22:24 God has not ignored or hated the suffering of the suffering person; He did not turn away from him, but when he called for help, God listened.
Psalms 22:25 I will praise you in a large gathering and fulfill my promises in front of those who respect God.
Psalms 22:26 Humble people will eat and be happy; they who look for God will praise him; your heart will live forever.
Psalms 22:27 People everywhere will remember and turn to God; all families of nations will worship before you.
Psalms 22:28 The kingdom belongs to God, and he rules over the nations.
Psalms 22:29 Everyone who is wealthy on earth will eat and worship; those who die will kneel before God, and no one can save their own life.
Psalms 22:30 A child will serve God; they will be known as God's people for generations.
Psalms 22:31 They will come and tell a future generation about God's goodness, because he has done this.
