Psalms 81:1 Sing loudly to God, our strength; make happy sounds to the God of Jacob.
Psalms 81:2 Sing a song, and bring the drum, the lovely harp with the zither.
Psalms 81:3 Sound the trumpet at the new moon, at the set time, on our special festival day.
Psalms 81:4 This was a rule for Israel, and a law from the God of Jacob.
Psalms 81:5 He made this a law in Joseph to be a witness, when he traveled through Egypt, where I heard a language I did not understand.
Psalms 81:6 I took the burden off his shoulder; his hands were freed from carrying heavy pots.
Psalms 81:7 You called for help in trouble, and I saved you; I answered you from the thunder's hiding place; I tested you at the waters of Meribah. Pause.
Psalms 81:8 Listen, my people, and I will speak to you: Israel, if you listen to me;
Psalms 81:9 You must not have any foreign gods; you should not bow down to any foreign gods.
Psalms 81:10 I am your God who brought you out of Egypt. Open your mouth wide, and I will fill it.
Psalms 81:11 But my people did not listen to me; Israel did not want me.
Psalms 81:12 So I let them follow their own desires, and they did what they thought was best.
Psalms 81:13 I wish my people had listened to me and Israel had followed my paths!
Psalms 81:14 I would quickly defeat their enemies and act against their opponents.
Psalms 81:15 God's enemies should have surrendered to him, but their punishment will last forever.
Psalms 81:16 He should have given them the best wheat to eat, and I should have satisfied you with honey from the rock.
