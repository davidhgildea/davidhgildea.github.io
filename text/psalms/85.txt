Psalms 85:1 God, you have been good to your land; you have freed Jacob's captives.
Psalms 85:2 You have forgiven your people's sins; you have covered all their wrongs. Pause and think about that.
Psalms 85:3 You have removed all your anger; you have turned away from the intensity of your rage.
Psalms 85:4 God of our salvation, turn us back to you and stop being angry with us.
Psalms 85:5 Will you stay angry with us forever? Will you keep your anger through all generations?
Psalms 85:6 Will you not bring us back to life so your people can be happy in you?
Psalms 85:7 Show us your mercy, God, and give us your salvation.
Psalms 85:8 I will listen to what God will say: He promises peace to His people and His holy ones, but they must not go back to foolish ways.
Psalms 85:9 God's rescue is close to those who respect him; so that honor may live in our land.
Psalms 85:10 Kindness and truth have come together; fairness and peace have embraced.
Psalms 85:11 Truth will grow from the ground; and fairness will look down from the sky.
Psalms 85:12 Yes, God will give what is good, and our land will produce its crops.
Psalms 85:13 Goodness will lead the way for him; and will guide us on his path.
