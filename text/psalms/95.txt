Psalms 95:1 Come, let's sing to God: let's shout joyfully to the rock who saves us.
Psalms 95:2 Let's come before God with thanksgiving and make a joyful noise to him with songs.
Psalms 95:3 God is a great God and a great King above all gods.
Psalms 95:4 In his hand are the world's depths; the hills' power is also his.
Psalms 95:5 The sea belongs to God, and he created it; his hands shaped the land.
Psalms 95:6 Come, let's worship and bow down; let's kneel before God our creator.
Psalms 95:7 He is our God; we are his people, the sheep he cares for. If you listen to his voice today,
Psalms 95:8 Do not make your heart hard, as when you challenged me, and as on the day of testing in the desert.
Psalms 95:9 When your ancestors tested me, tried me, and saw what I did.
Psalms 95:10 For forty years I was upset with that generation; I said, "They are people whose hearts go astray, and they do not understand my paths."
Psalms 95:11 I promised in my anger that they would not enter my place of rest.
