Psalms 97:1 God rules; let the earth be happy; let the many islands be joyful.
Psalms 97:2 Clouds and darkness surround God; fairness and justice are where his throne is.
Psalms 97:3 A fire goes before him and burns up his enemies all around.
Psalms 97:4 God's lightning lit up the world; the earth saw it and shook.
Psalms 97:5 The hills melted like wax when God appeared, when the God of the whole earth came near.
Psalms 97:6 The sky shows God's goodness, and everyone sees his greatness.
Psalms 97:7 Let those who worship idols and are proud of their false gods be confused: let all gods bow down to Him.
Psalms 97:8 Zion heard and was happy; the women of Judah were joyful because of your decisions, God.
Psalms 97:9 For you, God, are higher than all the earth; you are lifted up far above all gods.
Psalms 97:10 You who love God, hate evil; God protects his holy people and saves them from the wicked.
Psalms 97:11 Light is given to the good, and joy to the honest in heart.
Psalms 97:12 Be happy with God, you good people; and say thanks when you think of his goodness.
