Psalms 86:1 Lean down your ear, God, listen to me: I am poor and in need.
Psalms 86:2 Keep me safe, because I am devoted to you; my God, rescue your servant who relies on you.
Psalms 86:3 Show me kindness, God, for I call out to you every day.
Psalms 86:4 Make your servant's heart happy, because I turn to you, God, with all my heart.
Psalms 86:5 You, God, are good and always ready to forgive; you are rich in kindness to all who call on you.
Psalms 86:6 Listen, God, to my prayer; pay attention to my requests.
Psalms 86:7 When I have problems, I will ask for your help because you will respond to me.
Psalms 86:8 There are no gods like you, God; no deeds can match what you do.
Psalms 86:9 Every nation you created will come and worship before you, God; they will honor your name.
Psalms 86:10 You are great and do amazing things; you are the only God.
Psalms 86:11 Teach me your way, God; I will follow your truth: make my heart whole to respect your name.
Psalms 86:12 I will praise you, God, with all my heart, and I will honor your name forever.
Psalms 86:13 Your kindness to me is great; you have saved me from the deepest darkness.
Psalms 86:14 God, proud people rise against me, and groups of violent men chase my soul; they do not consider you.
Psalms 86:15 But you, God, are filled with compassion and kindness, very patient, and rich in mercy and truth.
Psalms 86:16 God, please pay attention to me and show me kindness; give your power to your servant and rescue your servant's mother's child.
Psalms 86:17 Show me a sign of your goodness, so those who hate me will see it and feel shame, because you, God, have helped and comforted me.
