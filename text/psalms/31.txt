Psalms 31:1 In you, God, I trust; let me never be embarrassed: save me because you are good.
Psalms 31:2 Lean down your ear to me; rescue me quickly: be my solid rock, a safe place to protect me.
Psalms 31:3 You are my rock and safe place; so for your name's sake, lead and guide me.
Psalms 31:4 Rescue me from the trap secretly set for me; for you are my power.
Psalms 31:5 I give my spirit into your care; you have saved me, God of truth.
Psalms 31:6 I dislike those who follow false beliefs, but I trust in God.
Psalms 31:7 I will be happy and celebrate your kindness because you have seen my problems; you have understood my struggles.
Psalms 31:8 You haven't trapped me with my enemy; you've given me space to move freely.
Psalms 31:9 Please be kind to me, God, because I am in trouble; my eyes are weak from sadness, and so are my heart and my stomach.
Psalms 31:10 I am worn out with sadness, and I sigh year after year. My strength is weak because of my wrongs, and my bones are wasting away.
Psalms 31:11 All my enemies insulted me, especially my neighbors, and my friends were afraid of me: people who saw me outside ran away from me.
Psalms 31:12 People have forgotten me like someone dead and gone; I am like a shattered pot.
Psalms 31:13 I have heard many people talk badly about me; I was surrounded by fear. While they planned together against me, they aimed to end my life.
Psalms 31:14 I trusted in you, God: I said, You are my God.
Psalms 31:15 My life is in your hands; save me from my enemies and those who chase me.
Psalms 31:16 Let your face shine on your servant; save me because of your mercy.
Psalms 31:17 God, don't let me be ashamed because I have called on you. Make the wicked be ashamed and silent in death.
Psalms 31:18 Silence the lying lips that say hurtful things with pride and scorn against good people.
Psalms 31:19 Oh how wonderful is your kindness, which you have set aside for those who respect you; which you have made for those who rely on you in front of all people!
Psalms 31:20 You will hide them in the secret of your presence from human pride; you will keep them safely in a shelter from the conflict of speech.
Psalms 31:21 God is blessed because he has shown me his wonderful kindness in a strong city.
Psalms 31:22 In my rush, I thought I was lost to you, but you heard my call for help when I cried out to you.
Psalms 31:23 Love God, all his holy people, because God protects those who are loyal and gives many blessings to those who act with pride.
Psalms 31:24 Have courage, and God will make your heart strong, all who hope in God.
