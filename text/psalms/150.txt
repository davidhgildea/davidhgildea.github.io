Psalms 150:1 Praise God. Praise him in his holy place; praise him in the sky that shows his strength.
Psalms 150:2 Praise God for his powerful deeds; praise him for his great majesty.
Psalms 150:3 Praise God with the trumpet's sound; praise God with the psaltery and harp.
Psalms 150:4 Praise God with drums and dancing; praise God with strings and pipes.
Psalms 150:5 Praise God with loud cymbals; praise God with clashing cymbals.
Psalms 150:6 Let everything that breathes praise God. Praise God.
