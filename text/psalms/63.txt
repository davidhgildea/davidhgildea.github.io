Psalms 63:1 God, you are my God; I will look for you early. My soul is thirsty for you, my body longs for you in a dry and thirsty land with no water.
Psalms 63:2 To see your power and your glory, just as I have seen you in the holy place.
Psalms 63:3 Because your kindness is better than life, my lips will praise you.
Psalms 63:4 I will bless you as long as I live; I will raise my hands in your name.
Psalms 63:5 My soul will be happy as with the best food; and my mouth will joyfully praise you.
Psalms 63:6 When I think of you on my bed and reflect on you during the night time.
Psalms 63:7 Because you have helped me, I will be happy in your protection.
Psalms 63:8 I follow you closely with all my heart; your strong hand supports me.
Psalms 63:9 Those who try to destroy me will be brought down to the deepest parts of the earth.
Psalms 63:10 They will die by the sword and become food for foxes.
Psalms 63:11 The king will be happy in God; everyone who makes promises by him will be proud: but the mouths of liars will be shut.
