Psalms 144:1 God blesses me with strength, teaches my hands to fight, and my fingers for battle.
Psalms 144:2 My goodness and my protector; my strong tower and my rescuer; my shield and the one I trust; who brings my people under my rule.
Psalms 144:3 God, what is a person that you notice them, or a human being that you care for them?
Psalms 144:4 People are like a breath; their days are like a fleeting shadow.
Psalms 144:5 Bend your heavens, God, and come down; touch the mountains, and they will smoke.
Psalms 144:6 Send out lightning and make them run away; fire your arrows and wipe them out.
Psalms 144:7 Reach down from above; free me and rescue me from deep waters, from the grip of foreigners.
Psalms 144:8 They talk nonsense, and their right hand is used for lying.
Psalms 144:9 I will sing a new song to you, God: on a harp and a ten-stringed instrument I will praise you.
Psalms 144:10 He gives salvation to kings and saves his servant David from the deadly sword.
Psalms 144:11 Save me and rescue me from foreigners whose words are meaningless and whose right hand is dishonest.
Psalms 144:12 Let our sons be like strong, young plants; let our daughters be like elegant pillars shaped to decorate a palace.
Psalms 144:13 Let our barns be full, supplying every kind of provision; let our sheep multiply by thousands and tens of thousands in our fields.
Psalms 144:14 Let our oxen be strong for work; let there be no break-ins or escapes; let there be no complaining in our streets.
Psalms 144:15 Happy are the people who are in such a situation; yes, happy are the people whose God is the Lord.
