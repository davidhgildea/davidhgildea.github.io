Psalms 131:1 God, my heart is not proud, nor are my eyes lofty. I do not concern myself with great or too difficult things.
Psalms 131:2 I have calmed and quieted myself like a child who no longer drinks milk from its mother; my soul is like that content child.
Psalms 131:3 Let Israel put their hope in God now and forever.
