Psalms 38:1 God, don't scold me in your anger or discipline me in your rage.
Psalms 38:2 Your arrows are stuck in me, and your hand presses on me hard.
Psalms 38:3 My body is not healthy because of your anger; my bones are not at peace because of my sin.
Psalms 38:4 My wrongdoings overwhelm me; they're like a heavy burden, too much for me to carry.
Psalms 38:5 My wounds smell bad and are infected because I acted foolishly.
Psalms 38:6 I am upset; I am bent over a lot; I spend the whole day sad.
Psalms 38:7 My body is full of a terrible sickness, and my flesh is not healthy.
Psalms 38:8 I am weak and very hurt; I have cried out because my heart is troubled.
Psalms 38:9 God, you know all I want; you hear my sighs.
Psalms 38:10 My heart beats fast, my strength is leaving me; even the light in my eyes is gone.
Psalms 38:11 My loved ones and friends keep their distance from my pain; and my relatives stand far away.
Psalms 38:12 Those who want to kill me set traps, and those who want to harm me talk about hurting me and plan lies all day.
Psalms 38:13 But I was like someone who cannot hear, and like someone who cannot speak, I did not open my mouth.
Psalms 38:14 I was like someone who doesn't hear and doesn't speak any criticisms.
Psalms 38:15 I put my hope in you, God, and I know you will listen, my God.
Psalms 38:16 I said, "Listen to me so that they won't celebrate if I stumble and use it against me."
Psalms 38:17 I am about to fall, and my sadness is always with me.
Psalms 38:18 I will admit my wrongs; I will be sorry for my sin.
Psalms 38:19 My enemies are active and powerful, and those who hate me for no reason have become many.
Psalms 38:20 Those who repay good with evil are against me, because I do what is right.
Psalms 38:21 Do not leave me, God; my God, please stay close to me.
Psalms 38:22 Hurry to help me, God my savior.
