Psalms 36:1 The bad person's wrongdoing tells me deep down that they don't respect God at all.
Psalms 36:2 He praises himself until his wrongdoings are seen as hateful.
Psalms 36:3 What he says is wrong and untruthful: he's stopped being wise and doing good.
Psalms 36:4 He plans trouble on his bed; he chooses a path that is not good; he does not hate evil.
Psalms 36:5 Your kindness, God, is as high as the heavens; your faithfulness extends to the clouds.
Psalms 36:6 Your goodness is as big as the mountains; your decisions are very deep: God, you protect both people and animals.
Psalms 36:7 Your love is wonderful, God! That's why people find safety in the shelter of your wings.
Psalms 36:8 They will be fully content with the good things from your house; and you will let them drink from your river of joys.
Psalms 36:9 With you is the source of life; by your light, we see light.
Psalms 36:10 Keep showing your love to those who know you, and your fairness to those who are true in their hearts.
Psalms 36:11 Don't let proud people attack me, and don't let the wicked push me away.
Psalms 36:12 The evil doers have fallen; they are pushed down and can't get back up.
