Psalms 140:1 Save me, God, from the bad person; protect me from the violent one.
Psalms 140:2 They plan trouble in their hearts; they always meet to fight.
Psalms 140:3 They have made their tongues sharp like a snake's; snake venom is under their lips. Pause and think about this.
Psalms 140:4 God, protect me from the wicked; keep me safe from the violent man who plans to make me fall.
Psalms 140:5 The proud have set a trap for me, and ropes; they have laid a net beside the path; they have placed snares for me. Pause and think about that.
Psalms 140:6 I said to God, "You are my God; listen to my pleas for help, O God."
Psalms 140:7 God, the source of my rescue, you have protected me during battle.
Psalms 140:8 Do not give the wicked what they want, God; do not let their evil plans succeed, so they won't become proud. Pause.
Psalms 140:9 Let the trouble their words cause come back on them.
Psalms 140:10 Let hot coals fall on them; let them be thrown into the fire, into deep holes, so they can't get up again.
Psalms 140:11 Don't let a person who speaks evil be secure on earth: evil will chase the violent man to bring him down.
Psalms 140:12 I know that God will support the cause of the suffering and the rights of the poor.
Psalms 140:13 Truly, good people will thank your name; honest people will live with you.
