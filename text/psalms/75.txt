Psalms 75:1 We thank you, God, we give you thanks, because your name is close and your amazing deeds show it.
Psalms 75:2 When I gather the people, I will judge fairly.
Psalms 75:3 The earth and everyone living on it are falling apart; I support its foundations. Pause and think about that.
Psalms 75:4 I told the foolish, "Don't act foolishly," and to the wicked, "Don't raise your power."
Psalms 75:5 Don't raise your horn proudly; don't talk with arrogance.
Psalms 75:6 Success doesn't come from the east, west, or south.
Psalms 75:7 God is the judge: He brings down one and lifts up another.
Psalms 75:8 In God's hand is a cup filled with strong red wine, mixed well; He pours it, and all the wicked on earth must drink its bitter remains.
Psalms 75:9 I will always tell of God's deeds; I will sing praise to Jacob's God.
Psalms 75:10 I will cut off all the power of the wicked, but the power of the good people will be lifted up.
