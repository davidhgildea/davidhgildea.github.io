Psalms 19:1 The skies show God's greatness; and the sky displays what his hands have made.
Psalms 19:2 Every day speaks, and every night teaches knowledge.
Psalms 19:3 Every language and speech can hear their voice.
Psalms 19:4 Their message has spread all over the world, and their words reach the ends of the earth. God has made a home in the sky for the sun.
Psalms 19:5 It is like a groom coming out of his room, happy like a runner ready to race.
Psalms 19:6 He comes from the farthest part of the sky, moves in a circle to the ends, and nothing can hide from its heat.
Psalms 19:7 God's law is perfect and refreshes the soul. God's promises are trustworthy, giving wisdom to the simple.
Psalms 19:8 God's laws are fair, making the heart glad; God's rule is clear, giving light to the eyes.
Psalms 19:9 Respecting God is pure, lasting forever; God's decisions are completely true and fair.
Psalms 19:10 They are more valuable than gold, even a lot of pure gold; they are also sweeter than honey, even honey from the comb.
Psalms 19:11 Your teachings warn your servant, and great reward comes from following them.
Psalms 19:12 Who can know their own mistakes? Cleanse me from my hidden faults.
Psalms 19:13 Help me avoid sins done in pride; don't let them control me. Then I will be blameless and free from serious guilt.
Psalms 19:14 May the words I speak and the thoughts in my heart please you, God, my strength and my savior.
