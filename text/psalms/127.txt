Psalms 127:1 Unless God builds the house, those who work on it waste their effort; unless God protects the city, the guard stays awake for nothing.
Psalms 127:2 It's useless to wake up early and go to bed late, working hard for a living; God gives rest to those he loves.
Psalms 127:3 Children are a gift from God, and the babies born to us are his reward.
Psalms 127:4 Like arrows in a strong person's hand are the children born in one's youth.
Psalms 127:5 A happy person has many children. They won't be embarrassed but will talk to their enemies confidently.
