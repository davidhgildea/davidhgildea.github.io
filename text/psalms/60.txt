Psalms 60:1 God, you have rejected us, scattered us, and been unhappy with us; please come back to us.
Psalms 60:2 You have made the earth shake; you have split it open: repair its cracks, for it is quaking.
Psalms 60:3 You have shown your people tough times; you have made us drink the wine of confusion.
Psalms 60:4 You have given a flag to those who respect you, so it can be shown because of the truth. Pause.
Psalms 60:5 Let your loved one be rescued; save them with your power and listen to me.
Psalms 60:6 God spoke in his holy place; I will be glad, I will split Shechem, and share out the Succoth valley.
Psalms 60:7 Gilead belongs to me, and Manasseh is mine; Ephraim is my strong head; Judah makes my laws.
Psalms 60:8 Moab is like my washbasin; I will throw my shoe over Edom; Philistia, celebrate because of me.
Psalms 60:9 Who will take me into the fortified city? Who will guide me into Edom?
Psalms 60:10 Won't you, God, who rejected us before, and you, God, who did not go with our armies?
Psalms 60:11 Help us in trouble, for human help is useless.
Psalms 60:12 With God's help, we will act bravely because he will defeat our enemies.
