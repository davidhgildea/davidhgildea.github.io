Psalms 55:1 Listen to my prayer, God, and do not ignore my request.
Psalms 55:2 Listen to me and hear me: I am sad and complaining, and I am loud.
Psalms 55:3 Because of the enemy's voice and the wicked's cruelty, they blame me for wrongs and hate me in anger.
Psalms 55:4 My heart hurts deeply inside me, and fears of death have come over me.
Psalms 55:5 I am scared and shaking, and horror has taken over me.
Psalms 55:6 I wished for wings like a dove so I could fly away and find peace.
Psalms 55:7 Look, then I would go far away and stay in the desert. Pause.
Psalms 55:8 I would hurry to escape from the windy storm and tempest.
Psalms 55:9 God, destroy and confuse their speech because I have seen violence and conflict in the city.
Psalms 55:10 Day and night they move around on its walls; trouble and sadness are in its center.
Psalms 55:11 Evil is everywhere in it: lies and trickery never leave its streets.
Psalms 55:12 It wasn't an enemy who insulted me; if it were, I could have endured it. It wasn't someone who hated me who boasted against me; if it were, I could have hidden from him.
Psalms 55:13 But you were my equal, my guide, and my friend.
Psalms 55:14 We shared good advice together and went to God's house with others.
Psalms 55:15 May death take them, and may they quickly go down to hell; for evil is in their homes and with them.
Psalms 55:16 I will call on God, and God will save me.
Psalms 55:17 I will pray in the evening, in the morning, and at noon. I will cry out loudly, and God will hear me.
Psalms 55:18 God has saved my life peacefully from the fight against me, because many were with me.
Psalms 55:19 God will listen and punish them, even the one from long ago. Pause. They don't change, so they don't fear God.
Psalms 55:20 He has attacked those who were at peace with him; he has broken his agreement.
Psalms 55:21 His mouth spoke nice words like butter, but his heart planned for war; his words were gentle like oil, but they were really sharp swords.
Psalms 55:22 Give your worries to God, and he will support you; he will never let good people be shaken.
Psalms 55:23 But you, God, will send the violent and lying people to a place of ruin: they won't live even half their expected years; but I will rely on you.
