Psalms 62:1 My soul truly waits for God; my rescue comes from Him.
Psalms 62:2 He alone is my rock and my savior; he protects me; I won't be shaken much.
Psalms 62:3 How long will you plan harm against someone? You will all be killed: you will be like a leaning wall or a shaky fence.
Psalms 62:4 They only plot to knock him from his high place; they enjoy lying; they praise with their words, but inside they curse. Pause and think about that.
Psalms 62:5 My soul, wait only for God; my hope comes from him.
Psalms 62:6 God is my only rock and savior; he protects me; I won't be shaken.
Psalms 62:7 God is my savior and my honor; my strong protector and my shelter is in God.
Psalms 62:8 Always trust in God; people, share your deepest feelings with Him: God is our safe place. Selah.
Psalms 62:9 Truly, people of low rank are worthless, and those of high rank are not truthful; if weighed, they are all less than worthless together.
Psalms 62:10 Do not trust in harsh treatment, and do not be proud of stealing. If you get richer, do not fix your heart on the money.
Psalms 62:11 God has spoken once; I have heard this twice: power belongs to God.
Psalms 62:12 To you, God, belongs kindness: because you give to each person based on what they do.
