Ephesians 4:1 So I, a prisoner for God, ask you to live a life that reflects the calling you have received.
Ephesians 4:2 With complete humility and gentleness, with patience, tolerating each other in love;
Ephesians 4:3 Trying to maintain the unity of the Spirit through the bond of peace.
Ephesians 4:4 There is one body and one spirit, just as you are called to one hope when you were called.
Ephesians 4:5 One God, one belief, one baptism.
Ephesians 4:6 There is one God, the Father of everyone, who is over all, works through all, and lives in all of you.
Ephesians 4:7 Each of us is given kindness by God as much as Christ has decided.
Ephesians 4:8 So he says, when he went up to the high place, he took prisoners and gave gifts to people.
Ephesians 4:9 He went up, but before that, he went down to the lower parts of the earth.
Ephesians 4:10 The one who came down is also the one who went up, way above all the skies, so he could be in everything.
Ephesians 4:11 He gave some to be apostles, others prophets, others evangelists, and others to be pastors and teachers.
Ephesians 4:12 To improve God's people, for serving work, for building up Christ's group:
Ephesians 4:13 Until we all reach unity in faith and understanding of God's Son, becoming mature, achieving the full standard of Christ.
Ephesians 4:14 We should no longer be like children, pushed around by every new teaching from deceitful people who are experts at tricking others.
Ephesians 4:15 Tell the truth with love, and grow in every way to become like Christ, who is the leader.
Ephesians 4:16 The whole body is closely joined and united by every supporting joint, and each part does its work, making the body grow and build itself up with love.
Ephesians 4:17 I say this and confirm it: from now on, don't live like the other non-Jews who follow their empty thoughts.
Ephesians 4:18 Their understanding is clouded, and they are cut off from God's life because they don't know any better and their hearts are blind.
Ephesians 4:19 They no longer feel guilty and have let themselves do indecent things, wanting more and more.
Ephesians 4:20 You have not learned about Christ in this way;
Ephesians 4:21 If you have heard Jesus and were taught by him, because truth is in Jesus.
Ephesians 4:22 Stop living the old way of life, which is ruined by seeking dishonest pleasures.
Ephesians 4:23 Let your mind's attitude become fresh and new.
Ephesians 4:24 Wear your new self, made like God in true goodness and holiness.
Ephesians 4:25 So stop lying and always tell the truth to each other, because we are all part of the same group.
Ephesians 4:26 Get angry, but don't sin. Don't let the sun set while you are still angry.
Ephesians 4:27 Do not let the devil have a way in.
Ephesians 4:28 If someone has been stealing, they should stop. Instead, they should work hard and do good things with their hands, so they can share with others who are in need.
Ephesians 4:29 Don't let any bad words come out of your mouth, but only good words that help others become stronger, so that it can bring kindness to those who listen.
Ephesians 4:30 Do not make God's Holy Spirit sad, for He has marked you for the day of being saved.
Ephesians 4:31 Get rid of all bitterness, rage, anger, shouting, and mean talk, along with all spitefulness.
Ephesians 4:32 Be kind to each other, be gentle, and forgive each other, just as God has forgiven you because of Christ.
