Hebrews 13:1 Keep loving each other like brothers and sisters.
Hebrews 13:2 Don't forget to welcome strangers, because by doing this some people have welcomed angels without realizing it.
Hebrews 13:3 Remember those in prison as if you were there with them, and think of those who are mistreated as if you were suffering too.
Hebrews 13:4 Marriage is respected by everyone, and the marriage bed should be kept pure. But God will judge those who sell sex and cheat in marriage.
Hebrews 13:5 Speak without wanting more, and be happy with what you have: because God has promised, I will always be with you and never abandon you.
Hebrews 13:6 We can say confidently, God helps me, so I will not be afraid of what anyone does to me.
Hebrews 13:7 Remember your leaders who taught you God's message; copy their faith, looking at how their lives ended.
Hebrews 13:8 Jesus Christ is the same in the past, today, and forever.
Hebrews 13:9 Do not be led away by various and strange teachings. It is good for our hearts to be strengthened by kindness, not by food rules which have not helped those who follow them.
Hebrews 13:10 We have an altar that those who work in the place of worship have no right to eat from.
Hebrews 13:11 The bodies of those animals, whose blood the high priest brings into the holy place for sin, are burned outside the camp.
Hebrews 13:12 So Jesus, to make the people holy with his blood, suffered outside the city gate.
Hebrews 13:13 Let's go out to him outside the camp, carrying his shame.
Hebrews 13:14 We have no lasting city here, but we look for one in the future.
Hebrews 13:15 So let us always offer to God our praise, which is saying thanks to him with our words.
Hebrews 13:16 Don't forget to do good and to share with others: God is very pleased with these kinds of sacrifices.
Hebrews 13:17 Listen to those who lead you, and follow their guidance; they are responsible for your well-being and must explain their actions, so let them do so happily, not sadly, because that would be bad for you.
Hebrews 13:18 Pray for us, because we believe we have a clear conscience and want to live right in everything.
Hebrews 13:19 I urge you to do this so I can return to you more quickly.
Hebrews 13:20 Now the God of peace, who brought back our Lord Jesus from the dead, the great shepherd of the sheep, through the blood of the eternal promise,
Hebrews 13:21 Help you be complete in every good action to do his will, doing in you what pleases him, through Jesus Christ; to whom be glory forever and ever. Amen.
Hebrews 13:22 And I ask you, friends, to accept these words of encouragement, for I have written a short letter to you.
Hebrews 13:23 Understand that our brother Timothy has been freed; if he arrives soon, I will visit you with him.
Hebrews 13:24 Greet all who lead you and all of God's people. Those in Italy send you greetings.
Hebrews 13:25 May God's kindness be with everyone. Amen.
