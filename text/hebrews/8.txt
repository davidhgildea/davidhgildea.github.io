Hebrews 8:1 Here's the main point of what we've said: We have a high priest who sits at the right side of the throne of God in heaven.
Hebrews 8:2 A servant of the holy place and the real tent that God set up, not humans.
Hebrews 8:3 Every high priest is chosen to give gifts and sacrifices, so this man must have something to offer too.
Hebrews 8:4 If he were on earth, he wouldn't be a priest because there are already priests who give gifts following the law.
Hebrews 8:5 Those who serve are working with a copy and shadow of the things in heaven, just as Moses was warned by God when he was about to build the tent: God told him, "Make sure you make everything according to the design shown to you on the mountain."
Hebrews 8:6 He now has a greater service because he is the mediator of a better agreement, which is based on better promises.
Hebrews 8:7 If the first agreement had been perfect, there would be no need to look for a second one.
Hebrews 8:8 Because he found faults with them, God says, "Look, the time is coming when I will make a new agreement with the people of Israel and the people of Judah."
Hebrews 8:9 This is not like the agreement I made with their ancestors when I took their hand to guide them out of Egypt; they did not keep my agreement, so I ignored them, says God.
Hebrews 8:10 This is the agreement that I will set up with the people of Israel after that time, says God; I will place my rules in their minds and write them on their hearts. I will be their God, and they will be my people.
Hebrews 8:11 They won't need to teach their neighbors or their brothers, saying, 'Know God,' because everyone, from the least to the greatest, will know me.
Hebrews 8:12 I will be kind to their wrongs, and I will not remember their sins and bad acts anymore.
Hebrews 8:13 By saying "a new agreement," he has made the first one old. And what is getting old and wearing out will soon disappear.
