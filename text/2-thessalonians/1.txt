2 Thessalonians 1:1 Paul, Silvanus, and Timothy, to the church of the Thessalonians in God our Father and Jesus Christ:
2 Thessalonians 1:2 May you have grace and peace from God our Father and Jesus Christ.
2 Thessalonians 1:3 We must always thank God for you, brothers, because it is right. Your faith is growing a lot, and the love you all have for each other is increasing.
2 Thessalonians 1:4 We are proud of you among God's churches because of your patience and faith during all the persecutions and troubles you go through.
2 Thessalonians 1:5 This clearly shows God's fair judgment, so that you can be seen as fit for God's kingdom, which you are also suffering for.
2 Thessalonians 1:6 God thinks it is fair to give trouble to those who trouble you.
2 Thessalonians 1:7 If you are troubled, find rest with us when the Lord Jesus comes from heaven with his powerful angels.
2 Thessalonians 1:8 With fiery flames, punishing those who do not know God and do not follow the teachings of our Lord Jesus Christ.
2 Thessalonians 1:9 They will be punished with never-ending ruin, away from God and his mighty glory.
2 Thessalonians 1:10 When he comes to receive glory in his holy people and to be marveled at among all who have believed (because you believed our testimony to you) on that day.
2 Thessalonians 1:11 So we always pray for you that God will think you worthy of his call, and will complete every good thing you desire and your work of faith with strength.
2 Thessalonians 1:12 May our Lord Jesus Christ's name be honored through you, and you through him, by the kindness of our God and the Lord Jesus Christ.
