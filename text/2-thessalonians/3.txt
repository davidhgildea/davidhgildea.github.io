2 Thessalonians 3:1 Friends, please pray for us, so that God's message can spread freely and be honored, just like with you.
2 Thessalonians 3:2 So that we can be rescued from people who are unfair and evil: not everyone has faith.
2 Thessalonians 3:3 God is faithful and will make you strong and protect you from evil.
2 Thessalonians 3:4 We trust in God about you, that you are and will continue to follow the instructions we give you.
2 Thessalonians 3:5 May God guide your hearts to love God and to wait patiently for Christ.
2 Thessalonians 3:6 We tell you, friends, speak for Jesus Christ and stay away from anyone who does not follow the teachings we gave and does not act right.
2 Thessalonians 3:7 You know how you should imitate us: we did not act improperly among you.
2 Thessalonians 3:8 We did not eat anyone's bread without paying; we worked hard day and night so we would not be a burden to any of you.
2 Thessalonians 3:9 Not because we lack the authority, but to be an example for you to imitate us.
2 Thessalonians 3:10 Even when we were with you, we told you: If anyone won't work, they shouldn't eat.
2 Thessalonians 3:11 We hear that some among you are acting out of line, not working at all, but meddling in others' business.
2 Thessalonians 3:12 We tell and encourage those people, by Jesus Christ, to work calmly and eat their own food.
2 Thessalonians 3:13 Friends, don't get tired of doing good.
2 Thessalonians 3:14 If anyone does not follow our instructions in this letter, take note of that person and do not associate with him, so he may feel ashamed.
2 Thessalonians 3:15 Don't see him as an enemy, but warn him as a brother.
2 Thessalonians 3:16 May God, the Lord of peace, give you peace at all times and in every way. May God be with all of you.
2 Thessalonians 3:17 This is my greeting in every letter, all written by me, Paul.
2 Thessalonians 3:18 May the kindness of our Lord Jesus Christ be with everyone. Amen.
