Matthew 15:1 Some teachers of the law and Pharisees from Jerusalem came to Jesus and asked,
Matthew 15:2 Why do your followers break the elders' tradition? They don't wash their hands before eating bread.
Matthew 15:3 But he replied and said to them, "Why do you also break God's command by following your tradition?"
Matthew 15:4 God commanded, saying, Respect your father and mother; and, Anyone who speaks badly of their father or mother must be put to death.
Matthew 15:5 But you say, if anyone tells their father or mother, 'What you could have gained from me is a gift to God',
Matthew 15:6 If someone does not respect their father or mother, you say they are free from guilt. This way, you cancel out God's rule because of your tradition.
Matthew 15:7 You pretenders, Isaiah was right when he prophesied about you, saying,
Matthew 15:8 These people come close to me with their words and honor me with their lips, but their hearts are far from me.
Matthew 15:9 They worship me for nothing, teaching human rules as if they were God's teachings.
Matthew 15:10 He called the crowd and said to them, "Listen and understand."
Matthew 15:11 What goes into the mouth does not make a person unclean; it's what comes out of the mouth that makes a person unclean.
Matthew 15:12 His disciples came and asked him, "Do you know that the Pharisees were upset when they heard what you said?"
Matthew 15:13 He replied, "Every plant that my Father in heaven did not plant will be pulled out."
Matthew 15:14 Leave them; they are blind guides. If a blind person leads another blind person, both will fall into a hole.
Matthew 15:15 Peter replied and asked him, "Explain this story to us."
Matthew 15:16 Jesus asked, "Do you still not understand?"
Matthew 15:17 Don't you understand that whatever goes into the mouth passes into the stomach and then is eliminated?
Matthew 15:18 The things that come out of the mouth start in the heart, and these make the person unclean.
Matthew 15:19 Evil thoughts, murders, cheating in marriage, sexual misconduct, stealing, lying, and insults all come from the heart.
Matthew 15:20 These things make a person unclean: but eating with unwashed hands does not make a person unclean.
Matthew 15:21 Jesus left that place and went to the region of Tyre and Sidon.
Matthew 15:22 Look, a Canaanite woman came from that area and called out to him, "Please have mercy on me, Sir, son of David; my daughter is suffering terribly because of an evil spirit."
Matthew 15:23 He did not reply to her at all. His followers came and urged him, "Tell her to go away; she keeps shouting at us."
Matthew 15:24 He replied, "I am only sent to the lost sheep of Israel."
Matthew 15:25 She came and worshiped him, saying, "Sir, help me."
Matthew 15:26 But he replied, "It's not right to take the children's bread and throw it to dogs."
Matthew 15:27 She said, "Yes, God, but even the dogs eat the crumbs that fall from their owners' table."
Matthew 15:28 Then Jesus replied to her, "Woman, your faith is strong; let it happen for you as you wish." And her daughter was healed at that moment.
Matthew 15:29 Jesus left that place and went near the Sea of Galilee; He climbed a mountain and sat down there.
Matthew 15:30 Many people came to him, bringing the lame, blind, mute, crippled, and many others, and placed them at Jesus' feet; and he healed them.
Matthew 15:31 The crowd was amazed to see those who couldn't speak talking, the injured healed, the lame walking, and the blind seeing; and they praised the God of Israel.
Matthew 15:32 Jesus called his followers to him and said, "I feel sorry for all the people because they have been with me for three days and have nothing to eat. I don't want to send them away hungry, or they might get weak on their journey."
Matthew 15:33 His followers asked him, "Where can we get enough bread in this remote place to feed such a large crowd?"
Matthew 15:34 Jesus asked them, "How many loaves do you have?" They replied, "Seven, and a few small fish."
Matthew 15:35 He told the people to sit down on the ground.
Matthew 15:36 He took the seven loaves and the fish, thanked God, broke them, and gave them to his followers, and they gave them to the crowd.
Matthew 15:37 Everyone ate and was satisfied. They collected seven baskets full of leftover pieces.
Matthew 15:38 About four thousand men ate, not counting women and children.
Matthew 15:39 He sent the crowd away, got into a boat, and went to the region of Magdala.
