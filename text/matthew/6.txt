Matthew 6 — Simplified Bible (simplified.bible)

Matthew 6:1 Be careful not to do good things in front of others just to be noticed, because if you do, you won't get any reward from your Father in heaven.
Matthew 6:2 When you give to the needy, don't announce it loudly like hypocrites in the synagogues and streets for attention. Truly, they've received their reward.
Matthew 6:3 When you give to those in need, don't let one hand know what the other is doing.
Matthew 6:4 Give your help to others quietly, and God, who sees what is done in secret, will reward you openly.
Matthew 6:5 When you pray, don't be like the fakers who like to pray in public places just to be seen by others. Truly, they have gotten all they will get.
Matthew 6:6 When you pray, go into your room and close the door, then pray to your Father who is unseen; and your Father, who sees what's done in secret, will reward you.
Matthew 6:7 When you pray, don't keep on repeating words with no meaning, like other people do. They believe they will be heard because they talk a lot.
Matthew 6:8 Do not be like them, because your Father knows what you need before you ask him.
Matthew 6:9 Pray like this: Our Father in heaven, may your name be honored.
Matthew 6:10 Let your kingdom come, let your will be done on earth, just like it is in heaven.
Matthew 6:11 Give us today the food we need.
Matthew 6:12 Forgive us what we owe, as we forgive those who owe us.
Matthew 6:13 Do not let us be tempted, but save us from evil: for the kingdom, the power, and the glory are yours, forever. Amen.
Matthew 6:14 If you forgive people for what they've done wrong, God in heaven will forgive you too.
Matthew 6:15 If you don't forgive others for their wrongs, God won't forgive your wrongs either.
Matthew 6:16 When you fast, don't be like the fakes who look sad to show others they are fasting. Truly, that's the only reward they will get.
Matthew 6:17 When you fast, put oil on your head and wash your face.
Matthew 6:18 Do not show others that you are fasting; only God, who is unseen, should know. And God, who sees what is done in secret, will reward you.
Matthew 6:19 Do not store up treasures for yourself on earth, where moths and rust destroy, and where thieves break in and steal.
Matthew 6:20 Store up for yourselves treasures in heaven, where moths and rust cannot destroy, and where thieves do not break in and steal.
Matthew 6:21 Where you keep your treasure is where your heart will be too.
Matthew 6:22 Your eye is like a lamp for your body. If your eye is clear, your whole body will be bright.
Matthew 6:23 But if your eye is bad, your whole body will be full of darkness. If the light inside you is dark, how deep is that darkness!
Matthew 6:24 A person can't serve two bosses: they will either hate one and love the other, or be loyal to one and not like the other. You can't serve God and money.
Matthew 6:25 So I tell you, don't worry about your life, what you will eat or drink; or about your body, what you will wear. Isn't life more than food and the body more than clothes?
Matthew 6:26 Look at the birds of the sky: they don't plant seeds, harvest, or store food in barns, but God feeds them. Aren't you worth more than they are?
Matthew 6:27 Who of you can add an inch to your height by worrying?
Matthew 6:28 Why worry about clothes? Look at the field lilies; they don't work or make cloth.
Matthew 6:29 I tell you, even Solomon in all his splendor was not dressed as beautifully as one of these.
Matthew 6:30 So, if God dresses the grass in the field, which is here today and thrown into the fire tomorrow, won't he dress you even more, you with little faith?
Matthew 6:31 So don't worry, asking, 'What will we eat?' or 'What will we drink?' or 'What will we wear?'
Matthew 6:32 The non-believers chase after all these things, but your Father in heaven knows you need them.
Matthew 6:33 First, look for God's kingdom and do what is right; then, all these things will be given to you.
Matthew 6:34 Do not worry about tomorrow, for tomorrow will worry about itself. Each day has enough trouble of its own.
