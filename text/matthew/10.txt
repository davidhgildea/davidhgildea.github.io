Matthew 10:1 When he gathered his twelve followers, he gave them authority over evil spirits, to drive them out, and to cure every kind of illness and disease.
Matthew 10:2 The twelve apostles' names are: The first is Simon, called Peter, and his brother Andrew; James, Zebedee's son, and his brother John.
Matthew 10:3 Philip and Bartholomew; Thomas and Matthew the tax collector; James son of Alphaeus, and Lebbaeus, also called Thaddaeus.
Matthew 10:4 Simon from Canaan, and Judas Iscariot, who betrayed him.
Matthew 10:5 Jesus sent out these twelve and told them, "Do not go to the Gentile areas or enter any Samaritan city.
Matthew 10:6 Go instead to the lost sheep of Israel's family.
Matthew 10:7 As you go, tell others, "God's kingdom is near."
Matthew 10:8 Cure sick people, make lepers clean, bring dead back to life, throw out demons: you got it for free, give it for free.
Matthew 10:9 Don't carry gold, silver, or coins in your wallets.
Matthew 10:10 Don't take a bag for your trip, or two coats, or shoes, or a walking stick: because a worker deserves his food.
Matthew 10:11 When you enter a city or town, find out who is good there and stay with them until you leave.
Matthew 10:12 When you enter a house, greet it.
Matthew 10:13 If the home is good, let your peace rest on it; if not, let your peace come back to you.
Matthew 10:14 If anyone does not welcome you or listen to your words, leave that home or town and shake the dust from your feet.
Matthew 10:15 I truly tell you, on Judgment Day, the land of Sodom and Gomorrah will have an easier time than that city.
Matthew 10:16 I send you out like sheep among wolves: so be as smart as snakes and as kind as doves.
Matthew 10:17 Be careful of people: they will hand you over to councils and whip you in their places of worship.
Matthew 10:18 You will stand before rulers and kings because of me, to witness to them and the nations.
Matthew 10:19 When they arrest you, don't worry about what to say, because you will be told what to say at that time.
Matthew 10:20 It's not you speaking, but your Father's Spirit speaking through you.
Matthew 10:21 A brother will turn in his brother to be killed, and a father will turn in his child. Children will rebel against their parents and have them killed.
Matthew 10:22 Everyone will hate you because of me, but if you keep going to the end, you will be saved.
Matthew 10:23 If people chase you away from one town, run to another. Truly, I tell you, you won't finish visiting the towns of Israel before the Son of Man arrives.
Matthew 10:24 A student is not greater than their teacher, nor is a servant greater than their master.
Matthew 10:25 A student should be like their teacher, and a servant like their boss. If people have called the head of the house Beelzebub, they will call the others in the house even worse names.
Matthew 10:26 Do not be afraid of them: because nothing is hidden that will not be shown; and nothing is secret that will not become known.
Matthew 10:27 What I say to you in secret, you must say in public; and what you hear whispered, you must announce from the rooftops.
Matthew 10:28 Do not be afraid of those who can kill the body but cannot kill the soul. Instead, be afraid of God who can destroy both soul and body in hell.
Matthew 10:29 Aren't two sparrows sold for a small coin? And none of them will fall to the ground without God knowing.
Matthew 10:30 Every hair on your head is counted.
Matthew 10:31 Do not be afraid; you are worth more than many sparrows.
Matthew 10:32 If anyone tells others about me, I will tell my Father in heaven about them.
Matthew 10:33 Whoever denies me before others, I will also deny them before my God in heaven.
Matthew 10:34 Don't think that I came to bring peace on earth: I didn't come to bring peace, but to bring a sword.
Matthew 10:35 I have come to cause disagreement between a man and his father, between a daughter and her mother, and between a daughter-in-law and her mother-in-law.
Matthew 10:36 A person's enemies will be the members of their own family.
Matthew 10:37 If you love your father or mother more than you love me, you are not good enough to be my follower. And if you love your son or daughter more than me, you are not good enough to be my follower.
Matthew 10:38 Anyone who does not take up their cross and follow me is not good enough for me.
Matthew 10:39 Whoever finds their life will lose it, and whoever loses their life for me will find it.
Matthew 10:40 Whoever welcomes you welcomes me, and whoever welcomes me welcomes the one who sent me.
Matthew 10:41 If you welcome a prophet because he is a prophet, you will get the same reward as a prophet; and if you welcome a good person because he is good, you will get the same reward as a good person.
Matthew 10:42 Anyone who gives even a cup of cold water to one of these little ones because they are my follower will truly not lose their reward.
