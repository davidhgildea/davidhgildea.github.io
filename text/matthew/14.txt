Matthew 14:1 At that time, Herod the ruler heard about Jesus's fame.
Matthew 14:2 He told his servants, "This is John the Baptist; he has risen from the dead, and that's why he can do powerful things."
Matthew 14:3 Herod arrested John, tied him up, and put him in jail because of Herodias, his brother Philip's wife.
Matthew 14:4 John told him, "You should not have her."
Matthew 14:5 He wanted to kill him but was afraid of the people, because they thought he was a prophet.
Matthew 14:6 On Herod's birthday, Herodias's daughter danced for them and made Herod happy.
Matthew 14:7 He promised with a promise to give her anything she asked for.
Matthew 14:8 Her mother had told her what to say, so she said, "Give me John the Baptist's head on a plate right here."
Matthew 14:9 The king felt sad, but because of his promise and the guests at his table, he ordered that her request be granted.
Matthew 14:10 He sent someone and had John beheaded in the prison.
Matthew 14:11 They brought his head on a platter, gave it to the young woman, and she took it to her mother.
Matthew 14:12 His followers came, picked up the body, buried it, and then went to tell Jesus.
Matthew 14:13 When Jesus learned about it, he left by boat to a remote desert area. Hearing this, the people walked from the towns to follow him.
Matthew 14:14 Jesus went out, saw many people, felt sorry for them, and healed their sick.
Matthew 14:15 In the evening, his followers came to him and said, "This is a remote place, and it's getting late; tell the crowd to leave so they can go to the nearby towns and buy food for themselves."
Matthew 14:16 Jesus said to them, "They don't need to leave; give them something to eat."
Matthew 14:17 They said to him, "We only have five loaves of bread and two fish here."
Matthew 14:18 He said, "Bring them here to me."
Matthew 14:19 He told the people to sit on the grass, took the five loaves and two fish, looked up to the sky, said a blessing, broke the bread, and gave it to his followers, who then gave it to everyone.
Matthew 14:20 Everyone ate and was satisfied, and they gathered twelve baskets full of leftover pieces.
Matthew 14:21 About five thousand men ate, not counting women and children.
Matthew 14:22 Right away, Jesus made his disciples get into a boat and go ahead of him to the other side while he sent the crowds away.
Matthew 14:23 After he told the crowds to go, he went up a mountain by himself to pray. When night came, he was there alone.
Matthew 14:24 The boat was in the middle of the sea, being thrown about by waves, because the wind was against it.
Matthew 14:25 In the last part of the night, Jesus went to them, walking on the water.
Matthew 14:26 When the disciples saw him walking on the water, they were scared and said, "It's a ghost!" and they screamed with fear.
Matthew 14:27 Right away Jesus spoke to them, saying, "Be happy; it's me; don't be scared."
Matthew 14:28 Peter answered, "Lord, if it's really you, tell me to come to you on the water."
Matthew 14:29 He said, "Come." So Peter got out of the boat and walked on the water to go to Jesus.
Matthew 14:30 But when he saw the strong wind, he was scared; and starting to sink, he shouted, "God, save me."
Matthew 14:31 Right away, Jesus reached out his hand, grabbed him, and said, "You with little faith, why did you doubt?"
Matthew 14:32 When they got into the boat, the wind stopped.
Matthew 14:33 The people in the boat came to him and honored him, saying, "Truly, you are the Son of God."
Matthew 14:34 After they crossed over, they arrived in the land of Gennesaret.
Matthew 14:35 When the people there recognized him, they spread the word throughout the region and brought all the sick to him.
Matthew 14:36 They asked to touch just the edge of his clothes, and everyone who touched it was completely healed.
