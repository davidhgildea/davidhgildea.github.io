Matthew 12:1 One Sabbath, Jesus walked through the grain fields, and his hungry disciples started to pick and eat some heads of grain.
Matthew 12:2 When the Pharisees saw this, they said to him, "Look, your disciples are doing what is not allowed on the Sabbath day."
Matthew 12:3 But he said to them, "Haven't you read what David did when he and his companions were hungry?
Matthew 12:4 He went into God's house and ate the bread that only priests should eat, which was against the law for him and his companions.
Matthew 12:5 Have you not read in the law that on Sabbath days the priests in the temple break the Sabbath rules, yet are not guilty?
Matthew 12:6 I tell you, someone greater than the temple is here.
Matthew 12:7 If you knew what it means 'I want kindness, not offerings,' you wouldn't have judged innocent people.
Matthew 12:8 The Son of man is master even of the Sabbath day.
Matthew 12:9 After he left that place, he went into their place of worship.
Matthew 12:10 Look, there was a man with a crippled hand. They asked, "Is it okay to heal on the Sabbath?" so they could blame him.
Matthew 12:11 He asked them, "Who among you, if you have one sheep and it falls into a hole on the Sabbath, wouldn't grab it and pull it out?"
Matthew 12:12 How much more valuable is a person than a sheep? So, it is right to do good on the Sabbath.
Matthew 12:13 Then he said to the man, "Stretch out your hand." And he stretched it out, and it was healed and became as healthy as the other one.
Matthew 12:14 Then the Pharisees left and planned together how to kill Jesus.
Matthew 12:15 When Jesus realized this, he left that place. Many people followed him, and he healed everyone.
Matthew 12:16 He ordered them not to tell anyone about him.
Matthew 12:17 This happened to fulfill what the prophet Isaiah had said:
Matthew 12:18 Look at my servant, whom I have picked; my loved one, who pleases me so much. I will give him my spirit, and he will bring fairness to all nations.
Matthew 12:19 He will not argue or shout; no one will hear his voice in the streets.
Matthew 12:20 He will not break a bent reed or put out a weak flame until he leads justice to victory.
Matthew 12:21 People from other nations will trust in his name.
Matthew 12:22 Someone controlled by a demon, who could not see or speak, was brought to Jesus, and he cured him so that he could both talk and see.
Matthew 12:23 Everyone was amazed and asked, "Isn't this David's son?"
Matthew 12:24 When the Pharisees heard this, they said, "This man doesn't drive out demons except by Beelzebub, the ruler of demons."
Matthew 12:25 Jesus knew what they were thinking and told them, "A kingdom that fights itself will be ruined; and a city or home that is split will not last."
Matthew 12:26 If Satan drives out Satan, he is fighting against himself; how can his kingdom last?
Matthew 12:27 If I drive out demons using Beelzebub, who do your own people use to drive them out? They will be the ones to judge you.
Matthew 12:28 If I drive out demons with God's Spirit, then God's kingdom has come to you.
Matthew 12:29 How can someone get into a strong man's house and take his stuff unless he first ties up the strong man? Then he can rob his house.
Matthew 12:30 If you're not with me, you're against me; if you don't gather with me, you spread things out.
Matthew 12:31 So I tell you, people will be forgiven for all kinds of sins and insults, but insults against the Holy Spirit will not be forgiven.
Matthew 12:32 Anyone who speaks against the Son of man can be forgiven, but anyone who speaks against the Holy Spirit will not be forgiven, either now or in the future.
Matthew 12:33 Make the tree good and its fruit will be good, or make the tree bad and its fruit will be bad, because a tree is recognized by its fruit.
Matthew 12:34 You people are like snakes! How can you say good things when you are evil? What fills the heart will come out of the mouth.
Matthew 12:35 A good person, from the good stored in their heart, produces good things; and a bad person, from the bad stored within, produces bad things.
Matthew 12:36 I tell you, everyone must explain all the useless words they speak on judgment day.
Matthew 12:37 What you say can save or condemn you.
Matthew 12:38 Some teachers of the law and Pharisees spoke up, saying, "Teacher, we want to see a miracle from you."
Matthew 12:39 He replied, "A wicked and unfaithful generation wants a sign, but no sign will be given to it except the sign of the prophet Jonah."
Matthew 12:40 Just as Jonah was in the fish for three days and three nights, the Son of Man will be in the earth for three days and three nights.
Matthew 12:41 The people of Nineveh will stand up at the time of judgment with the people of this time and condemn them: because they turned away from their sins when Jonah preached; and look, someone greater than Jonah is here.
Matthew 12:42 The queen from the south will stand up on judgment day with the people of this time, and she will judge them. She traveled from the farthest part of the earth to listen to Solomon's wisdom; but look, someone greater than Solomon is here.
Matthew 12:43 When an evil spirit leaves a person, it wanders through waterless areas looking for rest but doesn't find any.
Matthew 12:44 Then he says, "I will go back to my house from where I left"; and when he arrives, he finds it empty, clean, and tidy.
Matthew 12:45 Then he goes and brings seven other spirits more evil than himself, and they come in and live there. So the last condition of that person is worse than at the beginning. This will also happen to this evil generation.
Matthew 12:46 While he was still talking to the people, look, his mother and brothers were standing outside, wanting to talk to him.
Matthew 12:47 Someone told him, "Look, your mother and brothers are outside wanting to talk to you."
Matthew 12:48 He replied to the person who spoke to him, "Who is my mother? And who are my brothers?"
Matthew 12:49 He reached out his hand to his followers and said, "Look, my mother and my brothers!"
Matthew 12:50 Anyone who does what my Father in heaven wants is my brother, sister, and mother.
