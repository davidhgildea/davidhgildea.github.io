Matthew 22:1 Jesus answered and told them another story using parables. He said,
Matthew 22:2 God's kingdom is like a king who arranged a wedding for his son.
Matthew 22:3 He sent his servants to invite those who had been asked to the wedding, but they did not want to come.
Matthew 22:4 He sent more servants and said, "Tell the invited guests, 'Look, I've made my dinner ready: my oxen and fattened animals are butchered, and everything is set. Come to the wedding feast.'"
Matthew 22:5 But they ignored it and went away, one to his farm, another to his business.
Matthew 22:6 The remaining people took his servants, treated them badly, and killed them.
Matthew 22:7 When the king heard this, he was angry. He sent his armies to kill those murderers and burn their city.
Matthew 22:8 He said to his servants, "The wedding is ready, but the guests who were invited were not worthy."
Matthew 22:9 Go to the main roads and invite everyone you find to the wedding.
Matthew 22:10 The servants went out to the roads and brought back everyone they found, both bad and good, and the wedding hall was filled with guests.
Matthew 22:11 When the king entered to look at the guests, he noticed a man there who was not wearing wedding clothes.
Matthew 22:12 He said to him, "Friend, how did you come in here without a wedding outfit?" And he had nothing to say.
Matthew 22:13 The king told his servants, "Tie up his hands and feet, take him out, and throw him into the dark place, where there will be crying and grinding of teeth."
Matthew 22:14 Many are invited, but few are selected.
Matthew 22:15 Then the Pharisees went and planned how to trap him with his words.
Matthew 22:16 They sent their followers to him with the Herodians, saying, "Teacher, we know you are honest and teach God's way truthfully. You don't favor anyone because you pay no attention to who they are."
Matthew 22:17 Tell us then, what do you think? Is it right to pay taxes to Caesar or not?
Matthew 22:18 But Jesus knew they were being evil and asked, "Why are you trying to trick me, you fakers?"
Matthew 22:19 Show me the tax money. And they brought him a coin.
Matthew 22:20 He asks them, "Whose picture and name are on this?"
Matthew 22:21 They said to him, "Caesar's." Then he said to them, "Give back to Caesar what belongs to Caesar, and to God what belongs to God."
Matthew 22:22 After hearing his words, they were amazed, then they left and went on their way.
Matthew 22:23 That day the Sadducees, who say there is no resurrection, came to him and asked him.
Matthew 22:24 Teacher, Moses said, "If a man dies without children, his brother should marry the widow and have children for his brother."
Matthew 22:25 Seven brothers were with us. The first brother died after he got married, and since he had no children, his wife became his brother's.
Matthew 22:26 The second one did the same, and the third, up to the seventh.
Matthew 22:27 Finally, the woman died too.
Matthew 22:28 So, at the resurrection, whose wife will she be, since all seven were married to her?
Matthew 22:29 Jesus answered them, "You are mistaken, because you don't know the Scriptures or God's power."
Matthew 22:30 In the resurrection, people do not marry or are married off, but are like God's angels in heaven.
Matthew 22:31 But about rising from the dead, haven't you read what God told you, saying,
Matthew 22:32 I am the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. God is not for the dead, but for the living.
Matthew 22:33 When the crowd heard this, they were amazed at his teaching.
Matthew 22:34 When the Pharisees heard that he had made the Sadducees quiet, they came together.
Matthew 22:35 One of them, a lawyer, asked him a question to test him, saying,
Matthew 22:36 Teacher, what is the most important command in the law?
Matthew 22:37 Jesus told him, "You should love God with all your heart, with all your soul, and with all your mind."
Matthew 22:38 This is the most important command.
Matthew 22:39 The second command is similar: Love your neighbor the same way you love yourself.
Matthew 22:40 All the laws and the teachings of the prophets are based on these two commandments.
Matthew 22:41 While the Pharisees were together, Jesus asked them,
Matthew 22:42 Asking, "What do you think about the Messiah? Who's his father?" They answered, "David's son."
Matthew 22:43 He asks them, "So why does David, inspired by the Spirit, call him 'Lord,' saying?
Matthew 22:44 God said to my master, "Sit at my right side until I make your enemies a place for your feet."
Matthew 22:45 If David calls him Lord, how is he David's son?
Matthew 22:46 No one could reply to him at all, nor did anyone dare to ask him more questions from that day on.
