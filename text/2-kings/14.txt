2 Kings 14:1 Amaziah, son of Joash, became king of Judah in the second year of Joash, son of Jehoahaz, king of Israel.
2 Kings 14:2 He was 25 years old when he became king and ruled for 29 years in Jerusalem. His mother's name was Jehoaddan from Jerusalem.
2 Kings 14:3 He did what was good in God's eyes, but not as well as David his ancestor did; he acted just like his father Joash.
2 Kings 14:4 However, the high places were not removed: the people still sacrificed and burned incense there.
2 Kings 14:5 As soon as he secured control of the kingdom, he killed the servants who had murdered his father, the king.
2 Kings 14:6 He did not kill the murderers' children. It was in Moses' law book, where God said, "Parents should not die for their children, nor children for their parents; every person should die for their own sin."
2 Kings 14:7 He killed ten thousand Edomites in the Valley of Salt, captured Selah through battle, and named it Joktheel, which is still its name today.
2 Kings 14:8 Amaziah sent people to talk to Jehoash, son of Jehoahaz and Jehu, king of Israel, to say, "Come, let's meet face to face."
2 Kings 14:9 King Jehoash of Israel sent a message to King Amaziah of Judah: "The small bush in Lebanon sent a message to the big tree in Lebanon, asking, 'Let your daughter marry my son.' But then a wild animal in Lebanon walked over and crushed the small bush.
2 Kings 14:10 You have truly defeated Edom, and now you are proud. Celebrate this victory but stay home. Why get involved in more conflict that will only bring harm to you and make you and Judah fall?
2 Kings 14:11 Amaziah wouldn't listen. So Jehoash, the king of Israel, went and faced Amaziah, the king of Judah, at Bethshemesh in Judah.
2 Kings 14:12 Judah was defeated by Israel, and everyone ran back to their tents.
2 Kings 14:13 King Jehoash of Israel captured King Amaziah of Judah, the son of Jehoash and grandson of Ahaziah, in Bethshemesh. He went to Jerusalem and broke down its wall from Ephraim Gate to Corner Gate, a distance of four hundred cubits.
2 Kings 14:14 He took all the gold and silver, every item found in God’s house, and in the king’s treasury, along with hostages, and went back to Samaria.
2 Kings 14:15 The other things Jehoash did, his strength, and his battles with Amaziah, king of Judah, are written in the Israelite Kings' history book, right?
2 Kings 14:16 Jehoash died like his ancestors and was buried in Samaria with the Israelite kings; his son Jeroboam became king after him.
2 Kings 14:17 Amaziah, son of Joash the king of Judah, lived 15 years after Jehoash son of Jehoahaz, the king of Israel, died.
2 Kings 14:18 Aren't the other things Amaziah did written in the book of the history of the kings of Judah?
2 Kings 14:19 They planned against him in Jerusalem, so he ran to Lachish, but they followed him to Lachish and killed him there.
2 Kings 14:20 They brought him on horses and buried him in Jerusalem with his ancestors in the city of David.
2 Kings 14:21 All the people of Judah made sixteen-year-old Azariah king after his father Amaziah.
2 Kings 14:22 He built Elath and gave it back to Judah after the king died.
2 Kings 14:23 In the fifteenth year of King Amaziah of Judah, Jeroboam son of King Joash began to rule as king in Israel's Samaria, and he ruled for forty-one years.
2 Kings 14:24 He did what was evil in God's eyes and did not stop doing the same sins Jeroboam, Nebat's son, who led Israel into sin, had done.
2 Kings 14:25 He brought back Israel's border from Hamath to the Sea of Arabah, just as God of Israel had promised through his prophet Jonah, son of Amittai, from Gathhepher.
2 Kings 14:26 God saw Israel's suffering, which was very severe, because no one was confined, abandoned, or able to help Israel.
2 Kings 14:27 God did not say he would erase Israel's name from under heaven; instead, he saved them through Jeroboam, the son of Joash.
2 Kings 14:28 The other things Jeroboam did, his achievements, his battles, and how he won back Damascus and Hamath for Israel—territories that were once Judah's—are these not recorded in the history book of Israel's kings?
2 Kings 14:29 Jeroboam died like his ancestors, the kings of Israel; and his son Zachariah became king after him.
