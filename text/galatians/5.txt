Galatians 5:1 Stay strong in the freedom that Christ has given us, and don't get tied up again in slavery.
Galatians 5:2 Listen, I, Paul, tell you that if you get circumcised, Christ will be of no benefit to you.
Galatians 5:3 I tell every circumcised man that he must follow the entire law.
Galatians 5:4 Christ no longer benefits you, whoever of you try to be right with God by the law; you have lost God's favor.
Galatians 5:5 We wait for the hope of being right with God through faith with the Spirit's help.
Galatians 5:6 In Jesus Christ, neither being circumcised nor not being circumcised matters, but having faith that shows itself through love does.
Galatians 5:7 You were running well; who stopped you from following the truth?
Galatians 5:8 This belief does not come from God who calls you.
Galatians 5:9 A small amount of yeast spreads through all the dough.
Galatians 5:10 I trust you in God to think the same. But anyone who causes you trouble will face their own punishment.
Galatians 5:11 And friends, if I still tell people to be circumcised, why am I still being persecuted? Then the problem with the cross would be gone.
Galatians 5:12 I wish those upsetting you were gone.
Galatians 5:13 Friends, you are called to be free; but do not use your freedom to indulge selfish desires, instead serve each other with love.
Galatians 5:14 All the law is summed up in one command: Love your neighbor as yourself.
Galatians 5:15 If you hurt and attack each other, be careful that you don't destroy each other.
Galatians 5:16 I say this: Live by the Spirit, and you will not satisfy the desires of the body.
Galatians 5:17 The body has desires against the Spirit, and the Spirit has desires against the body. They are opposed to each other, so you cannot do what you want.
Galatians 5:18 But if the Spirit leads you, you are not bound by the law.
Galatians 5:19 The actions that come from our human nature are clear. They are: being unfaithful in marriage, having sex without being married, being impure, and wanting forbidden pleasures.
Galatians 5:20 Worshiping idols, using magic, hating, fighting, wanting to be better than others, anger, arguing, dividing people, and wrong beliefs,
Galatians 5:21 Jealousy, killings, getting drunk, wild parties, and things like these: I warn you now, as I did before, that people who do such things will not receive God's kingdom.
Galatians 5:22 The Spirit's fruit is love, happiness, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, and faith.
Galatians 5:23 Gentleness, self-control: there is no law against these things.
Galatians 5:24 Those who belong to Christ have put to death their human desires and wants.
Galatians 5:25 If we live by the Spirit, let's also follow the Spirit's lead.
Galatians 5:26 Let's not seek empty praise, causing each other trouble or being jealous of each other.
