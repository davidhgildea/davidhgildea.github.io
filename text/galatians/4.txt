Galatians 4:1 I tell you, an heir is no different from a servant while he is still a child, even if he owns everything.
Galatians 4:2 But is under teachers and managers until the time set by the father.
Galatians 4:3 Just like that, when we were young, we were trapped under the world's basic rules.
Galatians 4:4 When the right time came, God sent his Son, born of a woman, born under the law.
Galatians 4:5 To free those who were under the law, so that we might be accepted as God's children.
Galatians 4:6 Because you are children, God has sent the spirit of his Son into your hearts, calling out, "Daddy, Father."
Galatians 4:7 So you are not a servant any longer, but a son; and if a son, then God's heir through Christ.
Galatians 4:8 But back then, when you didn't know God, you worshiped things that are not really gods.
Galatians 4:9 But now, knowing God, or actually being known by God, why do you go back to the weak and poor basic things you want to be slaves to again?
Galatians 4:10 You keep track of days, months, seasons, and years.
Galatians 4:11 I am worried about you, in case my hard work for you was for nothing.
Galatians 4:12 Friends, I urge you, be like me, because I am like you: you have done me no wrong.
Galatians 4:13 You know that despite my physical weakness, I first shared the good news with you.
Galatians 4:14 You did not look down on or turn away from my physical illness. Instead, you welcomed me as if I were an angel of God, just like Jesus Christ.
Galatians 4:15 Where is the happiness you talked about? I know that if you could, you would have given me your own eyes.
Galatians 4:16 Have I become your enemy by telling you the truth?
Galatians 4:17 They are eagerly trying to influence you, but not in a good way; indeed, they want to shut you out so that you will try to win their approval.
Galatians 4:18 It is good to always be energetically supportive of a good cause, not just when I am with you.
Galatians 4:19 My dear children, I am working hard for you again, like a mother giving birth, until you truly become like Christ.
Galatians 4:20 I want to be with you now and change how I speak because I'm unsure about you.
Galatians 4:21 Ask yourselves, you who want to follow the law, don't you listen to what the law says?
Galatians 4:22 It is written that Abraham had two sons, one with a slave woman and the other with a free woman.
Galatians 4:23 The child of the slave woman was born in the ordinary way, but the child of the free woman was born because of God's promise.
Galatians 4:24 These things represent two agreements; one from Mount Sinai, leading to slavery, which is Hagar.
Galatians 4:25 This Hagar represents Mount Sinai in Arabia and corresponds to the current Jerusalem, for she and her children live in slavery.
Galatians 4:26 Jerusalem above is free and is our mother.
Galatians 4:27 It is written, "Celebrate, you who cannot have children; shout out loud, you who do not go through labor pain, because the woman who is alone will have many more children than the woman with a husband."
Galatians 4:28 We, brothers and sisters, are like Isaac, children of God's promise.
Galatians 4:29 But like before, the person born in a normal human way made life hard for the person born with God's Spirit, and it's still like that now.
Galatians 4:30 What does the scripture say? Send away the slave woman and her child, because the slave woman's child will not share the inheritance with the free woman's child.
Galatians 4:31 So, friends, we are not the children of a slave woman, but of the free woman.
