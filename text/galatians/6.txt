Galatians 6:1 Brothers, if someone makes a mistake, those of you who are spiritual should help them with gentleness. Think about yourself, so you are not tempted too.
Galatians 6:2 Help each other with your troubles, and in this way, you obey the law of Christ.
Galatians 6:3 If someone thinks they are important when they are not, they are fooling themselves.
Galatians 6:4 Let everyone test their own actions, then they can take pride in themselves without comparing to someone else.
Galatians 6:5 Each person must carry their own load.
Galatians 6:6 Let the person who learns from the teachings share with their teacher in every good way.
Galatians 6:7 Don't be fooled; no one can mock God: whatever a person plants, that's what they will harvest.
Galatians 6:8 Whoever plants for selfish reasons will harvest ruin, but whoever plants for the Spirit will harvest eternal life.
Galatians 6:9 Let's not get tired of doing good, because at the right time we will get a reward if we don't give up.
Galatians 6:10 Whenever we can, we should help everyone, especially those who share our faith.
Galatians 6:11 You see how big a letter I have written to you with my own hand.
Galatians 6:12 Many people want to look good outwardly, so they pressure you to get circumcised, just to avoid being persecuted for following Christ's teachings.
Galatians 6:13 Those who are circumcised don't follow the law; they just want you to be circumcised so they can boast about what they did to you.
Galatians 6:14 I would never boast except about the cross of Jesus Christ, which makes the world dead to me and me dead to the world.
Galatians 6:15 In Christ Jesus, being circumcised or not doesn't matter, but being a new person does.
Galatians 6:16 Everyone who follows this rule will have peace and mercy, as will all of God's people.
Galatians 6:17 From now on let no one cause me trouble, because I have the scars of Jesus on my body.
Galatians 6:18 Friends, may the kindness of our Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit. Amen.
