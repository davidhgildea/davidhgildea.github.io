Lamentations 3:1 I am the man who has seen suffering because of his anger.
Lamentations 3:2 He has guided me and taken me to a place of darkness, not light.
Lamentations 3:3 Certainly, he has turned against me; he keeps acting against me all day long.
Lamentations 3:4 He made my skin and flesh old; he broke my bones.
Lamentations 3:5 He has built against me and surrounded me with bitterness and hardship.
Lamentations 3:6 He has put me in dark places, like those long dead.
Lamentations 3:7 God has surrounded me so I can't escape; He has made my chains heavy.
Lamentations 3:8 When I cry and yell, God ignores my prayer.
Lamentations 3:9 He has blocked my paths with cut stone; he has made my ways twisted.
Lamentations 3:10 He was to me like a bear waiting to attack, and like a lion hiding.
Lamentations 3:11 He has changed my path and torn me apart; he has left me lonely.
Lamentations 3:12 He has aimed his bow, and made me the target for his arrow.
Lamentations 3:13 He made the arrows from his bag hit my body.
Lamentations 3:14 I was mocked by all my people; they sang about me all day long.
Lamentations 3:15 God has filled me with bitterness; he has made me very bitter.
Lamentations 3:16 He has also broken my teeth with gravel stones; he has covered me with ashes.
Lamentations 3:17 You took my peace far away; I forgot what good fortune is.
Lamentations 3:18 I said, "My strength and my hope are gone because of God."
Lamentations 3:19 I remember my suffering and my sadness, the bitterness and the poison.
Lamentations 3:20 I still remember them deep in my heart, and I am humbled.
Lamentations 3:21 I remember this and so I have hope.
Lamentations 3:22 It is because of God's kindness that we are not destroyed, for His compassion never ends.
Lamentations 3:23 Every morning they are new; your faithfulness is great.
Lamentations 3:24 God is all I need, says my soul; so I will put my hope in Him.
Lamentations 3:25 God is good to those who wait for Him, to the person who looks for Him.
Lamentations 3:26 It is good for a person to have hope and to wait quietly for God to save.
Lamentations 3:27 It is good for a person to deal with hard times when young.
Lamentations 3:28 He sits alone and stays quiet because he has taken it upon himself.
Lamentations 3:29 He puts his mouth to the ground; perhaps there is hope.
Lamentations 3:30 He offers his face to the one who hits him; he is overwhelmed with insults.
Lamentations 3:31 God will not reject forever.
Lamentations 3:32 Even though God may bring sadness, He will show mercy because of His great kindness.
Lamentations 3:33 God does not cause pain or sadness to people without reason.
Lamentations 3:34 To step on all the captives of the earth.
Lamentations 3:35 To deny a person justice in the presence of God,
Lamentations 3:36 God does not approve of ruining a person's case.
Lamentations 3:37 Who is the one who can make something happen if God has not commanded it?
Lamentations 3:38 Doesn't both good and bad come from the mouth of God?
Lamentations 3:39 Why should a living person complain, a person for being punished for their sins?
Lamentations 3:40 Let's examine our ways, and turn back to God.
Lamentations 3:41 Let's raise our hearts and hands to God in heaven.
Lamentations 3:42 We have done wrong and rebelled; you have not forgiven us.
Lamentations 3:43 You have been angry with us, chased us, killed us without mercy.
Lamentations 3:44 You have surrounded yourself with a cloud, so our prayers cannot get through.
Lamentations 3:45 You have made us like trash and garbage among the people.
Lamentations 3:46 All our enemies have spoken against us.
Lamentations 3:47 Fear and a trap have come upon us, along with ruin and destruction.
Lamentations 3:48 My eyes flow with tears for the ruin of my people.
Lamentations 3:49 My eye cries non-stop without any break.
Lamentations 3:50 Until God looks down from heaven and sees.
Lamentations 3:51 My eyes hurt my heart because of what happens to the girls of my city.
Lamentations 3:52 My enemies chased me hard, like a bird, for no reason.
Lamentations 3:53 They have ended my life in the prison, and thrown a stone at me.
Lamentations 3:54 Water poured over my head; then I thought, I am finished.
Lamentations 3:55 I called your name, God, from the deep pit.
Lamentations 3:56 You have heard my voice; do not ignore me when I sigh and cry out.
Lamentations 3:57 You came close when I called you; you said, Don't be afraid.
Lamentations 3:58 God, you have fought for my soul; you have saved my life.
Lamentations 3:59 God, you have seen the wrong done to me: judge my case.
Lamentations 3:60 You have seen all their revenge and all their plans against me.
Lamentations 3:61 You have heard their insults, God, and all their plans against me.
Lamentations 3:62 The words of those against me and their plans all day long.
Lamentations 3:63 I see them when they sit and when they stand; I am their music.
Lamentations 3:64 Give them what they deserve, God, based on what they have done.
Lamentations 3:65 Make their hearts sad, and put your curse on them.
Lamentations 3:66 Punish and wipe them out in anger from beneath the skies of God.
