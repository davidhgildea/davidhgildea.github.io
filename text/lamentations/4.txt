Lamentations 4:1 The gold has lost its shine! The purest gold is no longer bright! The sacred stones are scattered at the beginning of every street.
Lamentations 4:2 The valuable children of Zion, once worth as much as fine gold, are now treated like common clay pots made by a potter.
Lamentations 4:3 Sea creatures nurse their young, but my people have become heartless, like ostriches in the desert.
Lamentations 4:4 The baby's tongue sticks to the top of its mouth because it's so thirsty; the little children beg for bread, but nobody gives any to them.
Lamentations 4:5 People who ate fine foods are now alone in the streets; those who wore red clothes hug trash heaps.
Lamentations 4:6 The punishment for my people's wrongs is worse than what Sodom faced, which was destroyed suddenly without anyone helping.
Lamentations 4:7 Her holy people were cleaner than snow, they were brighter than milk, their bodies were redder than jewels, and they shone like sapphires.
Lamentations 4:8 Their faces are darker than black; they are unrecognizable in the streets: their skin sticks to their bones; it is dried up and has become like a stick.
Lamentations 4:9 People killed by the sword are better off than those who die of hunger, for the hungry waste away, suffering for lack of food from the fields.
Lamentations 4:10 Compassionate women have cooked their own children; they became their food during my people's ruin.
Lamentations 4:11 God has carried out his anger; he has unleashed his intense wrath and started a fire in Zion that has consumed its foundations.
Lamentations 4:12 Earth's rulers and everyone living in the world would not have believed that the foe and enemy could come into Jerusalem's gates.
Lamentations 4:13 Because her prophets sinned and her priests did wrong, spilling the blood of innocent people among her,
Lamentations 4:14 They wandered around like blind people in the streets; they made themselves dirty with blood, so no one could touch their clothes.
Lamentations 4:15 They called out to them, "Leave; you are unclean; go away, do not touch." As they ran and roamed, among the nations it was said, "They will not stay there anymore."
Lamentations 4:16 God's anger has scattered them; He will no longer look after them: they did not respect the priests, they did not honor the old people.
Lamentations 4:17 We keep looking for help that never comes, and we have waited for a country that cannot rescue us.
Lamentations 4:18 They chase us so we can't walk in our streets; our end is close, our days are over, for our end has arrived.
Lamentations 4:19 Our enemies chase us faster than eagles in the sky; they chased us on the mountains and set traps for us in the desert.
Lamentations 4:20 The one we breathed through, God's chosen one, was captured by them, and we thought we would live safely under his protection even among foreign nations.
Lamentations 4:21 Be happy and glad, you daughter of Edom, living in the land of Uz; the cup will also come to you: you will get drunk and undress.
Lamentations 4:22 Your punishment is finished, daughter of Zion; you won't be taken away anymore: but he will punish your sins, daughter of Edom; he will expose your wrongs.
