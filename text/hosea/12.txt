Hosea 12:1 Ephraim chases the wind and follows the east wind: he keeps adding lies and destruction; they make agreements with the Assyrians, and oil is taken to Egypt.
Hosea 12:2 God also has an argument with Judah and will punish Jacob for his actions; He will repay him based on what he has done.
Hosea 12:3 In the womb, he grabbed his brother's heel, and with his strength, he struggled with God.
Hosea 12:4 Yes, he had power over the angel and won; he cried and asked for mercy; he met him in Bethel, where he spoke with us.
Hosea 12:5 Even God of all; God is his name to remember.
Hosea 12:6 So turn to your God: practice kindness and fairness, and always depend on your God.
Hosea 12:7 He is a trader with dishonest scales in his hands; he loves to cheat.
Hosea 12:8 Ephraim said, "I have become rich and gained wealth. In all my work, no one will find any wrong that is sin in me."
Hosea 12:9 I am God, who brought you out of Egypt, and I will make you live in tents again, like during the festival.
Hosea 12:10 I have spoken through the prophets, given many visions, and taught in parables through the prophets' work.
Hosea 12:11 Is there wrongdoing in Gilead? Surely they are worthless; they offer bulls in Gilgal; yes, their altars are like piles of stones in the field rows.
Hosea 12:12 Jacob ran away to Syria, and Israel worked to marry a wife, and to get a wife he took care of sheep.
Hosea 12:13 God led Israel out of Egypt through a prophet, and by a prophet they were kept safe.
Hosea 12:14 Ephraim made God very angry, so God will make him face the consequences of his actions, and will make sure his shame remains on him.
