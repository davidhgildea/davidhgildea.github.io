1 Chronicles 25:1 David and the leaders assigned the sons of Asaph, Heman, and Jeduthun to prophesy using harps, lyres, and cymbals. They counted how many people each job needed.
1 Chronicles 25:2 Asaph's sons were Zaccur, Joseph, Nethaniah, and Asarelah. They worked under Asaph and made prophecies following the king's instructions.
1 Chronicles 25:3 From Jeduthun: Jeduthun's sons were Gedaliah, Zeri, Jeshaiah, Hashabiah, and Mattithiah, six in total. They worked under their father Jeduthun, who played the harp to thank and praise God.
1 Chronicles 25:4 Heman had these sons: Bukkiah, Mattaniah, Uzziel, Shebuel, Jerimoth, Hananiah, Hanani, Eliathah, Giddalti, Romamtiezer, Joshbekashah, Mallothi, Hothir, and Mahazioth.
1 Chronicles 25:5 All these were Heman's sons; he was the king's prophet and spoke God's messages. God gave Heman fourteen sons and three daughters.
1 Chronicles 25:6 All these were guided by their father to make music in God's house, using cymbals, stringed instruments, and harps, to serve in God's house following the king's command to Asaph, Jeduthun, and Heman.
1 Chronicles 25:7 The total number of musicians skilled in God's songs, including their relatives, was 288.
1 Chronicles 25:8 They drew lots to take turns, from the least to the greatest, from the teacher to the student.
1 Chronicles 25:9 The first turn came for Asaph's group, led by Joseph. The second went to Gedaliah and his group, which included his brothers and sons, making twelve in total.
1 Chronicles 25:10 The third group was Zaccur's, he, his sons, and his brothers, totaling twelve.
1 Chronicles 25:11 The fourth one was Izri; he, his sons, and his brothers totaled twelve.
1 Chronicles 25:12 The fifth group was Nethaniah, his sons and his brothers, totaling twelve.
1 Chronicles 25:13 Bukkiah was the sixth, with his sons and brothers totaling twelve.
1 Chronicles 25:14 The seventh was Jesharelah; he and his sons and brothers totaled twelve.
1 Chronicles 25:15 The eighth group was led by Jeshaiah, including him, his sons, and his brothers, totaling twelve people.
1 Chronicles 25:16 The ninth group was for Mattaniah, along with his sons and brothers, totaling twelve.
1 Chronicles 25:17 The tenth group was for Shimei, his sons, and brothers—there were twelve of them.
1 Chronicles 25:18 Azareel was the eleventh, with his sons and brothers adding up to twelve.
1 Chronicles 25:19 Hashabiah was the twelfth, along with his sons and brothers, making twelve in all.
1 Chronicles 25:20 The thirteenth lot fell to Shubael, and together with his sons and brothers, they numbered twelve.
1 Chronicles 25:21 Mattithiah's group, with his sons and brothers, numbered twelve.
1 Chronicles 25:22 The fifteenth lot fell to Jeremoth, along with his sons and brothers, totaling twelve.
1 Chronicles 25:23 The sixteenth group was Hananiah, his sons, and his brothers, totaling twelve people.
1 Chronicles 25:24 Joshbekashah's group, including himself, his sons, and his brothers, was twelve people.
1 Chronicles 25:25 Hanani's group, which included himself, his sons, and his brothers, had twelve members.
1 Chronicles 25:26 Mallothi's group, including himself, his sons, and his brothers, was twelve.
1 Chronicles 25:27 Eliathah's group, including himself, his sons, and his brothers, was twelve.
1 Chronicles 25:28 The twenty-first was to Hothir, he and his sons and brothers totaled twelve.
1 Chronicles 25:29 Giddalti, along with his sons and brothers, totaled twelve for the twenty-second group.
1 Chronicles 25:30 The twenty-third group was Mahazioth, he, his sons, and his brothers, totaling twelve.
1 Chronicles 25:31 The twenty-fourth group was Romamti-ezer, along with his sons and brothers, totaling twelve.
