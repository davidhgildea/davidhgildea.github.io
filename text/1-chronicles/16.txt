1 Chronicles 16:1 They brought God's ark and placed it inside the tent David set up for it; they gave burnt sacrifices and peace offerings to God.
1 Chronicles 16:2 After David finished giving the burnt offerings and the peace offerings, he blessed the people in God's name.
1 Chronicles 16:3 He gave every person in Israel, both men and women, a loaf of bread, a nice piece of meat, and a bottle of wine.
1 Chronicles 16:4 He chose some Levites to serve before God's ark, to keep records, and to thank and praise the God of Israel.
1 Chronicles 16:5 Asaph was the leader, with Zechariah, Jeiel, Shemiramoth, Jehiel, Mattithiah, Eliab, Benaiah, and Obed-edom next to him. Jeiel played the psalteries and harps, while Asaph played the cymbals.
1 Chronicles 16:6 Benaiah and Jahaziel, who were priests, always played trumpets in front of God's covenant box.
1 Chronicles 16:7 On that day, David first gave this psalm to thank God to Asaph and his brothers.
1 Chronicles 16:8 Give thanks to God, call on his name, tell everyone about what he has done.
1 Chronicles 16:9 Sing to him, sing songs to him, talk about all his amazing works.
1 Chronicles 16:10 Celebrate God's holy name: let those who look for God be happy in their hearts.
1 Chronicles 16:11 Look for God and his power, always search for his presence.
1 Chronicles 16:12 Remember the amazing things God has done, his miracles, and the decisions he has spoken.
1 Chronicles 16:13 You descendants of Israel, God's servants, you offspring of Jacob, his chosen people.
1 Chronicles 16:14 He is our God; his decisions are everywhere on earth.
1 Chronicles 16:15 Always remember God's agreement, the promise he gave for a thousand generations.
1 Chronicles 16:16 About the promise God made to Abraham, and the pledge to Isaac;
1 Chronicles 16:17 God made it a law for Jacob and a forever promise to Israel.
1 Chronicles 16:18 God said, "I will give you the land of Canaan as your inheritance."
1 Chronicles 16:19 When you were only a few people, strangers in the land.
1 Chronicles 16:20 As they moved from one country to another and from one kingdom to a different nation;
1 Chronicles 16:21 He did not let anyone harm them; indeed, he scolded kings for their protection.
1 Chronicles 16:22 "God said, 'Do not harm my chosen ones, and do not hurt my prophets.'"
1 Chronicles 16:23 Sing to God, all the earth; show his salvation every day.
1 Chronicles 16:24 Tell everyone about God's greatness; share the amazing things He does with all people.
1 Chronicles 16:25 God is great and deserves much praise; we should also fear him more than any other gods.
1 Chronicles 16:26 All other people's gods are just idols, but God created the skies.
1 Chronicles 16:27 God's presence brings glory and honor; His place is filled with strength and joy.
1 Chronicles 16:28 Give to God, you families of the people, give to God glory and strength.
1 Chronicles 16:29 Give God the honor that belongs to his name; bring a gift and come before him; worship God in the beauty of being holy.
1 Chronicles 16:30 All the earth, be in awe of God; the world will be firm and not shake.
1 Chronicles 16:31 Let the sky be happy, and the earth be joyful, and let people tell all countries, "God rules."
1 Chronicles 16:32 Let the sea make noise and everything in it; let the fields be happy and everything in them.
1 Chronicles 16:33 The trees of the forest will sing when God comes, because he is coming to judge the world.
1 Chronicles 16:34 Give thanks to God, for he is good, for his kindness lasts forever.
1 Chronicles 16:35 Say to God, "Save us, Savior, bring us together, and free us from other nations, so we can thank your holy name and find joy in praising you."
1 Chronicles 16:36 Praise God of Israel forever and ever. And all the people said, "Amen," and praised God.
1 Chronicles 16:37 He left Asaph and his brothers there in front of God's covenant chest to serve continually, as each day needed.
1 Chronicles 16:38 Obededom and his 68 brothers, along with the sons of Jeduthun and Hosah, served as gatekeepers.
1 Chronicles 16:39 Priest Zadok and his fellow priests served at God's tent on the high ground in Gibeon.
1 Chronicles 16:40 To give burnt sacrifices to God on the altar for burnt sacrifices always in the morning and in the evening, and to do everything that is written in God's law that he gave to Israel.
1 Chronicles 16:41 Along with them were Heman and Jeduthun, and the others who were selected and named, to thank God, because his mercy lasts forever.
1 Chronicles 16:42 Heman and Jeduthun joined them with trumpets and cymbals to make music and with God's instruments. Jeduthun's sons were gatekeepers.
1 Chronicles 16:43 All the people went to their own homes, and David went back to bless his family.
