1 Chronicles 1:1 Adam, Seth, Enosh.
1 Chronicles 1:2 Kenan, Mahalaleel, Jared,
1 Chronicles 1:3 Enoch, Methuselah, Lemach.
1 Chronicles 1:4 Noah, Shem, Ham, and Japheth.
1 Chronicles 1:5 Japheth's sons are Gomer, Magog, Madai, Javan, Tubal, Meshech, and Tiras.
1 Chronicles 1:6 Gomer's sons were Ashkenaz, Riphath, and Togarmah.
1 Chronicles 1:7 Javan's sons were Elishah, Tarshish, Kittim, and Dodanim.
1 Chronicles 1:8 Ham's sons were Cush, Mizraim, Put, and Canaan.
1 Chronicles 1:9 Cush had sons named Seba, Havilah, Sabta, Raamah, and Sabtecha. Raamah had sons named Sheba and Dedan.
1 Chronicles 1:10 Cush had a son named Nimrod, who became very powerful on earth.
1 Chronicles 1:11 Mizraim had children named Ludim, Anamim, Lehabim, and Naphtuhim.
1 Chronicles 1:12 And people from Pathrus, Casluh, who were the ancestors of the Philistines, and from Caphtor.
1 Chronicles 1:13 Canaan had Zidon, his first son, and Heth.
1 Chronicles 1:14 The Jebusite, the Amorite, and the Girgashite too,
1 Chronicles 1:15 The Hivite, the Arkite, and the Sinite,
1 Chronicles 1:16 The people from Arvad, from Zemar, and from Hamath.
1 Chronicles 1:17 Shem's sons were Elam, Asshur, Arphaxad, Lud, Aram, Uz, Hul, Gether, and Meshech.
1 Chronicles 1:18 Arphaxad had a son named Shelah, and Shelah had a son named Eber.
1 Chronicles 1:19 Eber had two sons: one was named Peleg, for during his time the earth was divided, and his brother was named Joktan.
1 Chronicles 1:20 Joktan had children named Almodad, Sheleph, Hazarmaveth, and Jerah.
1 Chronicles 1:21 Hadoram, Uzal, and Diklah too,
1 Chronicles 1:22 Ebal, Abimael, and Sheba,
1 Chronicles 1:23 Ophir, Havilah, and Jobab were all Joktan's sons.
1 Chronicles 1:24 Shem, Arphaxad, Shelah,
1 Chronicles 1:25 Eber, Peleg, Reu,
1 Chronicles 1:26 Serug, Nahor, Terah.
1 Chronicles 1:27 Abram is Abraham.
1 Chronicles 1:28 Abraham's sons: Isaac and Ishmael.
1 Chronicles 1:29 This lists their families: Ishmael's first son was Nebaioth, followed by Kedar, Adbeel, and Mibsam.
1 Chronicles 1:30 Mishma, Dumah, Massa, Hadad, and Tema
1 Chronicles 1:31 Jetur, Naphish, and Kedemah are Ishmael's sons.
1 Chronicles 1:32 Abraham's other wife Keturah had sons: Zimran, Jokshan, Medan, Midian, Ishbak, and Shuah. Jokshan had two sons: Sheba and Dedan.
1 Chronicles 1:33 Midian's sons were Ephah, Epher, Henoch, Abida, and Eldaah. They were all Keturah's sons.
1 Chronicles 1:34 Abraham had a son named Isaac. Isaac's sons were Esau and Israel.
1 Chronicles 1:35 Esau's sons: Eliphaz, Reuel, Jeush, Jaalam, and Korah.
1 Chronicles 1:36 Eliphaz's sons were Teman, Omar, Zephi, Gatam, Kenaz, Timna, and Amalek.
1 Chronicles 1:37 Reuel's sons were Nahath, Zerah, Shammah, and Mizzah.
1 Chronicles 1:38 Seir's sons were Lotan, Shobal, Zibeon, Anah, Dishon, Ezar, and Dishan.
1 Chronicles 1:39 Lotan's sons were Hori and Homam, and Timna was Lotan's sister.
1 Chronicles 1:40 Shobal's sons were Alian, Manahath, Ebal, Shephi, and Onam. Zibeon's sons were Aiah and Anah.
1 Chronicles 1:41 Anah's sons were Dishon. Dishon's sons were Amram, Eshban, Ithran, and Cheran.
1 Chronicles 1:42 Ezer's sons were Bilhan, Zavan, and Jakan. Dishan's sons were Uz and Aran.
1 Chronicles 1:43 These were the kings who ruled in Edom before any king ruled over Israel: Bela son of Beor, and his city was called Dinhabah.
1 Chronicles 1:44 After Bela died, Jobab, Zerah's son from Bozrah, became king in his place.
1 Chronicles 1:45 After Jobab died, Husham from Temanite lands became ruler in his place.
1 Chronicles 1:46 When Husham died, Hadad son of Bedad, who defeated Midian in Moab's fields, became king after him. His city was named Avith.
1 Chronicles 1:47 After Hadad died, Samlah from Masrekah became the ruler in his place.
1 Chronicles 1:48 After Samlah died, Shaul from Rehoboth on the river became king in his place.
1 Chronicles 1:49 After Saul died, Baalhanan, Achbor's son, became king in his place.
1 Chronicles 1:50 After Baalhanan died, Hadad became king. His city was named Pai, and his wife was Mehetabel, Matred's daughter and Mezahab's granddaughter.
1 Chronicles 1:51 Hadad also died. The leaders of Edom were: Leader Timnah, Leader Aliah, Leader Jetheth.
1 Chronicles 1:52 Leader Aholibamah, leader Elah, leader Pinon,
1 Chronicles 1:53 Chief Kenaz, Chief Teman, Chief Mibzar,
1 Chronicles 1:54 Leader Magdiel, leader Iram. These are the leaders of Edom.
