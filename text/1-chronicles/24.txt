1 Chronicles 24:1 These are the groups of Aaron's sons: Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar.
1 Chronicles 24:2 Nadab and Abihu died before their father and had no kids, so Eleazar and Ithamar became priests.
1 Chronicles 24:3 David assigned roles to them: Zadok from Eleazar's descendants and Ahimelech from Ithamar's descendants, based on their duties in their service.
1 Chronicles 24:4 There were more leaders from Eleazar's sons than from Ithamar's sons, and they were split up this way: Sixteen leaders from Eleazar's family and eight from Ithamar's family.
1 Chronicles 24:5 They were separated by drawing lots, each group equally; because leaders of the sanctuary and leaders of God's house came from both Eleazar's descendants and Ithamar's descendants.
1 Chronicles 24:6 Shemaiah, Nethaneel's son and a writer, recorded names for the king, leaders, priest Zadok, Ahimelech (Abiathar's son), and the head priests and Levites. They chose one main family for Eleazar and one for Ithamar.
1 Chronicles 24:7 The first turn went to Jehoiarib, the second to Jedaiah.
1 Chronicles 24:8 The third to Harim, the fourth to Seorim.
1 Chronicles 24:9 The fifth lot came out for Malchijah, the sixth for Mijamin.
1 Chronicles 24:10 The seventh to Hakkoz, the eighth to Abijah.
1 Chronicles 24:11 The ninth for Jeshuah, the tenth for Shecaniah.
1 Chronicles 24:12 Eliashib got the eleventh turn, and Jakim got the twelfth.
1 Chronicles 24:13 The thirteenth is to Huppah, the fourteenth is to Jeshebeab.
1 Chronicles 24:14 The fifteenth lot fell to Bilgah, the sixteenth to Immer.
1 Chronicles 24:15 The seventeenth lot fell to Hezir, the eighteenth to Aphses.
1 Chronicles 24:16 The nineteenth lot fell to Pethahiah, the twentieth to Jehezekel.
1 Chronicles 24:17 The twenty-first lot was for Jachin, the twenty-second lot was for Gamul.
1 Chronicles 24:18 The twenty-third lot fell to Delaiah, the twenty-fourth to Maaziah.
1 Chronicles 24:19 These were the rules for them in serving and entering God's house, following their customs under the leadership of Aaron their father, as God of Israel had commanded him.
1 Chronicles 24:20 The other descendants of Levi included: from Amram's line, Shubael; and from Shubael's line, Jehdeiah.
1 Chronicles 24:21 About Rehabiah: Isshiah was the first of Rehabiah's sons.
1 Chronicles 24:22 From the Izharites, Shelomoth; from Shelomoth's sons, Jahath.
1 Chronicles 24:23 Hebron's sons were Jeriah first, Amariah second, Jahaziel third, and Jekameam fourth.
1 Chronicles 24:24 Uzziel's sons included Michah, and Michah's sons included Shamir.
1 Chronicles 24:25 Michah's brother was Isshiah. Isshiah's son was Zechariah.
1 Chronicles 24:26 Merari's sons were Mahli and Mushi; and Jaaziah's son was Beno.
1 Chronicles 24:27 Merari's sons were Jaaziah, Beno, Shoham, Zaccur, and Ibri.
1 Chronicles 24:28 Mahli's family included Eleazar, but he had no children.
1 Chronicles 24:29 About Kish: Kish's son was Jerahmeel.
1 Chronicles 24:30 Mushi's sons were Mahli, Eder, and Jerimoth. These were the Levites' sons, grouped by their fathers' families.
1 Chronicles 24:31 They also cast lots like their brothers, the sons of Aaron, in front of King David, Zadok, Ahimelech, and the leaders of the priests' and Levites' families, with the most important fathers facing their younger brothers.
