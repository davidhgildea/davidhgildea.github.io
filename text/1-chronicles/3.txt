1 Chronicles 3:1 These were David's sons born in Hebron: the first, Amnon, with Ahinoam from Jezreel; the second, Daniel, with Abigail from Carmel.
1 Chronicles 3:2 The third, Absalom, Maachah's son and Talmai the king of Geshur's grandson; the fourth, Adonijah, Haggith's son.
1 Chronicles 3:3 The fifth son was Shephatiah with Abital; the sixth was Ithream with Eglah, his wife.
1 Chronicles 3:4 He had six children in Hebron, where he ruled for seven and a half years; then in Jerusalem, he ruled for thirty-three years.
1 Chronicles 3:5 In Jerusalem, he had four sons by Bathshua, Ammiel's daughter: Shimea, Shobab, Nathan, and Solomon.
1 Chronicles 3:6 Ibhar, Elishama, and Eliphelet too.
1 Chronicles 3:7 Nogah, Nepheg, and Japhia,
1 Chronicles 3:8 Elishama, Eliada, and Eliphelet, nine.
1 Chronicles 3:9 All these were David's sons, not including the sons from his other women, and their sister Tamar.
1 Chronicles 3:10 Solomon's son was Rehoboam, his son was Abia, his son was Asa, and his son was Jehoshaphat.
1 Chronicles 3:11 Joram's son, Ahaziah's son, Joash's son,
1 Chronicles 3:12 Amaziah's son, Azariah's son, Jotham's son,
1 Chronicles 3:13 Ahaz's son, Hezekiah's son, Manasseh's son,
1 Chronicles 3:14 Amon was his son, and then Josiah was his son.
1 Chronicles 3:15 Josiah's sons were, the oldest Johanan, the next Jehoiakim, the third Zedekiah, and the youngest Shallum.
1 Chronicles 3:16 Jehoiakim's sons were Jeconiah and then Zedekiah.
1 Chronicles 3:17 Jeconiah's children were Assir and his son Salathiel.
1 Chronicles 3:18 Malchiram, Pedaiah, Shenazar, Jecamiah, Hoshama, and Nedabiah were there too.
1 Chronicles 3:19 Pedaiah had sons: Zerubbabel and Shimei. Zerubbabel's children were Meshullam, Hananiah, and their sister Shelomith.
1 Chronicles 3:20 Hashubah, Ohel, Berechiah, Hasadiah, and Jushabhesed were five men.
1 Chronicles 3:21 Hananiah's sons were Pelatiah and Jesaiah. Rephaiah's, Arnan's, Obadiah's, and Shechaniah's were their families.
1 Chronicles 3:22 The children of Shechaniah were Shemaiah, and Shemaiah's children were Hattush, Igeal, Bariah, Neariah, and Shaphat—six in total.
1 Chronicles 3:23 Neariah had three sons: Elioenai, Hezekiah, and Azrikam.
1 Chronicles 3:24 Elioenai's sons were Hodaiah, Eliashib, Pelaiah, Akkub, Johanan, Dalaiah, and Anani, seven in total.
