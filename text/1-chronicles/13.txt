1 Chronicles 13:1 David talked with the leaders of thousands and hundreds, and with every chief.
1 Chronicles 13:2 David said to all the people of Israel, "If it seems good to you and it is God's will, let's send a message to our brothers everywhere who remain in the land of Israel, and to the priests and Levites in their towns and surrounding areas, so they can join us."
1 Chronicles 13:3 Let's bring back God's ark to us; we didn't ask for its guidance when Saul was king.
1 Chronicles 13:4 And the whole group agreed to do it because everyone thought it was the right thing to do.
1 Chronicles 13:5 David brought all the people of Israel from the Shihor River near Egypt to Hemath to move God's ark from Kirjathjearim.
1 Chronicles 13:6 David and all of Israel went to Baalah, also known as Kirjathjearim in Judah, to bring back the ark of God, called by His name and resting between the cherubim.
1 Chronicles 13:7 They moved God's ark on a new cart from Abinadab's house; Uzza and Ahio drove the cart.
1 Chronicles 13:8 David and everyone in Israel danced and played music for God with all their strength, using songs, harps, lyres, tambourines, cymbals, and trumpets.
1 Chronicles 13:9 When they got to Chidon's threshing floor, Uzza reached out to steady the ark because the oxen tripped.
1 Chronicles 13:10 God became angry with Uzza and struck him down because he touched the ark, and Uzza died there in front of God.
1 Chronicles 13:11 David was upset because God had punished Uzza, so that place is named Perezuzza even now.
1 Chronicles 13:12 David was scared of God that day and asked, "How can I bring God's ark to my house?"
1 Chronicles 13:13 David did not bring the ark to his own city but took it to the house of Obed-edom, a man from Gath.
1 Chronicles 13:14 God's chest stayed with Obededom's family in his home for three months. And God blessed Obededom's home and all his belongings.
