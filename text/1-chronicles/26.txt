1 Chronicles 26:1 About the groups of gatekeepers: Meshelemiah, son of Kore and descendant of Asaph, was from the Korahites.
1 Chronicles 26:2 Meshelemiah had sons: Zechariah the oldest, Jediael the second, Zebadiah the third, Jathniel the fourth,
1 Chronicles 26:3 Elam was number five, Jehohanan number six, Elioenai number seven.
1 Chronicles 26:4 Obededom's sons were Shemaiah the oldest, Jehozabad the second, Joah the third, Sacar the fourth, and Nethaneel the fifth.
1 Chronicles 26:5 Ammiel was number six, Issachar number seven, Peulthai number eight, because God blessed him.
1 Chronicles 26:6 Shemaiah's son had sons who led over their father's household because they were brave and strong.
1 Chronicles 26:7 Shemaiah's sons were Othni, Rephael, Obed, and Elzabad. Their strong brothers were Elihu and Semachiah.
1 Chronicles 26:8 All these were sons of Obededom, along with their sons and brothers, strong and able for the work. There were sixty-two of Obededom's family.
1 Chronicles 26:9 Meshelemiah had sons and brothers, strong men, totaling eighteen.
1 Chronicles 26:10 Hosah, from Merari's descendants, had sons; Simri was the leader, because even if he wasn't the firstborn, his father made him the leader.
1 Chronicles 26:11 Hilkiah was number two, Tebaliah number three, Zechariah number four: Hosah had thirteen sons and brothers in total.
1 Chronicles 26:12 These groups were the gatekeepers, among the leaders, taking turns to serve in God's house.
1 Chronicles 26:13 They drew lots, both young and old, by their families, for each gate.
1 Chronicles 26:14 The land to the east was given to Shelemiah. For his son Zechariah, who was a wise advisor, they drew lots; Zechariah's land was to the north.
1 Chronicles 26:15 To Obededom in the south; and to his sons the gatehouse.
1 Chronicles 26:16 Shuppim and Hosah were chosen by lot for the west side, near Shallecheth Gate by the ascending road, each group opposite the other.
1 Chronicles 26:17 To the east there were six Levites, to the north four each day, to the south four each day, and at Asuppim two by two.
1 Chronicles 26:18 In the west at Parbar, there are four guards at the road and two at Parbar.
1 Chronicles 26:19 These are the groups of gatekeepers from the descendants of Kore and the descendants of Merari.
1 Chronicles 26:20 Ahijah the Levite was in charge of the treasures of God's house and the treasures given to God.
1 Chronicles 26:21 The descendants of Laadan, who were part of the Gershon family of Levites, included the family leaders, and Jehieli was their chief.
1 Chronicles 26:22 Jehieli's sons were Zetham and his brother Joel, who were in charge of God's house treasures.
1 Chronicles 26:23 From the families of Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel:
1 Chronicles 26:24 Shebuel, Gershom's son and Moses's grandson, was in charge of the treasures.
1 Chronicles 26:25 His brothers through Eliezer were: Rehabiah his son, Jeshaiah his son, Joram his son, Zichri his son, and Shelomith his son.
1 Chronicles 26:26 Shelomith and his brothers took care of all the special treasure gifts that King David, the family leaders, the commanders of thousands and hundreds, and the army leaders had given to God.
1 Chronicles 26:27 From the goods taken in wars, they set apart some to support the house of God.
1 Chronicles 26:28 Everything that Samuel the prophet, Saul son of Kish, Abner son of Ner, and Joab son of Zeruiah had dedicated, along with anything else dedicated by anyone, was under the care of Shelomith and his brothers.
1 Chronicles 26:29 Chenaniah and his sons from the Izharites were in charge of Israel's external affairs, serving as officers and judges.
1 Chronicles 26:30 From the Hebron people, Hashabiah and his brothers, brave men totaling 1,700, were leaders in Israel on the west side of the Jordan River, handling all of God's work and the king's service.
1 Chronicles 26:31 Jerijah was the leader of the Hebronites, tracing back through his ancestors. In King David's fortieth year as king, they looked for these people and found brave warriors in Jazer of Gilead.
1 Chronicles 26:32 His brothers, brave men, numbered 2,700 leaders, whom King David appointed to oversee the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh, for all matters related to God and the king's business.
