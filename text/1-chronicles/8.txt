1 Chronicles 8:1 Benjamin had a first son named Bela, a second named Ashbel, and a third named Aharah.
1 Chronicles 8:2 Nohah was the fourth, and Rapha was the fifth.
1 Chronicles 8:3 Bela's sons were Addar, Gera, and Abihud.
1 Chronicles 8:4 Abishua, Naaman, and Ahoah,
1 Chronicles 8:5 And Gera, and Shephuphan, and Huram.
1 Chronicles 8:6 Ehud's sons were leaders of Geba's families, and they were moved to Manahath.
1 Chronicles 8:7 Naaman, Ahiah, and Gera were sent away, and they had Uzza and Ahihud.
1 Chronicles 8:8 Shaharaim had children in Moab after he sent his family away; his wives were Hushim and Baara.
1 Chronicles 8:9 He had children with his wife Hodesh: Jobab, Zibia, Mesha, and Malcham.
1 Chronicles 8:10 Jeuz, Shachia, and Mirma were his sons, leaders of their families.
1 Chronicles 8:11 Hushim had two sons: Abitub and Elpaal.
1 Chronicles 8:12 Elpaal's sons were Eber, Misham, and Shamed, who built the cities of Ono and Lod and their surrounding towns.
1 Chronicles 8:13 Beriah and Shema were also leaders of Aijalon's founding families, and they forced out the people of Gath.
1 Chronicles 8:14 Ahio, Shashak, and Jeremoth,
1 Chronicles 8:15 Zebadiah, Arad, and Ader,
1 Chronicles 8:16 Michael, Ispah, and Joha were the sons of Beriah.
1 Chronicles 8:17 Zebadiah, Meshullam, Hezeki, and Heber,
1 Chronicles 8:18 Ishmerai, Jezliah, and Jobab were the sons of Elpaal.
1 Chronicles 8:19 Jakim, Zichri, and Zabdi,
1 Chronicles 8:20 Elienai, Zilthai, and Eliel,
1 Chronicles 8:21 Adaiah, Beraiah, and Shimrath were Shimhi's sons.
1 Chronicles 8:22 Ishpan, Heber, and Eliel,
1 Chronicles 8:23 Abdon, Zichri, and Hanan,
1 Chronicles 8:24 Hananiah, Elam, and Antothijah,
1 Chronicles 8:25 Iphedeiah and Penuel were Shashak's sons.
1 Chronicles 8:26 Shamsherai, Shehariah, and Athaliah.
1 Chronicles 8:27 Jaresiah, Eliah, and Zichri were the sons of Jeroham.
1 Chronicles 8:28 These were leaders of the families, important men by their ancestry. They lived in Jerusalem.
1 Chronicles 8:29 In Gibeon, the founder of the city lived; his wife's name was Maachah.
1 Chronicles 8:30 His oldest son was Abdon, and he also had Zur, Kish, Baal, and Nadab.
1 Chronicles 8:31 Gedor, Ahio, and Zacher.
1 Chronicles 8:32 Mikloth had a son named Shimeah. They also lived near their relatives in Jerusalem.
1 Chronicles 8:33 Ner was the father of Kish, Kish was the father of Saul, Saul was the father of Jonathan, Malchishua, Abinadab, and Eshbaal.
1 Chronicles 8:34 Jonathan's son was Meribbaal, and Meribbaal had a son named Micah.
1 Chronicles 8:35 Micah's sons were Pithon, Melech, Tarea, and Ahaz.
1 Chronicles 8:36 And Ahaz was the father of Jehoadah; Jehoadah was the father of Alemeth, Azmaveth, and Zimri; and Zimri was the father of Moza.
1 Chronicles 8:37 Moza had a son named Binea. Binea's son was Rapha, Rapha's son was Eleasah, and Eleasah's son was Azel.
1 Chronicles 8:38 Azel had six sons named Azrikam, Bocheru, Ishmael, Sheariah, Obadiah, and Hanan. These were all Azel's sons.
1 Chronicles 8:39 Eshek's brother had three sons: Ulam the first, Jehush the second, and Eliphelet the third.
1 Chronicles 8:40 Ulam's sons were brave warriors, skilled with bows, and had lots of sons and grandsons, totaling 150. All these were from Benjamin's family.
