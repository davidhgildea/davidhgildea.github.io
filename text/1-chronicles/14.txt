1 Chronicles 14:1 Hiram, the king of Tyre, sent messengers to David with cedar wood, stone workers, and carpenters to build a house for him.
1 Chronicles 14:2 David realized that God had established him as king over Israel because his kingdom was highly honored due to the people of Israel.
1 Chronicles 14:3 David married more women in Jerusalem and had more sons and daughters.
1 Chronicles 14:4 These are the names of his children born in Jerusalem: Shammua, Shobab, Nathan, and Solomon.
1 Chronicles 14:5 Ibhar, Elishua, and Elpalet,
1 Chronicles 14:6 Nogah, Nepheg, and Japhia,
1 Chronicles 14:7 Elishama, Beeliada, and Eliphalet.
1 Chronicles 14:8 When the Philistines learned David was made king over all of Israel, they all went to find David. David found out and went to face them.
1 Chronicles 14:9 The Philistines came and spread out in the Rephaim Valley.
1 Chronicles 14:10 David asked God, "Should I go fight the Philistines? Will you give them to me?" And God said to him, "Go; I will give them to you."
1 Chronicles 14:11 They went to Baalperazim, and David defeated them there. Then David said, God has helped me defeat my enemies like water bursting through. So they named that place Baalperazim.
1 Chronicles 14:12 After they had abandoned their gods, David ordered them to be burned.
1 Chronicles 14:13 The Philistines once more spread out in the valley.
1 Chronicles 14:14 David asked God again, and God told him, "Do not chase them; go around them and attack from near the mulberry trees."
1 Chronicles 14:15 When you hear a rustling in the mulberry trees, then move out to fight because God has gone ahead of you to strike down the Philistine army.
1 Chronicles 14:16 David did as God told him, and they defeated the Philistine army from Gibeon to Gazer.
1 Chronicles 14:17 David became famous in all the lands and God made all nations fear him.
