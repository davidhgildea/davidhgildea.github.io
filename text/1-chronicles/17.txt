1 Chronicles 17:1 One day, while David was at home, he said to Nathan the prophet, "Look, I live in a house made of cedar wood, but God's covenant chest is still under a tent."
1 Chronicles 17:2 Then Nathan told David, "Do all that you want to do; because God is with you."
1 Chronicles 17:3 That night, God spoke to Nathan and said,
1 Chronicles 17:4 Go and tell David my servant, God says, You must not build a house for me to live in.
1 Chronicles 17:5 Since the day I led Israel up to this day, I have not lived in a house; instead, I moved from one tent to another and from one shelter to another.
1 Chronicles 17:6 Everywhere I have gone with all Israel, did I ask any of the leaders of Israel, whom I told to take care of my people, 'Why haven't you built me a cedar house?'
1 Chronicles 17:7 So now, tell my servant David this: God says, "I chose you from the pasture and from tending sheep to be the leader of my people Israel."
1 Chronicles 17:8 I have always been with you wherever you have gone, and I have defeated all your enemies for you. I have given you a reputation as great as any of the famous people on the earth.
1 Chronicles 17:9 I will choose a place for my people Israel and make them settle there. They will live there and not be uprooted again; the children of wickedness will not harm them anymore like before.
1 Chronicles 17:10 Ever since I told judges to lead my people Israel, I will also defeat all your enemies. Also, I tell you that God will build you a house.
1 Chronicles 17:11 When your time ends and you join your ancestors, I will bring up one of your descendants, one of your sons, and make his kingdom strong.
1 Chronicles 17:12 He will build a house for me, and I will make his throne last forever.
1 Chronicles 17:13 I will be his father, and he will be my son. I will not remove my mercy from him as I did with the one before you.
1 Chronicles 17:14 I will establish him in my house and kingdom forever; his throne will be secure forever.
1 Chronicles 17:15 Nathan told David everything just as he had seen and heard.
1 Chronicles 17:16 King David came and sat before God and said, "Who am I, God, and what is my house, that you have brought me this far?"
1 Chronicles 17:17 Even this was a small thing to you, God; you have also talked about your servant's family for a long time ahead, and you have treated me like someone with high status, O God.
1 Chronicles 17:18 What more can David say to you about the honor of your servant? You know your servant well.
1 Chronicles 17:19 God, for your servant's benefit and following your own will, you have done all these great things and revealed them.
1 Chronicles 17:20 God, no one is like you, and there is no other God except you, just as we have heard.
1 Chronicles 17:21 What other nation on earth is like your people, Israel, whom God saved to be his own, to make himself a great and feared name by removing other nations from before your people whom he rescued from Egypt?
1 Chronicles 17:22 You made Israel your people forever, and you, God, became their God.
1 Chronicles 17:23 So now, God, let what you have said about your servant and his family be true forever, and do as you have promised.
1 Chronicles 17:24 Let it be firm, so your name can be honored forever, as people say, "God commanded the armies is the God of Israel and is a God to Israel." And let David's family, your servant's house, stand strong in your presence.
1 Chronicles 17:25 You, my God, have told your servant that you will build a house for him: so your servant felt moved to pray to you.
1 Chronicles 17:26 And now, God, you are God, and have promised this kindness to your servant.
1 Chronicles 17:27 So now, please bless your servant's house so it will always be before you. Because when you bless, God, it is blessed forever.
