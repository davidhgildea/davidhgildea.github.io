1 Chronicles 21:1 Satan acted against Israel and tempted David to count the people of Israel.
1 Chronicles 21:2 David told Joab and the people's leaders, "Go, count the people of Israel from Beersheba to Dan; then tell me the total so I can know it."
1 Chronicles 21:3 Joab replied, "May God increase his people a hundred times over what they are. But my king, aren't they all your servants? So why does my king want this? Why would he bring guilt on Israel?"
1 Chronicles 21:4 But the king's command was stronger than Joab's objections. So Joab left, traveled all over Israel, and returned to Jerusalem.
1 Chronicles 21:5 Joab told David how many people there were. In Israel, there were one million one hundred thousand soldiers with swords; in Judah, there were four hundred seventy thousand soldiers with swords.
1 Chronicles 21:6 But Levi and Benjamin were not included by him because Joab found the king's command detestable.
1 Chronicles 21:7 God was unhappy with this; so he punished Israel.
1 Chronicles 21:8 David said to God, "I have sinned a lot because I did this. But now, please forgive your servant's wrong; I have been very foolish."
1 Chronicles 21:9 God spoke to Gad, David's prophet, saying,
1 Chronicles 21:10 Go and tell David, 'God says, I am giving you three choices: pick one, so I can do it for you.'
1 Chronicles 21:11 So Gad went to David and said, "This is what God tells you, 'Choose for yourself'
1 Chronicles 21:12 You can choose: three years with not enough food; or three months running from your enemies as they chase you; or three days of a deadly disease in the land, with God's angel bringing destruction everywhere in Israel. Now decide what answer I should take back to the one who sent me.
1 Chronicles 21:13 David told Gad, "I am in big trouble. Let me be punished by God because His mercy is very great, but don't let me be punished by people."
1 Chronicles 21:14 God brought a disease on Israel, and seventy thousand men died.
1 Chronicles 21:15 God sent an angel to destroy Jerusalem, but as the destruction began, God saw what was happening, felt sorry for the harm, and told the angel to stop. The angel then stopped near the threshing floor of Ornan the Jebusite.
1 Chronicles 21:16 David looked up and saw God's angel standing between earth and sky, with a drawn sword in his hand extended over Jerusalem. Then David and the leaders of Israel, wearing rough clothes, fell face down.
1 Chronicles 21:17 David said to God, "Wasn't it me who ordered the counting of the people? It was me who sinned and did wrong. But these sheep, what have they done? Please let your punishment be on me and my family, not on your people, so they don't suffer."
1 Chronicles 21:18 Then God's angel told Gad to tell David to go and build an altar for God on Ornan the Jebusite's threshing floor.
1 Chronicles 21:19 David went up following Gad's instructions, which he gave in God's name.
1 Chronicles 21:20 Ornan turned around, saw the angel, and his four sons with him hid. Ornan was threshing wheat.
1 Chronicles 21:21 When David arrived at Ornan's place, Ornan saw him, left the threshing floor, and bowed down to David with his face to the ground.
1 Chronicles 21:22 David said to Ornan, "Let me have this threshing floor to build an altar for God. Sell it to me at its full price, so that the sickness will stop hurting the people."
1 Chronicles 21:23 Ornan said to David, "Take this and let the king do what seems right to him: Look, I give you the oxen for the burnt offerings, the threshing tools for wood, and the wheat for the grain offering; I give it all."
1 Chronicles 21:24 King David said to Ornan, "No, I will really buy it for the full price. I won't take what is yours for God or give burnt offerings for free."
1 Chronicles 21:25 David paid Ornan six hundred gold shekels for the land.
1 Chronicles 21:26 David made an altar for God, gave burnt and peace offerings, prayed to God, and God responded with fire from heaven on the burnt offering altar.
1 Chronicles 21:27 God told the angel to put his sword back in its sheath.
1 Chronicles 21:28 At that time, David saw that God had answered him at Ornan the Jebusite's threshing floor, and he made sacrifices there.
1 Chronicles 21:29 The tent of God that Moses set up in the desert, and the altar for burnt sacrifices, were then on the hill at Gibeon.
1 Chronicles 21:30 David could not approach to ask God because he was afraid of the angel's sword.
