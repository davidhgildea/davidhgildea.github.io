1 Chronicles 4:1 Judah's sons were: Pharez, Hezron, Carmi, Hur, and Shobal.
1 Chronicles 4:2 Reaiah, Shobal's son, had a son named Jahath; Jahath had sons named Ahumai and Lahad. These are the clans of the Zorathites.
1 Chronicles 4:3 These were Etam's descendants: Jezreel, Ishma, and Idbash; their sister was named Hazelelponi.
1 Chronicles 4:4 Penuel was Gedor's father, and Ezer was Hushah's father. They were Hur's sons, who was Ephrath's first child and Bethlehem's father.
1 Chronicles 4:5 Ashur from Tekoa had two wives, Helah and Naarah.
1 Chronicles 4:6 Naarah had sons named Ahuzam, Hepher, Temeni, and Haahashtari. These were Naarah's sons.
1 Chronicles 4:7 Helah's sons were Zereth, Jezoar, and Ethnan.
1 Chronicles 4:8 Coz had children Anub and Zobebah, and the relatives of Aharhel, Harum's son.
1 Chronicles 4:9 Jabez was more respected than his brothers; his mother named him Jabez, saying, "Because I gave birth to him in pain."
1 Chronicles 4:10 Jabez prayed to the God of Israel and said, "Please bless me greatly, expand my territory, be with me, and keep me from harm so that I will not suffer." And God gave him what he asked for.
1 Chronicles 4:11 Chelub, Shuah's brother, had a son named Mehir, who was Eshton's father.
1 Chronicles 4:12 Eshton had sons called Bethrapha, Paseah, and Tehinnah, who was father of Irnahash. These men were from Rechah.
1 Chronicles 4:13 Kenaz had two sons named Othniel and Seraiah, and Othniel had a son named Hathath.
1 Chronicles 4:14 Meonothai fathered Ophrah, and Seraiah fathered Joab, who was the leader in the Valley of Craftsmen because they were skilled workers.
1 Chronicles 4:15 Caleb, Jephunneh's son, had three sons: Iru, Elah, and Naam. Elah had a son named Kenaz.
1 Chronicles 4:16 Jehaleleel's sons were Ziph, Ziphah, Tiria, and Asareel.
1 Chronicles 4:17 Ezra had sons named Jether, Mered, Epher, and Jalon. He also had a daughter named Miriam, another son named Shammai, and Ishbah, who became the father of Eshtemoa.
1 Chronicles 4:18 Jehudijah, his wife, had three sons: Jered, who started the family of Gedor; Heber, who started the family of Socho; and Jekuthiel, who started the family of Zanoah. All of them were the sons of Bithiah, Pharaoh's daughter, whom Mered married.
1 Chronicles 4:19 Hodiah's sons, Naham's sister and the father of Keilah from Garm and Eshtemoa from Maachah.
1 Chronicles 4:20 Shimon's sons were Amnon, Rinnah, Benhanan, and Tilon. Ishi's sons were Zoheth and Benzoheth.
1 Chronicles 4:21 Shelah, Judah's son, had sons: Er, who started Lecah's family; Laadah, who started Mareshah's family; and families who made fine linen in Ashbea's house.
1 Chronicles 4:22 Jokim and the men of Chozeba, Joash, and Saraph ruled in Moab, and Jashubilehem. These are old stories.
1 Chronicles 4:23 These were the potters and the people who lived near the plants and bushes; there they lived and worked for the king.
1 Chronicles 4:24 Simeon's sons were Nemuel, Jamin, Jarib, Zerah, and Shaul.
1 Chronicles 4:25 Shallum's son, Mibsam's son, Mishma's son.
1 Chronicles 4:26 Mishma's sons were Hamuel, then Zacchur, and then Shimei.
1 Chronicles 4:27 Shimei had sixteen sons and six daughters, but his brothers didn't have many children, nor did their family grow as much as the family of Judah.
1 Chronicles 4:28 They lived in Beersheba, Moladah, and Hazarshual.
1 Chronicles 4:29 In Bilhah, in Ezem, and in Tolad,
1 Chronicles 4:30 In Bethuel, in Hormah, and in Ziklag,
1 Chronicles 4:31 In Bethmarcaboth, Hazarsusim, Bethbirei, and Shaaraim were their cities until David became king.
1 Chronicles 4:32 Their villages were Etam, Ain, Rimmon, Tochen, and Ashan—five cities.
1 Chronicles 4:33 They had villages near those cities, up to Baal, where they lived and kept their family records.
1 Chronicles 4:34 Meshobab, Jamlech, and Joshah, who was Amaziah's son,
1 Chronicles 4:35 Joel and Jehu, Josibiah's son, Seraiah's grandson, Asiel's great-grandson.
1 Chronicles 4:36 Elioenai, Jaakobah, Jeshohaiah, Asaiah, Adiel, Jesimiel, and Benaiah.
1 Chronicles 4:37 Ziza, son of Shiphi, grandson of Allon, great-grandson of Jedaiah, and descendant of Shimri and Shemaiah.
1 Chronicles 4:38 These named were leaders in their families, and their ancestors' family grew a lot.
1 Chronicles 4:39 They went to Gedor's entrance, to the valley's east side, to find grazing land for their sheep.
1 Chronicles 4:40 They found rich and good grasslands, the land was spacious, calm, and peaceful; for the people from Ham had lived there long ago.
1 Chronicles 4:41 These named people came during Hezekiah's rule in Judah, attacked their tents, destroyed the local dwellings, and lived there since it was good land for their sheep.
1 Chronicles 4:42 Some from Simeon's tribe, five hundred men, went to Mount Seir, led by Pelatiah, Neariah, Rephaiah, and Uzziel, Ishi's sons.
1 Chronicles 4:43 They defeated the remaining Amalekites who had escaped, and lived there until today.
