1 Chronicles 7:1 Issachar had four sons: Tola, Puah, Jashub, and Shimrom.
1 Chronicles 7:2 Tola's sons were Uzzi, Rephaiah, Jeriel, Jahmai, Jibsam, and Shemuel, leaders of their family. They were brave and strong. During David's time, their family had 22,600 men.
1 Chronicles 7:3 Uzzi's sons were Izrahiah, and Izrahiah's sons were Michael, Obadiah, Joel, and Ishiah, five leaders in total.
1 Chronicles 7:4 With them, according to their families, were 36,000 soldiers grouped by ancestors, because they had many wives and children.
1 Chronicles 7:5 Their relatives in all the Issachar families were brave and strong warriors, counted in their family records as 87,000 men.
1 Chronicles 7:6 Benjamin had three sons: Bela, Becher, and Jediael.
1 Chronicles 7:7 Bela had five sons: Ezbon, Uzzi, Uzziel, Jerimoth, and Iri. They were leaders in their family and strong warriors. Their family records showed 22,034 descendants.
1 Chronicles 7:8 Becher's sons were Zemira, Joash, Eliezer, Elioenai, Omri, Jerimoth, Abiah, Anathoth, and Alameth. These are all Becher's sons.
1 Chronicles 7:9 The count of these men, according to their family lines and generations, leaders of their ancestors' houses, brave warriors, was twenty thousand and two hundred.
1 Chronicles 7:10 Jediael's sons were Bilhan, and Bilhan's sons were Jeush, Benjamin, Ehud, Chenaanah, Zethan, Tharshish, and Ahishahar.
1 Chronicles 7:11 All these were Jediael's sons, leaders of their families, brave warriors, numbering 17,200 men ready for war and battle.
1 Chronicles 7:12 Shuppim and Huppim, Ir's children, and Hushim, Aher's sons.
1 Chronicles 7:13 Naphtali's sons were Jahziel, Guni, Jezer, and Shallum; Bilhah was their mother.
1 Chronicles 7:14 Manasseh's sons were Ashriel, born from his wife, and Machir, Gilead's father, born from his Aramean concubine.
1 Chronicles 7:15 Machir married Huppim and Shuppim's sister, named Maachah; the second was named Zelophehad, who had daughters.
1 Chronicles 7:16 Maachah, Machir's wife, had a son and named him Peresh; his brother was named Sheresh; the sons of Sheresh were Ulam and Rakem.
1 Chronicles 7:17 Ulam's sons were Bedan. These were Gilead's sons, Machir's son, Manasseh's son.
1 Chronicles 7:18 His sister Hammoleketh had three children: Ishod, Abiezer, and Mahalah.
1 Chronicles 7:19 Shemidah's sons were Ahian, Shechem, Likhi, and Aniam.
1 Chronicles 7:20 Ephraim's sons were Shuthelah, his son Bered, his son Tahath, his son Eladah, and another son named Tahath.
1 Chronicles 7:21 Zabad's son, Shuthelah, Ezer, and Elead were killed by the people of Gath who were from that land because they tried to steal their cattle.
1 Chronicles 7:22 Ephraim, their father, was sad for many days, and his brothers came to make him feel better.
1 Chronicles 7:23 When he was with his wife, she got pregnant and had a baby boy. He named him Beriah because they had trouble in the family.
1 Chronicles 7:24 His daughter Sherah built lower Bethhoron, upper Bethhoron, and Uzzensherah.
1 Chronicles 7:25 Rephah was his son, along with Resheph, then Telah his son, and then Tahan his son.
1 Chronicles 7:26 Laadan's son, Ammihud's son, Elishama's son.
1 Chronicles 7:27 Not his son, Joshua his son.
1 Chronicles 7:28 Their belongings and homes were in Bethel and its surrounding towns, to the east Naaran, to the west Gezer and its towns; also Shechem and its towns, all the way to Gaza and its towns.
1 Chronicles 7:29 Near Manasseh's land, were the cities of Bethshean, Taanach, Megiddo, and Dor. The descendants of Joseph, Israel's son, lived in these places.
1 Chronicles 7:30 Asher's children were Imnah, Isuah, Ishuai, Beriah, and their sister, Serah.
1 Chronicles 7:31 The sons of Beriah were Heber and Malchiel, and Malchiel was the father of Birzavith.
1 Chronicles 7:32 Heber had children: Japhlet, Shomer, Hotham, and their sister Shua.
1 Chronicles 7:33 Japhlet's sons were Pasach, Bimhal, and Ashvath. These are Japhlet's children.
1 Chronicles 7:34 Shamer's sons were Ahi, Rohgah, Jehubbah, and Aram.
1 Chronicles 7:35 Helem's sons were Zophah, Imna, Shelesh, and Amal.
1 Chronicles 7:36 Zophah's sons were Suah, Harnepher, Shual, Beri, and Imrah.
1 Chronicles 7:37 Bezer, Hod, Shamma, Shilshah, Ithran, and Beera.
1 Chronicles 7:38 Jether's sons were Jephunneh, Pispah, and Ara.
1 Chronicles 7:39 Ulla's sons were Arah, Haniel, and Rezia.
1 Chronicles 7:40 All these were Asher's children, leaders of their family, brave and strong. They were top among rulers. The total of their skilled warriors was 26,000 men.
