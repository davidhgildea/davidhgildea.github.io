1 Chronicles 15:1 David built himself homes in the city of David, made a spot ready for God's ark, and set up a tent for it.
1 Chronicles 15:2 David said, only the Levites should carry God's ark because God chose them to carry it and to serve Him forever.
1 Chronicles 15:3 David brought all of Israel to Jerusalem to move God's ark to the spot he had set up for it.
1 Chronicles 15:4 David gathered Aaron's descendants and the Levites.
1 Chronicles 15:5 From Kohath's descendants, Uriel was the leader, and with him were 120 relatives.
1 Chronicles 15:6 From Merari's descendants: Asaiah was the leader, with 220 of his relatives.
1 Chronicles 15:7 From Gershom's descendants, Joel was the leader, with 130 in his family.
1 Chronicles 15:8 From Elizaphan's descendants: Shemaiah was the leader, with two hundred of his relatives.
1 Chronicles 15:9 From Hebron's sons, Eliel was the leader, with eighty of his brothers.
1 Chronicles 15:10 From Uzziel's sons: Amminadab was the leader, and he had 112 brothers.
1 Chronicles 15:11 David called for the priests Zadok and Abiathar, and for the Levites Uriel, Asaiah, Joel, Shemaiah, Eliel, and Amminadab.
1 Chronicles 15:12 He said to them, "You are the leaders of the Levites' ancestors. Make yourselves and your brothers holy so you can bring God's ark to the place I have made ready for it."
1 Chronicles 15:13 Because we didn't do it right the first time, God punished us since we didn't follow the proper way to seek him.
1 Chronicles 15:14 The priests and Levites made themselves pure to carry the ark of God of Israel.
1 Chronicles 15:15 The Levite children carried God's ark on their shoulders with the poles in place, as Moses ordered by God's command.
1 Chronicles 15:16 David told the Levite leaders to choose their relatives to sing and play music with lyres, harps, and cymbals, making joyful sounds with loud voices.
1 Chronicles 15:17 The Levites chose Heman, Joel's son; and from his brothers, they chose Asaph, Berechiah's son; and from the Merari family, Ethan, Kushaiah's son.
1 Chronicles 15:18 Also with them were their fellow workers of the next rank: Zechariah, Ben, Jaaziel, Shemiramoth, Jehiel, Unni, Eliab, Benaiah, Maaseiah, Mattithiah, Elipheleh, Mikneiah, Obededom, and Jeiel, the gatekeepers.
1 Chronicles 15:19 The singers, Heman, Asaph, and Ethan, were chosen to play bronze cymbals.
1 Chronicles 15:20 Zechariah, Aziel, Shemiramoth, Jehiel, Unni, Eliab, Maaseiah, and Benaiah played the harps following the Alamoth style.
1 Chronicles 15:21 Mattithiah, Elipheleh, Mikneiah, Obededom, Jeiel, and Azaziah played their harps tuned to the octave to lead the music.
1 Chronicles 15:22 Chenaniah, the head of the Levites, was in charge of the music. He taught others about music because he was talented.
1 Chronicles 15:23 Berechiah and Elkanah were guards for the ark.
1 Chronicles 15:24 Shebaniah, Jehoshaphat, Nethaneel, Amasai, Zechariah, Benaiah, and Eliezer, who were priests, blew trumpets in front of God's ark, while Obededom and Jehiah guarded the doors of the ark.
1 Chronicles 15:25 David, the leaders of Israel, and the commanders of groups of a thousand went to happily move God's covenant chest from Obed-edom's home.
1 Chronicles 15:26 When God helped the Levites carrying the ark of God's covenant, they sacrificed seven bulls and seven rams.
1 Chronicles 15:27 David wore a fine linen robe, and so did all the Levites carrying the ark, the singers, and Chenaniah who led them in music. David also wore a linen ephod.
1 Chronicles 15:28 So all of Israel carried the ark of God's covenant with shouting, with cornet sounds, trumpets, cymbals, and made music with lyres and harps.
1 Chronicles 15:29 When the ark of God's covenant arrived at David's city, Michal, Saul's daughter, watched from a window as King David danced and played music, and she felt contempt for him in her heart.
