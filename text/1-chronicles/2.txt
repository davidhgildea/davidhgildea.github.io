1 Chronicles 2:1 These are Israel's sons: Reuben, Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, and Zebulun.
1 Chronicles 2:2 Dan, Joseph, Benjamin, Naphtali, Gad, Asher.
1 Chronicles 2:3 Judah's sons were Er, Onan, and Shelah. These three were born to him by Shua's daughter, a Canaanite woman. Er, Judah's first son, was wicked in God's eyes, so God took his life.
1 Chronicles 2:4 Tamar, his daughter-in-law, gave birth to Pharez and Zerah for him. Judah had a total of five sons.
1 Chronicles 2:5 Pharez's sons were Hezron and Hamul.
1 Chronicles 2:6 Zerah's sons were Zimri, Ethan, Heman, Calcol, and Dara: five in total.
1 Chronicles 2:7 Carmi had a son named Achar, who caused trouble in Israel by sinning with the cursed item.
1 Chronicles 2:8 Ethan's son was Azariah.
1 Chronicles 2:9 Hezron's sons were Jerahmeel, Ram, and Chelubai.
1 Chronicles 2:10 Ram fathered Amminadab; and Amminadab fathered Nahshon, the leader of Judah's descendants.
1 Chronicles 2:11 Nahshon had a son named Salma, and Salma had a son named Boaz.
1 Chronicles 2:12 Boaz had Obed, and Obed had Jesse.
1 Chronicles 2:13 Jesse had his first son Eliab, his second son Abinadab, and his third son Shimma.
1 Chronicles 2:14 Nethaneel was fourth, Raddai was fifth,
1 Chronicles 2:15 Ozem was the sixth, David was the seventh.
1 Chronicles 2:16 His sisters were Zeruiah and Abigail. Zeruiah's sons were Abishai, Joab, and Asahel, three in total.
1 Chronicles 2:17 Abigail gave birth to Amasa, and Amasa's father was Jether the Ishmaelite.
1 Chronicles 2:18 Caleb, Hezron's son, had kids with his wife Azubah and Jerioth. Their sons were Jesher, Shobab, and Ardon.
1 Chronicles 2:19 After Azubah died, Caleb married Ephrath, who gave birth to Hur.
1 Chronicles 2:20 Hur had a son named Uri, and Uri had a son named Bezaleel.
1 Chronicles 2:21 Later, Hezron married the daughter of Machir, father of Gilead, when he was 60 years old, and she gave birth to Segub.
1 Chronicles 2:22 Segub was the father of Jair, who owned twenty-three cities in Gilead.
1 Chronicles 2:23 He captured Geshur and Aram with Jair's villages and Kenath with its surrounding towns, a total of sixty cities. All these were the lands of Machir, Gilead's father.
1 Chronicles 2:24 After Hezron died in Calebephratah, his wife Abiah had a son named Ashur, who became the founder of Tekoa.
1 Chronicles 2:25 Jerahmeel, Hezron's oldest son, had these sons: Ram, his firstborn, Bunah, Oren, Ozem, and Ahijah.
1 Chronicles 2:26 Jerahmeel also had another wife named Atarah; she was Onam's mother.
1 Chronicles 2:27 Ram's firstborn Jerahmeel had sons: Maaz, Jamin, and Eker.
1 Chronicles 2:28 Onam's sons were Shammai and Jada. Shammai's sons were Nadab and Abishur.
1 Chronicles 2:29 Abishur's wife was named Abihail, and she had two sons for him, Ahban and Molid.
1 Chronicles 2:30 Nadab's sons were Seled and Appaim, but Seled died without having any kids.
1 Chronicles 2:31 Appaim's sons were Ishi, Ishi's sons were Sheshan, and Sheshan's children were Ahlai.
1 Chronicles 2:32 Jada, the brother of Shammai, had two sons: Jether and Jonathan. But Jether died without having any children.
1 Chronicles 2:33 Jonathan's sons were Peleth and Zaza. These were Jerahmeel's sons.
1 Chronicles 2:34 Sheshan had only daughters, not sons. Sheshan's servant was an Egyptian named Jarha.
1 Chronicles 2:35 Sheshan gave his daughter as a wife to Jarha, his servant, and she gave birth to a son named Attai.
1 Chronicles 2:36 Attai fathered Nathan, and Nathan fathered Zabad.
1 Chronicles 2:37 Zabad had a son named Ephlal, who had a son named Obed.
1 Chronicles 2:38 Obed had a son named Jehu, and Jehu had a son named Azariah.
1 Chronicles 2:39 Azariah had a son named Helez, and Helez had a son named Eleasah.
1 Chronicles 2:40 Eleasah had a son named Sisamai, and Sisamai had a son named Shallum.
1 Chronicles 2:41 Shallum had a son named Jekamiah, and Jekamiah had a son named Elishama.
1 Chronicles 2:42 Caleb's brother Jerahmeel had sons. Mesha was the oldest, and he became the father of Ziph. Mareshah's sons became the fathers of Hebron.
1 Chronicles 2:43 Hebron's sons were Korah, Tappuah, Rekem, and Shema.
1 Chronicles 2:44 Shema had Raham, who became the father of Jorkoam, and Rekem had Shammai.
1 Chronicles 2:45 Shammai's son was Maon, and Maon was Bethzur's father.
1 Chronicles 2:46 Ephah, Caleb's woman, had Haran, Moza, and Gazez; and Haran had Gazez.
1 Chronicles 2:47 Jahdai's sons were Regem, Jotham, Gesham, Pelet, Ephah, and Shaaph.
1 Chronicles 2:48 Maachah, who was with Caleb, had two sons named Sheber and Tirhanah.
1 Chronicles 2:49 She also gave birth to Shaaph, who was Madmannah's ancestor, Sheva, who was Machbenah's ancestor, and the ancestor of Gibea; Caleb's daughter was Achsa.
1 Chronicles 2:50 Caleb, Hur's son and Ephratah's oldest, had these sons: Shobal started Kirjathjearim.
1 Chronicles 2:51 Salma was the ancestor of Bethlehem; Hareph was the ancestor of Bethgader.
1 Chronicles 2:52 Shobal, the father of Kirjathjearim, had sons: Haroeh and some of the Manahethites.
1 Chronicles 2:53 The families of Kirjathjearim were the Ithrites, the Puhites, the Shumathites, and the Mishraites. From these families came the Zareathites and the Eshtaulites.
1 Chronicles 2:54 Salma's children included Bethlehem, the people of Netophah, Ataroth, Joab's family, half of the people of Manahath, and the Zorites.
1 Chronicles 2:55 The families of writers who lived in Jabez were the Tirathites, the Shimeathites, and the Suchathites. They are the Kenites who come from Hemath, the ancestor of the Rechab family.
