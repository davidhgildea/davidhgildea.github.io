Acts 11:1 The apostles and believers in Judea heard that non-Jews also accepted God's message.
Acts 11:2 When Peter arrived in Jerusalem, those who were circumcised argued with him.
Acts 11:3 You are accused of going to uncircumcised men and eating with them.
Acts 11:4 But Peter explained the story from the start, and told it to them step by step, saying,
Acts 11:5 I was in Joppa praying when I fell into a trance and saw a vision. I saw something like a large sheet coming down from heaven, held by its four corners, and it came down to me.
Acts 11:6 I looked closely and saw animals with four feet, wild creatures, crawling things, and birds of the air.
Acts 11:7 I heard a voice telling me, "Get up, Peter; kill and eat."
Acts 11:8 But I said, "No, God, because I have never eaten anything that is not pure or clean."
Acts 11:9 The voice from heaven told me again, "What God has made clean, do not call ordinary."
Acts 11:10 This happened three times, and then everything was taken back up into heaven.
Acts 11:11 Look, right away three men who had been sent to me from Caesarea arrived at the house where I was staying.
Acts 11:12 The Spirit told me to go with them without doubting. Also, these six friends came with me, and we went into the man's house.
Acts 11:13 He showed us how he saw an angel in his house who stood and said to him, "Send men to Joppa and ask for Simon, also known as Peter."
Acts 11:14 Someone will tell you messages by which you and your whole family will be saved.
Acts 11:15 As I started to talk, the Holy Spirit came on them, just like it did on us at first.
Acts 11:16 I remembered what God said: John baptized with water, but you will be baptized with the Holy Spirit.
Acts 11:17 Since God gave them the same gift as he gave us who believed in Jesus Christ, who was I to oppose God?
Acts 11:18 When they heard this, they became quiet and praised God, saying, "So God has also given to non-Jews the chance to turn from their sins and receive life."
Acts 11:19 Those forced away by the troubles starting with Stephen went to places like Phoenicia, Cyprus, and Antioch, telling God's message only to Jews.
Acts 11:20 Some of them were from Cyprus and Cyrene, and when they arrived in Antioch, they spoke to the Greeks, telling them about Jesus.
Acts 11:21 God helped them, and many people believed and turned to God.
Acts 11:22 News about these events reached the church in Jerusalem, so they sent Barnabas to go to Antioch.
Acts 11:23 When he arrived and saw God's grace, he was happy and encouraged everyone to stay loyal to God with a determined heart.
Acts 11:24 He was a good man, filled with the Holy Spirit and faith, and many people joined God.
Acts 11:25 Barnabas went to Tarsus to look for Saul.
Acts 11:26 When he found him, he took him to Antioch. For a whole year, they met with the church and taught many people. It was in Antioch that the disciples were first called Christians.
Acts 11:27 During this time, prophets traveled from Jerusalem to Antioch.
Acts 11:28 One of them, named Agabus, stood up and showed by the Spirit that a big famine would happen all over the world, and it did happen when Claudius Caesar was in power.
Acts 11:29 The followers decided to send help to their fellow believers in Judaea, each one giving as much as they could.
Acts 11:30 They did this and sent it to the leaders through Barnabas and Saul.
