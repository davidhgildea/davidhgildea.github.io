Acts 8:1 Saul agreed to his death. There was a big persecution against the church in Jerusalem; and everyone was scattered across Judaea and Samaria, except the apostles.
Acts 8:2 Faithful people took Stephen to bury him and cried a lot for him.
Acts 8:3 Saul caused great trouble for the church, going into houses and dragging out men and women to put them in jail.
Acts 8:4 So those who were spread out went all around telling God's message.
Acts 8:5 Philip went to the city of Samaria and told the people about Christ.
Acts 8:6 The people unanimously paid attention to what Philip said, listening and watching the miracles he performed.
Acts 8:7 Many people with evil spirits shouted loudly as the spirits left them. And many who could not move or walk were healed.
Acts 8:8 The city was filled with great happiness.
Acts 8:9 A man named Simon lived in that city before. He practiced magic, amazing the people of Samaria, and claimed to be someone important.
Acts 8:10 Everyone listened to him closely, from the least important to the most important, saying, "This man has great power from God."
Acts 8:11 They respected him because for a long time he had amazed them with his magic.
Acts 8:12 When people believed what Philip was saying about God's kingdom and Jesus Christ, they got baptized, both men and women.
Acts 8:13 Simon also believed and after his baptism, he stayed with Philip, amazed by the miracles and signs he saw.
Acts 8:14 When the apostles in Jerusalem learned that Samaria had accepted God's word, they sent Peter and John to them.
Acts 8:15 When they arrived, they prayed for them so they could receive the Holy Spirit.
Acts 8:16 Because none of them had received the Holy Spirit yet; they had only been baptized in the name of Jesus.
Acts 8:17 They placed their hands on them and they received the Holy Spirit.
Acts 8:18 When Simon noticed that the Holy Spirit was given after the apostles laid their hands on people, he offered them money.
Acts 8:19 Give me this power too, so anyone I touch can receive the Holy Spirit.
Acts 8:20 Peter told him, "May your money be lost with you, because you believed that God's gift can be bought with money."
Acts 8:21 You have no part or share in this matter, because your heart is not right with God.
Acts 8:22 Turn away from your evil ways and ask God to forgive the thoughts of your heart.
Acts 8:23 I see that you are full of bitterness and trapped in sin.
Acts 8:24 Simon answered, "Please pray to God for me so that none of the things you talked about will happen to me."
Acts 8:25 After they told about God and shared his message, they went back to Jerusalem and spoke about Jesus in many Samaritan towns.
Acts 8:26 God's messenger spoke to Philip, saying, "Get up and go south on the road from Jerusalem to Gaza, which is in the desert."
Acts 8:27 He got up and went, and there was a man from Ethiopia, a high-ranking official under Queen Candace of the Ethiopians, who was in charge of all her wealth and had come to Jerusalem to worship.
Acts 8:28 He was coming back, sitting in his chariot, and reading the prophet Isaiah.
Acts 8:29 Then the Spirit told Philip, "Go close and join this chariot."
Acts 8:30 Philip ran to him, heard him reading the prophet Isaiah, and asked, "Do you understand what you are reading?"
Acts 8:31 He asked, "How can I understand unless someone explains it to me?" So he invited Philip to come up and sit beside him.
Acts 8:32 The part of the scripture he read said, "He was led like a sheep to be killed, and like a quiet lamb before someone cutting its wool, he did not open his mouth."
Acts 8:33 In his low state, he was denied justice: and who can speak of his descendants? For his life is taken from the earth.
Acts 8:34 The eunuch asked Philip, "Please, who is the prophet talking about? Himself or someone else?"
Acts 8:35 Then Philip started speaking and, using that part of the Bible, told him about Jesus.
Acts 8:36 As they traveled, they found some water. The eunuch said, "Look, here's water. What's stopping me from getting baptized?"
Acts 8:37 Philip said, "If you believe with all your heart, you can." And he replied, "I believe Jesus Christ is God's Son."
Acts 8:38 He told the chariot to stop. Then both Philip and the eunuch went down into the water, and Philip baptized him.
Acts 8:39 After they came up from the water, God's Spirit took Philip away, and the eunuch didn't see him again but continued on his journey happily.
Acts 8:40 Philip appeared in Azotus, and as he traveled, he shared the message in all the cities until he reached Caesarea.
