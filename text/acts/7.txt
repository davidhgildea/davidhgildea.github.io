Acts 7:1 Then the high priest asked, "Is this true?"
Acts 7:2 He said, "Listen, men and fathers, the glorious God appeared to our ancestor Abraham in Mesopotamia before he lived in Haran.
Acts 7:3 God told him, "Leave your country and your relatives, and go to the land I will show you."
Acts 7:4 He left the land of the Chaldeans, lived in Haran, and after his father died, God moved him to this land where you live now.
Acts 7:5 God did not give him any land to own, not even a place to step on. But God promised to give it to him and to his children after him, even though he had no child yet.
Acts 7:6 God said in this way: That his descendants would live in a foreign land; and they would be made slaves and treated badly for four hundred years.
Acts 7:7 God said: I will judge the nation where they are slaves, and afterwards, they will come out and worship me here.
Acts 7:8 God made a promise with Abraham about circumcision: So Abraham had a son, Isaac, and circumcised him on the eighth day; Isaac had Jacob; and Jacob had twelve sons who became leaders.
Acts 7:9 The family leaders, jealous of Joseph, sold him as a slave to Egypt; but God stayed with him.
Acts 7:10 God saved him from all his troubles, gave him favor and wisdom before Pharaoh, king of Egypt, and Pharaoh made him ruler over Egypt and his whole house.
Acts 7:11 A famine spread over all of Egypt and Canaan, bringing much suffering, and our ancestors couldn't find food.
Acts 7:12 When Jacob found out there was grain in Egypt, he sent our ancestors there first.
Acts 7:13 Joseph revealed his identity to his brothers a second time, and his family became known to Pharaoh.
Acts 7:14 Joseph sent for his father Jacob and all his relatives, 75 people in total.
Acts 7:15 Jacob went to Egypt and he and our ancestors died there.
Acts 7:16 They were taken to Shechem and placed in the tomb that Abraham purchased from the sons of Hamor, Shechem's father.
Acts 7:17 As the time for God's promise to Abraham came near, the people increased and became many in Egypt.
Acts 7:18 A new king came to power who did not know about Joseph.
Acts 7:19 This person tricked our relatives and treated our ancestors badly, making them throw out their babies so they would not survive.
Acts 7:20 During this time, Moses was born, was very beautiful, and was cared for in his father's home for three months.
Acts 7:21 When he was thrown away, Pharaoh's daughter picked him up and cared for him as her own son.
Acts 7:22 Moses knew all the wisdom of the Egyptians and was powerful in speaking and acting.
Acts 7:23 When he was 40 years old, he felt the urge to see his fellow Israelites.
Acts 7:24 He saw someone being treated unfairly, so he defended him, took revenge for the one who was hurt, and struck down the Egyptian.
Acts 7:25 He thought his brothers would realize that God would use him to save them, but they didn't understand.
Acts 7:26 The next day he appeared to them as they were fighting, wanting to reconcile them, saying, "Friends, you are brothers; why are you hurting each other?"
Acts 7:27 The person who wronged his neighbor pushed him away, asking, "Who made you our ruler and judge?"
Acts 7:28 Will you kill me like you killed the Egyptian yesterday?
Acts 7:29 Moses ran away after hearing this and lived as a foreigner in the land of Midian, where he had two sons.
Acts 7:30 After forty years passed, he saw an angel of God in a fire inside a bush in the desert near Mount Sinai.
Acts 7:31 When Moses saw it, he was amazed at the sight. As he went closer to look, God's voice spoke to him.
Acts 7:32 I am the God of your ancestors, the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. Moses shook with fear and did not dare to look.
Acts 7:33 God said to him, "Take off your shoes because the place where you are standing is holy ground."
Acts 7:34 I have seen my people's suffering in Egypt, heard their cries, and have come to rescue them. Now go, I will send you to Egypt.
Acts 7:35 They rejected Moses, asking, "Who made you a leader and judge?" But God chose him to be their leader and rescuer, with the help of the angel who met him in the bush.
Acts 7:36 God led them out after showing them amazing things and signs in Egypt, at the Red Sea, and in the desert for forty years.
Acts 7:37 This is Moses, who told the Israelites, "God will bring up a prophet like me from among your brothers; you must listen to him."
Acts 7:38 This is the one who was with the angel in the church in the wilderness, on Mount Sinai, and with our ancestors: he received the living messages to give to us.
Acts 7:39 Our ancestors did not want to obey him. Instead, they pushed him away and wanted to return to Egypt in their hearts.
Acts 7:40 Tell Aaron to make us gods to lead us: for this Moses who took us out of Egypt, we don't know what happened to him.
Acts 7:41 They made a calf back then, gave offerings to the idol and were happy with what they had done.
Acts 7:42 God let them worship the stars of the sky. As the prophets wrote, "People of Israel, did you bring me animal sacrifices for forty years in the desert?"
Acts 7:43 Yes, you carried the tent of Moloch and the star of your god Remphan, idols you made to worship them: and I will take you away beyond Babylon.
Acts 7:44 Our ancestors had the tent of testimony in the desert, as God commanded, telling Moses to make it like the design he had seen.
Acts 7:45 Our ancestors, including Joshua, took over the land of the nations, which God had cleared away for them, all the way until the time of David.
Acts 7:46 He found favor with God and wanted to build a home for the God of Jacob.
Acts 7:47 Solomon built a house for God.
Acts 7:48 However, the highest does not live in buildings made by hands, as the prophet says.
Acts 7:49 Heaven is where I sit as king, and the earth is a place to rest my feet. What kind of home could you build for me? What place could be my resting spot?
Acts 7:50 Didn't my hand make all these things?
Acts 7:51 You are stubborn and closed in your heart and ears, you always resist the Holy Spirit, just as your ancestors did.
Acts 7:52 Which prophets did your ancestors not attack? They killed those who told about the arrival of the Righteous One, whom you have now betrayed and murdered.
Acts 7:53 They received the law given through angels but have not followed it.
Acts 7:54 When they heard this, they felt deep pain in their hearts and gritted their teeth at him.
Acts 7:55 He, filled with the Holy Spirit, looked intently into heaven and saw God's glory, with Jesus standing at God's right side.
Acts 7:56 And said, Look, I see the sky opened, and the Son of man standing at God's right side.
Acts 7:57 They shouted loudly, covered their ears, and all rushed at him together.
Acts 7:58 They threw him out of the city and stoned him. The witnesses left their coats near a young man named Saul.
Acts 7:59 They threw stones at Stephen, who called out to God, saying, "Lord Jesus, take my spirit."
Acts 7:60 He kneeled and shouted, "God, do not hold this sin against them." After he said this, he passed away.
