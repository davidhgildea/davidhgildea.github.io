Acts 20:1 After the disturbance stopped, Paul gathered the disciples, hugged them, and left to go to Macedonia.
Acts 20:2 After he traveled through those areas and encouraged the people a lot, he went to Greece.
Acts 20:3 He stayed there three months. When the Jews planned to catch him as he prepared to sail to Syria, he decided to go back through Macedonia.
Acts 20:4 Going with him to Asia were Sopater from Berea; from Thessalonica, Aristarchus and Secundus; Gaius from Derbe; Timothy; and from Asia, Tychicus and Trophimus.
Acts 20:5 Those who went ahead waited for us in Troas.
Acts 20:6 We left Philippi after the Festival of Unleavened Bread and reached Troas in five days, where we stayed for seven days.
Acts 20:7 On the first day of the week, when the followers met to share a meal, Paul spoke to them, planning to leave the next day. He kept talking until midnight.
Acts 20:8 There were many lamps in the upstairs room where they met.
Acts 20:9 A young man named Eutychus was sitting in a window and fell into a deep sleep while Paul was speaking for a long time. He fell asleep, dropped from the third story, and was picked up dead.
Acts 20:10 Paul went down, hugged him, and said, "Don't worry; he's still alive."
Acts 20:11 After he returned, shared food, ate, and talked for many hours until morning, he left.
Acts 20:12 They brought the young man back alive, and they were very comforted.
Acts 20:13 We went ahead to the ship and sailed to Assos to pick up Paul, as he had planned to join us there on foot.
Acts 20:14 When he joined us at Assos, we brought him aboard and sailed to Mitylene.
Acts 20:15 We sailed from there and reached Chios the next day; the following day we went to Samos and stopped at Trogyllium, and the day after, we arrived at Miletus.
Acts 20:16 Paul decided to pass by Ephesus because he did not want to stay in Asia. He hurried to try to be in Jerusalem for Pentecost.
Acts 20:17 From Miletus, he sent a message to Ephesus and invited the church leaders.
Acts 20:18 When they arrived, he told them, "You are aware, from the first day I arrived in Asia, how I have always acted with you."
Acts 20:19 Serving God with complete humility, and with many tears and trials that happened to me because of the Jews' secret plots.
Acts 20:20 I held back nothing useful for you, but showed and taught you openly and in homes.
Acts 20:21 Telling Jews and Greeks to turn to God and believe in our Lord Jesus Christ.
Acts 20:22 And now, I go to Jerusalem, tied by the spirit, not knowing what will happen to me there.
Acts 20:23 The Holy Spirit warns me in every city that chains and troubles are waiting for me.
Acts 20:24 These things do not bother me, and I do not consider my life important to myself, so I can complete my work happily, and the service I received from Lord Jesus, to share the good news of God's kindness.
Acts 20:25 Now I know that all of you, whom I have taught about God's kingdom, will not see me again.
Acts 20:26 Today, I tell you that I am innocent of harming anyone.
Acts 20:27 I have not avoided telling you everything God wants you to know.
Acts 20:28 Be careful to look after yourselves and everyone in your group, because the Holy Spirit has made you leaders to care for God's church that he bought with his blood.
Acts 20:29 I know that after I leave, fierce wolves will come among you and not be kind to the sheep.
Acts 20:30 From among you, some will come, saying wrong things to lead followers to themselves.
Acts 20:31 So be alert and remember that for three years I never stopped warning each of you, day and night, with tears.
Acts 20:32 Now, friends, I entrust you to God and to his kind message, which can strengthen you and provide you with a share among all who are made holy.
Acts 20:33 I have not desired anyone's money, gold, or clothes.
Acts 20:34 Yes, you know that these hands have worked for my needs and for those with me.
Acts 20:35 I have shown you in every way, by working hard you must help the weak and remember what Jesus said, "It is better to give than to get."
Acts 20:36 After saying this, he knelt down and prayed with everyone.
Acts 20:37 They all cried hard, hugged Paul, and kissed him.
Acts 20:38 They were very sad because he said they would never see him again. And they went with him to the boat.
