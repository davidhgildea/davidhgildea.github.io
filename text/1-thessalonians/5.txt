1 Thessalonians 5:1 About the times and dates, friends, you don't need me to write to you.
1 Thessalonians 5:2 You know very well that the day of God will come like a thief in the night.
1 Thessalonians 5:3 When people say, "Peace and safety," then sudden ruin will hit them, like labor pains on a pregnant woman, and they won't escape.
1 Thessalonians 5:4 But friends, you are not in the dark, so that the day should surprise you like a thief.
1 Thessalonians 5:5 You are all children of light and of the day; we are not of the night or of darkness.
1 Thessalonians 5:6 So let's not sleep like others; instead, let's stay alert and clear-headed.
1 Thessalonians 5:7 People who sleep, sleep at night; and those who get drunk, get drunk at night.
1 Thessalonians 5:8 Let us who live in the light be clear-minded, wearing faith and love as a protective vest, and the hope of being saved as a helmet.
1 Thessalonians 5:9 God has not chosen us for anger, but to gain salvation through our Lord Jesus Christ.
1 Thessalonians 5:10 He died for us so that, awake or asleep, we might live with him.
1 Thessalonians 5:11 Encourage each other and build each other up, just as you are already doing.
1 Thessalonians 5:12 We ask you, friends, to recognize those who work hard among you, who guide you in the Lord, and who warn you.
1 Thessalonians 5:13 Value them greatly with love because of their work and live peacefully with each other.
1 Thessalonians 5:14 We urge you, friends, to warn those who are not following the rules, encourage those who are discouraged, help those who are weak, and be patient with everyone.
1 Thessalonians 5:15 Make sure no one pays back wrong for wrong to anyone; but always try to do what is good, for each other and for everyone else.
1 Thessalonians 5:16 Be happy always.
1 Thessalonians 5:17 Keep praying all the time.
1 Thessalonians 5:18 In everything, be thankful: because this is what God wants for you in Christ Jesus.
1 Thessalonians 5:19 Do not put out the Spirit's fire.
1 Thessalonians 5:20 Do not look down on prophecies.
1 Thessalonians 5:21 Test everything; keep what is good.
1 Thessalonians 5:22 Stay away from anything that looks evil.
1 Thessalonians 5:23 May the God of peace make you completely holy, and I hope God keeps your spirit, soul, and body without fault until our Lord Jesus Christ comes.
1 Thessalonians 5:24 God, who calls you, is faithful and will do what he promises.
1 Thessalonians 5:25 Brothers and sisters, pray for us.
1 Thessalonians 5:26 Say hello to all the brothers and sisters with a holy kiss.
1 Thessalonians 5:27 I ask you seriously in God's name to make sure this letter is read to all the believers.
1 Thessalonians 5:28 May the kindness of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you. Amen.
