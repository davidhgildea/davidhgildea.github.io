1 Thessalonians 1:1 Paul, Silvanus, and Timothy, to the Thessalonian church in God and Jesus Christ: may you have grace and peace from God our Father and Jesus Christ.
1 Thessalonians 1:2 We always thank God for all of you, remembering you in our prayers.
1 Thessalonians 1:3 We constantly remember your faith in action, love's hard work, and hopeful patience in our Lord Jesus Christ, before God our Father.
1 Thessalonians 1:4 Dear friends, know that God has chosen you.
1 Thessalonians 1:5 Our good news didn't come to you just by words, but also with power, God's Spirit, and strong confidence; as you know how we acted among you for your benefit.
1 Thessalonians 1:6 You followed our example and God's, accepting the message in great trouble yet with Holy Spirit's joy.
1 Thessalonians 1:7 So you became examples to all the believers in Macedonia and Achaia.
1 Thessalonians 1:8 From you, the message of God has spread not only in Macedonia and Achaia but also everywhere your faith in God is known; so we don't need to say anything more.
1 Thessalonians 1:9 People tell how we came to you, and how you turned to God from idols to serve the true and living God.
1 Thessalonians 1:10 To wait for his Son from heaven, whom God raised from the dead, Jesus who saves us from the coming anger.
