Genesis 4:1 Adam was intimate with his wife Eve; she became pregnant and gave birth to Cain, saying, "I have had a son with God's help."
Genesis 4:2 She also gave birth to his brother Abel. Abel took care of sheep, while Cain worked the soil.
Genesis 4:3 After some time, Cain gave some of his crops as an offering to God.
Genesis 4:4 Abel also gave the best of his sheep and their fat. And God respected Abel and his gift.
Genesis 4:5 God did not accept Cain and his offering. Cain became very angry, and his face showed it.
Genesis 4:6 God asked Cain, "Why are you angry? And why do you look upset?"
Genesis 4:7 If you do good, won't you be accepted? But if you don't do good, sin is waiting to enter. It desires to control you, but you must rule over it.
Genesis 4:8 Cain spoke to his brother Abel, and while they were in the field, Cain attacked Abel and killed him.
Genesis 4:9 God asked Cain, "Where is your brother Abel?" Cain replied, "I don't know. Am I supposed to look after my brother?"
Genesis 4:10 He asked, "What have you done? Your brother's blood calls out to me from the ground."
Genesis 4:11 You are now cursed from the ground, which opened up to take your brother's blood from your hands.
Genesis 4:12 When you farm the land, it will no longer give you its best crops; you will be a wanderer and a runaway on the earth.
Genesis 4:13 Cain said to God, "My punishment is too much for me to handle."
Genesis 4:14 Look, you have forced me to leave the land today; I will be hidden from you and I will wander the earth; anyone who finds me will kill me.
Genesis 4:15 God said to him, "So, if anyone kills Cain, they will be punished seven times more." And God put a mark on Cain to prevent anyone who found him from killing him.
Genesis 4:16 Cain left God's presence and lived in the land of Nod, east of Eden.
Genesis 4:17 Cain was intimate with his wife; she became pregnant and gave birth to Enoch. Then he built a city and named it Enoch, after his son.
Genesis 4:18 Enoch had a son named Irad. Irad had a son named Mehujael. Mehujael had a son named Methusael. Methusael had a son named Lamech.
Genesis 4:19 Lamech married two women: one named Adah and the other named Zillah.
Genesis 4:20 Adah gave birth to Jabal: he became the ancestor of those who live in tents and keep livestock.
Genesis 4:21 His brother's name was Jubal; he was the first musician who played the harp and flute.
Genesis 4:22 Zillah also had Tubalcain, a teacher of all metalworkers in brass and iron, and Tubalcain's sister was Naamah.
Genesis 4:23 Lamech told his wives, Adah and Zillah, "Listen to me; you, Lamech's wives, hear what I say: I have killed a man because he wounded me, and a young man because he hurt me."
Genesis 4:24 If God punishes someone seven times for hurting Cain, then for hurting Lamech, God will punish seventy-seven times.
Genesis 4:25 Adam was close with his wife again, and she had a son named Seth. She said, "God has given me another child to replace Abel, who Cain killed."
Genesis 4:26 Seth also had a son, and he named him Enos. This is when people began to pray to God.
