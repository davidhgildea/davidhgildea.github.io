Genesis 9:1 God blessed Noah and his sons and said to them, "Have many children, increase in number, and fill the earth."
Genesis 9:2 Every land animal, bird in the sky, creature that moves on the ground, and fish in the sea will be afraid of you; they are all given into your control.
Genesis 9:3 All living creatures that move shall be food for you; just as I have given you all plants.
Genesis 9:4 Do not eat meat that still has its life in it, which is its blood.
Genesis 9:5 I will certainly demand an accounting for human lifeblood; from every animal and from each person I will demand it, and I will require it from anyone who takes another person's life.
Genesis 9:6 Anyone who takes a person's life must have their own life taken by others because God made humans in his own image.
Genesis 9:7 You, be fruitful and increase in number; produce a lot on the earth and grow more.
Genesis 9:8 God spoke to Noah and his sons, saying,
Genesis 9:9 I will make my promise with you and your descendants after you.
Genesis 9:10 And with every animal with you, the birds, the livestock, and all the wild animals on earth with you; from all that came out of the ark, to every wild animal on earth.
Genesis 9:11 I will make my promise with you, no living things will be wiped out by a flood again, and there will not be another flood to ruin the earth.
Genesis 9:12 God said, "This is the sign of the promise I make between me and you and every living creature with you, for all time to come."
Genesis 9:13 I put my rainbow in the cloud, and it will be a sign of an agreement between me and the earth.
Genesis 9:14 When I bring a cloud over the earth, you will see the bow in the cloud.
Genesis 9:15 I will remember my agreement with you and every living being; never again will the waters turn into a flood to destroy all life.
Genesis 9:16 The rainbow will be in the sky, and when I see it, I will remember the forever promise between God and every living being of all kinds on the earth.
Genesis 9:17 God told Noah, "This is the sign of the promise I made with all living things on earth."
Genesis 9:18 Noah's sons who left the ark were Shem, Ham, and Japheth; and Ham was Canaan's father.
Genesis 9:19 Noah had three sons, and from them the whole world was populated.
Genesis 9:20 Noah started to farm and planted a vineyard.
Genesis 9:21 He drank the wine, got drunk, and lay uncovered inside his tent.
Genesis 9:22 Ham, Canaan's father, saw his father naked and told his two brothers outside.
Genesis 9:23 Shem and Japheth took a cloth, put it on their shoulders, walked backward, and covered their father's bare body. Their faces were turned away, and they did not see their father unclothed.
Genesis 9:24 Noah woke up from drinking wine and realized what his youngest son had done to him.
Genesis 9:25 He said, "Cursed is Canaan; he will be the lowest servant to his brothers."
Genesis 9:26 He said, "God bless Shem; Canaan will be his servant."
Genesis 9:27 God will make Japheth's land and people grow, and he will live with Shem's family; Canaan will serve him.
Genesis 9:28 Noah lived for 350 years after the flood.
Genesis 9:29 Noah lived for 950 years, then he died.
