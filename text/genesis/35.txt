Genesis 35:1 God told Jacob, "Get up and go to Bethel to live. There, build an altar to God who showed Himself to you when you were running away from your brother Esau."
Genesis 35:2 Jacob told his family and everyone with him, "Get rid of the foreign gods you have, make yourselves clean, and change your clothes."
Genesis 35:3 Let's get up and go to Bethel. There I will build an altar to God, who helped me when I was in trouble and who has been with me wherever I have gone.
Genesis 35:4 They gave Jacob all the foreign gods they had and their earrings; Jacob buried them under the oak near Shechem.
Genesis 35:5 They traveled, and God's terror was on the surrounding cities, so they did not chase Jacob's sons.
Genesis 35:6 Jacob arrived at Luz, in Canaan, also known as Bethel, with everyone who was with him.
Genesis 35:7 He made an altar there and named the place Elbethel, because God had shown himself to him as he was running from his brother.
Genesis 35:8 Rebekah's caregiver Deborah died and they buried her under an oak tree near Bethel. They named the place "Oak of Weeping."
Genesis 35:9 God showed Himself to Jacob again after he left Padanaram and blessed him.
Genesis 35:10 God said to him, "Your name is Jacob. Your name will no longer be Jacob, but Israel will be your name." And he named him Israel.
Genesis 35:11 God told him, "I am God Almighty: have many children and grow into a nation and many nations will come from you, and you will have kings among your descendants."
Genesis 35:12 I will give you and your descendants the land I gave to Abraham and Isaac.
Genesis 35:13 God left him at the place where he had spoken with him.
Genesis 35:14 Jacob put up a stone pillar where he had talked with God. He poured out a drink offering on it, and also poured oil on it.
Genesis 35:15 Jacob named the place where God spoke with him Bethel.
Genesis 35:16 They left Bethel, and were not far from Ephrath when Rachel started to give birth, and it was difficult.
Genesis 35:17 When she was giving birth with difficulty, the midwife told her, "Don't be afraid; you will have another son too."
Genesis 35:18 As she was dying, she named her son Benoni, but his father named him Benjamin.
Genesis 35:19 Rachel died and was buried on the road to Ephrath, now called Bethlehem.
Genesis 35:20 Jacob put up a stone marker on her burial site; this is Rachel's grave marker to this day.
Genesis 35:21 Israel traveled and set up his tent past the Tower of Edar.
Genesis 35:22 When Israel lived in that land, Reuben slept with Bilhah, his father's partner, and Israel found out. Jacob had twelve sons.
Genesis 35:23 Leah's sons: Reuben, Jacob's first son, Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, and Zebulun.
Genesis 35:24 Rachel's sons are Joseph and Benjamin.
Genesis 35:25 Bilhah, Rachel's servant, had two sons: Dan and Naphtali.
Genesis 35:26 Zilpah, Leah's servant, had two sons with Jacob: Gad and Asher. They were born to Jacob in Padanaram.
Genesis 35:27 Jacob went to his father Isaac in Mamre, to the town of Arbah, now called Hebron, where Abraham and Isaac had lived.
Genesis 35:28 Isaac lived for 180 years.
Genesis 35:29 Isaac passed away, died, and joined his ancestors, being very old: and his sons Esau and Jacob buried him.
