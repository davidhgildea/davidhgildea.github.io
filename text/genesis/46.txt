Genesis 46:1 Israel traveled with everything he owned, went to Beersheba, and gave sacrifices to the God of his father Isaac.
Genesis 46:2 God spoke to Israel in a night vision, saying, "Jacob, Jacob." And he replied, "I'm here."
Genesis 46:3 He said, "I am God, your father's God. Do not be afraid to go to Egypt, because I will make you into a great nation there."
Genesis 46:4 I will go down to Egypt with you, and I will surely bring you back again, and Joseph will close your eyes.
Genesis 46:5 Jacob left Beersheba, and Israel's sons took Jacob their father, their children, and their wives in the carts that Pharaoh had provided for him.
Genesis 46:6 They took their animals and belongings, which they had acquired in Canaan, and went to Egypt, with Jacob and all his descendants.
Genesis 46:7 He brought his sons, grandsons, daughters, granddaughters, and all his family with him to Egypt.
Genesis 46:8 These are the names of Israel's children who went to Egypt: Jacob and his sons, Reuben, Jacob's oldest.
Genesis 46:9 Reuben's sons were Hanoch, Phallu, Hezron, and Carmi.
Genesis 46:10 Simeon's sons were Jemuel, Jamin, Ohad, Jachin, Zohar, and Shaul, whose mother was a Canaanite woman.
Genesis 46:11 The sons of Levi were Gershon, Kohath, and Merari.
Genesis 46:12 Judah had sons; Er, Onan, Shelah, Pharez, and Zarah. But, Er and Onan died in Canaan. Pharez had sons, Hezron and Hamul.
Genesis 46:13 Issachar's sons were Tola, Phuvah, Job, and Shimron.
Genesis 46:14 Zebulun's sons were Sered, Elon, and Jahleel.
Genesis 46:15 Leah had six sons and a daughter named Dinah with Jacob in Padanaram. In total, Jacob had thirty-three children.
Genesis 46:16 Gad's sons were Ziphion, Haggi, Shuni, Ezbon, Eri, Arodi, and Areli.
Genesis 46:17 Asher's sons were Jimnah, Ishuah, Isui, and Beriah, and their sister was Serah. Beriah's sons were Heber and Malchiel.
Genesis 46:18 These are Zilpah's sons, given by Laban to his daughter Leah, and she had them with Jacob—a total of sixteen people.
Genesis 46:19 Rachel, Jacob's wife, had two sons: Joseph and Benjamin.
Genesis 46:20 In Egypt, Joseph had two sons named Manasseh and Ephraim, born to him by Asenath, who was the daughter of Potipherah, the priest of On.
Genesis 46:21 Benjamin's sons were Belah, Becher, Ashbel, Gera, Naaman, Ehi, Rosh, Muppim, Huppim, and Ard.
Genesis 46:22 Rachel had fourteen children with Jacob.
Genesis 46:23 Dan's sons: Hushim.
Genesis 46:24 Naphtali's sons were Jahzeel, Guni, Jezer, and Shillem.
Genesis 46:25 These are Bilhah's sons, whom Laban gave to his daughter Rachel, and she had them for Jacob: in total, there were seven people.
Genesis 46:26 Everyone who went to Egypt with Jacob, except for the wives of his sons, totaled 66 people.
Genesis 46:27 Joseph had two sons born to him in Egypt. In total, seventy people from Jacob's family came to Egypt.
Genesis 46:28 He sent Judah ahead to Joseph to show the way to Goshen, and they arrived in Goshen.
Genesis 46:29 Joseph prepared his chariot, went to meet his father Israel in Goshen, showed himself to him, hugged him tightly, and cried on his shoulder for a long time.
Genesis 46:30 Israel said to Joseph, "Now I can die happily because I have seen you and you are still alive."
Genesis 46:31 Joseph told his brothers and his father's family, "I will go and tell Pharaoh, 'My brothers and my father's family from Canaan have come to me.'"
Genesis 46:32 The men are shepherds because their job has been to take care of animals; and they have brought their sheep, their cattle, and everything they own.
Genesis 46:33 When the king of Egypt asks you what you do,
Genesis 46:34 Tell them, "We have always worked with animals, from when we were young to now, just like our ancestors did. This is so you can live in Goshen, because Egyptians dislike all shepherds."
