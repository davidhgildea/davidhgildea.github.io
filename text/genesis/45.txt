Genesis 45:1 Joseph could not hold back in front of everyone present; he shouted, "Everyone leave me!" No one stayed with him when Joseph revealed his identity to his brothers.
Genesis 45:2 He cried loudly, and the Egyptians and Pharaoh's household heard it.
Genesis 45:3 Joseph said to his brothers, "I am Joseph. Is our father still alive?" But his brothers couldn't answer him because they were upset to see him.
Genesis 45:4 Joseph told his brothers, "Please come closer to me." They came closer. Then he said, "I am your brother Joseph, the one you sold into Egypt."
Genesis 45:5 So now, don't be sad or mad at yourselves for selling me here; because God sent me ahead of you to save lives.
Genesis 45:6 For two years now, there has been famine in the land, and for the next five years, there will be no plowing or harvesting.
Genesis 45:7 God sent me ahead of you to keep your family alive on the earth, and to save your lives with a big rescue.
Genesis 45:8 It was not you who sent me here, but God. He has made me like a father to Pharaoh, the boss of his household, and the leader of all Egypt.
Genesis 45:9 Hurry and go to my father, and tell him, 'Your son Joseph says, God has made me ruler over all of Egypt: come to me, don't delay.'
Genesis 45:10 You will live in the land of Goshen, close to me, with your children, grandchildren, sheep, cattle, and all your possessions.
Genesis 45:11 I will provide for you there, because five more years of famine are coming, so that you, your family, and all you have won't become poor.
Genesis 45:12 Look, your eyes and my brother Benjamin's eyes can see that it is really me talking to you.
Genesis 45:13 Tell my father about all my success in Egypt and everything you've seen; hurry and bring my father here.
Genesis 45:14 He hugged his brother Benjamin and cried; and Benjamin cried on his shoulder.
Genesis 45:15 He kissed all his brothers and cried on them; then his brothers spoke with him.
Genesis 45:16 People in Pharaoh's palace heard that Joseph's brothers had come, and this news made Pharaoh and his servants very happy.
Genesis 45:17 Pharaoh said to Joseph, "Tell your brothers to do this: Load your animals and go to the land of Canaan."
Genesis 45:18 Bring your father and your families to me, and I will give you the best parts of Egypt, and you will have plenty to eat.
Genesis 45:19 God has ordered you to do this: Get carts from Egypt for your children and wives, and bring your father here.
Genesis 45:20 Don't worry about your belongings; all the best of Egypt is yours.
Genesis 45:21 The Israelites did as told, and Joseph gave them carts as Pharaoh ordered, and also gave them food for the journey.
Genesis 45:22 He gave clothes to all of them, but he gave Benjamin three hundred silver coins and five sets of clothes.
Genesis 45:23 He sent his father the following: ten donkeys carrying Egypt's best goods, and ten female donkeys loaded with grain, bread, and food for his journey.
Genesis 45:24 He sent his brothers off, and they left. He told them, "Make sure you don't argue on the way."
Genesis 45:25 They left Egypt and arrived in Canaan to meet their father Jacob.
Genesis 45:26 They told him, "Joseph is still alive and he rules over all of Egypt." But Jacob felt weak because he did not believe them.
Genesis 45:27 They told him everything Joseph said to them, and when he saw the carts Joseph sent to bring him, Jacob their father felt better.
Genesis 45:28 Israel said, "It is enough; my son Joseph is still alive. I will go and see him before I die."
