Genesis 11:1 Everyone on earth spoke the same language.
Genesis 11:2 As they traveled from the east, they found a flat area in the land of Shinar and they settled there.
Genesis 11:3 They said to each other, "Come, let's make bricks and bake them well." They used bricks instead of stones and tar for mortar.
Genesis 11:4 They said, "Come on, let's build a city and a tower that reaches to the sky, so we can make a name for ourselves and not be spread out over the whole world."
Genesis 11:5 God came down to see the city and the tower that people were building.
Genesis 11:6 God said, "Look, the people are united, and they all speak the same language; this is what they have started to do: now they can do anything they think of."
Genesis 11:7 Let's go down and mix up their language, so they can't understand each other.
Genesis 11:8 So God spread them out from there all over the earth, and they stopped building the city.
Genesis 11:9 So they named it Babel, because there God mixed up everyone's language and scattered them all over the world.
Genesis 11:10 These are Shem's family records: Shem was 100 years old and fathered Arphaxad two years after the flood.
Genesis 11:11 After Shem became the father of Arphaxad, he lived 500 years and had other sons and daughters.
Genesis 11:12 Arphaxad was 35 years old when he became the father of Salah.
Genesis 11:13 After Arphaxad became the father of Salah, he lived 403 years and had other sons and daughters.
Genesis 11:14 Salah was thirty years old when he became the father of Eber.
Genesis 11:15 Salah lived for 403 years after Eber's birth and had more sons and daughters.
Genesis 11:16 Eber lived 34 years and then became the father of Peleg.
Genesis 11:17 Eber lived for 430 years after the birth of Peleg and had more sons and daughters.
Genesis 11:18 Peleg was thirty years old when he became the father of Reu.
Genesis 11:19 Peleg lived for 209 years after Reu was born, and he had more sons and daughters.
Genesis 11:20 Reu was thirty-two when he became the father of Serug.
Genesis 11:21 Reu lived for 207 years after Serug was born, and he had more sons and daughters.
Genesis 11:22 Serug was thirty years old when he had Nahor.
Genesis 11:23 Serug lived for 200 years after Nahor was born and had more sons and daughters.
Genesis 11:24 Nahor lived 29 years and had a son named Terah.
Genesis 11:25 After Nahor had his son Terah, he lived 119 more years and had more sons and daughters.
Genesis 11:26 Terah lived for 70 years and had three sons: Abram, Nahor, and Haran.
Genesis 11:27 Here are the descendants of Terah: Terah was the father of Abram, Nahor, and Haran; and Haran was the father of Lot.
Genesis 11:28 Haran died before his father Terah in his birthplace, in Ur of the Chaldees.
Genesis 11:29 Abram and Nahor married. Abram's wife was Sarai, and Nahor's wife was Milcah, Haran's daughter, who was also Iscah's father.
Genesis 11:30 Sarai could not have children; she had none.
Genesis 11:31 Terah took his son Abram, his grandson Lot, and his daughter-in-law Sarai, Abram's wife; and they left Ur of the Chaldees to go to Canaan. But they stopped at Haran and lived there.
Genesis 11:32 Terah lived for 205 years and then he died in Haran.
