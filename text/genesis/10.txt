Genesis 10:1 Here are the family lines of Noah's sons: Shem, Ham, and Japheth. These sons had their own children after the flood.
Genesis 10:2 Japheth's sons were Gomer, Magog, Madai, Javan, Tubal, Meshech, and Tiras.
Genesis 10:3 Gomer's sons were Ashkenaz, Riphath, and Togarmah.
Genesis 10:4 Javan's sons were Elishah, Tarshish, Kittim, and Dodanim.
Genesis 10:5 These people spread out over the islands and lands of non-Jewish peoples; each group by its language and family, in their own countries.
Genesis 10:6 Ham's sons were Cush, Mizraim, Phut, and Canaan.
Genesis 10:7 Cush had several sons: Seba, Havilah, Sabtah, Raamah, and Sabtechah. Raamah had two sons: Sheba and Dedan.
Genesis 10:8 Cush had a son named Nimrod, who became a powerful leader on Earth.
Genesis 10:9 He was a strong hunter before God; that's why people say, "Like Nimrod, a strong hunter before God."
Genesis 10:10 His kingdom started with Babel, Erech, Accad, and Calneh in Shinar's land.
Genesis 10:11 From that country, Asshur left and built Nineveh, the city Rehoboth, and Calah.
Genesis 10:12 Resen is between Nineveh and Calah; it is a great city.
Genesis 10:13 Mizraim had sons named Ludim, Anamim, Lehabim, and Naphtuhim.
Genesis 10:14 And the people of Pathrus, Casluh, (from whom the Philistines came), and Caphtor.
Genesis 10:15 Canaan had Sidon, his oldest son, and Heth.
Genesis 10:16 And the Jebusite, the Amorite, and the Girgasite,
Genesis 10:17 The Hivite, the Arkite, and the Sinite,
Genesis 10:18 The Arvadites, Zemarites, and Hamathites were there too. Later, the Canaanite families spread out.
Genesis 10:19 The Canaanites' land started at Sidon, went to Gerar and then to Gaza; from there it reached all the way to Sodom, Gomorrah, Admah, Zeboim, and Lasha.
Genesis 10:20 These are Ham's sons, grouped by their families, languages, lands, and nations.
Genesis 10:21 Shem, the ancestor of all Eber's descendants and Japheth's older brother, also had children.
Genesis 10:22 Shem's children were Elam, Asshur, Arphaxad, Lud, and Aram.
Genesis 10:23 Aram's children were Uz, Hul, Gether, and Mash.
Genesis 10:24 Arphaxad had a son named Salah, and Salah had a son named Eber.
Genesis 10:25 Eber had two sons. One was named Peleg, because the earth was divided during his life. His brother was named Joktan.
Genesis 10:26 Joktan was the father of Almodad, Sheleph, Hazarmaveth, and Jerah.
Genesis 10:27 Hadoram, Uzal, and Diklah
Genesis 10:28 And Obal, Abimael, and Sheba,
Genesis 10:29 Ophir, Havilah, and Jobab were all Joktan's sons.
Genesis 10:30 Their home was from Mesha to Sephar, a mountain in the east.
Genesis 10:31 These are Shem's sons, by their families, languages, in their countries, and by their nations.
Genesis 10:32 These are the families of Noah's sons, grouped by their descendants, within their countries. And from these families the nations were spread out over the earth after the flood.
