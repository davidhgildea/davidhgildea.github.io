Genesis 34:1 Dinah, Leah's daughter whom she had with Jacob, went out to meet the local girls.
Genesis 34:2 When Shechem, son of Hamor the Hivite and ruler of the land, saw her, he grabbed her, slept with her, and dishonored her.
Genesis 34:3 He felt a deep connection with Dinah, Jacob's daughter, loved her, and spoke gently to her.
Genesis 34:4 Shechem said to his father Hamor, "Get me this girl to be my wife."
Genesis 34:5 Jacob found out someone had mistreated his daughter Dinah. His sons were out in the fields with the livestock, so Jacob stayed quiet until they returned.
Genesis 34:6 Hamor, Shechem's father, went to talk to Jacob.
Genesis 34:7 Jacob's sons returned from the fields when they heard the news. They felt deeply hurt and very angry because someone did something very foolish in Israel by sleeping with Jacob's daughter, which should never have happened.
Genesis 34:8 Hamor spoke to them, saying, "My son Shechem deeply desires to marry your daughter. Please let her become his wife."
Genesis 34:9 Marry with us, give us your daughters, and take our daughters for yourselves.
Genesis 34:10 You will live with us, and the land will be open to you; live in it, trade in it, and own property in it.
Genesis 34:11 Shechem said to her father and brothers, "I hope you will favor me, and I will give whatever you ask of me."
Genesis 34:12 Ask for any amount of money or gifts for a dowry, and I will pay what you ask; but let me marry the young woman.
Genesis 34:13 Jacob's sons lied to Shechem and his father Hamor when they spoke because Shechem had disgraced their sister Dinah.
Genesis 34:14 They told them, "We can't do this and let our sister marry a man who is not circumcised; that would be shameful for us."
Genesis 34:15 We agree with this condition: if every man among you gets circumcised, like us.
Genesis 34:16 We will let you marry our daughters, and we will marry your daughters, and we will live with you, and we will become one group of people.
Genesis 34:17 But if you won't listen to us and agree to be circumcised, then we will take our daughter and leave.
Genesis 34:18 Hamor and his son Shechem liked what they heard.
Genesis 34:19 The young man did not hesitate to do it because he liked Jacob's daughter, and he was more respectable than everyone in his family.
Genesis 34:20 Hamor and his son Shechem went to the entrance of their city and talked with the men of their city, saying,
Genesis 34:21 These men are friendly towards us; so let them live in the land and do business there; the land is big enough for them; we can marry their daughters, and they can marry ours.
Genesis 34:22 The men will agree to live with us and become one people only if every male among us gets circumcised just like they are.
Genesis 34:23 Won't their animals, belongings, and all their beasts become ours? Just agree with them, and they will live with us.
Genesis 34:24 Everyone in the city listened to Hamor and his son Shechem, and every man was circumcised just as they were.
Genesis 34:25 On the third day, when they were in pain, Jacob's two sons, Simeon and Levi, Dinah's brothers, each took a sword, attacked the city with courage, and killed all the men.
Genesis 34:26 They killed Hamor and his son Shechem with their swords, took Dinah from Shechem's house, and left.
Genesis 34:27 Jacob's sons attacked the dead men and looted the city because those men had dishonored their sister.
Genesis 34:28 They took their sheep, oxen, donkeys, and everything in the city and fields.
Genesis 34:29 They captured all their riches, children, and wives, and took everything in the house.
Genesis 34:30 Jacob said to Simeon and Levi, "You have caused me problems, making me look bad to the people here, the Canaanites and Perizzites. We are few in number; they will unite against me, attack, and wipe out me and my family."
Genesis 34:31 They asked, "Should he treat our sister like a prostitute?"
