Genesis 29:1 Jacob continued his travel and reached the land of the eastern people.
Genesis 29:2 He saw a well in the field, with three groups of sheep nearby; they used that well to give water to the sheep, and a large stone covered the well's opening.
Genesis 29:3 All the flocks were brought there. They moved the stone from the well's opening, gave water to the sheep, and put the stone back over the well's opening where it was before.
Genesis 29:4 Jacob asked them, "My brothers, where are you from?" They replied, "We are from Haran."
Genesis 29:5 He asked them, "Do you know Laban, Nahor's son?" They replied, "Yes, we know him."
Genesis 29:6 He asked them, "Is he doing well?" They replied, "He is well," and look, Rachel, his daughter, is coming with the sheep.
Genesis 29:7 He said, "Look, the day is still long; it's not time to bring the cattle together. Give the sheep water and go feed them."
Genesis 29:8 They said, "We can't until all the sheep are together and the stone is moved from the well's opening; then we give the sheep water."
Genesis 29:9 While he was still talking to them, Rachel arrived with her father's sheep because she was taking care of them.
Genesis 29:10 When Jacob saw Rachel, Laban's daughter, and Laban's sheep, he went up, moved the stone from the well, and watered Laban's sheep.
Genesis 29:11 Jacob kissed Rachel, then he cried out loud.
Genesis 29:12 Jacob explained to Rachel that he was her dad's relative and the son of Rebekah, then she quickly went to tell her dad.
Genesis 29:13 When Laban heard about his nephew Jacob, he ran to greet him, hugged and kissed him, and took him to his home. Then Jacob told Laban everything.
Genesis 29:14 Laban said to him, "You are truly my family, my own flesh and bone." And he stayed with Laban for a month.
Genesis 29:15 Laban said to Jacob, "Just because you are my brother, should you work for me for free? Tell me, what do you want to be paid?"
Genesis 29:16 Laban had two daughters: the older was named Leah, and the younger was named Rachel.
Genesis 29:17 Leah had soft eyes; but Rachel was very beautiful.
Genesis 29:18 Jacob loved Rachel and said, "I will work for you seven years to marry your younger daughter Rachel."
Genesis 29:19 Laban said, "It's better for me to give her to you than to another man. Stay with me."
Genesis 29:20 Jacob worked seven years for Rachel; they felt like only a few days to him because he loved her so much.
Genesis 29:21 Jacob said to Laban, "Give me my wife because the time is complete, so I can be with her."
Genesis 29:22 Laban brought all the local men together and prepared a big meal.
Genesis 29:23 In the evening, he brought his daughter Leah to him, and they were together.
Genesis 29:24 Laban gave his daughter Leah his servant Zilpah to be her maid.
Genesis 29:25 In the morning, he saw it was Leah. He asked Laban, "What have you done to me? Didn't I work for you to marry Rachel? Why have you tricked me?"
Genesis 29:26 Laban said, "In our country, we don't allow the younger daughter to marry before the older one."
Genesis 29:27 Finish her seven days, and we will let you marry the other one if you work for me for another seven years.
Genesis 29:28 Jacob did this and finished the week; then he also married Rachel, his daughter.
Genesis 29:29 Laban gave his daughter Rachel his servant Bilhah to be her maid.
Genesis 29:30 He also went to Rachel, loved Rachel more than Leah, and worked for seven more years.
Genesis 29:31 When God saw that Leah was not loved, he let her have children, but Rachel could not have children.
Genesis 29:32 Leah became pregnant, had a son, and named him Reuben, because she said, "God has seen my troubles; now my husband will love me."
Genesis 29:33 She became pregnant again and gave birth to a son. She said, "God has heard that I was unloved, so He has given me another son." She named him Simeon.
Genesis 29:34 She became pregnant again and had a son. She said, "Now my husband will feel close to me, because I have given birth to three sons for him." That is why she named him Levi.
Genesis 29:35 She became pregnant again and had a son. She said, "Now I will praise God." So she named him Judah and stopped having children.
