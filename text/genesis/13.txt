Genesis 13:1 Abram left Egypt with his wife, all his possessions, and Lot, heading south.
Genesis 13:2 Abram was very wealthy with many animals, silver, and gold.
Genesis 13:3 He traveled from the south to Bethel, to the spot where he first pitched his tent, between Bethel and Ai.
Genesis 13:4 To the altar spot he first made, Abram called on God's name there.
Genesis 13:5 Lot, who traveled with Abram, had sheep, cattle, and tents.
Genesis 13:6 The land could not support them living together because they had too much stuff, so they could not stay together.
Genesis 13:7 Abram's cattle herders and Lot's cattle herders argued, and the Canaanite and Perizzite people were living in the land at that time.
Genesis 13:8 Abram said to Lot, "Please, let's not have any arguments between you and me, or between our herders, because we are family."
Genesis 13:9 Isn't all the land in front of you? Please separate from me. If you choose the left side, I will go to the right; if you go to the right side, I will go to the left.
Genesis 13:10 Lot looked around and saw the whole Jordan Valley, which had plenty of water everywhere, like God's garden or the land of Egypt, before God destroyed Sodom and Gomorrah, all the way to Zoar.
Genesis 13:11 Lot picked the whole Jordan valley for himself; then Lot traveled east. So they went their separate ways.
Genesis 13:12 Abram lived in Canaan, and Lot lived in the cities of the plain, and set up his tent near Sodom.
Genesis 13:13 The Sodom men were very bad and sinned a lot against God.
Genesis 13:14 God said to Abram, after Lot had left him, "Now, lift up your eyes and look from where you are, to the north, south, east, and west."
Genesis 13:15 I will give you and your descendants all the land you can see, forever.
Genesis 13:16 I will make your descendants as many as the dust of the earth; if someone can count the dust, then they can count your descendants too.
Genesis 13:17 Get up, walk around the land through its length and width; for I will give it to you.
Genesis 13:18 Abram moved his tent, went to live in Mamre's plain in Hebron, and built an altar for God there.
