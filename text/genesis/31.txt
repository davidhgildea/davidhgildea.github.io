Genesis 31:1 He heard Laban's sons say, "Jacob has taken everything our father owned, and from our father's wealth, he has gained all this wealth."
Genesis 31:2 Jacob saw that Laban's attitude toward him was not the same as before.
Genesis 31:3 God said to Jacob, "Go back to the land of your fathers and your relatives, and I will be with you."
Genesis 31:4 Jacob sent for Rachel and Leah to come to the field where his sheep were.
Genesis 31:5 He told them, "I can tell that your father doesn't look at me the same way as before; but my father's God has been with me."
Genesis 31:6 You know I have served your father with all my strength.
Genesis 31:7 Your father tricked me and changed my pay ten times, but God did not let him harm me.
Genesis 31:8 If he promised, "You will be paid with speckled animals," then all the animals were speckled. And if he promised, "Your pay will be the striped ones," then all the animals were striped.
Genesis 31:9 God has taken your father's animals and given them to me.
Genesis 31:10 When the cattle mated, I looked up and in my dream I saw that the male sheep jumping on the cattle had stripes, spots, and gray patches.
Genesis 31:11 The angel of God spoke to me in a dream and said, "Jacob." I answered, "I am here."
Genesis 31:12 He said, "Look up and see, all the male sheep mating with the flock are striped, spotted, and dark-colored, because I have seen everything that Laban is doing to you."
Genesis 31:13 I am the God of Bethel, where you poured oil on the stone and made a promise to me. Now get up, leave this land, and go back to the land of your relatives.
Genesis 31:14 Rachel and Leah replied, "Do we still have any share or inheritance in our father's home?"
Genesis 31:15 Doesn't he think of us as strangers? He sold us and has completely spent our money.
Genesis 31:16 All the wealth that God took from our father belongs to us and our children. So now, do whatever God has told you to do.
Genesis 31:17 Then Jacob got up, put his sons and wives on camels.
Genesis 31:18 He took all his animals and everything he owned, the livestock he had acquired in Paddan Aram, to go to his father Isaac in Canaan.
Genesis 31:19 Laban went to cut his sheep's wool, and Rachel took the idols that belonged to her father.
Genesis 31:20 Jacob left without telling Laban the Syrian, so Laban didn't know that he had run away.
Genesis 31:21 So he ran away with everything he owned; he got up, crossed the river, and headed for Mount Gilead.
Genesis 31:22 On the third day, Laban heard that Jacob had run away.
Genesis 31:23 He took his brothers with him and chased him for seven days; they caught up to him at Mount Gilead.
Genesis 31:24 God appeared to Laban the Syrian in a dream at night and told him, "Be careful not to say anything to Jacob, whether it's good or bad."
Genesis 31:25 Laban caught up with Jacob. Jacob had set up his tent on the hill, and Laban and his brothers camped on the hill of Gilead.
Genesis 31:26 Laban said to Jacob, "What have you done? You've left without telling me and taken my daughters as if they were captured by force."
Genesis 31:27 Why did you run away secretly and leave without telling me? If you had told me, I could have sent you off happily with music and songs, with drums and harps.
Genesis 31:28 You didn't let me kiss my sons and daughters goodbye? You acted foolishly by doing this.
Genesis 31:29 I have the power to harm you, but your father's God told me last night to be careful not to say anything good or bad to Jacob.
Genesis 31:30 And now, even though you really wanted to leave because you missed your father's home, why did you steal my gods?
Genesis 31:31 Jacob replied to Laban, "I was scared that you might forcefully take your daughters away from me."
Genesis 31:32 If you find your gods with anyone, that person should not survive. Let our relatives see what's yours with me and take it. Jacob did not know Rachel had taken them.
Genesis 31:33 Laban went into Jacob's tent, Leah's tent, and the two female servants' tents; but he didn't find them. Then he left Leah's tent and went into Rachel's tent.
Genesis 31:34 Rachel took the idols and put them in the camel's saddle, then she sat on them. Laban searched the whole tent but did not find them.
Genesis 31:35 She said to her father, "Please don't be upset that I can't get up in front of you; I'm having my period." He searched but didn't find the idols.
Genesis 31:36 Jacob was angry and argued with Laban. Jacob replied, "Laban, what have I done wrong? What is my sin that you have chased after me so eagerly?"
Genesis 31:37 Since you looked through all my things, what of your household items did you find? Put it here in front of my brothers and your brothers, so they can decide between us.
Genesis 31:38 I have been with you for twenty years; your female sheep and goats have not lost their babies, and I have not eaten the males of your flock.
Genesis 31:39 I didn't bring you animals killed by beasts; I covered the loss myself. Whether they were stolen during the day or night, you made me responsible.
Genesis 31:40 I was like this: In the daytime, drought drained me, and at night, frost; and I could not sleep.
Genesis 31:41 I have been in your house for twenty years; I worked for you fourteen years to marry your two daughters, and six years for your livestock; and you have changed my pay ten times.
Genesis 31:42 If my father's God, Abraham's God, and the one Isaac fears had not been with me, you would have sent me away with nothing. But God saw my troubles and my hard work, and he corrected you last night.
Genesis 31:43 Laban replied to Jacob, "These daughters are my daughters, these children are my children, these animals are my animals, and everything you see is mine. What can I do today for my daughters or for their children they have given birth to?"
Genesis 31:44 So come on, let's make an agreement, you and I, and it will serve as evidence between us.
Genesis 31:45 Jacob took a stone and set it up as a pillar.
Genesis 31:46 Jacob told his brothers, "Get some stones." So they gathered stones and built a pile. They ate there by the pile.
Genesis 31:47 Laban named it Jegarsahadutha, but Jacob named it Galeed.
Genesis 31:48 Laban said, "This pile of stones is a witness between us today. That's why it was named Galeed."
Genesis 31:49 And Mizpah; for he said, "May God watch over us when we are apart from each other."
Genesis 31:50 If you hurt my daughters, or if you marry other women besides my daughters, no one is with us; see, God is a witness between you and me.
Genesis 31:51 Laban said to Jacob, "Look at this pile of stones and look at this pillar that I've put between us.
Genesis 31:52 This pile of stones will be a witness, and this stone pillar will be a witness, that I will not go past this pile to hurt you, and you will not go past this pile and pillar to hurt me.
Genesis 31:53 God of Abraham and Nahor, the God of their father, decides between us. And Jacob promised, respecting his father Isaac.
Genesis 31:54 Jacob made a sacrifice on the mountain, invited his brothers to share a meal, and they ate and stayed overnight on the mountain.
Genesis 31:55 Early in the morning, Laban got up, kissed his sons and daughters, blessed them, and then he left and went back to his home.
