Genesis 5:1 This book lists Adam's descendants. On the day God made humans, he made them to look like himself.
Genesis 5:2 God created them male and female; he blessed them and named them Human when they were created.
Genesis 5:3 Adam lived 130 years, had a son who looked like him, and named him Seth.
Genesis 5:4 Adam lived 800 years after the birth of Seth, and he had other sons and daughters.
Genesis 5:5 Adam lived for 930 years, and then he died.
Genesis 5:6 Seth lived 105 years and had a son named Enos.
Genesis 5:7 After Seth had Enos, he lived 807 years and had other sons and daughters.
Genesis 5:8 Seth lived 912 years, and then he died.
Genesis 5:9 Enos was ninety years old when he became the father of Cainan.
Genesis 5:10 After Enos had his son Cainan, he lived 815 more years and had other sons and daughters.
Genesis 5:11 Enos lived for 905 years; then he died.
Genesis 5:12 Cainan was seventy years old when he became the father of Mahalaleel.
Genesis 5:13 Cainan lived 840 years after he had Mahalaleel, and had more sons and daughters.
Genesis 5:14 Cainan lived for 910 years, and then he died.
Genesis 5:15 Mahalaleel was sixty-five years old when he became the father of Jared.
Genesis 5:16 After Mahalaleel had Jared, he lived 830 years and had more sons and daughters.
Genesis 5:17 Mahalaleel lived for 895 years; then he died.
Genesis 5:18 Jared lived 162 years and then had a son named Enoch.
Genesis 5:19 After Jared had Enoch, he lived for 800 more years and had other sons and daughters.
Genesis 5:20 Jared lived 962 years, and then he died.
Genesis 5:21 Enoch was 65 years old when he became the father of Methuselah.
Genesis 5:22 Enoch lived closely with God for 300 years after Methuselah was born, and he had other sons and daughters.
Genesis 5:23 Enoch lived for 365 years.
Genesis 5:24 Enoch lived close to God, and then he disappeared because God took him away.
Genesis 5:25 Methuselah lived 187 years and had a son named Lamech.
Genesis 5:26 After Methuselah became the father of Lamech, he lived 782 more years and had other sons and daughters.
Genesis 5:27 Methuselah lived 969 years, then he died.
Genesis 5:28 Lamech lived 182 years and had a son.
Genesis 5:29 He named him Noah, saying, "He will bring us comfort from our hard work and the pain of our hands caused by the ground that God has cursed."
Genesis 5:30 After Lamech had Noah, he lived for 595 more years and had other sons and daughters.
Genesis 5:31 Lamech lived a total of 777 years, and then he died.
Genesis 5:32 Noah was 500 years old when he had his sons Shem, Ham, and Japheth.
