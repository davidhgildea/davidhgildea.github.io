James 3:1 My friends, don't all try to be teachers, because we know that we will be judged more strictly.
James 3:2 We all make many mistakes. If someone does not make mistakes when they speak, they are perfect and can control their whole body too.
James 3:3 Look, we put pieces in horse's mouths so they follow us; we guide their whole body.
James 3:4 Look at the ships too. Even though they are big and strong winds push them, a tiny rudder can steer them wherever the pilot chooses.
James 3:5 The tongue is small, yet it talks about big things. See how a small fire can start something so huge!
James 3:6 The tongue is like a fire; it is full of wrong. It can spoil your whole life and is itself set on fire by hell.
James 3:7 Every kind of animal, bird, snake, and sea creature can be tamed and has been tamed by humans.
James 3:8 No one can control the tongue; it is a restless evil, filled with deadly poison.
James 3:9 We use our words to bless God, the Father, and with those same words, we curse people who are made to look like God.
James 3:10 From the same mouth come both blessings and curses. My brothers, this should not be the case.
James 3:11 Can a spring pour out both fresh and bitter water from the same opening?
James 3:12 My friends, can a fig tree produce olives, or a grapevine produce figs? Just as a spring can't give both salty and fresh water.
James 3:13 Who is wise and has knowledge among you? Let them show their good actions with a humble attitude that comes from wisdom.
James 3:14 If you feel bitter jealousy and conflict in your hearts, don't boast or deny the truth.
James 3:15 This wisdom does not come from heaven but is from the earth, appealing to our senses, and evil.
James 3:16 Where jealousy and conflict exist, confusion and all kinds of wrong actions are found.
James 3:17 The wisdom from above is first pure, then peaceful, kind, willing to listen, full of mercy and good actions, fair, and sincere.
James 3:18 The good results of right living are planted in peace by those who work for peace.
