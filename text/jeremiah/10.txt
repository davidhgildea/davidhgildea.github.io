Jeremiah 10:1 Listen to the message that God is giving you, people of Israel.
Jeremiah 10:2 God says, "Don't follow the heathen's ways, and don't be scared of the sky signs; the heathen are scared of them."
Jeremiah 10:3 The ways of the people are worthless: someone cuts down a tree from the woods, made by a worker using an axe.
Jeremiah 10:4 They decorate it with silver and gold; they use nails and hammers to secure it so it doesn't move.
Jeremiah 10:5 They stand straight like palm trees but cannot speak. They need to be carried since they cannot walk. Do not fear them; they cannot do harm, nor can they do good.
Jeremiah 10:6 No one is like you, God; you are great, and your name is powerful.
Jeremiah 10:7 Who wouldn't respect you, King of nations? It belongs to you; because among all the wise people of the nations, and in all their kingdoms, no one is like you.
Jeremiah 10:8 But they are all senseless and foolish; their idol is just a teaching about worthless things.
Jeremiah 10:9 Silver made into sheets is brought from Tarshish and gold from Uphaz, made by craftsmen and metalworkers. Their clothes are blue and purple, all created by skilled artisans.
Jeremiah 10:10 God is the real God, the living God, and a forever king: when he is angry, the earth will shake, and the countries won't stand his anger.
Jeremiah 10:11 Tell them, "The gods who did not create the sky and the earth will vanish from the earth and from under the sky."
Jeremiah 10:12 God made the earth with his power, set up the world with his wisdom, and spread out the skies with his understanding.
Jeremiah 10:13 When he speaks, many waters form in the sky, and he makes vapors rise from the earth's edges; he creates lightning with rain, and brings wind from his storerooms.
Jeremiah 10:14 Everyone is foolish in what they know; every metal worker is ashamed of their idol, because what they pour as an image is a lie, and there is no life in them.
Jeremiah 10:15 They are worthless and make mistakes; when it's time for them to be judged, they will be destroyed.
Jeremiah 10:16 Jacob's share is not like theirs: he is the maker of everything; and Israel is the tribe he inherits: God of all armies is his name.
Jeremiah 10:17 Collect your belongings from the land, person living in the strong place.
Jeremiah 10:18 God says, "I will throw out the people living in the land this time and trouble them so they realize it."
Jeremiah 10:19 I am in trouble because I am hurt! My injury is serious, but I thought, This is truly sad, and I must endure it.
Jeremiah 10:20 My tent is ruined, all its ropes are snapped; my children have left me, and they are gone. No one is left to put up my tent or hang my curtains.
Jeremiah 10:21 The leaders have become foolish and have not looked for God: so they will not succeed, and all their flocks will be spread out.
Jeremiah 10:22 Look, the sound of the report has arrived, and a big disturbance from the north to leave the towns of Judah empty, and a home for dragons.
Jeremiah 10:23 God, I understand that a person's path is not decided by themselves: it is not for the one who walks to choose their own way.
Jeremiah 10:24 God, correct me fairly, not in anger, or I will be destroyed.
Jeremiah 10:25 Release your anger on the nations that don't know you, and on the peoples who don't pray to you: for they have attacked Jacob, destroyed him, and left his home empty.
