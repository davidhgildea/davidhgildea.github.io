Jeremiah 48:1 God says this about Moab: "Trouble is coming to Nebo because it's destroyed. Kiriathaim is captured and shamed. Misgab is terrified and upset."
Jeremiah 48:2 Moab will not be praised anymore; in Heshbon, they plan to destroy it. Come, let's wipe it out as a nation. Madmen, you too will be destroyed; the sword will chase you.
Jeremiah 48:3 Cries and loud noises will come from Horonaim, along with destruction and major damage.
Jeremiah 48:4 Moab is ruined; her young children have made a cry heard.
Jeremiah 48:5 As people climb up Luhith, they keep crying. As they go down to Horonaim, enemies hear a loud sound of ruin.
Jeremiah 48:6 Run away, save yourselves, and be like a bush in the desert.
Jeremiah 48:7 Because you trusted in what you did and in your riches, you will be captured: and Chemosh will be taken away as a prisoner with his priests and leaders.
Jeremiah 48:8 The destroyer will come to every city, and every city will not be spared; the valley will also wither, and the flat land will be ruined, as God has said.
Jeremiah 48:9 Give Moab wings to fly away and escape, because its cities will be empty, with no one living in them.
Jeremiah 48:10 Cursed is the one who does God's work with trickery, and cursed is the one who holds back his sword from drawing blood.
Jeremiah 48:11 Moab has lived comfortably since he was young, settled without being disturbed, and has not been moved from one container to another, nor has he been taken captive; so his ways have stayed the same, and his character has not changed.
Jeremiah 48:12 So, look, the time will come, God says, when I will send travelers to him who will make him wander, empty his containers, and smash his jars.
Jeremiah 48:13 Moab will feel shame for Chemosh just as the people of Israel felt shame for their trust in Bethel.
Jeremiah 48:14 How can you say, "We are powerful and strong warriors"?
Jeremiah 48:15 Moab is destroyed, and people have left its towns, and its best young men have been killed, says the King, whose name is God of armies.
Jeremiah 48:16 Moab's disaster is coming soon, and his trouble is speeding up.
Jeremiah 48:17 Everyone around him, mourn for him; and everyone who knows his name, ask, "How is the powerful stick broken, and the lovely stick!"
Jeremiah 48:18 You who live in Dibon, come down from your high place and sit in thirst; for the destroyer of Moab will attack you and break down your safe places.
Jeremiah 48:19 People of Aroer, stand by the road, watch, and ask both the person who is running away and the person who has escaped, "What happened?"
Jeremiah 48:20 Moab is embarrassed; it is destroyed: wail and weep; say it in Arnon, that Moab is ruined.
Jeremiah 48:21 God's judgment has come to the flat land, to Holon, to Jahazah, and to Mephaath.
Jeremiah 48:22 On Dibon, Nebo, and Bethdiblathaim,
Jeremiah 48:23 On Kiriathaim, on Bethgamul, and on Bethmeon,
Jeremiah 48:24 And on Kerioth, and on Bozrah, and on all the cities of Moab, far and near.
Jeremiah 48:25 Moab's strength is gone, and his power is broken, says God.
Jeremiah 48:26 Make him drunk because he acted arrogantly against God. Moab will roll in his own throw-up, and he will be mocked as well.
Jeremiah 48:27 Didn't you mock Israel? Did you catch him with thieves? You've been happy talking about him.
Jeremiah 48:28 You who live in Moab, leave the cities, live in the cliffs, and be like the dove that builds her nest on the edge of a cave's entrance.
Jeremiah 48:29 We have heard how proud Moab is, very proud indeed, of his greatness, his arrogance, his pride, and his conceited heart.
Jeremiah 48:30 I know his anger, says God, but it won't happen like that; his lies won't succeed.
Jeremiah 48:31 So, I will wail for Moab and cry for all of Moab; my heart will grieve for the people of Kirheres.
Jeremiah 48:32 O Sibmah vine, I will cry for you like the cries of Jazer: your branches have spread across the sea, even to Jazer's sea: the destroyer has attacked your summer fruit and harvest.
Jeremiah 48:33 Happiness and joy have been removed from the fertile fields and the land of Moab; I have made the wine stop flowing from the winepresses: no one will step on grapes with cheers; their cheers won't be heard.
Jeremiah 48:34 People are screaming from Heshbon to Elealeh and Jahaz, and from Zoar to Horonaim, like young cows. Even Nimrim's waters will be empty.
Jeremiah 48:35 I will stop people in Moab from making sacrifices on the high hills and burning incense to their gods, says God.
Jeremiah 48:36 So my heart will cry for Moab like flutes, and my heart will be like flutes for the people of Kir Heres, for the wealth they gained is gone.
Jeremiah 48:37 Every head will be shaved, every beard trimmed; all hands will be scratched, and around the waist, there will be rough cloth.
Jeremiah 48:38 People will mourn on all the rooftops in Moab and in its streets, because I have shattered Moab like a useless pot, says God.
Jeremiah 48:39 They will cry out, "How it is destroyed! How Moab has run away in shame!" So Moab will become a joke and a terror to all nearby.
Jeremiah 48:40 For this is what God says: Look, he will soar like an eagle and spread his wings over Moab.
Jeremiah 48:41 Kerioth is captured, and the strongholds are taken by surprise, and on that day, the brave soldiers in Moab will be as scared as a woman having a baby.
Jeremiah 48:42 Moab will be ruined and no longer be a nation because it boasted against God.
Jeremiah 48:43 Danger, a hole, and a trap will come to you, people of Moab, says God.
Jeremiah 48:44 The one who runs from danger will fall into a hole, and whoever climbs out of the hole will get caught in a trap, because I will bring disaster on Moab when I punish them, says God.
Jeremiah 48:45 Those who ran away stayed in the shade of Heshbon because of the enemy. But a fire will start from Heshbon, and a flame from Sihon, and it will burn the edges of Moab and the tops of the noisy people's heads.
Jeremiah 48:46 Sadness comes to you, Moab! The followers of Chemosh are destroyed: your sons are captured, and your daughters are captured.
Jeremiah 48:47 But I will restore Moab from captivity in the future, says God. This is God's judgment on Moab.
