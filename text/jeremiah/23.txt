Jeremiah 23:1 God says, "Trouble will come to the leaders who ruin and disperse the sheep of my land!"
Jeremiah 23:2 So this is what God of Israel says to the leaders who take care of my people: You have scattered my sheep and made them run away, and you did not care for them. Look, I will punish you for the bad things you've done, says God.
Jeremiah 23:3 I will bring the remaining ones of my flock from all the countries where I sent them, and they will return to their homes; they will have many children and grow in number.
Jeremiah 23:4 I will put shepherds in charge of them who will take care of them; they will not be afraid or discouraged or be in need anymore, says God.
Jeremiah 23:5 Look, the time will come, God says, when I will bring forth a righteous descendant from David, and a King will rule successfully, carrying out fair and just decisions on the earth.
Jeremiah 23:6 During his time, Judah will be saved and Israel will live in safety. And he will be called God Our Righteousness.
Jeremiah 23:7 So, God says, "Look, the time is coming when people won't say anymore, 'God is alive, who brought the Israelites out of Egypt.'"
Jeremiah 23:8 But God lives, who brought and led the descendants of Israel out of the north and from all countries where I had sent them; and they will live in their own land.
Jeremiah 23:9 My heart is broken because of the prophets; my whole body trembles; I am like a drunk man, beaten by wine, because of God and his holy words.
Jeremiah 23:10 The land is full of cheaters; because of cursing, the land is sad; the nice parts of the desert have dried out, people's actions are bad, and their power is not used rightly.
Jeremiah 23:11 Both the prophet and the priest are unholy; yes, I have seen their evil in my house, says God.
Jeremiah 23:12 So their path will be like slippery roads in the dark: they will be pushed and will fall on it, because I will bring disaster on them, the time when I punish them, says God.
Jeremiah 23:13 I have seen foolishness in Samaria's prophets; they spoke messages from Baal and led my people Israel astray.
Jeremiah 23:14 I have also seen terrible things among Jerusalem's prophets: they are unfaithful and live in lies; they encourage the wicked so no one turns from their evil ways; to me, they are like Sodom and its people like Gomorrah.
Jeremiah 23:15 So this is what God says about the prophets: I will give them bitter food to eat and poison water to drink, because the prophets of Jerusalem have spread corruption throughout the land.
Jeremiah 23:16 God says, "Don't listen to the prophets who speak to you; they fill you with false hopes. They talk about visions from their own minds, not from God."
Jeremiah 23:17 They keep telling those who hate me, "God has said, you will have peace"; and they tell everyone who follows their own desires, "No harm will come to you."
Jeremiah 23:18 Who has been in God's council and understood and listened to his message? Who has paid attention to his message and heard it?
Jeremiah 23:19 Look, a violent storm from God has come out in anger, a terrible storm: it will hit the wicked very hard.
Jeremiah 23:20 God's anger will not stop until He has done what He plans. In the future, you will fully understand this.
Jeremiah 23:21 I did not send these prophets, but they went; I did not speak to them, but they spoke my message.
Jeremiah 23:22 If they had followed my advice and had made my people listen to my words, then they would have turned away from their bad behavior and their wicked actions.
Jeremiah 23:23 Am I not a close God, says God, and not a distant one?
Jeremiah 23:24 Can anyone hide in secret places where I won't see him? says God. Don't I fill heaven and earth? says God.
Jeremiah 23:25 I have heard the prophets who tell lies in my name, saying, "I had a dream, I had a dream."
Jeremiah 23:26 How long will the prophets who tell lies plan this in their hearts? Yes, they are prophets led by their own deceit.
Jeremiah 23:27 They try to make my people forget my name with the dreams they share with each other, just like their ancestors forgot my name for Baal.
Jeremiah 23:28 The prophet who has a dream should share that dream; and whoever has my message should speak it truthfully. What do the worthless bits matter compared to the grain? says God.
Jeremiah 23:29 Isn't my word like a fire? says God, and like a hammer that breaks the rock into pieces?
Jeremiah 23:30 So, I am against the prophets who take my words from each other, says God.
Jeremiah 23:31 Look, I am against the prophets, says God, who speak on their own and claim, 'God says.'
Jeremiah 23:32 Look, I am against those who tell false dreams, says God, and share them, leading my people astray with their lies and carelessness; I did not send them or tell them to do this: so they will not help my people at all, says God.
Jeremiah 23:33 When the people, a prophet, or a priest ask you, "What is God's message?" You should say, "What message? God says He will abandon you."
Jeremiah 23:34 If a prophet, a priest, or anyone else claims, "This is God's message," I will punish that person and his family.
Jeremiah 23:35 Everyone should ask their neighbor and their brother, "What has God answered?" and "What has God said?"
Jeremiah 23:36 You should not talk about God's burden anymore: everyone's own words will be their burden because you have twisted the words of the living God, our God of all.
Jeremiah 23:37 You should ask the prophet, "What has God replied to you?" and "What has God said?"
Jeremiah 23:38 Since you say, "God's message," this is what God says: "Because you call it 'God's message,' even though I told you not to call it 'God's message,'"
Jeremiah 23:39 So, look, I myself will completely forget you, and I will leave you, and the city I gave to you and your ancestors, and I will throw you out of my sight.
Jeremiah 23:40 I will make you suffer lasting shame and disgrace that will never be forgotten.
