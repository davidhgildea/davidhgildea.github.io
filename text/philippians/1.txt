Philippians 1:1 Paul and Timothy, who serve Jesus Christ, send greetings to all God's holy people in Philippi, along with the overseers and helpers.
Philippians 1:2 May grace and peace be with you from God our Father and from Jesus Christ.
Philippians 1:3 I thank God every time I remember you.
Philippians 1:4 I always pray for all of you with joy in every one of my prayers.
Philippians 1:5 For sharing in the good news from the beginning until now;
Philippians 1:6 I am sure that God, who started a good work in you, will keep going until it is finished when Jesus Christ comes.
Philippians 1:7 It is right for me to feel this way about all of you, because I hold you in my heart. You all share in God's grace with me, whether I'm in prison or defending and confirming the good news.
Philippians 1:8 God knows how much I deeply miss all of you with the love of Jesus Christ.
Philippians 1:9 I pray that your love grows more and more, along with your knowledge and insight.
Philippians 1:10 You should choose what is best so that you may be true and blameless until the day of Christ.
Philippians 1:11 Being filled with good things that come from doing right, which are through Jesus Christ, for the glory and praise of God.
Philippians 1:12 I want you to know, friends, that what has happened to me has really helped spread the good news.
Philippians 1:13 So that everyone in the palace and everywhere else knows that I am in chains for Christ.
Philippians 1:14 Many believers in God, seeing my imprisonment, are now much braver to talk about God's message fearlessly.
Philippians 1:15 Some people spread the message about Christ because they are jealous and like to argue; but others do it with good intentions.
Philippians 1:16 Some preach about Christ with bad intentions, not honestly, thinking they will make my imprisonment harder.
Philippians 1:17 But the other preaches out of love, knowing that I stand to defend the gospel.
Philippians 1:18 So what? In every way, whether fake or real, Christ is talked about; and I'm happy about this, yes, and I'll keep being happy.
Philippians 1:19 I know that this will lead to my rescue because of your prayers and Jesus Christ's Spirit's help.
Philippians 1:20 I really hope and expect that I won't be ashamed in any way. I want to be very brave now as I always have been, and have Christ honored in my body, whether I live or die.
Philippians 1:21 For me, living means following Christ, and dying is a benefit.
Philippians 1:22 If I continue living in my body, this means I can keep working. But I do not know what I should choose.
Philippians 1:23 I am torn between two choices: wanting to leave and be with Christ, which is much better.
Philippians 1:24 Staying alive is more important for your sake.
Philippians 1:25 Because I am sure of this, I know that I will stay and be with all of you to help your faith grow and to share joy in believing.
Philippians 1:26 I want you to be happier about Jesus Christ because of my visit to you again.
Philippians 1:27 Make sure you live in a way that fits with the good news about Christ. This way, whether I visit you or I'm away, I can hear about you. I want to know that you are standing strong together with one purpose, working as a team for the faith the good news brings.
Philippians 1:28 Don't be scared of your enemies at all: to them it's a clear sign they'll be ruined, but for you it means being saved, which comes from God.
Philippians 1:29 God has granted you the gift not only to believe in Christ but also to endure hardship for him.
Philippians 1:30 You are facing the same struggle that you saw I had, and now you hear that I still have.
