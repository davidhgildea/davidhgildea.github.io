Philippians 2:1 So, if there is any comfort from being united with Christ, if any love brings comfort, if we share in the Spirit, if there is any warmth and kindness,
Philippians 2:2 Make my joy complete by agreeing with each other, sharing the same love, and being united in thoughts and purpose.
Philippians 2:3 Do nothing out of fighting or empty pride; instead, with humble minds, value others more than yourselves.
Philippians 2:4 Don't just look out for your own interests, but also for the interests of others.
Philippians 2:5 Have the same mindset as Jesus Christ did.
Philippians 2:6 Even though he was like God, he did not see being equal with God as something to hold onto.
Philippians 2:7 He made himself humble, became a servant, and was born as a human.
Philippians 2:8 Being found as a man, he became humble and obeyed to the point of death, even dying on a cross.
Philippians 2:9 So God has lifted him high and given him a name greater than any other name.
Philippians 2:10 Everyone should kneel to honor Jesus' name - those in heaven, on earth, and under the earth.
Philippians 2:11 Everyone should agree that Jesus Christ is the Lord to honor God the Father.
Philippians 2:12 So, my dear friends, just as you have always followed, not only when I am with you but even more now that I'm not, keep working on your own rescue with respect and care.
Philippians 2:13 God is the one who makes you want to do and actually do what pleases Him.
Philippians 2:14 Do everything without complaining or arguing.
Philippians 2:15 So that you may be pure and good, children of God, without fault, in a wicked and twisted world, where you shine like stars.
Philippians 2:16 Offering the message of life; so I can be joyful when Christ comes, knowing my efforts were not wasted.
Philippians 2:17 Yes, and if I am like a drink offering on the offering and service of your faith, I am happy and share joy with all of you.
Philippians 2:18 For this reason, you too be glad and share my joy.
Philippians 2:19 I hope Jesus will let me send Timothy to you soon, so I can feel better after learning how you are doing.
Philippians 2:20 I have no one else who truly cares for your well-being.
Philippians 2:21 Everyone looks out for their own interests, not those of Jesus Christ.
Philippians 2:22 You know how he has shown himself, like a son with his father, working with me to spread the good news.
Philippians 2:23 So I plan to send him to you soon, once I know what will happen with me.
Philippians 2:24 I trust in God that I will also come soon.
Philippians 2:25 I thought it was important to send Epaphroditus to you – my brother, coworker, and fellow soldier – who is also your messenger and has helped me with my needs.
Philippians 2:26 He really missed you all and was very worried because you heard he was ill.
Philippians 2:27 He was really sick, almost dying, but God showed mercy on him; not just on him, but on me too, so I wouldn't have even more sadness.
Philippians 2:28 I sent him with extra care so that when you see him again, you can be happy and I can be less sad.
Philippians 2:29 Welcome him in the name of God with joy; and respect such people.
Philippians 2:30 He was near death because of his work for Christ, risking his life to help me when you could not.
