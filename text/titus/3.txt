Titus 3:1 Remind them to follow rulers and authorities, to listen to leaders, and to be prepared for all good actions.
Titus 3:2 Don't say bad things about anyone, avoid fighting, be kind, and show humbleness to everyone.
Titus 3:3 We were once foolish, disobedient, misled, chasing many desires and pleasures, living with meanness and jealousy, full of hate, and hating each other.
Titus 3:4 But then God our Savior showed us kindness and love.
Titus 3:5 We are not saved by our good deeds, but by God's kindness. He saved us by giving us a new birth and a fresh start through the Holy Spirit.
Titus 3:6 He gave to us generously through Jesus Christ our Savior.
Titus 3:7 Being made right with God by His kindness, we should become inheritors based on the hope of everlasting life.
Titus 3:8 This true saying I want you to always confirm: those who trust in God should work hard to do good. These actions are good and helpful to people.
Titus 3:9 Stay away from silly questions, family histories, arguments, and fights about the law; they are useless and pointless.
Titus 3:10 Turn away from someone who keeps causing divisions after being warned once and then again.
Titus 3:11 Understand that someone like that has gone wrong, is sinning, and has judged himself guilty.
Titus 3:12 When I send Artemas or Tychicus to you, make sure to join me in Nicopolis, because I plan to stay there during the winter.
Titus 3:13 Take care to help Zenas the lawyer and Apollos on their journey carefully, so they lack nothing.
Titus 3:14 Let ours also learn to do good deeds for important needs, so they are not unproductive.
Titus 3:15 Everyone with me says hello to you. Say hi to those who love us because of our shared faith. May kindness be with all of you. Goodbye.
