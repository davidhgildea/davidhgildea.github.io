Titus 2:1 Teach what matches with good teaching.
Titus 2:2 Older men should be self-controlled, serious, wise, strong in faith, loving, and patient.
Titus 2:3 Older women should also act in a way that fits with a holy life, not spreading rumors, not drinking too much, and teaching what is good.
Titus 2:4 To help young women be sensible, love their husbands, and love their children.
Titus 2:5 To be wise, pure, take care of their homes, kind, and listen to their husbands, so God's message is not spoken against.
Titus 2:6 Advise young men also to be self-controlled.
Titus 2:7 In everything, be an example of good actions: teach with honesty, seriousness, and truthfulness.
Titus 2:8 Speak clearly and rightly so no one can find fault with you, and those who disagree might feel embarrassed because they can't say anything bad about you.
Titus 2:9 Tell workers to listen to their bosses, try hard to make them happy in everything, and not talk back.
Titus 2:10 Do not steal, but show complete loyalty, so that in everything you may make the teachings of God our Savior attractive.
Titus 2:11 God's grace that offers salvation has been shown to everyone.
Titus 2:12 This teaches us to say no to ungodly ways and worldly desires, and to live self-controlled, upright, and godly lives in this world.
Titus 2:13 Waiting for the happy hope and the wonderful coming of the great God and our Savior Jesus Christ.
Titus 2:14 He gave himself for us, so he could rescue us from all wrongs and clean us for himself, to be his special people who are eager to do good.
Titus 2:15 Talk about these things, encourage others, and correct them with full authority. Don't let anyone look down on you.
