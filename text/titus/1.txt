Titus 1:1 Paul, a servant of God and an apostle of Jesus Christ, follows the faith of God's chosen ones and recognizes the truth that leads to a godly life.
Titus 1:2 Hoping for eternal life, which God, who cannot lie, promised before the world started.
Titus 1:3 God showed his message at the right time through preaching, which was given to me by the order of God our Savior.
Titus 1:4 To Titus, my true son in our shared faith: May you have kindness, compassion, and peace from God the Father and Jesus Christ our Savior.
Titus 1:5 I left you in Crete so you could fix what was needed and appoint leaders in each town, as I told you.
Titus 1:6 If anyone is without fault, married to one wife, and has trustworthy children who are not accused of being wild or disobedient.
Titus 1:7 A leader in the church must be without fault, as God's manager; not stubborn, not quick to anger, not addicted to alcohol, not violent, not greedy for money.
Titus 1:8 But someone who welcomes guests, likes good people, is self-controlled, fair, holy, and disciplined;
Titus 1:9 Sticking to the true message as taught to him, so he can use solid teaching to encourage and prove wrong those who disagree.
Titus 1:10 There are many who do not obey, who talk for no good reason and trick others, especially those who insist on circumcision.
Titus 1:11 These people who confuse entire families by teaching wrong things for dishonest gain must be silenced.
Titus 1:12 One of their own prophets said, 'People from Crete are always lying, they're bad creatures, and they are lazy.'
Titus 1:13 This testimony is true. So correct them firmly, so they will have a strong faith.
Titus 1:14 Do not pay attention to Jewish stories or human rules that turn people away from the truth.
Titus 1:15 To the pure, everything is pure; but to those who are spoiled and don't believe, nothing is pure because their minds and hearts are spoiled.
Titus 1:16 They claim to know God, but their actions deny Him, being hateful, disobedient, and useless for any good work.
