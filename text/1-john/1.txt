1 John 1:1 What was there from the start, which we have heard, seen with our own eyes, and touched with our hands, is about the message of life.
1 John 1:2 The life appeared, we have seen it, and we tell you about it. We show you the eternal life that was with God and was shown to us.
1 John 1:3 We tell you what we've seen and heard so you can join us. Our friendship is with God and his Son Jesus Christ.
1 John 1:4 We write these things to you so that your joy can be complete.
1 John 1:5 We heard a message from him and now tell you: God is light, with no darkness at all.
1 John 1:6 If we claim to have a close relationship with God but live in the wrong way, we are lying and not living by the truth.
1 John 1:7 But if we live in the truth, as he is in the truth, we share a bond with each other, and the blood of Jesus Christ his Son cleans us from all sin.
1 John 1:8 If we claim we have no sin, we lie to ourselves, and we don't have the truth.
1 John 1:9 If we admit our sins, God is loyal and fair to forgive us our sins, and to make us clean from all wrongdoing.
1 John 1:10 If we say we haven't sinned, we call God a liar, and his message isn't in us.
