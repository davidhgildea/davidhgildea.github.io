2 Samuel 22:1 David sang to God this song when God saved him from all his enemies and from Saul.
2 Samuel 22:2 He said, "God is my rock, my fortress, and my savior."
2 Samuel 22:3 God is my protector; I will trust in him: he is my shield, my strength to save me, my safe place, and my shelter, my savior; you protect me from harm.
2 Samuel 22:4 I will call on God, who deserves praise: so I will be saved from my enemies.
2 Samuel 22:5 When the waves of death surrounded me, the attacks of evil men scared me.
2 Samuel 22:6 The pains of death surrounded me; death's traps were stopping me.
2 Samuel 22:7 In my trouble, I called to God and shouted for help. He heard my voice from his place, and my call reached his ears.
2 Samuel 22:8 The earth shook and quaked; the bases of the sky trembled and moved because God was angry.
2 Samuel 22:9 Smoke rose from his nose, and fire from his mouth consumed; coals blazed from it.
2 Samuel 22:10 He bent the skies and came down; darkness was under his feet.
2 Samuel 22:11 He rode on a cherub and flew; he appeared on the wings of the wind.
2 Samuel 22:12 He made dark shelters around him, with dark waters and heavy clouds in the sky.
2 Samuel 22:13 Bright light from God made coals of fire start burning.
2 Samuel 22:14 God thundered from heaven, and the highest one spoke aloud.
2 Samuel 22:15 He shot arrows and spread them out; he sent lightning and confused them.
2 Samuel 22:16 The paths of the sea were shown, the base of the earth was uncovered, when God scolded, with the strong wind from his nose.
2 Samuel 22:17 He reached down from on high and took hold of me; he pulled me out of deep waters.
2 Samuel 22:18 God saved me from my powerful enemy and from those who hated me because they were too strong for me.
2 Samuel 22:19 They stopped me when I was in trouble, but God supported me.
2 Samuel 22:20 God brought me out into a spacious place; he freed me because he was pleased with me.
2 Samuel 22:21 God rewarded me for doing right; he repaid me because my hands were clean.
2 Samuel 22:22 I have followed God's paths and have not turned away from my God to do wrong.
2 Samuel 22:23 I kept all God's rules in mind, and I did not turn away from his laws.
2 Samuel 22:24 I was also honest before God, and I have kept myself from doing wrong.
2 Samuel 22:25 So God has rewarded me for doing right; He has seen my clean actions.
2 Samuel 22:26 You are kind to those who are kind, and you are good to those who are good.
2 Samuel 22:27 You are pure to those who are pure, but you seem not good to those who are not good.
2 Samuel 22:28 You will save those who are suffering, but you are watching the proud, to bring them down.
2 Samuel 22:29 You are my light, God, and God will brighten my darkness.
2 Samuel 22:30 With your help, I have charged through an army; with my God, I have jumped over a wall.
2 Samuel 22:31 God's way is perfect; his word is proven; he protects all who trust in him.
2 Samuel 22:32 Who is God besides the LORD? Who is strong like our God?
2 Samuel 22:33 God is my strength and power, and he makes my path perfect.
2 Samuel 22:34 He makes my feet as sure as a deer's: and places me on high ground.
2 Samuel 22:35 He teaches my hands to fight; so that my arms can break a steel bow.
2 Samuel 22:36 You have given me the shield of your salvation, and your kindness has made me strong.
2 Samuel 22:37 You have made my steps wide beneath me; so my feet did not slide.
2 Samuel 22:38 I chased my enemies and defeated them; I did not stop until I had completely beaten them.
2 Samuel 22:39 I have destroyed them and injured them so they could not get up; yes, they have fallen beneath my feet.
2 Samuel 22:40 You have given me strength to fight; you have made those who attacked me fall under my control.
2 Samuel 22:41 You have also let me defeat my enemies and destroy those who hate me.
2 Samuel 22:42 They looked for help, but no one could save them; they even cried to God, but he did not respond.
2 Samuel 22:43 I crushed them into dust on the ground, stomped them into mud on the street, and scattered them everywhere.
2 Samuel 22:44 You have also saved me from my people's conflicts and kept me as the leader of nations; people I didn't know will serve me.
2 Samuel 22:45 Foreigners will come to me and obey as soon as they hear me.
2 Samuel 22:46 Foreigners will grow weak and be scared from their hiding spots.
2 Samuel 22:47 God lives; praise to my rock; and may the God who saves me be lifted high.
2 Samuel 22:48 God avenges me and makes other people submit to me.
2 Samuel 22:49 You lead me away from my enemies; you lift me up above my opponents; you save me from violent people.
2 Samuel 22:50 So I will thank you, God, among the nations, and sing praises to your name.
2 Samuel 22:51 He is the saving tower for his king and shows kindness to his chosen one, David, and his descendants forever.
