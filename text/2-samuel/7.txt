2 Samuel 7:1 When the king was in his house, and God had given him peace from all his enemies;
2 Samuel 7:2 The king said to the prophet Nathan, "Look, I live in a cedar house, but God's ark stays in a tent."
2 Samuel 7:3 Nathan told the king, "Go ahead and do everything you want to do because God is with you."
2 Samuel 7:4 That night, God's message came to Nathan, saying,
2 Samuel 7:5 Go and tell my servant David, God says, Will you build me a house to live in?
2 Samuel 7:6 Since I led the people of Israel out of Egypt, I have never lived in a house. Instead, I have moved in a tent and in a shelter.
2 Samuel 7:7 In every place where I have been with all of Israel's children, did I ever ask any tribe I told to take care of my people Israel, "Why don't you build me a cedar house?"
2 Samuel 7:8 So now, tell my servant David this: God says, 'I chose you from the pasture and from tending the sheep to be the leader of my people, Israel.'
2 Samuel 7:9 I was with you wherever you went, and I defeated all your enemies, making your name as great as the most famous people on earth.
2 Samuel 7:10 I will provide a home for my people Israel and settle them so they can live in their own place without moving again; evil people will not trouble them anymore, like in the past.
2 Samuel 7:11 Ever since I appointed leaders for my people Israel and gave you peace from all your enemies, God has promised to build you a house.
2 Samuel 7:12 When your time comes to an end and you die, I will raise up your descendants to follow you, one who will come from your own body, and I will make his kingdom strong.
2 Samuel 7:13 He will build a house for my name, and I will establish his kingdom's throne forever.
2 Samuel 7:14 I will be his father, and he will be my son. If he does wrong, I will correct him with punishment from people and their whippings.
2 Samuel 7:15 My kindness will not leave him, as I took it from Saul, whom I removed before you.
2 Samuel 7:16 Your family and kingdom will last forever; your rule will go on forever.
2 Samuel 7:17 Nathan told David everything he had heard and seen.
2 Samuel 7:18 Then King David went in, sat before God, and said, "Who am I, God, and what is my family, that you have brought me this far?"
2 Samuel 7:19 This was still a small thing to you, God, but you also promised good things for your servant's family far into the future. Is this how people normally act, God?
2 Samuel 7:20 What more can David say to you? You, God, know your servant.
2 Samuel 7:21 For the sake of your promise and your own desire, you have done all these amazing things so that your servant could understand them.
2 Samuel 7:22 You are great, God, because there is no one like you, and there is no other God, just as we have heard.
2 Samuel 7:23 What other nation on earth is like your people Israel? God chose them to be his own, to make himself known, and to perform great and awe-inspiring acts for them—clearing out nations and their gods to free Israel from Egypt.
2 Samuel 7:24 You have made the people of Israel your own people forever, and you, God, have become their God.
2 Samuel 7:25 Now, God, make true your promise about your servant and his family forever, and do as you have promised.
2 Samuel 7:26 May your name be honored always, as people say, "God rules over Israel," and may David's family remain strong in your presence.
2 Samuel 7:27 You, God of all and God of Israel, have told your servant that you will build a house for him. So your servant has decided to pray this prayer to you.
2 Samuel 7:28 And now, God, you are that God, your words are true, and you have promised this goodness to your servant.
2 Samuel 7:29 Please bless your servant's house so it can last forever in your presence. You, God, have promised this; may your blessing make your servant's house forever blessed.
